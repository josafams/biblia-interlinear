The whole Bible, word by word.

The 66 books in the original, each word with transliteration, pronunciation, Strong's number and an English gloss, and under each verse the text of the Berean Standard Bible. Free, open and without ads — in the browser, on the phone, in EPUB and in PDF.

Listen to the original

The verse is read in Hebrew or Greek by the device's voice, highlighting each word; it can speak more slowly.

Reading modes

Read only the Berean Standard Bible, like an ordinary Bible, only the original, both side by side, the interlinear or the study mode — or build your own.

Highlight and take notes

Colors on verses, passages or words of the original, notes, bookmarks and tags — all kept on your device, without an account.

PDF notebook and backup

Export your notes and highlights as a study notebook in PDF, with the text and the interlinear. Download everything in one file and import it on another device.

Optional: sign in with Google and your notes and reading mode follow you on the phone and the computer. They stay in a private app folder in your Drive — the project has no server and does not see your data.

Books to download: EPUB and PDF

They are on the repository's Releases page and GitHub builds them again with each correction to the data, so the links always bring the latest version. In the EPUB, each word of the original is a note that opens on tap (it works on the Kindle). The PDF is A4, with the word cards, the Berean Standard Bible under each verse and bookmarks by book and chapter.

On the Kindle: send the EPUB with Send to Kindle (up to 200 MB from the app or website; by email, up to 50 MB — use the OT, the NT or one book).

How it is made

Only open sources, from start to finish

Everything can be copied and redistributed. The glosses are the project's and are not part of the Berean Standard Bible.

Limitations: the glosses come from automatic alignment and are sometimes misplaced; themes and introductions still await human review. The ⚑ sign on a verse shows what the automatic review found.

  1. 1
    Original texts

    Hebrew and Aramaic from the Westminster Leningrad Codex (Open Scriptures) and Greek from the SBL Greek New Testament with the morphology of MorphGNT.

  2. 2
    Berean Standard Bible

    The running text in English, verse by verse, with the numbering of English Bibles.

  3. 3
    Glosses by alignment

    Each word of the original is linked to the part of the Berean Standard Bible that translates it (eflomal), completed by rules and by review.

  4. 4
    Lexicon and introductions

    Strong's dictionary, the introduction of each book and the theme of each chapter.

  5. 5
    Automatic reviews and tests

    Checks on the data and tests of the website with each change, before publishing the website, the app, the EPUBs and the PDFs.

Found a wrong gloss?

Anyone can suggest corrections to glosses, lexicon entries and introductions. The guide explains the way, from the file to the pull request.
