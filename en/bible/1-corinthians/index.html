1 Corinthians Πρὸς Κορινθίους Αʹ

In 1 Corinthians, Paul writes from Ephesus to a church he himself had founded and that was going through many problems at once. Some said “I follow Paul,” others “I follow Apollos” or “I follow Cephas” (1:12); there was a case of incestuous union, lawsuits between brothers in the courts, doubts about marriage and about eating meat offered to idols, disorder at the Lord's Supper and disputes over spiritual gifts. Paul deals with each question from the standpoint of the cross of Christ, which contradicts the wisdom and prestige valued by the city, and of love, which puts the good of the other above one's own freedom. Chapter 13, on love, is among Paul's best-known texts, and chapter 15 is his longest exposition on the resurrection of Jesus and of the dead.

Structure

  1. Divisions and the wisdom of the cross chs. 1–4

    Paul combats the factions formed around leaders: the cross is “foolishness” to the world and the power of God to those who believe, and the apostles are only servants.

  2. Problems of conduct chs. 5–6

    A man living with his father's wife, brothers suing each other in pagan courts and the use of the body with prostitutes.

  3. Marriage and celibacy ch. 7

    Answers to the questions in the Corinthians' letter about married life, separation, virgins and widows.

  4. Meat offered to idols chs. 8–10

    The knowledge that idols are nothing does not authorize wounding a brother's conscience; Paul gives his own example of one who gives up rights and recalls the history of Israel in the wilderness.

  5. The community's worship chs. 11–14

    Head covering when praying and prophesying, the Lord's Supper, the gifts of the Spirit, love as the more excellent way and order between prophecy and tongues.

  6. The resurrection ch. 15

    The tradition received about the death and the appearances of Christ and the answer to those who denied the resurrection of the dead.

  7. Collection and farewell ch. 16

    The collection for the Christians in Jerusalem, travel plans, recommendations and greetings.
