Micah 6

God's case against His people

Open in the interlinear reader

שִׁמְעוּhear נָאnow אֵת[obj] אֲשֶׁרthat יְהוָהLORD אֹמֵרsays קוּםarise רִיבplead your case אֶתwith הֶהָרִיםthe mountains וְתִשְׁמַעְנָהhear הַגְּבָעוֹתthe hills קוֹלֶךָyour voice

Hear now what the LORD says: “Arise, plead your case before the mountains, and let the hills hear your voice.

6:2

שִׁמְעוּhear הָרִיםmountains אֶת[obj] רִיבindictment יְהוָהLORD’s וְהָאֵתָנִיםyou enduring מֹסְדֵיfoundations אָרֶץearth כִּיfor רִיבcase לַיהוָהLORD has a עִםagainst עַמּוֹhis people וְעִםagainst יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael יִתְוַכָּחargue

Hear, O mountains, the LORD’s indictment, you enduring foundations of the earth. For the LORD has a case against His people, and He will argue it against Israel:

6:3

עַמִּיmy people מֶהwhat עָשִׂיתִיdone לְךָto you וּמָהhow הֶלְאֵתִיךָwearied you עֲנֵהtestify בִּיagainst me

‘My people, what have I done to you? Testify against Me how I have wearied you!

6:4

כִּיfor הֶעֱלִתִיךָbrought you מֵאֶרֶץfrom the land מִצְרַיִםMiriam וּמִבֵּיתfrom the house עֲבָדִיםslavery פְּדִיתִיךָredeemed you וָאֶשְׁלַחI sent לְפָנֶיךָbefore you אֶת[obj] מֹשֶׁהMoses אַהֲרֹןAaron וּמִרְיָםand Miriam

For I brought you up from the land of Egypt and redeemed you from the house of slavery. I sent Moses before you, as well as Aaron and Miriam.

6:5

עַמִּיmy people זְכָרremember נָאplease מַהwhat יָּעַץcounseled בָּלָקBalak מֶלֶךְking מוֹאָבMoab וּמֶהand what עָנָהanswered אֹתוֹhim בִּלְעָםBalaam בֶּןson בְּעוֹרBeor מִןfrom הַשִּׁטִּיםShittim עַדto הַגִּלְגָּלGilgal that לְמַעַןso דַּעַתacknowledge צִדְקוֹתrighteousness יְהוָהLORD

My people, remember what Balak king of Moab counseled and what Balaam son of Beor answered. Remember your journey from Shittim to Gilgal, so that you may acknowledge the righteousness of the LORD.’”

6:6

בַּמָּהwith what אֲקַדֵּםcome before יְהוָהLORD אִכַּףbow לֵאלֹהֵיbefore the God מָרוֹםhigh הַאֲקַדְּמֶנּוּshould I come to him בְעוֹלוֹתwith burnt offerings בַּעֲגָלִיםcalves בְּנֵיyear old שָׁנָהold

With what shall I come before the LORD when I bow before the God on high? Should I come to Him with burnt offerings, with year-old calves?

6:7

הֲיִרְצֶהpleased יְהוָהLORD בְּאַלְפֵיwith thousands אֵילִיםrams בְּרִבְבוֹתwith ten thousand נַחֲלֵיrivers שָׁמֶןoil הַאֶתֵּןshall I בְּכוֹרִיpresent my firstborn פִּשְׁעִיmy transgression פְּרִיfruit בִטְנִיmy body חַטַּאתsin נַפְשִׁיmy soul

Would the LORD be pleased with thousands of rams, with ten thousand rivers of oil? Shall I present my firstborn for my transgression, the fruit of my body for the sin of my soul?

6:8

הִגִּידshown לְךָto you אָדָםman מַהwhat טּוֹבgood וּמָהand what יְהוָהLORD דּוֹרֵשׁrequire מִמְּךָof you כִּיbut אִםif עֲשׂוֹתact מִשְׁפָּטjustly וְאַהֲבַתto love חֶסֶדmercy וְהַצְנֵעַhumbly לֶכֶתwalk עִםwith אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God

He has shown you, O man, what is good. And what does the LORD require of you but to act justly, to love mercy, and to walk humbly with your God?

6:9

קוֹלvoice יְהוָהLORD לָעִירto the city יִקְרָאcalls וְתוּשִׁיָּהsound wisdom יִרְאֶהfear שְׁמֶךָyour name שִׁמְעוּheed מַטֶּהrod וּמִיwho יְעָדָהּordained it

The voice of the LORD calls out to the city (and it is sound wisdom to fear Your name): “Heed the rod and the One who ordained it.

6:10

עוֹדlonger הַאִשׁcan I forget בֵּיתhouse רָשָׁעwicked אֹצְרוֹתtreasures רֶשַׁעwickedness וְאֵיפַתephah רָזוֹןshort which זְעוּמָהaccursed

Can I forget any longer, O house of the wicked, the treasures of wickedness and the short ephah, which is accursed?

6:11

הַאֶזְכֶּהcan I excuse בְּמֹאזְנֵיscales רֶשַׁעdishonest וּבְכִיסor bags אַבְנֵיweights מִרְמָהfalse

Can I excuse dishonest scales or bags of false weights?

6:12

אֲשֶׁרthat עֲשִׁירֶיהָwealthy מָלְאוּfull חָמָסviolence וְיֹשְׁבֶיהָand its residents דִּבְּרוּspeak שָׁקֶרlies וּלְשׁוֹנָםtheir tongues are רְמִיָּהdeceitful בְּפִיהֶםin their mouths

For the wealthy of the city are full of violence, and its residents speak lies; their tongues are deceitful in their mouths.

6:13

וְגַםand also אֲנִיI הֶחֱלֵיתִיam הַכּוֹתֶךָstriking you הַשְׁמֵםseverely עַלon חַטֹּאתֶךָyour sins

Therefore I am striking you severely, to ruin you because of your sins.

6:14

אַתָּהyou תֹאכַלeat וְלֹאbut not תִשְׂבָּעsatisfied וְיֶשְׁחֲךָand your hunger בְּקִרְבֶּךָin your among וְתַסֵּגacquire וְלֹאwill not תַפְלִיטpreserve וַאֲשֶׁרand what תְּפַלֵּטsave לַחֶרֶבto the sword אֶתֵּןgive

You will eat but not be satisfied, and your hunger will remain with you. What you acquire, you will not preserve; and what you save, I will give to the sword.

6:15

אַתָּהyou תִזְרַעsow וְלֹאbut not תִקְצוֹרreap אַתָּהyou תִדְרֹךְpress זַיִתolives וְלֹאbut not תָסוּךְanoint שֶׁמֶןoil וְתִירוֹשׁwill tread grapes וְלֹאbut not תִשְׁתֶּהdrink יָּיִןwine

You will sow but not reap; you will press olives but not anoint yourselves with oil; you will tread grapes but not drink the wine.

6:16

וְיִשְׁתַּמֵּרyou have kept חֻקּוֹתstatutes עָמְרִיOmri וְכֹלand all מַעֲשֵׂהpractices בֵיתhouse אַחְאָבAhab’s וַתֵּלְכוּand went בְּמֹעֲצוֹתָםfollowed their counsel לְמַעַןwill תִּתִּיmake אֹתְךָyou לְשַׁמָּהa desolation וְיֹשְׁבֶיהָand your inhabitants an לִשְׁרֵקָהobject of contempt וְחֶרְפַּתscorn עַמִּיnations תִּשָּׂאוּbear

You have kept the statutes of Omri and all the practices of Ahab’s house; you have followed their counsel. Therefore I will make you a desolation, and your inhabitants an object of contempt; you will bear the scorn of the nations.”
