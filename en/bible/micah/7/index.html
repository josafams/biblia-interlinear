Micah 7

Lament, trust and forgiveness

Woe is me! For I am like one gathering summer fruit at the gleaning of the vineyard; there is no cluster to eat, no early fig that I crave.

7:2

The godly man has perished from the earth; there is no one upright among men. They all lie in wait for blood; they hunt one another with a net.

7:3

Both hands are skilled at evil; the prince and the judge demand a bribe. When the powerful utters his evil desire, they all conspire together.

7:4

טוֹבָםbest of them כְּחֵדֶקlike a brier יָשָׁרupright מִמְּסוּכָהsharper than a hedge יוֹםday מְצַפֶּיךָyour watchmen פְּקֻדָּתְךָyour visitation בָאָהcome עַתָּהnow תִהְיֶהtime מְבוּכָתָםtheir confusion

The best of them is like a brier; the most upright is sharper than a hedge of thorns. The day for your watchmen has come, the day of your visitation. Now is the time of their confusion.

7:5

אַלdo not תַּאֲמִינוּrely בְרֵעַon a friend אַלdo not תִּבְטְחוּtrust בְּאַלּוּףin a companion מִשֹּׁכֶבֶתlies חֵיקֶךָyour arms שְׁמֹרseal the פִּתְחֵיdoors פִיךָyour mouth

Do not rely on a friend; do not trust in a companion. Seal the doors of your mouth from her who lies in your arms.

7:6

כִּיfor בֵןson מְנַבֵּלdishonors אָבfather בַּתdaughter קָמָהrises בְאִמָּהּagainst her mother כַּלָּהdaughter בַּחֲמֹתָהּlaw mother law אֹיְבֵיenemies אִישׁman’s אַנְשֵׁיown בֵיתוֹhis household

For a son dishonors his father, a daughter rises against her mother, and a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law. A man’s enemies are the members of his own household.

7:7

וַאֲנִיand I בַּיהוָהLORD אֲצַפֶּהI אוֹחִילָהwait לֵאלֹהֵיfor the God יִשְׁעִיmy salvation יִשְׁמָעֵנִיhear אֱלֹהָיGod

But as for me, I will look to the LORD; I will wait for the God of my salvation. My God will hear me.

7:8

אַלdo not תִּשְׂמְחִיgloat אֹיַבְתִּיme my enemy לִיto me כִּיfor נָפַלְתִּיfallen קָמְתִּיarise כִּיfor אֵשֵׁבsit בַּחֹשֶׁךְin darkness the יְהוָהLORD אוֹרlight לִיto me

Do not gloat over me, my enemy! Though I have fallen, I will arise; though I sit in darkness, the LORD will be my light.

7:9

זַעַףrage יְהוָהLORD אֶשָּׂאendure כִּיbecause חָטָאתִיsinned לוֹto him עַדuntil אֲשֶׁרthat יָרִיבargues רִיבִיmy case וְעָשָׂהand executes מִשְׁפָּטִיjustice for me יוֹצִיאֵנִיbring me לָאוֹרinto the light אֶרְאֶהsee בְּצִדְקָתוֹhis righteousness

Because I have sinned against Him, I must endure the rage of the LORD, until He argues my case and executes justice for me. He will bring me into the light; I will see His righteousness.

7:10

וְתֵרֶאsee אֹיַבְתִּיmy enemy וּתְכַסֶּהָcovered with she בוּשָׁהshame הָאֹמְרָהwho said אֵלַיto me אַיּוֹwhere is יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהָיִךְyour God עֵינַיmy eyes תִּרְאֶינָּהsee בָּהּin her עַתָּהnow תִּהְיֶהbe לְמִרְמָסbe trampled כְּטִיטlike mud חוּצוֹתstreets

Then my enemy will see and will be covered with shame— she who said to me, “Where is the LORD your God?” My eyes will see her; at that time she will be trampled like mud in the streets.

7:11

יוֹםday לִבְנוֹתfor rebuilding גְּדֵרָיִךְyour walls יוֹםday הַהוּאthat יִרְחַקfor extending חֹקboundary

The day for rebuilding your walls will come— the day for extending your boundary.

7:12

יוֹםday הוּאthat וְעָדֶיךָand to you יָבוֹאcome לְמִנִּיto you from אַשּׁוּרAssyria וְעָרֵיand the cities מָצוֹרEgypt וּלְמִנִּיfrom מָצוֹרEgypt וְעַדto נָהָרEuphrates וְיָםsea מִיָּםsea וְהַרand mountain הָהָרto mountain

On that day they will come to you from Assyria and the cities of Egypt, even from Egypt to the Euphrates, from sea to sea and mountain to mountain.

7:13

וְהָיְתָהand be הָאָרֶץthe earth לִשְׁמָמָהbecome desolate עַלon יֹשְׁבֶיהָits inhabitants מִפְּרִיas the fruit מַעַלְלֵיהֶםtheir deeds

Then the earth will become desolate because of its inhabitants, as the fruit of their deeds.

7:14

רְעֵהshepherd עַמְּךָpeople בְשִׁבְטֶךָwith staff your צֹאןflock נַחֲלָתֶךָyour inheritance שֹׁכְנִיlive לְבָדָדalone יַעַרwoodland בְּתוֹךְsurrounded by כַּרְמֶלpastures יִרְעוּgraze בָשָׁןBashan וְגִלְעָדand Gilead כִּימֵיdays עוֹלָםold

Shepherd with Your staff Your people, the flock of Your inheritance. They live alone in a woodland, surrounded by pastures. Let them graze in Bashan and Gilead, as in the days of old.

7:15

כִּימֵיdays צֵאתְךָwhen you מֵאֶרֶץfrom land מִצְרָיִםEgypt אַרְאֶנּוּshow נִפְלָאוֹתwonders

As in the days when you came out of Egypt, I will show My wonders.

7:16

Nations will see and be ashamed, deprived of all their might. They will put their hands over their mouths, and their ears will become deaf.

7:17

יְלַחֲכוּlick עָפָרdust כַּנָּחָשׁlike a snake כְּזֹחֲלֵיlike reptiles slithering אֶרֶץground יִרְגְּזוּcrawl מִמִּסְגְּרֹתֵיהֶםfrom their holes אֶלto יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God יִפְחָדוּtremble וְיִרְאוּin fear מִמֶּךָּof you

They will lick the dust like a snake, like reptiles slithering on the ground. They will crawl from their holes in the presence of the LORD our God; they will tremble in fear of You.

7:18

מִיwho אֵלGod כָּמוֹךָlike you נֹשֵׂאpardons עָוֺןiniquity וְעֹבֵרand passes עַלover פֶּשַׁעtransgression לִשְׁאֵרִיתof the remnant נַחֲלָתוֹhis inheritance לֹאnot הֶחֱזִיקretain לָעַדforever אַפּוֹhis anger כִּיbecause חָפֵץdelights חֶסֶדloving devotion הוּאhe

Who is a God like You, who pardons iniquity and passes over the transgression of the remnant of His inheritance— who does not retain His anger forever, because He delights in loving devotion?

7:19

He will again have compassion on us; He will vanquish our iniquities. You will cast out all our sins into the depths of the sea.

7:20

תִּתֵּןwill אֱמֶתfaithfulness לְיַעֲקֹבto Jacob חֶסֶדloving devotion לְאַבְרָהָםto Abraham אֲשֶׁרthat נִשְׁבַּעְתָּswore לַאֲבֹתֵינוּto our fathers מִימֵיfrom the days קֶדֶםold

You will show faithfulness to Jacob and loving devotion to Abraham, as You swore to our fathers from the days of old.
