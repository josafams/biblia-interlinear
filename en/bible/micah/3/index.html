Micah 3

Against leaders, prophets and priests

Open in the interlinear reader

3:1

וָאֹמַרthen I said שִׁמְעוּhear נָאnow רָאשֵׁיleaders יַעֲקֹבJacob וּקְצִינֵיyou rulers בֵּיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael הֲלוֹאshould not לָכֶםto you לָדַעַתknow אֶת[obj] הַמִּשְׁפָּטjustice

Then I said: “Hear now, O leaders of Jacob, you rulers of the house of Israel. Should you not know justice?

3:2

שֹׂנְאֵיhate טוֹבgood וְאֹהֲבֵיand love רָעevil גֹּזְלֵיtear עוֹרָםskin מֵעֲלֵיהֶםfrom the them וּשְׁאֵרָםand strip the flesh מֵעַלfrom the עַצְמוֹתָםtheir bones

You hate good and love evil. You tear the skin from my people and strip the flesh from their bones.

3:3

וַאֲשֶׁרyou אָכְלוּeat שְׁאֵרflesh עַמִּיmy people וְעוֹרָםafter stripping מֵעֲלֵיהֶםfrom the them הִפְשִׁיטוּskin וְאֶתand [obj] עַצְמֹתֵיהֶםtheir bones פִּצֵּחוּchop וּפָרְשׂוּup flesh כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had בַּסִּירfor the cooking pot וּכְבָשָׂרlike meat בְּתוֹךְin among קַלָּחַתcauldron

You eat the flesh of my people after stripping off their skin and breaking their bones. You chop them up like flesh for the cooking pot, like meat in a cauldron.”

3:4

אָזthen יִזְעֲקוּcry out אֶלto יְהוָהLORD וְלֹאnot יַעֲנֶהanswer אוֹתָםthem וְיַסְתֵּרwill hide פָּנָיוhis face מֵהֶםfrom them בָּעֵתat that time הַהִיאhe כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had הֵרֵעוּevil מַעַלְלֵיהֶםtheir deeds

Then they will cry out to the LORD, but He will not answer them. At that time He will hide His face from them because of the evil they have done.

3:5

כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD עַלfor הַנְּבִיאִיםthe prophets הַמַּתְעִיםwho lead אֶת[obj] עַמִּיmy people הַנֹּשְׁכִיםchew בְּשִׁנֵּיהֶםwith their teeth וְקָרְאוּproclaim שָׁלוֹםpeace וַאֲשֶׁרwho לֹאnot יִתֵּןputs עַלin פִּיהֶםtheir mouths וְקִדְּשׁוּbut declare עָלָיוagainst one מִלְחָמָהwar

This is what the LORD says: “As for the prophets who lead My people astray, who proclaim peace while they chew with their teeth, but declare war against one who puts nothing in their mouths:

3:6

לָכֵןtherefore לַיְלָהnight לָכֶםto you מֵחָזוֹןwithout visions וְחָשְׁכָהand darkness לָכֶםto you מִקְּסֹםwithout divination וּבָאָהand come הַשֶּׁמֶשׁthe sun עַלon הַנְּבִיאִיםthese prophets וְקָדַרblack עֲלֵיהֶםover them הַיּוֹםthe daylight

Therefore night will come over you without visions, and darkness without divination. The sun will set on these prophets, and the daylight will turn black over them.

3:7

וּבֹשׁוּashamed הַחֹזִיםthe seers וְחָפְרוּashamed and הַקֹּסְמִיםthe diviners וְעָטוּmouths עַלon שָׂפָםcover כֻּלָּםall their כִּיbecause אֵיןno מַעֲנֵהanswer אֱלֹהִיםGod

Then the seers will be ashamed and the diviners will be disgraced. They will all cover their mouths because there is no answer from God.”

3:8

וְאוּלָםhowever אָנֹכִיam מָלֵאתִיfilled כֹחַpower אֶת[obj] רוּחַspirit יְהוָהLORD וּמִשְׁפָּטwith justice וּגְבוּרָהand courage לְהַגִּידto declare לְיַעֲקֹבto Jacob פִּשְׁעוֹhis transgression וּלְיִשְׂרָאֵלand to Israel חַטָּאתוֹhis sin

As for me, however, I am filled with power by the Spirit of the LORD, with justice and courage, to declare to Jacob his transgression and to Israel his sin.

3:9

שִׁמְעוּhear נָאnow זֹאתthis רָאשֵׁיleaders בֵּיתhouse יַעֲקֹבJacob וּקְצִינֵיand rulers בֵּיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael הַמֲתַעֲבִיםwho despise מִשְׁפָּטjustice וְאֵתand [obj] כָּלall הַיְשָׁרָהthat is right יְעַקֵּשׁוּpervert

Now hear this, O leaders of the house of Jacob and rulers of the house of Israel, who despise justice and pervert all that is right,

3:10

בֹּנֶהbuild צִיּוֹןZion בְּדָמִיםwith bloodshed וִירוּשָׁלִַםand Jerusalem בְּעַוְלָהwith iniquity

who build Zion with bloodshed and Jerusalem with iniquity.

3:11

רָאשֶׁיהָher leaders בְּשֹׁחַדfor a bribe יִשְׁפֹּטוּjudge וְכֹהֲנֶיהָher priests בִּמְחִירfor a price יוֹרוּteach וּנְבִיאֶיהָand her prophets בְּכֶסֶףfor money יִקְסֹמוּpractice divination וְעַלupon יְהוָהLORD יִשָּׁעֵנוּlean לֵאמֹרsaying הֲלוֹאis not יְהוָהLORD בְּקִרְבֵּנוּamong us לֹאno תָבוֹאcome עָלֵינוּupon us רָעָהdisaster

Her leaders judge for a bribe, her priests teach for a price, and her prophets practice divination for money. Yet they lean upon the LORD, saying, “Is not the LORD among us? No disaster can come upon us.”

3:12

לָכֵןtherefore בִּגְלַלְכֶםbecause of you צִיּוֹןZion שָׂדֶהfield תֵחָרֵשׁplowed וִירוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem עִיִּיןwill תִּהְיֶהbecome וְהַרmount הַבַּיִתthe temple לְבָמוֹתa wooded ridge יָעַרwooded

Therefore, because of you, Zion will be plowed like a field, Jerusalem will become a heap of rubble, and the temple mount a wooded ridge.
