Micah 1

Judgment on Samaria and Jerusalem

דְּבַרword יְהוָהLORD אֲשֶׁרthat הָיָהcame אֶלto מִיכָהMicah הַמֹּרַשְׁתִּיthe Moreshite בִּימֵיin the days יוֹתָםJotham אָחָזAhaz יְחִזְקִיָּהHezekiah מַלְכֵיkings יְהוּדָהJudah אֲשֶׁרthat חָזָהsaw עַלon שֹׁמְרוֹןSamaria וִירוּשָׁלִָםand Jerusalem

This is the word of the LORD that came to Micah the Moreshite in the days of Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah—what he saw regarding Samaria and Jerusalem:

1:2

שִׁמְעוּhear עַמִּיםpeoples כֻּלָּםall הַקְשִׁיבִיlisten אֶרֶץearth וּמְלֹאָהּand everyone in it וִיהִיand be אֲדֹנָיLord יְהוִהGOD בָּכֶםagainst you לְעֵדwitness אֲדֹנָיLord מֵהֵיכַלtemple קָדְשׁוֹhis holy

Hear, O peoples, all of you; listen, O earth, and everyone in it! May the Lord GOD bear witness against you, the Lord from His holy temple.

1:3

כִּיfor הִנֵּהbehold יְהוָהLORD יֹצֵאforth מִמְּקוֹמוֹfrom his dwelling place וְיָרַדhe will come down וְדָרַךְand tread עַלon בָּמֳתֵיhigh places אָרֶץearth

For behold, the LORD comes forth from His dwelling place; He will come down and tread on the high places of the earth.

1:4

וְנָמַסּוּwill melt הֶהָרִיםthe mountains תַּחְתָּיוbeneath him וְהָעֲמָקִיםand the valleys יִתְבַּקָּעוּsplit apart כַּדּוֹנַגlike wax מִפְּנֵיbefore הָאֵשׁthe fire כְּמַיִםlike water מֻגָּרִיםrushing בְּמוֹרָדdown a slope

The mountains will melt beneath Him, and the valleys will split apart, like wax before the fire, like water rushing down a slope.

1:5

בְּפֶשַׁעfor the transgression יַעֲקֹבJacob כָּלall זֹאתthis וּבְחַטֹּאותand the sins בֵּיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael מִיwhat פֶשַׁעtransgression יַעֲקֹבJacob הֲלוֹאis it not שֹׁמְרוֹןSamaria וּמִיand what בָּמוֹתhigh place יְהוּדָהJudah הֲלוֹאis it not יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem

All this is for the transgression of Jacob and the sins of the house of Israel. What is the transgression of Jacob? Is it not Samaria? And what is the high place of Judah? Is it not Jerusalem?

1:6

וְשַׂמְתִּיwill make שֹׁמְרוֹןSamaria לְעִיa heap of rubble הַשָּׂדֶהthe open field לְמַטָּעֵיa planting כָרֶםvineyard וְהִגַּרְתִּיwill pour לַגַּיinto the valley אֲבָנֶיהָher stones וִיסֹדֶיהָand her foundations אֲגַלֶּהexpose

Therefore I will make Samaria a heap of rubble in the open field, a planting area for a vineyard. I will pour her stones into the valley and expose her foundations.

1:7

וְכָלall פְּסִילֶיהָher carved images יֻכַּתּוּpieces וְכָלpieces all אֶתְנַנֶּיהָher wages יִשָּׂרְפוּburned בָאֵשׁin the fire וְכָלall עֲצַבֶּיהָher idols אָשִׂיםI שְׁמָמָהdestroy כִּיfor מֵאֶתְנַןthe wages זוֹנָהprostitute קִבָּצָהcollected וְעַדand to אֶתְנַןused זוֹנָהprostitute יָשׁוּבוּagain

All her carved images will be smashed to pieces; all her wages will be burned in the fire, and I will destroy all her idols. Since she collected the wages of a prostitute, they will be used again on a prostitute.

1:8

עַלon זֹאתthis אֶסְפְּדָהlament וְאֵילִילָהand wail אֵילְכָהwalk שׁוֹלָלbarefoot וְעָרוֹםand naked אֶעֱשֶׂהI מִסְפֵּדhowl כַּתַּנִּיםlike a jackal וְאֵבֶלand mourn כִּבְנוֹתlike an ostrich יַעֲנָהostriches

Because of this I will lament and wail; I will walk barefoot and naked. I will howl like a jackal and mourn like an ostrich.

1:9

כִּיfor אֲנוּשָׁהincurable מַכּוֹתֶיהָher wound כִּיfor בָאָהreached עַדuntil יְהוּדָהJudah נָגַעit עַדuntil שַׁעַרgate עַמִּיmy people עַדfar יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem

For her wound is incurable; it has reached even Judah; it has approached the gate of my people, as far as Jerusalem itself.

1:10

בְּגַתin Gath אַלdo not תַּגִּידוּtell בָּכוֹweeping אַלdo not תִּבְכּוּweep בְּבֵיתBeth לְעַפְרָהBeth עָפָרdust הִתְפַּלָּשִׁיleaphrah

Do not tell it in Gath; do not weep at all. Roll in the dust in Beth-leaphrah.

1:11

עִבְרִיdepart לָכֶםto you יוֹשֶׁבֶתdwellers שָׁפִירShaphir עֶרְיָהnakedness בֹשֶׁתshameful לֹאnot יָצְאָהcome out יוֹשֶׁבֶתdwellers צַאֲנָןZaanan מִסְפַּדBeth בֵּיתsupport הָאֵצֶלis יִקַּחtaken מִכֶּםfrom you עֶמְדָּתוֹmourning its support

Depart in shameful nakedness, O dwellers of Shaphir. The dwellers of Zaanan will not come out. Beth-ezel is in mourning; its support is taken from you.

1:12

כִּיfor חָלָהpined לְטוֹבfor good יוֹשֶׁבֶתdwellers מָרוֹתMaroth כִּיbut יָרַדdown רָעcalamity מֵאֵתfrom the יְהוָהLORD לְשַׁעַרto the gate יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem

For the dwellers of Maroth pined for good, but calamity came down from the LORD, even to the gate of Jerusalem.

1:13

רְתֹםharness הַמֶּרְכָּבָהchariot לָרֶכֶשׁhorses יוֹשֶׁבֶתdweller לָכִישׁLachish רֵאשִׁיתbeginning חַטָּאתsin הִיאthe לְבַתto daughter צִיּוֹןZion כִּיfor בָךְin you נִמְצְאוּfound פִּשְׁעֵיtransgressions יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

Harness your chariot horses, O dweller of Lachish. You were the beginning of sin to the Daughter of Zion, for the transgressions of Israel were found in you.

1:14

לָכֵןtherefore תִּתְּנִיsend שִׁלּוּחִיםfarewell gifts עַלto מוֹרֶשֶׁתMoresheth גַּתgath בָּתֵּיhouses אַכְזִיבAchzib prove לְאַכְזָבwill deceptive לְמַלְכֵיto the kings יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

Therefore, send farewell gifts to Moresheth-gath; the houses of Achzib will prove deceptive to the kings of Israel.

1:15

עֹדagain הַיֹּרֵשׁa conqueror אָבִיbring לָךְto you יוֹשֶׁבֶתdweller מָרֵשָׁהMareshah עַדto עֲדֻּלָּםAdullam יָבוֹאcome כְּבוֹדglory יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

I will again bring a conqueror against you, O dweller of Mareshah. The glory of Israel will come to Adullam.

1:16

קָרְחִיbald וָגֹזִּיand cut עַלon בְּנֵיchildren תַּעֲנוּגָיִךְmourning for your הַרְחִבִיprecious קָרְחָתֵךְyour hair כַּנֶּשֶׁרas an eagle כִּיfor גָלוּexile מִמֵּךְfrom you

Shave yourselves bald and cut off your hair in mourning for your precious children; make yourselves as bald as an eagle, for they will go from you into exile.
