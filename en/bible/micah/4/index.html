Micah 4

The mountain of the LORD in the last days

Open in the interlinear reader

4:1

וְהָיָהand be בְּאַחֲרִיתin last הַיָּמִיםthe days יִהְיֶהbe הַרmountain בֵּיתhouse יְהוָהLORD נָכוֹןestablished בְּרֹאשׁas the chief הֶהָרִיםthe mountains וְנִשָּׂאwill raised הוּאhe מִגְּבָעוֹתabove the hills וְנָהֲרוּstream עָלָיוto it עַמִּיםpeoples

In the last days the mountain of the house of the LORD will be established as the chief of the mountains; it will be raised above the hills, and the peoples will stream to it.

4:2

וְהָלְכוּcome גּוֹיִםnations רַבִּיםmany וְאָמְרוּand say לְכוּcome וְנַעֲלֶהlet us go up אֶלto הַרmountain יְהוָהLORD וְאֶלto בֵּיתhouse אֱלֹהֵיGod יַעֲקֹבJacob וְיוֹרֵנוּhe will teach us מִדְּרָכָיוhis ways וְנֵלְכָהwalk בְּאֹרְחֹתָיוin his paths כִּיfor מִצִּיּוֹןfrom Zion תֵּצֵאgo forth תוֹרָהlaw וּדְבַרand the word יְהוָהLORD מִירוּשָׁלִָםfrom Jerusalem

And many nations will come and say: “Come, let us go up to the mountain of the LORD, to the house of the God of Jacob. He will teach us His ways, so that we may walk in His paths.” For the law will go forth from Zion and the word of the LORD from Jerusalem.

4:3

וְשָׁפַטwill judge בֵּיןbetween עַמִּיםpeoples רַבִּיםmany וְהוֹכִיחַand arbitrate לְגוֹיִםfor nations עֲצֻמִיםstrong עַדfar רָחוֹקwide וְכִתְּתוּbeat חַרְבֹתֵיהֶםtheir swords לְאִתִּיםinto plowshares וַחֲנִיתֹתֵיהֶםand their spears לְמַזְמֵרוֹתinto pruning hooks לֹאno יִשְׂאוּtake גּוֹיnation אֶלagainst גּוֹיnation חֶרֶבsword וְלֹאand not יִלְמְדוּןtrain עוֹדlonger מִלְחָמָהwar

Then He will judge between many peoples and arbitrate for strong nations far and wide. Then they will beat their swords into plowshares and their spears into pruning hooks. Nation will no longer take up the sword against nation, nor will they train anymore for war.

4:4

וְיָשְׁבוּsit אִישׁman תַּחַתunder גַּפְנוֹhis own vine וְתַחַתand under תְּאֵנָתוֹhis own fig tree וְאֵיןwith no מַחֲרִידfrighten כִּיfor פִיmouth יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts דִּבֵּרspoken

And each man will sit under his own vine and under his own fig tree, with no one to frighten him. For the mouth of the LORD of Hosts has spoken.

4:5

כִּיfor כָּלeach הָעַמִּיםthe peoples יֵלְכוּwalk אִישׁman בְּשֵׁםin the name אֱלֹהָיוhis god וַאֲנַחְנוּyet we נֵלֵךְwalk בְּשֵׁםin the name יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God לְעוֹלָםforever וָעֶדand ever

Though each of the peoples may walk in the name of his god, yet we will walk in the name of the LORD our God forever and ever.

4:6

בַּיּוֹםon that day הַהוּאthat נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD אֹסְפָהgather הַצֹּלֵעָהthe lame וְהַנִּדָּחָהwill the outcast אֲקַבֵּצָהassemble וַאֲשֶׁרeven those whom הֲרֵעֹתִיafflicted

“On that day,” declares the LORD, “I will gather the lame; I will assemble the outcast, even those whom I have afflicted.

4:7

וְשַׂמְתִּיmake אֶת[obj] הַצֹּלֵעָהthe lame לִשְׁאֵרִיתinto a remnant וְהַנַּהֲלָאָהand the outcast לְגוֹיinto a nation עָצוּםstrong וּמָלַךְrule יְהוָהLORD עֲלֵיהֶםover them בְּהַרin Mount צִיּוֹןZion מֵעַתָּהfrom now וְעַדand to עוֹלָםforever

And I will make the lame into a remnant, and the outcast into a strong nation. Then the LORD will rule over them in Mount Zion from that day and forever.

4:8

וְאַתָּהand you מִגְדַּלwatchtower עֵדֶרflock עֹפֶלstronghold בַּתdaughter צִיּוֹןZion עָדֶיךָto you תֵּאתֶהsovereignty וּבָאָהwill come הַמֶּמְשָׁלָהdominion הָרִאשֹׁנָהthe former מַמְלֶכֶתrestored לְבַתto the daughter יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem

And you, O watchtower of the flock, O stronghold of the Daughter of Zion— the former dominion will be restored to you; sovereignty will come to the Daughter of Jerusalem.”

4:9

עַתָּהnow לָמָּהto why תָרִיעִיcry רֵעַaloud הֲמֶלֶךְking אֵיןno בָּךְamong you אִםif יוֹעֲצֵךְyour counselor אָבָדperished כִּיthat הֶחֱזִיקֵךְgrips you חִילanguish כַּיּוֹלֵדָהlike a woman in labor

Why do you now cry aloud? Is there no king among you? Has your counselor perished so that anguish grips you like a woman in labor?

4:10

חוּלִיwrithe agony וָגֹחִיin agony בַּתdaughter צִיּוֹןZion כַּיּוֹלֵדָהlabor כִּיfor עַתָּהnow תֵצְאִיleave מִקִּרְיָהleave the city וְשָׁכַנְתְּand camp בַּשָּׂדֶהin the open fields וּבָאתwill go עַדto בָּבֶלBabylon שָׁםthere תִּנָּצֵלִיrescued שָׁםthere יִגְאָלֵךְredeem you יְהוָהLORD מִכַּףfrom the hand אֹיְבָיִךְyour enemies

Writhe in agony, O Daughter of Zion, like a woman in labor. For now you will leave the city and camp in the open fields. You will go to Babylon; there you will be rescued; there the LORD will redeem you from the hand of your enemies!

4:11

וְעַתָּהbut now נֶאֶסְפוּassembled עָלַיִךְagainst you גּוֹיִםnations רַבִּיםmany הָאֹמְרִיםsaying תֶּחֱנָףdefiled וְתַחַזfeast בְּצִיּוֹןon Zion עֵינֵינוּour eyes

But now many nations have assembled against you, saying, “Let her be defiled, and let us feast our eyes on Zion.”

4:12

וְהֵמָּהbut they לֹאnot יָדְעוּknow מַחְשְׁבוֹתthoughts יְהוָהLORD וְלֹאor הֵבִינוּunderstand עֲצָתוֹhis plan כִּיfor קִבְּצָםgathered them כֶּעָמִירlike sheaves to the גֹּרְנָהthreshing floor

But they do not know the thoughts of the LORD or understand His plan, for He has gathered them like sheaves to the threshing floor.

4:13

קוּמִיrise וָדוֹשִׁיand thresh בַתdaughter צִיּוֹןZion כִּיfor קַרְנֵךְyou horns אָשִׂיםgive בַּרְזֶלiron וּפַרְסֹתַיִךְand hooves אָשִׂיםmake נְחוּשָׁהbronze וַהֲדִקּוֹתbreak to עַמִּיםpeoples רַבִּיםmany וְהַחֲרַמְתִּיdevote לַיהוָהto the LORD בִּצְעָםtheir gain וְחֵילָםtheir wealth לַאֲדוֹןto the Lord כָּלall הָאָרֶץthe earth

Rise and thresh, O Daughter of Zion, for I will give you horns of iron and hooves of bronze to break to pieces many peoples. Then you will devote their gain to the LORD, their wealth to the Lord of all the earth.
