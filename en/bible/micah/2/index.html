Micah 2

Against those who covet fields and houses

Open in the interlinear reader

2:1

הוֹיWoe חֹשְׁבֵיdevise אָוֶןiniquity וּפֹעֲלֵיand plot רָעevil עַלon מִשְׁכְּבוֹתָםtheir beds בְּאוֹרat light הַבֹּקֶרmorning’s יַעֲשׂוּהָaccomplish it כִּיbecause יֶשׁthere לְאֵלpower יָדָםtheir hands

Woe to those who devise iniquity and plot evil on their beds! At morning’s light they accomplish it because the power is in their hands.

2:2

וְחָמְדוּthey covet שָׂדוֹתfields וְגָזָלוּand seize וּבָתִּיםhouses וְנָשָׂאוּtake וְעָשְׁקוּthey deprive גֶּבֶרman וּבֵיתוֹhis home וְאִישׁman וְנַחֲלָתוֹhis inheritance

They covet fields and seize them; they take away houses. They deprive a man of his home, a fellow man of his inheritance.

2:3

לָכֵןtherefore כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD הִנְנִיsays I am חֹשֵׁבplanning עַלagainst הַמִּשְׁפָּחָהthis nation הַזֹּאתthis רָעָהdisaster אֲשֶׁרwhich לֹאnot תָמִישׁוּfree מִשָּׁםfrom there צַוְּארֹתֵיכֶםyour necks וְלֹאnot תֵלְכוּwalk רוֹמָהproudly כִּיfor עֵתtime רָעָהcalamity הִיאis

Therefore this is what the LORD says: “I am planning against this nation a disaster from which you cannot free your necks. Then you will not walk so proudly, for it will be a time of calamity.

2:4

בַּיּוֹםin that day הַהוּאwill יִשָּׂאtake עֲלֵיכֶםagainst you מָשָׁלproverb וְנָהָהand taunt נְהִיbitter נִהְיָהlamentation אָמַרwe שָׁדוֹדutterly נְשַׁדֻּנוּruined חֵלֶקportion עַמִּיmy people יָמִירchanged אֵיךְhow יָמִישׁremoved לִיto me לְשׁוֹבֵבto traitors שָׂדֵינוּour fields יְחַלֵּקallotted

In that day they will take up a proverb against you and taunt you with this bitter lamentation: ‘We are utterly ruined! He has changed the portion of my people. How He has removed it from me! He has allotted our fields to traitors.’”

2:5

לָכֵןtherefore לֹאno יִהְיֶהone לְךָto מַשְׁלִיךְdivide חֶבֶלland בְּגוֹרָלby lot בִּקְהַלin the assembly יְהוָהLORD

Therefore, you will have no one in the assembly of the LORD to divide the land by lot.

2:6

אַלnot תַּטִּפוּpreach יַטִּיפוּןpreach לֹאnot יַטִּפוּpreach לָאֵלֶּהto these לֹאnot יִסַּגovertake כְּלִמּוֹתdisgrace

“Do not preach,” they preach. “Do not preach these things; disgrace will not overtake us.”

2:7

הֶאָמוּרsaid בֵּיתhouse יַעֲקֹבJacob הֲקָצַרimpatient רוּחַspirit יְהוָהLORD אִםif אֵלֶּהthese מַעֲלָלָיוthings he הֲלוֹאdoes do not דְבָרַיmy words יֵיטִיבוּgood עִםto הַיָּשָׁרwho uprightly הוֹלֵךְwalks

Should it be said, O house of Jacob, “Is the Spirit of the LORD impatient? Are these the things He does?” Do not My words bring good to him who walks uprightly?

2:8

וְאֶתְמוּלbut of late עַמִּיmy people לְאוֹיֵבan enemy יְקוֹמֵםrisen מִמּוּלfrom front שַׂלְמָהrobe אֶדֶרmagnificent תַּפְשִׁטוּןstrip מֵעֹבְרִיםfrom passersby בֶּטַחunsuspecting שׁוּבֵיreturning מִלְחָמָהbattle

But of late My people have risen up like an enemy: You strip off the splendid robe from unsuspecting passersby like men returning from battle.

2:9

נְשֵׁיwomen עַמִּיmy people תְּגָרְשׁוּןdrive מִבֵּיתhomes תַּעֲנֻגֶיהָpleasant you מֵעַלfrom the עֹלָלֶיהָtheir children תִּקְחוּtake הֲדָרִיmy blessing לְעוֹלָםforever

You drive the women of My people from their pleasant homes. You take away My blessing from their children forever.

2:10

קוּמוּarise וּלְכוּand depart כִּיfor לֹאnot זֹאתthis הַמְּנוּחָהrest בַּעֲבוּרbecause טָמְאָהdefilement תְּחַבֵּלbrings destruction וְחֶבֶלgrievous נִמְרָץdestruction

Arise and depart, for this is not your place of rest, because its defilement brings destruction— a grievous destruction!

2:11

לוּif אִישׁman הֹלֵךְcome רוּחַwind וָשֶׁקֶרand falsely כִּזֵּבsay אַטִּףpreach לְךָto you לַיַּיִןof wine וְלַשֵּׁכָרand strong drink וְהָיָהhe would be מַטִּיףjust preacher הָעָםthis people הַזֶּהthis

If a man of wind were to come and say falsely, “I will preach to you of wine and strong drink,” he would be just the preacher for this people!

2:12

אָסֹףtake אֶאֱסֹףgather יַעֲקֹבJacob כֻּלָּךְall of you קַבֵּץgather אֲקַבֵּץcollect שְׁאֵרִיתremnant יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael יַחַדtogether אֲשִׂימֶנּוּbring them כְּצֹאןlike sheep בָּצְרָהpen כְּעֵדֶרlike a flock בְּתוֹךְin the midst הַדָּבְרוֹof its pasture תְּהִימֶנָהnoisy מֵאָדָםa noisy throng

I will surely gather all of you, O Jacob; I will collect the remnant of Israel. I will bring them together like sheep in a pen, like a flock in the midst of its pasture— a noisy throng.

2:13

עָלָהgo הַפֹּרֵץwho breaks לִפְנֵיהֶםbefore them פָּרְצוּbreak וַיַּעֲבֹרוּthrough שַׁעַרgate וַיֵּצְאוּand go בוֹby it וַיַּעֲבֹרwill pass מַלְכָּםtheir king לִפְנֵיהֶםbefore them וַיהוָהLORD בְּרֹאשָׁםas their leader

One who breaks open the way will go up before them; they will break through the gate, and go out by it. Their King will pass through before them, the LORD as their leader.
