These were the sons of Reuben the firstborn of Israel. Though he was the firstborn, his birthright was given to the sons of Joseph son of Israel, because Reuben defiled his father’s bed. So he is not reckoned according to birthright.

And though Judah prevailed over his brothers and a ruler came from him, the birthright belonged to Joseph.

The sons of Reuben, the firstborn of Israel: Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi.

5:4

בְּנֵיdescendants יוֹאֵלJoel שְׁמַעְיָהShemaiah בְנוֹhis son גּוֹגGog בְּנוֹhis son שִׁמְעִיShimei בְנוֹhis son

The descendants of Joel:

5:5

מִיכָהMicah בְנוֹson his son רְאָיָהReaiah בְנוֹhis son בַּעַלBaal בְּנוֹhis son

Shemaiah his son, Gog his son, Shimei his son, Micah his son, Reaiah his son, Baal his son,

בְּאֵרָהBeerah בְנוֹhis son אֲשֶׁרwhom הֶגְלָהcarried תִּלְּגַתTiglath פִּלְנְאֶסֶרpileser מֶלֶךְking אַשֻּׁרAssyria הוּאhe נָשִׂיאleader לָראוּבֵנִיinto exile

and Beerah his son, whom Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria carried into exile.

5:7

וְאֶחָיוhis relatives לְמִשְׁפְּחֹתָיוby clans are בְּהִתְיַחֵשׂrecorded in לְתֹלְדוֹתָםBeerah הָרֹאשׁleader יְעִיאֵלReubenites וּזְכַרְיָהוּand Zechariah

Beerah was a leader of the Reubenites. His relatives by their clans are recorded in their genealogy:

5:8

וּבֶלַעand Bela בֶּןson עָזָזAzaz בֶּןson שֶׁמַעShema בֶּןson יוֹאֵלJoel הוּאhe יוֹשֵׁבsettled בַּעֲרֹעֵרin Aroer וְעַדand as far נְבוֹNebo וּבַעַלand Baal מְעוֹןmeon

Jeiel the chief, Zechariah, and Bela son of Azaz, the son of Shema, the son of Joel. They settled in Aroer and as far as Nebo and Baal-meon.

5:9

וְלַמִּזְרָחalso in the east יָשַׁבsettled עַדfar לְבוֹאas the edge מִדְבָּרָהdesert that extends לְמִןto from הַנָּהָרthe Euphrates פְּרָתRiver כִּיbecause מִקְנֵיהֶםtheir livestock had רָבוּincreased בְּאֶרֶץin the land גִּלְעָדGilead

They also settled in the east as far as the edge of the desert that extends to the Euphrates River, because their livestock had increased in the land of Gilead.

5:10

וּבִימֵיduring the days שָׁאוּלSaul עָשׂוּwaged מִלְחָמָהwar עִםagainst הַהַגְרִאִיםthe Hagrites וַיִּפְּלוּwho were defeated בְּיָדָםat their hands וַיֵּשְׁבוּand they occupied בְּאָהֳלֵיהֶםthe homes עַלon כָּלthroughout פְּנֵיthe region מִזְרָחeast לַגִּלְעָדof Gilead

During the days of Saul they waged war against the Hagrites, who were defeated at their hands, and they occupied the homes of the Hagrites throughout the region east of Gilead.

5:11

וּבְנֵיdescendants גָדGad לְנֶגְדָּםto before them יָשְׁבוּlived בְּאֶרֶץin the land הַבָּשָׁןof Bashan עַדfar סַלְכָהSalecah

The descendants of Gad lived next to the Reubenites in the land of Bashan, as far as Salecah:

5:12

יוֹאֵלJoel הָרֹאשׁthe chief וְשָׁפָםShapham הַמִּשְׁנֶהthe second וְיַעְנַיthen Jaanai וְשָׁפָטand Shaphat בַּבָּשָׁןlived in Bashan

Joel was the chief, Shapham the second, then Jaanai and Shaphat, who lived in Bashan.

5:13

וַאֲחֵיהֶםtheir kinsmen לְבֵיתto house אֲבוֹתֵיהֶםfamilies were מִיכָאֵלMichael וּמְשֻׁלָּםMeshullam וְשֶׁבַעSheba וְיוֹרַיJorai וְיַעְכָּןJacan וְזִיעַZia וָעֵבֶרand Eber שִׁבְעָהseven

Their kinsmen by families were Michael, Meshullam, Sheba, Jorai, Jacan, Zia, and Eber—seven in all.

5:14

אֵלֶּהthese בְּנֵיsons אֲבִיחַיִלAbihail בֶּןson חוּרִיHuri בֶּןson יָרוֹחַJaroah בֶּןson גִּלְעָדGilead בֶּןson מִיכָאֵלMichael בֶּןson יְשִׁישַׁיJeshishai בֶּןson יַחְדּוֹJahdo בֶּןson בּוּזBuz

These were the sons of Abihail son of Huri, the son of Jaroah, the son of Gilead, the son of Michael, the son of Jeshishai, the son of Jahdo, the son of Buz.

5:15

אֲחִיAhi בֶּןson עַבְדִּיאֵלAbdiel בֶּןson גּוּנִיGuni רֹאשׁhead לְבֵיתto house אֲבוֹתָםtheir family

Ahi son of Abdiel, the son of Guni, was head of their family.

5:16

וַיֵּשְׁבוּthey lived בַּגִּלְעָדin Gilead בַּבָּשָׁןin Bashan וּבִבְנֹתֶיהָand its towns וּבְכָלand throughout מִגְרְשֵׁיpasturelands שָׁרוֹןSharon עַלon תּוֹצְאוֹתָםtheir out at

They lived in Gilead, in Bashan and its towns, and throughout the pasturelands of Sharon.

5:17

כֻּלָּםall of them הִתְיַחְשׂוּgenealogies בִּימֵיduring the reigns יוֹתָםJotham מֶלֶךְking יְהוּדָהJudah וּבִימֵיand in days יָרָבְעָםJeroboam מֶלֶךְking יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

All of them were reckoned in the genealogies during the reigns of Jotham king of Judah and Jeroboam king of Israel.

5:18

בְּנֵיsons רְאוּבֵןReubenites וְגָדִיGadites וַחֲצִיand the half שֵׁבֶטtribe מְנַשֶּׁהManasseh מִןfrom בְּנֵיvaliant חַיִלvaliant אֲנָשִׁיםmen נֹשְׂאֵיcarried מָגֵןshield וְחֶרֶבsword וְדֹרְכֵיand drew קֶשֶׁתbow וּלְמוּדֵיand were trained מִלְחָמָהwarriors אַרְבָּעִים44 וְאַרְבָּעָה760 אֶלֶף000 וּשְׁבַעand 700 מֵאוֹתhundreds וְשִׁשִּׁיםand sixty יֹצְאֵיfit צָבָאbattle

The Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh had 44,760 warriors—valiant men who carried the shield and sword, drew the bow, and were trained for battle.

5:19

וַיַּעֲשׂוּthey waged מִלְחָמָהwar עִםagainst הַהַגְרִיאִיםthe Hagrites וִיטוּרJetur וְנָפִישׁwell as Naphish וְנוֹדָבand Nodab

They waged war against the Hagrites, as well as Jetur, Naphish, and Nodab.

5:20

וַיֵּעָזְרוּhelped עֲלֵיהֶםagainst their enemies וַיִּנָּתְנוּdelivered בְיָדָםinto their hands הַהַגְרִיאִיםthe Hagrites וְכֹלand all שֶׁעִמָּהֶםwho with them כִּיbecause לֵאלֹהִיםto God זָעֲקוּcried out בַּמִּלְחָמָהin battle וְנַעְתּוֹרprayers לָהֶםto them כִּיbecause בָטְחוּtrust בוֹin

And because they cried out to God in battle, they were helped against their enemies, and the Hagrites and all their allies were delivered into their hands. Because they put their trust in God, He answered their prayers.

5:21

וַיִּשְׁבּוּthey seized מִקְנֵיהֶםlivestock גְּמַלֵּיהֶםcamels חֲמִשִּׁים250 אֶלֶף000 וְצֹאןsheep מָאתַיִםtwo hundred וַחֲמִשִּׁים50 אֶלֶף000 וַחֲמוֹרִיםdonkeys also אַלְפָּיִם000 וְנֶפֶשׁthey took אָדָםcaptives מֵאָה100 אָלֶף000

They seized the livestock of the Hagrites—50,000 camels, 250,000 sheep, and 2,000 donkeys. They also took 100,000 captives,

5:22

כִּיfor חֲלָלִיםslain רַבִּיםmany נָפָלוּfell כִּיbecause מֵהָאֱלֹהִיםto God הַמִּלְחָמָהthe battle וַיֵּשְׁבוּand they occupied תַחְתֵּיהֶםunder them עַדuntil הַגֹּלָהthe exile

and many others fell slain, because the battle belonged to God. And they occupied the land until the exile.

5:23

וּבְנֵיnow the people חֲצִיhalf שֵׁבֶטtribe מְנַשֶּׁהManasseh יָשְׁבוּsettled בָּאָרֶץin the land מִבָּשָׁןfrom Bashan עַדto בַּעַלBaal חֶרְמוֹןBaal וּשְׂנִירSenir known וְהַרMount חֶרְמוֹןHermon הֵמָּהwere רָבוּnumerous

Now the people of the half-tribe of Manasseh were numerous. They settled in the land from Bashan to Baal-hermon (that is, Senir, also known as Mount Hermon).

5:24

וְאֵלֶּהthese רָאשֵׁיheads בֵיתhouse אֲבוֹתָםtheir families וְעֵפֶרEpher וְיִשְׁעִיIshi וֶאֱלִיאֵלEliel וְעַזְרִיאֵלAzriel וְיִרְמְיָהJeremiah וְהוֹדַוְיָהHodaviah וְיַחְדִּיאֵלand Jahdiel אֲנָשִׁיםmen גִּבּוֹרֵיmighty men חַיִלvalor אַנְשֵׁיwere שֵׁמוֹתnames רָאשִׁיםheads לְבֵיתto house אֲבוֹתָםtheir fathers

These were the heads of their families: Epher, Ishi, Eliel, Azriel, Jeremiah, Hodaviah, and Jahdiel.

5:25

וַיִּמְעֲלוּunfaithful בֵּאלֹהֵיto the God אֲבוֹתֵיהֶםtheir fathers and וַיִּזְנוּand they prostituted אַחֲרֵיafter אֱלֹהֵיgods עַמֵּיpeoples הָאָרֶץthe land אֲשֶׁרwhom הִשְׁמִידdestroyed אֱלֹהִיםGod מִפְּנֵיהֶםhad before them

They were mighty men of valor, famous men, and heads of their families. But they were unfaithful to the God of their fathers, and they prostituted themselves with the gods of the peoples of the land, whom God had destroyed before them.

5:26

So the God of Israel stirred up the spirit of Pul king of Assyria (that is, Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria) to take the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh into exile. And he brought them to Halah, Habor, Hara, and the river of Gozan, where they remain to this day.
