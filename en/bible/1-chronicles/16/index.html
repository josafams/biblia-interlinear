1 Chronicles 16

The song of praise before the ark

Open in the interlinear reader

16:1

וַיָּבִיאוּso they brought אֶת[obj] אֲרוֹןark הָאֱלֹהִיםof God וַיַּצִּיגוּand placed אֹתוֹhim בְּתוֹךְinside הָאֹהֶלthe tent אֲשֶׁרthat had נָטָהpitched לוֹfor it דָּוִידDavid וַיַּקְרִיבוּand they presented עֹלוֹתburnt offerings וּשְׁלָמִיםand peace לִפְנֵיbefore הָאֱלֹהִיםGod

So they brought the ark of God and placed it inside the tent that David had pitched for it. And they presented burnt offerings and peace offerings before God.

16:2

וַיְכַלfinished דָּוִידDavid מֵהַעֲלוֹתhad sacrificing הָעֹלָהthe burnt offerings וְהַשְּׁלָמִיםand peace offerings וַיְבָרֶךְhe blessed אֶת[obj] הָעָםthe people בְּשֵׁםin the name יְהוָהLORD

When David had finished sacrificing the burnt offerings and peace offerings, he blessed the people in the name of the LORD.

16:3

וַיְחַלֵּקthen he distributed לְכָלto every אִישׁman יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael מֵאִישׁfrom men וְעַדand to אִשָּׁהwoman לְאִישׁto man כִּכַּרloaf לֶחֶםbread וְאֶשְׁפָּרdate וַאֲשִׁישָׁהcake and a raisin cake

Then he distributed to every man and woman of Israel a loaf of bread, a date cake, and a raisin cake.

16:4

וַיִּתֵּןDavid appointed לִפְנֵיto before אֲרוֹןark יְהוָהLORD מִןsome הַלְוִיִּםthe Levites מְשָׁרְתִיםminister וּלְהַזְכִּירto celebrate וּלְהוֹדוֹתto give thanks וּלְהַלֵּלand to praise לַיהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

David appointed some of the Levites to minister before the ark of the LORD, to celebrate, to give thanks, and to praise the LORD, the God of Israel.

16:5

אָסָףAsaph הָרֹאשׁthe chief וּמִשְׁנֵהוּwas second then זְכַרְיָהZechariah יְעִיאֵלJeiel וּשְׁמִירָמוֹתShemiramoth וִיחִיאֵלJehiel וּמַתִּתְיָהMattithiah וֶאֱלִיאָבEliab וּבְנָיָהוּBenaiah וְעֹבֵדObed אֱדֹםedom וִיעִיאֵלand Jeiel בִּכְלֵיto play נְבָלִיםharps וּבְכִנֹּרוֹתand lyres וְאָסָףwhile Asaph בַּמְצִלְתַּיִםsounded the cymbals מַשְׁמִיעַproclaim

Asaph was the chief, Zechariah was second, then Jeiel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Mattithiah, Eliab, Benaiah, Obed-edom, and Jeiel. They were to play the harps and lyres, while Asaph sounded the cymbals

16:6

וּבְנָיָהוּBenaiah וְיַחֲזִיאֵלand Jahaziel הַכֹּהֲנִיםthe priests בַּחֲצֹצְרוֹתblew the trumpets תָּמִידregularly לִפְנֵיbefore אֲרוֹןark בְּרִיתcovenant הָאֱלֹהִיםof God

and the priests Benaiah and Jahaziel blew the trumpets regularly before the ark of the covenant of God.

16:7

בַּיּוֹםon that day הַהוּאthat אָזthen נָתַןgiven דָּוִידDavid בָּרֹאשׁfirst committed לְהֹדוֹתthanksgiving לַיהוָהto the LORD בְּיַדin hand אָסָףAsaph וְאֶחָיוand his brothers

On that day David first committed to Asaph and his brothers this song of thanksgiving to the LORD:

16:8

הוֹדוּgive thanks לַיהוָהto the LORD קִרְאוּcall בִשְׁמוֹupon his name make הוֹדִיעוּknown בָעַמִּיםamong the nations עֲלִילֹתָיוhis deeds

“Give thanks to the LORD; call upon His name; make known His deeds among the nations.

16:9

שִׁירוּsing לוֹto him זַמְּרוּsing praises לוֹto him שִׂיחוּtell בְּכָלall נִפְלְאֹתָיוhis wonders

Sing to Him, sing praises to Him; tell of all His wonders.

16:10

הִתְהַלְלוּglory בְּשֵׁםname קָדְשׁוֹhis holy יִשְׂמַחrejoice לֵבhearts מְבַקְשֵׁיseek יְהוָהLORD

Glory in His holy name; let the hearts of those who seek the LORD rejoice.

16:11

דִּרְשׁוּseek יְהוָהLORD וְעֻזּוֹand his strength בַּקְּשׁוּseek פָנָיוhis face תָּמִידalways

Seek out the LORD and His strength; seek His face always.

16:12

זִכְרוּremember נִפְלְאֹתָיוwonders he אֲשֶׁרhas עָשָׂהdone מֹפְתָיוhis marvels וּמִשְׁפְּטֵיand the judgments פִיהוּpronounced

Remember the wonders He has done, His marvels, and the judgments He has pronounced,

16:13

זֶרַעoffspring יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael עַבְדּוֹhis servant בְּנֵיsons יַעֲקֹבJacob בְּחִירָיוhis chosen

O offspring of His servant Israel, O sons of Jacob, His chosen ones.

16:14

הוּאhe יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God בְּכָלthroughout הָאָרֶץthe earth מִשְׁפָּטָיוhis judgments

He is the LORD our God; His judgments carry throughout the earth.

16:15

זִכְרוּremember לְעוֹלָםforever בְּרִיתוֹhis covenant דָּבָרword צִוָּהordained לְאֶלֶףfor a thousand דּוֹרgenerations

Remember His covenant forever, the word He ordained for a thousand generations—

16:16

אֲשֶׁרthat כָּרַתcovenant made אֶתwith אַבְרָהָםAbraham וּשְׁבוּעָתוֹand the oath he swore לְיִצְחָקto Isaac

the covenant He made with Abraham, and the oath He swore to Isaac.

16:17

וַיַּעֲמִידֶהָhe confirmed it לְיַעֲקֹבto Jacob לְחֹקas a decree לְיִשְׂרָאֵלto Israel בְּרִיתcovenant עוֹלָםeverlasting

He confirmed it to Jacob as a decree, to Israel as an everlasting covenant:

16:18

לֵאמֹרI לְךָto you אֶתֵּןgive אֶרֶץland כְּנָעַןCanaan חֶבֶלportion נַחֲלַתְכֶםyour inheritance

‘I will give you the land of Canaan as the portion of your inheritance.’

16:19

בִּהְיוֹתְכֶםwhen they were מְתֵיfew מִסְפָּרnumber כִּמְעַטfew וְגָרִיםand strangers בָּהּin the land

When they were few in number, few indeed, and strangers in the land,

16:20

וַיִּתְהַלְּכוּthey wandered מִגּוֹיfrom nation אֶלto גּוֹיnation וּמִמַּמְלָכָהfrom one kingdom אֶלto עַםpeople אַחֵרanother

they wandered from nation to nation, from one kingdom to another.

16:21

לֹאno הִנִּיחַlet לְאִישׁman לְעָשְׁקָםoppress them וַיּוֹכַחhe rebuked עֲלֵיהֶםon their מְלָכִיםkings

He let no man oppress them; He rebuked kings on their behalf:

16:22

אַלdo not תִּגְּעוּtouch בִּמְשִׁיחָיmy anointed וּבִנְבִיאַיto my prophets אַלdo תָּרֵעוּharm

‘Do not touch My anointed ones! Do no harm to My prophets!’

16:23

שִׁירוּsing לַיהוָהto the LORD כָּלall הָאָרֶץthe earth בַּשְּׂרוּproclaim מִיּוֹםafter day אֶלto יוֹםday יְשׁוּעָתוֹhis salvation

Sing to the LORD, all the earth. Proclaim His salvation day after day.

16:24

סַפְּרוּdeclare בַגּוֹיִםamong the nations אֶת[obj] כְּבוֹדוֹhis glory בְּכָלamong all הָעַמִּיםpeoples נִפְלְאֹתָיוhis wonderful deeds

Declare His glory among the nations, His wonderful deeds among all peoples.

16:25

כִּיfor גָדוֹלgreat יְהוָהLORD וּמְהֻלָּלpraised מְאֹדgreatly וְנוֹרָאfeared הוּאhe עַלon כָּלall אֱלֹהִיםgods

For great is the LORD, and greatly to be praised; He is to be feared above all gods.

16:26

כִּיfor כָּלall אֱלֹהֵיgods הָעַמִּיםthe nations אֱלִילִיםidols וַיהוָהLORD שָׁמַיִםheavens עָשָׂהmade

For all the gods of the nations are idols, but it is the LORD who made the heavens.

16:27

הוֹדsplendor וְהָדָרand majesty לְפָנָיוare before him עֹזstrength וְחֶדְוָהand joy בִּמְקֹמוֹfill his dwelling

Splendor and majesty are before Him; strength and joy fill His dwelling.

16:28

הָבוּascribe לַיהוָהto the LORD מִשְׁפְּחוֹתfamilies עַמִּיםnations הָבוּascribe לַיהוָהto the LORD כָּבוֹדglory וָעֹזand strength

Ascribe to the LORD, O families of the nations, ascribe to the LORD glory and strength.

16:29

הָבוּascribe לַיהוָהto the LORD כְּבוֹדglory שְׁמוֹhis name שְׂאוּbring מִנְחָהoffering וּבֹאוּand come לְפָנָיוbefore him הִשְׁתַּחֲווּworship לַיהוָהthe LORD בְּהַדְרַתin the splendor קֹדֶשׁholiness

Ascribe to the LORD the glory due His name; bring an offering and come before Him. Worship the LORD in the splendor of His holiness;

16:30

חִילוּtremble מִלְּפָנָיוbefore him כָּלall הָאָרֶץthe earth אַףhow much תִּכּוֹןestablished תֵּבֵלworld בַּלcannot תִּמּוֹטmoved

tremble before Him, all the earth. The world is firmly established; it cannot be moved.

16:31

יִשְׂמְחוּlet הַשָּׁמַיִםthe heavens וְתָגֵלglad and הָאָרֶץthe earth וְיֹאמְרוּlet them say בַגּוֹיִםamong the nations יְהוָהLORD מָלָךְreigns

Let the heavens be glad, and the earth rejoice. Let them say among the nations, ‘The LORD reigns!’

16:32

יִרְעַםlet הַיָּםthe sea וּמְלוֹאוֹresound and יַעֲלֹץlet הַשָּׂדֶהthe fields וְכָלand all אֲשֶׁרthat בּוֹin them

Let the sea resound, and all that fills it; let the fields exult, and all that is in them.

16:33

אָזthen יְרַנְּנוּsing עֲצֵיtrees הַיָּעַרthe forest מִלִּפְנֵיfor joy before יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor בָאcoming לִשְׁפּוֹטto judge אֶת[obj] הָאָרֶץthe earth

Then the trees of the forest will sing for joy before the LORD, for He is coming to judge the earth.

16:34

הוֹדוּgive thanks לַיהוָהto the LORD כִּיfor טוֹבgood כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving

Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good; His loving devotion endures forever.

16:35

וְאִמְרוּthen cry הוֹשִׁיעֵנוּsave us אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׁעֵנוּour salvation וְקַבְּצֵנוּgather וְהַצִּילֵנוּand deliver us מִןfrom הַגּוֹיִםthe nations לְהֹדוֹתgive thanks לְשֵׁםname קָדְשֶׁךָyour holy לְהִשְׁתַּבֵּחַmay glory בִּתְהִלָּתֶךָin your praise

Then cry out: ‘Save us, O God of our salvation; gather and deliver us from the nations, that we may give thanks to Your holy name, that we may glory in Your praise.’

16:36

בָּרוּךְblessed יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael מִןfrom הָעוֹלָםeverlasting וְעַדto הָעֹלָםeverlasting וַיֹּאמְרוּsaid כָלall הָעָםthe people אָמֵןAmen וְהַלֵּלand praise לַיהוָהthe LORD

Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting.” Then all the people said, “Amen!” and “Praise the LORD!”

16:37

וַיַּעֲזָבso David left שָׁםthere לִפְנֵיbefore אֲרוֹןark בְּרִיתcovenant יְהוָהLORD לְאָסָףAsaph וּלְאֶחָיוand his brothers לְשָׁרֵתto minister לִפְנֵיto before הָאָרוֹןrequirements תָּמִידregularly לִדְבַרto word יוֹםdaily בְּיוֹמוֹin his day

So David left Asaph and his brothers there before the ark of the covenant of the LORD, to minister there regularly according to the daily requirements,

16:38

וְעֹבֵדalong with Obed אֱדֹםedom וַאֲחֵיהֶםrelatives שִׁשִּׁיםsixty וּשְׁמוֹנָהeight וְעֹבֵדObed אֱדֹםedom בֶּןson יְדִיתוּןJeduthun וְחֹסָהand also Hosah לְשֹׁעֲרִיםto be gatekeepers

along with Obed-edom and his sixty-eight relatives. Obed-edom son of Jeduthun, and also Hosah, were to be gatekeepers.

16:39

וְאֵתand [obj] צָדוֹקZadok הַכֹּהֵןthe priest וְאֶחָיוand his fellow הַכֹּהֲנִיםpriests לִפְנֵיbefore מִשְׁכַּןtabernacle יְהוָהLORD בַּבָּמָהat the high place אֲשֶׁרthat בְּגִבְעוֹןin Gibeon

And David left Zadok the priest and his fellow priests before the tabernacle of the LORD at the high place in Gibeon

16:40

לְהַעֲלוֹתofferings עֹלוֹתburnt offerings לַיהוָהto the LORD עַלon מִזְבַּחaltar הָעֹלָהof burnt תָּמִידofferings לַבֹּקֶרofferings morning וְלָעָרֶבand evening וּלְכָלaccording to all הַכָּתוּבthat was written בְּתוֹרַתin the law יְהוָהLORD אֲשֶׁרwhich צִוָּהcommanded עַלon יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

to regularly present burnt offerings to the LORD on the altar of burnt offerings, morning and evening, according to all that was written in the Law of the LORD, which He had commanded Israel to keep.

16:41

וְעִמָּהֶםwith them הֵימָןHeman וִידוּתוּןJeduthun וּשְׁאָרand the rest הַבְּרוּרִיםthose chosen אֲשֶׁרthat נִקְּבוּdesignated בְּשֵׁמוֹתby name לְהֹדוֹתto give thanks לַיהוָהto the LORD כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

With them were Heman, Jeduthun, and the rest of those chosen and designated by name to give thanks to the LORD, for “His loving devotion endures forever.”

16:42

וְעִמָּהֶםhad with them הֵימָןHeman וִידוּתוּןJeduthun חֲצֹצְרוֹתtrumpets וּמְצִלְתַּיִםand cymbals לְמַשְׁמִיעִיםfor the music וּכְלֵיand instruments שִׁירsongs הָאֱלֹהִיםGod וּבְנֵיand the sons יְדוּתוּןJeduthun לַשָּׁעַרstationed at the gate

Heman and Jeduthun had with them trumpets and cymbals for the music and instruments for the songs of God. And the sons of Jeduthun were stationed at the gate.

16:43

וַיֵּלְכוּdeparted כָלall הָעָםthe people אִישׁman לְבֵיתוֹfor their homes וַיִּסֹּבand returned דָּוִידDavid לְבָרֵךְto bless אֶת[obj] בֵּיתוֹhis household

Then all the people departed for their homes, and David returned home to bless his household.
