1 Chronicles 17

The promise of a house for David

Open in the interlinear reader

17:1

וַיְהִיand was כַּאֲשֶׁרhad יָשַׁבsettled דָּוִידDavid בְּבֵיתוֹinto his palace וַיֹּאמֶרhe said דָּוִידDavid אֶלto נָתָןNathan הַנָּבִיאthe prophet הִנֵּהhere אָנֹכִיam יוֹשֵׁבliving בְּבֵיתin a house הָאֲרָזִיםof cedar וַאֲרוֹןwhile the ark בְּרִיתcovenant יְהוָהLORD תַּחַתunder יְרִיעוֹתtent

After David had settled into his palace, he said to Nathan the prophet, “Here I am, living in a house of cedar, while the ark of the covenant of the LORD is under a tent.”

17:2

וַיֹּאמֶרand replied נָתָןNathan אֶלto דָּוִידDavid כֹּלall אֲשֶׁרthat בִּלְבָבְךָin your heart עֲשֵׂהdo כִּיfor הָאֱלֹהִיםGod עִמָּךְwith you

And Nathan replied to David, “Do all that is in your heart, for God is with you.”

17:3

וַיְהִיand was בַּלַּיְלָהthat night הַהוּאthat וַיְהִיand was דְּבַרword אֱלֹהִיםGod אֶלto נָתָןNathan לֵאמֹרto saying

But that night the word of God came to Nathan, saying,

17:4

לֵךְgo וְאָמַרְתָּand tell אֶלto דָּוִידDavid עַבְדִּיmy servant כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD לֹאnot אַתָּהyou תִּבְנֶהbuild לִּיto me הַבַּיִתa house לָשָׁבֶתto dwell

“Go and tell My servant David that this is what the LORD says: You are not the one to build Me a house in which to dwell.

17:5

כִּיfor לֹאnot יָשַׁבְתִּיdwelt בְּבַיִתin a house מִןfrom הַיּוֹםthe day אֲשֶׁרthat הֶעֱלֵיתִיbrought אֶת[obj] יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael עַדuntil הַיּוֹםthis day הַזֶּהthis וָאֶהְיֶהmoved מֵאֹהֶלfrom tent אֶלto אֹהֶלtent וּמִמִּשְׁכָּןand dwelling to dwelling

For I have not dwelt in a house from the day I brought Israel up out of Egypt until this day, but I have moved from tent to tent and dwelling to dwelling.

17:6

בְּכֹלin all אֲשֶׁרthat הִתְהַלַּכְתִּיjourneys בְּכָלwith all יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites הֲדָבָרasked דִּבַּרְתִּיspoken אֶתwith אַחַדany שֹׁפְטֵיjudges יִשְׂרָאֵלleaders אֲשֶׁרthat צִוִּיתִיappointed לִרְעוֹתto shepherd אֶת[obj] עַמִּיmy people לֵאמֹרto saying לָמָּהto why לֹאnot בְנִיתֶםbuilt לִיto me בֵּיתhouse אֲרָזִיםcedar

In all My journeys with all the Israelites, have I ever asked any of the leaders I appointed to shepherd My people, ‘Why haven’t you built Me a house of cedar?’

17:7

וְעַתָּהnow then כֹּהthis תֹאמַרtell לְעַבְדִּיto my servant לְדָוִידDavid כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts אֲנִיI לְקַחְתִּיךָtook you מִןfrom הַנָּוֶהthe pasture מִןfrom אַחֲרֵיfollowing הַצֹּאןthe flock לִהְיוֹתto be נָגִידruler עַלover עַמִּיmy people יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

Now then, you are to tell My servant David that this is what the LORD of Hosts says: I took you from the pasture, from following the flock, to be the ruler over My people Israel.

17:8

וָאֶהְיֶהI have been עִמְּךָwith you בְּכֹלwherever אֲשֶׁרhave הָלַכְתָּgone וָאַכְרִיתcut off אֶת[obj] כָּלall אוֹיְבֶיךָyour enemies מִפָּנֶיךָfrom before you וְעָשִׂיתִיmake לְךָfor you שֵׁםname כְּשֵׁםname like הַגְּדוֹלִיםthe greatest אֲשֶׁרthat בָּאָרֶץin the land

I have been with you wherever you have gone, and I have cut off all your enemies from before you. Now I will make for you a name like the greatest in the land.

17:9

וְשַׂמְתִּיand I מָקוֹםplace לְעַמִּיfor my people יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וּנְטַעְתִּיהוּand will plant them וְשָׁכַןdwell תַּחְתָּיוplace of their own וְלֹאno יִרְגַּזdisturbed עוֹדmore וְלֹאno יוֹסִיפוּlonger בְנֵיsons עַוְלָהwickedness לְבַלֹּתוֹoppress them כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had בָּרִאשׁוֹנָהat the beginning

And I will provide a place for My people Israel and will plant them so that they may dwell in a place of their own and be disturbed no more. No longer will the sons of wickedness oppress them as they did at the beginning

17:10

וּלְמִיָּמִיםsince the day אֲשֶׁרthat צִוִּיתִיappointed שֹׁפְטִיםjudges עַלover עַמִּיmy people יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וְהִכְנַעְתִּיsubdue אֶת[obj] כָּלall אוֹיְבֶיךָyour enemies וָאַגִּדmoreover I declare לָךְto you וּבַיִתhouse יִבְנֶהbuild לְּךָfor you יְהוָהLORD

and have done since the day I appointed judges over My people Israel. And I will subdue all your enemies. Moreover, I declare to you that the LORD will build a house for you.

17:11

וְהָיָהand be כִּיwhen מָלְאוּfulfilled יָמֶיךָyour days לָלֶכֶתgo to עִםwith אֲבֹתֶיךָyour fathers וַהֲקִימוֹתִיwill raise אֶת[obj] זַרְעֲךָyour descendant אַחֲרֶיךָafter you אֲשֶׁרthat יִהְיֶהone מִבָּנֶיךָof your sons וַהֲכִינוֹתִיestablish אֶת[obj] מַלְכוּתוֹhis kingdom

And when your days are fulfilled and you go to be with your fathers, I will raise up your descendant after you, one of your own sons, and I will establish his kingdom.

17:12

הוּאhe יִבְנֶהbuild לִּיfor בָּיִתhouse וְכֹנַנְתִּיestablish אֶת[obj] כִּסְאוֹhis throne עַדuntil עוֹלָםforever

He will build a house for Me, and I will establish his throne forever.

17:13

אֲנִיI אֶהְיֶהwill לּוֹto him לְאָבfather וְהוּאand he will יִהְיֶהbe לִּיto me לְבֵןson וְחַסְדִּיmy loving devotion לֹאnever אָסִירremove מֵעִמּוֹfrom presence him כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had הֲסִירוֹתִיremoved מֵאֲשֶׁרfrom had הָיָהwas לְפָנֶיךָyour predecessor

I will be his Father, and he will be My son. And I will never remove My loving devotion from him as I removed it from your predecessor.

17:14

וְהַעֲמַדְתִּיהוּset him בְּבֵיתִיover my house וּבְמַלְכוּתִיand my kingdom עַדuntil הָעוֹלָםforever וְכִסְאוֹand his throne יִהְיֶהbe נָכוֹןestablished עַדuntil עוֹלָםforever

But I will set him over My house and My kingdom forever, and his throne will be established forever.”

17:15

כְּכֹלall הַדְּבָרִיםthe words הָאֵלֶּהthis וּכְכֹלentire הֶחָזוֹןvision הַזֶּהthis כֵּןso דִּבֶּרrelayed נָתָןNathan אֶלto דָּוִידDavid

So Nathan relayed to David all the words of this entire vision.

17:16

וַיָּבֹאand came הַמֶּלֶךְking דָּוִידDavid וַיֵּשֶׁבwent in sat לִפְנֵיbefore יְהוָהLORD וַיֹּאמֶרand said מִיwho אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהִיםGod וּמִיand what בֵיתִיmy house כִּיthat הֲבִיאֹתַנִיbrought me עַדfar הֲלֹםthis

Then King David went in, sat before the LORD, and said, “Who am I, O LORD God, and what is my house, that You have brought me this far?

17:17

וַתִּקְטַןsmall זֹאתthis בְּעֵינֶיךָin your eyes אֱלֹהִיםGod וַתְּדַבֵּרspoken עַלon בֵּיתhouse עַבְדְּךָyour servant לְמֵרָחוֹקthe future of וּרְאִיתַנִיand have regarded me כְּתוֹרlike estate הָאָדָםa man הַמַּעֲלָהdistinction יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהִיםGod

And as if this was a small thing in Your eyes, O God, You have spoken about the future of the house of Your servant and have regarded me as a man of great distinction, O LORD God.

17:18

מַהwhat יּוֹסִיףmore עוֹדmore דָּוִידDavid אֵלֶיךָto you לְכָבוֹדfor so honoring אֶת[obj] עַבְדֶּךָyour servant וְאַתָּהfor you אֶת[obj] עַבְדְּךָyour servant יָדָעְתָּknow

What more can David say to You for so honoring Your servant? For You know Your servant,

17:19

יְהוָהLORD בַּעֲבוּרfor the sake עַבְדְּךָyour servant וּכְלִבְּךָown heart you עָשִׂיתָaccomplished אֵת[obj] כָּלall הַגְּדוּלָּהthis great הַזֹּאתthis לְהֹדִיעַand revealed אֶת[obj] כָּלall הַגְּדֻלּוֹתgreatness

O LORD. For the sake of Your servant and according to Your own heart, You have accomplished this great thing and revealed all Your greatness.

17:20

יְהוָהLORD אֵיןnone כָּמוֹךָlike you וְאֵיןno אֱלֹהִיםGod זוּלָתֶךָbut you בְּכֹלaccording to everything אֲשֶׁרhave שָׁמַעְנוּheard בְּאָזְנֵינוּwith our own ears

O LORD, there is none like You, and there is no God but You, according to everything we have heard with our own ears.

17:21

וּמִיand who כְּעַמְּךָlike your people יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael גּוֹיnation אֶחָדone בָּאָרֶץon earth אֲשֶׁרwhom הָלַךְwent הָאֱלֹהִיםGod לִפְדּוֹתto redeem לוֹto him עָםpeople לָשׂוּםmade לְךָyou a שֵׁםname גְּדֻלּוֹתgreat וְנֹרָאוֹתand awesome לְגָרֵשׁby driving מִפְּנֵיfrom before עַמְּךָyour people אֲשֶׁרwhom פָּדִיתָredeemed מִמִּצְרַיִםfrom Egypt גּוֹיִםnations

And who is like Your people Israel—the one nation on earth whom God went out to redeem as a people for Himself? You made a name for Yourself through great and awesome wonders by driving out nations from before Your people, whom You redeemed from Egypt.

17:22

וַתִּתֵּןmade אֶת[obj] עַמְּךָyour people יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael לְךָto you לְעָםvery own עַדuntil עוֹלָםforever וְאַתָּהand you יְהוָהLORD הָיִיתָbecome לָהֶםto them לֵאלֹהִיםGod

For You have made Your people Israel Your very own forever, and You, O LORD, have become their God.

17:23

וְעַתָּהand now יְהוָהLORD הַדָּבָרthe word אֲשֶׁרhave דִּבַּרְתָּspoken עַלon עַבְדְּךָyour servant וְעַלand on בֵּיתוֹhis house יֵאָמֵןestablished עַדuntil עוֹלָםforever וַעֲשֵׂהdo כַּאֲשֶׁרas you have דִּבַּרְתָּpromised

And now, O LORD, let the word You have spoken concerning Your servant and his house be established forever. Do as You have promised,

17:24

וְיֵאָמֵןestablished וְיִגְדַּלand magnified שִׁמְךָyour name עַדuntil עוֹלָםforever לֵאמֹרwhen it is said יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael אֱלֹהִיםGod לְיִשְׂרָאֵלover Israel וּבֵיתand may the house דָּוִידDavid עַבְדְּךָyour servant נָכוֹןestablished לְפָנֶיךָbefore you

so that Your name will be established and magnified forever when it is said, ‘The LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, is God over Israel.’ And may the house of Your servant David be established before You.

17:25

כִּיfor אַתָּהyou אֱלֹהַיmy God גָּלִיתָrevealed אֶת[obj] אֹזֶןear עַבְדְּךָservant that you לִבְנוֹתwill build לוֹa בָּיִתhouse עַלfor כֵּןtherefore מָצָאfound עַבְדְּךָyour servant לְהִתְפַּלֵּלcourage pray לְפָנֶיךָto before you

For You, my God, have revealed to Your servant that You will build a house for him. Therefore Your servant has found the courage to pray before You.

17:26

וְעַתָּהand now יְהוָהLORD אַתָּהyou הוּאhe הָאֱלֹהִיםare God וַתְּדַבֵּרpromised עַלto עַבְדְּךָyour servant הַטּוֹבָהthis goodness הַזֹּאתthis

And now, O LORD, You are God! And You have promised this goodness to Your servant.

17:27

וְעַתָּהso now הוֹאַלְתָּpleased לְבָרֵךְto bless אֶת[obj] בֵּיתhouse עַבְדְּךָyour servant לִהְיוֹתthat it may לְעוֹלָםforever לְפָנֶיךָbefore you כִּיfor אַתָּהyou יְהוָהLORD בֵּרַכְתָּblessed וּמְבֹרָךְblessed לְעוֹלָםforever

So now You have been pleased to bless the house of Your servant, that it may continue forever before You. For You, O LORD, have blessed it, and it will be blessed forever.”
