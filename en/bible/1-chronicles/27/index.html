This is the list of the Israelites—the heads of families, the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, and their officers who served the king in every matter concerning the divisions on rotating military duty each month throughout the year. There were 24,000 men in each division:

27:2

עַלon הַמַּחֲלֹקֶתthe division which was assigned הָרִאשׁוֹנָהthe first לַחֹדֶשׁmonth הָרִאשׁוֹןfirst יָשָׁבְעָםJashobeam בֶּןson זַבְדִּיאֵלZabdiel וְעַלwas in מַחֲלֻקְתּוֹ24 עֶשְׂרִיםthere וְאַרְבָּעָהmen אָלֶף000

Jashobeam son of Zabdiel was in charge of the first division, which was assigned the first month. There were 24,000 men in his division.

27:3

מִןfrom בְּנֵיdescendant פֶרֶץPerez הָרֹאשׁand chief לְכָלof all שָׂרֵיcommanders הַצְּבָאוֹתthe army לַחֹדֶשׁfor the month הָרִאשׁוֹןfirst

He was a descendant of Perez and chief of all the army commanders for the first month.

27:4

וְעַלand on מַחֲלֹקֶת24 הַחֹדֶשׁmonth הַשֵּׁנִיthe second דּוֹדַיDodai הָאֲחוֹחִיthe Ahohite וּמַחֲלֻקְתּוֹdivision for וּמִקְלוֹתand Mikloth הַנָּגִידthe leader וְעַלin מַחֲלֻקְתּוֹ24 עֶשְׂרִים000 וְאַרְבָּעָהmen אָלֶףdivision

Dodai the Ahohite was in charge of the division for the second month, and Mikloth was the leader. There were 24,000 men in his division.

27:5

שַׂרcommander chief הַצָּבָאarmy as הַשְּׁלִישִׁיthe third לַחֹדֶשׁfor the month הַשְּׁלִישִׁיthird was בְּנָיָהוּBenaiah בֶןson יְהוֹיָדָעJehoiada הַכֹּהֵןthe priest רֹאשׁwere וְעַלin מַחֲלֻקְתּוֹ24 עֶשְׂרִיםthere וְאַרְבָּעָהmen אָלֶף000

The third army commander, as chief for the third month, was Benaiah son of Jehoiada the priest. There were 24,000 men in his division.

27:6

הוּאhe בְנָיָהוּBenaiah גִּבּוֹרmighty הַשְּׁלֹשִׁיםthe thirty וְעַלand was over הַשְּׁלֹשִׁיםthe thirty וּמַחֲלֻקְתּוֹhis division עַמִּיזָבָדAmmizabad בְּנוֹhis son

This Benaiah was mighty among the Thirty and was over the Thirty, and his son Ammizabad was in charge of his division.

27:7

הָרְבִיעִיthe fourth לַחֹדֶשׁfor the month הָרְבִיעִיfourth was עֲשָׂהAsahel אֵלAsahel אֲחִיbrother יוֹאָבJoab’s וּזְבַדְיָהZebadiah בְנוֹhis son אַחֲרָיוafter him וְעַלin מַחֲלֻקְתּוֹ24 עֶשְׂרִים000 וְאַרְבָּעָהmen אָלֶףdivision

The fourth, for the fourth month, was Joab’s brother Asahel, and his son Zebadiah was commander after him. There were 24,000 men in his division.

27:8

הַחַמִישִׁיthe fifth לַחֹדֶשׁfor the month הַחֲמִישִׁיfifth was הַשַּׂרthe commander שַׁמְהוּתShamhuth הַיִּזְרָחIzrahite וְעַלin מַחֲלֻקְתּוֹ24 עֶשְׂרִים000 וְאַרְבָּעָהmen אָלֶףdivision

The fifth, for the fifth month, was the commander Shamhuth the Izrahite. There were 24,000 men in his division.

27:9

הַשִּׁשִּׁיthe sixth לַחֹדֶשׁfor the month הַשִּׁשִּׁיsixth was עִירָאIra בֶןson עִקֵּשׁIkkesh הַתְּקוֹעִיTekoite וְעַלin מַחֲלֻקְתּוֹ24 עֶשְׂרִים000 וְאַרְבָּעָהmen אָלֶףdivision

The sixth, for the sixth month, was Ira son of Ikkesh the Tekoite. There were 24,000 men in his division.

27:10

הַשְּׁבִיעִיthe seventh לַחֹדֶשׁfor the month הַשְּׁבִיעִיseventh was חֶלֶץHelez הַפְּלוֹנִיPelonite מִןfrom בְּנֵיsons אֶפְרָיִםEphraim וְעַלin מַחֲלֻקְתּוֹEphraimite עֶשְׂרִיםthere וְאַרְבָּעָה24 אָלֶף000

The seventh, for the seventh month, was Helez the Pelonite, an Ephraimite. There were 24,000 men in his division.

27:11

הַשְּׁמִינִיthe eighth לַחֹדֶשׁfor the month הַשְּׁמִינִיeighth was סִבְּכַיSibbecai הַחֻשָׁתִיHushathite לַזַּרְחִיa Zerahite וְעַלin מַחֲלֻקְתּוֹ24 עֶשְׂרִים000 וְאַרְבָּעָהmen אָלֶףdivision

The eighth, for the eighth month, was Sibbecai the Hushathite, a Zerahite. There were 24,000 men in his division.

27:12

הַתְּשִׁיעִיthe ninth לַחֹדֶשׁfor the month הַתְּשִׁיעִיninth was אֲבִיעֶזֶרAbiezer הָעַנְּתֹתִיAnathothite לַבֵּןto the son יְמִינִיBenjamite וְעַלin מַחֲלֻקְתּוֹ24 עֶשְׂרִים000 וְאַרְבָּעָהmen אָלֶףdivision

The ninth, for the ninth month, was Abiezer the Anathothite, a Benjamite. There were 24,000 men in his division.

27:13

הָעֲשִׂירִיthe tenth לַחֹדֶשׁfor the month הָעֲשִׂירִיtenth was מַהְרַיMaharai הַנְּטוֹפָתִיNetophathite לַזַּרְחִיa Zerahite וְעַלin מַחֲלֻקְתּוֹ24 עֶשְׂרִים000 וְאַרְבָּעָהmen אָלֶףdivision

The tenth, for the tenth month, was Maharai the Netophathite, a Zerahite. There were 24,000 men in his division.

27:14

עַשְׁתֵּיeleventh עָשָׂרeleventh לְעַשְׁתֵּיto eleventh עָשָׂרtwelve הַחֹדֶשׁmonth בְּנָיָהBenaiah הַפִּרְעָתוֹנִיPirathonite מִןfrom בְּנֵיsons אֶפְרָיִםEphraim וְעַלin מַחֲלֻקְתּוֹhis division עֶשְׂרִים24 וְאַרְבָּעָהmen אָלֶף000

The eleventh, for the eleventh month, was Benaiah the Pirathonite, an Ephraimite. There were 24,000 men in his division.

27:15

הַשְּׁנֵיםthe twelfth עָשָׂרtwelfth לִשְׁנֵיםfor twelfth עָשָׂרtwelve הַחֹדֶשׁthe month חֶלְדַּיHeldai הַנְּטוֹפָתִיNetophathite לְעָתְנִיאֵלOthniel וְעַלin מַחֲלֻקְתּוֹ24 עֶשְׂרִים000 וְאַרְבָּעָהmen אָלֶףdivision

The twelfth, for the twelfth month, was Heldai the Netophathite, from the family of Othniel. There were 24,000 men in his division.

27:16

וְעַלand on שִׁבְטֵיtribes יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael לָראוּבֵנִיthe Reubenites נָגִידofficers אֱלִיעֶזֶרEliezer בֶּןson זִכְרִיZichri לַשִּׁמְעוֹנִיthe Simeonites שְׁפַטְיָהוּShephatiah בֶּןson מַעֲכָהMaacah

These officers were in charge of the tribes of Israel: Over the Reubenites was Eliezer son of Zichri; over the Simeonites was Shephatiah son of Maacah;

27:17

לְלֵוִיover Levi חֲשַׁבְיָהHashabiah בֶןson קְמוּאֵלKemuel לְאַהֲרֹןover Aaron צָדוֹקZadok

over Levi was Hashabiah son of Kemuel; over Aaron was Zadok;

27:18

לִיהוּדָהover Judah אֱלִיהוּElihu מֵאֲחֵיof brothers דָוִידDavid’s לְיִשָׂשכָרover Issachar עָמְרִיOmri בֶּןson מִיכָאֵלMichael

over Judah was Elihu, one of David’s brothers; over Issachar was Omri son of Michael;

27:19

לִזְבוּלֻןover Zebulun יִשְׁמַעְיָהוּIshmaiah בֶּןson עֹבַדְיָהוּObadiah לְנַפְתָּלִיover Naphtali יְרִימוֹתJerimoth בֶּןson עַזְרִיאֵלAzriel

over Zebulun was Ishmaiah son of Obadiah; over Naphtali was Jerimoth son of Azriel;

27:20

לִבְנֵיto sons אֶפְרַיִםEphraimites הוֹשֵׁעַHoshea בֶּןson עֲזַזְיָהוּAzaziah לַחֲצִיhalf שֵׁבֶטtribes מְנַשֶּׁהManasseh יוֹאֵלJoel בֶּןson פְּדָיָהוּPedaiah

over the Ephraimites was Hoshea son of Azaziah; over one of the half-tribes of Manasseh was Joel son of Pedaiah;

27:21

לַחֲצִיover the half הַמְנַשֶּׁהtribe of Manasseh גִּלְעָדָהGilead יִדּוֹIddo בֶּןson זְכַרְיָהוּZechariah לְבִנְיָמִןover Benjamin יַעֲשִׂיאֵלJaasiel בֶּןson אַבְנֵרAbner

over the half-tribe of Manasseh in Gilead was Iddo son of Zechariah; over Benjamin was Jaasiel son of Abner;

27:22

לְדָןover Dan עֲזַרְאֵלAzarel בֶּןson יְרֹחָםJeroham אֵלֶּהthese שָׂרֵיleaders שִׁבְטֵיtribes יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

and over Dan was Azarel son of Jeroham. These were the leaders of the tribes of Israel.

27:23

וְלֹאdid not נָשָׂאcarried דָוִידDavid מִסְפָּרָםcount לְמִבֶּןmen aged עֶשְׂרִיםtwenty שָׁנָהaged וּלְמָטָּהor under כִּיbecause אָמַרsaid יְהוָהLORD לְהַרְבּוֹתwould make אֶת[obj] יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael כְּכוֹכְבֵיas numerous as the stars הַשָּׁמָיִםthe sky

David did not count the men aged twenty or under, because the LORD had said that He would make Israel as numerous as the stars of the sky.

27:24

יוֹאָבJoab בֶּןson צְרוּיָהZeruiah הֵחֵלbegan לִמְנוֹתto count וְלֹאand not כִלָּהfinish וַיְהִיand was בָזֹאתbecause of this קֶצֶףcensus wrath עַלupon יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וְלֹאnot עָלָהentered הַמִּסְפָּרthe number בְּמִסְפַּרin the Book דִּבְרֵיwords הַיָּמִיםthe Chronicles לַמֶּלֶךְof king דָּוִידDavid

Joab son of Zeruiah began to count the men but did not finish. For because of this census wrath came upon Israel, and the number was not entered in the Book of the Chronicles of King David.

27:25

וְעַלand in אֹצְרוֹתcharge הַמֶּלֶךְthe royal עַזְמָוֶתAzmaveth בֶּןson עֲדִיאֵלAdiel וְעַלand on הָאֹצָרוֹתthe storehouses בַּשָּׂדֶהin the country בֶּעָרִיםin the cities וּבַכְּפָרִיםin the villages וּבַמִּגְדָּלוֹתand in the fortresses יְהוֹנָתָןJonathan בֶּןson עֻזִּיָּהוּUzziah

Azmaveth son of Adiel was in charge of the royal storehouses. Jonathan son of Uzziah was in charge of the storehouses in the country, in the cities, in the villages, and in the fortresses.

27:26

וְעַלand in עֹשֵׂיdo מְלֶאכֶתworkers הַשָּׂדֶהthe fields לַעֲבֹדַתtilled הָאֲדָמָהthe soil עֶזְרִיEzri בֶּןson כְּלוּבChelub

Ezri son of Chelub was in charge of the workers in the fields who tilled the soil.

27:27

וְעַלand on הַכְּרָמִיםthe vineyards שִׁמְעִיShimei הָרָמָתִיthe Ramathite וְעַלand on שֶׁבַּכְּרָמִיםthe vineyards לְאֹצְרוֹתcharge הַיַּיִןthe wine זַבְדִּיZabdi הַשִּׁפְמִיthe Shiphmite

Shimei the Ramathite was in charge of the vineyards. Zabdi the Shiphmite was in charge of the produce of the vineyards for the wine vats.

27:28

וְעַלand on הַזֵּיתִיםthe olive וְהַשִּׁקְמִיםand sycamore trees אֲשֶׁרthat בַּשְּׁפֵלָהin the foothills בַּעַלBaal חָנָןhanan הַגְּדֵרִיthe Gederite וְעַלin אֹצְרוֹתstores הַשֶּׁמֶןolive oil יוֹעָשׁoil

Baal-hanan the Gederite was in charge of the olive and sycamore trees in the foothills. Joash was in charge of the stores of olive oil.

27:29

וְעַלand on הַבָּקָרthe herds הָרֹעִיםgrazing בַּשָּׁרוֹןin Sharon שִׁרְטַיShitrai הַשָּׁרוֹנִיSharon וְעַלand on הַבָּקָרthe herds בָּעֲמָקִיםin the valleys שָׁפָטShaphat בֶּןson עַדְלָיAdlai

Shitrai the Sharonite was in charge of the herds grazing in Sharon. Shaphat son of Adlai was in charge of the herds in the valleys.

27:30

וְעַלand on הַגְּמַלִּיםthe camels אוֹבִילObil הַיִּשְׁמְעֵלִיthe Ishmaelite וְעַלand on הָאֲתֹנוֹתthe donkeys יֶחְדְּיָהוּJehdeiah הַמֵּרֹנֹתִיthe Meronothite

Obil the Ishmaelite was in charge of the camels. Jehdeiah the Meronothite was in charge of the donkeys.

27:31

וְעַלand on הַצֹּאןthe flocks יָזִיזJaziz הַהַגְרִיthe Hagrite כָּלall אֵלֶּהthese שָׂרֵיofficials הָרְכוּשׁproperty אֲשֶׁרthat לַמֶּלֶךְin charge of king דָּוִידDavid’s

Jaziz the Hagrite was in charge of the flocks. All these officials were in charge of King David’s property.

27:32

וִיהוֹנָתָןJonathan דּוֹדuncle דָּוִידDavid’s יוֹעֵץcounselor אִישׁman מֵבִיןinsight וְסוֹפֵרand scribe הוּאa וִיחִיאֵלJehiel בֶּןson חַכְמוֹנִיHachmoni attended עִםto בְּנֵיsons הַמֶּלֶךְthe king

David’s uncle Jonathan was a counselor; he was a man of insight and a scribe. Jehiel son of Hachmoni attended to the sons of the king.

27:33

וַאֲחִיתֹפֶלAhithophel יוֹעֵץcounselor לַמֶּלֶךְthe king’s וְחוּשַׁיHushai הָאַרְכִּיthe Archite רֵעַfriend הַמֶּלֶךְthe king’s

Ahithophel was the king’s counselor. Hushai the Archite was the king’s friend.

27:34

וְאַחֲרֵיand after אֲחִיתֹפֶלAhithophel יְהוֹיָדָעJehoiada בֶּןson בְּנָיָהוּBenaiah וְאֶבְיָתָרthen by Abiathar וְשַׂרwas the commander צָבָאarmy לַמֶּלֶךְof the king’s יוֹאָבJoab

Ahithophel was succeeded by Jehoiada son of Benaiah, then by Abiathar. Joab was the commander of the king’s army.
