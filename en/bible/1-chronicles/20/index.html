1 Chronicles 20

The capture of Rabbah and the giants of Gath

In the spring, at the time when kings march out to war, Joab led out the army and ravaged the land of the Ammonites. He came to Rabbah and besieged it, but David remained in Jerusalem. And Joab attacked Rabbah and demolished it.

Then David took the crown from the head of their king. It was found to weigh a talent of gold and was set with precious stones, and it was placed on David’s head. And David took a great amount of plunder from the city.

20:3

וְאֶתand [obj] הָעָםthe people אֲשֶׁרwho בָּהּin her הוֹצִיאbrought וַיָּשַׂרwork בַּמְּגֵרָהwith saws וּבַחֲרִיצֵיand axes הַבַּרְזֶלiron וּבַמְּגֵרוֹתpicks וְכֵןsame יַעֲשֶׂהdid דָוִידDavid לְכֹלto all עָרֵיcities בְנֵיsons עַמּוֹןAmmonite וַיָּשָׁבreturned דָּוִידDavid וְכָלand all הָעָםhis troops יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem

David brought out the people who were there and put them to work with saws, iron picks, and axes. And he did the same to all the Ammonite cities. Then David and all his troops returned to Jerusalem.

20:4

Some time later, war broke out with the Philistines at Gezer. At that time Sibbecai the Hushathite killed Sippai, a descendant of the Rephaim, and the Philistines were subdued.

20:5

וַתְּהִיand was עוֹדagain מִלְחָמָהbattle אֶתwith פְּלִשְׁתִּיםPhilistines וַיַּךְkilled אֶלְחָנָןElhanan בֶּןson יָעִירLahmi אֶת[obj] לַחְמִיLahmi אֲחִיbrother גָּלְיָתGoliath הַגִּתִּיthe Gittite וְעֵץshaft of whose חֲנִיתוֹspear was כִּמְנוֹרlike a weaver’s אֹרְגִיםbeam

Once again there was a battle with the Philistines, and Elhanan son of Jair killed Lahmi the brother of Goliath the Gittite, the shaft of whose spear was like a weaver’s beam.

20:6

וַתְּהִיand there was עוֹדstill מִלְחָמָהbattle בְּגַתat Gath וַיְהִיwhere there was אִישׁman מִדָּהgreat stature וְאֶצְבְּעֹתָיוwith fingers שֵׁשׁsix וָשֵׁשׁand six עֶשְׂרִיםtwenty וְאַרְבַּעfour וְגַםhe too הוּאwas נוֹלַדdescended לְהָרָפָאfrom Rapha

And there was still another battle at Gath, where there was a man of great stature with six fingers on each hand and six toes on each foot—twenty-four in all. He too was descended from Rapha,

20:7

וַיְחָרֵףand when he taunted אֶת[obj] יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וַיַּכֵּהוּkilled him יְהוֹנָתָןJonathan בֶּןson שִׁמְעָאShimei אֲחִיbrother דָוִידDavid’s

and when he taunted Israel, Jonathan the son of David’s brother Shimei killed him.

20:8

אֵלthese נוּלְּדוּdescendants לְהָרָפָאof Rapha בְּגַתin Gath וַיִּפְּלוּfell בְיַדat the hands דָּוִידDavid וּבְיַדand his עֲבָדָיוhis servants

So these descendants of Rapha in Gath fell at the hands of David and his servants.
