1 Chronicles 8

The tribe of Benjamin and the family of Saul

Open in the interlinear reader

8:1

וּבִנְיָמִןBenjamin הוֹלִידfather אֶת[obj] בֶּלַעBela בְּכֹרוֹhis firstborn אַשְׁבֵּלAshbel הַשֵּׁנִיthe second וְאַחְרַחAharah הַשְּׁלִישִׁיthe third

Benjamin was the father of Bela, his firstborn; Ashbel was the second born, Aharah the third,

8:2

נוֹחָהNohah הָרְבִיעִיthe fourth וְרָפָאand Rapha הַחֲמִישִׁיthe fifth

Nohah the fourth, and Rapha the fifth.

8:3

וַיִּהְיוּand were בָנִיםsons לְבָלַעof Bela אַדָּרBela וְגֵרָאand Gera וַאֲבִיהוּדand Abiyhuwd

The sons of Bela:

8:4

וַאֲבִישׁוּעַAbishua וְנַעֲמָןNaaman וַאֲחוֹחַAhoah

Addar, Gera, Abihud, Abishua, Naaman, Ahoah,

8:5

וְגֵרָאGera וּשְׁפוּפָןShephuphan וְחוּרָםand Huram

Gera, Shephuphan, and Huram.

8:6

וְאֵלֶּהthese בְּנֵיdescendants אֵחוּדEhud אֵלֶּהthese הֵםwere רָאשֵׁיheads אָבוֹתfamilies לְיוֹשְׁבֵיliving גֶבַעGeba וַיַּגְלוּםand were exiled אֶלto מָנָחַתManahath

These were the descendants of Ehud who were the heads of the families living in Geba and were exiled to Manahath:

8:7

וְנַעֲמָןNaaman וַאֲחִיָּהAhijah וְגֵרָאand Gera הוּאhe הֶגְלָםcarried them into exile וְהוֹלִידfather אֶת[obj] עֻזָּאUzza וְאֶתand [obj] אֲחִיחֻדAhihud

Naaman, Ahijah, and Gera, who carried them into exile and who was the father of Uzza and Ahihud.

8:8

וְשַׁחֲרַיִםShaharaim הוֹלִידhad sons בִּשְׂדֵהin the country מוֹאָבMoab מִןfrom שִׁלְחוֹdivorced אֹתָםthem חוּשִׁיםHushim וְאֶתand [obj] בַּעֲרָאBaara נָשָׁיוhis wives

Shaharaim had sons in the country of Moab after he had divorced his wives Hushim and Baara.

8:9

וַיּוֹלֶדhis sons מִןfrom חֹדֶשׁHodesh אִשְׁתּוֹhis wife אֶת[obj] יוֹבָבJobab וְאֶתand [obj] צִבְיָאTsibya וְאֶתand [obj] מֵישָׁאMeysha וְאֶתand [obj] מַלְכָּםHodesh

His sons by his wife Hodesh:

8:10

וְאֶתand [obj] יְעוּץJobab וְאֶתand [obj] שָׂכְיָהMesha וְאֶתand [obj] מִרְמָהMirmah אֵלֶּהthese בָנָיוhis sons רָאשֵׁיheads אָבוֹתfamilies

Jobab, Zibia, Mesha, Malcam, Jeuz, Sachia, and Mirmah. These were his sons, heads of families.

8:11

וּמֵחֻשִׁיםalso had by Hushim הוֹלִידsons אֶת[obj] אֲבִיטוּבAbitub וְאֶתand [obj] אֶלְפָּעַלElpaal

He also had sons by Hushim: Abitub and Elpaal.

8:12

וּבְנֵיsons אֶלְפַּעַלElpaal עֵבֶרEber וּמִשְׁעָםand Misham וָשָׁמֶדand Shemer הוּאhe בָּנָהbuilt אֶת[obj] אוֹנוֹOno וְאֶתand [obj] לֹדLod וּבְנֹתֶיהָand her daughters

The sons of Elpaal:

8:13

וּבְרִעָהvillages Beriah וָשֶׁמַעand Shema הֵמָּהwere רָאשֵׁיheads הָאָבוֹתof families לְיוֹשְׁבֵיof the inhabitants אַיָּלוֹןAijalon הֵמָּהthey הִבְרִיחוּdrove אֶת[obj] יוֹשְׁבֵיinhabitants גַתGath

Eber, Misham, Shemed (who built Ono and Lod with its villages), and Beriah and Shema (who were the heads of families of the inhabitants of Aijalon and who drove out the inhabitants of Gath).

8:14

וְאַחְיוֹAhio שָׁשָׁקShashak וִירֵמוֹתJeremoth

Ahio, Shashak, Jeremoth,

8:15

וּזְבַדְיָהZebadiah וַעֲרָדArad וָעָדֶרEder

Zebadiah, Arad, Eder,

8:16

וּמִיכָאֵלMichael וְיִשְׁפָּהIshpah וְיוֹחָאand Joha בְּנֵיsons בְרִיעָהBeriah

Michael, Ishpah, and Joha were the sons of Beriah.

8:17

וּזְבַדְיָהZebadiah וּמְשֻׁלָּםMeshullam וְחִזְקִיHizki וָחָבֶרHeber

Zebadiah, Meshullam, Hizki, Heber,

8:18

וְיִשְׁמְרַיIshmerai וְיִזְלִיאָהIzliah and וְיוֹבָבJobab בְּנֵיsons אֶלְפָּעַלElpaal

Ishmerai, Izliah, and Jobab were the sons of Elpaal.

8:19

וְיָקִיםJakim וְזִכְרִיZichri וְזַבְדִּיZabdi

Jakim, Zichri, Zabdi,

8:20

וֶאֱלִיעֵנַיElienai וְצִלְּתַיZillethai וֶאֱלִיאֵלEliel

Elienai, Zillethai, Eliel,

8:21

וַעֲדָיָהAdaiah וּבְרָאיָהBeraiah וְשִׁמְרָתand Shimrath בְּנֵיsons שִׁמְעִיShimei

Adaiah, Beraiah, and Shimrath were the sons of Shimei.

8:22

וְיִשְׁפָּןIshpan וָעֵבֶרEber וֶאֱלִיאֵלEliel

Ishpan, Eber, Eliel,

8:23

וְעַבְדּוֹןAbdon וְזִכְרִיZichri וְחָנָןHanan

Abdon, Zichri, Hanan,

8:24

וַחֲנַנְיָהHananiah וְעֵילָםElam וְעַנְתֹתִיָּהAnthothijah

Hananiah, Elam, Anthothijah,

8:25

וְיִפְדְיָהIphdeiah וּפְנוּאֵלand Penuel בְּנֵיsons שָׁשָׁקShashak

Iphdeiah, and Penuel were the sons of Shashak.

8:26

וְשַׁמְשְׁרַיShamsherai וּשְׁחַרְיָהShehariah וַעֲתַלְיָהAthaliah

Shamsherai, Shehariah, Athaliah,

8:27

וְיַעֲרֶשְׁיָהJaareshiah וְאֵלִיָּהElijah וְזִכְרִיand Zichri בְּנֵיsons יְרֹחָםJeroham

Jaareshiah, Elijah, and Zichri were the sons of Jeroham.

8:28

אֵלֶּהthese רָאשֵׁיheads אָבוֹתfamilies לְתֹלְדוֹתָםaccording to their genealogies רָאשִׁיםchiefs אֵלֶּהthese יָשְׁבוּlived בִירוּשָׁלִָםin Jerusalem

All these were heads of families, the chiefs according to their genealogies, and they lived in Jerusalem.

8:29

וּבְגִבְעוֹןin Gibeon יָשְׁבוּlived אֲבִיfather גִבְעוֹןGibeon וְשֵׁםname אִשְׁתּוֹhis wife’s מַעֲכָהMaacah

Jeiel the father of Gibeon lived in Gibeon. His wife’s name was Maacah,

8:30

וּבְנוֹhis son הַבְּכוֹרwas firstborn עַבְדּוֹןAbdon וְצוּרthen Zur וְקִישׁKish וּבַעַלBaal וְנָדָבNadab

and Abdon was his firstborn son, then Zur, Kish, Baal, Nadab,

8:31

וּגְדוֹרGedor וְאַחְיוֹAhio וָזָכֶרZecher

Gedor, Ahio, Zecher,

8:32

וּמִקְלוֹתand Mikloth הוֹלִידfather אֶת[obj] שִׁמְאָהShimeah וְאַףthese also הֵמָּהthey נֶגֶדbefore אֲחֵיהֶםtheir brothers יָשְׁבוּlived בִירוּשָׁלִַםin Jerusalem עִםwith אֲחֵיהֶםtheir relatives

and Mikloth, who was the father of Shimeah. These also lived alongside their relatives in Jerusalem.

8:33

וְנֵרNer הוֹלִידfather אֶת[obj] קִישׁKish וְקִישׁKish הוֹלִידfather אֶת[obj] שָׁאוּלSaul וְשָׁאוּלand Saul הוֹלִידfather אֶת[obj] יְהוֹנָתָןJonathan וְאֶתand [obj] מַלְכִּיMalchishua שׁוּעַMalchishua וְאֶתand [obj] אֲבִינָדָבAbinadab וְאֶתand [obj] אֶשְׁבָּעַלEsh baal

Ner was the father of Kish, Kish was the father of Saul, and Saul was the father of Jonathan, Malchishua, Abinadab, and Esh-baal.

8:34

וּבֶןson יְהוֹנָתָןJonathan מְרִיבMerib בָּעַלbaal וּמְרִיבand Merib בַּעַלbaal הוֹלִידfather אֶת[obj] מִיכָהMicah

The son of Jonathan: Merib-baal, and Merib-baal was the father of Micah.

8:35

וּבְנֵיsons מִיכָהMicah פִּיתוֹןPithon וָמֶלֶךְand Melech וְתַאְרֵעַTarea וְאָחָזand Ahaz

The sons of Micah: Pithon, Melech, Tarea, and Ahaz.

8:36

וְאָחָזAhaz הוֹלִידwas אֶת[obj] יְהוֹעַדָּהJehoaddah וִיהוֹעַדָּהJehoaddah הוֹלִידfather אֶת[obj] עָלֶמֶתAlemeth וְאֶתand [obj] עַזְמָוֶתAzmaveth וְאֶתand [obj] זִמְרִיZimri וְזִמְרִיand Zimri הוֹלִידfather אֶת[obj] מוֹצָאMoza

Ahaz was the father of Jehoaddah, Jehoaddah was the father of Alemeth, Azmaveth, and Zimri, and Zimri was the father of Moza.

8:37

וּמוֹצָאMoza הוֹלִידfather אֶת[obj] בִּנְעָאBinea רָפָהRaphah בְנוֹhis son אֶלְעָשָׂהEleasah בְנוֹhis son and אָצֵלAzel בְּנוֹhis son

Moza was the father of Binea. Raphah was his son, Eleasah his son, and Azel his son.

8:38

וּלְאָצֵלAzel had שִׁשָּׁהsix בָנִיםsons וְאֵלֶּהand these שְׁמוֹתָםtheir names עַזְרִיקָםAzrikam בֹּכְרוּBocheru וְיִשְׁמָעֵאלIshmael וּשְׁעַרְיָהSheariah וְעֹבַדְיָהObadiah וְחָנָןand Hanan כָּלall אֵלֶּהthese בְּנֵיsons אָצַלAzel

Azel had six sons, and these were their names: Azrikam, Bocheru, Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah, and Hanan. All these were the sons of Azel.

8:39

וּבְנֵיsons עֵשֶׁקEshek אָחִיוhis brother אוּלָםUlam בְּכֹרוֹhis firstborn יְעוּשׁJeush הַשֵּׁנִיthe second וֶאֱלִיפֶלֶטand Eliphelet הַשְּׁלִשִׁיEshek

The sons of his brother Eshek:

8:40

וַיִּהְיוּwas בְנֵיsons אוּלָםUlam אֲנָשִׁיםmen גִּבֹּרֵיmighty חַיִלvalor דֹּרְכֵיEliphelet קֶשֶׁתarchers וּמַרְבִּיםand they had many בָּנִיםsons וּבְנֵיand sons בָנִיםgrandsons מֵאָה150 וַחֲמִשִּׁים150 כָּלall אֵלֶּהthese מִבְּנֵיwere the descendants בִנְיָמִןBenjamin

Ulam was his firstborn, Jeush second, and Eliphelet third. The sons of Ulam were mighty men of valor, archers, and they had many sons and grandsons—150 in all. All these were the descendants of Benjamin.
