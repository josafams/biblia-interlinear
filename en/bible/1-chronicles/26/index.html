And Obed-edom also had sons: Shemaiah the firstborn, Jehozabad the second, Joah the third, Sachar the fourth, Nethanel the fifth,

עַמִּיאֵלAmmiel הַשִּׁשִּׁיthe sixth יִשָׂשכָרIssachar הַשְּׁבִיעִיthe seventh פְּעֻלְּתַיPeullethai הַשְּׁמִינִיthe eighth כִּיfor בֵרֲכוֹhad blessed אֱלֹהִיםGod

Ammiel the sixth, Issachar the seventh, and Peullethai the eighth. For God had blessed Obed-edom.

26:6

וְלִשְׁמַעְיָהShemaiah בְנוֹhis son נוֹלַדborn בָּנִיםsons הַמִּמְשָׁלִיםwho ruled לְבֵיתover their אֲבִיהֶםtheir families כִּיbecause גִבּוֹרֵיstrong men חַיִלcapable הֵמָּהthey

Also to his son Shemaiah were born sons who ruled over their families because they were strong, capable men.

26:7

בְּנֵיsons שְׁמַעְיָהShemaiah עָתְנִיOthni וּרְפָאֵלand Rephael וְעוֹבֵדObed and אֶלְזָבָדElzabad אֶחָיוhis brothers בְּנֵיwere חָיִלcapable אֱלִיהוּElihu וּסְמַכְיָהוּand Semachiah

Shemaiah’s sons were Othni, Rephael, Obed, and Elzabad; his brothers were Elihu and Semachiah, also capable men.

26:8

כָּלall אֵלֶּהthese מִבְּנֵיwere descendants עֹבֵדedom אֱדֹםObed הֵמָּהthey וּבְנֵיהֶםand their sons וַאֲחֵיהֶםand brothers were אִישׁmen חַיִלcapable בַּכֹּחַwith strength לַעֲבֹדָהwork שִׁשִּׁים62 וּשְׁנַיִםin all לְעֹבֵדin from edom אֱדֹםObed

All these were descendants of Obed-edom; they and their sons and brothers were capable men with strength to do the work—62 in all from Obed-edom.

26:9

וְלִמְשֶׁלֶמְיָהוּMeshelemiah also had בָּנִיםsons וְאַחִיםand brothers בְּנֵיwere חָיִלcapable שְׁמוֹנָה18 עָשָׂר18

Meshelemiah also had sons and brothers who were capable men—18 in all.

26:10

וּלְחֹסָהHosah מִןfrom בְּנֵיsons מְרָרִיMerarite בָּנִיםsons שִׁמְרִיShimri הָרֹאשׁthe first כִּיfor לֹאnot הָיָהwas בְכוֹרfirstborn וַיְשִׂימֵהוּhis had appointed אָבִיהוּfather לְרֹאשׁas the first

Hosah the Merarite also had sons: Shimri the first (although he was not the firstborn, his father had appointed him as the first),

26:11

חִלְקִיָּהוּHilkiah הַשֵּׁנִיthe second טְבַלְיָהוּTebaliah הַשְּׁלִשִׁיthe third זְכַרְיָהוּZechariah הָרְבִעִיthe fourth כָּלall בָּנִיםsons וְאַחִיםand brothers לְחֹסָהHosah 13 in שְׁלֹשָׁהnumbered עָשָׂר13

Hilkiah the second, Tebaliah the third, and Zechariah the fourth. The sons and brothers of Hosah numbered 13 in all.

26:12

לְאֵלֶּהthese מַחְלְקוֹתdivisions הַשֹּׁעֲרִיםthe gatekeepers לְרָאשֵׁיchief הַגְּבָרִיםmen had מִשְׁמָרוֹתduties לְעֻמַּתjust אֲחֵיהֶםtheir brothers לְשָׁרֵתministering בְּבֵיתin the house יְהוָהLORD

These divisions of the gatekeepers, through their chief men, had duties for ministering in the house of the LORD, just as their brothers did.

26:13

וַיַּפִּילוּthey cast גוֹרָלוֹתlots כַּקָּטֹןyoung כַּגָּדוֹלold לְבֵיתto their אֲבוֹתָםtheir families לְשַׁעַרfor each gate וָשָׁעַרand alike

They cast lots for each gate, according to their families, young and old alike.

26:14

וַיִּפֹּלfell הַגּוֹרָלthe lot מִזְרָחָהeast gate לְשֶׁלֶמְיָהוּto Shelemiah וּזְכַרְיָהוּZechariah בְנוֹhis son יוֹעֵץcounselor בְּשֶׂכֶלwise הִפִּילוּcast גּוֹרָלוֹתlots וַיֵּצֵאand went גוֹרָלוֹlot צָפוֹנָהnorth gate

The lot for the East Gate fell to Shelemiah. Then lots were cast for his son Zechariah, a wise counselor, and the lot for the North Gate fell to him.

26:15

לְעֹבֵדlot אֱדֹםObed נֶגְבָּהsouth gate וּלְבָנָיוhis sons בֵּיתhouse הָאֲסֻפִּיםthe storehouses

The lot for the South Gate fell to Obed-edom, and the lot for the storehouses to his sons.

26:16

לְשֻׁפִּיםto Shuppim וּלְחֹסָהand Hosah לַמַּעֲרָבfor the west עִםwith שַׁעַרgate שַׁלֶּכֶתShallecheth Gate בַּמְסִלָּהon the ascending highway הָעוֹלָהthe up מִשְׁמָרguards לְעֻמַּתstationed at every מִשְׁמָרwatch

The lots for the West Gate and the Shallecheth Gate on the ascending highway fell to Shuppim and Hosah. There were guards stationed at every watch.

26:17

לַמִּזְרָחthe east הַלְוִיִּםLevites שִׁשָּׁהsix לַצָּפוֹנָהthe north לַיּוֹםto the day אַרְבָּעָהfour לַנֶּגְבָּהon the south לַיּוֹםto the day אַרְבָּעָהfour וְלָאֲסֻפִּיםat the storehouse שְׁנַיִםtwo שְׁנָיִםpairs

Each day there were six Levites on the east, four on the north, four on the south, and two pairs at the storehouse.

26:18

לַפַּרְבָּרfor the court לַמַּעֲרָבon the west אַרְבָּעָהfour לַמְסִלָּהat the highway שְׁנַיִםtwo לַפַּרְבָּרat the court

As for the court on the west, there were four at the highway and two at the court.

26:19

אֵלֶּהthese מַחְלְקוֹתdivisions הַשֹּׁעֲרִיםthe gatekeepers לִבְנֵיdescendants הַקָּרְחִיof Korah וְלִבְנֵיand to sons מְרָרִיMerari

These were the divisions of the gatekeepers who were descendants of Korah and Merari.

26:20

וְהַלְוִיִּםtheir Levites אֲחִיָּהfellow עַלon אוֹצְרוֹתtreasuries בֵּיתhouse הָאֱלֹהִיםof God וּלְאֹצְרוֹתand the treasuries הַקֳּדָשִׁיםthe dedicated

Now their fellow Levites were in charge of the treasuries of the house of God and the treasuries of the dedicated things.

26:21

בְּנֵיdescendants לַעְדָּןLadan בְּנֵיwere הַגֵּרְשֻׁנִּיwho Gershonites לְלַעְדָּןLadan רָאשֵׁיheads הָאָבוֹתthe families לְלַעְדָּןof Ladan הַגֵּרְשֻׁנִּיthe Gershonite יְחִיאֵלִיJehieli

From the descendants of Ladan, who were Gershonites through Ladan and heads of the families of Ladan the Gershonite, were Jehieli,

26:22

בְּנֵיsons יְחִיאֵלִיJehieli זֵתָםZetham וְיוֹאֵלJoel אָחִיוhis brother עַלin אֹצְרוֹתtreasuries בֵּיתhouse יְהוָהLORD

the sons of Jehieli, Zetham, and his brother Joel. They were in charge of the treasuries of the house of the LORD.

26:23

לַעַמְרָמִיthe Amramites לַיִּצְהָרִיthe Izharites לַחֶבְרוֹנִיthe Hebronites לָעָזִּיאֵלִיand the Uzzielites

From the Amramites, the Izharites, the Hebronites, and the Uzzielites:

26:24

וּשְׁבֻאֵלShebuel בֶּןdescendant גֵּרְשׁוֹםGershom בֶּןson מֹשֶׁהMoses נָגִידofficer עַלin הָאֹצָרוֹתthe treasuries

Shebuel, a descendant of Gershom son of Moses, was the officer in charge of the treasuries.

26:25

וְאֶחָיוhis relatives לֶאֱלִיעֶזֶרEliezer רְחַבְיָהוּincluded Rehabiah בְנוֹhis son וִישַׁעְיָהוּJeshaiah בְנוֹhis son וְיֹרָםJoram בְּנוֹhis son וְזִכְרִיZichri בְנוֹhis son וּשְׁלֹמִיתand Shelomith בְּנוֹhis son

His relatives through Eliezer included Rehabiah his son, Jeshaiah his son, Joram his son, Zichri his son, and Shelomith his son.

26:26

הוּאhe שְׁלֹמוֹתShelomith וְאֶחָיוand his brothers עַלon כָּלall אֹצְרוֹתtreasuries הַקֳּדָשִׁיםthe things אֲשֶׁרthat הִקְדִּישׁdedicated דָּוִידDavid הַמֶּלֶךְby king וְרָאשֵׁיheads הָאָבוֹתfamilies לְשָׂרֵיthe commanders הָאֲלָפִיםof thousands וְהַמֵּאוֹתand of hundreds וְשָׂרֵיcommanders הַצָּבָאthe army

This Shelomith and his brothers were in charge of all the treasuries for the things dedicated by King David, by the heads of families who were the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, and by the army commanders.

26:27

מִןfrom הַמִּלְחָמוֹתtheir battles וּמִןhad some הַשָּׁלָלthe plunder הִקְדִּישׁוּdedicated לְחַזֵּקto the repair לְבֵיתof the house יְהוָהLORD

They had dedicated some of the plunder from their battles to the repair of the house of the LORD.

26:28

וְכֹלeverything had הַהִקְדִּישׁdedicated שְׁמוּאֵלSamuel הָרֹאֶהthe seer וְשָׁאוּלSaul בֶּןson קִישׁKish וְאַבְנֵרAbner בֶּןson נֵרNer וְיוֹאָבand Joab בֶּןson צְרוּיָהZeruiah כֹּלeverything הַמַּקְדִּישׁdedicated עַלon יַדunder שְׁלֹמִיתShelomith וְאֶחָיוand his brothers

Everything that had been dedicated by Samuel the seer, Saul son of Kish, Abner son of Ner, and Joab son of Zeruiah, along with everything else that was dedicated, was under the care of Shelomith and his brothers.

26:29

לַיִּצְהָרִיthe Izharites כְּנַנְיָהוּChenaniah וּבָנָיוand his sons לַמְּלָאכָהduties הַחִיצוֹנָהthe outside עַלover יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael לְשֹׁטְרִיםas officers וּלְשֹׁפְטִיםand judges

From the Izharites, Chenaniah and his sons had the outside duties as officers and judges over Israel.

26:30

לַחֶבְרוֹנִיthe Hebronites חֲשַׁבְיָהוּHashabiah וְאֶחָיוand his relatives בְּנֵי700 חַיִלcapable אֶלֶף1 700 וּשְׁבַעand 700 מֵאוֹתhad עַלon פְּקֻדַּתaffairs יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael מֵעֵבֶרwest לַיַּרְדֵּןof the Jordan מַעְרָבָהwest toward לְכֹלfor all מְלֶאכֶתwork יְהוָהLORD וְלַעֲבֹדַתand for the service הַמֶּלֶךְthe king

From the Hebronites, Hashabiah and his relatives, 1,700 capable men, had charge of the affairs of Israel west of the Jordan for all the work of the LORD and for the service of the king.

26:31

לַחֶבְרוֹנִיfor the Hebronites יְרִיָּהJerijah הָרֹאשׁthe chief לַחֶבְרוֹנִיof the Hebronites לְתֹלְדֹתָיוaccording to the genealogies of his לְאָבוֹתancestors בִּשְׁנַתyear הָאַרְבָּעִיםthe fortieth לְמַלְכוּתof reign דָּוִידDavid’s נִדְרָשׁוּsearched וַיִּמָּצֵאfound בָהֶםwere among גִּבּוֹרֵיstrong men חַיִלcapable בְּיַעְזֵירat Jazer גִּלְעָדGilead

As for the Hebronites, Jerijah was the chief of the Hebronites, according to the genealogies of his ancestors. In the fortieth year of David’s reign the records were searched, and strong, capable men were found among the Hebronites at Jazer in Gilead.

26:32

וְאֶחָיוJerijah’s relatives בְּנֵיwere חַיִלcapable אַלְפַּיִם2 וּשְׁבַע700 מֵאוֹתmen רָאשֵׁיheads הָאָבוֹתof families וַיַּפְקִידֵםappointed them דָּוִידDavid הַמֶּלֶךְking עַלover הָראוּבֵנִיthe Reubenites וְהַגָּדִיthe Gadites וַחֲצִיand the half שֵׁבֶטtribe הַמְנַשִּׁיof Manasseh לְכָלfor every דְּבַרmatter הָאֱלֹהִיםpertaining to God וּדְבַרaffairs הַמֶּלֶךְthe king

Among Jerijah’s relatives there were 2,700 capable men who were heads of families. King David appointed them over the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh for every matter pertaining to God and to the affairs of the king.
