1 Chronicles 13

The first attempt to bring the ark

And he said to the whole assembly of Israel, “If it seems good to you, and if this is of the LORD our God, let us send word far and wide to the rest of our brothers in all the land of Israel, and also to the priests and Levites in their cities and pasturelands, so that they may join us.

13:3

וְנָסֵבָּהbring אֶת[obj] אֲרוֹןark אֱלֹהֵינוּour God אֵלֵינוּto us כִּיfor לֹאnot דְרַשְׁנֻהוּinquire of him בִּימֵיin the days שָׁאוּלSaul

Then let us bring back the ark of our God, for we did not inquire of Him in the days of Saul.”

13:4

And because this proposal seemed right to all the people, the whole assembly agreed to it.

13:5

So David assembled all Israel, from the River Shihor in Egypt to Lebo-hamath, to bring the ark of God from Kiriath-jearim.

13:6

וַיַּעַלwent up דָּוִידDavid וְכָלand all יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael בַּעֲלָתָהBaalah אֶלto קִרְיַתKiriath יְעָרִיםjearim אֲשֶׁרthat לִיהוּדָהJudah לְהַעֲלוֹתto bring up מִשָּׁםfrom there אֵת[obj] אֲרוֹןark הָאֱלֹהִיםof God יְהוָהLORD יוֹשֵׁבenthroned הַכְּרוּבִיםbetween the cherubim the ark אֲשֶׁרthat נִקְרָאcalled שֵׁםname

David and all Israel went up to Baalah of Judah (that is, Kiriath-jearim) to bring up from there the ark of God the LORD, who is enthroned between the cherubim—the ark that is called by the Name.

13:7

וַיַּרְכִּיבוּso they carried אֶת[obj] אֲרוֹןark הָאֱלֹהִיםof God עַלon עֲגָלָהcart חֲדָשָׁהnew מִבֵּיתfrom the house אֲבִינָדָבAbinadab וְעֻזָּאwith Uzzah וְאַחְיוֹand Ahio נֹהֲגִיםguiding בָּעֲגָלָהthe cart

So they carried the ark of God from the house of Abinadab on a new cart, with Uzzah and Ahio guiding the cart.

13:8

וְדָוִידDavid וְכָלand all יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites מְשַׂחֲקִיםcelebrating לִפְנֵיbefore הָאֱלֹהִיםGod בְּכָלwith all עֹזmight וּבְשִׁירִיםwith songs וּבְכִנֹּרוֹתlyres וּבִנְבָלִיםharps וּבְתֻפִּיםand with tambourines וּבִמְצִלְתַּיִםcymbals וּבַחֲצֹצְרוֹתand trumpets

David and all the Israelites were celebrating before God with all their might, with songs and on harps and lyres, with tambourines, cymbals, and trumpets.

13:9

וַיָּבֹאוּwhen they came עַדto גֹּרֶןthreshing floor כִּידֹןChidon וַיִּשְׁלַחreached out and עֻזָּאUzzah אֶת[obj] יָדוֹhis hand לֶאֱחֹזhold אֶת[obj] הָאָרוֹןthe ark כִּיbecause שָׁמְטוּoxen הַבָּקָרthe oxen

When they came to the threshing floor of Chidon, Uzzah reached out and took hold of the ark, because the oxen had stumbled.

13:10

And the anger of the LORD burned against Uzzah, and He struck him down because he had put his hand on the ark. So he died there before God.

13:11

וַיִּחַרangry לְדָוִידDavid became כִּיbecause פָרַץburst יְהוָהLORD פֶּרֶץforth בְּעֻזָּאagainst Uzzah וַיִּקְרָאso he named לַמָּקוֹםthat place הַהוּאthat פֶּרֶץPerez עֻזָּאPerez עַדto הַיּוֹםthis day הַזֶּהthis

Then David became angry because the LORD had burst forth against Uzzah; so he named that place Perez-uzzah, as it is called to this day.

13:12

וַיִּירָאfeared דָוִידDavid אֶת[obj] הָאֱלֹהִיםGod בַּיּוֹםthat day הַהוּאthat לֵאמֹרand asked הֵיךְhow אָבִיאbring אֵלַיto me אֵת[obj] אֲרוֹןark הָאֱלֹהִיםof God

That day David feared God and asked, “How can I ever bring the ark of God to me?”

13:13

וְלֹאand not הֵסִירmove דָּוִידDavid אֶת[obj] הָאָרוֹןthe ark אֵלָיוto him אֶלto עִירcity דָּוִידDavid וַיַּטֵּהוּinstead he took it אֶלto בֵּיתhouse עֹבֵדObed אֱדֹםedom הַגִּתִּיthe Gittite

So he did not move the ark with him to the City of David; instead, he took it aside to the house of Obed-edom the Gittite.

13:14

Thus the ark of God remained with the family of Obed-edom in his house for three months, and the LORD blessed his household and everything he owned.
