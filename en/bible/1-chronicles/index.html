1 Chronicles דִּבְרֵי הַיָּמִים

1 Chronicles retells the history of Israel from the standpoint of Jerusalem and the temple. Chapters 1 to 9 are genealogies running from Adam to the community that returned from exile, with emphasis on the tribe of Judah, the family of David and the tribe of Levi. After a brief account of Saul's death (ch. 10), the book concentrates on David: his acclamation as king, the conquest of Jerusalem, the arrival of the ark in the city and the promise of a lasting dynasty. The final part deals with the preparations for the temple and the organization of priests, Levites, singers and gatekeepers, which David leaves ready for Solomon before he dies.

Structure

  1. Genealogies chs. 1–9

    From Adam to the twelve tribes, with emphasis on Judah, the house of David and Levi, down to the inhabitants of Jerusalem after the exile.

  2. David becomes king chs. 10–12

    The death of Saul, David's anointing at Hebron, the conquest of Jerusalem and the warriors who supported him.

  3. The ark in Jerusalem chs. 13–16

    The first failed attempt, the transfer carried out by the Levites and the worship of praise before the ark.

  4. The promise and the wars chs. 17–21

    The promise of a dynasty, the victories over the neighboring peoples and the census that leads to the choice of the site of the temple.

  5. Preparations for the temple chs. 22–29

    Materials, divisions of priests and Levites, musicians, gatekeepers and officials; David's last words and Solomon's succession.
