1 Chronicles 18

David's victories

Open in the interlinear reader

18:1

וַיְהִיsome time אַחֲרֵיlater כֵןthis וַיַּךְdefeated דָּוִידDavid אֶת[obj] פְּלִשְׁתִּיםPhilistines וַיַּכְנִיעֵםsubdued them וַיִּקַּחand took אֶת[obj] גַּתGath וּבְנֹתֶיהָand its villages מִיַּדfrom the hand פְּלִשְׁתִּיםPhilistines

Some time later, David defeated the Philistines, subdued them, and took Gath and its villages from the hand of the Philistines.

18:2

וַיַּךְDavid also defeated אֶת[obj] מוֹאָבMoabites וַיִּהְיוּand they became מוֹאָבMoab עֲבָדִיםsubject לְדָוִידto David נֹשְׂאֵיbrought מִנְחָהtribute

David also defeated the Moabites, and they became subject to David and brought him tribute.

18:3

וַיַּךְalso defeated דָּוִידDavid אֶת[obj] הֲדַדְעֶזֶרHadadezer מֶלֶךְking צוֹבָהZobah חֲמָתָהHamath בְּלֶכְתּוֹhad marched out לְהַצִּיבto establish יָדוֹhis along בִּנְהַרEuphrates פְּרָתRiver

As far as Hamath, David also defeated King Hadadezer of Zobah, who had marched out to establish his dominion along the Euphrates River.

18:4

וַיִּלְכֹּדcaptured דָּוִידDavid מִמֶּנּוּfrom him אֶלֶףthousand רֶכֶבchariots וְשִׁבְעַתseven אֲלָפִיםthousand פָּרָשִׁיםcharioteers וְעֶשְׂרִיםand twenty אֶלֶףthousand אִישׁman רַגְלִיfoot soldiers וַיְעַקֵּרand he hamstrung דָּוִידDavid אֶת[obj] כָּלall הָרֶכֶבthe chariots וַיּוֹתֵרhorses מִמֶּנּוּfrom us מֵאָהhundred רָכֶבchariots

David captured from him a thousand chariots, seven thousand charioteers, and twenty thousand foot soldiers, and he hamstrung all the horses except a hundred he kept for the chariots.

18:5

וַיָּבֹאcame אֲרַםArameans דַּרְמֶשֶׂקDamascus לַעְזוֹרto help לַהֲדַדְעֶזֶרHadadezer מֶלֶךְking צוֹבָהZobah וַיַּךְstruck דָּוִידDavid בַּאֲרָםdown עֶשְׂרִיםtwenty וּשְׁנַיִםtwo אֶלֶףthousand אִישׁmen

When the Arameans of Damascus came to help King Hadadezer of Zobah, David struck down twenty-two thousand men.

18:6

וַיָּשֶׂםthen he placed דָּוִידgarrisons בַּאֲרַםin Aram דַּרְמֶשֶׂקDamascus וַיְהִיbecame אֲרָםAram לְדָוִידto David עֲבָדִיםsubject נֹשְׂאֵיbrought מִנְחָהtribute וַיּוֹשַׁעvictorious יְהוָהLORD לְדָוִידDavid בְּכֹלwherever אֲשֶׁרthat הָלָךְwent

Then he placed garrisons in Aram of Damascus, and the Arameans became subject to David and brought him tribute. So the LORD made David victorious wherever he went.

18:7

וַיִּקַּחand took דָּוִידDavid אֵת[obj] שִׁלְטֵיshields הַזָּהָבthe gold אֲשֶׁרthat הָיוּbelonged עַלto עַבְדֵיofficers הֲדַדְעָזֶרHadadezer וַיְבִיאֵםand brought them יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem

And David took the gold shields that belonged to the officers of Hadadezer and brought them to Jerusalem.

18:8

וּמִטִּבְחַתfrom Tibhath וּמִכּוּןCun עָרֵיcities הֲדַדְעֶזֶרHadadezer לָקַחtook דָּוִידDavid נְחֹשֶׁתbronze רַבָּהlarge מְאֹדvery בָּהּwith which עָשָׂהmade שְׁלֹמֹהSolomon אֶת[obj] יָםsea הַנְּחֹשֶׁתthe bronze וְאֶתand [obj] הָעַמּוּדִיםthe pillars וְאֵתand [obj] כְּלֵיarticles הַנְּחֹשֶׁתvarious bronze

And from Tibhath and Cun, cities of Hadadezer, David took a large amount of bronze, with which Solomon made the bronze Sea, the pillars, and various bronze articles.

18:9

וַיִּשְׁמַעheard תֹּעוּTou מֶלֶךְking חֲמָתHamath כִּיthat הִכָּהdefeated דָוִידDavid אֶת[obj] כָּלentire חֵילarmy הֲדַדְעֶזֶרHadadezer מֶלֶךְking צוֹבָהZobah

When King Tou of Hamath heard that David had defeated the entire army of Hadadezer king of Zobah,

18:10

וַיִּשְׁלַחhe sent אֶת[obj] הֲדוֹרָםHadoram בְּנוֹhis son אֶלto הַמֶּלֶךְking דָּוִידDavid לִשְׁאָלgreet לוֹto ask him לְשָׁלוֹםto peace וּלְבָרֲכוֹand bless him עַלfor אֲשֶׁרhad נִלְחַםfighting בַּהֲדַדְעֶזֶרHadadezer וַיַּכֵּהוּand defeating כִּיfor אִישׁwho מִלְחֲמוֹתwar תֹּעוּTou הָיָהwas הֲדַדְעָזֶרHadadezer וְכֹלall kinds כְּלֵיarticles זָהָבgold וָכֶסֶףand silver וּנְחֹשֶׁתand bronze

he sent his son Hadoram to greet King David and bless him for fighting and defeating Hadadezer, who had been at war with Tou. Hadoram brought all kinds of articles of gold and silver and bronze,

18:11

גַּםand אֹתָםthem הִקְדִּישׁdedicated הַמֶּלֶךְking דָּוִידDavid לַיהוָהto the LORD עִםwith הַכֶּסֶףthe silver וְהַזָּהָבand gold אֲשֶׁרhad נָשָׂאcarried מִכָּלfrom all הַגּוֹיִםthese nations מֵאֱדוֹםfrom Edom וּמִמּוֹאָבand Moab וּמִבְּנֵיand from descendants עַמּוֹןAmmonites וּמִפְּלִשְׁתִּיםPhilistines וּמֵעֲמָלֵקand Amalekites

and King David dedicated these to the LORD, along with the silver and gold he had carried off from all these nations—from Edom and Moab, and from the Ammonites, Philistines, and Amalekites.

18:12

וְאַבְשַׁיAbishai בֶּןson צְרוּיָהZeruiah הִכָּהstruck אֶת[obj] אֱדוֹםEdomites בְּגֵיאin the Valley הַמֶּלַחSalt שְׁמוֹנָהeighteen עָשָׂרeighteen אָלֶףthousand

Moreover, Abishai son of Zeruiah struck down eighteen thousand Edomites in the Valley of Salt.

18:13

וַיָּשֶׂםhe placed בֶּאֱדוֹםin Edom נְצִיבִיםgarrisons וַיִּהְיוּand were כָלall אֱדוֹםEdom עֲבָדִיםsubject לְדָוִידto David וַיּוֹשַׁעvictorious יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] דָּוִידDavid בְּכֹלwherever אֲשֶׁרthat הָלָךְwent

He placed garrisons in Edom, and all the Edomites were subject to David. So the LORD made David victorious wherever he went.

18:14

וַיִּמְלֹךְthus reigned דָּוִידDavid עַלover כָּלall יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וַיְהִיand was עֹשֶׂהadministered מִשְׁפָּטjustice וּצְדָקָהand righteousness לְכָלfor all עַמּוֹhis people

Thus David reigned over all Israel and administered justice and righteousness for all his people:

18:15

וְיוֹאָבJoab בֶּןson צְרוּיָהZeruiah עַלover הַצָּבָאthe army וִיהוֹשָׁפָטJehoshaphat בֶּןson אֲחִילוּדAhilud מַזְכִּירrecorder

Joab son of Zeruiah was over the army; Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was the recorder;

18:16

וְצָדוֹקZadok בֶּןson אֲחִיטוּבAhitub וַאֲבִימֶלֶךְand Ahimelech בֶּןson אֶבְיָתָרAbiathar כֹּהֲנִיםpriests וְשַׁוְשָׁאShavsha סוֹפֵרscribe

Zadok son of Ahitub and Ahimelech son of Abiathar were priests; Shavsha was the scribe;

18:17

וּבְנָיָהוּBenaiah בֶּןson יְהוֹיָדָעJehoiada עַלover הַכְּרֵתִיthe Cherethites וְהַפְּלֵתִיand Pelethites וּבְנֵיand sons דָוִידDavid’s הָרִאשֹׁנִיםchief לְיַדto beside הַמֶּלֶךְthe king’s

Benaiah son of Jehoiada was over the Cherethites and Pelethites; and David’s sons were chief officials at the king’s side.
