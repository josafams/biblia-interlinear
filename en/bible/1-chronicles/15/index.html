1 Chronicles 15

The Levites carry the ark to Jerusalem

Open in the interlinear reader

15:1

וַיַּעַשׂDavid constructed לוֹfor himself בָתִּיםbuildings בְּעִירin the city דָּוִידDavid וַיָּכֶןand he prepared מָקוֹםplace לַאֲרוֹןfor the ark הָאֱלֹהִיםof God וַיֶּטand pitched לוֹfor it אֹהֶלtent

David constructed buildings for himself in the City of David, and he prepared a place for the ark of God and pitched a tent for it.

15:2

אָזthen אָמַרsaid דָּוִידDavid לֹאno לָשֵׂאתmay carry אֶת[obj] אֲרוֹןark הָאֱלֹהִיםof God כִּיbecause אִםif הַלְוִיִּםthe Levites כִּיfor בָםthe בָּחַרchosen יְהוָהLORD לָשֵׂאתto carry אֶת[obj] אֲרוֹןark יְהוָהLORD וּלְשָׁרְתוֹand to minister him עַדuntil עוֹלָםforever

Then David said, “No one but the Levites may carry the ark of God, because the LORD has chosen them to carry the ark of the LORD and to minister before Him forever.”

15:3

וַיַּקְהֵלand assembled דָּוִידDavid אֶת[obj] כָּלall יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael אֶלto יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem לְהַעֲלוֹתto bring up אֶת[obj] אֲרוֹןark יְהוָהLORD אֶלto מְקוֹמוֹplace he אֲשֶׁרhad הֵכִיןprepared לוֹfor it

And David assembled all Israel in Jerusalem to bring up the ark of the LORD to the place he had prepared for it.

15:4

וַיֶּאֱסֹףthen he gathered דָּוִידDavid אֶת[obj] בְּנֵיdescendants אַהֲרֹןAaron וְאֶתand [obj] הַלְוִיִּםthe Levites

Then he gathered together the descendants of Aaron and the Levites:

לִבְנֵיto sons קְהָתKohathites אוּרִיאֵלUriel הַשָּׂרthe chief וְאֶחָיוhis relatives מֵאָה120 וְעֶשְׂרִיםand 120

From the Kohathites, Uriel the chief and 120 of his relatives;

15:6

לִבְנֵיto sons מְרָרִיMerarites עֲשָׂיָהAsaiah הַשָּׂרthe chief וְאֶחָיוhis relatives מָאתַיִםrelatives וְעֶשְׂרִים220

from the Merarites, Asaiah the chief and 220 of his relatives;

15:7

לִבְנֵיto sons גֵּרְשׁוֹםGershomites יוֹאֵלJoel הַשָּׂרthe chief וְאֶחָיוhis relatives מֵאָהrelatives וּשְׁלֹשִׁים130

from the Gershomites, Joel the chief and 130 of his relatives;

15:8

לִבְנֵיto sons אֱלִיצָפָןElizaphanites שְׁמַעְיָהShemaiah הַשָּׂרthe chief וְאֶחָיוhis relatives מָאתָיִם200

from the Elizaphanites, Shemaiah the chief and 200 of his relatives;

15:9

לִבְנֵיto sons חֶבְרוֹןHebronites אֱלִיאֵלEliel הַשָּׂרthe chief וְאֶחָיוhis relatives שְׁמוֹנִים80

from the Hebronites, Eliel the chief and 80 of his relatives;

15:10

לִבְנֵיto sons עֻזִּיאֵלUzzielites עַמִּינָדָבAmminadab הַשָּׂרthe chief וְאֶחָיוhis relatives מֵאָהrelatives וּשְׁנֵיםand two עָשָׂר112

and from the Uzzielites, Amminadab the chief and 112 of his relatives.

15:11

וַיִּקְרָאsummoned דָוִידDavid לְצָדוֹקZadok וּלְאֶבְיָתָרand Abiathar הַכֹּהֲנִיםthe priests וְלַלְוִיִּםand the Levites לְאוּרִיאֵלUriel עֲשָׂיָהAsaiah וְיוֹאֵלJoel שְׁמַעְיָהShemaiah וֶאֱלִיאֵלEliel וְעַמִּינָדָבand Amminadab

David summoned the priests Zadok and Abiathar and the Levites Uriel, Asaiah, Joel, Shemaiah, Eliel, and Amminadab.

15:12

וַיֹּאמֶרand he said לָהֶםto them אַתֶּםyou רָאשֵׁיheads הָאָבוֹתfamilies לַלְוִיִּםthe Levitical הִתְקַדְּשׁוּconsecrate אַתֶּםyou וַאֲחֵיכֶםand your relatives וְהַעֲלִיתֶםbring אֵת[obj] אֲרוֹןark יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael אֶלto הֲכִינוֹתִיprepared לוֹfor it

And he said to them, “You are the heads of the Levitical families. You and your relatives must consecrate yourselves so that you may bring the ark of the LORD, the God of Israel, to the place I have prepared for it.

15:13

כִּיbecause לְמַto why בָּרִאשׁוֹנָהwith us the first לֹאnot אַתֶּםyou פָּרַץburst יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God בָּנוּagainst us כִּיfor לֹאnot דְרַשְׁנֻהוּconsult him כַּמִּשְׁפָּטabout the proper

It was because you Levites were not with us the first time that the LORD our God burst forth in anger against us. For we did not consult Him about the proper order.”

15:14

וַיִּתְקַדְּשׁוּconsecrated הַכֹּהֲנִיםthe priests וְהַלְוִיִּםand Levites לְהַעֲלוֹתto bring up אֶת[obj] אֲרוֹןark יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

So the priests and Levites consecrated themselves to bring up the ark of the LORD, the God of Israel.

15:15

וַיִּשְׂאוּcarried בְנֵיsons הַלְוִיִּםLevites the אֵת[obj] אֲרוֹןark הָאֱלֹהִיםof God כַּאֲשֶׁרas had צִוָּהcommanded מֹשֶׁהMoses כִּדְבַרword יְהוָהLORD בִּכְתֵפָםon their shoulders בַּמֹּטוֹתwith the poles עֲלֵיהֶםon them

And the Levites carried the ark of God on their shoulders with the poles, as Moses had commanded in accordance with the word of the LORD.

15:16

וַיֹּאמֶרalso told דָּוִידDavid לְשָׂרֵיthe leaders הַלְוִיִּםthe Levites לְהַעֲמִידto appoint אֶת[obj] אֲחֵיהֶםtheir relatives הַמְשֹׁרְרִיםas singers בִּכְלֵיby instruments שִׁירmusical נְבָלִיםharps וְכִנֹּרוֹתlyres וּמְצִלְתָּיִםand cymbals מַשְׁמִיעִיםmusic לְהָרִיםto lift בְּקוֹלup their voices with לְשִׂמְחָהwith joy

David also told the leaders of the Levites to appoint their relatives as singers to lift up their voices with joy, accompanied by musical instruments—harps, lyres, and cymbals.

15:17

וַיַּעֲמִידוּappointed הַלְוִיִּםthe Levites אֵת[obj] הֵימָןHeman בֶּןson יוֹאֵלJoel וּמִןfrom אֶחָיוhis brothers אָסָףAsaph בֶּןson בֶּרֶכְיָהוּBerechiah וּמִןfrom בְּנֵיsons מְרָרִיMerarites אֲחֵיהֶםtheir brothers אֵיתָןEthan בֶּןson קוּשָׁיָהוּKushaiah

So the Levites appointed Heman son of Joel; from his brothers, Asaph son of Berechiah; from their brothers the Merarites, Ethan son of Kushaiah;

15:18

וְעִמָּהֶםand with them אֲחֵיהֶםtheir brothers הַמִּשְׁנִיםnext in rank Jaaziel זְכַרְיָהוּZechariah בֵּןrank וְיַעֲזִיאֵלand Jaaziel וּשְׁמִירָמוֹתShemiramoth וִיחִיאֵלJehiel וְעֻנִּיUnni אֱלִיאָבEliab וּבְנָיָהוּBenaiah וּמַעֲשֵׂיָהוּMaaseiah וּמַתִּתְיָהוּMattithiah וֶאֱלִיפְלֵהוּEliphelehu וּמִקְנֵיָהוּMikneiah וְעֹבֵדObed אֱדֹםedom וִיעִיאֵלand Jeiel הַשֹּׁעֲרִיםthe gatekeepers

and with them their brothers next in rank: Zechariah, Jaaziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, and the gatekeepers Obed-edom and Jeiel.

15:19

וְהַמְשֹׁרְרִיםthe musicians הֵימָןHeman אָסָףAsaph וְאֵיתָןand Ethan בִּמְצִלְתַּיִםto sound נְחֹשֶׁתbronze לְהַשְׁמִיעַto joined

The musicians Heman, Asaph, and Ethan were to sound the bronze cymbals.

15:20

וּזְכַרְיָהZechariah וַעֲזִיאֵלAziel וּשְׁמִירָמוֹתShemiramoth וִיחִיאֵלJehiel וְעֻנִּיUnni וֶאֱלִיאָבEliab וּמַעֲשֵׂיָהוּMaaseiah וּבְנָיָהוּand Benaiah בִּנְבָלִיםto play the harps עַלto עֲלָמוֹתaccording Alamoth

Zechariah, Aziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Maaseiah, and Benaiah were to play the harps according to Alamoth.

15:21

וּמַתִּתְיָהוּAnd Mattithiah וֶאֱלִיפְלֵהוּEliphelehu וּמִקְנֵיָהוּMikneiah וְעֹבֵדObed אֱדֹםedom וִיעִיאֵלJeiel וַעֲזַזְיָהוּAzaziah בְּכִנֹּרוֹתmusic with lyres עַלon הַשְּׁמִינִיתSheminith לְנַצֵּחַaccording to

And Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, Obed-edom, Jeiel, and Azaziah were to lead the music with lyres according to Sheminith.

15:22

וּכְנַנְיָהוּChenaniah שַׂרdirector הַלְוִיִּםthe head Levite בְּמַשָּׂאmusic יָסֹרdiscipline בַּמַּשָּׂאin the burden כִּיfor מֵבִיןskilled הוּאwas

Chenaniah the head Levite was the director of the music because he was highly skilled.

15:23

וּבֶרֶכְיָהBerechiah וְאֶלְקָנָהand Elkanah שֹׁעֲרִיםguardians לָאָרוֹןthe ark

Berechiah and Elkanah were to be guardians of the ark.

15:24

וּשְׁבַנְיָהוּShebaniah וְיוֹשָׁפָטJoshaphat וּנְתַנְאֵלNethanel וַעֲמָשַׂיAmasai וּזְכַרְיָהוּZechariah וּבְנָיָהוּBenaiah וֶאֱלִיעֶזֶרand Eliezer הַכֹּהֲנִיםthe priests מַחְצְרִיםblow בַּחֲצֹצְרוֹתthe trumpets לִפְנֵיbefore אֲרוֹןark הָאֱלֹהִיםof God וְעֹבֵדObed אֱדֹםedom וִיחִיָּהand Jehiah שֹׁעֲרִיםguardians לָאָרוֹןof the ark

Shebaniah, Joshaphat, Nethanel, Amasai, Zechariah, Benaiah, and Eliezer—the priests—were to blow the trumpets before the ark of God. Obed-edom and Jehiah were also to be guardians of the ark.

15:25

וַיְהִיand was דָוִידDavid וְזִקְנֵיelders יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וְשָׂרֵיand the commanders הָאֲלָפִיםof thousands הַהֹלְכִיםwent לְהַעֲלוֹתto bring אֶת[obj] אֲרוֹןark בְּרִיתcovenant יְהוָהLORD מִןfrom בֵּיתhouse עֹבֵדObed אֱדֹםedom בְּשִׂמְחָהwith rejoicing

So David, the elders of Israel, and the commanders of thousands went with rejoicing to bring the ark of the covenant of the LORD from the house of Obed-edom.

15:26

וַיְהִיand was בֶּעְזֹרbecause helped הָאֱלֹהִיםGod the אֶת[obj] הַלְוִיִּםLevites נֹשְׂאֵיcarrying אֲרוֹןark בְּרִיתcovenant יְהוָהLORD וַיִּזְבְּחוּthey sacrificed שִׁבְעָהseven פָרִיםbulls וְשִׁבְעָהand seven אֵילִיםrams

And because God helped the Levites who were carrying the ark of the covenant of the LORD, they sacrificed seven bulls and seven rams.

15:27

וְדָוִידNow David מְכֻרְבָּלdressed בִּמְעִילin a robe בּוּץfine linen וְכָלas were all הַלְוִיִּםthe Levites הַנֹּשְׂאִיםwho were carrying אֶת[obj] הָאָרוֹןthe ark וְהַמְשֹׁרְרִיםwell as the singers וּכְנַנְיָהand Chenaniah הַשַּׂרdirector הַמַּשָּׂאthe director of music הַמְשֹׁרְרִיםthe singers וְעַלand on דָּוִידDavid אֵפוֹדephod בָּדlinen

Now David was dressed in a robe of fine linen, as were all the Levites who were carrying the ark, as well as the singers and Chenaniah, the director of music for the singers. David also wore a linen ephod.

15:28

וְכָלso all יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael מַעֲלִיםbrought אֶת[obj] אֲרוֹןark בְּרִיתcovenant יְהוָהLORD בִּתְרוּעָהwith shouting וּבְקוֹלwith the sounding שׁוֹפָרrams horns וּבַחֲצֹצְרוֹתand trumpets וּבִמְצִלְתָּיִםand with cymbals מַשְׁמִעִיםmusic בִּנְבָלִיםharps וְכִנֹּרוֹתand lyres

So all Israel brought up the ark of the covenant of the LORD with shouting, with the sounding of rams’ horns and trumpets, and with cymbals and the music of harps and lyres.

15:29

וַיְהִיand was אֲרוֹןark בְּרִיתcovenant יְהוָהLORD בָּאentering עַדuntil עִירcity דָּוִידDavid וּמִיכַלMichal בַּתdaughter שָׁאוּלSaul’s נִשְׁקְפָהlooked down בְּעַדfrom הַחַלּוֹןa window וַתֵּרֶאand saw אֶת[obj] הַמֶּלֶךְking דָּוִידDavid מְרַקֵּדdancing וּמְשַׂחֵקand celebrating וַתִּבֶזand she despised לוֹto him בְּלִבָּהּin her heart

As the ark of the covenant of the LORD was entering the City of David, Saul’s daughter Michal looked down from a window and saw King David dancing and celebrating, and she despised him in her heart.
