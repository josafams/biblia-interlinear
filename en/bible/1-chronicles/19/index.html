1 Chronicles 19

War against the Ammonites and Arameans

Open in the interlinear reader

19:1

וַיְהִיsome time אַחֲרֵיlater כֵןthis וַיָּמָתdied and נָחָשׁNahash מֶלֶךְking בְּנֵיsons עַמּוֹןAmmonites וַיִּמְלֹךְhis בְּנוֹson תַּחְתָּיוunder him

Some time later, Nahash king of the Ammonites died and was succeeded by his son.

19:2

וַיֹּאמֶרand said דָּוִידDavid אֶעֱשֶׂהshow חֶסֶדshow kindness עִםto חָנוּןHanun בֶּןson נָחָשׁNahash כִּיbecause עָשָׂהshowed אָבִיוhis father עִמִּיto me חֶסֶדkindness וַיִּשְׁלַחso sent דָּוִידDavid מַלְאָכִיםmessengers לְנַחֲמוֹto console עַלon אָבִיוhis father וַיָּבֹאוּarrived עַבְדֵיservants דָוִידDavid’s אֶלto אֶרֶץland בְּנֵיsons עַמּוֹןAmmonites אֶלto חָנוּןHanun לְנַחֲמוֹto console him

And David said, “I will show kindness to Hanun son of Nahash, because his father showed kindness to me.” So David sent messengers to console Hanun concerning his father. But when David’s servants arrived in the land of the Ammonites to console him,

19:3

וַיֹּאמְרוּsaid שָׂרֵיprinces בְנֵיsons עַמּוֹןAmmonites לְחָנוּןto Hanun הַמְכַבֵּדjust because דָּוִידDavid אֶת[obj] אָבִיךָyour father בְּעֵינֶיךָin your sight כִּיfor שָׁלַחsent לְךָto you מְנַחֲמִיםcomforters הֲלֹאnot בַּעֲבוּרreally לַחְקֹרto explore וְלַהֲפֹךְoverthrow וּלְרַגֵּלspy it out and הָאָרֶץthe land בָּאוּcome עֲבָדָיוhis servants אֵלֶיךָto you

the princes of the Ammonites said to Hanun, “Just because David has sent you comforters, do you really believe he is showing respect for your father? Have not his servants come to you to explore the land, spy it out, and overthrow it?”

19:4

וַיִּקַּחso took חָנוּןHanun אֶת[obj] עַבְדֵיservants דָוִידDavid’s וַיְגַלְּחֵםshaved their beards וַיִּכְרֹתcut off אֶת[obj] מַדְוֵיהֶםtheir garments בַּחֵצִיat the hips עַדuntil הַמִּפְשָׂעָהhips וַיְשַׁלְּחֵםand sent them

So Hanun took David’s servants, shaved their beards, cut off their garments at the hips, and sent them away.

19:5

וַיֵּלְכוּwhen someone came וַיַּגִּידוּand told לְדָוִידDavid עַלon הָאֲנָשִׁיםhis men וַיִּשְׁלַחhe sent לִקְרָאתָםto meet them כִּיfor הָיוּwere הָאֲנָשִׁיםthe men had been thoroughly נִכְלָמִיםhumiliated מְאֹדvery וַיֹּאמֶרtold הַמֶּלֶךְthe king them שְׁבוּstay בִירֵחוֹin Jericho עַדuntil אֲשֶׁרhave יְצַמַּחgrown זְקַנְכֶםyour beards וְשַׁבְתֶּםthen return

When someone came and told David about his men, he sent messengers to meet them, since the men had been thoroughly humiliated. The king told them, “Stay in Jericho until your beards have grown back, and then return.”

19:6

וַיִּרְאוּrealized בְּנֵיsons עַמּוֹןAmmonites כִּיthat הִתְבָּאֲשׁוּstench עִםto דָּוִידDavid וַיִּשְׁלַחsent חָנוּןHanun וּבְנֵיand sons עַמּוֹןAmmonites אֶלֶףthousand כִּכַּרtalents כֶּסֶףsilver לִשְׂכֹּרto hire לָהֶםfor themselves מִןfrom אֲרַםAram נַהֲרַיִםnaharaim וּמִןand from אֲרַםAram מַעֲכָהmaacah וּמִצּוֹבָהand Zobah רֶכֶבchariots וּפָרָשִׁיםand horsemen

When the Ammonites realized that they had become a stench to David, Hanun and the Ammonites sent a thousand talents of silver to hire for themselves chariots and horsemen from Aram-naharaim, Aram-maacah, and Zobah.

19:7

וַיִּשְׂכְּרוּso they hired לָהֶםfor themselves שְׁנַיִםtwo וּשְׁלֹשִׁיםthirty אֶלֶףthousand רֶכֶבchariots וְאֶתand [obj] מֶלֶךְking מַעֲכָהMaacah וְאֶתand [obj] עַמּוֹhis troops וַיָּבֹאוּwho came וַיַּחֲנוּand camped לִפְנֵיnear מֵידְבָאMedeba וּבְנֵיand sons עַמּוֹןAmmonites נֶאֶסְפוּcame מֵעָרֵיהֶםfrom their cities וַיָּבֹאוּand marched לַמִּלְחָמָהfor battle

So they hired for themselves thirty-two thousand chariots, as well as the king of Maacah with his troops, who came and camped near Medeba while the Ammonites came from their cities and marched out for battle.

19:8

וַיִּשְׁמַעhearing דָּוִידDavid וַיִּשְׁלַחsent אֶת[obj] יוֹאָבJoab וְאֵתand [obj] כָּלentire צָבָאarmy הַגִּבּוֹרִיםof mighty men

On hearing this, David sent Joab and the entire army of mighty men.

19:9

וַיֵּצְאוּmarched בְּנֵיsons עַמּוֹןAmmonites וַיַּעַרְכוּand arrayed מִלְחָמָהbattle פֶּתַחat the entrance הָעִירthe city וְהַמְּלָכִיםwhile the kings אֲשֶׁרhad בָּאוּcome לְבַדָּםstayed by themselves בַּשָּׂדֶהin the open country

The Ammonites marched out and arrayed themselves for battle at the entrance to the city, while the kings who had come stayed by themselves in the open country.

19:10

וַיַּרְאsaw יוֹאָבJoab כִּיwhen הָיְתָהwas פְנֵיface הַמִּלְחָמָהthe battle אֵלָיוto him פָּנִיםbefore וְאָחוֹרand behind וַיִּבְחַרhe selected מִכָּלfrom all בָּחוּרbest בְּיִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וַיַּעֲרֹךְand arrayed לִקְרַאתthem against אֲרָםArameans

When Joab saw the battle lines before him and behind him, he selected some of the best men of Israel and arrayed them against the Arameans.

19:11

וְאֵתand [obj] יֶתֶרrest הָעָםthe forces נָתַןplaced בְּיַדunder אַבְשַׁיAbishai אָחִיוhis brother וַיַּעַרְכוּwho arrayed לִקְרַאתthem against בְּנֵיsons עַמּוֹןAmmonites

And he placed the rest of the forces under the command of his brother Abishai, who arrayed them against the Ammonites.

19:12

וַיֹּאמֶרsaid אִםif תֶּחֱזַקstrong מִמֶּנִּיfrom me אֲרָםArameans וְהָיִיתָcome לִּיto my לִתְשׁוּעָהrescue וְאִםand if בְּנֵיsons עַמּוֹןAmmonites יֶחֶזְקוּstrong מִמְּךָfrom you וְהוֹשַׁעְתִּיךָyour rescue

“If the Arameans are too strong for me,” said Joab, “then you will come to my rescue. And if the Ammonites are too strong for you, then I will come to your rescue.

19:13

חֲזַקstrong וְנִתְחַזְּקָהfight bravely בְּעַדfor עַמֵּנוּour people וּבְעַדand for עָרֵיcities אֱלֹהֵינוּour God וַיהוָהmay the LORD הַטּוֹבwhat is good בְּעֵינָיוin his sight יַעֲשֶׂהdo

Be strong and let us fight bravely for our people and for the cities of our God. May the LORD do what is good in His sight.”

19:14

וַיִּגַּשׁand approached יוֹאָבJoab וְהָעָםand his troops אֲשֶׁרthat עִמּוֹwith him לִפְנֵיto before אֲרָםArameans לַמִּלְחָמָהto the battle וַיָּנוּסוּfled מִפָּנָיוbefore him

So Joab and his troops advanced to fight the Arameans, who fled before him.

19:15

וּבְנֵיand sons עַמּוֹןAmmonites רָאוּsaw כִּיthat נָסfled אֲרָםArameans וַיָּנוּסוּhad fled גַםtoo הֵםthey מִפְּנֵיtoo before אַבְשַׁיAbishai אָחִיוbrother וַיָּבֹאוּand they entered הָעִירָהthe city וַיָּבֹאso went יוֹאָבJoab יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem

When the Ammonites saw that the Arameans had fled, they too fled before Joab’s brother Abishai, and they entered the city. So Joab went back to Jerusalem.

19:16

וַיַּרְאsaw אֲרָםArameans כִּיthat נִגְּפוּdefeated לִפְנֵיto before יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וַיִּשְׁלְחוּthey sent מַלְאָכִיםmessengers וַיּוֹצִיאוּto bring אֶת[obj] אֲרָםArameans אֲשֶׁרthat מֵעֵבֶרfrom beyond הַנָּהָרthe Euphrates וְשׁוֹפַךְwith Shophach שַׂרcommander צְבָאarmy הֲדַדְעֶזֶרHadadezer’s לִפְנֵיהֶםleading them

When the Arameans saw that they had been defeated by Israel, they sent messengers to bring more Arameans from beyond the Euphrates, with Shophach the commander of Hadadezer’s army leading them.

19:17

וַיֻּגַּדwas reported לְדָוִידto David וַיֶּאֱסֹףhe gathered אֶת[obj] כָּלall יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וַיַּעֲבֹרcrossed הַיַּרְדֵּןthe Jordan וַיָּבֹאadvanced אֲלֵהֶםto them וַיַּעֲרֹךְand arrayed אֲלֵהֶםagainst them וַיַּעֲרֹךְwhen lined דָּוִידDavid לִקְרַאתto engage אֲרָםArameans מִלְחָמָהbattle וַיִּלָּחֲמוּthey fought עִמּוֹagainst him

When this was reported to David, he gathered all Israel, crossed the Jordan, advanced toward the Arameans, and arrayed for battle against them. When David lined up to engage them in battle, they fought against him.

19:18

וַיָּנָסfled אֲרָםArameans מִלִּפְנֵיbefore יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וַיַּהֲרֹגand killed דָּוִידDavid מֵאֲרָםfrom Aram שִׁבְעַתseven אֲלָפִיםthousand רֶכֶבcharioteers וְאַרְבָּעִיםand forty אֶלֶףthousand אִישׁtheir רַגְלִיfoot soldiers וְאֵתand [obj] שׁוֹפַךְShophach שַׂרcommander הַצָּבָאtheir army הֵמִיתkilled

But the Arameans fled before Israel, and David killed seven thousand of their charioteers and forty thousand foot soldiers. He also killed Shophach the commander of their army.

19:19

וַיִּרְאוּsaw עַבְדֵיsubjects הֲדַדְעֶזֶרHadadezer’s כִּיthat נִגְּפוּdefeated לִפְנֵיto before יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וַיַּשְׁלִימוּthey made peace עִםwith דָּוִידDavid וַיַּעַבְדֻהוּand became subject to him וְלֹאand not אָבָהunwilling אֲרָםArameans לְהוֹשִׁיעַto help אֶת[obj] בְּנֵיsons עַמּוֹןAmmonites עוֹדlonger

When Hadadezer’s subjects saw that they had been defeated by Israel, they made peace with David and became subject to him. So the Arameans were unwilling to help the Ammonites anymore.
