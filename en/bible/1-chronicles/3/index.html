1 Chronicles 3

The family of David and the royal line

3:1

וְאֵלֶּהthese הָיוּwere בְּנֵיsons דָויִדDavid אֲשֶׁרwho נוֹלַדborn לוֹto him בְּחֶבְרוֹןin Hebron הַבְּכוֹרthe firstborn אַמְנֹןAmnon לַאֲחִינֹעַםby Ahinoam הַיִּזְרְעֵאלִיתof Jezreel שֵׁנִיsecond דָּנִיֵּאלDaniel לַאֲבִיגַיִלby Abigail הַכַּרְמְלִיתof Carmel

These were the sons of David who were born to him in Hebron: The firstborn was Amnon by Ahinoam of Jezreel; the second was Daniel by Abigail of Carmel;

3:2

הַשְּׁלִשִׁיthe third לְאַבְשָׁלוֹםAbsalom בֶּןson מַעֲכָהMaacah בַּתdaughter תַּלְמַיTalmai מֶלֶךְking גְּשׁוּרGeshur הָרְבִיעִיthe fourth אֲדֹנִיָּהAdonijah בֶןson חַגִּיתHaggith

the third was Absalom the son of Maacah daughter of King Talmai of Geshur; the fourth was Adonijah the son of Haggith;

3:3

הַחֲמִישִׁיthe fifth שְׁפַטְיָהShephatiah לַאֲבִיטָלby Abital הַשִּׁשִּׁיthe sixth יִתְרְעָםIthream לְעֶגְלָהIthream by אִשְׁתּוֹhis wife

the fifth was Shephatiah by Abital; and the sixth was Ithream by his wife Eglah.

3:4

שִׁשָּׁהsix נוֹלַדsons were born לוֹto David בְחֶבְרוֹןin Hebron וַיִּמְלָךְwhere he reigned שָׁםthere שֶׁבַעseven שָׁנִיםyears וְשִׁשָּׁהand six חֳדָשִׁיםmonths וּשְׁלֹשִׁיםthirty וְשָׁלוֹשׁthree שָׁנָהyears מָלַךְreigned בִּירוּשָׁלִָםin Jerusalem

These six sons were born to David in Hebron, where he reigned seven years and six months. And David reigned in Jerusalem thirty-three years,

3:5

וְאֵלֶּהand these נוּלְּדוּsons born לוֹto him בִּירוּשָׁלָיִםin Jerusalem שִׁמְעָאShimea וְשׁוֹבָבShobab וְנָתָןNathan וּשְׁלֹמֹהand Solomon אַרְבָּעָהfour לְבַתBathsheba שׁוּעַBathsheba בַּתdaughter עַמִּיאֵלAmmiel

and these sons were born to him in Jerusalem: Shimea, Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon. These four were born to him by Bathsheba daughter of Ammiel.

3:6

וְיִבְחָרIbhar וֶאֱלִישָׁמָעand Elishama וֶאֱלִיפָלֶטEliphelet

David’s other sons were Ibhar, Elishua, Eliphelet,

3:7

וְנֹגַהּNogah וְנֶפֶגNepheg וְיָפִיעַJaphia

Nogah, Nepheg, Japhia,

3:8

וֶאֱלִישָׁמָעElishama וְאֶלְיָדָעEliada וֶאֱלִיפֶלֶטand Eliphelet תִּשְׁעָהnine

Elishama, Eliada, and Eliphelet—nine in all.

3:9

כֹּלall בְּנֵיsons דָוִידDavid מִלְּבַדbesides בְּנֵיsons פִילַגְשִׁיםconcubines וְתָמָרAnd Tamar אֲחוֹתָםtheir sister

These were all the sons of David, besides the sons by his concubines. And Tamar was their sister.

3:10

וּבֶןson שְׁלֹמֹהSolomon’s רְחַבְעָםRehoboam אֲבִיָּהAbijah בְנוֹhis son אָסָאAsa בְנוֹhis son יְהוֹשָׁפָטJehoshaphat בְּנוֹhis son

Solomon’s son was Rehoboam:

3:11

יוֹרָםJoram בְּנוֹson אֲחַזְיָהוּAhaziah בְנוֹson his יוֹאָשׁJoash בְּנוֹson his son

Abijah was his son, Asa his son, Jehoshaphat his son, Joram his son, Ahaziah his son, Joash his son,

3:12

אֲמַצְיָהוּAmaziah בְנוֹhis son עֲזַרְיָהAzariah בְנוֹhis son יוֹתָםJotham בְּנוֹhis son

Amaziah his son, Azariah his son, Jotham his son,

3:13

אָחָזAhaz בְּנוֹhis son חִזְקִיָּהוּHezekiah בְנוֹhis son מְנַשֶּׁהManasseh בְנוֹhis son

Ahaz his son, Hezekiah his son, Manasseh his son,

3:14

אָמוֹןAmon בְּנוֹhis son יֹאשִׁיָּהוּJosiah בְנוֹhis son

Amon his son, and Josiah his son.

3:15

וּבְנֵיsons יֹאשִׁיָּהוּJosiah הַבְּכוֹרthe firstborn יוֹחָנָןJohanan הַשֵּׁנִיthe second יְהוֹיָקִיםJehoiakim הַשְּׁלִשִׁיthe third צִדְקִיָּהוּZedekiah הָרְבִיעִיthe fourth שַׁלּוּםShallum

The sons of Josiah: Johanan was the firstborn, Jehoiakim the second, Zedekiah the third, and Shallum the fourth.

3:16

וּבְנֵיand sons יְהוֹיָקִיםJehoiakim יְכָנְיָהJeconiah בְנוֹhis son צִדְקִיָּהZedekiah בְנוֹhis son

The successors of Jehoiakim: Jeconiah his son, and Zedekiah.

3:17

וּבְנֵיdescendants יְכָנְיָהJeconiah אַסִּרAssir שְׁאַלְתִּיאֵלcaptive בְּנוֹhis son

The descendants of Jeconiah the captive:

3:18

וּמַלְכִּירָםMalchiram וּפְדָיָהPedaiah וְשֶׁנְאַצַּרShenazzar יְקַמְיָהJekamiah הוֹשָׁמָעHoshama וּנְדַבְיָהand Nedabiah

Shealtiel his son, Malchiram, Pedaiah, Shenazzar, Jekamiah, Hoshama, and Nedabiah.

3:19

וּבְנֵיsons פְדָיָהPedaiah זְרֻבָּבֶלZerubbabel וְשִׁמְעִיand Shimei וּבֶןchildren זְרֻבָּבֶלZerubbabel מְשֻׁלָּםMeshullam וַחֲנַנְיָהand Hananiah וּשְׁלֹמִיתand Shelomith אֲחוֹתָםtheir sister

The sons of Pedaiah: Zerubbabel and Shimei. The children of Zerubbabel:

3:20

וַחֲשֻׁבָהand Hananiah sister וָאֹהֶלtheir Ohel וּבֶרֶכְיָהBerechiah וַחֲסַדְיָהHasadiah and יוּשַׁבhesed חֶסֶדhesed חָמֵשׁfive

Meshullam and Hananiah, their sister Shelomith, and five others: Hashubah, Ohel, Berechiah, Hasadiah, and Jushab-hesed.

3:21

וּבֶןdescendants חֲנַנְיָהHananiah פְּלַטְיָהPelatiah וִישַׁעְיָהJeshaiah and בְּנֵיsons רְפָיָהRephaiah בְּנֵיsons אַרְנָןArnan בְּנֵיsons עֹבַדְיָהObadiah בְּנֵיsons שְׁכַנְיָהShecaniah

The descendants of Hananiah: Pelatiah, Jeshaiah, and the sons of Rephaiah, of Arnan, of Obadiah, and of Shecaniah.

3:22

וּבְנֵיsix descendants שְׁכַנְיָהShecaniah שְׁמַעְיָהShemaiah וּבְנֵיand his sons שְׁמַעְיָהShemaiah חַטּוּשׁHattush וְיִגְאָלIgal וּבָרִיחַBariah וּנְעַרְיָהNeariah וְשָׁפָטand Shaphat שִׁשָּׁהsix

The six descendants of Shecaniah were Shemaiah and his sons: Hattush, Igal, Bariah, Neariah, and Shaphat.

3:23

וּבֶןsons נְעַרְיָהNeariah אֶלְיוֹעֵינַיElioenai וְחִזְקִיָּהHizkiah וְעַזְרִיקָםand Azrikam שְׁלֹשָׁהthree

The sons of Neariah: Elioenai, Hizkiah, and Azrikam—three in all.

3:24

וּבְנֵיsons אֶלְיוֹעֵינַיElioenai הוֹדַוְיָהוּHodaviah וְאֶלְיָשִׁיבEliashib וּפְלָיָהPelaiah וְעַקּוּבAkkub וְיוֹחָנָןJohanan וּדְלָיָהDelaiah וַעֲנָנִיand Anani שִׁבְעָהseven

The sons of Elioenai: Hodaviah, Eliashib, Pelaiah, Akkub, Johanan, Delaiah, and Anani—seven in all.
