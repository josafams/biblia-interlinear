1 Chronicles 23

The Levites and their duties

When David was old and full of years, he installed his son Solomon as king over Israel.

Then he gathered all the leaders of Israel, as well as the priests and Levites.

The Levites thirty years of age or older were counted, and the total number of men was 38,000.

23:4

“Of these,” said David, “24,000 are to oversee the work of the house of the LORD, 6,000 are to be officers and judges,

23:5

4,000 are to be gatekeepers, and 4,000 are to praise the LORD with the instruments I have made for giving praise.”

23:6

וַיֶּחָלְקֵםthen divided the Levites דָּוִידDavid מַחְלְקוֹתdivisions לִבְנֵיaccording to the sons לֵוִיLevi לְגֵרְשׁוֹןGershom קְהָתKohath וּמְרָרִיand Merari

Then David divided the Levites into divisions according to the sons of Levi: Gershom, Kohath, and Merari.

23:7

לַגֵּרְשֻׁנִּיThe Gershonites לַעְדָּןLadan וְשִׁמְעִיand Shimei

The Gershonites: Ladan and Shimei.

23:8

בְּנֵיsons לַעְדָּןLadan הָרֹאשׁthe first יְחִיאֵלJehiel וְזֵתָםZetham וְיוֹאֵלand Joel שְׁלֹשָׁהthree

The sons of Ladan: Jehiel the first, Zetham, and Joel—three in all.

23:9

בְּנֵיsons שִׁמְעִיShimei שְׁלוֹמִיתShelomoth וַחֲזִיאֵלHaziel וְהָרָןand Haran שְׁלֹשָׁהthree אֵלֶּהthese רָאשֵׁיheads הָאָבוֹתthe families לְלַעְדָּןof Ladan

The sons of Shimei: Shelomoth, Haziel, and Haran—three in all. These were the heads of the families of Ladan.

23:10

וּבְנֵיand the sons שִׁמְעִיShimei יַחַתJahath זִינָאZina וִיעוּשׁJeush וּבְרִיעָהBeriah אֵלֶּהthese בְנֵיsons שִׁמְעִיShimei אַרְבָּעָהfour

And the sons of Shimei: Jahath, Zina, Jeush, and Beriah. These were the sons of Shimei—four in all.

23:11

וַיְהִיwas יַחַתJahath הָרֹאשׁthe first וְזִיזָהand Zizah הַשֵּׁנִיthe second וִיעוּשׁbut Jeush וּבְרִיעָהand Beriah לֹאnot הִרְבּוּmany בָנִיםsons וַיִּהְיוּso they were לְבֵיתcounted as אָבfamily לִפְקֻדָּהassignment אֶחָתsingle

Jahath was the first and Zizah was the second; but Jeush and Beriah did not have many sons, so they were counted as one family and received a single assignment.

23:12

בְּנֵיsons קְהָתKohath עַמְרָםAmram יִצְהָרIzhar חֶבְרוֹןHebron וְעֻזִּיאֵלand Uzziel אַרְבָּעָהfour

The sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel—four in all.

23:13

בְּנֵיsons עַמְרָםAmram אַהֲרֹןAaron וּמֹשֶׁהand Moses וַיִּבָּדֵלset אַהֲרֹןAaron לְהַקְדִּישׁוֹto consecrate קֹדֶשׁmost קָדָשִׁיםholy things הוּאhe וּבָנָיוhis descendants עַדuntil עוֹלָםforever לְהַקְטִירto burn incense לִפְנֵיbefore יְהוָהLORD לְשָׁרְתוֹto minister before him וּלְבָרֵךְand to pronounce blessings בִּשְׁמוֹin his name עַדuntil עוֹלָםforever

The sons of Amram: Aaron and Moses. Aaron and his descendants were set apart forever to consecrate the most holy things, to burn incense before the LORD, to minister before Him, and to pronounce blessings in His name forever.

23:14

וּמֹשֶׁהas for Moses אִישׁman הָאֱלֹהִיםof God בָּנָיוhis sons יִקָּרְאוּnamed עַלon שֵׁבֶטtribe הַלֵּוִיof Levi

As for Moses the man of God, his sons were named among the tribe of Levi.

23:15

בְּנֵיsons מֹשֶׁהMoses גֵּרְשֹׁםGershom וֶאֱלִיעֶזֶרand Eliezer

The sons of Moses: Gershom and Eliezer.

23:16

בְּנֵיdescendants גֵרְשׁוֹםGershom שְׁבוּאֵלShebuel הָרֹאשׁthe first

The descendants of Gershom: Shebuel was the first.

23:17

וַיִּהְיוּand were בְנֵיdescendants אֱלִיעֶזֶרEliezer רְחַבְיָהRehabiah הָרֹאשׁwas the first וְלֹאdid not הָיָהwas לֶאֱלִיעֶזֶרEliezer בָּנִיםsons אֲחֵרִיםother וּבְנֵיbut the sons רְחַבְיָהRehabiah רָבוּnumerous לְמָעְלָהto upward toward

The descendants of Eliezer: Rehabiah was the first. Eliezer did not have any other sons, but the sons of Rehabiah were very numerous.

23:18

בְּנֵיsons יִצְהָרIzhar שְׁלֹמִיתShelomith הָרֹאשׁthe first

The sons of Izhar: Shelomith was the first.

23:19

בְּנֵיsons חֶבְרוֹןHebron יְרִיָּהוּJeriah הָרֹאשׁthe first אֲמַרְיָהAmariah הַשֵּׁנִיthe second יַחֲזִיאֵלJahaziel הַשְּׁלִישִׁיthe third וִיקַמְעָםand Jekameam הָרְבִיעִיthe fourth

The sons of Hebron: Jeriah was the first, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, and Jekameam the fourth.

23:20

בְּנֵיsons עֻזִּיאֵלUzziel מִיכָהMicah הָרֹאשׁthe first וְיִשִּׁיָּהand Isshiah הַשֵּׁנִיthe second

The sons of Uzziel: Micah was the first and Isshiah the second.

23:21

בְּנֵיsons מְרָרִיMerari מַחְלִיMahli וּמוּשִׁיand Mushi בְּנֵיsons מַחְלִיMahli אֶלְעָזָרEleazar וְקִישׁand Kish

The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi.

23:22

וַיָּמָתsons of Mahli אֶלְעָזָרEleazar וְלֹאand not הָיוּhaving לוֹto him בָּנִיםsons כִּיfor אִםif בָּנוֹתdaughters וַיִּשָּׂאוּםtheir cousins בְּנֵיsons קִישׁKish אֲחֵיהֶםmarried them

The sons of Mahli: Eleazar and Kish. Eleazar died without having any sons; he had only daughters. Their cousins, the sons of Kish, married them.

23:23

בְּנֵיsons מוּשִׁיMushi מַחְלִיMahli וְעֵדֶרEder וִירֵמוֹתand Jeremoth שְׁלֹשָׁהthree

The sons of Mushi: Mahli, Eder, and Jeremoth—three in all.

23:24

אֵלֶּהthese בְנֵיdescendants לֵוִיLevi לְבֵיתby their אֲבֹתֵיהֶםtheir families רָאשֵׁיheads הָאָבוֹתof families לִפְקוּדֵיהֶםregistered בְּמִסְפַּרindividually by שֵׁמוֹתname לְגֻלְגְּלֹתָםto their one עֹשֵׂהworked הַמְּלָאכָהthe work לַעֲבֹדַתin the service בֵּיתhouse יְהוָהLORD מִבֶּןfrom a old עֶשְׂרִיםtwenty שָׁנָהyears וָמָעְלָהage or older

These were the descendants of Levi by their families—the heads of families, registered individually by name—those twenty years of age or older who worked in the service of the house of the LORD.

23:25

כִּיfor אָמַרsaid דָּוִידDavid הֵנִיחַrest יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael לְעַמּוֹhis people וַיִּשְׁכֹּןdwell בִּירוּשָׁלִַםin Jerusalem עַדuntil לְעוֹלָםforever

For David had said, “The LORD, the God of Israel, has given rest to His people and has come to dwell in Jerusalem forever.

23:26

וְגַםand also לַלְוִיִּםthe Levites אֵיןno לָשֵׂאתto carry אֶת[obj] הַמִּשְׁכָּןthe tabernacle וְאֶתand [obj] כָּלany כֵּלָיוarticles its לַעֲבֹדָתוֹfor service

So now the Levites no longer need to carry the tabernacle or any of the articles for its service.”

23:27

כִּיfor בְדִבְרֵיinstructions דָוִידDavid הָאַחֲרֹנִיםthe final הֵמָּהwere מִסְפַּרcounted בְּנֵיsons לֵוִיLevites מִבֶּןfrom a old עֶשְׂרִיםtwenty שָׁנָהyears וּלְמָעְלָהand to older

For according to the final instructions of David, the Levites twenty years of age or older were counted,

23:28

כִּיbut מַעֲמָדָםtheir duty לְיַדto assist בְּנֵיdescendants אַהֲרֹןAaron לַעֲבֹדַתwith the service בֵּיתhouse יְהוָהLORD עַלfor הַחֲצֵרוֹתthe courts וְעַלand on הַלְּשָׁכוֹתchambers וְעַלand on טָהֳרַתpurification לְכָלof all קֹדֶשׁholy וּמַעֲשֵׂהand the work עֲבֹדַתservice בֵּיתhouse הָאֱלֹהִיםof God

but their duty was to assist the descendants of Aaron with the service of the house of the LORD, being responsible for the courts and chambers, the purification of all the holy things, and the work of the service of the house of God,

23:29

וּלְלֶחֶםwell as for the rows הַמַּעֲרֶכֶתthe showbread וּלְסֹלֶתthe fine flour לְמִנְחָהfor the grain offering וְלִרְקִיקֵיthe wafers הַמַּצּוֹתof unleavened bread וְלַמַּחֲבַתthe baking וְלַמֻּרְבָּכֶתthe mixing וּלְכָלand all מְשׂוּרָהquantity וּמִדָּהand size

as well as for the rows of the showbread, the fine flour for the grain offering, the wafers of unleavened bread, the baking, the mixing, and all measurements of quantity and size.

23:30

וְלַעֲמֹדalso to stand בַּבֹּקֶרevery בַּבֹּקֶרmorning לְהֹדוֹתto give thanks וּלְהַלֵּלand praise לַיהוָהto the LORD וְכֵןand same לָעָרֶבin the evening

They were also to stand every morning to give thanks and praise to the LORD, and likewise in the evening.

23:31

Whenever burnt offerings were presented to the LORD on the Sabbaths, New Moons, and appointed feasts, they were to serve regularly before the LORD in the numbers prescribed for them.

23:32

וְשָׁמְרוּand keep אֶת[obj] מִשְׁמֶרֶתcharge אֹהֶלTent מוֹעֵדMeeting וְאֵתand [obj] מִשְׁמֶרֶתresponsibilities הַקֹּדֶשׁthe holy וּמִשְׁמֶרֶתand under בְּנֵיdescendants אַהֲרֹןAaron אֲחֵיהֶםtheir brothers לַעֲבֹדַתservice בֵּיתhouse יְהוָהLORD

So the Levites were to carry out the responsibilities for the Tent of Meeting and the Holy Place, and, under their brothers the descendants of Aaron, the service of the house of the LORD.
