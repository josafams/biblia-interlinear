Judges 13

The birth of Samson announced

Again the Israelites did evil in the sight of the LORD, so He delivered them into the hand of the Philistines for forty years.

וַיְהִיnow there was אִישׁman אֶחָדone מִצָּרְעָהfrom Zorah מִמִּשְׁפַּחַתfrom the clan הַדָּנִיthe Danites וּשְׁמוֹnamed מָנוֹחַManoah וְאִשְׁתּוֹwife was עֲקָרָהbarren וְלֹאand no יָלָדָהhad children

Now there was a man from Zorah named Manoah, from the clan of the Danites, whose wife was barren and had no children.

13:3

The angel of the LORD appeared to the woman and said to her, “It is true that you are barren and have no children; but you will conceive and give birth to a son.

13:4

וְעַתָּהnow הִשָּׁמְרִיcareful נָאplease וְאַלnot תִּשְׁתִּיdrink יַיִןwine וְשֵׁכָרor strong drink וְאַלand not תֹּאכְלִיeat כָּלanything טָמֵאunclean

Now please be careful not to drink wine or strong drink, and not to eat anything unclean.

13:5

For behold, you will conceive and give birth to a son. And no razor shall come over his head, because the boy will be a Nazirite to God from the womb, and he will begin the deliverance of Israel from the hand of the Philistines.”

13:6

וַתָּבֹאwent הָאִשָּׁהthe woman וַתֹּאמֶרand told לְאִישָׁהּher husband לֵאמֹרto saying אִישׁman הָאֱלֹהִיםof God בָּאcame אֵלַיto me וּמַרְאֵהוּhis appearance was כְּמַרְאֵהlike appearance מַלְאַךְangel הָאֱלֹהִיםof God נוֹרָאawesome מְאֹדvery וְלֹאI did not שְׁאִלְתִּיהוּask him אֵיwhere מִזֶּהfrom this הוּאhe וְאֶתand [obj] שְׁמוֹhis name לֹאnot הִגִּידtell לִיto me

So the woman went and told her husband, “A man of God came to me. His appearance was like the angel of God, exceedingly awesome. I did not ask him where he came from, and he did not tell me his name.

13:7

וַיֹּאמֶרbut he said לִיto me הִנָּךְbehold you הָרָהconceive וְיֹלַדְתְּand give birth בֵּןson וְעַתָּהnow therefore אַלdo not תִּשְׁתִּיdrink יַיִןwine וְשֵׁכָרor strong drink וְאַלand do not תֹּאכְלִיeat כָּלanything טֻמְאָהunclean כִּיbecause נְזִירNazirite אֱלֹהִיםGod יִהְיֶהbe הַנַּעַרthe boy מִןfrom הַבֶּטֶןthe womb עַדuntil יוֹםday מוֹתוֹhis death

But he said to me, ‘Behold, you will conceive and give birth to a son. Now, therefore, do not drink wine or strong drink, and do not eat anything unclean, because the boy will be a Nazirite to God from the womb until the day of his death.’”

13:8

וַיֶּעְתַּרthen prayed מָנוֹחַManoah אֶלto יְהוָהLORD וַיֹּאמַרand said בִּיplease אֲדוֹנָיLord let אִישׁman הָאֱלֹהִיםof God אֲשֶׁרyou שָׁלַחְתָּsent יָבוֹאcome נָאplease עוֹדagain אֵלֵינוּto וְיוֹרֵנוּteach us מַהhow נַּעֲשֶׂהdo לַנַּעַרto raise the boy הַיּוּלָּדborn

Then Manoah prayed to the LORD, “Please, O Lord, let the man of God You sent us come to us again to teach us how to raise the boy who is to be born.”

13:9

וַיִּשְׁמַעand listened הָאֱלֹהִיםGod בְּקוֹלto voice מָנוֹחַManoah וַיָּבֹאreturned מַלְאַךְangel הָאֱלֹהִיםof God עוֹדlonger אֶלto הָאִשָּׁהthe woman וְהִיאand was יוֹשֶׁבֶתsitting בַּשָּׂדֶהin the field וּמָנוֹחַManoah אִישָׁהּher husband אֵיןnot עִמָּהּwith her

And God listened to the voice of Manoah, and the angel of God returned to the woman as she was sitting in the field; but her husband Manoah was not with her.

13:10

וַתְּמַהֵרquickly הָאִשָּׁהthe woman וַתָּרָץran וַתַּגֵּדto tell לְאִישָׁהּher husband וַתֹּאמֶרand said אֵלָיוto him הִנֵּהbehold נִרְאָהappeared אֵלַיto me הָאִישׁthe man אֲשֶׁרwho בָּאcame בַיּוֹםthe other day אֵלָיto me

The woman ran quickly to tell her husband, “Behold, the man who came to me the other day has reappeared!”

13:11

וַיָּקָםso got וַיֵּלֶךְand followed מָנוֹחַManoah אַחֲרֵיfollowed אִשְׁתּוֹhis wife וַיָּבֹאwhen he came אֶלto הָאִישׁthe man וַיֹּאמֶרhe asked לוֹto him הַאַתָּהare you הָאִישׁthe man אֲשֶׁרwho דִּבַּרְתָּspoke אֶלto הָאִשָּׁהmy wife וַיֹּאמֶרhe said אָנִיam

So Manoah got up and followed his wife. When he came to the man, he asked, “Are you the man who spoke to my wife?” “I am,” he said.

13:12

וַיֹּאמֶרthen asked מָנוֹחַManoah עַתָּהnow יָבֹאcome דְבָרֶיךָyour words מַהwhat יִּהְיֶהbe מִשְׁפַּטjustice הַנַּעַרthe boy’s וּמַעֲשֵׂהוּand mission

Then Manoah asked, “When your words come to pass, what will be the boy’s rule of life and mission?”

13:13

וַיֹּאמֶרand said מַלְאַךְangel יְהוָהLORD אֶלto מָנוֹחַManoah מִכֹּלto everything אֲשֶׁרthat אָמַרְתִּיtold אֶלto הָאִשָּׁהwife תִּשָּׁמֵרdo

So the angel of the LORD answered Manoah, “Your wife is to do everything I told her.

13:14

מִכֹּלanything אֲשֶׁרthat יֵצֵאcomes מִגֶּפֶןfrom vine הַיַּיִןdrink לֹאnot תֹאכַלany וְיַיִןwine וְשֵׁכָרor strong אַלdo not תֵּשְׁתְּdrink וְכָלanything טֻמְאָהunclean אַלdo תֹּאכַלeat כֹּלeverything אֲשֶׁרhave צִוִּיתִיהָcommanded תִּשְׁמֹרher

She must not eat anything that comes from the vine, nor drink any wine or strong drink, nor eat anything unclean. She must do everything I have commanded her.”

13:15

וַיֹּאמֶרsaid מָנוֹחַManoah אֶלto מַלְאַךְangel יְהוָהLORD נַעְצְרָהstay נָּאplease אוֹתָךְyou וְנַעֲשֶׂהprepare לְפָנֶיךָto your before גְּדִיyoung עִזִּיםgoat

“Please stay here,” Manoah said to the angel of the LORD, “and we will prepare a young goat for you.”

13:16

וַיֹּאמֶרand said מַלְאַךְangel יְהוָהLORD אֶלto מָנוֹחַManoah אִםif תַּעְצְרֵנִיstay I לֹאnot אֹכַלeat בְּלַחְמֶךָyour food וְאִםbut if תַּעֲשֶׂהprepare עֹלָהburnt offering לַיהוָהto the LORD תַּעֲלֶנָּהoffer it כִּיfor לֹאnot יָדַעknow מָנוֹחַManoah כִּיthat מַלְאַךְangel יְהוָהLORD הוּאthe

And the angel of the LORD replied, “Even if I stay, I will not eat your food. But if you prepare a burnt offering, offer it to the LORD.” For Manoah did not know that it was the angel of the LORD.

13:17

וַיֹּאמֶרthen said מָנוֹחַManoah אֶלto מַלְאַךְangel יְהוָהLORD מִיwhat שְׁמֶךָyour name כִּיthat יָבֹאcomes דְבָרְךָyour word וְכִבַּדְנוּךָhonor you

Then Manoah said to the angel of the LORD, “What is your name, so that we may honor you when your word comes to pass?”

13:18

וַיֹּאמֶרsaid לּוֹto him מַלְאַךְangel יְהוָהLORD לָמָּהwhy זֶּהyou תִּשְׁאַלask לִשְׁמִיmy name וְהוּאis פֶלִאיbeyond comprehension

“Why do you ask my name,” said the angel of the LORD, “since it is beyond comprehension?”

13:19

וַיִּקַּחthen took מָנוֹחַManoah אֶת[obj] גְּדִיyoung הָעִזִּיםgoat וְאֶתand [obj] הַמִּנְחָהa grain offering וַיַּעַלand offered עַלon הַצּוּרa rock לַיהוָהto the LORD וּמַפְלִאmarvelous לַעֲשׂוֹתdid וּמָנוֹחַManoah וְאִשְׁתּוֹand his wife רֹאִיםlooked

Then Manoah took a young goat and a grain offering and offered them on a rock to the LORD. And as Manoah and his wife looked on, the LORD did a marvelous thing.

13:20

וַיְהִיand was בַעֲלוֹתwent הַלַּהַבthe flame מֵעַלfrom the הַמִּזְבֵּחַthe altar הַשָּׁמַיְמָהthe sky וַיַּעַלascended מַלְאַךְangel יְהוָהLORD בְּלַהַבin flame הַמִּזְבֵּחַthe altar וּמָנוֹחַWhen Manoah וְאִשְׁתּוֹand his wife רֹאִיםsaw וַיִּפְּלוּthis they fell עַלon פְּנֵיהֶםfacedown to אָרְצָהground

When the flame went up from the altar to the sky, the angel of the LORD ascended in the flame. When Manoah and his wife saw this, they fell facedown to the ground.

13:21

וְלֹאnot יָסַףagain עוֹדlonger מַלְאַךְangel יְהוָהLORD לְהֵרָאֹהdid appear אֶלto מָנוֹחַManoah וְאֶלand to אִשְׁתּוֹhis wife אָזthen יָדַעrealized מָנוֹחַManoah כִּיwhen מַלְאַךְangel יְהוָהLORD הוּאthe

And when the angel of the LORD did not appear again to Manoah and his wife, Manoah realized that it had been the angel of the LORD.

13:22

וַיֹּאמֶרhe said מָנוֹחַgoing אֶלto אִשְׁתּוֹhis wife מוֹתdie נָמוּתdie כִּיfor אֱלֹהִיםGod רָאִינוּseen

“We are going to die,” he said to his wife, “for we have seen God!”

13:23

וַתֹּאמֶרreplied לוֹto him אִשְׁתּוֹwife לוּif חָפֵץintended יְהוָהLORD לַהֲמִיתֵנוּto kill us לֹאnot לָקַחaccepted מִיָּדֵנוּfrom our hands עֹלָהburnt וּמִנְחָהand the grain וְלֹאand not הֶרְאָנוּshown us אֶת[obj] כָּלall אֵלֶּהthese וְכָעֵתspoken לֹאor הִשְׁמִיעָנוּspoken us כָּזֹאתthis

But his wife replied, “If the LORD had intended to kill us, He would not have accepted the burnt offering and the grain offering from our hands, nor would He have shown us all these things or spoken to us this way.”

13:24

וַתֵּלֶדgave birth הָאִשָּׁהthe woman בֵּןson וַתִּקְרָאand she אֶת[obj] שְׁמוֹnamed him שִׁמְשׁוֹןSamson וַיִּגְדַּלgrew הַנַּעַרthe boy וַיְבָרְכֵהוּblessed him יְהוָהLORD

So the woman gave birth to a son and named him Samson. The boy grew, and the LORD blessed him.

13:25

וַתָּחֶלbegan רוּחַspirit יְהוָהLORD לְפַעֲמוֹto stir him בְּמַחֲנֵהat Mahaneh דָןdan בֵּיןbetween צָרְעָהZorah וּבֵיןand between אֶשְׁתָּאֹלEshtaol

And the Spirit of the LORD began to stir him at Mahaneh-dan, between Zorah and Eshtaol.
