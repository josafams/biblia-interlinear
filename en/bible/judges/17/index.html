Judges 17

Micah and his household shrine

Now a man named Micah from the hill country of Ephraim

17:2

וַיֹּאמֶרsaid לְאִמּוֹto his mother אֶלֶףeleven וּמֵאָהhundred shekels הַכֶּסֶףof silver אֲשֶׁרthat לֻקַּחtaken לָךְto you וְאַתְּyou אָלִיתutter a curse וְגַםhave אָמַרְתְּI בְּאָזְנַיheard הִנֵּהhere הַכֶּסֶףthe silver אִתִּיwith me אֲנִיI לְקַחְתִּיוtook it וַתֹּאמֶרsaid אִמּוֹhis mother בָּרוּךְblessed בְּנִיmy son לַיהוָהby the LORD

said to his mother, “The eleven hundred shekels of silver that were taken from you and about which I heard you utter a curse—I have the silver here with me; I took it.” Then his mother said, “Blessed be my son by the LORD!”

17:3

וַיָּשֶׁבreturned אֶת[obj] אֶלֶףeleven וּמֵאָהhundred shekels הַכֶּסֶףsilver לְאִמּוֹto his mother וַתֹּאמֶרshe said אִמּוֹhis mother הַקְדֵּשׁwholly הִקְדַּשְׁתִּיdedicate אֶת[obj] הַכֶּסֶףthe silver לַיהוָהto the LORD מִיָּדִיfrom my hand לִבְנִיson’s benefit לַעֲשׂוֹתto make פֶּסֶלgraven וּמַסֵּכָהand a molten וְעַתָּהwill now אֲשִׁיבֶנּוּreturn it לָךְto you

And when he had returned the eleven hundred shekels of silver to his mother, she said, “I wholly dedicate the silver to the LORD for my son’s benefit, to make a graven image and a molten idol. Therefore I will now return it to you.”

17:4

וַיָּשֶׁבso he returned אֶת[obj] הַכֶּסֶףthe silver לְאִמּוֹto his mother וַתִּקַּחand she took אִמּוֹtwo מָאתַיִםhundred shekels כֶּסֶףsilver וַתִּתְּנֵהוּand gave them לַצּוֹרֵףto a silversmith וַיַּעֲשֵׂהוּwho made them פֶּסֶלgraven וּמַסֵּכָהand a molten וַיְהִיand they were בְּבֵיתin the house מִיכָיְהוּMicah

So he returned the silver to his mother, and she took two hundred shekels of silver and gave them to a silversmith, who made them into a graven image and a molten idol. And they were placed in the house of Micah.

17:5

וְהָאִישׁnow this man מִיכָהMicah לוֹto him בֵּיתshrine אֱלֹהִיםGod וַיַּעַשׂand he made אֵפוֹדephod וּתְרָפִיםand some household idols וַיְמַלֵּאand ordained אֶת[obj] יַדhand אַחַדone מִבָּנָיוof his sons וַיְהִיand was לוֹto him לְכֹהֵןpriest

Now this man Micah had a shrine, and he made an ephod and some household idols, and ordained one of his sons as his priest.

17:6

בַּיָּמִיםin those days הָהֵםthose אֵיןno מֶלֶךְking בְּיִשְׂרָאֵלin Israel אִישׁeveryone הַיָּשָׁרwhat was right בְּעֵינָיוin his own eyes יַעֲשֶׂהdid

In those days there was no king in Israel; everyone did what was right in his own eyes.

17:7

וַיְהִיand there was נַעַרyoung מִבֵּיתfrom Bethlehem לֶחֶםBethlehem in יְהוּדָהJudah מִמִּשְׁפַּחַתwithin the clan יְהוּדָהJudah וְהוּאand he לֵוִיLevite וְהוּאand he גָרresiding שָׁםthere

And there was a young Levite from Bethlehem in Judah who had been residing within the clan of Judah.

17:8

וַיֵּלֶךְtraveled הָאִישׁthis man מֵהָעִירleft the city מִבֵּיתof Bethlehem לֶחֶםBethlehem יְהוּדָהJudah לָגוּרto settle בַּאֲשֶׁרin where יִמְצָאfind וַיָּבֹאcame הַרhill country אֶפְרַיִםEphraim עַדto בֵּיתhouse מִיכָהMicah’s לַעֲשׂוֹתto do דַּרְכּוֹhis way

This man left the city of Bethlehem in Judah to settle where he could find a place. And as he traveled, he came to Micah’s house in the hill country of Ephraim.

17:9

וַיֹּאמֶרand said לוֹto him מִיכָהMicah מֵאַיִןwhere תָּבוֹאcome וַיֹּאמֶרasked אֵלָיוto him לֵוִיLevite אָנֹכִיam מִבֵּיתfrom Bethlehem לֶחֶםBethlehem יְהוּדָהJudah וְאָנֹכִיand I am הֹלֵךְway לָגוּרto settle בַּאֲשֶׁרin where אֶמְצָאfind

“Where are you from?” Micah asked him. “I am a Levite from Bethlehem in Judah,” he replied, “and I am on my way to settle wherever I can find a place.”

17:10

וַיֹּאמֶרsaid לוֹto him מִיכָהMicah שְׁבָהstay עִמָּדִיwith me וֶהְיֵהand be לִיto me לְאָבfather וּלְכֹהֵןand priest וְאָנֹכִיand I אֶתֶּןgive לְךָto you עֲשֶׂרֶתten כֶּסֶףshekels silver לַיָּמִיםof per year וְעֵרֶךְsuit בְּגָדִיםclothes וּמִחְיָתֶךָand your provisions וַיֵּלֶךְwent הַלֵּוִיthe Levite

“Stay with me,” Micah said to him, “and be my father and priest, and I will give you ten shekels of silver per year, a suit of clothes, and your provisions.” So the Levite went in

17:11

וַיּוֹאֶלand agreed הַלֵּוִיthe Levite לָשֶׁבֶתto stay אֶתwith הָאִישׁman וַיְהִיbecame הַנַּעַרthe young לוֹto him כְּאַחַדlike one מִבָּנָיוa son to Micah

and agreed to stay with him, and the young man became like a son to Micah.

17:12

וַיְמַלֵּאordained מִיכָהMicah אֶת[obj] יַדhand הַלֵּוִיthe Levite וַיְהִיbecame לוֹto him הַנַּעַרthe young לְכֹהֵןpriest וַיְהִיand lived בְּבֵיתin house מִיכָהhis

Micah ordained the Levite, and the young man became his priest and lived in his house.

17:13

וַיֹּאמֶרthen said מִיכָהMicah עַתָּהnow יָדַעְתִּיknow כִּיthat יֵיטִיבgood יְהוָהLORD לִיto me כִּיbecause הָיָהbecome לִיto me הַלֵּוִיa Levite לְכֹהֵןpriest

Then Micah said, “Now I know that the LORD will be good to me, because a Levite has become my priest.”
