Haggai חַגַּי

Haggai gathers four messages, all dated to the second year of the Persian king Darius, in 520 BC. The Jews who had returned from exile had built their houses, but left the temple in ruins. The prophet links the poor harvests to this neglect, and the governor Zerubbabel, the high priest Joshua and the people resume the work. Haggai announces that the glory of the new temple will be greater than that of the first and ends with a promise to Zerubbabel.

Structure

  1. Call to rebuild ch. 1

    The house of God lies in ruins; the leaders and the people listen and begin the work.

  2. Promises for the new temple ch. 2

    The future glory of the temple, a lesson about clean and unclean and the promise to Zerubbabel, called a signet ring.

Chapters

  1. 1 The call to rebuild the temple
  2. 2 The glory of the new temple and Zerubbabel
