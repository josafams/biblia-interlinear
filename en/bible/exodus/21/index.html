Exodus 21

Laws on servants, assaults and damages

Open in the interlinear reader

21:1

וְאֵלֶּהthese הַמִּשְׁפָּטִיםthe ordinances אֲשֶׁרthat תָּשִׂיםset לִפְנֵיהֶםto before them

“These are the ordinances that you are to set before them:

21:2

כִּיif תִקְנֶהbuy עֶבֶדservant עִבְרִיHebrew שֵׁשׁsix שָׁנִיםyears יַעֲבֹדserve וּבַשְּׁבִעִתbut in the seventh יֵצֵאgo לַחָפְשִׁיyear he shall free paying חִנָּםwithout anything

If you buy a Hebrew servant, he is to serve you for six years. But in the seventh year, he shall go free without paying anything.

21:3

אִםif בְּגַפּוֹalone יָבֹאarrived בְּגַפּוֹalone he is to יֵצֵאleave אִםif בַּעַלhusband אִשָּׁהwife הוּאhe וְיָצְאָהto leave אִשְׁתּוֹhis wife עִמּוֹwith him

If he arrived alone, he is to leave alone; if he arrived with a wife, she is to leave with him.

21:4

אִםif אֲדֹנָיוhis master יִתֶּןgives לוֹto him אִשָּׁהwife וְיָלְדָהand she bears לוֹto him בָנִיםsons אוֹor בָנוֹתdaughters הָאִשָּׁהthe woman וִילָדֶיהָand her children תִּהְיֶהbelong לַאדֹנֶיהָto her master וְהוּאand he יֵצֵאgo בְגַפּוֹfree

If his master gives him a wife and she bears him sons or daughters, the woman and her children shall belong to her master, and only the man shall go free.

21:5

וְאִםbut if אָמֹרdeclares יֹאמַרI הָעֶבֶדthe servant אָהַבְתִּיlove אֶת[obj] אֲדֹנִיmy master and my אֶת[obj] אִשְׁתִּיwife וְאֶתand [obj] בָּנָיchildren I לֹאnot אֵצֵאgo חָפְשִׁיfree

But if the servant declares, ‘I love my master and my wife and children; I do not want to go free,’

21:6

וְהִגִּישׁוֹthen his אֲדֹנָיוmaster אֶלto הָאֱלֹהִיםthe judges וְהִגִּישׁוֹand he shall take him אֶלto הַדֶּלֶתthe door אוֹor אֶלto הַמְּזוּזָהdoorpost וְרָצַעand pierce אֲדֹנָיוmaster אֶת[obj] אָזְנוֹear בַּמַּרְצֵעַwith an awl וַעֲבָדוֹserve his לְעֹלָםfor life

then his master is to bring him before the judges. And he shall take him to the door or doorpost and pierce his ear with an awl. Then he shall serve his master for life.

21:7

וְכִיand if יִמְכֹּרsells אִישׁman אֶת[obj] בִּתּוֹhis daughter לְאָמָהas a servant לֹאnot תֵצֵאgo כְּצֵאתfree as הָעֲבָדִיםthe menservants

And if a man sells his daughter as a servant, she is not to go free as the menservants do.

21:8

אִםif רָעָהis displeasing בְּעֵינֵיin the eyes אֲדֹנֶיהָher master אֲשֶׁרhad לוֹfor himself יְעָדָהּmust allow her וְהֶפְדָּהּto be redeemed לְעַםto people נָכְרִיforeigners לֹאno יִמְשֹׁלright לְמָכְרָהּto sell her בְּבִגְדוֹfaith בָהּwith her

If she is displeasing in the eyes of her master who had designated her for himself, he must allow her to be redeemed. He has no right to sell her to foreigners, since he has broken faith with her.

21:9

וְאִםand if לִבְנוֹfor his son יִיעָדֶנָּהchooses her כְּמִשְׁפַּטlike prescribed הַבָּנוֹתa daughter יַעֲשֶׂהdeal לָּהּto her

And if he chooses her for his son, he must deal with her as with a daughter.

21:10

אִםif אַחֶרֶתanother יִקַּחtakes לוֹto him שְׁאֵרָהּwife כְּסוּתָהּclothing וְעֹנָתָהּmarital לֹאnot יִגְרָעreduce

If he takes another wife, he must not reduce the food, clothing, or marital rights of his first wife.

21:11

וְאִםif however שְׁלָשׁthree אֵלֶּהthese לֹאnot יַעֲשֶׂהprovide לָהּto וְיָצְאָהgo חִנָּםwithout אֵיןno כָּסֶףpayment

If, however, he does not provide her with these three things, she is free to go without monetary payment.

21:12

מַכֵּהstrikes אִישׁman וָמֵתand kills מוֹתput יוּמָתdeath

Whoever strikes and kills a man must surely be put to death.

21:13

וַאֲשֶׁרand what לֹאnot צָדָהwait וְהָאֱלֹהִיםbut God אִנָּהallowed לְיָדוֹto his beside וְשַׂמְתִּיappoint לְךָfor you מָקוֹםplace אֲשֶׁרthat יָנוּסflee שָׁמָּהthere toward

If, however, he did not lie in wait, but God allowed it to happen, then I will appoint for you a place where he may flee.

21:14

וְכִיbut if יָזִדschemes אִישׁman עַלagainst רֵעֵהוּhis neighbor לְהָרְגוֹto kill him בְעָרְמָהaway מֵעִםfrom presence מִזְבְּחִיmy altar תִּקָּחֶנּוּtake him לָמוּתput to death

But if a man schemes and acts willfully against his neighbor to kill him, you must take him away from My altar to be put to death.

21:15

וּמַכֵּהwhoever strikes אָבִיוhis father וְאִמּוֹor mother must מוֹתsurely be put יוּמָתdeath

Whoever strikes his father or mother must surely be put to death.

21:16

וְגֹנֵבwhoever kidnaps אִישׁman וּמְכָרוֹhe sells him וְנִמְצָאfound בְיָדוֹin his possession מוֹתput יוּמָתdeath

Whoever kidnaps another man must be put to death, whether he sells him or the man is found in his possession.

21:17

וּמְקַלֵּלwho curses אָבִיוhis father וְאִמּוֹor mother must be מוֹתsurely put יוּמָתdeath

Anyone who curses his father or mother must surely be put to death.

21:18

וְכִיif יְרִיבֻןquarreling אֲנָשִׁיםone וְהִכָּהand strikes אִישׁman אֶת[obj] רֵעֵהוּother בְּאֶבֶןwith a stone אוֹor בְאֶגְרֹףa fist וְלֹאnot יָמוּתdie וְנָפַלbut is confined לְמִשְׁכָּבto bed

If men are quarreling and one strikes the other with a stone or a fist, and he does not die but is confined to bed,

21:19

אִםif יָקוּםget וְהִתְהַלֵּךְand walk בַּחוּץoutside עַלon מִשְׁעַנְתּוֹstaff וְנִקָּהshall go unpunished הַמַּכֶּהwho struck רַקNevertheless שִׁבְתּוֹhis lost יִתֵּןgive וְרַפֹּאcompletely יְרַפֵּאhealed

then the one who struck him shall go unpunished, as long as the other can get up and walk around outside with his staff. Nevertheless, he must compensate the man for his lost work and see that he is completely healed.

21:20

וְכִיif יַכֶּהstrikes אִישׁman אֶת[obj] עַבְדּוֹhis manservant אוֹor אֶת[obj] אֲמָתוֹmaidservant בַּשֵּׁבֶטwith a rod וּמֵתdies תַּחַתunder יָדוֹhis hand he נָקֹםsurely יִנָּקֵםpunished

If a man strikes his manservant or maidservant with a rod, and the servant dies by his hand, he shall surely be punished.

21:21

אַךְhowever אִםif יוֹםday אוֹor יוֹמַיִםday יַעֲמֹדtwo לֹאnot יֻקַּםpunished כִּיfor כַסְפּוֹgets הוּאis

However, if the servant gets up after a day or two, the owner shall not be punished, since the servant is his property.

21:22

וְכִיif יִנָּצוּfighting אֲנָשִׁיםmen וְנָגְפוּstrike אִשָּׁהwoman הָרָהpregnant וְיָצְאוּand go יְלָדֶיהָher child וְלֹאno יִהְיֶהborn אָסוֹןinjury עָנוֹשׁfined יֵעָנֵשׁfined כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had יָשִׁיתwoman’s עָלָיוon him בַּעַלhusband הָאִשָּׁהdemands וְנָתַןcourt בִּפְלִלִיםallows

If men who are fighting strike a pregnant woman and her child is born prematurely, but there is no further injury, he shall surely be fined as the woman’s husband demands and as the court allows.

21:23

וְאִםbut if אָסוֹןserious injury יִהְיֶהresults וְנָתַתָּהthen you נֶפֶשׁlife תַּחַתfor נָפֶשׁlife

But if a serious injury results, then you must require a life for a life—

21:24

עַיִןeye תַּחַתfor עַיִןeye שֵׁןtooth תַּחַתfor שֵׁןtooth יָדhand תַּחַתfor יָדhand רֶגֶלfoot תַּחַתfor רָגֶלfoot

eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot,

21:25

כְּוִיָּהburn תַּחַתfor כְּוִיָּהwound פֶּצַעwounds תַּחַתunder פָּצַעstripe חַבּוּרָהstripe תַּחַתfor חַבּוּרָהstripe

burn for burn, wound for wound, and stripe for stripe.

21:26

וְכִיif יַכֶּהstrikes אִישׁman אֶת[obj] עֵיןeye עַבְדּוֹhis manservant אוֹor אֶת[obj] עֵיןeyes אֲמָתוֹmaidservant he וְשִׁחֲתָהּand destruction her לַחָפְשִׁיservant free as compensation יְשַׁלְּחֶנּוּgo תַּחַתfor עֵינוֹeye

If a man strikes and blinds the eye of his manservant or maidservant, he must let the servant go free as compensation for the eye.

21:27

וְאִםand if שֵׁןknocks עַבְדּוֹhis manservant אוֹor שֵׁןtooth אֲמָתוֹmaidservant he יַפִּילgo לַחָפְשִׁיmust let the servant free יְשַׁלְּחֶנּוּcompensation תַּחַתfor שִׁנּוֹtooth

And if he knocks out the tooth of his manservant or maidservant, he must let the servant go free as compensation for the tooth.

21:28

וְכִיif יִגַּחgores שׁוֹרox אֶת[obj] אִישׁman אוֹor אֶת[obj] אִשָּׁהwoman וָמֵתmust surely סָקוֹלstoned יִסָּקֵלstoned הַשּׁוֹרthe ox וְלֹאnot יֵאָכֵלeaten אֶת[obj] בְּשָׂרוֹits meat וּבַעַלbut the owner הַשּׁוֹרthe ox נָקִיnot

If an ox gores a man or woman to death, the ox must surely be stoned, and its meat must not be eaten. But the owner of the ox shall not be held responsible.

21:29

וְאִםbut if שׁוֹרox נַגָּחhabit הוּאhe מִתְּמֹלa of goring שִׁלְשֹׁםgoring וְהוּעַדwarned בִּבְעָלָיוits owner has וְלֹאyet does not יִשְׁמְרֶנּוּrestrain it וְהֵמִיתand it kills אִישׁman אוֹor אִשָּׁהwoman הַשּׁוֹרthe ox יִסָּקֵלstoned וְגַםalso בְּעָלָיוits owner יוּמָתput death

But if the ox has a habit of goring, and its owner has been warned yet does not restrain it, and it kills a man or woman, then the ox must be stoned and its owner must also be put to death.

21:30

אִםif כֹּפֶרpayment יוּשַׁתdemanded עָלָיוon him וְנָתַןinstead he פִּדְיֹןredeem נַפְשׁוֹhis life כְּכֹלfull אֲשֶׁרthat יוּשַׁתdemanded עָלָיוon him

If payment is demanded of him instead, he may redeem his life by paying the full amount demanded of him.

21:31

אוֹif בֵןson יִגָּחox אוֹor בַתdaughter יִגָּחgores כַּמִּשְׁפָּטlike the prescribed הַזֶּהthis יֵעָשֶׂהdone לּוֹto him

If the ox gores a son or a daughter, it shall be done to him according to the same rule.

21:32

אִםif עֶבֶדservant יִגַּחgores הַשּׁוֹרthe ox אוֹor אָמָהmanservant maidservant כֶּסֶףsilver שְׁלֹשִׁיםthirty שְׁקָלִיםshekels יִתֵּןpay לַאדֹנָיוto the master וְהַשּׁוֹרand the ox יִסָּקֵלstoned

If the ox gores a manservant or maidservant, the owner must pay thirty shekels of silver to the master of that servant, and the ox must be stoned.

21:33

וְכִיif יִפְתַּחopens אִישׁman בּוֹרpit אוֹor כִּיfor יִכְרֶהdigs אִישׁman בֹּרpit וְלֹאand not יְכַסֶּנּוּcover it וְנָפַלfalls שָׁמָּהthere toward שּׁוֹרox אוֹor חֲמוֹרdonkey

If a man opens or digs a pit and fails to cover it, and an ox or a donkey falls into it,

21:34

בַּעַלowner הַבּוֹרthe pit יְשַׁלֵּםrestitution כֶּסֶףpay יָשִׁיבrepay לִבְעָלָיוits owner וְהַמֵּתand the dead יִהְיֶהanimal לּוֹto him

the owner of the pit shall make restitution; he must pay its owner, and the dead animal will be his.

21:35

וְכִיif יִגֹּףinjures שׁוֹרox אִישׁman’s אֶת[obj] שׁוֹרox רֵעֵהוּhis neighbor’s וָמֵתand it dies וּמָכְרוּthey must sell אֶת[obj] הַשּׁוֹרthe ox הַחַיlive וְחָצוּand divide אֶת[obj] כַּסְפּוֹproceeds וְגַםthey also אֶת[obj] הַמֵּתmust the dead יֶחֱצוּןdivide

If a man’s ox injures his neighbor’s ox and it dies, they must sell the live one and divide the proceeds; they also must divide the dead animal.

21:36

אוֹif נוֹדַעknown כִּיthat שׁוֹרox נַגָּחhabit הוּאa מִתְּמוֹלhad a שִׁלְשֹׁםgoring וְלֹאand not יִשְׁמְרֶנּוּrestrain בְּעָלָיוowner שַׁלֵּםpay compensation יְשַׁלֵּםfull שׁוֹרox תַּחַתfor הַשּׁוֹרox וְהַמֵּתand the dead יִהְיֶהanimal לּוֹto him

But if it was known that the ox had a habit of goring, yet its owner failed to restrain it, he shall pay full compensation, ox for ox, and the dead animal will be his.
