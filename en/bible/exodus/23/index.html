Exodus 23

Justice, rest and the three annual feasts

Open in the interlinear reader

23:1

לֹאnot תִשָּׂאspread שֵׁמַעreport שָׁוְאfalse אַלdo not תָּשֶׁתset יָדְךָyour hand עִםwith רָשָׁעwicked לִהְיֹתto be עֵדa witness חָמָסmalicious

“You shall not spread a false report. Do not join the wicked by being a malicious witness.

23:2

לֹאnot תִהְיֶהshall אַחֲרֵיfollow רַבִּיםcrowd לְרָעֹתin wrongdoing וְלֹאand not תַעֲנֶהtestify עַלin רִבlawsuit לִנְטֹתnot pervert justice אַחֲרֵיafter רַבִּיםcrowd לְהַטֹּתby siding

You shall not follow the crowd in wrongdoing. When you testify in a lawsuit, do not pervert justice by siding with the crowd.

23:3

וְדָלpoor לֹאnot תֶהְדַּרfavoritism בְּרִיבוֹin his lawsuit

And do not show favoritism to a poor man in his lawsuit.

23:4

כִּיif תִפְגַּעencounter שׁוֹרox אֹיִבְךָyour enemy’s אוֹor חֲמֹרוֹdonkey you must תֹּעֶהstray הָשֵׁבreturn תְּשִׁיבֶנּוּreturn him לוֹto him

If you encounter your enemy’s stray ox or donkey, you must return it to him.

23:5

כִּיif תִרְאֶהsee חֲמוֹרdonkey שֹׂנַאֲךָhates you רֹבֵץfallen תַּחַתunder מַשָּׂאוֹits load וְחָדַלְתָּdo leave מֵעֲזֹבnot leave לוֹto him עָזֹבforsaken תַּעֲזֹבleave עִמּוֹhim with it

If you see the donkey of one who hates you fallen under its load, do not leave it there; you must help him with it.

23:6

לֹאnot תַטֶּהdeny מִשְׁפַּטjustice אֶבְיֹנְךָpoor בְּרִיבוֹin their lawsuits

You shall not deny justice to the poor in their lawsuits.

23:7

מִדְּבַרaccusation שֶׁקֶרfalse תִּרְחָקfar וְנָקִיinnocent וְצַדִּיקor the just אַלdo not תַּהֲרֹגkill כִּיfor לֹאnot אַצְדִּיקacquit רָשָׁעguilty

Stay far away from a false accusation. Do not kill the innocent or the just, for I will not acquit the guilty.

23:8

וְשֹׁחַדbribe לֹאnot תִקָּחaccept כִּיfor הַשֹּׁחַדa bribe יְעַוֵּרblinds פִּקְחִיםsee וִיסַלֵּףand twists דִּבְרֵיwords צַדִּיקִיםrighteous

Do not accept a bribe, for a bribe blinds those who see and twists the words of the righteous.

23:9

וְגֵרforeign resident לֹאnot תִלְחָץoppress וְאַתֶּםsince you יְדַעְתֶּםknow אֶת[obj] נֶפֶשׁyourselves הַגֵּרto be foreigners כִּיfor גֵרִיםforeigners הֱיִיתֶםwere בְּאֶרֶץin the land מִצְרָיִםEgypt

Do not oppress a foreign resident, since you yourselves know how it feels to be foreigners; for you were foreigners in the land of Egypt.

23:10

וְשֵׁשׁfor six שָׁנִיםyears תִּזְרַעsow אֶת[obj] אַרְצֶךָyour land וְאָסַפְתָּand gather אֶת[obj] תְּבוּאָתָהּits produce

For six years you are to sow your land and gather its produce,

23:11

וְהַשְּׁבִיעִתbut in the seventh תִּשְׁמְטֶנָּהyear you must וּנְטַשְׁתָּהּit and lie fallow וְאָכְלוּeat אֶבְיֹנֵיpoor עַמֶּךָyour people וְיִתְרָםrest תֹּאכַלconsume חַיַּתwild animals הַשָּׂדֶהthe field כֵּןsame תַּעֲשֶׂהdo לְכַרְמְךָwith your vineyard לְזֵיתֶךָand olive grove

but in the seventh year you must let it rest and lie fallow, so that the poor among your people may eat from the field and the wild animals may consume what they leave. Do the same with your vineyard and olive grove.

23:12

שֵׁשֶׁתsix יָמִיםdays תַּעֲשֶׂהdo מַעֲשֶׂיךָyour work וּבַיּוֹםbut on the day הַשְּׁבִיעִיseventh תִּשְׁבֹּתcease לְמַעַןso יָנוּחַrest שׁוֹרְךָyour ox וַחֲמֹרֶךָand your donkey וְיִנָּפֵשׁrefreshed בֶּןson אֲמָתְךָyour maidservant וְהַגֵּרwell as the foreign resident

For six days you are to do your work, but on the seventh day you must cease, so that your ox and your donkey may rest and the son of your maidservant may be refreshed, as well as the foreign resident.

23:13

וּבְכֹלto everything אֲשֶׁרhave אָמַרְתִּיsaid אֲלֵיכֶםto you תִּשָּׁמֵרוּkeep וְשֵׁםnames אֱלֹהִיםother אֲחֵרִיםgods לֹאnot תַזְכִּירוּinvoke לֹאnot יִשָּׁמַעheard עַלon פִּיךָyour lips

Pay close attention to everything I have said to you. You must not invoke the names of other gods; they must not be heard on your lips.

23:14

שָׁלֹשׁthree רְגָלִיםtimes תָּחֹגcelebrate feast לִיto בַּשָּׁנָהa year

Three times a year you are to celebrate a feast to Me.

23:15

אֶת[obj] חַגfeast הַמַּצּוֹתunleavened תִּשְׁמֹרkeep שִׁבְעַתseven יָמִיםdays תֹּאכַלeat מַצּוֹתbread כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had צִוִּיתִךָcommanded you לְמוֹעֵדat the appointed time חֹדֶשׁmonth הָאָבִיבAbib כִּיbecause בוֹto יָצָאתָcame מִמִּצְרָיִםout of Egypt וְלֹאno יֵרָאוּappear פָנַיbefore me רֵיקָםempty handed

You are to keep the Feast of Unleavened Bread as I commanded you: At the appointed time in the month of Abib you are to eat unleavened bread for seven days, because that was the month you came out of Egypt. No one may appear before Me empty-handed.

23:16

וְחַגfeast הַקָּצִירof harvest בִּכּוּרֵיfirstfruits מַעֲשֶׂיךָproduce אֲשֶׁרthat תִּזְרַעsow בַּשָּׂדֶהin the field וְחַגfeast הָאָסִףof Ingathering בְּצֵאתin went הַשָּׁנָהyear בְּאָסְפְּךָwhen you gather אֶת[obj] מַעֲשֶׂיךָyour produce מִןfrom הַשָּׂדֶהthe field

You are also to keep the Feast of Harvest with the firstfruits of the produce from what you sow in the field. And keep the Feast of Ingathering at the end of the year, when you gather your produce from the field.

23:17

שָׁלֹשׁthree פְּעָמִיםtimes בַּשָּׁנָהa year יֵרָאֶהappear כָּלall זְכוּרְךָyour males אֶלto פְּנֵיbefore הָאָדֹןthe GOD יְהוָהLord

Three times a year all your males are to appear before the Lord GOD.

23:18

לֹאnot תִזְבַּחoffer עַלon חָמֵץanything leavened דַּםblood זִבְחִיmy sacrifices וְלֹאand not יָלִיןremain חֵלֶבfat חַגִּיmy feast עַדuntil בֹּקֶרmorning

You must not offer the blood of My sacrifices with anything leavened, nor may the fat of My feast remain until morning.

23:19

רֵאשִׁיתbest בִּכּוּרֵיfirstfruits אַדְמָתְךָyour soil תָּבִיאbring בֵּיתhouse יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God you לֹאnot תְבַשֵּׁלcook גְּדִיyoung goat בַּחֲלֵבmilk אִמּוֹits mother’s

Bring the best of the firstfruits of your soil to the house of the LORD your God. You must not cook a young goat in its mother’s milk.

23:20

הִנֵּהbehold אָנֹכִיam שֹׁלֵחַsending מַלְאָךְangel לְפָנֶיךָbefore you לִשְׁמָרְךָto protect you בַּדָּרֶךְalong the way וְלַהֲבִיאֲךָand to bring you אֶלto הַמָּקוֹםthe place אֲשֶׁרhave הֲכִנֹתִיprepared

Behold, I am sending an angel before you to protect you along the way and to bring you to the place I have prepared.

23:21

הִשָּׁמֶרpay מִפָּנָיוattention to him וּשְׁמַעand listen בְּקֹלוֹto his voice אַלdo not תַּמֵּרdefy בּוֹin him כִּיfor לֹאnot יִשָּׂאforgive לְפִשְׁעֲכֶםrebellion כִּיfor שְׁמִיmy name בְּקִרְבּוֹin his among

Pay attention to him and listen to his voice; do not defy him, for he will not forgive rebellion, since My Name is in him.

23:22

כִּיbut אִםif שָׁמֹעַlisten תִּשְׁמַעcarefully בְּקֹלוֹto his voice וְעָשִׂיתָand do כֹּלeverything אֲשֶׁרthat אֲדַבֵּרsay וְאָיַבְתִּיenemy אֶת[obj] אֹיְבֶיךָyour enemies וְצַרְתִּיand a foe אֶת[obj] צֹרְרֶיךָyour foes

But if you will listen carefully to his voice and do everything I say, I will be an enemy to your enemies and a foe to your foes.

23:23

כִּיfor יֵלֵךְgo מַלְאָכִיmy angel לְפָנֶיךָbefore you וֶהֱבִיאֲךָand bring you אֶלinto הָאֱמֹרִיAmorites וְהַחִתִּיland of the Hittites וְהַפְּרִזִּיPerizzites וְהַכְּנַעֲנִיCanaanites הַחִוִּיHivites וְהַיְבוּסִיand Jebusites וְהִכְחַדְתִּיוannihilate them

For My angel will go before you and bring you into the land of the Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Canaanites, Hivites, and Jebusites, and I will annihilate them.

23:24

לֹאnot תִשְׁתַּחֲוֶהbow down לֵאלֹהֵיהֶםto their gods וְלֹאor תָעָבְדֵםserve them וְלֹאor תַעֲשֶׂהfollow כְּמַעֲשֵׂיהֶםtheir practices כִּיfor הָרֵסdemolish תְּהָרְסֵםdemolish them וְשַׁבֵּרpieces תְּשַׁבֵּרstones מַצֵּבֹתֵיהֶםsmash their sacred

You must not bow down to their gods or serve them or follow their practices. Instead, you are to demolish them and smash their sacred stones to pieces.

23:25

וַעֲבַדְתֶּםshall serve אֵת[obj] יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיכֶםyour God וּבֵרַךְbless אֶת[obj] לַחְמְךָyour bread וְאֶתand [obj] מֵימֶיךָyour water וַהֲסִרֹתִיaway מַחֲלָהsickness מִקִּרְבֶּךָfrom among you

So you shall serve the LORD your God, and He will bless your bread and your water. And I will take away sickness from among you.

23:26

לֹאno תִהְיֶהwill מְשַׁכֵּלָהmiscarry וַעֲקָרָהor be barren בְּאַרְצֶךָin your land אֶת[obj] מִסְפַּרnumber יָמֶיךָyour days אֲמַלֵּאfill

No woman in your land will miscarry or be barren; I will fulfill the number of your days.

23:27

אֶת[obj] אֵימָתִיmy terror אֲשַׁלַּחsend לְפָנֶיךָahead of you וְהַמֹּתִיand throw confusion אֶת[obj] כָּלevery הָעָםnation אֲשֶׁרyou תָּבֹאencounter בָּהֶםin them וְנָתַתִּיI will make אֶת[obj] כָּלall אֹיְבֶיךָyour enemies אֵלֶיךָto you עֹרֶףrun

I will send My terror ahead of you and throw into confusion every nation you encounter. I will make all your enemies turn and run.

23:28

וְשָׁלַחְתִּיwill send אֶת[obj] הַצִּרְעָהthe hornet לְפָנֶיךָbefore you וְגֵרְשָׁהto drive אֶת[obj] הַחִוִּיthe Hivites אֶת[obj] הַכְּנַעֲנִיand Canaanites וְאֶתand [obj] הַחִתִּיHittites מִלְּפָנֶיךָfrom to your before

I will send the hornet before you to drive the Hivites and Canaanites and Hittites out of your way.

23:29

לֹאnot אֲגָרְשֶׁנּוּdrive them מִפָּנֶיךָout before you בְּשָׁנָהyear אֶחָתsingle פֶּןotherwise תִּהְיֶהbecome הָאָרֶץthe land שְׁמָמָהdesolate וְרַבָּהmultiply עָלֶיךָagainst you חַיַּתanimals הַשָּׂדֶהwild would

I will not drive them out before you in a single year; otherwise the land would become desolate and wild animals would multiply against you.

23:30

מְעַטlittle מְעַטlittle אֲגָרְשֶׁנּוּdrive them מִפָּנֶיךָout ahead of you עַדuntil אֲשֶׁרyou תִּפְרֶהfruitful וְנָחַלְתָּand possess אֶת[obj] הָאָרֶץthe land

Little by little I will drive them out ahead of you, until you become fruitful and possess the land.

23:31

וְשַׁתִּיestablish אֶת[obj] גְּבֻלְךָyour borders מִיַּםfrom the Red סוּףsea וְעַדto יָםsea פְּלִשְׁתִּיםPhilistines וּמִמִּדְבָּרand from the desert עַדto הַנָּהָרthe Euphrates כִּיfor אֶתֵּןdeliver בְּיֶדְכֶםinto your hand אֵת[obj] יֹשְׁבֵיinhabitants הָאָרֶץthe land וְגֵרַשְׁתָּמוֹwill drive them מִפָּנֶיךָout before you

And I will establish your borders from the Red Sea to the Sea of the Philistines, and from the desert to the Euphrates. For I will deliver the inhabitants into your hand, and you will drive them out before you.

23:32

לֹאno תִכְרֹתmake לָהֶםto them וְלֵאלֹהֵיהֶםor with their gods בְּרִיתcovenant

You shall make no covenant with them or with their gods.

23:33

לֹאnot יֵשְׁבוּremain בְּאַרְצְךָin your land פֶּןlest יַחֲטִיאוּcause אֹתְךָyou לִיto כִּיfor תַעֲבֹדserve אֶת[obj] אֱלֹהֵיהֶםtheir gods כִּיfor יִהְיֶהbe לְךָa לְמוֹקֵשׁsnare to

They must not remain in your land, lest they cause you to sin against Me. For if you serve their gods, it will surely be a snare to you.”
