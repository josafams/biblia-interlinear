וְהָיָהbe עַלon מֵצַחworn אַהֲרֹןAaron’s וְנָשָׂאmay bear אַהֲרֹןAaron אֶת[obj] עֲוֺןiniquity הַקֳּדָשִׁיםthe holy אֲשֶׁרthat יַקְדִּישׁוּconsecrate בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael לְכָלto all מַתְּנֹתgifts קָדְשֵׁיהֶםtheir holy וְהָיָהshall be עַלon מִצְחוֹhis forehead תָּמִידalways לְרָצוֹןmay be acceptable לָהֶםto them לִפְנֵיbefore יְהוָהLORD

And it will be worn on Aaron’s forehead, so that he may bear the iniquity of the holy things that the sons of Israel consecrate with regard to all their holy gifts. It shall always be on his forehead, so that they may be acceptable before the LORD.

28:39

וְשִׁבַּצְתָּweave הַכְּתֹנֶתthe tunic שֵׁשׁfine linen וְעָשִׂיתָmake מִצְנֶפֶתturban שֵׁשׁfine linen וְאַבְנֵטsash תַּעֲשֶׂהfashion מַעֲשֵׂהwork רֹקֵםembroidered

You are to weave the tunic with fine linen, make the turban of fine linen, and fashion an embroidered sash.

28:40

וְלִבְנֵיfor sons אַהֲרֹןAaron’s תַּעֲשֶׂהmake כֻתֳּנֹתtunics וְעָשִׂיתָand make לָהֶםto them אַבְנֵטִיםsashes וּמִגְבָּעוֹתand headbands תַּעֲשֶׂהdo לָהֶםto לְכָבוֹדglory וּלְתִפְאָרֶתand splendor

Make tunics, sashes, and headbands for Aaron’s sons, to give them glory and splendor.

28:41

וְהִלְבַּשְׁתָּafter you put אֹתָםthem אֶת[obj] אַהֲרֹןgarments אָחִיךָyour brother וְאֶתand [obj] בָּנָיוhis sons אִתּוֹwith him וּמָשַׁחְתָּanoint אֹתָםthem וּמִלֵּאתָordain אֶת[obj] יָדָםtheir hand וְקִדַּשְׁתָּand consecrate אֹתָםthem וְכִהֲנוּmay serve לִיto me

After you put these garments on your brother Aaron and his sons, anoint them, ordain them, and consecrate them so that they may serve Me as priests.

28:42

וַעֲשֵׂהmake לָהֶםto them מִכְנְסֵיundergarments בָדlinen לְכַסּוֹתto cover בְּשַׂרflesh עֶרְוָהextending מִמָּתְנַיִםfrom waist וְעַדto יְרֵכַיִםthigh יִהְיוּbe

Make linen undergarments to cover their bare flesh, extending from waist to thigh.

28:43

וְהָיוּand be עַלon אַהֲרֹןAaron וְעַלand on בָּנָיוhis sons בְּבֹאָםwhenever they enter אֶלto אֹהֶלTent מוֹעֵדMeeting אוֹor בְגִשְׁתָּםapproach אֶלto הַמִּזְבֵּחַthe altar לְשָׁרֵתto minister בַּקֹּדֶשׁin the holy וְלֹאnot יִשְׂאוּincur עָוֺןguilt וָמֵתוּand die חֻקַּתstatute עוֹלָםpermanent לוֹfor Aaron וּלְזַרְעוֹand his descendants אַחֲרָיוafter him

Aaron and his sons must wear them whenever they enter the Tent of Meeting or approach the altar to minister in the Holy Place, so that they will not incur guilt and die. This is to be a permanent statute for Aaron and his descendants.
