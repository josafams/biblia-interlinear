Exodus 22

Restitution, loans and protection of the weak

Open in the interlinear reader

22:1

כִּיif יִגְנֹבsteals אִישׁman שׁוֹרox אוֹor שֶׂהsheep וּטְבָחוֹand slaughters אוֹor מְכָרוֹsells it חֲמִשָּׁהfive בָקָרoxen יְשַׁלֵּםrepay תַּחַתfor הַשּׁוֹרox וְאַרְבַּעand four צֹאןsheep תַּחַתfor הַשֶּׂהa sheep

“If a man steals an ox or a sheep and slaughters or sells it, he must repay five oxen for an ox and four sheep for a sheep.

22:2

אִםif בַּמַּחְתֶּרֶתbreaking in יִמָּצֵאcaught הַגַּנָּבthief וְהֻכָּהand is beaten וָמֵתto death אֵיןno לוֹto him דָּמִיםbloodshed

If a thief is caught breaking in and is beaten to death, no one shall be guilty of bloodshed.

22:3

אִםif זָרְחָהhappens הַשֶּׁמֶשׁguilt עָלָיוfor his דָּמִיםbloodshed לוֹto him שַׁלֵּםfull יְשַׁלֵּםrestitution אִםif אֵיןnothing לוֹto him וְנִמְכַּרshall be sold בִּגְנֵבָתוֹfor his theft

But if it happens after sunrise, there is guilt for his bloodshed. A thief must make full restitution; if he has nothing, he himself shall be sold for his theft.

22:4

אִםif הִמָּצֵאactually תִמָּצֵאfound בְיָדוֹin his הַגְּנֵבָהpossession מִשּׁוֹרwhether ox עַדuntil חֲמוֹרdonkey עַדuntil שֶׂהsheep חַיִּיםalive שְׁנַיִםdouble יְשַׁלֵּםpay

If what was stolen is actually found alive in his possession—whether ox or donkey or sheep—he must pay back double.

22:5

כִּיif יַבְעֶרgrazes אִישׁman שָׂדֶהfield אוֹor כֶרֶםvineyard וְשִׁלַּחand allows אֶת[obj] בְּעִירוֹhis livestock וּבִעֵרgraze בִּשְׂדֵהfield אַחֵרsomeone מֵיטַבelse’s שָׂדֵהוּfield he וּמֵיטַבbest כַּרְמוֹvineyard יְשַׁלֵּםrestitution

If a man grazes his livestock in a field or vineyard and allows them to stray so that they graze in someone else’s field, he must make restitution from the best of his own field or vineyard.

22:6

כִּיif תֵצֵאout אֵשׁfire וּמָצְאָהand spreads קֹצִיםthornbushes וְנֶאֱכַלconsumes גָּדִישׁstacked אוֹor הַקָּמָהstanding grain אוֹor הַשָּׂדֶהthe whole field שַׁלֵּםfull יְשַׁלֵּםmake restitution הַמַּבְעִרthe fire אֶת[obj] הַבְּעֵרָהstarted

If a fire breaks out and spreads to thornbushes so that it consumes stacked or standing grain, or the whole field, the one who started the fire must make full restitution.

22:7

כִּיif יִתֵּןgives אִישׁman אֶלto רֵעֵהוּhis neighbor כֶּסֶףmoney אוֹor כֵלִיםgoods לִשְׁמֹרfor safekeeping וְגֻנַּבstolen מִבֵּיתhouse הָאִישׁthe man אִםif יִמָּצֵאcaught הַגַּנָּבthief יְשַׁלֵּםpay שְׁנָיִםdouble

If a man gives his neighbor money or goods for safekeeping and they are stolen from the neighbor’s house, the thief, if caught, must pay back double.

22:8

אִםif לֹאnot יִמָּצֵאfound הַגַּנָּבthe thief וְנִקְרַבand present בַּעַלowner הַבַּיִתthe house אֶלto הָאֱלֹהִיםappear the אִםif לֹאnot שָׁלַחhe יָדוֹjudges בִּמְלֶאכֶתproperty רֵעֵהוּneighbor’s property

If the thief is not found, the owner of the house must appear before the judges to determine whether he has taken his neighbor’s property.

22:9

עַלin כָּלall דְּבַרcases פֶּשַׁעillegal עַלon שׁוֹרox עַלon חֲמוֹרdonkey עַלon שֶׂהsheep עַלon שַׂלְמָהgarment עַלon כָּלany אֲבֵדָהlost item אֲשֶׁרthat יֹאמַרclaims כִּיfor הוּאis זֶהthis עַדuntil הָאֱלֹהִיםthe judges יָבֹאbring דְּבַרcases שְׁנֵיהֶםboth parties אֲשֶׁרwhom יַרְשִׁיעֻןguilty אֱלֹהִיםjudges יְשַׁלֵּםpay שְׁנַיִםdouble לְרֵעֵהוּto his neighbor

In all cases of illegal possession of an ox, a donkey, a sheep, a garment, or any lost item that someone claims, ‘This is mine,’ both parties shall bring their cases before the judges. The one whom the judges find guilty must pay back double to his neighbor.

22:10

כִּיif יִתֵּןgives אִישׁman אֶלto רֵעֵהוּhis neighbor חֲמוֹרdonkey אוֹa שׁוֹרox אוֹor שֶׂהsheep וְכָלany בְּהֵמָהanimal לִשְׁמֹרto be cared וּמֵתdies אוֹor נִשְׁבַּרinjured אוֹor נִשְׁבָּהstolen אֵיןno רֹאֶהwatching

If a man gives a donkey, an ox, a sheep, or any other animal to be cared for by his neighbor, but it dies or is injured or stolen while no one is watching,

22:11

שְׁבֻעַתoath before יְהוָהLORD תִּהְיֶהbe בֵּיןbetween שְׁנֵיהֶםparties to אִםor לֹאor not שָׁלַחdetermine יָדוֹhis hand בִּמְלֶאכֶתproperty רֵעֵהוּhis neighbor’s וְלָקַחmust accept בְּעָלָיוowner וְלֹאand require no יְשַׁלֵּםrestitution

an oath before the LORD shall be made between the parties to determine whether or not the man has taken his neighbor’s property. The owner must accept the oath and require no restitution.

22:12

וְאִםbut if גָּנֹבactually יִגָּנֵבstolen מֵעִמּוֹfrom presence him יְשַׁלֵּםrestitution לִבְעָלָיוto the owner

But if the animal was actually stolen from the neighbor, he must make restitution to the owner.

22:13

אִםif טָרֹףtorn יִטָּרֵףpieces יְבִאֵהוּbring it עֵדevidence הַטְּרֵפָהthe torn carcass לֹאnot יְשַׁלֵּםrestitution

If the animal was torn to pieces, he shall bring it as evidence; he need not make restitution for the torn carcass.

22:14

וְכִיif יִשְׁאַלborrows אִישׁman מֵעִםfrom presence רֵעֵהוּhis neighbor וְנִשְׁבַּרinjured אוֹor מֵתdies בְּעָלָיוits owner is אֵיןnot עִמּוֹwith him שַׁלֵּםmake יְשַׁלֵּםfull

If a man borrows an animal from his neighbor and it is injured or dies while its owner is not present, he must make full restitution.

22:15

אִםif בְּעָלָיוowner was עִמּוֹwith him לֹאno יְשַׁלֵּםrestitution אִםif שָׂכִירanimal rented הוּאwas בָּאcome בִּשְׂכָרוֹfee covers the loss

If the owner was present, no restitution is required. If the animal was rented, the fee covers the loss.

22:16

וְכִיif יְפַתֶּהseduces אִישׁman בְּתוּלָהvirgin אֲשֶׁרwho לֹאnot אֹרָשָׂהpledged in marriage וְשָׁכַבand sleeps עִמָּהּwith her מָהֹרfull יִמְהָרֶנָּהdowry for her לּוֹto be his לְאִשָּׁהwife

If a man seduces a virgin who is not pledged in marriage and sleeps with her, he must pay the full dowry for her to be his wife.

22:17

אִםif מָאֵןabsolutely יְמָאֵןrefuses אָבִיהָher father לְתִתָּהּto give her לוֹto him כֶּסֶףmust יִשְׁקֹלpay כְּמֹהַרan amount comparable הַבְּתוּלֹתvirgin

If her father absolutely refuses to give her to him, the man still must pay an amount comparable to the bridal price of a virgin.

22:18

מְכַשֵּׁפָהallow לֹאnot תְחַיֶּהlive

You must not allow a sorceress to live.

22:19

כָּלwhoever שֹׁכֵבlies עִםwith בְּהֵמָהanimal מוֹתsurely יוּמָתdeath

Whoever lies with an animal must surely be put to death.

22:20

זֹבֵחַsacrifices לָאֱלֹהִיםto any god יָחֳרָםother בִּלְתִּיno לַיהוָהthan the LORD לְבַדּוֹalone he

If anyone sacrifices to any god other than the LORD alone, he must be set apart for destruction.

22:21

וְגֵרand foreigner לֹאnot תוֹנֶהexploit וְלֹאor תִלְחָצֶנּוּoppress כִּיfor גֵרִיםforeign resident הֱיִיתֶםwere בְּאֶרֶץin the land מִצְרָיִםEgypt

You must not exploit or oppress a foreign resident, for you yourselves were foreigners in the land of Egypt.

22:22

כָּלany אַלְמָנָהwidow וְיָתוֹםor orphan לֹאnot תְעַנּוּןmistreat

You must not mistreat any widow or orphan.

22:23

אִםif עַנֵּהafflicted תְעַנֶּהmistreat אֹתוֹhim כִּיfor אִםif צָעֹקcry יִצְעַקout אֵלַיto me שָׁמֹעַsurely אֶשְׁמַעhear צַעֲקָתוֹtheir cry

If you do mistreat them, and they cry out to Me in distress, I will surely hear their cry.

22:24

וְחָרָהwill be kindled אַפִּיmy anger וְהָרַגְתִּיkill אֶתְכֶםyou בֶּחָרֶבwith the sword וְהָיוּbecome נְשֵׁיכֶםyour wives אַלְמָנוֹתwidows וּבְנֵיכֶםand your children יְתֹמִיםfatherless

My anger will be kindled, and I will kill you with the sword; then your wives will become widows and your children will be fatherless.

22:25

אִםif כֶּסֶףmoney תַּלְוֶהlend אֶת[obj] עַמִּיmy people אֶת[obj] הֶעָנִיwho is poor עִמָּךְamong you לֹאnot תִהְיֶהact לוֹto him כְּנֹשֶׁהas a creditor לֹאnot תְשִׂימוּןkeep some עָלָיוon him נֶשֶׁךְinterest

If you lend money to one of My people among you who is poor, you must not act as a creditor to him; you are not to charge him interest.

22:26

אִםif חָבֹלcollateral תַּחְבֹּלcollateral שַׂלְמַתcloak רֵעֶךָyour neighbor’s עַדuntil בֹּאsunset הַשֶּׁמֶשׁby sunset תְּשִׁיבֶנּוּreturn it לוֹto him

If you take your neighbor’s cloak as collateral, return it to him by sunset,

22:27

כִּיbecause הִואis כְסוּתוֹcloak לְבַדָּהּthe only הִואis שִׂמְלָתוֹhis cloak לְעֹרוֹfor his body בַּמֶּהwhat יִשְׁכָּבsleep וְהָיָהand be כִּיif יִצְעַקcries out אֵלַיto me וְשָׁמַעְתִּיwill hear כִּיfor חַנּוּןcompassionate אָנִיam

because his cloak is the only covering he has for his body. What else will he sleep in? And if he cries out to Me, I will hear, for I am compassionate.

22:28

אֱלֹהִיםGod לֹאor תְקַלֵּלcurse וְנָשִׂיאruler בְעַמְּךָyour people לֹאnot תָאֹרcurse

You must not blaspheme God or curse the ruler of your people.

22:29

מְלֵאָתְךָhold וְדִמְעֲךָyour granaries or לֹאnot תְאַחֵרvats בְּכוֹרfirstborn בָּנֶיךָyour sons תִּתֶּןgive לִּיto me

You must not hold back offerings from your granaries or vats. You are to give Me the firstborn of your sons.

22:30

כֵּןso תַּעֲשֶׂהdo לְשֹׁרְךָwith your cattle לְצֹאנֶךָand your sheep שִׁבְעַתseven יָמִיםdays יִהְיֶהstay עִםwith אִמּוֹmothers בַּיּוֹםon the day הַשְּׁמִינִיeighth תִּתְּנוֹgive them לִיto me

You shall do likewise with your cattle and your sheep. Let them stay with their mothers for seven days, but on the eighth day you are to give them to Me.

22:31

וְאַנְשֵׁיpeople קֹדֶשׁholy תִּהְיוּןbe לִיto my וּבָשָׂרmeat בַּשָּׂדֶהin the field טְרֵפָהmauled animal found לֹאnot תֹאכֵלוּeat לַכֶּלֶבto the dogs תַּשְׁלִכוּןthrow אֹתוֹhim

You are to be My holy people. You must not eat the meat of a mauled animal found in the field; you are to throw it to the dogs.
