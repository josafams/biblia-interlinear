Exodus 33

Moses intercedes and asks to see the glory

Then the LORD said to Moses, “Leave this place, you and the people you brought up out of the land of Egypt, and go to the land that I promised to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob when I said, ‘I will give it to your descendants.’

And I will send an angel before you, and I will drive out the Canaanites, Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.

Go up to a land flowing with milk and honey. But I will not go with you, because you are a stiff-necked people; otherwise, I might destroy you on the way.”

33:4

וַיִּשְׁמַעtidings הָעָםthe people אֶת[obj] הַדָּבָרheard הָרָעthese bad הַזֶּהthis וַיִּתְאַבָּלוּmourning וְלֹאand no שָׁתוּput אִישׁone עֶדְיוֹjewelry עָלָיוon

When the people heard these bad tidings, they went into mourning, and no one put on any of his jewelry.

33:5

וַיֹּאמֶרhad said יְהוָהLORD אֶלto מֹשֶׁהMoses אֱמֹרtell אֶלto בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites אַתֶּםyou עַםpeople קְשֵׁהnecked עֹרֶףstiff רֶגַעmoment אֶחָדsingle אֶעֱלֶהgo בְקִרְבְּךָin your among וְכִלִּיתִיךָI would destroy you וְעַתָּהnow הוֹרֵדtake עֶדְיְךָyour jewelry מֵעָלֶיךָfrom the you וְאֵדְעָהand I will decide מָהwhat אֶעֱשֶׂהdo לָּךְto

For the LORD had said to Moses, “Tell the Israelites, ‘You are a stiff-necked people. If I should go with you for a single moment, I would destroy you. Now take off your jewelry, and I will decide what to do with you.’”

33:6

וַיִּתְנַצְּלוּand deliver בְנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites אֶת[obj] עֶדְיָםstripped themselves of their jewelry מֵהַרfrom Mount חוֹרֵבHoreb onward

So the Israelites stripped themselves of their jewelry from Mount Horeb onward.

Now Moses used to take the tent and pitch it at a distance outside the camp. He called it the Tent of Meeting, and anyone inquiring of the LORD would go to the Tent of Meeting outside the camp.

33:8

וְהָיָהthen whenever כְּצֵאתwent מֹשֶׁהMoses אֶלto הָאֹהֶלthe tent יָקוּמוּrise כָּלall הָעָםthe people וְנִצְּבוּwould stand אִישׁman פֶּתַחat entrances אָהֳלוֹtheir own tents וְהִבִּיטוּand watch אַחֲרֵיafter מֹשֶׁהMoses עַדuntil בֹּאוֹentered הָאֹהֱלָהthe tent

Then, whenever Moses went out to the tent, all the people would stand at the entrances to their own tents and watch Moses until he entered the tent.

33:9

וְהָיָהwould come כְּבֹאas entered מֹשֶׁהMoses הָאֹהֱלָהthe tent יֵרֵדgo down עַמּוּדpillar הֶעָנָןcloud וְעָמַדand remain פֶּתַחat the entrance הָאֹהֶלthe LORD וְדִבֶּרwould speak עִםwith מֹשֶׁהMoses

As Moses entered the tent, the pillar of cloud would come down and remain at the entrance, and the LORD would speak with Moses.

33:10

וְרָאָהsaw כָלall הָעָםpeople the אֶת[obj] עַמּוּדpillar הֶעָנָןcloud עֹמֵדstanding פֶּתַחat entrance הָאֹהֶלthe tent וְקָםthey would stand כָּלall הָעָםown וְהִשְׁתַּחֲוּוּand worship אִישׁeach פֶּתַחentrance אָהֳלוֹhis tent

When all the people saw the pillar of cloud standing at the entrance to the tent, they would stand up and worship, each one at the entrance to his own tent.

33:11

וְדִבֶּרwould speak יְהוָהLORD אֶלto מֹשֶׁהMoses פָּנִיםface אֶלto פָּנִיםface כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had יְדַבֵּרspeaks אִישׁman אֶלto רֵעֵהוּhis friend Moses וְשָׁבthen would return אֶלto הַמַּחֲנֶהthe camp וּמְשָׁרְתוֹbut his assistant יְהוֹשֻׁעַJoshua בִּןson נוּןNun נַעַרyoung לֹאnot יָמִישׁleave מִתּוֹךְfrom among הָאֹהֶלthe tent

Thus the LORD would speak to Moses face to face, as a man speaks to his friend. Then Moses would return to the camp, but his young assistant Joshua son of Nun would not leave the tent.

33:12

וַיֹּאמֶרthen said מֹשֶׁהMoses אֶלto יְהוָהLORD רְאֵהlook אַתָּהyou אֹמֵרhave אֵלַיto me הַעַלlead אֶת[obj] הָעָםthis people הַזֶּהthis וְאַתָּהbut you לֹאnot הוֹדַעְתַּנִיme know אֵת[obj] אֲשֶׁרwhom תִּשְׁלַחsend עִמִּיwith me וְאַתָּהyet you אָמַרְתָּsaid יְדַעְתִּיךָknow you בְשֵׁםby name וְגַםand you have מָצָאתָfound חֵןfavor בְּעֵינָיin my sight

Then Moses said to the LORD, “Look, You have been telling me, ‘Lead this people up,’ but You have not let me know whom You will send with me. Yet You have said, ‘I know you by name, and you have found favor in My sight.’

33:13

וְעַתָּהnow אִםif נָאI מָצָאתִיfound חֵןfavor בְּעֵינֶיךָin your sight הוֹדִעֵנִיme know נָאplease אֶת[obj] דְּרָכֶךָyour ways וְאֵדָעֲךָI know you לְמַעַןmay אֶמְצָאfind חֵןfavor בְּעֵינֶיךָin your sight וּרְאֵהremember כִּיthat עַמְּךָyour people הַגּוֹיthis nation הַזֶּהis

Now if indeed I have found favor in Your sight, please let me know Your ways, that I may know You and find favor in Your sight. Remember that this nation is Your people.”

33:14

And the LORD answered, “My Presence will go with you, and I will give you rest.”

“If Your Presence does not go with us,” Moses replied, “do not lead us up from here.

For how then can it be known that Your people and I have found favor in Your sight, unless You go with us? How else will we be distinguished from all the other people on the face of the earth?”

33:17

So the LORD said to Moses, “I will do this very thing you have asked, for you have found favor in My sight, and I know you by name.”

Then Moses said, “Please show me Your glory.”

33:19

וַיֹּאמֶרreplied אֲנִיI אַעֲבִירpass כָּלall טוּבִיmy goodness עַלto פָּנֶיךָbefore you וְקָרָאתִיproclaim בְשֵׁםname יְהוָהLORD לְפָנֶיךָin your presence וְחַנֹּתִיwill have mercy אֶת[obj] אֲשֶׁרwhom אָחֹןmercy וְרִחַמְתִּיcompassion אֶת[obj] אֲשֶׁרwhom אֲרַחֵםon

“I will cause all My goodness to pass before you,” the LORD replied, “and I will proclaim My name—the LORD—in your presence. I will have mercy on whom I have mercy, and I will have compassion on whom I have compassion.”

33:20

וַיֹּאמֶרbut he added לֹאnot תוּכַלcannot לִרְאֹתsee אֶת[obj] פָּנָיmy face כִּיfor לֹאno יִרְאַנִיsee me הָאָדָםthe man וָחָיand live

But He added, “You cannot see My face, for no one can see Me and live.”

The LORD continued, “There is a place near Me where you are to stand upon a rock,

33:22

וְהָיָהand when בַּעֲבֹרpasses כְּבֹדִיmy glory וְשַׂמְתִּיךָwill put you בְּנִקְרַתin a cleft הַצּוּרthe rock וְשַׂכֹּתִיand cover כַפִּיmy hand עָלֶיךָon you עַדuntil עָבְרִיI have passed

and when My glory passes by, I will put you in a cleft of the rock and cover you with My hand until I have passed by.

33:23

Then I will take My hand away, and you will see My back; but My face must not be seen.”
