Exodus 20

The Ten Commandments

20:1

וַיְדַבֵּרand spoke אֱלֹהִיםGod אֵת[obj] כָּלall הַדְּבָרִיםthese words הָאֵלֶּהthese לֵאמֹרto saying

And God spoke all these words:

20:2

“I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery.

20:3

לֹאnot יִהְיֶהshall לְךָto you אֱלֹהִיםgods אֲחֵרִיםother gods עַלon פָּנָיַbefore me

You shall have no other gods before Me.

20:4

לֹאnot תַעֲשֶׂהmake לְךָfor yourself פֶסֶלidol וְכָלanything תְּמוּנָהform אֲשֶׁרthat בַּשָּׁמַיִםin the heavens מִמַּעַלabove וַאֲשֶׁרand what בָּאָרֶץon the earth מִתָּחַתbelow וַאֲשֶׁרand what בַּמַּיִםin the waters מִתַּחַתbeneath לָאָרֶץto the ground

You shall not make for yourself an idol in the form of anything in the heavens above, on the earth below, or in the waters beneath.

20:5

לֹאnot תִשְׁתַּחֲוֶהbow down לָהֶםto them וְלֹאor תָעָבְדֵםworship them כִּיfor אָנֹכִיI יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God אֵלGod קַנָּאjealous visiting פֹּקֵדvisiting עֲוֺןiniquity אָבֹתfathers עַלon בָּנִיםchildren עַלto שִׁלֵּשִׁיםthird וְעַלand on רִבֵּעִיםfourth לְשֹׂנְאָיthose who hate me

You shall not bow down to them or worship them; for I, the LORD your God, am a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers on their children to the third and fourth generations of those who hate Me,

20:6

וְעֹשֶׂהbut showing חֶסֶדloving devotion לַאֲלָפִיםto a thousand generations לְאֹהֲבַיthose who love me וּלְשֹׁמְרֵיand keep מִצְוֺתָיmy commandments

but showing loving devotion to a thousand generations of those who love Me and keep My commandments.

20:7

לֹאnot תִשָּׂאtake אֶת[obj] שֵׁםname יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God לַשָּׁוְאin vain כִּיfor לֹאnot יְנַקֶּהleave unpunished יְהוָהLORD אֵת[obj] אֲשֶׁרwho יִשָּׂאtakes אֶת[obj] שְׁמוֹhis name לַשָּׁוְאin vain

You shall not take the name of the LORD your God in vain, for the LORD will not leave anyone unpunished who takes His name in vain.

20:8

זָכוֹרremember אֶת[obj] יוֹםday הַשַּׁבָּתthe Sabbath לְקַדְּשׁוֹby keeping it holy

Remember the Sabbath day by keeping it holy.

20:9

שֵׁשֶׁתsix יָמִיםdays תַּעֲבֹדlabor וְעָשִׂיתָand do כָּלall מְלַאכְתֶּךָyour work

Six days you shall labor and do all your work,

20:10

וְיוֹםday הַשְּׁבִיעִיthe seventh שַׁבָּתSabbath לַיהוָהto the LORD אֱלֹהֶיךָGod לֹאnot תַעֲשֶׂהdo כָלany מְלָאכָהwork אַתָּהyou וּבִנְךָnor your son וּבִתֶּךָor daughter עַבְדְּךָyour manservant וַאֲמָתְךָor maidservant or וּבְהֶמְתֶּךָor livestock וְגֵרְךָnor the foreigner אֲשֶׁרthat בִּשְׁעָרֶיךָwithin your gates

but the seventh day is a Sabbath to the LORD your God, on which you must not do any work—neither you, nor your son or daughter, nor your manservant or maidservant or livestock, nor the foreigner within your gates.

20:11

כִּיfor שֵׁשֶׁתsix יָמִיםdays עָשָׂהmade יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] הַשָּׁמַיִםthe heavens וְאֶתand [obj] הָאָרֶץthe earth אֶת[obj] הַיָּםthe sea וְאֶתand [obj] כָּלall אֲשֶׁרthat בָּםin them וַיָּנַחand rest בַּיּוֹםon the day הַשְּׁבִיעִיseventh עַלon כֵּןtherefore בֵּרַךְblessed יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] יוֹםday הַשַּׁבָּתthe Sabbath וַיְקַדְּשֵׁהוּand set it apart as holy

For in six days the LORD made the heavens and the earth and the sea and all that is in them, but on the seventh day He rested. Therefore the LORD blessed the Sabbath day and set it apart as holy.

20:12

כַּבֵּדhonor אֶת[obj] אָבִיךָyour father וְאֶתand [obj] אִמֶּךָmother לְמַעַןmay יַאֲרִכוּןlong יָמֶיךָdays עַלin הָאֲדָמָהthe land אֲשֶׁרthat יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God נֹתֵןgiving לָךְto you

Honor your father and mother, so that your days may be long in the land that the LORD your God is giving you.

20:13

לֹאnot תִּרְצָחmurder

You shall not murder.

20:14

לֹאnot תִּנְאָףcommit adultery

You shall not commit adultery.

20:15

לֹאnot תִּגְנֹבsteal

You shall not steal.

20:16

לֹאnot תַעֲנֶהbear בְרֵעֲךָagainst your neighbor עֵדwitness שָׁקֶרfalse

You shall not bear false witness against your neighbor.

20:17

לֹאnot תַחְמֹדcovet בֵּיתhouse רֵעֶךָyour neighbor’s לֹאnot תַחְמֹדcovet אֵשֶׁתwife רֵעֶךָyour neighbor’s וְעַבְדּוֹor his manservant וַאֲמָתוֹor maidservant or his וְשׁוֹרוֹox וַחֲמֹרוֹor donkey וְכֹלor anything אֲשֶׁרthat לְרֵעֶךָbelongs to your neighbor

You shall not covet your neighbor’s house. You shall not covet your neighbor’s wife, or his manservant or maidservant, or his ox or donkey, or anything that belongs to your neighbor.”

20:18

וְכָלwhen all הָעָםthe people רֹאִיםwitnessed אֶת[obj] הַקּוֹלֹתthe thunder וְאֶתand [obj] הַלַּפִּידִםlightning וְאֵתand [obj] קוֹלsounding הַשֹּׁפָרthe ram’s horn וְאֶתand [obj] הָהָרthe mountain עָשֵׁןenveloped smoke וַיַּרְאin smoke הָעָםthe people וַיָּנֻעוּthey trembled וַיַּעַמְדוּand stood מֵרָחֹקat a distance

When all the people witnessed the thunder and lightning, the sounding of the ram’s horn, and the mountain enveloped in smoke, they trembled and stood at a distance.

20:19

וַיֹּאמְרוּthey said אֶלto מֹשֶׁהMoses דַּבֵּרspeak אַתָּהyou עִמָּנוּto וְנִשְׁמָעָהlisten וְאַלbut do not יְדַבֵּרspeak עִמָּנוּto us אֱלֹהִיםGod פֶּןor נָמוּתdie

“Speak to us yourself and we will listen,” they said to Moses. “But do not let God speak to us, or we will die.”

20:20

וַיֹּאמֶרreplied מֹשֶׁהMoses אֶלto הָעָםthe people אַלdo not תִּירָאוּafraid כִּיfor לְבַעֲבוּרto in that נַסּוֹתtest אֶתְכֶםyou בָּאcome הָאֱלֹהִיםGod וּבַעֲבוּרand in that תִּהְיֶהbe יִרְאָתוֹfear of him עַלon פְּנֵיכֶםbefore you לְבִלְתִּיto not תֶחֱטָאוּsinning

“Do not be afraid,” Moses replied. “For God has come to test you, so that the fear of Him may be before you, to keep you from sinning.”

20:21

וַיַּעֲמֹדstood הָעָםthe people מֵרָחֹקat a distance וּמֹשֶׁהas Moses נִגַּשׁapproached אֶלto הָעֲרָפֶלthe thick darkness אֲשֶׁרthat שָׁםwhere הָאֱלֹהִיםGod

And the people stood at a distance as Moses approached the thick darkness where God was.

20:22

וַיֹּאמֶרsaid יְהוָהLORD אֶלto מֹשֶׁהMoses כֹּהthis תֹאמַרis what אֶלto בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites אַתֶּםyou רְאִיתֶםseen כִּיfor מִןfrom הַשָּׁמַיִםheaven דִּבַּרְתִּיspoken עִמָּכֶםto you

Then the LORD said to Moses, “This is what you are to tell the Israelites: ‘You have seen for yourselves that I have spoken to you from heaven.

20:23

לֹאnot תַעֲשׂוּןmake אִתִּיwith me אֱלֹהֵיgods כֶסֶףsilver וֵאלֹהֵיgods זָהָבgold לֹאnot תַעֲשׂוּmake לָכֶםfor yourselves

You are not to make any gods alongside Me; you are not to make for yourselves gods of silver or gold.

20:24

מִזְבַּחaltar אֲדָמָהearth תַּעֲשֶׂהmake לִּיfor me וְזָבַחְתָּand sacrifice עָלָיוon it אֶת[obj] עֹלֹתֶיךָyour burnt offerings וְאֶתand [obj] שְׁלָמֶיךָpeace offerings אֶת[obj] צֹאנְךָyour sheep וְאֶתand [obj] בְּקָרֶךָcattle בְּכָלin every הַמָּקוֹםplace אֲשֶׁרthat אַזְכִּירremembered אֶת[obj] שְׁמִיmy name אָבוֹאcome אֵלֶיךָto you וּבֵרַכְתִּיךָand bless you

You are to make for Me an altar of earth, and sacrifice on it your burnt offerings and peace offerings, your sheep and goats and cattle. In every place where I cause My name to be remembered, I will come to you and bless you.

20:25

וְאִםnow if מִזְבַּחaltar אֲבָנִיםstones תַּעֲשֶׂהdo לִּיfor me לֹאnot תִבְנֶהbuild אֶתְהֶןthem גָּזִיתstones כִּיfor חַרְבְּךָif you הֵנַפְתָּuse a chisel עָלֶיהָon it וַתְּחַלְלֶהָwill defile it

Now if you make an altar of stones for Me, you must not build it with stones shaped by tools; for if you use a chisel on it, you will defile it.

20:26

וְלֹאnot תַעֲלֶהgo בְמַעֲלֹתon steps עַלto מִזְבְּחִיmy altar אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot תִגָּלֶהexposed עֶרְוָתְךָyour nakedness עָלָיוon it

And you must not go up to My altar on steps, lest your nakedness be exposed on it.’
