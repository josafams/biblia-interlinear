Exodus 5

Pharaoh refuses and increases the forced labor

After that, Moses and Aaron went to Pharaoh and said, “This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: ‘Let My people go, so that they may hold a feast to Me in the wilderness.’”

But Pharaoh replied, “Who is the LORD that I should obey His voice and let Israel go? I do not know the LORD, and I will not let Israel go.”

“The God of the Hebrews has met with us,” they answered. “Please let us go on a three-day journey into the wilderness to sacrifice to the LORD our God, or He may strike us with plagues or with the sword.”

5:4

וַיֹּאמֶרsaid אֲלֵהֶםto them מֶלֶךְking מִצְרַיִםEgypt לָמָּהwhy מֹשֶׁהMoses וְאַהֲרֹןand Aaron תַּפְרִיעוּdraw אֶת[obj] הָעָםthe people מִמַּעֲשָׂיוfrom their work לְכוּget לְסִבְלֹתֵיכֶםto your labor

But the king of Egypt said to them, “Moses and Aaron, why do you draw the people away from their work? Get back to your labor!”

5:5

וַיֹּאמֶרalso said פַּרְעֹהPharaoh הֵןlook רַבִּיםnumerous עַתָּהnow עַםpeople הָאָרֶץthe land וְהִשְׁבַּתֶּםstopping אֹתָםthem מִסִּבְלֹתָםfrom their labor

Pharaoh also said, “Look, the people of the land are now numerous, and you would be stopping them from their labor.”

5:6

That same day Pharaoh commanded the taskmasters of the people and their foremen:

“You shall no longer supply the people with straw for making bricks. They must go and gather their own straw.

But require of them the same quota of bricks as before; do not reduce it. For they are lazy; that is why they are crying out, ‘Let us go and sacrifice to our God.’

5:9

תִּכְבַּדmake הָעֲבֹדָהthe work עַלon הָאֲנָשִׁיםthe men וְיַעֲשׂוּand do בָהּin her וְאַלand pay no יִשְׁעוּattention בְּדִבְרֵיto these שָׁקֶרlies

Make the work harder on the men so they will be occupied and pay no attention to these lies.”

5:10

וַיֵּצְאוּwent נֹגְשֵׂיtaskmasters הָעָםthe people וְשֹׁטְרָיוand foremen וַיֹּאמְרוּand said אֶלto הָעָםthem לֵאמֹרis כֹּהthis אָמַרwhat says פַּרְעֹהPharaoh אֵינֶנִּיam no נֹתֵןgiving לָכֶםto you תֶּבֶןstraw

So the taskmasters and foremen of the people went out and said to them, “This is what Pharaoh says: ‘I am no longer giving you straw.

5:11

אַתֶּםyou לְכוּgo קְחוּget לָכֶםto you תֶּבֶןstraw מֵאֲשֶׁרfrom had תִּמְצָאוּfind כִּיbut אֵיןno נִגְרָעreduced מֵעֲבֹדַתְכֶםyour workload will in דָּבָרword

Go and get your own straw wherever you can find it; but your workload will in no way be reduced.’”

5:12

וַיָּפֶץscattered הָעָםthe people בְּכָלall over אֶרֶץland מִצְרָיִםEgypt לְקֹשֵׁשׁto gather קַשׁstubble לַתֶּבֶןfor straw

So the people scattered all over the land of Egypt to gather stubble for straw.

5:13

וְהַנֹּגְשִׂיםthe taskmasters אָצִיםpressing לֵאמֹרthem saying כַּלּוּfulfill מַעֲשֵׂיכֶםyour works דְּבַרquota each יוֹםday בְּיוֹמוֹin his day כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had בִּהְיוֹתin was הַתֶּבֶןstraw was provided

The taskmasters kept pressing them, saying, “Fulfill your quota each day, just as you did when straw was provided.”

5:14

וַיֻּכּוּthen Israelite שֹׁטְרֵיforemen בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelite אֲשֶׁרhad שָׂמוּset עֲלֵהֶםover the people נֹגְשֵׂיtaskmasters פַרְעֹהPharaoh’s לֵאמֹרand asked מַדּוּעַwhy לֹאnot כִלִּיתֶםfulfilled חָקְכֶםfulfilled your quota לִלְבֹּןto white כִּתְמוֹלyesterday שִׁלְשֹׁםbricks גַּםeven תְּמוֹלyesterday גַּםeven הַיּוֹםtoday

Then the Israelite foremen, whom Pharaoh’s taskmasters had set over the people, were beaten and asked, “Why have you not fulfilled your quota of bricks yesterday or today, as you did before?”

5:15

וַיָּבֹאוּwent שֹׁטְרֵיforemen בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelite וַיִּצְעֲקוּand appealed אֶלto פַּרְעֹהPharaoh לֵאמֹרto saying לָמָּהwhy תַעֲשֶׂהdo כֹהthis לַעֲבָדֶיךָyour servants

So the Israelite foremen went and appealed to Pharaoh: “Why are you treating your servants this way?

5:16

תֶּבֶןstraw אֵיןno נִתָּןgiven לַעֲבָדֶיךָto your servants וּלְבֵנִיםbricks אֹמְרִיםtold לָנוּto us עֲשׂוּmake וְהִנֵּהlook עֲבָדֶיךָyour servants מֻכִּיםbeaten וְחָטָאתown עַמֶּךָyour people

No straw has been given to your servants, yet we are told, ‘Make bricks!’ Look, your servants are being beaten, but the fault is with your own people.”

5:17

וַיֹּאמֶרPharaoh replied נִרְפִּיםslackers אַתֶּםyou נִרְפִּיםslackers עַלon כֵּןso אַתֶּםyou אֹמְרִיםsaying נֵלְכָהgo נִזְבְּחָהsacrifice לַיהוָהto the LORD

“You are slackers!” Pharaoh replied. “Slackers! That is why you keep saying, ‘Let us go and sacrifice to the LORD.’

5:18

וְעַתָּהnow לְכוּget עִבְדוּwork וְתֶבֶןstraw לֹאno יִנָּתֵןgiven לָכֶםto you וְתֹכֶןquota לְבֵנִיםbricks תִּתֵּנּוּdeliver

Now get to work. You will be given no straw, yet you must deliver the full quota of bricks.”

5:19

וַיִּרְאוּrealized שֹׁטְרֵיforemen בְנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelite אֹתָםthem בְּרָעin trouble לֵאמֹרtold לֹאnot תִגְרְעוּreduce מִלִּבְנֵיכֶםof bricks דְּבַרquota יוֹםdaily בְּיוֹמוֹin his day

The Israelite foremen realized they were in trouble when they were told, “You must not reduce your daily quota of bricks.”

5:20

וַיִּפְגְּעוּthey confronted אֶת[obj] מֹשֶׁהMoses וְאֶתand [obj] אַהֲרֹןAaron נִצָּבִיםstood לִקְרָאתָםwaiting to meet them בְּצֵאתָםin went them מֵאֵתfrom the פַּרְעֹהPharaoh

When they left Pharaoh, they confronted Moses and Aaron, who stood waiting to meet them.

5:21

וַיֹּאמְרוּand said אֲלֵהֶםto them יֵרֶאlook יְהוָהLORD עֲלֵיכֶםupon you וְיִשְׁפֹּטand judge אֲשֶׁרyou הִבְאַשְׁתֶּםstench אֶת[obj] רֵיחֵנוּmade us a בְּעֵינֵיbefore פַרְעֹהPharaoh וּבְעֵינֵיand his עֲבָדָיוhis officials לָתֶתplaced חֶרֶבsword בְּיָדָםin their hand לְהָרְגֵנוּto kill us

“May the LORD look upon you and judge you,” the foremen said, “for you have made us a stench before Pharaoh and his officials; you have placed in their hand a sword to kill us!”

5:22

וַיָּשָׁבso returned מֹשֶׁהMoses אֶלto יְהוָהLORD וַיֹּאמַרand asked אֲדֹנָיLord לָמָהwhy הֲרֵעֹתָהtrouble לָעָםupon this people הַזֶּהis this לָמָּהwhy זֶּהyou שְׁלַחְתָּנִיsent me

So Moses returned to the LORD and asked, “Lord, why have You brought trouble upon this people? Is this why You sent me?

5:23

וּמֵאָזand from then בָּאתִיwent אֶלto פַּרְעֹהPharaoh לְדַבֵּרto speak בִּשְׁמֶךָin your name הֵרַעtrouble לָעָםon this people הַזֶּהyou וְהַצֵּלand surely לֹאnot הִצַּלְתָּdelivered אֶת[obj] עַמֶּךָyour people

Ever since I went to Pharaoh to speak in Your name, he has brought trouble on this people, and You have not delivered Your people in any way.”
