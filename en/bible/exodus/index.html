Exodus שְׁמוֹת

Exodus tells how Jacob's descendants, already numerous in Egypt, pass from slavery to freedom and become a people bound to God by a covenant. Chapters 1 to 15 narrate the oppression under Pharaoh, the call of Moses at the bush that burned without being consumed, the plagues on Egypt, the first Passover and the crossing of the sea. After a march through the wilderness, the people reach Mount Sinai, where they hear the Ten Commandments, receive a body of laws and commit themselves to the covenant (chs. 19–24). The last part revolves around the tabernacle, the portable sanctuary that would mark God's presence among Israel: the instructions for making it, the crisis of the golden calf and the carrying out of the work, which ends with the glory of the LORD filling the tent.

Structure

  1. Oppression and call chs. 1–4

    Israel grows and is enslaved; Moses is born, raised at court, flees to Midian and is called by God at Mount Horeb.

  2. The confrontation with Pharaoh chs. 5–11

    Moses and Aaron ask for the people's release; in the face of Pharaoh's refusals, nine plagues strike Egypt and the tenth is announced.

  3. Passover and deliverance chs. 12–15

    The first Passover, the death of Egypt's firstborn, the departure, the crossing of the sea and the song of victory (15:1–21).

  4. Toward Sinai chs. 15–18

    Thirst, hunger and conflicts in the wilderness: the waters of Marah, the manna, water from the rock, the battle against Amalek and Jethro's visit.

  5. The covenant at Sinai chs. 19–24

    God reveals Himself on the mountain, gives the Ten Commandments and the laws of the so-called Book of the Covenant (20:22–23:33), and the covenant is confirmed with blood (24:8).

  6. Instructions for the tabernacle chs. 25–31

    On the mountain, Moses receives the pattern of the sanctuary, its furnishings and the priestly garments, as well as the rules for consecrating the priests.

  7. The golden calf and the covenant renewed chs. 32–34

    While Moses is on the mountain, the people worship a golden calf; Moses intercedes, goes up again with new tablets and the covenant is renewed.

  8. The building of the tabernacle chs. 35–40

    The people's offerings, the work of Bezalel and Oholiab and the setting up of the sanctuary, on which the cloud descends.
