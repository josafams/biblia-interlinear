Exodus 6

The promise renewed and the family of Moses

6:1

וַיֹּאמֶרsaid יְהוָהLORD אֶלto מֹשֶׁהMoses עַתָּהnow תִרְאֶהsee אֲשֶׁרthat אֶעֱשֶׂהdo לְפַרְעֹהto Pharaoh כִּיfor בְיָדhand חֲזָקָהmighty יְשַׁלְּחֵםlet the people וּבְיָדhand חֲזָקָהstrong יְגָרְשֵׁםdrive them מֵאַרְצוֹout of his land

But the LORD said to Moses, “Now you will see what I will do to Pharaoh, for because of My mighty hand he will let the people go; because of My strong hand he will drive them out of his land.”

6:2

God also told Moses, “I am the LORD.

6:3

I appeared to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob as God Almighty, but by My name the LORD I did not make Myself known to them.

6:4

וְגַםI also הֲקִמֹתִיestablished אֶת[obj] בְּרִיתִיmy covenant אִתָּםwith them לָתֵתto give לָהֶםto them אֶת[obj] אֶרֶץland כְּנָעַןCanaan אֵת[obj] אֶרֶץland מְגֻרֵיהֶםwhere they lived אֲשֶׁרthat גָּרוּforeigners בָהּin her

I also established My covenant with them to give them the land of Canaan, the land where they lived as foreigners.

6:5

וְגַםand also אֲנִיI שָׁמַעְתִּיheard אֶת[obj] נַאֲקַתgroaning בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites אֲשֶׁרwhom מִצְרַיִםEgyptians מַעֲבִדִיםenslaving אֹתָםthem וָאֶזְכֹּרhave remembered אֶת[obj] בְּרִיתִיmy covenant

Furthermore, I have heard the groaning of the Israelites, whom the Egyptians are enslaving, and I have remembered My covenant.

6:6

לָכֵןtherefore אֱמֹרtell לִבְנֵיto sons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD וְהוֹצֵאתִיbring אֶתְכֶםyou מִתַּחַתfrom under סִבְלֹתyoke מִצְרַיִםEgyptians וְהִצַּלְתִּיand deliver אֶתְכֶםyou מֵעֲבֹדָתָםfrom their bondage וְגָאַלְתִּיwill redeem אֶתְכֶםyou בִּזְרוֹעַarm נְטוּיָהoutstretched וּבִשְׁפָטִיםjudgment גְּדֹלִיםmighty

Therefore tell the Israelites: ‘I am the LORD, and I will bring you out from under the yoke of the Egyptians and deliver you from their bondage. I will redeem you with an outstretched arm and with mighty acts of judgment.

6:7

וְלָקַחְתִּיwill take אֶתְכֶםyou לִיto me לְעָםown people וְהָיִיתִיbe לָכֶםto you לֵאלֹהִיםGod וִידַעְתֶּםknow כִּיthat אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיכֶםyour God הַמּוֹצִיאwho brought אֶתְכֶםyou מִתַּחַתfrom under סִבְלוֹתyoke מִצְרָיִםEgyptians

I will take you as My own people, and I will be your God. Then you will know that I am the LORD your God, who brought you out from under the yoke of the Egyptians.

6:8

וְהֵבֵאתִיbring אֶתְכֶםyou אֶלinto הָאָרֶץthe land אֲשֶׁרthat נָשָׂאתִיswore אֶת[obj] יָדִיmy hand לָתֵתto give אֹתָהּher לְאַבְרָהָםto Abraham לְיִצְחָקIsaac וּלְיַעֲקֹבJacob וְנָתַתִּיwill give אֹתָהּher לָכֶםto you מוֹרָשָׁהpossession אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD

And I will bring you into the land that I swore to give to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. I will give it to you as a possession. I am the LORD!’”

6:9

וַיְדַבֵּרrelayed מֹשֶׁהMoses כֵּןthis אֶלto בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites וְלֹאnot שָׁמְעוּlisten אֶלto מֹשֶׁהhim מִקֹּצֶרon account רוּחַspirit וּמֵעֲבֹדָהand bondage קָשָׁהcruel

Moses relayed this message to the Israelites, but on account of their broken spirit and cruel bondage, they did not listen to him.

6:10

וַיְדַבֵּרso the said יְהוָהLORD אֶלto מֹשֶׁהMoses לֵּאמֹרto saying

So the LORD said to Moses,

6:11

בֹּאgo דַבֵּרtell אֶלto פַּרְעֹהPharaoh מֶלֶךְking מִצְרָיִםEgypt וִישַׁלַּחto let אֶת[obj] בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites מֵאַרְצוֹout of his land

“Go and tell Pharaoh king of Egypt to let the Israelites go out of his land.”

6:12

וַיְדַבֵּרand spoke מֹשֶׁהMoses לִפְנֵיpresence יְהוָהLORD’s לֵאמֹרreplied הֵןif בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites לֹאnot שָׁמְעוּlisten אֵלַיto me וְאֵיךְthen why יִשְׁמָעֵנִיwould listen to me פַרְעֹהPharaoh וַאֲנִיI am עֲרַלunskilled שְׂפָתָיִםspeech

But in the LORD’s presence Moses replied, “If the Israelites will not listen to me, then why would Pharaoh listen to me, since I am unskilled in speech?”

6:13

וַיְדַבֵּרspoke יְהוָהLORD אֶלto מֹשֶׁהMoses וְאֶלand to אַהֲרֹןAaron וַיְצַוֵּםand gave them אֶלto בְּנֵיboth the יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites וְאֶלand to פַּרְעֹהPharaoh מֶלֶךְking מִצְרָיִםEgypt לְהוֹצִיאto bring אֶת[obj] בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites מֵאֶרֶץland מִצְרָיִםEgypt

Then the LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron and gave them a charge concerning both the Israelites and Pharaoh king of Egypt, to bring the Israelites out of the land of Egypt.

6:14

אֵלֶּהthese רָאשֵׁיheads בֵיתhouses אֲבֹתָםtheir fathers בְּנֵיsons רְאוּבֵןReuben בְּכֹרfirstborn יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael חֲנוֹךְHanoch וּפַלּוּאand Pallu חֶצְרוֹןHezron וְכַרְמִיand Carmi אֵלֶּהthese מִשְׁפְּחֹתclans רְאוּבֵןReuben

These were the heads of their fathers’ houses: The sons of Reuben, the firstborn of Israel, were Hanoch and Pallu, Hezron and Carmi. These were the clans of Reuben.

6:15

וּבְנֵיsons שִׁמְעוֹןSimeon יְמוּאֵלJemuel וְיָמִיןJamin וְאֹהַדOhad וְיָכִיןJachin וְצֹחַרZohar וְשָׁאוּלand Shaul בֶּןson הַכְּנַעֲנִיתa Canaanite אֵלֶּהthese מִשְׁפְּחֹתclans שִׁמְעוֹןSimeon

The sons of Simeon were Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul, the son of a Canaanite woman. These were the clans of Simeon.

6:16

וְאֵלֶּהthese שְׁמוֹתnames בְּנֵיsons לֵוִיLevi לְתֹלְדֹתָםaccording to their records גֵּרְשׁוֹןGershon וּקְהָתKohath וּמְרָרִיand Merari וּשְׁנֵיand years חַיֵּיlived לֵוִיLevi שֶׁבַעseven וּשְׁלֹשִׁים137 וּמְאַתand hundred שָׁנָהyears

These were the names of the sons of Levi according to their records: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. Levi lived 137 years.

6:17

בְּנֵיsons גֵרְשׁוֹןGershon לִבְנִיLibni וְשִׁמְעִיand Shimei לְמִשְׁפְּחֹתָםby their clans

The sons of Gershon were Libni and Shimei, by their clans.

6:18

וּבְנֵיsons קְהָתKohath עַמְרָםAmram וְיִצְהָרIzhar וְחֶבְרוֹןHebron וְעֻזִּיאֵלand Uzziel וּשְׁנֵיand years חַיֵּיlived קְהָתKohath שָׁלֹשׁlived וּשְׁלֹשִׁים133 וּמְאַתand hundred שָׁנָהyears

The sons of Kohath were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. Kohath lived 133 years.

6:19

וּבְנֵיsons מְרָרִיMerari מַחְלִיMahli וּמוּשִׁיand Mushi אֵלֶּהthese מִשְׁפְּחֹתclans הַלֵּוִיLevites לְתֹלְדֹתָםaccording to their records

The sons of Merari were Mahli and Mushi. These were the clans of the Levites according to their records.

6:20

וַיִּקַּחand married עַמְרָםAmram אֶת[obj] יוֹכֶבֶדJochebed דֹּדָתוֹhis sister לוֹto him לְאִשָּׁהshe וַתֵּלֶדand bore לוֹto him אֶת[obj] אַהֲרֹןAaron וְאֶתand [obj] מֹשֶׁהMoses וּשְׁנֵיand years חַיֵּיlived עַמְרָםAmram שֶׁבַעseven וּשְׁלֹשִׁים137 וּמְאַתand hundred שָׁנָהyears

And Amram married his father’s sister Jochebed, and she bore him Aaron and Moses. Amram lived 137 years.

6:21

וּבְנֵיsons יִצְהָרIzhar קֹרַחKorah וָנֶפֶגNepheg וְזִכְרִיand Zichri

The sons of Izhar were Korah, Nepheg, and Zichri.

6:22

וּבְנֵיsons עֻזִּיאֵלUzziel מִישָׁאֵלMishael וְאֶלְצָפָןElzaphan וְסִתְרִיand Sithri

The sons of Uzziel were Mishael, Elzaphan, and Sithri.

6:23

וַיִּקַּחand married אַהֲרֹןAaron אֶת[obj] אֱלִישֶׁבַעElisheba בַּתdaughter עַמִּינָדָבAmminadab אֲחוֹתsister נַחְשׁוֹןNahshon לוֹto him לְאִשָּׁהshe וַתֵּלֶדbore לוֹto him אֶת[obj] נָדָבNadab וְאֶתand [obj] אֲבִיהוּאAbihu אֶת[obj] אֶלְעָזָרEleazar וְאֶתand [obj] אִיתָמָרIthamar

And Aaron married Elisheba, the daughter of Amminadab and sister of Nahshon, and she bore him Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.

6:24

וּבְנֵיsons קֹרַחKorah אַסִּירAssir וְאֶלְקָנָהElkanah וַאֲבִיאָסָףand Abiasaph אֵלֶּהthese מִשְׁפְּחֹתclans הַקָּרְחִיof Korahites

The sons of Korah were Assir, Elkanah, and Abiasaph. These were the clans of the Korahites.

6:25

וְאֶלְעָזָרEleazar בֶּןson אַהֲרֹןAaron’s לָקַחmarried לוֹto him מִבְּנוֹתof the daughters פּוּטִיאֵלPutiel לוֹto him לְאִשָּׁהshe וַתֵּלֶדand bore לוֹto him אֶת[obj] פִּינְחָסPhinehas אֵלֶּהthese רָאשֵׁיheads אֲבוֹתfamilies הַלְוִיִּםthe Levite לְמִשְׁפְּחֹתָםby their clans

Aaron’s son Eleazar married one of the daughters of Putiel, and she bore him Phinehas. These were the heads of the Levite families by their clans.

6:26

It was this Aaron and Moses to whom the LORD said, “Bring the Israelites out of the land of Egypt by their divisions.”

6:27

הֵםwere הַמְדַבְּרִיםthe ones who spoke אֶלto פַּרְעֹהPharaoh מֶלֶךְking מִצְרַיִםEgypt לְהוֹצִיאto bring אֶת[obj] בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites מִמִּצְרָיִםout of Egypt הוּאhe מֹשֶׁהMoses וְאַהֲרֹןand Aaron

Moses and Aaron were the ones who spoke to Pharaoh king of Egypt in order to bring the Israelites out of Egypt.

6:28

Now on the day that the LORD spoke to Moses in Egypt,

But in the LORD’s presence Moses replied, “Since I am unskilled in speech, why would Pharaoh listen to me?”
