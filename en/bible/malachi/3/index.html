Malachi 3

The messenger of the covenant and the tithes

“Behold, I will send My messenger, who will prepare the way before Me. Then the Lord whom you seek will suddenly come to His temple—the Messenger of the covenant, in whom you delight—see, He is coming,” says the LORD of Hosts.

3:2

But who can endure the day of His coming? And who can stand when He appears? For He will be like a refiner’s fire, like a launderer’s soap.

3:3

וְיָשַׁבsit מְצָרֵףrefiner וּמְטַהֵרand purifier כֶּסֶףsilver וְטִהַרhe will purify אֶת[obj] בְּנֵיsons לֵוִיLevi וְזִקַּקand refine אֹתָםthem כַּזָּהָבlike gold וְכַכָּסֶףand silver וְהָיוּand be לַיהוָהto the LORD מַגִּישֵׁיpresent מִנְחָהofferings בִּצְדָקָהin righteousness

And He will sit as a refiner and purifier of silver; He will purify the sons of Levi and refine them like gold and silver. Then they will present offerings to the LORD in righteousness.

3:4

וְעָרְבָהplease לַיהוָהthe LORD מִנְחַתofferings יְהוּדָהJudah וִירוּשָׁלִָםand Jerusalem כִּימֵיas in days עוֹלָםold וּכְשָׁנִיםand years קַדְמֹנִיּוֹתgone

Then the offerings of Judah and Jerusalem will please the LORD, as in days of old and years gone by.

3:5

וְקָרַבְתִּיnear אֲלֵיכֶםto you לַמִּשְׁפָּטfor judgment וְהָיִיתִיbe עֵדa witness מְמַהֵרswift בַּמְכַשְּׁפִיםagainst sorcerers וּבַמְנָאֲפִיםand adulterers וּבַנִּשְׁבָּעִיםand perjurers against לַשָּׁקֶרto the falsely וּבְעֹשְׁקֵיdefraud שְׂכַרlaborers שָׂכִירoppressors אַלְמָנָהwidowed וְיָתוֹםand fatherless וּמַטֵּיand deny justice גֵרforeigner וְלֹאbut do not יְרֵאוּנִיfear me אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts

“Then I will draw near to you for judgment. And I will be a swift witness against sorcerers and adulterers and perjurers, against oppressors of the widowed and fatherless, and against those who defraud laborers of their wages and deny justice to the foreigner but do not fear Me,” says the LORD of Hosts.

3:6

כִּיbecause אֲנִיI יְהוָהLORD לֹאnot שָׁנִיתִיchange וְאַתֶּםyou בְּנֵיdescendants יַעֲקֹבJacob לֹאnot כְלִיתֶםdestroyed

“Because I, the LORD, do not change, you descendants of Jacob have not been destroyed.

3:7

לְמִימֵיfrom the days אֲבֹתֵיכֶםfathers you סַרְתֶּםturned מֵחֻקַּיfrom my statutes וְלֹאand have not שְׁמַרְתֶּםkept שׁוּבוּreturn אֵלַיto me וְאָשׁוּבָהreturn אֲלֵיכֶםto you אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts וַאֲמַרְתֶּםbut you ask בַּמֶּהhow נָשׁוּבreturn

Yet from the days of your fathers, you have turned away from My statutes and have not kept them. Return to Me, and I will return to you,” says the LORD of Hosts. “But you ask, ‘How can we return?’

3:8

הֲיִקְבַּעrob אָדָםman אֱלֹהִיםGod כִּיfor אַתֶּםyou קֹבְעִיםrobbing אֹתִיme וַאֲמַרְתֶּםbut you ask בַּמֶּהhow קְבַעֲנוּךָrob you הַמַּעֲשֵׂרin tithes וְהַתְּרוּמָהand offerings

Will a man rob God? Yet you are robbing Me! But you ask, ‘How do we rob You?’ In tithes and offerings.

3:9

בַּמְּאֵרָהwith a curse אַתֶּםyou נֵאָרִיםcursed וְאֹתִיand me אַתֶּםyou קֹבְעִיםrobbing הַגּוֹיnation are still כֻּלּוֹwhole

You are cursed with a curse, yet you—the whole nation—are still robbing Me.

3:10

הָבִיאוּbring אֶת[obj] כָּלfull הַמַּעֲשֵׂרthe tithe אֶלinto בֵּיתhouse הָאוֹצָרthe storehouse וִיהִיbe טֶרֶףfood בְּבֵיתִיin my house וּבְחָנוּנִיtest me נָאplease בָּזֹאתin this אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts אִםif לֹאnot אֶפְתַּחopen לָכֶםto you אֵת[obj] אֲרֻבּוֹתwindows הַשָּׁמַיִםof heaven וַהֲרִיקֹתִיand pour לָכֶםfor you בְּרָכָהblessing עַדuntil בְּלִיwithout דָיmeasure

Bring the full tithe into the storehouse, so that there may be food in My house. Test Me in this,” says the LORD of Hosts. “See if I will not open the windows of heaven and pour out for you blessing without measure.

3:11

וְגָעַרְתִּיI will rebuke the devourer לָכֶםfor you בָּאֹכֵלthat it וְלֹאwill not יַשְׁחִתdestroy לָכֶםto you אֶת[obj] פְּרִיproduce fruit הָאֲדָמָהland וְלֹאnot תְשַׁכֵּלfail לָכֶםto הַגֶּפֶןthe vine בַּשָּׂדֶהin field אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts

I will rebuke the devourer for you, so that it will not destroy the fruits of your land, and the vine in your field will not fail to produce fruit,” says the LORD of Hosts.

3:12

וְאִשְּׁרוּcall blessed אֶתְכֶםyou כָּלall הַגּוֹיִםthe nations כִּיfor תִהְיוּbe אַתֶּםyou אֶרֶץland חֵפֶץdelight אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts

“Then all the nations will call you blessed, for you will be a land of delight,” says the LORD of Hosts.

3:13

“Your words against Me have been harsh,” says the LORD. “Yet you ask, ‘What have we spoken against You?’

3:14

אֲמַרְתֶּםsaid שָׁוְאfutile עֲבֹדserve אֱלֹהִיםGod וּמַהwhat בֶּצַעgained כִּיfor שָׁמַרְנוּkeeping מִשְׁמַרְתּוֹhis requirements וְכִיand if הָלַכְנוּwalking קְדֹרַנִּיתmournfully מִפְּנֵיbefore יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts

You have said, ‘It is futile to serve God. What have we gained by keeping His requirements and walking mournfully before the LORD of Hosts?

3:15

וְעַתָּהso now אֲנַחְנוּwe מְאַשְּׁרִיםcall זֵדִיםarrogant גַּםeven נִבְנוּonly evildoers עֹשֵׂיdo רִשְׁעָהevildoers prosper גַּםeven בָּחֲנוּtest אֱלֹהִיםGod וַיִּמָּלֵטוּand escape

So now we call the arrogant blessed. Not only do evildoers prosper, they even test God and escape.’”

3:16

At that time those who feared the LORD spoke with one another, and the LORD listened and heard them. So a scroll of remembrance was written before Him regarding those who feared the LORD and honored His name.

3:17

וְהָיוּthey will be לִיto me אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts לַיּוֹםon the day אֲשֶׁרthat אֲנִיI עֹשֶׂהprepare סְגֻלָּהtreasured possession וְחָמַלְתִּיspare עֲלֵיהֶםon them כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had יַחְמֹלspares אִישׁman עַלon בְּנוֹhis own son הָעֹבֵדwho serves אֹתוֹhim

“They will be Mine,” says the LORD of Hosts, “on the day when I prepare My treasured possession. And I will spare them as a man spares his own son who serves him.

3:18

וְשַׁבְתֶּםagain וּרְאִיתֶםdistinguish בֵּיןbetween צַדִּיקrighteous לְרָשָׁעand the wicked בֵּיןbetween עֹבֵדserve אֱלֹהִיםGod לַאֲשֶׁרand those who לֹאnot עֲבָדוֹdo

So you will again distinguish between the righteous and the wicked, between those who serve God and those who do not.”
