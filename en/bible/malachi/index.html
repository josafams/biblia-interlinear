Malachi מַלְאָכִי

Malachi addresses the community of Judah some decades after the rebuilding of the temple, when the enthusiasm had cooled. The book is organized in dialogues: God states something, the people answer with a question (“How…?”) and God explains. The accusations deal with sacrifices of defective animals, negligent priests, broken marriages, injustice and withheld tithes. The book announces a messenger who will prepare the way and ends with the promise of the sending of the prophet Elijah before the day of the LORD.

Structure

  1. God's love and the despised worship chs. 1–2

    God affirms His love; priests and people offer the worst; the covenant with Levi and faithfulness in marriage.

  2. The coming day chs. 2–4

    The complaint that God does not do justice, the messenger of the covenant, the tithes, the scroll of remembrance and the promise of Elijah.

Chapters

  1. 1 God's love and the blemished offerings
  2. 2 A warning to the priests and marital faithfulness
  3. 3 The messenger of the covenant and the tithes
  4. 4 The coming day and the prophet Elijah
