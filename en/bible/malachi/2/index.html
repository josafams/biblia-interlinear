Malachi 2

A warning to the priests and marital faithfulness

“And now this decree is for you, O priests:

If you do not listen, and if you do not take it to heart to honor My name,” says the LORD of Hosts, “I will send a curse among you, and I will curse your blessings. Yes, I have already begun to curse them, because you are not taking it to heart.

2:3

Behold, I will rebuke your descendants, and I will spread dung on your faces, the waste from your feasts, and you will be carried off with it.

2:4

וִידַעְתֶּםknow כִּיthat שִׁלַּחְתִּיsent אֲלֵיכֶםto you אֵת[obj] הַמִּצְוָהthis commandment הַזֹּאתthis לִהְיוֹתcontinue בְּרִיתִיmy covenant אֶתwith לֵוִיLevi אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts

Then you will know that I have sent you this commandment so that My covenant with Levi may continue,” says the LORD of Hosts.

2:5

“My covenant with him was one of life and peace, which I gave to him; it called for reverence, and he revered Me and stood in awe of My name.

2:6

תּוֹרַתinstruction אֱמֶתtrue הָיְתָהwas בְּפִיהוּin his mouth וְעַוְלָהand false לֹאnot נִמְצָאfound בִשְׂפָתָיוon his lips בְּשָׁלוֹםin peace וּבְמִישׁוֹרand uprightness הָלַךְwalked אִתִּיwith me וְרַבִּיםmany הֵשִׁיבturned מֵעָוֺןfrom iniquity

True instruction was in his mouth, and nothing false was found on his lips. He walked with Me in peace and uprightness, and he turned many from iniquity.

2:7

כִּיfor שִׂפְתֵיlips כֹהֵןpriest יִשְׁמְרוּpreserve דַעַתknowledge וְתוֹרָהinstruction יְבַקְשׁוּseek מִפִּיהוּfrom his mouth כִּיbecause מַלְאַךְmessenger יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts הוּאhe

For the lips of a priest should preserve knowledge, and people should seek instruction from his mouth, because he is the messenger of the LORD of Hosts.

2:8

וְאַתֶּםbut you סַרְתֶּםdeparted מִןfrom הַדֶּרֶךְthe way הִכְשַׁלְתֶּםstumble רַבִּיםmany בַּתּוֹרָהinstruction שִׁחַתֶּםviolated בְּרִיתcovenant הַלֵּוִיLevi אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts

But you have departed from the way, and your instruction has caused many to stumble. You have violated the covenant of Levi,” says the LORD of Hosts.

2:9

וְגַםhave אֲנִיI נָתַתִּיmade אֶתְכֶםyou נִבְזִיםdespised וּשְׁפָלִיםand humiliated לְכָלbefore all הָעָםthe people כְּפִיbecause you אֲשֶׁרhave אֵינְכֶםnot שֹׁמְרִיםkept אֶת[obj] דְּרָכַיmy ways וְנֹשְׂאִיםbut have shown partiality פָּנִיםpartiality בַּתּוֹרָהin matters of the law

“So I in turn have made you despised and humiliated before all the people, because you have not kept My ways, but have shown partiality in matters of the law.”

2:10

הֲלוֹאdo not אָבfather אֶחָדone לְכֻלָּנוּwe all הֲלוֹאdid not אֵלGod אֶחָדone בְּרָאָנוּcreate us מַדּוּעַwhy נִבְגַּדbreak faith אִישׁman בְּאָחִיוwith one another לְחַלֵּלto profane בְּרִיתcovenant אֲבֹתֵינוּour fathers

Do we not all have one Father? Did not one God create us? Why then do we break faith with one another so as to profane the covenant of our fathers?

2:11

בָּגְדָהbroken faith יְהוּדָהJudah וְתוֹעֵבָהabomination נֶעֶשְׂתָהcommitted בְיִשְׂרָאֵלin Israel וּבִירוּשָׁלִָםand in Jerusalem כִּיfor חִלֵּלprofaned יְהוּדָהJudah קֹדֶשׁsanctuary יְהוָהLORD’s אֲשֶׁרthat אָהֵבbeloved וּבָעַלmarrying בַּתdaughter אֵלgod נֵכָרforeign

Judah has broken faith; an abomination has been committed in Israel and in Jerusalem. For Judah has profaned the LORD’s beloved sanctuary by marrying the daughter of a foreign god.

2:12

יַכְרֵתcut off יְהוָהLORD לָאִישׁfor the man אֲשֶׁרwho יַעֲשֶׂנָּהdoes עֵרawake וְעֹנֶהand aware מֵאָהֳלֵיfrom the tents יַעֲקֹבJacob וּמַגִּישׁand kingdoms מִנְחָהoffering לַיהוָהto the LORD צְבָאוֹתHosts

As for the man who does this, may the LORD cut off from the tents of Jacob everyone who is awake and aware—even if he brings an offering to the LORD of Hosts.

2:13

וְזֹאתand this שֵׁנִיתanother תַּעֲשׂוּdo כַּסּוֹתcover דִּמְעָהtears אֶת[obj] מִזְבַּחaltar יְהוָהLORD בְּכִיweeping וַאֲנָקָהand groaning מֵאֵיןbecause he no עוֹדlonger פְּנוֹתregards אֶלto הַמִּנְחָהofferings וְלָקַחַתor receives them רָצוֹןgladly מִיֶּדְכֶםfrom your hands

And this is another thing you do: You cover the altar of the LORD with tears, with weeping and groaning, because He no longer regards your offerings or receives them gladly from your hands.

2:14

וַאֲמַרְתֶּםyet you ask עַלon מָהwhy עַלon כִּיbecause יְהוָהLORD הֵעִידwitness בֵּינְךָbetween you וּבֵיןand the אֵשֶׁתwife נְעוּרֶיךָyour youth אֲשֶׁרwhom אַתָּהyou בָּגַדְתָּהbroken faith בָּהּagainst וְהִיאand is חֲבֶרְתְּךָyour companion וְאֵשֶׁתand wife בְּרִיתֶךָcovenant

Yet you ask, “Why?” It is because the LORD has been a witness between you and the wife of your youth, against whom you have broken faith, though she is your companion and your wife by covenant.

2:15

Has not the LORD made them one, having a portion of the Spirit? And why one? Because He seeks godly offspring. So guard yourselves in your spirit and do not break faith with the wife of your youth.

2:16

כִּיfor שָׂנֵאhate שַׁלַּחdivorce אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וְכִסָּהcovers חָמָסviolence עַלon לְבוּשׁוֹhis garment אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts וְנִשְׁמַרְתֶּםso guard בְּרוּחֲכֶםin your spirit וְלֹאand do not תִבְגֹּדוּbreak faith

“For I hate divorce,” says the LORD, the God of Israel. “He who divorces his wife covers his garment with violence,” says the LORD of Hosts. So guard yourselves in your spirit and do not break faith.

2:17

הוֹגַעְתֶּםwearied יְהוָהLORD בְּדִבְרֵיכֶםwith your words וַאֲמַרְתֶּםyet you ask בַּמָּהhow הוֹגָעְנוּwearied בֶּאֱמָרְכֶםby saying כָּלall עֹשֵׂהdo רָעevil טוֹבgood בְּעֵינֵיin the sight יְהוָהLORD וּבָהֶםand in them הוּאhe חָפֵץdelights אוֹor אַיֵּהwhere אֱלֹהֵיGod הַמִּשְׁפָּטof justice

You have wearied the LORD with your words; yet you ask, “How have we wearied Him?” By saying, “All who do evil are good in the sight of the LORD, and in them He delights,” or, “Where is the God of justice?”
