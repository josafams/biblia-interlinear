2 John Ἰωάννου Βʹ

2 John is a note from “the elder” to a “chosen lady” and her children. He rejoices because some of them are walking in the truth, recalls the old commandment to love one another and warns against deceivers who do not acknowledge that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh: they must not be received into the house. It ends with the hope of a visit.

Structure

  1. Greeting ch. 1

    The elder greets the chosen lady and her children, “whom I love in the truth” (1:1–3).

  2. Walking in love ch. 1

    Joy over those who walk in the truth and the request that they love one another, keeping the commandments (1:4–6).

  3. Beware of deceivers ch. 1

    Whoever does not confess the coming of Jesus Christ in the flesh must not be received or greeted (1:7–11).

  4. Farewell ch. 1

    The elder prefers to speak “face to face” and sends greetings from the children of the chosen sister (1:12–13).

Chapters

  1. 1 To the elect lady: truth and love

Key verses

And now I urge you, dear lady—not as a new commandment to you, but one we have had from the beginning—that we love one another. And this is love, that we walk according to His commandments. This is the very commandment you have heard from the beginning, that you must walk in love.

2 John 1:5–6

For many deceivers have gone out into the world, refusing to confess the coming of Jesus Christ in the flesh. Any such person is the deceiver and the antichrist.
