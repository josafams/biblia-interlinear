2 Kings 7

The end of the siege of Samaria

Then Elisha said, “Hear the word of the LORD! This is what the LORD says: ‘About this time tomorrow at the gate of Samaria, a seah of fine flour will sell for a shekel, and two seahs of barley will sell for a shekel.’”

וַיַּעַןanswered הַשָּׁלִישׁthe officer אֲשֶׁרthat לַמֶּלֶךְthe king נִשְׁעָןleaned עַלon יָדוֹarm אֶת[obj] אִישׁman הָאֱלֹהִיםof God וַיֹּאמַרand said הִנֵּהlook יְהוָהLORD עֹשֶׂהmake אֲרֻבּוֹתwindows בַּשָּׁמַיִםin heaven הֲיִהְיֶהhappen הַדָּבָרthe word הַזֶּהthis וַיֹּאמֶרreplied Elisha but הִנְּכָהsee רֹאֶהsee בְּעֵינֶיךָeyes וּמִשָּׁםwill לֹאnot תֹאכֵלeat

But the officer on whose arm the king leaned answered the man of God, “Look, even if the LORD were to make windows in heaven, could this really happen?” “You will see it with your own eyes,” replied Elisha, “but you will not eat any of it.”

7:3

וְאַרְבָּעָהfour אֲנָשִׁיםmen הָיוּwere מְצֹרָעִיםleprosy פֶּתַחat entrance הַשָּׁעַרgate וַיֹּאמְרוּand they said אִישׁone אֶלto רֵעֵהוּanother מָהwhy אֲנַחְנוּwe יֹשְׁבִיםsit פֹּהhere עַדuntil מָתְנוּdie

Now there were four men with leprosy at the entrance of the city gate, and they said to one another, “Why just sit here until we die?

7:4

אִםif אָמַרְנוּsay נָבוֹאgo הָעִירinto the city וְהָרָעָבthe famine בָּעִירin the city וָמַתְנוּdie שָׁםthere וְאִםbut if יָשַׁבְנוּsit פֹהhere וָמָתְנוּalso die וְעַתָּהso now לְכוּcome וְנִפְּלָהand distressed אֶלto מַחֲנֵהcamp אֲרָםArameans אִםif יְחַיֻּנוּlet us נִחְיֶהlive וְאִםwill live if יְמִיתֻנוּkill us וָמָתְנוּwill die

If we say, ‘Let us go into the city,’ we will die there from the famine in the city; but if we sit here, we will also die. So come now, let us go over to the camp of the Arameans. If they let us live, we will live; if they kill us, we will die.”

7:5

וַיָּקוּמוּso they arose בַנֶּשֶׁףat twilight לָבוֹאand went אֶלto מַחֲנֵהcamp אֲרָםArameans וַיָּבֹאוּcame עַדto קְצֵהoutskirts מַחֲנֵהcamp אֲרָםAram וְהִנֵּהand there was אֵיןnot שָׁםa אִישׁman

So they arose at twilight and went to the camp of the Arameans. But when they came to the outskirts of the camp, there was not a man to be found.

7:6

For the Lord had caused the Arameans to hear the sound of chariots, horses, and a great army, so that they said to one another, “Look, the king of Israel must have hired the kings of the Hittites and Egyptians to attack us.”

7:7

וַיָּקוּמוּarisen וַיָּנוּסוּand fled בַנֶּשֶׁףat twilight וַיַּעַזְבוּabandoning אֶת[obj] אָהֳלֵיהֶםtheir tents וְאֶתand [obj] סוּסֵיהֶםhorses וְאֶתand [obj] חֲמֹרֵיהֶםdonkeys הַמַּחֲנֶהthe camp כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had הִיאwas וַיָּנֻסוּrun אֶלto נַפְשָׁםtheir lives

Thus the Arameans had arisen and fled at twilight, abandoning their tents and horses and donkeys. The camp was intact, and they had run for their lives.

7:8

וַיָּבֹאוּwhen הַמְצֹרָעִיםthe lepers הָאֵלֶּהthese עַדuntil קְצֵהedge הַמַּחֲנֶהthe camp וַיָּבֹאוּthey went אֶלinto אֹהֶלtent אֶחָדone וַיֹּאכְלוּto eat וַיִּשְׁתּוּand drink וַיִּשְׂאוּthen they carried מִשָּׁםfrom there כֶּסֶףsilver וְזָהָבgold וּבְגָדִיםand clothing וַיֵּלְכוּand went וַיַּטְמִנוּand hid וַיָּשֻׁבוּreturning וַיָּבֹאוּthey entered אֶלto אֹהֶלtent אַחֵרanother וַיִּשְׂאוּcarried off some מִשָּׁםfrom there וַיֵּלְכוּitems וַיַּטְמִנוּhid

When the lepers reached the edge of the camp, they went into a tent to eat and drink. Then they carried off the silver, gold, and clothing, and went and hid them. On returning, they entered another tent, carried off some items from there, and hid them.

7:9

וַיֹּאמְרוּfinally they said אִישׁone אֶלto רֵעֵהוּanother are לֹאnot כֵןis אֲנַחְנוּwe עֹשִׂיםdoing הַיּוֹםis right today הַזֶּהis a יוֹםday בְּשֹׂרָהgood news הוּאhe וַאֲנַחְנוּwe מַחְשִׁיםsilent וְחִכִּינוּand wait עַדuntil אוֹרlight הַבֹּקֶרmorning וּמְצָאָנוּour will overtake עָווֹןsin וְעַתָּהnow therefore לְכוּlet וְנָבֹאָהus go וְנַגִּידָהand tell בֵּיתhousehold הַמֶּלֶךְthe king’s

Finally, they said to one another, “We are not doing what is right. Today is a day of good news. If we are silent and wait until morning light, our sin will overtake us. Now, therefore, let us go and tell the king’s household.”

7:10

וַיָּבֹאוּso they went וַיִּקְרְאוּand called אֶלto שֹׁעֵרgatekeepers הָעִירthe city וַיַּגִּידוּwe לָהֶםto them לֵאמֹרsaying בָּאנוּwent אֶלto מַחֲנֵהcamp אֲרָםAramean וְהִנֵּהwas אֵיןno שָׁםthere אִישׁone וְקוֹלand sound אָדָםtrace כִּיfor אִםnot הַסּוּסhorses אָסוּרtethered וְהַחֲמוֹרand donkeys אָסוּרconfined וְאֹהָלִיםand the tents כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had הֵמָּהwere

So they went and called out to the gatekeepers of the city, saying, “We went to the Aramean camp and no one was there—not a trace—only tethered horses and donkeys, and the tents were intact.”

7:11

וַיִּקְרָאshouted הַשֹּׁעֲרִיםthe gatekeepers וַיַּגִּידוּreported בֵּיתhouse הַמֶּלֶךְthe king’s פְּנִימָהhousehold

The gatekeepers shouted the news, and it was reported to the king’s household.

7:12

וַיָּקָםgot הַמֶּלֶךְthe king לַיְלָהnight וַיֹּאמֶרand said אֶלto עֲבָדָיוhis servants אַגִּידָהtell נָּאlet לָכֶםto you אֵת[obj] אֲשֶׁרthat עָשׂוּdone לָנוּto us אֲרָםArameans יָדְעוּknow כִּיfor רְעֵבִיםstarving אֲנַחְנוּwe וַיֵּצְאוּleft מִןfrom הַמַּחֲנֶהthe camp לְהֵחָבֵהto hide בַשָּׂדֶהin the field לֵאמֹרthinking כִּיwhen יֵצְאוּcome out מִןout of הָעִירthe city וְנִתְפְּשֵׂםwill take them חַיִּיםalive וְאֶלand to הָעִירthe city נָבֹאenter

So the king got up in the night and said to his servants, “Let me tell you what the Arameans have done to us. They know we are starving, so they have left the camp to hide in the field, thinking, ‘When they come out of the city, we will take them alive and enter the city.’”

7:13

וַיַּעַןand answered אֶחָדone מֵעֲבָדָיוof his servants וַיֹּאמֶרreplied וְיִקְחוּscouts נָאplease חֲמִשָּׁהfive מִןof הַסּוּסִיםthe horses הַנִּשְׁאָרִיםthat remain אֲשֶׁרthat נִשְׁאֲרוּleft בָהּin the city הִנָּםbehold them כְּכָלall הֲמוֹןmultitude יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites אֲשֶׁרthat נִשְׁאֲרוּleft בָהּin her הִנָּםsee כְּכָלall הֲמוֹןmultitude יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites אֲשֶׁרthat תָּמּוּdoomed וְנִשְׁלְחָהsend וְנִרְאֶהand find

But one of his servants replied, “Please, have scouts take five of the horses that remain in the city. Their plight will be no worse than all the Israelites who are left here. You can see that all the Israelites here are doomed. So let us send them and find out.”

7:14

וַיִּקְחוּthen the scouts took שְׁנֵיtwo רֶכֶבchariots סוּסִיםhorses וַיִּשְׁלַחsent הַמֶּלֶךְthe king אַחֲרֵיafter מַחֲנֵהarmy אֲרָםAramean לֵאמֹרsaying לְכוּgo וּרְאוּand see

Then the scouts took two chariots with horses, and the king sent them after the Aramean army, saying, “Go and see.”

7:15

וַיֵּלְכוּand they אַחֲרֵיהֶםafter them עַדfar הַיַּרְדֵּןthe Jordan וְהִנֵּהand indeed כָלwhole הַדֶּרֶךְway מְלֵאָהlittered בְגָדִיםclothing וְכֵלִיםand equipment אֲשֶׁרhad הִשְׁלִיכוּthrown אֲרָםArameans בְּחָפְזָםin haste וַיָּשֻׁבוּreturned הַמַּלְאָכִיםthe scouts וַיַּגִּדוּand told לַמֶּלֶךְthe king

And they tracked them as far as the Jordan, and indeed, the whole way was littered with the clothing and equipment the Arameans had thrown off in haste. So the scouts returned and told the king.

7:16

Then the people went out and plundered the camp of the Arameans. It was then that a seah of fine flour sold for a shekel, and two seahs of barley sold for a shekel, according to the word of the LORD.

7:17

Now the king had appointed the officer on whose arm he leaned to be in charge of the gate, but the people trampled him in the gateway, and he died, just as the man of God had foretold when the king had come to him.

7:18

It happened just as the man of God had told the king: “About this time tomorrow at the gate of Samaria, two seahs of barley will sell for a shekel, and a seah of fine flour will sell for a shekel.”

7:19

וַיַּעַןanswered הַשָּׁלִישׁthe officer אֶת[obj] אִישׁman הָאֱלֹהִיםGod וַיֹּאמַרand said וְהִנֵּהlook even יְהוָהLORD עֹשֶׂהmake אֲרֻבּוֹתwindows בַּשָּׁמַיִםin heaven הֲיִהְיֶהhappen כַּדָּבָרhad הַזֶּהthis וַיֹּאמֶרreplied הִנְּךָbehold you רֹאֶהsee בְּעֵינֶיךָwith your own eyes וּמִשָּׁםbut you will לֹאnot תֹאכֵלeat

And the officer had answered the man of God, “Look, even if the LORD were to make windows in heaven, could this really happen?” So Elisha had replied, “You will see it with your own eyes, but you will not eat any of it!”

7:20

וַיְהִיhappened לוֹto him כֵּןso וַיִּרְמְסוּtrampled אֹתוֹhim הָעָםthe people בַּשַּׁעַרin the gateway וַיָּמֹתand he died

And that is just what happened to him. The people trampled him in the gateway, and he died.
