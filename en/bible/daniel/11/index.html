Then the king will do as he pleases and will exalt and magnify himself above every god, and he will speak monstrous things against the God of gods. He will be successful until the time of wrath is completed, for what has been decreed must be accomplished.

11:37

וְעַלfor אֱלֹהֵיgods אֲבֹתָיוhis fathers לֹאno יָבִיןregard וְעַלnor for חֶמְדַּתdesired נָשִׁיםwomen וְעַלfor כָּלany other אֱלוֹהַּgod לֹאnot יָבִיןunderstand כִּיbecause עַלon כֹּלall יִתְגַּדָּלmagnify

He will show no regard for the gods of his fathers, nor for the one desired by women, nor for any other god, because he will magnify himself above them all.

11:38

וְלֶאֱלֹהַּa god מָעֻזִּיםfortresses עַלon כַּנּוֹplace he יְכַבֵּדhonor וְלֶאֱלוֹהַּa god אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot יְדָעֻהוּknow אֲבֹתָיוfathers יְכַבֵּדhonors בְּזָהָבwith gold וּבְכֶסֶףsilver וּבְאֶבֶןstones יְקָרָהprecious וּבַחֲמֻדוֹתand riches

And in their place, he will honor a god of fortresses—a god his fathers did not know—with gold, silver, precious stones, and riches.

11:39

וְעָשָׂהhe will attack לְמִבְצְרֵיthe strongest מָעֻזִּיםfortresses עִםwith אֱלוֹהַּgod נֵכָרforeign אֲשֶׁרwho יַכִּירacknowledge יַרְבֶּהgreatly כָבוֹדhonor וְהִמְשִׁילָםmaking them rulers בָּרַבִּיםover many וַאֲדָמָהland יְחַלֵּקdistributing בִּמְחִירfor a price

He will attack the strongest fortresses with the help of a foreign god and will greatly honor those who acknowledge him, making them rulers over many and distributing the land for a price.

11:40

וּבְעֵתat the time קֵץend יִתְנַגַּחstorm עִמּוֹagainst him מֶלֶךְking הַנֶּגֶבthe south וְיִשְׂתָּעֵרwill engage עָלָיוhim in מֶלֶךְking הַצָּפוֹןthe north בְּרֶכֶבwith chariots וּבְפָרָשִׁיםhorsemen וּבָאֳנִיּוֹתand ships רַבּוֹתmany וּבָאinvading בַאֲרָצוֹתcountries וְשָׁטַףand sweeping וְעָבָרthrough them

At the time of the end, the king of the South will engage him in battle, but the king of the North will storm out against him with chariots, horsemen, and many ships, invading many countries and sweeping through them like a flood.

11:41

וּבָאalso invade בְּאֶרֶץland הַצְּבִיthe beautiful וְרַבּוֹתand many יִכָּשֵׁלוּfall וְאֵלֶּהbut these יִמָּלְטוּdelivered מִיָּדוֹfrom his hand אֱדוֹםEdom וּמוֹאָבMoab וְרֵאשִׁיתand the leaders בְּנֵיsons עַמּוֹןAmmonites

He will also invade the Beautiful Land, and many countries will fall. But these will be delivered from his hand: Edom, Moab, and the leaders of the Ammonites.

11:42

וְיִשְׁלַחwill extend יָדוֹhis power בַּאֲרָצוֹתover many countries וְאֶרֶץland מִצְרַיִםEgypt לֹאnot תִהְיֶהeven לִפְלֵיטָהwill escape

He will extend his power over many countries, and not even the land of Egypt will escape.

11:43

וּמָשַׁלhe will gain בְּמִכְמַנֵּיtreasures הַזָּהָבgold וְהַכֶּסֶףand silver וּבְכֹלand over all חֲמֻדוֹתriches מִצְרָיִםEgypt וְלֻבִיםand the Libyans וְכֻשִׁיםand Cushites בְּמִצְעָדָיוalso submit to him

He will gain control of the treasures of gold and silver and over all the riches of Egypt, and the Libyans and Cushites will also submit to him.

11:44

וּשְׁמֻעוֹתbut news יְבַהֲלֻהוּwill alarm him מִמִּזְרָחfrom the east וּמִצָּפוֹןand the north וְיָצָאgo בְּחֵמָאwith fury גְדֹלָהgreat לְהַשְׁמִידto destroy וּלְהַחֲרִיםand devote them to destruction רַבִּיםmany

But news from the east and the north will alarm him, and he will go out with great fury to destroy many and devote them to destruction.

11:45

וְיִטַּעroyal אָהֳלֶיtents אַפַּדְנוֹpitch his בֵּיןbetween יַמִּיםsea לְהַרmountain צְבִיbeautiful קֹדֶשׁholy וּבָאand come עַדuntil קִצּוֹmeet his end וְאֵיןwith no עוֹזֵרhelp לוֹto him

He will pitch his royal tents between the sea and the beautiful holy mountain, but he will meet his end with no one to help him.
