Daniel 1

Daniel and his friends at the court of Babylon

In the third year of the reign of Jehoiakim king of Judah, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came to Jerusalem and besieged it.

And the Lord delivered into his hand Jehoiakim king of Judah, along with some of the articles from the house of God. He carried these off to the land of Shinar, to the house of his god, where he put them in the treasury of his god.

Then the king ordered Ashpenaz, the chief of his court officials, to bring in some Israelites from the royal family and the nobility—

1:4

young men without blemish, handsome, gifted in all wisdom, knowledgeable, quick to understand, and qualified to serve in the king’s palace—and to teach them the language and literature of the Chaldeans.

1:5

The king assigned them daily provisions of the royal food and wine. They were to be trained for three years, after which they were to enter the king’s service.

1:6

וַיְהִיwere some בָהֶםamong these מִבְּנֵיyoung יְהוּדָהJudah דָּנִיֵּאלDaniel חֲנַנְיָהHananiah מִישָׁאֵלMishael וַעֲזַרְיָהand Azariah

Among these young men were some from Judah: Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah.

1:7

וַיָּשֶׂםgave לָהֶםto them שַׂרchief הַסָּרִיסִיםofficial שֵׁמוֹתnames וַיָּשֶׂםhe gave לְדָנִיֵּאלto Daniel בֵּלְטְשַׁאצַּרBelteshazzar וְלַחֲנַנְיָהto Hananiah שַׁדְרַךְShadrach וּלְמִישָׁאֵלto Mishael מֵישַׁךְMeshach וְלַעֲזַרְיָהand to Azariah עֲבֵדAbednego נְגוֹAbednego

The chief official gave them new names: To Daniel he gave the name Belteshazzar; to Hananiah, Shadrach; to Mishael, Meshach; and to Azariah, Abednego.

1:8

וַיָּשֶׂםbut made דָּנִיֵּאלDaniel עַלon לִבּוֹhis mind he אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot יִתְגָּאַלdefile בְּפַתְבַּגfood הַמֶּלֶךְthe king’s וּבְיֵיןor wine מִשְׁתָּיוhis banquet וַיְבַקֵּשׁso he asked מִשַּׂרthe chief הַסָּרִיסִיםofficial אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot יִתְגָּאָלpermission

But Daniel made up his mind that he would not defile himself with the king’s food or wine. So he asked the chief official for permission not to defile himself.

1:9

וַיִּתֵּןgranted הָאֱלֹהִיםGod had אֶת[obj] דָּנִיֵּאלDaniel לְחֶסֶדfavor וּלְרַחֲמִיםand compassion לִפְנֵיto before שַׂרchief הַסָּרִיסִיםofficial

Now God had granted Daniel favor and compassion from the chief official,

1:10

וַיֹּאמֶרbut he said שַׂרcommander הַסָּרִיסִיםthe official לְדָנִיֵּאלto Daniel יָרֵאfear אֲנִיI אֶת[obj] אֲדֹנִיmy lord הַמֶּלֶךְthe king אֲשֶׁרwho מִנָּהassigned אֶת[obj] מַאֲכַלְכֶםyour food וְאֶתand [obj] מִשְׁתֵּיכֶםdrink אֲשֶׁרthat לָמָּהfor why should יִרְאֶהsee אֶת[obj] פְּנֵיכֶםyour faces זֹעֲפִיםlooking thinner מִןthan הַיְלָדִיםthe other young אֲשֶׁרthat כְּגִילְכֶםage you וְחִיַּבְתֶּםwould endanger אֶת[obj] רֹאשִׁיmy head לַמֶּלֶךְbefore the king

but he said to Daniel, “I fear my lord the king, who has assigned your food and drink. For why should he see your faces looking thinner than those of the other young men your age? You would endanger my head before the king!”

1:11

וַיֹּאמֶרthen said דָּנִיֵּאלDaniel אֶלto הַמֶּלְצַרthe steward אֲשֶׁרhad מִנָּהappointed שַׂרchief הַסָּרִיסִיםthe official עַלover דָּנִיֵּאלDaniel חֲנַנְיָהHananiah מִישָׁאֵלMishael וַעֲזַרְיָהand Azariah

Then Daniel said to the steward whom the chief official had appointed over Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah,

1:12

“Please test your servants for ten days. Let us be given only vegetables to eat and water to drink.

1:13

Then compare our appearances with those of the young men who are eating the royal food, and deal with your servants according to what you see.”

1:14

וַיִּשְׁמַעso he consented לָהֶםto לַדָּבָרto the thing הַזֶּהthis וַיְנַסֵּםand tested them יָמִיםdays עֲשָׂרָהten

So he consented to this and tested them for ten days.

1:15

וּמִקְצָתand at the end יָמִיםdays עֲשָׂרָהten נִרְאָהlooked מַרְאֵיהֶםhealthier and טוֹבbetter וּבְרִיאֵיand nourished בָּשָׂרflesh מִןthan כָּלall הַיְלָדִיםthe young הָאֹכְלִיםwho were eating אֵת[obj] פַּתְבַּגfood הַמֶּלֶךְthe king’s

And at the end of ten days, they looked healthier and better nourished than all the young men who were eating the king’s food.

1:16

וַיְהִיand was הַמֶּלְצַרthe steward נֹשֵׂאcontinued אֶת[obj] פַּתְבָּגָםwithhold their choice food וְיֵיןand the wine מִשְׁתֵּיהֶםdrink וְנֹתֵןand he gave לָהֶםto them זֵרְעֹנִיםvegetables

So the steward continued to withhold their choice food and the wine they were to drink, and he gave them vegetables instead.

1:17

וְהַיְלָדִיםyoung הָאֵלֶּהthese אַרְבַּעְתָּםfour נָתַןgave לָהֶםto them הָאֱלֹהִיםGod מַדָּעknowledge וְהַשְׂכֵּלand understanding בְּכָלin every סֵפֶרliterature וְחָכְמָהand wisdom וְדָנִיֵּאלAnd Daniel הֵבִיןinsight בְּכָלkind חָזוֹןvisions וַחֲלֹמוֹתand dreams

To these four young men God gave knowledge and understanding in every kind of literature and wisdom. And Daniel had insight into all kinds of visions and dreams.

1:18

וּלְמִקְצָתnow at the end הַיָּמִיםthe time אֲשֶׁרthat אָמַרis what הַמֶּלֶךְthe king לַהֲבִיאָםto bring them וַיְבִיאֵםpresented שַׂרchief הַסָּרִיסִיםthe official לִפְנֵיto before נְבֻכַדְנֶצַּרNebuchadnezzar

Now at the end of the time specified by the king, the chief official presented them to Nebuchadnezzar.

1:19

וַיְדַבֵּרspoke אִתָּםwith them הַמֶּלֶךְthe king וְלֹאno נִמְצָאfound מִכֻּלָּםamong all כְּדָנִיֵּאלequal to Daniel חֲנַנְיָהHananiah מִישָׁאֵלMishael וַעֲזַרְיָהAzariah וַיַּעַמְדוּso they entered לִפְנֵיservice הַמֶּלֶךְthe king’s

And the king spoke with them, and among all the young men he found no one equal to Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah. So they entered the king’s service.

1:20

In every matter of wisdom and understanding about which the king consulted them, he found them ten times better than all the magicians and enchanters in his entire kingdom.

1:21

וַיְהִיand was דָּנִיֵּאלDaniel עַדuntil שְׁנַתyear אַחַתfirst לְכוֹרֶשׁCyrus הַמֶּלֶךְof king

And Daniel remained there until the first year of King Cyrus.
