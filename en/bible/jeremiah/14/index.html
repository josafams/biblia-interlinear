Jeremiah 14

The drought and the false prophets

Open in the interlinear reader

14:1

אֲשֶׁרthat הָיָהcame דְבַרword יְהוָהLORD אֶלto יִרְמְיָהוּJeremiah עַלon דִּבְרֵיwords הַבַּצָּרוֹתthe drought

This is the word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah concerning the drought:

14:2

אָבְלָהmourns יְהוּדָהJudah וּשְׁעָרֶיהָand her gates אֻמְלְלוּlanguish קָדְרוּwail לָאָרֶץfor the land וְצִוְחַתand a cry יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem עָלָתָהgoes

“Judah mourns and her gates languish. Her people wail for the land, and a cry goes up from Jerusalem.

14:3

וְאַדִּרֵיהֶםnobles their שָׁלְחוּsend צְעִירֵיהֶםservants לַמָּיִםfor water בָּאוּgo עַלto גֵּבִיםcisterns לֹאno מָצְאוּfind מַיִםwater שָׁבוּreturn כְלֵיהֶםtheir jars רֵיקָםempty בֹּשׁוּashamed וְהָכְלְמוּand humiliated וְחָפוּthey cover רֹאשָׁםtheir heads

The nobles send their servants for water; they go to the cisterns, but find no water; their jars return empty. They are ashamed and humiliated; they cover their heads.

14:4

בַּעֲבוּרin sake הָאֲדָמָהthe ground חַתָּהcracked כִּיbecause לֹאno הָיָהwas גֶשֶׁםrain בָּאָרֶץon the land בֹּשׁוּashamed אִכָּרִיםfarmers חָפוּcover רֹאשָׁםtheir heads

The ground is cracked because no rain has fallen on the land. The farmers are ashamed; they cover their heads.

14:5

כִּיfor גַםeven אַיֶּלֶתdoe בַּשָּׂדֶהin the field יָלְדָהher וְעָזוֹבdeserts כִּיbecause לֹאno הָיָהthere דֶּשֶׁאgrass

Even the doe in the field deserts her newborn fawn because there is no grass.

14:6

וּפְרָאִיםwild donkeys עָמְדוּstand עַלon שְׁפָיִםbarren heights שָׁאֲפוּpant רוּחַair כַּתַּנִּיםlike jackals כָּלוּfail עֵינֵיהֶםtheir eyes כִּיfor אֵיןno עֵשֶׂבpasture

Wild donkeys stand on barren heights; they pant for air like jackals; their eyes fail for lack of pasture.”

14:7

אִםif עֲוֺנֵינוּour iniquities עָנוּtestify בָנוּagainst us יְהוָהLORD עֲשֵׂהact לְמַעַןsake שְׁמֶךָyour name כִּיfor רַבּוּmany מְשׁוּבֹתֵינוּour rebellions לְךָto you חָטָאנוּsinned

Although our iniquities testify against us, O LORD, act for the sake of Your name. Indeed, our rebellions are many; we have sinned against You.

14:8

מִקְוֵהhope יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael מוֹשִׁיעוֹits Savior בְּעֵתin times צָרָהdistress לָמָּהto why תִהְיֶהare כְּגֵרlike a stranger בָּאָרֶץin the land וּכְאֹרֵחַlike a traveler נָטָהstays לָלוּןa night

O Hope of Israel, its Savior in times of distress, why are You like a stranger in the land, like a traveler who stays but a night?

14:9

לָמָּהto why תִהְיֶהare כְּאִישׁlike a man נִדְהָםsurprise כְּגִבּוֹרlike a warrior לֹאnot יוּכַלpowerless לְהוֹשִׁיעַto save וְאַתָּהyet you בְקִרְבֵּנוּamong us יְהוָהLORD וְשִׁמְךָyour name עָלֵינוּon us נִקְרָאcalled אַלdo not תַּנִּחֵנוּforsake us

Why are You like a man taken by surprise, like a warrior powerless to save? Yet You are among us, O LORD, and we are called by Your name. Do not forsake us!

14:10

כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD לָעָםabout this people הַזֶּהthis כֵּןso אָהֲבוּlove לָנוּעַto wander רַגְלֵיהֶםtheir feet לֹאnot חָשָׂכוּrestrained וַיהוָהso the LORD לֹאnot רָצָםaccept them עַתָּהnow יִזְכֹּרremember עֲוֺנָםtheir guilt וְיִפְקֹדand call חַטֹּאתָםtheir sins

This is what the LORD says about this people: “Truly they love to wander; they have not restrained their feet. So the LORD does not accept them; He will now remember their guilt and call their sins to account.”

14:11

וַיֹּאמֶרsaid יְהוָהLORD אֵלָיto me אַלdo not תִּתְפַּלֵּלpray בְּעַדfor הָעָםpeople הַזֶּהthis לְטוֹבָהwell

Then the LORD said to me, “Do not pray for the well-being of this people.

14:12

כִּיfor יָצֻמוּfast אֵינֶנִּיI will not שֹׁמֵעַlisten אֶלto רִנָּתָםtheir cry וְכִיand if יַעֲלוּoffer עֹלָהburnt offerings וּמִנְחָהand grain offerings אֵינֶנִּיI will not רֹצָםaccept them כִּיfor בַּחֶרֶבby sword וּבָרָעָבand famine וּבַדֶּבֶרand plague אָנֹכִיI מְכַלֶּהfinish אוֹתָםthem

Although they may fast, I will not listen to their cry; although they may offer burnt offerings and grain offerings, I will not accept them. Instead, I will finish them off by sword and famine and plague.”

14:13

וָאֹמַרreplied אֲהָהּah אֲדֹנָיLord I יְהוִהGOD הִנֵּהlook הַנְּבִאִיםthe prophets אֹמְרִיםtelling לָהֶםto them לֹאnot תִרְאוּsee חֶרֶבsword וְרָעָבor suffer famine לֹאnot יִהְיֶהwill לָכֶםto you כִּיfor שְׁלוֹםpeace אֱמֶתlasting אֶתֵּןgive לָכֶםto you בַּמָּקוֹםin this place הַזֶּהthis

“Ah, Lord GOD!” I replied, “Look, the prophets are telling them, ‘You will not see the sword or suffer famine, but I will give you lasting peace in this place.’”

14:14

וַיֹּאמֶרreplied יְהוָהLORD אֵלַיto me שֶׁקֶרlies הַנְּבִאִיםthe prophets נִבְּאִיםprophesying בִּשְׁמִיin my name לֹאnot שְׁלַחְתִּיםsend them וְלֹאor צִוִּיתִיםappoint them וְלֹאor דִבַּרְתִּיspeak אֲלֵיהֶםto them חֲזוֹןvision שֶׁקֶרfalse וְקֶסֶםdivination וֶאֱלִילworthless וְתַרְמִיתand delusion לִבָּםtheir own minds הֵמָּהthey מִתְנַבְּאִיםprophesying לָכֶםto you

“The prophets are prophesying lies in My name,” replied the LORD. “I did not send them or appoint them or speak to them. They are prophesying to you a false vision, a worthless divination, the futility and delusion of their own minds.

14:15

לָכֵןtherefore כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD עַלon הַנְּבִאִיםthe prophets הַנִּבְּאִיםwho prophesy בִּשְׁמִיin my name וַאֲנִיI did לֹאnot שְׁלַחְתִּיםsend them וְהֵמָּהyet they אֹמְרִיםsay חֶרֶבsword וְרָעָבor famine לֹאno יִהְיֶהwill בָּאָרֶץtouch this land הַזֹּאתthis בַּחֶרֶבby sword וּבָרָעָבand famine יִתַּמּוּend הַנְּבִאִיםthese very prophets הָהֵמָּהthose

Therefore this is what the LORD says about the prophets who prophesy in My name: I did not send them, yet they say, ‘No sword or famine will touch this land.’ By sword and famine these very prophets will meet their end!

14:16

וְהָעָםand the people אֲשֶׁרwhom הֵמָּהthey נִבְּאִיםprophesy לָהֶםto them יִהְיוּbe מֻשְׁלָכִיםthrown בְּחֻצוֹתinto the streets יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem מִפְּנֵיfrom before הָרָעָבfamine וְהַחֶרֶבand sword וְאֵיןwill be no מְקַבֵּרbury לָהֵמָּהto them הֵמָּהthey נְשֵׁיהֶםwives their וּבְנֵיהֶםsons or their וּבְנֹתֵיהֶםdaughters וְשָׁפַכְתִּיwill pour עֲלֵיהֶםown upon אֶת[obj] רָעָתָםevil them

And the people to whom they prophesy will be thrown into the streets of Jerusalem because of famine and sword. There will be no one to bury them or their wives, their sons or their daughters. I will pour out their own evil upon them.

14:17

You are to speak this word to them: ‘My eyes overflow with tears; day and night they do not cease, for the virgin daughter of my people has been shattered by a crushing blow, a severely grievous wound.

14:18

If I go out to the country, I see those slain by the sword; if I enter the city, I see those ravaged by famine! For both prophet and priest travel to a land they do not know.’”

14:19

הֲמָאֹסreject מָאַסְתָּrejected completely אֶת[obj] יְהוּדָהJudah אִםif בְּצִיּוֹןZion גָּעֲלָהdespise נַפְשֶׁךָhave you מַדּוּעַwhy הִכִּיתָנוּstricken us וְאֵיןand no לָנוּto us מַרְפֵּאhealing קַוֵּהhoped לְשָׁלוֹםfor peace וְאֵיןbut no טוֹבgood וּלְעֵתand for the time מַרְפֵּאhealing וְהִנֵּהand there was בְעָתָהterror

Have You rejected Judah completely? Do You despise Zion? Why have You stricken us so that we are beyond healing? We hoped for peace, but no good has come, and for the time of healing, but there was only terror.

14:20

יָדַעְנוּacknowledge יְהוָהLORD רִשְׁעֵנוּour wickedness עֲוֺןguilt אֲבוֹתֵינוּour fathers כִּיfor חָטָאנוּsinned לָךְto you

We acknowledge our wickedness, O LORD, the guilt of our fathers; indeed, we have sinned against You.

14:21

For the sake of Your name do not despise us; do not disgrace Your glorious throne. Remember Your covenant with us; do not break it.

14:22

הֲיֵשׁthere בְּהַבְלֵיthe worthless הַגּוֹיִםthe nations מַגְשִׁמִיםrain וְאִםand if הַשָּׁמַיִםthe skies יִתְּנוּsend רְבִבִיםshowers הֲלֹאis not אַתָּהyou הוּאhe יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God וּנְקַוֶּהhope לָּךְto you כִּיfor אַתָּהyou עָשִׂיתָdone אֶת[obj] כָּלall אֵלֶּהthese

Can the worthless idols of the nations bring rain? Do the skies alone send showers? Is this not by You, O LORD our God? So we put our hope in You, for You have done all these things.
