Jeremiah 8

A people that does not repent; lament

Open in the interlinear reader

8:1

“At that time,” declares the LORD, “the bones of the kings of Judah, the bones of the officials, the bones of the priests, the bones of the prophets, and the bones of the people of Jerusalem will be removed from their graves.

8:2

וּשְׁטָחוּםexposed לַשֶּׁמֶשׁto the sun וְלַיָּרֵחַand moon וּלְכֹלand to all צְבָאhost הַשָּׁמַיִםof heaven אֲשֶׁרwhich have אֲהֵבוּםthey loved וַאֲשֶׁרand what עֲבָדוּםserved וַאֲשֶׁרand what הָלְכוּwalked אַחֲרֵיהֶםfollowed וַאֲשֶׁרand what דְּרָשׁוּםconsulted וַאֲשֶׁרand what הִשְׁתַּחֲווּworshiped לָהֶםto them לֹאnot יֵאָסְפוּgathered וְלֹאor but יִקָּבֵרוּburied לְדֹמֶןbecome like dung lying עַלon פְּנֵיface הָאֲדָמָהthe ground יִהְיוּbe

They will be exposed to the sun and moon, and to all the host of heaven which they have loved, served, followed, consulted, and worshiped. Their bones will not be gathered up or buried, but will become like dung lying on the ground.

8:3

וְנִבְחַרchoose מָוֶתdeath מֵחַיִּיםover life לְכֹלwherever הַשְּׁאֵרִיתremnant הַנִּשְׁאָרִיםthe remained מִןof הַמִּשְׁפָּחָהthis family הָרָעָהevil הַזֹּאתthis בְּכָלin all הַמְּקֹמוֹתthe places הַנִּשְׁאָרִיםthe remained אֲשֶׁרhave הִדַּחְתִּיםbanished them שָׁםthere נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts

And wherever I have banished them, the remnant of this evil family will choose death over life,” declares the LORD of Hosts.

8:4

וְאָמַרְתָּtell אֲלֵיהֶםto them כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD הֲיִפְּלוּdo men fall וְלֹאand not יָקוּמוּget אִםif יָשׁוּבagain וְלֹאand not יָשׁוּבreturn

So you are to tell them this is what the LORD says: “Do men fall and not get up again? Does one turn away and not return?

8:5

מַדּוּעַwhy שׁוֹבְבָהturned הָעָםthese people הַזֶּהthis יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem מְשֻׁבָהalways נִצַּחַתturn הֶחֱזִיקוּcling בַּתַּרְמִיתto deceit מֵאֲנוּrefuse לָשׁוּבto return

Why then have these people turned away? Why does Jerusalem always turn away? They cling to deceit; they refuse to return.

8:6

הִקְשַׁבְתִּיlistened וָאֶשְׁמָעand heard לוֹאnot כֵןis יְדַבֵּרוּspeak אֵיןno אִישׁone נִחָםrepents עַלon רָעָתוֹhis wickedness לֵאמֹרasking מֶהwhat עָשִׂיתִיdone כֻּלֹּהeveryone שָׁבpursued בִּמְרוּצָתָםown course כְּסוּסlike a horse שׁוֹטֵףcharging בַּמִּלְחָמָהinto battle

I have listened and heard; they do not speak what is right. No one repents of his wickedness, asking, ‘What have I done?’ Everyone has pursued his own course like a horse charging into battle.

8:7

גַּםeven חֲסִידָהstork בַשָּׁמַיִםin the sky יָדְעָהknows מוֹעֲדֶיהָher appointed seasons וְתֹרturtledove וְסִיסswift וְעָגוּרand the thrush שָׁמְרוּkeep אֶת[obj] עֵתtime בֹּאָנָהmigration וְעַמִּיbut my people לֹאnot יָדְעוּknow אֵת[obj] מִשְׁפַּטrequirements יְהוָהLORD

Even the stork in the sky knows her appointed seasons. The turtledove, the swift, and the thrush keep their time of migration, but My people do not know the requirements of the LORD.

8:8

אֵיכָהhow can תֹאמְרוּsay חֲכָמִיםwise אֲנַחְנוּwe וְתוֹרַתand the law יְהוָהLORD אִתָּנוּwith us אָכֵןwhen הִנֵּהbehold לַשֶּׁקֶרthe lying עָשָׂהproduced עֵטpen שֶׁקֶרdeception סֹפְרִיםscribes

How can you say, ‘We are wise, and the Law of the LORD is with us,’ when in fact the lying pen of the scribes has produced a deception?

8:9

הֹבִישׁוּshame חֲכָמִיםwise חַתּוּdismayed וַיִּלָּכֵדוּand trapped הִנֵּהbehold בִדְבַרword יְהוָהLORD מָאָסוּrejected וְחָכְמַתwisdom מֶהwhat לָהֶםto them

The wise will be put to shame; they will be dismayed and trapped. Since they have rejected the word of the LORD, what wisdom do they really have?

8:10

לָכֵןtherefore אֶתֵּןgive אֶת[obj] נְשֵׁיהֶםtheir wives לַאֲחֵרִיםto other שְׂדוֹתֵיהֶםtheir fields לְיוֹרְשִׁיםto new owners כִּיfor מִקָּטֹןfrom the least וְעַדthem to גָּדוֹלgreatest כֻּלֹּהall are בֹּצֵעַgreedy בָּצַעgain מִנָּבִיאfrom prophet וְעַדto כֹּהֵןpriest כֻּלֹּהall עֹשֶׂהpractice שָּׁקֶרdeceit

Therefore I will give their wives to other men and their fields to new owners. For from the least of them to the greatest, all are greedy for gain; from prophet to priest, all practice deceit.

8:11

וַיְרַפּוּthey dress אֶת[obj] שֶׁבֶרwound בַּתdaughter עַמִּיmy people עַלon נְקַלָּהvery little care לֵאמֹרsaying שָׁלוֹםpeace שָׁלוֹםpeace וְאֵיןno שָׁלוֹםpeace

They dress the wound of the daughter of My people with very little care, saying, ‘Peace, peace,’ when there is no peace at all.

8:12

הֹבִשׁוּare כִּיfor תוֹעֵבָהabomination עָשׂוּcommitted גַּםhave בּוֹשׁshame לֹאno יֵבֹשׁוּshame וְהִכָּלֵםshame at all לֹאnot יָדָעוּknow לָכֵןto blush so יִפְּלוּfall בַנֹּפְלִיםamong the fallen בְּעֵתwhen פְּקֻדָּתָםpunish them יִכָּשְׁלוּcollapse אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD

Are they ashamed of the abomination they have committed? No, they have no shame at all; they do not even know how to blush. So they will fall among the fallen; when I punish them, they will collapse, says the LORD.

8:13

אָסֹףtake אֲסִיפֵםaway their נְאֻםdeclares יְהֹוָהLORD אֵיןno עֲנָבִיםgrapes בַּגֶּפֶןon the vine וְאֵיןand no תְּאֵנִיםfigs בַּתְּאֵנָהon the tree וְהֶעָלֶהand even the leaf נָבֵלwither וָאֶתֵּןwhatever I have given לָהֶםto them יַעַבְרוּםlost to them

I will take away their harvest, declares the LORD. There will be no grapes on the vine, nor figs on the tree, and even the leaf will wither. Whatever I have given them will be lost to them.”

8:14

עַלon מָהwhy אֲנַחְנוּwe יֹשְׁבִיםsitting הֵאָסְפוּgather וְנָבוֹאtogether let us flee אֶלto עָרֵיcities הַמִּבְצָרthe fortified וְנִדְּמָהand perish שָּׁםthere כִּיfor יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God הֲדִמָּנוּdoomed us וַיַּשְׁקֵנוּhe has given us מֵיwater רֹאשׁpoisoned כִּיbecause חָטָאנוּsinned לַיהוָהagainst the LORD

Why are we just sitting here? Gather together, let us flee to the fortified cities and perish there, for the LORD our God has doomed us. He has given us poisoned water to drink, because we have sinned against the LORD.

8:15

קַוֵּהhoped לְשָׁלוֹםfor peace וְאֵיןbut no טוֹבgood לְעֵתtime מַרְפֵּהhealing וְהִנֵּהand there was בְעָתָהterror

We hoped for peace, but no good has come, for a time of healing, but there was only terror.

8:16

מִדָּןfrom Dan נִשְׁמַעheard נַחְרַתsnorting סוּסָיוhorses is מִקּוֹלat the sound מִצְהֲלוֹתneighing אַבִּירָיוmighty רָעֲשָׁהquakes כָּלwhole הָאָרֶץthe land וַיָּבוֹאוּthey come וַיֹּאכְלוּto devour אֶרֶץland וּמְלוֹאָהּand everything in it עִירcity וְיֹשְׁבֵיdwell בָהּin it

The snorting of enemy horses is heard from Dan. At the sound of the neighing of mighty steeds, the whole land quakes. They come to devour the land and everything in it, the city and all who dwell in it.

8:17

כִּיfor הִנְנִיbehold I מְשַׁלֵּחַsend בָּכֶםamong you נְחָשִׁיםsnakes צִפְעֹנִיםvipers אֲשֶׁרthat אֵיןno לָהֶםto them לָחַשׁcharmed וְנִשְּׁכוּbite אֶתְכֶםyou נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD

“For behold, I will send snakes among you, vipers that cannot be charmed, and they will bite you,” declares the LORD.

8:18

מַבְלִיגִיתִיhealing עֲלֵיon יָגוֹןsorrow עָלַיon me לִבִּיheart דַוָּיfaint

My sorrow is beyond healing; my heart is faint within me.

8:19

הִנֵּהbehold קוֹלlisten שַׁוְעַתcry בַּתdaughter עַמִּיmy people מֵאֶרֶץfrom a land מַרְחַקִּיםfar הַיהוָהis the LORD אֵיןno בְּצִיּוֹןin Zion אִםif מַלְכָּהּher king אֵיןno בָּהּin her מַדּוּעַwhy הִכְעִסוּנִיprovoked me בִּפְסִלֵיהֶםwith their carved images בְּהַבְלֵיwith their worthless idols נֵכָרforeign

Listen to the cry of the daughter of my people from a land far away: “Is the LORD no longer in Zion? Is her King no longer there?” “Why have they provoked Me to anger with their carved images, with their worthless foreign idols?”

8:20

עָבַרpassed קָצִירharvest כָּלָהended קָיִץsummer וַאֲנַחְנוּbut we לוֹאnot נוֹשָׁעְנוּsaved

“The harvest has passed, the summer has ended, but we have not been saved.”

8:21

עַלfor שֶׁבֶרbrokenness בַּתdaughter עַמִּיpeople I am הָשְׁבָּרְתִּיcrushed קָדַרְתִּיmourn שַׁמָּהhorror הֶחֱזִקָתְנִיgripped me

For the brokenness of the daughter of my people I am crushed. I mourn; horror has gripped me.

8:22

הַצֳרִיbalm אֵיןno בְּגִלְעָדin Gilead אִםif רֹפֵאphysician אֵיןno שָׁםthere כִּיfor מַדּוּעַwhy לֹאnot עָלְתָהbeen אֲרֻכַתhealth בַּתdaughter עַמִּיmy people

Is there no balm in Gilead? Is no physician there? Why then has the health of the daughter of my people not been restored?
