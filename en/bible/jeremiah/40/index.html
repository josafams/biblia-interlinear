Jeremiah 40

Jeremiah freed; Gedaliah governor

Open in the interlinear reader

40:1

This is the word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD after Nebuzaradan captain of the guard had released him at Ramah, having found him bound in chains among all the captives of Jerusalem and Judah who were being exiled to Babylon.

and now the LORD has fulfilled it; He has done just as He said. Because you people have sinned against the LORD and have not obeyed His voice, this thing has happened to you.

40:4

וְעַתָּהbut now הִנֵּהbehold פִתַּחְתִּיךָfreeing you הַיּוֹםtoday מִןfrom הָאזִקִּיםthe chains אֲשֶׁרthat עַלon יָדֶךָyour wrists אִםif טוֹבit בְּעֵינֶיךָpleases you לָבוֹאto come אִתִּיwith me בָבֶלBabylon בֹּאcome וְאָשִׂיםtake אֶת[obj] עֵינִיmy eyes עָלֶיךָon you וְאִםbut if רַעwrong בְּעֵינֶיךָit seems לָבוֹאto come אִתִּיwith me בָבֶלBabylon חֲדָלno farther רְאֵהlook כָּלwhole הָאָרֶץthe land לְפָנֶיךָbefore you אֶלto טוֹבgood וְאֶלand to הַיָּשָׁרright בְּעֵינֶיךָseems לָלֶכֶתto go שָׁמָּהthere לֵךְgo

But now, behold, I am freeing you today from the chains that were on your wrists. If it pleases you to come with me to Babylon, then come, and I will take care of you. But if it seems wrong to you to come with me to Babylon, go no farther. Look, the whole land is before you. Wherever it seems good and right to you, go there.”

40:5

But before Jeremiah turned to go, Nebuzaradan added, “Return to Gedaliah son of Ahikam, the son of Shaphan, whom the king of Babylon has appointed over the cities of Judah, and stay with him among the people, or go anywhere else that seems right.” Then the captain of the guard gave him a ration and a gift and released him.

40:6

וַיָּבֹאso went יִרְמְיָהוּJeremiah אֶלto גְּדַלְיָהGedaliah בֶןson אֲחִיקָםAhikam הַמִּצְפָּתָהat Mizpah וַיֵּשֶׁבand stayed אִתּוֹwith him בְּתוֹךְamong הָעָםthe people הַנִּשְׁאָרִיםwho were left בָּאָרֶץin the land

So Jeremiah went to Gedaliah son of Ahikam at Mizpah and stayed with him among the people who were left in the land.

40:7

וַיִּשְׁמְעוּwhen כָלall שָׂרֵיcommanders הַחֲיָלִיםthe armies אֲשֶׁרthat בַּשָּׂדֶהin the field הֵמָּהthey וְאַנְשֵׁיהֶםand men כִּיthat הִפְקִידappointed מֶלֶךְking בָּבֶלBabylon אֶת[obj] גְּדַלְיָהוּGedaliah בֶןson אֲחִיקָםAhikam בָּאָרֶץover the land וְכִיand that הִפְקִידcharge אִתּוֹwith him אֲנָשִׁיםmen וְנָשִׁיםwomen וָטָףand children וּמִדַּלַּתwho were the poorest הָאָרֶץof the land מֵאֲשֶׁרand had לֹאnot הָגְלוּexiled בָּבֶלָהBabylon

When all the commanders and men of the armies in the field heard that the king of Babylon had appointed Gedaliah son of Ahikam over the land and that he had put him in charge of the men, women, and children who were the poorest of the land and had not been exiled to Babylon,

40:8

וַיָּבֹאוּthey came אֶלto גְּדַלְיָהGedaliah הַמִּצְפָּתָהat Mizpah וְיִשְׁמָעֵאלIshmael בֶּןson נְתַנְיָהוּNethaniah וְיוֹחָנָןJohanan וְיוֹנָתָןand Jonathan בְּנֵיsons קָרֵחַKareah וּשְׂרָיָהSeraiah בֶןson תַּנְחֻמֶתTanhumeth וּבְנֵיsons עֵיפַיEphai הַנְּטֹפָתִיthe Netophathite וִיזַנְיָהוּand Jezaniah בֶּןson הַמַּעֲכָתִיthe Maacathite הֵמָּהthey וְאַנְשֵׁיהֶםand their men

they came to Gedaliah at Mizpah—Ishmael son of Nethaniah, Johanan and Jonathan the sons of Kareah, Seraiah son of Tanhumeth, the sons of Ephai the Netophathite, and Jezaniah son of the Maacathite—they and their men.

40:9

Gedaliah son of Ahikam, the son of Shaphan, swore an oath to them and their men, assuring them, “Do not be afraid to serve the Chaldeans. Live in the land and serve the king of Babylon, and it will go well with you.

40:10

וַאֲנִיand I הִנְנִיI will יֹשֵׁבstay בַּמִּצְפָּהin Mizpah לַעֲמֹדto represent לִפְנֵיbefore הַכַּשְׂדִּיםthe Chaldeans אֲשֶׁרwho יָבֹאוּcome אֵלֵינוּto us וְאַתֶּםas for you אִסְפוּgather יַיִןwine וְקַיִץgrapes summer fruit וְשֶׁמֶןand oil וְשִׂמוּplace them בִּכְלֵיכֶםin your storage jars וּשְׁבוּand live בְּעָרֵיכֶםin the cities you אֲשֶׁרhave תְּפַשְׂתֶּםtaken

As for me, I will stay in Mizpah to represent you before the Chaldeans who come to us. As for you, gather wine grapes, summer fruit, and oil, place them in your storage jars, and live in the cities you have taken.”

40:11

וְגַםand also כָּלall הַיְּהוּדִיםthe Jews אֲשֶׁרthat בְּמוֹאָבin Moab וּבִבְנֵיand in sons עַמּוֹןAmmon וּבֶאֱדוֹםEdom וַאֲשֶׁרand what בְּכָלall הָאֲרָצוֹתthe other lands שָׁמְעוּheard כִּיthat נָתַןhad מֶלֶךְking בָּבֶלBabylon שְׁאֵרִיתremnant לִיהוּדָהin Judah וְכִיand if הִפְקִידappointed עֲלֵיהֶםover them אֶת[obj] גְּדַלְיָהוּGedaliah בֶּןson אֲחִיקָםAhikam בֶּןson שָׁפָןShaphan

When all the Jews in Moab, Ammon, Edom, and all the other lands heard that the king of Babylon had left a remnant in Judah and had appointed Gedaliah son of Ahikam, the son of Shaphan, over them,

40:12

וַיָּשֻׁבוּthey returned כָלall הַיְּהוּדִיםreturned מִכָּלfrom all הַמְּקֹמוֹתthe places אֲשֶׁרwhich נִדְּחוּbanished שָׁםthere וַיָּבֹאוּand came אֶרֶץland יְהוּדָהJudah אֶלto גְּדַלְיָהוּGedaliah הַמִּצְפָּתָהat Mizpah וַיַּאַסְפוּand they gathered יַיִןwine וָקַיִץgrapes and summer fruit הַרְבֵּהabundance מְאֹדvery

they all returned from all the places to which they had been banished and came to the land of Judah, to Gedaliah at Mizpah. And they gathered an abundance of wine grapes and summer fruit.

40:13

וְיוֹחָנָןJohanan בֶּןson קָרֵחַKareah וְכָלand all שָׂרֵיcommanders הַחֲיָלִיםthe armies אֲשֶׁרthat בַּשָּׂדֶהin the field בָּאוּcame אֶלto גְּדַלְיָהוּGedaliah הַמִּצְפָּתָהat Mizpah

Meanwhile, Johanan son of Kareah and all the commanders of the armies in the field came to Gedaliah at Mizpah

וַיֹּאמְרוּand said אֵלָיוto him הֲיָדֹעַare you תֵּדַעaware כִּיthat בַּעֲלִיסBaalis מֶלֶךְking בְּנֵיsons עַמּוֹןAmmonites שָׁלַחsent אֶת[obj] יִשְׁמָעֵאלIshmael בֶּןson נְתַנְיָהNethaniah לְהַכֹּתְךָto take your נָפֶשׁlife וְלֹאnot הֶאֱמִיןbelieve לָהֶםto them גְּדַלְיָהוּGedaliah בֶּןson אֲחִיקָםAhikam

and said to him, “Are you aware that Baalis king of the Ammonites has sent Ishmael son of Nethaniah to take your life?” But Gedaliah son of Ahikam did not believe them.

40:15

Then Johanan son of Kareah spoke privately to Gedaliah at Mizpah. “Let me go and kill Ishmael son of Nethaniah,” he said. “No one will know it. Why should he take your life and scatter all the people of Judah who have gathered to you, so that the remnant of Judah would perish?”

40:16

וַיֹּאמֶרsaid גְּדַלְיָהוּGedaliah בֶןson אֲחִיקָםAhikam אֶלto יוֹחָנָןJohanan בֶּןson קָרֵחַKareah אַלdo not תַּעֲשֵׂהdo אֶת[obj] הַדָּבָרa thing הַזֶּהthis כִּיfor שֶׁקֶרlie אַתָּהyou דֹבֵרsaying אֶלto יִשְׁמָעֵאלIshmael

But Gedaliah son of Ahikam said to Johanan son of Kareah, “Do not do such a thing! What you are saying about Ishmael is a lie.”
