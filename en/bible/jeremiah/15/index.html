Jeremiah 15

Then the LORD said to me: “Even if Moses and Samuel should stand before Me, My heart would not go out to this people. Send them from My presence, and let them go.

If they ask you, ‘Where shall we go?’ you are to tell them that this is what the LORD says: ‘Those destined for death, to death; those destined for the sword, to the sword; those destined for famine, to famine; and those destined for captivity, to captivity.’

15:3

וּפָקַדְתִּיwill appoint עֲלֵיהֶםover them אַרְבַּעfour מִשְׁפָּחוֹתkinds of נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] הַחֶרֶבthe sword לַהֲרֹגto kill וְאֶתand [obj] הַכְּלָבִיםthe dogs לִסְחֹבto drag וְאֶתand [obj] עוֹףbirds הַשָּׁמַיִםthe air וְאֶתand [obj] בֶּהֱמַתbeasts הָאָרֶץthe earth לֶאֱכֹלto devour וּלְהַשְׁחִיתand destroy

I will appoint over them four kinds of destroyers, declares the LORD: the sword to kill, the dogs to drag away, and the birds of the air and beasts of the earth to devour and destroy.

15:4

וּנְתַתִּיםwill make them לְזַעֲוָהa horror לְכֹלto all מַמְלְכוֹתkingdoms הָאָרֶץthe earth בִּגְלַלbecause מְנַשֶּׁהManasseh בֶןson יְחִזְקִיָּהוּHezekiah מֶלֶךְking יְהוּדָהJudah עַלon אֲשֶׁרthat עָשָׂהdid בִּירוּשָׁלִָםin Jerusalem

I will make them a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth because of what Manasseh son of Hezekiah king of Judah did in Jerusalem.

15:5

כִּיfor מִיwho יַחְמֹלpity עָלַיִךְon you יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem וּמִיwho יָנוּדmourn לָךְfor you וּמִיwho יָסוּרturn aside לִשְׁאֹלto ask לְשָׁלֹםabout welfare לָךְto you

Who will have pity on you, O Jerusalem? Who will mourn for you? Who will turn aside to ask about your welfare?

15:6

You have forsaken Me, declares the LORD. You have turned your back. So I will stretch out My hand against you and I will destroy you; I am weary of showing compassion.

15:7

וָאֶזְרֵםI will scatter them בְּמִזְרֶהwith a winnowing fork בְּשַׁעֲרֵיat the gates הָאָרֶץthe land שִׁכַּלְתִּיbereave אִבַּדְתִּיdestroy אֶת[obj] עַמִּיmy people מִדַּרְכֵיהֶםfrom their ways לוֹאnot שָׁבוּturned

I will scatter them with a winnowing fork at the gates of the land. I will bereave and destroy My people who have not turned from their ways.

15:8

עָצְמוּtheir לִיto me אַלְמְנֹתָוwidows more מֵחוֹלnumerous than the sand יַמִּיםsea הֵבֵאתִיbring לָהֶםa עַלagainst אֵםmothers בָּחוּרyoung men שֹׁדֵדdestroyer בַּצָּהֳרָיִםat noon הִפַּלְתִּיbring עָלֶיהָupon פִּתְאֹםsuddenly עִירanguish וּבֶהָלוֹתand dismay

I will make their widows more numerous than the sand of the sea. I will bring a destroyer at noon against the mothers of young men. I will suddenly bring upon them anguish and dismay.

15:9

אֻמְלְלָהfaint יֹלֶדֶתmother הַשִּׁבְעָהseven נָפְחָהbreathe נַפְשָׁהּbreath her בָּאlast שִׁמְשָׁהּsun בְּעֹדstill יוֹמָםday בּוֹשָׁהdisgraced וְחָפֵרָהand humiliated וּשְׁאֵרִיתָםand the rest I will לַחֶרֶבto the sword אֶתֵּןput לִפְנֵיin the presence אֹיְבֵיהֶםtheir enemies נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD

The mother of seven will grow faint; she will breathe her last breath. Her sun will set while it is still day; she will be disgraced and humiliated. And the rest I will put to the sword in the presence of their enemies,” declares the LORD.

15:10

אוֹיWoe לִיto me אִמִּיmy mother כִּיthat יְלִדְתִּנִיborne me אִישׁman רִיבstrife וְאִישׁand man מָדוֹןconflict לְכָלin all הָאָרֶץthe land לֹאnot נָשִׁיתִיlent וְלֹאand not נָשׁוּborrowed בִיin me כֻּלֹּהeveryone מְקַלְלַונִיcurses me

Woe to me, my mother, that you have borne me, a man of strife and conflict in all the land. I have neither lent nor borrowed, yet everyone curses me.

15:11

אָמַרsaid יְהוָהLORD אִםif לֹאnot שֵׁרִיתִיךָdeliver you לְטוֹבfor a good אִםif לוֹאnot הִפְגַּעְתִּיintercede בְךָwith your בְּעֵתin your time רָעָהtrouble וּבְעֵתin your time צָרָהdistress אֶת[obj] הָאֹיֵבenemy

The LORD said: “Surely I will deliver you for a good purpose; surely I will intercede with your enemy in your time of trouble, in your time of distress.

15:12

הֲיָרֹעַcan anyone smash בַּרְזֶלiron בַּרְזֶלiron מִצָּפוֹןfrom the north וּנְחֹשֶׁתor bronze

Can anyone smash iron— iron from the north—or bronze?

15:13

חֵילְךָyour wealth וְאוֹצְרוֹתֶיךָand your treasures לָבַזas plunder אֶתֵּןgive לֹאnot בִמְחִירcharge וּבְכָלfor all חַטֹּאותֶיךָyour sins וּבְכָלwithin all גְּבוּלֶיךָyour borders

Your wealth and your treasures I will give up as plunder, without charge for all your sins within all your borders.

15:14

וְהַעֲבַרְתִּיenslave אֶת[obj] אֹיְבֶיךָyour enemies בְּאֶרֶץin a land לֹאnot יָדָעְתָּknow כִּיfor אֵשׁfire קָדְחָהkindle בְאַפִּיmy anger עֲלֵיכֶםagainst you תּוּקָדburn

Then I will enslave you to your enemies in a land you do not know, for My anger will kindle a fire that will burn against you.”

15:15

You understand, O LORD; remember me and attend to me. Avenge me against my persecutors. In Your patience, do not take me away. Know that I endure reproach for Your honor.

15:16

נִמְצְאוּfound דְבָרֶיךָyour words וָאֹכְלֵםand I ate them וַיְהִיand was דְבָרְךָyour words לִיto me לְשָׂשׂוֹןjoy וּלְשִׂמְחַתand heart’s לְבָבִיmy delight כִּיfor נִקְרָאbear שִׁמְךָyour name עָלַיon me יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod צְבָאוֹתHosts

Your words were found, and I ate them. Your words became my joy and my heart’s delight. For I bear Your name, O LORD God of Hosts.

15:17

I never sat with the band of revelers, nor did I celebrate with them. Because Your hand was on me, I sat alone, for You have filled me with indignation.

15:18

Why is my pain unending, and my wound incurable, refusing to be healed? You have indeed become like a mirage to me— water that is not there.

15:19

לָכֵןtherefore כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD אִםif תָּשׁוּבreturn וַאֲשִׁיבְךָwill restore you לְפָנַיmy presence תַּעֲמֹדstand וְאִםand if תּוֹצִיאtake יָקָרspeak מִזּוֹלֵלnoble instead of worthless כְּפִיmy spokesman תִהְיֶהbe יָשֻׁבוּturn הֵמָּהthey אֵלֶיךָto you וְאַתָּהbut you לֹאnot תָשׁוּבturn אֲלֵיהֶםto them

Therefore this is what the LORD says: “If you return, I will restore you; you will stand in My presence. And if you speak words that are noble instead of worthless, you will be My spokesman. It is they who must turn to you, but you must not turn to them.

15:20

וּנְתַתִּיךָmake you לָעָםto this people הַזֶּהthis לְחוֹמַתwall נְחֹשֶׁתbronze בְּצוּרָהfortified וְנִלְחֲמוּthey will fight אֵלֶיךָagainst you וְלֹאbut will not יוּכְלוּovercome לָךְto you כִּיfor אִתְּךָwith you אֲנִיam לְהוֹשִׁיעֲךָto save וּלְהַצִּילֶךָand deliver you נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD

Then I will make you a wall to this people, a fortified wall of bronze; they will fight against you but will not overcome you, for I am with you to save and deliver you, declares the LORD.

15:21

וְהִצַּלְתִּיךָwill deliver you מִיַּדfrom the hand רָעִיםwicked וּפְדִתִיךָand redeem you מִכַּףfrom the grasp עָרִצִיםruthless

I will deliver you from the hand of the wicked and redeem you from the grasp of the ruthless.”
