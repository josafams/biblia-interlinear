Jeremiah 28

Hananiah breaks the wooden yoke

In the fifth month of that same year, the fourth year, near the beginning of the reign of King Zedekiah of Judah, the prophet Hananiah son of Azzur, who was from Gibeon, said to me in the house of the LORD in the presence of the priests and all the people:

“This is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: ‘I have broken the yoke of the king of Babylon.

Within two years I will restore to this place all the articles of the house of the LORD that Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon removed from here and carried to Babylon.

And I will restore to this place Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim king of Judah, along with all the exiles from Judah who went to Babylon,’ declares the LORD, ‘for I will break the yoke of the king of Babylon.’”

28:5

Then the prophet Jeremiah replied to the prophet Hananiah in the presence of the priests and all the people who were standing in the house of the LORD.

28:6

“Amen!” Jeremiah said. “May the LORD do so! May the LORD fulfill the words you have prophesied, and may He restore the articles of His house and all the exiles back to this place from Babylon.

28:7

אַךְNevertheless שְׁמַעlisten נָאnow הַדָּבָרthis message הַזֶּהthis אֲשֶׁרthat אָנֹכִיam דֹּבֵרspeaking בְּאָזְנֶיךָin your hearing וּבְאָזְנֵיand in the hearing כָּלall הָעָםthe people

Nevertheless, listen now to this message I am speaking in your hearing and in the hearing of all the people.

28:8

הַנְּבִיאִיםthe prophets אֲשֶׁרthat הָיוּwere לְפָנַיto my before וּלְפָנֶיךָpreceded you and מִןof הָעוֹלָםold who וַיִּנָּבְאוּprophesied אֶלagainst אֲרָצוֹתlands רַבּוֹתmany וְעַלand on מַמְלָכוֹתkingdoms גְּדֹלוֹתgreat לְמִלְחָמָהwar וּלְרָעָהdisaster וּלְדָבֶרand plague

The prophets of old who preceded you and me prophesied war, disaster, and plague against many lands and great kingdoms.

28:9

הַנָּבִיאthe prophet אֲשֶׁרwho יִנָּבֵאprophesies לְשָׁלוֹםpeace בְּבֹאin came דְּבַרword הַנָּבִיאthe prophet יִוָּדַעknown הַנָּבִיאthe prophet אֲשֶׁרthat שְׁלָחוֹsent יְהוָהLORD בֶּאֱמֶתhas truly

As for the prophet who prophesies peace, only if the word of the prophet comes true will the prophet be recognized as one the LORD has truly sent.”

28:10

וַיִּקַּחtook חֲנַנְיָהHananiah הַנָּבִיאthe prophet אֶת[obj] הַמּוֹטָהthe yoke מֵעַלfrom the צַוַּארneck יִרְמְיָהJeremiah הַנָּבִיאthe prophet וַיִּשְׁבְּרֵהוּand broke it

Then the prophet Hananiah took the yoke off the neck of Jeremiah the prophet and broke it.

28:11

וַיֹּאמֶרand said חֲנַנְיָהHananiah לְעֵינֵיin the presence כָלall הָעָםthe people לֵאמֹרproclaimed is כֹּהthis what אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD כָּכָהthis way אֶשְׁבֹּרbreak אֶת[obj] עֹלyoke נְבֻכַדְנֶאצַּרNebuchadnezzar מֶלֶךְking בָּבֶלBabylon בְּעוֹדin still שְׁנָתַיִםtwo years יָמִיםdays מֵעַלfrom the צַוַּארneck כָּלall הַגּוֹיִםthe nations וַיֵּלֶךְwent יִרְמְיָהJeremiah הַנָּבִיאthe prophet לְדַרְכּוֹon his way

And in the presence of all the people Hananiah proclaimed, “This is what the LORD says: ‘In this way, within two years I will break the yoke of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon off the neck of all the nations.’” At this, Jeremiah the prophet went on his way.

28:12

וַיְהִיbut shortly דְבַרword יְהוָהLORD אֶלto יִרְמְיָהJeremiah אַחֲרֵיafter שְׁבוֹרbroken חֲנַנְיָהHananiah הַנָּבִיאthe prophet had אֶת[obj] הַמּוֹטָהthe yoke מֵעַלfrom the צַוַּארneck יִרְמְיָהJeremiah הַנָּבִיאthe prophet לֵאמֹרto saying

But shortly after Hananiah the prophet had broken the yoke off his neck, the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah:

28:13

הָלוֹךְgo וְאָמַרְתָּand tell אֶלto חֲנַנְיָהHananiah לֵאמֹרthat is כֹּהthis what אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD מוֹטֹתyoke עֵץwood שָׁבָרְתָּbroken וְעָשִׂיתָfashioned תַחְתֵּיהֶןplace מֹטוֹתyoke בַּרְזֶלiron

“Go and tell Hananiah that this is what the LORD says: ‘You have broken a yoke of wood, but in its place you have fashioned a yoke of iron.’

28:14

כִּיfor כֹהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael עֹלyoke בַּרְזֶלiron נָתַתִּיput עַלon צַוַּארneck כָּלall הַגּוֹיִםthese nations הָאֵלֶּהthese לַעֲבֹדmake serve אֶת[obj] נְבֻכַדְנֶאצַּרNebuchadnezzar מֶלֶךְking בָּבֶלBabylon וַעֲבָדֻהוּserve him וְגַםI have even אֶת[obj] חַיַּתbeasts הַשָּׂדֶהthe field נָתַתִּיgiven לוֹto him

For this is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: ‘I have put a yoke of iron on the neck of all these nations to make them serve Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and they will serve him. I have even given him control of the beasts of the field.’”

28:15

וַיֹּאמֶרsaid יִרְמְיָהJeremiah הַנָּבִיאthe prophet אֶלto חֲנַנְיָהHananiah הַנָּבִיאthe prophet שְׁמַעlisten נָאplease חֲנַנְיָהHananiah לֹאnot שְׁלָחֲךָsend you יְהוָהLORD וְאַתָּהbut you הִבְטַחְתָּpersuaded אֶת[obj] הָעָםthis people הַזֶּהthis עַלin שָׁקֶרlie

Then the prophet Jeremiah said to the prophet Hananiah, “Listen, Hananiah! The LORD did not send you, but you have persuaded this people to trust in a lie.

28:16

לָכֵןtherefore כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD הִנְנִיsays I am מְשַׁלֵּחֲךָremove you מֵעַלfrom the פְּנֵיface הָאֲדָמָהthe earth הַשָּׁנָהthis year אַתָּהyou מֵתdie כִּיbecause סָרָהpreached rebellion דִבַּרְתָּspoken אֶלagainst יְהוָהLORD

Therefore this is what the LORD says: ‘I am about to remove you from the face of the earth. You will die this year because you have preached rebellion against the LORD.’”

28:17

וַיָּמָתdied חֲנַנְיָהHananiah הַנָּבִיאthe prophet בַּשָּׁנָהyear הַהִיאthat very בַּחֹדֶשׁin the month הַשְּׁבִיעִיseventh

And in the seventh month of that very year, the prophet Hananiah died.
