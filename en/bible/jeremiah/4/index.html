Jeremiah 4

A call to repentance; the enemy from the north

“If you will return, O Israel, return to Me,” declares the LORD. “If you will remove your detestable idols from My sight and no longer waver,

and if you can swear, ‘As surely as the LORD lives,’ in truth, in justice, and in righteousness, then the nations will be blessed by Him, and in Him they will glory.”

4:3

For this is what the LORD says to the men of Judah and Jerusalem: “Break up your unplowed ground, and do not sow among the thorns.

4:4

Circumcise yourselves to the LORD, and remove the foreskins of your hearts, O men of Judah and people of Jerusalem. Otherwise, My wrath will break out like fire and burn with no one to extinguish it, because of your evil deeds.”

4:5

הַגִּידוּannounce בִיהוּדָהin Judah וּבִירוּשָׁלִַםin Jerusalem הַשְׁמִיעוּproclaim וְאִמְרוּand say תִּקְעוּblow שׁוֹפָרram’s horn בָּאָרֶץthroughout the land קִרְאוּcry מַלְאוּaloud וְאִמְרוּand say הֵאָסְפוּassemble וְנָבוֹאָהflee אֶלto עָרֵיcities הַמִּבְצָרthe fortified

Announce in Judah, proclaim in Jerusalem, and say: “Blow the ram’s horn throughout the land. Cry aloud and say, ‘Assemble yourselves and let us flee to the fortified cities.’

4:6

שְׂאוּraise נֵסsignal flag צִיּוֹנָהtoward Zion הָעִיזוּrefuge אַלdo not תַּעֲמֹדוּdelay כִּיfor רָעָהdisaster אָנֹכִיam מֵבִיאbringing מִצָּפוֹןfrom the north וְשֶׁבֶרand destruction גָּדוֹלterrible

Raise a signal flag toward Zion. Seek refuge! Do not delay! For I am bringing disaster from the north, and terrible destruction.

4:7

עָלָהgone אַרְיֵהlion מִסֻּבְּכוֹfrom his thicket וּמַשְׁחִיתand a destroyer גּוֹיִםnations נָסַעset יָצָאout מִמְּקֹמוֹleft his lair לָשׂוּםto lay אַרְצֵךְyour land לְשַׁמָּהwaste עָרַיִךְyour cities תִּצֶּינָהruins מֵאֵיןfrom without יוֹשֵׁבuninhabited

A lion has gone up from his thicket, and a destroyer of nations has set out. He has left his lair to lay waste your land. Your cities will be reduced to ruins and lie uninhabited.

4:8

עַלon זֹאתthis חִגְרוּput שַׂקִּיםsackcloth סִפְדוּmourn וְהֵילִילוּand wail כִּיfor לֹאnot שָׁבturned חֲרוֹןfierce אַףanger יְהֹוָהLORD מִמֶּנּוּfrom us

So put on sackcloth, mourn and wail, for the fierce anger of the LORD has not turned away from us.”

4:9

וְהָיָהand be בַיּוֹםin that day הַהוּאthat נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD יֹאבַדperish לֵבheart הַמֶּלֶךְthe king וְלֵבcourage הַשָּׂרִיםofficials וְנָשַׁמּוּwill lose הַכֹּהֲנִיםpriests וְהַנְּבִיאִיםand the prophets יִתְמָהוּastounded

“In that day,” declares the LORD, “the king and officials will lose their courage. The priests will tremble in fear, and the prophets will be astounded.”

4:10

וָאֹמַרthen I said אֲהָהּah אֲדֹנָיLord יְהוִהGOD אָכֵןhow הַשֵּׁאcompletely הִשֵּׁאתָdeceived לָעָםthis people הַזֶּהthis וְלִירוּשָׁלִַםand Jerusalem לֵאמֹרby saying שָׁלוֹםpeace יִהְיֶהbe לָכֶםto you וְנָגְעָהand touched חֶרֶבsword עַדuntil הַנָּפֶשׁour throats

Then I said, “Ah, Lord GOD, how completely You have deceived this people and Jerusalem by saying, ‘You will have peace,’ while a sword is at our throats.”

4:11

בָּעֵתat that time הַהִיאthe will יֵאָמֵרsaid לָעָםto this people הַזֶּהthis וְלִירוּשָׁלִַםand to Jerusalem רוּחַwind צַחsearing שְׁפָיִיםbarren heights בַּמִּדְבָּרin the desert דֶּרֶךְtoward בַּתdaughter עַמִּיmy people לוֹאnot לִזְרוֹתto winnow וְלוֹאor לְהָבַרto sift

At that time it will be said to this people and to Jerusalem, “A searing wind from the barren heights in the desert blows toward the daughter of My people, but not to winnow or to sift;

4:12

רוּחַwind מָלֵאstrong מֵאֵלֶּהfrom these יָבוֹאcomes לִיto me עַתָּהnow גַּםalso אֲנִיI אֲדַבֵּרpronounce מִשְׁפָּטִיםjudgments אוֹתָםthem

a wind too strong for that comes from Me. Now I also pronounce judgments against them.”

4:13

הִנֵּהbehold כַּעֲנָנִיםlike the clouds יַעֲלֶהadvances וְכַסּוּפָהlike the whirlwind מַרְכְּבוֹתָיוhis chariots קַלּוּswifter מִנְּשָׁרִיםthan eagles סוּסָיוhorses are אוֹיWoe לָנוּto us כִּיfor שֻׁדָּדְנוּruined

Behold, he advances like the clouds, his chariots like the whirlwind. His horses are swifter than eagles. Woe to us, for we are ruined!

4:14

כַּבְּסִיwash מֵרָעָהevil from לִבֵּךְyour heart O יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem לְמַעַןso תִּוָּשֵׁעִיsaved עַדhow מָתַיlong תָּלִיןharbor בְּקִרְבֵּךְwithin you מַחְשְׁבוֹתthoughts אוֹנֵךְwicked

Wash the evil from your heart, O Jerusalem, so that you may be saved. How long will you harbor wicked thoughts within you?

4:15

כִּיfor קוֹלvoice מַגִּידresounds מִדָּןfrom Dan וּמַשְׁמִיעַproclaiming אָוֶןdisaster מֵהַרfrom the hills אֶפְרָיִםEphraim

For a voice resounds from Dan, proclaiming disaster from the hills of Ephraim.

4:16

הַזְכִּירוּwarn לַגּוֹיִםthe nations הִנֵּהbehold הַשְׁמִיעוּproclaim עַלto יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem נֹצְרִיםbesieging בָּאִיםcomes מֵאֶרֶץland הַמֶּרְחָקa distant וַיִּתְּנוּthey raise עַלagainst עָרֵיcities יְהוּדָהJudah קוֹלָםtheir voices

Warn the nations now! Proclaim to Jerusalem: “A besieging army comes from a distant land; they raise their voices against the cities of Judah.

4:17

כְּשֹׁמְרֵיlike men guarding שָׂדַיfield הָיוּthey עָלֶיהָon her מִסָּבִיבsurround כִּיfor אֹתִיme מָרָתָהrebelled against נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD

They surround her like men guarding a field, because she has rebelled against Me,” declares the LORD.

4:18

דַּרְכֵּךְyour ways וּמַעֲלָלַיִךְand deeds עָשׂוֹbrought אֵלֶּהthis לָךְto you זֹאתthis רָעָתֵךְyour punishment כִּיfor מָרbitter כִּיbecause נָגַעpierces עַדto לִבֵּךְheart

“Your ways and deeds have brought this upon you. This is your punishment; how bitter it is, because it pierces to the heart!”

4:19

מֵעַיmy anguish מֵעַיanguish I אוֹחִילָהpain קִירוֹתoh לִבִּיchest my heart הֹמֶהpounds לִּיto me לִבִּיmy heart לֹאnot אַחֲרִישׁsilent כִּיfor קוֹלsound שׁוֹפָרhorn שָׁמַעַתְּheard נַפְשִׁיI have תְּרוּעַתshout מִלְחָמָהbattle

My anguish, my anguish! I writhe in pain! Oh, the pain in my chest! My heart pounds within me; I cannot be silent. For I have heard the sound of the horn, the alarm of battle.

4:20

שֶׁבֶרdisaster עַלon שֶׁבֶרdisaster נִקְרָאproclaimed כִּיfor שֻׁדְּדָהlaid כָּלwhole הָאָרֶץland פִּתְאֹםwaste שֻׁדְּדוּdestroyed אֹהָלַיmy tents רֶגַעinstant יְרִיעֹתָיmy curtains

Disaster after disaster is proclaimed, for the whole land is laid waste. My tents are destroyed in an instant, my curtains in a moment.

4:21

עַדhow מָתַיlong אֶרְאֶהsee נֵּסsignal flag אֶשְׁמְעָהhear קוֹלsound שׁוֹפָרhorn

How long must I see the signal flag and hear the sound of the horn?

4:22

כִּיfor אֱוִילfools עַמִּיmy people אוֹתִיme לֹאnot יָדָעוּknown בָּנִיםchildren סְכָלִיםfoolish הֵמָּהthey are וְלֹאand not נְבוֹנִיםunderstanding הֵמָּהthey חֲכָמִיםskilled הֵמָּהare לְהָרַעin doing evil וּלְהֵיטִיבto do good לֹאnot יָדָעוּknow

“For My people are fools; they have not known Me. They are foolish children, without understanding. They are skilled in doing evil, but they know not how to do good.”

4:23

רָאִיתִיlooked אֶת[obj] הָאָרֶץthe earth וְהִנֵּהand it was תֹהוּformless וָבֹהוּand void וְאֶלI looked to הַשָּׁמַיִםthe heavens וְאֵיןno אוֹרָםhad light

I looked at the earth, and it was formless and void; I looked to the heavens, and they had no light.

4:24

רָאִיתִיlooked הֶהָרִיםthe mountains וְהִנֵּהand behold רֹעֲשִׁיםquaking וְכָלall הַגְּבָעוֹתthe hills הִתְקַלְקָלוּswaying

I looked at the mountains, and behold, they were quaking; all the hills were swaying.

4:25

רָאִיתִיlooked וְהִנֵּהand saw אֵיןno הָאָדָםman וְכָלwas left all עוֹףbirds הַשָּׁמַיִםthe air had נָדָדוּfled

I looked, and no man was left; all the birds of the air had fled.

4:26

רָאִיתִיlooked וְהִנֵּהand saw הַכַּרְמֶלthe fruitful הַמִּדְבָּרwas a desert וְכָלall עָרָיוits cities נִתְּצוּtorn down מִפְּנֵיbefore יְהוָהLORD מִפְּנֵיbefore חֲרוֹןfierce אַפּוֹhis anger

I looked, and the fruitful land was a desert. All its cities were torn down before the LORD, before His fierce anger.

4:27

כִּיfor כֹהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD שְׁמָמָהdesolate תִהְיֶהthe כָּלwhole הָאָרֶץland וְכָלָהfinish לֹאnot אֶעֱשֶׂהdo

For this is what the LORD says: “The whole land will be desolate, but I will not finish its destruction.

4:28

Therefore the earth will mourn and the heavens above will grow dark. I have spoken, I have planned, and I will not relent or turn back.”

4:29

מִקּוֹלat the sound פָּרָשׁhorseman וְרֹמֵהhorseman and קֶשֶׁתarcher בֹּרַחַתflees כָּלevery הָעִירcity בָּאוּenter בֶּעָבִיםthe thickets וּבַכֵּפִיםand among the rocks עָלוּclimb כָּלevery הָעִירcity עֲזוּבָהabandoned וְאֵיןno יוֹשֵׁבinhabitant בָּהֵןin drink them אִישׁleft

Every city flees at the sound of the horseman and archer. They enter the thickets and climb among the rocks. Every city is abandoned; no inhabitant is left.

4:30

And you, O devastated one, what will you do, though you dress yourself in scarlet, though you adorn yourself with gold jewelry, though you enlarge your eyes with paint? You adorn yourself in vain; your lovers despise you; they want to take your life.

4:31

כִּיfor קוֹלcry כְּחוֹלָהI שָׁמַעְתִּיhear צָרָהanguish כְּמַבְכִּירָהlike one bearing קוֹלcry בַּתdaughter צִיּוֹןZion תִּתְיַפֵּחַgasping תְּפָרֵשׂbreath כַּפֶּיהָher hands אוֹיWoe נָאplease לִיto me כִּיfor עָיְפָהfaints נַפְשִׁיmy soul לְהֹרְגִיםbefore the murderers

For I hear a cry like a woman in labor, a cry of anguish like one bearing her first child— the cry of the Daughter of Zion gasping for breath, stretching out her hands to say, “Woe is me, for my soul faints before the murderers!”
