Jeremiah 12

Jeremiah's complaint and God's answer

12:1

צַדִּיקrighteous אַתָּהyou יְהוָהLORD כִּיwhen אָרִיבplead אֵלֶיךָto you אַךְyet מִשְׁפָּטִיםjudgments אֲדַבֵּרwish אוֹתָךְyou מַדּוּעַwhy דֶּרֶךְway רְשָׁעִיםwicked צָלֵחָהprosper שָׁלוּease כָּלall בֹּגְדֵיfaithless בָגֶדlive

Righteous are You, O LORD, when I plead before You. Yet about Your judgments I wish to contend with You: Why does the way of the wicked prosper? Why do all the faithless live at ease?

12:2

נְטַעְתָּםplanted them גַּםand שֹׁרָשׁוּroot יֵלְכוּthey גַּםand עָשׂוּproduced פֶרִיfruit קָרוֹבnear אַתָּהyou בְּפִיהֶםon their lips וְרָחוֹקbut far מִכִּלְיוֹתֵיהֶםfrom their hearts

You planted them, and they have taken root. They have grown and produced fruit. You are ever on their lips, but far from their hearts.

12:3

וְאַתָּהbut you יְהוָהLORD יְדַעְתָּנִיknow me תִּרְאֵנִיsee me וּבָחַנְתָּand test לִבִּיmy heart אִתָּךְwith you הַתִּקֵםdrag away the wicked כְּצֹאןlike sheep לְטִבְחָהto the slaughter וְהַקְדִּשֵׁםand set them apart לְיוֹםfor the day הֲרֵגָהcarnage

But You know me, O LORD; You see me and test my heart toward You. Drag away the wicked like sheep to the slaughter and set them apart for the day of carnage.

12:4

עַדhow מָתַיlong תֶּאֱבַלmourn הָאָרֶץthe land וְעֵשֶׂבand the grass כָּלevery הַשָּׂדֶהfield יִיבָשׁwithered מֵרָעַתevil יֹשְׁבֵיresidents בָהּits the סָפְתָהswept בְהֵמוֹתanimals וָעוֹףand birds כִּיfor אָמְרוּsaid לֹאnot יִרְאֶהsee אֶת[obj] אַחֲרִיתֵנוּour end

How long will the land mourn and the grass of every field be withered? Because of the evil of its residents, the animals and birds have been swept away, for the people have said, “He cannot see what our end will be.”

12:5

כִּיif אֶתwith רַגְלִיםfoot רַצְתָּהthey וַיַּלְאוּךָworn you וְאֵיךְhow can תְּתַחֲרֶהcompete אֶתwith הַסּוּסִיםhorses וּבְאֶרֶץland שָׁלוֹםpeaceful אַתָּהyou בוֹטֵחַstumble וְאֵיךְhow תַּעֲשֶׂהdo בִּגְאוֹןin the thickets הַיַּרְדֵּןthe Jordan

“If you have raced with men on foot and they have worn you out, how can you compete with horses? If you stumble in a peaceful land, how will you do in the thickets of the Jordan?

12:6

כִּיfor גַםalso אַחֶיךָyour brothers your וּבֵיתown household אָבִיךָfather’s גַּםeven הֵמָּהthey בָּגְדוּbetrayed בָךְin you גַּםeven הֵמָּהthey קָרְאוּcried אַחֲרֶיךָafter you מָלֵאdo אַלnot תַּאֲמֵןtrust בָּםin them כִּיfor יְדַבְּרוּspeak אֵלֶיךָto you טוֹבוֹתwell

Even your brothers— your own father’s household— even they have betrayed you; even they have cried aloud against you. Do not trust them, though they speak well of you.

12:7

עָזַבְתִּיforsaken אֶת[obj] בֵּיתִיmy house נָטַשְׁתִּיabandoned אֶת[obj] נַחֲלָתִיmy inheritance I נָתַתִּיgiven אֶת[obj] יְדִדוּתlove נַפְשִׁיmy life בְּכַףinto the hands אֹיְבֶיהָher enemies

I have forsaken My house; I have abandoned My inheritance. I have given the love of My life into the hands of her enemies.

12:8

הָיְתָהbecome לִּיto נַחֲלָתִיinheritance כְּאַרְיֵהlike a lion בַיָּעַרin the forest נָתְנָהroared עָלַיagainst me בְּקוֹלָהּin her voice עַלon כֵּןtherefore שְׂנֵאתִיהָhate her

My inheritance has become to Me like a lion in the forest. She has roared against Me; therefore I hate her.

12:9

הַעַיִטprey צָבוּעַbird נַחֲלָתִיmy inheritance לִיto me הַעַיִטprey סָבִיבcircling עָלֶיהָagainst her לְכוּgo אִסְפוּgather כָּלall חַיַּתbeasts הַשָּׂדֶהthe field הֵתָיוּbring לְאָכְלָהto devour

Is not My inheritance to Me like a speckled bird of prey with other birds of prey circling against her? Go, gather all the beasts of the field; bring them to devour her.

12:10

רֹעִיםshepherds רַבִּיםmany שִׁחֲתוּdestroyed כַרְמִיmy vineyard בֹּסְסוּtrampled אֶת[obj] חֶלְקָתִיmy plot נָתְנוּturned אֶת[obj] חֶלְקַתfield חֶמְדָּתִיmy pleasant לְמִדְבַּרa wasteland שְׁמָמָהdesolate

Many shepherds have destroyed My vineyard; they have trampled My plot of ground. They have turned My pleasant field into a desolate wasteland.

12:11

שָׂמָהּmade it לִשְׁמָמָהa desolation אָבְלָהmourns עָלַיon me שְׁמֵמָהdesolate נָשַׁמָּהwaste כָּלall הָאָרֶץthe land כִּיbut אֵיןno אִישׁman שָׂםtakes it עַלto לֵבheart

They have made it a desolation; desolate before Me, it mourns. All the land is laid waste, but no man takes it to heart.

12:12

עַלover כָּלall שְׁפָיִםbarren heights בַּמִּדְבָּרin the wilderness בָּאוּcome שֹׁדְדִיםdestroyers כִּיfor חֶרֶבsword לַיהוָהLORD אֹכְלָהdevours מִקְצֵהfrom one end אֶרֶץearth וְעַדto קְצֵהother הָאָרֶץthe land אֵיןno שָׁלוֹםpeace לְכָלto all בָּשָׂרflesh

Over all the barren heights in the wilderness the destroyers have come, for the sword of the LORD devours from one end of the earth to the other. No flesh has peace.

12:13

זָרְעוּsown חִטִּיםwheat וְקֹצִיםbut thorns קָצָרוּharvested נֶחְלוּill לֹאno יוֹעִלוּavail וּבֹשׁוּshame מִתְּבוּאֹתֵיכֶםof your harvest מֵחֲרוֹןfierce אַףanger יְהוָהLORD

They have sown wheat but harvested thorns. They have exhausted themselves to no avail. Bear the shame of your harvest because of the fierce anger of the LORD.”

12:14

This is what the LORD says: “As for all My evil neighbors who attack the inheritance that I bequeathed to My people Israel, I am about to uproot them from their land, and I will uproot the house of Judah from among them.

12:15

וְהָיָהand be אַחֲרֵיafter נָתְשִׁיuprooted I אוֹתָםthem אָשׁוּבonce וְרִחַמְתִּיםagain have compassion on them וַהֲשִׁבֹתִיםand return each אִישׁone לְנַחֲלָתוֹto his inheritance וְאִישׁand man לְאַרְצוֹto his land

But after I have uprooted them, I will once again have compassion on them and return each one to his inheritance and to his land.

12:16

וְהָיָהand be אִםif לָמֹדdiligently learn יִלְמְדוּlearn אֶת[obj] דַּרְכֵיways עַמִּיmy people לְהִשָּׁבֵעַand swear בִּשְׁמִיby my name חַיsurely יְהוָהLORD כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had לִמְּדוּtaught אֶת[obj] עַמִּיmy people לְהִשָּׁבֵעַto swear בַּבָּעַלby Baal וְנִבְנוּestablished בְּתוֹךְamong עַמִּיmy people

And if they will diligently learn the ways of My people and swear by My name, saying, ‘As surely as the LORD lives’—just as they once taught My people to swear by Baal—then they will be established among My people.

12:17

וְאִםbut if לֹאnot יִשְׁמָעוּobey וְנָתַשְׁתִּיuproot אֶת[obj] הַגּוֹיthat nation הַהוּאwill נָתוֹשׁuproot וְאַבֵּדand destroy נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD

But if they will not obey, then I will uproot that nation; I will uproot it and destroy it, declares the LORD.”
