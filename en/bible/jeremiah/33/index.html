Jeremiah 33

A promise of restoration and of the Branch of David

While Jeremiah was still confined in the courtyard of the guard, the word of the LORD came to him a second time:

33:2

“Thus says the LORD who made the earth, the LORD who formed it and established it, the LORD is His name:

33:3

Call to Me, and I will answer and show you great and unsearchable things you do not know.

33:4

For this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says about the houses of this city and the palaces of the kings of Judah that have been torn down for defense against the siege ramps and the sword:

33:5

The Chaldeans are coming to fight and to fill those places with the corpses of the men I will strike down in My anger and in My wrath. I have hidden My face from this city because of all its wickedness.

33:6

הִנְנִיI מַעֲלֶהbring לָּהּto it אֲרֻכָהhealth וּמַרְפֵּאand healing וּרְפָאתִיםheal its people וְגִלֵּיתִיand reveal לָהֶםto them עֲתֶרֶתabundance שָׁלוֹםpeace וֶאֱמֶתand truth

Nevertheless, I will bring to it health and healing, and I will heal its people and reveal to them the abundance of peace and truth.

33:7

וַהֲשִׁבֹתִיI will אֶת[obj] שְׁבוּתrestore יְהוּדָהJudah וְאֵתand [obj] שְׁבוּתfrom captivity יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וּבְנִתִיםand will rebuild them כְּבָרִאשֹׁנָהas in former

I will restore Judah and Israel from captivity and will rebuild them as in former times.

33:8

וְטִהַרְתִּיםcleanse them מִכָּלfrom all עֲוֺנָםiniquity they אֲשֶׁרhave חָטְאוּcommitted לִיto me וְסָלַחְתִּיand will forgive לְכָלall עֲוֺנוֹתֵיהֶםtheir sins אֲשֶׁרthat חָטְאוּsins לִיto me וַאֲשֶׁרand what פָּשְׁעוּrebellion בִיagainst me

And I will cleanse them from all the iniquity they have committed against Me, and will forgive all their sins of rebellion against Me.

33:9

וְהָיְתָהwill לִּיto me לְשֵׁםrenown שָׂשׂוֹןjoy לִתְהִלָּהpraise וּלְתִפְאֶרֶתand glory לְכֹלbefore all גּוֹיֵיnations הָאָרֶץthe earth אֲשֶׁרwho יִשְׁמְעוּhear אֶת[obj] כָּלall הַטּוֹבָהthe good אֲשֶׁרthat אָנֹכִיI עֹשֶׂהdo אֹתָםthem וּפָחֲדוּthey tremble וְרָגְזוּwill tremble in awe עַלin כָּלall הַטּוֹבָהthe goodness וְעַלand on כָּלall הַשָּׁלוֹםprosperity אֲשֶׁרthat אָנֹכִיI עֹשֶׂהprovide לָּהּfor it

So this city will bring Me renown, joy, praise, and glory before all the nations of the earth, who will hear of all the good I do for it. They will tremble in awe because of all the goodness and prosperity that I will provide for it.

33:10

כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD עוֹדagain יִשָּׁמַעheard בַּמָּקוֹםin this place הַזֶּהthis אֲשֶׁרthat אַתֶּםyou אֹמְרִיםsay חָרֵבwasteland הוּאis מֵאֵיןa without אָדָםman וּמֵאֵיןand from no בְּהֵמָהbeast בְּעָרֵיin the cities יְהוּדָהJudah וּבְחֻצוֹתand in the streets יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem הַנְשַׁמּוֹתthat are deserted מֵאֵיןfrom without אָדָםman וּמֵאֵיןand from no יוֹשֵׁבinhabited וּמֵאֵיןand from no בְּהֵמָהbeast

This is what the LORD says: In this place you say is a wasteland without man or beast, in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem that are deserted—inhabited by neither man nor beast—there will be heard again

33:11

the sounds of joy and gladness, the voices of the bride and bridegroom, and the voices of those bringing thank offerings into the house of the LORD, saying: ‘Give thanks to the LORD of Hosts, for the LORD is good; His loving devotion endures forever.’ For I will restore the land from captivity as in former times, says the LORD.

33:12

כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts עוֹדlonger יִהְיֶהbe בַּמָּקוֹםplace הַזֶּהthis הֶחָרֵבdesolate מֵאֵיןwithout אָדָםman וְעַדand to בְּהֵמָהbeast וּבְכָלand in all עָרָיוits cities נְוֵהpastures רֹעִיםshepherds מַרְבִּצִיםrest צֹאןflocks

This is what the LORD of Hosts says: In this desolate place, without man or beast, and in all its cities, there will once more be pastures for shepherds to rest their flocks.

33:13

בְּעָרֵיin the cities הָהָרthe hill בְּעָרֵיcountry הַשְּׁפֵלָהthe foothills וּבְעָרֵיcities הַנֶּגֶבthe Negev וּבְאֶרֶץin the land בִּנְיָמִןBenjamin וּבִסְבִיבֵיsurrounding יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem וּבְעָרֵיand in the cities יְהוּדָהJudah עֹדagain תַּעֲבֹרְנָהpass הַצֹּאןthe flocks עַלon יְדֵיhands מוֹנֶהcounts אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD

In the cities of the hill country, the foothills, and the Negev, in the land of Benjamin and the cities surrounding Jerusalem, and in the cities of Judah, the flocks will again pass under the hands of the one who counts them, says the LORD.

33:14

הִנֵּהbehold יָמִיםdays בָּאִיםcoming נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD וַהֲקִמֹתִיfulfill אֶת[obj] הַדָּבָרthe promise הַטּוֹבgracious that אֲשֶׁרthat דִּבַּרְתִּיspoken אֶלto בֵּיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וְעַלand on בֵּיתhouse יְהוּדָהJudah

Behold, the days are coming, declares the LORD, when I will fulfill the gracious promise that I have spoken to the house of Israel and the house of Judah.

33:15

בַּיָּמִיםin those days הָהֵםthose וּבָעֵתand at that time הַהִיאwill אַצְמִיחַcause to sprout לְדָוִדfor David צֶמַחbranch צְדָקָהrighteous וְעָשָׂהadminister מִשְׁפָּטjustice וּצְדָקָהand righteousness בָּאָרֶץin the land

In those days and at that time I will cause to sprout for David a righteous Branch, and He will administer justice and righteousness in the land.

33:16

בַּיָּמִיםin those days הָהֵםthose תִּוָּשַׁעsaved יְהוּדָהJudah וִירוּשָׁלִַםand Jerusalem תִּשְׁכּוֹןdwell לָבֶטַחsecurely וְזֶהand this אֲשֶׁרwhich יִקְרָאcalled לָהּto her יְהוָהLORD צִדְקֵנוּour righteousness

In those days Judah will be saved, and Jerusalem will dwell securely, and this is the name by which it will be called: The LORD Our Righteousness.

33:17

כִּיfor כֹהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD לֹאnever יִכָּרֵתnever lack לְדָוִדDavid אִישׁman יֹשֵׁבsit עַלon כִּסֵּאthrone בֵיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

For this is what the LORD says: David will never lack a man to sit on the throne of the house of Israel,

33:18

וְלַכֹּהֲנִיםnor will the priests הַלְוִיִּםwho are Levites לֹאnot יִכָּרֵתfail אִישׁman מִלְּפָנָיbefore me מַעֲלֶהoffer עוֹלָהburnt offerings וּמַקְטִירto burn מִנְחָהgrain offerings וְעֹשֶׂהand to present זֶּבַחsacrifices כָּלall הַיָּמִיםthe days

nor will the priests who are Levites ever fail to have a man before Me to offer burnt offerings, to burn grain offerings, and to present sacrifices.”

33:19

וַיְהִיand was דְּבַרword יְהוָהLORD אֶלto יִרְמְיָהוּJeremiah לֵאמוֹרto saying

And the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah:

33:20

“This is what the LORD says: If you can break My covenant with the day and My covenant with the night, so that day and night cease to occupy their appointed time,

33:21

then My covenant may also be broken with David My servant and with My ministers the Levites who are priests, so that David will not have a son to reign on his throne.

33:22

As the hosts of heaven cannot be counted and as the sand on the seashore cannot be measured, so too will I multiply the descendants of My servant David and the Levites who minister before Me.”

33:23

Moreover, the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah:

“Have you not noticed what these people are saying: ‘The LORD has rejected the two families He had chosen’? So they despise My people and no longer regard them as a nation.

33:25

This is what the LORD says: If I have not established My covenant with the day and the night and the fixed order of heaven and earth,

33:26

then I would also reject the descendants of Jacob and of My servant David, so as not to take from his descendants rulers over the descendants of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. For I will restore them from captivity and will have compassion on them.”
