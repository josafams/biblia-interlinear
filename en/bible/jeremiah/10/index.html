Jeremiah 10

Idols and the true God

Open in the interlinear reader

10:1

שִׁמְעוּhear אֶת[obj] הַדָּבָרthe word אֲשֶׁרthat דִּבֶּרspeaks יְהוָהLORD עֲלֵיכֶםto you בֵּיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

Hear the word that the LORD speaks to you, O house of Israel.

10:2

כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD אֶלto דֶּרֶךְways הַגּוֹיִםthe nations אַלdo not תִּלְמָדוּlearn וּמֵאֹתוֹתby the signs הַשָּׁמַיִםthe heavens אַלor תֵּחָתּוּterrified כִּיfor יֵחַתּוּterrified הַגּוֹיִםthe nations מֵהֵמָּהby them

This is what the LORD says: “Do not learn the ways of the nations or be terrified by the signs in the heavens, though the nations themselves are terrified by them.

10:3

כִּיfor חֻקּוֹתcustoms הָעַמִּיםthe peoples הֶבֶלworthless הוּאhe כִּיfor עֵץtree מִיַּעַרfrom the forest כְּרָתוֹcut מַעֲשֵׂהshaped יְדֵיhands חָרָשׁcraftsman בַּמַּעֲצָדwith a chisel

For the customs of the peoples are worthless; they cut down a tree from the forest; it is shaped with a chisel by the hands of a craftsman.

10:4

בְּכֶסֶףwith silver וּבְזָהָבand gold יְיַפֵּהוּadorn it בְּמַסְמְרוֹתnails וּבְמַקָּבוֹתwith hammer and יְחַזְּקוּםharden them וְלוֹאnot יָפִיקtotter

They adorn it with silver and gold and fasten it with hammer and nails, so that it will not totter.

10:5

כְּתֹמֶרlike scarecrows מִקְשָׁהcucumber הֵמָּהthey וְלֹאand not יְדַבֵּרוּspeak נָשׂוֹאtake יִנָּשׂוּאcarried כִּיbecause לֹאnot יִצְעָדוּwalk אַלdo not תִּירְאוּfear מֵהֶםof them כִּיfor לֹאno יָרֵעוּharm וְגַםand also הֵיטֵיבgood אֵיןno אוֹתָםthem

Like scarecrows in a cucumber patch, their idols cannot speak. They must be carried because they cannot walk. Do not fear them, for they can do no harm, and neither can they do any good.”

10:6

מֵאֵיןis none כָּמוֹךָlike you יְהוָהLORD גָּדוֹלgreat אַתָּהyou are וְגָדוֹלmighty שִׁמְךָyour name בִּגְבוּרָהin power

There is none like You, O LORD. You are great, and Your name is mighty in power.

10:7

מִיwho לֹאnot יִרָאֲךָfear you מֶלֶךְking הַגּוֹיִםnations this כִּיfor לְךָto you יָאָתָהdue כִּיfor בְכָלamong all חַכְמֵיwise הַגּוֹיִםthe nations וּבְכָלand in all מַלְכוּתָםtheir kingdoms מֵאֵיןis none כָּמוֹךָlike you

Who would not fear You, O King of nations? This is Your due. For among all the wise men of the nations, and in all their kingdoms, there is none like You.

10:8

וּבְאַחַתbut they are altogether יִבְעֲרוּsenseless וְיִכְסָלוּand foolish מוּסַרinstructed הֲבָלִיםworthless עֵץwood הוּאhe

But they are altogether senseless and foolish, instructed by worthless idols made of wood!

10:9

כֶּסֶףsilver מְרֻקָּעhammered מִתַּרְשִׁישׁfrom Tarshish יוּבָאbrought וְזָהָבand gold מֵאוּפָזfrom Uphaz מַעֲשֵׂהwork חָרָשׁcraftsman וִידֵיhands צוֹרֵףgoldsmith תְּכֵלֶתblue וְאַרְגָּמָןand purple לְבוּשָׁםtheir clothes מַעֲשֵׂהfashioned חֲכָמִיםskilled כֻּלָּםall

Hammered silver is brought from Tarshish, and gold from Uphaz— the work of a craftsman from the hands of a goldsmith. Their clothes are blue and purple, all fashioned by skilled workers.

10:10

וַיהוָהbut the LORD אֱלֹהִיםGod אֱמֶתtrue הוּאis אֱלֹהִיםGod חַיִּיםliving וּמֶלֶךְand king עוֹלָםeternal מִקִּצְפּוֹat his wrath תִּרְעַשׁquakes הָאָרֶץthe earth וְלֹאand not יָכִלוּendure גוֹיִםnations זַעְמוֹhis indignation

But the LORD is the true God; He is the living God and eternal King. The earth quakes at His wrath, and the nations cannot endure His indignation.

10:11

כִּדְנָהlike this תֵּאמְרוּןtell לְהוֹםto them אֱלָהַיָּאthese gods who דִּיthe שְׁמַיָּאthe heavens וְאַרְקָאnor the earth לָאnot עֲבַדוּmade יֵאבַדוּperish מֵאַרְעָאfrom this earth וּמִןand from תְּחוֹתunder שְׁמַיָּאthese heavens אֵלֶּהthese

Thus you are to tell them: “These gods, who have made neither the heavens nor the earth, will perish from this earth and from under these heavens.”

10:12

עֹשֵׂהmade אֶרֶץearth בְּכֹחוֹby his power he מֵכִיןestablished תֵּבֵלworld בְּחָכְמָתוֹby his wisdom וּבִתְבוּנָתוֹby his understanding נָטָהstretched שָׁמָיִםheavens

The LORD made the earth by His power; He established the world by His wisdom and stretched out the heavens by His understanding.

10:13

לְקוֹלroar תִּתּוֹcauses הֲמוֹןthunders מַיִםwaters בַּשָּׁמַיִםin the heavens וַיַּעֲלֶהrise נְשִׂאִיםclouds מִקְצֵהfrom the ends הָאָרֶץthe earth בְּרָקִיםlightning לַמָּטָרthe rain עָשָׂהdone וַיּוֹצֵאand brings forth רוּחַwind מֵאֹצְרֹתָיוfrom his storehouses

When He thunders, the waters in the heavens roar; He causes the clouds to rise from the ends of the earth. He generates the lightning with the rain and brings forth the wind from His storehouses.

10:14

נִבְעַרsenseless כָּלevery אָדָםman מִדַּעַתand of knowledge הֹבִישׁshame כָּלevery צוֹרֵףgoldsmith מִפָּסֶלby his idols כִּיfor שֶׁקֶרfraud נִסְכּוֹhis molten images וְלֹאno רוּחַbreath בָּםin them

Every man is senseless and devoid of knowledge; every goldsmith is put to shame by his idols. For his molten images are a fraud, and there is no breath in them.

10:15

הֶבֶלworthless הֵמָּהare מַעֲשֵׂהwork תַּעְתֻּעִיםmocked בְּעֵתin the time פְּקֻדָּתָםtheir punishment they יֹאבֵדוּperish

They are worthless, a work to be mocked. In the time of their punishment they will perish.

10:16

לֹאnot כְאֵלֶּהlike these חֵלֶקportion יַעֲקֹבJacob כִּיfor יוֹצֵרMaker הַכֹּלall הוּאhe וְיִשְׂרָאֵלand Israel שֵׁבֶטtribe נַחֲלָתוֹhis inheritance יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts שְׁמוֹhis name

The Portion of Jacob is not like these, for He is the Maker of all things, and Israel is the tribe of His inheritance— the LORD of Hosts is His name.

10:17

אִסְפִּיgather מֵאֶרֶץfrom land כִּנְעָתֵךְthis you יֹשֶׁבֶתlive בַּמָּצוֹרunder siege

Gather up your belongings from this land, you who live under siege.

10:18

כִּיfor כֹהthis what אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD הִנְנִיbehold קוֹלֵעַsling אֶת[obj] יוֹשְׁבֵיinhabitants הָאָרֶץthe land בַּפַּעַםat this time הַזֹּאתthis וַהֲצֵרוֹתִיand bring distress לָהֶםto them לְמַעַןso יִמְצָאוּcaptured

For this is what the LORD says: “Behold, at this time I will sling out the inhabitants of the land and bring distress upon them so that they may be captured.”

10:19

אוֹיWoe לִיto me עַלon שִׁבְרִיbrokenness my נַחְלָהwound is grievous מַכָּתִיwound וַאֲנִיbut I אָמַרְתִּיsaid אַךְbut זֶהthis חֳלִיsickness וְאֶשָּׂאֶנּוּbear it

Woe to me because of my brokenness; my wound is grievous! But I said, “This is truly my sickness, and I must bear it.”

10:20

אָהֳלִיmy tent שֻׁדָּדdestroyed וְכָלand all מֵיתָרַיropes נִתָּקוּsnapped בָּנַיsons have יְצָאֻנִיdeparted from me וְאֵינָםand are no אֵיןno נֹטֶהpitch עוֹדmore אָהֳלִיmy tent וּמֵקִיםor set יְרִיעוֹתָיmy curtains

My tent is destroyed, and all its ropes are snapped. My sons have departed from me and are no more. I have no one left to pitch my tent or set up my curtains.

10:21

כִּיfor נִבְעֲרוּsenseless הָרֹעִיםthe shepherds וְאֶתand [obj] יְהוָהLORD לֹאnot דָרָשׁוּseek עַלon כֵּןtherefore לֹאnot הִשְׂכִּילוּprospered וְכָלand all מַרְעִיתָםtheir flock נָפוֹצָהscattered

For the shepherds have become senseless; they do not seek the LORD. Therefore they have not prospered, and all their flock is scattered.

10:22

קוֹלsound שְׁמוּעָהreport הִנֵּהbehold בָאָהcoming וְרַעַשׁcommotion גָּדוֹלgreat מֵאֶרֶץfrom the land צָפוֹןnorth לָשׂוּםto put אֶת[obj] עָרֵיcities יְהוּדָהJudah שְׁמָמָהdesolation מְעוֹןhaunt תַּנִּיםjackals

Listen! The sound of a report is coming— a great commotion from the land to the north. The cities of Judah will be made a desolation, a haunt for jackals.

10:23

יָדַעְתִּיknow יְהוָהLORD כִּיthat לֹאnot לָאָדָםa man’s דַּרְכּוֹway לֹאnot no לְאִישׁown one who הֹלֵךְwalks וְהָכִיןdirects אֶת[obj] צַעֲדוֹhis own steps

I know, O LORD, that a man’s way is not his own; no one who walks directs his own steps.

10:24

יַסְּרֵנִיcorrect me יְהוָהLORD אַךְbut only בְּמִשְׁפָּטwith justice אַלnot בְּאַפְּךָin your anger פֶּןor תַּמְעִטֵנִיbring me

Correct me, O LORD, but only with justice— not in Your anger, or You will bring me to nothing.

10:25

שְׁפֹךְpour חֲמָתְךָyour wrath עַלon הַגּוֹיִםthe nations אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot יְדָעוּךָacknowledge you וְעַלand on מִשְׁפָּחוֹתfamilies אֲשֶׁרthat בְּשִׁמְךָon your name לֹאnot קָרָאוּcall כִּיfor אָכְלוּdevoured אֶת[obj] יַעֲקֹבJacob וַאֲכָלֻהוּthey have consumed him וַיְכַלֻּהוּand finished him וְאֶתand [obj] נָוֵהוּdevastated his הֵשַׁמּוּdevastated homeland

Pour out Your wrath on the nations that do not acknowledge You, and on the families that do not call on Your name. For they have devoured Jacob; they have consumed him and finished him off; they have devastated his homeland.
