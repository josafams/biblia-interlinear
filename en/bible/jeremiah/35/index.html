Jeremiah 35

The faithfulness of the Rechabites

Open in the interlinear reader

35:1

הַדָּבָרthe word אֲשֶׁרthat הָיָהcame אֶלto יִרְמְיָהוּJeremiah מֵאֵתfrom the יְהוָהLORD בִּימֵיin the days יְהוֹיָקִיםJehoiakim בֶּןson יֹאשִׁיָּהוּJosiah מֶלֶךְking יְהוּדָהJudah לֵאמֹרto saying

This is the word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD in the days of Jehoiakim son of Josiah king of Judah:

35:2

הָלוֹךְgo אֶלto בֵּיתhouse הָרֵכָבִיםthe Rechabites וְדִבַּרְתָּspeak אוֹתָםthem וַהֲבִאוֹתָםand bring them בֵּיתhouse יְהוָהLORD אֶלto אַחַתone הַלְּשָׁכוֹתthe chambers וְהִשְׁקִיתָto offer אוֹתָםthem יָיִןwine

“Go to the house of the Rechabites, speak to them, and bring them to one of the chambers of the house of the LORD to offer them a drink of wine.”

35:3

וָאֶקַּחso I took אֶת[obj] יַאֲזַנְיָהJaazaniah בֶןson יִרְמְיָהוּJeremiah בֶּןson חֲבַצִּנְיָהHabazziniah וְאֶתand [obj] אֶחָיוhis brothers וְאֶתand [obj] כָּלall בָּנָיוhis sons וְאֵתand [obj] כָּלentire בֵּיתhouse הָרֵכָבִיםthe Rechabites

So I took Jaazaniah son of Jeremiah, the son of Habazziniah, and his brothers and all his sons—the entire house of the Rechabites—

35:4

וָאָבִאand I brought אֹתָםthem בֵּיתhouse יְהוָהLORD אֶלto לִשְׁכַּתchamber בְּנֵיsons חָנָןHanan בֶּןson יִגְדַּלְיָהוּIgdaliah אִישׁman הָאֱלֹהִיםGod this אֲשֶׁרthat אֵצֶלnear לִשְׁכַּתchamber הַשָּׂרִיםthe officials אֲשֶׁרwhich מִמַּעַלwas above לְלִשְׁכַּתthe chamber מַעֲשֵׂיָהוּMaaseiah בֶןson שַׁלֻּםShallum שֹׁמֵרkeeper הַסַּףthe doorkeeper

and I brought them into the house of the LORD, to a chamber occupied by the sons of Hanan son of Igdaliah, a man of God. This room was near the chamber of the officials, which was above the chamber of Maaseiah son of Shallum the doorkeeper.

35:5

וָאֶתֵּןthen I set לִפְנֵיbefore בְּנֵיsons בֵיתhouse הָרֵכָבִיםthe Rechabites גְּבִעִיםcups מְלֵאִיםfull יַיִןwine וְכֹסוֹתand some וָאֹמַרand I said אֲלֵיהֶםto them שְׁתוּdrink יָיִןwine

Then I set pitchers full of wine and some cups before the men of the house of the Rechabites, and I said to them, “Drink some wine.”

35:6

וַיֹּאמְרוּwe do לֹאnot נִשְׁתֶּהdrink יָּיִןwine כִּיfor יוֹנָדָבJonadab בֶּןson רֵכָבRechab אָבִינוּour forefather צִוָּהcommanded עָלֵינוּon us לֵאמֹרreplied לֹאnot תִשְׁתּוּdrink יַיִןwine אַתֶּםyou וּבְנֵיכֶםnor your descendants עַדto עוֹלָםever

“We do not drink wine,” they replied, “for our forefather Jonadab son of Rechab commanded us, ‘Neither you nor your descendants are ever to drink wine.

35:7

וּבַיִתhouse לֹאnot תִבְנוּbuild וְזֶרַעseed לֹאor תִזְרָעוּsow וְכֶרֶםvineyard לֹאor תִטָּעוּplant וְלֹאnot יִהְיֶהbe לָכֶםfor you כִּיfor בָּאֳהָלִיםin tents תֵּשְׁבוּlive כָּלall יְמֵיכֶםyour days לְמַעַןso תִּחְיוּlives יָמִיםlong רַבִּיםtime עַלin פְּנֵיface הָאֲדָמָהthe land אֲשֶׁרthat אַתֶּםyou גָּרִיםwander שָׁםthere

Nor are you ever to build a house or sow seed or plant a vineyard. Those things are not for you. Instead, you must live in tents all your lives, so that you may live a long time in the land where you wander.’

35:8

וַנִּשְׁמַעobeyed בְּקוֹלthe voice יְהוֹנָדָבJonadab בֶּןson רֵכָבRechab אָבִינוּour forefather לְכֹלin all אֲשֶׁרthat צִוָּנוּcommanded us לְבִלְתִּיnot שְׁתוֹתdrunk יַיִןwine כָּלall יָמֵינוּour days אֲנַחְנוּwe נָשֵׁינוּour wives בָּנֵינוּour sons וּבְנֹתֵינוּand daughters

And we have obeyed the voice of our forefather Jonadab son of Rechab in all he commanded us. So we have not drunk wine all our lives—neither we nor our wives nor our sons and daughters.

35:9

וּלְבִלְתִּיand to cease בְּנוֹתbuilt בָּתִּיםhouses לְשִׁבְתֵּנוּto live we וְכֶרֶםvineyards וְשָׂדֶהor fields וָזֶרַעor crops לֹאnot יִהְיֶהowned לָּנוּto us

Nor have we built houses in which to live, and we have not owned any vineyards or fields or crops.

35:10

וַנֵּשֶׁבlived בָּאֳהָלִיםin tents וַנִּשְׁמַעand have obeyed וַנַּעַשׂand done כְּכֹלlike all אֲשֶׁרthat צִוָּנוּcommanded יוֹנָדָבJonadab אָבִינוּforefather

But we have lived in tents and have obeyed and done exactly as our forefather Jonadab commanded us.

35:11

וַיְהִיand was בַּעֲלוֹתmarched נְבוּכַדְרֶאצַּרNebuchadnezzar מֶלֶךְking בָּבֶלBabylon אֶלinto הָאָרֶץthe land וַנֹּאמֶרwe said בֹּאוּcome וְנָבוֹאlet us go into יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem מִפְּנֵיescape חֵילarmies הַכַּשְׂדִּיםthe Chaldeans וּמִפְּנֵיand from face חֵילarmy אֲרָםArameans וַנֵּשֶׁבremained בִּירוּשָׁלִָםin Jerusalem

So when Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon marched into the land, we said: ‘Come, let us go into Jerusalem to escape the armies of the Chaldeans and the Arameans.’ So we have remained in Jerusalem.”

35:12

וַיְהִיand was דְּבַרword יְהוָהLORD אֶלto יִרְמְיָהוּJeremiah לֵאמֹרto saying

Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah:

35:13

כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael הָלֹךְgo וְאָמַרְתָּand tell לְאִישׁthe men יְהוּדָהJudah וּלְיוֹשְׁבֵיand the residents יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem הֲלוֹאyou not תִקְחוּaccept מוּסָרdiscipline לִשְׁמֹעַand obey אֶלto דְּבָרַיmy words נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD

“This is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: Go and tell the men of Judah and the residents of Jerusalem: ‘Will you not accept discipline and obey My words?’ declares the LORD.

35:14

הוּקַםrise אֶת[obj] דִּבְרֵיwords יְהוֹנָדָבJonadab בֶּןson רֵכָבRechab אֲשֶׁרhave צִוָּהcommanded אֶת[obj] בָּנָיוhis sons לְבִלְתִּיnot to שְׁתוֹתdrink יַיִןwine וְלֹאnot שָׁתוּdrunk עַדto הַיּוֹםthis day הַזֶּהthis very כִּיbecause שָׁמְעוּobeyed אֵת[obj] מִצְוַתcommand אֲבִיהֶםtheir forefather וְאָנֹכִיbut I דִּבַּרְתִּיspoken אֲלֵיכֶםto you הַשְׁכֵּםagain וְדַבֵּרand you וְלֹאnot שְׁמַעְתֶּםobeyed אֵלָיto me

The words of Jonadab son of Rechab have been carried out. He commanded his sons not to drink wine, and they have not drunk it to this very day because they have obeyed the command of their forefather. But I have spoken to you again and again, and you have not obeyed Me!

35:15

וָאֶשְׁלַחsent אֲלֵיכֶםto you אֶת[obj] כָּלall עֲבָדַיmy servants הַנְּבִאִיםthe prophets הַשְׁכֵּיםagain וְשָׁלֹחַagain לֵאמֹרI שֻׁבוּturn נָאnow אִישׁeach מִדַּרְכּוֹways הָרָעָהwicked וְהֵיטִיבוּand correct מַעַלְלֵיכֶםyour actions וְאַלdo not תֵּלְכוּgo אַחֲרֵיafter אֱלֹהִיםother אֲחֵרִיםgods לְעָבְדָםto serve them וּשְׁבוּlive אֶלto הָאֲדָמָהthe land אֲשֶׁרthat נָתַתִּיgiven לָכֶםto you וְלַאֲבֹתֵיכֶםand your fathers you וְלֹאnot הִטִּיתֶםinclined אֶת[obj] אָזְנְכֶםyour ear וְלֹאor שְׁמַעְתֶּםlistened אֵלָיto me

Again and again I have sent you all My servants the prophets, proclaiming: ‘Turn now, each of you, from your wicked ways, and correct your actions. Do not go after other gods to serve them. Live in the land that I have given to you and your fathers.’ But you have not inclined your ear or listened to Me.

35:16

כִּיfor הֵקִימוּchanging בְּנֵיsons יְהוֹנָדָבJonadab בֶּןson רֵכָבRechab carried אֶת[obj] מִצְוַתcommand אֲבִיהֶםtheir forefather אֲשֶׁרthat צִוָּםgave them וְהָעָםbut these people הַזֶּהhave לֹאnot שָׁמְעוּlistened אֵלָיto me

Yes, the sons of Jonadab son of Rechab carried out the command their forefather gave them, but these people have not listened to Me.

35:17

לָכֵןtherefore כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod צְבָאוֹתHosts אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael הִנְנִיbehold I מֵבִיאbring אֶלto יְהוּדָהJudah וְאֶלand to כָּלall יוֹשְׁבֵיresidents יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem אֵת[obj] כָּלall הָרָעָהthe disaster אֲשֶׁרhave דִּבַּרְתִּיpronounced עֲלֵיהֶםagainst them יַעַןbecause דִּבַּרְתִּיspoken אֲלֵיהֶםto them וְלֹאnot שָׁמֵעוּobeyed וָאֶקְרָאcalled לָהֶםto them וְלֹאnot עָנוּanswered

Therefore this is what the LORD God of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: ‘Behold, I will bring to Judah and to all the residents of Jerusalem all the disaster I have pronounced against them, because I have spoken to them but they have not obeyed, and I have called to them but they have not answered.’”

35:18

וּלְבֵיתto the house הָרֵכָבִיםthe Rechabites אָמַרsaid יִרְמְיָהוּJeremiah כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael יַעַןbecause אֲשֶׁרhave שְׁמַעְתֶּםobeyed עַלon מִצְוַתcommand יְהוֹנָדָבJonadab אֲבִיכֶםyour forefather וַתִּשְׁמְרוּand have kept אֶת[obj] כָּלall מִצְוֺתָיוhis commandments וַתַּעֲשׂוּand have done כְּכֹלdone all אֲשֶׁרthat צִוָּהcharged אֶתְכֶםyou

Then Jeremiah said to the house of the Rechabites: “This is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: ‘Because you have obeyed the command of your forefather Jonadab and have kept all his commandments and have done all that he charged you to do,

35:19

לָכֵןto therefore כֹּהthis what אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael לֹאnever יִכָּרֵתfail אִישׁman לְיוֹנָדָבJonadab בֶּןson רֵכָבRechab עֹמֵדstand לְפָנַיbefore me כָּלall הַיָּמִיםthe days

this is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: Jonadab son of Rechab will never fail to have a man to stand before Me.’”
