Jeremiah 6

The siege of Jerusalem announced

Open in the interlinear reader

6:1

הָעִזוּcover בְּנֵיsons בִניָמִןBenjamin מִקֶּרֶבfrom among יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem וּבִתְקוֹעַin Tekoa תִּקְעוּsound שׁוֹפָרram’s horn וְעַלover בֵּיתBeth הַכֶּרֶםsend שְׂאוּup מַשְׂאֵתsignal כִּיfor רָעָהdisaster נִשְׁקְפָהlooms מִצָּפוֹןfrom the north וְשֶׁבֶרeven destruction גָּדוֹלgreat

“Run for cover, O sons of Benjamin; flee from Jerusalem! Sound the ram’s horn in Tekoa; send up a signal over Beth-haccherem, for disaster looms from the north, even great destruction.

6:2

הַנָּוָהis beautiful וְהַמְּעֻנָּגָהand delicate דָּמִיתִיdestroy בַּתdaughter צִיּוֹןZion

Though she is beautiful and delicate, I will destroy the Daughter of Zion.

6:3

אֵלֶיהָagainst יָבֹאוּcome רֹעִיםshepherds וְעֶדְרֵיהֶםand flocks תָּקְעוּpitch עָלֶיהָon her אֹהָלִיםtents סָבִיבall around רָעוּtending אִישׁeach אֶת[obj] יָדוֹhis own portion

Shepherds and their flocks will come against her; they will pitch their tents all around her, each tending his own portion:

6:4

קַדְּשׁוּprepare עָלֶיהָagainst her מִלְחָמָהbattle קוּמוּrise וְנַעֲלֶהus attack בַצָּהֳרָיִםat noon אוֹיWoe לָנוּto us כִּיfor פָנָהturned הַיּוֹםthe daylight כִּיfor יִנָּטוּfading צִלְלֵיshadows עָרֶבevening

‘Prepare for battle against her; rise up, let us attack at noon. Woe to us, for the daylight is fading; the evening shadows grow long.

6:5

קוּמוּrise וְנַעֲלֶהus attack בַלָּיְלָהby night וְנַשְׁחִיתָהand destroy אַרְמְנוֹתֶיהָher fortresses

Rise up, let us attack by night and destroy her fortresses!’”

6:6

כִּיfor כֹהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts כִּרְתוּcut עֵצָהtrees וְשִׁפְכוּand raise a siege ramp עַלagainst יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem סֹלְלָהsiege ramp הִיאis הָעִירthis city הָפְקַדpunished כֻּלָּהּnothing עֹשֶׁקoppression בְּקִרְבָּהּin her midst

For this is what the LORD of Hosts says: “Cut down the trees and raise a siege ramp against Jerusalem. This city must be punished; there is nothing but oppression in her midst.

6:7

כְּהָקִירgushes בַּיִרwell מֵימֶיהָits water כֵּןso הֵקֵרָהpours רָעָתָהּher evil חָמָסviolence וָשֹׁדand destruction יִשָּׁמַעresound בָּהּin her עַלon פָּנַיbefore me תָּמִידever חֳלִיsickness וּמַכָּהand wounds

As a well gushes its water, so she pours out her evil. Violence and destruction resound in her; sickness and wounds are ever before Me.

6:8

הִוָּסְרִיforewarned יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem פֶּןor תֵּקַעturn נַפְשִׁיI will מִמֵּךְfrom you פֶּןor אֲשִׂימֵךְmake you שְׁמָמָהdesolation אֶרֶץland לוֹאnot נוֹשָׁבָהinhabitant

Be forewarned, O Jerusalem, or I will turn away from you; I will make you a desolation, a land without inhabitant.”

6:9

כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהֹוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts עוֹלֵלglean יְעוֹלְלוּthoroughly כַגֶּפֶןas a vine שְׁאֵרִיתremnant יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael הָשֵׁבrestore יָדְךָyour hand כְּבוֹצֵרlike a grape עַלover סַלְסִלּוֹתgatherer

This is what the LORD of Hosts says: “Glean the remnant of Israel as thoroughly as a vine. Pass your hand once more like a grape gatherer over the branches.”

6:10

עַלto מִיwhom אֲדַבְּרָהspeak וְאָעִידָהthis warning וְיִשְׁמָעוּwho will listen הִנֵּהlook עֲרֵלָהclosed אָזְנָםtheir ears וְלֹאand not יוּכְלוּcannot לְהַקְשִׁיבhear הִנֵּהsee דְבַרword יְהוָהLORD הָיָהbecome לָהֶםto them לְחֶרְפָּהfind לֹאno יַחְפְּצוּpleasure בוֹin it

To whom can I give this warning? Who will listen to me? Look, their ears are closed, so they cannot hear. See, the word of the LORD has become offensive to them; they find no pleasure in it.

6:11

וְאֵתand [obj] חֲמַתwrath יְהוָהLORD’s מָלֵאתִיfull נִלְאֵיתִיtired הָכִילholding שְׁפֹךְpour עַלon עוֹלָלchildren בַּחוּץin the street וְעַלand on סוֹדfriends בַּחוּרִיםyoung men יַחְדָּוtogether כִּיfor גַםboth אִישׁhusband עִםwith אִשָּׁהwife יִלָּכֵדוּcaptured זָקֵןold עִםwith מְלֵאthe old יָמִיםvery

But I am full of the LORD’s wrath; I am tired of holding it back. “Pour it out on the children in the street, and on the young men gathered together. For both husband and wife will be captured, the old and the very old alike.

6:12

וְנָסַבּוּturned בָתֵּיהֶםtheir houses לַאֲחֵרִיםto others שָׂדוֹתfields וְנָשִׁיםand wives יַחְדָּוtogether כִּיfor אַטֶּהstretch אֶת[obj] יָדִיmy hand עַלagainst יֹשְׁבֵיinhabitants הָאָרֶץthe land נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD

Their houses will be turned over to others, their fields and wives as well, for I will stretch out My hand against the inhabitants of the land,” declares the LORD.

6:13

כִּיfor מִקְּטַנָּםfrom the least of them וְעַדto גְּדוֹלָםgreatest כֻּלּוֹall are בּוֹצֵעַgreedy בָּצַעgain וּמִנָּבִיאfrom prophet וְעַדto כֹּהֵןpriest כֻּלּוֹall עֹשֶׂהpractice שָּׁקֶרdeceit

“For from the least of them to the greatest, all are greedy for gain; from prophet to priest, all practice deceit.

6:14

וַיְרַפְּאוּthey dress אֶת[obj] שֶׁבֶרwound עַמִּיmy people עַלon נְקַלָּהvery little care לֵאמֹרsaying שָׁלוֹםpeace שָׁלוֹםpeace וְאֵיןno שָׁלוֹםpeace

They dress the wound of My people with very little care, saying, ‘Peace, peace,’ when there is no peace at all.

6:15

הֹבִישׁוּare כִּיfor תוֹעֵבָהabomination עָשׂוּcommitted גַּםhave בּוֹשׁshame לֹאno יֵבוֹשׁוּshame גַּםeven הַכְלִיםall לֹאnot יָדָעוּknow לָכֵןto blush so יִפְּלוּfall בַנֹּפְלִיםamong the fallen בְּעֵתwhen פְּקַדְתִּיםpunish them יִכָּשְׁלוּcollapse אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD

Are they ashamed of the abomination they have committed? No, they have no shame at all; they do not even know how to blush. So they will fall among the fallen; when I punish them, they will collapse,” says the LORD.

6:16

כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD עִמְדוּstand עַלon דְּרָכִיםcrossroads וּרְאוּand look וְשַׁאֲלוּask לִנְתִבוֹתpaths עוֹלָםancient אֵיwhere זֶהis דֶרֶךְway הַטּוֹבthe good וּלְכוּthen walk בָהּin it וּמִצְאוּfind מַרְגּוֹעַrest לְנַפְשְׁכֶםfor your souls וַיֹּאמְרוּbut they said לֹאnot נֵלֵךְwalk

This is what the LORD says: “Stand at the crossroads and look. Ask for the ancient paths: ‘Where is the good way?’ Then walk in it, and you will find rest for your souls. But they said, ‘We will not walk in it!’

6:17

וַהֲקִמֹתִיI appointed עֲלֵיכֶםover you צֹפִיםwatchmen הַקְשִׁיבוּlisten לְקוֹלfor the sound שׁוֹפָרram’s horn וַיֹּאמְרוּbut they answered לֹאnot נַקְשִׁיבlisten

I appointed watchmen over you and said, ‘Listen for the sound of the ram’s horn.’ But they answered, ‘We will not listen!’

6:18

לָכֵןtherefore שִׁמְעוּhear הַגּוֹיִםnations וּדְעִיand learn עֵדָהcongregations אֶת[obj] אֲשֶׁרthat בָּםto them

Therefore hear, O nations, and learn, O congregations, what will happen to them.

6:19

שִׁמְעִיhear הָאָרֶץearth הִנֵּהbehold אָנֹכִיam מֵבִיאbringing רָעָהdisaster אֶלto הָעָםthis people הַזֶּהthis פְּרִיfruit מַחְשְׁבוֹתָםtheir own schemes כִּיbecause עַלon דְּבָרַיmy word לֹאno הִקְשִׁיבוּattention וְתוֹרָתִיmy instruction וַיִּמְאֲסוּand have rejected בָהּin her

Hear, O earth! I am bringing disaster on this people, the fruit of their own schemes, because they have paid no attention to My word and have rejected My instruction.

6:20

לָמָּהwhat זֶּהthis לִיto me לְבוֹנָהfrankincense מִשְּׁבָאfrom Sheba תָבוֹאcome וְקָנֶהor sweet הַטּוֹבcane מֵאֶרֶץland מֶרְחָקdistant עֹלוֹתֵיכֶםyour burnt offerings לֹאnot לְרָצוֹןacceptable וְזִבְחֵיכֶםyour sacrifices לֹאnot עָרְבוּplease לִיto me

What use to Me is frankincense from Sheba or sweet cane from a distant land? Your burnt offerings are not acceptable; your sacrifices do not please Me.”

6:21

לָכֵןtherefore כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD הִנְנִיsays I נֹתֵןlay אֶלto הָעָםthis people הַזֶּהthis מִכְשֹׁלִיםstumbling וְכָשְׁלוּblocks בָםin them אָבוֹתfathers וּבָנִיםand sons יַחְדָּוalike שָׁכֵןneighbors וְרֵעוֹfriends and וְאָבָדוּwill perish

Therefore this is what the LORD says: “I will lay stumbling blocks before this people; fathers and sons alike will be staggered; friends and neighbors will perish.”

6:22

כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD הִנֵּהbehold עַםarmy בָּאcoming מֵאֶרֶץfrom the land צָפוֹןnorth וְגוֹיnation גָּדוֹלgreat יֵעוֹרstirred מִיַּרְכְּתֵיfrom the ends אָרֶץearth

This is what the LORD says: “Behold, an army is coming from the land of the north; a great nation is stirred up from the ends of the earth.

6:23

קֶשֶׁתbow וְכִידוֹןand spear יַחֲזִיקוּgrasp אַכְזָרִיcruel הוּאhe וְלֹאand not יְרַחֵמוּmerciless קוֹלָםtheir voice כַּיָּםlike the sea יֶהֱמֶהunease וְעַלupon סוּסִיםhorses יִרְכָּבוּride עָרוּךְlined כְּאִישׁlike men לַמִּלְחָמָהin formation עָלַיִךְagainst you בַּתdaughter צִיּוֹןZion

They grasp the bow and spear; they are cruel and merciless. Their voice roars like the sea, and they ride upon horses, lined up like men in formation against you, O Daughter of Zion.”

6:24

שָׁמַעְנוּheard אֶת[obj] שָׁמְעוֹreport רָפוּhang limp יָדֵינוּour hands צָרָהanguish הֶחֱזִיקַתְנוּgripped us חִילpain כַּיּוֹלֵדָהlabor

We have heard the report; our hands hang limp. Anguish has gripped us, pain like that of a woman in labor.

6:25

אַלdo not תֵּצְאוּgo הַשָּׂדֶהthe fields וּבַדֶּרֶךְthe road אַלdo not תֵּלֵכוּwalk כִּיfor חֶרֶבsword לְאֹיֵבenemy מָגוֹרterror מִסָּבִיבon every side

Do not go out to the fields; do not walk the road. For the enemy has a sword; terror is on every side.

6:26

בַּתdaughter עַמִּיmy people חִגְרִיdress שָׂקsackcloth וְהִתְפַּלְּשִׁיand roll בָאֵפֶרin ashes אֵבֶלmourn יָחִידonly son עֲשִׂיdo לָךְfor מִסְפַּדwailing תַּמְרוּרִיםbitter כִּיfor פִתְאֹםsuddenly יָבֹאcome הַשֹּׁדֵדthe destroyer עָלֵינוּupon us

O daughter of my people, dress yourselves in sackcloth and roll in ashes. Mourn with bitter wailing, as you would for an only son, for suddenly the destroyer will come upon us.

6:27

בָּחוֹןhave נְתַתִּיךָappointed you בְעַמִּיto examine my people מִבְצָרore וְתֵדַעso you may know וּבָחַנְתָּand try אֶת[obj] דַּרְכָּםtheir ways

“I have appointed you to examine My people like ore, so you may know and try their ways.

6:28

כֻּלָּםall are סָרֵיhardened סוֹרְרִיםrebels הֹלְכֵיwalking רָכִילslanderers נְחֹשֶׁתbronze וּבַרְזֶלand iron כֻּלָּםall of them מַשְׁחִיתִיםcorrupt הֵמָּהare

All are hardened rebels, walking around as slanderers. They are bronze and iron; all of them are corrupt.

6:29

נָחַרbellows fiercely מַפֻּחַblow blasting מֵאֵשׁfire תַּםrefining עֹפָרֶתlead לַשָּׁוְאvain צָרַףproceeds צָרוֹףgoldsmith וְרָעִיםfor the wicked לֹאnot נִתָּקוּpurged

The bellows blow fiercely, blasting away the lead with fire. The refining proceeds in vain, for the wicked are not purged.

6:30

כֶּסֶףsilver נִמְאָסrejected קָרְאוּcalled לָהֶםto them כִּיbecause מָאַסrejected יְהוָהLORD בָּהֶםin them

They are called rejected silver, because the LORD has rejected them.”
