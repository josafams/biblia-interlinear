Jeremiah 16

Jeremiah forbidden to marry; the future return

Open in the interlinear reader

16:1

וַיְהִיand was דְבַרword יְהוָהLORD אֵלַיto me לֵאמֹרto saying

Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

16:2

לֹאnot תִקַּחmarry לְךָto you אִשָּׁהmust וְלֹאor יִהְיוּbe לְךָto you בָּנִיםsons וּבָנוֹתor daughters בַּמָּקוֹםin this place הַזֶּהthis

“You must not marry or have sons or daughters in this place.”

16:3

כִּיfor כֹהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD עַלon הַבָּנִיםthe sons וְעַלand on הַבָּנוֹתdaughters הַיִּלּוֹדִיםborn בַּמָּקוֹםin this place הַזֶּהthis וְעַלand on אִמֹּתָםmothers הַיֹּלְדוֹתwho bore אוֹתָםthem וְעַלand on אֲבוֹתָםfathers הַמּוֹלִדִיםwho fathered אוֹתָםthem בָּאָרֶץin this land הַזֹּאתthis

For this is what the LORD says concerning the sons and daughters born in this place, and the mothers who bore them, and the fathers who fathered them in this land:

16:4

מְמוֹתֵיdeadly תַחֲלֻאִיםdiseases יָמֻתוּdie לֹאnot יִסָּפְדוּmourned וְלֹאor but יִקָּבֵרוּburied לְדֹמֶןlike dung עַלon פְּנֵיface הָאֲדָמָהthe ground יִהְיוּbe וּבַחֶרֶבby sword וּבָרָעָבand famine יִכְלוּfinished וְהָיְתָהand be נִבְלָתָםtheir corpses לְמַאֲכָלbecome food לְעוֹףfor the birds הַשָּׁמַיִםthe air וּלְבֶהֱמַתand beasts הָאָרֶץthe earth

“They will die from deadly diseases. They will not be mourned or buried, but will lie like dung on the ground. They will be finished off by sword and famine, and their corpses will become food for the birds of the air and beasts of the earth.”

16:5

כִּיfor כֹהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD אַלdo not תָּבוֹאenter בֵּיתhouse מַרְזֵחַfuneral meal וְאַלdo not תֵּלֵךְgo לִסְפּוֹדto mourn וְאַלor תָּנֹדsympathy לָהֶםfor כִּיfor אָסַפְתִּיremoved אֶת[obj] שְׁלוֹמִיmy peace my מֵאֵתfrom the הָעָםthis people הַזֶּהthis נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] הַחֶסֶדloving devotion וְאֶתand [obj] הָרַחֲמִיםmy compassion

Indeed, this is what the LORD says: “Do not enter a house where there is a funeral meal. Do not go to mourn or show sympathy, for I have removed from this people My peace, My loving devotion, and My compassion,” declares the LORD.

16:6

וּמֵתוּdie גְדֹלִיםgreat וּקְטַנִּיםand small בָּאָרֶץin this land הַזֹּאתthis לֹאnot יִקָּבֵרוּburied וְלֹאor יִסְפְּדוּmourned לָהֶםto them וְלֹאand not יִתְגֹּדַדcut וְלֹאhimself or יִקָּרֵחַshave לָהֶםfor them

“Both great and small will die in this land. They will not be buried or mourned, nor will anyone cut himself or shave his head for them.

16:7

וְלֹאno יִפְרְסוּfood לָהֶםto them עַלon אֵבֶלmourn לְנַחֲמוֹto comfort עַלon מֵתdead וְלֹאnot יַשְׁקוּeven אוֹתָםthem כּוֹסcup תַּנְחוּמִיםconsolation עַלfor אָבִיוfather וְעַלand on אִמּוֹmother

No food will be offered to comfort those who mourn the dead; not even a cup of consolation will be given for the loss of a father or mother.

16:8

וּבֵיתhouse where מִשְׁתֶּהfeasting לֹאnot תָבוֹאenter לָשֶׁבֶתand sit אוֹתָםthem לֶאֱכֹלto eat וְלִשְׁתּוֹתand drink

You must not enter a house where there is feasting and sit down with them to eat and drink.

16:9

כִּיfor כֹהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael הִנְנִיsays I am מַשְׁבִּיתremove מִןfrom הַמָּקוֹםthis place הַזֶּהthis לְעֵינֵיכֶםbefore your very eyes וּבִימֵיכֶםand in your days קוֹלsounds שָׂשׂוֹןjoy וְקוֹלand sound שִׂמְחָהgladness קוֹלvoice חָתָןbridegroom וְקוֹלvoices כַּלָּהbride

For this is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: I am going to remove from this place, before your very eyes and in your days, the sounds of joy and gladness, the voices of the bride and bridegroom.

16:10

וְהָיָהand be כִּיfor תַגִּידtell לָעָםthese people הַזֶּהthis אֵת[obj] כָּלall הַדְּבָרִיםthese things הָאֵלֶּהthese וְאָמְרוּthey will ask אֵלֶיךָto you עַלon מֶהwhy דִבֶּרpronounced יְהוָהLORD עָלֵינוּagainst us אֵת[obj] כָּלall הָרָעָהthis disaster הַגְּדוֹלָהgreat הַזֹּאתthis וּמֶהwhat עֲוֺנֵנוּour guilt וּמֶהwhat חַטָּאתֵנוּsin that we אֲשֶׁרhave חָטָאנוּcommitted לַיהוָהagainst the LORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God

When you tell these people all these things, they will ask you, ‘Why has the LORD pronounced all this great disaster against us? What is our guilt? What is the sin that we have committed against the LORD our God?’

16:11

וְאָמַרְתָּanswer אֲלֵיהֶםto them עַלon אֲשֶׁרhave עָזְבוּforsaken אֲבוֹתֵיכֶםyour fathers אוֹתִיme נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD וַיֵּלְכוּand went אַחֲרֵיfollowed אֱלֹהִיםother אֲחֵרִיםgods וַיַּעַבְדוּםand served וַיִּשְׁתַּחֲווּand worshiped לָהֶםto them וְאֹתִיand me עָזָבוּabandoned וְאֶתand [obj] תּוֹרָתִיmy instruction לֹאnot שָׁמָרוּkeep

Then you are to answer them: ‘It is because your fathers have forsaken Me, declares the LORD, and followed other gods, and served and worshiped them. They abandoned Me and did not keep My instruction.

16:12

וְאַתֶּםand you הֲרֵעֹתֶםevil לַעֲשׂוֹתhave done מֵאֲבוֹתֵיכֶםthan your fathers וְהִנְּכֶםsee הֹלְכִיםway אִישׁeach אַחֲרֵיafter שְׁרִרוּתstubbornness לִבּוֹhis heart הָרָעevil לְבִלְתִּיto not שְׁמֹעַhears אֵלָיto me

And you have done more evil than your fathers. See how each of you follows the stubbornness of his evil heart instead of obeying Me.

16:13

וְהֵטַלְתִּיcast אֶתְכֶםyou מֵעַלfrom the הָאָרֶץthis land הַזֹּאתthis עַלon הָאָרֶץinto a land אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot יְדַעְתֶּםknown אַתֶּםyou וַאֲבוֹתֵיכֶםnor your fathers וַעֲבַדְתֶּםwill serve שָׁםthere אֶת[obj] אֱלֹהִיםgods אֲחֵרִיםother gods יוֹמָםday וָלַיְלָהand night אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot אֶתֵּןwill לָכֶםto you חֲנִינָהfavor

So I will cast you out of this land into a land that neither you nor your fathers have known. There you will serve other gods day and night, for I will show you no favor.’

16:14

לָכֵןto therefore הִנֵּהbehold יָמִיםdays בָּאִיםcoming נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD וְלֹאno יֵאָמֵרsay עוֹדlonger חַיsurely יְהוָהLORD אֲשֶׁרwho הֶעֱלָהbrought אֶת[obj] בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites מֵאֶרֶץland מִצְרָיִםEgypt

Yet behold, the days are coming, declares the LORD, when they will no longer say, ‘As surely as the LORD lives, who brought the Israelites up out of the land of Egypt.’

16:15

כִּיfor אִםif חַיsurely יְהוָהLORD אֲשֶׁרwho הֶעֱלָהbrought אֶת[obj] בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites מֵאֶרֶץland צָפוֹןnorth וּמִכֹּלand all הָאֲרָצוֹתthe other lands אֲשֶׁרwhich had הִדִּיחָםbanished them שָׁמָּהthere toward וַהֲשִׁבֹתִיםwill return them עַלto אַדְמָתָםtheir land אֲשֶׁרthat נָתַתִּיgave לַאֲבוֹתָםto their forefathers

Instead they will say, ‘As surely as the LORD lives, who brought the Israelites up out of the land of the north and all the other lands to which He had banished them.’ For I will return them to their land that I gave to their forefathers.

16:16

הִנְנִיnow I שֹׁלֵחַsend לְדַיָּגִיםfor fishermen רַבִּיםmany נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD וְדִיגוּםcatch them וְאַחֲרֵיafter כֵןthat אֶשְׁלַחsend לְרַבִּיםfor many צַיָּדִיםhunters וְצָדוּםhunt them מֵעַלon כָּלevery הַרmountain וּמֵעַלand from the כָּלall גִּבְעָהhill וּמִנְּקִיקֵיeven from the clefts הַסְּלָעִיםthe rocks

But for now I will send for many fishermen, declares the LORD, and they will catch them. After that I will send for many hunters, and they will hunt them down on every mountain and hill, even from the clefts of the rocks.

16:17

כִּיfor עֵינַיmy eyes עַלon כָּלall דַּרְכֵיהֶםtheir ways לֹאnot נִסְתְּרוּhidden מִלְּפָנָיfrom my face וְלֹאnot נִצְפַּןconcealed עֲוֺנָםtheir guilt מִנֶּגֶדfrom distance עֵינָיmy eyes

For My eyes are on all their ways. They are not hidden from My face, and their guilt is not concealed from My eyes.

16:18

וְשִׁלַּמְתִּיrepay רִאשׁוֹנָהfirst מִשְׁנֵהdouble עֲוֺנָםtheir iniquity וְחַטָּאתָםand their sin עַלon חַלְּלָםthey have defiled אֶת[obj] אַרְצִיmy land בְּנִבְלַתwith the carcasses שִׁקּוּצֵיהֶםdetestable idols וְתוֹעֲבוֹתֵיהֶםtheir abominations מָלְאוּfilled אֶת[obj] נַחֲלָתִיmy inheritance

And I will first repay them double their iniquity and their sin, because they have defiled My land with the carcasses of their detestable idols, and they have filled My inheritance with their abominations.”

16:19

יְהוָהLORD עֻזִּיmy strength וּמָעֻזִּיand my fortress וּמְנוּסִיmy refuge בְּיוֹםin the day צָרָהdistress אֵלֶיךָto you גּוֹיִםnations יָבֹאוּcome מֵאַפְסֵיfrom the ends אָרֶץearth וְיֹאמְרוּand they will say אַךְbut שֶׁקֶרlies נָחֲלוּinherited אֲבוֹתֵינוּour fathers הֶבֶלworthless idols וְאֵיןno בָּםin them מוֹעִילbenefit

O LORD, my strength and my fortress, my refuge in the day of distress, the nations will come to You from the ends of the earth, and they will say, “Our fathers inherited nothing but lies, worthless idols of no benefit at all.

16:20

הֲיַעֲשֶׂהcan make לּוֹfor himself אָדָםman אֱלֹהִיםgods וְהֵמָּהare לֹאnot אֱלֹהִיםgods

Can man make gods for himself? Such are not gods!”

16:21

לָכֵןtherefore הִנְנִיbehold I מוֹדִיעָםinform them בַּפַּעַםand this time הַזֹּאתI אוֹדִיעֵםthem know אֶת[obj] יָדִיmy power וְאֶתand [obj] גְּבוּרָתִיmy might וְיָדְעוּknow כִּיthat שְׁמִיmy name יְהוָהLORD

“Therefore behold, I will inform them, and this time I will make them know My power and My might; then they will know that My name is the LORD.
