Jeremiah 46

Oracle against Egypt

46:1

This is the word of the LORD about the nations—the word that came to Jeremiah the prophet

46:2

לְמִצְרַיִםconcerning Egypt עַלon חֵילarmy פַּרְעֹהPharaoh נְכוֹPharaoh מֶלֶךְking מִצְרַיִםEgypt אֲשֶׁרwhich הָיָהwas עַלon נְהַרEuphrates פְּרָתPharaoh בְּכַרְכְּמִשׁat Carchemish אֲשֶׁרthat הִכָּהdefeated נְבוּכַדְרֶאצַּרNebuchadnezzar מֶלֶךְking בָּבֶלBabylon בִּשְׁנַתyear הָרְבִיעִיתthe fourth לִיהוֹיָקִיםof Jehoiakim בֶּןson יֹאשִׁיָּהוּJosiah מֶלֶךְking יְהוּדָהJudah

concerning Egypt and the army of Pharaoh Neco king of Egypt, which was defeated at Carchemish on the Euphrates River by Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon in the fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah king of Judah:

46:3

עִרְכוּdeploy מָגֵןshields small וְצִנָּהsmall and large וּגְשׁוּadvance לַמִּלְחָמָהfor battle

“Deploy your shields, small and large; advance for battle!

46:4

אִסְרוּharness הַסּוּסִיםthe horses וַעֲלוּmount הַפָּרָשִׁיםthe steeds וְהִתְיַצְּבוּtake your positions בְּכוֹבָעִיםwith helmets מִרְקוּpolish הָרְמָחִיםspears לִבְשׁוּput הַסִּרְיֹנֹתon armor

Harness the horses; mount the steeds; take your positions with helmets on! Polish your spears; put on armor!

46:5

מַדּוּעַwhy רָאִיתִיseeing הֵמָּהthey חַתִּיםare terrified נְסֹגִיםterrified they אָחוֹרretreating וְגִבּוֹרֵיהֶםtheir warriors יֻכַּתּוּretreating וּמָנוֹסdefeated they נָסוּflee וְלֹאand not הִפְנוּlooking מָגוֹרterror מִסָּבִיבon every side נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD

Why am I seeing this? They are terrified, they are retreating; their warriors are defeated, they flee in haste without looking back; terror is on every side!” declares the LORD.

46:6

אַלdo not יָנוּסflee הַקַּלthe swift וְאַלand do not יִמָּלֵטescape הַגִּבּוֹרthe warrior צָפוֹנָהnorth עַלon יַדhand נְהַרriver פְּרָתEuphrates כָּשְׁלוּstumble וְנָפָלוּand fall

“The swift cannot flee, and the warrior cannot escape! In the north by the River Euphrates they stumble and fall.

46:7

מִיwho זֶהthis כַּיְאֹרthe Nile like יַעֲלֶהrising כַּנְּהָרוֹתrivers יִתְגָּעֲשׁוּchurn מֵימָיוwhose waters

Who is this, rising like the Nile, like rivers whose waters churn?

46:8

מִצְרַיִםEgypt כַּיְאֹרlike the Nile יַעֲלֶהits וְכַנְּהָרוֹתlike rivers יִתְגֹּעֲשׁוּchurn מָיִםwaters וַיֹּאמֶרand said אַעֲלֶהrise אֲכַסֶּהcover אֶרֶץearth אֹבִידָהdestroy עִירcities וְיֹשְׁבֵיand people בָהּin her

Egypt rises like the Nile, and its waters churn like rivers, boasting, ‘I will rise and cover the earth; I will destroy the cities and their people.’

46:9

עֲלוּadvance הַסּוּסִיםhorses וְהִתְהֹלְלוּrace furiously הָרֶכֶבchariots וְיֵצְאוּlet הַגִּבּוֹרִיםthe warriors כּוּשׁCush וּפוּטand Put תֹּפְשֵׂיcarrying מָגֵןshields וְלוּדִיםmen of Lydia תֹּפְשֵׂיLydia the דֹּרְכֵיdrawing קָשֶׁתbow

Advance, O horses! Race furiously, O chariots! Let the warriors come forth— Cush and Put carrying their shields, men of Lydia drawing the bow.

46:10

וְהַיּוֹםfor that day הַהוּאthat לַאדֹנָיto the Lord יְהוִהGOD צְבָאוֹתHosts יוֹםday נְקָמָהvengeance לְהִנָּקֵםvengeance against מִצָּרָיוagainst his foes וְאָכְלָהwill devour חֶרֶבsword וְשָׂבְעָהsatisfied וְרָוְתָהquenched מִדָּמָםwith their blood כִּיfor זֶבַחsacrifice לַאדֹנָיLord יְהוִהGOD צְבָאוֹתHosts בְּאֶרֶץin the land צָפוֹןnorth אֶלto נְהַרriver פְּרָתEuphrates

For that day belongs to the Lord GOD of Hosts, a day of vengeance against His foes. The sword will devour until it is satisfied, until it is quenched with their blood. For the Lord GOD of Hosts will hold a sacrifice in the land of the north by the River Euphrates.

46:11

עֲלִיgo גִלְעָדGilead וּקְחִיand bring צֳרִיbalm בְּתוּלַתvirgin בַּתdaughter מִצְרָיִםEgypt לַשָּׁוְאin vain הִרְבֵּיתmany רְפֻאוֹתtry remedies תְּעָלָהdug trench אֵיןno לָךְfor you

Go up to Gilead for balm, O Virgin Daughter of Egypt! In vain you try many remedies, but for you there is no healing.

46:12

שָׁמְעוּheard גוֹיִםnations קְלוֹנֵךְyour shame וְצִוְחָתֵךְand your outcry מָלְאָהfills הָאָרֶץthe earth כִּיbecause גִבּוֹרwarrior בְּגִבּוֹרover warrior כָּשָׁלוּstumbles יַחְדָּיוtogether נָפְלוּfallen שְׁנֵיהֶםboth of them

The nations have heard of your shame, and your outcry fills the earth, because warrior stumbles over warrior and both of them have fallen together.”

46:13

הַדָּבָרthe word אֲשֶׁרthat דִּבֶּרspoke יְהוָהLORD אֶלto יִרְמְיָהוּJeremiah הַנָּבִיאthe prophet לָבוֹאabout the coming נְבוּכַדְרֶאצַּרNebuchadnezzar מֶלֶךְking בָּבֶלBabylon לְהַכּוֹתto strike אֶת[obj] אֶרֶץland מִצְרָיִםEgypt

This is the word that the LORD spoke to Jeremiah the prophet about the coming of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon to strike the land of Egypt:

46:14

הַגִּידוּannounce בְמִצְרַיִםin Egypt וְהַשְׁמִיעוּand proclaim בְמִגְדּוֹלin Migdol וְהַשְׁמִיעוּproclaim בְנֹףin Memphis וּבְתַחְפַּנְחֵסand Tahpanhes אִמְרוּsay הִתְיַצֵּבpositions וְהָכֵןand prepare לָךְto you כִּיfor אָכְלָהdevours חֶרֶבsword סְבִיבֶיךָaround you

“Announce it in Egypt, and proclaim it in Migdol; proclaim it in Memphis and Tahpanhes: ‘Take your positions and prepare yourself, for the sword devours those around you.’

46:15

מַדּוּעַwhy נִסְחַףwarriors אַבִּירֶיךָyour warriors לֹאnot עָמַדstand כִּיfor יְהוָהLORD הֲדָפוֹthrust them

Why have your warriors been laid low? They cannot stand, for the LORD has thrust them down.

46:16

הִרְבָּהcontinue כּוֹשֵׁלstumble גַּםindeed נָפַלfallen אִישׁone אֶלto רֵעֵהוּanother וַיֹּאמְרוּthey say קוּמָהget וְנָשֻׁבָהlet us return אֶלto עַמֵּנוּour people וְאֶלand to אֶרֶץland מוֹלַדְתֵּנוּour birth מִפְּנֵיfrom before חֶרֶבsword הַיּוֹנָהthe oppressor

They continue to stumble; indeed, they have fallen over one another. They say, ‘Get up! Let us return to our people and to the land of our birth, away from the sword of the oppressor.’

46:17

קָרְאוּcry שָׁםthere פַּרְעֹהPharaoh מֶלֶךְking מִצְרַיִםEgypt שָׁאוֹןnoise הֶעֱבִירpass הַמּוֹעֵדthe appointed time

There they will cry out: ‘Pharaoh king of Egypt was all noise; he has let the appointed time pass him by.’

46:18

חַיsurely אָנִיI נְאֻםdeclares הַמֶּלֶךְthe king יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתwhose שְׁמוֹname כִּיfor כְּתָבוֹרlike Tabor בֶּהָרִיםamong the mountains וּכְכַרְמֶלand like Carmel בַּיָּםby the sea יָבוֹאcome

As surely as I live, declares the King, whose name is the LORD of Hosts, there will come one who is like Tabor among the mountains and like Carmel by the sea.

46:19

כְּלֵיbags גוֹלָהexile עֲשִׂיdo לָךְfor O יוֹשֶׁבֶתdwelling בַּתdaughter מִצְרָיִםEgypt כִּיfor נֹףMemphis לְשַׁמָּהbe laid waste תִהְיֶהbe וְנִצְּתָהdestroyed and מֵאֵיןfrom without יוֹשֵׁבuninhabited

Pack your bags for exile, O daughter dwelling in Egypt! For Memphis will be laid waste, destroyed and uninhabited.

46:20

עֶגְלָהheifer יְפֵהvery fair פִיָּהvery fair מִצְרָיִםEgypt קֶרֶץbeautiful מִצָּפוֹןfrom the north בָּאcoming בָאagainst

Egypt is a beautiful heifer, but a gadfly from the north is coming against her.

46:21

גַּםeven שְׂכִרֶיהָmercenaries בְקִרְבָּהּamong her כְּעֶגְלֵיlike calves מַרְבֵּקfattened כִּיfor גַםtoo הֵמָּהthey הִפְנוּturn נָסוּflee יַחְדָּיוtogether לֹאnot עָמָדוּstand כִּיfor יוֹםday אֵידָםcalamity בָּאcoming עֲלֵיהֶםupon them עֵתtime פְּקֻדָּתָםtheir punishment

Even the mercenaries among her are like fattened calves. They too will turn back; together they will flee, they will not stand their ground, for the day of calamity is coming upon them— the time of their punishment.

46:22

קוֹלָהּwill hiss כַּנָּחָשׁlike a fleeing serpent יֵלֵךְgo כִּיfor בְחַיִלin force יֵלֵכוּgo וּבְקַרְדֻּמּוֹתwith axes בָּאוּcome לָהּto her כְּחֹטְבֵיlike woodsmen cutting עֵצִיםtrees

Egypt will hiss like a fleeing serpent, for the enemy will advance in force; with axes they will come against her like woodsmen cutting down trees.

46:23

כָּרְתוּchop יַעְרָהּher forest נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor לֹאnot יֵחָקֵרmay כִּיfor רַבּוּnumerous מֵאַרְבֶּהthan locusts וְאֵיןand no לָהֶםto them מִסְפָּרcounted

They will chop down her forest, declares the LORD, dense though it may be, for they are more numerous than locusts; they cannot be counted.

46:24

הֹבִישָׁהdisgraced בַּתdaughter מִצְרָיִםEgypt נִתְּנָהdelivered בְּיַדinto the hands עַםpeople צָפוֹןnorth

The Daughter of Egypt will be put to shame; she will be delivered into the hands of the people of the north.”

46:25

אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael הִנְנִיbehold I am פוֹקֵדpunish אֶלto אָמוֹןAmon מִנֹּאof Thebes וְעַלand on פַּרְעֹהPharaoh וְעַלand on מִצְרַיִםEgypt וְעַלand on אֱלֹהֶיהָher gods וְעַלand on מְלָכֶיהָand kings וְעַלand on פַּרְעֹהPharaoh וְעַלand on הַבֹּטְחִיםwho trust בּוֹin

The LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: “Behold, I am about to punish Amon god of Thebes, along with Pharaoh, Egypt with her gods and kings, and those who trust in Pharaoh.

46:26

וּנְתַתִּיםwill deliver them בְּיַדinto the hands מְבַקְשֵׁיseek נַפְשָׁםtheir lives וּבְיַדand in hand נְבוּכַדְרֶאצַּרNebuchadnezzar מֶלֶךְking בָּבֶלBabylon וּבְיַדand his עֲבָדָיוhis officers וְאַחֲרֵיbut after כֵןthis תִּשְׁכֹּןinhabited כִּימֵיas in days קֶדֶםold נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD

I will deliver them into the hands of those who seek their lives—of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and his officers. But after this, Egypt will be inhabited as in days of old, declares the LORD.

46:27

וְאַתָּהbut you אַלdo not תִּירָאafraid עַבְדִּיmy servant יַעֲקֹבJacob וְאַלand do not תֵּחַתdismayed יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael כִּיfor הִנְנִיI surely מוֹשִׁעֲךָsave you מֵרָחוֹקdistant וְאֶתand [obj] זַרְעֲךָyour descendants מֵאֶרֶץfrom the land שִׁבְיָםtheir captivity וְשָׁבwill return יַעֲקוֹבJacob וְשָׁקַטto quiet וְשַׁאֲנַןand ease וְאֵיןwith no מַחֲרִידafraid

But you, O Jacob My servant, do not be afraid, and do not be dismayed, O Israel. For I will surely save you out of a distant place, your descendants from the land of their captivity! Jacob will return to quiet and ease, with no one to make him afraid.

46:28

אַתָּהyou אַלdo not תִּירָאafraid עַבְדִּיmy servant יַעֲקֹבJacob נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor אִתְּךָwith you אָנִיam כִּיfor אֶעֱשֶׂהdo כָלָהcompletely destroy בְּכָלall הַגּוֹיִםthe nations אֲשֶׁרwhich הִדַּחְתִּיךָbanished you שָׁמָּהthere toward וְאֹתְךָand you לֹאnot אֶעֱשֶׂהdo כָלָהcompletely destroy וְיִסַּרְתִּיךָdiscipline you לַמִּשְׁפָּטjustly וְנַקֵּהand will by לֹאno אֲנַקֶּךָּmeans you unpunished

And you, My servant Jacob, do not be afraid, declares the LORD, for I am with you. Though I will completely destroy all the nations to which I have banished you, I will not completely destroy you. Yet I will discipline you justly, and will by no means leave you unpunished.”
