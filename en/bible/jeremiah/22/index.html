saying, ‘Hear the word of the LORD, O king of Judah, who sits on the throne of David—you and your officials and your people who enter these gates.

22:3

This is what the LORD says: Administer justice and righteousness. Rescue the victim of robbery from the hand of his oppressor. Do no wrong or violence to the foreigner, the fatherless, or the widow. Do not shed innocent blood in this place.

22:4

For if you will indeed carry out these commands, then kings who sit on David’s throne will enter through the gates of this palace riding on chariots and horses—they and their officials and their people.

22:5

וְאִםbut if לֹאnot תִשְׁמְעוּobey אֶת[obj] הַדְּבָרִיםthese words הָאֵלֶּהthese בִּיby myself נִשְׁבַּעְתִּיswear נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD כִּיthat לְחָרְבָּהpile of rubble יִהְיֶהbecome הַבַּיִתthis house הַזֶּהthis

But if you do not obey these words, then I swear by Myself, declares the LORD, that this house will become a pile of rubble.’”

22:6

כִּיfor כֹהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD עַלon בֵּיתhouse מֶלֶךְking יְהוּדָהJudah גִּלְעָדGilead אַתָּהyou לִיto me רֹאשׁsummit הַלְּבָנוֹןof Lebanon אִםif לֹאnot אֲשִׁיתְךָturn you מִדְבָּרdesert עָרִיםcities לֹאnot נוֹשָׁבוּuninhabited

For this is what the LORD says concerning the house of the king of Judah: “You are like Gilead to Me, like the summit of Lebanon; but I will surely turn you into a desert, like cities that are uninhabited.

22:7

וְקִדַּשְׁתִּיwill appoint destroyers עָלֶיךָagainst you מַשְׁחִתִיםeach אִישׁman וְכֵלָיוwith his weapons וְכָרְתוּcut מִבְחַרchoicest אֲרָזֶיךָyour cedars וְהִפִּילוּand throw עַלon הָאֵשׁthe fire

I will appoint destroyers against you, each man with his weapons, and they will cut down the choicest of your cedars and throw them into the fire.

22:8

וְעָבְרוּpass גּוֹיִםnations רַבִּיםmany עַלon הָעִירthis city הַזֹּאתthis וְאָמְרוּand ask אִישׁone אֶלto רֵעֵהוּanother עַלon מֶהwhy עָשָׂהdone יְהוָהLORD כָּכָהsuch לָעִירto this city הַגְּדוֹלָהgreat הַזֹּאתthis

And many nations will pass by this city and ask one another, ‘Why has the LORD done such a thing to this great city?’

22:9

וְאָמְרוּreply עַלon אֲשֶׁרhave עָזְבוּforsaken אֶת[obj] בְּרִיתcovenant יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיהֶםtheir God וַיִּשְׁתַּחֲווּand have worshiped לֵאלֹהִיםgods אֲחֵרִיםother gods וַיַּעַבְדוּםand served

Then people will reply, ‘Because they have forsaken the covenant of the LORD their God and have worshiped and served other gods.’”

22:10

אַלdo not תִּבְכּוּweep לְמֵתfor the dead וְאַלking do not תָּנֻדוּmourn לוֹto him בְּכוּweep בָכוֹweep bitterly לַהֹלֵךְexiled כִּיfor לֹאnever יָשׁוּבreturn עוֹדlonger וְרָאָהto see אֶת[obj] אֶרֶץland מוֹלַדְתּוֹhis native

Do not weep for the dead king; do not mourn his loss. Weep bitterly for the one who is exiled, for he will never return to see his native land.

22:11

כִּיfor כֹהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD אֶלto שַׁלֻּםShallum בֶּןson יֹאשִׁיָּהוּJosiah מֶלֶךְking יְהוּדָהJudah הַמֹּלֵךְwho succeeded his תַּחַתunder יֹאשִׁיָּהוּJosiah אָבִיוhis father but אֲשֶׁרhas יָצָאgone forth מִןfrom הַמָּקוֹםthis place הַזֶּהwill לֹאnever יָשׁוּבreturn שָׁםthere עוֹדlonger

For this is what the LORD says concerning Shallum son of Josiah, king of Judah, who succeeded his father Josiah but has gone forth from this place: “He will never return,

22:12

כִּיbut בִּמְקוֹםin place אֲשֶׁרwhich הִגְלוּexiled אֹתוֹhim שָׁםwill יָמוּתdie וְאֶתand [obj] הָאָרֶץland הַזֹּאתthis לֹאnever יִרְאֶהsee עוֹדagain

but he will die in the place to which he was exiled; he will never see this land again.”

22:13

הוֹיWoe בֹּנֶהbuilds בֵיתוֹhis palace בְּלֹאin without צֶדֶקunrighteousness וַעֲלִיּוֹתָיוand his upper rooms בְּלֹאin without מִשְׁפָּטjustice בְּרֵעֵהוּmakes his countrymen יַעֲבֹדserve חִנָּםwithout וּפֹעֲלוֹwages לֹאnot יִתֶּןpay לוֹto their

“Woe to him who builds his palace by unrighteousness, and his upper rooms without justice, who makes his countrymen serve without pay, and fails to pay their wages,

22:14

הָאֹמֵרwho says אֶבְנֶהbuild לִּיto me בֵּיתpalace מִדּוֹתspacious וַעֲלִיּוֹתwith upper rooms מְרֻוָּחִיםrooms וְקָרַעso he cuts לוֹto him חַלּוֹנָיwindows וְסָפוּןpanels בָּאָרֶזwith cedar וּמָשׁוֹחַand paints בַּשָּׁשַׁרwith vermilion

who says, ‘I will build myself a great palace, with spacious upper rooms.’ So he cuts windows in it, panels it with cedar, and paints it with vermilion.

22:15

הֲתִמְלֹךְking כִּיfor אַתָּהyou מְתַחֲרֶהexcel בָאָרֶזin cedar אָבִיךָyour father הֲלוֹאdid not אָכַלfood וְשָׁתָהand drink וְעָשָׂהhe administered מִשְׁפָּטjustice וּצְדָקָהand righteousness אָזthen טוֹבwell לוֹto him

Does it make you a king to excel in cedar? Did not your father have food and drink? He administered justice and righteousness, and so it went well with him.

22:16

דָּןtook דִּיןcause עָנִיpoor וְאֶבְיוֹןand needy אָזthen טוֹבwell הֲלוֹאis this not הִיאis הַדַּעַתto know אֹתִיme נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD

He took up the cause of the poor and needy, and so it went well with him. Is this not what it means to know Me?” declares the LORD.

22:17

כִּיbut אֵיןnothing עֵינֶיךָyour eyes וְלִבְּךָand heart כִּיfor אִםif עַלon בִּצְעֶךָdishonest gain וְעַלon דַּםshedding הַנָּקִיinnocent לִשְׁפּוֹךְblood וְעַלon הָעֹשֶׁקpracticing extortion oppression וְעַלand on הַמְּרוּצָהthe violence לַעֲשׂוֹתto do

“But your eyes and heart are set on nothing except your own dishonest gain, on shedding innocent blood, on practicing extortion and oppression.”

22:18

לָכֵןtherefore כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD אֶלto יְהוֹיָקִיםJehoiakim בֶּןson יֹאשִׁיָּהוּJosiah מֶלֶךְking יְהוּדָהJudah לֹאnot יִסְפְּדוּmourn לוֹfor him הוֹיAlas אָחִיmy brother וְהוֹיAlas אָחוֹתsister לֹאnot יִסְפְּדוּmourn לוֹfor him הוֹיAlas אָדוֹןmaster וְהוֹיAlas הֹדֹהhis splendor

Therefore this is what the LORD says concerning Jehoiakim son of Josiah king of Judah: “They will not mourn for him: ‘Alas, my brother! Alas, my sister!’ They will not mourn for him: ‘Alas, my master! Alas, his splendor!’

22:19

קְבוּרַתlike חֲמוֹרdonkey יִקָּבֵרburied סָחוֹבdragged וְהַשְׁלֵךְand thrown מֵהָלְאָהoutside לְשַׁעֲרֵיthe gates יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem

He will be buried like a donkey, dragged away and thrown outside the gates of Jerusalem.

22:20

עֲלִיgo הַלְּבָנוֹןto Lebanon וּצְעָקִיand cry out וּבַבָּשָׁןin Bashan תְּנִיraise קוֹלֵךְyour voice וְצַעֲקִיcry out מֵעֲבָרִיםfrom Abarim כִּיfor נִשְׁבְּרוּcrushed כָּלall מְאַהֲבָיִךְyour lovers

Go up to Lebanon and cry out; raise your voice in Bashan; cry out from Abarim, for all your lovers have been crushed.

22:21

דִּבַּרְתִּיI אֵלַיִךְto you בְּשַׁלְוֺתַיִךְsecure you אָמַרְתְּsaid לֹאnot אֶשְׁמָעlisten זֶהthis דַרְכֵּךְyour way מִנְּעוּרַיִךְfrom youth that you כִּיfor לֹאnot שָׁמַעַתְּobeyed בְּקוֹלִיmy voice

I warned you when you were secure. You said, ‘I will not listen.’ This has been your way from youth, that you have not obeyed My voice.

22:22

כָּלall רֹעַיִךְshepherds you תִּרְעֶהshepherds רוּחַwind וּמְאַהֲבַיִךְand your lovers בַּשְּׁבִיinto captivity יֵלֵכוּgo כִּיfor אָזthen תֵּבֹשִׁיashamed וְנִכְלַמְתְּand humiliated מִכֹּלbecause of all רָעָתֵךְyour wickedness

The wind will drive away all your shepherds, and your lovers will go into captivity. Then you will be ashamed and humiliated because of all your wickedness.

22:23

יֹשַׁבְתְּinhabitant בַּלְּבָנוֹןof Lebanon מְקֻנַּנְתְּnestled בָּאֲרָזִיםin the cedars מַהhow נֵּחַנְתְּgroan בְּבֹאcome לָךְto you חֲבָלִיםpangs חִילanguish כַּיֹּלֵדָהlike a woman in labor

O inhabitant of Lebanon, nestled in the cedars, how you will groan when pangs of anguish come upon you, agony like a woman in labor.”

22:24

“As surely as I live,” declares the LORD, “even if you, Coniah son of Jehoiakim king of Judah, were a signet ring on My right hand, I would pull you off.

22:25

In fact, I will hand you over to those you dread, who want to take your life—to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and to the Chaldeans.

22:26

וְהֵטַלְתִּיwill hurl אֹתְךָyou וְאֶתand [obj] אִמְּךָmother אֲשֶׁרwho יְלָדַתְךָgave you birth עַלon הָאָרֶץland אַחֶרֶתanother אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot יֻלַּדְתֶּםborn שָׁםthere וְשָׁםboth will תָּמוּתוּdie

I will hurl you and the mother who gave you birth into another land, where neither of you were born—and there you both will die.

22:27

וְעַלto הָאָרֶץthe land אֲשֶׁרthat הֵםthey מְנַשְּׂאִיםlift אֶת[obj] נַפְשָׁםwhich you לָשׁוּבwill שָׁםthere שָׁמָּהthere toward לֹאnever יָשׁוּבוּreturn

You will never return to the land for which you long.”

22:28

Is this man Coniah a despised and shattered pot, a jar that no one wants? Why are he and his descendants hurled out and cast into a land they do not know?

22:29

O land, land, land, hear the word of the LORD!

22:30

This is what the LORD says: “Enroll this man as childless, a man who will not prosper in his lifetime. None of his descendants will prosper to sit on the throne of David or to rule again in Judah.”
