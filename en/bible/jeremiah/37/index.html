Jeremiah 37

Jeremiah arrested as a deserter

Open in the interlinear reader

37:1

וַיִּמְלָךְmade מֶלֶךְking צִדְקִיָּהוּZedekiah בֶּןson יֹאשִׁיָּהוּJosiah תַּחַתplace כָּנְיָהוּConiah בֶּןson יְהוֹיָקִיםJehoiakim אֲשֶׁרthat הִמְלִיךְreigned נְבוּכַדְרֶאצַּרNebuchadnezzar מֶלֶךְking בָּבֶלBabylon בְּאֶרֶץin land יְהוּדָהJudah

Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon made Zedekiah son of Josiah the king of Judah, and he reigned in place of Coniah son of Jehoiakim.

37:2

וְלֹאand not שָׁמַעobey הוּאhe וַעֲבָדָיוand his officers וְעַםand the people הָאָרֶץthe land אֶלto דִּבְרֵיwords יְהוָהLORD אֲשֶׁרhad דִּבֶּרspoken בְּיַדthrough יִרְמְיָהוּJeremiah הַנָּבִיאthe prophet

But he and his officers and the people of the land refused to obey the words that the LORD had spoken through Jeremiah the prophet.

37:3

וַיִּשְׁלַחsent הַמֶּלֶךְking צִדְקִיָּהוּZedekiah אֶת[obj] יְהוּכַלJehucal בֶּןson שֶׁלֶמְיָהShelemiah וְאֶתand [obj] צְפַנְיָהוּZephaniah בֶןson מַעֲשֵׂיָהMaaseiah הַכֹּהֵןthe priest the אֶלto יִרְמְיָהוּJeremiah הַנָּבִיאthe prophet לֵאמֹרwith the message הִתְפַּלֶּלpray נָאplease בַעֲדֵנוּfor us אֶלto יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God

Yet King Zedekiah sent Jehucal son of Shelemiah and Zephaniah the priest, the son of Maaseiah, to Jeremiah the prophet with the message, “Please pray to the LORD our God for us!”

37:4

וְיִרְמְיָהוּNow Jeremiah בָּאcome וְיֹצֵאand go בְּתוֹךְamong הָעָםthe people וְלֹאthey had not נָתְנוּput אֹתוֹhim בֵּיתhouse הַכְּלוּאprison

Now Jeremiah was free to come and go among the people, for they had not yet put him in prison.

37:5

וְחֵילarmy פַּרְעֹהPharaoh’s יָצָאhad מִמִּצְרָיִםleft Egypt וַיִּשְׁמְעוּand when הַכַּשְׂדִּיםthe Chaldeans הַצָּרִיםwho were besieging עַלon יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem אֶת[obj] שִׁמְעָםreport they וַיֵּעָלוּthey withdrew מֵעַלfrom the יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem

Pharaoh’s army had left Egypt, and when the Chaldeans who were besieging Jerusalem heard the report, they withdrew from Jerusalem.

37:6

וַיְהִיand was דְּבַרword יְהוָהLORD אֶלto יִרְמְיָהוּJeremiah הַנָּבִיאthe prophet לֵאמֹרto saying

Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah the prophet:

37:7

כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael כֹּהthis תֹאמְרוּsays אֶלto מֶלֶךְking יְהוּדָהJudah הַשֹּׁלֵחַwho sent אֶתְכֶםyou אֵלַיto me לְדָרְשֵׁנִיto inquire me הִנֵּהbehold חֵילarmy פַּרְעֹהPharaoh’s הַיֹּצֵאwhich has marched out לָכֶםto you לְעֶזְרָהhelp שָׁבback לְאַרְצוֹto its own land מִצְרָיִםEgypt

“This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says that you are to tell the king of Judah, who sent you to Me: Behold, Pharaoh’s army, which has marched out to help you, will go back to its own land of Egypt.

37:8

וְשָׁבוּreturn הַכַּשְׂדִּיםthe Chaldeans וְנִלְחֲמוּand fight עַלagainst הָעִירthis city הַזֹּאתthis וּלְכָדֻהָthey will capture it וּשְׂרָפֻהָand burn it בָאֵשׁin the fire

Then the Chaldeans will return and fight against this city. They will capture it and burn it down.

37:9

כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD אַלdo not תַּשִּׁאוּdeceive נַפְשֹׁתֵיכֶםyourselves לֵאמֹרby saying הָלֹךְgo יֵלְכוּgo מֵעָלֵינוּfrom for הַכַּשְׂדִּיםChaldeans כִּיfor לֹאnot יֵלֵכוּgo

This is what the LORD says: Do not deceive yourselves by saying, ‘The Chaldeans will go away for good,’ for they will not!

37:10

כִּיfor אִםif הִכִּיתֶםstrike כָּלentire חֵילarmy כַּשְׂדִּיםChaldeans הַנִּלְחָמִיםthat is fighting אִתְּכֶםagainst you וְנִשְׁאֲרוּand only בָםin their אֲנָשִׁיםthey מְדֻקָּרִיםwounded אִישׁmen בְּאָהֳלוֹtents would still יָקוּמוּget וְשָׂרְפוּand burn אֶת[obj] הָעִירthis city הַזֹּאתthis בָּאֵשׁin the fire

Indeed, if you were to strike down the entire army of the Chaldeans that is fighting against you, and only wounded men remained in their tents, they would still get up and burn this city down.”

37:11

וְהָיָהand be בְּהֵעָלוֹתwithdrew חֵילarmy הַכַּשְׂדִּיםthe Chaldean מֵעַלfrom for יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem מִפְּנֵיfor fear חֵילarmy פַּרְעֹהPharaoh’s

When the Chaldean army withdrew from Jerusalem for fear of Pharaoh’s army,

37:12

וַיֵּצֵאand went יִרְמְיָהוּJeremiah מִירוּשָׁלִַםstarted to leave Jerusalem לָלֶכֶתto go אֶרֶץland בִּנְיָמִןBenjamin לַחֲלִקto claim portion מִשָּׁםhis there בְּתוֹךְamong הָעָםthe people

Jeremiah started to leave Jerusalem to go to the land of Benjamin to claim his portion there among the people.

37:13

וַיְהִיbut when הוּאhe בְּשַׁעַרreached the gate בִּנְיָמִןBenjamin וְשָׁםand there בַּעַלcaptain פְּקִדֻתguard וּשְׁמוֹwhose name was יִרְאִיָּיהIrijah בֶּןson שֶׁלֶמְיָהShelemiah בֶּןson חֲנַנְיָהHananiah וַיִּתְפֹּשׂseized אֶת[obj] יִרְמְיָהוּJeremiah הַנָּבִיאhim לֵאמֹרand said אֶלto הַכַּשְׂדִּיםthe Chaldeans אַתָּהyou נֹפֵלdeserting

But when he reached the Gate of Benjamin, the captain of the guard, whose name was Irijah son of Shelemiah, the son of Hananiah, seized him and said, “You are deserting to the Chaldeans!”

37:14

וַיֹּאמֶרreplied יִרְמְיָהוּJeremiah שֶׁקֶרlie אֵינֶנִּיam not נֹפֵלdeserting עַלto הַכַּשְׂדִּיםthe Chaldeans וְלֹאnot שָׁמַעlisten אֵלָיוto him וַיִּתְפֹּשׂinstead he arrested יִרְאִיָּיהIrijah בְּיִרְמְיָהוּJeremiah וַיְבִאֵהוּand took him אֶלto הַשָּׂרִיםthe officials

“That is a lie,” Jeremiah replied. “I am not deserting to the Chaldeans!” But Irijah would not listen to him; instead, he arrested Jeremiah and took him to the officials.

37:15

וַיִּקְצְפוּwere angry הַשָּׂרִיםthe officials עַלon יִרְמְיָהוּJeremiah וְהִכּוּand they beat אֹתוֹhim וְנָתְנוּand placed אוֹתוֹhim בֵּיתhouse הָאֵסוּרjail in the בֵּיתhouse יְהוֹנָתָןJonathan הַסֹּפֵרthe scribe כִּיfor אֹתוֹhim עָשׂוּmade לְבֵיתto house הַכֶּלֶאa prison

The officials were angry with Jeremiah, and they beat him and placed him in jail in the house of Jonathan the scribe, for it had been made into a prison.

37:16

כִּיfor בָאwent יִרְמְיָהוּJeremiah אֶלto בֵּיתhouse הַבּוֹרthe dungeon וְאֶלinto הַחֲנֻיוֹתa cell וַיֵּשֶׁבremained שָׁםthere יִרְמְיָהוּJeremiah יָמִיםlong רַבִּיםtime

So Jeremiah went into a cell in the dungeon and remained there a long time.

37:17

וַיִּשְׁלַחsent הַמֶּלֶךְking צִדְקִיָּהוּZedekiah וַיִּקָּחֵהוּand received him וַיִּשְׁאָלֵהוּwhere he asked him הַמֶּלֶךְthe king בְּבֵיתוֹhis palace בַּסֵּתֶרprivately וַיֹּאמֶרand said הֲיֵשׁis there דָּבָרword מֵאֵתfrom the יְהוָהLORD וַיֹּאמֶרthere is יִרְמְיָהוּJeremiah יֵשׁthere וַיֹּאמֶרand said בְּיַדinto the hand מֶלֶךְking בָּבֶלBabylon תִּנָּתֵןdelivered

Later, King Zedekiah sent for Jeremiah and received him in his palace, where he asked him privately, “Is there a word from the LORD?” “There is,” Jeremiah replied. “You will be delivered into the hand of the king of Babylon.”

37:18

וַיֹּאמֶרthen asked יִרְמְיָהוּJeremiah אֶלto הַמֶּלֶךְking צִדְקִיָּהוּZedekiah מֶהhow חָטָאתִיsinned לְךָto you וְלַעֲבָדֶיךָor your servants וְלָעָםor these people הַזֶּהthat you כִּיfor נְתַתֶּםput אוֹתִיme אֶלto בֵּיתhouse הַכֶּלֶאprison

Then Jeremiah asked King Zedekiah, “How have I sinned against you or your servants or these people, that you have put me in prison?

37:19

וְאַיֵּהwhere נְבִיאֵיכֶםyour prophets אֲשֶׁרwho נִבְּאוּprophesied לָכֶםto you לֵאמֹרto saying לֹאnot יָבֹאcome מֶלֶךְking בָּבֶלBabylon עֲלֵיכֶםagainst you וְעַלand on הָאָרֶץthis land הַזֹּאתthis

Where are your prophets who prophesied to you, claiming, ‘The king of Babylon will not come against you or this land’?

37:20

וְעַתָּהbut now שְׁמַעlisten נָאplease אֲדֹנִיmy lord הַמֶּלֶךְthe king תִּפָּלfall נָאplease תְחִנָּתִיmy petition לְפָנֶיךָcome before you וְאַלdo not תְּשִׁבֵנִיsend me back בֵּיתhouse יְהוֹנָתָןJonathan הַסֹּפֵרthe scribe וְלֹאor אָמוּתdie שָׁםthere

But now please listen, O my lord the king. May my petition come before you. Do not send me back to the house of Jonathan the scribe, or I will die there.”

37:21

וַיְצַוֶּהand commanded הַמֶּלֶךְking צִדְקִיָּהוּZedekiah וַיַּפְקִדוּgave orders אֶת[obj] יִרְמְיָהוּJeremiah בַּחֲצַרin the courtyard הַמַּטָּרָהthe guard וְנָתֹןand given לוֹa כִכַּרloaf לֶחֶםbread לַיּוֹםdaily מִחוּץfrom the street הָאֹפִיםthe bakers עַדuntil תֹּםperished כָּלall הַלֶּחֶםthe bread מִןfrom הָעִירthe city וַיֵּשֶׁבso remained יִרְמְיָהוּJeremiah בַּחֲצַרin the courtyard הַמַּטָּרָהthe guard

So King Zedekiah gave orders for Jeremiah to be placed in the courtyard of the guard and given a loaf of bread daily from the street of the bakers, until all the bread in the city was gone. So Jeremiah remained in the courtyard of the guard.
