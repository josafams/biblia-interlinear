Revelation Ἀποκάλυψις Ἰωάννου

Revelation reports the visions that a Christian named John received on the island of Patmos and that he sends to seven churches of the province of Asia. It begins with a vision of the risen Christ and with seven messages, one for each church, which praise, rebuke and call to faithfulness. Then the gaze turns to God's throne in heaven, where the Lamb who was slain receives the sealed scroll; there follow series of seven seals, seven trumpets and seven bowls, interspersed with visions of the woman and the dragon, the two beasts and the fall of Babylon, the great city that oppresses the peoples. The book ends with the final defeat of evil, the judgment and the vision of a new heaven, a new earth and the new Jerusalem, where God dwells with His people and there is no more death or mourning.

Structure

  1. Prologue and opening vision ch. 1

    Title, greeting to the seven churches and the vision of “one like a son of man” among seven lampstands, who tells John to write.

  2. Messages to the seven churches chs. 2–3

    Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamum, Thyatira, Sardis, Philadelphia and Laodicea receive praise, censure and promises “to the one who overcomes.”

  3. The throne and the Lamb chs. 4–5

    Heavenly worship around the throne; only the Lamb who was slain is worthy to open the scroll sealed with seven seals.

  4. The seven seals chs. 6–8

    The horsemen, the martyrs, the cosmic signs; a pause shows the 144,000 sealed and an innumerable multitude; the seventh seal brings silence in heaven (6:1–8:5).

  5. The seven trumpets chs. 8–11

    Calamities on the earth, the sea and the heavens; the angel with the little scroll, the two witnesses and the seventh trumpet announcing the kingdom of God (8:6–11:19).

  6. The conflict with the dragon chs. 12–14

    The woman, the child and the dragon; the beast that rises from the sea and the one that rises from the earth; the Lamb on Mount Zion, the angels' proclamations and the harvests of the earth.

  7. The seven bowls chs. 15–16

    The victors sing beside the sea of glass; seven angels pour out the bowls of wrath; the kings gather at the place called Armageddon.

  8. The fall of Babylon chs. 17–19

    The great prostitute seated on the beast, the fall of the city, the lament of the kings and merchants and the praise in heaven (17:1–19:10).

  9. Victory and judgment chs. 19–20

    The rider called Faithful and True defeats the beast; Satan is bound for a thousand years, released and defeated; the dead are judged before the great white throne (19:11–20:15).

  10. The new creation chs. 21–22

    A new heaven and a new earth; the new Jerusalem comes down out of heaven; the river and the tree of life (21:1–22:5).

  11. Epilogue ch. 22

    Warnings, the promise “Yes, I am coming soon” and the church's answer: “Come, Lord Jesus!” (22:6–21).
