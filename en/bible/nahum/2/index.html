Nahum 2

The attack and fall of Nineveh

Open in the interlinear reader

One who scatters advances against you, O Nineveh. Guard the fortress! Watch the road! Brace yourselves! Summon all your strength!

2:2

כִּיfor שָׁבrestore יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] גְּאוֹןsplendor יַעֲקֹבJacob כִּגְאוֹןlike the splendor יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael כִּיfor בְקָקוּםlaid them בֹּקְקִיםwaste וּזְמֹרֵיהֶםand ruined שִׁחֵתוּvine

For the LORD will restore the splendor of Jacob like the splendor of Israel, though destroyers have laid them waste and ruined the branches of their vine.

2:3

The shields of his mighty men are red; the valiant warriors are dressed in scarlet. The fittings of the chariots flash like fire on the day they are prepared, and the spears of cypress have been brandished.

2:4

בַּחוּצוֹתin the streets יִתְהוֹלְלוּpraise הָרֶכֶבthe chariots יִשְׁתַּקְשְׁקוּןdash בָּרְחֹבוֹתthrough the streets מַרְאֵיהֶןrush כַּלַּפִּידִםlike torches כַּבְּרָקִיםdarting about like lightning יְרוֹצֵצוּrun

The chariots dash through the streets; they rush around the plazas, appearing like torches, darting about like lightning.

2:5

יִזְכֹּרsummons אַדִּירָיוhis nobles יִכָּשְׁלוּstumble בַּהֲלִיכָתָםas they advance they יְמַהֲרוּrace חוֹמָתָהּits wall וְהֻכַןset הַסֹּכֵךְthe protective shield

He summons his nobles; they stumble as they advance. They race to its wall; the protective shield is set in place.

2:6

שַׁעֲרֵיgates הַנְּהָרוֹתthe river נִפְתָּחוּopen וְהַהֵיכָלand the palace נָמוֹגcollapses

The river gates are thrown open and the palace collapses.

2:7

וְהֻצַּבdecreed גֻּלְּתָהexiled הֹעֲלָתָהup וְאַמְהֹתֶיהָher maidservants מְנַהֲגוֹתmoan כְּקוֹלlike roar יוֹנִיםdoves מְתֹפְפֹתbeat עַלupon לִבְבֵהֶןtheir breasts

It is decreed that the city be exiled and carried away; her maidservants moan like doves, and beat upon their breasts.

2:8

וְנִינְוֵהNineveh כִבְרֵכַתlike a pool מַיִםwater מִימֵיthroughout her days הִיאis וְהֵמָּהand they נָסִיםdraining away עִמְדוּstop עֲמֹדוּstop וְאֵיןbut no מַפְנֶהturns

Nineveh has been like a pool of water throughout her days, but now it is draining away. “Stop! Stop!” they cry, but no one turns back.

2:9

בֹּזּוּplunder כֶסֶףsilver בֹּזּוּplunder זָהָבgold וְאֵיןthere is no קֵצֶהend לַתְּכוּנָהto the treasure כָּבֹדabundance מִכֹּלof every כְּלִיarticle חֶמְדָּהprecious

“Plunder the silver! Plunder the gold!” There is no end to the treasure, an abundance of every precious thing.

2:10

She is emptied! Yes, she is desolate and laid waste! Hearts melt, knees knock, bodies tremble, and every face grows pale!

2:11

אַיֵּהwhere מְעוֹןlair אֲרָיוֹתlions וּמִרְעֶהor the feeding הוּאis לַכְּפִרִיםground of the young lions אֲשֶׁרthat הָלַךְprowled אַרְיֵהtheir לָבִיאlioness שָׁםthere גּוּרcubs אַרְיֵהlion וְאֵיןnothing מַחֲרִידfrighten

Where is the lions’ lair or the feeding ground of the young lions, where the lion and lioness prowled with their cubs, with nothing to frighten them away?

2:12

אַרְיֵהlion טֹרֵףmauled בְּדֵיenough גֹרוֹתָיוits cubs וּמְחַנֵּקand strangled לְלִבְאֹתָיוfor the lioness it וַיְמַלֵּאfilled טֶרֶףprey חֹרָיוkill וּמְעֹנֹתָיוdens טְרֵפָהmauled prey

The lion mauled enough for its cubs and strangled prey for the lioness. It filled its dens with the kill, and its lairs with mauled prey.

2:13

“Behold, I am against you,” declares the LORD of Hosts. “I will send your chariots up in smoke, and the sword will devour your young lions. I will cut off your prey from the earth, and the voices of your messengers will no longer be heard.”
