Nahum נַחוּם

Nahum announces the fall of Nineveh, capital of Assyria, the empire that dominated the Near East for more than a century and oppressed Israel and Judah. The book begins with a poem about the LORD, slow to anger, but who does not leave the guilty unpunished, and then describes, in vivid images, the attack on the city and its ruin. For Judah, the news is proclaimed as good news of peace.

Structure

  1. The God who judges ch. 1

    A poem about the LORD's power, followed by the announcement of deliverance for Judah.

  2. The fall of Nineveh chs. 2–3

    The attack, the plunder and the ruin of the city; the comparison with Thebes and the final lament with no healing.

Chapters

  1. 1 The wrath and the goodness of the LORD
  2. 2 The attack and fall of Nineveh
  3. 3 Woe to the city of blood
