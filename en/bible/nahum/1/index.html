Nahum 1

The wrath and the goodness of the LORD

Open in the interlinear reader

This is the burden against Nineveh, the book of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite:

1:2

The LORD is a jealous and avenging God; the LORD is avenging and full of wrath. The LORD takes vengeance on His foes and reserves wrath for His enemies.

1:3

יְהֹוָהLORD אֶרֶךְslow אַפַּיִםanger וּגְדָלand great כֹּחַpower וְנַקֵּהguilty לֹאno יְנַקֶּהmeans leave יְהוָהLORD בְּסוּפָהwhirlwind storm וּבִשְׂעָרָהand in tempest דַּרְכּוֹhis path וְעָנָןand clouds אֲבַקdust beneath רַגְלָיוhis feet

The LORD is slow to anger and great in power; the LORD will by no means leave the guilty unpunished. His path is in the whirlwind and storm, and clouds are the dust beneath His feet.

1:4

גּוֹעֵרrebukes בַּיָּםthe sea וַיַּבְּשֵׁהוּand dries it וְכָלhe makes all הַנְּהָרוֹתthe rivers הֶחֱרִיבrun אֻמְלַלdry בָּשָׁןBashan וְכַרְמֶלand Carmel וּפֶרַחwither and the flower לְבָנוֹןLebanon אֻמְלָלwilts

He rebukes the sea and dries it up; He makes all the rivers run dry. Bashan and Carmel wither, and the flower of Lebanon wilts.

1:5

הָרִיםmountains רָעֲשׁוּquake מִמֶּנּוּfrom us וְהַגְּבָעוֹתand the hills הִתְמֹגָגוּmelt וַתִּשָּׂאand lifted הָאָרֶץthe earth מִפָּנָיוat his presence וְתֵבֵלworld וְכָלand all יֹשְׁבֵיdwellers בָהּin her

The mountains quake before Him, and the hills melt away; the earth trembles at His presence— the world and all its dwellers.

1:6

לִפְנֵיto before זַעְמוֹhis indignation מִיwho יַעֲמוֹדwithstand וּמִיand can יָקוּםendure בַּחֲרוֹןburning אַפּוֹanger his חֲמָתוֹwrath is נִתְּכָהpoured כָאֵשׁlike fire וְהַצֻּרִיםeven rocks נִתְּצוּshattered מִמֶּנּוּfrom him

Who can withstand His indignation? Who can endure His burning anger? His wrath is poured out like fire; even rocks are shattered before Him.

1:7

טוֹבgood יְהוָהLORD לְמָעוֹזa stronghold בְּיוֹםin the day צָרָהdistress וְיֹדֵעַhe cares חֹסֵיtrust בוֹin him

The LORD is good, a stronghold in the day of distress; He cares for those who trust in Him.

1:8

וּבְשֶׁטֶףflood עֹבֵרoverwhelming כָּלָהend יַעֲשֶׂהmake מְקוֹמָהּNineveh וְאֹיְבָיוand his enemies יְרַדֶּףpursue חֹשֶׁךְdarkness

But with an overwhelming flood He will make an end of Nineveh and pursue His enemies into darkness.

1:9

מַהwhat תְּחַשְּׁבוּןplot אֶלagainst יְהוָהLORD כָּלָהend הוּאwill עֹשֶׂהbring לֹאnot תָקוּםrise פַּעֲמַיִםtime צָרָהsecond

Whatever you plot against the LORD, He will bring to an end. Affliction will not rise up a second time.

1:10

כִּיfor עַדuntil סִירִיםentangled סְבֻכִיםentangled וּכְסָבְאָםand like the drink סְבוּאִיםdrunkard אֻכְּלוּconsumed כְּקַשׁlike stubble יָבֵשׁdry מָלֵאfully

For they will be entangled as with thorns and consumed like the drink of a drunkard— like stubble that is fully dry.

1:11

מִמֵּךְfrom you יָצָאforth חֹשֵׁבa plotter עַלagainst יְהוָהLORD רָעָהevil יֹעֵץcounselor בְּלִיָּעַלwickedness

From you, O Nineveh, comes forth a plotter of evil against the LORD, a counselor of wickedness.

1:12

כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD אִםif שְׁלֵמִיםallied וְכֵןand same רַבִּיםnumerous וְכֵןand same נָגֹזּוּcut וְעָבָרand pass וְעִנִּתִךְafflicted you לֹאno אֲעַנֵּךְmistreat you עוֹדlonger

This is what the LORD says: “Though they are allied and numerous, yet they will be cut down and pass away. Though I have afflicted you, O Judah, I will afflict you no longer.

1:13

וְעַתָּהwill now אֶשְׁבֹּרbreak מֹטֵהוּtheir yoke מֵעָלָיִךְfrom the you וּמוֹסְרֹתַיִךְneck and אֲנַתֵּקtear

For I will now break their yoke from your neck and tear away your shackles.”

1:14

The LORD has issued a command concerning you, O Nineveh: “There will be no descendants to carry on your name. I will cut off the carved image and cast idol from the house of your gods; I will prepare your grave, for you are contemptible.”

1:15

הִנֵּהlook עַלto הֶהָרִיםthe mountains רַגְלֵיfeet מְבַשֵּׂרgood מַשְׁמִיעַproclaims שָׁלוֹםpeace חָגִּיcelebrate יְהוּדָהJudah חַגַּיִךְyour feasts שַׁלְּמִיfulfill נְדָרָיִךְyour vows כִּיfor לֹאnever יוֹסִיףagain עוֹדagain לַעֲבָרmarch through בָּךְin you בְּלִיַּעַלwicked כֻּלֹּהhis all נִכְרָתcut off

Look to the mountains— the feet of one who brings good news, who proclaims peace! Celebrate your feasts, O Judah; fulfill your vows. For the wicked will never again march through you; they will be utterly cut off.
