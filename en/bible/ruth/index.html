Ruth רוּת

Ruth is a short narrative set “in the days when the judges ruled.” After losing her husband and her two sons in Moab, Naomi returns to Bethlehem accompanied by her Moabite daughter-in-law Ruth, who refuses to leave her. Ruth gleans grain in the field of Boaz, a relative of Naomi, and, at her mother-in-law's initiative, asks him for protection as the family's kinsman-redeemer. Boaz settles the matter before the elders, marries Ruth, and the couple's son, Obed, appears in a genealogy that ends with David.

Structure

  1. Loss and return ch. 1

    Elimelech's family in Moab, the deaths and the return of Naomi and Ruth to Bethlehem.

  2. In the field of Boaz ch. 2

    Ruth gleans grain and meets the generosity of Boaz.

  3. At the threshing floor ch. 3

    At night, Ruth asks Boaz to take on the role of kinsman-redeemer.

  4. At the city gate ch. 4

    The redemption before the elders, the marriage, the birth of Obed and the genealogy down to David.

Chapters

  1. 1 Naomi and Ruth return from Moab
  2. 2 Ruth gleans in the field of Boaz
  3. 3 Ruth at the threshing floor of Boaz
  4. 4 Boaz redeems the inheritance; Obed is born
