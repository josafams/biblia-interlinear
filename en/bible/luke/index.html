Luke Κατὰ Λουκᾶν

Luke is the first part of a two-volume work that continues in the Acts of the Apostles, both dedicated to a certain Theophilus. The author says he has investigated everything carefully in order to write an orderly account (1:1–4) and places the story of Jesus within the framework of the Roman Empire, with mentions of emperors, governors and high priests. The book tells side by side the births of John the Baptist and of Jesus, follows the ministry in Galilee and devotes a long section to the journey to Jerusalem (9:51–19:27), where parables that only Luke records are concentrated, such as the Good Samaritan and the Prodigal Son. The poor, women, foreigners and those considered sinners are given prominence, as are prayer, joy and the work of the Holy Spirit. The gospel begins and ends in the temple in Jerusalem, after the appearances of the Risen One and the farewell at Bethany.

Structure

  1. Prologue and childhood chs. 1–2

    The dedication to Theophilus (1:1–4); the announcements and births of John and of Jesus, narrated in parallel, the songs of Mary, Zechariah and Simeon, and Jesus at twelve years old in the temple.

  2. Preparation chs. 3–4

    John's preaching, the baptism, Jesus' genealogy back to Adam, the temptation and the reading of Isaiah in the synagogue at Nazareth (4:16–30), which announces the program of the ministry.

  3. Ministry in Galilee chs. 4–9

    Healings and calls, the Sermon on the Plain (6:20–49), the forgiven woman, the women who accompany Jesus, the mission of the Twelve, Peter's confession and the transfiguration (to 9:50).

  4. The journey to Jerusalem chs. 9–19

    From 9:51 on, Jesus resolves to go up to Jerusalem; in this long section are most of Luke's own parables, teachings on prayer, wealth and discipleship and encounters such as that with Zacchaeus (to 19:27).

  5. In Jerusalem chs. 19–21

    The entry into the city, the weeping over Jerusalem, the cleansing of the temple, the debates with the leaders and the discourse on the destruction of the city and the coming of the Son of Man.

  6. The passion chs. 22–23

    The Last Supper, the prayer on the Mount of Olives, the arrest, the hearings before the Council, Pilate and Herod, the crucifixion and the burial.

  7. Resurrection and farewell ch. 24

    The empty tomb, the encounter on the road to Emmaus, the appearance to the eleven in Jerusalem and the farewell at Bethany, which prepares the beginning of Acts.
