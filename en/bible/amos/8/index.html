Amos 8

The basket of summer fruit

Open in the interlinear reader

כֹּהthis הִרְאַנִיshowed me אֲדֹנָיLord יְהוִהGOD וְהִנֵּהI saw כְּלוּבbasket קָיִץsummer fruit

This is what the Lord GOD showed me: I saw a basket of summer fruit.

8:2

“Amos, what do you see?” He asked. “A basket of summer fruit,” I replied. So the LORD said to me, “The end has come for My people Israel; I will no longer spare them.”

8:3

“In that day,” declares the Lord GOD, “the songs of the temple will turn to wailing. Many will be the corpses, strewn in silence everywhere!”

8:4

שִׁמְעוּhear זֹאתthis הַשֹּׁאֲפִיםwho trample אֶבְיוֹןneedy וְלַשְׁבִּיתand to end עֲנִיֵּיpoor אָרֶץland

Hear this, you who trample the needy, who do away with the poor of the land,

8:5

לֵאמֹרasking מָתַיwhen will יַעֲבֹרpass הַחֹדֶשׁthe new moon וְנַשְׁבִּירָהmay sell שֶּׁבֶרgrain וְהַשַּׁבָּתwhen will the Sabbath וְנִפְתְּחָהmay market בָּרwheat לְהַקְטִיןreduce אֵיפָהephah וּלְהַגְדִּילand increase שֶׁקֶלshekel וּלְעַוֵּתlet us cheat מֹאזְנֵיdishonest מִרְמָהscales

asking, “When will the New Moon be over, that we may sell grain? When will the Sabbath end, that we may market wheat? Let us reduce the ephah and increase the shekel; let us cheat with dishonest scales.

8:6

לִקְנוֹתus buy בַּכֶּסֶףthe with silver דַּלִּיםpoor וְאֶבְיוֹןand the needy בַּעֲבוּרfor a pair נַעֲלָיִםsandals וּמַפַּלselling even בַּרchaff נַשְׁבִּירwheat

Let us buy the poor with silver and the needy for a pair of sandals, selling even the chaff with the wheat!”

8:7

נִשְׁבַּעsworn יְהוָהLORD בִּגְאוֹןby the pride יַעֲקֹבJacob אִםif אֶשְׁכַּחforget לָנֶצַחto forever כָּלany מַעֲשֵׂיהֶםtheir deeds

The LORD has sworn by the Pride of Jacob: “I will never forget any of their deeds.

8:8

הַעַלfor זֹאתthis לֹאnot תִרְגַּזquake הָאָרֶץthe land וְאָבַלmourn כָּלall יוֹשֵׁבdwellers בָּהּin her וְעָלְתָהwill swell כָאֹרlike the Nile כֻּלָּהּall וְנִגְרְשָׁהsurge and וְנִשְׁקְעָהthen subside כִּיאוֹרlike the Nile מִצְרָיִםEgypt

Will not the land quake for this, and all its dwellers mourn? All of it will swell like the Nile; it will surge and then subside like the Nile in Egypt.

8:9

וְהָיָהand be בַּיּוֹםin that day הַהוּאthat נְאֻםdeclares אֲדֹנָיLord I יְהוִהGOD וְהֵבֵאתִיgo הַשֶּׁמֶשׁthe sun בַּצָּהֳרָיִםat noon וְהַחֲשַׁכְתִּיdarken לָאָרֶץthe earth בְּיוֹםin day אוֹרdaytime

And in that day, declares the Lord GOD, I will make the sun go down at noon, and I will darken the earth in the daytime.

8:10

וְהָפַכְתִּיI will turn חַגֵּיכֶםyour feasts לְאֵבֶלinto mourning וְכָלand all שִׁירֵיכֶםyour songs לְקִינָהinto lamentation וְהַעֲלֵיתִיwear עַלto כָּלeveryone מָתְנַיִםwear שָׂקsackcloth וְעַלand on כָּלevery רֹאשׁhead קָרְחָהshaved וְשַׂמְתִּיהָI will make it כְּאֵבֶלmourning יָחִידonly son וְאַחֲרִיתָהּand its outcome כְּיוֹםday מָרbitter

I will turn your feasts into mourning and all your songs into lamentation. I will cause everyone to wear sackcloth and every head to be shaved. I will make it like a time of mourning for an only son, and its outcome like a bitter day.

8:11

הִנֵּהbehold יָמִיםdays בָּאִיםcoming נְאֻםdeclares אֲדֹנָיLord יְהוִהGOD וְהִשְׁלַחְתִּיsend רָעָבfamine בָּאָרֶץon the land לֹאnot רָעָבfamine לַלֶּחֶםof bread וְלֹאor צָמָאthirst לַמַּיִםfor water כִּיbut אִםif לִשְׁמֹעַof hearing אֵת[obj] דִּבְרֵיwords יְהוָהLORD

Behold, the days are coming, declares the Lord GOD, when I will send a famine on the land— not a famine of bread or a thirst for water, but a famine of hearing the words of the LORD.

8:12

וְנָעוּwill stagger מִיָּםfrom sea עַדto יָםsea וּמִצָּפוֹןand from north וְעַדto מִזְרָחeast יְשׁוֹטְטוּroam לְבַקֵּשׁseeking אֶת[obj] דְּבַרword יְהוָהLORD וְלֹאnot יִמְצָאוּfind

People will stagger from sea to sea and roam from north to east, seeking the word of the LORD, but they will not find it.

8:13

בַּיּוֹםin that day הַהוּאwill תִּתְעַלַּפְנָהfaint הַבְּתוּלֹתwomen הַיָּפוֹתlovely וְהַבַּחוּרִיםthe young men well בַּצָּמָאfrom thirst

In that day the lovely young women— the young men as well— will faint from thirst.

8:14

הַנִּשְׁבָּעִיםwho swear בְּאַשְׁמַתby the guilt שֹׁמְרוֹןSamaria וְאָמְרוּand say חֵיsurely אֱלֹהֶיךָyour god O דָּןDan וְחֵיor as surely דֶּרֶךְway בְּאֵרBeersheba שָׁבַעBeersheba lives וְנָפְלוּthey will fall וְלֹאnever יָקוּמוּrise עוֹדagain

Those who swear by the guilt of Samaria and say, ‘As surely as your god lives, O Dan,’ or, ‘As surely as the way of Beersheba lives’— they will fall, never to rise again.”
