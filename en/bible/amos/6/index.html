Amos 6

Woe to those at ease

Open in the interlinear reader

6:1

הוֹיWoe הַשַּׁאֲנַנִּיםthose at ease בְּצִיּוֹןin Zion וְהַבֹּטְחִיםand those secure בְּהַרon Mount שֹׁמְרוֹןSamaria נְקֻבֵיdistinguished רֵאשִׁיתforemost הַגּוֹיִםthe nation וּבָאוּand come לָהֶםto whom בֵּיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

Woe to those at ease in Zion and those secure on Mount Samaria, the distinguished ones of the foremost nation, to whom the house of Israel comes.

6:2

עִבְרוּcross כַלְנֵהCalneh וּרְאוּand see וּלְכוּgo מִשָּׁםfrom there חֲמַתgreat רַבָּהHamath וּרְדוּthen go down גַתGath פְּלִשְׁתּיםPhilistines הֲטוֹבִיםare you better מִןthan הַמַּמְלָכוֹתthese kingdoms הָאֵלֶּהthese אִםif רַבlarger גְּבוּלָםtheir territory מִגְּבֻלְכֶםfrom your border

Cross over to Calneh and see; go from there to the great Hamath; then go down to Gath of the Philistines. Are you better than these kingdoms? Is their territory larger than yours?

6:3

הַמְנַדִּיםdismiss לְיוֹםthe day רָעcalamity וַתַּגִּישׁוּןand bring near שֶׁבֶתreign חָמָסviolence

You dismiss the day of calamity and bring near a reign of violence.

6:4

הַשֹּׁכְבִיםlie עַלon מִטּוֹתbeds inlaid שֵׁןivory וּסְרֻחִיםand lounge עַלupon עַרְשׂוֹתָםcouches וְאֹכְלִיםyou dine כָּרִיםlambs מִצֹּאןfrom the flock וַעֲגָלִיםand calves מִתּוֹךְfrom among מַרְבֵּקstall

You lie on beds inlaid with ivory, and lounge upon your couches. You dine on lambs from the flock and calves from the stall.

6:5

הַפֹּרְטִיםimprovise עַלon פִּיmouth הַנָּבֶלthe harp כְּדָוִידlike David חָשְׁבוּinvent לָהֶםto them כְּלֵיinstruments שִׁירmusical

You improvise songs on the harp like David and invent your own musical instruments.

6:6

הַשֹּׁתִיםdrink בְּמִזְרְקֵיby the bowlful יַיִןwine וְרֵאשִׁיתfinest שְׁמָנִיםyourselves יִמְשָׁחוּanoint וְלֹאand not נֶחְלוּto grieve עַלover שֵׁבֶרruin יוֹסֵףJoseph

You drink wine by the bowlful and anoint yourselves with the finest oils, but you fail to grieve over the ruin of Joseph.

6:7

לָכֵןtherefore עַתָּהnow יִגְלוּinto exile בְּרֹאשׁinto as the first גֹּלִיםcaptives וְסָרand your feasting מִרְזַחlounging סְרוּחִיםend

Therefore, you will now go into exile as the first of the captives, and your feasting and lounging will come to an end.

6:8

נִשְׁבַּעsworn אֲדֹנָיLord has יְהוִהGOD בְּנַפְשׁוֹby himself נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod צְבָאוֹתHosts מְתָאֵבdeclared abhor אָנֹכִיI אֶת[obj] גְּאוֹןpride יַעֲקֹבJacob’s וְאַרְמְנֹתָיוand his citadels שָׂנֵאתִיdetest וְהִסְגַּרְתִּיdeliver עִירcity וּמְלֹאָהּand everything in it

The Lord GOD has sworn by Himself—the LORD, the God of Hosts, has declared: “I abhor Jacob’s pride and detest his citadels, so I will deliver up the city and everything in it.”

6:9

וְהָיָהand there אִםif יִוָּתְרוּleft עֲשָׂרָהten אֲנָשִׁיםmen בְּבַיִתin house אֶחָדone וָמֵתוּdie

And if there are ten men left in one house, they too will die.

6:10

וּנְשָׂאוֹand when דּוֹדוֹrelative וּמְסָרְפוֹbodies picks them לְהוֹצִיאto remove עֲצָמִיםremove מִןfrom הַבַּיִתthe house וְאָמַרhe will call לַאֲשֶׁרto whom בְּיַרְכְּתֵיone הַבַּיִתinside הַעוֹדstill עִמָּךְwith you וְאָמַרand say אָפֶסnone וְאָמַרwill answer הָסsilence כִּיfor לֹאnot לְהַזְכִּירbe invoked בְּשֵׁםthe name יְהוָהLORD

And when the relative who is to burn the bodies picks them up to remove them from the house, he will call to one inside, “Is anyone else with you?” “None,” that person will answer. “Silence,” the relative will retort, “for the name of the LORD must not be invoked.”

6:11

כִּיfor הִנֵּהbehold יְהוָהLORD מְצַוֶּהcommand וְהִכָּהgives הַבַּיִתhouse הַגָּדוֹלthe great רְסִיסִיםsmashed וְהַבַּיִתhouse הַקָּטֹןsmall to בְּקִעִיםrubble

For the LORD gives a command: “The great house will be smashed to pieces, and the small house to rubble.”

6:12

הַיְרֻצוּןdo gallop בַּסֶּלַעon the cliffs סוּסִיםhorses אִםif יַחֲרוֹשׁplow בַּבְּקָרִיםthe sea with oxen כִּיbut הֲפַכְתֶּםturned לְרֹאשׁinto poison מִשְׁפָּטjustice וּפְרִיand the fruit צְדָקָהrighteousness לְלַעֲנָהinto wormwood

“Do horses gallop on the cliffs? Does one plow the sea with oxen? But you have turned justice into poison and the fruit of righteousness into wormwood—

6:13

הַשְּׂמֵחִיםwho rejoice לְלֹאin Lo דָבָרdebar הָאֹמְרִיםand say הֲלוֹאdid not בְחָזְקֵנוּkarnaim by לָקַחְנוּtake לָנוּto us קַרְנָיִםhorns

you who rejoice in Lo-debar and say, ‘Did we not take Karnaim by our own strength?’

6:14

כִּיfor הִנְנִיbehold I מֵקִיםraise עֲלֵיכֶםagainst you בֵּיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod הַצְּבָאוֹתHosts גּוֹיnation וְלָחֲצוּoppress אֶתְכֶםyou מִלְּבוֹאfrom Brook חֲמָתhamath עַדto נַחַלBrook הָעֲרָבָהthe Arabah

For behold, I will raise up a nation against you, O house of Israel,” declares the LORD, the God of Hosts, “and they will oppress you from Lebo-hamath to the Brook of the Arabah.”
