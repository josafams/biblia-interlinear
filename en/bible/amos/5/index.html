Amos 5

Lament and a call to justice

“Fallen is Virgin Israel, never to rise again. She lies abandoned on her land, with no one to raise her up.”

5:3

This is what the Lord GOD says: “The city that marches out a thousand strong will only see a hundred return, and the one that marches out a hundred strong will have but ten left in the house of Israel.”

5:4

כִּיfor כֹהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD לְבֵיתto the house יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael דִּרְשׁוּנִיseek me וִחְיוּand live

For this is what the LORD says to the house of Israel: “Seek Me and live!

5:5

וְאַלdo not תִּדְרְשׁוּseek בֵּיתBethel אֵלBethel וְהַגִּלְגָּלor go to Gilgal לֹאnot תָבֹאוּjourney וּבְאֵרand Beersheba שֶׁבַעBeersheba לֹאnot תַעֲבֹרוּgo כִּיfor הַגִּלְגָּלGilgal גָּלֹהcarried יִגְלֶהexile וּבֵיתand Bethel אֵלBethel יִהְיֶהcome לְאָוֶןto nothing

Do not seek Bethel or go to Gilgal; do not journey to Beersheba, for Gilgal will surely go into exile, and Bethel will come to nothing.

5:6

דִּרְשׁוּseek אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD וִחְיוּand live פֶּןor יִצְלַחsweep כָּאֵשׁlike fire through the בֵּיתhouse יוֹסֵףJoseph וְאָכְלָהwill devour וְאֵיןeverything with no מְכַבֶּהextinguish לְבֵיתat Bethel to אֵלBethel

Seek the LORD and live, or He will sweep like fire through the house of Joseph; it will devour everything, with no one at Bethel to extinguish it.

5:7

הַהֹפְכִיםwho turn לְלַעֲנָהinto wormwood מִשְׁפָּטjustice וּצְדָקָהand cast righteousness לָאָרֶץto the ground הִנִּיחוּleft

There are those who turn justice into wormwood and cast righteousness to the ground.

5:8

עֹשֵׂהmade כִימָהPleiades וּכְסִילand Orion וְהֹפֵךְwho turns לַבֹּקֶרinto dawn צַלְמָוֶתdarkness וְיוֹםand day לַיְלָהnight הֶחְשִׁיךְsummons הַקּוֹרֵאwho summons the לְמֵיthe waters הַיָּםthe sea וַיִּשְׁפְּכֵםand pours them עַלover פְּנֵיface הָאָרֶץthe earth יְהוָהLORD שְׁמוֹhis name

He who made the Pleiades and Orion, who turns darkness into dawn and darkens day into night, who summons the waters of the sea and pours them over the face of the earth— the LORD is His name—

5:9

הַמַּבְלִיגflashes שֹׁדdestruction עַלon עָזstrong וְשֹׁדso that fury עַלupon מִבְצָרstronghold יָבוֹאcomes

He flashes destruction on the strong, so that fury comes upon the stronghold.

5:10

שָׂנְאוּhate בַשַּׁעַרin the gate מוֹכִיחַreproves וְדֹבֵרspeaks תָּמִיםintegrity יְתָעֵבוּdespise

There are those who hate the one who reproves in the gate and despise him who speaks with integrity.

5:11

לָכֵןtherefore יַעַןbecause בּוֹשַׁסְכֶםyou trample עַלon דָּלpoor וּמַשְׂאַתtax בַּרgrain תִּקְחוּexact מִמֶּנּוּfrom him בָּתֵּיhouses גָזִיתstone בְּנִיתֶםbuilt וְלֹאwill never תֵשְׁבוּlive בָםin כַּרְמֵיvineyards חֶמֶדlush נְטַעְתֶּםplanted וְלֹאwill never תִשְׁתּוּdrink אֶת[obj] יֵינָםwine

Therefore, because you trample on the poor and exact from him a tax of grain, you will never live in the stone houses you have built; you will never drink the wine from the lush vineyards you have planted.

5:12

כִּיfor יָדַעְתִּיknow רַבִּיםmany פִּשְׁעֵיכֶםyour transgressions וַעֲצֻמִיםmany and חַטֹּאתֵיכֶםsins צֹרְרֵיoppress צַדִּיקrighteous לֹקְחֵיtaking כֹפֶרbribes וְאֶבְיוֹנִיםpoor בַּשַּׁעַרin the gate הִטּוּdeprive

For I know that your transgressions are many and your sins are numerous. You oppress the righteous by taking bribes; you deprive the poor of justice in the gate.

5:13

לָכֵןtherefore הַמַּשְׂכִּילthe prudent בָּעֵתin such times הַהִיאthe יִדֹּםsilent כִּיfor עֵתdays רָעָהevil הִיאis

Therefore, the prudent keep silent in such times, for the days are evil.

5:14

דִּרְשׁוּseek טוֹבgood וְאַלnot רָעevil לְמַעַןso תִּחְיוּlive וִיהִיand be כֵןthis יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod צְבָאוֹתHosts אִתְּכֶםwith you כַּאֲשֶׁרlike have אֲמַרְתֶּםhave claimed

Seek good, not evil, so that you may live. And the LORD, the God of Hosts, will be with you, as you have claimed.

5:15

שִׂנְאוּhate רָעevil וְאֶהֱבוּand love טוֹבgood וְהַצִּיגוּestablish בַשַּׁעַרin the gate מִשְׁפָּטjustice אוּלַיperhaps יֶחֱנַןgracious יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod צְבָאוֹתHosts שְׁאֵרִיתremnant יוֹסֵףJoseph

Hate evil and love good; establish justice in the gate. Perhaps the LORD, the God of Hosts, will be gracious to the remnant of Joseph.”

5:16

לָכֵןtherefore כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod צְבָאוֹתHosts אֲדֹנָיLord בְּכָלin all רְחֹבוֹתpublic squares מִסְפֵּדwailing וּבְכָלin all חוּצוֹתstreets יֹאמְרוּsay הוֹAlas הוֹAlas וְקָרְאוּwill be summoned אִכָּרfarmer אֶלto אֵבֶלmourn וּמִסְפֵּדand mourners אֶלto יוֹדְעֵיknow נֶהִיwail

Therefore this is what the LORD, the God of Hosts, the Lord, says: “There will be wailing in all the public squares and cries of ‘Alas! Alas!’ in all the streets. The farmer will be summoned to mourn, and the mourners to wail.

5:17

וּבְכָלin all כְּרָמִיםvineyards מִסְפֵּדwailing כִּיfor אֶעֱבֹרpass בְּקִרְבְּךָthrough your midst אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD

There will be wailing in all the vineyards, for I will pass through your midst,” says the LORD.

5:18

הוֹיWoe הַמִּתְאַוִּיםwho long אֶת[obj] יוֹםday יְהוָהLORD לָמָּהwhat זֶּהwill לָכֶםto you יוֹםday יְהוָהLORD הוּאwill חֹשֶׁךְdarkness וְלֹאand not אוֹרlight

Woe to you who long for the Day of the LORD! What will the Day of the LORD be for you? It will be darkness and not light.

5:19

כַּאֲשֶׁרwho יָנוּסflees אִישׁman מִפְּנֵיfrom before הָאֲרִיa lion וּפְגָעוֹonly to encounter הַדֹּבa bear וּבָאor who enters הַבַּיִתhis house וְסָמַךְand rests יָדוֹhis hand עַלagainst הַקִּירthe wall וּנְשָׁכוֹbitten by הַנָּחָשׁa snake

It will be like a man who flees from a lion, only to encounter a bear, or who enters his house and rests his hand against the wall, only to be bitten by a snake.

5:20

הֲלֹאwill not חֹשֶׁךְdarkness יוֹםday יְהוָהLORD וְלֹאand not אוֹרlight וְאָפֵלeven gloom וְלֹאwith no נֹגַהּbrightness לוֹto him

Will not the Day of the LORD be darkness and not light, even gloom with no brightness in it?

5:21

שָׂנֵאתִיhate מָאַסְתִּיdespise חַגֵּיכֶםyour feasts וְלֹאand not אָרִיחַstench בְּעַצְּרֹתֵיכֶםyour solemn assemblies

“I hate, I despise your feasts! I cannot stand the stench of your solemn assemblies.

5:22

כִּיfor אִםif תַּעֲלוּoffer לִיto me עֹלוֹתburnt offerings וּמִנְחֹתֵיכֶםand grain offerings לֹאnot אֶרְצֶהaccept וְשֶׁלֶםpeace offerings מְרִיאֵיכֶםfattened cattle לֹאno אַבִּיטregard

Even though you offer Me burnt offerings and grain offerings, I will not accept them; for your peace offerings of fattened cattle I will have no regard.

5:23

הָסֵרaway מֵעָלַיfrom the me הֲמוֹןthe noise שִׁרֶיךָyour songs וְזִמְרַתmusic נְבָלֶיךָyour harps לֹאnot אֶשְׁמָעlisten

Take away from Me the noise of your songs! I will not listen to the music of your harps.

5:24

וְיִגַּלroll כַּמַּיִםlike a river מִשְׁפָּטjustice וּצְדָקָהand righteousness כְּנַחַלstream אֵיתָןflowing

But let justice roll on like a river, and righteousness like an ever-flowing stream.

5:25

הַזְּבָחִיםsacrifices וּמִנְחָהand offerings הִגַּשְׁתֶּםbring לִיto me בַמִּדְבָּרin the wilderness אַרְבָּעִיםforty שָׁנָהyears בֵּיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

Did you bring Me sacrifices and offerings forty years in the wilderness, O house of Israel?

5:26

וּנְשָׂאתֶםand bear אֵת[obj] סִכּוּתalong מַלְכְּכֶםSakkuth your וְאֵתand [obj] כִּיּוּןking צַלְמֵיכֶםidols כּוֹכַבstar אֱלֹהֵיכֶםgod אֲשֶׁרthat עֲשִׂיתֶםmade לָכֶםfor yourselves

You have taken along Sakkuth your king and Kaiwan your star god, the idols you made for yourselves.

5:27

וְהִגְלֵיתִיwill send into exile אֶתְכֶםyou מֵהָלְאָהbeyond לְדַמָּשֶׂקDamascus אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod צְבָאוֹתHosts שְׁמוֹname is

Therefore I will send you into exile beyond Damascus,” says the LORD, whose name is the God of Hosts.
