Amos 3

The responsibility of the chosen people

Hear this word that the LORD has spoken against you, O children of Israel, against the whole family that I brought up out of the land of Egypt:

3:2

רַקonly אֶתְכֶםyou יָדַעְתִּיknown מִכֹּלfrom all מִשְׁפְּחוֹתfamilies הָאֲדָמָהthe earth עַלon כֵּןtherefore אֶפְקֹדpunish עֲלֵיכֶםyou for אֵת[obj] כָּלall עֲוֺנֹתֵיכֶםyour iniquities

“Only you have I known from all the families of the earth; therefore I will punish you for all your iniquities.”

3:3

הֲיֵלְכוּcan walk שְׁנַיִםtwo יַחְדָּוtogether בִּלְתִּיwithout אִםif נוֹעָדוּagreeing where

Can two walk together without agreeing where to go?

3:4

הֲיִשְׁאַגroar אַרְיֵהlion בַּיַּעַרin the forest וְטֶרֶףprey אֵיןno לוֹto him הֲיִתֵּןgive כְּפִירyoung lion קוֹלוֹgrowl מִמְּעֹנָתוֹin his den בִּלְתִּיnothing אִםif לָכָדcaught

Does a lion roar in the forest when he has no prey? Does a young lion growl in his den if he has caught nothing?

3:5

הֲתִפֹּלfall צִפּוֹרbird עַלin פַּחsnare הָאָרֶץland וּמוֹקֵשׁwhere bait אֵיןno לָהּto her הֲיַעֲלֶהspring פַּחtrap מִןfrom הָאֲדָמָהthe ground וְלָכוֹדwhen it has לֹאnot יִלְכּוֹדcatch

Does a bird land in a snare where no bait has been set? Does a trap spring from the ground when it has nothing to catch?

3:6

אִםif יִתָּקַעsounds שׁוֹפָרram’s horn בְּעִירin a city וְעָםpeople לֹאnot יֶחֱרָדוּtremble אִםif תִּהְיֶהcomes רָעָהcalamity בְּעִירto a city וַיהוָהLORD לֹאnot עָשָׂהcaused

If a ram’s horn sounds in a city, do the people not tremble? If calamity comes to a city, has not the LORD caused it?

3:7

כִּיfor לֹאnot יַעֲשֶׂהdoes אֲדֹנָיLord יְהוִהGOD דָּבָרnothing כִּיfor אִםif גָּלָהrevealed סוֹדוֹrevealing his אֶלto עֲבָדָיוhis servants הַנְּבִיאִיםthe prophets

Surely the Lord GOD does nothing without revealing His plan to His servants the prophets.

3:8

אַרְיֵהlion שָׁאָגroared מִיwho לֹאnot יִירָאfear אֲדֹנָיLord יְהוִהGOD דִּבֶּרspoken מִיwho לֹאnot יִנָּבֵאprophesy

The lion has roared— who will not fear? The Lord GOD has spoken— who will not prophesy?

3:9

הַשְׁמִיעוּproclaim עַלto אַרְמְנוֹתcitadels בְּאַשְׁדּוֹדAshdod וְעַלand to אַרְמְנוֹתcitadels בְּאֶרֶץin land מִצְרָיִםEgypt וְאִמְרוּand say הֵאָסְפוּassemble עַלon הָרֵיmountains שֹׁמְרוֹןSamaria וּרְאוּsee מְהוּמֹתunrest רַבּוֹתgreat בְּתוֹכָהּin her among וַעֲשׁוּקִיםoppression בְּקִרְבָּהּin her midst

Proclaim to the citadels of Ashdod and to the citadels of Egypt: “Assemble on the mountains of Samaria; see the great unrest in the city and the acts of oppression in her midst.”

3:10

וְלֹאthey not יָדְעוּknow עֲשׂוֹתdo נְכֹחָהright נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD הָאוֹצְרִיםstore חָמָסviolence וָשֹׁדand destruction בְּאַרְמְנוֹתֵיהֶםin their citadels

“For they know not how to do right,” declares the LORD. “They store up violence and destruction in their citadels.”

3:11

לָכֵןtherefore כֹּהthis אָמַרis what אֲדֹנָיLord GOD יְהוִהsays צַרenemy וּסְבִיבwill surround הָאָרֶץthe land וְהוֹרִדhe will pull down מִמֵּךְfrom you עֻזֵּךְstrongholds וְנָבֹזּוּand plunder אַרְמְנוֹתָיִךְyour citadels

Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: “An enemy will surround the land; he will pull down your strongholds and plunder your citadels.”

3:12

כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had יַצִּילsnatches הָרֹעֶהthe shepherd מִפִּיfrom the mouth הָאֲרִיthe lion שְׁתֵּיtwo כְרָעַיִםlegs אוֹor בְדַלpiece אֹזֶןear כֵּןso יִנָּצְלוּrescued בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites הַיֹּשְׁבִיםdwelling בְּשֹׁמְרוֹןin Samaria בִּפְאַתcorner מִטָּהbed וּבִדְמֶשֶׁקor the cushion עָרֶשׂcouch

This is what the LORD says: “As the shepherd snatches from the mouth of the lion two legs or a piece of an ear, so the Israelites dwelling in Samaria will be rescued having just the corner of a bed or the cushion of a couch.

3:13

שִׁמְעוּhear וְהָעִידוּand testify בְּבֵיתagainst the house יַעֲקֹבJacob נְאֻםdeclares אֲדֹנָיLord יְהוִהGOD אֱלֹהֵיGod הַצְּבָאוֹתof Hosts

Hear and testify against the house of Jacob, declares the Lord GOD, the God of Hosts.

3:14

כִּיfor בְּיוֹםon day פָּקְדִיI punish פִשְׁעֵיtransgressions יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael עָלָיוfor their וּפָקַדְתִּיwill visit destruction עַלon מִזְבְּחוֹתaltars בֵּיתBethel אֵלBethel וְנִגְדְּעוּwill be cut קַרְנוֹתhorns הַמִּזְבֵּחַthe altar וְנָפְלוּfall לָאָרֶץto the ground

On the day I punish Israel for their transgressions, I will visit destruction on the altars of Bethel; the horns of the altar will be cut off, and they will fall to the ground.

3:15

וְהִכֵּיתִיI will tear בֵיתhouse הַחֹרֶףthe winter עַלon בֵּיתhouse הַקָּיִץwith the summer וְאָבְדוּalso perish בָּתֵּיhouses הַשֵּׁןivory וְסָפוּperish and בָּתִּיםhouses רַבִּיםgreat נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD

I will tear down the winter house along with the summer house; the houses of ivory will also perish, and the great houses will come to an end,” declares the LORD.
