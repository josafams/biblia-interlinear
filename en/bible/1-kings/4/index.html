Ahishar was in charge of the palace; and Adoniram son of Abda was in charge of the forced labor.

Solomon had twelve governors over all Israel to provide food for the king and his household. Each one would arrange provisions for one month of the year,

וְאֵלֶּהand these שְׁמוֹתָםtheir names בֶּןBen חוּרhur בְּהַרin the hill country אֶפְרָיִםEphraim

and these were their names: Ben-hur in the hill country of Ephraim;

Ben-deker in Makaz, in Shaalbim, in Beth-shemesh, and in Elon-beth-hanan;

Ben-hesed in Arubboth (Socoh and all the land of Hepher belonged to him);

Ben-abinadab in Naphath-dor (Taphath, a daughter of Solomon, was his wife);

Baana son of Ahilud in Taanach, in Megiddo, and in all of Beth-shean next to Zarethan below Jezreel, from Beth-shean to Abel-meholah and on past Jokmeam;

4:13

בֶּןBen גֶּבֶרgeber בְּרָמֹתin Ramoth גִּלְעָדGilead לוֹto him חַוֺּתvillages יָאִירJair בֶּןson מְנַשֶּׁהManasseh אֲשֶׁרthat בַּגִּלְעָדin Gilead לוֹto him חֶבֶלregion אַרְגֹּבArgob אֲשֶׁרthat בַּבָּשָׁןin Bashan שִׁשִּׁיםsixty עָרִיםcities גְּדֹלוֹתgreat חוֹמָהwalls וּבְרִיחַand bars נְחֹשֶׁתbronze

Ben-geber in Ramoth-gilead (the villages of Jair son of Manasseh in Gilead belonged to him, as well as the region of Argob in Bashan with its sixty great cities with walls and bronze bars);

4:14

אֲחִינָדָבAhinadab בֶּןson עִדֹּאIddo מַחֲנָיְמָהMahanaim

Ahinadab son of Iddo in Mahanaim;

4:15

אֲחִימַעַץAhimaaz בְּנַפְתָּלִיin Naphtali גַּםeven הוּאhe לָקַחmarried אֶת[obj] בָּשְׂמַתBasemath בַּתdaughter שְׁלֹמֹהSolomon לְאִשָּׁהto wife

Ahimaaz in Naphtali (he had married Basemath, a daughter of Solomon);

4:16

בַּעֲנָאBaana בֶּןson חוּשָׁיHushai בְּאָשֵׁרin Asher וּבְעָלוֹתand in Aloth

Baana son of Hushai in Asher and in Aloth;

4:17

יְהוֹשָׁפָטJehoshaphat בֶּןson פָּרוּחַParuah בְּיִשָׂשכָרin Issachar

Jehoshaphat son of Paruah in Issachar;

שִׁמְעִיShimei בֶןson אֵלָאEla בְּבִנְיָמִןin Benjamin

Shimei son of Ela in Benjamin;

4:19

גֶּבֶרGeber בֶּןson אֻרִיUri בְּאֶרֶץin the land גִּלְעָדGilead אֶרֶץterritories סִיחוֹןSihon מֶלֶךְking הָאֱמֹרִיthe Amorites וְעֹגand of Og מֶלֶךְking הַבָּשָׁןof Bashan וּנְצִיבalso governor אֶחָדone אֲשֶׁרthat בָּאָרֶץin the land

Geber son of Uri in the land of Gilead, including the territories of Sihon king of the Amorites and of Og king of Bashan. There was also one governor in the land of Judah.

4:20

יְהוּדָהJudah וְיִשְׂרָאֵלand Israel רַבִּיםnumerous כַּחוֹלas the sand אֲשֶׁרthat עַלon הַיָּםthe seashore לָרֹבto abundance אֹכְלִיםeating וְשֹׁתִיםand drinking וּשְׂמֵחִיםand rejoicing

The people of Judah and Israel were as numerous as the sand on the seashore, and they were eating and drinking and rejoicing.

4:21

And Solomon reigned over all the kingdoms from the Euphrates to the land of the Philistines, as far as the border of Egypt. These kingdoms offered tribute and served Solomon all the days of his life.

4:22

וַיְהִיand was לֶחֶםprovisions שְׁלֹמֹהSolomon’s לְיוֹםday אֶחָדsingle שְׁלֹשִׁיםthirty כֹּרcors סֹלֶתfine flour וְשִׁשִּׁיםsixty כֹּרcors קָמַחmeal

Solomon’s provisions for a single day were thirty cors of fine flour, sixty cors of meal,

4:23

עֲשָׂרָהten בָקָרoxen בְּרִאִיםfat וְעֶשְׂרִיםtwenty בָּקָרoxen רְעִיrange וּמֵאָהand a hundred צֹאןsheep לְבַדto addition מֵאַיָּלas deer וּצְבִיgazelles וְיַחְמוּרroebucks וּבַרְבֻּרִיםand poultry אֲבוּסִיםfattened poultry

ten fat oxen, twenty range oxen, and a hundred sheep, as well as deer, gazelles, roebucks, and fattened poultry.

4:24

כִּיfor הוּאhe רֹדֶהdominion בְּכָלover everything עֵבֶרwest הַנָּהָרthe Euphrates מִתִּפְסַחfrom Tiphsah וְעַדto עַזָּהGaza בְּכָלover all מַלְכֵיkings עֵבֶרwest הַנָּהָרthe kingdoms וְשָׁלוֹםpeace הָיָהhad לוֹto him מִכָּלon all עֲבָרָיוsides מִסָּבִיבsides

For Solomon had dominion over everything west of the Euphrates—over all the kingdoms from Tiphsah to Gaza—and he had peace on all sides.

4:25

וַיֵּשֶׁבdwelt יְהוּדָהJudah וְיִשְׂרָאֵלand Israel לָבֶטַחsecurely אִישׁman תַּחַתunder גַּפְנוֹvine his וְתַחַתand under תְּאֵנָתוֹown fig tree מִדָּןfrom Dan וְעַדto each בְּאֵרBeersheba שָׁבַעBeersheba כֹּלthroughout יְמֵיdays שְׁלֹמֹהSolomon

Throughout the days of Solomon, Judah and Israel dwelt securely from Dan to Beersheba, each man under his own vine and his own fig tree.

4:26

וַיְהִיand was לִשְׁלֹמֹהSolomon had אַרְבָּעִים4 אֶלֶף000 אֻרְוֺתstalls סוּסִיםhorses לְמֶרְכָּבוֹfor his chariot וּשְׁנֵיםand 12 עָשָׂר12 אֶלֶף000 פָּרָשִׁיםhorses

Solomon had 4,000 stalls for his chariot horses and 12,000 horses.

4:27

וְכִלְכְּלוּprovided הַנִּצָּבִיםthe governors הָאֵלֶּהthese אֶת[obj] הַמֶּלֶךְfor king שְׁלֹמֹהSolomon וְאֵתand [obj] כָּלall הַקָּרֵבwho came אֶלto שֻׁלְחַןtable הַמֶּלֶךְthe king שְׁלֹמֹהsaw אִישׁeach חָדְשׁוֹmonth לֹאnot יְעַדְּרוּlacking דָּבָרword

Each month the governors in turn provided food for King Solomon and all who came to his table. They saw to it that nothing was lacking.

4:28

וְהַשְּׂעֹרִיםquotas of barley וְהַתֶּבֶןand straw לַסּוּסִיםfor the chariot horses וְלָרָכֶשׁand other horses יָבִאוּbrought אֶלto הַמָּקוֹםthe place אֲשֶׁרthat יִהְיֶהbe שָּׁםthere אִישׁman כְּמִשְׁפָּטוֹlike his prescribed

Each one also brought to the required place their quotas of barley and straw for the chariot horses and other horses.

4:29

וַיִּתֵּןand gave אֱלֹהִיםGod חָכְמָהwisdom לִשְׁלֹמֹהSolomon וּתְבוּנָהinsight and understanding הַרְבֵּהand beyond מְאֹדvery וְרֹחַבdeep לֵבheart כַּחוֹלlike the sand אֲשֶׁרthat עַלon שְׂפַתseashore הַיָּםthe seashore

And God gave Solomon wisdom, exceedingly deep insight, and understanding beyond measure, like the sand on the seashore.

4:30

וַתֵּרֶבwas greater חָכְמַתwisdom שְׁלֹמֹהSolomon’s מֵחָכְמַתfrom wisdom כָּלall בְּנֵיmen קֶדֶםeast וּמִכֹּלthan all חָכְמַתwisdom מִצְרָיִםEgypt

Solomon’s wisdom was greater than that of all the men of the East, greater than all the wisdom of Egypt.

4:31

He was wiser than all men—wiser than Ethan the Ezrahite, and wiser than Heman, Calcol, and Darda, the sons of Mahol. And his fame spread throughout the surrounding nations.

4:32

וַיְדַבֵּרSolomon composed שְׁלֹשֶׁתthree אֲלָפִיםthousand מָשָׁלproverbs וַיְהִיand was שִׁירוֹhis songs חֲמִשָּׁהfive וָאָלֶףthousand and

Solomon composed three thousand proverbs, and his songs numbered a thousand and five.

4:33

וַיְדַבֵּרhe spoke עַלon הָעֵצִיםof trees מִןfrom הָאֶרֶזthe cedar אֲשֶׁרthat בַּלְּבָנוֹןin Lebanon וְעַדto הָאֵזוֹבthe hyssop אֲשֶׁרthat יֹצֵאgrowing בַּקִּירin the wall וַיְדַבֵּרand he taught עַלon הַבְּהֵמָהanimals וְעַלand on הָעוֹףbirds וְעַלand on הָרֶמֶשׂreptiles וְעַלand on הַדָּגִיםfish

He spoke of trees, from the cedar in Lebanon to the hyssop growing in the wall, and he taught about animals, birds, reptiles, and fish.

4:34

So men of all nations came to listen to Solomon’s wisdom, sent by all the kings of the earth, who had heard of his wisdom.
