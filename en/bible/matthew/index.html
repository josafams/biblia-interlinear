Matthew Κατὰ Ματθαῖον

Matthew narrates the life of Jesus of Nazareth, from the genealogy and the birth in Bethlehem to the death in Jerusalem, the resurrection and the sending of the disciples to all nations. The book insists that Jesus is the Messiah awaited by Israel, “the son of David, the son of Abraham” (1:1), and shows again and again that His actions fulfill what the prophets had said. Much of the gospel is taken up by teachings, gathered into five long discourses, the first of which is the Sermon on the Mount (chs. 5–7). Prominent are the proclamation of the “kingdom of heaven,” the debates with Pharisees and scribes about the interpretation of the Law and the life of the community of disciples, which only this gospel calls a “church” (16:18; 18:17).

Structure

  1. Jesus' origin and childhood chs. 1–2

    The genealogy from Abraham to Jesus, the birth in Bethlehem, the visit of the Magi, the flight to Egypt and the move to Nazareth.

  2. Preparation chs. 3–4

    The preaching of John the Baptist, the baptism of Jesus, the temptation in the wilderness and the call of the first disciples by the Sea of Galilee.

  3. The Sermon on the Mount chs. 5–7

    First discourse: the Beatitudes, Jesus and the Law, almsgiving, prayer and fasting done in secret, and the comparison between those who build on the rock and those who build on the sand.

  4. Miracles and mission chs. 8–10

    A series of healings and other miracles, the call of Matthew and the second discourse, with the instructions to the Twelve sent out to the towns of Israel.

  5. Opposition and parables chs. 11–13

    John the Baptist's question, the arguments about the Sabbath and the charge that Jesus acted by the power of Beelzebul; in the third discourse, the parables of the Kingdom.

  6. Jesus forms His disciples chs. 14–18

    The feedings of the crowds, Peter's confession, the first announcements of the passion, the transfiguration and the fourth discourse, on life together in the community.

  7. Toward Jerusalem and debates in the temple chs. 19–23

    Teachings on the way to Judea, the entry into Jerusalem, the cleansing of the temple, parables of judgment and the controversies with the leaders, which end in the woes against the scribes and Pharisees.

  8. The discourse on the end chs. 24–25

    Fifth discourse, on the Mount of Olives: the destruction of the temple, the coming of the Son of Man and parables about keeping watch and giving account.

  9. Passion and resurrection chs. 26–28

    The anointing at Bethany, the Last Supper, the arrest in Gethsemane, the trials, the crucifixion, the empty tomb and the final sending of the disciples.
