Hosea 4

The charge against the people and the priests

Hear the word of the LORD, O children of Israel, for the LORD has a case against the people of the land: “There is no truth, no loving devotion, and no knowledge of God in the land!

4:2

Cursing and lying, murder and stealing, and adultery are rampant; one act of bloodshed follows another.

4:3

Therefore the land mourns, and all who dwell in it will waste away with the beasts of the field and the birds of the air; even the fish of the sea disappear.

4:4

אַךְbut אִישׁman אַלno יָרֵבcontend וְאַלlet no יוֹכַחreproof אִישׁman וְעַמְּךָfor your people כִּמְרִיבֵיcontend כֹהֵןpriest

But let no man contend; let no man offer reproof; for your people are like those who contend with a priest.

4:5

וְכָשַׁלְתָּwill stumble הַיּוֹםby day וְכָשַׁלstumble גַּםwill נָבִיאprophet עִמְּךָwith you לָיְלָהnight וְדָמִיתִיdestroy אִמֶּךָyour mother

You will stumble by day, and the prophet will stumble with you by night; so I will destroy your mother—

4:6

נִדְמוּperish עַמִּיmy people מִבְּלִיdestroyed for lack הַדָּעַתof knowledge כִּיbecause אַתָּהyou הַדַּעַתknowledge מָאַסְתָּrejected וְאֶמְאָסְאךָalso reject you מִכַּהֵןpriests since לִיto me וַתִּשְׁכַּחyou have forgotten תּוֹרַתlaw אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God אֶשְׁכַּחforget בָּנֶיךָyour children גַּםalso אָנִיI

My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge. Because you have rejected knowledge, I will also reject you as My priests. Since you have forgotten the law of your God, I will also forget your children.

4:7

כְּרֻבָּםthey multiplied כֵּןso חָטְאוּsinned לִיto me כְּבוֹדָםtheir glory בְּקָלוֹןdisgrace אָמִירexchanged

The more they multiplied, the more they sinned against Me; they exchanged their Glory for a thing of disgrace.

4:8

חַטַּאתsins עַמִּיmy people יֹאכֵלוּfeed וְאֶלand to עֲוֺנָםiniquity יִשְׂאוּhearts נַפְשׁוֹset hearts

They feed on the sins of My people and set their hearts on iniquity.

4:9

וְהָיָהbe כָעָםlike people כַּכֹּהֵןlike priest וּפָקַדְתִּיwill punish עָלָיוfor דְּרָכָיוtheir ways וּמַעֲלָלָיוdeeds אָשִׁיבrepay לוֹfor their

And it shall be like people, like priest. I will punish both of them for their ways and repay them for their deeds.

4:10

וְאָכְלוּthey will eat וְלֹאbut not יִשְׂבָּעוּsatisfied הִזְנוּpromiscuous וְלֹאbut not יִפְרֹצוּmultiply כִּיfor אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD עָזְבוּstopped לִשְׁמֹרobeying

They will eat but not be satisfied; they will be promiscuous but not multiply. For they have stopped obeying the LORD.

4:11

זְנוּתpromiscuity וְיַיִןwine וְתִירוֹשׁand new wine יִקַּחtake לֵבunderstanding

Promiscuity, wine, and new wine take away understanding.

4:12

עַמִּיmy people בְּעֵצוֹtheir wooden יִשְׁאָלconsult וּמַקְלוֹidols and their divining rods יַגִּידinform לוֹto him כִּיfor רוּחַspirit זְנוּנִיםprostitution הִתְעָהastray וַיִּזְנוּharlot מִתַּחַתfrom under אֱלֹהֵיהֶםtheir God

My people consult their wooden idols, and their divining rods inform them. For a spirit of prostitution leads them astray and they have played the harlot against their God.

4:13

עַלon רָאשֵׁיmountaintops הֶהָרִיםthe mountaintops יְזַבֵּחוּsacrifice וְעַלon הַגְּבָעוֹתthe hills יְקַטֵּרוּburn תַּחַתunder אַלּוֹןoak וְלִבְנֶהpoplar and וְאֵלָהterebinth כִּיbecause טוֹבpleasant צִלָּהּshade עַלto כֵּןso תִּזְנֶינָהprostitution בְּנוֹתֵיכֶםyour daughters וְכַלּוֹתֵיכֶםand daughters תְּנָאַפְנָהadultery

They sacrifice on the mountaintops and burn offerings on the hills, under oak, poplar, and terebinth, because their shade is pleasant. And so your daughters turn to prostitution and your daughters-in-law to adultery.

4:14

לֹאnot אֶפְקוֹדpunish עַלon בְּנוֹתֵיכֶםyour daughters כִּיwhen תִזְנֶינָהprostitute themselves וְעַלin כַּלּוֹתֵיכֶםyour daughters law כִּיwhen תְנָאַפְנָהcommit adultery כִּיfor הֵםthey עִםwith הַזֹּנוֹתprostitutes יְפָרֵדוּseparated וְעִםwith הַקְּדֵשׁוֹתshrine prostitutes יְזַבֵּחוּoffer sacrifices וְעָםso a people לֹאnot יָבִיןunderstanding יִלָּבֵטruin

I will not punish your daughters when they prostitute themselves, nor your daughters-in-law when they commit adultery. For the men themselves go off with prostitutes and offer sacrifices with shrine prostitutes. So a people without understanding will come to ruin.

4:15

אִםif זֹנֶהprostitute אַתָּהyou יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael אַלdo יֶאְשַׁםavoid such guilt יְהוּדָהJudah וְאַלnot תָּבֹאוּjourney הַגִּלְגָּלto Gilgal וְאַלdo not תַּעֲלוּgo up בֵּיתBeth אָוֶןaven וְאַלand do not תִּשָּׁבְעוּswear חַיsurely יְהוָהLORD

Though you prostitute yourself, O Israel, may Judah avoid such guilt! Do not journey to Gilgal, do not go up to Beth-aven, and do not swear on oath, ‘As surely as the LORD lives!’

4:16

כִּיfor כְּפָרָהheifer סֹרֵרָהstubborn סָרַרobstinate יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael עַתָּהnow יִרְעֵםshepherd them יְהוָהLORD כְּכֶבֶשׂlike lambs בַּמֶּרְחָבin an open meadow

For Israel is as obstinate as a stubborn heifer. Can the LORD now shepherd them like lambs in an open meadow?

4:17

חֲבוּרjoined עֲצַבִּיםidols אֶפְרָיִםEphraim הַנַּחleave לוֹto him

Ephraim is joined to idols; leave him alone!

4:18

סָרturn סָבְאָםtheir rulers הַזְנֵהprostitution הִזְנוּrulers אָהֲבוּdearly הֵבוּlove קָלוֹןdisgrace מָגִנֶּיהָher shields

When their liquor is gone, they turn to prostitution; their rulers dearly love disgrace.

4:19

צָרַרwrapped רוּחַwhirlwind אוֹתָהּher בִּכְנָפֶיהָin its wings וְיֵבֹשׁוּshame מִזִּבְחוֹתָםtheir sacrifices

The whirlwind has wrapped them in its wings, and their sacrifices will bring them shame.
