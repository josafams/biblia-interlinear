Hosea 2

The unfaithful wife and the new betrothal

Open in the interlinear reader

2:1

אִמְרוּsay לַאֲחֵיכֶםof your brothers עַמִּיmy people וְלַאֲחוֹתֵיכֶםand of your sisters רֻחָמָהloved

“Say of your brothers, ‘My people,’ and of your sisters, ‘My loved one.’

2:2

רִיבוּrebuke בְאִמְּכֶםyour mother רִיבוּrebuke כִּיfor הִיאis לֹאnot אִשְׁתִּיmy wife וְאָנֹכִיand I am לֹאnot אִישָׁהּher husband let her וְתָסֵרremove זְנוּנֶיהָadultery her מִפָּנֶיהfrom face וְנַאֲפוּפֶיהָand the unfaithfulness מִבֵּיןfrom between שָׁדֶיהָher breasts

Rebuke your mother, rebuke her, for she is not My wife, and I am not her husband. Let her remove the adultery from her face and the unfaithfulness from between her breasts.

2:3

פֶּןotherwise אַפְשִׁיטֶנָּהstrip her עֲרֻמָּהnaked וְהִצַּגְתִּיהָand expose her כְּיוֹםlike the day הִוָּלְדָהּher birth וְשַׂמְתִּיהָwill make her כַמִּדְבָּרlike a desert וְשַׁתִּהָand turn her כְּאֶרֶץa land צִיָּהparched וַהֲמִתִּיהָher die בַּצָּמָאthirst

Otherwise, I will strip her naked and expose her like the day of her birth. I will make her like a desert and turn her into a parched land, and I will let her die of thirst.

2:4

וְאֶתand [obj] בָּנֶיהָher children לֹאno אֲרַחֵםcompassion on כִּיbecause בְנֵיchildren זְנוּנִיםadultery הֵמָּהthey

I will have no compassion on her children, because they are the children of adultery.

2:5

כִּיfor זָנְתָהharlot אִמָּםtheir mother הֹבִישָׁהplayed הוֹרָתָםconceived them כִּיfor אָמְרָהthought אֵלְכָהgo אַחֲרֵיafter מְאַהֲבַיmy lovers נֹתְנֵיgive לַחְמִיme bread וּמֵימַיand water צַמְרִיwool וּפִשְׁתִּיand linen שַׁמְנִיoil וְשִׁקּוּיָיand drink

For their mother has played the harlot and has conceived them in disgrace. For she thought, ‘I will go after my lovers, who give me bread and water, wool and linen, oil and drink.’

2:6

לָכֵןtherefore הִנְנִיbehold I שָׂךְhedge אֶת[obj] דַּרְכֵּךְpath בַּסִּירִיםwith thorns וְגָדַרְתִּיwill enclose אֶת[obj] גְּדֵרָהּwall וּנְתִיבוֹתֶיהָway לֹאnot תִמְצָאfind

Therefore, behold, I will hedge up her path with thorns; I will enclose her with a wall, so she cannot find her way.

2:7

וְרִדְּפָהshe will pursue אֶת[obj] מְאַהֲבֶיהָher lovers וְלֹאbut not תַשִּׂיגcatch אֹתָםthem וּבִקְשָׁתַםshe will seek them וְלֹאbut not תִמְצָאfind וְאָמְרָהsay אֵלְכָהgo וְאָשׁוּבָהwill return אֶלto אִישִׁיmy husband הָרִאשׁוֹןfirst כִּיfor טוֹבbetter לִיto me אָזthen מֵעָתָּהthan now

She will pursue her lovers but not catch them; she will seek them but not find them. Then she will say, ‘I will return to my first husband, for then I was better off than now.’

2:8

וְהִיאand she לֹאnot יָדְעָהacknowledge כִּיthat אָנֹכִיI נָתַתִּיgave לָהּto her הַדָּגָןgrain וְהַתִּירוֹשׁnew wine וְהַיִּצְהָרand oil וְכֶסֶףsilver הִרְבֵּיתִיlavished לָהּto her וְזָהָבand gold עָשׂוּcrafted לַבָּעַלBaal

For she does not acknowledge that it was I who gave her grain, new wine, and oil, who lavished on her silver and gold— which they crafted for Baal.

2:9

לָכֵןtherefore אָשׁוּבback וְלָקַחְתִּיtake דְגָנִיmy grain בְּעִתּוֹin its time וְתִירוֹשִׁיand my new wine בְּמוֹעֲדוֹin its season וְהִצַּלְתִּיwill take צַמְרִיmy wool וּפִשְׁתִּיand linen לְכַסּוֹתgiven to cover אֶת[obj] עֶרְוָתָהּher nakedness

Therefore I will take back My grain in its time and My new wine in its season; I will take away My wool and linen, which were given to cover her nakedness.

2:10

וְעַתָּהand now אֲגַלֶּהexpose אֶת[obj] נַבְלֻתָהּher lewdness לְעֵינֵיin the sight מְאַהֲבֶיהָher lovers וְאִישׁand one לֹאno יַצִּילֶנָּהdeliver her מִיָּדִיout of my hands

And then I will expose her lewdness in the sight of her lovers, and no one will deliver her out of My hands.

2:11

וְהִשְׁבַּתִּיend כָּלall מְשׂוֹשָׂהּher exultation חַגָּהּher feasts חָדְשָׁהּnew Moons וְשַׁבַּתָּהּand Sabbaths וְכֹלall מוֹעֲדָהּher appointed feasts

I will put an end to all her exultation: her feasts, New Moons, and Sabbaths— all her appointed feasts.

2:12

וַהֲשִׁמֹּתִיI will destroy גַּפְנָהּher vines וּתְאֵנָתָהּand fig trees she אֲשֶׁרwhich אָמְרָהthinks אֶתְנָהreward הֵמָּהare לִיto me אֲשֶׁרthat נָתְנוּwages paid לִיto me מְאַהֲבָיlovers I will וְשַׂמְתִּיםmake them לְיַעַרinto a thicket וַאֲכָלָתַםdevour them חַיַּתbeasts הַשָּׂדֶהthe field

I will destroy her vines and fig trees, which she thinks are the wages paid by her lovers. So I will make them into a thicket, and the beasts of the field will devour them.

2:13

וּפָקַדְתִּיwill punish עָלֶיהָher for אֶת[obj] יְמֵיdays הַבְּעָלִיםthe Baals אֲשֶׁרthat תַּקְטִירburned incense לָהֶםto them וַתַּעַדherself נִזְמָהּrings and וְחֶלְיָתָהּand jewelry and וַתֵּלֶךְand went אַחֲרֵיafter מְאַהֲבֶיהָher lovers וְאֹתִיand me שָׁכְחָהforgot נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD

I will punish her for the days of the Baals when she burned incense to them, when she decked herself with rings and jewelry, and went after her lovers. But Me she forgot,” declares the LORD.

2:14

לָכֵןtherefore הִנֵּהbehold אָנֹכִיI מְפַתֶּיהָallure her וְהֹלַכְתִּיהָand lead her הַמִּדְבָּרthe wilderness וְדִבַּרְתִּיand speak עַלto לִבָּהּher tenderly

“Therefore, behold, I will allure her and lead her to the wilderness, and speak to her tenderly.

2:15

וְנָתַתִּיwill give לָהּto her אֶת[obj] כְּרָמֶיהָvineyards and מִשָּׁםfrom there וְאֶתand [obj] עֵמֶקValley עָכוֹרAchor לְפֶתַחinto a gateway תִּקְוָהhope וְעָנְתָהshe will respond שָּׁמָּהthere כִּימֵיdays נְעוּרֶיהָher youth וִּכְיוֹםday עֲלֹתָהּshe came up מֵאֶרֶץfrom land מִצְרָיִםEgypt

There I will give back her vineyards and make the Valley of Achor into a gateway of hope. There she will respond as she did in the days of her youth, as in the day she came up out of Egypt.

2:16

וְהָיָהand be בַיּוֹםin that day הַהוּאthat נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD תִּקְרְאִיcall אִישִׁיme my husband וְלֹאand no תִקְרְאִיcall לִיto me עוֹדlonger בַּעְלִיmy Master

In that day,” declares the LORD, “you will call Me ‘my Husband,’ and no longer call Me ‘my Master.’

2:17

וַהֲסִרֹתִיwill remove אֶת[obj] שְׁמוֹתnames הַבְּעָלִיםthe Baals מִפִּיהָfrom her lips וְלֹאno יִזָּכְרוּinvoked עוֹדlonger בִּשְׁמָםwill their names

For I will remove from her lips the names of the Baals; no longer will their names be invoked.

2:18

וְכָרַתִּיwill make לָהֶםfor them בְּרִיתcovenant בַּיּוֹםon that day הַהוּאthat עִםwith חַיַּתbeasts הַשָּׂדֶהthe field וְעִםand with עוֹףbirds הַשָּׁמַיִםthe air וְרֶמֶשׂand the creatures הָאֲדָמָהthe ground וְקֶשֶׁתabolish bow וְחֶרֶבand sword וּמִלְחָמָהand weapons אֶשְׁבּוֹרweapons war מִןof הָאָרֶץthe land וְהִשְׁכַּבְתִּיםthem lie לָבֶטַחin safety

On that day I will make a covenant for them with the beasts of the field and the birds of the air and the creatures that crawl on the ground. And I will abolish bow and sword and weapons of war in the land, and will make them lie down in safety.

2:19

וְאֵרַשְׂתִּיךְbetroth you לִיto me לְעוֹלָםforever וְאֵרַשְׂתִּיךְwill betroth you לִיto me בְּצֶדֶקin righteousness וּבְמִשְׁפָּטand justice וּבְחֶסֶדin loving devotion וּבְרַחֲמִיםand compassion

So I will betroth you to Me forever; I will betroth you in righteousness and justice, in loving devotion and compassion.

2:20

וְאֵרַשְׂתִּיךְbetroth you לִיto me בֶּאֱמוּנָהin faithfulness וְיָדַעַתְּknow אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD

And I will betroth you in faithfulness, and you will know the LORD.”

2:21

וְהָיָהand be בַּיּוֹםon that day הַהוּאwill אֶעֱנֶהrespond נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD אֶעֱנֶהrespond אֶת[obj] הַשָּׁמָיִםthe heavens וְהֵםand they יַעֲנוּrespond אֶת[obj] הָאָרֶץthe earth

“On that day I will respond—” declares the LORD— “I will respond to the heavens, and they will respond to the earth.

2:22

וְהָאָרֶץand the earth תַּעֲנֶהrespond אֶת[obj] הַדָּגָןthe grain וְאֶתand [obj] הַתִּירוֹשׁthe new wine וְאֶתand [obj] הַיִּצְהָרoil וְהֵםand they יַעֲנוּrespond אֶת[obj] יִזְרְעֶאלJezreel

And the earth will respond to the grain, to the new wine and oil, and they will respond to Jezreel.

2:23

וּזְרַעְתִּיהָsow her לִּיto me בָּאָרֶץown in the land וְרִחַמְתִּיcompassion אֶת[obj] לֹאno רֻחָמָהon Compassion וְאָמַרְתִּיwill say לְלֹאNot עַמִּיpeople עַמִּיmy people אַתָּהyou וְהוּאand they will יֹאמַרsay אֱלֹהָיmy God

And I will sow her as My own in the land, and I will have compassion on ‘No Compassion.’ I will say to those called ‘Not My People,’ ‘You are My people,’ and they will say, ‘You are my God.’”
