Hosea 10

Israel, a vine that multiplied altars

10:1

גֶּפֶןvine בּוֹקֵקluxuriant yielding יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael פְּרִיfruit יְשַׁוֶּהyielding לּוֹfor himself כְּרֹבlike abundance לְפִרְיוֹhis fruit הִרְבָּהincreased לַמִּזְבְּחוֹתthe altars כְּטוֹבbetter לְאַרְצוֹhis land produced הֵיטִיבוּbetter מַצֵּבוֹתsacred pillars

Israel was a luxuriant vine, yielding fruit for himself. The more his fruit increased, the more he increased the altars. The better his land produced, the better he made the sacred pillars.

10:2

חָלַקdevious לִבָּםtheir hearts עַתָּהnow יֶאְשָׁמוּguilt הוּאwill יַעֲרֹףbreak מִזְבְּחוֹתָםaltars and יְשֹׁדֵדdestroyed מַצֵּבוֹתָםdemolish their sacred pillars

Their hearts are devious; now they must bear their guilt. The LORD will break down their altars and demolish their sacred pillars.

10:3

Surely now they will say, “We have no king, for we do not revere the LORD. What can a king do for us?”

10:4

דִּבְּרוּspeak דְבָרִיםwords אָלוֹתmere שָׁוְאfalse כָּרֹתoaths בְּרִיתmake covenants וּפָרַחso springs כָּרֹאשׁlike poisonous weeds מִשְׁפָּטjudgment עַלin תַּלְמֵיfurrows שָׂדָיfield

They speak mere words; with false oaths they make covenants. So judgment springs up like poisonous weeds in the furrows of a field.

10:5

לְעֶגְלוֹתfor the calf בֵּיתBeth אָוֶןaven יָגוּרוּfear שְׁכַןpeople שֹׁמְרוֹןSamaria כִּיfor אָבַלmourn עָלָיוon him עַמּוֹpeople וּכְמָרָיוidolatrous priests those who עָלָיוon him יָגִילוּrejoiced עַלin for כְּבוֹדוֹits glory כִּיfor גָלָהinto exile מִמֶּנּוּfrom them

The people of Samaria will fear for the calf of Beth-aven. Indeed, its people will mourn with its idolatrous priests— those who rejoiced in its glory— for it has been taken from them into exile.

10:6

גַּםeven אוֹתוֹhim לְאַשּׁוּרto Assyria יוּבָלcarried מִנְחָהtribute לְמֶלֶךְking יָרֵבgreat בָּשְׁנָהwill אֶפְרַיִםEphraim יִקָּחseized וְיֵבוֹשׁwith shame יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael מֵעֲצָתוֹashamed of its

Yes, it will be carried to Assyria as tribute to the great king. Ephraim will be seized with shame; Israel will be ashamed of its wooden idols.

10:7

נִדְמֶהcarried שֹׁמְרוֹןSamaria מַלְכָּהּher king כְּקֶצֶףlike a twig עַלon פְּנֵיsurface מָיִםwater

Samaria will be carried off with her king like a twig on the surface of the water.

10:8

וְנִשְׁמְדוּwill be destroyed בָּמוֹתhigh places אָוֶןaven חַטַּאתsin יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael קוֹץthorns וְדַרְדַּרand thistles יַעֲלֶהovergrow עַלon מִזְבְּחוֹתָםtheir altars וְאָמְרוּsay לֶהָרִיםto the mountains כַּסּוּנוּcover us וְלַגְּבָעוֹתand to the hills נִפְלוּfall עָלֵינוּon us

The high places of Aven will be destroyed— it is the sin of Israel; thorns and thistles will overgrow their altars. Then they will say to the mountains, “Cover us!” and to the hills, “Fall on us!”

10:9

מִימֵיsince the days הַגִּבְעָהof Gibeah חָטָאתָsinned יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael שָׁםthere עָמָדוּremained לֹאnot תַשִּׂיגֵםovertake בַּגִּבְעָהthe in Gibeah מִלְחָמָהbattle עַלon בְּנֵיsons עַלְוָהiniquity

Since the days of Gibeah you have sinned, O Israel, and there you have remained. Did not the battle in Gibeah overtake the sons of iniquity?

10:10

בְּאַוָּתִיin my whenever וְאֶסֳּרֵםwill chasten them וְאֻסְּפוּgathered עֲלֵיהֶםagainst them עַמִּיםnations בְּאָסְרָםput them in לִשְׁתֵּיdouble עוֹנֹתָםtheir transgression

I will chasten them when I please; nations will be gathered against them to put them in bondage for their double transgression.

10:11

וְאֶפְרַיִםEphraim עֶגְלָהheifer מְלֻמָּדָהtrained אֹהַבְתִּיloves לָדוּשׁto thresh וַאֲנִיbut I עָבַרְתִּיa yoke עַלon טוּבfair צַוָּארָהּher neck אַרְכִּיבharness אֶפְרַיִםEphraim יַחֲרוֹשׁbreak יְהוּדָהJudah יְשַׂדֶּדplow לוֹto him יַעֲקֹבJacob

Ephraim is a well-trained heifer that loves to thresh; but I will place a yoke on her fair neck. I will harness Ephraim, Judah will plow, and Jacob will break the hard ground.

10:12

זִרְעוּsow לָכֶםfor yourselves לִצְדָקָהrighteousness קִצְרוּreap לְפִיto mouth חֶסֶדloving devotion נִירוּbreak לָכֶםto you נִירunplowed ground וְעֵתtime לִדְרוֹשׁto seek אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD עַדuntil יָבוֹאcomes וְיֹרֶהand sends צֶדֶקrighteousness לָכֶםto you

Sow for yourselves righteousness and reap the fruit of loving devotion; break up your unplowed ground. For it is time to seek the LORD until He comes and sends righteousness upon you like rain.

10:13

חֲרַשְׁתֶּםplowed רֶשַׁעwickedness עַוְלָתָהand injustice קְצַרְתֶּםreaped אֲכַלְתֶּםeaten פְּרִיfruit כָחַשׁlies כִּיbecause בָטַחְתָּtrusted בְדַרְכְּךָin your own way בְּרֹבin the multitude גִּבּוֹרֶיךָyour mighty men

You have plowed wickedness and reaped injustice; you have eaten the fruit of lies. Because you have trusted in your own way and in the multitude of your mighty men,

10:14

the roar of battle will rise against your people, so that all your fortresses will be demolished as Shalman devastated Beth-arbel in the day of battle, when mothers were dashed to pieces along with their children.

10:15

כָּכָהthis עָשָׂהdone לָכֶםto you בֵּיתBethel אֵלBethel מִפְּנֵיfrom before רָעַתwickedness רָעַתְכֶםyour great wickedness בַּשַּׁחַרwhen the day נִדְמֹהdawns נִדְמָהperish מֶלֶךְking יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

Thus it will be done to you, O Bethel, because of your great wickedness. When the day dawns, the king of Israel will be utterly cut off.
