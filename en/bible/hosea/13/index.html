Hosea 13

The fall of Ephraim and Samaria

Open in the interlinear reader

13:1

כְּדַבֵּרwhen spoke אֶפְרַיִםEphraim רְתֵתtrembling נָשָׂאexalted הוּאwas בְּיִשְׂרָאֵלin Israel וַיֶּאְשַׁםbut he incurred guilt בַּבַּעַלthrough Baal וַיָּמֹתand he died

When Ephraim spoke, there was trembling; he was exalted in Israel. But he incurred guilt through Baal, and he died.

13:2

וְעַתָּהnow יוֹסִפוּmore לַחֲטֹאsin וַיַּעְשׂוּand make לָהֶםfor themselves מַסֵּכָהcast images מִכַּסְפָּםfrom their silver כִּתְבוּנָםskillfully עֲצַבִּיםidols מַעֲשֵׂהwork חָרָשִׁיםcraftsmen כֻּלֹּהall לָהֶםto them הֵםthey אֹמְרִיםsay זֹבְחֵיoffer sacrifice אָדָםhuman עֲגָלִיםcalves יִשָּׁקוּןkiss

Now they sin more and more and make for themselves cast images, idols skillfully made from their silver, all of them the work of craftsmen. People say of them, “They offer human sacrifice and kiss the calves!”

13:3

לָכֵןtherefore יִהְיוּbe כַּעֲנַןmist בֹּקֶרmorning וְכַטַּלlike the dew מַשְׁכִּיםearly הֹלֵךְvanishes כְּמֹץlike chaff יְסֹעֵרblown מִגֹּרֶןfrom a threshing floor וּכְעָשָׁןlike smoke מֵאֲרֻבָּהwindow

Therefore they will be like the morning mist, like the early dew that vanishes, like chaff blown from a threshing floor, like smoke through an open window.

13:4

וְאָנֹכִיyet I am יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God מֵאֶרֶץsince the land מִצְרָיִםEgypt וֵאלֹהִיםGod זוּלָתִיbut me לֹאno תֵדָעknow וּמוֹשִׁיעַSavior אַיִןno בִּלְתִּיcease me

Yet I am the LORD your God ever since the land of Egypt; you know no God but Me, for there is no Savior besides Me.

13:5

אֲנִיI יְדַעְתִּיךָknew you בַּמִּדְבָּרin the wilderness בְּאֶרֶץin the land תַּלְאֻבוֹתdrought

I knew you in the wilderness, in the land of drought.

13:6

כְּמַרְעִיתָםwhen they had pasture they וַיִּשְׂבָּעוּbecame satisfied when שָׂבְעוּsatisfied וַיָּרָםbecame proud and לִבָּםtheir hearts עַלon כֵּןso שְׁכֵחוּנִיforgot me

When they had pasture, they became satisfied; when they were satisfied, their hearts became proud, and as a result they forgot Me.

13:7

וָאֱהִיpounce לָהֶםto them כְּמוֹlike שָׁחַלlion כְּנָמֵרlike a leopard עַלon דֶּרֶךְpath אָשׁוּרlurk

So like a lion I will pounce on them; like a leopard I will lurk by the path.

13:8

אֶפְגְּשֵׁםattack them כְּדֹבlike a bear שַׁכּוּלrobbed וְאֶקְרַעtear סְגוֹרopen לִבָּםopen their chests וְאֹכְלֵםwill devour them שָׁםthere כְּלָבִיאlike a lion חַיַּתwild הַשָּׂדֶהbeast would תְּבַקְּעֵםtear them apart

Like a bear robbed of her cubs I will attack them, and I will tear open their chests. There I will devour them like a lion, like a wild beast would tear them apart.

13:9

שִׁחֶתְךָdestroyed O יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael כִּיbecause בִיagainst me בְעֶזְרֶךָagainst your helper

You are destroyed, O Israel, because you are against Me— against your helper.

13:10

אֱהִיwhere מַלְכְּךָyour king אֵפוֹאnow וְיוֹשִׁיעֲךָto save you בְּכָלin all עָרֶיךָyour cities וְשֹׁפְטֶיךָand the rulers אֲשֶׁרwhom אָמַרְתָּsaid תְּנָהgive לִּיto me מֶלֶךְking וְשָׂרִיםand princes

Where is your king now to save you in all your cities, and the rulers to whom you said, “Give me a king and princes”?

13:11

אֶתֶּןgave לְךָto you מֶלֶךְking בְּאַפִּיin my anger I וְאֶקַּחtook בְּעֶבְרָתִיin my wrath

So in My anger I gave you a king, and in My wrath I took him away.

13:12

צָרוּרbound עֲוֺןiniquity אֶפְרָיִםEphraim צְפוּנָהup חַטָּאתוֹhis sin

The iniquity of Ephraim is bound up; his sin is stored up.

13:13

חֶבְלֵיpains יוֹלֵדָהlabor יָבֹאוּcome לוֹto him הוּאhe בֵןson לֹאnot חָכָםarrives כִּיwhen עֵתtime לֹאnot יַעֲמֹדpresent בְּמִשְׁבַּרat the opening בָּנִיםsons

Labor pains come upon him, but he is an unwise son. When the time arrives, he fails to present himself at the opening of the womb.

13:14

מִיַּדfrom the power שְׁאוֹלSheol אֶפְדֵּםransom them מִמָּוֶתfrom death אֶגְאָלֵםredeem them אֱהִיwhere דְבָרֶיךָyour plagues מָוֶתdeath אֱהִיwhere קָטָבְךָplagues O שְׁאוֹלSheol נֹחַםcompassion יִסָּתֵרhidden מֵעֵינָיfrom my eyes

I will ransom them from the power of Sheol; I will redeem them from Death. Where, O Death, are your plagues? Where, O Sheol, is your sting? Compassion is hidden from My eyes.

13:15

כִּיfor הוּאhe בֵּןamong אַחִיםbrothers יַפְרִיאflourishes יָבוֹאcome קָדִיםeast רוּחַwind יְהוָהLORD מִמִּדְבָּרfrom the desert עֹלֶהrising וְיֵבוֹשׁfail מְקוֹרוֹhis fountain וְיֶחֱרַבrun dry מַעְיָנוֹhis spring הוּאwill יִשְׁסֶהplunder אוֹצַרtreasury כָּלevery כְּלִיarticle חֶמְדָּהprecious

Although he flourishes among his brothers, an east wind will come— a wind from the LORD rising up from the desert. His fountain will fail, and his spring will run dry. The wind will plunder his treasury of every precious article.

13:16

תֶּאְשַׁםguilt שֹׁמְרוֹןSamaria כִּיbecause מָרְתָהrebelled בֵּאלֹהֶיהָagainst her God בַּחֶרֶבby the sword יִפֹּלוּfall עֹלְלֵיהֶםtheir little יְרֻטָּשׁוּdashed to pieces וְהָרִיּוֹתָיוand their pregnant women ripped יְבֻקָּעוּopen

Samaria will bear her guilt because she has rebelled against her God. They will fall by the sword; their little ones will be dashed to pieces, and their pregnant women ripped open.
