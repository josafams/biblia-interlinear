Hosea 8

Sow the wind and reap the whirlwind

Open in the interlinear reader

8:1

אֶלto חִכְּךָyour lips שֹׁפָרram’s horn כַּנֶּשֶׁרan eagle looms עַלover בֵּיתhouse יְהוָהLORD יַעַןbecause עָבְרוּtransgressed בְרִיתִיmy covenant וְעַלand against תּוֹרָתִיmy law פָּשָׁעוּrebelled

Put the ram’s horn to your lips! An eagle looms over the house of the LORD, because the people have transgressed My covenant and rebelled against My law.

8:2

לִיto me יִזְעָקוּcries out אֱלֹהַיour God יְדַעֲנוּךָknow you יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

Israel cries out to Me, “O our God, we know You!”

8:3

זָנַחrejected יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael טוֹבgood אוֹיֵבenemy יִרְדְּפוֹpursue him

But Israel has rejected good; an enemy will pursue him.

8:4

הֵםthey הִמְלִיכוּset וְלֹאbut not מִמֶּנִּיfrom me הֵשִׂירוּprinces וְלֹאand not יָדָעְתִּיapproval כַּסְפָּםtheir silver וּזְהָבָםand gold they עָשׂוּmake לָהֶםto them עֲצַבִּיםidols לְמַעַןto יִכָּרֵתdestruction

They set up kings, but not by Me. They make princes, but without My approval. With their silver and gold they make themselves idols, to their own destruction.

8:5

זָנַחrejected עֶגְלֵךְyour calf O שֹׁמְרוֹןSamaria חָרָהburns אַפִּיmy anger burns בָּםagainst them עַדhow מָתַיlong לֹאnot יוּכְלוּincapable נִקָּיֹןinnocence

He has rejected your calf, O Samaria. My anger burns against them. How long will they be incapable of innocence?

8:6

כִּיfor מִיִּשְׂרָאֵלfrom Israel וְהוּאa חָרָשׁcraftsman עָשָׂהוּmade it וְלֹאnot אֱלֹהִיםGod הוּאhe כִּיfor שְׁבָבִיםbroken to pieces יִהְיֶהbe עֵגֶלcalf שֹׁמְרוֹןSamaria

For this thing is from Israel— a craftsman made it, and it is not God. It will be broken to pieces, that calf of Samaria.

8:7

כִּיfor רוּחַwind יִזְרָעוּsow וְסוּפָתָהwhirlwind יִקְצֹרוּreap קָמָהstanding grain אֵיןno לוֹto him צֶמַחsprouts בְּלִיwithout יַעֲשֶׂהyield קֶּמַחflour אוּלַיif יַעֲשֶׂהproduce זָרִיםforeigners יִבְלָעֻהוּwould swallow it

For they sow the wind, and they shall reap the whirlwind. There is no standing grain; what sprouts fails to yield flour. Even if it should produce, the foreigners would swallow it up.

8:8

נִבְלַעswallowed יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael עַתָּהnow הָיוּare בַגּוֹיִםamong the nations כִּכְלִיvessel אֵיןno חֵפֶץworthless בּוֹin him

Israel is swallowed up! Now they are among the nations like a worthless vessel.

8:9

כִּיfor הֵמָּהthey עָלוּgone up אַשּׁוּרAssyria פֶּרֶאwild donkey בּוֹדֵדmust לוֹto him אֶפְרַיִםEphraim הִתְנוּhired אֲהָבִיםlovers

For they have gone up to Assyria like a wild donkey on its own. Ephraim has hired lovers.

8:10

גַּםeven כִּיfor יִתְנוּhire allies בַגּוֹיִםamong the nations עַתָּהnow אֲקַבְּצֵםround them וַיָּחֵלּוּbegin מְּעָטdiminish מִמַּשָּׂאdiminish under מֶלֶךְking שָׂרִיםprinces

Though they hire allies among the nations, I will now round them up, and they will begin to diminish under the oppression of the king of princes.

8:11

כִּיfor הִרְבָּהmultiplied אֶפְרַיִםEphraim מִזְבְּחֹתaltars לַחֲטֹאfor sin הָיוּbecame לוֹto him מִזְבְּחוֹתaltars לַחֲטֹאfor sinning

Though Ephraim multiplied the altars for sin, they became his altars for sinning.

8:12

אֶכְתָּבwrite לוֹwrote for them רֻבֵּיgreat תּוֹרָתִיmy law כְּמוֹas זָרstrange נֶחְשָׁבוּregarded

Though I wrote for them the great things of My law, they regarded them as something strange.

8:13

זִבְחֵיsacrifices הַבְהָבַיgifts to me יִזְבְּחוּoffer בָשָׂרmeat וַיֹּאכֵלוּeat יְהוָהLORD לֹאnot רָצָםaccept them עַתָּהnow יִזְכֹּרremember עֲוֺנָםtheir iniquity וְיִפְקֹדand punish חַטֹּאותָםtheir sins הֵמָּהthey מִצְרַיִםEgypt יָשׁוּבוּreturn

Though they offer sacrifices as gifts to Me, and though they eat the meat, the LORD does not accept them. Now He will remember their iniquity and punish their sins: They will return to Egypt.

8:14

וַיִּשְׁכַּחforgotten יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael אֶת[obj] עֹשֵׂהוּhis Maker וַיִּבֶןand built הֵיכָלוֹתpalaces וִיהוּדָהJudah הִרְבָּהmultiplied עָרִיםcities בְּצֻרוֹתfortified וְשִׁלַּחְתִּיsend אֵשׁfire בְּעָרָיוupon their cities it וְאָכְלָהconsume אַרְמְנֹתֶיהָtheir citadels

Israel has forgotten his Maker and built palaces; Judah has multiplied its fortified cities. But I will send fire upon their cities, and it will consume their citadels.
