Hosea 14

A call to return and a promise of healing

Open in the interlinear reader

14:1

שׁוּבָהreturn יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael עַדto יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God כִּיfor כָשַׁלְתָּstumbled בַּעֲוֺנֶךָby your iniquity

Return, O Israel, to the LORD your God, for you have stumbled by your iniquity.

14:2

קְחוּbring עִמָּכֶםwith you דְּבָרִיםconfessions וְשׁוּבוּand return אֶלto יְהוָהLORD אִמְרוּsay אֵלָיוto him כָּלall תִּשָּׂאtake עָוֺןiniquity וְקַחand receive טוֹבgraciously וּנְשַׁלְּמָהand fulfill פָרִיםpresent שְׂפָתֵינוּour lips

Bring your confessions and return to the LORD. Say to Him: “Take away all our iniquity and receive us graciously, that we may present the fruit of our lips.

14:3

אַשּׁוּרAssyria לֹאnot יוֹשִׁיעֵנוּsave us עַלon סוּסhorses לֹאnot נִרְכָּבride וְלֹאwill never נֹאמַרsay עוֹדagain אֱלֹהֵינוּour gods לְמַעֲשֵׂהto the work יָדֵינוּour hands אֲשֶׁרthat בְּךָin you יְרֻחַםcompassion יָתוֹםfatherless

Assyria will not save us, nor will we ride on horses. We will never again say, ‘Our gods!’ to the work of our own hands. For in You the fatherless find compassion.”

14:4

אֶרְפָּאheal מְשׁוּבָתָםtheir apostasy אֹהֲבֵםlove them נְדָבָהfreely כִּיfor שָׁבturned אַפִּיmy anger מִמֶּנּוּfrom them

I will heal their apostasy; I will freely love them, for My anger has turned away from them.

14:5

אֶהְיֶהbe כַטַּלlike the dew לְיִשְׂרָאֵלto Israel יִפְרַחblossom כַּשּׁוֹשַׁנָּהlike the lily וְיַךְand take שָׁרָשָׁיוroot כַּלְּבָנוֹןlike the cedars of Lebanon

I will be like the dew to Israel; he will blossom like the lily and take root like the cedars of Lebanon.

14:6

יֵלְכוּgo יֹנְקוֹתָיוhis shoots וִיהִיbe כַזַּיִתlike the olive tree הוֹדוֹsplendor וְרֵיחַfragrance לוֹto him כַּלְּבָנוֹןlike the cedars of Lebanon

His shoots will sprout, and his splendor will be like the olive tree, his fragrance like the cedars of Lebanon.

14:7

יָשֻׁבוּreturn יֹשְׁבֵיdwell בְצִלּוֹin his shade יְחַיּוּgrow דָגָןgrain וְיִפְרְחוּand blossom כַגָּפֶןlike the vine זִכְרוֹhis renown כְּיֵיןlike the wine לְבָנוֹןLebanon

They will return and dwell in his shade; they will grow grain and blossom like the vine. His renown will be like the wine of Lebanon.

14:8

אֶפְרַיִםEphraim מַהwhat לִּיto me עוֹדlonger לָעֲצַבִּיםwith idols אֲנִיI עָנִיתִיanswer וַאֲשׁוּרֶנּוּand watch over him אֲנִיam כִּבְרוֹשׁcypress רַעֲנָןflourishing מִמֶּנִּיfrom me פֶּרְיְךָyour fruit נִמְצָאfound

O Ephraim, what have I to do anymore with idols? It is I who answer and watch over him. I am like a flourishing cypress; your fruit comes from Me.

14:9

מִיwhoever חָכָםwise וְיָבֵןlet him understand אֵלֶּהthese נָבוֹןdiscerning וְיֵדָעֵםlet him know them כִּיfor יְשָׁרִיםright דַּרְכֵיways יְהוָהLORD וְצַדִּקִיםand the righteous יֵלְכוּwalk בָםin them וּפֹשְׁעִיםbut the rebellious יִכָּשְׁלוּstumble בָםin them

Whoever is wise, let him understand these things; whoever is discerning, let him know them. For the ways of the LORD are right, and the righteous walk in them but the rebellious stumble in them.
