Hosea 11

God's love for the rebellious son

Open in the interlinear reader

11:1

כִּיwhen נַעַרchild יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וָאֹהֲבֵהוּI loved him וּמִמִּצְרַיִםand out of Egypt קָרָאתִיcalled לִבְנִיmy son

When Israel was a child, I loved him, and out of Egypt I called My son.

11:2

קָרְאוּcalled לָהֶםto them כֵּןso הָלְכוּthey מִפְּנֵיהֶםfrom their before לַבְּעָלִיםto the Baals יְזַבֵּחוּsacrificed וְלַפְּסִלִיםto carved images יְקַטֵּרוּןburned incense

But the more I called Israel, the farther they departed from Me. They sacrificed to the Baals and burned incense to carved images.

11:3

וְאָנֹכִיwas I תִרְגַּלְתִּיtaught לְאֶפְרַיִםEphraim to walk קָחָםtaking them עַלin זְרוֹעֹתָיוarms וְלֹאbut they never יָדְעוּrealized כִּיthat רְפָאתִיםhealed them

It was I who taught Ephraim to walk, taking them in My arms, but they never realized that it was I who healed them.

11:4

בְּחַבְלֵיwith cords אָדָםman אֶמְשְׁכֵםkindness בַּעֲבֹתוֹתwith ropes אַהֲבָהlove וָאֶהְיֶהand been לָהֶםto them כִּמְרִימֵיlifted עֹלyoke עַלon לְחֵיהֶםtheir necks וְאַטand bent אֵלָיוto them אוֹכִילfeed

I led them with cords of kindness, with ropes of love; I lifted the yoke from their necks and bent down to feed them.

11:5

לֹאnot יָשׁוּבreturn אֶלto אֶרֶץland מִצְרַיִםEgypt וְאַשּׁוּרAssyria הוּאhe מַלְכּוֹruled by כִּיbecause מֵאֲנוּrefused לָשׁוּבto repent

Will they not return to the land of Egypt and be ruled by Assyria because they refused to repent?

11:6

וְחָלָהand waited חֶרֶבsword בְּעָרָיוthrough their cities it וְכִלְּתָהwill destroy בַדָּיוtheir gates וְאָכָלָהand consume מִמֹּעֲצוֹתֵיהֶםin their own plans

A sword will flash through their cities; it will destroy the bars of their gates and consume them in their own plans.

11:7

וְעַמִּיmy people תְלוּאִיםbent לִמְשׁוּבָתִיon turning from me וְאֶלthough they to עַלhigh יִקְרָאֻהוּcall יַחַדtogether לֹאno יְרוֹמֵםexalt

My people are bent on turning from Me. Though they call to the Most High, He will by no means exalt them.

11:8

אֵיךְhow could אֶתֶּנְךָgive you אֶפְרַיִםEphraim אֲמַגֶּנְךָsurrender you O יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael אֵיךְhow could אֶתֶּנְךָmake you כְאַדְמָהlike Admah אֲשִׂימְךָI treat you כִּצְבֹאיִםlike Zeboiim נֶהְפַּךְturned עָלַיon me לִבִּיheart יַחַדtogether נִכְמְרוּcompassion נִחוּמָיstirred

How could I give you up, O Ephraim? How could I surrender you, O Israel? How could I make you like Admah? How could I treat you like Zeboiim? My heart is turned within Me; My compassion is stirred!

11:9

לֹאnot אֶעֱשֶׂהexecute חֲרוֹןfull fury אַפִּיfury of my anger לֹאnot אָשׁוּבturn back לְשַׁחֵתto destroy אֶפְרָיִםEphraim כִּיfor אֵלGod אָנֹכִיam וְלֹאand not אִישׁman בְּקִרְבְּךָamong you קָדוֹשׁholy וְלֹאnot אָבוֹאcome בְּעִירin wrath

I will not execute the full fury of My anger; I will not turn back to destroy Ephraim. For I am God and not man— the Holy One among you— and I will not come in wrath.

11:10

אַחֲרֵיafter יְהוָהLORD יֵלְכוּwalk כְּאַרְיֵהlike a lion יִשְׁאָגroar כִּיwhen הוּאhe יִשְׁאַגroars וְיֶחֶרְדוּwill come trembling בָנִיםchildren מִיָּםfrom the west

They will walk after the LORD; He will roar like a lion. When He roars, His children will come trembling from the west.

11:11

יֶחֶרְדוּtrembling כְצִפּוֹרlike birds מִמִּצְרַיִםfrom Egypt וּכְיוֹנָהand like doves מֵאֶרֶץfrom the land אַשּׁוּרAssyria וְהוֹשַׁבְתִּיםsettle them עַלin בָּתֵּיהֶםtheir homes נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD

They will come trembling like birds from Egypt and like doves from the land of Assyria. Then I will settle them in their homes, declares the LORD.

11:12

סְבָבֻנִיsurrounds me בְכַחַשׁwith lies אֶפְרַיִםEphraim וּבְמִרְמָהwith deceit בֵּיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וִיהוּדָהbut Judah עֹדstill רָדwalks עִםwith אֵלGod וְעִםto קְדוֹשִׁיםholy נֶאֱמָןfaithful

Ephraim surrounds Me with lies, the house of Israel with deceit; but Judah still walks with God and is faithful to the Holy One.
