Then Moses summoned all Israel and said to them: Hear, O Israel, the statutes and ordinances that I declare in your hearing this day. Learn them and observe them carefully.

5:2

יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God כָּרַתmade עִמָּנוּwith us בְּרִיתcovenant בְּחֹרֵבat Horeb

The LORD our God made a covenant with us at Horeb.

5:3

He did not make this covenant with our fathers, but with all of us who are alive here today.

5:4

The LORD spoke with you face to face out of the fire on the mountain.

5:5

אָנֹכִיI עֹמֵדstanding בֵּיןbetween יְהוָהLORD וּבֵינֵיכֶםand between you בָּעֵתat that time הַהִואwas לְהַגִּידto declare לָכֶםto you אֶת[obj] דְּבַרword יְהוָהLORD כִּיbecause יְרֵאתֶםafraid מִפְּנֵיfrom before הָאֵשׁthe fire וְלֹאand would not עֲלִיתֶםgo בָּהָרup the mountain לֵאמֹרand he said

At that time I was standing between the LORD and you to declare to you the word of the LORD, because you were afraid of the fire and would not go up the mountain. And He said:

5:6

אָנֹכִיam יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God אֲשֶׁרwho הוֹצֵאתִיךָbrought you מֵאֶרֶץland מִצְרַיִםEgypt מִבֵּיתhouse עֲבָדִיםslavery

“I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery.

5:7

לֹאno יִהְיֶהshall לְךָto you אֱלֹהִיםother אֲחֵרִיםgods עַלon פָּנָיַbefore me

You shall have no other gods before Me.

5:8

לֹאnot תַעֲשֶׂהmake לְךָfor yourself פֶסֶלidol כָּלanything תְּמוּנָהform אֲשֶׁרthat בַּשָּׁמַיִםin the heavens מִמַּעַלabove וַאֲשֶׁרand what בָּאָרֶץon the earth מִתָּחַתbelow וַאֲשֶׁרand what בַּמַּיִםin the waters מִתַּחַתbeneath לָאָרֶץto the ground

You shall not make for yourself an idol in the form of anything in the heavens above, on the earth below, or in the waters beneath.

5:9

לֹאnot תִשְׁתַּחֲוֶהbow down לָהֶםto them וְלֹאor תָעָבְדֵםworship them כִּיfor אָנֹכִיI יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God אֵלGod קַנָּאjealous visiting פֹּקֵדpunish עֲוֺןiniquity אָבוֹתfathers עַלon בָּנִיםchildren וְעַלand on שִׁלֵּשִׁיםthird וְעַלand on רִבֵּעִיםfourth לְשֹׂנְאָיhate me

You shall not bow down to them or worship them; for I, the LORD your God, am a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers on their children to the third and fourth generations of those who hate Me,

5:10

וְעֹשֶׂהbut showing חֶסֶדloving devotion לַאֲלָפִיםto a thousand generations לְאֹהֲבַיthose who love me וּלְשֹׁמְרֵיand keep מִצְוֺתָיmy commandments

but showing loving devotion to a thousand generations of those who love Me and keep My commandments.

5:11

לֹאnot תִשָּׂאtake אֶת[obj] שֵׁםname יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God לַשָּׁוְאin vain כִּיfor לֹאnot יְנַקֶּהleave unpunished יְהוָהLORD אֵת[obj] אֲשֶׁרwho יִשָּׂאtakes אֶת[obj] שְׁמוֹhis name לַשָּׁוְאin vain

You shall not take the name of the LORD your God in vain, for the LORD will not leave anyone unpunished who takes His name in vain.

5:12

שָׁמוֹרobserve אֶת[obj] יוֹםday הַשַׁבָּתthe Sabbath לְקַדְּשׁוֹby keeping it holy כַּאֲשֶׁרhas צִוְּךָcommanded you יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָGod

Observe the Sabbath day by keeping it holy, as the LORD your God has commanded you.

5:13

שֵׁשֶׁתsix יָמִיםdays תַּעֲבֹדlabor וְעָשִׂיתָand do כָּלall מְלַאכְתֶּךָyour work

Six days you shall labor and do all your work,

5:14

וְיוֹםday הַשְּׁבִיעִיthe seventh שַׁבָּתSabbath לַיהוָהto the LORD אֱלֹהֶיךָGod לֹאnot תַעֲשֶׂהdo כָלany מְלָאכָהwork אַתָּהyou וּבִנְךָnor your son וּבִתֶּךָor daughter וְעַבְדְּךָyour manservant וַאֲמָתֶךָor maidservant וְשׁוֹרְךָnor your ox וַחֲמֹרְךָor donkey וְכָלor any בְּהֶמְתֶּךָyour livestock וְגֵרְךָnor the foreigner אֲשֶׁרthat בִּשְׁעָרֶיךָwithin your gates לְמַעַןmay יָנוּחַrest עַבְדְּךָyour manservant וַאֲמָתְךָand maidservant כָּמוֹךָas you

but the seventh day is a Sabbath to the LORD your God, on which you must not do any work—neither you, nor your son or daughter, nor your manservant or maidservant, nor your ox or donkey or any of your livestock, nor the foreigner within your gates, so that your manservant and maidservant may rest as you do.

5:15

וְזָכַרְתָּremember כִּיthat עֶבֶדslave הָיִיתָwere בְּאֶרֶץin the land מִצְרַיִםEgypt וַיֹּצִאֲךָyour brought יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָGod you מִשָּׁםout of there בְּיָדhand חֲזָקָהmighty וּבִזְרֹעַand an arm נְטוּיָהoutstretched עַלon כֵּןthat צִוְּךָcommanded you יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God לַעֲשׂוֹתto keep אֶת[obj] יוֹםday הַשַׁבָּתthe Sabbath

Remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt, and that the LORD your God brought you out of there with a mighty hand and an outstretched arm. That is why the LORD your God has commanded you to keep the Sabbath day.

5:16

כַּבֵּדhonor אֶת[obj] אָבִיךָyour father וְאֶתand [obj] אִמֶּךָyour mother כַּאֲשֶׁרhas צִוְּךָcommanded יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָGod לְמַעַןso יַאֲרִיכֻןlive יָמֶיךָyour days וּלְמַעַןand may יִיטַבwell לָךְto you עַלin הָאֲדָמָהthe land אֲשֶׁרthat יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God נֹתֵןgiving לָךְto you

Honor your father and your mother, as the LORD your God has commanded you, so that your days may be long and that it may go well with you in the land that the LORD your God is giving you.

5:17

לֹאnot תִּרְצָחmurder

You shall not murder.

5:18

וְלֹאshall not תִּנְאָףcommit adultery

You shall not commit adultery.

5:19

וְלֹאshall not תִּגְנֹבsteal

You shall not steal.

5:20

וְלֹאshall not תַעֲנֶהbear בְרֵעֲךָagainst your neighbor עֵדwitness שָׁוְאfalse

You shall not bear false witness against your neighbor.

5:21

וְלֹאshall not תַחְמֹדcovet אֵשֶׁתwife רֵעֶךָneighbor’s you וְלֹאshall not תִתְאַוֶּהcovet בֵּיתhouse רֵעֶךָyour neighbor’s שָׂדֵהוּfield his וְעַבְדּוֹand his servant וַאֲמָתוֹmanservant or maidservant or his שׁוֹרוֹox וַחֲמֹרוֹor donkey וְכֹלor anything אֲשֶׁרthat לְרֵעֶךָto your neighbor

You shall not covet your neighbor’s wife. You shall not covet your neighbor’s house or field, or his manservant or maidservant, or his ox or donkey, or anything that belongs to your neighbor.”

5:22

אֶת[obj] הַדְּבָרִיםcommandments הָאֵלֶּהthese דִּבֶּרspoke יְהוָהLORD אֶלto כָּלwhole קְהַלְכֶםyour assembly בָּהָרon the mountain מִתּוֹךְfrom among הָאֵשׁthe fire הֶעָנָןthe cloud וְהָעֲרָפֶלand the deep darkness קוֹלvoice גָּדוֹלloud וְלֹאand not יָסָףadded more וַיִּכְתְּבֵםand he wrote them עַלon שְׁנֵיtwo לֻחֹתtablets אֲבָנִיםstone וַיִּתְּנֵםand gave them אֵלָיto me

The LORD spoke these commandments in a loud voice to your whole assembly out of the fire, the cloud, and the deep darkness on the mountain; He added nothing more. And He wrote them on two tablets of stone and gave them to me.

5:23

וַיְהִיand was כְּשָׁמְעֲכֶםwhen you heard אֶת[obj] הַקּוֹלthe voice מִתּוֹךְfrom among הַחֹשֶׁךְthe darkness וְהָהָרwhile the mountain בֹּעֵרblazing בָּאֵשׁwith fire וַתִּקְרְבוּןapproached אֵלַיto me כָּלall רָאשֵׁיheads שִׁבְטֵיכֶםyour tribes וְזִקְנֵיכֶםand your elders

And when you heard the voice out of the darkness while the mountain was blazing with fire, all the heads of your tribes and your elders approached me,

5:24

וַתֹּאמְרוּand you said הֵןbehold הֶרְאָנוּshown יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּGod אֶת[obj] כְּבֹדוֹhis glory וְאֶתand [obj] גָּדְלוֹgreatness וְאֶתand [obj] קֹלוֹhis voice שָׁמַעְנוּheard מִתּוֹךְfrom among הָאֵשׁof fire הַיּוֹםthe today הַזֶּהhave רָאִינוּseen כִּיthat יְדַבֵּרspeaks אֱלֹהִיםGod אֶתwith הָאָדָםa man וָחָיlive

and you said, “Behold, the LORD our God has shown us His glory and greatness, and we have heard His voice out of the fire. Today we have seen that a man can live even if God speaks with him.

5:25

וְעַתָּהbut now לָמָּהwhy should נָמוּתdie כִּיfor תֹאכְלֵנוּconsume us הָאֵשׁfire הַגְּדֹלָהgreat הַזֹּאתthis אִםif יֹסְפִיםagain אֲנַחְנוּwe לִשְׁמֹעַhear אֶת[obj] קוֹלvoice יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God עוֹדlonger וָמָתְנוּwill die

But now, why should we die? For this great fire will consume us, and we will die, if we hear the voice of the LORD our God any longer.

5:26

כִּיfor מִיwho כָלall בָּשָׂרflesh אֲשֶׁרhas שָׁמַעheard קוֹלvoice אֱלֹהִיםGod חַיִּיםliving מְדַבֵּרspeaking מִתּוֹךְfrom among הָאֵשׁthe fire כָּמֹנוּas we וַיֶּחִיand survived

For who of all flesh has heard the voice of the living God speaking out of the fire, as we have, and survived?

5:27

קְרַבnear אַתָּהyou וּשֲׁמָעand listen אֵת[obj] כָּלall אֲשֶׁרthat יֹאמַרsays יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God וְאַתְּthen you תְּדַבֵּרtell אֵלֵינוּto us אֵת[obj] כָּלeverything אֲשֶׁרthat יְדַבֵּרspeaks יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God אֵלֶיךָto you וְשָׁמַעְנוּwill listen וְעָשִׂינוּand obey

Go near and listen to all that the LORD our God says. Then you can tell us everything the LORD our God tells you; we will listen and obey.”

5:28

וַיִּשְׁמַעheard יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] קוֹלvoice דִּבְרֵיכֶםwords you בְּדַבֶּרְכֶםspoke אֵלָיto me וַיֹּאמֶרand he said יְהוָהLORD אֵלַיto me שָׁמַעְתִּיheard אֶת[obj] קוֹלvoice דִּבְרֵיwords הָעָםthat these people הַזֶּהhave אֲשֶׁרhave דִּבְּרוּspoken אֵלֶיךָto you הֵיטִיבוּwell כָּלall אֲשֶׁרthat דִּבֵּרוּspoken

And the LORD heard the words you spoke to me, and He said to me, “I have heard the words that these people have spoken to you. They have done well in all that they have spoken.

5:29

If only they had such a heart to fear Me and keep all My commandments always, so that it might be well with them and with their children forever.

5:30

לֵךְgo אֱמֹרtell לָהֶםto them שׁוּבוּreturn לָכֶםto your לְאָהֳלֵיכֶםtents

Go and tell them: ‘Return to your tents.’

5:31

וְאַתָּהbut you פֹּהhere עֲמֹדstand עִמָּדִיwith me וַאֲדַבְּרָהmay speak אֵלֶיךָto you אֵת[obj] כָּלall הַמִּצְוָהthe commandments וְהַחֻקִּיםand statutes וְהַמִּשְׁפָּטִיםand ordinances אֲשֶׁרyou תְּלַמְּדֵםteach them וְעָשׂוּto follow בָאָרֶץin the land אֲשֶׁרthat אָנֹכִיam נֹתֵןgiving לָהֶםto them לְרִשְׁתָּהּto possess

But you stand here with Me, that I may speak to you all the commandments and statutes and ordinances you are to teach them to follow in the land that I am giving them to possess.”

5:32

וּשְׁמַרְתֶּםso be careful לַעֲשׂוֹתto do כַּאֲשֶׁרhas צִוָּהcommanded יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיכֶםyour God אֶתְכֶםyou לֹאnot תָסֻרוּturn aside יָמִיןright וּשְׂמֹאלleft

So be careful to do as the LORD your God has commanded you; you are not to turn aside to the right or to the left.

5:33

בְּכָלin all הַדֶּרֶךְthe ways אֲשֶׁרthat צִוָּהcommanded יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיכֶםyour God אֶתְכֶםyou תֵּלֵכוּso לְמַעַןmay תִּחְיוּןlive וְטוֹבand prosper לָכֶםto you וְהַאֲרַכְתֶּםand prolong יָמִיםdays בָּאָרֶץin the land אֲשֶׁרthat תִּירָשׁוּןpossess

You must walk in all the ways that the LORD your God has commanded you, so that you may live and prosper and prolong your days in the land that you will possess.
