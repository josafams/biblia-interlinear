Deuteronomy 14

Clean foods and the tithe

Open in the interlinear reader

14:1

בָּנִיםsons אַתֶּםyou are לַיהוָהthe LORD אֱלֹהֵיכֶםyour God לֹאnot תִתְגֹּדְדוּcut וְלֹאor תָשִׂימוּor קָרְחָהshave בֵּיןbetween עֵינֵיכֶםyour foreheads לָמֵתdead

You are sons of the LORD your God; do not cut yourselves or shave your foreheads on behalf of the dead,

14:2

כִּיfor עַםpeople קָדוֹשׁholy אַתָּהyou לַיהוָהto the LORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God וּבְךָthe בָּחַרchosen יְהוָהLORD לִהְיוֹתto be לוֹa לְעַםpeople for סְגֻלָּהprized possession מִכֹּלout of all הָעַמִּיםthe peoples אֲשֶׁרthat עַלon פְּנֵיface הָאֲדָמָהthe earth

for you are a people holy to the LORD your God. The LORD has chosen you to be a people for His prized possession out of all the peoples on the face of the earth.

14:3

לֹאnot תֹאכַלeat כָּלany תּוֹעֵבָהdetestable

You must not eat any detestable thing.

14:4

זֹאתthis הַבְּהֵמָהthe animals אֲשֶׁרthat תֹּאכֵלוּeat שׁוֹרox שֵׂהsheep כְשָׂבִיםsheep וְשֵׂהand sheep עִזִּיםgoat

These are the animals that you may eat: The ox, the sheep, the goat,

14:5

אַיָּלdeer וּצְבִיgazelle וְיַחְמוּרroe וְאַקּוֹgoat וְדִישֹׁןibex וּתְאוֹantelope וָזָמֶרand the mountain sheep

the deer, the gazelle, the roe deer, the wild goat, the ibex, the antelope, and the mountain sheep.

14:6

וְכָלmay any בְּהֵמָהanimal מַפְרֶסֶתsplit פַּרְסָהeat וְשֹׁסַעַתhas שֶׁסַעsplit שְׁתֵּיtwo פְרָסוֹתhoof מַעֲלַתchews גֵּרָהdivided בַּבְּהֵמָהin the animal אֹתָהּher תֹּאכֵלוּeat

You may eat any animal that has a split hoof divided in two and that chews the cud.

14:7

אַךְbut אֶת[obj] זֶהhave לֹאnot תֹאכְלוּeat מִמַּעֲלֵיchew הַגֵּרָהthat the cud וּמִמַּפְרִיסֵיor a divided הַפַּרְסָהhoof הַשְּׁסוּעָהthe tear אֶת[obj] הַגָּמָלthe camel וְאֶתand [obj] הָאַרְנֶבֶתthe rabbit וְאֶתand [obj] הַשָּׁפָןthe rock badger כִּיfor מַעֲלֵהcompletely גֵרָהcud הֵמָּהthey וּפַרְסָהand does לֹאnot הִפְרִיסוּdivided טְמֵאִיםunclean הֵםare לָכֶםto

But of those that chew the cud or have a completely divided hoof, you are not to eat the following: the camel, the rabbit, or the rock badger.

14:8

וְאֶתand [obj] הַחֲזִירthe pig כִּיfor מַפְרִיסdivided פַּרְסָהhoof does הוּאhe וְלֹאnot גֵרָהchew טָמֵאunclean הוּאis לָכֶםfor you מִבְּשָׂרָםits meat לֹאnot תֹאכֵלוּeat וּבְנִבְלָתָםcarcass לֹאor תִגָּעוּtouch

Although they chew the cud, they do not have a divided hoof. They are unclean for you, as well as the pig; though it has a divided hoof, it does not chew the cud. It is unclean for you. You must not eat its meat or touch its carcass.

14:9

אֶת[obj] זֶהthe תֹּאכְלוּcreatures מִכֹּלof all אֲשֶׁרthat בַּמָּיִםin the water כֹּלanything אֲשֶׁרyou לוֹto him סְנַפִּירfins וְקַשְׂקֶשֶׂתfins and scales תֹּאכֵלוּeat

Of all the creatures that live in the water, you may eat anything with fins and scales,

14:10

וְכֹלanything אֲשֶׁרthat אֵיןnot לוֹto him סְנַפִּירfins וְקַשְׂקֶשֶׂתfins and scales לֹאnot תֹאכֵלוּeat טָמֵאunclean הוּאis לָכֶםfor you

but you may not eat anything that does not have fins and scales; it is unclean for you.

14:11

כָּלany צִפּוֹרbird טְהֹרָהclean תֹּאכֵלוּeat

You may eat any clean bird,

14:12

וְזֶהbut these אֲשֶׁרyou לֹאnot תֹאכְלוּeat מֵהֶםof them הַנֶּשֶׁרthe eagle וְהַפֶּרֶסthe bearded וְהָעָזְנִיָּהvulture the black vulture

but these you may not eat: the eagle, the bearded vulture, the black vulture,

14:13

וְהָרָאָהthe red וְאֶתand [obj] הָאַיָּהkite וְהַדַּיָּהthe falcon לְמִינָהּany kind of kite

the red kite, the falcon, any kind of kite,

14:14

וְאֵתand [obj] כָּלany עֹרֵבraven לְמִינוֹkind

any kind of raven,

14:15

וְאֵתand [obj] בַּתostrich הַיַּעֲנָהscreech וְאֶתand [obj] הַתַּחְמָסthe owl וְאֶתand [obj] הַשָּׁחַףthe gull וְאֶתand [obj] הַנֵּץof hawk לְמִינֵהוּany kind

the ostrich, the screech owl, the gull, any kind of hawk,

14:16

אֶת[obj] הַכּוֹסthe little וְאֶתand [obj] הַיַּנְשׁוּףowl the great owl וְהַתִּנְשָׁמֶתthe white owl

the little owl, the great owl, the white owl,

14:17

וְהַקָּאָתthe desert owl וְאֶתand [obj] הָרָחָמָהthe osprey וְאֶתand [obj] הַשָּׁלָךְthe cormorant

the desert owl, the osprey, the cormorant,

14:18

וְהַחֲסִידָהthe stork וְהָאֲנָפָהstork לְמִינָהּany kind וְהַדּוּכִיפַתheron the hoopoe וְהָעֲטַלֵּףor the bat

the stork, any kind of heron, the hoopoe, or the bat.

14:19

וְכֹלall שֶׁרֶץflying הָעוֹףinsects טָמֵאunclean הוּאhe לָכֶםfor you לֹאnot יֵאָכֵלוּeaten

All flying insects are unclean for you; they may not be eaten.

14:20

כָּלany עוֹףbird טָהוֹרclean תֹּאכֵלוּeat

But you may eat any clean bird.

14:21

לֹאnot תֹאכְלוּeat כָלany נְבֵלָהcarcass לַגֵּרto the foreigner אֲשֶׁרthat בִּשְׁעָרֶיךָwithin your gates תִּתְּנֶנָּהgive it וַאֲכָלָהּeat it אוֹor מָכֹרsell לְנָכְרִיto a foreigner כִּיfor עַםpeople קָדוֹשׁholy אַתָּהyou לַיהוָהto the LORD אֱלֹהֶיךָGod you לֹאnot תְבַשֵּׁלcook גְּדִיyoung goat בַּחֲלֵבmilk אִמּוֹits mother’s

You are not to eat any carcass; you may give it to the foreigner residing within your gates, and he may eat it, or you may sell it to a foreigner. For you are a holy people belonging to the LORD your God. You must not cook a young goat in its mother’s milk.

14:22

עַשֵּׂרaside תְּעַשֵּׂרset אֵת[obj] כָּלall תְּבוּאַתproduce זַרְעֶךָbrought הַיֹּצֵאforth הַשָּׂדֶהfields שָׁנָהyear שָׁנָהyears

You must be sure to set aside a tenth of all the produce brought forth each year from your fields.

14:23

וְאָכַלְתָּeat לִפְנֵיpresence יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God בַּמָּקוֹםat the place אֲשֶׁרthat יִבְחַרchoose לְשַׁכֵּןas a dwelling שְׁמוֹhis name שָׁםa מַעְשַׂרtenth דְּגָנְךָyour grain תִּירֹשְׁךָnew wine וְיִצְהָרֶךָand oil וּבְכֹרֹתand the firstborn בְּקָרְךָyour herds וְצֹאנֶךָand flocks לְמַעַןmay תִּלְמַדlearn לְיִרְאָהto fear אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God כָּלall הַיָּמִיםalways

And you are to eat a tenth of your grain, new wine, and oil, and the firstborn of your herds and flocks, in the presence of the LORD your God at the place He will choose as a dwelling for His Name, so that you may learn to fear the LORD your God always.

14:24

וְכִיbut if יִרְבֶּהdistance מִמְּךָfrom you הַדֶּרֶךְgreat כִּיfor לֹאnot תוּכַלyou שְׂאֵתוֹcarry כִּיthat יִרְחַקfar מִמְּךָfrom you הַמָּקוֹםthe place אֲשֶׁרthat יִבְחַרchoose יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God לָשׂוּםto put שְׁמוֹhis name שָׁםthere כִּיbecause יְבָרֶכְךָblessed you יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָGod

But if the distance is too great for you to carry that with which the LORD your God has blessed you, because the place where the LORD your God will choose to put His Name is too far away,

14:25

וְנָתַתָּהthen exchange בַּכָּסֶףit for money וְצַרְתָּtake הַכֶּסֶףthe money בְּיָדְךָin your hand וְהָלַכְתָּand go אֶלto הַמָּקוֹםthe place אֲשֶׁרthat יִבְחַרchoose יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God בּוֹin him

then exchange it for money, take the money in your hand, and go to the place the LORD your God will choose.

14:26

וְנָתַתָּהspend הַכֶּסֶףthe money בְּכֹלon anything אֲשֶׁרyou תְּאַוֶּהdesire נַפְשְׁךָyour soul בַּבָּקָרcattle וּבַצֹּאןsheep וּבַיַּיִןwine וּבַשֵּׁכָרstrong drink וּבְכֹלor anything אֲשֶׁרthat תִּשְׁאָלְךָask you נַפְשֶׁךָwish you וְאָכַלְתָּto feast שָּׁםthere לִפְנֵיin the presence יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God וְשָׂמַחְתָּand rejoice אַתָּהyou וּבֵיתֶךָwith your household

Then you may spend the money on anything you desire: cattle, sheep, wine, strong drink, or anything you wish. You are to feast there in the presence of the LORD your God and rejoice with your household.

14:27

וְהַלֵּוִיthe Levite אֲשֶׁרthat בִּשְׁעָרֶיךָwithin your gates לֹאnot תַעַזְבֶנּוּneglect כִּיfor אֵיןno לוֹto him חֵלֶקportion וְנַחֲלָהor inheritance עִמָּךְamong you

And do not neglect the Levite within your gates, since he has no portion or inheritance among you.

14:28

מִקְצֵהat the end שָׁלֹשׁthree שָׁנִיםyears תּוֹצִיאbring אֶת[obj] כָּלall מַעְשַׂרtenth תְּבוּאָתְךָyour produce בַּשָּׁנָהfor that year הַהִואthat וְהִנַּחְתָּand lay בִּשְׁעָרֶיךָwithin your gates

At the end of every three years, bring a tenth of all your produce for that year and lay it up within your gates.

14:29

וּבָאand come הַלֵּוִיthe Levite כִּיbecause אֵיןno לוֹto him חֵלֶקportion וְנַחֲלָהor inheritance עִמָּךְamong you וְהַגֵּרthe foreigner וְהַיָּתוֹםthe fatherless וְהָאַלְמָנָהand the widow אֲשֶׁרthat בִּשְׁעָרֶיךָwithin your gates וְאָכְלוּcome and eat וְשָׂבֵעוּand be satisfied לְמַעַןwill יְבָרֶכְךָbless יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָGod בְּכָלin all מַעֲשֵׂהwork יָדְךָyour hands אֲשֶׁרthat תַּעֲשֶׂהdo

Then the Levite (because he has no portion or inheritance among you), the foreigner, the fatherless, and the widow within your gates may come and eat and be satisfied. And the LORD your God will bless you in all the work of your hands.
