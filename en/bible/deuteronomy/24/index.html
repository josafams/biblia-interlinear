Deuteronomy 24

Divorce, loans and protection of the poor

Open in the interlinear reader

24:1

כִּיif יִקַּחmarries אִישׁman אִשָּׁהwoman וּבְעָלָהּand husband her וְהָיָהbecomes אִםif לֹאnot תִמְצָאfind חֵןdispleasing בְּעֵינָיוin his sight כִּיbecause מָצָאfinds בָהּin her עֶרְוַתindecency דָּבָרword וְכָתַבhe may write לָהּto her סֵפֶרcertificate כְּרִיתֻתdivorce וְנָתַןit בְּיָדָהּhand to her וְשִׁלְּחָהּand send her מִבֵּיתוֹfrom his house

If a man marries a woman, but she becomes displeasing to him because he finds some indecency in her, he may write her a certificate of divorce, hand it to her, and send her away from his house.

24:2

וְיָצְאָהafter leaving מִבֵּיתוֹhis house וְהָלְכָהshe goes וְהָיְתָהand becomes לְאִישׁman’s אַחֵרanother

If, after leaving his house, she goes and becomes another man’s wife,

24:3

וּשְׂנֵאָהּhates her הָאִישׁman הָאַחֲרוֹןthe second וְכָתַבwrites לָהּto her סֵפֶרcertificate כְּרִיתֻתdivorce וְנָתַןit בְּיָדָהּhands to her וְשִׁלְּחָהּand sends her מִבֵּיתוֹfrom his house אוֹor כִיif יָמוּתdies הָאִישׁthe man הָאַחֲרוֹןthe second אֲשֶׁרthat לְקָחָהּtook her לוֹto him לְאִשָּׁהto wife

and the second man hates her, writes her a certificate of divorce, hands it to her, and sends her away from his house, or if he dies,

24:4

לֹאnot יוּכַלremarry בַּעְלָהּhusband הָרִאשׁוֹןfirst אֲשֶׁרwho שִׁלְּחָהּdivorced her לָשׁוּבto return לְקַחְתָּהּto take her לִהְיוֹתto be לוֹto him לְאִשָּׁהshe אַחֲרֵיafter אֲשֶׁרhas הֻטַּמָּאָהdefiled כִּיfor that תוֹעֵבָהabomination הִואis לִפְנֵיto before יְהוָהLORD וְלֹאyou must not תַחֲטִיאsin אֶת[obj] הָאָרֶץthe land אֲשֶׁרthat יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God נֹתֵןgiving לְךָto you נַחֲלָהinheritance

then the husband who divorced her first may not remarry her after she has been defiled, for that is an abomination to the LORD. You must not bring sin upon the land that the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance.

24:5

כִּיif יִקַּחtake אִישׁman אִשָּׁהmarried חֲדָשָׁהnewly לֹאnot יֵצֵאsent בַּצָּבָאto war וְלֹאand or יַעֲבֹרpressed עָלָיוon him לְכָלany דָּבָרword נָקִיduty יִהְיֶהstay לְבֵיתוֹto at home שָׁנָהyear אֶחָתone וְשִׂמַּחand bring joy אֶת[obj] אִשְׁתּוֹwife he אֲשֶׁרhas לָקָחmarried

If a man is newly married, he must not be sent to war or be pressed into any duty. For one year he is free to stay at home and bring joy to the wife he has married.

24:6

לֹאnot יַחֲבֹלtake רֵחַיִםmillstones וָרָכֶבmillstone כִּיfor נֶפֶשׁlife הוּאthat חֹבֵלsecurity

Do not take a pair of millstones or even an upper millstone as security for a debt, because that would be taking one’s livelihood as security.

24:7

כִּיif יִמָּצֵאcaught אִישׁman גֹּנֵבkidnapping נֶפֶשׁone מֵאֶחָיוof his brothers מִבְּנֵיfrom descendants יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelite וְהִתְעַמֶּרhe treats בּוֹin him וּמְכָרוֹor sells him וּמֵתmust die הַגַּנָּבthe kidnapper הַהוּאthat וּבִעַרְתָּpurge הָרָעthe evil מִקִּרְבֶּךָfrom among you

If a man is caught kidnapping one of his Israelite brothers, whether he treats him as a slave or sells him, the kidnapper must die. So you must purge the evil from among you.

24:8

הִשָּׁמֶרcases בְּנֶגַעinfectious הַצָּרַעַתskin diseases לִשְׁמֹרbe careful מְאֹדvery וְלַעֲשׂוֹתto follow כְּכֹלeverything אֲשֶׁרthat יוֹרוּinstruct אֶתְכֶםyou הַכֹּהֲנִיםpriests הַלְוִיִּםthe Levitical כַּאֲשֶׁרas I have צִוִּיתִםcommanded them תִּשְׁמְרוּcareful לַעֲשׂוֹתto do

In cases of infectious skin diseases, be careful to diligently follow everything the Levitical priests instruct you. Be careful to do as I have commanded them.

24:9

זָכוֹרremember אֵת[obj] אֲשֶׁרthat עָשָׂהdid יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God לְמִרְיָםto Miriam בַּדֶּרֶךְon the journey בְּצֵאתְכֶםafter you came מִמִּצְרָיִםout of Miriam

Remember what the LORD your God did to Miriam on the journey after you came out of Egypt.

24:10

כִּיwhen תַשֶּׁהlend בְרֵעֲךָto your neighbor מַשַּׁאתlend מְאוּמָהanything לֹאnot תָבֹאenter אֶלto בֵּיתוֹhis house לַעֲבֹטto collect עֲבֹטוֹsecurity

When you lend anything to your neighbor, do not enter his house to collect security.

24:11

בַּחוּץto outside תַּעֲמֹדstand וְהָאִישׁwhile the man אֲשֶׁרwhom אַתָּהyou נֹשֶׁהlending בוֹin him יוֹצִיאbring אֵלֶיךָto you אֶת[obj] הַעֲבוֹטthe security הַחוּצָהout to

You are to stand outside while the man to whom you are lending brings the security out to you.

24:12

וְאִםif אִישׁa man עָנִיpoor הוּאhe לֹאnot תִשְׁכַּבsleep בַּעֲבֹטוֹwith the security

If he is a poor man, you must not go to sleep with the security in your possession;

24:13

הָשֵׁבreturn תָּשִׁיבreturn it לוֹto him אֶת[obj] הַעֲבוֹטthe security כְּבֹאlike entered הַשֶּׁמֶשׁsunset וְשָׁכַבsleep בְּשַׂלְמָתוֹin his own cloak וּבֵרֲכֶךָּand bless you וּלְךָand to you תִּהְיֶהbe צְדָקָהcredited לִפְנֵיbefore יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God

be sure to return it to him by sunset, so that he may sleep in his own cloak and bless you, and this will be credited to you as righteousness before the LORD your God.

24:14

לֹאnot תַעֲשֹׁקoppress שָׂכִירhired hand עָנִיpoor וְאֶבְיוֹןand needy מֵאַחֶיךָbrother אוֹor מִגֵּרְךָa foreigner אֲשֶׁרthat בְּאַרְצְךָin your land בִּשְׁעָרֶיךָtowns

Do not oppress a hired hand who is poor and needy, whether he is a brother or a foreigner residing in one of your towns.

24:15

בְּיוֹמוֹday תִתֵּןpay שְׂכָרוֹhis wages וְלֹאand not תָבוֹאsunset עָלָיוon him הַשֶּׁמֶשׁsunset כִּיbecause עָנִיpoor הוּאhe is וְאֵלָיוand to him הוּאhe נֹשֵׂאdepends אֶת[obj] נַפְשׁוֹhis soul וְלֹאand not יִקְרָאcry עָלֶיךָagainst you אֶלto יְהוָהLORD וְהָיָהbe בְךָin you חֵטְאsin

You are to pay his wages each day before sunset, because he is poor and depends on them. Otherwise he may cry out to the LORD against you, and you will be guilty of sin.

24:16

לֹאnot יוּמְתוּput אָבוֹתfathers עַלfor בָּנִיםchildren וּבָנִיםnor children לֹאnot יוּמְתוּdeath עַלfor אָבוֹתfathers אִישeach בְּחֶטְאוֹhis own sin יוּמָתוּdie

Fathers shall not be put to death for their children, nor children for their fathers; each is to die for his own sin.

24:17

לֹאnot תַטֶּהdeny מִשְׁפַּטjustice גֵּרforeigner יָתוֹםfatherless וְלֹאand do not תַחֲבֹלtake בֶּגֶדcloak אַלְמָנָהwidow’s

Do not deny justice to the foreigner or the fatherless, and do not take a widow’s cloak as security.

24:18

וְזָכַרְתָּremember כִּיthat עֶבֶדslaves הָיִיתָwere בְּמִצְרַיִםin Egypt וַיִּפְדְּךָyour redeemed יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָGod you מִשָּׁםfrom there עַלon כֵּןtherefore אָנֹכִיam מְצַוְּךָcommanding you לַעֲשׂוֹתto do אֶת[obj] הַדָּבָרthe word הַזֶּהthis

Remember that you were slaves in Egypt, and the LORD your God redeemed you from that place. Therefore I am commanding you to do this.

24:19

כִּיif תִקְצֹרharvesting קְצִירְךָyou are harvesting בְשָׂדֶךָin your field וְשָׁכַחְתָּand forget עֹמֶרsheaf בַּשָּׂדֶהin the field לֹאnot תָשׁוּבback לְקַחְתּוֹto get it לַגֵּרthe foreigner לַיָּתוֹםthe fatherless וְלָאַלְמָנָהand the widow יִהְיֶהso לְמַעַןmay יְבָרֶכְךָbless you יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָGod בְּכֹלin all מַעֲשֵׂהwork יָדֶיךָyour hands

If you are harvesting in your field and forget a sheaf there, do not go back to get it. It is to be left for the foreigner, the fatherless, and the widow, so that the LORD your God may bless you in all the work of your hands.

24:20

כִּיwhen תַחְבֹּטbeat זֵיתְךָolives from your trees לֹאnot תְפָאֵרbranches אַחֲרֶיךָafter you לַגֵּרfor the foreigner לַיָּתוֹםthe fatherless וְלָאַלְמָנָהand the widow יִהְיֶהbe

When you beat the olives from your trees, you must not go over the branches again. What remains will be for the foreigner, the fatherless, and the widow.

24:21

כִּיwhen תִבְצֹרgrapes כַּרְמְךָvineyard you לֹאnot תְעוֹלֵלvines אַחֲרֶיךָafter you לַגֵּרfor the foreigner לַיָּתוֹםthe fatherless וְלָאַלְמָנָהand the widow יִהְיֶהbe

When you gather the grapes of your vineyard, you must not go over the vines again. What remains will be for the foreigner, the fatherless, and the widow.

24:22

וְזָכַרְתָּremember כִּיthat עֶבֶדslaves הָיִיתָwere בְּאֶרֶץin the land מִצְרָיִםEgypt עַלon כֵּןtherefore אָנֹכִיam מְצַוְּךָcommanding you לַעֲשׂוֹתto do אֶת[obj] הַדָּבָרthe word הַזֶּהthis

Remember that you were slaves in the land of Egypt. Therefore I am commanding you to do this.
