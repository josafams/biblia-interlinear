Deuteronomy 27

An altar on Ebal and solemn curses

Then Moses and the elders of Israel commanded the people: “Keep all the commandments I am giving you today.

Write on them all the words of this law when you have crossed over to enter the land that the LORD your God is giving you, a land flowing with milk and honey, just as the LORD, the God of your fathers, has promised you.

27:4

וְהָיָהand be בְּעָבְרְכֶםwhen you have crossed אֶת[obj] הַיַּרְדֵּןthe Jordan תָּקִימוּset אֶת[obj] הָאֲבָנִיםthese stones הָאֵלֶּהthese אֲשֶׁרthat אָנֹכִיI am מְצַוֶּהcommanding אֶתְכֶםyou הַיּוֹםtoday בְּהַרon Mount עֵיבָלEbal וְשַׂדְתָּcoat אוֹתָםthem בַּשִּׂידwith plaster

And when you have crossed the Jordan, you are to set up these stones on Mount Ebal, as I am commanding you today, and you are to coat them with plaster.

27:5

וּבָנִיתָbuild שָּׁםthere מִזְבֵּחַaltar לַיהוָהto the LORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God מִזְבַּחaltar אֲבָנִיםstones לֹאnot תָנִיףtool עֲלֵיהֶםon them בַּרְזֶלiron

Moreover, you are to build there an altar to the LORD your God, an altar of stones. You must not use any iron tool on them.

27:6

אֲבָנִיםstones שְׁלֵמוֹתuncut תִּבְנֶהbuild אֶת[obj] מִזְבַּחaltar יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God וְהַעֲלִיתָand offer עָלָיוupon it עוֹלֹתburnt offerings לַיהוָהto the LORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God

You shall build the altar of the LORD your God with uncut stones and offer upon it burnt offerings to the LORD your God.

27:7

וְזָבַחְתָּto sacrifice שְׁלָמִיםpeace offerings וְאָכַלְתָּeating שָּׁםthere וְשָׂמַחְתָּand rejoicing לִפְנֵיin the presence יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God

There you are to sacrifice your peace offerings, eating them and rejoicing in the presence of the LORD your God.

27:8

וְכָתַבְתָּwrite עַלupon הָאֲבָנִיםthese stones אֶת[obj] כָּלall דִּבְרֵיwords הַתּוֹרָהof law הַזֹּאתthis בַּאֵרdistinctly הֵיטֵבyou

And you shall write distinctly upon these stones all the words of this law.”

27:9

וַיְדַבֵּרspoke מֹשֶׁהMoses וְהַכֹּהֲנִיםand the priests הַלְוִיִּםLevitical אֶלto כָּלall יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael לֵאמֹרto saying הַסְכֵּתsilent וּשְׁמַעand listen יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael הַיּוֹםthis day הַזֶּהhave נִהְיֵיתָbecome לְעָםthe people לַיהוָהthe LORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God

Then Moses and the Levitical priests spoke to all Israel: “Be silent, O Israel, and listen! This day you have become the people of the LORD your God.

27:10

You shall therefore obey the voice of the LORD your God and follow His commandments and statutes I am giving you today.”

27:11

וַיְצַוcommanded מֹשֶׁהMoses אֶת[obj] הָעָםthe people בַּיּוֹםon that day הַהוּאthat לֵאמֹרto saying

On that day Moses commanded the people:

27:12

אֵלֶּהthese יַעַמְדוּstand לְבָרֵךְto bless אֶת[obj] הָעָםthe people עַלon הַרMount גְּרִזִיםGerizim בְּעָבְרְכֶםwhen you have crossed אֶת[obj] הַיַּרְדֵּןthe Jordan שִׁמְעוֹןSimeon וְלֵוִיLevi וִיהוּדָהJudah וְיִשָּׂשכָרIssachar וְיוֹסֵףJoseph וּבִנְיָמִןand Benjamin

“When you have crossed the Jordan, these tribes shall stand on Mount Gerizim to bless the people: Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Joseph, and Benjamin.

27:13

וְאֵלֶּהand these יַעַמְדוּstand עַלon הַקְּלָלָהdeliver the curse בְּהַרon Mount עֵיבָלEbal רְאוּבֵןReuben גָּדGad וְאָשֵׁרAsher וּזְבוּלֻןZebulun דָּןDan וְנַפְתָּלִיNaphtali

And these tribes shall stand on Mount Ebal to deliver the curse: Reuben, Gad, Asher, Zebulun, Dan, and Naphtali.

27:14

וְעָנוּthen הַלְוִיִּםthe Levites וְאָמְרוּshall proclaim אֶלto כָּלevery אִישׁIsraelite יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelite קוֹלloud voice רָםhigh

Then the Levites shall proclaim in a loud voice to every Israelite:

27:15

‘Cursed is the man who makes a carved idol or molten image—an abomination to the LORD, the work of the hands of a craftsman—and sets it up in secret.’ And let all the people say, ‘Amen!’

27:16

אָרוּרcursed מַקְלֶהdishonors אָבִיוhis father וְאִמּוֹor mother וְאָמַרand let כָּלall הָעָםthe people אָמֵןsay Amen

‘Cursed is he who dishonors his father or mother.’ And let all the people say, ‘Amen!’

‘Cursed is he who moves his neighbor’s boundary stone.’ And let all the people say, ‘Amen!’

‘Cursed is he who lets a blind man wander in the road.’ And let all the people say, ‘Amen!’

27:19

אָרוּרcursed מַטֶּהwithholds מִשְׁפַּטjustice גֵּרforeigner יָתוֹםfatherless וְאַלְמָנָהor the widow וְאָמַרand let כָּלall הָעָםthe people אָמֵןsay Amen

‘Cursed is he who withholds justice from the foreigner, the fatherless, or the widow.’ And let all the people say, ‘Amen!’

‘Cursed is he who lies with any animal.’ And let all the people say, ‘Amen!’

אָרוּרcursed שֹׁכֵבsleeps עִםwith אֲחֹתוֹhis sister בַּתdaughter אָבִיוhis father אוֹor בַתdaughter אִמּוֹhis mother וְאָמַרand let כָּלall הָעָםthe people אָמֵןsay Amen

‘Cursed is he who sleeps with his sister, the daughter of his father or the daughter of his mother.’ And let all the people say, ‘Amen!’
