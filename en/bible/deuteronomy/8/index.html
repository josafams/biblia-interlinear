Deuteronomy 8

Remember the wilderness in the good land

Open in the interlinear reader

8:1

כָּלevery הַמִּצְוָהcommandment אֲשֶׁרthat אָנֹכִיam מְצַוְּךָgiving you הַיּוֹםtoday תִּשְׁמְרוּןcarefully לַעֲשׂוֹתfollow לְמַעַןso תִּחְיוּןlive וּרְבִיתֶםand multiply וּבָאתֶםand enter וִירִשְׁתֶּםand possess אֶת[obj] הָאָרֶץthe land אֲשֶׁרthat נִשְׁבַּעswore יְהוָהLORD לַאֲבֹתֵיכֶםto give your fathers

You must carefully follow every commandment I am giving you today, so that you may live and multiply, and enter and possess the land that the LORD swore to give your fathers.

8:2

וְזָכַרְתָּremember אֶת[obj] כָּלall הַדֶּרֶךְthe way אֲשֶׁרthat הֹלִיכֲךָled you יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God זֶהthese אַרְבָּעִיםforty שָׁנָהyears בַּמִּדְבָּרin the wilderness לְמַעַןso עַנֹּתְךָhumble you לְנַסֹּתְךָand test you לָדַעַתto know אֶת[obj] אֲשֶׁרthat בִּלְבָבְךָin your heart הֲתִשְׁמֹרwould keep מִצְוֺתָיוhis commandments אִםor לֹאnot

Remember that these forty years the LORD your God led you all the way in the wilderness, so that He might humble you and test you in order to know what was in your heart, whether or not you would keep His commandments.

8:3

וַיְעַנְּךָhe humbled you וַיַּרְעִבֶךָand in your hunger וַיַּאֲכִלְךָeat אֶת[obj] הַמָּןmanna אֲשֶׁרwhich לֹאnot יָדַעְתָּyou וְלֹאand not יָדְעוּןknown אֲבֹתֶיךָyour fathers לְמַעַןso הוֹדִעֲךָyou understand כִּיthat לֹאnot עַלon הַלֶּחֶםbread לְבַדּוֹalone יִחְיֶהlive הָאָדָםman כִּיbut עַלon כָּלevery מוֹצָאcomes פִיmouth יְהוָהLORD יִחְיֶהword הָאָדָםthe man

He humbled you, and in your hunger He gave you manna to eat, which neither you nor your fathers had known, so that you might understand that man does not live on bread alone, but on every word that comes from the mouth of the LORD.

8:4

שִׂמְלָתְךָyour clothing did לֹאnot בָלְתָהwear מֵעָלֶיךָfrom the you וְרַגְלְךָand your feet did לֹאnot בָצֵקָהswell זֶהthese אַרְבָּעִיםforty שָׁנָהyears

Your clothing did not wear out and your feet did not swell during these forty years.

8:5

וְיָדַעְתָּso know עִםwith לְבָבֶךָyour heart כִּיthat כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had יְיַסֵּרdisciplines אִישׁman אֶת[obj] בְּנוֹhis son יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God מְיַסְּרֶךָּdisciplines you

So know in your heart that just as a man disciplines his son, so the LORD your God disciplines you.

8:6

וְשָׁמַרְתָּshall keep אֶת[obj] מִצְוֺתcommandments יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God לָלֶכֶתwalking בִּדְרָכָיוin his ways וּלְיִרְאָהand fearing אֹתוֹhim

Therefore you shall keep the commandments of the LORD your God, walking in His ways and fearing Him.

8:7

כִּיfor יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God מְבִיאֲךָbringing you אֶלinto אֶרֶץland טוֹבָהgood אֶרֶץland נַחֲלֵיbrooks מָיִםsprings עֲיָנֹתsprings וּתְהֹמֹתfountains and יֹצְאִיםcome out בַּבִּקְעָהthrough the valleys וּבָהָרand hills

For the LORD your God is bringing you into a good land, a land of brooks and fountains and springs that flow through the valleys and hills;

8:8

אֶרֶץland חִטָּהwheat וּשְׂעֹרָהbarley וְגֶפֶןvines וּתְאֵנָהfig trees וְרִמּוֹןand pomegranates אֶרֶץland זֵיתolive שֶׁמֶןoil וּדְבָשׁand honey

a land of wheat, barley, vines, fig trees, and pomegranates; a land of olive oil and honey;

8:9

אֶרֶץland אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot בְמִסְכֵּנֻתin scarceness תֹּאכַלeat בָּהּin her לֶחֶםfood לֹאnot תֶחְסַרscarcity כֹּלnothing בָּהּin her אֶרֶץland אֲשֶׁרthat אֲבָנֶיהָrocks are בַרְזֶלiron וּמֵהֲרָרֶיהָand whose hills are תַּחְצֹבmined for נְחֹשֶׁתcopper

a land where you will eat food without scarcity, where you will lack nothing; a land whose rocks are iron and whose hills are ready to be mined for copper.

8:10

וְאָכַלְתָּwhen you eat וְשָׂבָעְתָּand are satisfied וּבֵרַכְתָּto bless אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God עַלfor הָאָרֶץthe land הַטֹּבָהgood that אֲשֶׁרhas נָתַןgiven לָךְto you

When you eat and are satisfied, you are to bless the LORD your God for the good land that He has given you.

8:11

הִשָּׁמֶרcareful לְךָto פֶּןnot תִּשְׁכַּחforget אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God לְבִלְתִּיto not שְׁמֹרkeep מִצְוֺתָיוhis commandments וּמִשְׁפָּטָיוand ordinances וְחֻקֹּתָיוand statutes אֲשֶׁרwhich אָנֹכִיam מְצַוְּךָgiving you הַיּוֹםthis day

Be careful not to forget the LORD your God by failing to keep His commandments and ordinances and statutes, which I am giving you this day.

8:12

פֶּןotherwise תֹּאכַלeat וְשָׂבָעְתָּand are satisfied וּבָתִּיםhouses טוֹבִיםfine תִּבְנֶהbuild וְיָשָׁבְתָּwhich to dwell

Otherwise, when you eat and are satisfied, when you build fine houses in which to dwell,

8:13

וּבְקָרְךָand when your herds וְצֹאנְךָand flocks יִרְבְּיֻןlarge וְכֶסֶףand silver וְזָהָבand gold יִרְבֶּהincrease לָּךְto you וְכֹלand all אֲשֶׁרthat לְךָto you יִרְבֶּהmultiplied

and when your herds and flocks grow large and your silver and gold increase and all that you have is multiplied,

8:14

וְרָםbecome proud לְבָבֶךָyour heart וְשָׁכַחְתָּforget אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God הַמּוֹצִיאֲךָwho brought you מֵאֶרֶץland מִצְרַיִםEgypt מִבֵּיתhouse עֲבָדִיםslavery

then your heart will become proud, and you will forget the LORD your God who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery.

8:15

הַמּוֹלִיכֲךָled you בַּמִּדְבָּרwilderness הַגָּדֹלvast וְהַנּוֹרָאand terrifying נָחָשׁsnakes שָׂרָףvenomous snakes וְעַקְרָבand scorpions וְצִמָּאוֹןthirsty and waterless אֲשֶׁרthat אֵיןno מָיִםwater הַמּוֹצִיאbrought לְךָto you מַיִםwater מִצּוּרfrom rock הַחַלָּמִישׁflint

He led you through the vast and terrifying wilderness with its venomous snakes and scorpions, a thirsty and waterless land. He brought you water from the rock of flint.

8:16

הַמַּאֲכִלְךָfed you מָןmanna בַּמִּדְבָּרin the wilderness אֲשֶׁרhad לֹאnot יָדְעוּןknown אֲבֹתֶיךָyour fathers לְמַעַןorder עַנֹּתְךָhumble you וּלְמַעַןso נַסֹּתֶךָtest you לְהֵיטִבְךָto prosper בְּאַחֲרִיתֶךָin the end

He fed you in the wilderness with manna that your fathers had not known, in order to humble you and test you, so that in the end He might cause you to prosper.

8:17

וְאָמַרְתָּmight say בִּלְבָבֶךָin your heart כֹּחִיpower וְעֹצֶםand strength יָדִיmy hands have עָשָׂהmade לִיfor me אֶת[obj] הַחַיִלthis wealth הַזֶּהthis

You might say in your heart, “The power and strength of my hands have made this wealth for me.”

8:18

וְזָכַרְתָּbut remember אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God כִּיthat הוּאis הַנֹּתֵןwho gives לְךָto you כֹּחַpower לַעֲשׂוֹתto gain חָיִלwealth לְמַעַןorder הָקִיםconfirm אֶת[obj] בְּרִיתוֹhis covenant אֲשֶׁרthat נִשְׁבַּעswore לַאֲבֹתֶיךָto your fathers כַּיּוֹםeven to this day הַזֶּהthis

But remember that it is the LORD your God who gives you the power to gain wealth, in order to confirm His covenant that He swore to your fathers even to this day.

8:19

וְהָיָהand be אִםif שָׁכֹחַever forget תִּשְׁכַּחforget אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God וְהָלַכְתָּand go אַחֲרֵיafter אֱלֹהִיםgods אֲחֵרִיםother וַעֲבַדְתָּםto worship וְהִשְׁתַּחֲוִיתָand bow down לָהֶםto them הַעִדֹתִיtestify בָכֶםagainst you הַיּוֹםtoday כִּיthat אָבֹדwill תֹּאבֵדוּןperish

If you ever forget the LORD your God and go after other gods to worship and bow down to them, I testify against you today that you will surely perish.

8:20

כַּגּוֹיִםlike the nations אֲשֶׁרthat יְהוָהLORD מַאֲבִידdestroyed מִפְּנֵיכֶםbefore you כֵּןso תֹאבֵדוּןperish עֵקֶבif you לֹאnot תִשְׁמְעוּןobey בְּקוֹלin voice יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיכֶםyour God

Like the nations that the LORD has destroyed before you, so you will perish if you do not obey the LORD your God.
