Deuteronomy 26

Firstfruits and tithes; the covenant confirmed

26:1

When you enter the land that the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance, and you take possession of it and settle in it,

you are to take some of the firstfruits of all your produce from the soil of the land that the LORD your God is giving you and put them in a basket. Then go to the place the LORD your God will choose as a dwelling for His Name,

26:3

וּבָאתָand go אֶלto הַכֹּהֵןthe priest אֲשֶׁרwho יִהְיֶהserving בַּיָּמִיםat that time הָהֵםthose וְאָמַרְתָּand say אֵלָיוto him הִגַּדְתִּיdeclare הַיּוֹםtoday לַיהוָהto the LORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God כִּיthat בָאתִיentered אֶלto הָאָרֶץthe land אֲשֶׁרthat נִשְׁבַּעswore יְהוָהLORD לַאֲבֹתֵינוּto our fathers לָתֶתto give לָנוּto us

to the priest who is serving at that time, and say to him, “I declare today to the LORD your God that I have entered the land that the LORD swore to our fathers to give us.”

26:4

וְלָקַחtake הַכֹּהֵןthe priest הַטֶּנֶאthe basket מִיָּדֶךָfrom your hands וְהִנִּיחוֹand place it לִפְנֵיbefore מִזְבַּחaltar יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God

Then the priest shall take the basket from your hands and place it before the altar of the LORD your God,

26:5

וְעָנִיתָand respond וְאָמַרְתָּdeclare לִפְנֵיto before יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God אֲרַמִּיAramean אֹבֵדwandering אָבִיmy father וַיֵּרֶדand he went down מִצְרַיְמָהEgypt וַיָּגָרand lived שָׁםthere בִּמְתֵיfew in number מְעָטfew וַיְהִיand became שָׁםthere לְגוֹיa nation גָּדוֹלgreat עָצוּםmighty וָרָבand numerous

and you are to declare before the LORD your God, “My father was a wandering Aramean, and he went down to Egypt few in number and lived there and became a great nation, mighty and numerous.

26:6

וַיָּרֵעוּmistreated אֹתָנוּus הַמִּצְרִיםthe Egyptians וַיְעַנּוּנוּand afflicted us וַיִּתְּנוּand gave עָלֵינוּus to עֲבֹדָהhard קָשָׁהlabor

But the Egyptians mistreated us and afflicted us, putting us to hard labor.

26:7

וַנִּצְעַקso we called out אֶלto יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod אֲבֹתֵינוּour fathers וַיִּשְׁמַעheard יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] קֹלֵנוּour voice וַיַּרְאand saw אֶת[obj] עָנְיֵנוּour affliction וְאֶתand [obj] עֲמָלֵנוּtoil וְאֶתand [obj] לַחֲצֵנוּoppression

So we called out to the LORD, the God of our fathers; and the LORD heard our voice and saw our affliction, toil, and oppression.

26:8

וַיּוֹצִאֵנוּbrought us יְהוָהLORD מִמִּצְרַיִםout of Egypt בְּיָדhand חֲזָקָהmighty וּבִזְרֹעַand an arm נְטוּיָהoutstretched וּבְמֹרָאwith terror גָּדֹלgreat וּבְאֹתוֹתsigns וּבְמֹפְתִיםand wonders

Then the LORD brought us out of Egypt with a mighty hand and an outstretched arm, with great terror, signs, and wonders.

26:9

וַיְבִאֵנוּand he brought us אֶלto הַמָּקוֹםthis place הַזֶּהthis וַיִּתֶּןand gave לָנוּto us אֶת[obj] הָאָרֶץthis land הַזֹּאתthis אֶרֶץland זָבַתflowing חָלָבmilk וּדְבָשׁand honey

And He brought us to this place and gave us this land, a land flowing with milk and honey.

26:10

וְעַתָּהand now הִנֵּהbehold הֵבֵאתִיbrought אֶת[obj] רֵאשִׁיתfirstfruits פְּרִיfruit הָאֲדָמָהthe land אֲשֶׁרthat נָתַתָּהgiven לִּיto me יְהוָהLORD וְהִנַּחְתּוֹplace לִפְנֵיbefore יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God וְהִשְׁתַּחֲוִיתָand bow לִפְנֵיdown before יְהוָהhim אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God

And now, behold, I have brought the firstfruits of the land that You, O LORD, have given me.” Then you are to place the basket before the LORD your God and bow down before Him.

26:11

וְשָׂמַחְתָּrejoice בְכָלin all הַטּוֹבthe good אֲשֶׁרhas נָתַןgiven לְךָto יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָGod וּלְבֵיתֶךָand your household אַתָּהyou וְהַלֵּוִיthe Levite וְהַגֵּרand the foreigner אֲשֶׁרthat בְּקִרְבֶּךָdwelling among you

So you shall rejoice—you, the Levite, and the foreigner dwelling among you—in all the good things the LORD your God has given to you and your household.

26:12

כִּיwhen תְכַלֶּהfinished לַעְשֵׂרlaying aside אֶת[obj] כָּלall מַעְשַׂרtenth תְּבוּאָתְךָyour produce בַּשָּׁנָהin the year הַשְּׁלִישִׁתthird שְׁנַתyear הַמַּעֲשֵׂרthe tithe וְנָתַתָּהto give לַלֵּוִיto the Levite לַגֵּרthe foreigner לַיָּתוֹםthe fatherless וְלָאַלְמָנָהand the widow וְאָכְלוּthey may eat בִשְׁעָרֶיךָwithin your gates וְשָׂבֵעוּand be filled

When you have finished laying aside a tenth of all your produce in the third year, the year of the tithe, you are to give it to the Levite, the foreigner, the fatherless, and the widow, that they may eat and be filled within your gates.

26:13

וְאָמַרְתָּshall declare לִפְנֵיin the presence יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God בִּעַרְתִּיremoved הַקֹּדֶשׁthe sacred מִןfrom הַבַּיִתhouse וְגַםand have נְתַתִּיוgiven it לַלֵּוִיto the Levite וְלַגֵּרthe foreigner לַיָּתוֹםthe fatherless וְלָאַלְמָנָהand the widow כְּכָלaccording to all מִצְוָתְךָcommandments you אֲשֶׁרhave צִוִּיתָנִיgiven me לֹאnot עָבַרְתִּיtransgressed מִמִּצְוֺתֶיךָyour commandments וְלֹאor שָׁכָחְתִּיforgotten

Then you shall declare in the presence of the LORD your God, “I have removed from my house the sacred portion and have given it to the Levite, the foreigner, the fatherless, and the widow, according to all the commandments You have given me. I have not transgressed or forgotten Your commandments.

26:14

לֹאnot אָכַלְתִּיeaten בְאֹנִיsacred מִמֶּנּוּfrom us וְלֹאor בִעַרְתִּיremoved מִמֶּנּוּfrom us בְּטָמֵאwhile unclean וְלֹאor נָתַתִּיoffered מִמֶּנּוּfrom us לְמֵתfor the dead שָׁמַעְתִּיobeyed בְּקוֹלin voice יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהָיmy God עָשִׂיתִיdone כְּכֹלeverything אֲשֶׁרyou צִוִּיתָנִיcommanded me

I have not eaten any of the sacred portion while in mourning, or removed any of it while unclean, or offered any of it for the dead. I have obeyed the LORD my God; I have done everything You commanded me.

26:15

הַשְׁקִיפָהlook down מִמְּעוֹןhabitation קָדְשְׁךָyour holy מִןfrom הַשָּׁמַיִםheaven וּבָרֵךְand bless אֶת[obj] עַמְּךָyour people אֶת[obj] יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וְאֵתand [obj] הָאֲדָמָהthe land אֲשֶׁרhave נָתַתָּהgiven לָנוּto us כַּאֲשֶׁרas you נִשְׁבַּעְתָּswore לַאֲבֹתֵינוּto our fathers אֶרֶץland זָבַתflowing חָלָבmilk וּדְבָשׁand honey

Look down from Your holy habitation, from heaven, and bless Your people Israel and the land You have given us as You swore to our fathers—a land flowing with milk and honey.”

26:16

הַיּוֹםthis day הַזֶּהthis יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God מְצַוְּךָcommands you לַעֲשׂוֹתto follow אֶת[obj] הַחֻקִּיםthese statutes הָאֵלֶּהthese וְאֶתand [obj] הַמִּשְׁפָּטִיםordinances וְשָׁמַרְתָּyou must be careful וְעָשִׂיתָto follow אוֹתָםthem בְּכָלwith all לְבָבְךָyour heart וּבְכָלand with all נַפְשֶׁךָyour soul

The LORD your God commands you this day to follow these statutes and ordinances. You must be careful to follow them with all your heart and with all your soul.

26:17

אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD הֶאֱמַרְתָּproclaimed הַיּוֹםtoday לִהְיוֹתto be לְךָto you לֵאלֹהִיםGod וְלָלֶכֶתwalk בִּדְרָכָיוin his ways וְלִשְׁמֹרkeep חֻקָּיוhis statutes וּמִצְוֺתָיוand commandments וּמִשְׁפָּטָיוand ordinances וְלִשְׁמֹעַand listen בְּקֹלוֹto his voice

Today you have proclaimed that the LORD is your God and that you will walk in His ways, keep His statutes and commandments and ordinances, and listen to His voice.

26:18

וַיהוָהLORD הֶאֱמִירְךָproclaimed that you הַיּוֹםtoday the לִהְיוֹתare לוֹto him לְעַםpeople סְגֻלָּהtreasured possession כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had דִּבֶּרpromised לָךְto you וְלִשְׁמֹרto keep כָּלall מִצְוֺתָיוhis commandments

And today the LORD has proclaimed that you are His people and treasured possession as He promised, that you are to keep all His commandments,

26:19

וּלְתִתְּךָhe will set you עֶלְיוֹןhigh עַלon כָּלall הַגּוֹיִםthe nations אֲשֶׁרhas עָשָׂהmade לִתְהִלָּהin praise וּלְשֵׁםand name וּלְתִפְאָרֶתand honor וְלִהְיֹתְךָand that you will be עַםpeople קָדֹשׁholy לַיהוָהto the LORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God כַּאֲשֶׁרas he has דִּבֵּרpromised

that He will set you high in praise and name and honor above all the nations He has made, and that you will be a holy people to the LORD your God, as He has promised.
