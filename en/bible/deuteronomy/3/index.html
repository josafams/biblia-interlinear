Deuteronomy 3

The victory over Og; Moses' request

Open in the interlinear reader

3:1

וַנֵּפֶןthen we turned וַנַּעַלand went דֶּרֶךְroad הַבָּשָׁןto Bashan וַיֵּצֵאcame עוֹגOg מֶלֶךְking הַבָּשָׁןof Bashan לִקְרָאתֵנוּto meet us הוּאhe וְכָלand whole עַמּוֹarmy לַמִּלְחָמָהin battle אֶדְרֶעִיEdrei

Then we turned and went up the road to Bashan, and Og king of Bashan and his whole army came out to meet us in battle at Edrei.

3:2

וַיֹּאמֶרsaid יְהוָהLORD אֵלַיto me אַלdo not תִּירָאfear אֹתוֹhim כִּיfor בְיָדְךָinto your hand נָתַתִּיdelivered אֹתוֹhim וְאֶתand [obj] כָּלall עַמּוֹhis people וְאֶתand [obj] אַרְצוֹhis land וְעָשִׂיתָdo לּוֹto him כַּאֲשֶׁרas you עָשִׂיתָdid לְסִיחֹןto Sihon מֶלֶךְking הָאֱמֹרִיthe Amorites אֲשֶׁרwho יוֹשֵׁבlived בְּחֶשְׁבּוֹןin Heshbon

But the LORD said to me, “Do not fear him, for I have delivered him into your hand, along with all his people and his land. Do to him as you did to Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon.”

3:3

וַיִּתֵּןdelivered יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God בְּיָדֵנוּinto our hands we גַּםalso אֶת[obj] עוֹגOg מֶלֶךְking הַבָּשָׁןof Bashan וְאֶתand [obj] כָּלwhole עַמּוֹhis army וַנַּכֵּהוּstruck them עַדuntil בִּלְתִּיno הִשְׁאִירsurvivor לוֹto him שָׂרִידleft

So the LORD our God also delivered Og king of Bashan and his whole army into our hands. We struck them down until no survivor was left.

3:4

וַנִּלְכֹּדwe captured אֶת[obj] כָּלall עָרָיוhis cities בָּעֵתat that time הַהִואwas לֹאnot הָיְתָהa single קִרְיָהcity אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot לָקַחְנוּtake מֵאִתָּםfrom the them שִׁשִּׁיםsixty עִירcity כָּלentire חֶבֶלregion אַרְגֹּבArgob מַמְלֶכֶתkingdom עוֹגOg בַּבָּשָׁןin Bashan

At that time we captured all sixty of his cities. There was not a single city we failed to take—the entire region of Argob, the kingdom of Og in Bashan.

3:5

כָּלall אֵלֶּהthese עָרִיםcities בְּצֻרוֹתfortified חוֹמָהwalls גְבֹהָהhigh דְּלָתַיִםgates וּבְרִיחַand bars לְבַדto addition מֵעָרֵיunwalled הַפְּרָזִיvillages הַרְבֵּהmany מְאֹדvery

All these cities were fortified with high walls and gates and bars, and there were many more unwalled villages.

3:6

וַנַּחֲרֵםwe devoted אוֹתָםthem כַּאֲשֶׁרas we had עָשִׂינוּdone לְסִיחֹןto Sihon מֶלֶךְking חֶשְׁבּוֹןHeshbon הַחֲרֵםutterly destroying כָּלevery עִירcity מְתִםmen הַנָּשִׁיםthe women וְהַטָּףand children

We devoted them to destruction, as we had done to Sihon king of Heshbon, utterly destroying the men, women, and children of every city.

3:7

וְכָלbut all הַבְּהֵמָהthe livestock וּשְׁלַלand plunder הֶעָרִיםthe cities בַּזּוֹנוּcarried off לָנוּfor ourselves

But all the livestock and plunder of the cities we carried off for ourselves.

3:8

וַנִּקַּחwe took בָּעֵתat that time הַהִואthat אֶת[obj] הָאָרֶץthe land מִיַּדfrom hand שְׁנֵיtwo מַלְכֵיkings הָאֱמֹרִיthe Hermon אֲשֶׁרthat בְּעֵבֶרacross הַיַּרְדֵּןthe Jordan מִנַּחַלValley אַרְנֹןArnon עַדfar הַרMount חֶרְמוֹןHermon

At that time we took from the two kings of the Amorites the land across the Jordan, from the Arnon Valley as far as Mount Hermon—

3:9

צִידֹנִיםSidonians יִקְרְאוּcall לְחֶרְמוֹןto Hermon שִׂרְיֹןSirion וְהָאֱמֹרִיbut the Amorites יִקְרְאוּcall לוֹto him שְׂנִירSenir

which the Sidonians call Sirion but the Amorites call Senir—

3:10

כֹּלall עָרֵיcities הַמִּישֹׁרthe plateau וְכָלall הַגִּלְעָדof Gilead וְכָלand all הַבָּשָׁןof Bashan עַדfar סַלְכָהSalecah וְאֶדְרֶעִיand Edrei עָרֵיcities מַמְלֶכֶתkingdom עוֹגOg בַּבָּשָׁןin the Bashan

all the cities of the plateau, all of Gilead, and all of Bashan as far as the cities of Salecah and Edrei in the kingdom of Og.

3:11

כִּיfor רַקonly עוֹגOg מֶלֶךְking הַבָּשָׁןof Bashan נִשְׁאַרremained מִיֶּתֶרof the remnant הָרְפָאִיםthe Rephaim הִנֵּהbehold עַרְשׂוֹhis bed עֶרֶשׂbed בַּרְזֶלiron הֲלֹהnot הִואis בְּרַבַּתin Rabbah בְּנֵיsons עַמּוֹןAmmonites תֵּשַׁעnine אַמּוֹתcubits אָרְכָּהּcubits long וְאַרְבַּעand four אַמּוֹתcubits רָחְבָּהּwide בְּאַמַּתin cubits אִישׁman

(For only Og king of Bashan had remained of the remnant of the Rephaim. His bed of iron, nine cubits long and four cubits wide, is still in Rabbah of the Ammonites.)

3:12

וְאֶתand [obj] הָאָרֶץland הַזֹּאתthis יָרַשְׁנוּpossession בָּעֵתat that time הַהִואthat מֵעֲרֹעֵרbeyond Aroer אֲשֶׁרthat עַלon נַחַלValley אַרְנֹןArnon וַחֲצִיand half הַרhill country הַגִּלְעָדof Gilead וְעָרָיוalong with its cities נָתַתִּיgave לָרֻאוּבֵנִיto the Reubenites וְלַגָּדִיand Gadites

So at that time we took possession of this land. To the Reubenites and Gadites I gave the land beyond Aroer along the Arnon Valley, and half the hill country of Gilead, along with its cities.

3:13

וְיֶתֶרrest הַגִּלְעָדGilead וְכָלand all הַבָּשָׁןof Bashan מַמְלֶכֶתkingdom עוֹגOg נָתַתִּיgave לַחֲצִיto the half שֵׁבֶטtribe הַמְנַשֶּׁהManasseh כֹּלentire חֶבֶלregion הָאַרְגֹּבArgob לְכָלwhole territory הַבָּשָׁןof Bashan הַהוּאthat יִקָּרֵאcalled אֶרֶץland רְפָאִיםRephaim

To the half-tribe of Manasseh I gave the rest of Gilead and all of Bashan, the kingdom of Og. (The entire region of Argob, the whole territory of Bashan, used to be called the land of the Rephaim.)

3:14

יָאִירJair בֶּןdescendant מְנַשֶּׁהManasseh לָקַחtook אֶת[obj] כָּלwhole חֶבֶלregion אַרְגֹּבArgob עַדfar גְּבוּלborder הַגְּשׁוּרִיthe Geshurites וְהַמַּעֲכָתִיMaacathites וַיִּקְרָאand he renamed אֹתָםthem עַלon שְׁמוֹafter himself אֶת[obj] הַבָּשָׁןBashan חַוֺּתHavvoth יָאִירHavvoth עַדto הַיּוֹםthis day הַזֶּהthis

Jair, a descendant of Manasseh, took the whole region of Argob as far as the border of the Geshurites and Maacathites. He renamed Bashan after himself, Havvoth-jair, by which it is called to this day.

3:15

וּלְמָכִירto Machir נָתַתִּיgave אֶת[obj] הַגִּלְעָדGilead

To Machir I gave Gilead,

3:16

וְלָרֻאוּבֵנִיand to the Reubenites וְלַגָּדִיand Gadites נָתַתִּיgave מִןfrom הַגִּלְעָדGilead וְעַדto נַחַלValley אַרְנֹןArnon תּוֹךְmiddle הַנַּחַלthe valley וּגְבֻלwas the border וְעַדand up to יַבֹּקJabbok הַנַּחַלriver the גְּבוּלborder בְּנֵיsons עַמּוֹןAmmonites

and to the Reubenites and Gadites I gave the territory from Gilead to the Arnon Valley (the middle of the valley was the border) and up to the Jabbok River, the border of the Ammonites.

3:17

וְהָעֲרָבָהin the Arabah וְהַיַּרְדֵּןThe Jordan וּגְבֻלriver מִכִּנֶּרֶתfrom Chinnereth וְעַדto יָםsea הָעֲרָבָהthe Arabah יָםsea הַמֶּלַחthe salt תַּחַתunder אַשְׁדֹּתslopes הַפִּסְגָּהof Pisgah מִזְרָחָהeast

The Jordan River in the Arabah bordered it from Chinnereth to the Sea of the Arabah (the Salt Sea) with the slopes of Pisgah to the east.

3:18

וָאֲצַוI commanded אֶתְכֶםyou בָּעֵתat that time הַהִואthat לֵאמֹרto saying יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיכֶםyour God נָתַןgiven לָכֶםto you אֶת[obj] הָאָרֶץthis land הַזֹּאתthis לְרִשְׁתָּהּto possess your חֲלוּצִיםarmed תַּעַבְרוּcross לִפְנֵיfor battle ahead אֲחֵיכֶםyour brothers בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites כָּלall בְּנֵיmen of חָיִלvalor

At that time I commanded you: “The LORD your God has given you this land to possess. All your men of valor are to cross over, armed for battle, ahead of your brothers, the Israelites.

3:19

רַקbut נְשֵׁיכֶםyour wives וְטַפְּכֶםyour children וּמִקְנֵכֶםand your livestock יָדַעְתִּיknow כִּיthat מִקְנֶהlivestock רַבmuch לָכֶםto you יֵשְׁבוּremain בְּעָרֵיכֶםin the cities אֲשֶׁרhave נָתַתִּיgiven לָכֶםto you

But your wives, your children, and your livestock—I know that you have much livestock—may remain in the cities I have given you,

3:20

עַדuntil אֲשֶׁרthat יָנִיחַrest יְהוָהLORD לַאֲחֵיכֶםto your brothers כָּכֶםas וְיָרְשׁוּpossession גַםtoo הֵםthey אֶת[obj] הָאָרֶץof the land אֲשֶׁרthat יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיכֶםyour God נֹתֵןgiving לָהֶםto them בְּעֵבֶרacross הַיַּרְדֵּןthe Jordan וְשַׁבְתֶּםreturn אִישׁman לִירֻשָּׁתוֹto the possession אֲשֶׁרhave נָתַתִּיgiven לָכֶםto you

until the LORD gives rest to your brothers as He has to you, and they too have taken possession of the land that the LORD your God is giving them across the Jordan. Then each of you may return to the possession I have given you.”

3:21

וְאֶתand [obj] יְהוֹשׁוּעַJoshua צִוֵּיתִיcommanded בָּעֵתat that time הַהִואthat לֵאמֹרI עֵינֶיךָyour eyes הָרֹאֹתseen אֵת[obj] כָּלall אֲשֶׁרthat עָשָׂהdone יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיכֶםyour God לִשְׁנֵיto two הַמְּלָכִיםthese kings הָאֵלֶּהthese כֵּןsame יַעֲשֶׂהdo יְהוָהLORD לְכָלto all הַמַּמְלָכוֹתthe kingdoms אֲשֶׁרthat אַתָּהyou are עֹבֵרabout enter שָׁמָּהthere toward

And at that time I commanded Joshua: “Your own eyes have seen all that the LORD your God has done to these two kings. The LORD will do the same to all the kingdoms you are about to enter.

3:22

לֹאnot תְִּירָאוּםafraid of them כִּיfor יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיכֶםyour God הוּאhe הַנִּלְחָםfight לָכֶםfor you

Do not be afraid of them, for the LORD your God Himself will fight for you.”

3:23

וָאֶתְחַנַּןalso pleaded אֶלto יְהוָהLORD בָּעֵתat that time הַהִואthat לֵאמֹרto saying

At that time I also pleaded with the LORD:

3:24

אֲדֹנָיLord יְהוִהGOD אַתָּהyou הַחִלּוֹתָbegun לְהַרְאוֹתto show אֶת[obj] עַבְדְּךָyour servant אֶת[obj] גָּדְלְךָgreatness וְאֶתand [obj] יָדְךָpower to your הַחֲזָקָהservant אֲשֶׁרthat מִיwhat אֵלgod בַּשָּׁמַיִםin heaven וּבָאָרֶץor on earth אֲשֶׁרthat יַעֲשֶׂהperform כְמַעֲשֶׂיךָsuch works וְכִגְבוּרֹתֶךָand mighty acts as yours

“O Lord GOD, You have begun to show Your greatness and power to Your servant. For what god in heaven or on earth can perform such works and mighty acts as Yours?

3:25

אֶעְבְּרָהcross נָּאplease וְאֶרְאֶהand see אֶת[obj] הָאָרֶץthe land הַטּוֹבָהgood אֲשֶׁרthat בְּעֵבֶרbeyond הַיַּרְדֵּןthe Jordan הָהָרthat pleasant hill country הַטּוֹבas well הַזֶּהthis וְהַלְּבָנוֹןwell as Lebanon

Please let me cross over and see the good land beyond the Jordan—that pleasant hill country as well as Lebanon!”

3:26

וַיִּתְעַבֵּרangry יְהוָהLORD בִּיwith me לְמַעַנְכֶםso you וְלֹאand not שָׁמַעlisten אֵלָיto me וַיֹּאמֶרsaid יְהוָהLORD אֵלַיto me רַבenough לָךְto you אַלdo not תּוֹסֶףis דַּבֵּרspeak אֵלַיto me עוֹדagain בַּדָּבָרabout this matter הַזֶּהthis

But the LORD was angry with me on account of you, and He would not listen to me. “That is enough,” the LORD said to me. “Do not speak to Me again about this matter.

3:27

עֲלֵהgo רֹאשׁtop הַפִּסְגָּהPisgah וְשָׂאand take עֵינֶיךָlook יָמָּהwest וְצָפֹנָהand north וְתֵימָנָהand south וּמִזְרָחָהand east וּרְאֵהsee בְעֵינֶיךָwith your own eyes כִּיfor לֹאnot תַעֲבֹרcross אֶת[obj] הַיַּרְדֵּןthis Jordan הַזֶּהthis

Go to the top of Pisgah and look to the west and north and south and east. See the land with your own eyes, for you will not cross this Jordan.

3:28

וְצַוbut commission אֶת[obj] יְהוֹשֻׁעַJoshua וְחַזְּקֵהוּencourage him וְאַמְּצֵהוּand strengthen him כִּיfor הוּאhe יַעֲבֹרcross לִפְנֵיover ahead הָעָםthe people הַזֶּהthis וְהוּאand he יַנְחִילenable אוֹתָםthem אֶת[obj] הָאָרֶץthe land אֲשֶׁרthat תִּרְאֶהsee

But commission Joshua, encourage him, and strengthen him, for he will cross over ahead of the people and enable them to inherit the land that you will see.”

3:29

וַנֵּשֶׁבso we stayed בַּגָּיְאin the valley מוּלopposite בֵּיתBeth פְּעוֹרpeor

So we stayed in the valley opposite Beth-peor.
