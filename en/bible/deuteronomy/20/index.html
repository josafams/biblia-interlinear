Deuteronomy 20

Laws of warfare

20:1

כִּיwhen תֵצֵאgo out לַמִּלְחָמָהto war עַלagainst אֹיְבֶיךָyour enemies וְרָאִיתָand see סוּסhorses וָרֶכֶבchariots and עַםarmy רַבlarger מִמְּךָthan yours לֹאnot תִירָאafraid מֵהֶםof them כִּיfor יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God עִמָּךְwith you הַמַּעַלְךָwho brought you מֵאֶרֶץland מִצְרָיִםEgypt

When you go out to war against your enemies and see horses, chariots, and an army larger than yours, do not be afraid of them; for the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, is with you.

20:2

וְהָיָהand be כְּקָרָבְכֶםyou are about אֶלto הַמִּלְחָמָהinto battle וְנִגַּשׁgo הַכֹּהֵןthe priest וְדִבֶּרand address אֶלto הָעָםthe army

When you are about to go into battle, the priest is to come forward and address the army,

20:3

וְאָמַרsaying אֲלֵהֶםto them שְׁמַעhear יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael אַתֶּםyou קְרֵבִיםgoing הַיּוֹםtoday לַמִּלְחָמָהare into battle עַלon אֹיְבֵיכֶםyour enemies אַלdo not יֵרַךְfainthearted לְבַבְכֶםfainthearted אַלdo תִּירְאוּafraid וְאַלnot תַּחְפְּזוּalarmed וְאַלor תַּעַרְצוּterrified מִפְּנֵיהֶםfrom their before

saying to them, “Hear, O Israel, today you are going into battle with your enemies. Do not be fainthearted or afraid; do not be alarmed or terrified because of them.

20:4

כִּיfor יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיכֶםyour God הַהֹלֵךְgoes עִמָּכֶםwith you לְהִלָּחֵםto fight לָכֶםfor you עִםagainst אֹיְבֵיכֶםyour enemies לְהוֹשִׁיעַvictory אֶתְכֶםyou

For the LORD your God goes with you to fight for you against your enemies, to give you the victory.”

20:5

וְדִבְּרוּaddress הַשֹּׁטְרִיםthe officers אֶלto הָעָםthe army לֵאמֹרsaying מִיwho הָאִישׁany man אֲשֶׁרthat בָּנָהbuilt בַיִתhouse חָדָשׁnew וְלֹאand not חֲנָכוֹdedicated it him יֵלֵךְlet וְיָשֹׁבhim return לְבֵיתוֹhome he פֶּןor may יָמוּתdie בַּמִּלְחָמָהin battle וְאִישׁand man אַחֵרanother יַחְנְכֶנּוּdedicate it

Furthermore, the officers are to address the army, saying, “Has any man built a new house and not dedicated it? Let him return home, or he may die in battle and another man dedicate it.

20:6

וּמִיand who הָאִישׁman אֲשֶׁרthat נָטַעplanted כֶּרֶםvineyard וְלֹאand not חִלְּלוֹbegun to enjoy its fruit יֵלֵךְlet וְיָשֹׁבhim return לְבֵיתוֹhome he פֶּןor may יָמוּתdie בַּמִּלְחָמָהin battle וְאִישׁand man אַחֵרanother יְחַלְּלֶנּוּenjoy its fruit

Has any man planted a vineyard and not begun to enjoy its fruit? Let him return home, or he may die in battle and another man enjoy its fruit.

20:7

וּמִיand who הָאִישׁman אֲשֶׁרthat אֵרַשׂpledged אִשָּׁהwoman וְלֹאand not לְקָחָהּmarried her יֵלֵךְlet וְיָשֹׁבhim return לְבֵיתוֹhome he פֶּןor יָמוּתdie בַּמִּלְחָמָהin battle וְאִישׁand man אַחֵרanother יִקָּחֶנָּהmarry her

Has any man become pledged to a woman and not married her? Let him return home, or he may die in battle and another man marry her.”

20:8

וְיָסְפוּand add הַשֹּׁטְרִיםthe officers לְדַבֵּרshall speak אֶלto הָעָםthe army וְאָמְרוּsaying מִיwho הָאִישׁman הַיָּרֵאafraid וְרַךְor fainthearted הַלֵּבָבfainthearted יֵלֵךְlet וְיָשֹׁבhim return לְבֵיתוֹhome וְלֹאwill not יִמַּסmelt אֶת[obj] לְבַבhearts אֶחָיוhis brothers כִּלְבָבוֹlike his own

Then the officers shall speak further to the army, saying, “Is any man afraid or fainthearted? Let him return home, so that the hearts of his brothers will not melt like his own.”

20:9

וְהָיָהand be כְּכַלֹּתfinished הַשֹּׁטְרִיםthe officers לְדַבֵּרaddressing אֶלto הָעָםthe army וּפָקְדוּthey are to appoint שָׂרֵיcommanders צְבָאוֹתHosts בְּרֹאשׁto lead הָעָםthe people

When the officers have finished addressing the army, they are to appoint commanders to lead it.

20:10

כִּיwhen תִקְרַבapproach אֶלto עִירcity לְהִלָּחֵםto fight עָלֶיהָagainst it וְקָרָאתָand proclaim אֵלֶיהָto make an לְשָׁלוֹםoffer of peace

When you approach a city to fight against it, you are to make an offer of peace.

20:11

וְהָיָהand be אִםif שָׁלוֹםpeace תַּעַנְךָaccept your וּפָתְחָהand open לָךְto you וְהָיָהtheir gates כָּלall הָעָםthe people הַנִּמְצָאthe found בָהּin her יִהְיוּbecome לְךָto you לָמַסforced laborers וַעֲבָדוּךָto serve you

If they accept your offer of peace and open their gates, all the people there will become forced laborers to serve you.

20:12

וְאִםbut if לֹאnot תַשְׁלִיםpeace עִמָּךְwith you וְעָשְׂתָהand wage עִמְּךָagainst you מִלְחָמָהwar וְצַרְתָּsiege עָלֶיהָּto that city

But if they refuse to make peace with you and wage war against you, lay siege to that city.

20:13

וּנְתָנָהּdelivered it יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God בְּיָדֶךָinto your hand you וְהִכִּיתָmust put אֶת[obj] כָּלevery זְכוּרָהּmale לְפִיto mouth חָרֶבsword

When the LORD your God has delivered it into your hand, you must put every male to the sword.

20:14

רַקbut הַנָּשִׁיםthe women וְהַטַּףchildren וְהַבְּהֵמָהlivestock וְכֹלand whatever אֲשֶׁרthat יִהְיֶהbe בָעִירin the city כָּלall שְׁלָלָהּits spoil תָּבֹזplunder לָךְto you וְאָכַלְתָּshall use the אֶת[obj] שְׁלַלspoil אֹיְבֶיךָyour enemies אֲשֶׁרthat נָתַןgiven יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God לָךְto you

But the women, children, livestock, and whatever else is in the city—all its spoil—you may take as plunder, and you shall use the spoil of your enemies that the LORD your God gives you.

20:15

כֵּןthis תַּעֲשֶׂהtreat לְכָלto all הֶעָרִיםthe cities הָרְחֹקֹתthat are far מִמְּךָfrom you מְאֹדvery אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot מֵעָרֵיbelong to הַגּוֹיִםthe nations הָאֵלֶּהthese הֵנָּהare

This is how you are to treat all the cities that are far away from you and do not belong to the nations nearby.

20:16

רַקhowever מֵעָרֵיin the cities הָעַמִּיםthe nations הָאֵלֶּהthe אֲשֶׁרthat יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God נֹתֵןgiving לְךָto you נַחֲלָהinheritance לֹאnot תְחַיֶּהleave alive כָּלanything נְשָׁמָהbreathes

However, in the cities of the nations that the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance, you must not leave alive anything that breathes.

20:17

כִּיfor הַחֲרֵםutterly destroying תַּחֲרִימֵםdevote them הַחִתִּיthe Hittites וְהָאֱמֹרִיAmorites הַכְּנַעֲנִיCanaanites וְהַפְּרִזִּיPerizzites הַחִוִּיHivites וְהַיְבוּסִיand Jebusites כַּאֲשֶׁרhas צִוְּךָcommanded you יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָGod

For you must devote them to complete destruction—the Hittites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites—as the LORD your God has commanded you,

20:18

לְמַעַןso אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot יְלַמְּדוּteach אֶתְכֶםyou לַעֲשׂוֹתto do כְּכֹלall תּוֹעֲבֹתָםdetestable things they אֲשֶׁרthat עָשׂוּdo לֵאלֹהֵיהֶםfor their gods וַחֲטָאתֶםsin לַיהוָהto against the LORD אֱלֹהֵיכֶםyour God

so that they cannot teach you to do all the detestable things they do for their gods, and so cause you to sin against the LORD your God.

20:19

כִּיwhen תָצוּרsiege אֶלto עִירcity יָמִיםfor רַבִּיםtime לְהִלָּחֵםfighting עָלֶיהָagainst it לְתָפְשָׂהּto capture it לֹאnot תַשְׁחִיתdestroy אֶת[obj] עֵצָהּits trees לִנְדֹּחַby putting עָלָיוto them גַּרְזֶןaxe כִּיbecause מִמֶּנּוּfrom us תֹאכֵלeat וְאֹתוֹand him לֹאnot תִכְרֹתcut כִּיthat הָאָדָםhuman עֵץtrees הַשָּׂדֶהthe field לָבֹאto come מִפָּנֶיךָfrom your before בַּמָּצוֹרbesiege

When you lay siege to a city for an extended time while fighting against it to capture it, you must not destroy its trees by putting an axe to them, because you can eat their fruit. You must not cut them down. Are the trees of the field human, that you should besiege them?

20:20

רַקbut עֵץtrees אֲשֶׁרthat תֵּדַעknow כִּיfor לֹאnot עֵץproduce מַאֲכָלfruit הוּאhe אֹתוֹhim תַשְׁחִיתdestroy וְכָרָתָּworks וּבָנִיתָto build מָצוֹרsiege עַלagainst הָעִירthe city אֲשֶׁרthat הִואis עֹשָׂהwaging עִמְּךָagainst you מִלְחָמָהwar עַדuntil רִדְתָּהּit falls

But you may destroy the trees that you know do not produce fruit. Use them to build siege works against the city that is waging war against you, until it falls.
