Deuteronomy 16

The three annual feasts; just judges

Open in the interlinear reader

16:1

שָׁמוֹרobserve אֶת[obj] חֹדֶשׁmonth הָאָבִיבof Abib וְעָשִׂיתָand celebrate פֶּסַחPassover לַיהוָהto the LORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God כִּיbecause בְּחֹדֶשׁin the month הָאָבִיבof Abib הוֹצִיאֲךָyour brought you יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָGod מִמִּצְרַיִםout of Egypt לָיְלָהnight

Observe the month of Abib and celebrate the Passover to the LORD your God, because in the month of Abib the LORD your God brought you out of Egypt by night.

16:2

וְזָבַחְתָּsacrifice פֶּסַחPassover לַיהוָהto the LORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God צֹאןflock וּבָקָרherd or בַּמָּקוֹםin the place אֲשֶׁרthat יִבְחַרchoose יְהוָהLORD לְשַׁכֵּןa dwelling שְׁמוֹhis name שָׁםthere

You are to offer to the LORD your God the Passover sacrifice from the herd or flock in the place the LORD will choose as a dwelling for His Name.

16:3

לֹאnot תֹאכַלeat עָלָיוon him חָמֵץleavened bread שִׁבְעַתseven יָמִיםdays תֹּאכַלeat עָלָיוon him מַצּוֹתunleavened לֶחֶםbread the bread עֹנִיaffliction כִּיbecause בְחִפָּזוֹןin haste יָצָאתָleft מֵאֶרֶץland מִצְרַיִםEgypt לְמַעַןmay תִּזְכֹּרremember אֶת[obj] יוֹםday צֵאתְךָyou left מֵאֶרֶץleft the land מִצְרַיִםEgypt כֹּלrest יְמֵיday חַיֶּיךָyour life

You must not eat leavened bread with it; for seven days you are to eat with it unleavened bread, the bread of affliction, because you left the land of Egypt in haste—so that you may remember for the rest of your life the day you left the land of Egypt.

16:4

וְלֹאno יֵרָאֶהappear לְךָto be שְׂאֹרleaven בְּכָלin all גְּבֻלְךָyour land שִׁבְעַתseven יָמִיםdays וְלֹאand not יָלִיןremain מִןof הַבָּשָׂרthe meat אֲשֶׁרyou תִּזְבַּחsacrifice בָּעֶרֶבin the evening בַּיּוֹםthe day הָרִאשׁוֹןfirst shall לַבֹּקֶרuntil morning

No leaven is to be found in all your land for seven days, and none of the meat you sacrifice in the evening of the first day shall remain until morning.

16:5

לֹאnot תוּכַלyou לִזְבֹּחַto sacrifice אֶת[obj] הַפָּסַחthe Passover בְּאַחַדin any שְׁעָרֶיךָtowns אֲשֶׁרthat יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God נֹתֵןgiving לָךְto you

You are not to sacrifice the Passover animal in any of the towns that the LORD your God is giving you.

16:6

כִּיfor אִםif אֶלto הַמָּקוֹםthe place אֲשֶׁרthat יִבְחַרchoose יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God לְשַׁכֵּןdwelling for שְׁמוֹhis name שָׁםthere תִּזְבַּחoffer אֶת[obj] הַפֶּסַחthe Passover בָּעָרֶבin the evening כְּבוֹאlike entered הַשֶּׁמֶשׁthe sun מוֹעֵדtime צֵאתְךָyou departed מִמִּצְרָיִםfrom Egypt

You must only offer the Passover sacrifice at the place the LORD your God will choose as a dwelling for His Name. Do this in the evening as the sun sets, at the same time you departed from Egypt.

16:7

וּבִשַּׁלְתָּshall roast וְאָכַלְתָּand eat בַּמָּקוֹםin the place אֲשֶׁרthat יִבְחַרchoose יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God בּוֹin him וּפָנִיתָand regard בַבֹּקֶרin the morning וְהָלַכְתָּshall return לְאֹהָלֶיךָto your tents

And you shall roast it and eat it in the place the LORD your God will choose, and in the morning you shall return to your tents.

16:8

שֵׁשֶׁתsix יָמִיםdays תֹּאכַלeat מַצּוֹתunleavened bread וּבַיּוֹםand on the day הַשְּׁבִיעִיseventh עֲצֶרֶתsolemn assembly לַיהוָהto the LORD אֱלֹהֶיךָGod and you לֹאnot תַעֲשֶׂהdo מְלָאכָהwork

For six days you must eat unleavened bread, and on the seventh day you shall hold a solemn assembly to the LORD your God, and you must not do any work.

16:9

שִׁבְעָהseven שָׁבֻעֹתweeks תִּסְפָּרcount לָךְto you מֵהָחֵלfirst חֶרְמֵשׁsickle בַּקָּמָהto the standing grain תָּחֵלbegin לִסְפֹּרto proclaim שִׁבְעָהseven שָׁבֻעוֹתweeks

You are to count off seven weeks from the time you first put the sickle to the standing grain.

16:10

וְעָשִׂיתָshall celebrate חַגfeast שָׁבֻעוֹתweeks לַיהוָהto the LORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God מִסַּתtribute נִדְבַתfreewill offering יָדְךָyour hand אֲשֶׁרthat תִּתֵּןgive כַּאֲשֶׁרhas יְבָרֶכְךָblessed you יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָGod

And you shall celebrate the Feast of Weeks to the LORD your God with a freewill offering that you give in proportion to how the LORD your God has blessed you,

16:11

וְשָׂמַחְתָּrejoice לִפְנֵיbefore יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God אַתָּהyou וּבִנְךָyour sons וּבִתֶּךָand daughters your וְעַבְדְּךָmenservants and וַאֲמָתֶךָand maidservants וְהַלֵּוִיand the Levite אֲשֶׁרthat בִּשְׁעָרֶיךָwithin your gates וְהַגֵּרthe foreigner וְהַיָּתוֹםthe fatherless וְהָאַלְמָנָהand the widows אֲשֶׁרthat בְּקִרְבֶּךָamong you בַּמָּקוֹםin the place אֲשֶׁרthat יִבְחַרchoose יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God לְשַׁכֵּןdwelling for שְׁמוֹhis name שָׁםthere

and you shall rejoice before the LORD your God in the place He will choose as a dwelling for His Name—you, your sons and daughters, your menservants and maidservants, and the Levite within your gates, as well as the foreigner, the fatherless, and the widows among you.

16:12

וְזָכַרְתָּremember כִּיthat עֶבֶדslaves הָיִיתָwere בְּמִצְרָיִםin Egypt וְשָׁמַרְתָּand carefully וְעָשִׂיתָfollow אֶת[obj] הַחֻקִּיםthese statutes הָאֵלֶּהthese

Remember that you were slaves in Egypt, and carefully follow these statutes.

16:13

חַגfeast הַסֻּכֹּתof Tabernacles תַּעֲשֶׂהcelebrate לְךָto שִׁבְעַתseven יָמִיםdays בְּאָסְפְּךָafter you have gathered מִגָּרְנְךָof your threshing floor וּמִיִּקְבֶךָand your winepress

You are to celebrate the Feast of Tabernacles for seven days after you have gathered the produce of your threshing floor and your winepress.

16:14

וְשָׂמַחְתָּrejoice בְּחַגֶּךָin your feast אַתָּהyou וּבִנְךָyour sons וּבִתֶּךָand daughters your וְעַבְדְּךָmenservants וַאֲמָתֶךָand maidservants וְהַלֵּוִיthe Levite וְהַגֵּרwell as the foreigner וְהַיָּתוֹםthe fatherless וְהָאַלְמָנָהand the widows אֲשֶׁרthat בִּשְׁעָרֶיךָin your gates

And you shall rejoice in your feast—you, your sons and daughters, your menservants and maidservants, and the Levite, as well as the foreigner, the fatherless, and the widows among you.

16:15

שִׁבְעַתseven יָמִיםdays תָּחֹגcelebrate a feast לַיהוָהto the LORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God בַּמָּקוֹםin the place אֲשֶׁרthat יִבְחַרchoose יְהוָהLORD כִּיbecause יְבָרֶכְךָbless יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָGod בְּכֹלin all תְּבוּאָתְךָyour produce וּבְכֹלand in all מַעֲשֵׂהwork יָדֶיךָyour hands וְהָיִיתָand be אַךְbut שָׂמֵחַjoy

For seven days you shall celebrate a feast to the LORD your God in the place He will choose, because the LORD your God will bless you in all your produce and in all the work of your hands, so that your joy will be complete.

16:16

שָׁלוֹשׁthree פְּעָמִיםtimes בַּשָּׁנָהa year יֵרָאֶהappear כָלall זְכוּרְךָyour men אֶת[obj] פְּנֵיbefore יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God בַּמָּקוֹםin the place אֲשֶׁרthat יִבְחָרchoose בְּחַגat the feast הַמַּצּוֹתof unleavened bread וּבְחַגthe feast הַשָּׁבֻעוֹתof weeks וּבְחַגand the feast הַסֻּכּוֹתof Tabernacles וְלֹאno יֵרָאֶהappear אֶת[obj] פְּנֵיbefore יְהוָהLORD רֵיקָםempty handed

Three times a year all your men are to appear before the LORD your God in the place He will choose: at the Feast of Unleavened Bread, the Feast of Weeks, and the Feast of Tabernacles. No one should appear before the LORD empty-handed.

16:17

אִישׁeveryone כְּמַתְּנַתgift as יָדוֹhe is able כְּבִרְכַּתblessing יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God אֲשֶׁרhas נָתַןgiven לָךְto you

Everyone must appear with a gift as he is able, according to the blessing the LORD your God has given you.

16:18

שֹׁפְטִיםjudges וְשֹׁטְרִיםand officials תִּתֶּןgive לְךָyou are to בְּכָלin every שְׁעָרֶיךָtown אֲשֶׁרthat יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God is נֹתֵןgiving לְךָto you לִשְׁבָטֶיךָtribes וְשָׁפְטוּjudge אֶת[obj] הָעָםthe people מִשְׁפַּטjudgment צֶדֶקrighteous

You are to appoint judges and officials for your tribes in every town that the LORD your God is giving you. They are to judge the people with righteous judgment.

16:19

לֹאnot תַטֶּהdeny מִשְׁפָּטjustice לֹאor תַכִּירshow פָּנִיםpartiality וְלֹאdo not תִקַּחaccept שֹׁחַדbribe כִּיfor הַשֹּׁחַדa bribe יְעַוֵּרblinds עֵינֵיeyes חֲכָמִיםwise וִיסַלֵּףand twists דִּבְרֵיwords צַדִּיקִםrighteous

Do not deny justice or show partiality. Do not accept a bribe, for a bribe blinds the eyes of the wise and twists the words of the righteous.

16:20

צֶדֶקrighteousness צֶדֶקjustice and justice תִּרְדֹּףpursue לְמַעַןso תִּחְיֶהlive וְיָרַשְׁתָּpossess אֶת[obj] הָאָרֶץthe land אֲשֶׁרthat יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God נֹתֵןgiving לָךְto you

Pursue justice, and justice alone, so that you may live, and you may possess the land that the LORD your God is giving you.

16:21

לֹאnot תִטַּעset לְךָto אֲשֵׁרָהAsherah pole כָּלany עֵץwooden אֵצֶלnext מִזְבַּחaltar יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God אֲשֶׁרyou תַּעֲשֶׂהbuild לָּךְfor

Do not set up any wooden Asherah pole next to the altar you will build for the LORD your God,

16:22

וְלֹאand do not תָקִיםset לְךָfor yourselves מַצֵּבָהsacred pillar אֲשֶׁרwhich שָׂנֵאhates יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God

and do not set up for yourselves a sacred pillar, which the LORD your God hates.
