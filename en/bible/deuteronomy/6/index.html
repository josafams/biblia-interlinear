Deuteronomy 6

Hear, O Israel: loving the LORD

6:3

Hear, O Israel, and be careful to observe them, so that you may prosper and multiply greatly in a land flowing with milk and honey, just as the LORD, the God of your fathers, has promised you.

6:4

שְׁמַעhear יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God יְהוָהLORD אֶחָדone

Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God, the LORD is One.

6:5

וְאָהַבְתָּshall love אֵת[obj] יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God בְּכָלwith all לְבָבְךָyour heart וּבְכָלand with all נַפְשְׁךָyour soul וּבְכָלand with all מְאֹדֶךָyour very

And you shall love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your strength.

6:6

וְהָיוּand be הַדְּבָרִיםthese words הָאֵלֶּהthese אֲשֶׁרthat אָנֹכִיam מְצַוְּךָcommanding you הַיּוֹםtoday עַלupon לְבָבֶךָyour hearts

These words I am commanding you today are to be upon your hearts.

6:7

וְשִׁנַּנְתָּםteach them diligently לְבָנֶיךָto your children וְדִבַּרְתָּand speak בָּםin them בְּשִׁבְתְּךָwhen you sit בְּבֵיתֶךָat home וּבְלֶכְתְּךָand when you walk בַדֶּרֶךְalong the road וּבְשָׁכְבְּךָwhen you lie וּבְקוּמֶךָand when you get

And you shall teach them diligently to your children and speak of them when you sit at home and when you walk along the road, when you lie down and when you get up.

6:8

וּקְשַׁרְתָּםtie them לְאוֹתas reminders עַלon יָדֶךָyour hands וְהָיוּand be לְטֹטָפֹתbind בֵּיןbetween עֵינֶיךָyour foreheads

Tie them as reminders on your hands and bind them on your foreheads.

6:9

וּכְתַבְתָּםwrite them עַלon מְזוּזֹתdoorposts בֵּיתֶךָyour houses וּבִשְׁעָרֶיךָand on your gates

Write them on the doorposts of your houses and on your gates.

6:10

וְהָיָהand be כִּיwhen יְבִיאֲךָbring you יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָGod brings you אֶלinto הָאָרֶץthe land אֲשֶׁרthat נִשְׁבַּעswore לַאֲבֹתֶיךָto your fathers לְאַבְרָהָםto Abraham לְיִצְחָקIsaac וּלְיַעֲקֹבJacob לָתֶתthat he would give לָךְto you עָרִיםcities גְּדֹלֹתgreat וְטֹבֹתand splendid אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot בָנִיתָbuild

And when the LORD your God brings you into the land He swore to your fathers, to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, that He would give you—a land with great and splendid cities that you did not build,

6:11

וּבָתִּיםwith houses מְלֵאִיםfull כָּלevery טוּבgood אֲשֶׁרwhich לֹאnot מִלֵּאתָfill וּבֹרֹתwith wells חֲצוּבִיםstonecutters אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot חָצַבְתָּdig כְּרָמִיםvineyards וְזֵיתִיםand olive groves אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot נָטָעְתָּplant וְאָכַלְתָּeat וְשָׂבָעְתָּand are satisfied

with houses full of every good thing with which you did not fill them, with wells that you did not dig, and with vineyards and olive groves that you did not plant—and when you eat and are satisfied,

6:12

הִשָּׁמֶרcareful לְךָto פֶּןnot תִּשְׁכַּחforget אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD אֲשֶׁרwho הוֹצִיאֲךָbrought you מֵאֶרֶץland מִצְרַיִםEgypt מִבֵּיתhouse עֲבָדִיםslavery

be careful not to forget the LORD who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery.

6:13

אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God תִּירָאfear וְאֹתוֹand him תַעֲבֹדserve וּבִשְׁמוֹoaths in his name תִּשָּׁבֵעַswear

Fear the LORD your God, serve Him only, and take your oaths in His name.

6:14

לֹאnot תֵלְכוּןwalk אַחֲרֵיfollow אֱלֹהִיםother אֲחֵרִיםgods מֵאֱלֹהֵיthe gods הָעַמִּיםthe peoples אֲשֶׁרthat סְבִיבוֹתֵיכֶםaround you

Do not follow other gods, the gods of the peoples around you.

6:15

For the LORD your God, who is among you, is a jealous God. Otherwise the anger of the LORD your God will be kindled against you, and He will wipe you off the face of the earth.

6:16

לֹאnot תְנַסּוּtest אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיכֶםyour God כַּאֲשֶׁרas you נִסִּיתֶםtested בַּמַּסָּהat Massah

Do not test the LORD your God as you tested Him at Massah.

6:17

You are to diligently keep the commandments of the LORD your God and the testimonies and statutes He has given you.

6:18

Do what is right and good in the sight of the LORD, so that it may be well with you and that you may enter and possess the good land that the LORD your God swore to give your fathers,

6:19

לַהֲדֹףdriving אֶת[obj] כָּלall אֹיְבֶיךָyour enemies מִפָּנֶיךָbefore you כַּאֲשֶׁרhas דִּבֶּרsaid יְהוָהLORD

driving out all your enemies before you, as the LORD has said.

6:20

In the future, when your son asks, “What is the meaning of the decrees and statutes and ordinances that the LORD our God has commanded you?”

6:21

then you are to tell him, “We were slaves of Pharaoh in Egypt, but the LORD brought us out of Egypt with a mighty hand.

6:22

וַיִּתֵּןinflicted יְהוָהLORD אוֹתֹתsigns וּמֹפְתִיםand wonders גְּדֹלִיםgreat וְרָעִיםand devastating בְּמִצְרַיִםon Egypt בְּפַרְעֹהon Pharaoh וּבְכָלand on all בֵּיתוֹhis household לְעֵינֵינוּbefore our eyes

Before our eyes the LORD inflicted great and devastating signs and wonders on Egypt, on Pharaoh, and on all his household.

6:23

וְאוֹתָנוּand us הוֹצִיאbrought out מִשָּׁםfrom there לְמַעַןto הָבִיאlead אֹתָנוּus לָתֶתin and give לָנוּto us אֶת[obj] הָאָרֶץthe land אֲשֶׁרthat had נִשְׁבַּעsworn לַאֲבֹתֵינוּto our fathers

But He brought us out from there to lead us in and give us the land that He had sworn to our fathers.

6:24

And the LORD commanded us to observe all these statutes and to fear the LORD our God, that we may always be prosperous and preserved, as we are to this day.

6:25

וּצְדָקָהrighteousness תִּהְיֶהbe לָּנוּto us כִּיif נִשְׁמֹרcareful לַעֲשׂוֹתto observe אֶת[obj] כָּלevery הַמִּצְוָהthese commandments הַזֹּאתthis לִפְנֵיbefore יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God כַּאֲשֶׁרas he has צִוָּנוּcommanded us

And if we are careful to observe every one of these commandments before the LORD our God, as He has commanded us, then that will be our righteousness.”
