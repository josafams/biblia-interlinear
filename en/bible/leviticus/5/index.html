Leviticus 5

Offerings for sin and for guilt

Open in the interlinear reader

5:1

וְנֶפֶשׁsomeone כִּיfor תֶחֱטָאsins וְשָׁמְעָהwhen he hears קוֹלpublic אָלָהfailing וְהוּאand he עֵדwitnessed אוֹor רָאָהseen אוֹor יָדָעlearned אִםif לוֹאnot יַגִּידtell וְנָשָׂאhe shall bear עֲוֺנוֹiniquity

“If someone sins by failing to testify when he hears a public charge about something he has witnessed, whether he has seen it or learned of it, he shall bear the iniquity.

5:2

Or if a person touches anything unclean—whether the carcass of any unclean wild animal or livestock or crawling creature—even if he is unaware of it, he is unclean and guilty.

5:3

אוֹor כִיif יִגַּעtouches בְּטֻמְאַתuncleanness אָדָםhuman לְכֹלanything טֻמְאָתוֹhis uncleanness אֲשֶׁרwhich יִטְמָאunclean בָּהּin her וְנֶעְלַםhe is unaware מִמֶּנּוּunaware of it וְהוּאwhen he יָדַעrealizes וְאָשֵׁםhe is guilty

Or if he touches human uncleanness—anything by which one becomes unclean—even if he is unaware of it, when he realizes it, he is guilty.

5:4

אוֹor נֶפֶשׁif כִּיfor תִשָּׁבַעswears לְבַטֵּאthoughtlessly בִשְׂפָתַיִםwith his lips לְהָרַעevil אוֹor לְהֵיטִיבdo good לְכֹלanything אֲשֶׁרthat יְבַטֵּאrashly pronounce הָאָדָםman בִּשְׁבֻעָהan oath וְנֶעְלַםunaware מִמֶּנּוּof it וְהוּאwhen he יָדַעrealizes וְאָשֵׁםhe is guilty לְאַחַתto one מֵאֵלֶּהfrom these

Or if someone swears thoughtlessly with his lips to do anything good or evil—in whatever matter a man may rashly pronounce an oath—even if he is unaware of it, when he realizes it, he is guilty in the matter.

5:5

וְהָיָהand be כִיfor יֶאְשַׁםincurs guilt לְאַחַתin one מֵאֵלֶּהof these וְהִתְוַדָּהhe must confess אֲשֶׁרthat חָטָאsin עָלֶיהָon her

If someone incurs guilt in one of these ways, he must confess the sin he has committed,

5:6

and he must bring his guilt offering to the LORD for the sin he has committed: a female lamb or goat from the flock as a sin offering. And the priest will make atonement for him concerning his sin.

5:7

וְאִםif however לֹאnot תַגִּיעtouches יָדוֹhis hand דֵּיafford שֶׂהlamb וְהֵבִיאhe may bring אֶת[obj] אֲשָׁמוֹrestitution for his אֲשֶׁרthat חָטָאsin שְׁתֵּיtwo תֹרִיםturtledoves אוֹor שְׁנֵיtwo בְנֵיyoung יוֹנָהpigeons לַיהוָהto the LORD אֶחָדone לְחַטָּאתas a sin offering וְאֶחָדand the other לְעֹלָהas a burnt offering

If, however, he cannot afford a lamb, he may bring to the LORD as restitution for his sin two turtledoves or two young pigeons—one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering.

5:8

וְהֵבִיאbring אֹתָםthem אֶלto הַכֹּהֵןthe priest וְהִקְרִיבshall present אֶת[obj] אֲשֶׁרwho לַחַטָּאתfor the sin offering רִאשׁוֹנָהfirst וּמָלַקtwist אֶת[obj] רֹאשׁוֹits head מִמּוּלat the front עָרְפּוֹits neck וְלֹאand not יַבְדִּילsevering

He is to bring them to the priest, who shall first present the one for the sin offering. He is to twist its head at the front of its neck without severing it;

5:9

וְהִזָּהsprinkle מִדַּםblood הַחַטָּאתthe sin offering עַלon קִירside הַמִּזְבֵּחַthe altar וְהַנִּשְׁאָרwhile the rest בַּדָּםthe blood יִמָּצֵהdrained אֶלto יְסוֹדbase הַמִּזְבֵּחַthe altar חַטָּאתsin offering הוּאhe

then he is to sprinkle some of the blood of the sin offering on the side of the altar, while the rest of the blood is drained out at the base of the altar. It is a sin offering.

5:10

וְאֶתand [obj] הַשֵּׁנִיsecond bird יַעֲשֶׂהprepare עֹלָהburnt offering כַּמִּשְׁפָּטaccording to the ordinance וְכִפֶּרwill make atonement עָלָיוfor him הַכֹּהֵןthe priest מֵחַטָּאתוֹfor the sin he אֲשֶׁרhas חָטָאcommitted וְנִסְלַחforgiven לוֹto him

And the priest must prepare the second bird as a burnt offering according to the ordinance. In this way the priest will make atonement for him for the sin he has committed, and he will be forgiven.

5:11

וְאִםbut if לֹאnot תַשִּׂיגafford יָדוֹhis hand לִשְׁתֵּיtwo תֹרִיםturtledoves אוֹor לִשְׁנֵיtwo בְנֵיyoung יוֹנָהpigeons וְהֵבִיאhe may bring אֶת[obj] קָרְבָּנוֹoffering he אֲשֶׁרthat חָטָאmust עֲשִׂירִתtenth הָאֵפָהof an ephah סֹלֶתfine flour לְחַטָּאתas a sin לֹאnot יָשִׂיםput עָלֶיהָon her שֶׁמֶןolive oil וְלֹאor יִתֵּןgive עָלֶיהָon it לְבֹנָהfrankincense כִּיbecause חַטָּאתsin offering הִיאis

But if he cannot afford two turtledoves or two young pigeons, he may bring a tenth of an ephah of fine flour as a sin offering. He must not put olive oil or frankincense on it, because it is a sin offering.

5:12

וֶהֱבִיאָהּto bring it אֶלto הַכֹּהֵןthe priest וְקָמַץand handful הַכֹּהֵןthe priest מִמֶּנָּהfrom it מְלוֹאhandful קֻמְצוֹhandful אֶת[obj] אַזְכָּרָתָהmemorial portion וְהִקְטִירand burn it on הַמִּזְבֵּחָהthe altar עַלon אִשֵּׁיofferings made by fire יְהוָהLORD חַטָּאתsin offering הִואis

He is to bring it to the priest, who shall take a handful from it as a memorial portion and burn it on the altar atop the offerings made by fire to the LORD; it is a sin offering.

5:13

וְכִפֶּרwill make atonement עָלָיוfor him הַכֹּהֵןthe priest עַלfor חַטָּאתוֹsins he אֲשֶׁרhas חָטָאcommitted מֵאַחַתany מֵאֵלֶּהany of these וְנִסְלַחforgiven לוֹto him וְהָיְתָהwill belong לַכֹּהֵןto the priest כַּמִּנְחָהlike the grain offering

In this way the priest will make atonement for him for any of these sins he has committed, and he will be forgiven. The remainder will belong to the priest, like the grain offering.”

5:14

וַיְדַבֵּרand spoke יְהוָהLORD אֶלto מֹשֶׁהMoses לֵּאמֹרsaid

Then the LORD said to Moses,

5:15

נֶפֶשׁsomeone כִּיfor תִמְעֹלacts unfaithfully מַעַלunfaithfully וְחָטְאָהand sins בִּשְׁגָגָהunintentionally against any מִקָּדְשֵׁיholy יְהוָהLORD’s וְהֵבִיאhe must bring אֶת[obj] אֲשָׁמוֹhis guilt offering לַיהוָהto the LORD אַיִלram תָּמִיםunblemished מִןfrom הַצֹּאןthe flock בְּעֶרְכְּךָproper value in כֶּסֶףsilver שְׁקָלִיםshekels בְּשֶׁקֶלshekel הַקֹּדֶשׁthe sanctuary לְאָשָׁםa guilt offering

“If someone acts unfaithfully and sins unintentionally against any of the LORD’s holy things, he must bring his guilt offering to the LORD: an unblemished ram from the flock, of proper value in silver shekels according to the sanctuary shekel; it is a guilt offering.

5:16

וְאֵתand [obj] אֲשֶׁרhas חָטָאregarding מִןfrom הַקֹּדֶשׁregarding holy יְשַׁלֵּםrestitution וְאֶתand [obj] חֲמִישִׁתוֹfifth of its יוֹסֵףvalue עָלָיוto it וְנָתַןand giving אֹתוֹhim לַכֹּהֵןto the priest וְהַכֹּהֵןbehalf יְכַפֵּרmake atonement עָלָיוon his בְּאֵילwith the ram הָאָשָׁםa guilt offering וְנִסְלַחforgiven לוֹto him

Regarding any holy thing he has harmed, he must make restitution by adding a fifth of its value to it and giving it to the priest, who will make atonement on his behalf with the ram as a guilt offering, and he will be forgiven.

5:17

וְאִםif נֶפֶשׁsomeone כִּיfor תֶחֱטָאsins וְעָשְׂתָהand violates אַחַתany מִכָּלany of the מִצְוֺתcommandments יְהוָהLORD’s אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot תֵעָשֶׂינָהdoes וְלֹאand not יָדַעunaware וְאָשֵׁםhe is guilty וְנָשָׂאand shall bear עֲוֺנוֹhis punishment

If someone sins and violates any of the LORD’s commandments even though he was unaware, he is guilty and shall bear his punishment.

5:18

וְהֵבִיאbring אַיִלram תָּמִיםunblemished מִןfrom הַצֹּאןthe flock בְּעֶרְכְּךָproper value לְאָשָׁםa guilt offering אֶלto הַכֹּהֵןthe priest וְכִפֶּרwill make atonement עָלָיוon his הַכֹּהֵןbehalf עַלfor שִׁגְגָתוֹwrong he אֲשֶׁרhas שָׁגָגcommitted in ignorance וְהוּאand he לֹאnot יָדַעknow וְנִסְלַחforgiven לוֹto him

He is to bring to the priest an unblemished ram of proper value from the flock as a guilt offering. Then the priest will make atonement on his behalf for the wrong he has committed in ignorance, and he will be forgiven.

5:19

אָשָׁםguilt offering הוּאwas אָשֹׁםcertainly אָשַׁםguilty לַיהוָהbefore the LORD

It is a guilt offering; he was certainly guilty before the LORD.”
