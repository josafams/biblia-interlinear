Leviticus 27

Vows, redemption and tithes

Open in the interlinear reader

27:1

וַיְדַבֵּרand spoke יְהוָהLORD אֶלto מֹשֶׁהMoses לֵּאמֹרsaid

Then the LORD said to Moses,

27:2

דַּבֵּרspeak אֶלto בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites וְאָמַרְתָּand say אֲלֵהֶםto them אִישׁwhen כִּיfor יַפְלִאspecial נֶדֶרvow בְּעֶרְכְּךָinvolving the value נְפָשֹׁתpersons לַיהוָהto the LORD

“Speak to the Israelites and say to them, ‘When someone makes a special vow to the LORD involving the value of persons,

27:3

וְהָיָהand be עֶרְכְּךָvaluation הַזָּכָרa male מִבֶּןfrom a old עֶשְׂרִיםtwenty שָׁנָהage וְעַדto בֶּןson שִׁשִּׁיםsixty שָׁנָהyears וְהָיָהshall be עֶרְכְּךָyour valuation חֲמִשִּׁיםfifty שֶׁקֶלshekels כֶּסֶףsilver בְּשֶׁקֶלshekel הַקֹּדֶשׁthe sanctuary

if the valuation concerns a male from twenty to sixty years of age, then your valuation shall be fifty shekels of silver, according to the sanctuary shekel.

27:4

וְאִםor if נְקֵבָהfemale הִואis וְהָיָהshall עֶרְכְּךָyour valuation שְׁלֹשִׁיםthirty שָׁקֶלshekels

Or if it is a female, then your valuation shall be thirty shekels.

27:5

וְאִםand if מִבֶּןfrom a old חָמֵשׁfive שָׁנִיםyears וְעַדto בֶּןson עֶשְׂרִיםtwenty שָׁנָהyears age וְהָיָהand be עֶרְכְּךָyour valuation הַזָּכָרthe male עֶשְׂרִיםtwenty שְׁקָלִיםshekels וְלַנְּקֵבָהand for the female עֲשֶׂרֶתten שְׁקָלִיםshekels

And if the person is from five to twenty years of age, then your valuation for the male shall be twenty shekels, and for the female ten shekels.

27:6

וְאִםnow if מִבֶּןfrom a old חֹדֶשׁmonth וְעַדto בֶּןson חָמֵשׁfive שָׁנִיםyears age וְהָיָהand be עֶרְכְּךָyour valuation הַזָּכָרthe male חֲמִשָּׁהfive שְׁקָלִיםshekels כָּסֶףsilver וְלַנְּקֵבָהand for the female עֶרְכְּךָyour valuation שְׁלֹשֶׁתthree שְׁקָלִיםshekels כָּסֶףsilver

Now if the person is from one month to five years of age, then your valuation for the male shall be five shekels of silver, and for the female three shekels of silver.

27:7

וְאִםand if מִבֶּןthe person שִׁשִּׁיםsixty שָׁנָהyears וָמַעְלָהage older אִםor זָכָרmale וְהָיָהbe עֶרְכְּךָyour valuation חֲמִשָּׁהfifteen עָשָׂרfifteen שָׁקֶלshekels וְלַנְּקֵבָהfor the female עֲשָׂרָהten שְׁקָלִיםshekels

And if the person is sixty years of age or older, then your valuation shall be fifteen shekels for the male and ten shekels for the female.

27:8

וְאִםbut if מָךְvow הוּאis מֵעֶרְכֶּךָvaluation is וְהֶעֱמִידוֹhe to present the person לִפְנֵיbefore הַכֹּהֵןthe priest וְהֶעֱרִיךְshall set אֹתוֹhim הַכֹּהֵןthe value עַלon פִּיmouth אֲשֶׁרthat תַּשִּׂיגone יַדhand הַנֹּדֵרmaking the vow יַעֲרִיכֶנּוּcan הַכֹּהֵןafford

But if the one making the vow is too poor to pay the valuation, he is to present the person before the priest, who shall set the value according to what the one making the vow can afford.

27:9

וְאִםif he בְּהֵמָהanimal אֲשֶׁרthat יַקְרִיבוּbrought מִמֶּנָּהfrom her קָרְבָּןoffering לַיהוָהto the LORD כֹּלany אֲשֶׁרthat יִתֵּןgiven מִמֶּנּוּfrom us לַיהוָהto the LORD יִהְיֶהshall be קֹּדֶשׁholy

If he vows an animal that may be brought as an offering to the LORD, any such animal given to the LORD shall be holy.

27:10

לֹאnot יַחֲלִיפֶנּוּreplace it וְלֹאor יָמִירexchange אֹתוֹhim טוֹבgood בְּרָעfor bad אוֹor רַעbad בְּטוֹבfor good וְאִםbut if הָמֵרdoes יָמִירsubstitute בְּהֵמָהanimal בִּבְהֵמָהanimal וְהָיָהand be הוּאthat וּתְמוּרָתוֹits substitute יִהְיֶהbe קֹּדֶשׁholy

He must not replace it or exchange it, either good for bad or bad for good. But if he does substitute one animal for another, both that animal and its substitute will be holy.

27:11

וְאִםbut if כָּלany בְּהֵמָהinvolves טְמֵאָהvow אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot יַקְרִיבוּbrought מִמֶּנָּהfrom her קָרְבָּןoffering לַיהוָהto the LORD וְהֶעֱמִידand present אֶת[obj] הַבְּהֵמָהthe animal לִפְנֵיpresented before הַכֹּהֵןthe priest

But if the vow involves any of the unclean animals that may not be brought as an offering to the LORD, the animal must be presented before the priest.

27:12

וְהֶעֱרִיךְshall set הַכֹּהֵןpriest אֹתָהּher בֵּיןbetween טוֹבhigh וּבֵיןand between רָעlow כְּעֶרְכְּךָvalue הַכֹּהֵןpriest כֵּןso יִהְיֶהbe

The priest shall set its value, whether high or low; as the priest values it, the price will be set.

27:13

וְאִםif however גָּאֹלowner יִגְאָלֶנָּהdecides וְיָסַףhe must add חֲמִישִׁתוֹfifth to עַלon עֶרְכֶּךָvalue

If, however, the owner decides to redeem the animal, he must add a fifth to its value.

27:14

וְאִישׁman כִּיif יַקְדִּשׁconsecrates אֶת[obj] בֵּיתוֹhis house קֹדֶשׁholy לַיהוָהto the LORD וְהֶעֱרִיכוֹvalue it הַכֹּהֵןthe priest בֵּיןbetween טוֹבgood וּבֵיןand between רָעbad כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had יַעֲרִיךְcompare אֹתוֹhim הַכֹּהֵןthe priest כֵּןso יָקוּםstand

Now if a man consecrates his house as holy to the LORD, then the priest shall value it either as good or bad. The price will stand just as the priest values it.

27:15

וְאִםbut if הַמַּקְדִּישׁwho consecrated יִגְאַלredeems אֶת[obj] בֵּיתוֹhouse it וְיָסַףhe must add חֲמִישִׁיתfifth כֶּסֶףassessed עֶרְכְּךָvalue עָלָיוon him וְהָיָהand will belong לוֹto him

But if he who consecrated his house redeems it, he must add a fifth to the assessed value, and it will belong to him.

27:16

וְאִםif מִשְּׂדֵהland אֲחֻזָּתוֹparcel of his יַקְדִּישׁconsecrates אִישׁman לַיהוָהto the LORD וְהָיָהbe עֶרְכְּךָyour valuation לְפִיrequired זַרְעוֹseed זֶרַעseed חֹמֶרhomer שְׂעֹרִיםbarley בַּחֲמִשִּׁיםfor fifty שֶׁקֶלshekels כָּסֶףsilver

If a man consecrates to the LORD a parcel of his land, then your valuation shall be proportional to the seed required for it—fifty shekels of silver for every homer of barley seed.

27:17

אִםif מִשְּׁנַתduring the year הַיֹּבֵלJubilee יַקְדִּישׁconsecrates שָׂדֵהוּhis field כְּעֶרְכְּךָaccording to your valuation יָקוּםstand

If he consecrates his field during the Year of Jubilee, the price will stand according to your valuation.

27:18

וְאִםbut if אַחַרafter הַיֹּבֵלthe Jubilee יַקְדִּישׁconsecrates שָׂדֵהוּhis field וְחִשַּׁבcalculate לוֹto הַכֹּהֵןthe priest אֶת[obj] הַכֶּסֶףthe price עַלin פִּיproportion הַשָּׁנִיםthe years הַנּוֹתָרֹתleft עַדuntil שְׁנַתyear הַיֹּבֵלof Jubilee וְנִגְרַעreduced מֵעֶרְכֶּךָyour valuation

But if he consecrates his field after the Jubilee, the priest is to calculate the price in proportion to the years left until the next Year of Jubilee, so that your valuation will be reduced.

27:19

וְאִםand if גָּאֹלdecides יִגְאַלredeem אֶת[obj] הַשָּׂדֶהthe field הַמַּקְדִּישׁwho consecrated אֹתוֹhim וְיָסַףhe must add חֲמִשִׁיתfifth כֶּסֶףassessed עֶרְכְּךָvalue עָלָיוon him וְקָםand rise לוֹto

And if the one who consecrated the field decides to redeem it, he must add a fifth to the assessed value, and it shall belong to him.

27:20

וְאִםand if לֹאnot יִגְאַלredeem אֶת[obj] הַשָּׂדֶהthe field וְאִםor if מָכַרsold אֶת[obj] הַשָּׂדֶהthe field לְאִישׁto man אַחֵרanother לֹאno יִגָּאֵלredeemed עוֹדlonger

If, however, he does not redeem the field, or if he has sold it to another man, it may no longer be redeemed.

27:21

וְהָיָהwhen הַשָּׂדֶהthe field בְּצֵאתוֹis released בַיֹּבֵלin the Jubilee קֹדֶשׁit לַיהוָהLORD כִּשְׂדֵהlike a field הַחֵרֶםdevoted לַכֹּהֵןthe priests תִּהְיֶהbecomes אֲחֻזָּתוֹproperty

When the field is released in the Jubilee, it will become holy, like a field devoted to the LORD; it becomes the property of the priests.

27:22

וְאִםnow if אֶת[obj] שְׂדֵהfield מִקְנָתוֹpurchased אֲשֶׁרwhich לֹאnot מִשְּׂדֵהfrom land אֲחֻזָּתוֹhis own property יַקְדִּישׁconsecrates לַיהוָהto the LORD

Now if a man consecrates to the LORD a field he has purchased, which is not a part of his own property,

27:23

וְחִשַּׁבcalculate לוֹfor him הַכֹּהֵןthe priest אֵת[obj] מִכְסַתsmall הָעֶרְכְּךָthe value עַדto שְׁנַתyear הַיֹּבֵלof Jubilee וְנָתַןpay אֶת[obj] הָעֶרְכְּךָthe assessed value בַּיּוֹםon that day הַהוּאa קֹדֶשׁsacred offering לַיהוָהto the LORD

then the priest shall calculate for him the value up to the Year of Jubilee, and the man shall pay the assessed value on that day as a sacred offering to the LORD.

27:24

בִּשְׁנַתin the year הַיּוֹבֵלof Jubilee יָשׁוּבreturn הַשָּׂדֶהthe field לַאֲשֶׁרwhom קָנָהוּit bought מֵאִתּוֹfrom the him לַאֲשֶׁרto whom לוֹto him אֲחֻזַּתproperty הָאָרֶץthe land

In the Year of Jubilee the field shall return to the one from whom it was bought—the original owner of the land.

27:25

וְכָלevery עֶרְכְּךָvaluation will יִהְיֶהbe בְּשֶׁקֶלshekel הַקֹּדֶשׁthe sanctuary עֶשְׂרִיםtwenty גֵּרָהgerahs יִהְיֶהbe הַשָּׁקֶלthe shekel

Every valuation will be according to the sanctuary shekel, twenty gerahs to the shekel.

27:26

אַךְbut בְּכוֹרfirstborn אֲשֶׁרthat יְבֻכַּרfirstborn belongs לַיהוָהto the LORD בִּבְהֵמָהlivestock לֹאno יַקְדִּישׁconsecrate אִישׁman אֹתוֹhim אִםif שׁוֹרox אִםor שֶׂהsheep לַיהוָהLORD’s הוּאis

But no one may consecrate a firstborn of the livestock, because a firstborn belongs to the LORD. Whether it is an ox or a sheep, it is the LORD’s.

27:27

וְאִםbut if בַּבְּהֵמָהamong the animals הַטְּמֵאָהunclean וּפָדָהredeem בְעֶרְכֶּךָaccording to your valuation וְיָסַףand add חֲמִשִׁתוֹfifth of its עָלָיוon him וְאִםif לֹאnot יִגָּאֵלredeemed וְנִמְכַּרsold בְּעֶרְכֶּךָaccording to your valuation

But if it is among the unclean animals, then he may redeem it according to your valuation and add a fifth of its value. If it is not redeemed, then it shall be sold according to your valuation.

27:28

אַךְbut כָּלsets חֵרֶםapart אֲשֶׁרthat יַחֲרִםsets אִישׁman לַיהוָהto the LORD מִכָּלfrom all אֲשֶׁרthat לוֹto him מֵאָדָםwhether a man וּבְהֵמָהanimal וּמִשְּׂדֵהland אֲחֻזָּתוֹhis inherited לֹאnot יִמָּכֵרsold וְלֹאor יִגָּאֵלredeemed כָּלeverything חֵרֶםdevoted קֹדֶשׁmost קָדָשִׁיםholy הוּאis לַיהוָהto the LORD

Nothing that a man sets apart to the LORD from all he owns—whether a man, an animal, or his inherited land—can be sold or redeemed; everything so devoted is most holy to the LORD.

27:29

כָּלno חֵרֶםset apart אֲשֶׁרthat יָחֳרַםdestruction מִןfrom הָאָדָםthe man לֹאnot יִפָּדֶהransomed מוֹתsurely יוּמָתdeath

No person set apart for destruction may be ransomed; he must surely be put to death.

27:30

וְכָלthus any מַעְשַׂרtithe הָאָרֶץthe land whether מִזֶּרַעfrom the seed הָאָרֶץthe land מִפְּרִיor the fruit הָעֵץthe trees לַיהוָהbelongs to the LORD הוּאis קֹדֶשׁholy לַיהוָהto the LORD

Thus any tithe from the land, whether from the seed of the land or the fruit of the trees, belongs to the LORD; it is holy to the LORD.

27:31

וְאִםif גָּאֹלowner יִגְאַלredeem אִישׁman מִמַּעַשְׂרוֹhis tithe he חֲמִשִׁיתוֹfifth to its יֹסֵףadd עָלָיוon him

If a man wishes to redeem part of his tithe, he must add a fifth to its value.

27:32

וְכָלevery מַעְשַׂרtenth בָּקָרherd וָצֹאןor flock כֹּלall אֲשֶׁרthat יַעֲבֹרpasses תַּחַתunder הַשָּׁבֶטthe rod הָעֲשִׂירִיshepherd’s יִהְיֶהbe קֹּדֶשׁholy לַיהוָהto the LORD

Every tenth animal from the herd or flock that passes under the shepherd’s rod will be holy to the LORD.

27:33

לֹאnot יְבַקֵּרinspect בֵּיןbetween טוֹבgood לָרַעor bad וְלֹאnot יְמִירֶנּוּsubstitution וְאִםbut if הָמֵרdoes יְמִירֶנּוּsubstitution וְהָיָהboth the animal הוּאhe וּתְמוּרָתוֹand its substitute יִהְיֶהbecome קֹדֶשׁholy לֹאnot יִגָּאֵלredeemed

He must not inspect whether it is good or bad, and he shall not make any substitution. But if he does make a substitution, both the animal and its substitute shall become holy; they cannot be redeemed.’”

27:34

אֵלֶּהthese הַמִּצְוֺתthe commandments אֲשֶׁרthat צִוָּהgave יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] מֹשֶׁהMoses אֶלto בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites בְּהַרon Mount סִינָיSinai

These are the commandments that the LORD gave to Moses for the Israelites on Mount Sinai.
