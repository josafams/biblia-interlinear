כִּיfor אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיכֶםyour God וְהִתְקַדִּשְׁתֶּםconsecrate yourselves therefore וִהְיִיתֶםand be קְדֹשִׁיםholy כִּיbecause קָדוֹשׁholy אָנִיam וְלֹאmust not תְטַמְּאוּdefile אֶת[obj] נַפְשֹׁתֵיכֶםyourselves בְּכָלby any הַשֶּׁרֶץcreature הָרֹמֵשׂthat crawls עַלon הָאָרֶץthe ground

For I am the LORD your God; consecrate yourselves, therefore, and be holy, because I am holy. You must not defile yourselves by any creature that crawls along the ground.

11:45

כִּיfor אֲנִיI am יְהוָהLORD הַמַּעֲלֶהwho brought up אֶתְכֶםyou מֵאֶרֶץland מִצְרַיִםEgypt לִהְיֹתto be לָכֶםto you לֵאלֹהִיםGod וִהְיִיתֶםtherefore be קְדֹשִׁיםholy כִּיbecause קָדוֹשׁholy אָנִיam

For I am the LORD, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt so that I would be your God; therefore be holy, because I am holy.

11:46

זֹאתthis תּוֹרַתlaw הַבְּהֵמָהanimals וְהָעוֹףbirds וְכֹלall נֶפֶשׁliving הַחַיָּהcreatures הָרֹמֶשֶׂתthat move בַּמָּיִםin the water וּלְכָלand all נֶפֶשׁlife הַשֹּׁרֶצֶתcreatures that crawl עַלon הָאָרֶץthe ground

This is the law regarding animals, birds, all living creatures that move in the water, and all creatures that crawl along the ground.

11:47

You must distinguish between the unclean and the clean, between animals that may be eaten and those that may not.’”
