For this reason the Israelites will bring to the LORD the sacrifices they have been offering in the open fields. They are to bring them to the priest at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting and offer them as sacrifices of peace to the LORD.

17:6

The priest will then sprinkle the blood on the altar of the LORD at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting and burn the fat as a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

17:7

They must no longer offer their sacrifices to the goat demons to which they have prostituted themselves. This will be a permanent statute for them for the generations to come.’

17:8

Tell them that if anyone from the house of Israel or any foreigner living among them offers a burnt offering or a sacrifice

but does not bring it to the entrance to the Tent of Meeting to sacrifice it to the LORD, that man must be cut off from his people.

וְאִישׁif אִישׁanyone מִבֵּיתfrom the house יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וּמִןand from הַגֵּרa foreigner הַגָּרliving בְּתוֹכָםamong them אֲשֶׁרthat יֹאכַלeats כָּלany דָּםblood וְנָתַתִּיwill set פָנַיmy face בַּנֶּפֶשׁagainst that person הָאֹכֶלֶתthe consuming אֶת[obj] הַדָּםthe blood וְהִכְרַתִּיand cut אֹתָהּher מִקֶּרֶבfrom among עַמָּהּhis people

If anyone from the house of Israel or a foreigner living among them eats any blood, I will set My face against that person and cut him off from among his people.

כִּיfor נֶפֶשׁlife הַבָּשָׂרthe flesh בַּדָּםin the blood הִואis וַאֲנִיand I נְתַתִּיוgiven it לָכֶםto you עַלupon הַמִּזְבֵּחַthe altar לְכַפֵּרto make atonement עַלfor נַפְשֹׁתֵיכֶםyour souls כִּיfor הַדָּםthe blood הוּאthat בַּנֶּפֶשׁfor the soul יְכַפֵּרatonement

For the life of the flesh is in the blood, and I have given it to you to make atonement for your souls upon the altar; for it is the blood that makes atonement for the soul.

17:12

עַלon כֵּןtherefore אָמַרְתִּיsay לִבְנֵיto sons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites כָּלnone נֶפֶשׁlife מִכֶּםof you לֹאnot תֹאכַלeat דָּםblood וְהַגֵּרnor may any foreigner הַגָּרliving בְּתוֹכְכֶםamong you לֹאnot יֹאכַלeat דָּםblood

Therefore I say to the Israelites, ‘None of you may eat blood, nor may any foreigner living among you eat blood.’

17:13

וְאִישׁand if אִישׁany מִבְּנֵיfrom descendants יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelite וּמִןand from הַגֵּרforeigner הַגָּרliving בְּתוֹכָםamong them אֲשֶׁרthat יָצוּדhunts צֵידwild חַיָּהanimal אוֹor עוֹףbird אֲשֶׁרthat יֵאָכֵלeaten וְשָׁפַךְhe must drain אֶת[obj] דָּמוֹits blood וְכִסָּהוּand cover it בֶּעָפָרwith dirt

And if any Israelite or foreigner living among them hunts down a wild animal or bird that may be eaten, he must drain its blood and cover it with dirt.

17:14

כִּיfor נֶפֶשׁlife כָּלall בָּשָׂרflesh דָּמוֹits blood בְנַפְשׁוֹhave הוּאhe וָאֹמַרtold לִבְנֵיto sons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites דַּםblood כָּלany בָּשָׂרliving thing לֹאnot תֹאכֵלוּeat כִּיbecause נֶפֶשׁlife כָּלall בָּשָׂרflesh דָּמוֹits blood הִואis כָּלwhoever אֹכְלָיוeats it יִכָּרֵתcut off

For the life of all flesh is its blood. Therefore I have told the Israelites, ‘You must not eat the blood of any living thing, because the life of all flesh is its blood; whoever eats it must be cut off.’

17:15

וְכָלand any נֶפֶשׁperson אֲשֶׁרwho תֹּאכַלeats נְבֵלָהanything found dead וּטְרֵפָהor mauled wild beasts בָּאֶזְרָחwhether native וּבַגֵּרor foreigner וְכִבֶּסmust wash בְּגָדָיוhis clothes וְרָחַץand bathe בַּמַּיִםwith water וְטָמֵאunclean עַדuntil הָעֶרֶבevening וְטָהֵרclean

And any person, whether native or foreigner, who eats anything found dead or mauled by wild beasts must wash his clothes and bathe with water, and he will be unclean until evening; then he will be clean.

17:16

וְאִםbut if לֹאnot יְכַבֵּסwash וּבְשָׂרוֹhis clothes and לֹאnot יִרְחָץbathe וְנָשָׂאbear עֲוֺנוֹhis iniquity

But if he does not wash his clothes and bathe himself, then he shall bear his iniquity.”
