Leviticus 15

Bodily discharges and uncleanness

Open in the interlinear reader

15:1

וַיְדַבֵּרsaid יְהוָהLORD אֶלto מֹשֶׁהMoses וְאֶלand to אַהֲרֹןAaron לֵאמֹרto saying

And the LORD said to Moses and Aaron,

15:2

דַּבְּרוּsay אֶלto בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites וַאֲמַרְתֶּםand say אֲלֵהֶםto them אִישׁany אִישׁman כִּיfor יִהְיֶהbe זָבbodily discharge מִבְּשָׂרוֹfrom his meat זוֹבוֹdischarge is טָמֵאunclean הוּאis

“Say to the Israelites, ‘When any man has a bodily discharge, the discharge is unclean.

15:3

וְזֹאתthis תִּהְיֶהbe טֻמְאָתוֹuncleanness is his בְּזוֹבוֹfrom discharge רָרallows בְּשָׂרוֹbody אֶת[obj] זוֹבוֹdischarge אוֹor הֶחְתִּיםblocks so בְּשָׂרוֹit his מִזּוֹבוֹdischarge will טֻמְאָתוֹuncleanness הִואis

This uncleanness is from his discharge, whether his body allows the discharge to flow or blocks it. So his discharge will bring about uncleanness.

15:4

כָּלany הַמִּשְׁכָּבbed אֲשֶׁרwhich יִשְׁכַּבlies עָלָיוon him הַזָּבthe discharge יִטְמָאunclean וְכָלand any הַכְּלִיfurniture אֲשֶׁרwhich יֵשֵׁבsits עָלָיוon he יִטְמָאunclean

Any bed on which the man with the discharge lies will be unclean, and any furniture on which he sits will be unclean.

15:5

וְאִישׁanyone אֲשֶׁרwho יִגַּעtouches בְּמִשְׁכָּבוֹhis bed must יְכַבֵּסwash בְּגָדָיוhis clothes וְרָחַץand bathe בַּמַּיִםwith water וְטָמֵאunclean עַדuntil הָעָרֶבevening

Anyone who touches his bed must wash his clothes and bathe with water, and he will be unclean until evening.

15:6

וְהַיֹּשֵׁבwhoever sits עַלon הַכְּלִיfurniture אֲשֶׁרwhich יֵשֵׁבsitting עָלָיוon הַזָּבthe discharge יְכַבֵּסwash בְּגָדָיוhis clothes וְרָחַץand bathe בַּמַּיִםwith water וְטָמֵאunclean עַדuntil הָעָרֶבevening

Whoever sits on furniture on which the man with the discharge was sitting must wash his clothes and bathe with water, and he will be unclean until evening.

15:7

וְהַנֹּגֵעַwhoever touches בִּבְשַׂרthe body הַזָּבdischarge יְכַבֵּסwash בְּגָדָיוhis clothes וְרָחַץand bathe בַּמַּיִםwith water וְטָמֵאunclean עַדuntil הָעָרֶבevening

Whoever touches the body of the man with a discharge must wash his clothes and bathe with water, and he will be unclean until evening.

15:8

וְכִיif יָרֹקman הַזָּבthe discharge בַּטָּהוֹרclean person וְכִבֶּסmust wash בְּגָדָיוhis clothes וְרָחַץand bathe בַּמַּיִםwith water וְטָמֵאunclean עַדuntil הָעָרֶבevening

If the man with the discharge spits on one who is clean, that person must wash his clothes and bathe with water, and he will be unclean until evening.

15:9

וְכָלany הַמֶּרְכָּבsaddle אֲשֶׁרwhich יִרְכַּבrides עָלָיוon him הַזָּבthe discharge יִטְמָאunclean

Any saddle on which the man with the discharge rides will be unclean.

15:10

וְכָלwhoever הַנֹּגֵעַtouches בְּכֹלanything אֲשֶׁרthat יִהְיֶהwas תַחְתָּיוunder him יִטְמָאunclean עַדuntil הָעָרֶבevening וְהַנּוֹשֵׂאand whoever carries אוֹתָםthem יְכַבֵּסwash בְּגָדָיוhis clothes וְרָחַץand bathe בַּמַּיִםwith water וְטָמֵאunclean עַדuntil הָעָרֶבevening

Whoever touches anything that was under him will be unclean until evening, and whoever carries such things must wash his clothes and bathe with water, and he will be unclean until evening.

15:11

וְכֹלanyone אֲשֶׁרwho יִגַּעtouches בּוֹwith הַזָּבthe discharge וְיָדָיוhis hands לֹאnot שָׁטַףrinsing בַּמָּיִםwith water וְכִבֶּסmust wash בְּגָדָיוhis clothes וְרָחַץand bathe בַּמַּיִםwith water וְטָמֵאunclean עַדuntil הָעָרֶבevening

If the man with the discharge touches anyone without first rinsing his hands with water, the one who was touched must wash his clothes and bathe with water, and he will be unclean until evening.

15:12

וּכְלִיpot חֶרֶשׂclay אֲשֶׁרthat יִגַּעtouches בּוֹwith הַזָּבthe discharge יִשָּׁבֵרbroken וְכָלand any כְּלִיutensil עֵץwooden יִשָּׁטֵףrinsed בַּמָּיִםwith water

Any clay pot that the man with the discharge touches must be broken, and any wooden utensil must be rinsed with water.

15:13

וְכִיand if יִטְהַרcleansed הַזָּבthe man has מִזּוֹבוֹfrom his discharge וְסָפַרhe must count לוֹto him שִׁבְעַתseven יָמִיםdays לְטָהֳרָתוֹfor his cleansing וְכִבֶּסwash בְּגָדָיוhis clothes וְרָחַץand bathe בְּשָׂרוֹhis flesh בְּמַיִםin water חַיִּיםfresh וְטָהֵרclean

When the man has been cleansed from his discharge, he must count off seven days for his cleansing, wash his clothes, and bathe himself in fresh water, and he shall be clean.

15:14

וּבַיּוֹםon the day הַשְּׁמִינִיeighth יִקַּחtake לוֹto שְׁתֵּיtwo תֹרִיםturtledoves אוֹor שְׁנֵיtwo בְּנֵיyoung יוֹנָהpigeons וּבָאcome לִפְנֵיbefore יְהוָהLORD אֶלto פֶּתַחentrance אֹהֶלTent מוֹעֵדMeeting וּנְתָנָםand give them אֶלto הַכֹּהֵןthe priest

On the eighth day he is to take two turtledoves or two young pigeons, come before the LORD at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting, and give them to the priest.

15:15

וְעָשָׂהto sacrifice אֹתָםthem הַכֹּהֵןthe priest אֶחָדone חַטָּאתsin offering וְהָאֶחָדand the other עֹלָהburnt offering וְכִפֶּרatonement עָלָיוfor the man הַכֹּהֵןthe priest לִפְנֵיbefore יְהוָהLORD מִזּוֹבוֹbecause of his discharge

The priest is to sacrifice them, one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering. In this way the priest will make atonement for the man before the LORD because of his discharge.

15:16

וְאִישׁwhen a man כִּיfor תֵצֵאgo מִמֶּנּוּfrom us שִׁכְבַתemission זָרַעsemen וְרָחַץhe must bathe בַּמַּיִםwith water אֶת[obj] כָּלwhole בְּשָׂרוֹbody וְטָמֵאunclean עַדuntil הָעָרֶבevening

When a man has an emission of semen, he must bathe his whole body with water, and he will be unclean until evening.

15:17

וְכָלany בֶּגֶדclothing וְכָלor עוֹרleather אֲשֶׁרwhich יִהְיֶהthere עָלָיוon שִׁכְבַתemission זָרַעsemen וְכֻבַּסmust be washed בַּמַּיִםwith water וְטָמֵאunclean עַדuntil הָעָרֶבevening

Any clothing or leather on which there is an emission of semen must be washed with water, and it will remain unclean until evening.

15:18

וְאִשָּׁהwoman and אֲשֶׁרthat יִשְׁכַּבlies אִישׁman אֹתָהּher שִׁכְבַתemission זָרַעsemen וְרָחֲצוּboth must bathe בַמַּיִםwith water וְטָמְאוּunclean עַדuntil הָעָרֶבevening

If a man lies with a woman and there is an emission of semen, both must bathe with water, and they will remain unclean until evening.

15:19

וְאִשָּׁהwhen a woman כִּיfor תִהְיֶהbe זָבָהdischarge consisting דָּםblood יִהְיֶהbe זֹבָהּdischarge בִּבְשָׂרָהּfrom body she שִׁבְעַתseven יָמִיםdays תִּהְיֶהbe בְנִדָּתָהּher menstruation for וְכָלand anyone הַנֹּגֵעַwho touches בָּהּin her יִטְמָאunclean עַדuntil הָעָרֶבevening

When a woman has a discharge consisting of blood from her body, she will be unclean due to her menstruation for seven days, and anyone who touches her will be unclean until evening.

15:20

וְכֹלanything אֲשֶׁרwhich תִּשְׁכַּבlies עָלָיוon him בְּנִדָּתָהּduring her יִטְמָאmenstruation וְכֹלand all אֲשֶׁרthat תֵּשֵׁבsits עָלָיוon him יִטְמָאunclean

Anything on which she lies or sits during her menstruation will be unclean,

15:21

וְכָלand anyone הַנֹּגֵעַwho touches בְּמִשְׁכָּבָהּher bed יְכַבֵּסwash בְּגָדָיוhis clothes וְרָחַץand bathe בַּמַּיִםwith water וְטָמֵאunclean עַדuntil הָעָרֶבevening

and anyone who touches her bed must wash his clothes and bathe with water, and he will be unclean until evening.

15:22

וְכָלwhoever הַנֹּגֵעַtouches בְּכָלany כְּלִיfurniture אֲשֶׁרwhich תֵּשֵׁבsitting עָלָיוon יְכַבֵּסwash בְּגָדָיוclothes וְרָחַץand bathe בַּמַּיִםwith water וְטָמֵאunclean עַדuntil הָעָרֶבevening

Whoever touches any furniture on which she was sitting must wash his clothes and bathe with water, and he will be unclean until evening.

15:23

וְאִםand if עַלon הַמִּשְׁכָּבa bed הוּאis אוֹor עַלon הַכְּלִיfurniture אֲשֶׁרwhich הִואwas יֹשֶׁבֶתsitting עָלָיוon him בְּנָגְעוֹtouches it בוֹin him יִטְמָאunclean עַדuntil הָעָרֶבevening

And whether it is a bed or furniture on which she was sitting, whoever touches it will be unclean until evening.

15:24

וְאִםif שָׁכֹבlie יִשְׁכַּבlies אִישׁman אֹתָהּher וּתְהִיand be נִדָּתָהּher menstrual עָלָיוon him וְטָמֵאunclean שִׁבְעַתseven יָמִיםdays וְכָלand any הַמִּשְׁכָּבbed אֲשֶׁרwhich יִשְׁכַּבlies עָלָיוon יִטְמָאunclean

If a man lies with her and her menstrual flow touches him, he will be unclean for seven days, and any bed on which he lies will become unclean.

15:25

וְאִשָּׁהwhen a woman כִּיfor יָזוּבa discharge זוֹבdischarge דָּמָהּher blood יָמִיםdays רַבִּיםmany בְּלֹאin without עֶתtime נִדָּתָהּher menstrual period אוֹor כִיif תָזוּבcontinues עַלon נִדָּתָהּher period כָּלall יְמֵיdays זוֹבdischarge טֻמְאָתָהּher unclean כִּימֵיdays נִדָּתָהּher menstruation תִּהְיֶהbe טְמֵאָהunclean הִואis

When a woman has a discharge of her blood for many days at a time other than her menstrual period, or if it continues beyond her period, she will be unclean all the days of her unclean discharge, just as she is during the days of her menstruation.

15:26

כָּלany הַמִּשְׁכָּבbed אֲשֶׁרwhich תִּשְׁכַּבlies עָלָיוon כָּלall יְמֵיdays זוֹבָהּher discharge כְּמִשְׁכַּבbed נִדָּתָהּduring her יִהְיֶהbe לָּהּto her וְכָלor any הַכְּלִיfurniture אֲשֶׁרwhich תֵּשֵׁבsits עָלָיוon טָמֵאunclean יִהְיֶהbe כְּטֻמְאַתmenstrual נִדָּתָהּperiod

Any bed on which she lies or any furniture on which she sits during the days of her discharge will be unclean, like her bed during her menstrual period.

15:27

וְכָלanyone הַנּוֹגֵעַwho touches בָּםin them יִטְמָאunclean וְכִבֶּסhe must wash בְּגָדָיוhis clothes וְרָחַץand bathe בַּמַּיִםwith water וְטָמֵאunclean עַדuntil הָעָרֶבevening

Anyone who touches these things will be unclean; he must wash his clothes and bathe with water, and he will be unclean until evening.

15:28

וְאִםand if טָהֲרָהcleansed מִזּוֹבָהּof her discharge she וְסָפְרָהmust count לָּהּto her שִׁבְעַתseven יָמִיםdays וְאַחַרand after תִּטְהָרceremonially clean

When a woman is cleansed of her discharge, she must count off seven days, and after that she will be ceremonially clean.

15:29

וּבַיּוֹםday הַשְּׁמִינִיthe eighth תִּקַּחtake לָהּshe is to שְׁתֵּיtwo תֹרִיםturtledoves אוֹor שְׁנֵיtwo בְּנֵיyoung יוֹנָהpigeons וְהֵבִיאָהand bring אוֹתָםthem אֶלto הַכֹּהֵןthe priest אֶלto פֶּתַחentrance אֹהֶלTent מוֹעֵדMeeting

On the eighth day she is to take two turtledoves or two young pigeons and bring them to the priest at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.

15:30

וְעָשָׂהto sacrifice הַכֹּהֵןthe priest אֶת[obj] הָאֶחָדone חַטָּאתsin offering וְאֶתand [obj] הָאֶחָדthe other עֹלָהburnt offering וְכִפֶּרwill atonement עָלֶיהָfor her הַכֹּהֵןthe priest לִפְנֵיbefore יְהוָהLORD מִזּוֹבdischarge טֻמְאָתָהּher unclean

The priest is to sacrifice one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering. In this way the priest will make atonement for her before the LORD for her unclean discharge.

15:31

וְהִזַּרְתֶּםseparate אֶת[obj] בְּנֵיchildren יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael מִטֻּמְאָתָםfrom their uncleanness וְלֹאnot יָמֻתוּdie בְּטֻמְאָתָםin their uncleanness בְּטַמְּאָםby defiling אֶת[obj] מִשְׁכָּנִיmy tabernacle אֲשֶׁרwhich בְּתוֹכָםis among them

You must keep the children of Israel separate from their uncleanness, so that they do not die by defiling My tabernacle, which is among them.

15:32

זֹאתthis תּוֹרַתlaw הַזָּבdischarge וַאֲשֶׁרwho has תֵּצֵאgo מִמֶּנּוּfrom us שִׁכְבַתemission זֶרַעsemen לְטָמְאָהunclean בָהּin her

This is the law of him who has a discharge, of the man who has an emission of semen whereby he is unclean,

15:33

וְהַדָּוָהa woman בְּנִדָּתָהּin her menstrual period וְהַזָּבwho has a discharge אֶת[obj] זוֹבוֹdischarge לַזָּכָרany male וְלַנְּקֵבָהor female וּלְאִישׁa man אֲשֶׁרwho יִשְׁכַּבlies עִםwith טְמֵאָהunclean

of a woman in her menstrual period, of any male or female who has a discharge, and of a man who lies with an unclean woman.’”
