Leviticus 12

Purification after childbirth

Open in the interlinear reader

12:1

וַיְדַבֵּרand spoke יְהוָהLORD אֶלto מֹשֶׁהMoses לֵּאמֹרsaid

Then the LORD said to Moses,

12:2

דַּבֵּרsay אֶלto בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites לֵאמֹרto saying אִשָּׁהwoman כִּיfor תַזְרִיעַpregnant וְיָלְדָהand gives birth זָכָרa son וְטָמְאָהwill be unclean שִׁבְעַתseven יָמִיםdays כִּימֵיdays נִדַּתduring דְּוֺתָהּher menstruation תִּטְמָאmenstruation

“Say to the Israelites, ‘A woman who becomes pregnant and gives birth to a son will be unclean for seven days, as she is during the days of her menstruation.

12:3

וּבַיּוֹםand on the day הַשְּׁמִינִיeighth the יִמּוֹלcircumcised בְּשַׂרflesh עָרְלָתוֹboy’s foreskin

And on the eighth day the flesh of the boy’s foreskin is to be circumcised.

12:4

וּשְׁלֹשִׁיםthirty יוֹםday וּשְׁלֹשֶׁתthree יָמִיםdays תֵּשֵׁבcontinue בִּדְמֵיpurification from טָהֳרָהbleeding בְּכָלanything קֹדֶשׁsacred לֹאnot תִגָּעtouch וְאֶלinto הַמִּקְדָּשׁthe sanctuary לֹאor תָבֹאgo עַדuntil מְלֹאתcomplete יְמֵיdays טָהֳרָהּher purification

The woman shall continue in purification from her bleeding for thirty-three days. She must not touch anything sacred or go into the sanctuary until the days of her purification are complete.

12:5

וְאִםif however נְקֵבָהhowever תֵלֵדbirth וְטָמְאָהwill be unclean שְׁבֻעַיִםweeks כְּנִדָּתָהּduring her menstruation וְשִׁשִּׁיםsixty יוֹםday וְשֵׁשֶׁתsix יָמִיםdays תֵּשֵׁבcontinue עַלin דְּמֵיblood טָהֳרָהpurification

If, however, she gives birth to a daughter, the woman will be unclean for two weeks as she is during her menstruation. Then she must continue in purification from her bleeding for sixty-six days.

12:6

וּבִמְלֹאתcomplete יְמֵיdays טָהֳרָהּher purification לְבֵןfor a son אוֹor לְבַתfor a daughter תָּבִיאbring כֶּבֶשׂlamb בֶּןold שְׁנָתוֹyear לְעֹלָהfor a burnt offering וּבֶןand a young יוֹנָהpigeon אוֹor תֹרturtledove לְחַטָּאתfor a sin offering אֶלto פֶּתַחat entrance אֹהֶלTent מוֹעֵדMeeting אֶלto הַכֹּהֵןthe priest

When the days of her purification are complete, whether for a son or for a daughter, she is to bring to the priest at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting a year-old lamb for a burnt offering and a young pigeon or a turtledove for a sin offering.

12:7

וְהִקְרִיבוֹpresent them לִפְנֵיthem before יְהוָהLORD וְכִפֶּרand make atonement עָלֶיהָfor her וְטָהֲרָהceremonially cleansed מִמְּקֹרfrom flow דָּמֶיהָblood זֹאתthis תּוֹרַתlaw הַיֹּלֶדֶתa woman birth לַזָּכָרto a male אוֹor לַנְּקֵבָהto a female

And the priest will present them before the LORD and make atonement for her; and she shall be ceremonially cleansed from her flow of blood. This is the law for a woman giving birth, whether to a male or to a female.

12:8

וְאִםbut if לֹאnot תִמְצָאfind יָדָהּshe afford דֵּיenough שֶׂהlamb וְלָקְחָהshe shall bring שְׁתֵּיtwo תֹרִיםturtledoves אוֹor שְׁנֵיtwo בְּנֵיyoung יוֹנָהpigeons אֶחָדone לְעֹלָהfor a burnt offering וְאֶחָדand the other לְחַטָּאתfor a sin offering וְכִפֶּרmake atonement עָלֶיהָfor her הַכֹּהֵןthe priest וְטָהֵרָהclean

But if she cannot afford a lamb, she shall bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons, one for a burnt offering and the other for a sin offering. Then the priest will make atonement for her, and she will be clean.’”
