Leviticus 19

Holiness in daily life and love of neighbor

Open in the interlinear reader

Then the LORD said to Moses,

19:2

דַּבֵּרspeak אֶלto כָּלwhole עֲדַתcongregation בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וְאָמַרְתָּand tell אֲלֵהֶםto them קְדֹשִׁיםholy תִּהְיוּbe כִּיbecause קָדוֹשׁholy אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיכֶםyour God

“Speak to the whole congregation of Israel and tell them: Be holy because I, the LORD your God, am holy.

19:3

Each of you must respect his mother and father, and you must keep My Sabbaths. I am the LORD your God.

19:4

אַלdo not תִּפְנוּturn אֶלto הָאֱלִילִיםidols וֵאלֹהֵיgods מַסֵּכָהmolten לֹאor תַעֲשׂוּmake לָכֶםfor yourselves אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיכֶםyour God

Do not turn to idols or make for yourselves molten gods. I am the LORD your God.

19:5

וְכִיand if תִזְבְּחוּsacrifice זֶבַחsacrifice שְׁלָמִיםpeace offering לַיהוָהto the LORD לִרְצֹנְכֶםfor your acceptance תִּזְבָּחֻהוּoffer it

When you sacrifice a peace offering to the LORD, you shall offer it for your acceptance.

19:6

בְּיוֹםon the day זִבְחֲכֶםyou sacrifice יֵאָכֵלeaten וּמִמָּחֳרָתor on the next day וְהַנּוֹתָרbut what remains עַדuntil יוֹםday הַשְּׁלִישִׁיthird בָּאֵשׁmust יִשָּׂרֵףburned

It shall be eaten on the day you sacrifice it, or on the next day; but what remains on the third day must be burned up.

19:7

וְאִםif הֵאָכֹלany יֵאָכֵלeaten בַּיּוֹםon the day הַשְּׁלִישִׁיthird it פִּגּוּלtainted הוּאwill לֹאnot יֵרָצֶהaccepted

If any of it is eaten on the third day, it is tainted and will not be accepted.

19:8

וְאֹכְלָיוwhoever eats it עֲוֺנוֹhis iniquity יִשָּׂאbear כִּיfor אֶת[obj] קֹדֶשׁholy יְהוָהLORD חִלֵּלprofaned וְנִכְרְתָהmust cut off הַנֶּפֶשׁthat person הַהִואthat מֵעַמֶּיהָfrom his people

Whoever eats it will bear his iniquity, for he has profaned what is holy to the LORD. That person must be cut off from his people.

19:9

וּבְקֻצְרְכֶםwhen you reap אֶת[obj] קְצִירharvest אַרְצְכֶםland you לֹאnot תְכַלֶּהfinish פְּאַתedges שָׂדְךָyour field לִקְצֹרto reap וְלֶקֶטgleanings קְצִירְךָyour harvest לֹאor תְלַקֵּטgather

When you reap the harvest of your land, you are not to reap to the very edges of your field or gather the gleanings of your harvest.

19:10

וְכַרְמְךָand your vineyard לֹאnot תְעוֹלֵלstrip וּפֶרֶטbare כַּרְמְךָyour vineyard לֹאor תְלַקֵּטgather לֶעָנִיfor the poor וְלַגֵּרand the foreigner תַּעֲזֹבleave אֹתָםthem אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיכֶםyour God

You must not strip your vineyard bare or gather its fallen grapes. Leave them for the poor and the foreigner. I am the LORD your God.

19:11

לֹאnot תִּגְנֹבוּsteal וְלֹאor תְכַחֲשׁוּdeceive וְלֹאnot תְשַׁקְּרוּlie אִישׁone בַּעֲמִיתוֹanother

You must not steal. You must not lie or deceive one another.

19:12

וְלֹאmust not תִשָּׁבְעוּswear בִשְׁמִיby my name לַשָּׁקֶרfalsely וְחִלַּלְתָּand so profane אֶת[obj] שֵׁםname אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD

You must not swear falsely by My name and so profane the name of your God. I am the LORD.

19:13

לֹאnot תַעֲשֹׁקdefraud אֶת[obj] רֵעֲךָyour neighbor וְלֹאand not תִגְזֹלrob לֹאnot תָלִיןwithhold פְּעֻלַּתwages שָׂכִירhired hand אִתְּךָwith you עַדuntil בֹּקֶרmorning

You must not defraud your neighbor or rob him. You must not withhold until morning the wages due a hired hand.

19:14

לֹאnot תְקַלֵּלcurse חֵרֵשׁdeaf וְלִפְנֵיbefore עִוֵּרblind לֹאor תִתֵּןplace מִכְשֹׁלstumbling block וְיָרֵאתָfear מֵּאֱלֹהֶיךָfear your God אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD

You must not curse the deaf or place a stumbling block before the blind, but you shall fear your God. I am the LORD.

19:15

לֹאnot תַעֲשׂוּdo עָוֶלpervert בַּמִּשְׁפָּטjustice לֹאnot תִשָּׂאshow פְנֵיpartiality דָלpoor וְלֹאor תֶהְדַּרfavoritism פְּנֵיface גָדוֹלrich בְּצֶדֶקfairly תִּשְׁפֹּטjudge עֲמִיתֶךָyour neighbor

You must not pervert justice; you must not show partiality to the poor or favoritism to the rich; you are to judge your neighbor fairly.

19:16

לֹאnot תֵלֵךְgo רָכִילslander בְּעַמֶּיךָamong your people you לֹאnot תַעֲמֹדendanger עַלon דַּםspreading רֵעֶךָyour neighbor אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD

You must not go about spreading slander among your people. You must not endanger the life of your neighbor. I am the LORD.

19:17

לֹאnot תִשְׂנָאharbor hatred אֶת[obj] אָחִיךָyour brother בִּלְבָבֶךָin your heart הוֹכֵחַdirectly תּוֹכִיחַrebuke אֶת[obj] עֲמִיתֶךָyour neighbor וְלֹאnot תִשָּׂאincur עָלָיוon חֵטְאguilt

You must not harbor hatred against your brother in your heart. Directly rebuke your neighbor, so that you will not incur guilt on account of him.

19:18

לֹאnot תִקֹּםrevenge וְלֹאor תִטֹּרgrudge אֶת[obj] בְּנֵיsons עַמֶּךָyour people וְאָהַבְתָּbut love לְרֵעֲךָyour neighbor כָּמוֹךָas yourself אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD

Do not seek revenge or bear a grudge against any of your people, but love your neighbor as yourself. I am the LORD.

19:19

אֶת[obj] חֻקֹּתַיmy statutes תִּשְׁמֹרוּkeep בְּהֶמְתְּךָlivestock you לֹאnot תַרְבִּיעַcrossbreed different כִּלְאַיִםtwo kinds שָׂדְךָyour fields לֹאnot תִזְרַעsow כִּלְאָיִםtwo וּבֶגֶדclothing כִּלְאַיִםtwo שַׁעַטְנֵזwear לֹאnot יַעֲלֶהseed עָלֶיךָon you

You are to keep My statutes. You shall not crossbreed two different kinds of livestock; you shall not sow your fields with two kinds of seed; and you shall not wear clothing made of two kinds of material.

19:20

וְאִישׁman כִּיif יִשְׁכַּבlies אֶתwith אִשָּׁהwoman שִׁכְבַתslave זֶרַעslave וְהִואand she שִׁפְחָהgirl נֶחֱרֶפֶתpromised לְאִישׁto another man וְהָפְדֵּהbut who לֹאnot נִפְדָּתָהredeemed אוֹor חֻפְשָׁהredeemed לֹאor נִתַּןgiven לָהּto בִּקֹּרֶתfreedom תִּהְיֶהthere לֹאnot יוּמְתוּdeath כִּיbecause לֹאnot חֻפָּשָׁהfreed

If a man lies carnally with a slave girl promised to another man but who has not been redeemed or given her freedom, there must be due punishment. But they are not to be put to death, because she had not been freed.

19:21

וְהֵבִיאhowever must bring אֶת[obj] אֲשָׁמוֹhis guilt offering לַיהוָהto the LORD אֶלto פֶּתַחentrance אֹהֶלTent מוֹעֵדMeeting אֵילram אָשָׁםguilt offering

The man, however, must bring a ram to the entrance to the Tent of Meeting as his guilt offering to the LORD.

19:22

וְכִפֶּרshall make atonement עָלָיוon his הַכֹּהֵןthe priest בְּאֵילwith the ram הָאָשָׁםthe guilt offering לִפְנֵיbehalf before יְהוָהLORD עַלfor חַטָּאתוֹsin he אֲשֶׁרhas חָטָאcommitted וְנִסְלַחforgiven לוֹto him מֵחַטָּאתוֹthe sin he אֲשֶׁרhas חָטָאcommitted

The priest shall make atonement on his behalf before the LORD with the ram of the guilt offering for the sin he has committed, and he will be forgiven the sin he has committed.

19:23

וְכִיand if תָבֹאוּenter אֶלto הָאָרֶץthe land וּנְטַעְתֶּםand plant כָּלany עֵץtree מַאֲכָלfood וַעֲרַלְתֶּםshall regard עָרְלָתוֹforbidden אֶת[obj] פִּרְיוֹfruit שָׁלֹשׁthree שָׁנִיםyears יִהְיֶהbe לָכֶםto you עֲרֵלִיםforbidden לֹאnot יֵאָכֵלeaten

When you enter the land and plant any kind of tree for food, you shall regard the fruit as forbidden. For three years it will be forbidden to you and must not be eaten.

19:24

וּבַשָּׁנָהin the year הָרְבִיעִתfourth יִהְיֶהbe כָּלall פִּרְיוֹits fruit קֹדֶשׁconsecrated הִלּוּלִיםpraise לַיהוָהto the LORD

In the fourth year all its fruit must be consecrated as a praise offering to the LORD.

19:25

וּבַשָּׁנָהbut in the year הַחֲמִישִׁתfifth תֹּאכְלוּeat אֶת[obj] פִּרְיוֹits fruit לְהוֹסִיףbe increased לָכֶםto you תְּבוּאָתוֹharvest will אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיכֶםyour God

But in the fifth year you may eat its fruit; thus your harvest will be increased. I am the LORD your God.

19:26

לֹאnot תֹאכְלוּeat עַלon הַדָּםwith blood לֹאor תְנַחֲשׁוּdivination sorcery וְלֹאmust not תְעוֹנֵנוּpractice

You must not eat anything with blood still in it. You must not practice divination or sorcery.

19:27

לֹאnot תַקִּפוּcircling פְּאַתhair רֹאשְׁכֶםyour head וְלֹאor תַשְׁחִיתcut אֵת[obj] פְּאַתedges זְקָנֶךָyour beard

You must not cut off the hair at the sides of your head or clip off the edges of your beard.

19:28

וְשֶׂרֶטbodies לָנֶפֶשׁfor the dead לֹאnot תִתְּנוּmake בִּבְשַׂרְכֶםany cuts in your וּכְתֹבֶתtattoo קַעֲקַעmarks לֹאor תִתְּנוּput בָּכֶםon yourselves אֲנִיI am יְהוָהLORD

You must not make any cuts in your bodies for the dead or put tattoo marks on yourselves. I am the LORD.

19:29

אַלnot תְּחַלֵּלdefile אֶת[obj] בִּתְּךָyour daughter לְהַזְנוֹתָהּby making her a prostitute וְלֹאor תִזְנֶהprostituted הָאָרֶץthe land וּמָלְאָהand filled הָאָרֶץthe land זִמָּהdepravity

You must not defile your daughter by making her a prostitute, or the land will be prostituted and filled with depravity.

19:30

אֶת[obj] שַׁבְּתֹתַיmy Sabbaths תִּשְׁמֹרוּkeep וּמִקְדָּשִׁיmy sanctuary תִּירָאוּreverence אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD

You must keep My Sabbaths and have reverence for My sanctuary. I am the LORD.

19:31

אַלnot תִּפְנוּturn אֶלto הָאֹבֹתmediums וְאֶלand to הַיִּדְּעֹנִיםspiritists אַלnot תְּבַקְשׁוּseek לְטָמְאָהdefiled בָהֶםby them אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיכֶםyour God

You must not turn to mediums or spiritists; do not seek them out, or you will be defiled by them. I am the LORD your God.

19:32

מִפְּנֵיpresence שֵׂיבָהelderly תָּקוּםrise וְהָדַרְתָּhonor פְּנֵיface זָקֵןaged וְיָרֵאתָand fear מֵּאֱלֹהֶיךָfear your God אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD

You are to rise in the presence of the elderly, honor the aged, and fear your God. I am the LORD.

19:33

וְכִיwhen יָגוּרresides אִתְּךָwith you גֵּרforeigner בְּאַרְצְכֶםin your land you לֹאnot תוֹנוּoppress אֹתוֹhim

When a foreigner resides with you in your land, you must not oppress him.

19:34

כְּאֶזְרָחas native born מִכֶּםfrom of you יִהְיֶהbe לָכֶםto you הַגֵּרthe foreigner הַגָּרliving אִתְּכֶםwith you וְאָהַבְתָּand love לוֹto him כָּמוֹךָas yourself כִּיfor גֵרִיםforeigners הֱיִיתֶםwere בְּאֶרֶץin the land מִצְרָיִםEgypt אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיכֶםyour God

You must treat the foreigner living among you as native-born and love him as yourself, for you were foreigners in the land of Egypt. I am the LORD your God.

19:35

לֹאnot תַעֲשׂוּdo עָוֶלuse בַּמִּשְׁפָּטdishonest בַּמִּדָּהof length בַּמִּשְׁקָלweight וּבַמְּשׂוּרָהor volume

You must not use dishonest measures of length, weight, or volume.

19:36

מֹאזְנֵיscales צֶדֶקhonest אַבְנֵיmaintain צֶדֶקrighteousness אֵיפַתephah צֶדֶקhonest וְהִיןand an hin צֶדֶקhonest יִהְיֶהbe לָכֶםto you אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיכֶםyour God אֲשֶׁרwho הוֹצֵאתִיbrought אֶתְכֶםyou מֵאֶרֶץland מִצְרָיִםEgypt

You shall maintain honest scales and weights, an honest ephah, and an honest hin. I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt.

19:37

וּשְׁמַרְתֶּםmust keep אֶת[obj] כָּלall חֻקֹּתַיmy statutes וְאֶתand [obj] כָּלall מִשְׁפָּטַיmy ordinances וַעֲשִׂיתֶםand follow אֹתָםthem אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD

You must keep all My statutes and all My ordinances and follow them. I am the LORD.”
