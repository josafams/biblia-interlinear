Leviticus 7

Laws of the guilt and peace offerings

“Now this is the law of the guilt offering, which is most holy:

7:2

The guilt offering must be slaughtered in the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered, and the priest shall sprinkle its blood on all sides of the altar.

And all the fat from it shall be offered: the fat tail, the fat that covers the entrails,

וְאֵתand [obj] שְׁתֵּיboth הַכְּלָיֹתkidneys וְאֶתand [obj] הַחֵלֶבthe fat אֲשֶׁרthat עֲלֵיהֶןon them אֲשֶׁרthat עַלon הַכְּסָלִיםthe loins וְאֶתand [obj] הַיֹּתֶרֶתthe lobe עַלon הַכָּבֵדthe liver עַלto הַכְּלָיֹתthe kidneys יְסִירֶנָּהremoved with

both kidneys with the fat on them near the loins, and the lobe of the liver, which is to be removed with the kidneys.

7:5

וְהִקְטִירshall burn on אֹתָםthem הַכֹּהֵןthe priest הַמִּזְבֵּחָהthe altar אִשֶּׁהmade by fire לַיהוָהto the LORD אָשָׁםguilt offering הוּאhe

The priest shall burn them on the altar as an offering made by fire to the LORD; it is a guilt offering.

7:6

כָּלevery זָכָרmale בַּכֹּהֲנִיםamong the priests יֹאכְלֶנּוּeat of it בְּמָקוֹםplace קָדוֹשׁholy יֵאָכֵלeaten קֹדֶשׁmost קָדָשִׁיםholy הוּאis

Every male among the priests may eat of it. It must be eaten in a holy place; it is most holy.

7:7

כַּחַטָּאתlike the sin כָּאָשָׁםoffering the same תּוֹרָהlaw אַחַתapplies לָהֶםto הַכֹּהֵןthe priest אֲשֶׁרwho יְכַפֶּרatonement בּוֹwith it לוֹto him יִהְיֶהbe

The guilt offering is like the sin offering; the same law applies to both. It belongs to the priest who makes atonement with it.

7:8

וְהַכֹּהֵןas for the priest הַמַּקְרִיבwho presents אֶת[obj] עֹלַתburnt offering אִישׁanyone עוֹרhide הָעֹלָהthe burnt offering אֲשֶׁרthat הִקְרִיבoffering לַכֹּהֵןto the priest לוֹto him יִהְיֶהbe

As for the priest who presents a burnt offering for anyone, the hide of that offering belongs to him.

7:9

וְכָלlikewise every מִנְחָהgrain offering אֲשֶׁרthat תֵּאָפֶהbaked בַּתַּנּוּרin an oven וְכָלor נַעֲשָׂהdone בַמַּרְחֶשֶׁתcooked in a pan וְעַלor on מַחֲבַתgriddle לַכֹּהֵןbelongs to the priest הַמַּקְרִיבwho presents אֹתָהּher לוֹto him תִהְיֶהbe

Likewise, every grain offering that is baked in an oven or cooked in a pan or on a griddle belongs to the priest who presents it,

7:10

וְכָלand every מִנְחָהgrain offering בְלוּלָהmixed בַשֶּׁמֶןwith oil וַחֲרֵבָהdry or לְכָלto all בְּנֵיsons אַהֲרֹןAaron תִּהְיֶהbe אִישׁman כְּאָחִיוlike his brother

and every grain offering, whether dry or mixed with oil, belongs equally to all the sons of Aaron.

7:11

וְזֹאתnow this תּוֹרַתlaw זֶבַחpeace הַשְּׁלָמִיםthe peace offering אֲשֶׁרthat יַקְרִיבpresent לַיהוָהto the LORD

Now this is the law of the peace offering that one may present to the LORD:

7:12

אִםif עַלin תּוֹדָהthanksgiving יַקְרִיבֶנּוּoffers וְהִקְרִיבoffer עַלon זֶבַחsacrifice הַתּוֹדָהthanksgiving חַלּוֹתcakes מַצּוֹתunleavened בְּלוּלֹתmixed בַּשֶּׁמֶןwith olive oil וּרְקִיקֵיwafers מַצּוֹתunleavened מְשֻׁחִיםcoated בַּשָּׁמֶןwith oil וְסֹלֶתfine flour מֻרְבֶּכֶתkneaded חַלֹּתcakes בְּלוּלֹתmixed בַּשָּׁמֶןwith oil

If he offers it in thanksgiving, then along with the sacrifice of thanksgiving he shall offer unleavened cakes mixed with olive oil, unleavened wafers coated with oil, and well-kneaded cakes of fine flour mixed with oil.

7:13

עַלon חַלֹּתcakes לֶחֶםbread חָמֵץleavened יַקְרִיבpresent קָרְבָּנוֹoffering עַלon זֶבַחpeace תּוֹדַתthanksgiving שְׁלָמָיוoffering

Along with his peace offering of thanksgiving he is to present an offering with cakes of leavened bread.

7:14

וְהִקְרִיבmust present מִמֶּנּוּfrom אֶחָדone מִכָּלof each קָרְבָּןoffering תְּרוּמָהcontribution לַיהוָהto the LORD לַכֹּהֵןbelongs to the priest הַזֹּרֵקwho sprinkles אֶת[obj] דַּםblood הַשְּׁלָמִיםthe peace offering לוֹto him יִהְיֶהbe

From the cakes he must present one portion of each offering as a contribution to the LORD. It belongs to the priest who sprinkles the blood of the peace offering.

7:15

וּבְשַׂרmeat זֶבַחsacrifice תּוֹדַתthanksgiving שְׁלָמָיוhis peace offering בְּיוֹםon the day קָרְבָּנוֹmust יֵאָכֵלeaten לֹאnot יַנִּיחַleft מִמֶּנּוּfrom us עַדuntil בֹּקֶרmorning

The meat of the sacrifice of his peace offering of thanksgiving must be eaten on the day he offers it; none of it may be left until morning.

7:16

וְאִםif however נֶדֶרvow אוֹor נְדָבָהfreewill זֶבַחsacrifice קָרְבָּנוֹoffering it בְּיוֹםon the day הַקְרִיבוֹpresents his אֶת[obj] זִבְחוֹsacrifice יֵאָכֵלeaten וּמִמָּחֳרָתon the next day וְהַנּוֹתָרbut the remainder מִמֶּנּוּfrom us יֵאָכֵלeaten

If, however, the sacrifice he offers is a vow or a freewill offering, it shall be eaten on the day he presents his sacrifice, but the remainder may be eaten on the next day.

7:17

וְהַנּוֹתָרbut any מִבְּשַׂרany meat הַזָּבַחthe sacrifice בַּיּוֹםday הַשְּׁלִישִׁיthe third בָּאֵשׁin the fire יִשָּׂרֵףburned

But any meat of the sacrifice remaining until the third day must be burned up.

7:18

וְאִםif הֵאָכֹלany יֵאָכֵלeaten מִבְּשַׂרmeat from זֶבַחpeace שְׁלָמָיוhis peace offering בַּיּוֹםon the day הַשְּׁלִישִׁיthird it לֹאnot יֵרָצֶהaccepted הַמַּקְרִיבwho presented אֹתוֹhim לֹאnot יֵחָשֵׁבcredited לוֹto פִּגּוּלabomination יִהְיֶהabomination וְהַנֶּפֶשׁand the one הָאֹכֶלֶתwho eats מִמֶּנּוּof it עֲוֺנָהּshall his iniquity תִּשָּׂאbear

If any of the meat from his peace offering is eaten on the third day, it will not be accepted. It will not be credited to the one who presented it; it shall be an abomination, and the one who eats of it shall bear his iniquity.

7:19

וְהַבָּשָׂרmeat אֲשֶׁרthat יִגַּעtouches בְּכָלanything טָמֵאunclean לֹאnot יֵאָכֵלeaten בָּאֵשׁup יִשָּׂרֵףburned וְהַבָּשָׂרany other meat כָּלanyone טָהוֹרceremonially clean יֹאכַלeat בָּשָׂרflesh

Meat that touches anything unclean must not be eaten; it is to be burned up. As for any other meat, anyone who is ceremonially clean may eat it.

7:20

וְהַנֶּפֶשׁbut if anyone אֲשֶׁרwho תֹּאכַלeats בָּשָׂרmeat מִזֶּבַחfrom peace הַשְּׁלָמִיםthe peace offering אֲשֶׁרthat לַיהוָהto the LORD וְטֻמְאָתוֹthat person עָלָיוon him וְנִכְרְתָהmust be cut off הַנֶּפֶשׁthe person הַהִואthat מֵעַמֶּיהָfrom people

But if anyone who is unclean eats meat from the peace offering that belongs to the LORD, that person must be cut off from his people.

7:21

וְנֶפֶשׁone כִּיif תִגַּעtouches בְּכָלanything טָמֵאunclean בְּטֻמְאַתuncleanness אָדָםhuman אוֹor בִּבְהֵמָהan animal טְמֵאָהunclean אוֹor בְּכָלany שֶׁקֶץdetestable טָמֵאunclean וְאָכַלand then eats מִבְּשַׂרmeat זֶבַחpeace הַשְּׁלָמִיםof the peace offering אֲשֶׁרthat לַיהוָהbelongs to the LORD וְנִכְרְתָהmust cut off הַנֶּפֶשׁthat person הַהִואthat מֵעַמֶּיהָfrom his people

If one touches anything unclean, whether human uncleanness, an unclean animal, or any unclean, detestable thing, and then eats any of the meat of the peace offering that belongs to the LORD, that person must be cut off from his people.”

7:22

וַיְדַבֵּרand spoke יְהוָהLORD אֶלto מֹשֶׁהMoses לֵּאמֹרsaid

Then the LORD said to Moses,

7:23

דַּבֵּרspeak אֶלto בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites לֵאמֹרand say כָּלany חֵלֶבfat שׁוֹרox וְכֶשֶׂבsheep וָעֵזor a goat לֹאnot תֹאכֵלוּeat

“Speak to the Israelites and say, ‘You are not to eat any of the fat of an ox, a sheep, or a goat.

7:24

וְחֵלֶבfat נְבֵלָהdead וְחֵלֶבand fat טְרֵפָהmauled יֵעָשֶׂהused לְכָלfor any other מְלָאכָהpurpose וְאָכֹלand eat לֹאnot תֹאכְלֻהוּeat it

The fat of an animal found dead or mauled by wild beasts may be used for any other purpose, but you must not eat it.

7:25

כִּיif כָּלanyone אֹכֵלeats חֵלֶבfat מִןof הַבְּהֵמָהanimal אֲשֶׁרwhich יַקְרִיבpresented מִמֶּנָּהfrom אִשֶּׁהan offering made by fire לַיהוָהto the LORD וְנִכְרְתָהmust be cut off הַנֶּפֶשׁthe one הָאֹכֶלֶתwho eats מֵעַמֶּיהָfrom his people

If anyone eats the fat of an animal from which an offering made by fire may be presented to the LORD, the one who eats it must be cut off from his people.

7:26

וְכָלany דָּםblood לֹאnot תֹאכְלוּeat בְּכֹלin any מוֹשְׁבֹתֵיכֶםyour dwellings לָעוֹףbird וְלַבְּהֵמָהor animal

You must not eat the blood of any bird or animal in any of your dwellings.

7:27

כָּלanyone נֶפֶשׁif אֲשֶׁרthat תֹּאכַלeats כָּלall דָּםblood וְנִכְרְתָהmust cut off הַנֶּפֶשׁperson הַהִואthat מֵעַמֶּיהָfrom his people

If anyone eats blood, that person must be cut off from his people.’”

7:28

וַיְדַבֵּרand spoke יְהוָהLORD אֶלto מֹשֶׁהMoses לֵּאמֹרsaid

Then the LORD said to Moses,

7:29

דַּבֵּרspeak אֶלto בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites לֵאמֹרand say הַמַּקְרִיבanyone who presents אֶת[obj] זֶבַחsacrifice שְׁלָמָיוpeace offering לַיהוָהto the LORD יָבִיאbring אֶת[obj] קָרְבָּנוֹmust it לַיהוָהLORD מִזֶּבַחsacrifice to שְׁלָמָיוhis peace offering

“Speak to the Israelites and say, ‘Anyone who presents a peace offering to the LORD must bring it as his sacrifice to the LORD.

7:30

יָדָיוown hands he תְּבִיאֶינָהbring אֵת[obj] אִשֵּׁיofferings made by fire יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] הַחֵלֶבthe fat עַלon הֶחָזֶהthe breast יְבִיאֶנּוּbring him אֵת[obj] הֶחָזֶהthe breast לְהָנִיףwave אֹתוֹhim תְּנוּפָהwave לִפְנֵיbefore יְהוָהLORD

With his own hands he is to bring the offerings made by fire to the LORD; he shall bring the fat, together with the breast, and wave the breast as a wave offering before the LORD.

7:31

וְהִקְטִירto burn הַכֹּהֵןthe priest אֶת[obj] הַחֵלֶבthe fat הַמִּזְבֵּחָהthe altar וְהָיָהand be הֶחָזֶהthe breast לְאַהֲרֹןbelongs to Aaron וּלְבָנָיוand his sons

The priest is to burn the fat on the altar, but the breast belongs to Aaron and his sons.

7:32

וְאֵתand [obj] שׁוֹקthigh הַיָּמִיןthe right תִּתְּנוּgive תְרוּמָהcontribution לַכֹּהֵןto the priest מִזִּבְחֵיfrom peace שַׁלְמֵיכֶםyour peace offering

And you are to give the right thigh to the priest as a contribution from your peace offering.

7:33

הַמַּקְרִיבwho presents אֶת[obj] דַּםblood הַשְּׁלָמִיםthe peace offering וְאֶתand [obj] הַחֵלֶבfat מִבְּנֵיson אַהֲרֹןAaron לוֹto him תִהְיֶהshall שׁוֹקthigh הַיָּמִיןthe right לְמָנָהas a portion

The son of Aaron who presents the blood and fat of the peace offering shall have the right thigh as a portion.

7:34

I have taken from the sons of Israel the breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the contribution of their peace offerings, and I have given them to Aaron the priest and his sons as a permanent portion from the sons of Israel.’”

This is the portion of the offerings made by fire to the LORD for Aaron and his sons since the day they were presented to serve the LORD as priests.

On the day they were anointed, the LORD commanded that this be given them by the sons of Israel. It is a permanent portion for the generations to come.

7:37

This is the law of the burnt offering, the grain offering, the sin offering, the guilt offering, the ordination offering, and the peace offering,

which the LORD gave Moses on Mount Sinai on the day He commanded the Israelites to present their offerings to the LORD in the Wilderness of Sinai.
