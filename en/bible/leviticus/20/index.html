And I will set My face against that man and cut him off from his people, because by giving his offspring to Molech, he has defiled My sanctuary and profaned My holy name.

And if the people of the land ever hide their eyes and fail to put to death the man who gives one of his children to Molech,

then I will set My face against that man and his family and cut off from among their people both him and all who follow him in prostituting themselves with Molech.

Whoever turns to mediums or spiritists to prostitute himself with them, I will also set My face against that person and cut him off from his people.

20:7

וְהִתְקַדִּשְׁתֶּםconsecrate yourselves therefore וִהְיִיתֶםand be קְדֹשִׁיםholy כִּיbecause אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיכֶםyour God

Consecrate yourselves, therefore, and be holy, because I am the LORD your God.

20:8

וּשְׁמַרְתֶּםkeep אֶת[obj] חֻקֹּתַיmy statutes וַעֲשִׂיתֶםand practice אֹתָםthem אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD מְקַדִּשְׁכֶםsanctifies you

And you shall keep My statutes and practice them. I am the LORD who sanctifies you.

20:9

If anyone curses his father or mother, he must be put to death. He has cursed his father or mother; his blood shall be upon him.

20:10

וְאִישׁman אֲשֶׁרthat יִנְאַףcommits adultery אֶתwith אֵשֶׁתanother wife אִישׁman’s אֲשֶׁרthat יִנְאַףcommit adultery אֶתwith אֵשֶׁתwife רֵעֵהוּhis neighbor מוֹתsurely יוּמַתdeath הַנֹּאֵףboth the adulterer וְהַנֹּאָפֶתand the adulteress

If a man commits adultery with another man’s wife—with the wife of his neighbor—both the adulterer and the adulteress must surely be put to death.

20:11

וְאִישׁif a man אֲשֶׁרthat יִשְׁכַּבlies אֶתwith אֵשֶׁתwife אָבִיוhis father’s עֶרְוַתnakedness אָבִיוhis father’s גִּלָּהuncovered מוֹתsurely יוּמְתוּdeath שְׁנֵיהֶםboth דְּמֵיהֶםblood בָּםin them

If a man lies with his father’s wife, he has uncovered his father’s nakedness. Both must surely be put to death; their blood is upon them.

20:12

וְאִישׁif a man אֲשֶׁרthat יִשְׁכַּבlies אֶתwith כַּלָּתוֹhis daughter מוֹתsurely יוּמְתוּdeath שְׁנֵיהֶםboth תֶּבֶלperversely עָשׂוּacted דְּמֵיהֶםtheir blood בָּםin them

If a man lies with his daughter-in-law, both must surely be put to death. They have acted perversely; their blood is upon them.

20:13

וְאִישׁif a man אֲשֶׁרthat יִשְׁכַּבlies אֶתwith זָכָרa man מִשְׁכְּבֵיman אִשָּׁהwoman תּוֹעֵבָהabomination עָשׂוּcommitted שְׁנֵיהֶםboth מוֹתsurely יוּמָתוּdeath דְּמֵיהֶםtheir blood בָּםin them

If a man lies with a man as with a woman, they have both committed an abomination. They must surely be put to death; their blood is upon them.

20:14

וְאִישׁif a man אֲשֶׁרthat יִקַּחmarries אֶת[obj] אִשָּׁהwoman וְאֶתand [obj] אִמָּהּher mother it זִמָּהdepraved הִואis בָּאֵשׁin the fire יִשְׂרְפוּburned אֹתוֹhim וְאֶתְהֶןand them וְלֹאno תִהְיֶהbe זִמָּהdepravity בְּתוֹכְכֶםamong you

If a man marries both a woman and her mother, it is depraved. Both he and they must be burned in the fire, so that there will be no depravity among you.

20:15

וְאִישׁif a man אֲשֶׁרthat יִתֵּןgive שְׁכָבְתּוֹcarnally בִּבְהֵמָהwith an animal מוֹתput יוּמָתdeath וְאֶתand [obj] הַבְּהֵמָהthe animal תַּהֲרֹגוּkill

If a man lies carnally with an animal, he must be put to death. And you are also to kill the animal.

20:16

וְאִשָּׁהif a woman אֲשֶׁרthat תִּקְרַבapproaches אֶלto כָּלany בְּהֵמָהanimal לְרִבְעָהto mate אֹתָהּwith it וְהָרַגְתָּmust kill אֶת[obj] הָאִשָּׁהthe woman וְאֶתand [obj] הַבְּהֵמָהthe animal מוֹתsurely יוּמָתוּput דְּמֵיהֶםtheir blood בָּםin them

If a woman approaches any animal to mate with it, you must kill both the woman and the animal. They must surely be put to death; their blood is upon them.

20:17

וְאִישׁif a man אֲשֶׁרthat יִקַּחmarries אֶת[obj] אֲחֹתוֹhis sister בַּתdaughter אָבִיוhis father אוֹor בַתdaughter אִמּוֹhis mother וְרָאָהand examine אֶת[obj] עֶרְוָתָהּsexual it וְהִיאis תִרְאֶהsee אֶת[obj] עֶרְוָתוֹrelations חֶסֶדdisgrace הוּאhe וְנִכְרְתוּmust be cut off לְעֵינֵיin the sight בְּנֵיsons עַמָּםtheir people עֶרְוַתnakedness אֲחֹתוֹhis sister גִּלָּהuncovered עֲוֺנוֹhis iniquity יִשָּׂאbear

If a man marries his sister, whether the daughter of his father or of his mother, and they have sexual relations, it is a disgrace. They must be cut off in the sight of their people. He has uncovered the nakedness of his sister; he shall bear his iniquity.

20:18

וְאִישׁif a man אֲשֶׁרthat יִשְׁכַּבlies אֶתwith אִשָּׁהwoman דָּוָהmenstruating woman וְגִלָּהexposed אֶת[obj] עֶרְוָתָהּsexual relations with her אֶת[obj] מְקֹרָהּsource of her הֶעֱרָהflow וְהִיאand she גִּלְּתָהuncovered אֶת[obj] מְקוֹרsource דָּמֶיהָher blood וְנִכְרְתוּmust be cut off שְׁנֵיהֶםboth of them מִקֶּרֶבfrom among עַמָּםtheir people

If a man lies with a menstruating woman and has sexual relations with her, he has exposed the source of her flow, and she has uncovered the source of her blood. Both of them must be cut off from among their people.

20:19

וְעֶרְוַתsexual relations אֲחוֹתsister אִמְּךָyour mother וַאֲחוֹתand sister אָבִיךָyour father לֹאnot תְגַלֵּהhave כִּיfor אֶת[obj] שְׁאֵרוֹexposing one’s own הֶעֱרָהkin עֲוֺנָםtheir iniquity יִשָּׂאוּbear

You must not have sexual relations with the sister of your mother or your father, for it is exposing one’s own kin; both shall bear their iniquity.

20:20

וְאִישׁif a man אֲשֶׁרthat יִשְׁכַּבlies אֶתwith דֹּדָתוֹuncle’s wife עֶרְוַתnakedness דֹּדוֹhis uncle גִּלָּהuncovered חֶטְאָםtheir sin יִשָּׂאוּbear עֲרִירִיםchildless יָמֻתוּdie

If a man lies with his uncle’s wife, he has uncovered the nakedness of his uncle. They will bear their sin; they shall die childless.

20:21

וְאִישׁman אֲשֶׁרthat יִקַּחmarries אֶת[obj] אֵשֶׁתwife אָחִיוbrother’s it נִדָּהimpurity הִואhe עֶרְוַתnakedness אָחִיוhis brother גִּלָּהuncovered עֲרִירִיםshall childless יִהְיוּbe

If a man marries his brother’s wife, it is an act of impurity. He has uncovered the nakedness of his brother; they shall be childless.

20:22

You are therefore to keep all My statutes and ordinances, so that the land where I am bringing you to live will not vomit you out.

You must not follow the statutes of the nations I am driving out before you. Because they did all these things, I abhorred them.

20:24

וָאֹמַרtold לָכֶםto you אַתֶּםyou תִּירְשׁוּinherit אֶת[obj] אַדְמָתָםtheir land וַאֲנִיsince I אֶתְּנֶנָּהgive it לָכֶםto you לָרֶשֶׁתas an inheritance אֹתָהּher אֶרֶץland זָבַתflowing חָלָבmilk וּדְבָשׁand honey אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיכֶםyour God אֲשֶׁרwho הִבְדַּלְתִּיset apart אֶתְכֶםyou מִןfrom הָעַמִּיםthe peoples

But I have told you that you will inherit their land, since I will give it to you as an inheritance—a land flowing with milk and honey. I am the LORD your God, who has set you apart from the peoples.

20:25

You are therefore to distinguish between clean and unclean animals and birds. Do not become contaminated by any animal or bird, or by anything that crawls on the ground; I have set these apart as unclean for you.

20:26

וִהְיִיתֶםto be לִיto me קְדֹשִׁיםholy כִּיbecause קָדוֹשׁholy אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD וָאַבְדִּלset apart אֶתְכֶםyou מִןfrom הָעַמִּיםthe nations לִהְיוֹתto be לִיto me

You are to be holy to Me because I, the LORD, am holy, and I have set you apart from the nations to be My own.

20:27

וְאִישׁman אוֹor אִשָּׁהwoman כִּיfor יִהְיֶהbe בָהֶםin them אוֹבmedium אוֹor יִדְּעֹנִיspiritist מוֹתmust surely יוּמָתוּdeath בָּאֶבֶןin the stone יִרְגְּמוּstoned אֹתָםthem דְּמֵיהֶםblood בָּםin them

A man or a woman who is a medium or spiritist must surely be put to death. They shall be stoned; their blood is upon them.’”
