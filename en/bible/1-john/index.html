1 John Ἰωάννου Αʹ

1 John is a writing of exhortation to communities shaken by the departure of a group that had begun to teach differently about Jesus (2:19). The author, who speaks as a witness of what he heard, saw and touched (1:1), offers criteria for recognizing true fellowship with God: walking in the light, acknowledging one's own sin, keeping the commandments, confessing that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh and, above all, loving the brothers. Two statements sum up the book: “God is light” (1:5) and “God is love” (4:8). The declared purpose is to give assurance to those who believe: “so that you may know that you have eternal life” (5:13).

Structure

  1. Prologue: the Word of life ch. 1

    The author proclaims what he heard, saw and touched, so that the readers may have fellowship with him, with the Father and with the Son (1:1–4).

  2. Walking in the light chs. 1–2

    God is light; whoever confesses sin is forgiven; knowing God is keeping His commandments; not loving the world; those who went out are “antichrists” (1:5–2:27).

  3. Children of God chs. 2–4

    We are children of God and will be like Him; whoever is of God practices righteousness and loves in deed and truth, unlike Cain; the spirits must be tested (2:28–4:6).

  4. God is love chs. 4–5

    Love comes from God and was shown in the sending of the Son; whoever loves God loves his brother; the faith that overcomes the world and the testimony of the Spirit, the water and the blood (4:7–5:12).

  5. Conclusion ch. 5

    The assurance of eternal life, prayer for brothers who sin and the final warning: “keep yourselves from idols” (5:13–21).

Chapters

  1. 1 The Word of life; walking in the light
  2. 2 Christ our advocate; the world and the antichrists
  3. 3 Children of God and love in practice
  4. 4 Testing the spirits; God is love
  5. 5 The faith that overcomes; testimony and eternal life
