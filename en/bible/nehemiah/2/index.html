Nehemiah 2

Nehemiah goes to Jerusalem and inspects the wall

2:1

וַיְהִיand was בְּחֹדֶשׁin the month נִיסָןNisan שְׁנַתyear עֶשְׂרִיםtwentieth לְאַרְתַּחְשַׁסְתְּאArtaxerxes הַמֶּלֶךְof king יַיִןwine לְפָנָיוset before him וָאֶשָּׂאI took אֶת[obj] הַיַּיִןthe wine וָאֶתְּנָהand gave לַמֶּלֶךְto the king וְלֹאI had never הָיִיתִיbeen רַעsad לְפָנָיוin his presence

Now in the month of Nisan, in the twentieth year of King Artaxerxes, when wine was set before him, I took the wine and gave it to the king. I had never been sad in his presence,

2:2

וַיֹּאמֶרsaid לִיto me הַמֶּלֶךְthe king מַדּוּעַwhy פָּנֶיךָyour face רָעִיםsad וְאַתָּהthough you are אֵינְךָnot חוֹלֶהill אֵיןno זֶהthis כִּיfor אִםif רֹעַsadness לֵבheart וָאִירָאwith fear הַרְבֵּהoverwhelmed מְאֹדvery

so the king said to me, “Why is your face sad, though you are not ill? This could only be sadness of the heart.” I was overwhelmed with fear

2:3

וָאֹמַרand replied לַמֶּלֶךְto the king הַמֶּלֶךְthe king לְעוֹלָםforever יִחְיֶהlive מַדּוּעַwhy לֹאnot יֵרְעוּsad פָנַיmy face אֲשֶׁרthat הָעִירthe city בֵּיתhouse קִבְרוֹתburied אֲבֹתַיmy fathers חֲרֵבָהruins וּשְׁעָרֶיהָand its gates אֻכְּלוּdestroyed בָאֵשׁby fire

and replied to the king, “May the king live forever! Why should I not be sad when the city where my fathers are buried lies in ruins, and its gates have been destroyed by fire?”

2:4

וַיֹּאמֶרreplied לִיto me הַמֶּלֶךְthe king עַלon מַהwhat זֶּהis אַתָּהyou מְבַקֵּשׁrequest וָאֶתְפַּלֵּלso I prayed אֶלto אֱלֹהֵיGod הַשָּׁמָיִםof heaven

“What is your request?” replied the king. So I prayed to the God of heaven

2:5

וָאֹמַרand answered לַמֶּלֶךְthe king אִםif עַלon הַמֶּלֶךְthe king טוֹבpleases וְאִםand if יִיטַבyour עַבְדְּךָyour servant לְפָנֶיךָyour sight I ask you אֲשֶׁרthat תִּשְׁלָחֵנִיsend me אֶלto יְהוּדָהJudah אֶלto עִירcity קִבְרוֹתwhere אֲבֹתַיmy fathers וְאֶבְנֶנָּהrebuild it

and answered the king, “If it pleases the king, and if your servant has found favor in your sight, I ask that you send me to Judah, to the city where my fathers are buried, so that I may rebuild it.”

2:6

וַיֹּאמֶרasked לִיto me הַמֶּלֶךְthe king וְהַשֵּׁגַלwith the queen יוֹשֶׁבֶתseated אֶצְלוֹbeside him עַדhow מָתַיlong יִהְיֶהwill מַהֲלָכֲךָyour journey וּמָתַיand when תָּשׁוּבreturn וַיִּיטַבso it לִפְנֵיpleased הַמֶּלֶךְthe king וַיִּשְׁלָחֵנִיto send me וָאֶתְּנָהand I set לוֹa זְמָןtime

Then the king, with the queen seated beside him, asked me, “How long will your journey take, and when will you return?” So it pleased the king to send me, and I set a time.

2:7

וָאוֹמַרalso said לַמֶּלֶךְto him אִםif עַלon הַמֶּלֶךְthe king טוֹבpleases אִגְּרוֹתletters יִתְּנוּgiven לִיto me עַלfor פַּחֲווֹתgovernors עֵבֶרwest הַנָּהָרthe Euphrates אֲשֶׁרthat יַעֲבִירוּנִיme safe passage עַדuntil אֲשֶׁרthat אָבוֹאreach אֶלto יְהוּדָהJudah

I also said to him, “If it pleases the king, may letters be given to me for the governors west of the Euphrates, so that they will grant me safe passage until I reach Judah.

2:8

וְאִגֶּרֶתletter אֶלto אָסָףAsaph שֹׁמֵרkeeper הַפַּרְדֵּסforest אֲשֶׁרthat לַמֶּלֶךְthe king’s אֲשֶׁרthat יִתֶּןgive לִיto me עֵצִיםtimber לְקָרוֹתto make beams for אֶת[obj] שַׁעֲרֵיgates הַבִּירָהthe citadel אֲשֶׁרthat לַבַּיִתto the temple וּלְחוֹמַתwall הָעִירthe city וְלַבַּיִתand for the house אֲשֶׁרthat אָבוֹאoccupy אֵלָיוto him וַיִּתֶּןgranted לִיto me הַמֶּלֶךְthe king כְּיַדgracious hand אֱלֹהַיmy God הַטּוֹבָהgracious עָלָיupon me

And may I have a letter to Asaph, keeper of the king’s forest, so that he will give me timber to make beams for the gates of the citadel to the temple, for the city wall, and for the house I will occupy.” And because the gracious hand of my God was upon me, the king granted my requests.

2:9

וָאָבוֹאthen I went אֶלto פַּחֲווֹתgovernors עֵבֶרwest הַנָּהָרthe Euphrates וָאֶתְּנָהand gave לָהֶםto them אֵת[obj] אִגְּרוֹתletters הַמֶּלֶךְthe king had וַיִּשְׁלַחalso sent עִמִּיwith me הַמֶּלֶךְthe king’s שָׂרֵיofficers חַיִלarmy וּפָרָשִׁיםand cavalry

Then I went to the governors west of the Euphrates and gave them the king’s letters. The king had also sent army officers and cavalry with me.

2:10

וַיִּשְׁמַעbut when סַנְבַלַּטSanballat הַחֹרֹנִיthe Horonite וְטוֹבִיָּהand Tobiah הָעֶבֶדthe official הָעַמֹּנִיAmmonite וַיֵּרַעand displeased לָהֶםto them רָעָהdeeply גְדֹלָהdisturbed אֲשֶׁרthat had בָּאcome אָדָםsomeone לְבַקֵּשׁto seek טוֹבָהwell לִבְנֵיto sons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites

But when Sanballat the Horonite and Tobiah the Ammonite official heard about this, they were deeply disturbed that someone had come to seek the well-being of the Israelites.

2:11

וָאָבוֹאafter I had arrived אֶלto יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem וָאֱהִיand had been שָׁםthere יָמִיםdays שְׁלֹשָׁהthree

After I had arrived in Jerusalem and had been there three days,

2:12

וָאָקוּםI set לַיְלָהnight אֲנִיI וַאֲנָשִׁיםmen מְעַטfew עִמִּיwith וְלֹאdid not הִגַּדְתִּיtell לְאָדָםanyone מָהwhat אֱלֹהַיmy God נֹתֵןlaid אֶלto לִבִּיmy heart לַעֲשׂוֹתto do לִירוּשָׁלִָםfor Jerusalem וּבְהֵמָהonly animal אֵיןno עִמִּיwith me כִּיfor אִםif הַבְּהֵמָהthe animal אֲשֶׁרwhich אֲנִיI רֹכֵבriding בָּהּin her

I set out at night with a few men. I did not tell anyone what my God had laid on my heart to do for Jerusalem. The only animal with me was the one on which I was riding.

2:13

וָאֵצְאָהso I went בְשַׁעַרgate הַגַּיאthe Valley לַיְלָהnight וְאֶלand to פְּנֵיface עֵיןeyes הַתַּנִּיןSerpent וְאֶלand to שַׁעַרgate הָאַשְׁפֹּתthe dung וָאֱהִיand been שֹׂבֵרinspected בְּחוֹמֹתthe walls יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem אֲשֶׁרthat had הֵםthey פְּרוּצִיםbroken וּשְׁעָרֶיהָand the gates that had been אֻכְּלוּdestroyed בָאֵשׁby fire

So I went out at night through the Valley Gate toward the Well of the Serpent and the Dung Gate, and I inspected the walls of Jerusalem that had been broken down and the gates that had been destroyed by fire.

2:14

וָאֶעֱבֹרthen I went אֶלto שַׁעַרgate הָעַיִןthe fountain וְאֶלand to בְּרֵכַתpool הַמֶּלֶךְthe king’s וְאֵיןno מָקוֹםroom לַבְּהֵמָהfor the animal לַעֲבֹרto get through תַּחְתָּיunder me

Then I went on to the Fountain Gate and the King’s Pool, but there was no room for the animal under me to get through;

2:15

וָאֱהִיand been עֹלֶהwent בַנַּחַלup the valley לַיְלָהnight וָאֱהִיand been שֹׂבֵרinspected בַּחוֹמָהthe wall וָאָשׁוּבthen I headed back וָאָבוֹאand reentered בְּשַׁעַרgate הַגַּיְאthe Valley וָאָשׁוּבand again

so I went up the valley by night and inspected the wall. Then I headed back and reentered through the Valley Gate.

2:16

וְהַסְּגָנִיםthe officials לֹאnot יָדְעוּknow אָנָהwhere הָלַכְתִּיgone וּמָהor what אֲנִיI עֹשֶׂהdoing וְלַיְּהוּדִיםJews וְלַכֹּהֲנִיםor priests וְלַחֹרִיםor nobles וְלַסְּגָנִיםor officials וּלְיֶתֶרor any other עֹשֵׂהMaker הַמְּלָאכָהother workers עַדuntil כֵּןso לֹאnot הִגַּדְתִּיtold

The officials did not know where I had gone or what I was doing, for I had not yet told the Jews or priests or nobles or officials or any other workers.

2:17

וָאוֹמַרthen I said אֲלֵהֶםto them אַתֶּםyou רֹאִיםsee הָרָעָהthe trouble אֲשֶׁרthat אֲנַחְנוּwe בָהּin אֲשֶׁרhave יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem חֲרֵבָהruins וּשְׁעָרֶיהָand its gates been נִצְּתוּburned בָאֵשׁin the fire לְכוּcome וְנִבְנֶהlet us rebuild אֶת[obj] חוֹמַתwall יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem וְלֹאno נִהְיֶהbe עוֹדlonger חֶרְפָּהdisgrace

Then I said to them, “You see the trouble we are in. Jerusalem lies in ruins, and its gates have been burned down. Come, let us rebuild the wall of Jerusalem, so that we will no longer be a disgrace.”

2:18

וָאַגִּידalso told לָהֶםto them אֶת[obj] יַדhand אֱלֹהַיmy God אֲשֶׁרthat הִיאis טוֹבָהgracious עָלַיupon me וְאַףand דִּבְרֵיwhat הַמֶּלֶךְthe king אֲשֶׁרhad אָמַרsaid לִיto me וַיֹּאמְרוּreplied and נָקוּםstart וּבָנִינוּrebuilding וַיְחַזְּקוּand hardened יְדֵיהֶםtheir hands לַטּוֹבָהto this good

I also told them about the gracious hand of my God upon me, and what the king had said to me. “Let us start rebuilding,” they replied, and they set their hands to this good work.

2:19

וַיִּשְׁמַעbut when סַנְבַלַּטSanballat הַחֹרֹנִיthe Horonite וְטֹבִיָּהTobiah הָעֶבֶדthe official הָעַמּוֹנִיAmmonite וְגֶשֶׁםand Geshem הָעַרְבִיthe Arab וַיַּלְעִגוּthey mocked לָנוּto us וַיִּבְזוּand ridiculed עָלֵינוּon us וַיֹּאמְרוּsaying מָהwhat הַדָּבָרheard הַזֶּהabout this אֲשֶׁרthat אַתֶּםyou עֹשִׂיםdoing הַעַלagainst הַמֶּלֶךְthe king אַתֶּםyou מֹרְדִיםrebelling

But when Sanballat the Horonite, Tobiah the Ammonite official, and Geshem the Arab heard about this, they mocked us and ridiculed us, saying, “What is this you are doing? Are you rebelling against the king?”

2:20

וָאָשִׁיבso I answered אוֹתָםthem דָּבָרword וָאוֹמַרand said לָהֶםto them אֱלֹהֵיGod הַשָּׁמַיִםof heaven הוּאis יַצְלִיחַsuccess לָנוּto us וַאֲנַחְנוּwe עֲבָדָיוhis servants נָקוּםstart וּבָנִינוּwill rebuilding וְלָכֶםand to you אֵיןno חֵלֶקportion וּצְדָקָהright וְזִכָּרוֹןor claim בִּירוּשָׁלִָםin Jerusalem

So I answered them and said, “The God of heaven is the One who will grant us success. We, His servants, will start rebuilding, but you have no portion, right, or claim in Jerusalem.”
