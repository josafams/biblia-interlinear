Nehemiah 5

Nehemiah defends the indebted poor

About that time there was a great outcry from the people and their wives against their fellow Jews.

5:2

וְיֵשׁand there אֲשֶׁרthat אֹמְרִיםsaying בָּנֵינוּwe our sons וּבְנֹתֵינוּand daughters אֲנַחְנוּwe רַבִּיםnumerous וְנִקְחָהmust get דָגָןgrain וְנֹאכְלָהeat וְנִחְיֶהand stay alive

Some were saying, “We and our sons and daughters are numerous. We must get grain in order to eat and stay alive.”

5:3

וְיֵשׁand there אֲשֶׁרthat אֹמְרִיםsaying שְׂדֹתֵינוּfields our וּכְרָמֵינוּvineyards וּבָתֵּינוּand our homes אֲנַחְנוּwe עֹרְבִיםmortgaging וְנִקְחָהto get דָגָןgrain בָּרָעָבduring the famine

Others were saying, “We are mortgaging our fields, our vineyards, and our homes to get grain during the famine.”

5:4

וְיֵשׁand there אֲשֶׁרthat אֹמְרִיםsaying לָוִינוּborrowed כֶסֶףmoney לְמִדַּתto pay הַמֶּלֶךְthe king’s שְׂדֹתֵינוּtax on our fields וּכְרָמֵינוּand vineyards

Still others were saying, “We have borrowed money to pay the king’s tax on our fields and vineyards.

5:5

וְעַתָּהand now כִּבְשַׂרlike flesh אַחֵינוּour countrymen בְּשָׂרֵנוּour flesh כִּבְנֵיהֶםchildren בָּנֵינוּchildren וְהִנֵּהand saw אֲנַחְנוּwe כֹבְשִׁיםsubjecting אֶת[obj] בָּנֵינוּour sons וְאֶתand [obj] בְּנֹתֵינוּdaughters לַעֲבָדִיםto slavery וְיֵשׁand there מִבְּנֹתֵינוּof our daughters נִכְבָּשׁוֹתenslaved וְאֵיןand no לְאֵלpowerless to redeem יָדֵנוּour hand וּשְׂדֹתֵינוּbecause our fields וּכְרָמֵינוּand vineyards belong לַאֲחֵרִיםto others

We and our children are just like our countrymen and their children, yet we are subjecting our sons and daughters to slavery. Some of our daughters are already enslaved, but we are powerless to redeem them because our fields and vineyards belong to others.”

5:6

וַיִּחַרangry לִיto me מְאֹדvery כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had שָׁמַעְתִּיheard אֶת[obj] זַעֲקָתָםtheir outcry וְאֵתand [obj] הַדְּבָרִיםthese complaints הָאֵלֶּהthese

When I heard their outcry and these complaints, I became extremely angry,

5:7

וַיִּמָּלֵךְand serious לִבִּיthought I עָלַיon me וָאָרִיבָהrebuked אֶתwith הַחֹרִיםthe nobles וְאֶתand with הַסְּגָנִיםofficials וָאֹמְרָהsaying לָהֶםto them מַשָּׁאusury אִישׁyour own בְּאָחִיוfrom own brothers אַתֶּםyou are נֹשִׁיםexacting וָאֶתֵּןso I עֲלֵיהֶםagainst them קְהִלָּהassembly גְדוֹלָהlarge

and after serious thought I rebuked the nobles and officials, saying, “You are exacting usury from your own brothers!” So I called a large assembly against them

5:8

and said, “We have done our best to buy back our Jewish brothers who were sold to foreigners, but now you are selling your own brothers, that they may be sold back to us!” But they remained silent, for they could find nothing to say.

5:9

וָאוֹמַרso I continued לֹאnot טוֹבright הַדָּבָרwhat אֲשֶׁרthat אַתֶּםyou are עֹשִׂיםdoing הֲלוֹאis not shouldn’t בְּיִרְאַתin the fear אֱלֹהֵינוּour God תֵּלֵכוּwalk מֵחֶרְפַּתreproach הַגּוֹיִםof foreign אוֹיְבֵינוּour enemies

So I continued, “What you are doing is not right. Shouldn’t you walk in the fear of our God to avoid the reproach of our foreign enemies?

5:10

וְגַםas well אֲנִיI אַחַיbrothers וּנְעָרַיand my servants have נֹשִׁיםlending בָּהֶםthe people כֶּסֶףmoney וְדָגָןand grain נַעַזְבָהchange נָּאplease אֶת[obj] הַמַּשָּׁאthis usury הַזֶּהthis

I, as well as my brothers and my servants, have been lending the people money and grain. Please, let us stop this usury.

5:11

הָשִׁיבוּrestore נָאplease לָהֶםto them כְּהַיּוֹםimmediately שְׂדֹתֵיהֶםtheir fields כַּרְמֵיהֶםvineyards זֵיתֵיהֶםolive groves וּבָתֵּיהֶםand houses וּמְאַתpercentage הַכֶּסֶףthe money וְהַדָּגָןgrain הַתִּירוֹשׁnew wine וְהַיִּצְהָרand oil אֲשֶׁרthat אַתֶּםyou נֹשִׁיםassessing בָּהֶםin them

Please restore to them immediately their fields, vineyards, olive groves, and houses, along with the percentage of the money, grain, new wine, and oil that you have been assessing them.”

5:12

וַיֹּאמְרוּreplied and נָשִׁיבrestore וּמֵהֶםfrom them לֹאnot נְבַקֵּשׁrequire כֵּןso נַעֲשֶׂהdo כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had אַתָּהyou אוֹמֵרsay וָאֶקְרָאso I summoned אֶת[obj] הַכֹּהֲנִיםthe priests וָאַשְׁבִּיעֵםoath that they לַעֲשׂוֹתwould do כַּדָּבָרwhat they had promised הַזֶּהthis

“We will restore it,” they replied, “and will require nothing more from them. We will do as you say.” So I summoned the priests and required of the nobles and officials an oath that they would do what they had promised.

5:13

גַּםalso חָצְנִיshook נָעַרְתִּיrobe וָאֹמְרָהand said כָּכָהthis יְנַעֵרshake הָאֱלֹהִיםGod אֶת[obj] כָּלevery הָאִישׁman אֲשֶׁרwho לֹאnot יָקִיםkeep אֶת[obj] הַדָּבָרthis promise הַזֶּהthis מִבֵּיתוֹout of his house וּמִיגִיעוֹand possession וְכָכָהmay such יִהְיֶהbe נָעוּרshaken וָרֵקand have nothing וַיֹּאמְרוּsaid כָלwhole הַקָּהָלthe assembly אָמֵןAmen וַיְהַלְלוּand they praised אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD וַיַּעַשׂdid הָעָםthe people כַּדָּבָרas they had promised הַזֶּהthis

I also shook out the folds of my robe and said, “May God likewise shake out of His house and possession every man who does not keep this promise. May such a man be shaken out and have nothing!” The whole assembly said, “Amen,” and they praised the LORD. And the people did as they had promised.

5:14

גַּםeven מִיּוֹםfrom the day אֲשֶׁרthat צִוָּהappointed אֹתִיme לִהְיוֹתto be פֶּחָםtheir governor בְּאֶרֶץin the land יְהוּדָהJudah מִשְּׁנַתfrom year עֶשְׂרִיםtwentieth וְעַדuntil שְׁנַתyear שְׁלֹשִׁיםthirty וּשְׁתַּיִםsecond לְאַרְתַּחְשַׁסְתְּאArtaxerxes הַמֶּלֶךְking שָׁנִיםyear years שְׁתֵּיםtwelve total עֶשְׂרֵהtwelve אֲנִיI וְאַחַיnor my brothers לֶחֶםfood הַפֶּחָהthe governor לֹאnot אָכַלְתִּיate

Furthermore, from the day King Artaxerxes appointed me to be their governor in the land of Judah, from his twentieth year until his thirty-second year (twelve years total), neither I nor my brothers ate the food allotted to the governor.

5:15

The governors before me had heavily burdened the people, taking from them bread and wine plus forty shekels of silver. Their servants also oppressed the people, but I did not do this because of my fear of God.

5:16

וְגַםand also בִּמְלֶאכֶתto the construction הַחוֹמָהthe wall הַזֹּאתthis הֶחֱזַקְתִּיrepairs וְשָׂדֶהland לֹאnot קָנִינוּacquire וְכָלand all נְעָרַיmy servants קְבוּצִיםgathered שָׁםthere עַלfor הַמְּלָאכָהthe work

Instead, I devoted myself to the construction of the wall, and all my servants were gathered there for the work; we did not acquire any land.

5:17

וְהַיְּהוּדִיםJews וְהַסְּגָנִיםand officials מֵאָה150 וַחֲמִשִּׁיםwere 150 אִישׁman וְהַבָּאִיםand the coming אֵלֵינוּto us מִןfrom הַגּוֹיִםthe nations אֲשֶׁרthat סְבִיבֹתֵינוּsurrounding עַלon שֻׁלְחָנִיmy table

There were 150 Jews and officials at my table, besides the guests from the surrounding nations.

5:18

Each day one ox, six choice sheep, and some fowl were prepared for me, and once every ten days an abundance of all kinds of wine was provided. But I did not demand the food allotted to the governor, because the burden on the people was so heavy.

5:19

זָכְרָהremember לִּיto me אֱלֹהַיmy God לְטוֹבָהfavorably כֹּלall אֲשֶׁרthat עָשִׂיתִיdone עַלfor הָעָםthis people הַזֶּהthis

Remember me favorably, O my God, for all that I have done for this people.
