Nehemiah 1

Nehemiah's prayer in Susa

1:1

דִּבְרֵיwords נְחֶמְיָהNehemiah בֶּןson חֲכַלְיָהHacaliah וַיְהִיand was בְחֹדֶשׁin the month כִּסְלֵיוChislev שְׁנַתyear עֶשְׂרִיםtwentieth וַאֲנִיwhile I הָיִיתִיwas בְּשׁוּשַׁןSusa הַבִּירָהthe citadel

These are the words of Nehemiah son of Hacaliah: In the month of Chislev, in the twentieth year, while I was in the citadel of Susa,

1:2

וַיָּבֹאand came חֲנָנִיHanani אֶחָדone מֵאַחַיof my brothers הוּאhe וַאֲנָשִׁיםwith men מִיהוּדָהfrom Judah וָאֶשְׁאָלֵםso I questioned them עַלon הַיְּהוּדִיםthe Jews הַפְּלֵיטָהthe remnant אֲשֶׁרwho had נִשְׁאֲרוּsurvived מִןfrom הַשֶּׁבִיthe exile וְעַלand on יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem

Hanani, one of my brothers, arrived with men from Judah. So I questioned them about the remnant of the Jews who had survived the exile, and also about Jerusalem.

1:3

וַיֹּאמְרוּand they told לִיto me הַנִּשְׁאָרִיםthe remnant אֲשֶׁרwho נִשְׁאֲרוּsurvived מִןfrom הַשְּׁבִיthe exile שָׁםthere בַּמְּדִינָהin the province בְּרָעָהin trouble גְדֹלָהgreat וּבְחֶרְפָּהand disgrace וְחוֹמַתwall יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem מְפֹרָצֶתbroken וּשְׁעָרֶיהָand its gates נִצְּתוּburned בָאֵשׁwith fire

And they told me, “The remnant who survived the exile are there in the province, in great trouble and disgrace. The wall of Jerusalem is broken down, and its gates are burned with fire.”

1:4

וַיְהִיand was כְּשָׁמְעִיwhen I heard אֶת[obj] הַדְּבָרִיםthese words הָאֵלֶּהthese יָשַׁבְתִּיsat וָאֶבְכֶּהand wept וָאֶתְאַבְּלָהI mourned יָמִיםdays וָאֱהִיand been צָםfasting וּמִתְפַּלֵּלand praying לִפְנֵיbefore אֱלֹהֵיGod הַשָּׁמָיִםof heaven

When I heard these words, I sat down and wept. I mourned for days, fasting and praying before the God of heaven.

1:5

וָאֹמַרthen I said אָנָּאO יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod הַשָּׁמַיִםof heaven הָאֵלGod הַגָּדוֹלgreat וְהַנּוֹרָאand awesome שֹׁמֵרkeeps הַבְּרִיתhis covenant וָחֶסֶדloving devotion לְאֹהֲבָיוlove him וּלְשֹׁמְרֵיand keep מִצְוֺתָיוhis commandments

Then I said: “O LORD, God of heaven, the great and awesome God who keeps His covenant of loving devotion with those who love Him and keep His commandments,

1:6

תְּהִיbe נָאlet אָזְנְךָyour ears קַשֶּׁבֶתattentive וְעֵינֶיךָyour eyes פְתֻוּחוֹתopen לִשְׁמֹעַto hear אֶלto תְּפִלַּתprayer עַבְדְּךָyour servant אֲשֶׁרthat אָנֹכִיI מִתְפַּלֵּלpray לְפָנֶיךָbefore you הַיּוֹםthe day יוֹמָםday וָלַיְלָהand night עַלfor בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites עֲבָדֶיךָyour servants וּמִתְוַדֶּהI confess עַלon חַטֹּאותsins בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites אֲשֶׁרhave חָטָאנוּcommitted לָךְto you וַאֲנִיboth I וּבֵיתhouse אָבִיmy father’s חָטָאנוּsinned

let Your eyes be open and Your ears attentive to hear the prayer that I, Your servant, now pray before You day and night for Your servants, the Israelites. I confess the sins that we Israelites have committed against You. Both I and my father’s house have sinned.

1:7

חֲבֹלbehaved חָבַלְנוּcorruptly לָךְto you וְלֹאand have not שָׁמַרְנוּkept אֶת[obj] הַמִּצְוֺתthe commandments וְאֶתand [obj] הַחֻקִּיםstatutes וְאֶתand [obj] הַמִּשְׁפָּטִיםordinances אֲשֶׁרthat צִוִּיתָgave אֶת[obj] מֹשֶׁהMoses עַבְדֶּךָyour servant

We have behaved corruptly against You and have not kept the commandments, statutes, and ordinances that You gave Your servant Moses.

1:8

זְכָרremember נָאplease אֶת[obj] הַדָּבָרthe word אֲשֶׁרthat צִוִּיתָcommanded אֶת[obj] מֹשֶׁהMoses עַבְדְּךָyour servant לֵאמֹרwhen said אַתֶּםyou תִּמְעָלוּunfaithful אֲנִיI אָפִיץscatter אֶתְכֶםyou בָּעַמִּיםamong the nations

Remember, I pray, the word that You commanded Your servant Moses when You said, ‘If you are unfaithful, I will scatter you among the nations,

1:9

וְשַׁבְתֶּםreturn אֵלַיto me וּשְׁמַרְתֶּםand keep מִצְוֺתַיmy commandments וַעֲשִׂיתֶםand practice אֹתָםthem אִםif יִהְיֶהbeen נִדַּחֲכֶםyour exiles בִּקְצֵהto farthest הַשָּׁמַיִםthe horizon מִשָּׁםfrom there אֲקַבְּצֵםgather them וַהֲבִיאוֹתִיםand bring them אֶלto הַמָּקוֹםthe place אֲשֶׁרhave בָּחַרְתִּיchosen לְשַׁכֵּןas a dwelling אֶת[obj] שְׁמִיmy name שָׁםthere

but if you return to Me and keep and practice My commandments, then even if your exiles have been banished to the farthest horizon, I will gather them from there and bring them to the place I have chosen as a dwelling for My Name.’

1:10

וְהֵםthey are עֲבָדֶיךָyour servants וְעַמֶּךָand your people you אֲשֶׁרyou פָּדִיתָredeemed בְּכֹחֲךָby your power הַגָּדוֹלgreat וּבְיָדְךָand hand הַחֲזָקָהmighty

They are Your servants and Your people. You redeemed them by Your great power and mighty hand.

1:11

אָנָּאO אֲדֹנָיLord תְּהִיbe נָאplease אָזְנְךָyour ear קַשֶּׁבֶתattentive אֶלto תְּפִלַּתprayer עַבְדְּךָyour servant וְאֶלand to תְּפִלַּתprayers עֲבָדֶיךָyour servants הַחֲפֵצִיםwho delight לְיִרְאָהto revere אֶת[obj] שְׁמֶךָyour name וְהַצְלִיחָהsuccess נָּאplease לְעַבְדְּךָgive your servant הַיּוֹםthis day וּתְנֵהוּand grant him לְרַחֲמִיםmercy לִפְנֵיin the sight הָאִישׁthis man הַזֶּהthis וַאֲנִיtime I הָיִיתִיwas מַשְׁקֶהcupbearer לַמֶּלֶךְto the king

O Lord, may Your ear be attentive to my prayer and to the prayers of Your servants who delight to revere Your name. Give Your servant success this day, I pray, and grant him mercy in the sight of this man.” (At that time I was the cupbearer to the king.)
