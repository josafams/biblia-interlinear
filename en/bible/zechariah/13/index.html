Zechariah 13

The fountain of cleansing and the stricken shepherd

בַּיּוֹםon that day הַהוּאa will יִהְיֶהbe מָקוֹרfountain נִפְתָּחopened לְבֵיתto the house דָּוִידDavid וּלְיֹשְׁבֵיand the people יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem לְחַטַּאתto cleanse וּלְנִדָּהand impurity

“On that day a fountain will be opened to the house of David and the people of Jerusalem, to cleanse them from sin and impurity.

13:2

וְהָיָהand be בַיּוֹםon that day הַהוּאthat נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts אַכְרִיתerase אֶת[obj] שְׁמוֹתnames הָעֲצַבִּיםthe idols מִןfrom הָאָרֶץthe land וְלֹאno יִזָּכְרוּremembered עוֹדlonger וְגַםand also אֶת[obj] הַנְּבִיאִיםthe prophets וְאֶתand [obj] רוּחַspirit הַטֻּמְאָהof impurity אַעֲבִירpass מִןfrom הָאָרֶץthe land

And on that day, declares the LORD of Hosts, I will erase the names of the idols from the land, and they will no longer be remembered. I will also remove the prophets and the spirit of impurity from the land.

13:3

וְהָיָהand be כִּיif יִנָּבֵאprophesies אִישׁanyone עוֹדstill וְאָמְרוּwill say אֵלָיוto him אָבִיוfather וְאִמּוֹand mother who him יֹלְדָיוbore לֹאnot תִחְיֶהalive כִּיbecause שֶׁקֶרfalsely דִּבַּרְתָּspoken בְּשֵׁםin the name יְהוָהLORD וּדְקָרֻהוּwhen he prophesies his אָבִיהוּfather וְאִמּוֹand mother who יֹלְדָיוbore him בְּהִנָּבְאוֹpierce him through

And if anyone still prophesies, his father and mother who bore him will say to him, ‘You shall not remain alive, because you have spoken falsely in the name of the LORD.’ When he prophesies, his father and mother who bore him will pierce him through.

13:4

וְהָיָהand be בַּיּוֹםon that day הַהוּאthat יֵבֹשׁוּashamed הַנְּבִיאִיםprophet אִישׁwho מֵחֶזְיֹנוֹof his vision בְּהִנָּבְאֹתוֹprophesies וְלֹאand will not יִלְבְּשׁוּput אַדֶּרֶתcloak שֵׂעָרhairy לְמַעַןorder כַּחֵשׁdeceive

And on that day every prophet who prophesies will be ashamed of his vision, and he will not put on a hairy cloak in order to deceive.

13:5

וְאָמַרhe will say לֹאnot נָבִיאprophet אָנֹכִיI אִישׁman עֹבֵדwork אֲדָמָהland אָנֹכִיI כִּיfor אָדָםman הִקְנַנִיpurchased מִנְּעוּרָיmy youth

He will say, ‘I am not a prophet; I work the land, for I was purchased as a servant in my youth.’

13:6

וְאָמַרsomeone asks אֵלָיוto him מָהwhat הַמַּכּוֹתthese wounds הָאֵלֶּהthese are the בֵּיןbetween יָדֶיךָyour chest וְאָמַרhe will answer אֲשֶׁרthat הֻכֵּיתִיreceived בֵּיתhouse מְאַהֲבָיmy friends

If someone asks him, ‘What are these wounds on your chest?’ he will answer, ‘These are the wounds I received in the house of my friends.’

13:7

חֶרֶבsword עוּרִיawake עַלagainst רֹעִיmy shepherd וְעַלagainst גֶּבֶרman עֲמִיתִיmy companion נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts הַךְstrike אֶת[obj] הָרֹעֶהthe shepherd וּתְפוּצֶיןָscattered הַצֹּאןthe sheep וַהֲשִׁבֹתִיturn יָדִיmy hand עַלagainst הַצֹּעֲרִיםthe little

Awake, O sword, against My Shepherd, against the man who is My Companion, declares the LORD of Hosts. Strike the Shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered, and I will turn My hand against the little ones.

13:8

וְהָיָהand be בְכָלin all הָאָרֶץthe land נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD פִּיthe שְׁנַיִםtwo בָּהּin her יִכָּרְתוּcut off יִגְוָעוּperish וְהַשְּׁלִשִׁיתbut a third יִוָּתֶרleft בָּהּin it

And in all the land, declares the LORD, two-thirds will be cut off and perish, but a third will be left in it.

13:9

וְהֵבֵאתִיwill bring אֶת[obj] הַשְּׁלִשִׁיתthis third בָּאֵשׁthrough the fire וּצְרַפְתִּיםwill refine them כִּצְרֹףlike refined אֶת[obj] הַכֶּסֶףsilver וּבְחַנְתִּיםand test them כִּבְחֹןlike test אֶת[obj] הַזָּהָבgold הוּאwill יִקְרָאcall בִשְׁמִיon my name וַאֲנִיand I אֶעֱנֶהanswer אֹתוֹhim אָמַרְתִּיsay עַמִּיmy people הוּאhe וְהוּאand they will יֹאמַרsay יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהָיour God

This third I will bring through the fire; I will refine them like silver and test them like gold. They will call on My name, and I will answer them. I will say, ‘They are My people,’ and they will say, ‘The LORD is our God.’”
