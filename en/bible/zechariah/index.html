Zechariah זְכַרְיָה

Zechariah was active together with Haggai, when the Jews who had returned from exile were rebuilding the temple. Chapters 1 to 8 contain eight night visions, full of symbols (horsemen, horns, a lampstand, a flying scroll), that announce the purification of the people and the return of God's presence to Jerusalem, as well as answers about fasting and promises of restoration. Chapters 9 to 14, undated, speak of a humble king who comes riding on a donkey's colt, of faithful and unfaithful shepherds and of the day when the LORD will be king over all the earth.

Structure

  1. Night visions chs. 1–6

    A call to repentance and eight visions about the judgment of the nations, the purification of Jerusalem and its leaders; the crown of the high priest Joshua.

  2. Fasting and the promises chs. 7–8

    A question about the fasts leads to recalling the justice required and announcing days of joy for Jerusalem.

  3. First oracle chs. 9–11

    The king who comes, the people gathered and the parable of the two staffs and the bad shepherds.

  4. Second oracle chs. 12–14

    Jerusalem attacked and defended, the mourning for the one who was pierced, the fountain of cleansing and the day of the LORD.
