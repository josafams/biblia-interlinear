Zechariah 10

The LORD gathers and strengthens His people

10:1

Ask the LORD for rain in springtime; the LORD makes the storm clouds, and He will give everyone showers of rain and crops in the field.

10:2

For idols speak deceit and diviners see illusions; they tell false dreams and offer empty comfort. Therefore the people wander like sheep, oppressed for lack of a shepherd.

10:3

“My anger burns against the shepherds, and I will punish the leaders. For the LORD of Hosts attends to His flock, the house of Judah; He will make them like His royal steed in battle.

10:4

מִמֶּנּוּfrom us פִנָּהcornerstone מִמֶּנּוּfrom Judah the יָתֵדtent peg מִמֶּנּוּfrom him קֶשֶׁתbow מִלְחָמָהbattle מִמֶּנּוּfrom us יֵצֵאgo כָלevery נוֹגֵשׂruler יַחְדָּוtogether

The cornerstone will come from Judah, the tent peg from him, as well as the battle bow and every ruler together.

10:5

וְהָיוּthey will be כְגִבֹּרִיםlike mighty men בּוֹסִיםenemy בְּטִיטin the mire חוּצוֹתstreets בַּמִּלְחָמָהin battle וְנִלְחֲמוּthey will fight כִּיbecause יְהוָהLORD עִמָּםwith them וְהֹבִישׁוּshame רֹכְבֵיput סוּסִיםhorsemen

They will be like mighty men in battle, trampling the enemy in the mire of the streets. They will fight because the LORD is with them, and they will put the horsemen to shame.

10:6

I will strengthen the house of Judah and save the house of Joseph. I will restore them because I have compassion on them, and they will be as though I had not rejected them. For I am the LORD their God, and I will answer them.

10:7

וְהָיוּwill be כְגִבּוֹרlike a mighty אֶפְרַיִםEphraim וְשָׂמַחglad לִבָּםtheir hearts כְּמוֹas יָיִןwine וּבְנֵיהֶםtheir children will יִרְאוּsee וְשָׂמֵחוּand be joyful יָגֵלrejoice לִבָּםtheir hearts בַּיהוָהin the LORD

Ephraim will be like a mighty man, and their hearts will be glad as with wine. Their children will see it and be joyful; their hearts will rejoice in the LORD.

10:8

אֶשְׁרְקָהwhistle לָהֶםfor them וַאֲקַבְּצֵםto gather כִּיfor פְדִיתִיםredeemed them וְרָבוּand numerous כְּמוֹas רָבוּnumerous

I will whistle for them to gather, for I have redeemed them; and they will be as numerous as they once were.

10:9

וְאֶזְרָעֵםthough I sow them בָּעַמִּיםamong the nations וּבַמֶּרְחַקִּיםin distant lands יִזְכְּרוּנִיremember me וְחָיוּlive אֶתwith בְּנֵיהֶםtheir children וָשָׁבוּand return

Though I sow them among the nations, they will remember Me in distant lands; they and their children will live and return.

10:10

וַהֲשִׁיבוֹתִיםwill bring them מֵאֶרֶץfrom land מִצְרַיִםEgypt וּמֵאַשּׁוּרfrom Assyria אֲקַבְּצֵםgather them וְאֶלto אֶרֶץthem גִּלְעָדGilead וּלְבָנוֹןand Lebanon אֲבִיאֵםbring them וְלֹאuntil no יִמָּצֵאfound לָהֶםfor them

I will bring them back from Egypt and gather them from Assyria. I will bring them to Gilead and Lebanon until no more room is found for them.

10:11

וְעָבַרthey will pass בַּיָּםthrough the sea צָרָהdistress וְהִכָּהand strike בַיָּםsea גַּלִּיםwaves וְהֹבִישׁוּdry כֹּלall מְצוּלוֹתdepths יְאֹרNile וְהוּרַדbrought גְּאוֹןpride אַשּׁוּרAssyria וְשֵׁבֶטand the scepter מִצְרַיִםEgypt יָסוּרdepart

They will pass through the sea of distress and strike the waves of the sea; all the depths of the Nile will dry up. The pride of Assyria will be brought down, and the scepter of Egypt will depart.

10:12

וְגִבַּרְתִּיםwill strengthen them בַּיהוָהin the LORD וּבִשְׁמוֹand in his name יִתְהַלָּכוּwalk נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD

I will strengthen them in the LORD, and in His name they will walk,” declares the LORD.
