Zechariah 14

The day of the LORD and the universal reign

הִנֵּהbehold יוֹםday בָּאcoming לַיהוָהLORD וְחֻלַּקdivided שְׁלָלֵךְplunder will be בְּקִרְבֵּךְin your presence

Behold, a day of the LORD is coming when your plunder will be divided in your presence.

14:2

For I will gather all the nations for battle against Jerusalem, and the city will be captured, the houses looted, and the women ravished. Half of the city will go into exile, but the rest of the people will not be removed from the city.

14:3

וְיָצָאgo יְהוָהLORD וְנִלְחַםto fight בַּגּוֹיִםagainst those nations הָהֵםthose כְּיוֹםlike day הִלָּחֲמוfights בְּיוֹםin the day קְרָבbattle

Then the LORD will go out to fight against those nations, as He fights in the day of battle.

14:4

On that day His feet will stand on the Mount of Olives, east of Jerusalem, and the Mount of Olives will be split in two from east to west, forming a great valley, with half the mountain moving to the north and half to the south.

14:5

וְנַסְתֶּםwill flee גֵּיאvalley הָרַיmy mountain כִּיfor יַגִּיעַit גֵּיextend הָרִיםmountains אֶלto אָצַלAzal וְנַסְתֶּםyou will flee כַּאֲשֶׁרas you נַסְתֶּםfled מִפְּנֵיfrom before הָרַעַשׁthe earthquake בִּימֵיin the days עֻזִּיָּהUzziah מֶלֶךְking יְהוּדָהJudah וּבָאcome יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהַיmy God כָּלall קְדֹשִׁיםholy עִמָּךְwith him

You will flee by My mountain valley, for it will extend to Azal. You will flee as you fled from the earthquake in the days of Uzziah king of Judah. Then the LORD my God will come, and all the holy ones with Him.

14:6

וְהָיָהand be בַּיּוֹםon that day הַהוּאthere will לֹאno יִהְיֶהbe אוֹרlight יְקָרוֹתcold וְקִפָּאוֹןor frost

On that day there will be no light, no cold or frost.

14:7

וְהָיָהwill be a יוֹםday אֶחָדone הוּאhe יִוָּדַעknown לַיהוָהto the LORD לֹאnot יוֹםday וְלֹאor לָיְלָהnight וְהָיָהand be לְעֵתwhen עֶרֶבevening יִהְיֶהbe אוֹרlight

It will be a day known only to the LORD, without day or night; but when evening comes, there will be light.

14:8

וְהָיָהand be בַּיּוֹםon that day הַהוּאwill יֵצְאוּout מַיִםwater חַיִּיםliving מִירוּשָׁלִַםfrom Jerusalem חֶצְיָםhalf אֶלto הַיָּםthe sea הַקַּדְמוֹנִיeastern וְחֶצְיָםhalf אֶלto הַיָּםthe western הָאַחֲרוֹןsea בַּקַּיִץin summer וּבָחֹרֶףand winter alike יִהְיֶהbe

And on that day living water will flow out from Jerusalem, half of it toward the Eastern Sea and the other half toward the Western Sea, in summer and winter alike.

14:9

וְהָיָהbecome יְהוָהLORD לְמֶלֶךְking עַלover כָּלall הָאָרֶץthe earth בַּיּוֹםon that day הַהוּאthe will יִהְיֶהbe יְהוָהLORD אֶחָדalone וּשְׁמוֹand his name אֶחָדalone

On that day the LORD will become King over all the earth—the LORD alone, and His name alone.

14:10

יִסּוֹבturned כָּלall הָאָרֶץthe land כָּעֲרָבָהinto a plain מִגֶּבַעfrom Geba לְרִמּוֹןto Rimmon נֶגֶבsouth יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem וְרָאֲמָהbut Jerusalem וְיָשְׁבָהand will remain תַחְתֶּיהָin her place לְמִשַּׁעַרgate בִּנְיָמִןBenjamin עַדto מְקוֹםsite שַׁעַרgate הָרִאשׁוֹןthe first עַדto שַׁעַרgate הַפִּנִּיםthe corner וּמִגְדַּלtower חֲנַנְאֵלHananel עַדto יִקְבֵיwinepresses הַמֶּלֶךְthe royal

All the land from Geba to Rimmon south of Jerusalem will be turned into a plain, but Jerusalem will be raised up and will remain in her place, from the Benjamin Gate to the site of the First Gate to the Corner Gate, and from the Tower of Hananel to the royal winepresses.

14:11

וְיָשְׁבוּwill live בָהּin her וְחֵרֶםdestruction לֹאnever יִהְיֶהutter עוֹדagain וְיָשְׁבָהdwell יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem לָבֶטַחsecurely

People will live there, and never again will there be an utter destruction. So Jerusalem will dwell securely.

14:12

וְזֹאתand this תִּהְיֶהbe הַמַּגֵּפָהthe plague אֲשֶׁרwhich יִגֹּףstrikes יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] כָּלall הָעַמִּיםthe peoples אֲשֶׁרwho צָבְאוּwarred עַלagainst יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem הָמֵקrot בְּשָׂרוֹflesh וְהוּאand he עֹמֵדstand עַלon רַגְלָיוfeet their וְעֵינָיוeyes will תִּמַּקְנָהrot בְחֹרֵיהֶןin their sockets their וּלְשׁוֹנוֹand tongues will תִּמַּקrot בְּפִיהֶםin their mouths

And this will be the plague with which the LORD strikes all the peoples who have warred against Jerusalem: Their flesh will rot while they stand on their feet, their eyes will rot in their sockets, and their tongues will rot in their mouths.

14:13

וְהָיָהand be בַּיּוֹםon that day הַהוּאthat תִּהְיֶהbe מְהוּמַתpanic יְהוָהLORD רַבָּהgreat בָּהֶםin them וְהֶחֱזִיקוּseize אִישׁeach יַדhand רֵעֵהוּanother וְעָלְתָהrise יָדוֹhand of one עַלagainst יַדhand רֵעֵהוּother

On that day a great panic from the LORD will come upon them, so that each will seize the hand of another, and the hand of one will rise against the other.

14:14

וְגַםwill also יְהוּדָהJudah תִּלָּחֵםfight בִּירוּשָׁלִָםat Jerusalem וְאֻסַּףand gathered חֵילwealth כָּלall הַגּוֹיִםthe nations סָבִיבsurrounding זָהָבgold וָכֶסֶףsilver וּבְגָדִיםand apparel לָרֹבin great abundance מְאֹדvery

Judah will also fight at Jerusalem, and the wealth of all the surrounding nations will be collected—gold, silver, and apparel in great abundance.

14:15

וְכֵןand same תִּהְיֶהwill מַגֵּפַתplague strike הַסּוּסthe horses הַפֶּרֶדand mules הַגָּמָלcamels וְהַחֲמוֹרand donkeys וְכָלand all הַבְּהֵמָהthe animals אֲשֶׁרthat יִהְיֶהbe בַּמַּחֲנוֹתin those camps הָהֵמָּהthose כַּמַּגֵּפָהlike the plague הַזֹּאתthis

And a similar plague will strike the horses and mules, camels and donkeys, and all the animals in those camps.

14:16

וְהָיָהand be כָּלall הַנּוֹתָרthe survivors מִכָּלfrom all הַגּוֹיִםthe nations הַבָּאִיםthat came עַלagainst יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem וְעָלוּwill go up מִדֵּיfrom whenever שָׁנָהyear בְשָׁנָהafter year לְהִשְׁתַּחֲוֺתto worship לְמֶלֶךְthe king יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts וְלָחֹגand to celebrate אֶת[obj] חַגfeast הַסֻּכּוֹתof Tabernacles

Then all the survivors from the nations that came against Jerusalem will go up year after year to worship the King, the LORD of Hosts, and to celebrate the Feast of Tabernacles.

14:17

וְהָיָהand be אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot יַעֲלֶהgo מֵאֵתany of the מִשְׁפְּחוֹתfamilies הָאָרֶץthe earth אֶלto יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem לְהִשְׁתַּחֲוֺתto worship לְמֶלֶךְthe king יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts וְלֹאnot עֲלֵיהֶםon them יִהְיֶהbe הַגָּשֶׁםthe rain

And should any of the families of the earth not go up to Jerusalem to worship the King, the LORD of Hosts, then the rain will not fall on them.

14:18

וְאִםand if מִשְׁפַּחַתclan מִצְרַיִםEgypt לֹאnot תַעֲלֶהgo וְלֹאand not בָאָהenter וְלֹאnot עֲלֵיהֶםon them תִּהְיֶהbe הַמַּגֵּפָהthe plague אֲשֶׁרwhich יִגֹּףstrikes יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] הַגּוֹיִםthe nations אֲשֶׁרwho לֹאnot יַעֲלוּgo לָחֹגto celebrate אֶת[obj] חַגfeast הַסֻּכּוֹתof Tabernacles

And if the people of Egypt will not go up and enter in, then the rain will not fall on them; this will be the plague with which the LORD strikes the nations who do not go up to celebrate the Feast of Tabernacles.

14:19

זֹאתthis תִּהְיֶהbe חַטַּאתpunishment מִצְרָיִםEgypt וְחַטַּאתand sin כָּלall הַגּוֹיִםthe nations אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot יַעֲלוּgo לָחֹגto celebrate אֶת[obj] חַגfeast הַסֻּכּוֹתof Tabernacles

This will be the punishment of Egypt and of all the nations that do not go up to celebrate the Feast of Tabernacles.

14:20

בַּיּוֹםon that day הַהוּאTHE will יִהְיֶהbe עַלon מְצִלּוֹתinscribed הַסּוּסthe horses קֹדֶשׁHOLY לַיהוָהTO LORD וְהָיָהand be הַסִּירוֹתthe cooking pots בְּבֵיתin the house יְהוָהLORD כַּמִּזְרָקִיםlike the sprinkling bowls לִפְנֵיbefore הַמִּזְבֵּחַthe altar

On that day, HOLY TO THE LORD will be inscribed on the bells of the horses, and the cooking pots in the house of the LORD will be like the sprinkling bowls before the altar.

14:21

וְהָיָהand be כָּלevery סִירpot בִּירוּשָׁלִַםin Jerusalem וּבִיהוּדָהJudah קֹדֶשׁholy לַיהוָהto the LORD צְבָאוֹתHosts וּבָאוּcome כָּלall הַזֹּבְחִיםwho sacrifice וְלָקְחוּand take מֵהֶםsome pots וּבִשְּׁלוּand cook בָהֶםin them וְלֹאno יִהְיֶהbe כְנַעֲנִיCanaanite עוֹדlonger בְּבֵיתin the house יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts בַּיּוֹםon that day הַהוּאthat

Indeed, every pot in Jerusalem and Judah will be holy to the LORD of Hosts, and all who sacrifice will come and take some pots and cook in them. And on that day there will no longer be a Canaanite in the house of the LORD of Hosts.
