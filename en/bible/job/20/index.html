Job 20

Zophar: the joy of the wicked is brief

Open in the interlinear reader

20:1

וַיַּעַןthen צֹפַרZophar הַנַּעֲמָתִיthe Naamathite וַיֹּאמַרreplied

Then Zophar the Naamathite replied:

20:2

לָכֵןso שְׂעִפַּיmy anxious יְשִׁיבוּנִיme answer וּבַעֲבוּרand in that חוּשִׁיturmoil בִיin me

“So my anxious thoughts compel me to answer, because of the turmoil within me.

20:3

מוּסַרrebuke כְּלִמָּתִיinsults me אֶשְׁמָעheard וְרוּחַand spirit מִבִּינָתִיmy understanding prompts a יַעֲנֵנִיreply

I have heard a rebuke that insults me, and my understanding prompts a reply.

20:4

הֲזֹאתdo you יָדַעְתָּknow מִנִּיfrom עַדantiquity מִנִּיfrom שִׂיםplaced אָדָםman עֲלֵיon אָרֶץearth

Do you not know that from antiquity, since man was placed on the earth,

20:5

כִּיfor רִנְנַתtriumph רְשָׁעִיםwicked מִקָּרוֹבhas been brief וְשִׂמְחַתand the joy חָנֵףgodless עֲדֵיuntil רָגַעmomentary

the triumph of the wicked has been brief and the joy of the godless momentary?

20:6

אִםif יַעֲלֶהarrogance לַשָּׁמַיִםreaches the heavens שִׂיאוֹhis וְרֹאשׁוֹand his head לָעָבthe clouds יַגִּיעַtouches

Though his arrogance reaches the heavens, and his head touches the clouds,

20:7

כְּגֶלֲלוֹlike his own dung לָנֶצַחforever יֹאבֵדperish רֹאָיוseen him יֹאמְרוּask אַיּוֹwhere is he

he will perish forever, like his own dung; those who had seen him will ask, ‘Where is he?’

20:8

כַּחֲלוֹםlike a dream יָעוּףfly וְלֹאnever יִמְצָאוּהוּfound he וְיֻדַּדwill be chased כְּחֶזְיוֹןlike a vision לָיְלָהnight

He will fly away like a dream, never to be found; he will be chased away like a vision in the night.

20:9

עַיִןeye שְׁזָפַתּוּsaw him וְלֹאno תוֹסִיףmore וְלֹאno עוֹדlonger תְּשׁוּרֶנּוּbehold מְקוֹמוֹplace

The eye that saw him will see him no more, and his place will no longer behold him.

20:10

בָּנָיוhis sons יְרַצּוּfavor דַלִּיםpoor וְיָדָיוown hands must תָּשֵׁבְנָהreturn אוֹנוֹhis wealth

His sons will seek the favor of the poor, for his own hands must return his wealth.

20:11

עַצְמוֹתָיוvigor מָלְאוּfills עֲלוּמָיוhis bones וְעִמּוֹwith him עַלin עָפָרdust תִּשְׁכָּבlie

The youthful vigor that fills his bones will lie down with him in the dust.

20:12

אִםif תַּמְתִּיקsweet בְּפִיוin his mouth רָעָהevil יַכְחִידֶנָּהconceals it תַּחַתunder לְשׁוֹנוֹhis tongue

Though evil is sweet in his mouth and he conceals it under his tongue,

20:13

יַחְמֹלthough עָלֶיהָon her וְלֹאand not יַעַזְבֶנָּהbear וְיִמְנָעֶנָּהand keeps it בְּתוֹךְin among חִכּוֹhis mouth

though he cannot bear to let it go and keeps it in his mouth,

20:14

לַחְמוֹfood בְּמֵעָיוin stomach his נֶהְפָּךְsours מְרוֹרַתvenom פְּתָנִיםcobras בְּקִרְבּוֹwithin him

yet in his stomach his food sours into the venom of cobras within him.

20:15

חַיִלwealth בָּלַעswallows וַיְקִאֶנּוּbut vomits it מִבִּטְנוֹfrom his stomach יוֹרִשֶׁנּוּforce אֵלGod

He swallows wealth but vomits it out; God will force it from his stomach.

20:16

רֹאשׁpoison פְּתָנִיםcobras יִינָקfangs תַּהַרְגֵהוּkill him לְשׁוֹןwill אֶפְעֶהviper

He will suck the poison of cobras; the fangs of a viper will kill him.

20:17

אַלnot יֵרֶאenjoy בִפְלַגּוֹתstreams נַהֲרֵיrivers נַחֲלֵיflowing דְּבַשׁhoney וְחֶמְאָהand cream

He will not enjoy the streams, the rivers flowing with honey and cream.

20:18

מֵשִׁיבreturn יָגָעfruit וְלֹאand not יִבְלָעlabor consuming כְּחֵילconsuming תְּמוּרָתוֹconsuming it וְלֹאand not יַעֲלֹסenjoy

He must return the fruit of his labor without consuming it; he cannot enjoy the profits of his trading.

20:19

כִּיfor רִצַּץoppressed עָזַבforsaken דַּלִּיםpoor בַּיִתhouses גָּזַלseized וְלֹאand not יִבֶנֵהוּbuild

For he has oppressed and forsaken the poor; he has seized houses he did not build.

20:20

כִּיbecause לֹאnever יָדַעsatisfied שָׁלֵוsatisfied בְּבִטְנוֹappetite בַּחֲמוּדוֹwith his treasure לֹאnot יְמַלֵּטescape

Because his appetite is never satisfied, he cannot escape with his treasure.

20:21

אֵיןnothing שָׂרִידleft לְאָכְלוֹthus עַלto כֵּןso לֹאnot יָחִילendure טוּבוֹhis prosperity

Nothing is left for him to consume; thus his prosperity will not endure.

20:22

בִּמְלֹאותfull שִׂפְקוֹmidst of his plenty יֵצֶרdistressed לוֹto him כָּלall יַדhand עָמֵלmisery תְּבוֹאֶנּוּcome upon him

In the midst of his plenty, he will be distressed; the full force of misery will come upon him.

20:23

יְהִיwhen לְמַלֵּאfilled בִטְנוֹhis stomach God יְשַׁלַּחvent בּוֹin him חֲרוֹןfury אַפּוֹfury upon him וְיַמְטֵרraining it down עָלֵימוֹon him בִּלְחוּמוֹon as he eats

When he has filled his stomach, God will vent His fury upon him, raining it down on him as he eats.

20:24

יִבְרַחflees מִנֵּשֶׁקweapon בַּרְזֶלiron תַּחְלְפֵהוּwill pierce him קֶשֶׁתarrow נְחוּשָׁהbronze

Though he flees from an iron weapon, a bronze-tipped arrow will pierce him.

20:25

שָׁלַףdrawn וַיֵּצֵאout מִגֵּוָהgleaming וּבָרָקpoint מִמְּרֹרָתוֹfrom his liver יַהֲלֹךְcome עָלָיוover him אֵמִיםterrors

It is drawn out of his back, the gleaming point from his liver. Terrors come over him.

20:26

כָּלtotal חֹשֶׁךְdarkness טָמוּןreserved לִצְפּוּנָיוreserved for תְּאָכְלֵהוּconsume him אֵשׁfire לֹאnot נֻפָּחtreasures יֵרַעdevour שָׂרִידleft בְּאָהֳלוֹin his tent

Total darkness is reserved for his treasures. A fire unfanned will consume him and devour what is left in his tent.

20:27

יְגַלּוּexpose שָׁמַיִםheavens עֲוֺנוֹhis iniquity וְאֶרֶץand the earth מִתְקוֹמָמָהrise לוֹto him

The heavens will expose his iniquity, and the earth will rise up against him.

20:28

יִגֶלremoved יְבוּלpossessions בֵּיתוֹhis house נִגָּרוֹתflowing בְּיוֹםon the day אַפּוֹGod’s wrath

The possessions of his house will be removed, flowing away on the day of God’s wrath.

20:29

זֶהthis חֵלֶקportion אָדָםman’s רָשָׁעwicked מֵאֱלֹהִיםfrom God וְנַחֲלַתinheritance אִמְרוֹappointed him מֵאֵלGod

This is the wicked man’s portion from God, the inheritance God has appointed him.”
