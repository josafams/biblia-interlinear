Job 3

Job curses the day he was born

Open in the interlinear reader

3:1

אַחֲרֵיafter כֵןthis פָּתַחopened אִיּוֹבJob אֶת[obj] פִּיהוּhis mouth וַיְקַלֵּלand cursed אֶת[obj] יוֹמוֹday of his birth

After this, Job opened his mouth and cursed the day of his birth.

3:2

וַיַּעַןand answered אִיּוֹבwhat וַיֹּאמַרhe said

And this is what he said:

3:3

יֹאבַדperish יוֹםday אִוָּלֶדbirth בּוֹin him וְהַלַּיְלָהand the night אָמַרsaid הֹרָהconceived גָבֶרboy

“May the day of my birth perish, and the night it was said, ‘A boy is conceived.’

3:4

הַיּוֹםthat day הַהוּאthat יְהִיmay חֹשֶׁךְdarkness אַלdo not יִדְרְשֵׁהוּseek him אֱלוֹהַּGod מִמָּעַלfrom above וְאַלmay no תּוֹפַעlight shine עָלָיוupon it נְהָרָהlight

If only that day had turned to darkness! May God above disregard it; may no light shine upon it.

3:5

יִגְאָלֻהוּreclaim it חֹשֶׁךְdarkness וְצַלְמָוֶתand gloom תִּשְׁכָּןsettle עָלָיוover it עֲנָנָהblackness יְבַעֲתֻהוּblackness כִּמְרִירֵיoverwhelm יוֹםday

May darkness and gloom reclaim it, and a cloud settle over it; may the blackness of the day overwhelm it.

3:6

הַלַּיְלָהthat night הַהוּאthat יִקָּחֵהוּtaken אֹפֶלdarkness אַלnot יִחַדְּappear בִּימֵיamong the days שָׁנָהyear בְּמִסְפַּרin number יְרָחִיםmonths אַלdo not יָבֹאentered

If only darkness had taken that night away! May it not appear among the days of the year; may it never be entered in any of the months.

3:7

הִנֵּהbehold הַלַּיְלָהthat night הַהוּאthat יְהִיbe גַלְמוּדbarren אַלdo not תָּבֹאcome רְנָנָהjoyful בוֹin him

Behold, may that night be barren; may no joyful voice come into it.

3:8

יִקְּבֻהוּcurse אֹרְרֵיcursed יוֹםday הָעֲתִידִיםthose prepared עֹרֵרrouse לִוְיָתָןLeviathan

May it be cursed by those who curse the day— those prepared to rouse Leviathan.

3:9

יֶחְשְׁכוּmorning כּוֹכְבֵיstars נִשְׁפּוֹdark may it יְקַוwait לְאוֹרfor daylight וָאַיִןand no וְאַלit not יִרְאֶהsee בְּעַפְעַפֵּיthe breaking שָׁחַרdawn

May its morning stars grow dark; may it wait in vain for daylight; may it not see the breaking of dawn.

3:10

כִּיfor לֹאnot סָגַרshut דַּלְתֵיdoors בִטְנִיwomb וַיַּסְתֵּרto hide עָמָלsorrow מֵעֵינָיfrom my eyes

For that night did not shut the doors of the womb to hide the sorrow from my eyes.

3:11

לָמָּהto why לֹּאnot מֵרֶחֶםat birth אָמוּתdie מִבֶּטֶןfrom the womb יָצָאתִיcame וְאֶגְוָעperish

Why did I not perish at birth; why did I not die as I came from the womb?

3:12

מַדּוּעַwhy קִדְּמוּנִיreceive me בִרְכָּיִםknees וּמַהand should שָּׁדַיִםbreasts כִּיthat אִינָקnursed

Why were there knees to receive me, and breasts that I should be nursed?

3:13

כִּיfor עַתָּהnow שָׁכַבְתִּיlying וְאֶשְׁקוֹטpeace יָשַׁנְתִּיasleep אָזthen יָנוּחַrest לִיto me

For now I would be lying down in peace; I would be asleep and at rest

3:14

עִםwith מְלָכִיםkings וְיֹעֲצֵיand counselors אָרֶץearth הַבֹּנִיםwho built חֳרָבוֹתruins לָמוֹto them

with kings and counselors of the earth, who built for themselves cities now in ruins,

3:15

אוֹor עִםwith שָׂרִיםprinces זָהָבgold לָהֶםto them הַמְמַלְאִיםwho filled בָּתֵּיהֶםtheir houses כָּסֶףsilver

or with princes who had gold, who filled their houses with silver.

3:16

אוֹor כְנֵפֶלlike a stillborn child טָמוּןhidden לֹאnot אֶהְיֶהwas כְּעֹלְלִיםchild like לֹאnever רָאוּsees אוֹרdaylight

Or why was I not hidden like a stillborn child, like an infant who never sees daylight?

3:17

שָׁםthere רְשָׁעִיםwicked חָדְלוּcease רֹגֶזraging וְשָׁםand there יָנוּחוּrest יְגִיעֵיweary כֹחַfind

There the wicked cease from raging, and there the weary find rest.

3:18

יַחַדtogether אֲסִירִיםcaptives שַׁאֲנָנוּenjoy their ease לֹאnot שָׁמְעוּhear קוֹלvoice נֹגֵשׂoppressor

The captives enjoy their ease; they do not hear the voice of the oppressor.

3:19

קָטֹןsmall וְגָדוֹלand great שָׁםthere הוּאhe וְעֶבֶדand the slave חָפְשִׁיfreed מֵאֲדֹנָיוfrom his master

Both small and great are there, and the slave is freed from his master.

3:20

לָמָּהwhy יִתֵּןgiven לְעָמֵלto the miserable אוֹרlight וְחַיִּיםand life לְמָרֵיto the bitter נָפֶשׁsoul

Why is light given to the miserable, and life to the bitter of soul,

3:21

הַמְחַכִּיםwho long לַמָּוֶתfor death וְאֵינֶנּוּthat does not וַיַּחְפְּרֻהוּand search for it מִמַּטְמוֹנִיםhidden treasure

who long for death that does not come, and search for it like hidden treasure,

3:22

הַשְּׂמֵחִיםwho rejoice אֱלֵיto גִילgreatly יָשִׂישׂוּexult כִּיwhen יִמְצְאוּfind קָבֶרgrave

who rejoice and greatly exult when they can find the grave?

3:23

לְגֶבֶרman אֲשֶׁרthat דַּרְכּוֹway is נִסְתָּרָהhidden וַיָּסֶךְwhom אֱלוֹהַּGod בַּעֲדוֹfor him

Why is life given to a man whose way is hidden, whom God has hedged in?

3:24

כִּיfor לִפְנֵיbefore לַחְמִיfood אַנְחָתִיmy groans תָבֹאcome וַיִּתְּכוּpour כַמַּיִםlike water שַׁאֲגֹתָיmy roaring

I sigh when food is put before me, and my groans pour out like water.

3:25

כִּיfor פַחַדthing פָּחַדְתִּיfeared וַיֶּאֱתָיֵנִיovertaken me וַאֲשֶׁרand what יָגֹרְתִּיdreaded יָבֹאbefallen לִיto me

For the thing I feared has overtaken me, and what I dreaded has befallen me.

3:26

לֹאnot שָׁלַוְתִּיease וְלֹאor שָׁקַטְתִּיquiet וְלֹאI have no נָחְתִּיrest וַיָּבֹאcome רֹגֶזtrouble

I am not at ease or quiet; I have no rest, for trouble has come.”
