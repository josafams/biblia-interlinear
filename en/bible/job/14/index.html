Job 14

Human frailty and the hope of a tree

Open in the interlinear reader

14:1

אָדָםman יְלוּדborn אִשָּׁהwoman קְצַרshort יָמִיםdays וּשְׂבַעand full רֹגֶזtrouble

“Man, who is born of woman, is short of days and full of trouble.

14:2

כְּצִיץlike a flower יָצָאforth וַיִּמָּלthen withers וַיִּבְרַחaway כַּצֵּלlike a fleeting shadow וְלֹאhe does not יַעֲמוֹדendure

Like a flower, he comes forth, then withers away; like a fleeting shadow, he does not endure.

14:3

אַףhow much עַלto זֶהyou פָּקַחְתָּopen עֵינֶךָyour eyes וְאֹתִיand me תָבִיאbring בְמִשְׁפָּטinto judgment עִמָּךְwith you

Do You open Your eyes to one like this? Will You bring him into judgment before You?

14:4

מִיwho יִתֵּןbring טָהוֹרclean מִטָּמֵאfrom unclean לֹאno אֶחָדone

Who can bring out clean from unclean? No one!

14:5

אִםif חֲרוּצִיםdetermined יָמָיוhis days מִסְפַּרnumber חֳדָשָׁיוhis months is אִתָּךְwith you חֻקָּיוhis statutes עָשִׂיתָset וְלֹאand not יַעֲבוֹרexceed

Since his days are determined and the number of his months is with You, and since You have set limits that he cannot exceed,

14:6

שְׁעֵהlook מֵעָלָיוfrom the him וְיֶחְדָּלrest עַדuntil יִרְצֶהenjoy כְּשָׂכִירas a hired hand יוֹמוֹhis day

look away from him and let him rest, so he can enjoy his day as a hired hand.

14:7

כִּיfor יֵשׁthere לָעֵץfor a tree תִּקְוָהhope אִםif יִכָּרֵתcut וְעוֹדagain and יַחֲלִיףsprout וְיֹנַקְתּוֹshoots will לֹאnot תֶחְדָּלfail

For there is hope for a tree: If it is cut down, it will sprout again, and its tender shoots will not fail.

14:8

אִםif יַזְקִיןold בָּאָרֶץin the ground שָׁרְשׁוֹits roots וּבֶעָפָרin the soil יָמוּתdies גִּזְעוֹits stump

If its roots grow old in the ground and its stump dies in the soil,

14:9

מֵרֵיחַat the scent מַיִםwater יַפְרִחַbud וְעָשָׂהand put קָצִירtwigs כְּמוֹlike נָטַעsapling

at the scent of water it will bud and put forth twigs like a sapling.

14:10

וְגֶבֶרbut a man יָמוּתdies וַיֶּחֱלָשׁand is laid low breathes וַיִּגְוַעhe breathes אָדָםman וְאַיּוֹand where is he

But a man dies and is laid low; he breathes his last, and where is he?

14:11

אָזְלוּdisappears מַיִםwater מִנִּיfrom יָםsea וְנָהָרand a river יֶחֱרַבparched וְיָבֵשׁand dry

As water disappears from the sea and a river becomes parched and dry,

14:12

וְאִישׁso a man שָׁכַבlies וְלֹאand does not יָקוּםrise עַדuntil בִּלְתִּיno שָׁמַיִםheavens לֹאnot יָקִיצוּawakened וְלֹאor יֵעֹרוּroused מִשְּׁנָתָםfrom sleep

so a man lies down and does not rise. Until the heavens are no more, he will not be awakened or roused from sleep.

14:13

מִיwho יִתֵּןonly בִּשְׁאוֹלin Sheol תַּצְפִּנֵנִיhide me תַּסְתִּירֵנִיhide עַדuntil שׁוּבreturn אַפֶּךָyour anger תָּשִׁיתappoint לִיfor me חֹקstatute וְתִזְכְּרֵנִיthen remember me

If only You would hide me in Sheol and conceal me until Your anger has passed! If only You would appoint a time for me and then remember me!

14:14

אִםif יָמוּתdies גֶּבֶרman הֲיִחְיֶהlive again כָּלall יְמֵיdays צְבָאִיservice I אֲיַחֵלwait עַדuntil בּוֹאcomes חֲלִיפָתִיmy renewal

When a man dies, will he live again? All the days of my hard service I will wait, until my renewal comes.

14:15

תִּקְרָאcall וְאָנֹכִיand I אֶעֱנֶךָּanswer you לְמַעֲשֵׂהdesire the work יָדֶיךָyour hands תִכְסֹףlong

You will call, and I will answer; You will desire the work of Your hands.

14:16

כִּיfor עַתָּהnow צְעָדַיmy steps תִּסְפּוֹרcount לֹאnot תִשְׁמוֹרkeep עַלon חַטָּאתִיmy sin

For then You would count my steps, but would not keep track of my sin.

14:17

חָתֻםsealed בִּצְרוֹרin a bag פִּשְׁעִיmy transgression would וַתִּטְפֹּלwould cover עַלover עֲוֺנִיmy iniquity

My transgression would be sealed in a bag, and You would cover over my iniquity.

14:18

וְאוּלָםbut as הַרmountain נוֹפֵלerodes יִבּוֹלcrumbles וְצוּרand a rock יֶעְתַּקdislodged מִמְּקֹמוֹfrom its place

But as a mountain erodes and crumbles and a rock is dislodged from its place,

14:19

אֲבָנִיםstones שָׁחֲקוּwears מַיִםwater תִּשְׁטֹףtorrents סְפִיחֶיהָwash away the עֲפַרsoil אָרֶץsoil וְתִקְוַתhope אֱנוֹשׁman’s הֶאֱבַדְתָּdestroy

as water wears away the stones and torrents wash away the soil, so You destroy a man’s hope.

14:20

תִּתְקְפֵהוּoverpower him לָנֶצַחforever וַיַּהֲלֹךְand he passes מְשַׁנֶּהchange פָנָיוhis countenance וַתְּשַׁלְּחֵהוּand send him

You forever overpower him, and he passes on; You change his countenance and send him away.

14:21

יִכְבְּדוּhonor בָנָיוhis sons וְלֹאhe does not יֵדָעknow וְיִצְעֲרוּlow וְלֹאand not יָבִיןunaware לָמוֹto them

If his sons receive honor, he does not know it; if they are brought low, he is unaware.

14:22

אַךְonly בְּשָׂרוֹhis flesh עָלָיוon him יִכְאָבpain וְנַפְשׁוֹbody and עָלָיוfor himself תֶּאֱבָלmourns

He feels only the pain of his own body and mourns only for himself.”
