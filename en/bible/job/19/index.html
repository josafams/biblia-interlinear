Job 19

Abandoned by all, Job awaits a redeemer

Open in the interlinear reader

19:1

וַיַּעַןthen answered אִיּוֹבJob וַיֹּאמַרand said

Then Job answered:

19:2

עַדhow אָנָהlong תּוֹגְיוּןtorment נַפְשִׁיyou me וּתְדַכְּאוּנַנִיand crush me בְמִלִּיםwith your words

“How long will you torment me and crush me with your words?

19:3

זֶהthis עֶשֶׂרten פְּעָמִיםtimes now תַּכְלִימוּנִיreproached me לֹאnot תֵבֹשׁוּshamelessly תַּהְכְּרוּmistreat לִיto me

Ten times now you have reproached me; you shamelessly mistreat me.

19:4

וְאַףeven אָמְנָםtruly שָׁגִיתִיastray אִתִּיwith me תָּלִיןerror מְשׁוּגָתִיconcerns me

Even if I have truly gone astray, my error concerns me alone.

19:5

אִםif אָמְנָםindeed עָלַיon me תַּגְדִּילוּexalt וְתוֹכִיחוּand use עָלַיmy against חֶרְפָּתִּיdisgrace me

If indeed you would exalt yourselves above me and use my disgrace against me,

19:6

דְּעוּunderstand אֵפוֹthen כִּיthat אֱלוֹהַּGod עִוְּתָנִיwronged me וּמְצוּדוֹand drawn his net עָלַיon me הִקִּיףencircles

then understand that it is God who has wronged me and drawn His net around me.

19:7

הֵןbehold אֶצְעַקcry out חָמָסviolence וְלֹאI get no אֵעָנֶהresponse אֲשַׁוַּעcall help וְאֵיןfor there is no מִשְׁפָּטjustice

Though I cry out, ‘Violence!’ I get no response; though I call for help, there is no justice.

19:8

אָרְחִיmy way גָדַרblocked וְלֹאand not אֶעֱבוֹרpass וְעַלand on נְתִיבוֹתַיveiled my paths חֹשֶׁךְdarkness יָשִׂיםputs

He has blocked my way so I cannot pass; He has veiled my paths with darkness.

19:9

כְּבוֹדִיmy honor מֵעָלַיfrom the me הִפְשִׁיטstripped וַיָּסַרand removed עֲטֶרֶתcrown רֹאשִׁיmy head

He has stripped me of my honor and removed the crown from my head.

19:10

יִתְּצֵנִיtears me down סָבִיבevery side וָאֵלַךְgone וַיַּסַּעhe uproots כָּעֵץlike a tree תִּקְוָתִיmy hope

He tears me down on every side until I am gone; He uproots my hope like a tree.

19:11

וַיַּחַרhis anger burns עָלַיagainst me אַפּוֹhis anger וַיַּחְשְׁבֵנִיand he counts me לוֹto him כְצָרָיוenemies

His anger burns against me, and He counts me among His enemies.

19:12

יַחַדtogether יָבֹאוּadvance גְדוּדָיוhis troops וַיָּסֹלּוּramp עָלַיagainst me דַּרְכָּםtheir way וַיַּחֲנוּand encamp סָבִיבaround לְאָהֳלִיmy tent

His troops advance together; they construct a ramp against me and encamp around my tent.

19:13

אַחַיbrothers מֵעָלַיfrom the me הִרְחִיקremoved וְיֹדְעַיmy acquaintances אַךְbut זָרוּabandoned מִמֶּנִּיfrom me

He has removed my brothers from me; my acquaintances have abandoned me.

19:14

חָדְלוּfailed קְרוֹבָיkinsmen וּמְיֻדָּעַיand my friends שְׁכֵחוּנִיforgotten me

My kinsmen have failed me, and my friends have forgotten me.

19:15

גָּרֵיguests בֵיתִיmy guests וְאַמְהֹתַיand maidservants me לְזָרas a stranger תַּחְשְׁבֻנִיcount נָכְרִיforeigner הָיִיתִיwas בְעֵינֵיהֶםin their sight

My guests and maidservants count me as a stranger; I am a foreigner in their sight.

19:16

לְעַבְדִּיI for my servant קָרָאתִיcall וְלֹאnot יַעֲנֶהanswer בְּמוֹwith פִיmy mouth אֶתְחַנֶּןimplore לוֹto him

I call for my servant, but he does not answer, though I implore him with my own mouth.

19:17

רוּחִיmy breath זָרָהrepulsive לְאִשְׁתִּיto my wife וְחַנֹּתִיloathsome לִבְנֵיto sons בִטְנִיmy womb

My breath is repulsive to my wife, and I am loathsome to my own family.

19:18

גַּםeven עֲוִילִיםlittle scorn מָאֲסוּboys בִיin me אָקוּמָהappear וַיְדַבְּרוּthey deride בִיin me

Even little boys scorn me; when I appear, they deride me.

19:19

תִּעֲבוּנִיdespise כָּלall מְתֵיbest סוֹדִיfriends me וְזֶהhave אָהַבְתִּיlove נֶהְפְּכוּturned בִיagainst me

All my best friends despise me, and those I love have turned against me.

19:20

בְּעוֹרִיmy skin וּבִבְשָׂרִיand flesh דָּבְקָהcling עַצְמִיmy bones I וָאֶתְמַלְּטָהhave escaped בְּעוֹרby the skin שִׁנָּיmy teeth

My skin and flesh cling to my bones; I have escaped by the skin of my teeth.

19:21

חָנֻּנִיpity on me חָנֻּנִיpity אַתֶּםyou רֵעָיfriends have כִּיfor יַדhand אֱלוֹהַּGod נָגְעָהstruck בִּיin me

Have pity on me, my friends, have pity, for the hand of God has struck me.

19:22

לָמָּהto why תִּרְדְּפֻנִיpersecute me כְמוֹas אֵלGod וּמִבְּשָׂרִיof my flesh לֹאnever תִשְׂבָּעוּenough

Why do you persecute me as God does? Will you never get enough of my flesh?

19:23

מִיwho יִתֵּןgive אֵפוֹthat וְיִכָּתְבוּןwere recorded מִלָּיmy words מִיwho יִתֵּןgive בַּסֵּפֶרin a book וְיֻחָקוּand inscribed

I wish that my words were recorded and inscribed in a book,

19:24

בְּעֵטby an stylus בַּרְזֶלiron וְעֹפָרֶתon lead or לָעַדchiseled בַּצּוּרin stone יֵחָצְבוּןforever

by an iron stylus on lead, or chiseled in stone forever.

19:25

וַאֲנִיbut I יָדַעְתִּיknow גֹּאֲלִיmy Redeemer חָיlives וְאַחֲרוֹןend עַלupon עָפָרearth יָקוּםstand

But I know that my Redeemer lives, and in the end He will stand upon the earth.

19:26

וְאַחַרeven after עוֹרִיmy skin נִקְּפוּdestroyed זֹאתthis וּמִבְּשָׂרִיyet in my flesh אֶחֱזֶהsee אֱלוֹהַּGod

Even after my skin has been destroyed, yet in my flesh I will see God.

19:27

אֲשֶׁרthat אֲנִיI אֶחֱזֶהsee לִּיfor וְעֵינַיmy eyes רָאוּbehold וְלֹאhim and not זָרstranger כָּלוּmy כִלְיֹתַיhow my בְּחֵקִיheart yearns within me

I will see Him for myself; my eyes will behold Him, and not as a stranger. How my heart yearns within me!

19:28

כִּיif תֹאמְרוּsay מַהwhat נִּרְדָּףpersecute לוֹto him וְשֹׁרֶשׁsince the root דָּבָרmatter נִמְצָאlies בִיwith him

If you say, ‘Let us persecute him, since the root of the matter lies with him,’

19:29

גּוּרוּthen לָכֶםto you מִפְּנֵיfrom before חֶרֶבsword כִּיbecause חֵמָהwrath עֲוֺנוֹתpunishment חָרֶבsword לְמַעַןso תֵּדְעוּןknow שַׁדּוּןis a judgment

then you should fear the sword yourselves, because wrath brings punishment by the sword, so that you may know there is a judgment.”
