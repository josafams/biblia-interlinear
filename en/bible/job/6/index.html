Job 6

Job laments his friends' lack of support

Open in the interlinear reader

6:1

וַיַּעַןand answered אִיּוֹבJob וַיֹּאמַרreplied

Then Job replied:

6:2

לוּif only שָׁקוֹלweighed יִשָּׁקֵלweighed כַּעְשִׂיmy grief וְהַוָּתִיmy calamity בְּמֹאזְנַיִםon the scales יִשְׂאוּbear יָחַדtogether

“If only my grief could be weighed and placed with my calamity on the scales.

6:3

כִּיfor עַתָּהnow מֵחוֹלoutweigh the sand יַמִּיםseas יִכְבָּדheavy עַלon כֵּןso דְּבָרַיmy words לָעוּrash

For then it would outweigh the sand of the seas— no wonder my words have been rash.

6:4

כִּיfor חִצֵּיarrows שַׁדַּיAlmighty עִמָּדִיwith me אֲשֶׁרthat חֲמָתָםtheir poison שֹׁתָהdrinks רוּחִיmy spirit בִּעוּתֵיterrors אֱלוֹהַּGod יַעַרְכוּנִיarrayed against me

For the arrows of the Almighty have pierced me; my spirit drinks in their poison; the terrors of God are arrayed against me.

6:5

הֲיִנְהַקdonkey bray פֶּרֶאwild donkey עֲלֵיover דֶשֶׁאfresh grass אִםor יִגְעֶהlow שּׁוֹרox עַלover בְּלִילוֹits fodder

Does a wild donkey bray over fresh grass, or an ox low over its fodder?

6:6

הֲיֵאָכֵלis food תָּפֵלtasteless מִבְּלִיwithout מֶלַחsalt אִםor יֶשׁthere טַעַםflavor בְּרִירin the white חַלָּמוּתegg

Is tasteless food eaten without salt, or is there flavor in the white of an egg?

6:7

מֵאֲנָהrefuses לִנְגּוֹעַto touch נַפְשִׁיmy soul הֵמָּהthey כִּדְוֵיloathsome לַחְמִיfood to me

My soul refuses to touch them; they are loathsome food to me.

6:8

מִיwho יִתֵּןonly תָּבוֹאfulfill שֶׁאֱלָתִיrequest וְתִקְוָתִיmy hope יִתֵּןgranted אֱלוֹהַּGod

If only my request were granted and God would fulfill my hope:

6:9

וְיֹאֵלand determined אֱלוֹהַּGod וִידַכְּאֵנִיto crush me יַתֵּרunleash יָדוֹhis hand וִיבַצְּעֵנִיand cut me

that God would be willing to crush me, to unleash His hand and cut me off!

6:10

וּתְהִיand be עוֹדstill נֶחָמָתִיme comfort וַאֲסַלְּדָהand joy unrelenting בְחִילָהthrough pain לֹאnot יַחְמוֹלmercy כִּיfor לֹאnot כִחַדְתִּיdenied אִמְרֵיwords קָדוֹשׁholy

It still brings me comfort, and joy through unrelenting pain, that I have not denied the words of the Holy One.

6:11

מַהwhat כֹּחִיstrength do I כִיthat אֲיַחֵלstill hope וּמַהwhat קִּצִּיmy future כִּיthat אַאֲרִיךְpatient נַפְשִׁיbe

What strength do I have, that I should still hope? What is my future, that I should be patient?

6:12

אִםif כֹּחַstrength אֲבָנִיםstone כֹּחִיmy strength אִםor בְּשָׂרִיmy flesh נָחוּשׁbronze

Is my strength like that of stone, or my flesh made of bronze?

6:13

הַאִםif אֵיןno עֶזְרָתִיhelp בִיin me וְתֻשִׁיָּהnow that success נִדְּחָהdriven מִמֶּנִּיfrom me

Is there any help within me now that success is driven from me?

6:14

לַמָּסa despairing מֵרֵעֵהוּhis friend חָסֶדkindness וְיִרְאַתfear שַׁדַּיAlmighty יַעֲזוֹבforsake

A despairing man should have the kindness of his friend, even if he forsakes the fear of the Almighty.

6:15

אַחַיmy brothers בָּגְדוּfaithless כְמוֹas נָחַלwadis כַּאֲפִיקas seasonal נְחָלִיםstreams יַעֲבֹרוּoverflow

But my brothers are as faithless as wadis, as seasonal streams that overflow,

6:16

הַקֹּדְרִיםdarkened מִנִּיof קָרַחice עָלֵימוֹon them יִתְעַלֶּםinflow שָׁלֶגmelting snow

darkened because of the ice and the inflow of melting snow,

6:17

בְּעֵתseason יְזֹרְבוּdry נִצְמָתוּchannels בְּחֻמּוֹin the heat נִדְעֲכוּvanishing מִמְּקוֹמָםfrom their place

but ceasing in the dry season and vanishing from their channels in the heat.

6:18

יִלָּפְתוּcaravans אָרְחוֹתcaravans turn דַּרְכָּםtheir routes יַעֲלוּgo בַתֹּהוּinto the wasteland וְיֹאבֵדוּand perish

Caravans turn aside from their routes; they go into the wasteland and perish.

6:19

הִבִּיטוּlook אָרְחוֹתcaravans תֵּמָאTema הֲלִיכֹתtravelers שְׁבָאSheba קִוּוּhope לָמוֹto

The caravans of Tema look for water; the travelers of Sheba hope to find it.

6:20

בֹּשׁוּthey כִּיbecause בָטָחhoped בָּאוּarrival עָדֶיהָuntil her וַיֶּחְפָּרוּconfounded

They are confounded because they had hoped; their arrival brings disappointment.

6:21

כִּיfor עַתָּהnow הֱיִיתֶםare לוֹto him תִּרְאוּsee חֲתַתterror וַתִּירָאוּafraid

For now you are of no help; you see terror, and you are afraid.

6:22

הֲכִיnot אָמַרְתִּיsaid הָבוּgive לִיto me וּמִכֹּחֲכֶםfrom your wealth שִׁחֲדוּbribe בַעֲדִיfor me

Have I ever said, ‘Give me something; offer me a bribe from your wealth;

6:23

וּמַלְּטוּנִיdeliver me מִיַּדfrom the hand צָרenemy וּמִיַּדfrom the grasp עָרִיצִיםruthless תִּפְדּוּנִיredeem me

deliver me from the hand of the enemy; redeem me from the grasp of the ruthless’?

6:24

הוֹרוּנִיteach me וַאֲנִיand I אַחֲרִישׁsilent וּמַהhow שָּׁגִיתִיerred הָבִינוּunderstand לִיto me

Teach me, and I will be silent. Help me understand how I have erred.

6:25

מַהhow נִּמְרְצוּpainful אִמְרֵיwords יֹשֶׁרhonest וּמַהbut what יּוֹכִיחַdoes הוֹכֵחַargument מִכֶּםfrom of you

How painful are honest words! But what does your argument prove?

6:26

הַלְהוֹכַחintend to correct my מִלִּיםwords תַּחְשֹׁבוּtreat וּלְרוּחַand treat as wind אִמְרֵיcry נֹאָשׁdespair

Do you intend to correct my words, and treat as wind my cry of despair?

6:27

אַףeven עַלon יָתוֹםorphan תַּפִּילוּcast וְתִכְרוּand barter עַלfor רֵיעֲכֶםyour friend

You would even cast lots for an orphan and barter away your friend.

6:28

וְעַתָּהbut now הוֹאִילוּplease פְנוּlook בִיin me וְעַלto פְּנֵיכֶםyour face אִםif אֲכַזֵּבlie

But now, please look at me. Would I lie to your face?

6:29

שֻׁבוּreconsider נָאplease אַלdo not תְּהִיbe עַוְלָהunjust וְשׁוּבוּreconsider עוֹדlonger צִדְקִיmy righteousness בָהּin her

Reconsider; do not be unjust. Reconsider, for my righteousness is at stake.

6:30

הֲיֵשׁis there בִּלְשׁוֹנִיon my tongue עַוְלָהiniquity אִםif חִכִּיmy mouth לֹאnot יָבִיןdiscern הַוּוֹתmalice

Is there iniquity on my tongue? Can my mouth not discern malice?
