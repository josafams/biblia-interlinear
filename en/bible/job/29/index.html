Job 29

Job remembers his days of honor

Open in the interlinear reader

29:1

וַיֹּסֶףand again אִיּוֹבJob שְׂאֵתcontinued מְשָׁלוֹcontinued his discourse וַיֹּאמַרand said

And Job continued his discourse:

29:2

מִיwho יִתְּנֵנִיI long כְיַרְחֵיmonths קֶדֶםgone כִּימֵיdays when אֱלוֹהַּGod יִשְׁמְרֵנִיwatched over me

“How I long for the months gone by, for the days when God watched over me,

29:3

בְּהִלּוֹin praise him נֵרוֹlamp shone עֲלֵיon רֹאשִׁיmy head לְאוֹרוֹby his light אֵלֶךְwalked חֹשֶׁךְdarkness

when His lamp shone above my head, and by His light I walked through the darkness,

29:4

כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had הָיִיתִיwas בִּימֵיin days חָרְפִּיmy prime בְּסוֹדwhen the friendship אֱלוֹהַּGod עֲלֵיon אָהֳלִיmy tent

when I was in my prime, when the friendship of God rested on my tent,

29:5

בְּעוֹדstill שַׁדַּיAlmighty עִמָּדִיwith me סְבִיבוֹתַיaround נְעָרָיchildren

when the Almighty was still with me and my children were around me,

29:6

בִּרְחֹץbathed הֲלִיכַיmy steps בְּחֵמָהin cream וְצוּרand the rock יָצוּקpoured עִמָּדִיme פַּלְגֵיstreams שָׁמֶןoil

when my steps were bathed in cream and the rock poured out for me streams of oil!

29:7

בְּצֵאתִיwhen I went שַׁעַרgate עֲלֵיon קָרֶתcity בָּרְחוֹבin the public square אָכִיןtook my מוֹשָׁבִיmy seat

When I went out to the city gate and took my seat in the public square,

29:8

רָאוּנִיsaw me נְעָרִיםyoung וְנֶחְבָּאוּand withdrew וִישִׁישִׁיםand the old קָמוּrose עָמָדוּfeet

the young men saw me and withdrew, and the old men rose to their feet.

29:9

שָׂרִיםprinces עָצְרוּrefrained בְמִלִּיםfrom speaking וְכַףhands יָשִׂימוּcovered לְפִיהֶםmouths with their

The princes refrained from speaking and covered their mouths with their hands.

29:10

קוֹלvoices נְגִידִיםofficers נֶחְבָּאוּhushed וּלְשׁוֹנָםand their tongues לְחִכָּםto the roofs of their דָּבֵקָהmouths

The voices of the nobles were hushed, and their tongues stuck to the roofs of their mouths.

29:11

כִּיfor אֹזֶןwho me שָׁמְעָהheard וַתְּאַשְּׁרֵנִיcalled me blessed וְעַיִןme רָאֲתָהsaw וַתְּעִידֵנִיcommended me

For those who heard me called me blessed, and those who saw me commended me,

29:12

כִּיbecause אֲמַלֵּטrescued עָנִיpoor מְשַׁוֵּעַcried וְיָתוֹםand the fatherless וְלֹאwho had no עֹזֵרhelper לוֹto him

because I rescued the poor who cried out and the fatherless who had no helper.

29:13

בִּרְכַּתdying אֹבֵדblessed עָלַיon me תָּבֹאcome וְלֵבheart אַלְמָנָהwidow’s אַרְנִןsing

The dying man blessed me, and I made the widow’s heart sing for joy.

29:14

צֶדֶקrighteousness לָבַשְׁתִּיput וַיִּלְבָּשֵׁנִיand it clothed me כִּמְעִילrobe וְצָנִיףand my turban מִשְׁפָּטִיjustice was my

I put on righteousness, and it clothed me; justice was my robe and my turban.

29:15

עֵינַיִםeyes הָיִיתִיserved לַעִוֵּרto the blind וְרַגְלַיִםand as feet לַפִּסֵּחַto the lame אָנִיI

I served as eyes to the blind and as feet to the lame.

29:16

אָבfather אָנֹכִיI לָאֶבְיוֹנִיםto the needy וְרִבcase לֹאnot יָדַעְתִּיknow אֶחְקְרֵהוּstranger

I was a father to the needy, and I took up the case of the stranger.

29:17

וָאֲשַׁבְּרָהI shattered מְתַלְּעוֹתfangs עַוָּלunjust וּמִשִּׁנָּיוfrom his teeth אַשְׁלִיךְsnatched טָרֶףprey

I shattered the fangs of the unjust and snatched the prey from his teeth.

29:18

וָאֹמַרso I thought עִםwith קִנִּיmy nest אֶגְוָעdie וְכַחוֹלas the sand אַרְבֶּהmultiply יָמִיםdays

So I thought: ‘I will die in my nest and multiply my days as the sand.

29:19

שָׁרְשִׁיmy roots פָתוּחַspread אֱלֵיto מָיִםwaters וְטַלand the dew יָלִיןrest nightly בִּקְצִירִיon my branches

My roots will spread out to the waters, and the dew will rest nightly on my branches.

29:20

כְּבוֹדִיmy glory חָדָשׁnew עִמָּדִיwith me וְקַשְׁתִּיand my bow בְּיָדִיin my hand תַחֲלִיףrenewed

My glory is ever new within me, and my bow is renewed in my hand.’

29:21

לִיto me שָׁמְעוּlistened וְיִחֵלּוּwith expectation וְיִדְּמוּwaiting לְמוֹsilently for עֲצָתִיmy counsel

Men listened to me with expectation, waiting silently for my counsel.

29:22

אַחֲרֵיafter דְבָרִיmy words לֹאno יִשְׁנוּspoke וְעָלֵימוֹon them תִּטֹּףdew מִלָּתִיmy speech

After my words, they spoke no more; my speech settled on them like dew.

29:23

וְיִחֲלוּthey waited כַמָּטָרas for rain לִיfor me וּפִיהֶםand their mouth פָּעֲרוּdrank לְמַלְקוֹשׁspring showers

They waited for me as for rain and drank in my words like spring showers.

29:24

אֶשְׂחַקsmiled אֲלֵהֶםto them לֹאnot יַאֲמִינוּbelieve וְאוֹרit the light פָּנַיmy countenance לֹאnot יַפִּילוּןprecious

If I smiled at them, they did not believe it; the light of my countenance was precious.

29:25

אֶבֲחַרchose דַּרְכָּםtheir course וְאֵשֵׁבand presided רֹאשׁchief וְאֶשְׁכּוֹןso I dwelt כְּמֶלֶךְas a king בַּגְּדוּדamong his troops כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had אֲבֵלִיםmourners יְנַחֵםcomforter

I chose their course and presided as chief. So I dwelt as a king among his troops, as a comforter of the mourners.
