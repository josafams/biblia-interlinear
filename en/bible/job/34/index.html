Job 34

Elihu defends the righteousness of God

Open in the interlinear reader

34:1

וַיַּעַןand answered אֱלִיהוּאElihu וַיֹּאמַרcontinued

Then Elihu continued:

34:2

שִׁמְעוּhear חֲכָמִיםwise מִלָּיmy words וְיֹדְעִיםmen הַאֲזִינוּgive ear לִיto me

“Hear my words, O wise men; give ear to me, O men of learning.

34:3

כִּיfor אֹזֶןear מִלִּיןwords תִּבְחָןtests וְחֵךְas the mouth יִטְעַםtastes לֶאֱכֹלfood

For the ear tests words as the mouth tastes food.

34:4

מִשְׁפָּטright נִבְחֲרָהchoose לָּנוּfor ourselves נֵדְעָהlearn בֵינֵינוּbetween us מַהwhat טּוֹבgood

Let us choose for ourselves what is right; let us learn together what is good.

34:5

כִּיfor אָמַרdeclared אִיּוֹבJob צָדַקְתִּיrighteous וְאֵלyet God הֵסִירdeprived מִשְׁפָּטִיme of justice

For Job has declared, ‘I am righteous, yet God has deprived me of justice.

34:6

עַלon מִשְׁפָּטִיmy case my אֲכַזֵּבwound אָנוּשׁincurable חִצִּיthough I am בְלִיwithout פָשַׁעtransgression

Would I lie about my case? My wound is incurable, though I am without transgression.’

34:7

מִיwhat גֶבֶרman כְּאִיּוֹבlike Job יִשְׁתֶּהdrinks לַּעַגderision כַּמָּיִםlike water

What man is like Job, who drinks up derision like water?

34:8

וְאָרַחhe keeps לְחֶבְרָהcompany עִםwith פֹּעֲלֵיevildoers אָוֶןevildoers וְלָלֶכֶתand walks עִםwith אַנְשֵׁיmen רֶשַׁעwicked

He keeps company with evildoers and walks with wicked men.

34:9

כִּיfor אָמַרsaid לֹאnot יִסְכָּןprofits גָּבֶרman בִּרְצֹתוֹhe should delight עִםwith אֱלֹהִיםGod

For he has said, ‘It profits a man nothing that he should delight in God.’

34:10

לָכֵןtherefore אַנֲשֵׁיmen לֵבָבunderstanding שִׁמְעוּlisten לִיto me חָלִלָהfar לָאֵלbe it from God מֵרֶשַׁעwrong וְשַׁדַּיAlmighty מֵעָוֶלto act unjustly

Therefore listen to me, O men of understanding. Far be it from God to do wrong, and from the Almighty to act unjustly.

34:11

כִּיfor פֹעַלdeeds אָדָםman’s יְשַׁלֶּםrepays לוֹto him וּכְאֹרַחways אִישׁman’s יַמְצִאֶנּוּhe brings

For according to a man’s deeds He repays him; according to a man’s ways He brings consequences.

34:12

אַףindeed אָמְנָםtrue אֵלGod לֹאnot יַרְשִׁיעַwickedly וְשַׁדַּיand the Almighty לֹאnot יְעַוֵּתpervert מִשְׁפָּטjustice

Indeed, it is true that God does not act wickedly, and the Almighty does not pervert justice.

34:13

מִיwho פָקַדcharge עָלָיוhim over אָרְצָהearth וּמִיwho שָׂםappointed תֵּבֵלworld כֻּלָּהּwhole

Who gave Him charge over the earth? Who appointed Him over the whole world?

34:14

אִםif יָשִׂיםset אֵלָיוto his לִבּוֹheart רוּחוֹspirit וְנִשְׁמָתוֹand breath אֵלָיוto it יֶאֱסֹףwithdraw

If He were to set His heart to it and withdraw His Spirit and breath,

34:15

יִגְוַעperish כָּלall בָּשָׂרflesh יָחַדtogether וְאָדָםand mankind עַלon עָפָרdust יָשׁוּבreturn

all flesh would perish together and mankind would return to the dust.

34:16

וְאִםif בִּינָהunderstanding שִׁמְעָהhear זֹּאתthis הַאֲזִינָהlisten לְקוֹלto voice מִלָּיmy words

If you have understanding, hear this; listen to my words.

34:17

הַאַףreally שׂוֹנֵאhates מִשְׁפָּטjustice יַחֲבוֹשׁgovern וְאִםand if צַדִּיקjust כַּבִּירmighty תַּרְשִׁיעַcondemn

Could one who hates justice govern? Will you condemn the just and mighty One,

34:18

הַאֲמֹרwho says לְמֶלֶךְto kings בְּלִיָּעַלworthless רָשָׁעwicked אֶלto נְדִיבִיםnobles

who says to kings, ‘You are worthless!’ and to nobles, ‘You are wicked,’

34:19

אֲשֶׁרwho לֹאnot נָשָׂאcarried פְּנֵיpartial שָׂרִיםprinces וְלֹאand does not נִכַּרfavor שׁוֹעַrich לִפְנֵיto before דָלpoor כִּיfor מַעֲשֵׂהwork יָדָיוhis hands כֻּלָּםall

who is not partial to princes and does not favor rich over poor? For they are all the work of His hands.

34:20

רֶגַעinstant יָמֻתוּdie וַחֲצוֹתin the middle לָיְלָהnight יְגֹעֲשׁוּconvulse עָםpeople וְיַעֲבֹרוּand pass וְיָסִירוּaway אַבִּירmighty לֹאnot בְיָדhuman hand

They die in an instant, in the middle of the night. The people convulse and pass away; the mighty are removed without human hand.

34:21

כִּיfor עֵינָיוhis eyes עַלon דַּרְכֵיways אִישׁman וְכָלevery צְעָדָיוhis step יִרְאֶהsees

For His eyes are on the ways of a man, and He sees his every step.

34:22

אֵיןno חֹשֶׁךְdarkness וְאֵיןand no צַלְמָוֶתdeep shadow לְהִסָּתֶרcan hide שָׁםwhere פֹּעֲלֵיworkers אָוֶןiniquity

There is no darkness or deep shadow where the workers of iniquity can hide.

34:23

כִּיfor לֹאnot עַלon אִישׁman יָשִׂיםputs עוֹדlonger לַהֲלֹךְor have אֶלto אֵלGod בַּמִּשְׁפָּטfor judgment

For God need not examine a man further or have him approach for judgment.

34:24

יָרֹעַshatters כַּבִּירִיםmighty לֹאnot חֵקֶרinquiry וַיַּעֲמֵדand sets אֲחֵרִיםothers תַּחְתָּםin their place

He shatters the mighty without inquiry and sets up others in their place.

34:25

לָכֵןtherefore יַכִּירrecognizes מַעְבָּדֵיהֶםoverthrows וְהָפַךְand turned לַיְלָהnight וְיִדַּכָּאוּcrushed

Therefore, He recognizes their deeds; He overthrows them in the night and they are crushed.

34:26

תַּחַתfor רְשָׁעִיםstrikes סְפָקָםwickedness בִּמְקוֹםin full רֹאִיםview

He strikes them for their wickedness in full view,

34:27

אֲשֶׁרthat עַלon כֵּןso סָרוּturned aside מֵאַחֲרָיוfrom following him וְכָלany דְּרָכָיוhis ways לֹאno הִשְׂכִּילוּregard

because they turned aside from Him and had no regard for any of His ways.

34:28

לְהָבִיאto come עָלָיוon him צַעֲקַתcry דָּלpoor וְצַעֲקַתoutcry עֲנִיִּיםafflicted יִשְׁמָעheard

They caused the cry of the poor to come before Him, and He heard the outcry of the afflicted.

34:29

וְהוּאbut when he יַשְׁקִטsilent וּמִיand can יַרְשִׁעַcondemn וְיַסְתֵּרhim when he hides פָּנִיםface וּמִיand can יְשׁוּרֶנּוּsee him וְעַלand over גּוֹיnation וְעַלand on אָדָםman יָחַדtogether

But when He remains silent, who can condemn Him? When He hides His face, who can see Him? Yet He watches over both man and nation,

34:30

מִמְּלֹךְnot rule אָדָםmen חָנֵףgodless מִמֹּקְשֵׁיor lay snares עָםpeople

that godless men should not rule or lay snares for the people.

34:31

כִּיfor אֶלto אֵלGod הֶאָמַרI נָשָׂאתִיendured לֹאno אֶחְבֹּלoffend more

Suppose someone says to God, ‘I have endured my punishment; I will offend no more.

34:32

בִּלְעֲדֵיcannot אֶחֱזֶהsee אַתָּהyou הֹרֵנִיteach me אִםif עָוֶלwrong פָּעַלְתִּיdone לֹאnot אֹסִיףagain

Teach me what I cannot see; if I have done wrong, I will not do it again.’

34:33

הַמֵעִמְּךָthe from with you יְשַׁלְמֶנָּהrepay you כִּיfor מָאַסְתָּterms כִּיwhen אַתָּהyou תִבְחַרchoose וְלֹאnot אָנִיI וּמַהwhat יָדַעְתָּknow דַבֵּרtell

Should God repay you on your own terms when you have disavowed His? You must choose, not I; so tell me what you know.

34:34

אַנְשֵׁיmen לֵבָבunderstanding יֹאמְרוּdeclare לִיto me וְגֶבֶרmen חָכָםwise שֹׁמֵעַhear לִיto me

Men of understanding will declare to me, and the wise men who hear me will say:

34:35

אִיּוֹבJob לֹאnot בְדַעַתknowledge יְדַבֵּרspeaks וּדְבָרָיוhis words לֹאnot בְהַשְׂכֵּילinsight

‘Job speaks without knowledge; his words lack insight.’

34:36

אָבִיonly יִבָּחֵןtried אִיּוֹבJob עַדto נֶצַחutmost עַלfor תְּשֻׁבֹתanswering בְּאַנְשֵׁיman אָוֶןwicked

If only Job were tried to the utmost for answering like a wicked man.

34:37

כִּיfor יֹסִיףadds עַלto חַטָּאתוֹhis sin פֶשַׁעrebellion בֵּינֵינוּamong us יִסְפּוֹקclaps וְיֶרֶבand multiplies אֲמָרָיוhis words לָאֵלagainst God

For he adds rebellion to his sin; he claps his hands among us and multiplies his words against God.”
