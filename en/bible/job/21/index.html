Job 21

Job observes that the wicked prosper

Open in the interlinear reader

21:1

וַיַּעַןthen answered אִיּוֹבJob וַיֹּאמַרand said

Then Job answered:

21:2

שִׁמְעוּlisten שָׁמוֹעַcarefully מִלָּתִיmy words וּתְהִיlet be זֹאתthis תַּנְחוּמֹתֵיכֶםyour consolation

“Listen carefully to my words; let this be your consolation to me.

21:3

שָׂאוּנִיbear with me וְאָנֹכִיwhile I אֲדַבֵּרspeak וְאַחַרthen after דַּבְּרִיspoken תַלְעִיגmocking

Bear with me while I speak; then, after I have spoken, you may go on mocking.

21:4

הֶאָנֹכִיthe I לְאָדָםagainst man שִׂיחִיmy complaint וְאִםand if מַדּוּעַwhy לֹאnot תִקְצַרimpatient רוּחִיmy spirit

Is my complaint against a man? Then why should I not be impatient?

21:5

פְּנוּlook אֵלַיto me וְהָשַׁמּוּand be appalled וְשִׂימוּput יָדhand עַלover פֶּהmouth

Look at me and be appalled; put your hand over your mouth.

21:6

וְאִםand if זָכַרְתִּיremember וְנִבְהָלְתִּיterror וְאָחַזhold and בְּשָׂרִיmy body פַּלָּצוּתhorror

When I remember, terror takes hold, and my body trembles in horror.

21:7

מַדּוּעַwhy רְשָׁעִיםwicked יִחְיוּlive עָתְקוּgrowing old גַּםand גָּבְרוּincreasing חָיִלpower

Why do the wicked live on, growing old and increasing in power?

21:8

זַרְעָםtheir descendants נָכוֹןestablished לִפְנֵיהֶםaround them עִמָּםwith them וְצֶאֱצָאֵיהֶםand offspring לְעֵינֵיהֶםbefore their eyes

Their descendants are established around them, and their offspring before their eyes.

21:9

בָּתֵּיהֶםtheir homes are שָׁלוֹםsafe מִפָּחַדfrom fear וְלֹאno שֵׁבֶטrod אֱלוֹהַּGod עֲלֵיהֶםupon them

Their homes are safe from fear; no rod of punishment from God is upon them.

21:10

שׁוֹרוֹtheir bulls עִבַּרpass וְלֹאand not יַגְעִלbreed fail תְּפַלֵּטfail פָּרָתוֹtheir cows וְלֹאand do not תְשַׁכֵּלmiscarry

Their bulls breed without fail; their cows bear calves and do not miscarry.

21:11

יְשַׁלְּחוּsend כַצֹּאןlike a flock עֲוִילֵיהֶםtheir little וְיַלְדֵיהֶםtheir children יְרַקֵּדוּןskip

They send forth their little ones like a flock; their children skip about,

21:12

יִשְׂאוּsinging כְּתֹףtambourine וְכִנּוֹרand lyre וְיִשְׂמְחוּand making merry לְקוֹלat the sound עוּגָבflute

singing to the tambourine and lyre and making merry at the sound of the flute.

21:13

יְכַלּוּspend בַטּוֹבin prosperity יְמֵיהֶםtheir days וּבְרֶגַעand go down שְׁאוֹלSheol יֵחָתּוּpeace

They spend their days in prosperity and go down to Sheol in peace.

21:14

וַיֹּאמְרוּyet they say לָאֵלto God סוּרleave מִמֶּנּוּfrom us וְדַעַתto know דְּרָכֶיךָyour ways לֹאno חָפָצְנוּdesire

Yet they say to God: ‘Leave us alone! For we have no desire to know Your ways.

21:15

מַהwhat שַׁדַּיAlmighty כִּיthat נַעַבְדֶנּוּserve him וּמַהand what נּוֹעִילgain כִּיif נִפְגַּעpray בּוֹto him

Who is the Almighty, that we should serve Him, and what would we gain if we pray to Him?’

21:16

הֵןbehold לֹאnot בְיָדָםin own hands טוּבָםprosperity עֲצַתcounsel רְשָׁעִיםwicked רָחֲקָהfar מֶנִּיfrom of me

Still, their prosperity is not in their own hands, so I stay far from the counsel of the wicked.

21:17

כַּמָּהhow נֵרlamp רְשָׁעִיםwicked יִדְעָךְoften וְיָבֹאcome עָלֵימוֹupon them אֵידָםdisaster חֲבָלִיםGod יְחַלֵּקapportion בְּאַפּוֹin his anger

How often is the lamp of the wicked put out? Does disaster come upon them? Does God, in His anger, apportion destruction?

21:18

יִהְיוּare they כְּתֶבֶןlike straw לִפְנֵיto before רוּחַwind וּכְמֹץlike chaff גְּנָבַתּוּswept סוּפָהstorm

Are they like straw before the wind, like chaff swept away by a storm?

21:19

אֱלוֹהַּGod יִצְפֹּןlays up לְבָנָיוfor his children אוֹנוֹhis iniquity יְשַׁלֵּםrepay אֵלָיוto him וְיֵדָעknow

It is said that God lays up one’s punishment for his children. Let God repay the man himself, so he will know it.

21:20

יִרְאוּsee עֵינָיוeyes his own כִּידוֹdestruction let him וּמֵחֲמַתwrath שַׁדַּיAlmighty יִשְׁתֶּהdrink

Let his eyes see his own destruction; let him drink for himself the wrath of the Almighty.

21:21

כִּיfor מַהwhat חֶפְצוֹdoes he care בְּבֵיתוֹabout his household אַחֲרָיוafter him וּמִסְפַּרwhen the number חֳדָשָׁיוhis months חֻצָּצוּrun

For what does he care about his household after him, when the number of his months has run out?

21:22

הַלְאֵלto God יְלַמֶּדteach דָּעַתknowledge וְהוּאsince he רָמִיםhigh יִשְׁפּוֹטjudges

Can anyone teach knowledge to God, since He judges those on high?

21:23

זֶהthis יָמוּתdies בְּעֶצֶםvigor תֻּמּוֹfull כֻּלּוֹcompletely שַׁלְאֲנַןsecure וְשָׁלֵיוand at ease

One man dies full of vigor, completely secure and at ease.

21:24

עֲטִינָיוhis body מָלְאוּbody חָלָבnourished וּמֹחַnourished and עַצְמוֹתָיוhis bones יְשֻׁקֶּהrich

His body is well nourished, and his bones are rich with marrow.

21:25

וְזֶהand this יָמוּתdies בְּנֶפֶשׁsoul מָרָהbitterness וְלֹאhaving never אָכַלtasted בַּטּוֹבָהprosperity

Yet another man dies in the bitterness of his soul, having never tasted prosperity.

21:26

יַחַדtogether עַלin עָפָרdust יִשְׁכָּבוּlie וְרִמָּהand worms תְּכַסֶּהcover עֲלֵיהֶםon them

But together they lie down in the dust, and worms cover them both.

21:27

הֵןbehold יָדַעְתִּיknow מַחְשְׁבוֹתֵיכֶםyour thoughts full וּמְזִמּוֹתwell the schemes עָלַיon me תַּחְמֹסוּwrong

Behold, I know your thoughts full well, the schemes by which you would wrong me.

21:28

כִּיfor תֹאמְרוּsay אַיֵּהwhere now בֵיתhouse נָדִיבnobleman’s וְאַיֵּהand where אֹהֶלtents מִשְׁכְּנוֹתdwell רְשָׁעִיםwicked

For you say, ‘Where now is the nobleman’s house, and where are the tents in which the wicked dwell?’

21:29

הֲלֹאyou never שְׁאֶלְתֶּםasked עוֹבְרֵיtravel דָרֶךְroads וְאֹתֹתָםaccept their reports לֹאnot תְנַכֵּרוּaccept

Have you never asked those who travel the roads? Do you not accept their reports?

21:30

כִּיfor לְיוֹםfrom the day אֵידcalamity יֵחָשֶׂךְspared רָעevil לְיוֹםfrom the day עֲבָרוֹתwrath יוּבָלוּled

Indeed, the evil man is spared from the day of calamity, delivered from the day of wrath.

21:31

מִיwho יַגִּידdenounces עַלto פָּנָיוhis face דַּרְכּוֹbehavior וְהוּאand he עָשָׂהdone מִיwho יְשַׁלֶּםrepays לוֹhim for

Who denounces his behavior to his face? Who repays him for what he has done?

21:32

וְהוּאhe is לִקְבָרוֹתto the grave יוּבָלcarried וְעַלover גָּדִישׁtomb יִשְׁקוֹדwatch

He is carried to the grave, and watch is kept over his tomb.

21:33

מָתְקוּsweet לוֹto him רִגְבֵיclods נָחַלvalley וְאַחֲרָיוbehind him כָּלeveryone אָדָםfollows יִמְשׁוֹךְfollows וּלְפָנָיוand those before him אֵיןwithout מִסְפָּרnumber

The clods of the valley are sweet to him; everyone follows behind him, and those before him are without number.

21:34

וְאֵיךְso how can תְּנַחֲמוּנִיcomfort me הָבֶלempty וּתְשׁוּבֹתֵיכֶםfor your answers full נִשְׁאַרremain מָעַלfalsehood

So how can you comfort me with empty words? For your answers remain full of falsehood.”
