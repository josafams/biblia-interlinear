Job 31

Job's oath of innocence

Open in the interlinear reader

31:1

בְּרִיתcovenant כָּרַתִּיmade לְעֵינָיmy eyes וּמָהhow then אֶתְבּוֹנֵןdesire עַלon בְּתוּלָהvirgin

“I have made a covenant with my eyes. How then could I gaze with desire at a virgin?

31:2

וּמֶהfor what חֵלֶקallotment אֱלוֹהַּGod מִמָּעַלfrom above וְנַחֲלַתor the heritage שַׁדַּיAlmighty מִמְּרֹמִיםon high

For what is the allotment of God from above, or the heritage from the Almighty on high?

31:3

הֲלֹאdoes not אֵידdisaster לְעַוָּלcome to the unjust וְנֵכֶרand calamity לְפֹעֲלֵיto the workers אָוֶןiniquity

Does not disaster come to the unjust and calamity to the workers of iniquity?

31:4

הֲלֹאdoes not הוּאhe יִרְאֶהsee דְרָכָיmy ways וְכָלevery צְעָדַיmy step יִסְפּוֹרcount

Does He not see my ways and count my every step?

31:5

אִםif הָלַכְתִּיwalked עִםwith שָׁוְאfalsehood וַתַּחַשׁrushed עַלto מִרְמָהdeceit רַגְלִיmy foot

If I have walked in falsehood or my foot has rushed to deceit,

31:6

יִשְׁקְלֵנִיweigh me בְמֹאזְנֵיwith scales צֶדֶקhonest וְיֵדַעhe may know אֱלוֹהַּGod תֻּמָּתִיmy integrity

let God weigh me with honest scales, that He may know my integrity.

31:7

אִםif תִּטֶּהturned אַשֻּׁרִיmy steps מִנִּיfrom הַדָּרֶךְthe path וְאַחַרand after עֵינַיmy eyes הָלַךְfollowed לִבִּיmy heart וּבְכַפַּיmy hands דָּבַקstuck מֻאוּםimpurity

If my steps have turned from the path, if my heart has followed my eyes, or if impurity has stuck to my hands,

31:8

אֶזְרְעָהsown וְאַחֵרthen may another יֹאכֵלeat וְצֶאֱצָאַיmay my crops יְשֹׁרָשׁוּuprooted

then may another eat what I have sown, and may my crops be uprooted.

31:9

אִםif נִפְתָּהenticed לִבִּיmy heart עַלon אִשָּׁהwife וְעַלand on פֶּתַחdoor רֵעִיmy neighbor’s אָרָבְתִּיlurked

If my heart has been enticed by my neighbor’s wife, or I have lurked at his door,

31:10

תִּטְחַןgrind grain לְאַחֵרfor another אִשְׁתִּיmy own wife וְעָלֶיהָand on her יִכְרְעוּןsleep אֲחֵרִיןother

then may my own wife grind grain for another, and may other men sleep with her.

31:11

כִּיfor הִיאthat זִמָּהheinous וְהוּאand he עָוֺןiniquity פְּלִילִיםjudged

For that would be a heinous crime, an iniquity to be judged.

31:12

כִּיfor אֵשׁfire הִיאthat עַדto אֲבַדּוֹןAbaddon תֹּאכֵלit וּבְכָלentire תְּבוּאָתִיmy harvest תְשָׁרֵשׁroot

For it is a fire that burns down to Abaddon; it would root out my entire harvest.

31:13

אִםif אֶמְאַסrejected מִשְׁפַּטcause עַבְדִּיmy manservant וַאֲמָתִיor maidservant בְּרִבָםcomplaint against עִמָּדִיagainst me

If I have rejected the cause of my manservant or maidservant when they made a complaint against me,

31:14

וּמָהwhat אֶעֱשֶׂהdo כִּיwhen יָקוּםrises אֵלGod וְכִיand if יִפְקֹדjudge מָהhow אֲשִׁיבֶנּוּanswer

what will I do when God rises to judge? How will I answer when called to account?

31:15

הֲלֹאdid not בַבֶּטֶןin the womb עֹשֵׂנִיmade me עָשָׂהוּmake them וַיְכֻנֶנּוּform us בָּרֶחֶםin the womb אֶחָדsame

Did not He who made me in the womb also make them? Did not the same One form us in the womb?

31:16

אִםif אֶמְנַעdenied מֵחֵפֶץthe desires דַּלִּיםpoor וְעֵינֵיeyes אַלְמָנָהwidow’s אֲכַלֶּהfail

If I have denied the desires of the poor or allowed the widow’s eyes to fail,

31:17

וְאֹכַלI have eaten פִּתִּיmy morsel לְבַדִּיalone וְלֹאnot אָכַלsharing יָתוֹםfatherless מִמֶּנָּהfrom her

if I have eaten my morsel alone, not sharing it with the fatherless—

31:18

כִּיfor מִנְּעוּרַיfrom my youth גְּדֵלַנִיI reared כְאָבfather וּמִבֶּטֶןwomb אִמִּיmy mother’s I אַנְחֶנָּהguided the widow

though from my youth I reared him as would a father, and from my mother’s womb I guided the widow—

31:19

אִםif אֶרְאֶהseen אוֹבֵדperish מִבְּלִיfor lack לְבוּשׁclothing וְאֵיןwithout כְּסוּתcloak לָאֶבְיוֹןa needy

if I have seen one perish for lack of clothing, or a needy man without a cloak,

31:20

אִםif לֹאnot בֵרֲכוּנִיblessed me חֲלָצָיוwarming him וּמִגֵּזwith the fleece כְּבָשַׂיmy lambs יִתְחַמָּםsheep

if his heart has not blessed me for warming him with the fleece of my sheep,

31:21

אִםif הֲנִיפוֹתִיlifted עַלagainst יָתוֹםfatherless יָדִיmy hand כִּיbecause אֶרְאֶהsaw בַשַּׁעַרin the gate עֶזְרָתִיsupport

if I have lifted up my hand against the fatherless because I saw that I had support in the gate,

31:22

כְּתֵפִיmy shoulder מִשִּׁכְמָהfrom shoulder blade תִפּוֹלfall וְאֶזְרֹעִיthen may my arm מִקָּנָהtorn from its תִשָּׁבֵרsocket

then may my arm fall from my shoulder and be torn from its socket.

31:23

כִּיfor פַחַדterrifies אֵלַיto me אֵידcalamity אֵלGod וּמִשְּׂאֵתוֹand his splendor לֹאnot אוּכָלoverpower

For calamity from God terrifies me, and His splendor I cannot overpower.

31:24

אִםif שַׂמְתִּיput זָהָבgold כִּסְלִיmy trust וְלַכֶּתֶםor called pure אָמַרְתִּיsaid מִבְטַחִיmy security

If I have put my trust in gold or called pure gold my security,

31:25

אִםif אֶשְׂמַחrejoiced כִּיfor רַבgreat חֵילִיmy wealth וְכִיbecause כַבִּירmuch מָצְאָהfound יָדִיmy hand

if I have rejoiced in my great wealth because my hand had gained so much,

31:26

אִםif אֶרְאֶהbeheld אוֹרsun כִּיfor יָהֵלradiance וְיָרֵחַor the moon יָקָרmoving הֹלֵךְsplendor

if I have beheld the sun in its radiance or the moon moving in splendor,

31:27

וַיִּפְתְּenticed בַּסֵּתֶרsecretly לִבִּיmy heart וַתִּשַּׁקkiss יָדִיmy hand לְפִיmy mouth

so that my heart was secretly enticed and my hand threw a kiss from my mouth,

31:28

גַּםalso הוּאhe עָוֺןiniquity פְּלִילִיjudged כִּיfor כִחַשְׁתִּיdenied לָאֵלGod מִמָּעַלfrom above

this would also be an iniquity to be judged, for I would have denied God on high.

31:29

אִםif אֶשְׂמַחrejoiced בְּפִידruin מְשַׂנְאִיmy enemy’s וְהִתְעֹרַרְתִּיor exulted כִּיwhen מְצָאוֹbefell him רָעevil

If I have rejoiced in my enemy’s ruin, or exulted when evil befell him—

31:30

וְלֹאI have not נָתַתִּיallowed לַחֲטֹאto sin חִכִּיmy mouth לִשְׁאֹלby asking בְּאָלָהcurse נַפְשׁוֹhis life

I have not allowed my mouth to sin by asking for his life with a curse—

31:31

אִםif לֹאnot אָמְרוּsaid מְתֵיmen אָהֳלִיmy house מִיwho יִתֵּןgive מִבְּשָׂרוֹfrom his meat לֹאnot נִשְׂבָּעfill

if the men of my house have not said, ‘Who is there who has not had his fill?’—

31:32

בַּחוּץon the street לֹאno יָלִיןlodge גֵּרstranger דְּלָתַיmy door לָאֹרַחbeen אֶפְתָּחopen

but no stranger had to lodge on the street, for my door has been open to the traveler—

31:33

אִםif כִּסִּיתִיcovered כְאָדָםlike Adam פְּשָׁעָיmy transgressions לִטְמוֹןby hiding בְּחֻבִּיmy in heart עֲוֺנִיguilt my

if I have covered my transgressions like Adam by hiding my guilt in my heart,

31:34

כִּיbecause אֶעֱרוֹץgreatly הָמוֹןcrowds רַבָּהfeared וּבוּזand the contempt מִשְׁפָּחוֹתclans יְחִתֵּנִיterrified me וָאֶדֹּםkept silent לֹאnot אֵצֵאgo פָתַחoutside

because I greatly feared the crowds and the contempt of the clans terrified me, so that I kept silent and would not go outside—

31:35

מִיwho יִתֶּןgive לִיto me שֹׁמֵעַhear לִיto me הֶןhere תָּוִיmy signature שַׁדַּיAlmighty יַעֲנֵנִיanswer me וְסֵפֶרlet accuser כָּתַבcompose אִישׁman רִיבִיindictment

(Oh, that I had one to hear me! Here is my signature. Let the Almighty answer me; let my accuser compose an indictment.

31:36

אִםif לֹאnot עַלon שִׁכְמִיmy shoulder אֶשָּׂאֶנּוּcarry אֶעֶנְדֶנּוּwear it עֲטָרוֹתcrown לִיto me

Surely I would carry it on my shoulder and wear it like a crown.

31:37

מִסְפַּרall צְעָדַיmy steps אַגִּידֶנּוּgive כְּמוֹlike נָגִידprince אֲקָרֲבֶנּוּapproach him

I would give account of all my steps; I would approach Him like a prince.)—

31:38

אִםif עָלַיagainst אַדְמָתִיland תִזְעָקcries out וְיַחַדtogether תְּלָמֶיהָits furrows יִבְכָּיוּןweep

if my land cries out against me and its furrows weep together,

31:39

אִםif כֹּחָהּits produce אָכַלְתִּיdevoured בְלִיwithout כָסֶףpayment וְנֶפֶשׁor broken בְּעָלֶיהָspirit of its הִפָּחְתִּיtenants

if I have devoured its produce without payment or broken the spirit of its tenants,

31:40

תַּחַתinstead חִטָּהwheat יֵצֵאlet חוֹחַbriers וְתַחַתinstead שְׂעֹרָהbarley בָאְשָׁהthus conclude תַּמּוּcompletely דִּבְרֵיwords אִיּוֹבJob

then let briers grow instead of wheat and stinkweed instead of barley.” Thus conclude the words of Job.
