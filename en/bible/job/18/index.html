Job 18

Bildad and the ruin of the wicked

18:1

Then Bildad the Shuhite replied:

18:2

עַדuntil אָנָהlong you תְּשִׂימוּןsome קִנְצֵיend לְמִלִּיןthese speeches תָּבִינוּshow וְאַחַרand after נְדַבֵּרtalk

“How long until you end these speeches? Show some sense, and then we can talk.

18:3

מַדּוּעַwhy נֶחְשַׁבְנוּregarded כַבְּהֵמָהas cattle נִטְמִינוּstupid בְּעֵינֵיכֶםin your sight

Why are we regarded as cattle, as stupid in your sight?

18:4

טֹרֵףtear נַפְשׁוֹwho yourself בְּאַפּוֹin anger הַלְמַעַנְךָthe so you תֵּעָזַבforsaken אָרֶץearth וְיֶעְתַּקmoved צוּרrocks מִמְּקֹמוֹfrom their place

You who tear yourself in anger— should the earth be forsaken on your account, or the rocks be moved from their place?

18:5

גַּםindeed אוֹרlamp רְשָׁעִיםwicked יִדְעָךְextinguished וְלֹאnot יִגַּהּglow שְׁבִיבflame אִשּׁוֹhis fire

Indeed, the lamp of the wicked is extinguished; the flame of his fire does not glow.

18:6

אוֹרlight חָשַׁךְdark בְּאָהֳלוֹin his tent וְנֵרוֹand the lamp עָלָיוon him יִדְעָךְgoes out

The light in his tent grows dark, and the lamp beside him goes out.

18:7

יֵצְרוּvigorous צַעֲדֵיstride אוֹנוֹshortened his וְתַשְׁלִיכֵהוּtrip him עֲצָתוֹschemes

His vigorous stride is shortened, and his own schemes trip him up.

18:8

כִּיfor שֻׁלַּחsent בְּרֶשֶׁתinto a net בְּרַגְלָיוown feet lead him וְעַלand on שְׂבָכָהwanders יִתְהַלָּךְwalk

For his own feet lead him into a net, and he wanders into its mesh.

18:9

יֹאחֵזtrap בְּעָקֵבheel פָּחsnare יַחֲזֵקgrips עָלָיוon him צַמִּיםgrips

A trap seizes his heel; a snare grips him.

18:10

טָמוּןhidden בָּאָרֶץin the ground חַבְלוֹnoose is וּמַלְכֻּדְתּוֹand a trap עֲלֵיin נָתִיבpath

A noose is hidden in the ground, and a trap lies in his path.

18:11

סָבִיבevery בִּעֲתֻהוּfrighten him בַלָּהוֹתterrors וֶהֱפִיצֻהוּand harass his לְרַגְלָיוevery step

Terrors frighten him on every side and harass his every step.

18:12

יְהִיbe רָעֵבdepleted אֹנוֹdepleted וְאֵידand calamity נָכוֹןready לְצַלְעוֹat his side

His strength is depleted, and calamity is ready at his side.

18:13

יֹאכַלdevours בַּדֵּיpatches עוֹרוֹhis skin יֹאכַלdevours בַּדָּיוhis poles בְּכוֹרfirstborn מָוֶתdeath

It devours patches of his skin; the firstborn of death devours his limbs.

18:14

יִנָּתֵקtorn מֵאָהֳלוֹtent מִבְטַחוֹshelter of his וְתַצְעִדֵהוּand is marched לְמֶלֶךְto the king בַּלָּהוֹתterrors

He is torn from the shelter of his tent and is marched off to the king of terrors.

18:15

תִּשְׁכּוֹןfire resides בְּאָהֳלוֹin his tent מִבְּלִיburning לוֹto him יְזֹרֶהrains down עַלon נָוֵהוּhis dwelling גָפְרִיתsulfur

Fire resides in his tent; burning sulfur rains down on his dwelling.

18:16

מִתַּחַתthe beneath שָׁרָשָׁיוroots him יִבָשׁוּdry וּמִמַּעַלand the above יִמַּלwither קְצִירוֹbranches him

The roots beneath him dry up, and the branches above him wither away.

18:17

זִכְרוֹmemory of him אָבַדperishes מִנִּיfrom אָרֶץearth וְלֹאno שֵׁםname לוֹto him עַלon פְּנֵיface חוּץland

The memory of him perishes from the earth, and he has no name in the land.

18:18

יֶהְדְּפֻהוּdriven מֵאוֹרfrom light אֶלinto חֹשֶׁךְdarkness וּמִתֵּבֵלworld יְנִדֻּהוּchased

He is driven from light into darkness and is chased from the inhabited world.

18:19

לֹאnot נִיןher offspring לוֹto him וְלֹאand no or נֶכֶדoffspring posterity בְּעַמּוֹamong his people וְאֵיןno שָׂרִידsurvivor בִּמְגוּרָיוwhere he

He has no offspring or posterity among his people, no survivor where he once lived.

18:20

עַלin יוֹמוֹwest נָשַׁמּוּappalled אַחֲרֹנִיםfate וְקַדְמֹנִיםeast אָחֲזוּtremble שָׂעַרhorror

Those in the west are appalled at his fate, while those in the east tremble in horror.

18:21

אַךְsurely אֵלֶּהthese מִשְׁכְּנוֹתdwelling עַוָּלwicked וְזֶהand the מְקוֹםplace לֹאnot יָדַעknow אֵלGod

Surely such is the dwelling of the wicked and the place of one who does not know God.”
