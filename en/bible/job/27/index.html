Job 27

Job holds on to his integrity

Open in the interlinear reader

27:1

וַיֹּסֶףand again אִיּוֹבJob שְׂאֵתcontinued מְשָׁלוֹcontinued his discourse וַיֹּאמַרand said

Job continued his discourse:

27:2

חַיsurely אֵלGod הֵסִירdeprived מִשְׁפָּטִיme of justice וְשַׁדַּיAlmighty הֵמַרembittered נַפְשִׁיmy soul

“As surely as God lives, who has deprived me of justice— the Almighty, who has embittered my soul—

27:3

כִּיfor כָלall עוֹדstill נִשְׁמָתִיmy breath בִיin me וְרוּחַand the breath אֱלוֹהַּGod בְּאַפִּיin my nostrils

as long as my breath is still within me and the breath of God remains in my nostrils,

27:4

אִםnot תְּדַבֵּרְנָהspeak שְׂפָתַיmy lips עַוְלָהwickedness וּלְשׁוֹנִיand my tongue אִםnot יֶהְגֶּהutter רְמִיָּהdeceit

my lips will not speak wickedness, and my tongue will not utter deceit.

27:5

חָלִילָהnever לִּיto me אִםif אַצְדִּיקthat אֶתְכֶםyou עַדuntil אֶגְוָעdie לֹאnot אָסִירmaintain תֻּמָּתִיmy integrity מִמֶּנִּיfrom me

I will never say that you are right; I will maintain my integrity until I die.

27:6

בְּצִדְקָתִיmy righteousness הֶחֱזַקְתִּיcling וְלֹאand never אַרְפֶּהָlet לֹאnot יֶחֱרַףaccuse לְבָבִיmy conscience מִיָּמָיlong as

I will cling to my righteousness and never let go. As long as I live, my conscience will not accuse me.

27:7

יְהִיbe כְרָשָׁעlike the wicked אֹיְבִיmy enemy וּמִתְקוֹמְמִיand rise me כְעַוָּלopponent like the unjust

May my enemy be like the wicked and my opponent like the unjust.

27:8

כִּיfor מַהwhat תִּקְוַתhope חָנֵףgodless כִּיwhen יִבְצָעcut כִּיwhen יֵשֶׁלtakes אֱלוֹהַּGod נַפְשׁוֹhis life

For what is the hope of the godless when he is cut off, when God takes away his life?

27:9

הַצַעֲקָתוֹhis cry יִשְׁמַעhear אֵלGod כִּיwhen תָבוֹאcomes עָלָיוupon him צָרָהdistress

Will God hear his cry when distress comes upon him?

27:10

אִםif עַלin שַׁדַּיAlmighty יִתְעַנָּגdelight יִקְרָאcall אֱלוֹהַּGod בְּכָלat all עֵתtimes

Will he delight in the Almighty? Will he call upon God at all times?

27:11

אוֹרֶהinstruct אֶתְכֶםyou בְּיַדin the power אֵלGod אֲשֶׁרthat עִםwith שַׁדַּיAlmighty לֹאnot אֲכַחֵדconceal

I will instruct you in the power of God. I will not conceal the ways of the Almighty.

27:12

הֵןsurely אַתֶּםyou כֻּלְּכֶםall of you חֲזִיתֶםseen וְלָמָּהfor yourselves why זֶּהthis הֶבֶלempty תֶּהְבָּלוּtalk

Surely all of you have seen it for yourselves. Why then do you keep up this empty talk?

27:13

זֶהthis חֵלֶקportion אָדָםman’s רָשָׁעwicked עִםwith אֵלGod וְנַחֲלַתheritage עָרִיצִיםruthless מִשַּׁדַּיfrom the Almighty יִקָּחוּreceive

This is the wicked man’s portion from God— the heritage the ruthless receive from the Almighty.

27:14

אִםif יִרְבּוּmany בָנָיוhis sons לְמוֹdestined חָרֶבsword וְצֶאֱצָאָיוand his offspring לֹאnever יִשְׂבְּעוּenough לָחֶםfood

Though his sons are many, they are destined for the sword; and his offspring will never have enough food.

27:15

שְׂרִידָיוhis survivors will בַּמָּוֶתby the plague יִקָּבֵרוּburied וְאַלְמְנֹתָיוand their widows לֹאnot תִבְכֶּינָהweep

His survivors will be buried by the plague, and their widows will not weep for them.

27:16

אִםif יִצְבֹּרheaps כֶּעָפָרlike dust כָּסֶףsilver וְכַחֹמֶרlike clay יָכִיןpiles מַלְבּוּשׁwardrobe

Though he heaps up silver like dust and piles up a wardrobe like clay,

27:17

יָכִיןlays וְצַדִּיקrighteous יִלְבָּשׁwear וְכֶסֶףand his silver נָקִיinnocent יַחֲלֹקdivided

what he lays up, the righteous will wear, and his silver will be divided by the innocent.

27:18

בָּנָהbuilt כָעָשׁlike a moth’s cocoon בֵּיתוֹhouse he וּכְסֻכָּהlike a hut עָשָׂהset נֹצֵרwatchman

The house he built is like a moth’s cocoon, like a hut set up by a watchman.

27:19

עָשִׁירwealthy יִשְׁכַּבlies וְלֹאand not יֵאָסֵףgathered עֵינָיוhis eyes פָּקַחopens וְאֵינֶנּוּand no him

He lies down wealthy, but will do so no more; when he opens his eyes, all is gone.

27:20

תַּשִּׂיגֵהוּovertake him כַמַּיִםlike a flood בַּלָּהוֹתterrors לַיְלָהnight גְּנָבַתּוּsweeps him סוּפָהtempest

Terrors overtake him like a flood; a tempest sweeps him away in the night.

27:21

יִשָּׂאֵהוּcarries him קָדִיםeast wind וְיֵלַךְgone וִישָׂעֲרֵהוּit sweeps him מִמְּקֹמוֹout of his place

The east wind carries him away, and he is gone; it sweeps him out of his place.

27:22

וְיַשְׁלֵךְit hurls עָלָיוagainst him וְלֹאand not יַחְמֹלmercy מִיָּדוֹfrom its power בָּרוֹחַflees יִבְרָחheadlong

It hurls itself against him without mercy as he flees headlong from its power.

27:23

יִשְׂפֹּקclaps עָלֵימוֹon him כַפֵּימוֹhands at him וְיִשְׁרֹקand hisses עָלָיוon him מִמְּקֹמוֹout of his place

It claps its hands at him and hisses him out of his place.
