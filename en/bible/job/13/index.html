Job 13

Job wants to argue his case with God

Open in the interlinear reader

13:1

הֶןindeed כֹּלall רָאֲתָהseen עֵינִיmy eyes שָׁמְעָהheard אָזְנִיmy ears וַתָּבֶןand understood לָהּto her

“Indeed, my eyes have seen all this; my ears have heard and understood.

13:2

כְּדַעְתְּכֶםwhat you know יָדַעְתִּיknow גַםalso אָנִיI am לֹאnot נֹפֵלinferior אָנֹכִיI מִכֶּםfrom of you

What you know, I also know; I am not inferior to you.

13:3

אוּלָםyet אֲנִיI אֶלto שַׁדַּיAlmighty אֲדַבֵּרspeak וְהוֹכֵחַand argue אֶלto אֵלGod אֶחְפָּץdesire

Yet I desire to speak to the Almighty and argue my case before God.

13:4

וְאוּלָםhowever אַתֶּםyou טֹפְלֵיsmear שָׁקֶרlies רֹפְאֵיphysicians אֱלִלworthless כֻּלְּכֶםyou are all

You, however, smear with lies; you are all worthless physicians.

13:5

מִיwho יִתֵּןwould הַחֲרֵשׁremain תַּחֲרִישׁוּןsilent וּתְהִיbe לָכֶםto you לְחָכְמָהwisdom

If only you would remain silent; for that would be your wisdom!

13:6

שִׁמְעוּhear נָאnow תוֹכַחְתִּיmy argument וְרִבוֹתplea שְׂפָתַיmy lips הַקְשִׁיבוּlisten

Hear now my argument, and listen to the plea of my lips.

13:7

הַלְאֵלon God’s תְּדַבְּרוּspeak עַוְלָהwickedly וְלוֹfor him תְּדַבְּרוּspeak רְמִיָּהdeceitfully

Will you speak wickedly on God’s behalf or speak deceitfully for Him?

13:8

הֲפָנָיוwould you show him תִּשָּׂאוּןpartiality אִםor לָאֵלin his defense תְּרִיבוּןdefense

Would you show Him partiality or argue in His defense?

13:9

הֲטוֹבwell כִּיwhen יַחְקֹרexamined could אֶתְכֶםyou אִםif כְּהָתֵלdeceive like בֶּאֱנוֹשׁa man תְּהָתֵלּוּmocked בוֹin him

Would it be well when He examined you? Could you deceive Him like a man?

13:10

הוֹכֵחַdirectly יוֹכִיחַrebuke אֶתְכֶםyou אִםif בַּסֵּתֶרsecretly פָּנִיםpartiality תִּשָּׂאוּןpartiality

Surely He would rebuke you if you secretly showed partiality.

13:11

הֲלֹאnot שְׂאֵתוֹhis majesty תְּבַעֵתterrify אֶתְכֶםyou וּפַחְדּוֹwould the dread of him יִפֹּלfall עֲלֵיכֶםupon you

Would His majesty not terrify you? Would the dread of Him not fall upon you?

13:12

זִכְרֹנֵיכֶםyour maxims מִשְׁלֵיproverbs אֵפֶרashes לְגַבֵּיdefenses חֹמֶרclay גַּבֵּיכֶםyour defenses

Your maxims are proverbs of ashes; your defenses are defenses of clay.

13:13

הַחֲרִישׁוּsilent מִמֶּנִּיfrom me וַאֲדַבְּרָהspeak אָנִיI וְיַעֲבֹרthen let come עָלַיto me מָהwhat

Be silent, and I will speak. Then let come to me what may.

13:14

עַלon מָהwhat אֶשָּׂאlift בְשָׂרִיmy flesh בְשִׁנָּיat risk וְנַפְשִׁיand my life אָשִׂיםtake בְּכַפִּיin my own hands

Why do I put myself at risk and take my life in my own hands?

13:15

הֵןbehold יִקְטְלֵנִיslay me לוֹto him אֲיַחֵלhope אַךְbut דְּרָכַיmy ways אֶלto פָּנָיוhis face אוֹכִיחַface

Though He slay me, I will hope in Him. I will still defend my ways to His face.

13:16

גַּםeven הוּאhe לִיto me לִישׁוּעָהsalvation כִּיfor לֹאno לְפָנָיוcan before him חָנֵףgodless יָבוֹאappear

Moreover, this will be my salvation, for no godless man can appear before Him.

13:17

שִׁמְעוּlisten שָׁמוֹעַcarefully מִלָּתִיmy words וְאַחֲוָתִיmy declaration ring בְּאָזְנֵיכֶםin your ears

Listen carefully to my words; let my declaration ring in your ears.

13:18

הִנֵּהbehold נָאnow עָרַכְתִּיprepared מִשְׁפָּטcase יָדַעְתִּיknow כִּיthat אֲנִיI אֶצְדָּקvindicated

Behold, now that I have prepared my case, I know that I will be vindicated.

13:19

מִיcan הוּאhe יָרִיבanyone indict עִמָּדִיme כִּיif עַתָּהnow אַחֲרִישׁsilent וְאֶגְוָעand die

Can anyone indict me? If so, I will be silent and die.

13:20

אַךְonly שְׁתַּיִםtwo אַלdo not תַּעַשׂdo עִמָּדִיthings me אָזthen מִפָּנֶיךָfrom your before לֹאnot אֶסָּתֵרhide

Only grant these two things to me, so that I need not hide from You:

13:21

כַּפְּךָwithdraw your hand מֵעָלַיfrom the me הַרְחַקfar וְאֵמָתְךָyour terror אַלdo not תְּבַעֲתַנִּיfrighten me

Withdraw Your hand from me, and do not let Your terror frighten me.

13:22

וּקְרָאthen call וְאָנֹכִיand I אֶעֱנֶהanswer אוֹor אֲדַבֵּרspeak וַהֲשִׁיבֵנִיreply

Then call me, and I will answer, or let me speak, and You can reply.

13:23

כַּמָּהhow many לִיto me עֲוֺנוֹתiniquities וְחַטָּאוֹתand sins פִּשְׁעִיmy transgression וְחַטָּאתִיand sin הֹדִיעֵנִיreveal

How many are my iniquities and sins? Reveal to me my transgression and sin.

13:24

לָמָּהto why פָנֶיךָyou your face תַסְתִּירhide וְתַחְשְׁבֵנִיand consider me לְאוֹיֵבas enemy לָךְto you

Why do You hide Your face and consider me as Your enemy?

13:25

הֶעָלֶהleaf נִדָּףwindblown תַּעֲרוֹץchase וְאֶתand [obj] קַשׁchaff יָבֵשׁdry תִּרְדֹּףpursue

Would You frighten a windblown leaf? Would You chase after dry chaff?

13:26

כִּיfor תִכְתֹּבrecord עָלַיagainst me מְרֹרוֹתbitter accusations וְתוֹרִישֵׁנִיand bequeath to me עֲוֺנוֹתiniquities נְעוּרָיmy youth

For You record bitter accusations against me and bequeath to me the iniquities of my youth.

13:27

וְתָשֵׂםyou put בַּסַּדin the stocks רַגְלַיmy feet וְתִשְׁמוֹרand stand watch כָּלall אָרְחוֹתָיmy paths עַלfor שָׁרְשֵׁיlimit רַגְלַיmy feet תִּתְחַקֶּהsoles

You put my feet in the stocks and stand watch over all my paths; You set a limit for the soles of my feet.

13:28

וְהוּאand he כְּרָקָבwastes away like יִבְלֶהrotten כְּבֶגֶדgarment אֲכָלוֹeaten עָשׁmoth

So man wastes away like something rotten, like a moth-eaten garment.
