Job 10

Job questions the Creator

Open in the interlinear reader

10:1

נָקְטָהloathe נַפְשִׁיmy soul בְּחַיָּיown life I אֶעֶזְבָהexpress עָלַיon me שִׂיחִיcomplaint אֲדַבְּרָהspeak בְּמַרin the bitterness נַפְשִׁיmy soul

“I loathe my own life; I will express my complaint and speak in the bitterness of my soul.

10:2

אֹמַרsay אֶלto אֱלוֹהַּGod אַלdo not תַּרְשִׁיעֵנִיcondemn me הוֹדִיעֵנִיme know עַלon מַהwhy תְּרִיבֵנִיprosecute me

I will say to God: Do not condemn me! Let me know why You prosecute me.

10:3

הֲטוֹבdoes it לְךָyou to כִּיfor תַעֲשֹׁקoppress כִּיfor תִמְאַסreject יְגִיעַwork כַּפֶּיךָyour hands וְעַלand on עֲצַתschemes רְשָׁעִיםwicked הוֹפָעְתָּshone forth

Does it please You to oppress me, to reject the work of Your hands and favor the schemes of the wicked?

10:4

הַעֵינֵיeyes בָשָׂרflesh לָךְto you אִםif כִּרְאוֹתsee as אֱנוֹשׁman תִּרְאֶהsees

Do You have eyes of flesh? Do You see as man sees?

10:5

הֲכִימֵיare days like אֱנוֹשׁmortal יָמֶיךָyour days אִםor שְׁנוֹתֶיךָyour years כִּימֵיlike days גָבֶרman

Are Your days like those of a mortal, or Your years like those of a man,

10:6

כִּיthat תְבַקֵּשׁseek לַעֲוֺנִיmy iniquity וּלְחַטָּאתִיmy sin תִדְרוֹשׁsearch

that You should seek my iniquity and search out my sin—

10:7

עַלon דַּעְתְּךָyou know כִּיthat לֹאnot אֶרְשָׁעguilty וְאֵיןno מִיָּדְךָfrom your hand מַצִּילdeliverance

though You know that I am not guilty, and there is no deliverance from Your hand?

10:8

יָדֶיךָyour hands עִצְּבוּנִיshaped me וַיַּעֲשׂוּנִיand did me יַחַדtogether סָבִיבformed וַתְּבַלְּעֵנִיand destroy me

Your hands shaped me and altogether formed me. Would You now turn and destroy me?

10:9

זְכָרremember נָאplease כִּיthat כַחֹמֶרlike clay עֲשִׂיתָנִיwould וְאֶלto עָפָרdust תְּשִׁיבֵנִיreturn me

Please remember that You molded me like clay. Would You now return me to dust?

10:10

הֲלֹאdid you not כֶחָלָבlike milk תַּתִּיכֵנִיpour me וְכַגְּבִנָּהlike cheese תַּקְפִּיאֵנִיcurdle me

Did You not pour me out like milk, and curdle me like cheese?

10:11

עוֹרskin וּבָשָׂרand flesh תַּלְבִּישֵׁנִיclothed me וּבַעֲצָמוֹתwith bones וְגִידִיםand sinews תְּסֹכְכֵנִיknit me together

You clothed me with skin and flesh, and knit me together with bones and sinews.

10:12

חַיִּיםlife וָחֶסֶדand loving devotion עָשִׂיתָdone עִמָּדִיwith me וּפְקֻדָּתְךָpreserved שָׁמְרָהcare רוּחִיmy spirit

You have granted me life and loving devotion, and Your care has preserved my spirit.

10:13

וְאֵלֶּהthese צָפַנְתָּconcealed בִלְבָבֶךָin your heart יָדַעְתִּיknow כִּיthat זֹאתthis עִמָּךְwith you

Yet You concealed these things in Your heart, and I know that this was in Your mind:

10:14

אִםif חָטָאתִיsinned וּשְׁמַרְתָּנִיnote and וּמֵעֲוֺנִיof iniquity לֹאnot תְנַקֵּנִיacquit me

If I sinned, You would take note, and would not acquit me of my iniquity.

10:15

אִםif רָשַׁעְתִּיguilty אַלְלַיwoe לִיto me וְצָדַקְתִּיrighteous לֹאnot אֶשָּׂאlift רֹאשִׁיhead I am שְׂבַעfull קָלוֹןshame וּרְאֵהand aware עָנְיִיmy affliction

If I am guilty, woe to me! And even if I am righteous, I cannot lift my head. I am full of shame and aware of my affliction.

10:16

וְיִגְאֶהI hold כַּשַּׁחַלlike a lion תְּצוּדֵנִיhunt me וְתָשֹׁבand again תִּתְפַּלָּאdisplay your power בִיagainst me

Should I hold my head high, You would hunt me like a lion, and again display Your power against me.

10:17

תְּחַדֵּשׁproduce עֵדֶיךָwitnesses נֶגְדִּיbefore me וְתֶרֶבand multiply כַּעַשְׂךָyour anger עִמָּדִיagainst חֲלִיפוֹתnew וְצָבָאhardships עִמִּיwith me

You produce new witnesses against me and multiply Your anger toward me. Hardships assault me in wave after wave.

10:18

וְלָמָּהwhy then מֵרֶחֶםfrom the womb הֹצֵאתָנִיbring me אֶגְוַעdied וְעַיִןand eye לֹאno תִרְאֵנִיseen me

Why then did You bring me from the womb? Oh, that I had died, and no eye had seen me!

10:19

כַּאֲשֶׁרhad לֹאnever הָיִיתִיcome אֶהְיֶהbeen מִבֶּטֶןfrom the womb לַקֶּבֶרto the grave אוּבָלled

If only I had never come to be, but had been carried from the womb to the grave.

10:20

הֲלֹאnot מְעַטfew יָמַיmy days וַחֲדָלand stop וְשִׁיתwithdraw מִמֶּנִּיfrom me וְאַבְלִיגָהcomfort מְּעָטlittle

Are my days not few? Withdraw from me, that I may have a little comfort,

10:21

בְּטֶרֶםbefore אֵלֵךְgo וְלֹאnever אָשׁוּבreturn אֶלto אֶרֶץland חֹשֶׁךְdarkness וְצַלְמָוֶתand gloom

before I go—never to return— to a land of darkness and gloom,

10:22

אֶרֶץland עֵיפָתָהutter כְּמוֹlike אֹפֶלdeep צַלְמָוֶתdeep shadow וְלֹאand not סְדָרִיםdisorder וַתֹּפַעlight כְּמוֹlike אֹפֶלdarkness

to a land of utter darkness, of deep shadow and disorder, where even the light is like darkness.”
