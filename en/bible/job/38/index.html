Job 38

The LORD answers from the whirlwind

Open in the interlinear reader

38:1

וַיַּעַןanswered יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] אִיּוֹבJob מנout מִןof הַסְּעָרָהthe whirlwind וַיֹּאמַרand said

Then the LORD answered Job out of the whirlwind and said:

38:2

מִיwho זֶהthis מַחְשִׁיךְobscures עֵצָהcounsel בְמִלִּיןby words בְּלִיwithout דָעַתknowledge

“Who is this who obscures My counsel by words without knowledge?

38:3

אֱזָרbrace yourself נָאnow כְגֶבֶרlike a man חֲלָצֶיךָyour warming וְאֶשְׁאָלְךָquestion you shall וְהוֹדִיעֵנִיinform me

Now brace yourself like a man; I will question you, and you shall inform Me.

38:4

אֵיפֹהwhere הָיִיתָwere בְּיָסְדִיfoundations אָרֶץearth הַגֵּדtell אִםif יָדַעְתָּyou בִינָהunderstanding

Where were you when I laid the foundations of the earth? Tell Me, if you have understanding.

38:5

מִיwho שָׂםfixed מְמַדֶּיהָits measurements כִּיfor תֵדָעknow אוֹor מִיwho נָטָהstretched עָלֶיהָon her קָּוmeasuring line

Who fixed its measurements? Surely you know! Or who stretched a measuring line across it?

38:6

עַלon מָהwhat אֲדָנֶיהָwere its הָטְבָּעוּfoundations אוֹor מִיwho יָרָהlaid אֶבֶןlaid פִּנָּתָהּits cornerstone

On what were its foundations set, or who laid its cornerstone,

38:7

בְּרָןsang יַחַדtogether כּוֹכְבֵיstars בֹקֶרmorning וַיָּרִיעוּshouted כָּלall בְּנֵיsons אֱלֹהִיםGod

while the morning stars sang together and all the sons of God shouted for joy?

38:8

וַיָּסֶךְwho enclosed בִּדְלָתַיִםbehind doors יָםsea בְּגִיחוֹwhen it burst מֵרֶחֶםfrom the womb יֵצֵאforth

Who enclosed the sea behind doors when it burst forth from the womb,

38:9

בְּשׂוּמִיwhen I made עָנָןclouds לְבֻשׁוֹits garment וַעֲרָפֶלand thick darkness חֲתֻלָּתוֹdarkness its blanket

when I made the clouds its garment and thick darkness its blanket,

38:10

וָאֶשְׁבֹּרand broke עָלָיוin חֻקִּיI fixed boundaries וָאָשִׂיםand set בְּרִיחַbars וּדְלָתָיִםand doors

when I fixed its boundaries and set in place its bars and doors,

38:11

וָאֹמַרand I declared עַדfar פֹּהhere תָבוֹאcome וְלֹאbut no תֹסִיףfarther וּפֹאhere יָשִׁיתclose בִּגְאוֹןproud גַּלֶּיךָyour waves

and I declared: ‘You may come this far, but no farther; here your proud waves must stop’?

38:12

הְמִיָּמֶיךָin your days have you צִוִּיתָcommanded בֹּקֶרmorning יִדַּעְתָּהassigned הַשַּׁחַרthe dawn מְקֹמוֹits place

In your days, have you commanded the morning or assigned the dawn its place,

38:13

לֶאֱחֹזmight spread בְּכַנְפוֹתto the ends הָאָרֶץthe earth וְיִנָּעֲרוּand shake רְשָׁעִיםwicked מִמֶּנָּהout of it

that it might spread to the ends of the earth and shake the wicked out of it?

38:14

תִּתְהַפֵּךְearth takes shape כְּחֹמֶרlike clay חוֹתָםseal hills וְיִתְיַצְּבוּits stand כְּמוֹlike לְבוּשׁgarment

The earth takes shape like clay under a seal; its hills stand out like the folds of a garment.

38:15

וְיִמָּנַעwithheld מֵרְשָׁעִיםfrom the wicked אוֹרָםlight וּזְרוֹעַarm רָמָהupraised תִּשָּׁבֵרbroken

Light is withheld from the wicked, and their upraised arm is broken.

38:16

הֲבָאתָhave you journeyed עַדto נִבְכֵיvents יָםsea וּבְחֵקֶרor in the trenches תְּהוֹםdeep הִתְהַלָּכְתָּwalked

Have you journeyed to the vents of the sea or walked in the trenches of the deep?

38:17

הֲנִגְלוּrevealed לְךָto you שַׁעֲרֵיgates מָוֶתdeath וְשַׁעֲרֵיgates צַלְמָוֶתshadow תִּרְאֶהseen

Have the gates of death been revealed to you? Have you seen the gates of the shadow of death?

38:18

הִתְבֹּנַנְתָּsurveyed עַדuntil רַחֲבֵיextent אָרֶץearth הַגֵּדtell אִםif יָדַעְתָּknow כֻלָּהּall this

Have you surveyed the extent of the earth? Tell Me, if you know all this.

38:19

אֵיwhere זֶהis הַדֶּרֶךְthe way יִשְׁכָּןhome אוֹרlight וְחֹשֶׁךְdarkness אֵיwhere זֶהyou מְקֹמוֹknow

Where is the way to the home of light? Do you know where darkness resides,

38:20

כִּיfor תִקָּחֶנּוּlead it אֶלto גְּבוּלוֹits border וְכִיand if תָבִיןknow נְתִיבוֹתpaths בֵּיתוֹits home

so you can lead it back to its border? Do you know the paths to its home?

38:21

יָדַעְתָּknow כִּיfor אָזthen תִּוָּלֵדborn וּמִסְפַּרand the number יָמֶיךָyour days רַבִּיםgreat

Surely you know, for you were already born! And the number of your days is great!

38:22

הֲבָאתָyou entered אֶלto אֹצְרוֹתstorehouses שָׁלֶגsnow וְאֹצְרוֹתstorehouses בָּרָדhail תִּרְאֶהobserved

Have you entered the storehouses of snow or observed the storehouses of hail,

38:23

אֲשֶׁרwhich חָשַׂכְתִּיhold in reserve לְעֶתfor times צָרtrouble לְיוֹםfor the day קְרָבwar וּמִלְחָמָהand battle

which I hold in reserve for times of trouble, for the day of war and battle?

38:24

אֵיwhich זֶהis הַדֶּרֶךְdirection the יֵחָלֶקdivided אוֹרlightning יָפֵץdispersed קָדִיםeast wind עֲלֵיover אָרֶץearth

In which direction is the lightning dispersed, or the east wind scattered over the earth?

38:25

מִיwho פִלַּגcuts a channel לַשֶּׁטֶףfor the flood תְּעָלָהclears וְדֶרֶךְpath לַחֲזִיזfor the thunderbolt קֹלוֹתthunder

Who cuts a channel for the flood or clears a path for the thunderbolt,

38:26

לְהַמְטִירto bring rain עַלon אֶרֶץland לֹאnot אִישׁman מִדְבָּרdesert לֹאno אָדָםman בּוֹin him

to bring rain on a barren land, on a desert where no man lives,

38:27

לְהַשְׂבִּיעַto satisfy שֹׁאָהparched וּמְשֹׁאָהwasteland וּלְהַצְמִיחַand make it sprout מֹצָאtender דֶשֶׁאtender grass

to satisfy the parched wasteland and make it sprout with tender grass?

38:28

הֲיֵשׁthere לַמָּטָרthe rain אָבfather אוֹa מִיwho הוֹלִידbegotten אֶגְלֵיdrops טָלdew

Does the rain have a father? Who has begotten the drops of dew?

38:29

מִבֶּטֶןfrom womb מִיwho יָצָאcame הַקָּרַחthe ice וּכְפֹרto the frost שָׁמַיִםheaven מִיwho יְלָדוֹbirth

From whose womb does the ice emerge? Who gives birth to the frost from heaven,

38:30

כָּאֶבֶןstone מַיִםwaters יִתְחַבָּאוּbecome hard וּפְנֵיand the surface תְהוֹםdeep יִתְלַכָּדוּfrozen

when the waters become hard as stone and the surface of the deep is frozen?

38:31

הַתְקַשֵּׁרcan you bind מַעֲדַנּוֹתchains כִּימָהPleiades אוֹor מֹשְׁכוֹתloosen כְּסִילOrion תְּפַתֵּחַbelt

Can you bind the chains of the Pleiades or loosen the belt of Orion?

38:32

הֲתֹצִיאcan you bring forth מַזָּרוֹתconstellations בְּעִתּוֹin their seasons וְעַיִשׁBear עַלon בָּנֶיהָher sons תַנְחֵםcubs

Can you bring forth the constellations in their seasons or lead out the Bear and her cubs?

38:33

הֲיָדַעְתָּdo you know חֻקּוֹתlaws שָׁמָיִםheavens אִםif תָּשִׂיםset מִשְׁטָרוֹtheir dominion בָאָרֶץdominion over the earth

Do you know the laws of the heavens? Can you set their dominion over the earth?

38:34

הֲתָרִיםpresent לָעָבthe clouds קוֹלֶךָyou command וְשִׁפְעַתflood מַיִםwater תְּכַסֶּךָּcovers you

Can you command the clouds so that a flood of water covers you?

38:35

הַתְשַׁלַּחsend בְּרָקִיםlightning bolts וְיֵלֵכוּtheir way וְיֹאמְרוּthey report לְךָto you הִנֵּנוּhere we

Can you send the lightning bolts on their way? Do they report to you, ‘Here we are’?

38:36

מִיwho שָׁתput בַּטֻּחוֹתin the heart חָכְמָהwisdom אוֹor מִיwho נָתַןgiven לַשֶּׂכְוִיto the mind בִינָהunderstanding

Who has put wisdom in the heart or given understanding to the mind?

38:37

מִיwho יְסַפֵּרcount שְׁחָקִיםclouds בְּחָכְמָהwisdom וְנִבְלֵיjars שָׁמַיִםheavens מִיwho can יַשְׁכִּיבtilt

Who has the wisdom to count the clouds? Or who can tilt the water jars of the heavens

38:38

בְּצֶקֶתhardens עָפָרdust לַמּוּצָקinto a mass וּרְגָבִיםand the clods of earth יְדֻבָּקוּstick

when the dust hardens into a mass and the clods of earth stick together?

38:39

הֲתָצוּדcan you hunt לְלָבִיאfor a lioness טָרֶףprey וְחַיַּתor satisfy כְּפִירִיםyoung lions תְּמַלֵּאfill

Can you hunt the prey for a lioness or satisfy the hunger of young lions

38:40

כִּיwhen יָשֹׁחוּcrouch בַמְּעוֹנוֹתin their dens יֵשְׁבוּwait בַסֻּכָּהin the thicket לְמוֹthicket אָרֶבthicket

when they crouch in their dens and lie in wait in the thicket?

38:41

מִיwho יָכִיןprovides לָעֹרֵבfor the raven צֵידוֹfood כִּיwhen יְלָדָיוyoung out אֶלto אֵלGod יְשַׁוֵּעוּcry יִתְעוּwander לִבְלִיfor lack אֹכֶלfood

Who provides food for the raven when its young cry out to God as they wander about for lack of food?
