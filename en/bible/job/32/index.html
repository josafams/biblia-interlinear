Job 32

Elihu enters the debate

32:1

וַיִּשְׁבְּתוּstopped שְׁלֹשֶׁתthree הָאֲנָשִׁיםthese men הָאֵלֶּהthese מֵעֲנוֹתanswering אֶת[obj] אִיּוֹבJob כִּיbecause הוּאhe was צַדִּיקrighteous בְּעֵינָיוin his own eyes

So these three men stopped answering Job, because he was righteous in his own eyes.

32:2

וַיִּחַרthis kindled אַףanger אֱלִיהוּאElihu בֶןson בַּרַכְאֵלBarachel הַבּוּזִיthe Buzite מִמִּשְׁפַּחַתof the family רָםRam בְּאִיּוֹבagainst Job חָרָהburned אַפּוֹwith anger עַלfor צַדְּקוֹjustifying himself נַפְשׁוֹhimself rather מֵאֱלֹהִיםthan God

This kindled the anger of Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite, of the family of Ram. He burned with anger against Job for justifying himself rather than God,

32:3

וּבִשְׁלֹשֶׁתagainst three רֵעָיוfriends חָרָהburned אַפּוֹburned עַלon אֲשֶׁרhad לֹאnot מָצְאוּfind מַעֲנֶהreply וַיַּרְשִׁיעוּrefute and yet had condemned אֶת[obj] אִיּוֹבJob

and he burned with anger against Job’s three friends because they had failed to refute Job, and yet had condemned him.

32:4

וֶאֱלִיהוּNow Elihu חִכָּהwaited אֶת[obj] אִיּוֹבJob בִּדְבָרִיםin words כִּיbecause זְקֵנִיםolder הֵמָּהwere מִמֶּנּוּthan he לְיָמִיםto days

Now Elihu had waited to speak to Job because the others were older than he.

32:5

וַיַּרְאsaw אֱלִיהוּאfurther כִּיfor אֵיןno מַעֲנֶהreply בְּפִיin mouth שְׁלֹשֶׁתthree הָאֲנָשִׁיםmen וַיִּחַרwas kindled אַפּוֹhis anger

But when he saw that the three men had no further reply, his anger was kindled.

32:6

וַיַּעַןand answered אֱלִיהוּאElihu בֶןson בַּרַכְאֵלBarachel הַבּוּזִיthe Buzite וַיֹּאמַרdeclared צָעִירI young אֲנִיam לְיָמִיםin years וְאַתֶּםwhile you יְשִׁישִׁיםold עַלon כֵּןso זָחַלְתִּיtimid וָאִירָאand afraid מֵחַוֺּתto tell דֵּעִיwhat I know אֶתְכֶםyou

So Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite declared: “I am young in years, while you are old; that is why I was timid and afraid to tell you what I know.

32:7

אָמַרְתִּיthought יָמִיםage יְדַבֵּרוּspeak וְרֹבand many שָׁנִיםyears יֹדִיעוּteach חָכְמָהwisdom

I thought that age should speak, and many years should teach wisdom.

32:8

אָכֵןbut רוּחַspirit הִיאis בֶאֱנוֹשׁin a man וְנִשְׁמַתbreath שַׁדַּיAlmighty תְּבִינֵםunderstanding

But there is a spirit in a man, the breath of the Almighty, that gives him understanding.

32:9

לֹאnot רַבִּיםold יֶחְכָּמוּwise וּזְקֵנִיםor the elderly יָבִינוּunderstand מִשְׁפָּטjustice

It is not only the old who are wise, or the elderly who understand justice.

32:10

לָכֵןtherefore אָמַרְתִּיsay שִׁמְעָהlisten לִּיto me אֲחַוֶּהdeclare דֵּעִיwhat אַףtoo אָנִיI

Therefore I say, ‘Listen to me; I too will declare what I know.’

32:11

הֵןindeed הוֹחַלְתִּיwaited לְדִבְרֵיכֶםyou spoke אָזִיןlistened עַדto תְּבוּנֹתֵיכֶםlistened your עַדuntil תַּחְקְרוּןsearched מִלִּיןwords

Indeed, I waited while you spoke; I listened to your reasoning; as you searched for words,

32:12

וְעָדֵיכֶםand to you אֶתְבּוֹנָןattention וְהִנֵּהand saw אֵיןno לְאִיּוֹבJob מוֹכִיחַwrong עוֹנֶהrebutted אֲמָרָיוhis arguments מִכֶּםnot one of you

I paid you full attention. But no one proved Job wrong; not one of you rebutted his arguments.

32:13

פֶּןnot תֹּאמְרוּclaim מָצָאנוּfound חָכְמָהwisdom אֵלGod יִדְּפֶנּוּrefute him לֹאnot אִישׁman

So do not claim, ‘We have found wisdom; let God, not man, refute him.’

32:14

וְלֹאnot עָרַךְdirected אֵלַיagainst me מִלִּיןwords וּבְאִמְרֵיכֶםwith your arguments לֹאnot אֲשִׁיבֶנּוּanswer him

But Job has not directed his words against me, and I will not answer him with your arguments.

32:15

חַתּוּdismayed לֹאno עָנוּfriends עוֹדmore הֶעְתִּיקוּescaped מֵהֶםof them מִלִּיםwords

Job’s friends are dismayed, with no more to say; words have escaped them.

32:16

וְהוֹחַלְתִּיwait now כִּיthat לֹאnot יְדַבֵּרוּspeak כִּיthat עָמְדוּstand לֹאno עָנוּreply עוֹדlonger

Must I wait, now that they are silent, now that they stand and no longer reply?

32:17

אַעֲנֶהanswer אַףyes אֲנִיI חֶלְקִיtoo אֲחַוֶּהdeclare דֵעִיwhat I אַףhow much אָנִיI

I too will answer; yes, I will declare what I know.

32:18

כִּיfor מָלֵתִיfull מִלִּיםwords הֱצִיקַתְנִיinflict me רוּחַspirit בִּטְנִיwithin me

For I am full of words, and my spirit within me compels me.

32:19

הִנֵּהbehold בִטְנִיmy belly כְּיַיִןlike unvented wine לֹאnot יִפָּתֵחַabout כְּאֹבוֹתwineskin חֲדָשִׁיםnew יִבָּקֵעַburst

Behold, my belly is like unvented wine; it is about to burst like a new wineskin.

32:20

אֲדַבְּרָהspeak וְיִרְוַחand find relief לִיto me אֶפְתַּחopen שְׂפָתַיmy lips וְאֶעֱנֶהand respond

I must speak and find relief; I must open my lips and respond.

32:21

אַלno נָאplease אֶשָּׂאlift פְנֵיpartial אִישׁone וְאֶלto אָדָםman לֹאnot אֲכַנֶּהflatter

I will be partial to no one, nor will I flatter any man.

32:22

כִּיfor לֹאnot יָדַעְתִּיknow אֲכַנֶּהflatter כִּמְעַטinstant יִשָּׂאֵנִיremove עֹשֵׂנִיMaker

For I do not know how to flatter, or my Maker would remove me in an instant.
