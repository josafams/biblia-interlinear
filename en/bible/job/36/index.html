Job 36

Elihu: affliction as instruction

Open in the interlinear reader

36:1

וַיֹּסֶףand again אֱלִיהוּאElihu וַיֹּאמַרcontinued

And Elihu continued:

36:2

כַּתַּרbear לִיto me זְעֵירlittle וַאֲחַוֶּךָּand I will show you כִּיthat עוֹדmore לֶאֱלוֹהַּto be said מִלִּיםGod’s

“Bear with me a little longer, and I will show you that there is more to be said on God’s behalf.

36:3

אֶשָּׂאlift דֵעִיmy knowledge לְמֵרָחוֹקfrom afar וּלְפֹעֲלִיto my Maker אֶתֵּןascribe צֶדֶקjustice

I get my knowledge from afar, and I will ascribe justice to my Maker.

36:4

כִּיfor אָמְנָםtruly לֹאnot שֶׁקֶרfalsehood מִלָּיmy words תְּמִיםperfect דֵּעוֹתknowledge עִמָּךְwith you

For truly my words are free of falsehood; one perfect in knowledge is with you.

36:5

הֶןindeed אֵלGod כַּבִּירmighty וְלֹאno יִמְאָסdespises כַּבִּירmighty כֹּחַstrength לֵבunderstanding

Indeed, God is mighty, but He despises no one; He is mighty in strength of understanding.

36:6

לֹאnot יְחַיֶּהalive רָשָׁעwicked וּמִשְׁפַּטjustice עֲנִיִּיםafflicted יִתֵּןgive

He does not keep the wicked alive, but He grants justice to the afflicted.

36:7

לֹאnot יִגְרַעoff מִצַּדִּיקoff the righteous עֵינָיוhis eyes וְאֶתwith מְלָכִיםkings לַכִּסֵּאexalts וַיֹּשִׁיבֵםand exalts them לָנֶצַחforever וַיִּגְבָּהוּand taller

He does not take His eyes off the righteous, but He enthrones them with kings and exalts them forever.

36:8

וְאִםand if אֲסוּרִיםbound בַּזִּקִּיםwith chains יִלָּכְדוּןcaught בְּחַבְלֵיin cords עֹנִיaffliction

And if men are bound with chains, caught in cords of affliction,

36:9

וַיַּגֵּדthen he tells לָהֶםto them פָּעֳלָםtheir deeds וּפִשְׁעֵיהֶםand how arrogantly they כִּיfor יִתְגַּבָּרוּtransgressed

then He tells them their deeds and how arrogantly they have transgressed.

36:10

וַיִּגֶלhe opens אָזְנָםtheir ears לַמּוּסָרto correction וַיֹּאמֶרand commands כִּיthat יְשֻׁבוּןturn מֵאָוֶןfrom iniquity

He opens their ears to correction and commands that they turn from iniquity.

36:11

אִםif יִשְׁמְעוּobey וְיַעֲבֹדוּand serve יְכַלּוּend יְמֵיהֶםtheir days בַּטּוֹבin prosperity וּשְׁנֵיהֶםand their years בַּנְּעִימִיםin happiness

If they obey and serve Him, then they end their days in prosperity and their years in happiness.

36:12

וְאִםbut if לֹאnot יִשְׁמְעוּobey בְּשֶׁלַחsword יַעֲבֹרוּcross וְיִגְוְעוּand die כִּבְלִיwithout דָעַתknowledge

But if they do not obey, then they perish by the sword and die without knowledge.

36:13

וְחַנְפֵיgodless לֵבheart יָשִׂימוּharbor אָףresentment לֹאnot יְשַׁוְּעוּcry help כִּיfor אֲסָרָםbinds them

The godless in heart harbor resentment; even when He binds them, they do not cry for help.

36:14

תָּמֹתdie בַּנֹּעַרin their youth נַפְשָׁםthey וְחַיָּתָםand their beasts בַּקְּדֵשִׁיםamong the male shrine prostitutes

They die in their youth, among the male shrine prostitutes.

36:15

יְחַלֵּץrescues עָנִיafflicted בְעָנְיוֹby their affliction וְיִגֶלand opens בַּלַּחַץin oppression אָזְנָםtheir ears

God rescues the afflicted by their affliction and opens their ears in oppression.

36:16

וְאַףindeed הֲסִיתְךָdrew you מִפִּיfrom the jaws צָרdistress רַחַבspacious לֹאnot מוּצָקbroad תַּחְתֶּיהָplace וְנַחַתand resolution שֻׁלְחָנְךָtable מָלֵאfull דָשֶׁןrichness

Indeed, He drew you from the jaws of distress to a spacious and broad place, to a table full of richness.

36:17

וְדִיןjudgment רָשָׁעwicked מָלֵאתָladen דִּיןjudgment וּמִשְׁפָּטand justice יִתְמֹכוּseized

But now you are laden with the judgment due the wicked; judgment and justice have seized you.

36:18

כִּיthat חֵמָהcareful פֶּןor יְסִיתְךָlures you בְסָפֶקwith riches וְרָבlet a large כֹּפֶרbribe אַלdo not יַטֶּךָּyou astray

Be careful that no one lures you with riches; do not let a large bribe lead you astray.

36:19

הֲיַעֲרֹךְcompare שׁוּעֲךָdistress לֹאnot בְצָרwealth וְכֹלor all מַאֲמַצֵּיmighty כֹחַeffort

Can your wealth or all your mighty effort keep you from distress?

36:20

אַלdo not תִּשְׁאַףlong הַלָּיְלָהthe night לַעֲלוֹתto up עַמִּיםpeople תַּחְתָּםunder them

Do not long for the night, when people vanish from their homes.

36:21

הִשָּׁמֶרcareful אַלnot תֵּפֶןturn אֶלto אָוֶןiniquity כִּיfor עַלon זֶהthis בָּחַרְתָּpreferred מֵעֹנִיto affliction

Be careful not to turn to iniquity, for this you have preferred to affliction.

36:22

הֶןbehold אֵלGod יַשְׂגִּיבexalted בְּכֹחוֹin his power מִיwho כָמֹהוּlike him מוֹרֶהteacher

Behold, God is exalted in His power. Who is a teacher like Him?

36:23

מִיwho פָקַדappointed עָלָיוon him דַּרְכּוֹway for him וּמִיand who אָמַרtold פָּעַלְתָּdone עַוְלָהwrong

Who has appointed His way for Him, or told Him, ‘You have done wrong’?

36:24

זְכֹרremember כִּיfor תַשְׂגִּיאmagnify פָעֳלוֹhis work אֲשֶׁרwhich שֹׁרְרוּpraised אֲנָשִׁיםmen

Remember to magnify His work, which men have praised in song.

36:25

כָּלall אָדָםmankind חָזוּseen בוֹin him אֱנוֹשׁmen יַבִּיטbehold מֵרָחוֹקfrom afar

All mankind has seen it; men behold it from afar.

36:26

הֶןindeed אֵלGod שַׂגִּיאgreat וְלֹאand not נֵדָעknowledge מִסְפַּרnumber שָׁנָיוhis years וְלֹאand not חֵקֶרunsearchable

Indeed, God is great—beyond our knowledge; the number of His years is unsearchable.

36:27

כִּיfor יְגָרַעdraws נִטְפֵיdrops מָיִםwater יָזֹקּוּdistill מָטָרrain לְאֵדוֹfrom the mist

For He draws up drops of water which distill the rain from the mist,

36:28

אֲשֶׁרwhich יִזְּלוּpour שְׁחָקִיםclouds יִרְעֲפוּshower abundantly עֲלֵיon אָדָםmankind רָבgreat

which the clouds pour out and shower abundantly on mankind.

36:29

אַףhow much אִםif יָבִיןunderstand מִפְרְשֵׂיhow עָבclouds תְּשֻׁאוֹתthunder סֻכָּתוֹhis pavilion

Furthermore, who can understand how the clouds spread out, how the thunder roars from His pavilion?

36:30

הֵןsee פָּרַשׂscatters עָלָיוon him אוֹרוֹlightning around him וְשָׁרְשֵׁיdepths הַיָּםthe sea כִּסָּהcovers

See how He scatters His lightning around Him and covers the depths of the sea.

36:31

כִּיfor בָםby these יָדִיןjudges עַמִּיםnations יִתֶּןprovides אֹכֶלfood לְמַכְבִּירin abundance

For by these He judges the nations and provides food in abundance.

36:32

עַלon כַּפַּיִםhands כִּסָּהcovered אוֹרlightning וַיְצַוand commands עָלֶיהָto its בְמַפְגִּיעַstrike mark

He fills His hands with lightning and commands it to strike its mark.

36:33

יַגִּידthunder עָלָיוon him רֵעוֹdeclares מִקְנֶהcattle אַףeven עַלon עוֹלֶהrising

The thunder declares His presence; even the cattle regard the rising storm.
