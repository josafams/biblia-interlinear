Job 39

God and the wild animals

Open in the interlinear reader

39:1

הֲיָדַעְתָּdo you know עֵתwhen לֶדֶתbear יַעֲלֵיgoats סָלַעmountain חֹלֵלbirth אַיָּלוֹתdoe תִּשְׁמֹרwatched

“Do you know when mountain goats give birth? Have you watched the doe bear her fawn?

39:2

תִּסְפֹּרcount יְרָחִיםmonths תְּמַלֶּאנָהpregnant וְיָדַעְתָּyou know עֵתtime לִדְתָּנָהbirth

Can you count the months they are pregnant? Do you know the time they give birth?

39:3

תִּכְרַעְנָהcrouch יַלְדֵיהֶןtheir young they תְּפַלַּחְנָהdeliver חֶבְלֵיהֶםtheir newborn תְּשַׁלַּחְנָהnewborn

They crouch down and bring forth their young; they deliver their newborn.

39:4

יַחְלְמוּones בְנֵיהֶםtheir young יִרְבּוּthrive בַבָּרin the open field יָצְאוּleave וְלֹאand do not שָׁבוּreturn לָמוֹto them

Their young ones thrive and grow up in the open field; they leave and do not return.

39:5

מִיwho שִׁלַּחset פֶּרֶאwild donkey חָפְשִׁיfree וּמֹסְרוֹתswift עָרוֹדwild ass מִיwho פִתֵּחַreleased

Who set the wild donkey free? Who released the swift donkey from the harness?

39:6

אֲשֶׁרthat שַׂמְתִּיmade עֲרָבָהwilderness בֵיתוֹhis home וּמִשְׁכְּנוֹתָיוhis dwelling מְלֵחָהsalt flats

I made the wilderness his home and the salt flats his dwelling.

39:7

יִשְׂחַקscorns לַהֲמוֹןthe tumult קִרְיָהcity תְּשֻׁאוֹתshouts נוֹגֵשׂdriver לֹאnever יִשְׁמָעhears

He scorns the tumult of the city and never hears the shouts of a driver.

39:8

יְתוּרroams הָרִיםmountains מִרְעֵהוּpasture searching וְאַחַרand after כָּלany יָרוֹקgreen יִדְרוֹשׁthing

He roams the mountains for pasture, searching for any green thing.

39:9

הֲיֹאבֶהconsent רֵּיםwild ox עָבְדֶךָserve you אִםif יָלִיןstay עַלon אֲבוּסֶךָyour manger

Will the wild ox consent to serve you? Will he stay by your manger at night?

39:10

הֲתִקְשָׁרcan you hold רֵיםfurrow בְּתֶלֶםfurrow with עֲבֹתוֹhis ropes אִםif יְשַׂדֵּדplow עֲמָקִיםvalleys אַחֲרֶיךָbehind you

Can you hold him to the furrow with a harness? Will he plow the valleys behind you?

39:11

הֲתִבְטַחcan you rely בּוֹon his כִּיfor רַבgreat כֹּחוֹstrength will וְתַעֲזֹבyou leave אֵלָיוto him יְגִיעֶךָyour hard work

Can you rely on his great strength? Will you leave your hard work to him?

39:12

הֲתַאֲמִיןcan you trust בּוֹin him כִּיfor יָשִׁיבbring זַרְעֶךָyour grain וְגָרְנְךָyour threshing floor יֶאֱסֹףgather

Can you trust him to bring in your grain and gather it to your threshing floor?

39:13

כְּנַףwings רְנָנִיםostrich נֶעֱלָסָהflap joyfully אִםif אֶבְרָהcannot חֲסִידָהstork וְנֹצָהand feathers

The wings of the ostrich flap joyfully, but cannot match the pinions and feathers of the stork.

39:14

כִּיfor תַעֲזֹבleaves לָאָרֶץon the ground בֵּצֶיהָher eggs וְעַלin עָפָרlets תְּחַמֵּםwarm

For she leaves her eggs on the ground and lets them warm in the sand.

39:15

וַתִּשְׁכַּחshe forgets כִּיthat רֶגֶלfoot תְּזוּרֶהָcrush them וְחַיַּתanimal הַשָּׂדֶהa wild תְּדוּשֶׁהָtrample them

She forgets that a foot may crush them, or a wild animal may trample them.

39:16

הִקְשִׁיחַtreats בָּנֶיהָher young לְּלֹאas if not לָהּto her לְרִיקin vain יְגִיעָהּher labor בְּלִיno פָחַדconcern

She treats her young harshly, as if not her own, with no concern that her labor was in vain.

39:17

כִּיfor הִשָּׁהּdeprived her אֱלוֹהַּGod חָכְמָהwisdom וְלֹאhe has not חָלַקendowed לָהּto her בַּבִּינָהwith understanding

For God has deprived her of wisdom; He has not endowed her with understanding.

39:18

כָּעֵתwhen she בַּמָּרוֹםproudly her תַּמְרִיאspreads wings תִּשְׂחַקlaughs לַסּוּסat the horse וּלְרֹכְבוֹand its rider

Yet when she proudly spreads her wings, she laughs at the horse and its rider.

39:19

הֲתִתֵּןdo you give לַסּוּסto the horse גְּבוּרָהstrength הֲתַלְבִּישׁor adorn צַוָּארוֹhis neck with רַעְמָהmane

Do you give strength to the horse or adorn his neck with a mane?

39:20

הְתַרְעִישֶׁנּוּhim leap כָּאַרְבֶּהlike a locust הוֹדstriking נַחְרוֹhis snorting אֵימָהterror

Do you make him leap like a locust, striking terror with his proud snorting?

39:21

יַחְפְּרוּpaws בָעֵמֶקin the valley וְיָשִׂישׂand rejoices בְּכֹחַin his strength יֵצֵאgo לִקְרַאתcharges נָשֶׁקbattle

He paws in the valley and rejoices in his strength; he charges into battle.

39:22

יִשְׂחַקlaughs לְפַחַדat fear וְלֹאand not יֵחָתfrightened וְלֹאhe does not יָשׁוּבturn מִפְּנֵיfrom before חָרֶבsword

He laughs at fear, frightened of nothing; he does not turn back from the sword.

39:23

עָלָיוon him תִּרְנֶהrattles אַשְׁפָּהquiver לַהַבflashing חֲנִיתspear וְכִידוֹןand lance

A quiver rattles at his side, along with a flashing spear and lance.

39:24

בְּרַעַשׁtrembling with וְרֹגֶזwith excitement he devours יְגַמֶּאdevours אָרֶץdistance וְלֹאand not יַאֲמִיןstand כִּיfor קוֹלsounds שׁוֹפָרram’s horn

Trembling with excitement, he devours the distance; he cannot stand still when the ram’s horn sounds.

39:25

בְּדֵיat the blast שֹׁפָרhorn יֹאמַרsays הֶאָחsnorts וּמֵרָחוֹקfrom afar יָרִיחַcatches מִלְחָמָהbattle רַעַםscent שָׂרִיםcaptains וּתְרוּעָהand the cry

At the blast of the horn, he snorts with fervor. He catches the scent of battle from afar— the shouts of captains and the cry of war.

39:26

הֲמִבִּינָתְךָyour understanding יַאֲבֶרflight נֵץhawk יִפְרֹשׂspread כְּנָפָיוhis wings לְתֵימָןtoward the south

Does the hawk take flight by your understanding and spread his wings toward the south?

39:27

אִםif עַלon פִּיךָyour command יַגְבִּיהַּsoar נָשֶׁרeagle וְכִיand if יָרִיםhigh קִנּוֹhis nest

Does the eagle soar at your command and make his nest on high?

39:28

סֶלַעcliff יִשְׁכֹּןdwells וְיִתְלֹנָןand lodges עַלon שֶׁןrocky סֶלַעcrag וּמְצוּדָהstronghold

He dwells on a cliff and lodges there; his stronghold is on a rocky crag.

39:29

מִשָּׁםfrom there חָפַרspies אֹכֶלfood לְמֵרָחוֹקfrom afar עֵינָיוhis eyes it יַבִּיטוּsee

From there he spies out food; his eyes see it from afar.

39:30

וְאֶפְרֹחָיוhis young יְעַלְעוּfeast דָםblood וּבַאֲשֶׁרand in you חֲלָלִיםslain שָׁםthere הוּאhe is

His young ones feast on blood; and where the slain are, there he is.”
