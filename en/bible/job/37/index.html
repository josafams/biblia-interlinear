Job 37

God in the storm, the snow and the rain

Open in the interlinear reader

37:1

אַףalso לְזֹאתat this יֶחֱרַדpounds לִבִּיmy heart וְיִתַּרand leaps מִמְּקוֹמוֹfrom its place

“At this my heart also pounds and leaps from its place.

37:2

שִׁמְעוּlisten שָׁמוֹעַclosely בְּרֹגֶזclosely to קֹלוֹhis voice וְהֶגֶהand the rumbling מִפִּיוfrom his mouth יֵצֵאgo

Listen closely to the thunder of His voice and the rumbling that comes from His mouth.

37:3

תַּחַתbeneath כָּלwhole הַשָּׁמַיִםthe sky יִשְׁרֵהוּunleashes his וְאוֹרוֹlightning עַלto כַּנְפוֹתends הָאָרֶץthe earth

He unleashes His lightning beneath the whole sky and sends it to the ends of the earth.

37:4

אַחֲרָיוafter him יִשְׁאַגroaring קוֹלsound יַרְעֵםthunders בְּקוֹלvoice גְּאוֹנוֹhis majestic וְלֹאhe does not יְעַקְּבֵםrestrain the lightning כִּיwhen יִשָּׁמַעresounds קוֹלוֹhis voice

Then there comes a roaring sound; He thunders with His majestic voice. He does not restrain the lightning when His voice resounds.

37:5

יַרְעֵםthunders אֵלGod wondrously בְּקוֹלוֹwith his voice נִפְלָאוֹתhe עֹשֶׂהdoes גְדֹלוֹתgreat וְלֹאand not נֵדָעcomprehend

God thunders wondrously with His voice; He does great things we cannot comprehend.

37:6

כִּיfor לַשֶּׁלַגto the snow יֹאמַרsays הֱוֵאfall אָרֶץearth וְגֶשֶׁםgentle מָטָרrain וְגֶשֶׁםpour מִטְרוֹתdownpour עֻזּוֹmighty

For He says to the snow, ‘Fall on the earth,’ and to the gentle rain, ‘Pour out a mighty downpour.’

37:7

בְּיַדhand כָּלevery אָדָםman יַחְתּוֹםso לָדַעַתknow כָּלall אַנְשֵׁיmen מַעֲשֵׂהוּhis work

He seals up the hand of every man, so that all men may know His work.

37:8

וַתָּבֹאenter חַיָּהwild animals בְמוֹlairs אָרֶבthicket וּבִמְעוֹנֹתֶיהָin their dens תִשְׁכֹּןsettle

The wild animals enter their lairs; they settle down in their dens.

37:9

מִןfrom הַחֶדֶרits chamber תָּבוֹאcomes סוּפָהtempest וּמִמְּזָרִיםfrom the driving north קָרָהcold

The tempest comes from its chamber, and the cold from the driving north winds.

37:10

מִנִּשְׁמַתby the breath אֵלGod יִתֶּןgive קָרַחice וְרֹחַבand the watery מַיִםexpanses בְּמוּצָקfrozen

By the breath of God the ice is formed and the watery expanses are frozen.

37:11

אַףhow much בְּרִיwith moisture יַטְרִיחַhe loads עָבclouds יָפִיץscatters עֲנַןcloud אוֹרוֹlightning through them

He loads the clouds with moisture; He scatters His lightning through them.

37:12

וְהוּאand he מְסִבּוֹתswirl מִתְהַפֵּךְwhirling בְּתַחְבּוּלֹתָיוwhirling at his לְפָעֳלָםdirection accomplishing כֹּלall אֲשֶׁרthat יְצַוֵּםcommands עַלover פְּנֵיface תֵבֵלall אָרְצָהearth

They swirl about, whirling at His direction, accomplishing all that He commands over the face of all the earth.

37:13

אִםif לְשֵׁבֶטfor punishment אִםor לְאַרְצוֹfor his land אִםif לְחֶסֶדin loving devotion יַמְצִאֵהוּfound him

Whether for punishment or for His land, He accomplishes this in His loving devotion.

37:14

הַאֲזִינָהlisten זֹּאתthis אִיּוֹבJob עֲמֹדstand וְהִתְבּוֹנֵןand consider נִפְלְאוֹתwonders אֵלGod

Listen to this, O Job; stand still and consider the wonders of God.

37:15

הֲתֵדַעdo you know בְּשׂוּםhow אֱלוֹהַּGod עֲלֵיהֶםon them וְהוֹפִיעַor makes the flash אוֹרlightning עֲנָנוֹclouds

Do you know how God dispatches the clouds or makes the lightning flash?

37:16

הֲתֵדַעdo you understand עַלon מִפְלְשֵׂיfloat עָבclouds מִפְלְאוֹתwonders תְּמִיםperfect דֵּעִיםknowledge

Do you understand how the clouds float, those wonders of Him who is perfect in knowledge?

37:17

אֲשֶׁרthat בְּגָדֶיךָyou clothes חַמִּיםget hot בְּהַשְׁקִטhot when אֶרֶץland מִדָּרוֹםunder the south wind

You whose clothes get hot when the land lies hushed under the south wind,

37:18

תַּרְקִיעַspread עִמּוֹwith him לִשְׁחָקִיםskies to חֲזָקִיםheat כִּרְאִיlike a mirror מוּצָקbronze

can you, like Him, spread out the skies to reflect the heat like a mirror of bronze?

37:19

הוֹדִיעֵנוּteach us we מַהwhat should נֹּאמַרsay לוֹto him לֹאnot נַעֲרֹךְcase מִפְּנֵיfaces are in חֹשֶׁךְdarkness

Teach us what we should say to Him; we cannot draw up our case when our faces are in darkness.

37:20

הַיְסֻפַּרtold לוֹto him כִּיthat אֲדַבֵּרspeak אִםif אָמַרask אִישׁman כִּיfor יְבֻלָּעswallowed

Should He be told that I want to speak? Would a man ask to be swallowed up?

37:21

וְעַתָּהnow לֹאno רָאוּgaze אוֹרsun בָּהִירbright הוּאis בַּשְּׁחָקִיםin the skies וְרוּחַafter the wind עָבְרָהswept וַתְּטַהֲרֵםthem clean

Now no one can gaze at the sun when it is bright in the skies after the wind has swept them clean.

37:22

מִצָּפוֹןnorth זָהָבgolden יֶאֱתֶהsurrounds עַלin אֱלוֹהַּsplendor נוֹרָאawesome הוֹדmajesty

Out of the north He comes in golden splendor; awesome majesty surrounds Him.

37:23

שַׁדַּיAlmighty לֹאnot מְצָאנֻהוּreach he שַׂגִּיאbeyond כֹחַpower וּמִשְׁפָּטjustice וְרֹבand great צְדָקָהrighteousness לֹאnot יְעַנֶּהoppress

The Almighty is beyond our reach; He is exalted in power! In His justice and great righteousness He does not oppress.

37:24

לָכֵןtherefore יְרֵאוּהוּfear him אֲנָשִׁיםmen לֹאnot יִרְאֶהpartial כָּלall חַכְמֵיwise לֵבheart

Therefore, men fear Him, for He is not partial to the wise in heart.”
