Job 7

Life is brief; a complaint to God

Open in the interlinear reader

7:1

הֲלֹאis not צָבָאconsigned labor לֶאֱנוֹשׁto labor עֲלֵיon אָרֶץearth וְכִימֵיdays like שָׂכִירhired hand יָמָיוhis days

“Is not man consigned to labor on earth? Are not his days like those of a hired hand?

7:2

כְּעֶבֶדlike a slave יִשְׁאַףlongs צֵלshade וּכְשָׂכִירlike a hireling יְקַוֶּהwaits פָעֳלוֹhis wages

Like a slave he longs for shade; like a hireling he waits for his wages.

7:3

כֵּןso הָנְחַלְתִּיallotted לִיto me יַרְחֵיmonths שָׁוְאfutility וְלֵילוֹתand nights עָמָלmisery מִנּוּappointed לִיto me

So I am allotted months of futility, and nights of misery are appointed me.

7:4

אִםif שָׁכַבְתִּיlie וְאָמַרְתִּיI think מָתַיwhen אָקוּםget וּמִדַּדdrags עָרֶבnight וְשָׂבַעְתִּיand I toss נְדֻדִיםturn עֲדֵיuntil נָשֶׁףdawn

When I lie down I think: ‘When will I get up?’ But the night drags on, and I toss and turn until dawn.

7:5

לָבַשׁclothed בְּשָׂרִיmy flesh רִמָּהworms וְגוּשׁand encrusted עָפָרdirt עוֹרִיmy skin רָגַעcracked וַיִּמָּאֵסand festering

My flesh is clothed with worms and encrusted with dirt; my skin is cracked and festering.

7:6

יָמַיmy days קַלּוּswifter מִנִּיthan אָרֶגweaver’s shuttle וַיִּכְלוּend בְּאֶפֶסwithout תִּקְוָהhope

My days are swifter than a weaver’s shuttle; they come to an end without hope.

7:7

זְכֹרremember כִּיthat רוּחַbreath חַיָּיmy life לֹאnever תָשׁוּבagain עֵינִיmy eyes לִרְאוֹתsee טוֹבhappiness

Remember that my life is but a breath. My eyes will never again see happiness.

7:8

לֹאno תְשׁוּרֵנִיsee me עֵיןeye רֹאִיbeholds עֵינֶיךָyou will look בִּיin me וְאֵינֶנִּיno

The eye that beholds me will no longer see me. You will look for me, but I will be no more.

7:9

כָּלָהvanishes עָנָןcloud וַיֵּלַךְand is gone כֵּןso יוֹרֵדdown שְׁאוֹלSheol לֹאnot יַעֲלֶהup

As a cloud vanishes and is gone, so he who goes down to Sheol does not come back up.

7:10

לֹאnever יָשׁוּבreturns עוֹדlonger לְבֵיתוֹto his house his וְלֹאno יַכִּירֶנּוּremembers him עוֹדmore מְקֹמוֹplace

He never returns to his house; his place remembers him no more.

7:11

גַּםeven אֲנִיI לֹאnot אֶחֱשָׂךְrestrain פִּיmy mouth I אֲדַבְּרָהspeak בְּצַרin the anguish רוּחִיmy spirit אָשִׂיחָהcomplain בְּמַרin the bitterness נַפְשִׁיmy soul

Therefore I will not restrain my mouth; I will speak in the anguish of my spirit; I will complain in the bitterness of my soul.

7:12

הֲיָםsea אָנִיI אִםor תַּנִּיןmonster כִּיthat תָשִׂיםkeep עָלַיon me מִשְׁמָרguard

Am I the sea, or the monster of the deep, that You must keep me under guard?

7:13

כִּיwhen אָמַרְתִּיthink תְּנַחֲמֵנִיcomfort עַרְשִׂיcouch יִשָּׂאbear בְשִׂיחִיwill ease my complaint מִשְׁכָּבִיbed

When I think my bed will comfort me and my couch will ease my complaint,

7:14

וְחִתַּתַּנִיthen you frighten me בַחֲלֹמוֹתwith dreams וּמֵחֶזְיֹנוֹתwith visions תְּבַעֲתַנִּיterrify me

then You frighten me with dreams and terrify me with visions,

7:15

וַתִּבְחַרwould prefer מַחֲנָקstrangling נַפְשִׁיmy life מָוֶתdeath מֵעַצְמוֹתָיthis

so that I would prefer strangling and death over my life in this body.

7:16

מָאַסְתִּיloathe לֹאnot לְעֹלָםforever אֶחְיֶהlive חֲדַלleave מִמֶּנִּיfrom me כִּיfor הֶבֶלbreath יָמָיmy days

I loathe my life! I would not live forever. Leave me alone, for my days are but a breath.

7:17

מָהwhat אֱנוֹשׁman כִּיthat תְגַדְּלֶנּוּexalt him וְכִיand if תָשִׁיתset אֵלָיוto him לִבֶּךָyour heart

What is man that You should exalt him, that You should set Your heart upon him,

7:18

וַתִּפְקְדֶנּוּthat you attend him לִבְקָרִיםto every morning לִרְגָעִיםmoment תִּבְחָנֶנּוּtest him

that You attend to him every morning, and test him every moment?

7:19

כַּמָּהlike how לֹאnever תִשְׁעֶהlook מִמֶּנִּיfrom me לֹאor תַרְפֵּנִיleave me עַדto בִּלְעִיswallow רֻקִּיmy spittle

Will You never look away from me, or leave me alone to swallow my spittle?

7:20

חָטָאתִיsinned מָהwhat אֶפְעַלdone לָךְto you נֹצֵרwatcher הָאָדָםof mankind לָמָהto why שַׂמְתַּנִיmade me לְמִפְגָּעyour target לָךְto you וָאֶהְיֶהand been עָלַיon me לְמַשָּׂאa burden to

If I have sinned, what have I done to You, O watcher of mankind? Why have You made me Your target, so that I am a burden to You?

7:21

וּמֶהwhy לֹאnot תִשָּׂאpardon פִשְׁעִיmy transgression וְתַעֲבִירand take away אֶת[obj] עֲוֺנִיmy iniquity כִּיfor עַתָּהnow לֶעָפָרin the dust אֶשְׁכָּבlie וְשִׁחֲרְתַּנִיwill seek me וְאֵינֶנִּיno

Why do You not pardon my transgression and take away my iniquity? For soon I will lie down in the dust; You will seek me, but I will be no more.”
