Job 11

Zophar calls Job to repentance

Open in the interlinear reader

11:1

וַיַּעַןthen צֹפַרZophar הַנַּעֲמָתִיthe Naamathite וַיֹּאמַרreplied

Then Zophar the Naamathite replied:

11:2

הֲרֹבshould this stream דְּבָרִיםwords לֹאnot יֵעָנֶהunanswered וְאִםand if אִישׁman שְׂפָתַיִםspeaker יִצְדָּקvindicated

“Should this stream of words go unanswered and such a speaker be vindicated?

11:3

בַּדֶּיךָyour babbling מְתִיםbabbling יַחֲרִישׁוsilence וַתִּלְעַגscoff וְאֵיןwithout מַכְלִםrebuke

Should your babbling put others to silence? Will you scoff without rebuke?

11:4

וַתֹּאמֶרyou have said זַךְdoctrine לִקְחִיsound וּבַרpure הָיִיתִיam בְעֵינֶיךָin your sight

You have said, ‘My doctrine is sound, and I am pure in Your sight.’

11:5

וְאוּלָםbut מִיwho יִתֵּןonly would אֱלוֹהַּGod דַּבֵּרspeak וְיִפְתַּחand open שְׂפָתָיוhis lips עִמָּךְagainst you

But if only God would speak and open His lips against you,

11:6

וְיַגֶּדand disclose לְךָto you תַּעֲלֻמוֹתsecrets חָכְמָהwisdom כִּיfor כִפְלַיִםtrue לְתוּשִׁיָּהwisdom וְדַעknow then כִּיthat יַשֶּׁהexacts לְךָto you אֱלוֹהַGod מֵעֲוֺנֶךָthan your iniquity

and disclose to you the secrets of wisdom, for true wisdom has two sides. Know then that God exacts from you less than your iniquity deserves.

11:7

הַחֵקֶרfathom the אֱלוֹהַGod תִּמְצָאfind אִםor עַדuntil תַּכְלִיתlimits שַׁדַּיAlmighty תִּמְצָאdiscover

Can you fathom the deep things of God or discover the limits of the Almighty?

11:8

גָּבְהֵיhigher שָׁמַיִםheavens מַהwhat תִּפְעָלdo עֲמֻקָּהdeeper מִשְּׁאוֹלthan Sheol מַהwhat תֵּדָעknow

They are higher than the heavens—what can you do? They are deeper than Sheol—what can you know?

11:9

אֲרֻכָּהmeasure מֵאֶרֶץthan the earth מִדָּהּher shall וּרְחָבָהand wider מִנִּיthan יָםsea

Their measure is longer than the earth and wider than the sea.

11:10

אִםif יַחֲלֹףcomes וְיַסְגִּירalong to imprison וְיַקְהִילor convenes וּמִיcourt who can יְשִׁיבֶנּוּstop him

If He comes along to imprison you, or convenes a court, who can stop Him?

11:11

כִּיfor הוּאhe יָדַעknows מְתֵיmen שָׁוְאdeceit וַיַּרְאhe sees אָוֶןiniquity וְלֹאhe not יִתְבּוֹנָןnote

Surely He knows the deceit of men. If He sees iniquity, does He not take note?

11:12

וְאִישׁman נָבוּבwitless יִלָּבֵבwise וְעַיִרcolt פֶּרֶאwild donkey אָדָםman יִוָּלֵדborn

But a witless man can no more become wise than the colt of a wild donkey can be born a man!

11:13

אִםif אַתָּהyou הֲכִינוֹתָdirect לִבֶּךָyour heart וּפָרַשְׂתָּand lift אֵלָיוto him כַּפֶּךָyour hands

As for you, if you direct your heart and lift up your hands to Him,

11:14

אִםif אָוֶןiniquity בְּיָדְךָin your hand הַרְחִיקֵהוּfar him וְאַלand allow no תַּשְׁכֵּןdwell בְּאֹהָלֶיךָin your tents עַוְלָהinjustice

if you put away the iniquity in your hand, and allow no injustice to dwell in your tents,

11:15

כִּיfor אָזthen תִּשָּׂאlift פָנֶיךָyour face מִמּוּםwithout shame וְהָיִיתָwill stand מֻצָקfirm וְלֹאand not תִירָאunafraid

then indeed you will lift up your face without shame; you will stand firm and unafraid.

11:16

כִּיfor אַתָּהyou עָמָלmisery תִּשְׁכָּחforget כְּמַיִםas waters עָבְרוּgone by תִזְכֹּרremember

For you will forget your misery, recalling it only as waters gone by.

11:17

וּמִצָּהֳרַיִםthan noonday יָקוּםstand חָלֶדits תָּעֻפָהbrighter כַּבֹּקֶרlike the morning תִּהְיֶהbe

Your life will be brighter than noonday; its darkness will be like the morning.

11:18

וּבָטַחְתָּwill be secure כִּיbecause יֵשׁthere תִּקְוָהhope וְחָפַרְתָּlook לָבֶטַחin safety תִּשְׁכָּבlie

You will be secure, because there is hope, and you will look around and lie down in safety.

11:19

וְרָבַצְתָּyou will lie down וְאֵיןwithout מַחֲרִידfear וְחִלּוּcourt פָנֶיךָyour favor רַבִּיםmany

You will lie down without fear, and many will court your favor.

11:20

וְעֵינֵיbut the eyes רְשָׁעִיםwicked תִּכְלֶינָהfail וּמָנוֹסand escape אָבַדescape will מִנְהֶםfrom them וְתִקְוָתָםthey will hope for their מַפַּחlast נָפֶשׁbreath

But the eyes of the wicked will fail, and escape will elude them; they will hope for their last breath.”
