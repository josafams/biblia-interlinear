Job 12

Job answers: divine wisdom and power

Open in the interlinear reader

12:1

וַיַּעַןthen answered אִיּוֹבJob וַיֹּאמַרand said

Then Job answered:

12:2

אָמְנָםtruly כִּיfor אַתֶּםare עָםpeople וְעִמָּכֶםwith תָּמוּתdie חָכְמָהwisdom

“Truly then you are the people with whom wisdom itself will die!

12:3

גַּםalso לִיhave a לֵבָבmind כְּמוֹכֶםlike you לֹאnot נֹפֵלinferior אָנֹכִיam מִכֶּםfrom of you וְאֶתand with מִיwho אֵיןnot כְּמוֹas אֵלֶּהthese

But I also have a mind; I am not inferior to you. Who does not know such things as these?

12:4

שְׂחֹקlaughingstock לְרֵעֵהוּto my friends אֶהְיֶהbe קֹרֵאcalled לֶאֱלוֹהַּon God וַיַּעֲנֵהוּand he answered שְׂחוֹקlaughingstock צַדִּיקrighteous תָּמִיםupright

I am a laughingstock to my friends, though I called on God, and He answered. The righteous and upright man is a laughingstock.

12:5

לַפִּידthe one at scorns בּוּזmisfortune לְעַשְׁתּוּתscorns as שַׁאֲנָןease נָכוֹןestablished לְמוֹעֲדֵיfate רָגֶלfeet

The one at ease scorns misfortune as the fate of those whose feet are slipping.

12:6

יִשְׁלָיוּease אֹהָלִיםtents לְשֹׁדְדִיםof robbers וּבַטֻּחוֹתsafe and לְמַרְגִּיזֵיprovoke אֵלGod לַאֲשֶׁרsecure those who הֵבִיאcarry אֱלוֹהַּgod בְּיָדוֹin their hands

The tents of robbers are safe, and those who provoke God are secure— those who carry their god in their hands.

12:7

וְאוּלָםbut שְׁאַלask נָאplease בְהֵמוֹתanimals וְתֹרֶךָּinstruct you ask וְעוֹףbirds הַשָּׁמַיִםthe air וְיַגֶּדtell לָךְto you

But ask the animals, and they will instruct you; ask the birds of the air, and they will tell you.

12:8

אוֹor שִׂיחַspeak לָאָרֶץto the earth וְתֹרֶךָּteach you וִיסַפְּרוּinform לְךָto you דְּגֵיfish הַיָּםthe sea

Or speak to the earth, and it will teach you; let the fish of the sea inform you.

12:9

מִיwho לֹאnot יָדַעknow בְּכָלall אֵלֶּהthese כִּיthat יַדhand יְהוָהLORD עָשְׂתָהdone זֹּאתthis

Which of all these does not know that the hand of the LORD has done this?

12:10

אֲשֶׁרthat בְּיָדוֹin his hand נֶפֶשׁlife כָּלevery חָיliving וְרוּחַbreath כָּלall בְּשַׂרmankind אִישׁman

The life of every living thing is in His hand, as well as the breath of all mankind.

12:11

הֲלֹאdoes not אֹזֶןear מִלִּיןwords תִּבְחָןtest וְחֵךְas the tongue אֹכֶלfood יִטְעַםtastes לוֹto him

Does not the ear test words as the tongue tastes its food?

12:12

בִּישִׁישִׁיםwith the elderly חָכְמָהwisdom וְאֹרֶךְlong יָמִיםlife תְּבוּנָהunderstanding

Wisdom is found with the elderly, and understanding comes with long life.

12:13

עִמּוֹwith him חָכְמָהwisdom וּגְבוּרָהand strength לוֹto עֵצָהcounsel וּתְבוּנָהand understanding

Wisdom and strength belong to God; counsel and understanding are His.

12:14

הֵןbehold יַהֲרוֹסtears down וְלֹאand not יִבָּנֶהrebuilt יִסְגֹּרimprisons עַלon אִישׁman וְלֹאand not יִפָּתֵחַreleased

What He tears down cannot be rebuilt; the man He imprisons cannot be released.

12:15

הֵןif יַעְצֹרholds בַּמַּיִםthe waters וְיִבָשׁוּthey dry וִישַׁלְּחֵםand if he releases them וְיַהַפְכוּthey overwhelm אָרֶץland

If He holds back the waters, they dry up, and if He releases them, they overwhelm the land.

12:16

עִמּוֹwith him עֹזpower וְתוּשִׁיָּהwisdom and לוֹto שֹׁגֵגdeceived וּמַשְׁגֶּהand the deceiver

True wisdom and power belong to Him. The deceived and the deceiver are His.

12:17

מוֹלִיךְhe leads יוֹעֲצִיםcounselors שׁוֹלָלbarefoot וְשֹׁפְטִיםjudges יְהוֹלֵלfools

He leads counselors away barefoot and makes fools of judges.

12:18

מוּסַרloosens מְלָכִיםkings פִּתֵּחַbonds וַיֶּאְסֹרand fastens אֵזוֹרbelt בְּמָתְנֵיהֶםaround their waists

He loosens the bonds placed by kings and fastens a belt around their waists.

12:19

מוֹלִיךְhe leads כֹּהֲנִיםpriests שׁוֹלָלbarefoot וְאֵתָנִיםand overthrows יְסַלֵּףestablished

He leads priests away barefoot and overthrows the established.

12:20

מֵסִירdeprives שָׂפָהspeech לְנֶאֱמָנִיםthe trusted וְטַעַםdiscernment זְקֵנִיםelders יִקָּחtakes

He deprives the trusted of speech and takes away the discernment of elders.

12:21

שׁוֹפֵךְpours בּוּזcontempt עַלon נְדִיבִיםnobles וּמְזִיחַand disarms אֲפִיקִיםdisarms רִפָּהmighty

He pours out contempt on nobles and disarms the mighty.

12:22

מְגַלֶּהreveals עֲמֻקוֹתdeep מִנִּיof חֹשֶׁךְdarkness וַיֹּצֵאand brought לָאוֹרinto light צַלְמָוֶתdeep shadows

He reveals the deep things of darkness and brings deep shadows into light.

12:23

מַשְׂגִּיאmakes לַגּוֹיִםnations וַיְאַבְּדֵםand destroys them שֹׁטֵחַenlarges לַגּוֹיִםnations וַיַּנְחֵםthen disperses them

He makes nations great and destroys them; He enlarges nations, then disperses them.

12:24

מֵסִירdeprives לֵבheart רָאשֵׁיleaders עַםreason הָאָרֶץthe earth’s וַיַּתְעֵםand makes them wander בְּתֹהוּin a trackless לֹאnot דָרֶךְwasteland

He deprives the earth’s leaders of reason and makes them wander in a trackless wasteland.

12:25

יְמַשְׁשׁוּgrope חֹשֶׁךְdarkness וְלֹאand not אוֹרlight וַיַּתְעֵםhe makes them stagger כַּשִּׁכּוֹרlike drunkards

They grope in the darkness without light; He makes them stagger like drunkards.
