Job 15

Eliphaz describes the fate of the wicked

Then Eliphaz the Temanite replied:

“Does a wise man answer with empty counsel or fill his belly with the hot east wind?

15:3

Should he argue with useless words or speeches that serve no purpose?

15:4

אַףeven אַתָּהyou תָּפֵרundermine יִרְאָהfear וְתִגְרַעand hinder שִׂיחָהmeditation לִפְנֵיbefore אֵלGod

But you even undermine the fear of God and hinder meditation before Him.

15:5

כִּיfor יְאַלֵּףinstructs עֲוֺנְךָiniquity your פִיךָmouth you וְתִבְחַרand choose לְשׁוֹןlanguage עֲרוּמִיםcrafty

For your iniquity instructs your mouth, and you choose the language of the crafty.

15:6

יַרְשִׁיעֲךָcondemns פִיךָmouth וְלֹאown not אָנִיI וּשְׂפָתֶיךָyour own lips יַעֲנוּtestify בָךְagainst you

Your own mouth, not mine, condemns you; your own lips testify against you.

15:7

הֲרִאישׁוֹןyou the first אָדָםman תִּוָּלֵדborn וְלִפְנֵיbefore גְבָעוֹתhills חוֹלָלְתָּforth

Were you the first man ever born? Were you brought forth before the hills?

15:8

הַבְסוֹדcouncil אֱלוֹהַGod תִּשְׁמָעlisten וְתִגְרַעor limit אֵלֶיךָto yourself חָכְמָהwisdom

Do you listen in on the council of God or limit wisdom to yourself?

15:9

מַהwhat יָּדַעְתָּknow וְלֹאwe do not נֵדָעyou understand תָּבִיןunderstand וְלֹאthat not עִמָּנוּto us הוּאis

What do you know that we do not? What do you understand that is not clear to us?

15:10

גַּםboth שָׂבgray haired גַּםand יָשִׁישׁaged בָּנוּon our כַּבִּירmuch מֵאָבִיךָolder than your father יָמִיםdays

Both the gray-haired and the aged are on our side— men much older than your father.

15:11

הַמְעַטnot enough מִמְּךָfrom you תַּנְחֻמוֹתconsolations אֵלGod וְדָבָרeven words לָאַטspoken gently עִמָּךְto you

Are the consolations of God not enough for you, even words spoken gently to you?

15:12

מַהwhy יִּקָּחֲךָyour carried לִבֶּךָheart you וּמַהand why יִּרְזְמוּןflash עֵינֶיךָyour eyes

Why has your heart carried you away, and why do your eyes flash,

15:13

כִּיfor תָשִׁיבturn אֶלagainst אֵלGod רוּחֶךָyour spirit וְהֹצֵאתָand pour מִפִּיךָfrom your mouth מִלִּיןwords

as you turn your spirit against God and pour such words from your mouth?

15:14

מָהwhat אֱנוֹשׁman כִּיthat יִזְכֶּהpure וְכִיthat יִצְדַּקrighteous יְלוּדborn אִשָּׁהwoman

What is man, that he should be pure, or one born of woman, that he should be righteous?

15:15

הֵןif בִּקְדֹשָׁיוin his holy לֹאno יַאֲמִיןtrust וְשָׁמַיִםeven the heavens לֹאnot זַכּוּpure בְעֵינָיוin his eyes

If God puts no trust in His holy ones, if even the heavens are not pure in His eyes,

15:16

אַףhow much כִּיfor נִתְעָבvile וְנֶאֱלָחand corrupt אִישׁwho שֹׁתֶהdrinks כַמַּיִםlike water עַוְלָהinjustice

how much less man, who is vile and corrupt, who drinks injustice like water?

15:17

אֲחַוְךָdescribe שְׁמַעlisten לִיto me וְזֶהhave חָזִיתִיseen וַאֲסַפֵּרָהwill inform

Listen to me and I will inform you. I will describe what I have seen,

15:18

אֲשֶׁרthat חֲכָמִיםwise יַגִּידוּdeclared וְלֹאand was not כִחֲדוּconcealed מֵאֲבוֹתָםfrom their fathers

what was declared by wise men and was not concealed from their fathers,

15:19

לָהֶםto whom לְבַדָּםalone נִתְּנָהgiven הָאָרֶץthe land וְלֹאwhen no עָבַרpassed זָרforeigner בְּתוֹכָםamong them

to whom alone the land was given when no foreigner passed among them.

15:20

כָּלall יְמֵיdays רָשָׁעwicked הוּאhe מִתְחוֹלֵלwrithes pain וּמִסְפַּרfew שָׁנִיםyears נִצְפְּנוּreserved לֶעָרִיץfor the ruthless

A wicked man writhes in pain all his days; only a few years are reserved for the ruthless.

15:21

קוֹלsounds פְּחָדִיםterror בְּאָזְנָיוfill his ears his בַּשָּׁלוֹםin his prosperity שׁוֹדֵדdestroyer יְבוֹאֶנּוּattacks him

Sounds of terror fill his ears; in his prosperity the destroyer attacks him.

15:22

לֹאnot יַאֲמִיןdespairs שׁוּבreturn מִנִּיfrom חֹשֶׁךְdarkness וְצָפוּיhe marked הוּאis אֱלֵיto חָרֶבsword

He despairs of his return from darkness; he is marked for the sword.

15:23

נֹדֵדwanders הוּאhe לַלֶּחֶםabout as food אַיֵּהwhere יָדַעknows כִּיfor נָכוֹןis בְּיָדוֹat hand יוֹםday חֹשֶׁךְdarkness

He wanders about as food for vultures; he knows the day of darkness is at hand.

15:24

יְבַעֲתֻהוּterrify him צַרdistress anguish וּמְצוּקָהdistress and תִּתְקְפֵהוּoverwhelming him כְּמֶלֶךְlike a king עָתִידpoised לַכִּידוֹרto attack

Distress and anguish terrify him, overwhelming him like a king poised to attack.

15:25

כִּיfor נָטָהstretched אֶלagainst אֵלGod יָדוֹhis hand וְאֶלagainst שַׁדַּיAlmighty יִתְגַּבָּרvaunted

For he has stretched out his hand against God and has vaunted himself against the Almighty,

15:26

יָרוּץrushing headlong אֵלָיוto him בְּצַוָּארin neck בַּעֲבִיa thick גַּבֵּיstudded מָגִנָּיוshield

rushing headlong at Him with a thick, studded shield.

15:27

כִּיfor כִסָּהcovered פָנָיוhis face בְּחֶלְבּוֹwith fat his וַיַּעַשׂand made פִּימָהwaistline bulges עֲלֵיon כָסֶלflesh

Though his face is covered with fat and his waistline bulges with flesh,

15:28

וַיִּשְׁכּוֹןhe will dwell עָרִיםcities נִכְחָדוֹתabandoned בָּתִּיםhouses לֹאnot יֵשְׁבוּsit לָמוֹto them אֲשֶׁרthat הִתְעַתְּדוּruined לְגַלִּיםto become rubble

he will dwell in ruined cities, in abandoned houses destined to become rubble.

15:29

לֹאno יֶעְשַׁרrich וְלֹאhis wealth will not יָקוּםendure חֵילוֹhis possessions וְלֹאwill not יִטֶּהoverspread לָאָרֶץthe land מִנְלָםperfection

He will no longer be rich; his wealth will not endure. His possessions will not overspread the land.

15:30

לֹאnot יָסוּרescape מִנִּיfrom חֹשֶׁךְdarkness יֹנַקְתּוֹhis shoots תְּיַבֵּשׁwither שַׁלְהָבֶתshoots וְיָסוּרaway בְּרוּחַbreath פִּיוGod’s mouth

He will not escape from the darkness; the flame will wither his shoots, and the breath of God’s mouth will carry him away.

15:31

אַלnot יַאֲמֵןdeceive בַּשָּׁיוin emptiness נִתְעָהemptiness כִּיfor שָׁוְאemptiness תִּהְיֶהbe תְמוּרָתוֹhis reward

Let him not deceive himself with trust in emptiness, for emptiness will be his reward.

15:32

בְּלֹאin without יוֹמוֹhis time תִּמָּלֵאfull וְכִפָּתוֹand his branch לֹאnot רַעֲנָנָהflourish

It will be paid in full before his time, and his branch will not flourish.

15:33

יַחְמֹסviolence כַּגֶּפֶןlike a vine בִּסְרוֹstripped of its וְיַשְׁלֵךְunripe grapes כַּזַּיִתlike an olive tree נִצָּתוֹsheds its blossoms

He will be like a vine stripped of its unripe grapes, like an olive tree that sheds its blossoms.

15:34

כִּיfor עֲדַתcompany חָנֵףgodless גַּלְמוּדbarren וְאֵשׁand fire אָכְלָהconsume אָהֳלֵיtents שֹׁחַדbribery

For the company of the godless will be barren, and fire will consume the tents of bribery.

15:35

הָרֹהconceive עָמָלtrouble וְיָלֹדand give birth אָוֶןevil וּבִטְנָםtheir womb תָּכִיןpregnant מִרְמָהdeceit

They conceive trouble and give birth to evil; their womb is pregnant with deceit.”
