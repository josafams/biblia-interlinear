Job 26

The greatness of God in creation

Open in the interlinear reader

26:1

וַיַּעַןthen answered אִיּוֹבJob וַיֹּאמַרand said

Then Job answered:

26:2

מֶהhow עָזַרְתָּhelped לְלֹאto not כֹחַpowerless הוֹשַׁעְתָּsaved זְרוֹעַarm לֹאnot עֹזfeeble

“How you have helped the powerless and saved the arm that is feeble!

26:3

מַהhow יָּעַצְתָּcounseled לְלֹאto not חָכְמָהunwise וְתוּשִׁיָּהsound לָרֹבinsight הוֹדָעְתָּknow

How you have counseled the unwise and provided fully sound insight!

26:4

אֶתto מִיwhom הִגַּדְתָּuttered מִלִּיןwords וְנִשְׁמַתand spirit מִיwho יָצְאָהspoke מִמֶּךָּfrom you

To whom have you uttered these words? And whose spirit spoke through you?

26:5

הָרְפָאִיםthe dead יְחוֹלָלוּtremble מִתַּחַתbeneath מַיִםwaters וְשֹׁכְנֵיהֶםdwell in them

The dead tremble— those beneath the waters and those who dwell in them.

26:6

עָרוֹםnaked שְׁאוֹלSheol נֶגְדּוֹbefore God וְאֵיןno כְּסוּתcovering לָאֲבַדּוֹןAbaddon

Sheol is naked before God, and Abaddon has no covering.

26:7

נֹטֶהstretches צָפוֹןnorth עַלover תֹּהוּempty תֹּלֶהspace אֶרֶץearth עַלupon בְּלִיnothing מָהnothing

He stretches out the north over empty space; He hangs the earth upon nothing.

26:8

צֹרֵרwraps מַיִםwaters בְּעָבָיוin his clouds וְלֹאnot נִבְקַעburst עָנָןclouds תַּחְתָּםunder their own

He wraps up the waters in His clouds, yet the clouds do not burst under their own weight.

26:9

מְאַחֵזcovers פְּנֵיface כִסֵּהfull moon פַּרְשֵׁזmoon spreading עָלָיוover it עֲנָנוֹhis cloud

He covers the face of the full moon, spreading over it His cloud.

26:10

חֹקinscribed חָגhorizon עַלon פְּנֵיface מָיִםwaters עַדuntil תַּכְלִיתboundary אוֹרlight עִםwith חֹשֶׁךְdarkness

He has inscribed a horizon on the face of the waters at the boundary between light and darkness.

26:11

עַמּוּדֵיfoundations שָׁמַיִםheaven יְרוֹפָפוּquake וְיִתְמְהוּastounded מִגַּעֲרָתוֹat his rebuke

The foundations of heaven quake, astounded at His rebuke.

26:12

בְּכֹחוֹby his power רָגַעstirred הַיָּםthe sea וּבִתְבוּנָתוֹby his understanding he מָחַץshattered רָהַבRahab

By His power He stirred the sea; by His understanding He shattered Rahab.

26:13

בְּרוּחוֹby his breath שָׁמַיִםskies שִׁפְרָהcleared חֹלֲלָהpierced יָדוֹhis hand נָחָשׁfleeing serpent בָּרִיחַserpent

By His breath the skies were cleared; His hand pierced the fleeing serpent.

26:14

הֶןindeed אֵלֶּהthese קְצוֹתfringes דְּרָכָיוhis ways וּמַהhow שֵּׁמֶץwhisper דָּבָרword נִשְׁמַעhear בּוֹin him וְרַעַםthunder גְּבוּרוֹתָיוhis power מִיcan יִתְבּוֹנָןunderstand

Indeed, these are but the fringes of His ways; how faint is the whisper we hear of Him! Who then can understand the thunder of His power?”
