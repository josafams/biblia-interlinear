Job 16

Miserable comforters; a witness in heaven

Open in the interlinear reader

16:1

וַיַּעַןthen answered אִיּוֹבJob וַיֹּאמַרand said

Then Job answered:

16:2

שָׁמַעְתִּיheard כְאֵלֶּהlike these רַבּוֹתmany מְנַחֲמֵיcomforters עָמָלmiserable כֻּלְּכֶםyou all

“I have heard many things like these; miserable comforters are you all.

16:3

הֲקֵץend לְדִבְרֵיwinded רוּחַwinded speeches אוֹor מַהwhat יַּמְרִיצְךָprovokes you כִּיfor תַעֲנֶהtestifying

Is there no end to your long-winded speeches? What provokes you to continue testifying?

16:4

גַּםalso אָנֹכִיI כָּכֶםlike you אֲדַבֵּרָהspeak לוּif יֵשׁthere נַפְשְׁכֶםyou תַּחַתplace נַפְשִׁיmy soul אַחְבִּירָהheap עֲלֵיכֶםagainst you בְּמִלִּיםup words וְאָנִיעָהand shake עֲלֵיכֶםon you בְּמוֹwith רֹאשִׁיmy head

I could also speak like you if you were in my place; I could heap up words against you and shake my head at you.

16:5

אֲאַמִּצְכֶםencourage you בְּמוֹwith פִיmy mouth וְנִידand the consolation שְׂפָתַיmy lips would יַחְשֹׂךְrelief

But I would encourage you with my mouth, and the consolation of my lips would bring relief.

16:6

אִםif אֲדַבְּרָהspeak לֹאnot יֵחָשֵׂךְrelieved כְּאֵבִיmy pain וְאַחְדְּלָהhold מַהhow מִנִּיfrom of me יַהֲלֹךְgo

Even if I speak, my pain is not relieved, and if I hold back, how will it go away?

16:7

אַךְsurely עַתָּהnow הֶלְאָנִיexhausted me הֲשִׁמּוֹתָdevastated כָּלall עֲדָתִיmy family

Surely He has now exhausted me; You have devastated all my family.

16:8

וַתִּקְמְטֵנִיyou have bound me לְעֵדbecome a witness הָיָהwas וַיָּקָםrises up and בִּיin me כַחֲשִׁיfrailty בְּפָנַיagainst me יַעֲנֶהtestifies

You have bound me, and it has become a witness; my frailty rises up and testifies against me.

16:9

אַפּוֹhis anger טָרַףtorn וַיִּשְׂטְמֵנִיand opposed me חָרַקteeth עָלַיon me בְּשִׁנָּיוhis teeth at צָרִיmy adversary יִלְטוֹשׁpierces עֵינָיוhis eyes לִיto me

His anger has torn me and opposed me; He gnashes His teeth at me. My adversary pierces me with His eyes.

16:10

פָּעֲרוּopen עָלַיagainst me בְּפִיהֶםtheir mouths בְּחֶרְפָּהwith contempt הִכּוּstrike לְחָיָיmy cheeks יַחַדtogether עָלַיagainst me יִתְמַלָּאוּןfilled

They open their mouths against me and strike my cheeks with contempt; they join together against me.

16:11

יַסְגִּירֵנִיdelivered me אֵלGod אֶלto עֲוִילunjust וְעַלto יְדֵיclutches רְשָׁעִיםwicked יִרְטֵנִיthrown me

God has delivered me to unjust men; He has thrown me to the clutches of the wicked.

16:12

שָׁלֵוease הָיִיתִיwas וַיְפַרְפְּרֵנִיbut he shattered me וְאָחַזhe seized בְּעָרְפִּיme by the neck וַיְפַצְפְּצֵנִיand crushed me וַיְקִימֵנִיhe has set me לוֹto him לְמַטָּרָהtarget

I was at ease, but He shattered me; He seized me by the neck and crushed me. He has set me up as His target;

16:13

יָסֹבּוּsurround עָלַיon me רַבָּיוhis archers יְפַלַּחpierces כִּלְיוֹתַיmy kidneys וְלֹאand not יַחְמוֹלmercy יִשְׁפֹּךְgall לָאָרֶץon the ground מְרֵרָתִיspills my

His archers surround me. He pierces my kidneys without mercy and spills my gall on the ground.

16:14

יִפְרְצֵנִיbreaks me פֶרֶץwound עַלupon פְּנֵיface פָרֶץwound יָרֻץrushes עָלַיon me כְּגִבּוֹרlike a mighty warrior

He breaks me with wound upon wound; He rushes me like a mighty warrior.

16:15

שַׂקsackcloth תָּפַרְתִּיsewn עֲלֵיover גִלְדִּיmy skin וְעֹלַלְתִּיI have buried בֶעָפָרin the dust קַרְנִיmy horn

I have sewn sackcloth over my skin; I have buried my horn in the dust.

16:16

פָּנַיmy face חֳמַרְמְרוּred מִנִּיfrom בֶכִיweeping וְעַלand on עַפְעַפַּיring my eyes צַלְמָוֶתdeep shadows

My face is red with weeping, and deep shadows ring my eyes;

16:17

עַלon לֹאnot חָמָסviolence בְּכַפָּיmy hands are free וּתְפִלָּתִיand my prayer זַכָּהpure

yet my hands are free of violence and my prayer is pure.

16:18

אֶרֶץearth אַלdo not תְּכַסִּיcover דָמִיmy blood וְאַלand do not יְהִיbe מָקוֹםlaid לְזַעֲקָתִיmy cry for

O earth, do not cover my blood; may my cry for help never be laid to rest.

16:19

גַּםeven עַתָּהnow הִנֵּהbehold בַשָּׁמַיִםin heaven עֵדִיmy witness וְשָׂהֲדִיand my advocate בַּמְּרוֹמִיםon high

Even now my witness is in heaven, and my advocate is on high.

16:20

מְלִיצַיscoffers רֵעָיfriends are my אֶלto אֱלוֹהַGod דָּלְפָהpour עֵינִיmy eyes

My friends are my scoffers as my eyes pour out tears to God.

16:21

וְיוֹכַחoh לְגֶבֶרthat a man might plead עִםwith אֱלוֹהַּGod וּבֶןand son אָדָםpleads לְרֵעֵהוּwith his neighbor

Oh, that a man might plead with God as he pleads with his neighbor!

16:22

כִּיfor שְׁנוֹתyears מִסְפָּרfew יֶאֱתָיוּpast וְאֹרַחway לֹאno אָשׁוּבreturn אֶהֱלֹךְgo

For when only a few years are past I will go the way of no return.
