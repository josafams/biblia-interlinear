Job 5

Eliphaz advises Job to seek God

Open in the interlinear reader

5:1

קְרָאcall נָאplease הֲיֵשׁthere עוֹנֶךָּwill answer וְאֶלto מִיwho מִקְּדֹשִׁיםof the holy תִּפְנֶהturn

“Call out if you please, but who will answer? To which of the holy ones will you turn?

5:2

כִּיfor לֶאֱוִילa fool יַהֲרָגslays כָּעַשׂsimple וּפֹתֶהresentment תָּמִיתkills קִנְאָהenvy

For resentment kills a fool, and envy slays the simple.

5:3

אֲנִיI רָאִיתִיseen אֱוִילfool מַשְׁרִישׁtaking root וָאֶקּוֹבhouse נָוֵהוּhis house פִתְאֹםsuddenly

I have seen a fool taking root, but suddenly his house was cursed.

5:4

יִרְחֲקוּfar בָנָיוhis sons מִיֶּשַׁעfrom safety וְיִדַּכְּאוּcrushed בַשַּׁעַרin court וְאֵיןcourt without מַצִּילdefender

His sons are far from safety, crushed in court without a defender.

5:5

אֲשֶׁרthat קְצִירוֹhis harvest רָעֵבhungry יֹאכֵלconsume וְאֶלand to מִצִּנִּיםfrom the thorns יִקָּחֵהוּtaking it וְשָׁאַףpant צַמִּיםthirsty after חֵילָםwealth

The hungry consume his harvest, taking it even from the thorns, and the thirsty pant after his wealth.

5:6

כִּיfor לֹאnot יֵצֵאspring מֵעָפָרfrom the dust אָוֶןdistress וּמֵאֲדָמָהfrom the ground לֹאnot יִצְמַחsprout עָמָלtrouble

For distress does not spring from the dust, and trouble does not sprout from the ground.

5:7

כִּיfor אָדָםman לְעָמָלto trouble יוּלָּדborn וּבְנֵיas surely רֶשֶׁףsparks יַגְבִּיהוּupward עוּףfly

Yet man is born to trouble as surely as sparks fly upward.

5:8

אוּלָםhowever אֲנִיI אֶדְרֹשׁappeal אֶלto אֵלGod וְאֶלand to אֱלֹהִיםhim אָשִׂיםlay דִּבְרָתִיmy before

However, if I were you, I would appeal to God and lay my cause before Him—

5:9

עֹשֶׂהdoes גְדֹלוֹתgreat וְאֵיןand no חֵקֶרunsearchable נִפְלָאוֹתwonders עַדuntil אֵיןwithout מִסְפָּרnumber

the One who does great and unsearchable things, wonders without number.

5:10

הַנֹּתֵןgives מָטָרrain עַלto פְּנֵיface אָרֶץearth וְשֹׁלֵחַand sends מַיִםwater עַלupon פְּנֵיface חוּצוֹתfields

He gives rain to the earth and sends water upon the fields.

5:11

לָשׂוּםsets שְׁפָלִיםlowly לְמָרוֹםon high וְקֹדְרִיםso that mourners שָׂגְבוּlifted יֶשַׁעsafety

He sets the lowly on high, so that mourners are lifted to safety.

5:12

מֵפֵרthwarts מַחְשְׁבוֹתschemes עֲרוּמִיםcrafty וְלֹאno תַעֲשֶׂינָהdo יְדֵיהֶםtheir hands תּוּשִׁיָּהsuccess

He thwarts the schemes of the crafty, so that their hands find no success.

5:13

לֹכֵדcatches חֲכָמִיםwise בְּעָרְמָםin their craftiness וַעֲצַתplans נִפְתָּלִיםcunning נִמְהָרָהcunning

He catches the wise in their craftiness, and sweeps away the plans of the cunning.

5:14

יוֹמָםby day יְפַגְּשׁוּencounter חֹשֶׁךְdarkness וְכַלַּיְלָהas in the night יְמַשְׁשׁוּgrope בַצָּהֳרָיִםat noon

They encounter darkness by day and grope at noon as in the night.

5:15

וַיֹּשַׁעhe saves מֵחֶרֶבfrom the sword מִפִּיהֶםin their mouth וּמִיַּדand from the clutches חָזָקpowerful אֶבְיוֹןneedy

He saves the needy from the sword in their mouth and from the clutches of the powerful.

5:16

וַתְּהִיand was לַדַּלthe poor תִּקְוָהhope וְעֹלָתָהand injustice קָפְצָהshuts פִּיהָits mouth

So the poor have hope, and injustice shuts its mouth.

5:17

הִנֵּהindeed אַשְׁרֵיblessed אֱנוֹשׁman יוֹכִחֶנּוּwhom corrects אֱלוֹהַּGod וּמוּסַרdiscipline שַׁדַּיAlmighty אַלdo not תִּמְאָסdespise

Blessed indeed is the man whom God corrects; so do not despise the discipline of the Almighty.

5:18

כִּיfor הוּאhe יַכְאִיבwounds וְיֶחְבָּשׁbut he also binds יִמְחַץstrikes וְיָדָיוbut his hands also תִּרְפֶּינָהheal

For He wounds, but He also binds; He strikes, but His hands also heal.

5:19

בְּשֵׁשׁfrom six צָרוֹתcalamities יַצִּילֶךָּrescue you וּבְשֶׁבַעin seven לֹאno יִגַּעtouch בְּךָin you רָעharm

He will rescue you from six calamities; no harm will touch you in seven.

5:20

בְּרָעָבin famine פָּדְךָredeem you מִמָּוֶתfrom death וּבְמִלְחָמָהand in battle מִידֵיfrom the stroke חָרֶבsword

In famine He will redeem you from death, and in battle from the stroke of the sword.

5:21

בְּשׁוֹטscourge לָשׁוֹןtongue תֵּחָבֵאhidden וְלֹאand will not תִירָאfear מִשֹּׁדhavoc כִּיwhen יָבוֹאcomes

You will be hidden from the scourge of the tongue, and will not fear havoc when it comes.

5:22

לְשֹׁדat destruction וּלְכָפָןand famine תִּשְׂחָקlaugh וּמֵחַיַּתthe beasts הָאָרֶץthe earth אַלnot תִּירָאfear

You will laugh at destruction and famine, and need not fear the beasts of the earth.

5:23

כִּיfor עִםwith אַבְנֵיstones הַשָּׂדֶהthe field בְרִיתֶךָcovenant וְחַיַּתanimals הַשָּׂדֶהthe wild הָשְׁלְמָהpeace לָךְto you

For you will have a covenant with the stones of the field, and the wild animals will be at peace with you.

5:24

וְיָדַעְתָּwill know כִּיthat שָׁלוֹםsecure אָהֳלֶךָyour tent וּפָקַדְתָּwhen נָוְךָyour home וְלֹאand not תֶחֱטָאamiss

You will know that your tent is secure, and find nothing amiss when inspecting your home.

5:25

וְיָדַעְתָּyou will know כִּיthat רַבmany זַרְעֶךָyour offspring וְצֶאֱצָאֶיךָyour descendants כְּעֵשֶׂבlike the grass הָאָרֶץthe earth

You will know that your offspring will be many, your descendants like the grass of the earth.

5:26

תָּבוֹאcome בְכֶלַחin full vigor אֱלֵיto קָבֶרgrave כַּעֲלוֹתlike a sheaf גָּדִישׁgrain gathered בְּעִתּוֹin season

You will come to the grave in full vigor, like a sheaf of grain gathered in season.

5:27

הִנֵּהindeed זֹאתthis חֲקַרְנוּהָinvestigated and it כֶּןso הִיאis שְׁמָעֶנָּהhear it וְאַתָּהand you דַעknow לָךְfor yourself

Indeed, we have investigated, and it is true! So hear it and know for yourself.”
