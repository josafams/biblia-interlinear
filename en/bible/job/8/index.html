Job 8

Bildad defends the justice of God

Open in the interlinear reader

8:1

וַיַּעַןand answered בִּלְדַּדBildad הַשּׁוּחִיthe Shuhite וַיֹאמַרreplied

Then Bildad the Shuhite replied:

8:2

עַדhow אָןlong תְּמַלֶּלsaying אֵלֶּהthese וְרוּחַwind כַּבִּירblustering אִמְרֵיwords פִיךָyour mouth

“How long will you go on saying such things? The words of your mouth are a blustering wind.

8:3

הַאֵלdoes God יְעַוֵּתpervert מִשְׁפָּטjustice וְאִםand if שַׁדַּיAlmighty יְעַוֵּתpervert צֶדֶקright

Does God pervert justice? Does the Almighty pervert what is right?

8:4

אִםif בָּנֶיךָyour children חָטְאוּsinned לוֹto him וַיְשַׁלְּחֵםhe gave them בְּיַדin hand פִּשְׁעָםtheir rebellion

When your children sinned against Him, He gave them over to their rebellion.

8:5

אִםif אַתָּהyou תְּשַׁחֵרearnestly seek אֶלto אֵלGod וְאֶלand to שַׁדַּיAlmighty תִּתְחַנָּןmercy

But if you would earnestly seek God and ask the Almighty for mercy,

8:6

אִםif זַךְpure וְיָשָׁרand upright אָתָּהyou כִּיfor עַתָּהnow יָעִירrouse עָלֶיךָon your וְשִׁלַּםand restore נְוַתestate צִדְקֶךָyour righteous

if you are pure and upright, even now He will rouse Himself on your behalf and restore your righteous estate.

8:7

וְהָיָהwere רֵאשִׁיתְךָmodest מִצְעָרmodest וְאַחֲרִיתְךָyour latter יִשְׂגֶּהflourish מְאֹדvery

Though your beginnings were modest, your latter days will flourish.

8:8

כִּיfor שְׁאַלinquire נָאplease לְדֹרof generations רִישׁוֹןpast וְכוֹנֵןand consider לְחֵקֶרthe discoveries אֲבוֹתָםtheir fathers

Please inquire of past generations and consider the discoveries of their fathers.

8:9

כִּיfor תְמוֹלborn yesterday אֲנַחְנוּwe וְלֹאand not נֵדָעknow כִּיbut צֵלshadow יָמֵינוּour days עֲלֵיon אָרֶץearth

For we were born yesterday and know nothing; our days on earth are but a shadow.

8:10

הֲלֹאwill not הֵםthey יוֹרוּךָteach you יֹאמְרוּtell לָךְto you וּמִלִּבָּםand from their understanding יוֹצִאוּbring מִלִּיםspeak

Will they not teach you and tell you, and speak from their understanding?

8:11

הֲיִגְאֶהdoes grow גֹּמֶאpapyrus בְּלֹאno בִצָּהmarsh יִשְׂגֶּהflourish אָחוּreeds בְלִיwithout מָיִםwater

Does papyrus grow where there is no marsh? Do reeds flourish without water?

8:12

עֹדֶנּוּwhile בְאִבּוֹthe shoots are לֹאnot יִקָּטֵףuncut וְלִפְנֵיand to before כָלquicker חָצִירgrass יִיבָשׁdry

While the shoots are still uncut, they dry up quicker than grass.

8:13

כֵּןso אָרְחוֹתdestiny כָּלall שֹׁכְחֵיforget אֵלGod וְתִקְוַתso the hope חָנֵףgodless תֹּאבֵדperish

Such is the destiny of all who forget God; so the hope of the godless will perish.

8:14

אֲשֶׁרthat יָקוֹטfragile כִּסְלוֹhis confidence וּבֵיתand house עַכָּבִישׁspider’s web מִבְטַחוֹhis security

His confidence is fragile; his security is in a spider’s web.

8:15

יִשָּׁעֵןleans עַלon בֵּיתוֹhis web וְלֹאand not יַעֲמֹדgives יַחֲזִיקholds fast בּוֹin him וְלֹאnot יָקוּםendure

He leans on his web, but it gives way; he holds fast, but it does not endure.

8:16

רָטֹבwatered הוּאhe לִפְנֵיto before שָׁמֶשׁsunshine וְעַלover גַּנָּתוֹgarden יֹנַקְתּוֹshoots תֵצֵאspreading

He is a well-watered plant in the sunshine, spreading its shoots over the garden.

8:17

עַלon גַּלwrap שָׁרָשָׁיוhis roots יְסֻבָּכוּrock בֵּיתhome אֲבָנִיםstones יֶחֱזֶהamong

His roots wrap around the rock heap; he looks for a home among the stones.

8:18

אִםif יְבַלְּעֶנּוּuprooted his מִמְּקוֹמוֹfrom place it וְכִחֶשׁwill disown בּוֹin him לֹאnever רְאִיתִיךָsaw you

If he is uprooted from his place, it will disown him, saying, ‘I never saw you.’

8:19

הֶןsurely הוּאis מְשׂוֹשׂjoy דַּרְכּוֹhis way וּמֵעָפָרfrom the dust אַחֵרothers יִצְמָחוּspring

Surely this is the joy of his way; yet others will spring from the dust.

8:20

הֶןbehold אֵלGod לֹאnot יִמְאַסreject תָּםblameless וְלֹאand not יַחֲזִיקstrengthen בְּיַדthe hand מְרֵעִיםevildoers

Behold, God does not reject the blameless, nor will He strengthen the hand of evildoers.

8:21

עַדuntil יְמַלֵּהfill שְׂחוֹקlaughter פִּיךָyour mouth וּשְׂפָתֶיךָand your lips תְרוּעָהshout

He will yet fill your mouth with laughter, and your lips with a shout of joy.

8:22

שֹׂנְאֶיךָyour enemies יִלְבְּשׁוּclothed בֹשֶׁתshame וְאֹהֶלand the tent רְשָׁעִיםwicked אֵינֶנּוּno more

Your enemies will be clothed in shame, and the tent of the wicked will be no more.”
