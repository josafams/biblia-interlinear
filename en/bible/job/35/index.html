Job 35

Elihu: God does not depend on man

Open in the interlinear reader

35:1

וַיַּעַןand answered אֱלִיהוּElihu וַיֹּאמַרsay

And Elihu went on to say:

35:2

הֲזֹאתthis חָשַׁבְתָּthink לְמִשְׁפָּטjust אָמַרְתָּsay צִדְקִיam more righteous מֵאֵלthan God

“Do you think this is just? You say, ‘I am more righteous than God.’

35:3

כִּיfor תֹאמַרask מַהwhat יִּסְכָּןbenefit לָךְto you מָהwhat אֹעִילbenefit מֵחַטָּאתִיfrom sin

For you ask, ‘What does it profit me, and what benefit do I gain apart from sin?’

35:4

אֲנִיI אֲשִׁיבְךָreply you מִלִּיןwords וְאֶתand [obj] רֵעֶיךָyour friends עִמָּךְto your

I will reply to you and to your friends as well.

35:5

הַבֵּטlook שָׁמַיִםheavens וּרְאֵהand see וְשׁוּרgaze שְׁחָקִיםclouds גָּבְהוּhigh מִמֶּךָּfrom you

Look to the heavens and see; gaze at the clouds high above you.

35:6

אִםif חָטָאתָsin מַהwhat תִּפְעָלdo you accomplish בּוֹagainst him וְרַבּוּyou multiply פְשָׁעֶיךָyour transgressions מַהwhat תַּעֲשֶׂהdo לּוֹto him

If you sin, what do you accomplish against Him? If you multiply your transgressions, what do you do to Him?

35:7

אִםif צָדַקְתָּrighteous מַהwhat תִּתֶּןgive לוֹto him אוֹor מַהwhat מִיָּדְךָfrom your hand יִקָּחreceive

If you are righteous, what do you give Him, or what does He receive from your hand?

35:8

לְאִישׁa man כָּמוֹךָlike רִשְׁעֶךָwickedness affects וּלְבֶןa son אָדָםman צִדְקָתֶךָyour righteousness

Your wickedness affects only a man like yourself, and your righteousness only a son of man.

35:9

מֵרֹבunder great עֲשׁוּקִיםoppression יַזְעִיקוּout יְשַׁוְּעוּcry מִזְּרוֹעַfrom the arm רַבִּיםmighty

Men cry out under great oppression; they plead for relief from the arm of the mighty.

35:10

וְלֹאbut no אָמַרasks אַיֵּהwhere אֱלוֹהַּGod עֹשָׂיmy Maker נֹתֵןgives זְמִרוֹתsongs בַּלָּיְלָהin the night

But no one asks, ‘Where is God my Maker, who gives us songs in the night,

35:11

מַלְּפֵנוּteaches us מִבַּהֲמוֹתthan the beasts אָרֶץearth וּמֵעוֹףthan the birds הַשָּׁמַיִםthe air יְחַכְּמֵנוּus wiser

who teaches us more than the beasts of the earth and makes us wiser than the birds of the air?’

35:12

שָׁםthere יִצְעֲקוּcry out וְלֹאnot יַעֲנֶהanswer מִפְּנֵיfrom before גְּאוֹןpride רָעִיםevil

There they cry out, but He does not answer, because of the pride of evil men.

35:13

אַךְsurely שָׁוְאempty pleas לֹאnot יִשְׁמַעlisten אֵלGod וְשַׁדַּיand the Almighty לֹאnot יְשׁוּרֶנָּהnote of it

Surely God does not listen to empty pleas, and the Almighty does not take note of it.

35:14

אַףhow much כִּיfor תֹאמַרsay לֹאnot תְשׁוּרֶנּוּsee him דִּיןcase לְפָנָיוbefore him וּתְחוֹלֵלwait לוֹfor him

How much less, then, when you say that you do not see Him, that your case is before Him and you must wait for Him,

35:15

וְעַתָּהand now כִּיfor אַיִןnot פָּקַדpunished אַפּוֹhis anger וְלֹאor יָדַעnotice בַּפַּשׁof folly מְאֹדvery

and further, that in His anger He has not punished or taken much notice of folly!

35:16

וְאִיּוֹבso Job הֶבֶלvain יִפְצֶהopens פִּיהוּhis mouth בִּבְלִיwithout דַעַתknowledge מִלִּיןwords יַכְבִּרmultiplies

So Job opens his mouth in vain and multiplies words without knowledge.”
