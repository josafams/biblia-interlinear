Job 30

Job's present humiliation

Open in the interlinear reader

30:1

וְעַתָּהbut now שָׂחֲקוּmock עָלַיon me צְעִירִיםyounger מִמֶּנִּיthan I am לְיָמִיםto days אֲשֶׁרthat מָאַסְתִּיrefused אֲבוֹתָםfathers לָשִׁיתto entrust עִםwith כַּלְבֵיdogs צֹאנִיmy sheep

“But now they mock me, men younger than I am, whose fathers I would have refused to entrust with my sheep dogs.

30:2

גַּםeven כֹּחַstrength יְדֵיהֶםtheir hands לָמָּהwhat לִּיto me עָלֵימוֹon them אָבַדperished כָּלַחvigor

What use to me was the strength of their hands, since their vigor had left them?

30:3

בְּחֶסֶרgaunt from poverty וּבְכָפָןand hunger גַּלְמוּדgnawed הַעֹרְקִיםthe dry land צִיָּהdry אֶמֶשׁnight שׁוֹאָהdesolate וּמְשֹׁאָהwasteland

Gaunt from poverty and hunger, they gnawed the dry land, and the desolate wasteland by night.

30:4

הַקֹּטְפִיםplucked מַלּוּחַmallow עֲלֵיon שִׂיחַshrubs וְשֹׁרֶשׁand the roots רְתָמִיםbroom tree לַחְמָםtheir food

They plucked mallow among the shrubs, and the roots of the broom tree were their food.

30:5

מִןfrom גֵּוamong יְגֹרָשׁוּbanished יָרִיעוּshouted עָלֵימוֹon them כַּגַּנָּבlike thieves

They were banished from among men, shouted down like thieves,

30:6

בַּעֲרוּץslopes נְחָלִיםwadis לִשְׁכֹּןamong חֹרֵיholes עָפָרground וְכֵפִיםrocks and

so that they lived on the slopes of the wadis, among the rocks and in holes in the ground.

30:7

בֵּיןamong שִׂיחִיםshrubs יִנְהָקוּhuddled תַּחַתbeneath חָרוּלhuddled יְסֻפָּחוּnettles

They cried out among the shrubs and huddled beneath the nettles.

30:8

בְּנֵיsons נָבָלsenseless גַּםand בְּנֵיnameless בְלִיwithout שֵׁםwere נִכְּאוּdriven מִןfrom הָאָרֶץthe land

A senseless and nameless brood, they were driven off the land.

30:9

וְעַתָּהand now נְגִינָתָםsong הָיִיתִיwas וָאֱהִיI have become לָהֶםa לְמִלָּהbyword among

And now they mock me in song; I have become a byword among them.

30:10

תִּעֲבוּנִיabhor me רָחֲקוּkeep far מֶנִּיfrom of me וּמִפָּנַיmy face לֹאnot חָשְׂכוּhesitate רֹקspit

They abhor me and keep far from me; they do not hesitate to spit in my face.

30:11

כִּיbecause יִתְרִיhas unstrung my פִתַּחbow וַיְעַנֵּנִיand afflicted me וְרֶסֶןrestraint מִפָּנַיoff in my presence שִׁלֵּחוּsent

Because God has unstrung my bow and afflicted me, they have cast off restraint in my presence.

30:12

עַלon יָמִיןright פִּרְחַחsnares יָקוּמוּarises רַגְלַיmy feet שִׁלֵּחוּsent וַיָּסֹלּוּand build עָלַיagainst me אָרְחוֹתsiege אֵידָםramps

The rabble arises at my right; they lay snares for my feet and build siege ramps against me.

30:13

נָתְסוּtear נְתִיבָתִיmy path לְהַוָּתִיfrom my destruction יֹעִילוּprofit לֹאno עֹזֵרrestrain לָמוֹto them

They tear up my path; they profit from my destruction, with no one to restrain them.

30:14

כְּפֶרֶץadvance as through רָחָבwide יֶאֱתָיוּbreach תַּחַתunder שֹׁאָהruins הִתְגַּלְגָּלוּkeep

They advance as through a wide breach; through the ruins they keep rolling in.

30:15

הָהְפַּךְturned עָלַיagainst me בַּלָּהוֹתterrors תִּרְדֹּףdignity כָּרוּחַas by the wind נְדִבָתִיdrive וּכְעָבlike a cloud עָבְרָהpassed יְשֻׁעָתִיprosperity has

Terrors are turned loose against me; they drive away my dignity as by the wind, and my prosperity has passed like a cloud.

30:16

וְעַתָּהand now עָלַיon me תִּשְׁתַּפֵּךְpoured נַפְשִׁיsoul יֹאחֲזוּנִיgrip me יְמֵיdays עֹנִיaffliction

And now my soul is poured out within me; days of affliction grip me.

30:17

לַיְלָהnight עֲצָמַיmy bones נִקַּרpierces מֵעָלָיfrom the me וְעֹרְקַיand gnawing pains לֹאnever יִשְׁכָּבוּןrest

Night pierces my bones, and my gnawing pains never rest.

30:18

בְּרָבwith great כֹּחַforce יִתְחַפֵּשׂgrasps לְבוּשִׁיmy garment כְּפִיby the collar כֻתָּנְתִּיmy tunic יַאַזְרֵנִיseizes me

With great force He grasps my garment; He seizes me by the collar of my tunic.

30:19

הֹרָנִיthrows me לַחֹמֶרinto the mud וָאֶתְמַשֵּׁלbecome כֶּעָפָרlike dust וָאֵפֶרand ashes

He throws me into the mud, and I have become like dust and ashes.

30:20

אֲשַׁוַּעcry אֵלֶיךָto you וְלֹאnot תַעֲנֵנִיanswer when I עָמַדְתִּיstand וַתִּתְבֹּנֶןmerely look בִּיin me

I cry out to You for help, but You do not answer; when I stand up, You merely look at me.

30:21

תֵּהָפֵךְturned לְאַכְזָרhave ruthlessly לִיto me בְּעֹצֶםwith your strong יָדְךָhand תִשְׂטְמֵנִיoppose me

You have ruthlessly turned on me; You oppose me with Your strong hand.

30:22

תִּשָּׂאֵנִיsnatch me up אֶלinto רוּחַwind תַּרְכִּיבֵנִיdrive me וּתְמֹגְגֵנִיtoss me תּוּשִׁיָּהstorm

You snatch me up into the wind and drive me before it; You toss me about in the storm.

30:23

כִּיfor יָדַעְתִּיknow מָוֶתdeath תְּשִׁיבֵנִיbring me וּבֵיתand house מוֹעֵדappointed לְכָלfor all חָיliving

Yes, I know that You will bring me down to death, to the place appointed for all the living.

30:24

אַךְyet לֹאno בְעִיstretches יִשְׁלַחout יָדhand אִםif בְּפִידוֹwhen he cries לָהֶןhelp שׁוּעַdistress

Yet no one stretches out his hand to a ruined man when he cries for help in his distress.

30:25

אִםif לֹאnot בָכִיתִיwept לִקְשֵׁהto stiff יוֹםthose עָגְמָהtrouble נַפְשִׁיmy soul not grieved לָאֶבְיוֹןfor the needy

Have I not wept for those in trouble? Has my soul not grieved for the needy?

30:26

כִּיfor טוֹבgood קִוִּיתִיhoped וַיָּבֹאcame when רָעevil וַאֲיַחֲלָהlooked לְאוֹרfor light וַיָּבֹאand came אֹפֶלdarkness

But when I hoped for good, evil came; when I looked for light, darkness fell.

30:27

מֵעַיam within רֻתְּחוּchurning וְלֹאand not דָמּוּrest קִדְּמֻנִיconfront me יְמֵיdays עֹנִיaffliction

I am churning within and cannot rest; days of affliction confront me.

30:28

קֹדֵרI הִלַּכְתִּיgo בְּלֹאnot by חַמָּהsun קַמְתִּיstand בַקָּהָלin the assembly אֲשַׁוֵּעַcry

I go about blackened, but not by the sun. I stand up in the assembly and cry for help.

30:29

אָחbrother הָיִיתִיbecome לְתַנִּיםa of jackals וְרֵעַcompanion לִבְנוֹתto daughters יַעֲנָהostriches

I have become a brother of jackals, a companion of ostriches.

30:30

עוֹרִיmy skin שָׁחַרblack מֵעָלָיfrom the me וְעַצְמִיand my bones חָרָהburn מִנִּיfrom חֹרֶבfever

My skin grows black and peels, and my bones burn with fever.

30:31

וַיְהִיand was לְאֵבֶלtuned to mourning כִּנֹּרִיmy harp וְעֻגָבִיand my flute לְקוֹלto the sound בֹּכִיםweeping

My harp is tuned to mourning and my flute to the sound of weeping.
