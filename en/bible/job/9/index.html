Job 9

How can anyone be righteous before God

Open in the interlinear reader

9:1

וַיַּעַןthen answered אִיּוֹבJob וַיֹּאמַרand said

Then Job answered:

9:2

אָמְנָםyes יָדַעְתִּיknow כִיthat כֵןso וּמַהbut how יִּצְדַּקcan אֱנוֹשׁmortal עִםwith אֵלGod

“Yes, I know that it is so, but how can a mortal be righteous before God?

9:3

אִםif יַחְפֹּץwished לָרִיבto contend עִמּוֹwith God he לֹאnot יַעֲנֶנּוּanswer him אַחַתone מִנִּיout אָלֶףthousand

If one wished to contend with God, he could not answer Him one time out of a thousand.

9:4

חֲכַםwise לֵבָבheart וְאַמִּיץand mighty כֹּחַstrength מִיwho הִקְשָׁהresisted אֵלָיוto him וַיִּשְׁלָםand prospered

God is wise in heart and mighty in strength. Who has resisted Him and prospered?

9:5

הַמַּעְתִּיקmoves הָרִיםmountains וְלֹאand not יָדָעוּknowledge אֲשֶׁרthat הֲפָכָםoverturns them בְּאַפּוֹin his anger

He moves mountains without their knowledge and overturns them in His anger.

9:6

הַמַּרְגִּיזshakes the אֶרֶץearth מִמְּקוֹמָהּfrom its place וְעַמּוּדֶיהָso that its foundations יִתְפַלָּצוּןtremble

He shakes the earth from its place, so that its foundations tremble.

9:7

הָאֹמֵרcommands לַחֶרֶסthe sun וְלֹאnot יִזְרָחshine וּבְעַדand for כּוֹכָבִיםstars יַחְתֹּםseals

He commands the sun not to shine; He seals off the stars.

9:8

נֹטֶהstretches שָׁמַיִםheavens לְבַדּוֹalone וְדוֹרֵךְand treads עַלon בָּמֳתֵיwaves יָםsea

He alone stretches out the heavens and treads on the waves of the sea.

9:9

עֹשֶׂהMaker עָשׁbear כְּסִילOrion וְכִימָהPleiades וְחַדְרֵיand the constellations תֵמָןsouth

He is the Maker of the Bear and Orion, of the Pleiades and the constellations of the south.

9:10

עֹשֶׂהdoes גְדֹלוֹתgreat עַדuntil אֵיןno חֵקֶרsearching וְנִפְלָאוֹתand wonders עַדuntil אֵיןwithout מִסְפָּרnumber

He does great things beyond searching out, and wonders without number.

9:11

הֵןbehold יַעֲבֹרpass עָלַיon me וְלֹאI would not אֶרְאֶהsee וְיַחֲלֹףmove וְלֹאI would not אָבִיןrecognize לוֹto him

Were He to pass by me, I would not see Him; were He to move, I would not recognize Him.

9:12

הֵןif יַחְתֹּףtakes מִיwho can יְשִׁיבֶנּוּstop him מִיwho יֹאמַרask אֵלָיוto him מַהwhat תַּעֲשֶׂהdoing

If He takes away, who can stop Him? Who dares to ask Him, ‘What are You doing?’

9:13

אֱלוֹהַּGod לֹאnot יָשִׁיבrestrain אַפּוֹhis anger תַּחְתָּיוbeneath him שָׁחֲחוּbowed עֹזְרֵיhelpers רָהַבRahab

God does not restrain His anger; the helpers of Rahab cower beneath Him.

9:14

אַףhow כִּיfor אָנֹכִיI אֶעֱנֶנּוּanswer him אֶבְחֲרָהchoose דְבָרַיmy arguments עִמּוֹagainst him

How then can I answer Him or choose my arguments against Him?

9:15

אֲשֶׁרthat אִםif צָדַקְתִּיright לֹאnot אֶעֱנֶהanswer לִמְשֹׁפְטִיmy judge for אֶתְחַנָּןbeg

For even if I were right, I could not answer. I could only beg my Judge for mercy.

9:16

אִםif קָרָאתִיsummoned וַיַּעֲנֵנִיhim and he answered me לֹאnot אַאֲמִיןbelieve כִּיfor יַאֲזִיןlisten קוֹלִיmy voice

If I summoned Him and He answered me, I do not believe He would listen to my voice.

9:17

אֲשֶׁרthat בִּשְׂעָרָהtempest יְשׁוּפֵנִיcrush me וְהִרְבָּהand multiply פְצָעַיmy wounds חִנָּםwithout cause

For He would crush me with a tempest and multiply my wounds without cause.

9:18

לֹאnot יִתְּנֵנִיlet me הָשֵׁבcatch רוּחִיmy breath כִּיbut יַשְׂבִּעַנִיoverwhelms me מַמְּרֹרִיםbitterness

He does not let me catch my breath, but overwhelms me with bitterness.

9:19

אִםif לְכֹחַstrength אַמִּיץmighty הִנֵּהindeed וְאִםif לְמִשְׁפָּטjustice מִיwho יוֹעִידֵנִיsummon him

If it is a matter of strength, He is indeed mighty! If it is a matter of justice, who can summon Him?

9:20

אִםif אֶצְדָּקrighteous פִּיmy mouth would יַרְשִׁיעֵנִיcondemn me תָּםblameless אָנִיI וַיַּעְקְשֵׁנִיwould declare me guilty

Even if I were righteous, my mouth would condemn me; if I were blameless, it would declare me guilty.

9:21

תָּםblameless אָנִיam I לֹאno אֵדַעconcern נַפְשִׁיmy soul אֶמְאַסdespise חַיָּיmy own life

Though I am blameless, I have no concern for myself; I despise my own life.

9:22

אַחַתsame הִיאis עַלon כֵּןso אָמַרְתִּיsay תָּםblameless וְרָשָׁעand the wicked הוּאhe מְכַלֶּהdestroys

It is all the same, and so I say, ‘He destroys both the blameless and the wicked.’

9:23

אִםif שׁוֹטscourge יָמִיתdeath פִּתְאֹםsudden לְמַסַּתdespair נְקִיִּםinnocent יִלְעָגmocks

When the scourge brings sudden death, He mocks the despair of the innocent.

9:24

אֶרֶץearth נִתְּנָהgiven בְיַדinto the hand רָשָׁעwicked פְּנֵיface שֹׁפְטֶיהָits judges יְכַסֶּהblindfolds אִםif לֹאnot אֵפוֹאthen מִיwho הוּאis

The earth is given into the hand of the wicked; He blindfolds its judges. If it is not He, then who is it?

9:25

וְיָמַיmy days קַלּוּswifter מִנִּיthan רָץrunner בָּרְחוּflee לֹאnot רָאוּseeing טוֹבָהgood

My days are swifter than a runner; they flee without seeing good.

9:26

חָלְפוּsweep עִםwith אֳנִיּוֹתboats אֵבֶהpapyrus כְּנֶשֶׁרlike an eagle יָטוּשׂswooping עֲלֵיon אֹכֶלprey

They sweep by like boats of papyrus, like an eagle swooping down on its prey.

9:27

אִםif אָמְרִיsay I אֶשְׁכְּחָהforget שִׂיחִיmy complaint אֶעֶזְבָהchange פָנַיchange my וְאַבְלִיגָהexpression and smile

If I were to say, ‘I will forget my complaint and change my expression and smile,’

9:28

יָגֹרְתִּיdread כָלall עַצְּבֹתָיmy sufferings יָדַעְתִּיknow כִּיthat לֹאnot תְנַקֵּנִיacquit me

I would still dread all my sufferings; I know that You will not acquit me.

9:29

אָנֹכִיI am אֶרְשָׁעguilty לָמָּהwhy should זֶּהthis הֶבֶלvain אִיגָעlabor

Since I am already found guilty, why should I labor in vain?

9:30

אִםif הִתְרָחַצְתִּיwash בְמֵיin water שָׁלֶגsnow וַהֲזִכּוֹתִיand pure בְּבֹרwith lye כַּפָּיmy hands

If I should wash myself with snow and cleanse my hands with lye,

9:31

אָזthen בַּשַּׁחַתinto the pit תִּטְבְּלֵנִיplunge me וְתִעֲבוּנִיwould despise שַׂלְמוֹתָיclothes

then You would plunge me into the pit, and even my own clothes would despise me.

9:32

כִּיfor לֹאnot אִישׁman כָּמֹנִיlike me אֶעֱנֶנּוּanswer him נָבוֹאtake יַחְדָּוtogether בַּמִּשְׁפָּטin the judgment

For He is not a man like me, that I can answer Him, that we can take each other to court.

9:33

לֹאnor יֵשׁthere בֵּינֵינוּbetween us מוֹכִיחַmediator יָשֵׁתlay יָדוֹhis hand עַלupon שְׁנֵינוּus both

Nor is there a mediator between us, to lay his hand upon us both.

9:34

יָסֵרremove מֵעָלַיfrom the me שִׁבְטוֹhis rod וְאֵמָתוֹso that his terror will אַלno תְּבַעֲתַנִּיfrighten me

Let Him remove His rod from me, so that His terror will no longer frighten me.

9:35

אַדַבְּרָהspeak וְלֹאand not אִירָאֶנּוּfear of him כִּיbut לֹאnot כֵןthis אָנֹכִיI am עִמָּדִיwith me

Then I would speak without fear of Him. But as it is, I am on my own.
