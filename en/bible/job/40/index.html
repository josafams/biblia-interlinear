Job 40

Job falls silent; Behemoth

Open in the interlinear reader

40:1

וַיַּעַןand answered יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] אִיּוֹבJob וַיֹּאמַרsaid to

And the LORD said to Job:

40:2

הֲרֹבfaultfinder contend עִםwith שַׁדַּיAlmighty יִסּוֹרlet מוֹכִיחַhim אֱלוֹהַּGod יַעֲנֶנָּהan answer

“Will the faultfinder contend with the Almighty? Let him who argues with God give an answer.”

40:3

וַיַּעַןthen answered אִיּוֹבJob אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD וַיֹּאמַרand said

Then Job answered the LORD:

40:4

הֵןbehold קַלֹּתִיinsignificant מָהhow אֲשִׁיבֶךָּreply to you יָדִיmy hand שַׂמְתִּיplace לְמוֹover פִיmy mouth

“Behold, I am insignificant. How can I reply to You? I place my hand over my mouth.

40:5

אַחַתonce דִּבַּרְתִּיspoken וְלֹאno אֶעֱנֶהanswer וּשְׁתַּיִםtwice וְלֹאand not אוֹסִיףadd

I have spoken once, but I have no answer— twice, but I have nothing to add.”

40:6

וַיַּעַןanswered יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] אִיּוֹבJob מנout מִןof סְעָרָהwhirlwind וַיֹּאמַרand said

Then the LORD answered Job out of the whirlwind and said:

40:7

אֱזָרbrace yourself נָאnow כְגֶבֶרlike a man חֲלָצֶיךָquestion אֶשְׁאָלְךָquestion you וְהוֹדִיעֵנִיinform me

“Now brace yourself like a man; I will question you, and you shall inform Me.

40:8

הַאַףwould you really תָּפֵרannul מִשְׁפָּטִיmy justice would תַּרְשִׁיעֵנִיcondemn me לְמַעַןto תִּצְדָּקjustify

Would you really annul My justice? Would you condemn Me to justify yourself?

40:9

וְאִםand if זְרוֹעַarm כָּאֵלlike God’s לָךְto you וּבְקוֹלwith a voice כָּמֹהוּlike his תַרְעֵםthunder

Do you have an arm like God’s? Can you thunder with a voice like His?

40:10

עֲדֵהadorn נָאplease גָאוֹןmajesty וָגֹבַהּand splendor וְהוֹדglory וְהָדָרhonor and תִּלְבָּשׁclothe

Then adorn yourself with majesty and splendor, and clothe yourself with honor and glory.

40:11

הָפֵץunleash עֶבְרוֹתwrath אַפֶּךָyour anger וּרְאֵהlook כָלevery גֵּאֶהproud וְהַשְׁפִּילֵהוּand bring him low

Unleash the fury of your wrath; look on every proud man and bring him low.

40:12

רְאֵהlook כָלevery גֵּאֶהproud הַכְנִיעֵהוּhumble him וַהֲדֹךְtrample רְשָׁעִיםwicked תַּחְתָּםunder them

Look on every proud man and humble him; trample the wicked where they stand.

40:13

טָמְנֵםbury them בֶּעָפָרin the dust יָחַדtogether פְּנֵיהֶםtheir face חֲבֹשׁimprison בַּטָּמוּןin the grave

Bury them together in the dust; imprison them in the grave.

40:14

וְגַםand also אֲנִיI אוֹדֶךָּconfess you כִּיthat תוֹשִׁעַsave לְךָto יְמִינֶךָright hand

Then I will confess to you that your own right hand can save you.

40:15

הִנֵּהlook נָאplease בְהֵמוֹתBehemoth אֲשֶׁרwhich עָשִׂיתִיmade עִמָּךְwith חָצִירgrass כַּבָּקָרlike an ox יֹאכֵלeat

Look at Behemoth, which I made along with you. He feeds on grass like an ox.

40:16

הִנֵּהsee נָאplease כֹחוֹstrength of his בְמָתְנָיוloins וְאֹנוֹand the power בִּשְׁרִירֵיin the muscles בִטְנוֹhis belly

See the strength of his loins and the power in the muscles of his belly.

40:17

יַחְפֹּץsways זְנָבוֹhis tail כְמוֹlike אָרֶזcedar גִּידֵיsinews פַחֲדָיוhis thighs יְשֹׂרָגוּknit

His tail sways like a cedar; the sinews of his thighs are tightly knit.

40:18

עֲצָמָיוhis bones אֲפִיקֵיtubes נְחוּשָׁהbronze גְּרָמָיוhis limbs כִּמְטִילrods בַּרְזֶלiron

His bones are tubes of bronze; his limbs are rods of iron.

40:19

הוּאis רֵאשִׁיתforemost דַּרְכֵיways אֵלGod’s הָעֹשׂוֹonly his Maker יַגֵּשׁMaker חַרְבּוֹsword against him

He is the foremost of God’s works; only his Maker can draw the sword against him.

40:20

כִּיfor בוּלyield הָרִיםhills יִשְׂאוּbear לוֹto him וְכָלwhile all חַיַּתbeasts הַשָּׂדֶהthe field יְשַׂחֲקוּplay שָׁםthere

The hills yield him their produce, while all the beasts of the field play nearby.

40:21

תַּחַתunder צֶאֱלִיםlotus plants יִשְׁכָּבlies בְּסֵתֶרhidden among קָנֶהreeds וּבִצָּהmarsh

He lies under the lotus plants, hidden among the reeds of the marsh.

40:22

יְסֻכֻּהוּlotus צֶאֱלִיםplants צִלֲלוֹconceal him יְסֻבּוּהוּsurround him עַרְבֵיwillows נָחַלbrook

The lotus plants conceal him in their shade; the willows of the brook surround him.

40:23

הֵןbehold יַעֲשֹׁקrages נָהָרriver לֹאnot יַחְפּוֹזBehemoth unafraid יִבְטַחsecure כִּיfor יָגִיחַsurges יַרְדֵּןJordan אֶלto פִּיהוּhis mouth

Though the river rages, Behemoth is unafraid; he remains secure, though the Jordan surges to his mouth.

40:24

בְּעֵינָיוlooks יִקָּחֶנּוּcapture בְּמוֹקְשִׁיםsnare יִנְקָבpierce אָףnose

Can anyone capture him as he looks on, or pierce his nose with a snare?
