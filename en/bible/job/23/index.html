Job 23

Job seeks God and does not find Him

Open in the interlinear reader

23:1

וַיַּעַןthen answered אִיּוֹבJob וַיֹּאמַרand said

Then Job answered:

23:2

גַּםeven הַיּוֹםtoday מְרִיbitter שִׂחִיmy complaint יָדִיhand is כָּבְדָהheavy עַלon אַנְחָתִיmy groaning

“Even today my complaint is bitter. His hand is heavy despite my groaning.

23:3

מִיwho יִתֵּןonly I יָדַעְתִּיknew וְאֶמְצָאֵהוּwhere to find him אָבוֹאgo עַדto תְּכוּנָתוֹhis seat

If only I knew where to find Him, so that I could go to His seat.

23:4

אֶעֶרְכָהcase לְפָנָיוbefore him מִשְׁפָּטjustice וּפִיand my mouth אֲמַלֵּאfill תוֹכָחוֹתarguments

I would plead my case before Him and fill my mouth with arguments.

23:5

אֵדְעָהlearn מִלִּיםhow יַעֲנֵנִיanswer וְאָבִינָהand consider מַהwhat יֹּאמַרsay לִיto me

I would learn how He would answer, and consider what He would say.

23:6

הַבְּרָבin his great כֹּחַpower יָרִיבcontend עִמָּדִיwith me לֹאno אַךְbut הוּאhe יָשִׂםtake note בִּיin me

Would He contend with me in His great power? No, He would certainly take note of me.

23:7

שָׁםthere יָשָׁרupright נוֹכָחreason עִמּוֹwith him וַאֲפַלְּטָהdelivered לָנֶצַחforever מִשֹּׁפְטִיfrom my judge

Then an upright man could reason with Him, and I would be delivered forever from my Judge.

23:8

הֵןif קֶדֶםeast אֶהֱלֹךְgo וְאֵינֶנּוּhe is not there and וְאָחוֹרI go west וְלֹאand not אָבִיןfind לוֹto him

If I go east, He is not there, and if I go west, I cannot find Him.

23:9

שְׂמֹאולnorth בַּעֲשֹׂתוֹat work וְלֹאand not אָחַזbehold יַעְטֹףturns יָמִיןsouth וְלֹאand not אֶרְאֶהsee

When He is at work in the north, I cannot behold Him; when He turns to the south, I cannot see Him.

23:10

כִּיfor יָדַעknows דֶּרֶךְway עִמָּדִיwith me בְּחָנַנִיtested me כַּזָּהָבas gold אֵצֵאforth

Yet He knows the way I have taken; when He has tested me, I will come forth as gold.

23:11

בַּאֲשֻׁרוֹfollowed in his tracks אָחֲזָהtracks רַגְלִיmy feet דַּרְכּוֹhis way שָׁמַרְתִּיkept וְלֹאand not אָטturning

My feet have followed in His tracks; I have kept His way without turning aside.

23:12

מִצְוַתcommand שְׂפָתָיוhis lips וְלֹאI have not אָמִישׁdeparted מֵחֻקִּיdaily צָפַנְתִּיtreasured אִמְרֵיwords פִיוhis mouth

I have not departed from the command of His lips; I have treasured the words of His mouth more than my daily bread.

23:13

וְהוּאand he is בְאֶחָדis unchangeable וּמִיand who can יְשִׁיבֶנּוּoppose him וְנַפְשׁוֹhe does what he אִוְּתָהdesires וַיָּעַשׂand made

But He is unchangeable, and who can oppose Him? He does what He desires.

23:14

כִּיfor יַשְׁלִיםcarries חֻקִּיdecree me וְכָהֵנָּהsuch רַבּוֹתmany עִמּוֹagainst

For He carries out His decree against me, and He has many such plans.

23:15

עַלon כֵּןtherefore מִפָּנָיוin his presence אֶבָּהֵלterrified אֶתְבּוֹנֵןconsider וְאֶפְחַדthis I fear מִמֶּנּוּfrom him

Therefore I am terrified in His presence; when I consider this, I fear Him.

23:16

וְאֵלGod הֵרַךְfaint לִבִּיmy heart וְשַׁדַּיAlmighty הִבְהִילָנִיterrified me

God has made my heart faint; the Almighty has terrified me.

23:17

כִּיfor לֹאnot נִצְמַתִּיsilenced מִפְּנֵיfrom before חֹשֶׁךְdarkness וּמִפָּנַיmy face כִּסָּהcovers אֹפֶלdarkness

Yet I am not silenced by the darkness, by the thick darkness that covers my face.
