Job 4

Eliphaz: the innocent do not perish

Open in the interlinear reader

4:1

וַיַּעַןthen אֱלִיפַזEliphaz הַתֵּימָנִיthe Temanite וַיֹּאמַרreplied

Then Eliphaz the Temanite replied:

4:2

הֲנִסָּהif one ventures דָבָרword אֵלֶיךָto you תִּלְאֶהwearied וַעְצֹרand halted בְּמִלִּיןfrom speaking מִיwho יוּכָלcan

“If one ventures a word with you, will you be wearied? Yet who can keep from speaking?

4:3

הִנֵּהsurely יִסַּרְתָּinstructed רַבִּיםmany וְיָדַיִםhands רָפוֹתfeeble תְּחַזֵּקstrengthened

Surely you have instructed many, and have strengthened their feeble hands.

4:4

כּוֹשֵׁלstumbled יְקִימוּןsteadied מִלֶּיךָyour words וּבִרְכַּיִםbraced כֹּרְעוֹתknees תְּאַמֵּץknees

Your words have steadied those who stumbled; you have braced the knees that were buckling.

4:5

כִּיbut עַתָּהnow תָּבוֹאcome אֵלֶיךָto you וַתֵּלֶאweary תִּגַּעstrikes עָדֶיךָuntil you וַתִּבָּהֵלdismayed

But now trouble has come upon you, and you are weary. It strikes you, and you are dismayed.

4:6

הֲלֹאnot יִרְאָתְךָyour reverence כִּסְלָתֶךָyour confidence תִּקְוָתְךָyour hope וְתֹםand the uprightness דְּרָכֶיךָyour ways

Is your reverence not your confidence, and the uprightness of your ways your hope?

4:7

זְכָרconsider נָאnow מִיwho הוּאhe נָקִיinnocent אָבָדperished וְאֵיפֹהor where יְשָׁרִיםupright נִכְחָדוּdestroyed

Consider now, I plead: Who, being innocent, has ever perished? Or where have the upright been destroyed?

4:8

כַּאֲשֶׁרas I have רָאִיתִיobserved חֹרְשֵׁיplow אָוֶןiniquity וְזֹרְעֵיsow עָמָלtrouble יִקְצְרֻהוּreap the same

As I have observed, those who plow iniquity and those who sow trouble reap the same.

4:9

מִנִּשְׁמַתby the breath אֱלוֹהַGod יֹאבֵדוּperish וּמֵרוּחַand by the blast אַפּוֹhis anger they are יִכְלוּconsumed

By the breath of God they perish, and by the blast of His anger they are consumed.

4:10

שַׁאֲגַתroaring אַרְיֵהlion וְקוֹלroar and שָׁחַלfierce lion וְשִׁנֵּיyet the teeth כְפִירִיםyoung lions נִתָּעוּbroken

The lion may roar, and the fierce lion may growl, yet the teeth of the young lions are broken.

4:11

לַיִשׁold lion אֹבֵדperishes מִבְּלִיfor lack טָרֶףprey וּבְנֵיand sons לָבִיאlioness יִתְפָּרָדוּscattered

The old lion perishes for lack of prey, and the cubs of the lioness are scattered.

4:12

וְאֵלַיto me דָּבָרword יְגֻנָּבsecretly וַתִּקַּחcaught אָזְנִיmy ears שֵׁמֶץwhisper מֶנְהוּof it

Now a word came to me secretly; my ears caught a whisper of it.

4:13

בִּשְׂעִפִּיםin disquieting מֵחֶזְיֹנוֹתvisions in לָיְלָהnight בִּנְפֹלfalls תַּרְדֵּמָהdeep sleep עַלon אֲנָשִׁיםmen

In disquieting visions in the night, when deep sleep falls on men,

4:14

פַּחַדfear קְרָאַנִיcame over me וּרְעָדָהand trembling וְרֹבand made all עַצְמוֹתַיmy bones הִפְחִידshudder

fear and trembling came over me and made all my bones shudder.

4:15

וְרוּחַthen a spirit עַלon פָּנַיpast my face יַחֲלֹףpast תְּסַמֵּרglided שַׂעֲרַתhair בְּשָׂרִיmy body

Then a spirit glided past my face, and the hair on my body bristled.

4:16

יַעֲמֹדstood וְלֹאnot אַכִּירcould discern מַרְאֵהוּits appearance תְּמוּנָהform לְנֶגֶדloomed before עֵינָיmy eyes דְּמָמָהwhispering וָקוֹלvoice אֶשְׁמָעheard

It stood still, but I could not discern its appearance; a form loomed before my eyes, and I heard a whispering voice:

4:17

הַאֱנוֹשׁcan a mortal מֵאֱלוֹהַthan God יִצְדָּקrighteous אִםor מֵעֹשֵׂהוּthan his Maker יִטְהַרpure גָּבֶרman

‘Can a mortal be more righteous than God, or a man more pure than his Maker?

4:18

הֵןif בַּעֲבָדָיוin his servants לֹאno יַאֲמִיןtrust וּבְמַלְאָכָיוhis angels with יָשִׂיםcharges תָּהֳלָהerror

If God puts no trust in His servants, and He charges His angels with error,

4:19

אַףhow much שֹׁכְנֵיdwell בָתֵּיhouses חֹמֶרclay אֲשֶׁרthat בֶּעָפָרin the dust יְסוֹדָםfoundations are יְדַכְּאוּםcrushed לִפְנֵיto before עָשׁmoth

how much more those who dwell in houses of clay, whose foundations are in the dust, who can be crushed like a moth!

4:20

מִבֹּקֶרfrom dawn לָעֶרֶבare smashed יֻכַּתּוּsmashed pieces מִבְּלִיunnoticed מֵשִׂיםthey לָנֶצַחforever יֹאבֵדוּperish

They are smashed to pieces from dawn to dusk; unnoticed, they perish forever.

4:21

הֲלֹאare not נִסַּעtent pulled יִתְרָםtheir cords בָּםin them יָמוּתוּdie וְלֹאand not בְחָכְמָהwisdom

Are not their tent cords pulled up, so that they die without wisdom?’
