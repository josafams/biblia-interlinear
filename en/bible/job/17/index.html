Job 17

Job without hope before the grave

Open in the interlinear reader

17:1

רוּחִיmy spirit חֻבָּלָהbroken יָמַיmy days נִזְעָכוּextinguished קְבָרִיםgrave לִיto me

“My spirit is broken; my days are extinguished; the grave awaits me.

17:2

אִםif לֹאnot הֲתֻלִיםmockers עִמָּדִיwith me וּבְהַמְּרוֹתָםat their rebellion תָּלַןrebellion עֵינִיmy eyes

Surely mockers surround me, and my eyes must gaze at their rebellion.

17:3

שִׂימָהgive נָּאplease עָרְבֵנִיpledge עִמָּךְwith you מִיwho הוּאwill לְיָדִיelse יִתָּקֵעַguarantor

Give me, I pray, the pledge You demand. Who else will be my guarantor?

17:4

כִּיfor לִבָּםclosed their צָפַנְתָּminds מִּשָּׂכֶלto understanding עַלon כֵּןtherefore לֹאnot תְרֹמֵםexalt

You have closed their minds to understanding; therefore You will not exalt them.

17:5

לְחֵלֶקa man יַגִּידdenounces רֵעִיםfriends וְעֵינֵיeyes בָנָיוhis children תִּכְלֶנָהfail

If a man denounces his friends for a price, the eyes of his children will fail.

17:6

וְהִצִּגַנִיhe has made me לִמְשֹׁלa byword עַמִּיםpeople וְתֹפֶתman whose לְפָנִיםin face אֶהְיֶהbe

He has made me a byword among the people, a man in whose face they spit.

17:7

וַתֵּכַהּgrown dim מִכַּעַשׂwith grief עֵינִיmy eyes וִיצֻרַיand my whole כַּצֵּלa shadow כֻּלָּםtheir all

My eyes have grown dim with grief, and my whole body is but a shadow.

17:8

יָשֹׁמּוּappalled יְשָׁרִיםupright עַלon זֹאתthis וְנָקִיand the innocent עַלagainst חָנֵףgodless יִתְעֹרָרstirred

The upright are appalled at this, and the innocent are stirred against the godless.

17:9

וְיֹאחֵזone holds צַדִּיקrighteous דַּרְכּוֹhis way וּטֳהָרclean יָדַיִםhands יֹסִיףgrows אֹמֶץstronger

Yet a righteous one holds to his way, and the one with clean hands grows stronger.

17:10

וְאוּלָםand named כֻּלָּםall תָּשֻׁבוּback וּבֹאוּcome and נָאplease וְלֹאwill not אֶמְצָאfind בָכֶםamong you חָכָםwise

But come back and try again, all of you. For I will not find a wise man among you.

17:11

יָמַיmy days עָבְרוּpassed זִמֹּתַיmy plans נִתְּקוּbroken מוֹרָשֵׁיdesires לְבָבִיmy heart

My days have passed; my plans are broken off— even the desires of my heart.

17:12

לַיְלָהnight לְיוֹםinto day יָשִׂימוּmaking אוֹרlight קָרוֹבnear מִפְּנֵיin the face חֹשֶׁךְdarkness

They have turned night into day, making light seem near in the face of darkness.

17:13

אִםif אֲקַוֶּהlook שְׁאוֹלSheol בֵּיתִיmy home בַּחֹשֶׁךְin darkness רִפַּדְתִּיspread יְצוּעָיmy bed

If I look for Sheol as my home, if I spread out my bed in darkness,

17:14

לַשַּׁחַתand say to קָרָאתִיcorruption אָבִיmy father אָתָּהyou אִמִּיmy mother וַאֲחֹתִיor my sister לָרִמָּהto the worm

and say to corruption, ‘You are my father,’ and to the worm, ‘My mother,’ or ‘My sister,’

17:15

וְאַיֵּהwhere אֵפוֹthen תִקְוָתִיmy hope וְתִקְוָתִיhope for me מִיwho can יְשׁוּרֶנָּהsee any

where then is my hope? Who can see any hope for me?

17:16

בַּדֵּיgates שְׁאֹלSheol תֵּרַדְנָהdown אִםif יַחַדtogether עַלon עָפָרdust נָחַתresolution

Will it go down to the gates of Sheol? Will we go down together into the dust?”
