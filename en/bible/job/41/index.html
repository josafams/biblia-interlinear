Job 41

Leviathan

Open in the interlinear reader

41:1

תִּמְשֹׁךְpull לִוְיָתָןLeviathan בְּחַכָּהwith a hook וּבְחֶבֶלwith a rope תַּשְׁקִיעַtie לְשֹׁנוֹhis tongue

“Can you pull in Leviathan with a hook or tie down his tongue with a rope?

41:2

הֲתָשִׂיםcan you put אַגְמוֹןcord בְּאַפּוֹthrough his nose וּבְחוֹחַwith a hook תִּקּוֹבpierce לֶחֱיוֹhis jaw

Can you put a cord through his nose or pierce his jaw with a hook?

41:3

הֲיַרְבֶּהwill he אֵלֶיךָto you תַּחֲנוּנִיםmercy אִםor יְדַבֵּרspeak אֵלֶיךָto you רַכּוֹתsoftly

Will he beg you for mercy or speak to you softly?

41:4

הֲיִכְרֹתwill he make בְּרִיתcovenant עִמָּךְwith you תִּקָּחֶנּוּtake him לְעֶבֶדas a slave for עוֹלָםlife

Will he make a covenant with you to take him as a slave for life?

41:5

הַתְשַׂחֶקpet בּוֹin him כַּצִּפּוֹרlike a bird וְתִקְשְׁרֶנּוּor put him לְנַעֲרוֹתֶיךָyour maidens

Can you pet him like a bird or put him on a leash for your maidens?

41:6

יִכְרוּtraders barter עָלָיוfor him חַבָּרִיםor יֶחֱצוּהוּdivide him בֵּיןamong כְּנַעֲנִיםmerchants

Will traders barter for him or divide him among the merchants?

41:7

הַתְמַלֵּאfill בְשֻׂכּוֹתwith harpoons עוֹרוֹhis hide וּבְצִלְצַלwith fishing דָּגִיםspears רֹאשׁוֹhis head

Can you fill his hide with harpoons or his head with fishing spears?

41:8

שִׂיםlay עָלָיוon him כַּפֶּךָhand זְכֹרremember מִלְחָמָהbattle אַלdo not תּוֹסַףrepeat

If you lay a hand on him, you will remember the battle and never repeat it!

41:9

הֵןsurely תֹּחַלְתּוֹhope נִכְזָבָהovercoming הֲגַםalso אֶלto מַרְאָיוsight of him יֻטָלoverwhelming

Surely hope of overcoming him is false. Is not the sight of him overwhelming?

41:10

לֹאno אַכְזָרfierce כִּיfor יְעוּרֶנּוּrouse Leviathan וּמִיthen who הוּאis לְפָנַיto against me יִתְיַצָּבstand

No one is so fierce as to rouse Leviathan. Then who is able to stand against Me?

41:11

מִיwho הִקְדִּימַנִיgiven to me וַאֲשַׁלֵּםI should repay תַּחַתunder כָּלeverything הַשָּׁמַיִםheaven לִיto me הוּאis

Who has given to Me that I should repay him? Everything under heaven is Mine.

41:12

לוֹto him אַחֲרִישׁsilent בַּדָּיוlimbs his וּדְבַרand word גְּבוּרוֹתpower וְחִיןand graceful עֶרְכּוֹform

I cannot keep silent about his limbs, his power and graceful form.

41:13

מִיwho גִלָּהstrip פְּנֵיface לְבוּשׁוֹapproach him בְּכֶפֶלwith a bridle רִסְנוֹbridle מִיwho יָבוֹאcome

Who can strip off his outer coat? Who can approach him with a bridle?

41:14

דַּלְתֵיjaws פָנָיוhis face מִיwho can פִתֵּחַopen סְבִיבוֹתfearsome שִׁנָּיוhis teeth אֵימָהfearsome

Who can open his jaws, ringed by his fearsome teeth?

41:15

גַּאֲוָהpride אֲפִיקֵיtogether מָגִנִּיםsealed סָגוּרtightly חוֹתָםsealed צָרtogether

His rows of scales are his pride, tightly sealed together.

41:16

אֶחָדone בְּאֶחָדto another יִגַּשׁוּnear וְרוּחַair לֹאno יָבוֹאcome בֵינֵיהֶםbetween them

One scale is so near to another that no air can pass between them.

41:17

אִישׁthey בְּאָחִיהוּto one another יְדֻבָּקוּjoined יִתְלַכְּדוּclasp וְלֹאand not יִתְפָּרָדוּseparated

They are joined to one another; they clasp and cannot be separated.

41:18

עֲטִישֹׁתָיוhis snorting תָּהֶלflashes אוֹרlight וְעֵינָיוand his eyes כְּעַפְעַפֵּיlike the rays שָׁחַרdawn

His snorting flashes with light, and his eyes are like the rays of dawn.

41:19

מִפִּיוfrom his mouth לַפִּידִיםfirebrands יַהֲלֹכוּgo כִּידוֹדֵיstream אֵשׁfiery יִתְמַלָּטוּsparks shoot

Firebrands stream from his mouth; fiery sparks shoot forth!

41:20

מִנְּחִירָיוbillows from his nostrils יֵצֵאgo עָשָׁןsmoke כְּדוּדnostrils as נָפוּחַboiling וְאַגְמֹןreeds

Smoke billows from his nostrils as from a boiling pot over burning reeds.

41:21

נַפְשׁוֹhis breath גֶּחָלִיםcoals תְּלַהֵטsets ablaze וְלַהַבand flames מִפִּיוfrom his mouth יֵצֵאpour

His breath sets coals ablaze, and flames pour from his mouth.

41:22

בְּצַוָּארוֹin his neck יָלִיןremain עֹזstrength וּלְפָנָיוbefore him תָּדוּץdismay דְּאָבָהleaps

Strength resides in his neck, and dismay leaps before him.

41:23

מַפְּלֵיfolds בְשָׂרוֹhis flesh דָבֵקוּjoined יָצוּקfirm עָלָיוon him בַּלnot יִמּוֹטshaken

The folds of his flesh are tightly joined; they are firm and immovable.

41:24

לִבּוֹhis chest יָצוּקhard כְּמוֹas אָבֶןrock וְיָצוּקas hard כְּפֶלַחmillstone תַּחְתִּיתlower

His chest is as hard as a rock, as hard as a lower millstone!

41:25

מִשֵּׂתוֹLeviathan rises יָגוּרוּthrashing אֵלִיםmighty מִשְּׁבָרִיםterrified יִתְחַטָּאוּwithdraw

When Leviathan rises up, the mighty are terrified; they withdraw before his thrashing.

41:26

מַשִּׂיגֵהוּreaches him חֶרֶבsword בְּלִיno תָקוּםstand חֲנִיתspear מַסָּעor dart וְשִׁרְיָהor arrow

The sword that reaches him has no effect, nor does the spear or dart or arrow.

41:27

יַחְשֹׁבregards לְתֶבֶןas straw בַּרְזֶלiron לְעֵץwood רִקָּבוֹןrotten נְחוּשָׁהbronze

He regards iron as straw and bronze as rotten wood.

41:28

לֹאno יַבְרִיחֶנּוּhim flee בֶןson קָשֶׁתarrow לְקַשׁchaff נֶהְפְּכוּovertook לוֹto him אַבְנֵיslingstones become קָלַעslingstones

No arrow can make him flee; slingstones become like chaff to him.

41:29

כְּקַשׁas straw נֶחְשְׁבוּregarded תוֹתָחclub וְיִשְׂחַקand he laughs לְרַעַשׁat the sound כִּידוֹןlance

A club is regarded as straw, and he laughs at the sound of the lance.

41:30

תַּחְתָּיוunder him חַדּוּדֵיundersides חָרֶשׂjagged potsherds יִרְפַּדspreading חָרוּץsledge עֲלֵיon טִיטmud

His undersides are jagged potsherds, spreading out the mud like a threshing sledge.

41:31

יַרְתִּיחַmakes כַּסִּירthe seethe like מְצוּלָהdepths יָםsea יָשִׂיםmakes כַּמֶּרְקָחָהlike a jar of ointment

He makes the depths seethe like a cauldron; he makes the sea like a jar of ointment.

41:32

אַחֲרָיוbehind him יָאִירglistening wake נָתִיבwake יַחְשֹׁבthink תְּהוֹםdeep לְשֵׂיבָהhad white hair

He leaves a glistening wake behind him; one would think the deep had white hair!

41:33

אֵיןnothing עַלon עָפָרearth מָשְׁלוֹhis equal הֶעָשׂוּthe carved לִבְלִיto lack חָתdevoid

Nothing on earth is his equal— a creature devoid of fear!

41:34

אֵת[obj] כָּלall גָּבֹהַּhaughty יִרְאֶהlooks הוּאhe מֶלֶךְking עַלover כָּלall בְּנֵיsons שָׁחַץproud

He looks down on all the haughty; he is king over all the proud.”
