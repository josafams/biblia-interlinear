Job 24

The injustice suffered by the poor

Open in the interlinear reader

24:1

מַדּוּעַwhy מִשַּׁדַּיthe Almighty לֹאnot נִצְפְּנוּreserve עִתִּיםtimes וְיֹדְעָיוwho know him לֹאnever חָזוּsee יָמָיוhis days

“Why does the Almighty not reserve times for judgment? Why may those who know Him never see His days?

24:2

גְּבֻלוֹתmove יַשִּׂיגוּboundary עֵדֶרflocks גָּזְלוּstolen וַיִּרְעוּthey pasture

Men move boundary stones; they pasture stolen flocks.

24:3

חֲמוֹרdonkey יְתוֹמִיםfatherless יִנְהָגוּdrive יַחְבְּלוּpledge שׁוֹרox אַלְמָנָהwidow’s

They drive away the donkey of the fatherless and take the widow’s ox in pledge.

24:4

יַטּוּpush אֶבְיוֹנִיםneedy מִדָּרֶךְoff the road יַחַדall חֻבְּאוּhiding עֲנִיֵּיpoor אָרֶץland

They push the needy off the road and force all the poor of the land into hiding.

24:5

הֵןindeed פְּרָאִיםwild donkeys בַּמִּדְבָּרin the desert יָצְאוּgo בְּפָעֳלָםto work מְשַׁחֲרֵיforaging לַטָּרֶףfor food the עֲרָבָהwasteland לוֹto him לֶחֶםfood לַנְּעָרִיםfor their children

Indeed, like wild donkeys in the desert, the poor go to work foraging for food; the wasteland is food for their children.

24:6

בַּשָּׂדֶהin the fields בְּלִילוֹfodder יִקְצוֹרוּgather וְכֶרֶםvineyards רָשָׁעwicked יְלַקֵּשׁוּglean

They gather fodder in the fields and glean the vineyards of the wicked.

24:7

עָרוֹםnaked יָלִינוּspend night מִבְּלִיwithout לְבוּשׁclothing וְאֵיןno כְּסוּתcovering בַּקָּרָהagainst the cold

Without clothing, they spend the night naked; they have no covering against the cold.

24:8

מִזֶּרֶםdrenched by הָרִיםmountain יִרְטָבוּrains וּמִבְּלִיand from without מַחְסֶהshelter חִבְּקוּhuddle צוּרrocks

Drenched by mountain rains, they huddle against the rocks for want of shelter.

24:9

יִגְזְלוּsnatched מִשֹּׁדfrom the breast יָתוֹםfatherless וְעַלnursing child עָנִיpoor יַחְבֹּלוּseized

The fatherless infant is snatched from the breast; the nursing child of the poor is seized for a debt.

24:10

עָרוֹםnaked הִלְּכוּwander בְּלִיwithout לְבוּשׁclothing וּרְעֵבִיםhungry נָשְׂאוּcarry עֹמֶרsheaves

Without clothing, they wander about naked. They carry the sheaves, but still go hungry.

24:11

בֵּיןbetween שׁוּרֹתָםcrush olives their יַצְהִירוּwalls יְקָבִיםwinepresses דָּרְכוּtread וַיִּצְמָאוּbut go thirsty

They crush olives within their walls; they tread the winepresses, but go thirsty.

24:12

מֵעִירfrom the city מְתִיםmen יִנְאָקוּgroan וְנֶפֶשׁand the souls חֲלָלִיםwounded תְּשַׁוֵּעַcry וֶאֱלוֹהַּyet God לֹאno יָשִׂיםcharges תִּפְלָהwrongdoing

From the city, men groan, and the souls of the wounded cry out, yet God charges no one with wrongdoing.

24:13

הֵמָּהthey הָיוּare בְּמֹרְדֵיrebel against אוֹרlight לֹאnot הִכִּירוּknowing דְרָכָיוits ways וְלֹאor יָשְׁבוּstaying בִּנְתִיבֹתָיוon its paths

Then there are those who rebel against the light, not knowing its ways or staying on its paths.

24:14

לָאוֹרwhen daylight יָקוּםrises רוֹצֵחַmurderer יִקְטָלkill עָנִיpoor וְאֶבְיוֹןand needy וּבַלַּיְלָהin the night יְהִיmay כַגַּנָּבlike a thief

When daylight is gone, the murderer rises to kill the poor and needy; in the night he is like a thief.

24:15

וְעֵיןeye נֹאֵףadulterer שָׁמְרָהwatches נֶשֶׁףtwilight לֵאמֹרfor thinking לֹאno תְשׁוּרֵנִיsee me עָיִןeye וְסֵתֶרhe covers פָּנִיםface יָשִׂיםputs

The eye of the adulterer watches for twilight. Thinking, ‘No eye will see me,’ he covers his face.

24:16

חָתַרdig בַּחֹשֶׁךְthe dark בָּתִּיםhouses יוֹמָםby day חִתְּמוּshut לָמוֹto them לֹאnever יָדְעוּexperience אוֹרlight

In the dark they dig through houses; by day they shut themselves in, never to experience the light.

24:17

כִּיfor יַחְדָּוtogether בֹּקֶרmorning לָמוֹto them צַלְמָוֶתdeep darkness כִּיfor יַכִּירfriends בַּלְהוֹתterrors צַלְמָוֶתdarkness

For to them, deep darkness is their morning; surely they are friends with the terrors of darkness!

24:18

קַלfoam הוּאhe עַלon פְּנֵיsurface מַיִםwater תְּקֻלַּלcursed חֶלְקָתָםtheir portion בָּאָרֶץthe land לֹאno יִפְנֶהturns דֶּרֶךְtoward כְּרָמִיםvineyards

They are but foam on the surface of the water; their portion of the land is cursed, so that no one turns toward their vineyards.

24:19

צִיָּהdrought גַםand חֹםheat יִגְזְלוּconsume מֵימֵיwaters שֶׁלֶגmelting snow שְׁאוֹלSheol חָטָאוּsinned

As drought and heat consume the melting snow, so Sheol steals those who have sinned.

24:20

יִשְׁכָּחֵהוּforgets רֶחֶםwomb מְתָקוֹforgets them רִמָּהworm עוֹדmore לֹאno יִזָּכֵרremembered וַתִּשָּׁבֵרbroken כָּעֵץlike a tree עַוְלָהinjustice

The womb forgets them; the worm feeds on them; they are remembered no more. So injustice is like a broken tree.

24:21

רֹעֶהprey עֲקָרָהbarren לֹאnot תֵלֵדchildless וְאַלְמָנָהwidow לֹאno יְיֵטִיבkindness

They prey on the barren and childless, and show no kindness to the widow.

24:22

וּמָשַׁךְdrags אַבִּירִיםmighty בְּכֹחוֹby his power God יָקוּםrising up וְלֹאthey have no יַאֲמִיןassurance בַּחַיִּיןof life

Yet by His power, God drags away the mighty; though rising up, they have no assurance of life.

24:23

יִתֶּןgives לוֹto him לָבֶטַחa sense וְיִשָּׁעֵןsecurity וְעֵינֵיהוּbut his eyes עַלon דַּרְכֵיהֶםtheir ways

He gives them a sense of security, but His eyes are on their ways.

24:24

רוֹמּוּexalted מְּעַטlittle וְאֵינֶנּוּand no him וְהֻמְּכוּlow and gathered כַּכֹּלlike all יִקָּפְצוּןothers וּכְרֹאשׁlike heads שִׁבֹּלֶתgrain יִמָּלוּwither

They are exalted for a moment, then they are gone; they are brought low and gathered up like all others; they are cut off like heads of grain.

24:25

וְאִםif לֹאnot אֵפוֹthen מִיwho can יַכְזִיבֵנִיprove me a liar וְיָשֵׂםand reduce לְאַלto do not מִלָּתִיmy words

If this is not so, then who can prove me a liar and reduce my words to nothing?”
