Job 22

Eliphaz accuses Job of injustice

Open in the interlinear reader

22:1

וַיַּעַןthen אֱלִיפַזEliphaz הַתֵּמָנִיthe Temanite וַיֹּאמַרreplied

Then Eliphaz the Temanite replied:

22:2

הַלְאֵלto God יִסְכָּןcan גָּבֶרman כִּיfor יִסְכֹּןbenefit עָלֵימוֹon him מַשְׂכִּילwise

“Can a man be of use to God? Can even a wise man benefit Him?

22:3

הַחֵפֶץit delight לְשַׁדַּיthe Almighty כִּיthat תִצְדָּקrighteous וְאִםhe if בֶּצַעprofit כִּיfor תַתֵּםblameless דְּרָכֶיךָyour ways

Does it delight the Almighty that you are righteous? Does He profit if your ways are blameless?

22:4

הֲמִיִּרְאָתְךָis it for your reverence יֹכִיחֶךָrebukes you יָבוֹאenters עִמְּךָagainst you בַּמִּשְׁפָּטinto judgment

Is it for your reverence that He rebukes you and enters into judgment against you?

22:5

הֲלֹאis not רָעָתְךָyour wickedness רַבָּהgreat וְאֵיןnot קֵץendless לַעֲוֺנֹתֶיךָyour iniquities

Is not your wickedness great? Are not your iniquities endless?

22:6

כִּיfor תַחְבֹּלsecurity אַחֶיךָyour brothers חִנָּםdeprived וּבִגְדֵיclothing עֲרוּמִּיםnaked תַּפְשִׁיטraided

For you needlessly demanded security from your brothers and deprived the naked of their clothing.

22:7

לֹאno מַיִםwater עָיֵףweary תַּשְׁקֶהgave וּמֵרָעֵבfrom the famished תִּמְנַעwithheld לָחֶםfood

You gave no water to the weary and withheld food from the famished,

22:8

וְאִישׁman זְרוֹעַmighty לוֹto הָאָרֶץthe land וּנְשׂוּאhonor פָנִיםface יֵשֶׁבlived בָּהּon it

while the land belonged to a mighty man, and a man of honor lived on it.

22:9

אַלְמָנוֹתwidows שִׁלַּחְתָּsent רֵיקָםempty handed וּזְרֹעוֹתand the strength יְתֹמִיםfatherless יְדֻכָּאcrushed

You sent widows away empty-handed, and the strength of the fatherless was crushed.

22:10

עַלon כֵּןtherefore סְבִיבוֹתֶיךָsurround you פַחִיםsnares וִיבַהֶלְךָand sudden פַּחַדterrifies פִּתְאֹםperil

Therefore snares surround you, and sudden peril terrifies you;

22:11

אוֹor חֹשֶׁךְdark לֹאnot תִרְאֶהsee וְשִׁפְעַתand a flood מַיִםwater תְּכַסֶּךָּcovers you

it is so dark you cannot see, and a flood of water covers you.

22:12

הֲלֹאis not אֱלוֹהַּGod גֹּבַהּhigh שָׁמָיִםheavens וּרְאֵהlook רֹאשׁthe highest כּוֹכָבִיםstars כִּיfor רָמּוּlofty

Is not God as high as the heavens? Look at the highest stars, how lofty they are!

22:13

וְאָמַרְתָּyet you say מַהwhat יָּדַעknow אֵלGod הַבְעַדthrough עֲרָפֶלthick darkness יִשְׁפּוֹטjudge

Yet you say: ‘What does God know? Does He judge through thick darkness?

22:14

עָבִיםthick clouds סֵתֶרveil לוֹto him וְלֹאnot יִרְאֶהsee וְחוּגvault שָׁמַיִםheaven יִתְהַלָּךְwalk

Thick clouds veil Him so He does not see us as He traverses the vault of heaven.’

22:15

הַאֹרַחpath עוֹלָםancient תִּשְׁמֹרkeep אֲשֶׁרthat דָּרְכוּtrod מְתֵיmen אָוֶןwicked

Will you stay on the ancient path that wicked men have trod?

22:16

אֲשֶׁרthat קֻמְּטוּsnatched וְלֹאand not עֵתtime נָהָרflood יוּצַקswept יְסוֹדָםtheir foundations

They were snatched away before their time, and their foundations were swept away by a flood.

22:17

הָאֹמְרִיםsaid לָאֵלto God סוּרdepart מִמֶּנּוּfrom us וּמַהwhat יִּפְעַלdo שַׁדַּיAlmighty לָמוֹto us

They said to God, ‘Depart from us. What can the Almighty do to us?’

22:18

וְהוּאhe מִלֵּאfilled בָתֵּיהֶםtheir houses טוֹבgood וַעֲצַתcounsel רְשָׁעִיםwicked רָחֲקָהfar מֶנִּיof

But it was He who filled their houses with good things; so I stay far from the counsel of the wicked.

22:19

יִרְאוּsee צַדִּיקִיםrighteous וְיִשְׂמָחוּand are glad וְנָקִיinnocent יִלְעַגmock לָמוֹto them

The righteous see it and are glad; the innocent mock them:

22:20

אִםif לֹאnot נִכְחַדdestroyed קִימָנוּour foes וְיִתְרָםtheir excess אָכְלָהconsumed אֵשׁfire

‘Surely our foes are destroyed, and fire has consumed their excess.’

22:21

הַסְכֶּןreconcile נָאnow עִמּוֹwith him וּשְׁלםpeace בָּהֶםin them תְּבוֹאַתְךָcome to you טוֹבָהgood

Reconcile now and be at peace with Him; thereby good will come to you.

22:22

קַחreceive נָאplease מִפִּיוfrom his mouth תּוֹרָהinstruction וְשִׂיםand lay אֲמָרָיוhis words בִּלְבָבֶךָin your heart

Receive instruction from His mouth, and lay up His words in your heart.

22:23

אִםif תָּשׁוּבreturn עַדto שַׁדַּיAlmighty תִּבָּנֶהrestored תַּרְחִיקremove עַוְלָהinjustice מֵאָהֳלֶךָfrom your tents

If you return to the Almighty, you will be restored. If you remove injustice from your tents

22:24

וְשִׁיתand consign עַלto עָפָרdust בָּצֶרgold וּבְצוּרto the stones נְחָלִיםravines אוֹפִירOphir

and consign your gold to the dust and the gold of Ophir to the stones of the ravines,

22:25

וְהָיָהand be שַׁדַּיAlmighty בְּצָרֶיךָyour gold וְכֶסֶףsilver תּוֹעָפוֹתfinest לָךְfor you

then the Almighty will be your gold and the finest silver for you.

22:26

כִּיfor אָזthen עַלin שַׁדַּיAlmighty תִּתְעַנָּגdelight וְתִשָּׂאand lift אֶלto אֱלוֹהַּGod פָּנֶיךָyour face

Surely then you will delight in the Almighty and lift up your face to God.

22:27

תַּעְתִּירpray אֵלָיוto him וְיִשְׁמָעֶךָּhear you וּנְדָרֶיךָfulfill your vows תְשַׁלֵּםfulfill

You will pray to Him, and He will hear you, and you will fulfill your vows.

22:28

וְתִגְזַרdecisions אוֹמֶרcarried וְיָקָםand up לָךְto you וְעַלon דְּרָכֶיךָyour ways נָגַהּshine אוֹרlight

Your decisions will be carried out, and light will shine on your ways.

22:29

כִּיwhen הִשְׁפִּילוּlow וַתֹּאמֶרand you say גֵּוָהlift וְשַׁחlowly עֵינַיִםeyes יוֹשִׁעַsave

When men are brought low and you say, ‘Lift them up!’ then He will save the lowly.

22:30

יְמַלֵּטdeliver אִיrescuing נָקִיinnocent וְנִמְלַטrescuing בְּבֹרthrough the cleanness כַּפֶּיךָyour hands

He will deliver even one who is not innocent, rescuing him through the cleanness of your hands.”
