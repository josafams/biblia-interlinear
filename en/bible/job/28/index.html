Job 28

Where wisdom is found

Open in the interlinear reader

28:1

כִּיfor יֵשׁthere לַכֶּסֶףa for silver מוֹצָאmine וּמָקוֹםand a place לַזָּהָבwhere gold יָזֹקּוּrefined

“Surely there is a mine for silver and a place where gold is refined.

28:2

בַּרְזֶלiron מֵעָפָרfrom the earth יֻקָּחtaken וְאֶבֶןand copper יָצוּקsmelted נְחוּשָׁהore

Iron is taken from the earth, and copper is smelted from ore.

28:3

קֵץend שָׂםputs לַחֹשֶׁךְto the darkness וּלְכָלand to all תַּכְלִיתprobes הוּאthe חוֹקֵרfarthest אֶבֶןrecesses אֹפֶלore וְצַלְמָוֶתin deepest darkness

Man puts an end to the darkness; he probes the farthest recesses for ore in deepest darkness.

28:4

פָּרַץfar נַחַלhuman מֵעִםfrom presence גָּרhabitation הַנִּשְׁכָּחִיםforgotten מִנִּיfrom רָגֶלfoot דַּלּוּman מֵאֱנוֹשׁfar from men נָעוּsways

Far from human habitation he cuts a shaft in places forgotten by the foot of man. Far from men he dangles and sways.

28:5

אֶרֶץearth מִמֶּנָּהfrom the יֵצֵאcome לָחֶםfood וְתַחְתֶּיהָbut from below it נֶהְפַּךְtransformed כְּמוֹas אֵשׁfire

Food may come from the earth, but from below it is transformed as by fire.

28:6

מְקוֹםsource סַפִּירsapphires אֲבָנֶיהָits rocks וְעַפְרֹתcontaining flecks זָהָבgold לוֹto him

Its rocks are the source of sapphires, containing flecks of gold.

28:7

נָתִיבpath לֹאno יְדָעוֹknows עָיִטprey וְלֹאno שְׁזָפַתּוּfalcon’s עֵיןeye אַיָּהseen

No bird of prey knows that path; no falcon’s eye has seen it.

28:8

לֹאnever הִדְרִיכֻהוּtrodden it בְנֵיbeasts שָׁחַץproud לֹאno עָדָהprowled עָלָיוover it שָׁחַלlion

Proud beasts have never trodden it; no lion has ever prowled over it.

28:9

בַּחַלָּמִישׁthe miner שָׁלַחstrikes יָדוֹhis hand הָפַךְoverturns מִשֹּׁרֶשׁat their base הָרִיםmountains

The miner strikes the flint; he overturns mountains at their base.

28:10

בַּצּוּרוֹתin the rocks יְאֹרִיםcanals בִּקֵּעַchannels וְכָלevery יְקָרtreasure רָאֲתָהseen עֵינוֹhis eyes spot

He hews out channels in the rocks, and his eyes spot every treasure.

28:11

מִבְּכִיstops up the נְהָרוֹתstreams חִבֵּשׁsources וְתַעֲלֻמָהּhidden יֹצִאbring אוֹרlight

He stops up the sources of the streams to bring what is hidden to light.

28:12

וְהַחָכְמָהwisdom מֵאַיִןwhere תִּמָּצֵאfound וְאֵיand where זֶהthis מְקוֹםdoes dwell בִּינָהunderstanding

But where can wisdom be found, and where does understanding dwell?

28:13

לֹאno יָדַעknow אֱנוֹשׁman עֶרְכָּהּits value וְלֹאand not תִמָּצֵאfound בְּאֶרֶץin the land הַחַיִּיםthe living

No man can know its value, nor is it found in the land of the living.

28:14

תְּהוֹםocean depths אָמַרsay לֹאnot בִיin me הִיאthe וְיָםwhile sea אָמַרdeclares אֵיןnot עִמָּדִיwith me

The ocean depths say, ‘It is not in me,’ while the sea declares, ‘It is not with me.’

28:15

לֹאnot יֻתַּןgive סְגוֹרbought תַּחְתֶּיהָunder her וְלֹאand not יִשָּׁקֵלweighed כֶּסֶףsilver מְחִירָהּits price be

It cannot be bought with gold, nor can its price be weighed out in silver.

28:16

לֹאnot תְסֻלֶּהvalued בְּכֶתֶםin the gold אוֹפִירOphir בְּשֹׁהַםin onyx יָקָרprecious וְסַפִּירor sapphire

It cannot be valued in the gold of Ophir, in precious onyx or sapphire.

28:17

לֹאnot יַעַרְכֶנָּהcompare to it זָהָבgold וּזְכוֹכִיתnor crystal וּתְמוּרָתָהּnor jewels כְּלִיfine פָזgold

Neither gold nor crystal can compare to it, nor jewels of fine gold be exchanged for it.

28:18

רָאמוֹתcoral וְגָבִישׁand quartz לֹאnot יִזָּכֵרmention וּמֶשֶׁךְprice חָכְמָהwisdom מִפְּנִינִיםbeyond rubies

Coral and quartz are unworthy of mention; the price of wisdom is beyond rubies.

28:19

לֹאnot יַעַרְכֶנָּהcompare to it פִּטְדַתtopaz כּוּשׁCush בְּכֶתֶםin pure gold טָהוֹרpure לֹאnot תְסֻלֶּהvalued

Topaz from Cush cannot compare to it, nor can it be valued in pure gold.

28:20

וְהַחָכְמָהthen does wisdom מֵאַיִןfrom where תָּבוֹאcome וְאֵיand where זֶהthis מְקוֹםdoes dwell בִּינָהunderstanding

From where then does wisdom come, and where does understanding dwell?

28:21

וְנֶעֶלְמָהit is hidden מֵעֵינֵיfrom the eyes כָלevery חָיliving וּמֵעוֹףand from the birds הַשָּׁמַיִםthe air נִסְתָּרָהconcealed

It is hidden from the eyes of every living thing and concealed from the birds of the air.

28:22

אֲבַדּוֹןAbaddon וָמָוֶתand death אָמְרוּsay בְּאָזְנֵינוּin our hearing שָׁמַעְנוּheard שִׁמְעָהּrumor about it

Abaddon and Death say, ‘We have heard a rumor about it.’

28:23

אֱלֹהִיםGod הֵבִיןunderstands דַּרְכָּהּits way וְהוּאand he יָדַעknows אֶת[obj] מְקוֹמָהּits place

But God understands its way, and He knows its place.

28:24

כִּיfor הוּאhe לִקְצוֹתto the ends הָאָרֶץthe earth יַבִּיטlooks תַּחַתunder כָּלeverything הַשָּׁמַיִםthe heavens יִרְאֶהsees

For He looks to the ends of the earth and sees everything under the heavens.

28:25

לַעֲשׂוֹתto do לָרוּחַthe wind מִשְׁקָלweight וּמַיִםwaters תִּכֵּןmeasured בְּמִדָּהin size

When God fixed the weight of the wind and measured out the waters,

28:26

בַּעֲשֹׂתוֹwhen he set לַמָּטָרfor the rain חֹקlimit וְדֶרֶךְand a path לַחֲזִיזfor the thunderbolt קֹלוֹתthunder

when He set a limit for the rain and a path for the thunderbolt,

28:27

אָזthen רָאָהּlooked at wisdom וַיְסַפְּרָהּand appraised it הֱכִינָהּestablished it וְגַםand also חֲקָרָהּsearched it

then He looked at wisdom and appraised it; He established it and searched it out.

28:28

וַיֹּאמֶרand he said לָאָדָםto man הֵןbehold יִרְאַתfear אֲדֹנָיLord הִיאthat is חָכְמָהwisdom וְסוּרand to turn מֵרָעaway from evil בִּינָהunderstanding

And He said to man, ‘Behold, the fear of the Lord, that is wisdom, and to turn away from evil is understanding.’”
