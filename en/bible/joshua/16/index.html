Joshua 16

The territory of Ephraim

The allotment for the descendants of Joseph extended from the Jordan at Jericho to the waters of Jericho on the east, through the wilderness that goes up from Jericho into the hill country of Bethel.

16:2

וְיָצָאit went מִבֵּיתfrom Bethel אֵלBethel לוּזָהLuz and וְעָבַרand proceeded אֶלto גְּבוּלborder הָאַרְכִּיthe Archites עֲטָרוֹתAtaroth

It went on from Bethel (that is, Luz) and proceeded to the border of the Archites in Ataroth.

16:3

וְיָרַדthen it descended יָמָּהwestward אֶלto גְּבוּלborder הַיַּפְלֵטִיthe Japhletites עַדfar גְּבוּלborder בֵּיתBeth חוֹרֹןhoron תַּחְתּוֹןlower וְעַדand on to גָּזֶרGezer וְהָיוּand it ended תֹצְאֹתָיוhis out at יָמָּהsea

Then it descended westward to the border of the Japhletites as far as the border of Lower Beth-horon and on to Gezer, and it ended at the Sea.

16:4

וַיִּנְחֲלוּreceived their inheritance בְנֵיsons יוֹסֵףJoseph מְנַשֶּׁהManasseh וְאֶפְרָיִםEphraim and

So Ephraim and Manasseh, the sons of Joseph, received their inheritance.

16:5

This was the territory of the descendants of Ephraim by their clans: The border of their inheritance went from Ataroth-addar in the east to Upper Beth-horon

16:6

וְיָצָאand out הַגְּבוּלtoward הַיָּמָּהthe sea הַמִּכְמְתָתFrom Michmethath מִצָּפוֹןfrom on the north וְנָסַבit turned הַגְּבוּלtoward מִזְרָחָהeastward toward תַּאֲנַתTaanath שִׁלֹהshiloh וְעָבַרand passed אוֹתוֹhim מִמִּזְרַחeast יָנוֹחָהJanoah on

and out toward the Sea. From Michmethath on the north it turned eastward toward Taanath-shiloh and passed by it to Janoah on the east.

16:7

וְיָרַדit went down מִיָּנוֹחָהFrom Janoah עֲטָרוֹתAtaroth וְנַעֲרָתָהand Naarah וּפָגַעand then reached בִּירִיחוֹJericho וְיָצָאand came out הַיַּרְדֵּןat the Jordan

From Janoah it went down to Ataroth and Naarah, and then reached Jericho and came out at the Jordan.

16:8

מִתַּפּוּחַFrom Tappuah יֵלֵךְwent הַגְּבוּלthe border יָמָּהwestward to נַחַלBrook קָנָהKanah וְהָיוּand ended תֹצְאֹתָיוhis out at הַיָּמָּהthe sea זֹאתthis נַחֲלַתinheritance מַטֵּהtribe בְנֵיsons אֶפְרַיִםEphraim לְמִשְׁפְּחֹתָםof the clans

From Tappuah the border went westward to the Brook of Kanah and ended at the Sea. This was the inheritance of the clans of the tribe of Ephraim,

16:9

וְהֶעָרִיםand the cities הַמִּבְדָּלוֹתset apart לִבְנֵיfor the descendants אֶפְרַיִםEphraim בְּתוֹךְwithin נַחֲלַתinheritance בְּנֵיsons מְנַשֶּׁהManasseh כָּלall הֶעָרִיםthe cities וְחַצְרֵיהֶןand villages

along with all the cities and villages set apart for the descendants of Ephraim within the inheritance of Manasseh.

16:10

וְלֹאand not הוֹרִישׁוּdrive אֶת[obj] הַכְּנַעֲנִיthe Canaanites הַיּוֹשֵׁבwho lived בְּגָזֶרin Gezer וַיֵּשֶׁבdwell הַכְּנַעֲנִיthe Canaanites בְּקֶרֶבamong אֶפְרַיִםEphraimites עַדto הַיּוֹםthis day הַזֶּהthis וַיְהִיbut they are לְמַסforced עֹבֵדlaborers

But they did not drive out the Canaanites who lived in Gezer. So the Canaanites dwell among the Ephraimites to this day, but they are forced laborers.
