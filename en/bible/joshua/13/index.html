Joshua 13

Land still to take; the eastern tribes

Now Joshua was old and well along in years, and the LORD said to him, “You are old and well along in years, but very much of the land remains to be possessed.

This is the land that remains:

All the territory of the Philistines and the Geshurites, from the Shihor east of Egypt to the territory of Ekron on the north (considered to be Canaanite territory)—that of the five Philistine rulers of Gaza, Ashdod, Ashkelon, Gath, and Ekron, as well as that of the Avvites;

13:4

מִתֵּימָןto the south כָּלall אֶרֶץland הַכְּנַעֲנִיthe Canaanites וּמְעָרָהMearah אֲשֶׁרthat לַצִּידֹנִיםof the Sidonians עַדto אֲפֵקָהAphek עַדfar גְּבוּלborder הָאֱמֹרִיthe Amorites

to the south, all the land of the Canaanites, from Mearah of the Sidonians to Aphek, as far as the border of the Amorites;

וְהָאָרֶץthe land הַגִּבְלִיthe Gebalites וְכָלand all הַלְּבָנוֹןLebanon מִזְרַחeast הַשֶּׁמֶשׁthe east מִבַּעַלfrom Baal גָּדgad תַּחַתbelow הַרMount חֶרְמוֹןHermon עַדto לְבוֹאLebo חֲמָתhamath

the land of the Gebalites; and all Lebanon to the east, from Baal-gad below Mount Hermon to Lebo-hamath.

All the inhabitants of the hill country from Lebanon to Misrephoth-maim—all the Sidonians—I Myself will drive out before the Israelites. Be sure to divide it by lot as an inheritance to Israel, as I have commanded you.

13:7

וְעַתָּהand now therefore חַלֵּקdivide אֶת[obj] הָאָרֶץthis land הַזֹּאתthis בְּנַחֲלָהas an inheritance לְתִשְׁעַתto nine הַשְּׁבָטִיםthe tribes וַחֲצִיand half הַשֵּׁבֶטthe tribe הַמְנַשֶּׁהof Manasseh

Now therefore divide this land as an inheritance to the nine tribes and the half-tribe of Manasseh.”

13:8

עִמּוֹwith הָראוּבֵנִיReubenites וְהַגָּדִיand Gadites had לָקְחוּreceived נַחֲלָתָםinheritance אֲשֶׁרhad נָתַןgiven לָהֶםto מֹשֶׁהMoses בְּעֵבֶרbeyond הַיַּרְדֵּןJordan מִזְרָחָהeast כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had נָתַןassigned לָהֶםto them מֹשֶׁהMoses עֶבֶדservant יְהוָהLORD

The other half of Manasseh, along with the Reubenites and Gadites, had received the inheritance Moses had given them beyond the Jordan to the east, just as Moses the servant of the LORD had assigned to them:

13:9

מֵעֲרוֹעֵרfrom Aroer אֲשֶׁרthat עַלon שְׂפַתrim נַחַלValley אַרְנוֹןArnon וְהָעִירalong with the city אֲשֶׁרthat בְּתוֹךְin the middle הַנַּחַלthe valley וְכָלwhole הַמִּישֹׁרthe plateau מֵידְבָאMedeba עַדfar דִּיבוֹןDibon

The area from Aroer on the rim of the Arnon Valley, along with the city in the middle of the valley, the whole plateau of Medeba as far as Dibon,

13:10

וְכֹלand all עָרֵיcities סִיחוֹןSihon מֶלֶךְking הָאֱמֹרִיthe Amorites אֲשֶׁרwho מָלַךְreigned בְּחֶשְׁבּוֹןin Heshbon עַדfar גְּבוּלborder בְּנֵיsons עַמּוֹןAmmonites

and all the cities of Sihon king of the Amorites who reigned in Heshbon, as far as the border of the Ammonites;

13:11

וְהַגִּלְעָדalso Gilead וּגְבוּלand the territory הַגְּשׁוּרִיthe Geshurites וְהַמַּעֲכָתִיand Maacathites וְכֹלall הַרMount חֶרְמוֹןHermon וְכָלand all הַבָּשָׁןBashan עַדfar סַלְכָהSalecah

also Gilead and the territory of the Geshurites and Maacathites, all of Mount Hermon, and all Bashan as far as Salecah—

13:12

כָּלwhole מַמְלְכוּתkingdom עוֹגOg בַּבָּשָׁןin Bashan אֲשֶׁרhad מָלַךְreigned בְּעַשְׁתָּרוֹתin Ashtaroth וּבְאֶדְרֶעִיand Edrei הוּאhe נִשְׁאַרremained מִיֶּתֶרas a remnant הָרְפָאִיםthe Rephaim וַיַּכֵּםhad struck them מֹשֶׁהMoses וַיֹּרִשֵׁםand dispossessed them

the whole kingdom of Og in Bashan, who had reigned in Ashtaroth and Edrei and had remained as a remnant of the Rephaim. Moses had struck them down and dispossessed them,

13:13

וְלֹאand not הוֹרִישׁוּdrive בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites אֶת[obj] הַגְּשׁוּרִיthe Geshurites וְאֶתand [obj] הַמַּעֲכָתִיthe Maacathites וַיֵּשֶׁבdwell גְּשׁוּרGeshur וּמַעֲכָתand Maacath בְּקֶרֶבamong יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites עַדto הַיּוֹםthis day הַזֶּהthis

but the Israelites did not drive out the Geshurites or the Maacathites. So Geshur and Maacath dwell among the Israelites to this day.

13:14

רַקhowever לְשֵׁבֶטto the tribe הַלֵּוִיof Levi לֹאno נָתַןgiven נַחֲלָהinheritance אִשֵּׁיofferings made by fire יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael הוּאhe נַחֲלָתוֹtheir inheritance כַּאֲשֶׁרhad דִּבֶּרpromised לוֹto him

To the tribe of Levi, however, Moses had given no inheritance. The offerings made by fire to the LORD, the God of Israel, are their inheritance, just as He had promised them.

13:15

וַיִּתֵּןgiven מֹשֶׁהMoses לְמַטֵּהtribe בְנֵיsons רְאוּבֵןReuben לְמִשְׁפְּחֹתָםto the clans

This is what Moses had given to the clans of the tribe of Reuben:

The territory from Aroer on the rim of the Arnon Valley, along with the city in the middle of the valley, to the whole plateau beyond Medeba,

to Heshbon and all its cities on the plateau, including Dibon, Bamoth-baal, Beth-baal-meon,

Jahaz, Kedemoth, Mephaath,

וְקִרְיָתַיִםKiriathaim וְשִׂבְמָהSibmah וְצֶרֶתZereth הַשַּׁחַרshahar בְּהַרon the hill הָעֵמֶקthe valley

Kiriathaim, Sibmah, Zereth-shahar on the hill in the valley,

13:20

וּבֵיתBeth פְּעוֹרpeor וְאַשְׁדּוֹתslopes הַפִּסְגָּהof Pisgah וּבֵיתand Beth הַיְשִׁמוֹתjeshimoth

Beth-peor, the slopes of Pisgah, and Beth-jeshimoth—

all the cities of the plateau and all the kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites, who reigned in Heshbon until Moses killed him and the chiefs of Midian (Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba), the princes of Sihon who lived in the land.

13:22

וְאֶתand [obj] בִּלְעָםBalaam בֶּןson בְּעוֹרBeor הַקּוֹסֵםthe diviner הָרְגוּkilled בְנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites בַּחֶרֶבsword אֶלto חַלְלֵיהֶםothers they

The Israelites also killed the diviner Balaam son of Beor along with the others they put to the sword.

And the border of the Reubenites was the bank of the Jordan. This was the inheritance of the clans of the Reubenites, including the cities and villages.

13:24

וַיִּתֵּןgiven מֹשֶׁהMoses לְמַטֵּהtribe גָדGad לִבְנֵיto sons גָדGad לְמִשְׁפְּחֹתָםclans

This is what Moses had given to the clans of the tribe of Gad:

The territory of Jazer, all the cities of Gilead, and half the land of the Ammonites as far as Aroer, near Rabbah;

13:26

וּמֵחֶשְׁבּוֹןterritory from Heshbon עַדto רָמַתRamath הַמִּצְפֶּהmizpeh וּבְטֹנִיםand Betonim וּמִמַּחֲנַיִםand from Mahanaim עַדto גְּבוּלborder לִדְבִרDebir

the territory from Heshbon to Ramath-mizpeh and Betonim, and from Mahanaim to the border of Debir;

and in the valley, Beth-haram, Beth-nimrah, Succoth, and Zaphon, with the rest of the kingdom of Sihon king of Heshbon (the territory on the east side of the Jordan up to the edge of the Sea of Chinnereth).

13:28

זֹאתthis נַחֲלַתinheritance בְּנֵיsons גָדGadites לְמִשְׁפְּחֹתָםthe clans הֶעָרִיםthe cities וְחַצְרֵיהֶםand villages

This was the inheritance of the clans of the Gadites, including the cities and villages.

וַיִּתֵּןgiven מֹשֶׁהMoses לַחֲצִיhalf שֵׁבֶטtribe מְנַשֶּׁהManasseh וַיְהִיand was לַחֲצִיto half מַטֵּהtribe בְנֵיdescendants מְנַשֶּׁהManasseh לְמִשְׁפְּחוֹתָםclans

This is what Moses had given to the clans of the half-tribe of Manasseh, that is, to half the tribe of the descendants of Manasseh:

וַיְהִיand was גְבוּלָםterritory מִמַּחֲנַיִםfrom Mahanaim כָּלall הַבָּשָׁןBashan כָּלall מַמְלְכוּתkingdom עוֹגOg מֶלֶךְking הַבָּשָׁןof Bashan וְכָלincluding all חַוֺּתtowns יָאִירJair אֲשֶׁרthat בַּבָּשָׁןin Bashan שִׁשִּׁיםsixty עִירcities

The territory from Mahanaim through all Bashan—all the kingdom of Og king of Bashan, including all the towns of Jair that are in Bashan, sixty cities;

13:31

וַחֲצִיhalf הַגִּלְעָדof Gilead וְעַשְׁתָּרוֹתand Ashtaroth וְאֶדְרֶעִיand Edrei עָרֵיcities מַמְלְכוּתroyal עוֹגOg בַּבָּשָׁןin Bashan לִבְנֵיdescendants מָכִירMachir בֶּןson מְנַשֶּׁהManasseh לַחֲצִיthat is half בְנֵיdescendants מָכִירMachir לְמִשְׁפְּחוֹתָםclans

half of Gilead; and Ashtaroth and Edrei, the royal cities of Og in Bashan. All this was for the clans of the descendants of Machir son of Manasseh, that is, half of the descendants of Machir.

13:32

These were the portions Moses had given them on the plains of Moab beyond the Jordan, east of Jericho.

To the tribe of Levi, however, Moses had given no inheritance. The LORD, the God of Israel, is their inheritance, just as He had promised them.
