Joshua 20

The cities of refuge

20:1

וַיְדַבֵּרand spoke יְהוָהLORD אֶלto יְהוֹשֻׁעַJoshua לֵאמֹרsaid

Then the LORD said to Joshua,

20:2

דַּבֵּרtell אֶלto בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites לֵאמֹרto saying תְּנוּdesignate לָכֶםto you אֶת[obj] עָרֵיcities הַמִּקְלָטof refuge אֲשֶׁרthat דִּבַּרְתִּיinstructed אֲלֵיכֶםto you בְּיַדthrough מֹשֶׁהMoses

“Tell the Israelites to designate the cities of refuge, as I instructed you through Moses,

לָנוּסflee שָׁמָּהthere רוֹצֵחַkills another מַכֵּהkills נֶפֶשׁlife בִּשְׁגָגָהunintentionally בִּבְלִיor accidentally דָעַתaccidentally וְהָיוּbe לָכֶםto you לְמִקְלָטrefuge מִגֹּאֵלfrom the avenger הַדָּםof blood

so that anyone who kills another unintentionally or accidentally may flee there. These will be your refuge from the avenger of blood.

20:4

וְנָסwhen someone flees אֶלto אַחַתone מֵהֶעָרִיםof these cities הָאֵלֶּהthese וְעָמַדstands פֶּתַחat the entrance שַׁעַרgate הָעִירthe city וְדִבֶּרand speak בְּאָזְנֵיstates זִקְנֵיelders הָעִירits הַהִיאthat אֶת[obj] דְּבָרָיוhis case וְאָסְפוּto bring אֹתוֹhim הָעִירָהinto the city אֲלֵיהֶםto them וְנָתְנוּand give לוֹto him מָקוֹםplace וְיָשַׁבto live עִמָּםamong them

When someone flees to one of these cities, stands at the entrance of the city gate, and states his case before its elders, they are to bring him into the city and give him a place to live among them.

20:5

וְכִיnow if יִרְדֹּףpursues גֹּאֵלavenger הַדָּםof blood אַחֲרָיוafter him וְלֹאthey must not יַסְגִּרוּsurrender אֶת[obj] הָרֹצֵחַthe manslayer בְּיָדוֹinto his hand כִּיbecause בִבְלִיwithout דַעַתaccidentally הִכָּהkilled אֶת[obj] רֵעֵהוּhis neighbor וְלֹאand not שֹׂנֵאprior הוּאhe לוֹto him מִתְּמוֹלfrom eloquent שִׁלְשׁוֹםmalice

Now if the avenger of blood pursues him, they must not surrender the manslayer into his hand, because that man killed his neighbor accidentally without prior malice.

20:6

וְיָשַׁבto stay בָּעִירin that city הַהִיאhe עַדuntil עָמְדוֹstands trial לִפְנֵיbefore הָעֵדָהthe assembly לַמִּשְׁפָּטto the judgment עַדuntil מוֹתdeath הַכֹּהֵןpriest הַגָּדוֹלthe high אֲשֶׁרthat יִהְיֶהserving בַּיָּמִיםat that time הָהֵםthose אָזthen יָשׁוּבreturn הָרוֹצֵחַthe manslayer וּבָאmay return אֶלto עִירוֹhis city וְאֶלand to בֵּיתוֹhome אֶלto הָעִירthe city אֲשֶׁרthat נָסfled מִשָּׁםfrom there

He is to stay in that city until he stands trial before the assembly and until the death of the high priest serving at that time. Then the manslayer may return to his own home in the city from which he fled.”

20:7

וַיַּקְדִּשׁוּso they set apart אֶת[obj] קֶדֶשׁKedesh בַּגָּלִילin Galilee בְּהַרin the hill country נַפְתָּלִיNaphtali וְאֶתand [obj] שְׁכֶםShechem בְּהַרin the hill country אֶפְרָיִםEphraim וְאֶתand [obj] קִרְיַתKiriath אַרְבַּעarba הִיאthat is חֶבְרוֹןHebron בְּהַרin the hill country יְהוּדָהJudah

So they set apart Kedesh in Galilee in the hill country of Naphtali, Shechem in the hill country of Ephraim, and Kiriath-arba (that is, Hebron) in the hill country of Judah.

20:8

וּמֵעֵבֶרand beyond לְיַרְדֵּןthe Jordan יְרִיחוֹJericho מִזְרָחָהeast נָתְנוּdesignated אֶת[obj] בֶּצֶרBezer בַּמִּדְבָּרon the wilderness בַּמִּישֹׁרplateau מִמַּטֵּהfrom the tribe רְאוּבֵןReuben וְאֶתand [obj] רָאמֹתRamoth בַּגִּלְעָדin Gilead מִמַּטֵּהfrom the tribe גָדGad וְאֶתand [obj] גּוֹלָןGolan בַּבָּשָׁןin Bashan מִמַּטֵּהfrom the tribe מְנַשֶּׁהManasseh

And beyond the Jordan, east of Jericho, they designated Bezer on the wilderness plateau from the tribe of Reuben, Ramoth in Gilead from the tribe of Gad, and Golan in Bashan from the tribe of Manasseh.

20:9

אֵלֶּהthese הָיוּare the עָרֵיcities הַמּוּעָדָהappointed לְכֹלfor all בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites וְלַגֵּרand foreigners הַגָּרthe living בְּתוֹכָםamong them לָנוּסmay flee שָׁמָּהthere כָּלanyone מַכֵּהkills נֶפֶשׁperson בִּשְׁגָגָהunintentionally וְלֹאand not יָמוּתdie בְּיַדby the hand גֹּאֵלavenger הַדָּםof blood עַדto עָמְדוֹstanding trial לִפְנֵיbefore הָעֵדָהthe assembly

These are the cities appointed for all the Israelites and foreigners among them, so that anyone who kills a person unintentionally may flee there and not die by the hand of the avenger of blood prior to standing trial before the assembly.
