Joshua 17

Manasseh and the daughters of Zelophehad

Now this was the allotment for the tribe of Manasseh as Joseph’s firstborn son, namely for Machir the firstborn of Manasseh and father of the Gileadites, who had received Gilead and Bashan because Machir was a man of war.

So this allotment was for the rest of the descendants of Manasseh—the clans of Abiezer, Helek, Asriel, Shechem, Hepher, and Shemida. These are the other male descendants of the clans of Manasseh son of Joseph.

17:3

וְלִצְלָפְחָדBut Zelophehad בֶּןson חֵפֶרHepher בֶּןson גִּלְעָדGilead בֶּןson מָכִירMachir בֶּןson מְנַשֶּׁהManasseh לֹאno הָיוּhad לוֹto him בָּנִיםsons כִּיbut אִםif בָּנוֹתdaughters וְאֵלֶּהthese שְׁמוֹתnames בְּנֹתָיוhis daughters מַחְלָהMahlah וְנֹעָהNoah חָגְלָהHoglah מִלְכָּהMilcah וְתִרְצָהand Tirzah

But Zelophehad son of Hepher (the son of Gilead, the son of Machir, the son of Manasseh) had no sons but only daughters. These are the names of his daughters: Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah.

17:4

וַתִּקְרַבְנָהthey approached לִפְנֵיto before אֶלְעָזָרEleazar הַכֹּהֵןthe priest וְלִפְנֵיand to before יְהוֹשֻׁעַJoshua בִּןson נוּןNun וְלִפְנֵיand to before הַנְּשִׂיאִיםthe leaders לֵאמֹרand said יְהוָהLORD צִוָּהcommanded אֶת[obj] מֹשֶׁהMoses לָתֶתto give לָנוּto us נַחֲלָהinheritance בְּתוֹךְamong אַחֵינוּour brothers וַיִּתֵּןso Joshua gave לָהֶםto them אֶלto פִּיcommand יְהוָהLORD נַחֲלָהinheritance בְּתוֹךְamong אֲחֵיbrothers אֲבִיהֶןtheir father’s

They approached Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the leaders, and said, “The LORD commanded Moses to give us an inheritance among our brothers.” So Joshua gave them an inheritance among their father’s brothers, in keeping with the command of the LORD.

17:5

וַיִּפְּלוּfell חַבְלֵיshares to מְנַשֶּׁהManasseh עֲשָׂרָהten לְבַדin addition מֵאֶרֶץto the land הַגִּלְעָדof Gilead וְהַבָּשָׁןand Bashan אֲשֶׁרthat מֵעֵבֶרbeyond לַיַּרְדֵּןthe Jordan

Thus ten shares fell to Manasseh, in addition to the land of Gilead and Bashan beyond the Jordan,

17:6

כִּיbecause בְּנוֹתdaughters מְנַשֶּׁהManasseh נָחֲלוּreceived נַחֲלָהinheritance בְּתוֹךְamong בָּנָיוhis sons וְאֶרֶץand the land הַגִּלְעָדof Gilead הָיְתָהbelonged לִבְנֵיsons מְנַשֶּׁהManasseh הַנּוֹתָרִיםthe rest

because the daughters of Manasseh received an inheritance among his sons. And the land of Gilead belonged to the rest of the sons of Manasseh.

17:7

וַיְהִיand was גְבוּלborder מְנַשֶּׁהManasseh מֵאָשֵׁרfrom Asher הַמִּכְמְתָתto Michmethath אֲשֶׁרthat עַלon פְּנֵיface שְׁכֶםShechem וְהָלַךְthen southward הַגְּבוּלthe border אֶלto הַיָּמִיןinclude אֶלto יֹשְׁבֵיinhabitants עֵיןEn תַּפּוּחַtappuah

Now the border of Manasseh went from Asher to Michmethath near Shechem, then southward to include the inhabitants of En-tappuah.

17:8

לִמְנַשֶּׁהto Manasseh הָיְתָהbelonged אֶרֶץregion תַּפּוּחַTappuah וְתַפּוּחַbut Tappuah אֶלto גְּבוּלborder מְנַשֶּׁהManasseh לִבְנֵיto sons אֶפְרָיִםEphraim

The region of Tappuah belonged to Manasseh, but Tappuah itself, on the border of Manasseh, belonged to Ephraim.

17:9

וְיָרַדand went down הַגְּבוּלthe border נַחַלcontinued קָנָהKanah נֶגְבָּהsouthward לַנַּחַלto the Brook עָרִיםcities הָאֵלֶּהthese לְאֶפְרַיִםto Ephraim בְּתוֹךְin among עָרֵיcities מְנַשֶּׁהManasseh וּגְבוּלbut the border מְנַשֶּׁהManasseh מִצְּפוֹןon the north לַנַּחַלof the brook וַיְהִיand ended תֹצְאֹתָיוhis out at הַיָּמָּהthe sea

From there the border continued southward to the Brook of Kanah. There were cities belonging to Ephraim among the cities of Manasseh, but the border of Manasseh was on the north side of the brook and ended at the Sea.

17:10

נֶגְבָּהto the south לְאֶפְרַיִםEphraim’s territory וְצָפוֹנָהnorth לִמְנַשֶּׁהManasseh’s to וַיְהִיwas הַיָּםthe sea גְּבוּלוֹits border וּבְאָשֵׁרand Asher on יִפְגְּעוּןadjoining מִצָּפוֹןthe north וּבְיִשָּׂשכָרand Issachar on מִמִּזְרָחon the east

Ephraim’s territory was to the south, and Manasseh’s was to the north, having the Sea as its border and adjoining Asher on the north and Issachar on the east.

17:11

וַיְהִיwas לִמְנַשֶּׁהManasseh בְּיִשָּׂשכָרWithin Issachar וּבְאָשֵׁרand Asher בֵּיתBeth שְׁאָןshean וּבְנוֹתֶיהָand her daughters וְיִבְלְעָםIbleam וּבְנוֹתֶיהָand her daughters וְאֶתand [obj] יֹשְׁבֵיinhabitants דֹארDor וּבְנוֹתֶיהָand her daughters וְיֹשְׁבֵיand people עֵיןEndor דֹּרEndor וּבְנֹתֶיהָand her daughters וְיֹשְׁבֵיand people תַעְנַךְTaanach וּבְנֹתֶיהָand her daughters וְיֹשְׁבֵיand people מְגִדּוֹMegiddo וּבְנוֹתֶיהָand her daughters שְׁלֹשֶׁתthree הַנָּפֶתsettlements

Within Issachar and Asher, Manasseh was assigned Beth-shean, Ibleam, Dor (that is, Naphath), Endor, Taanach, and Megiddo, each with their surrounding settlements.

17:12

וְלֹאand not יָכְלוּunable בְּנֵיdescendants מְנַשֶּׁהManasseh לְהוֹרִישׁto occupy אֶת[obj] הֶעָרִיםthese cities הָאֵלֶּהthese וַיּוֹאֶלwere determined הַכְּנַעֲנִיthe Canaanites לָשֶׁבֶתto stay בָּאָרֶץin this land הַזֹּאתthis

But the descendants of Manasseh were unable to occupy these cities, because the Canaanites were determined to stay in this land.

17:13

וַיְהִיhowever when כִּיfor חָזְקוּstronger בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites וַיִּתְּנוּthey put אֶת[obj] הַכְּנַעֲנִיthe Canaanites לָמַסto forced labor וְהוֹרֵשׁdrive לֹאnot הוֹרִישׁוֹdriven him

However, when the Israelites grew stronger, they put the Canaanites to forced labor; but they failed to drive them out completely.

17:14

וַיְדַבְּרוּand relayed בְּנֵיsons יוֹסֵףJoseph אֶתwith יְהוֹשֻׁעַJoshua לֵאמֹרsaid to מַדּוּעַwhy נָתַתָּהgiven לִּיto me נַחֲלָהinheritance גּוֹרָלlots אֶחָדone וְחֶבֶלonly portion אֶחָדone וַאֲנִיand I עַםpeople רָבmany עַדuntil אֲשֶׁרthat עַדuntil כֹּהthis בֵּרְכַנִיblessed us abundantly יְהוָהLORD

Then the sons of Joseph said to Joshua, “Why have you given us only one portion as an inheritance? We have many people, because the LORD has blessed us abundantly.”

17:15

וַיֹּאמֶרanswered אֲלֵיהֶםto them יְהוֹשֻׁעַJoshua אִםif עַםpeople רַבmany אַתָּהyou עֲלֵהgo לְךָto הַיַּעְרָהthe forest וּבֵרֵאתָand clear לְךָfor yourself שָׁםthere בְּאֶרֶץin the land הַפְּרִזִּיthe Perizzites וְהָרְפָאִיםand the Rephaim כִּיfor אָץsmall לְךָto you הַרhill country אֶפְרָיִםEphraim

Joshua answered them, “If you have so many people that the hill country of Ephraim is too small for you, go to the forest and clear for yourself an area in the land of the Perizzites and the Rephaim.”

17:16

וַיֹּאמְרוּthey replied בְּנֵיsons יוֹסֵףJoseph לֹאnot יִמָּצֵאenough לָנוּfor us הָהָרthe hill country וְרֶכֶבchariots בַּרְזֶלiron בְּכָלall הַכְּנַעֲנִיCanaanites הַיֹּשֵׁבwho live בְּאֶרֶץin land הָעֵמֶקthe valley לַאֲשֶׁרhave בְּבֵיתin Beth שְׁאָןshean וּבְנוֹתֶיהָwith its towns וְלַאֲשֶׁרand to those בְּעֵמֶקin the Valley יִזְרְעֶאלJezreel

“The hill country is not enough for us,” they replied, “and all the Canaanites who live in the valley have iron chariots, both in Beth-shean with its towns and in the Valley of Jezreel.”

17:17

וַיֹּאמֶרso said יְהוֹשֻׁעַJoshua אֶלto בֵּיתhouse יוֹסֵףJoseph לְאֶפְרַיִםto Ephraim וְלִמְנַשֶּׁהand Manasseh לֵאמֹרhave עַםpeople רַבmany אַתָּהyou וְכֹחַand strength גָּדוֹלgreat לָךְto you לֹאnot יִהְיֶהjust לְךָto you גּוֹרָלallotment אֶחָדone

So Joshua said to the house of Joseph—to Ephraim and Manasseh—“You have many people and great strength. You shall not have just one allotment,

17:18

כִּיbecause הַרhill country יִהְיֶהbe לָּךְto you כִּיfor יַעַרforest הוּאhe וּבֵרֵאתוֹclear it its וְהָיָהwill be לְךָto you תֹּצְאֹתָיוalthough כִּיfor תוֹרִישׁdrive אֶת[obj] הַכְּנַעֲנִיthe Canaanites כִּיfor רֶכֶבchariots בַּרְזֶלiron לוֹto him כִּיfor חָזָקstrong הוּאhe

because the hill country will be yours as well. It is a forest; clear it, and its farthest limits will be yours. Although the Canaanites have iron chariots and although they are strong, you can drive them out.”
