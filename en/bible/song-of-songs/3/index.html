Song of Songs 3

A search by night and Solomon's procession

On my bed at night I sought the one I love; I sought him, but did not find him.

3:2

I will arise now and go about the city, through the streets and squares. I will seek the one I love. So I sought him but did not find him.

3:3

מְצָאוּנִיI encountered הַשֹּׁמְרִיםthe watchmen הַסֹּבְבִיםtheir rounds בָּעִירthe city אֵת[obj] שֶׁאָהֲבָהone I love נַפְשִׁיhave you רְאִיתֶםseen

I encountered the watchmen on their rounds of the city: “Have you seen the one I love?”

3:4

כִּמְעַטI had שֶׁעָבַרְתִּיpassed מֵהֶםof them עַדuntil שֶׁמָּצָאתִיI found אֵת[obj] שֶׁאָהֲבָהone I love נַפְשִׁיmy soul אֲחַזְתִּיוheld him וְלֹאand would not אַרְפֶּנּוּlet עַדuntil שֶׁהֲבֵיאתִיוhad brought him אֶלto בֵּיתhouse אִמִּיmy mother’s וְאֶלto חֶדֶרchamber הוֹרָתִיconceived me

I had just passed them when I found the one I love. I held him and would not let go until I had brought him to my mother’s house, to the chamber of the one who conceived me.

3:5

הִשְׁבַּעְתִּיadjure אֶתְכֶםyou בְּנוֹתdaughters יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem בִּצְבָאוֹתby the gazelles אוֹor בְּאַיְלוֹתand does הַשָּׂדֶהthe field אִםif תָּעִירוּarouse וְאִםor תְּעוֹרְרוּawaken אֶת[obj] הָאַהֲבָהlove עַדuntil שֶׁתֶּחְפָּץtime is right

O daughters of Jerusalem, I adjure you by the gazelles and does of the field: Do not arouse or awaken love until the time is right.

3:6

מִיwho זֹאתthis עֹלָהcoming up מִןfrom הַמִּדְבָּרthe wilderness כְּתִימֲרוֹתlike a column עָשָׁןsmoke מְקֻטֶּרֶתscented מוֹרmyrrh וּלְבוֹנָהand frankincense מִכֹּלfrom all אַבְקַתspices רוֹכֵלmerchant

Who is this coming up from the wilderness like a column of smoke, scented with myrrh and frankincense from all the spices of the merchant?

3:7

הִנֵּהbehold מִטָּתוֹcarriage שֶׁלִּשְׁלֹמֹהis Solomon’s שִׁשִּׁיםsixty גִּבֹּרִיםmightiest סָבִיבaround לָהּto her מִגִּבֹּרֵיmen יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

Behold, it is Solomon’s carriage, escorted by sixty of the mightiest men of Israel.

3:8

כֻּלָּםall are אֲחֻזֵיskilled חֶרֶבsword מְלֻמְּדֵיexperienced מִלְחָמָהwarfare אִישׁeach חַרְבּוֹhis sword עַלon יְרֵכוֹhis side prepared מִפַּחַדfor the terror בַּלֵּילּוֹתthe night

All are skilled with the sword, experienced in warfare. Each has his sword at his side prepared for the terror of the night.

3:9

אַפִּרְיוֹןcarriage עָשָׂהmade לוֹto him הַמֶּלֶךְking שְׁלֹמֹהSolomon מֵעֲצֵיtimber הַלְּבָנוֹןLebanon

King Solomon has made his carriage out of the timber of Lebanon.

3:10

עַמּוּדָיוits posts עָשָׂהmade כֶסֶףsilver רְפִידָתוֹits base זָהָבgold מֶרְכָּבוֹits seat אַרְגָּמָןpurple תּוֹכוֹits interior רָצוּףfabric אַהֲבָהlove מִבְּנוֹתby the daughters יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem

He has made its posts of silver, its base of gold, its seat of purple fabric. Its interior is inlaid with love by the daughters of Jerusalem.

3:11

צְאֶינָהcome out וּרְאֶינָהand gaze בְּנוֹתdaughters צִיּוֹןZion בַּמֶּלֶךְat king שְׁלֹמֹהSolomon בָּעֲטָרָהwearing the crown שֶׁעִטְּרָהthe crown לּוֹto him אִמּוֹmother בְּיוֹםon the day חֲתֻנָּתוֹhis wedding וּבְיוֹםthe day שִׂמְחַתrejoicing לִבּוֹhis heart’s

Come out, O daughters of Zion, and gaze at King Solomon, wearing the crown his mother bestowed on the day of his wedding— the day of his heart’s rejoicing.
