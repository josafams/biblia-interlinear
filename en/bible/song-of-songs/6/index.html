Song of Songs 6

The beloved in the garden; the only one among all

Open in the interlinear reader

6:1

אָנָהwhere הָלַךְgone דּוֹדֵךְyour beloved O הַיָּפָהmost beautiful בַּנָּשִׁיםamong women אָנָהwhich way פָּנָהturned דוֹדֵךְyour beloved וּנְבַקְשֶׁנּוּwill seek him עִמָּךְwith you

Where has your beloved gone, O most beautiful among women? Which way has he turned? We will seek him with you.

6:2

דּוֹדִיmy beloved יָרַדgone down לְגַנּוֹto his garden לַעֲרוּגוֹתto the beds הַבֹּשֶׂםof spices לִרְעוֹתto pasture his flock בַּגַּנִּיםin the gardens וְלִלְקֹטand to gather שׁוֹשַׁנִּיםlilies

My beloved has gone down to his garden, to the beds of spices, to pasture his flock in the gardens and to gather lilies.

6:3

אֲנִיI לְדוֹדִיbelong to my beloved וְדוֹדִיand my beloved לִיto me הָרֹעֶהpastures his flock בַּשׁוֹשַׁנִּיםamong the lilies

I belong to my beloved and he belongs to me; he pastures his flock among the lilies.

6:4

יָפָהbeautiful אַתְּare רַעְיָתִיmy darling כְּתִרְצָהas Tirzah נָאוָהlovely כִּירוּשָׁלִָםas Jerusalem אֲיֻמָּהmajestic כַּנִּדְגָּלוֹתas troops with banners

You are as beautiful, my darling, as Tirzah, as lovely as Jerusalem, as majestic as troops with banners.

6:5

הָסֵבִּיturn עֵינַיִךְyour eyes מִנֶּגְדִּיfrom distance me שֶׁהֵםthey הִרְהִיבֻנִיovercome me שַׂעְרֵךְyour hair כְּעֵדֶרlike a flock הָעִזִּיםof goats שֶׁגָּלְשׁוּstreaming מִןfrom הַגִּלְעָדGilead

Turn your eyes away from me, for they have overcome me. Your hair is like a flock of goats streaming down from Gilead.

6:6

שִׁנַּיִךְyour teeth כְּעֵדֶרlike a flock הָרְחֵלִיםof sheep שֶׁעָלוּcoming up מִןfrom הָרַחְצָהthe washing שֶׁכֻּלָּםeach has מַתְאִימוֹתtwin וְשַׁכֻּלָהtwin and אֵיןnot בָּהֶםin them

Your teeth are like a flock of sheep coming up from the washing; each has its twin, and not one of them is lost.

6:7

כְּפֶלַחlike a slice הָרִמּוֹןof pomegranate רַקָּתֵךְyour brow מִבַּעַדbrow behind לְצַמָּתֵךְyour veil

Your brow behind your veil is like a slice of pomegranate.

6:8

שִׁשִּׁיםsixty הֵמָּהare מְּלָכוֹתqueens וּשְׁמֹנִיםand eighty פִּילַגְשִׁיםconcubines וַעֲלָמוֹתand maidens אֵיןwithout מִסְפָּרnumber

There are sixty queens and eighty concubines, and maidens without number,

6:9

אַחַתone הִיאis יוֹנָתִיmy dove my תַמָּתִיperfect אַחַתone הִיאis לְאִמָּהּunique the of the mother בָּרָהfavorite הִיאis לְיוֹלַדְתָּהּbore her רָאוּהָsee her בָנוֹתmaidens וַיְאַשְּׁרוּהָand call her blessed מְלָכוֹתqueens וּפִילַגְשִׁיםand concubines וַיְהַלְלוּהָsing her praises

but my dove, my perfect one, is unique, the favorite of the mother who bore her. The maidens see her and call her blessed; the queens and concubines sing her praises.

6:10

מִיwho זֹאתthis הַנִּשְׁקָפָהwho shines כְּמוֹlike שָׁחַרdawn יָפָהfair כַלְּבָנָהas the moon בָּרָהbright כַּחַמָּהas the sun אֲיֻמָּהmajestic כַּנִּדְגָּלוֹתas the stars in procession

Who is this who shines like the dawn, as fair as the moon, as bright as the sun, as majestic as the stars in procession?

6:11

אֶלto גִּנַּתwalnut אֱגוֹזgrove יָרַדְתִּיwent down לִרְאוֹתto see בְּאִבֵּיblossoms הַנָּחַלthe valley לִרְאוֹתto see הֲפָרְחָהbloom הַגֶּפֶןthe vines הֵנֵצוּbudding הָרִמֹּנִיםor the pomegranates

I went down to the walnut grove to see the blossoms of the valley, to see if the vines were budding or the pomegranates were in bloom.

6:12

לֹאnot יָדַעְתִּיrealized נַפְשִׁיmy desire שָׂמַתְנִיset me מַרְכְּבוֹתchariots עַמִּיmy people נָדִיבroyal

Before I realized it, my desire had set me among the royal chariots of my people.

6:13

שׁוּבִיcome שׁוּבִיback הַשּׁוּלַמִּיתShulammite שׁוּבִיback שׁוּבִיreturn וְנֶחֱזֶהmay gaze בָּךְin you מַהwhy תֶּחֱזוּlook בַּשּׁוּלַמִּיתat the Shulammite כִּמְחֹלַתdance הַמַּחֲנָיִםthe of Mahanaim

Come back, come back, O Shulammite! Come back, come back, that we may gaze upon you. Why do you look at the Shulammite, as on the dance of Mahanaim?
