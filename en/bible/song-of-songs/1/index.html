Song of Songs 1

Desire and praise between the lovers

Open in the interlinear reader

1:1

שִׁירsong הַשִּׁירִיםsongs אֲשֶׁרthat לִשְׁלֹמֹהSolomon’s

This is Solomon’s Song of Songs.

1:2

יִשָּׁקֵנִיkiss me מִנְּשִׁיקוֹתwith the kisses פִּיהוּhis mouth כִּיfor טוֹבִיםgood דֹּדֶיךָyour love מִיָּיִןthan wine

Let him kiss me with the kisses of his mouth! For your love is more delightful than wine.

1:3

לְרֵיחַthe fragrance שְׁמָנֶיךָyour perfume טוֹבִיםpleasing שֶׁמֶןperfume תּוּרַקpoured שְׁמֶךָyour name עַלon כֵּןso עֲלָמוֹתmaidens אֲהֵבוּךָadore you

The fragrance of your perfume is pleasing; your name is like perfume poured out. No wonder the maidens adore you.

1:4

מָשְׁכֵנִיtake me אַחֲרֶיךָafter you נָּרוּצָהhurry הֱבִיאַנִיbring me הַמֶּלֶךְthe king חֲדָרָיוhis chambers נָגִילָהrejoice וְנִשְׂמְחָהand delight בָּךְin you נַזְכִּירָהpraise דֹדֶיךָyour love מִיַּיִןthan wine מֵישָׁרִיםright אֲהֵבוּךָadore you

Take me away with you—let us hurry! May the king bring me to his chambers. We will rejoice and delight in you; we will praise your love more than wine. It is only right that they adore you.

1:5

שְׁחוֹרָהdark אֲנִיI am וְנָאוָהyet lovely בְּנוֹתdaughters יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem כְּאָהֳלֵיlike the tents קֵדָרKedar כִּירִיעוֹתlike the curtains שְׁלֹמֹהSolomon

I am dark, yet lovely, O daughters of Jerusalem, like the tents of Kedar, like the curtains of Solomon.

1:6

אַלdo not תִּרְאוּנִיstare I שֶׁאֲנִיI am שְׁחַרְחֹרֶתdark שֶׁשֱּׁזָפַתְנִיgazed upon me הַשָּׁמֶשׁthe sun בְּנֵיsons אִמִּיmy mother’s נִחֲרוּangry בִיwith me שָׂמֻנִיmade me נֹטֵרָהkeeper אֶת[obj] הַכְּרָמִיםthe vineyards כַּרְמִיmy vineyard שֶׁלִּיwho to me לֹאnot נָטָרְתִּיneglected

Do not stare because I am dark, for the sun has gazed upon me. My mother’s sons were angry with me; they made me a keeper of the vineyards, but my own vineyard I have neglected.

1:7

הַגִּידָהtell לִּיto me שֶׁאָהֲבָהlove where נַפְשִׁיone I אֵיכָהhow תִרְעֶהpasture אֵיכָהhow תַּרְבִּיץrest בַּצָּהֳרָיִםthem at midday שַׁלָּמָהwhy should I אֶהְיֶהbe כְּעֹטְיָהlike a veiled עַלon עֶדְרֵיflocks חֲבֵרֶיךָyour companions

Tell me, O one I love, where do you pasture your sheep? Where do you rest them at midday? Why should I be like a veiled woman beside the flocks of your companions?

1:8

אִםif לֹאnot תֵדְעִיknow לָךְto you הַיָּפָהfairest בַּנָּשִׁיםof women צְאִיfollow לָךְto you בְּעִקְבֵיthe tracks הַצֹּאןthe flock וּרְעִיand graze אֶת[obj] גְּדִיֹּתַיִךְyour young goats עַלon מִשְׁכְּנוֹתtents הָרֹעִיםthe shepherds

If you do not know, O fairest of women, follow the tracks of the flock, and graze your young goats near the tents of the shepherds.

1:9

לְסֻסָתִיto a mare בְּרִכְבֵיamong chariots פַרְעֹהPharaoh’s דִּמִּיתִיךְcompare you רַעְיָתִיmy darling

I compare you, my darling, to a mare among Pharaoh’s chariots.

1:10

נָאווּbeautiful לְחָיַיִךְyour cheeks בַּתֹּרִיםwith ornaments צַוָּארֵךְyour neck בַּחֲרוּזִיםwith strings of jewels

Your cheeks are beautiful with ornaments, your neck with strings of jewels.

1:11

תּוֹרֵיornaments זָהָבgold נַעֲשֶׂהmake לָּךְto you עִםwith נְקֻדּוֹתstudded beads הַכָּסֶףof silver

We will make you ornaments of gold, studded with beads of silver.

1:12

עַדuntil שֶׁהַמֶּלֶךְthe king בִּמְסִבּוֹat his table נִרְדִּיmy perfume נָתַןspread רֵיחוֹits fragrance

While the king was at his table, my perfume spread its fragrance.

1:13

צְרוֹרsachet הַמֹּרmyrrh דּוֹדִיmy beloved לִיto me בֵּיןbetween שָׁדַיmy breasts יָלִיןremain

My beloved is to me a sachet of myrrh resting between my breasts.

1:14

אֶשְׁכֹּלcluster הַכֹּפֶרof henna דּוֹדִיmy beloved לִיto me בְּכַרְמֵיin the vineyards עֵיןEn גֶּדִיgedi

My beloved is to me a cluster of henna blossoms in the vineyards of En-gedi.

1:15

הִנָּךְbehold you יָפָהbeautiful רַעְיָתִיmy darling הִנָּךְbehold you יָפָהbeautiful עֵינַיִךְyour eyes יוֹנִיםdoves

How beautiful you are, my darling! Oh, how very beautiful! Your eyes are like doves.

1:16

הִנְּךָbehold you יָפֶהhandsome דוֹדִיmy beloved אַףhow נָעִיםdelightful אַףhow much עַרְשֵׂנוּour bed רַעֲנָנָהgrass

How handsome you are, my beloved! Oh, how delightful! The soft grass is our bed.

1:17

קֹרוֹתbeams בָּתֵּינוּour house אֲרָזִיםcedars רַהִיטֵנוּour rafters בְּרוֹתִיםrafters

The beams of our house are cedars; our rafters are fragrant firs.
