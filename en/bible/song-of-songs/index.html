Song of Songs שִׁיר הַשִּׁירִים

The Song of Songs is a collection of love poems in which a woman and a man speak of each other and to each other, with the participation of a chorus, the “daughters of Jerusalem.” The poems celebrate desire, the beauty of the body and the joy of meeting, with images drawn from gardens, vineyards, spices, animals and landscapes of the land of Israel. There are also moments of absence and searching, such as the two night scenes in which the beloved woman looks for her beloved through the city (3:1–4; 5:2–8). The sentence “My beloved is mine and I am his” (2:16), taken up with variations in 6:3 and 7:10, sums up the mutuality of love, and the high point of the book affirms that “love is as strong as death” (8:6). God is not mentioned explicitly, but Jews and Christians have for centuries also read the book as an image of the love between God and His people.

Structure

  1. Desire and first meetings chs. 1–2

    The beloved woman longs for her beloved and speaks to the daughters of Jerusalem; the two exchange praises, until the first plea not to awaken love before its time (1:1–2:7).

  2. Spring and search chs. 2–3

    At the end of winter, the beloved calls her to come away; at night, she searches for him through the city and finds him (2:8–3:5).

  3. The procession and the garden chs. 3–5

    Solomon's carriage on his wedding day, the description of the beloved woman's beauty and the image of her as a locked garden (3:6–5:1).

  4. Missed and found again chs. 5–6

    The beloved knocks at the door and goes away; she searches for him, describes him to the daughters of Jerusalem and says he has gone down to his garden (5:2–6:3).

  5. The beauty of the Shulammite chs. 6–8

    New praises of the beloved woman, unique among all, and her invitation to go together to the countryside (6:4–8:4).

  6. As strong as death ch. 8

    The affirmation of the strength of love, the words of the brothers, Solomon's vineyard and the final call (8:5–14).

Chapters

  1. 1 Desire and praise between the lovers
  2. 2 Spring arrives and the beloved calls
  3. 3 A search by night and Solomon's procession
  4. 4 The beauty of the bride, a garden locked
  5. 5 Missed at the door; a portrait of the beloved
  6. 6 The beloved in the garden; the only one among all
  7. 7 The beauty of the Shulammite; an invitation to the fields
  8. 8 Love is as strong as death

Key verses

O daughters of Jerusalem, I adjure you by the gazelles and does of the field: Do not arouse or awaken love until the time is right.

Song of Songs 2:7

My beloved is mine and I am his; he pastures his flock among the lilies.

Song of Songs 2:16

Set me as a seal over your heart, as a seal upon your arm. For love is as strong as death, its jealousy as unrelenting as Sheol. Its sparks are fiery flames, the fiercest blaze of all. Mighty waters cannot quench love; rivers cannot sweep it away. If a man were to give all the wealth of his house for love, his offer would be utterly scorned.
