Song of Songs 2

Spring arrives and the beloved calls

Open in the interlinear reader

2:1

אֲנִיam חֲבַצֶּלֶתrose הַשָּׁרוֹןof Sharon שׁוֹשַׁנַּתlily הָעֲמָקִיםthe valley

I am a rose of Sharon, a lily of the valley.

2:2

כְּשׁוֹשַׁנָּהlike a lily בֵּיןamong הַחוֹחִיםthe thorns כֵּןso רַעְיָתִיmy darling בֵּיןamong הַבָּנוֹתthe maidens

Like a lily among the thorns is my darling among the maidens.

2:3

Like an apple tree among the trees of the forest is my beloved among the young men. I delight to sit in his shade, and his fruit is sweet to my taste.

2:4

הֱבִיאַנִיbrought me אֶלto בֵּיתhouse הַיָּיִןwine וְדִגְלוֹand his banner עָלַיover me אַהֲבָהlove

He has brought me to the house of wine, and his banner over me is love.

2:5

סַמְּכוּנִיsustain me בָּאֲשִׁישׁוֹתwith raisins רַפְּדוּנִיrefresh me בַּתַּפּוּחִיםwith apples כִּיfor חוֹלַתam אַהֲבָהlove אָנִיI

Sustain me with raisins; refresh me with apples, for I am faint with love.

2:6

שְׂמֹאלוֹhis left תַּחַתunder לְרֹאשִׁיmy head וִימִינוֹand his right תְּחַבְּקֵנִיarm embraces me

His left hand is under my head, and his right arm embraces me.

2:7

הִשְׁבַּעְתִּיadjure אֶתְכֶםyou בְּנוֹתdaughters יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem בִּצְבָאוֹתby the gazelles אוֹor בְּאַיְלוֹתand does הַשָּׂדֶהthe field אִםif תָּעִירוּarouse וְאִםor תְּעוֹרְרוּawaken אֶת[obj] הָאַהֲבָהlove עַדuntil שֶׁתֶּחְפָּץtime is right

O daughters of Jerusalem, I adjure you by the gazelles and does of the field: Do not arouse or awaken love until the time is right.

2:8

קוֹלlisten דּוֹדִיmy beloved approaches הִנֵּהlook זֶהhere בָּאcomes מְדַלֵּגleaping עַלon הֶהָרִיםthe mountains מְקַפֵּץbounding עַלover הַגְּבָעוֹתthe hills

Listen! My beloved approaches. Look! Here he comes, leaping across the mountains, bounding over the hills.

2:9

דּוֹמֶהlike דוֹדִיmy beloved לִצְבִיa gazelle אוֹor לְעֹפֶרto young הָאַיָּלִיםstag הִנֵּהlook זֶהthis עוֹמֵדstands אַחַרbehind כָּתְלֵנוּour wall מַשְׁגִּיחַgazing מִןfrom הַחֲלֹּנוֹתthe windows מֵצִיץpeering מִןfrom הַחֲרַכִּיםthe lattice

My beloved is like a gazelle or a young stag. Look, he stands behind our wall, gazing through the windows, peering through the lattice.

2:10

עָנָהanswer דוֹדִיmy beloved וְאָמַרcalls לִיto me קוּמִיarise לָךְto you רַעְיָתִיdarling יָפָתִיmy beautiful וּלְכִיand go לָךְto you

My beloved calls to me, “Arise, my darling. Come away with me, my beautiful one.

2:11

כִּיfor הִנֵּהbehold הַסְּתָיוthe winter עָבָרpast הַגֶּשֶׁםthe rain חָלַףover and הָלַךְgone לוֹto him

For now the winter is past; the rain is over and gone.

2:12

הַנִּצָּנִיםthe flowers נִרְאוּappeared בָאָרֶץin the countryside עֵתseason הַזָּמִירof singing הִגִּיעַcome וְקוֹלand cooing הַתּוֹרthe of turtledoves נִשְׁמַעheard בְּאַרְצֵנוּin our land

The flowers have appeared in the countryside; the season of singing has come, and the cooing of turtledoves is heard in our land.

2:13

הַתְּאֵנָהthe fig חָנְטָהtree פַגֶּיהָripens its וְהַגְּפָנִיםthe vines סְמָדַרblossoming נָתְנוּspread רֵיחַfragrance קוּמִיarise לָךְto you רַעְיָתִיdarling יָפָתִיmy beautiful וּלְכִיcome away with לָךְto you

The fig tree ripens its figs; the blossoming vines spread their fragrance. Arise, come away, my darling; come away with me, my beautiful one.”

2:14

יוֹנָתִיmy dove בְּחַגְוֵיin the clefts הַסֶּלַעthe rock בְּסֵתֶרin the crevices הַמַּדְרֵגָהthe cliff הַרְאִינִיme see אֶתּ[obj] מַרְאַיִךְyour face הַשְׁמִיעִינִיme hear אֶת[obj] קוֹלֵךְyour voice כִּיfor קוֹלֵךְyour voice עָרֵבsweet וּמַרְאֵיךְand your countenance נָאוֶהlovely

O my dove in the clefts of the rock, in the crevices of the cliff, let me see your face, let me hear your voice; for your voice is sweet, and your countenance is lovely.

2:15

אֶחֱזוּcatch לָנוּfor us שׁוּעָלִיםfoxes שׁוּעָלִיםfoxes קְטַנִּיםlittle מְחַבְּלִיםruin כְּרָמִיםvineyards וּכְרָמֵינוּfor our vineyards סְמָדַרbloom

Catch for us the foxes— the little foxes that ruin the vineyards— for our vineyards are in bloom.

2:16

דּוֹדִיmy beloved לִיto me וַאֲנִיand I am לוֹto him הָרֹעֶהpastures his flock בַּשּׁוֹשַׁנִּיםamong the lilies

My beloved is mine and I am his; he pastures his flock among the lilies.

2:17

עַדuntil שֶׁיָּפוּחַthe breaks הַיּוֹםthe day וְנָסוּand flee הַצְּלָלִיםshadows סֹבturn דְּמֵהlike לְךָbe a דוֹדִיmy beloved לִצְבִיgazelle אוֹor לְעֹפֶרa young הָאַיָּלִיםstag עַלon הָרֵיmountains בָתֶרBether

Before the day breaks and shadows flee, turn, my beloved, and be like a gazelle or a young stag on the mountains of Bether.
