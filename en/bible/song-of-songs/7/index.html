Song of Songs 7

The beauty of the Shulammite; an invitation to the fields

Open in the interlinear reader

7:1

מַהhow יָּפוּbeautiful פְעָמַיִךְyour feet בַּנְּעָלִיםsandaled בַּתdaughter נָדִיבprince חַמּוּקֵיcurves יְרֵכַיִךְof your thighs כְּמוֹlike חֲלָאִיםjewels מַעֲשֵׂהhandiwork יְדֵיhands אָמָּןmaster

How beautiful are your sandaled feet, O daughter of the prince! The curves of your thighs are like jewels, the handiwork of a master.

7:2

שָׁרְרֵךְyour navel אַגַּןnavel הַסַּהַרa rounded goblet אַלdo not יֶחְסַרlacks הַמָּזֶגblended בִּטְנֵךְwine your עֲרֵמַתwaist חִטִּיםwheat סוּגָהencircled בַּשּׁוֹשַׁנִּיםby the lilies

Your navel is a rounded goblet; it never lacks blended wine. Your waist is a mound of wheat encircled by the lilies.

7:3

שְׁנֵיtwo שָׁדַיִךְyour breasts כִּשְׁנֵיlike two עֳפָרִיםfawns תָּאֳמֵיtwins צְבִיָּהgazelle

Your breasts are like two fawns, twins of a gazelle.

7:4

צַוָּארֵךְyour neck כְּמִגְדַּלlike a tower הַשֵּׁןof ivory עֵינַיִךְyour eyes בְּרֵכוֹתpools בְּחֶשְׁבּוֹןHeshbon עַלon שַׁעַרgate בַּתBath רַבִּיםrabbim אַפֵּךְyour nose כְּמִגְדַּלlike the tower הַלְּבָנוֹןof Lebanon צוֹפֶהfacing פְּנֵיtoward דַמָּשֶׂקDamascus

Your neck is like a tower made of ivory; your eyes are like the pools of Heshbon by the gate of Bath-rabbim; your nose is like the tower of Lebanon, facing toward Damascus.

7:5

רֹאשֵׁךְyour head עָלַיִךְon you כַּכַּרְמֶלlike Mount Carmel וְדַלַּתhair רֹאשֵׁךְyour head כָּאַרְגָּמָןlike purple threads the מֶלֶךְking אָסוּרcaptured בָּרְהָטִיםin your tresses

Your head crowns you like Mount Carmel, the hair of your head like purple threads; the king is captured in your tresses.

7:6

מַהhow יָּפִיתfair וּמַהand what נָּעַמְתְּpleasant אַהֲבָהlove בַּתַּעֲנוּגִיםwith your delights

How fair and pleasant you are, O love, with your delights!

7:7

זֹאתthis קוֹמָתֵךְyour stature דָּמְתָהlike לְתָמָרa palm tree וְשָׁדַיִךְyour breasts לְאַשְׁכֹּלוֹתare clusters

Your stature is like a palm tree; your breasts are clusters of fruit.

7:8

אָמַרְתִּיsaid אֶעֱלֶהclimb בְתָמָרthe palm tree אֹחֲזָהhold בְּסַנְסִנָּיוits fruit וְיִהְיוּand be נָאplease שָׁדַיִךְyour breasts כְּאֶשְׁכְּלוֹתlike clusters הַגֶּפֶןthe vine וְרֵיחַfragrance אַפֵּךְyour breath כַּתַּפּוּחִיםlike apples

I said, “I will climb the palm tree; I will take hold of its fruit.” May your breasts be like clusters of the vine, the fragrance of your breath like apples,

7:9

וְחִכֵּךְand your mouth כְּיֵיןwine הַטּוֹבthe finest הוֹלֵךְflow לְדוֹדִיmy beloved לְמֵישָׁרִיםsmoothly to דּוֹבֵבgliding gently שִׂפְתֵיlips יְשֵׁנִיםteeth

and your mouth like the finest wine. May it flow smoothly to my beloved, gliding gently over lips and teeth.

7:10

אֲנִיI לְדוֹדִיbelong to my beloved וְעָלַיfor me תְּשׁוּקָתוֹhis desire

I belong to my beloved, and his desire is for me.

7:11

לְכָהcome דוֹדִיmy beloved let us נֵצֵאgo הַשָּׂדֶהthe countryside נָלִינָהnight בַּכְּפָרִיםamong the wildflowers

Come, my beloved, let us go to the countryside; let us spend the night among the wildflowers.

7:12

נַשְׁכִּימָהearly לַכְּרָמִיםto the vineyards נִרְאֶהsee אִםif פָּרְחָהbudded הַגֶּפֶןthe vine פִּתַּחopened הַסְּמָדַרin bloom הֵנֵצוּif הָרִמּוֹנִיםthe pomegranates שָׁםthere אֶתֵּןgive אֶת[obj] דֹּדַיmy love לָךְto you

Let us go early to the vineyards to see if the vine has budded, if the blossom has opened, if the pomegranates are in bloom— there I will give you my love.

7:13

הַדּוּדָאִיםthe mandrakes נָתְנוּsend רֵיחַfragrance וְעַלand on פְּתָחֵינוּour door כָּלevery מְגָדִיםdelicacy חֲדָשִׁיםnew גַּםwell יְשָׁנִיםold דּוֹדִיmy beloved צָפַנְתִּיtreasured לָךְfor you

The mandrakes send forth a fragrance, and at our door is every delicacy, new as well as old, that I have treasured up for you, my beloved.
