Ecclesiastes 7

Sayings on what is better

Open in the interlinear reader

7:1

טוֹבgood שֵׁםname מִשֶּׁמֶןthan perfume טוֹבfine וְיוֹםand one’s day הַמָּוֶתof death מִיּוֹםthan day הִוָּלְדוֹbirth

A good name is better than fine perfume, and one’s day of death is better than his day of birth.

7:2

טוֹבbetter לָלֶכֶתto go אֶלto בֵּיתhouse אֵבֶלmourning מִלֶּכֶתfrom walk אֶלto בֵּיתhouse מִשְׁתֶּהfeasting בַּאֲשֶׁרin where הוּאis סוֹףend כָּלevery הָאָדָםman וְהַחַיand the living יִתֵּןtake אֶלto לִבּוֹheart

It is better to enter a house of mourning than a house of feasting, since death is the end of every man, and the living should take this to heart.

7:3

טוֹבbetter כַּעַסsorrow מִשְּׂחֹקthan laughter כִּיfor בְרֹעַa sad פָּנִיםcountenance יִיטַבgood לֵבheart

Sorrow is better than laughter, for a sad countenance is good for the heart.

7:4

לֵבheart חֲכָמִיםwise בְּבֵיתin the house אֵבֶלmourning וְלֵבbut the heart כְּסִילִיםfools בְּבֵיתin the house שִׂמְחָהpleasure

The heart of the wise is in the house of mourning, but the heart of fools is in the house of pleasure.

7:5

טוֹבbetter לִשְׁמֹעַto heed גַּעֲרַתrebuke חָכָםwise מֵאִישׁman’s than שֹׁמֵעַlisten שִׁירsong כְּסִילִיםfools

It is better to heed a wise man’s rebuke than to listen to the song of fools.

7:6

כִּיfor כְקוֹלlike crackling הַסִּירִיםthe of thorns תַּחַתunder הַסִּירthe pot כֵּןso שְׂחֹקlaughter הַכְּסִילthe fool וְגַםthis too זֶהthis is הָבֶלfutile

For like the crackling of thorns under the pot, so is the laughter of the fool. This too is futile.

7:7

כִּיfor הָעֹשֶׁקextortion יְהוֹלֵלturns חָכָםwise וִיאַבֵּדand a bribe אֶת[obj] לֵבheart מַתָּנָהcorrupts

Surely extortion turns a wise man into a fool, and a bribe corrupts the heart.

7:8

טוֹבbetter אַחֲרִיתend דָּבָרmatter מֵרֵאשִׁיתוֹthan the beginning and טוֹבbetter אֶרֶךְpatient רוּחַspirit מִגְּבַהּthan proud רוּחַwind

The end of a matter is better than the beginning, and a patient spirit is better than a proud one.

7:9

אַלdo not תְּבַהֵלquickly בְּרוּחֲךָin your spirit לִכְעוֹסprovoked כִּיfor כַעַסanger settles בְּחֵיקin the lap כְּסִילִיםfool יָנוּחַrest

Do not be quickly provoked in your spirit, for anger settles in the lap of a fool.

7:10

אַלdo not תֹּאמַרsay מֶהwhy הָיָהwere שֶׁהַיָּמִיםdays הָרִאשֹׁנִיםthe old הָיוּwere טוֹבִיםbetter מֵאֵלֶּהthan these כִּיfor לֹאnot מֵחָכְמָהis unwise שָׁאַלְתָּask עַלon זֶהthis

Do not say, “Why were the old days better than these?” For it is unwise of you to ask about this.

7:11

טוֹבָהgood חָכְמָהwisdom עִםwith נַחֲלָהinheritance וְיֹתֵרand it benefits לְרֹאֵיsee הַשָּׁמֶשׁthe sun

Wisdom, like an inheritance, is good, and it benefits those who see the sun.

7:12

כִּיfor בְּצֵלshelter הַחָכְמָהwisdom בְּצֵלlike הַכָּסֶףmoney is a וְיִתְרוֹןand advantage דַּעַתknowledge הַחָכְמָהthat wisdom תְּחַיֶּהlife בְעָלֶיהָits owner

For wisdom, like money, is a shelter, and the advantage of knowledge is that wisdom preserves the life of its owner.

7:13

רְאֵהconsider אֶת[obj] מַעֲשֵׂהwork הָאֱלֹהִיםGod כִּיfor מִיwho יוּכַלcan לְתַקֵּןstraighten אֵת[obj] אֲשֶׁרthat עִוְּתוֹhe has bent

Consider the work of God: Who can straighten what He has bent?

7:14

בְּיוֹםin the day טוֹבָהprosperity הֱיֵהbe בְטוֹבjoyful וּבְיוֹםbut in the day רָעָהadversity רְאֵהconsider גַּםeven אֶת[obj] זֶהthis לְעֻמַּתone זֶהthese עָשָׂהmade הָאֱלֹהִיםother עַלon דִּבְרַתbefore שֶׁלֹּאwho not יִמְצָאdiscover הָאָדָםa man אַחֲרָיוafter him מְאוּמָהanything

In the day of prosperity, be joyful, but in the day of adversity, consider this: God has made one of these along with the other, so that a man cannot discover anything that will come after him.

7:15

אֶת[obj] הַכֹּלboth of these רָאִיתִיseen בִּימֵיlife הֶבְלִיmy futile יֵשׁhave צַדִּיקrighteous אֹבֵדperishing בְּצִדְקוֹin his righteousness וְיֵשׁand there רָשָׁעwicked מַאֲרִיךְlong בְּרָעָתוֹin his wickedness

In my futile life I have seen both of these: A righteous man perishing in his righteousness, and a wicked man living long in his wickedness.

7:16

אַלdo not תְּהִיbe צַדִּיקoverly righteous הַרְבֵּהmuch וְאַלand do not תִּתְחַכַּםwise יוֹתֵרgained לָמָּהwhy should תִּשּׁוֹמֵםdestroy

Do not be overly righteous, and do not make yourself too wise. Why should you destroy yourself?

7:17

אַלdo not תִּרְשַׁעexcessively הַרְבֵּהwicked and וְאַלand do not תְּהִיbe סָכָלfool לָמָּהwhy should תָמוּתdie בְּלֹאin without עִתֶּךָyour time

Do not be excessively wicked, and do not be a fool. Why should you die before your time?

7:18

טוֹבgood אֲשֶׁרthat תֶּאֱחֹזgrasp בָּזֶהin here וְגַםand מִזֶּהfrom this אַלnot תַּנַּחlet אֶת[obj] יָדֶךָyour hand כִּיfor יְרֵאfears אֱלֹהִיםGod יֵצֵאfollow אֶת[obj] כֻּלָּםboth warnings

It is good to grasp the one and not let the other slip from your hand. For he who fears God will follow both warnings.

7:19

הַחָכְמָהwisdom תָּעֹזmakes לֶחָכָםthe wise מֵעֲשָׂרָהthan ten שַׁלִּיטִיםrulers אֲשֶׁרthat הָיוּwere בָּעִירin a city

Wisdom makes the wise man stronger than ten rulers in a city.

7:20

כִּיfor אָדָםthere אֵיןno צַדִּיקrighteous בָּאָרֶץon earth אֲשֶׁרwho יַעֲשֶׂהdoes טּוֹבgood וְלֹאand never יֶחֱטָאsins

Surely there is no righteous man on earth who does good and never sins.

7:21

גַּםeven לְכָלto every הַדְּבָרִיםword אֲשֶׁרthat יְדַבֵּרוּspoken אַלnot תִּתֵּןpay לִבֶּךָattention אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot תִשְׁמַעhear אֶת[obj] עַבְדְּךָyour servant מְקַלְלֶךָcursing you

Do not pay attention to every word that is spoken, or you may hear your servant cursing you.

7:22

כִּיfor גַּםeven פְּעָמִיםtimes רַבּוֹתmany יָדַעknow לִבֶּךָyour heart אֲשֶׁרthat גַּםhave אַתָּהyou קִלַּלְתָּcursed אֲחֵרִיםothers

For you know in your heart that many times you yourself have cursed others.

7:23

כָּלall זֹהthis נִסִּיתִיtested בַחָכְמָהby wisdom אָמַרְתִּיsaying אֶחְכָּמָהwise וְהִיאbut it was רְחוֹקָהbeyond מִמֶּנִּיfrom me

All this I tested by wisdom, saying, “I resolve to be wise.” But it was beyond me.

7:24

רָחוֹקreach מַהwhat שֶּׁהָיָהwho been וְעָמֹקand very עָמֹקdeep מִיwho יִמְצָאֶנּוּfathom it

What exists is out of reach and very deep. Who can fathom it?

7:25

סַבּוֹתִיdirected אֲנִיI וְלִבִּיmy mind לָדַעַתto understand וְלָתוּרto explore וּבַקֵּשׁto search חָכְמָהwisdom וְחֶשְׁבּוֹןand explanations וְלָדַעַתand to understand רֶשַׁעwickedness כֶּסֶלstupidity וְהַסִּכְלוּתand the folly הוֹלֵלוֹתmadness

I directed my mind to understand, to explore, to search out wisdom and explanations, and to understand the stupidity of wickedness and the folly of madness.

7:26

וּמוֹצֶאand find אֲנִיI מַרbitter מִמָּוֶתthan death אֶת[obj] הָאִשָּׁהthe woman אֲשֶׁרwho הִיאis מְצוֹדִיםnet וַחֲרָמִיםnet and whose לִבָּהּwhose heart אֲסוּרִיםsnare יָדֶיהָwhose hands are טוֹבpleases לִפְנֵיto before הָאֱלֹהִיםwho God יִמָּלֵטescapes מִמֶּנָּהfrom her וְחוֹטֵאbut the sinner יִלָּכֶדensnared בָּהּin her

And I find more bitter than death the woman who is a snare, whose heart is a net, and whose hands are chains. The man who pleases God escapes her, but the sinner is ensnared.

7:27

רְאֵהbehold זֶהthis מָצָאתִיdiscovered אָמְרָהsays קֹהֶלֶתTeacher אַחַתone לְאַחַתto another לִמְצֹאto find חֶשְׁבּוֹןexplanation

“Behold,” says the Teacher, “I have discovered this by adding one thing to another to find an explanation.

7:28

אֲשֶׁרthat עוֹדstill בִּקְשָׁהsearching נַפְשִׁיmy soul וְלֹאbut not מָצָאתִיfinding אָדָםman אֶחָדone מֵאֶלֶףamong a thousand מָצָאתִיfound וְאִשָּׁהwoman בְכָלamong all אֵלֶּהthese לֹאnot מָצָאתִיfound

While my soul was still searching but not finding, among a thousand I have found one upright man, but among all these I have not found one such woman.

7:29

לְבַדonly רְאֵהsee זֶהthis מָצָאתִיfound אֲשֶׁרhave עָשָׂהdone הָאֱלֹהִיםthat God אֶת[obj] הָאָדָםmade men יָשָׁרupright וְהֵמָּהbut they בִקְשׁוּsought חִשְּׁבֹנוֹתschemes רַבִּיםmany

Only this have I found: I have discovered that God made men upright, but they have sought out many schemes.”
