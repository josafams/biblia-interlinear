Ecclesiastes 11

Acting without knowing the future; youth

Open in the interlinear reader

11:1

שַׁלַּחcast לַחְמְךָyour bread עַלupon פְּנֵיface הַמָּיִםthe waters כִּיfor בְרֹבafter many הַיָּמִיםdays תִּמְצָאֶנּוּfind it again

Cast your bread upon the waters, for after many days you will find it again.

11:2

תֶּןdivide חֵלֶקportion לְשִׁבְעָהamong seven וְגַםor even לִשְׁמוֹנָהeight כִּיfor לֹאnot תֵדַעknow מַהwhat יִּהְיֶהbe רָעָהdisaster עַלon הָאָרֶץthe land

Divide your portion among seven, or even eight, for you do not know what disaster may befall the land.

11:3

אִםif יִמָּלְאוּfull הֶעָבִיםthe clouds גֶּשֶׁםrain עַלupon הָאָרֶץthe earth יָרִיקוּpour וְאִםand if יִפּוֹלfalls עֵץtree בַּדָּרוֹםto the south וְאִםor בַּצָּפוֹןto the north מְקוֹםplace שֶׁיִּפּוֹלwhere it falls הָעֵץit שָׁםthere יְהוּאlie

If the clouds are full, they will pour out rain upon the earth; whether a tree falls to the south or to the north, in the place where it falls, there it will lie.

11:4

שֹׁמֵרwatches רוּחַwind לֹאnot יִזְרָעsow וְרֹאֶהobserves בֶעָבִיםthe clouds לֹאnot יִקְצוֹרreap

He who watches the wind will fail to sow, and he who observes the clouds will fail to reap.

11:5

כַּאֲשֶׁרas you אֵינְךָnot יוֹדֵעַknow מַהhow דֶּרֶךְpath הָרוּחַthe wind כַּעֲצָמִיםbones בְּבֶטֶןwomb הַמְּלֵאָהmother’s כָּכָהyou לֹאnot תֵדַעunderstand אֶת[obj] מַעֲשֵׂהwork הָאֱלֹהִיםof God אֲשֶׁרthat יַעֲשֶׂהMaker אֶת[obj] הַכֹּלof all

As you do not know the path of the wind, or how the bones are formed in a mother’s womb, so you cannot understand the work of God, the Maker of all things.

11:6

בַּבֹּקֶרin the morning זְרַעsow אֶת[obj] זַרְעֶךָyour seed וְלָעֶרֶבin the evening אַלdo not תַּנַּחrest יָדֶךָyour hands כִּיfor אֵינְךָyou do not יוֹדֵעknow אֵיwhich זֶהwill יִכְשָׁרsucceed הֲזֶהwhether this אוֹor זֶהthis וְאִםor if שְׁנֵיהֶםboth will כְּאֶחָדlike one טוֹבִיםprosper

Sow your seed in the morning, and do not rest your hands in the evening, for you do not know which will succeed, whether this or that, or if both will equally prosper.

11:7

וּמָתוֹקsweet הָאוֹרlight וְטוֹבand it pleases לַעֵינַיִםthe eyes לִרְאוֹתto see אֶת[obj] הַשָּׁמֶשׁthe sun

Light is sweet, and it pleases the eyes to see the sun.

11:8

כִּיif אִםif שָׁנִיםyears הַרְבֵּהmany יִחְיֶהlives הָאָדָםa man בְּכֻלָּםin them all יִשְׂמָחrejoice וְיִזְכֹּרbut him remember אֶת[obj] יְמֵיdays הַחֹשֶׁךְof darkness כִּיfor הַרְבֵּהmany יִהְיוּbe כָּלeverything שֶׁבָּאcome is הָבֶלfutile

So if a man lives many years, let him rejoice in them all. But let him remember the days of darkness, for they will be many. Everything to come is futile.

11:9

שְׂמַחrejoice בָּחוּרyoung בְּיַלְדוּתֶיךָwhile you are young וִיטִיבְךָand well you לִבְּךָheart be glad בִּימֵיin the days בְחוּרוֹתֶךָyour youth וְהַלֵּךְwalk בְּדַרְכֵיin the ways לִבְּךָyour heart וּבְמַרְאֵיand in the sight עֵינֶיךָyour eyes וְדָעbut know כִּיthat עַלfor כָּלall אֵלֶּהthese יְבִיאֲךָbring you הָאֱלֹהִיםGod בַּמִּשְׁפָּטto judgment

Rejoice, O young man, while you are young, and let your heart be glad in the days of your youth. Walk in the ways of your heart and in the sight of your eyes, but know that for all these things God will bring you to judgment.

11:10

וְהָסֵרso banish כַּעַסsorrow מִלִּבֶּךָfrom your heart וְהַעֲבֵרand cast רָעָהpain מִבְּשָׂרֶךָfrom your body כִּיfor הַיַּלְדוּתyouth וְהַשַּׁחֲרוּתand vigor הָבֶלfleeting

So banish sorrow from your heart, and cast off pain from your body, for youth and vigor are fleeting.
