Ecclesiastes 9

The same fate for all; enjoy life

Open in the interlinear reader

9:1

כִּיfor אֶת[obj] כָּלall זֶהthis נָתַתִּיtook אֶלto לִבִּיheart וְלָבוּרand concluded אֶת[obj] כָּלall זֶהthis אֲשֶׁרthat הַצַּדִּיקִיםthe righteous וְהַחֲכָמִיםand the wise וַעֲבָדֵיהֶםas well as their deeds are בְּיַדin hands הָאֱלֹהִיםGod’s גַּםeven אַהֲבָהlove גַםalso שִׂנְאָהhate אֵיןnot יוֹדֵעַknow הָאָדָםman הַכֹּלwhat לִפְנֵיהֶםahead

So I took all this to heart and concluded that the righteous and the wise, as well as their deeds, are in God’s hands. Man does not know what lies ahead, whether love or hate.

9:2

הַכֹּלthe all כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had לַכֹּלfor all מִקְרֶהfate אֶחָדone לַצַּדִּיקfor the righteous וְלָרָשָׁעand the wicked לַטּוֹבfor the good וְלַטָּהוֹרfor the clean וְלַטָּמֵאand the unclean וְלַזֹּבֵחַfor the one who sacrifices וְלַאֲשֶׁרwho אֵינֶנּוּnot it זֹבֵחַrefuses כַּטּוֹבthe good כַּחֹטֶאthe sinner as הַנִּשְׁבָּעvow כַּאֲשֶׁרwho שְׁבוּעָהoath יָרֵאvow

It is the same for all: There is a common fate for the righteous and the wicked, for the good and the bad, for the clean and the unclean, for the one who sacrifices and the one who does not. As it is for the good, so it is for the sinner; as it is for the one who makes a vow, so it is for the one who refuses to take a vow.

9:3

זֶהthis רָעevil בְּכֹלin everything אֲשֶׁרthat נַעֲשָׂהdone תַּחַתunder הַשֶּׁמֶשׁthe sun כִּיfor מִקְרֶהfate אֶחָדone לַכֹּלfor everyone וְגַםand also לֵבhearts בְּנֵיsons הָאָדָםof men מָלֵאfull רָעevil וְהוֹלֵלוֹתand madness בִּלְבָבָםin their heart בְּחַיֵּיהֶםalive וְאַחֲרָיוand after him אֶלto הַמֵּתִיםthe dead

This is an evil in everything that is done under the sun: There is one fate for everyone. Furthermore, the hearts of men are full of evil and madness while they are alive, and afterward they join the dead.

9:4

כִּיfor מִיwho אֲשֶׁרthat יְחֻבַּרhowever אֶלto כָּלall הַחַיִּיםthe living יֵשׁthere בִּטָּחוֹןhope כִּיfor לְכֶלֶבdog חַיlive הוּאis טוֹבbetter מִןthan הָאַרְיֵהa lion הַמֵּתdead

There is hope, however, for anyone who is among the living; for even a live dog is better than a dead lion.

9:5

כִּיfor הַחַיִּיםthe living יוֹדְעִיםknow שֶׁיָּמֻתוּdie וְהַמֵּתִיםbut the dead אֵינָםno them יוֹדְעִיםknow מְאוּמָהnothing וְאֵיןthey have no עוֹדlonger לָהֶםto them שָׂכָרreward כִּיbecause נִשְׁכַּחforgotten זִכְרָםmemory of them

For the living know that they will die, but the dead know nothing. They have no further reward, because the memory of them is forgotten.

9:6

גַּםeven אַהֲבָתָםlove their גַּםeven שִׂנְאָתָםhate their גַּםand קִנְאָתָםenvy כְּבָרalready אָבָדָהvanished וְחֵלֶקand portion אֵיןno לָהֶםto them עוֹדagain לְעוֹלָםa share בְּכֹלin all אֲשֶׁרthat נַעֲשָׂהdone תַּחַתunder הַשָּׁמֶשׁthe sun

Their love, their hate, and their envy have already vanished, and they will never again have a share in all that is done under the sun.

9:7

לֵךְgo אֱכֹלeat בְּשִׂמְחָהwith joy לַחְמֶךָyour bread וּשֲׁתֵהand drink בְלֶבheart טוֹבcheerful יֵינֶךָyour wine כִּיfor כְבָרalready רָצָהapproved הָאֱלֹהִיםGod אֶת[obj] מַעֲשֶׂיךָyour works

Go, eat your bread with joy, and drink your wine with a cheerful heart, for God has already approved your works:

9:8

בְּכָלalways עֵתalways יִהְיוּbe בְגָדֶיךָyour garments לְבָנִיםwhite וְשֶׁמֶןoil עַלfor רֹאשְׁךָyour head אַלdo not יֶחְסָרspare

Let your garments always be white, and never spare the oil for your head.

9:9

רְאֵהenjoy חַיִּיםlife עִםwith אִשָּׁהwife אֲשֶׁרthat אָהַבְתָּbeloved כָּלall יְמֵיdays חַיֵּיlife הֶבְלֶךָfleeting אֲשֶׁרthat נָתַןgiven לְךָto you תַּחַתunder הַשֶּׁמֶשׁthe sun כֹּלall יְמֵיdays הֶבְלֶךָyour fleeting כִּיfor הוּאis חֶלְקְךָyour portion בַּחַיִּיםin life וּבַעֲמָלְךָand in your labor אֲשֶׁרthat אַתָּהyou עָמֵלtoil תַּחַתunder הַשָּׁמֶשׁthe sun

Enjoy life with your beloved wife all the days of the fleeting life that God has given you under the sun—all your fleeting days. For this is your portion in life and in your labor under the sun.

9:10

כֹּלwhatever אֲשֶׁרyou תִּמְצָאfind יָדְךָyour hands לַעֲשׂוֹתto do בְּכֹחֲךָwith all your might עֲשֵׂהdo כִּיfor אֵיןno מַעֲשֶׂהwork וְחֶשְׁבּוֹןor planning וְדַעַתor knowledge וְחָכְמָהor wisdom בִּשְׁאוֹלin Sheol אֲשֶׁרthat אַתָּהyou הֹלֵךְgoing שָׁמָּהthere

Whatever you find to do with your hands, do it with all your might, for in Sheol, where you are going, there is no work or planning or knowledge or wisdom.

9:11

שַׁבְתִּיreturn וְרָאֹהI saw תַחַתunder הַשֶּׁמֶשׁthe sun the race כִּיfor לֹאnot לַקַּלִּיםto the swift הַמֵּרוֹץthe race וְלֹאand not לַגִּבּוֹרִיםto the strong הַמִּלְחָמָהthe battle וְגַםand also לֹאnot לַחֲכָמִיםto the wise לֶחֶםbread וְגַםand also לֹאnot לַנְּבֹנִיםto the intelligent עֹשֶׁרwealth וְגַםand also לֹאnot לַיֹּדְעִיםto the skillful חֵןfavor כִּיfor עֵתtime וָפֶגַעand chance יִקְרֶהhappen אֶת[obj] כֻּלָּםall

I saw something else under the sun: The race is not to the swift, nor the battle to the strong; neither is the bread to the wise, nor the wealth to the intelligent, nor the favor to the skillful. For time and chance happen to all.

9:12

כִּיfor גַּםeven לֹאno יֵדַעknows הָאָדָםman אֶת[obj] עִתּוֹhis time כַּדָּגִיםlike fish שֶׁנֶּאֱחָזִיםcaught בִּמְצוֹדָהnet רָעָהcruel net וְכַצִּפֳּרִיםor birds הָאֲחֻזוֹתtrapped בַּפָּחin a snare כָּהֵםare יוּקָשִׁיםensnared בְּנֵיsons הָאָדָםmen לְעֵתan time רָעָהevil כְּשֶׁתִּפּוֹלthat falls עֲלֵיהֶםupon them פִּתְאֹםsuddenly

For surely no man knows his time: Like fish caught in a cruel net or birds trapped in a snare, so men are ensnared in an evil time that suddenly falls upon them.

9:13

גַּםalso זֹהthis רָאִיתִיseen חָכְמָהwisdom תַּחַתunder הַשָּׁמֶשׁthe sun וּגְדוֹלָהgreat הִיאwas אֵלָיto me

I have also seen this wisdom under the sun, and it was great to me:

9:14

עִירcity קְטַנָּהsmall וַאֲנָשִׁיםwith men בָּהּin her מְעָטfew וּבָאcame אֵלֶיהָagainst it מֶלֶךְking גָּדוֹלmighty וְסָבַבsurrounded אֹתָהּher וּבָנָהand built עָלֶיהָagainst it מְצוֹדִיםsiege ramps גְּדֹלִיםlarge

There was a small city with few men. A mighty king came against it, surrounded it, and built large siege ramps against it.

9:15

וּמָצָאfound בָהּin the city אִישׁman מִסְכֵּןpoor חָכָםwise וּמִלַּטand saved הוּאhe אֶת[obj] הָעִירthe city בְּחָכְמָתוֹby his wisdom וְאָדָםyet one לֹאno זָכַרremembered אֶת[obj] הָאִישׁthat man הַמִּסְכֵּןpoor הַהּוּאthat

Now a poor wise man was found in the city, and he saved the city by his wisdom. Yet no one remembered that poor man.

9:16

וְאָמַרְתִּיand said אָנִיI טוֹבָהbetter חָכְמָהwisdom מִגְּבוּרָהthan strength וְחָכְמַתbut the wisdom הַמִּסְכֵּןthe poor בְּזוּיָהdespised וּדְבָרָיוand his words are אֵינָםnot נִשְׁמָעִיםheeded

And I said, “Wisdom is better than strength, but the wisdom of the poor man is despised, and his words are not heeded.”

9:17

דִּבְרֵיwords חֲכָמִיםwise בְּנַחַתin resolution נִשְׁמָעִיםheeded מִזַּעֲקַתthe shouts מוֹשֵׁלruler בַּכְּסִילִיםamong fools

The calm words of the wise are heeded over the shouts of a ruler among fools.

9:18

טוֹבָהbetter חָכְמָהwisdom מִכְּלֵיthan weapons קְרָבwar וְחוֹטֶאbut sinner אֶחָדone יְאַבֵּדdestroys טוֹבָהgood הַרְבֵּהmuch

Wisdom is better than weapons of war, but one sinner destroys much good.
