Ecclesiastes 5

Vows to God and the love of money

5:1

Guard your steps when you go to the house of God. Draw near to listen rather than to offer the sacrifice of fools, who do not know that they do wrong.

5:2

אַלdo תְּבַהֵלquick עַלto פִּיךָspeak וְלִבְּךָyour heart אַלnot יְמַהֵרhasty לְהוֹצִיאto utter דָבָרword לִפְנֵיbefore הָאֱלֹהִיםGod כִּיfor הָאֱלֹהִיםGod בַּשָּׁמַיִםin heaven וְאַתָּהand you עַלon הָאָרֶץearth עַלon כֵּןso יִהְיוּbe דְבָרֶיךָyour words מְעַטִּיםfew

Do not be quick to speak, and do not be hasty in your heart to utter a word before God. After all, God is in heaven and you are on earth. So let your words be few.

5:3

כִּיfor בָּאcomes הַחֲלוֹםa dream בְּרֹבthrough many עִנְיָןcares וְקוֹלso the speech כְּסִילfool בְּרֹבwith many דְּבָרִיםwords

As a dream comes through many cares, so the speech of a fool comes with many words.

5:4

כַּאֲשֶׁרwhen you תִּדֹּרmake נֶדֶרvow לֵאלֹהִיםto God אַלdo not תְּאַחֵרdelay לְשַׁלְּמוֹin fulfilling it כִּיbecause אֵיןno חֵפֶץpleasure בַּכְּסִילִיםin fools אֵת[obj] אֲשֶׁרthat תִּדֹּרvow שַׁלֵּםfulfill

When you make a vow to God, do not delay in fulfilling it, because He takes no pleasure in fools. Fulfill your vow.

5:5

טוֹבbetter אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot תִדֹּרvow מִשֶׁתִּדּוֹרvow וְלֹאand not תְשַׁלֵּםfulfill

It is better not to vow than to make a vow and not fulfill it.

5:6

אַלdo not תִּתֵּןlet אֶת[obj] פִּיךָyour mouth לַחֲטִיאto sin אֶת[obj] בְּשָׂרֶךָyour flesh וְאַלand do not תֹּאמַרtell לִפְנֵיto before הַמַּלְאָךְthe messenger כִּיthat שְׁגָגָהvow הִיאwas לָמָּהa mistake why יִקְצֹףangry הָאֱלֹהִיםshould God עַלon קוֹלֶךָyour voice וְחִבֵּלand destroy אֶת[obj] מַעֲשֵׂהwork יָדֶיךָyour hands

Do not let your mouth cause your flesh to sin, and do not tell the messenger that your vow was a mistake. Why should God be angry with your words and destroy the work of your hands?

5:7

כִּיfor בְרֹבas many חֲלֹמוֹתdreams וַהֲבָלִיםfutility וּדְבָרִיםwords הַרְבֵּהmany כִּיfor אֶת[obj] הָאֱלֹהִיםGod יְרָאfear

For as many dreams bring futility, so do many words. Therefore, fear God.

5:8

אִםif עֹשֶׁקoppression רָשׁpoor וְגֵזֶלand the denial מִשְׁפָּטjustice וָצֶדֶקand righteousness תִּרְאֶהsee בַמְּדִינָהin the province אַלdo not תִּתְמַהּastonished עַלon הַחֵפֶץthe matter כִּיfor גָבֹהַּofficial מֵעַלfrom the גָּבֹהַsuperior שֹׁמֵרwatched וּגְבֹהִיםand others higher עֲלֵיהֶםover them

If you see the oppression of the poor and the denial of justice and righteousness in the province, do not be astonished at the matter; for one official is watched by a superior, and others higher still are over them.

5:9

וְיִתְרוֹןproduce אֶרֶץearth בַּכֹּלby all the הוּאhe מֶלֶךְking לְשָׂדֶהfrom the fields נֶעֱבָדprofits

The produce of the earth is taken by all; the king himself profits from the fields.

5:10

אֹהֵבloves כֶּסֶףmoney לֹאnever יִשְׂבַּעsatisfied כֶּסֶףmoney וּמִיand he who אֹהֵבloves בֶּהָמוֹןwealth לֹאnever תְבוּאָהincome גַּםtoo זֶהthis is הָבֶלfutile

He who loves money is never satisfied by money, and he who loves wealth is never satisfied by income. This too is futile.

5:11

בִּרְבוֹתin multiplied הַטּוֹבָהgood רַבּוּincrease אוֹכְלֶיהָconsume them וּמַהwhat then כִּשְׁרוֹןprofit לִבְעָלֶיהָto the owner כִּיfor אִםif רְאוּתbehold עֵינָיוhis eyes

When good things increase, so do those who consume them; what then is the profit to the owner, except to behold them with his eyes?

5:12

מְתוּקָהsweet שְׁנַתsleep הָעֹבֵדthe worker אִםif מְעַטlittle וְאִםor הַרְבֵּהmuch יֹאכֵלeats וְהַשָּׂבָעbut the abundance לֶעָשִׁירof the rich אֵינֶנּוּno him מַנִּיחַpermits לוֹto him לִישׁוֹןno sleep

The sleep of the worker is sweet, whether he eats little or much, but the abundance of the rich man permits him no sleep.

5:13

יֵשׁthere רָעָהevil חוֹלָהgrievous רָאִיתִיseen תַּחַתunder הַשָּׁמֶשׁthe sun עֹשֶׁרwealth שָׁמוּרhoarded the לִבְעָלָיוowner לְרָעָתוֹthe harm of its

There is a grievous evil I have seen under the sun: wealth hoarded to the harm of its owner,

5:14

וְאָבַדor lost הָעֹשֶׁרwealth הַהוּאa בְּעִנְיַןin failed רָעventure וְהוֹלִידand father בֵּןson וְאֵיןand no בְּיָדוֹin his hand מְאוּמָהnothing

or wealth lost in a failed venture, so when that man has a son there is nothing to pass on.

5:15

כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had יָצָאcame מִבֶּטֶןwomb אִמּוֹhis mother’s עָרוֹםnaked יָשׁוּבagain לָלֶכֶתdepart כְּשֶׁבָּאas he arrived וּמְאוּמָהhe nothing לֹאnot יִשָּׂאtakes בַעֲמָלוֹfor his labor שֶׁיֹּלֵךְwho take בְּיָדוֹin his hands

As a man came from his mother’s womb, so he will depart again, naked as he arrived. He takes nothing for his labor to carry in his hands.

5:16

וְגַםthis too זֹהthis רָעָהevil חוֹלָהgrievous כָּלlike all עֻמַּתclose שֶׁבָּאis born כֵּןso יֵלֵךְdepart וּמַהwhat יִּתְרוֹןdoes he gain לוֹto him שֶׁיַּעֲמֹלhe toils לָרוּחַfor the wind

This too is a grievous evil: Exactly as a man is born, so he will depart. What does he gain as he toils for the wind?

5:17

גַּםeven כָּלall יָמָיוhis days he בַּחֹשֶׁךְin darkness יֹאכֵלeats וְכָעַסwith sorrow הַרְבֵּהmuch וְחָלְיוֹsickness וָקָצֶףand anger

Moreover, all his days he eats in darkness, with much sorrow, sickness, and anger.

5:18

הִנֵּהhere אֲשֶׁרthat רָאִיתִיseen אָנִיI טוֹבgood אֲשֶׁרthat יָפֶהfitting לֶאֶכוֹלto eat וְלִשְׁתּוֹתand drink וְלִרְאוֹתand to find טוֹבָהsatisfaction בְּכָלin all עֲמָלוֹlabor one שֶׁיַּעֲמֹלone does תַּחַתunder הַשֶּׁמֶשׁthe sun מִסְפַּרfew יְמֵיdays חַיָּיוlife אֲשֶׁרhas נָתַןgiven לוֹto him הָאֱלֹהִיםthat God כִּיfor הוּאis חֶלְקוֹhis lot

Here is what I have seen to be good and fitting: to eat and drink, and to find satisfaction in all the labor one does under the sun during the few days of life that God has given him—for this is his lot.

5:19

Furthermore, God has given riches and wealth to every man, and He has enabled him to enjoy them, to accept his lot, and to rejoice in his labor. This is a gift from God.

5:20

כִּיfor לֹאnot הַרְבֵּהmuch יִזְכֹּרseldom אֶת[obj] יְמֵיdays חַיָּיוhis life כִּיbecause הָאֱלֹהִיםGod keeps מַעֲנֶהoccupied בְּשִׂמְחַתwith the joy לִבּוֹhis heart

For a man seldom considers the days of his life, because God keeps him occupied with the joy of his heart.
