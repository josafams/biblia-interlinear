Ecclesiastes 12

Old age, death and the conclusion

Remember your Creator in the days of your youth, before the days of adversity come and the years approach of which you will say, “I find no pleasure in them,”

before the light of the sun, moon, and stars is darkened, and the clouds return after the rain,

12:3

on the day the keepers of the house tremble and the strong men stoop, when those grinding cease because they are few and those watching through windows see dimly,

when the doors to the street are shut and the sound of the mill fades away, when one rises at the sound of a bird and all the daughters of song grow faint,

12:5

when men fear the heights and dangers of the road, when the almond tree blossoms, the grasshopper loses its spring, and the caper berry shrivels— for then man goes to his eternal home and mourners walk the streets.

12:6

עַדuntil אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot יֵרָתֵקsnapped חֶבֶלcord הַכֶּסֶףthe silver וְתָרֻץcrushed גֻּלַּתbowl הַזָּהָבthe golden וְתִשָּׁבֶרshattered כַּדpitcher עַלon הַמַּבּוּעַthe spring וְנָרֹץand the wheel הַגַּלְגַּלis broken אֶלto הַבּוֹרthe well

Remember Him before the silver cord is snapped and the golden bowl is crushed, before the pitcher is shattered at the spring and the wheel is broken at the well,

12:7

וְיָשֹׁבreturns הֶעָפָרthe dust עַלto הָאָרֶץthe ground כְּשֶׁהָיָהwhich it came וְהָרוּחַand the spirit תָּשׁוּבreturns אֶלto הָאֱלֹהִיםGod אֲשֶׁרwho נְתָנָהּgave it

before the dust returns to the ground from which it came and the spirit returns to God who gave it.

12:8

הֲבֵלfutility הֲבָלִיםfutilities אָמַרsays הַקּוֹהֶלֶתthe Teacher הַכֹּלeverything הָבֶלfutile

“Futility of futilities,” says the Teacher. “Everything is futile!”

12:9

וְיֹתֵרand gained שֶׁהָיָהnot only was קֹהֶלֶתTeacher חָכָםwise עוֹדlonger לִמַּדtaught דַּעַתknowledge אֶת[obj] הָעָםthe people וְאִזֵּןhe pondered וְחִקֵּרsearched out and תִּקֵּןarranged מְשָׁלִיםproverbs הַרְבֵּהmany

Not only was the Teacher wise, but he also taught the people knowledge; he pondered, searched out, and arranged many proverbs.

12:10

בִּקֵּשׁsearched קֹהֶלֶתTeacher לִמְצֹאto find דִּבְרֵיsayings חֵפֶץdelightful וְכָתוּבand to record יֹשֶׁרaccurate דִּבְרֵיwords אֱמֶתtruth

The Teacher searched to find delightful sayings and to record accurate words of truth.

12:11

דִּבְרֵיwords חֲכָמִיםwise כַּדָּרְבֹנוֹתlike goads the anthologies וּכְמַשְׂמְרוֹתlike firmly נְטוּעִיםfirmly embedded בַּעֲלֵיnails אֲסֻפּוֹתnails driven נִתְּנוּdelivered מֵרֹעֶהshepherd אֶחָדsingle

The words of the wise are like goads, and the anthologies of the masters are like firmly embedded nails driven by a single Shepherd.

12:12

וְיֹתֵרand gained מֵהֵמָּהfrom they בְּנִיmy son הִזָּהֵרwarned עֲשׂוֹתmaking סְפָרִיםbooks הַרְבֵּהmany אֵיןno קֵץend וְלַהַגand study הַרְבֵּהmuch יְגִעַתwearies בָּשָׂרbody

And by these, my son, be further warned: There is no end to the making of many books, and much study wearies the body.

12:13

סוֹףconclusion דָּבָרmatter הַכֹּלall נִשְׁמָעheard אֶת[obj] הָאֱלֹהִיםGod יְרָאfear וְאֶתand [obj] מִצְוֺתָיוhis commandments שְׁמוֹרkeep כִּיbecause זֶהthis כָּלwhole הָאָדָםduty of man

When all has been heard, the conclusion of the matter is this: Fear God and keep His commandments, because this is the whole duty of man.

12:14

כִּיfor אֶת[obj] כָּלevery מַעֲשֶׂהdeed הָאֱלֹהִיםGod יָבִאbring בְמִשְׁפָּטinto judgment עַלon כָּלevery נֶעְלָםhidden אִםif טוֹבgood וְאִםor רָעevil

For God will bring every deed into judgment, along with every hidden thing, whether good or evil.
