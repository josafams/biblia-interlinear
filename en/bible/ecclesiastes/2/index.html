Ecclesiastes 2

The king tries pleasure, works and wisdom

Open in the interlinear reader

2:1

אָמַרְתִּיsaid אֲנִיI בְּלִבִּיin my heart לְכָהcome נָּאnow אֲנַסְּכָהtest you בְשִׂמְחָהwith pleasure וּרְאֵהenjoy בְטוֹבis good וְהִנֵּהand saw גַםalso הוּאhe הָבֶלfutile

I said to myself, “Come now, I will test you with pleasure; enjoy what is good!” But it proved to be futile.

2:2

לִשְׂחוֹקof laughter אָמַרְתִּיis מְהוֹלָלfolly וּלְשִׂמְחָהand of pleasure מַהwhat זֹּהthis עֹשָׂהaccomplish

I said of laughter, “It is folly,” and of pleasure, “What does it accomplish?”

2:3

תַּרְתִּיsought בְלִבִּיto cheer my לִמְשׁוֹךְto draw בַּיַּיִןwith wine אֶת[obj] בְּשָׂרִיbody וְלִבִּיmy mind still me נֹהֵגguiding בַּחָכְמָהwith wisdom וְלֶאֱחֹזand to embrace בְּסִכְלוּתfolly עַדuntil אֲשֶׁרthat אֶרְאֶהsee אֵיwhat זֶהthis טוֹבworthwhile לִבְנֵיto sons הָאָדָםmen אֲשֶׁרthat יַעֲשׂוּdo תַּחַתunder הַשָּׁמַיִםheaven during the מִסְפַּרfew יְמֵיdays חַיֵּיהֶםtheir lives

I sought to cheer my body with wine and to embrace folly—my mind still guiding me with wisdom—until I could see what was worthwhile for men to do under heaven during the few days of their lives.

2:4

הִגְדַּלְתִּיexpanded מַעֲשָׂיmy pursuits I בָּנִיתִיbuilt לִיto me בָּתִּיםhouses נָטַעְתִּיplanted לִיfor myself כְּרָמִיםvineyards

I expanded my pursuits. I built houses and planted vineyards for myself.

2:5

עָשִׂיתִיmade לִיfor myself גַּנּוֹתgardens וּפַרְדֵּסִיםand parks וְנָטַעְתִּיwhere I planted בָהֶםin them עֵץtrees כָּלall פֶּרִיfruit

I made gardens and parks for myself, where I planted all kinds of fruit trees.

2:6

עָשִׂיתִיbuilt לִיto me בְּרֵכוֹתgroves מָיִםreservoirs לְהַשְׁקוֹתto water מֵהֶםof יַעַרflourishing צוֹמֵחַflourishing עֵצִיםtrees

I built reservoirs to water my groves of flourishing trees.

2:7

קָנִיתִיacquired עֲבָדִיםmenservants וּשְׁפָחוֹתand maidservants וּבְנֵיand servants were בַיִתhouse הָיָהborn לִיto me גַּםalso מִקְנֶהowned בָקָרherds וָצֹאןand flocks הַרְבֵּהmore הָיָהwas לִיto me מִכֹּלthan anyone שֶׁהָיוּwho were לְפָנַיbefore me בִּירוּשָׁלִָםin Jerusalem

I acquired menservants and maidservants, and servants were born in my house. I also owned more herds and flocks than anyone in Jerusalem before me,

2:8

כָּנַסְתִּיaccumulated לִיfor myself גַּםeven כֶּסֶףsilver וְזָהָבand gold וּסְגֻלַּתand the treasure מְלָכִיםkings וְהַמְּדִינוֹתand provinces עָשִׂיתִיI לִיto myself שָׁרִיםmale female וְשָׁרוֹתand singers וְתַעֲנוּגֹתand the delights בְּנֵיsons הָאָדָםof men שִׁדָּהmany concubines וְשִׁדּוֹתand many concubines

and I accumulated for myself silver and gold and the treasure of kings and provinces. I gathered to myself male and female singers, and the delights of the sons of men—many concubines.

2:9

וְגָדַלְתִּיso I became great וְהוֹסַפְתִּיand even מִכֹּלsurpassed all שֶׁהָיָהwho had לְפָנַיpreceded me בִּירוּשָׁלִָםin Jerusalem אַףand חָכְמָתִיmy wisdom עָמְדָהremained לִּיto me

So I became great and surpassed all in Jerusalem who had preceded me; and my wisdom remained with me.

2:10

וְכֹלanything אֲשֶׁרthat שָׁאֲלוּcounsel עֵינַיmy eyes לֹאnot אָצַלְתִּיdesired מֵהֶםof them לֹאnot מָנַעְתִּיdeny אֶת[obj] לִבִּיmy heart מִכָּלno שִׂמְחָהpleasure כִּיfor לִבִּיmy heart שָׂמֵחַdelight מִכָּלin all עֲמָלִיmy work וְזֶהand this הָיָהwas חֶלְקִיreward מִכָּלfor all עֲמָלִיmy labor

Anything my eyes desired, I did not deny myself. I refused my heart no pleasure. For my heart took delight in all my work, and this was the reward for all my labor.

2:11

וּפָנִיתִיyet when אֲנִיI בְּכָלconsidered all מַעֲשַׂיworks that my שֶׁעָשׂוּhad accomplished יָדַיhands וּבֶעָמָלhad toiled שֶׁעָמַלְתִּיhad toiled achieve לַעֲשׂוֹתto do וְהִנֵּהand saw הַכֹּלeverything הֶבֶלfutile וּרְעוּתpursuit רוּחַwind וְאֵיןthere was nothing יִתְרוֹןgained תַּחַתunder הַשָּׁמֶשׁthe sun

Yet when I considered all the works that my hands had accomplished and what I had toiled to achieve, I found everything to be futile, a pursuit of the wind; there was nothing to be gained under the sun.

2:12

וּפָנִיתִיthen turned אֲנִיI לִרְאוֹתto consider חָכְמָהwisdom וְהוֹלֵלוֹתand madness וְסִכְלוּתand folly כִּיfor מֶהwhat הָאָדָםthe successor שֶׁיָּבוֹאwho come אַחֲרֵיafter הַמֶּלֶךְking’s אֵת[obj] אֲשֶׁרthat כְּבָרalready עָשׂוּהוּaccomplished

Then I turned to consider wisdom and madness and folly; for what more can the king’s successor do than what has already been accomplished?

2:13

וְרָאִיתִיand saw אָנִיI שֶׁיֵּשׁwho there יִתְרוֹןdoes gain לַחָכְמָהwisdom מִןfrom הַסִּכְלוּתfolly כִּיתְרוֹןlike does gain הָאוֹרas light מִןfrom הַחֹשֶׁךְdarkness

And I saw that wisdom exceeds folly, just as light exceeds darkness:

2:14

הֶחָכָםthe wise עֵינָיוeyes his בְּרֹאשׁוֹin head וְהַכְּסִילbut the fool בַּחֹשֶׁךְin darkness הוֹלֵךְwalks וְיָדַעְתִּיrealize גַםalso אָנִיI שֶׁמִּקְרֶהthat fate אֶחָדone יִקְרֶהovercomes אֶת[obj] כֻּלָּםthem both

The wise man has eyes in his head, but the fool walks in darkness. Yet I also came to realize that one fate overcomes them both.

2:15

וְאָמַרְתִּיso said אֲנִיI בְּלִבִּיin my heart כְּמִקְרֵהfate הַכְּסִילthe fool גַּםalso אֲנִיI יִקְרֵנִיbefall me וְלָמָּהwhat חָכַמְתִּיwise אֲנִיI אָזthen יוֹתֵרgained וְדִבַּרְתִּיand I said בְלִבִּיin my heart שֶׁגַּםthat too זֶהthis הָבֶלfutile

So I said to myself, “The fate of the fool will also befall me. What then have I gained by being wise?” And I said to myself that this too is futile.

2:16

כִּיfor אֵיןno זִכְרוֹןremembrance לֶחָכָםof the wise עִםwith הַכְּסִילthe fool לְעוֹלָםseeing בְּשֶׁכְּבָרthat both הַיָּמִיםthe days הַבָּאִיםto come הַכֹּלthe all נִשְׁכָּחforgotten וְאֵיךְand how can יָמוּתdie הֶחָכָםthe wise עִםwith הַכְּסִילthe fool

For there is no lasting remembrance of the wise, just as with the fool, seeing that both will be forgotten in the days to come. Alas, the wise man will die just like the fool!

2:17

וְשָׂנֵאתִיso I hated אֶת[obj] הַחַיִּיםlife כִּיbecause רַעgrievous עָלַיto me הַמַּעֲשֶׂהthe work שֶׁנַּעֲשָׂהthat is done תַּחַתunder הַשָּׁמֶשׁthe sun כִּיfor הַכֹּלeverything הֶבֶלfutile וּרְעוּתand a pursuit רוּחַwind

So I hated life, because the work that is done under the sun was grievous to me. For everything is futile and a pursuit of the wind.

2:18

וְשָׂנֵאתִיhated אֲנִיI אֶת[obj] כָּלall עֲמָלִיtoiled שֶׁאֲנִיwhich I עָמֵלtoiled תַּחַתunder הַשָּׁמֶשׁthe sun שֶׁאַנִּיחֶנּוּbecause I must leave it לָאָדָםto the man שֶׁיִּהְיֶהwho comes אַחֲרָיafter me

I hated all for which I had toiled under the sun, because I must leave it to the man who comes after me.

2:19

וּמִיand who יוֹדֵעַknows הֶחָכָםwise יִהְיֶהbe אוֹor סָכָלfoolish וְיִשְׁלַטtake over בְּכָלover all עֲמָלִיlabor שֶׁעָמַלְתִּיworked וְשֶׁחָכַמְתִּיskillfully תַּחַתunder הַשָּׁמֶשׁthe sun גַּםtoo זֶהthis is הָבֶלfutile

And who knows whether that man will be wise or foolish? Yet he will take over all the labor at which I have worked skillfully under the sun. This too is futile.

2:20

וְסַבּוֹתִיand around אֲנִיI לְיַאֵשׁto despair אֶת[obj] לִבִּיmy heart עַלover כָּלall הֶעָמָלthe labor שֶׁעָמַלְתִּיthat I had done תַּחַתunder הַשָּׁמֶשׁthe sun

So my heart began to despair over all the labor that I had done under the sun.

2:21

כִּיwhen יֵשׁthere אָדָםman שֶׁעֲמָלוֹwho has labored בְּחָכְמָהwith wisdom וּבְדַעַתknowledge וּבְכִשְׁרוֹןand skill וּלְאָדָםto a man שֶׁלֹּאwho has not עָמַלworked בּוֹin him יִתְּנֶנּוּgive his חֶלְקוֹportion גַּםtoo זֶהthis is הֶבֶלfutile וְרָעָהevil רַבָּהgreat

When there is a man who has labored with wisdom, knowledge, and skill, and he must give his portion to a man who has not worked for it, this too is futile and a great evil.

2:22

כִּיfor מֶהwhat הֹוֶהdoes לָאָדָםa man בְּכָלfor all עֲמָלוֹtoil וּבְרַעְיוֹןand striving with לִבּוhis heart שֶׁהוּאwhich he עָמֵלlabors תַּחַתunder הַשָּׁמֶשׁthe sun

For what does a man get for all the toil and striving with which he labors under the sun?

2:23

כִּיfor כָלall יָמָיוhis days מַכְאֹבִיםfilled וָכַעַסwith grief and עִנְיָנוֹhis task גַּםeven בַּלַּיְלָהat night לֹאnot שָׁכַבrest לִבּוֹhis mind גַּםtoo זֶהthis is הֶבֶלfutile הוּאis

Indeed, all his days are filled with grief, and his task is sorrowful; even at night, his mind does not rest. This too is futile.

2:24

אֵיןnothing טוֹבbetter בָּאָדָםfor a man שֶׁיֹּאכַלthan to eat וְשָׁתָהand drink וְהֶרְאָהand enjoy אֶת[obj] נַפְשׁוֹhave טוֹבgood בַּעֲמָלוֹhis work גַּםalso זֹהthis רָאִיתִיseen אָנִיI כִּיthat מִיַּדfrom the hand הָאֱלֹהִיםGod הִיאis

Nothing is better for a man than to eat and drink and enjoy his work. I have also seen that this is from the hand of God.

2:25

כִּיfor מִיwho יֹאכַלeat וּמִיand who can יָחוּשׁenjoyment חוּץenjoyment מִמֶּנִּיfrom him

For apart from Him, who can eat and who can find enjoyment?

2:26

כִּיfor לְאָדָםto the man שֶׁטּוֹבwho is pleasing לְפָנָיוin his sight נָתַןgives חָכְמָהwisdom וְדַעַתand knowledge וְשִׂמְחָהand joy וְלַחוֹטֶאbut to the sinner נָתַןhe assigns עִנְיָןtask לֶאֱסוֹףof gathering וְלִכְנוֹסand accumulating לָתֵתhand לְטוֹבpleases לִפְנֵיto before הָאֱלֹהִיםGod this גַּםtoo זֶהthis is הֶבֶלfutile וּרְעוּתand a pursuit רוּחַwind

To the man who is pleasing in His sight, He gives wisdom and knowledge and joy, but to the sinner He assigns the task of gathering and accumulating that which he will hand over to one who pleases God. This too is futile and a pursuit of the wind.
