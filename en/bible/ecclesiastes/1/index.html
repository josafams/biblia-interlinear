Ecclesiastes 1

Nothing new under the sun

1:1

דִּבְרֵיwords קֹהֶלֶתTeacher בֶּןson דָּוִדDavid מֶלֶךְking בִּירוּשָׁלִָםin Jerusalem

These are the words of the Teacher, the son of David, king in Jerusalem:

1:2

הֲבֵלfutility הֲבָלִיםfutilities אָמַרsays קֹהֶלֶתTeacher הֲבֵלfutility הֲבָלִיםfutilities הַכֹּלeverything הָבֶלfutile

“Futility of futilities,” says the Teacher, “futility of futilities! Everything is futile!”

1:3

מַהwhat יִּתְרוֹןdoes לָאָדָםa man from בְּכָלall עֲמָלוֹhis labor שֶׁיַּעֲמֹלwhich he toils תַּחַתunder הַשָּׁמֶשׁthe sun

What does a man gain from all his labor, at which he toils under the sun?

1:4

דּוֹרgenerations הֹלֵךְcome וְדוֹרand generations בָּאgo וְהָאָרֶץbut the earth לְעוֹלָםforever עֹמָדֶתremains

Generations come and generations go, but the earth remains forever.

1:5

וְזָרַחrises הַשֶּׁמֶשׁthe sun וּבָאand come הַשָּׁמֶשׁthe sun sets וְאֶלto מְקוֹמוֹwhere it שׁוֹאֵףhurries זוֹרֵחַrises הוּאhe שָׁםthere

The sun rises and the sun sets; it hurries back to where it rises.

1:6

הוֹלֵךְsouthward אֶלto דָּרוֹםwind וְסוֹבֵבthen turns אֶלto צָפוֹןnorthward סוֹבֵבround סֹבֵבround הוֹלֵךְwalks הָרוּחַit swirls וְעַלon סְבִיבֹתָיוhis around שָׁבreturning הָרוּחַcourse

The wind blows southward, then turns northward; round and round it swirls, ever returning on its course.

1:7

כָּלall הַנְּחָלִיםthe rivers הֹלְכִיםflow אֶלinto הַיָּםthe sea וְהַיָּםyet the sea אֵינֶנּוּno him מָלֵאfull אֶלto מְקוֹםplace שֶׁהַנְּחָלִיםwhich the streams הֹלְכִיםcome שָׁםthere הֵםthey שָׁבִיםagain לָלָכֶתflow

All the rivers flow into the sea, yet the sea is never full; to the place from which the streams come, there again they flow.

1:8

כָּלall הַדְּבָרִיםthings יְגֵעִיםwearisome לֹאnot יוּכַלcan אִישׁone לְדַבֵּרdescribe לֹאnot תִשְׂבַּעsatisfied עַיִןeye לִרְאוֹתwith seeing וְלֹאand not תִמָּלֵאcontent אֹזֶןear מִשְּׁמֹעַwith hearing

All things are wearisome, more than one can describe; the eye is not satisfied with seeing, nor the ear content with hearing.

1:9

מַהwhat שֶּׁהָיָהbeen הוּאwill שֶׁיִּהְיֶהbe וּמַהagain and what שֶׁנַּעֲשָׂהhas been done הוּאhe שֶׁיֵּעָשֶׂהdone וְאֵיןand no כָּלnothing חָדָשׁnew תַּחַתunder הַשָּׁמֶשׁthe sun

What has been will be again, and what has been done will be done again; there is nothing new under the sun.

1:10

יֵשׁthere דָּבָרcase שֶׁיֹּאמַרwhere one can say רְאֵהlook זֶהthis חָדָשׁnew הוּאhe כְּבָרalready הָיָהexisted לְעֹלָמִיםin the ages אֲשֶׁרthat הָיָהwas מִלְּפָנֵנוּbefore us

Is there a case where one can say, “Look, this is new”? It has already existed in the ages before us.

1:11

אֵיןno זִכְרוֹןremembrance לָרִאשֹׁנִיםthose who came before וְגַםand also לָאַחֲרֹנִיםto come שֶׁיִּהְיוּwill לֹאnot יִהְיֶהbe לָהֶםto them זִכָּרוֹןremembered עִםwith שֶׁיִּהְיוּwho follow after לָאַחֲרֹנָהto the last

There is no remembrance of those who came before, and those yet to come will not be remembered by those who follow after.

1:12

אֲנִיI קֹהֶלֶתTeacher הָיִיתִיwas מֶלֶךְking עַלover יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael בִּירוּשָׁלִָםin Jerusalem

I, the Teacher, was king over Israel in Jerusalem.

1:13

וְנָתַתִּיand I set אֶת[obj] לִבִּיmy mind לִדְרוֹשׁto seek וְלָתוּרand explore בַּחָכְמָהby wisdom עַלon כָּלall אֲשֶׁרthat נַעֲשָׂהdone תַּחַתunder הַשָּׁמָיִםheaven הוּאa עִנְיַןburden רָעheavy נָתַןlaid אֱלֹהִיםGod לִבְנֵיupon the sons הָאָדָםof men לַעֲנוֹתto occupy בּוֹin him

And I set my mind to seek and explore by wisdom all that is done under heaven. What a heavy burden God has laid upon the sons of men to occupy them!

1:14

רָאִיתִיseen אֶת[obj] כָּלall הַמַּעֲשִׂיםthe things שֶׁנַּעֲשׂוּthat are done תַּחַתunder הַשָּׁמֶשׁthe sun וְהִנֵּהand saw הַכֹּלthem all הֶבֶלfutile וּרְעוּתpursuit רוּחַwind

I have seen all the things that are done under the sun, and have found them all to be futile, a pursuit of the wind.

1:15

מְעֻוָּתcrooked לֹאnot יוּכַלcannot לִתְקֹןbe straightened וְחֶסְרוֹןlacking לֹאnot יוּכַלcannot לְהִמָּנוֹתbe counted

What is crooked cannot be straightened, and what is lacking cannot be counted.

1:16

דִּבַּרְתִּיsaid אֲנִיI עִםto לִבִּיmy heart לֵאמֹרto saying אֲנִיI הִנֵּהbehold הִגְדַּלְתִּיgrown וְהוֹסַפְתִּיand increased חָכְמָהwisdom עַלon כָּלall אֲשֶׁרthat הָיָהwas לְפָנַיthose before me עַלover יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem וְלִבִּיand my mind רָאָהobserved הַרְבֵּהa wealth חָכְמָהwisdom וָדָעַתand knowledge

I said to myself, “Behold, I have grown and increased in wisdom beyond all those before me who were over Jerusalem, and my mind has observed a wealth of wisdom and knowledge.”

1:17

וָאֶתְּנָהso I set לִבִּיmy mind לָדַעַתto know חָכְמָהwisdom וְדַעַתand know הוֹלֵלוֹתmadness וְשִׂכְלוּתand folly יָדַעְתִּיlearned שֶׁגַּםthat too זֶהthis הוּאis רַעְיוֹןpursuit רוּחַwind

So I set my mind to know wisdom and madness and folly; I learned that this, too, is a pursuit of the wind.

1:18

כִּיfor בְּרֹבwith much חָכְמָהwisdom רָבmuch כָּעַסsorrow וְיוֹסִיףand more דַּעַתknowledge יוֹסִיףgrows מַכְאוֹבgrief increases

For with much wisdom comes much sorrow, and as knowledge grows, grief increases.
