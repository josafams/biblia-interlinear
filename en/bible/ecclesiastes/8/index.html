Ecclesiastes 8

The king's power; justice delayed

Open in the interlinear reader

8:1

מִיwho כְּהֶחָכָםlike the wise וּמִיwho יוֹדֵעַknows פֵּשֶׁרinterpretation דָּבָרmatter חָכְמַתwisdom אָדָםman’s תָּאִירbrightens פָּנָיוhis face וְעֹזand the sternness פָּנָיוhis face יְשֻׁנֶּאchanged

Who is like the wise man? Who knows the interpretation of a matter? A man’s wisdom brightens his face, and the sternness of his face is changed.

8:2

אֲנִיI פִּיcommand מֶלֶךְking’s שְׁמוֹרkeep וְעַלand on דִּבְרַתbefore שְׁבוּעַתoath before אֱלֹהִיםGod

Keep the king’s command, I say, because of your oath before God.

8:3

אַלdo not תִּבָּהֵלhasten מִפָּנָיוleave his presence תֵּלֵךְgo אַלdo not תַּעֲמֹדpersist בְּדָבָרin word רָעbad כִּיfor כָּלwhatever אֲשֶׁרthat יַחְפֹּץpleases יַעֲשֶׂהdo

Do not hasten to leave his presence, and do not persist in a bad cause, for he will do whatever he pleases.

8:4

בַּאֲשֶׁרin where דְּבַרword מֶלֶךְking’s שִׁלְטוֹןsupreme וּמִיand who יֹאמַרsay לוֹto him מַהwhat תַּעֲשֶׂהdoing

For the king’s word is supreme, and who can say to him, “What are you doing?”

8:5

שׁוֹמֵרkeeps מִצְוָהcommand לֹאno יֵדַעknow דָּבָרword רָעharm וְעֵתtime וּמִשְׁפָּטand procedure יֵדַעknows לֵבheart חָכָםwise

Whoever keeps his command will come to no harm, and a wise heart knows the right time and procedure.

8:6

כִּיfor לְכָלto every חֵפֶץpurpose יֵשׁthere עֵתtime וּמִשְׁפָּטand procedure כִּיfor רָעַתmisery הָאָדָםa man’s רַבָּהweighs heavily עָלָיוupon him

For there is a right time and procedure to every purpose, though a man’s misery weighs heavily upon him.

8:7

כִּיfor אֵינֶנּוּno one יֹדֵעַknows מַהwhat שֶּׁיִּהְיֶהhappen כִּיfor כַּאֲשֶׁרwho יִהְיֶהbe מִיwho can יַגִּידtell לוֹto

Since no one knows what will happen, who can tell him what is to come?

8:8

אֵיןno אָדָםman שַׁלִּיטpower בָּרוּחַover the wind לִכְלוֹאto contain אֶת[obj] הָרוּחַthe spirit וְאֵיןso no שִׁלְטוֹןauthority בְּיוֹםover day הַמָּוֶתof death וְאֵיןas no מִשְׁלַחַתdischarged בַּמִּלְחָמָהin wartime וְלֹאnot יְמַלֵּטwill release רֶשַׁעwickedness אֶת[obj] בְּעָלָיוwho practice it

As no man has power over the wind to contain it, so no one has authority over his day of death. As no one can be discharged in wartime, so wickedness will not release those who practice it.

8:9

אֶת[obj] כָּלall זֶהthis רָאִיתִיseen וְנָתוֹןapplying אֶת[obj] לִבִּיmy mind לְכָלto every מַעֲשֶׂהdeed אֲשֶׁרthat נַעֲשָׂהdone תַּחַתunder הַשָּׁמֶשׁthe sun עֵתtime אֲשֶׁרthat שָׁלַטlords הָאָדָםone בְּאָדָםman לְרַעown detriment לוֹto his

All this I have seen, applying my mind to every deed that is done under the sun; there is a time when one man lords it over another to his own detriment.

8:10

וּבְכֵןand in therefore רָאִיתִיsaw רְשָׁעִיםwicked קְבֻרִיםburial וָבָאוּgo וּמִמְּקוֹםplace קָדוֹשׁholy יְהַלֵּכוּand they וְיִשְׁתַּכְּחוּthey were praised בָעִירin the city אֲשֶׁרthat כֵּןso עָשׂוּdone גַּםtoo זֶהthis is הָבֶלfutile

Then too, I saw the burial of the wicked who used to go in and out of the holy place, and they were praised in the city where they had done so. This too is futile.

8:11

אֲשֶׁרthat אֵיןnot נַעֲשָׂהexecuted פִתְגָםsentence מַעֲשֵׂהcrime הָרָעָהthe disaster מְהֵרָהspeedily עַלon כֵּןso מָלֵאfilled לֵבhearts בְּנֵיsons הָאָדָםmen בָּהֶםfully on לַעֲשׂוֹתdoing רָעevil

When the sentence for a crime is not speedily executed, the hearts of men become fully set on doing evil.

8:12

אֲשֶׁרthat חֹטֶאsinner עֹשֶׂהdoes רָעevil מְאַתhundred וּמַאֲרִיךְlong לוֹto him כִּיfor גַּםalso יוֹדֵעַknow אָנִיI אֲשֶׁרthat יִהְיֶהwill טּוֹבwell לְיִרְאֵיfear הָאֱלֹהִיםGod אֲשֶׁרwho יִירְאוּreverent מִלְּפָנָיוin his presence

Although a sinner does evil a hundred times and still lives long, yet I also know that it will go well with those who fear God, who are reverent in His presence.

8:13

וְטוֹבwell לֹאnot יִהְיֶהthem לָרָשָׁעbecause the wicked וְלֹאnot יַאֲרִיךְlengthen יָמִיםdays כַּצֵּלlike a shadow אֲשֶׁרthat אֵינֶנּוּit will not יָרֵאfear מִלִּפְנֵיfrom to before אֱלֹהִיםGod

Yet because the wicked do not fear God, it will not go well with them, and their days will not lengthen like a shadow.

8:14

יֶשׁthere הֶבֶלfutility אֲשֶׁרthat נַעֲשָׂהdone עַלon הָאָרֶץthe earth אֲשֶׁרthat יֵשׁthere צַדִּיקִיםrighteous אֲשֶׁרwho מַגִּיעַget אֲלֵהֶםto them כְּמַעֲשֵׂהwhat the actions הָרְשָׁעִיםthe wicked וְיֵשׁand there רְשָׁעִיםwicked שֶׁמַּגִּיעַwho get אֲלֵהֶםto them כְּמַעֲשֵׂהactions of הַצַּדִּיקִיםthe righteous אָמַרְתִּיsay שֶׁגַּםthat too זֶהthis is הָבֶלfutile

There is a futility that is done on the earth: There are righteous men who get what the actions of the wicked deserve, and there are wicked men who get what the actions of the righteous deserve. I say that this too is futile.

8:15

וְשִׁבַּחְתִּיso commended אֲנִיI אֶת[obj] הַשִּׂמְחָהthe enjoyment אֲשֶׁרthat אֵיןnothing טוֹבbetter לָאָדָםfor a man תַּחַתunder הַשֶּׁמֶשׁthe sun כִּיfor אִםif לֶאֱכוֹלto eat וְלִשְׁתּוֹתand drink וְלִשְׂמוֹחַand be merry for וְהוּאwill יִלְוֶנּוּaccompany him בַעֲמָלוֹin his labor during יְמֵיdays חַיָּיוhis life אֲשֶׁרthat נָתַןgives לוֹto him הָאֱלֹהִיםGod תַּחַתunder הַשָּׁמֶשׁthe sun

So I commended the enjoyment of life, because there is nothing better for a man under the sun than to eat and drink and be merry. For this joy will accompany him in his labor during the days of his life that God gives him under the sun.

8:16

כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had נָתַתִּיapplied אֶת[obj] לִבִּיmy mind לָדַעַתto know חָכְמָהwisdom וְלִרְאוֹתand to observe אֶת[obj] הָעִנְיָןthe task אֲשֶׁרthat נַעֲשָׂהperforms עַלon הָאָרֶץthe earth כִּיfor גַםeven בַּיּוֹםthe day וּבַלַּיְלָהor in the night שֵׁנָהsleep בְּעֵינָיוhis eyes אֵינֶנּוּnot רֹאֶהsee

When I applied my mind to know wisdom and to observe the task that one performs on the earth—though his eyes do not see sleep in the day or even in the night—

8:17

וְרָאִיתִיI saw אֶת[obj] כָּלevery מַעֲשֵׂהwork הָאֱלֹהִיםof God כִּיthat לֹאnot יוּכַלunable הָאָדָםa man לִמְצוֹאto comprehend אֶת[obj] הַמַּעֲשֶׂהthe work אֲשֶׁרthat נַעֲשָׂהdone תַחַתunder הַשֶּׁמֶשׁthe sun בְּשֶׁלin who blame אֲשֶׁרthat יַעֲמֹלefforts הָאָדָםhis efforts לְבַקֵּשׁto search וְלֹאand not יִמְצָאfind וְגַםmeaning even אִםif יֹאמַרclaims הֶחָכָםthe wise לָדַעַתto know לֹאnot יוּכַלunable לִמְצֹאto comprehend

I saw every work of God, and that a man is unable to comprehend the work that is done under the sun. Despite his efforts to search it out, he cannot find its meaning; even if the wise man claims to know, he is unable to comprehend.
