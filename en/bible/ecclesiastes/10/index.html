Ecclesiastes 10

A little folly spoils wisdom

Open in the interlinear reader

10:1

זְבוּבֵיflies מָוֶתdead יַבְאִישׁstench יַבִּיעַperfumer’s שֶׁמֶןoil רוֹקֵחַperfumer’s יָקָרso מֵחָכְמָהwisdom מִכָּבוֹדand honor סִכְלוּתfolly מְעָטlittle

As dead flies bring a stench to the perfumer’s oil, so a little folly outweighs wisdom and honor.

10:2

לֵבheart חָכָםwise לִימִינוֹto the right וְלֵבbut the heart כְּסִילfool לִשְׂמֹאלוֹto the left

A wise man’s heart inclines to the right, but the heart of a fool to the left.

10:3

וְגַםeven בַּדֶּרֶךְalong the road כְּשֶׁסָּכָלas the fool הֹלֵךְwalks לִבּוֹhis sense חָסֵרlacking וְאָמַרis לַכֹּלeveryone that סָכָלfool הוּאa

Even as the fool walks along the road, his sense is lacking, and he shows everyone that he is a fool.

10:4

אִםif רוּחַtemper הַמּוֹשֵׁלthe ruler’s תַּעֲלֶהflares עָלֶיךָagainst you מְקוֹמְךָabandon your אַלdo not תַּנַּחabandon post כִּיfor מַרְפֵּאcalmness יַנִּיחַcalmness חֲטָאִיםlays offenses גְּדוֹלִיםgreat

If the ruler’s temper flares against you, do not abandon your post, for calmness lays great offenses to rest.

10:5

יֵשׁthere רָעָהevil רָאִיתִיseen תַּחַתunder הַשָּׁמֶשׁthe sun כִּשְׁגָגָהan error שֶׁיֹּצָאthat proceeds מִלִּפְנֵיfrom to before הַשַּׁלִּיטthe ruler

There is an evil I have seen under the sun— an error that proceeds from the ruler:

10:6

נִתַּןappointed הַסֶּכֶלfolly בַּמְּרוֹמִיםto heights רַבִּיםgreat וַעֲשִׁירִיםbut the rich בַּשֵּׁפֶלin lowly positions יֵשֵׁבוּsit

Folly is appointed to great heights, but the rich sit in lowly positions.

10:7

רָאִיתִיseen עֲבָדִיםslaves עַלon סוּסִיםhorseback וְשָׂרִיםwhile princes הֹלְכִיםgo כַּעֲבָדִיםlike slaves עַלon הָאָרֶץfoot

I have seen slaves on horseback, while princes go on foot like slaves.

10:8

חֹפֵרdigs גּוּמָּץpit בּוֹin him יִפּוֹלfall וּפֹרֵץbreaches גָּדֵרwall יִשְּׁכֶנּוּbitten by נָחָשׁsnake

He who digs a pit may fall into it, and he who breaches a wall may be bitten by a snake.

10:9

מַסִּיעַquarries אֲבָנִיםstones יֵעָצֵבbe injured בָּהֶםby them בּוֹקֵעַsplits עֵצִיםlogs יִסָּכֶןendangers בָּםin them

The one who quarries stones may be injured by them, and he who splits logs endangers himself.

10:10

אִםif קֵהָהaxe הַבַּרְזֶלthe axe וְהוּאis לֹאnot פָנִיםface קִלְקַלdull וַחֲיָלִיםstrength יְגַבֵּרexerted וְיִתְרוֹןbut produces הַכְשֵׁרproduces success חָכְמָהskill

If the axe is dull and the blade unsharpened, more strength must be exerted, but skill produces success.

10:11

אִםif יִשֹּׁךְbites הַנָּחָשׁthe snake בְּלוֹאin without לָחַשׁcharmed וְאֵיןthere is no יִתְרוֹןprofit לְבַעַלfor charmer הַלָּשׁוֹןthe charmer

If the snake bites before it is charmed, there is no profit for the charmer.

10:12

דִּבְרֵיwords פִיmouth חָכָםwise חֵןgracious וְשִׂפְתוֹתbut the lips כְּסִילfool תְּבַלְּעֶנּוּconsume him

The words of a wise man’s mouth are gracious, but the lips of a fool consume him.

10:13

תְּחִלַּתbeginning דִּבְרֵיtalk פִיהוּhis mouth סִכְלוּתfolly וְאַחֲרִיתand the end פִּיהוּhis speech הוֹלֵלוּתmadness רָעָהevil

The beginning of his talk is folly, and the end of his speech is evil madness.

10:14

וְהַסָּכָלyet the fool יַרְבֶּהmultiplies דְבָרִיםwords לֹאno יֵדַעknows הָאָדָםone מַהwhat שֶׁיִּהְיֶהwho be וַאֲשֶׁרand what יִהְיֶהcome מֵאַחֲרָיוafter him מִיwho can יַגִּידtell לוֹto him

Yet the fool multiplies words. No one knows what is coming, and who can tell him what will come after him?

10:15

עֲמַלtoil הַכְּסִילִיםa fool תְּיַגְּעֶנּוּwearies him אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot יָדַעknow לָלֶכֶתthe way אֶלto עִירcity

The toil of a fool wearies him, for he does not know the way to the city.

10:16

אִיWoe לָךְto you אֶרֶץland שֶׁמַּלְכֵּךְwhose king נָעַרyouth וְשָׂרַיִךְand whose princes feast בַּבֹּקֶרin the morning יֹאכֵלוּeat

Woe to you, O land whose king is a youth, and whose princes feast in the morning.

10:17

אַשְׁרֵיךְblessed are you אֶרֶץland שֶׁמַּלְכֵּךְwhose king בֶּןson חוֹרִיםnobles וְשָׂרַיִךְand whose princes feast בָּעֵתat the proper time יֹאכֵלוּeat בִּגְבוּרָהfor strength וְלֹאand not בַשְּׁתִיfor drunkenness

Blessed are you, O land whose king is a son of nobles, and whose princes feast at the proper time— for strength and not for drunkenness.

10:18

בַּעֲצַלְתַּיִםthrough laziness יִמַּךְroof הַמְּקָרֶהthe caves וּבְשִׁפְלוּתand in idleness יָדַיִםhands יִדְלֹףidle הַבָּיִתthe house

Through laziness the roof caves in, and in the hands of the idle, the house leaks.

10:19

לִשְׂחוֹקfor laughter עֹשִׂיםprepared לֶחֶםbread וְיַיִןand wine יְשַׂמַּחmerry חַיִּיםlife וְהַכֶּסֶףbut money is the יַעֲנֶהanswer אֶת[obj] הַכֹּלfor everything

A feast is prepared for laughter, and wine makes life merry, but money is the answer for everything.

10:20

גַּםeven בְּמַדָּעֲךָyour thoughts מֶלֶךְking אַלdo not תְּקַלֵּלcurse וּבְחַדְרֵיbedroom מִשְׁכָּבְךָyour bedroom אַלor תְּקַלֵּלcurse עָשִׁירrich כִּיfor עוֹףbird הַשָּׁמַיִםthe air יוֹלִיךְtake אֶת[obj] הַקּוֹלspeech וּבַעַלand husband כְּנָפַיִםwinged creature יַגֵּידreport דָּבָרwords

Do not curse the king even in your thoughts, or curse the rich even in your bedroom, for a bird of the air may carry your words, and a winged creature may report your speech.
