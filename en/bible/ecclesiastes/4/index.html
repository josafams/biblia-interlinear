Ecclesiastes 4

The oppressed; two are better than one

Open in the interlinear reader

4:1

וְשַׁבְתִּיagain אֲנִיI וָאֶרְאֶהlooked and considered אֶת[obj] כָּלall הָעֲשֻׁקִיםthe oppression אֲשֶׁרthat נַעֲשִׂיםdone תַּחַתplace under הַשָּׁמֶשׁthe sun וְהִנֵּהI saw דִּמְעַתtears הָעֲשֻׁקִיםthe oppressed וְאֵיןno לָהֶםto them מְנַחֵםcomforter וּמִיַּדhands עֹשְׁקֵיהֶםtheir oppressors כֹּחַpower וְאֵיןno לָהֶםto them מְנַחֵםcomforter

Again I looked, and I considered all the oppression taking place under the sun. I saw the tears of the oppressed, and they had no comforter; the power lay in the hands of their oppressors, and there was no comforter.

4:2

וְשַׁבֵּחַso admired אֲנִיI אֶת[obj] הַמֵּתִיםthe dead שֶׁכְּבָרwho had already מֵתוּdied מִןfrom הַחַיִּיםthe living אֲשֶׁרwho הֵמָּהare חַיִּיםalive עֲדֶנָהstill

So I admired the dead, who had already died, above the living, who are still alive.

4:3

וְטוֹבbut better מִשְּׁנֵיהֶםthan both אֵת[obj] אֲשֶׁרwho עֲדֶןstill לֹאnot הָיָהexisted אֲשֶׁרwho לֹאnot רָאָהseen אֶת[obj] הַמַּעֲשֶׂהthe work הָרָעevil אֲשֶׁרthat נַעֲשָׂהdone תַּחַתunder הַשָּׁמֶשׁthe sun

But better than both is he who has not yet existed, who has not seen the evil that is done under the sun.

4:4

וְרָאִיתִיsaw אֲנִיI אֶת[obj] כָּלall עָמָלlabor וְאֵתand [obj] כָּלsuccess כִּשְׁרוֹןsuccess spring הַמַּעֲשֶׂהthe work כִּיthat הִיאa קִנְאַתenvy אִישׁman’s מֵרֵעֵהוּof his neighbor גַּםtoo זֶהthis is הֶבֶלfutile וּרְעוּתand a pursuit רוּחַwind

I saw that all labor and success spring from a man’s envy of his neighbor. This too is futile and a pursuit of the wind.

4:5

הַכְּסִילthe fool חֹבֵקfolds אֶת[obj] יָדָיוhis hands וְאֹכֵלand consumes אֶת[obj] בְּשָׂרוֹhis own flesh

The fool folds his hands and consumes his own flesh.

4:6

טוֹבbetter מְלֹאhandful כַףhandful נָחַתtranquility מִמְּלֹאthan two חָפְנַיִםhandfuls עָמָלtoil וּרְעוּתand pursuit רוּחַwind

Better one handful with tranquility than two handfuls with toil and pursuit of the wind.

4:7

וְשַׁבְתִּיagain אֲנִיI וָאֶרְאֶהsaw הֶבֶלfutility תַּחַתunder הַשָּׁמֶשׁthe sun

Again, I saw futility under the sun.

4:8

יֵשׁthere אֶחָדone וְאֵיןwithout שֵׁנִיsecond גַּםeven בֵּןson וָאָחor brother אֵיןno לוֹto him וְאֵיןno קֵץend לְכָלto all עֲמָלוֹlabor his גַּםeven עֵינוֹeyes are לֹאnot תִשְׂבַּעcontent עֹשֶׁרwealth וּלְמִיfor whom אֲנִיI עָמֵלtoil וּמְחַסֵּרand bereave אֶת[obj] נַפְשִׁיmy soul מִטּוֹבָהof enjoyment גַּםtoo זֶהthis הֶבֶלfutile וְעִנְיַןtask רָעmiserable הוּאa

There is a man all alone, without even a son or brother. And though there is no end to his labor, his eyes are still not content with his wealth: “For whom do I toil and bereave my soul of enjoyment?” This too is futile—a miserable task.

4:9

טוֹבִיםbetter הַשְּׁנַיִםtwo מִןthan הָאֶחָדone אֲשֶׁרthat יֵשׁhave לָהֶםa שָׂכָרreturn טוֹבgood בַּעֲמָלָםfor their labor

Two are better than one, because they have a good return for their labor.

4:10

כִּיfor אִםif יִפֹּלוּfalls הָאֶחָדone יָקִיםup אֶת[obj] חֲבֵרוֹcompanion וְאִילוֹbut pity הָאֶחָדthe one שֶׁיִּפּוֹלwho falls וְאֵיןwithout שֵׁנִיanother לַהֲקִימוֹto help him

For if one falls down, his companion can lift him up; but pity the one who falls without another to help him up!

4:11

גַּםeven אִםif יִשְׁכְּבוּlie שְׁנַיִםtwo וְחַםwarm לָהֶםto them וּלְאֶחָדbut can one אֵיךְhow יֵחָםwarm

Again, if two lie down together, they will keep warm; but how can one keep warm alone?

4:12

וְאִםand if יִתְקְפוֹoverpowered הָאֶחָדone הַשְּׁנַיִםtwo יַעַמְדוּresist נֶגְדּוֹbefore him וְהַחוּטmoreover a cord הַמְשֻׁלָּשׁof three strands לֹאnot בִמְהֵרָהquickly יִנָּתֵקbroken

And though one may be overpowered, two can resist. Moreover, a cord of three strands is not quickly broken.

4:13

טוֹבbetter יֶלֶדyouth מִסְכֵּןpoor וְחָכָםbut wise מִמֶּלֶךְking זָקֵןold וּכְסִילbut foolish אֲשֶׁרwho לֹאno יָדַעknows לְהִזָּהֵרwarning עוֹדlonger

Better is a poor but wise youth than an old but foolish king who no longer knows how to take a warning.

4:14

כִּיfor מִבֵּיתfrom prison הָסוּרִיםthe prison יָצָאcome לִמְלֹךְto the kingship כִּיfor גַּםeven בְּמַלְכוּתוֹin his own kingdom נוֹלַדborn רָשׁpoor

For the youth has come from the prison to the kingship, though he was born poor in his own kingdom.

4:15

רָאִיתִיsaw אֶת[obj] כָּלall הַחַיִּיםwho lived הַמְהַלְּכִיםand walked תַּחַתunder הַשָּׁמֶשׁthe sun עִםwith הַיֶּלֶדthe youth הַשֵּׁנִיthis second אֲשֶׁרwho יַעֲמֹדstand תַּחְתָּיוunder him

I saw that all who lived and walked under the sun followed this second one, the youth who succeeded the king.

4:16

אֵיןno קֵץlimit לְכָלto all הָעָםthe people לְכֹלto all אֲשֶׁרwho הָיָהwere לִפְנֵיהֶםbefore them גַּםeven הָאַחֲרוֹנִיםthe successor לֹאnot יִשְׂמְחוּcelebrated בוֹby those who כִּיfor גַםtoo זֶהthis הֶבֶלfutile וְרַעְיוֹןand a pursuit רוּחַwind

There is no limit to all the people who were before them. Yet the successor will not be celebrated by those who come even later. This too is futile and a pursuit of the wind.
