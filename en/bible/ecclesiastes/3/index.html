Ecclesiastes 3

A time for everything

Open in the interlinear reader

3:1

לַכֹּלto everything זְמָןseason וְעֵתand a time לְכָלfor every חֵפֶץpurpose תַּחַתunder הַשָּׁמָיִםheaven

To everything there is a season, and a time for every purpose under heaven:

3:2

עֵתtime לָלֶדֶתto be born וְעֵתand a time לָמוּתto die עֵתtime לָטַעַתto plant וְעֵתand a time לַעֲקוֹרto uproot נָטוּעַuproot

a time to be born and a time to die, a time to plant and a time to uproot,

3:3

עֵתtime לַהֲרוֹגto kill וְעֵתand a time לִרְפּוֹאto heal עֵתtime לִפְרוֹץto break וְעֵתand a time לִבְנוֹתto build

a time to kill and a time to heal, a time to break down and a time to build,

3:4

עֵתtime לִבְכּוֹתto weep וְעֵתand a time לִשְׂחוֹקto laugh עֵתtime סְפוֹדmourn וְעֵתand a time רְקוֹדdance

a time to weep and a time to laugh, a time to mourn and a time to dance,

3:5

עֵתtime לְהַשְׁלִיךְto cast אֲבָנִיםstones וְעֵתand a time כְּנוֹסgather אֲבָנִיםstones עֵתtime לַחֲבוֹקto embrace וְעֵתand a time לִרְחֹקto refrain מֵחַבֵּקfrom embracing

a time to cast away stones and a time to gather stones together, a time to embrace and a time to refrain from embracing,

3:6

עֵתtime לְבַקֵּשׁto search וְעֵתand a time לְאַבֵּדlost עֵתtime לִשְׁמוֹרto keep וְעֵתand a time לְהַשְׁלִיךְto discard

a time to search and a time to count as lost, a time to keep and a time to discard,

3:7

עֵתtime לִקְרוֹעַto tear וְעֵתand a time לִתְפּוֹרto mend עֵתtime לַחֲשׁוֹתto be silent וְעֵתand a time לְדַבֵּרto speak

a time to tear and a time to mend, a time to be silent and a time to speak,

3:8

עֵתtime לֶאֱהֹבto love וְעֵתand a time לִשְׂנֹאto hate עֵתtime מִלְחָמָהwar וְעֵתand a time שָׁלוֹםpeace

a time to love and a time to hate, a time for war and a time for peace.

3:9

מַהwhat יִּתְרוֹןdoes הָעוֹשֶׂהthe worker בַּאֲשֶׁרin where הוּאhe עָמֵלtoil

What does the worker gain from his toil?

3:10

רָאִיתִיseen אֶת[obj] הָעִנְיָןthe burden אֲשֶׁרthat נָתַןlaid אֱלֹהִיםGod לִבְנֵיupon the sons הָאָדָםof men לַעֲנוֹתto occupy בּוֹin him

I have seen the burden that God has laid upon the sons of men to occupy them.

3:11

אֶת[obj] הַכֹּלeverything עָשָׂהmade יָפֶהbeautiful בְעִתּוֹin its time he גַּםalso אֶת[obj] הָעֹלָםeternity נָתַןset בְּלִבָּםin the hearts men מִבְּלִיof yet אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot יִמְצָאfathom הָאָדָםthe man אֶת[obj] הַמַּעֲשֶׂהthe work אֲשֶׁרthat עָשָׂהdone הָאֱלֹהִיםthat God מֵרֹאשׁfrom beginning וְעַדto סוֹףend

He has made everything beautiful in its time. He has also set eternity in the hearts of men, yet they cannot fathom the work that God has done from beginning to end.

3:12

יָדַעְתִּיknow כִּיthat אֵיןnothing טוֹבbetter בָּםin them כִּיfor אִםif לִשְׂמוֹחַto rejoice וְלַעֲשׂוֹתand do טוֹבgood בְּחַיָּיוwhile they live

I know that there is nothing better for them than to rejoice and do good while they live,

3:13

וְגַםand also כָּלevery הָאָדָםman שֶׁיֹּאכַלshould eat וְשָׁתָהand drink וְרָאָהand find טוֹבsatisfaction בְּכָלin all עֲמָלוֹhis labor מַתַּתgift אֱלֹהִיםGod הִיאis

and also that every man should eat and drink and find satisfaction in all his labor—this is the gift of God.

3:14

יָדַעְתִּיknow כִּיthat כָּלeverything אֲשֶׁרthat יַעֲשֶׂהdoes הָאֱלֹהִיםGod הוּאhe יִהְיֶהbe לְעוֹלָםendures forever עָלָיוon him אֵיןnothing לְהוֹסִיףcan added וּמִמֶּנּוּor from it אֵיןno לִגְרֹעַtaken וְהָאֱלֹהִיםGod עָשָׂהdoes שֶׁיִּרְאוּwho fear מִלְּפָנָיוshould fear him

I know that everything God does endures forever; nothing can be added to it or taken from it. God does it so that they should fear Him.

3:15

מַהwhat שֶּׁהָיָהwho been כְּבָרalready הוּאhe וַאֲשֶׁרand what לִהְיוֹתwill be כְּבָרalready הָיָהbeen וְהָאֱלֹהִיםfor God יְבַקֵּשׁseeks אֶת[obj] נִרְדָּףpassed

What exists has already been, and what will be has already been, for God will call to account what has passed.

3:16

וְעוֹדand still רָאִיתִיsaw תַּחַתunder הַשָּׁמֶשׁthe sun מְקוֹםplace הַמִּשְׁפָּטof judgment שָׁמָּהthere הָרֶשַׁעis wickedness וּמְקוֹםplace הַצֶּדֶקof righteousness שָׁמָּהthere הָרָשַׁעis wickedness

Furthermore, I saw under the sun that in the place of judgment there is wickedness, and in the place of righteousness there is wickedness.

3:17

אָמַרְתִּיsaid אֲנִיI בְּלִבִּיin my heart אֶת[obj] הַצַּדִּיקthe righteous וְאֶתand [obj] הָרָשָׁעthe wicked יִשְׁפֹּטjudge הָאֱלֹהִיםGod כִּיfor עֵתtime לְכָלfor every חֵפֶץactivity וְעַלand on כָּלevery הַמַּעֲשֶׂהdeed שָׁםthere

I said in my heart, “God will judge the righteous and the wicked, since there is a time for every activity and every deed.”

3:18

אָמַרְתִּיsaid אֲנִיI בְּלִבִּיin my heart עַלfor דִּבְרַתbefore בְּנֵיsons הָאָדָםof men לְבָרָםtests them הָאֱלֹהִיםGod וְלִרְאוֹתso that they may see שְׁהֶםthat they are בְּהֵמָהbeasts הֵמָּהthey לָהֶםfor themselves

I said to myself, “As for the sons of men, God tests them so that they may see for themselves that they are but beasts.”

3:19

כִּיfor מִקְרֶהfates בְנֵיsons הָאָדָםboth men וּמִקְרֶהand fate הַבְּהֵמָהbeasts are the וּמִקְרֶהand fate אֶחָדsame לָהֶםto them כְּמוֹתas dies זֶהthis כֵּןso מוֹתdies זֶהthis וְרוּחַbreath אֶחָדother לַכֹּלall the וּמוֹתַרhave הָאָדָםman מִןfrom הַבְּהֵמָהthe animals אָיִןno כִּיfor הַכֹּלeverything הָבֶלfutile

For the fates of both men and beasts are the same: As one dies, so dies the other—they all have the same breath. Man has no advantage over the animals, since everything is futile.

3:20

הַכֹּלall הוֹלֵךְgo אֶלto מָקוֹםplace אֶחָדone הַכֹּלall הָיָהcome מִןfrom הֶעָפָרdust וְהַכֹּלand all שָׁבreturn אֶלto הֶעָפָרdust

All go to one place; all come from dust, and all return to dust.

3:21

מִיwho יוֹדֵעַknows רוּחַspirit בְּנֵיsons הָאָדָםman הָעֹלָהrises הִיאthe לְמָעְלָהupward וְרוּחַand spirit הַבְּהֵמָהthe animal הַיֹּרֶדֶתthe descend הִיאis לְמַטָּהdescends into לָאָרֶץinto the earth

Who knows if the spirit of man rises upward and the spirit of the animal descends into the earth?

3:22

וְרָאִיתִיI have seen כִּיthat אֵיןnothing טוֹבbetter מֵאֲשֶׁרfrom had יִשְׂמַחenjoy הָאָדָםa man בְּמַעֲשָׂיוto his work כִּיbecause הוּאthat is חֶלְקוֹhis lot כִּיfor מִיwho יְבִיאֶנּוּbring him לִרְאוֹתto see בְּמֶהwhat שֶׁיִּהְיֶהwill come אַחֲרָיוafter him

I have seen that there is nothing better for a man than to enjoy his work, because that is his lot. For who can bring him to see what will come after him?
