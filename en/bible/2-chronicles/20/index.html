וַיֵּלֶךְand Jehoshaphat walked בְּדֶרֶךְin the way אָבִיוhis father אָסָאAsa וְלֹאand not סָרturn מִמֶּנָּהfrom it לַעֲשׂוֹתdid הַיָּשָׁרwas right בְּעֵינֵיin the eyes יְהוָהLORD

And Jehoshaphat walked in the way of his father Asa and did not turn away from it; he did what was right in the eyes of the LORD.

20:33

אַךְhowever הַבָּמוֹתthe high places לֹאnot סָרוּremoved וְעוֹדand still הָעָםthe people לֹאnot הֵכִינוּset לְבָבָםtheir hearts לֵאלֹהֵיon the God אֲבֹתֵיהֶםtheir fathers

The high places, however, were not removed; the people had not yet set their hearts on the God of their fathers.

20:34

וְיֶתֶרrest דִּבְרֵיacts יְהוֹשָׁפָטJehoshaphat הָרִאשֹׁנִיםfrom beginning וְהָאַחֲרֹנִיםto end הִנָּםthey are indeed כְּתוּבִיםwritten בְּדִבְרֵיin the Chronicles יֵהוּאJehu בֶןson חֲנָנִיHanani אֲשֶׁרwhich הֹעֲלָהrecorded עַלin סֵפֶרBook מַלְכֵיkings יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

As for the rest of the acts of Jehoshaphat, from beginning to end, they are indeed written in the Chronicles of Jehu son of Hanani, which are recorded in the Book of the Kings of Israel.

20:35

וְאַחֲרֵיlater כֵןthis אֶתְחַבַּרalliance יְהוֹשָׁפָטJehoshaphat מֶלֶךְking יְהוּדָהJudah עִםwith אֲחַזְיָהAhaziah מֶלֶךְking יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael הוּאhe הִרְשִׁיעַacted wickedly לַעֲשׂוֹתmade an

Later, Jehoshaphat king of Judah made an alliance with Ahaziah king of Israel, who acted wickedly.

20:36

וַיְחַבְּרֵהוּthey agreed עִמּוֹwith him לַעֲשׂוֹתto make אֳנִיּוֹתships לָלֶכֶתto go תַּרְשִׁישׁTarshish וַיַּעֲשׂוּbuilt אֳנִיּוֹתships בְּעֶצְיוֹןin Ezion גָּבֶרgeber

They agreed to make ships to go to Tarshish, and these were built in Ezion-geber.

20:37

וַיִּתְנַבֵּאprophesied אֱלִיעֶזֶרEliezer בֶּןson דֹּדָוָהוּDodavahu מִמָּרֵשָׁהof Mareshah עַלagainst יְהוֹשָׁפָטJehoshaphat לֵאמֹרsaying כְּהִתְחַבֶּרְךָallied yourself עִםwith אֲחַזְיָהוּAhaziah פָּרַץdestroyed יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] מַעֲשֶׂיךָyour works וַיִּשָּׁבְרוּwrecked אֳנִיּוֹתships וְלֹאand not עָצְרוּsail לָלֶכֶתto sail אֶלto תַּרְשִׁישׁTarshish

Then Eliezer son of Dodavahu of Mareshah prophesied against Jehoshaphat, saying, “Because you have allied yourself with Ahaziah, the LORD has destroyed your works.” So the ships were wrecked and were unable to sail to Tarshish.
