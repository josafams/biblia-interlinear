2 Chronicles 14

Asa reforms worship and defeats Zerah

Then Abijah rested with his fathers and was buried in the City of David. And his son Asa reigned in his place, and in his days the land was at peace for ten years.

And Asa did what was good and right in the eyes of the LORD his God.

He removed the foreign altars and high places, shattered the sacred pillars, and chopped down the Asherah poles.

14:4

וַיֹּאמֶרhe commanded לִיהוּדָהJudah לִדְרוֹשׁto seek אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod אֲבוֹתֵיהֶםtheir fathers וְלַעֲשׂוֹתand to observe הַתּוֹרָהthe law וְהַמִּצְוָהand the commandments

He commanded the people of Judah to seek the LORD, the God of their fathers, and to observe the law and the commandments.

14:5

וַיָּסַרalso removed מִכָּלfrom all עָרֵיcities יְהוּדָהJudah אֶת[obj] הַבָּמוֹתthe high places וְאֶתand [obj] הַחַמָּנִיםincense altars וַתִּשְׁקֹטpeace הַמַּמְלָכָהthe kingdom לְפָנָיוunder him

He also removed the high places and incense altars from all the cities of Judah, and under him the kingdom was at peace.

14:6

וַיִּבֶןbuilt עָרֵיcities מְצוּרָהfortified בִּיהוּדָהin Judah כִּיbecause שָׁקְטָהpeace הָאָרֶץthe land וְאֵיןdays no עִמּוֹwith him מִלְחָמָהwar בַּשָּׁנִיםin the years הָאֵלֶּהthese כִּיfor הֵנִיחַrest יְהוָהLORD לוֹto him

Because the land was at peace, Asa built fortified cities in Judah. In those days no one made war with him, because the LORD had given him rest.

14:7

וַיֹּאמֶרso he said לִיהוּדָהJudah נִבְנֶהbuild אֶת[obj] הֶעָרִיםthese cities הָאֵלֶּהthese וְנָסֵבand surround חוֹמָהwalls וּמִגְדָּלִיםand towers דְּלָתַיִםdoors וּבְרִיחִיםand bars עוֹדֶנּוּstill הָאָרֶץthe land לְפָנֵינוּto our before כִּיbecause דָרַשְׁנוּsought אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God we דָּרַשְׁנוּsought וַיָּנַחrest לָנוּto us מִסָּבִיבon every side וַיִּבְנוּso they built וַיַּצְלִיחוּand prospered

So he said to the people of Judah, “Let us build these cities and surround them with walls and towers, with doors and bars. The land is still ours because we have sought the LORD our God. We have sought Him and He has given us rest on every side.” So they built and prospered.

14:8

וַיְהִיand was לְאָסָאAsa had חַיִלarmy נֹשֵׂאbearing צִנָּהlarge shields וָרֹמַחand spears מִיהוּדָהfrom Judah שְׁלֹשׁ300 מֵאוֹת300 men אֶלֶף000 וּמִבִּנְיָמִןfrom Benjamin נֹשְׂאֵיbearing מָגֵןsmall shields וְדֹרְכֵיand drawing קֶשֶׁתbow מָאתַיִםmen וּשְׁמוֹנִיםand 280 אָלֶף000 כָּלall אֵלֶּהthese גִּבּוֹרֵיmighty men חָיִלvalor

Asa had an army of 300,000 men from Judah bearing large shields and spears, and 280,000 men from Benjamin bearing small shields and drawing the bow. All these were mighty men of valor.

14:9

וַיֵּצֵאcame אֲלֵיהֶםto them זֶרַחZerah הַכּוּשִׁיthe Cushite בְּחַיִלwith an army אֶלֶף1 000 אֲלָפִים000 וּמַרְכָּבוֹתand chariots שְׁלֹשׁ300 מֵאוֹת300 וַיָּבֹאand they advanced עַדfar מָרֵשָׁהMareshah

Then Zerah the Cushite came against them with an army of 1,000,000 men and 300 chariots, and they advanced as far as Mareshah.

14:10

וַיֵּצֵאso marched אָסָאAsa לְפָנָיוagainst him וַיַּעַרְכוּand lined מִלְחָמָהbattle בְּגֵיאin the Valley צְפַתָהZephathah לְמָרֵשָׁהnear Mareshah

So Asa marched out against him and lined up in battle formation in the Valley of Zephathah near Mareshah.

14:11

וַיִּקְרָאthen cried אָסָאAsa אֶלto יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהָיוhis God וַיֹּאמַרthere is יְהוָהLORD אֵיןno עִמְּךָwith you לַעְזוֹרto help בֵּיןthe רַבmighty לְאֵיןto nothing כֹּחַpowerless עָזְרֵנוּhelp us יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God כִּיfor עָלֶיךָon you נִשְׁעַנּוּrely וּבְשִׁמְךָand in your name בָאנוּcome עַלagainst הֶהָמוֹןthis multitude הַזֶּהthis יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God אַתָּהyou are אַלnot יַעְצֹרprevail עִמְּךָagainst you אֱנוֹשׁmortal

Then Asa cried out to the LORD his God: “O LORD, there is no one besides You to help the powerless against the mighty. Help us, O LORD our God, for we rely on You, and in Your name we have come against this multitude. O LORD, You are our God. Do not let a mere mortal prevail against You.”

14:12

וַיִּגֹּףstruck יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] הַכּוּשִׁיםthe Cushites לִפְנֵיbefore אָסָאAsa וְלִפְנֵיand to before יְהוּדָהJudah וַיָּנֻסוּfled הַכּוּשִׁיםthe Cushites

So the LORD struck down the Cushites before Asa and Judah, and the Cushites fled.

14:13

וַיִּרְדְּפֵםpursued them אָסָאAsa וְהָעָםand his army אֲשֶׁרthat עִמּוֹwith him עַדfar לִגְרָרas Gerar וַיִּפֹּלfell and מִכּוּשִׁיםThe Cushites לְאֵיןcould not לָהֶםto them מִחְיָהrecover כִּיfor נִשְׁבְּרוּcrushed לִפְנֵיbefore יְהוָהLORD וְלִפְנֵיand to before מַחֲנֵהוּhis army וַיִּשְׂאוּJudah carried שָׁלָלplunder הַרְבֵּהgreat מְאֹדvery

Then Asa and his army pursued them as far as Gerar. The Cushites fell and could not recover, for they were crushed before the LORD and His army. So the people of Judah carried off a great amount of plunder

14:14

וַיַּכּוּand attacked אֵת[obj] כָּלall הֶעָרִיםthe cities סְבִיבוֹתaround גְּרָרGerar כִּיbecause הָיָהwas פַחַדterror יְהוָהLORD עֲלֵיהֶםupon וַיָּבֹזּוּthey plundered אֶת[obj] כָּלall הֶעָרִיםthe cities כִּיfor בִזָּהplunder רַבָּהmuch הָיְתָהthere בָהֶםin them

and attacked all the cities around Gerar, because the terror of the LORD had fallen upon them. They plundered all the cities, since there was much plunder there.

14:15

וְגַםthey also אָהֳלֵיtents מִקְנֶהherdsmen הִכּוּattacked וַיִּשְׁבּוּand carried צֹאןsheep לָרֹבoff many וּגְמַלִּיםand camels וַיָּשֻׁבוּthen they returned יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem

They also attacked the tents of the herdsmen and carried off many sheep and camels. Then they returned to Jerusalem.
