2 Chronicles 11

Rehoboam fortifies Judah

When Rehoboam arrived in Jerusalem, he mobilized the house of Judah and Benjamin—180,000 chosen warriors—to fight against Israel and restore the kingdom to Rehoboam.

“Tell Rehoboam son of Solomon king of Judah and all the Israelites in Judah and Benjamin

that this is what the LORD says: ‘You are not to go up and fight against your brothers. Each of you must return home, for this word is from Me.’” So they listened to the words of the LORD and turned back from going against Jeroboam.

וַיֵּשֶׁבcontinued to live רְחַבְעָםRehoboam בִּירוּשָׁלִָםin Jerusalem וַיִּבֶןand he built עָרִיםcities לְמָצוֹרfor defense בִּיהוּדָהin Judah

Rehoboam continued to live in Jerusalem, and he built up cities for defense in Judah.

He built up Bethlehem, Etam, Tekoa,

Beth-zur, Soco, Adullam,

Gath, Mareshah, Ziph,

Adoraim, Lachish, Azekah,

Zorah, Aijalon, and Hebron, the fortified cities in Judah and Benjamin.

11:11

וַיְחַזֵּקhe strengthened אֶת[obj] הַמְּצֻרוֹתtheir fortresses וַיִּתֵּןand put בָּהֶםin them נְגִידִיםofficers וְאֹצְרוֹתwith supplies מַאֲכָלfood וְשֶׁמֶןoil וָיָיִןand wine

He strengthened their fortresses and put officers in them, with supplies of food, oil, and wine.

11:12

וּבְכָלin all עִירcity וָעִירcities צִנּוֹתshields וּרְמָחִיםand spears וַיְחַזְּקֵםand strengthened them לְהַרְבֵּהgreatly מְאֹדgreatly וַיְהִיbelonged לוֹto him יְהוּדָהJudah וּבִנְיָמִןand Benjamin

He also put shields and spears in all the cities and strengthened them greatly. So Judah and Benjamin belonged to him.

11:13

וְהַכֹּהֲנִיםmoreover the priests וְהַלְוִיִּםand Levites אֲשֶׁרthat בְּכָלdistricts throughout יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael הִתְיַצְּבוּstood עָלָיוon him מִכָּלfrom all גְּבוּלָםRehoboam

Moreover, the priests and Levites from all their districts throughout Israel stood with Rehoboam.

11:14

כִּיfor עָזְבוּleft הַלְוִיִּםthe Levites אֶת[obj] מִגְרְשֵׁיהֶםtheir pasturelands וַאֲחֻזָּתָםand their possessions וַיֵּלְכוּand went לִיהוּדָהto Judah וְלִירוּשָׁלִָםand Jerusalem כִּיbecause הִזְנִיחָםrejected them יָרָבְעָםJeroboam וּבָנָיוand his sons מִכַּהֵןas priests לַיהוָהof the LORD

For the Levites left their pasturelands and their possessions and went to Judah and Jerusalem, because Jeroboam and his sons had rejected them as priests of the LORD.

11:15

וַיַּעֲמֶדand Jeroboam appointed לוֹto him כֹּהֲנִיםpriests לַבָּמוֹתfor the high places וְלַשְּׂעִירִיםand for the goat demons וְלָעֲגָלִיםand calf אֲשֶׁרhad עָשָׂהmade

And Jeroboam appointed his own priests for the high places and for the goat demons and calf idols he had made.

Those from every tribe of Israel who had set their hearts to seek the LORD their God followed the Levites to Jerusalem to sacrifice to the LORD, the God of their fathers.

11:17

וַיְחַזְּקוּso they strengthened אֶת[obj] מַלְכוּתkingdom יְהוּדָהJudah וַיְאַמְּצוּand supported אֶת[obj] רְחַבְעָםRehoboam בֶּןson שְׁלֹמֹהSolomon לְשָׁנִיםfor years שָׁלוֹשׁthree כִּיbecause הָלְכוּwalked בְּדֶרֶךְin the way דָּוִידDavid וּשְׁלֹמֹהand Solomon לְשָׁנִיםfor years שָׁלוֹשׁthree

So they strengthened the kingdom of Judah and supported Rehoboam son of Solomon for three years, because they walked for three years in the way of David and Solomon.

11:18

וַיִּקַּחand took לוֹto him רְחַבְעָםRehoboam אִשָּׁהmarried אֶת[obj] מָחֲלַתMahalath בַּתdaughter יְרִימוֹתJerimoth בֶּןson דָּוִידDavid’s אֲבִיהַיִלAbihail בַּתdaughter אֱלִיאָבEliab בֶּןson יִשָׁיJesse’s

And Rehoboam married Mahalath, who was the daughter of David’s son Jerimoth and of Abihail, the daughter of Jesse’s son Eliab.

11:19

וַתֵּלֶדshe bore לוֹto him בָּנִיםsons אֶת[obj] יְעוּשׁJeush וְאֶתand [obj] שְׁמַרְיָהShemariah וְאֶתand [obj] זָהַםZaham

She bore sons to him: Jeush, Shemariah, and Zaham.

11:20

וְאַחֲרֶיהָafter her לָקַחmarried אֶת[obj] מַעֲכָהMaacah בַּתdaughter אַבְשָׁלוֹםAbsalom וַתֵּלֶדand she bore לוֹto him אֶת[obj] אֲבִיָּהAbijah וְאֶתand [obj] עַתַּיAttai וְאֶתand [obj] זִיזָאZiza וְאֶתand [obj] שְׁלֹמִיתShelomith

After her, he married Maacah daughter of Absalom, and she bore to him Abijah, Attai, Ziza, and Shelomith.

11:21

וַיֶּאֱהַבloved רְחַבְעָםRehoboam אֶת[obj] מַעֲכָהMaacah בַתdaughter אַבְשָׁלוֹםAbsalom מִכָּלthan all נָשָׁיוhis wives וּפִילַגְשָׁיוand concubines כִּיfor נָשִׁיםwives שְׁמוֹנֶהeighteen עֶשְׂרֵהeighteen נָשָׂאcarried וּפִילַגְשִׁיםand concubines שִׁשִּׁיםsixty וַיּוֹלֶדfather עֶשְׂרִיםtwenty וּשְׁמוֹנָהeight בָּנִיםsons וְשִׁשִּׁיםand sixty בָּנוֹתdaughters

Rehoboam loved Maacah daughter of Absalom more than all his wives and concubines. In all, he had eighteen wives and sixty concubines, and he was the father of twenty-eight sons and sixty daughters.

11:22

וַיַּעֲמֵדappointed לָרֹאשׁchief רְחַבְעָםRehoboam אֶת[obj] אֲבִיָּהAbijah בֶןson מַעֲכָהMaacah לְנָגִידas prince בְּאֶחָיוamong his brothers כִּיfor לְהַמְלִיכוֹto make him king

Rehoboam appointed Abijah son of Maacah as chief prince among his brothers, intending to make him king.

11:23

וַיָּבֶןalso acted wisely וַיִּפְרֹץby dispersing מִכָּלthroughout בָּנָיוhis sons לְכָלthe districts אַרְצוֹתlands יְהוּדָהJudah וּבִנְיָמִןand Benjamin לְכֹלto all עָרֵיcities הַמְּצֻרוֹתthe fortified וַיִּתֵּןhe gave לָהֶםto them הַמָּזוֹןprovisions לָרֹבabundant וַיִּשְׁאַלand sought הֲמוֹןmany נָשִׁיםwives

Rehoboam also acted wisely by dispersing some of his sons throughout the districts of Judah and Benjamin, and to all the fortified cities. He gave them abundant provisions and sought many wives for them.
