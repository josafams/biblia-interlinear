2 Chronicles 13

Abijah defeats Jeroboam

Open in the interlinear reader

13:1

בִּשְׁנַתyear שְׁמוֹנֶהeighteenth עֶשְׂרֵהeighteenth לַמֶּלֶךְking יָרָבְעָםJeroboam’s וַיִּמְלֹךְreign became אֲבִיָּהAbijah עַלon יְהוּדָהJudah

In the eighteenth year of Jeroboam’s reign, Abijah became king of Judah,

13:2

שָׁלוֹשׁthree שָׁנִיםyears מָלַךְreigned בִּירוּשָׁלִַםin Jerusalem וְשֵׁםname אִמּוֹhis mother’s was מִיכָיָהוּMicaiah בַתdaughter אוּרִיאֵלUriel מִןfrom גִּבְעָהGibeah וּמִלְחָמָהwar הָיְתָהwas בֵּיןbetween אֲבִיָּהAbijah וּבֵיןand between יָרָבְעָםJeroboam

and he reigned in Jerusalem three years. His mother’s name was Micaiah daughter of Uriel; she was from Gibeah. And there was war between Abijah and Jeroboam.

13:3

וַיֶּאְסֹרwent אֲבִיָּהAbijah אֶת[obj] הַמִּלְחָמָהinto battle בְּחַיִלwith an army גִּבּוֹרֵיmighty men מִלְחָמָהbattle אַרְבַּע400 מֵאוֹת400 אֶלֶף000 אִישׁmen בָּחוּרchosen וְיָרָבְעָםwhile Jeroboam עָרַךְdrew עִמּוֹagainst him מִלְחָמָהformation בִּשְׁמוֹנֶהagainst with 800 מֵאוֹתhundreds אֶלֶף000 אִישׁmen בָּחוּרchosen גִּבּוֹרmighty חָיִלvalor

Abijah went into battle with an army of 400,000 chosen men, while Jeroboam drew up in formation against him with 800,000 chosen and mighty men of valor.

13:4

וַיָּקָםthen stood אֲבִיָּהAbijah מֵעַלon לְהַרMount צְמָרַיִםZemaraim אֲשֶׁרthat בְּהַרin the hill country אֶפְרָיִםEphraim וַיֹּאמֶרand said שְׁמָעוּנִיhear me יָרָבְעָםJeroboam וְכָלand all יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

Then Abijah stood on Mount Zemaraim in the hill country of Ephraim and said, “Hear me, O Jeroboam and all Israel!

13:5

הֲלֹאdo not לָכֶםto you לָדַעַתknow כִּיthat יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael נָתַןgiven מַמְלָכָהkingship לְדָוִידto David עַלon יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael לְעוֹלָםforever לוֹto him וּלְבָנָיוand his descendants בְּרִיתcovenant מֶלַחsalt

Do you not know that the LORD, the God of Israel, has given the kingship of Israel to David and his descendants forever by a covenant of salt?

13:6

וַיָּקָםrose יָרָבְעָםJeroboam בֶּןson נְבָטNebat עֶבֶדservant שְׁלֹמֹהSolomon בֶןson דָּוִידDavid וַיִּמְרֹדand rebelled עַלagainst אֲדֹנָיוhis master

Yet Jeroboam son of Nebat, a servant of Solomon son of David, rose up and rebelled against his master.

13:7

וַיִּקָּבְצוּgathered עָלָיוon him אֲנָשִׁיםmen רֵקִיםworthless בְּנֵיsons בְלִיַּעַלwicked וַיִּתְאַמְּצוּresist עַלto רְחַבְעָםRehoboam בֶּןson שְׁלֹמֹהSolomon וּרְחַבְעָםwhen he הָיָהwas נַעַרyoung וְרַךְinexperienced לֵבָבheart וְלֹאand not הִתְחַזַּקresist לִפְנֵיהֶםto their before

Then worthless and wicked men gathered around him to resist Rehoboam son of Solomon when he was young, inexperienced, and unable to resist them.

13:8

וְעַתָּהand now אַתֶּםyou אֹמְרִיםthink לְהִתְחַזֵּקcan resist לִפְנֵיto before מַמְלֶכֶתkingdom יְהוָהLORD בְּיַדin the hands בְּנֵיof descendants דָוִידDavid’s וְאַתֶּםdescendants you הָמוֹןarmy רָבvast army וְעִמָּכֶםwith you עֶגְלֵיcalves זָהָבgolden אֲשֶׁרthat עָשָׂהmade לָכֶםfor you יָרָבְעָםJeroboam לֵאלֹהִיםas gods

And now you think you can resist the kingdom of the LORD, which is in the hands of David’s descendants. You are indeed a vast army, and you have with you the golden calves that Jeroboam made for you as gods.

13:9

הֲלֹאdid you not הִדַּחְתֶּםdrive אֶת[obj] כֹּהֲנֵיpriests יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] בְּנֵיsons אַהֲרֹןAaron וְהַלְוִיִּםthe Levites וַתַּעֲשׂוּand did לָכֶםfor כֹּהֲנִיםpriests כְּעַמֵּיpeoples הָאֲרָצוֹתother lands כָּלwhoever הַבָּאnow comes לְמַלֵּאto consecrate יָדוֹhis hand בְּפַרin bull בֶּןyoung בָּקָרbull וְאֵילִםand rams שִׁבְעָהseven וְהָיָהbecome כֹהֵןpriest לְלֹאthat are not אֱלֹהִיםgods

But did you not drive out the priests of the LORD, the sons of Aaron, and the Levites? And did you not make priests for yourselves as do the peoples of other lands? Now whoever comes to consecrate himself with a young bull and seven rams can become a priest of things that are not gods.

13:10

וַאֲנַחְנוּus יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God we וְלֹאwe have not עֲזַבְנֻהוּforsaken him וְכֹהֲנִיםpriests מְשָׁרְתִיםminister לַיהוָהto the LORD בְּנֵיsons אַהֲרֹןAaron וְהַלְוִיִּםand the Levites בַּמְלָאכֶתto their duties

But as for us, the LORD is our God. We have not forsaken Him; the priests who minister to the LORD are sons of Aaron, and the Levites attend to their duties.

13:11

וּמַקְטִרִיםthey present לַיהוָהto LORD עֹלוֹתburnt offerings בַּבֹּקֶרevery בַּבֹּקֶרmorning וּבָעֶרֶבand every evening בָּעֶרֶבin the evening וּקְטֹרֶתand fragrant incense סַמִּיםfragrant וּמַעֲרֶכֶתshowbread לֶחֶםrows עַלon הַשֻּׁלְחָןtable הַטָּהוֹרceremonially clean וּמְנוֹרַתlampstand הַזָּהָבthe gold וְנֵרֹתֶיהָlamps לְבָעֵרlight בָּעֶרֶבevery evening בָּעֶרֶבcarrying out the כִּיfor שֹׁמְרִיםwatchmen אֲנַחְנוּwe אֶת[obj] מִשְׁמֶרֶתrequirements יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God וְאַתֶּםwhile you עֲזַבְתֶּםforsaken אֹתוֹhim

Every morning and every evening they present burnt offerings and fragrant incense to the LORD. They set out the rows of showbread on the ceremonially clean table, and every evening they light the lamps of the gold lampstand. We are carrying out the requirements of the LORD our God, while you have forsaken Him.

13:12

וְהִנֵּהnow behold עִמָּנוּwith us בָרֹאשׁas our head הָאֱלֹהִיםGod וְכֹהֲנָיוand his priests וַחֲצֹצְרוֹתwith their trumpets הַתְּרוּעָהsound לְהָרִיעַthe battle call עֲלֵיכֶםagainst you בְּנֵיchildren יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael אַלdo not תִּלָּחֲמוּfight עִםagainst יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod אֲבֹתֵיכֶםyour fathers כִּיfor לֹאnot תַצְלִיחוּsucceed

Now behold, God Himself is with us as our head, and His priests with their trumpets sound the battle call against you. O children of Israel, do not fight against the LORD, the God of your fathers, for you will not succeed.”

13:13

וְיָרָבְעָםNow Jeroboam הֵסֵבaround אֶת[obj] הַמַּאְרָבambush לָבוֹאtroops to מֵאַחֲרֵיהֶםfrom following them וַיִּהְיוּwas לִפְנֵיin front יְהוּדָהJudah וְהַמַּאְרָבthe ambush מֵאַחֲרֵיהֶםwas behind them

Now Jeroboam had sent troops around to ambush from the rear, so that while he was in front of Judah, the ambush was behind them.

13:14

וַיִּפְנוּwhen turned יְהוּדָהJudah וְהִנֵּהand saw לָהֶםto them הַמִּלְחָמָהthe battle פָּנִיםbefore וְאָחוֹרand behind וַיִּצְעֲקוּthem they cried out לַיהוָהto the LORD וְהַכֹּהֲנִיםthen the priests מַחְצְרִיםblew בַּחֲצֹצְרוֹתthe trumpets

When Judah turned and discovered that the battle was both before and behind them, they cried out to the LORD. Then the priests blew the trumpets,

13:15

וַיָּרִיעוּraised אִישׁmen יְהוּדָהJudah וַיְהִיand when בְּהָרִיעַthe battle אִישׁraised יְהוּדָהcry וְהָאֱלֹהִיםGod נָגַףrouted אֶת[obj] יָרָבְעָםJeroboam וְכָלand all יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael לִפְנֵיbefore אֲבִיָּהAbijah וִיהוּדָהJudah

and the men of Judah raised the battle cry. And when they raised the cry, God routed Jeroboam and all Israel before Abijah and Judah.

13:16

וַיָּנוּסוּfled בְנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites מִפְּנֵיbefore יְהוּדָהJudah וַיִּתְּנֵםand delivered them אֱלֹהִיםGod בְּיָדָםinto their hands

So the Israelites fled before Judah, and God delivered them into their hands.

13:17

וַיַּכּוּstruck בָהֶםthem with אֲבִיָּהAbijah וְעַמּוֹand his people מַכָּהblow רַבָּהmighty וַיִּפְּלוּfell חֲלָלִיםslain מִיִּשְׂרָאֵלof Israel חֲמֵשׁ500 מֵאוֹת500 אֶלֶף000 אִישׁmen בָּחוּרchosen

Then Abijah and his people struck them with a mighty blow, and 500,000 chosen men of Israel fell slain.

13:18

וַיִּכָּנְעוּsubdued בְנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites בָּעֵתat that time הַהִיאthat וַיֶּאֶמְצוּprevailed בְּנֵיmen יְהוּדָהJudah כִּיbecause נִשְׁעֲנוּrelied עַלon יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod אֲבוֹתֵיהֶםtheir fathers

Thus the Israelites were subdued at that time, and the men of Judah prevailed because they relied on the LORD, the God of their fathers.

13:19

וַיִּרְדֹּףpursued אֲבִיָּהAbijah אַחֲרֵיafter יָרָבְעָםJeroboam וַיִּלְכֹּדand captured מִמֶּנּוּsome עָרִיםcities אֶת[obj] בֵּיתBethel אֵלBethel וְאֶתand [obj] בְּנוֹתֶיהָher daughters וְאֶתand [obj] יְשָׁנָהJeshanah וְאֶתand [obj] בְּנוֹתֶיהָher daughters וְאֶתand [obj] עֶפְרַיִןEphron וּבְנֹתֶיהָalong their villages

Abijah pursued Jeroboam and captured some cities from him: Bethel, Jeshanah, and Ephron, along with their villages.

13:20

וְלֹאdid not עָצַרrecover כֹּחַpower יָרָבְעָםJeroboam עוֹדagain בִּימֵיduring the days אֲבִיָּהוּAbijah וַיִּגְּפֵהוּstruck him יְהוָהLORD וַיָּמֹתand he died

Jeroboam did not again recover his power during the days of Abijah, and the LORD struck him down and he died.

13:21

וַיִּתְחַזֵּקstrong אֲבִיָּהוּAbijah וַיִּשָּׂאand lifted לוֹto him נָשִׁיםmarried wives אַרְבַּעfourteen עֶשְׂרֵהfourteen וַיּוֹלֶדand became the father עֶשְׂרִיםtwenty וּשְׁנַיִםtwo בָּנִיםsons וְשֵׁשׁand sixteen עֶשְׂרֵהsixteen בָּנוֹתdaughters

But Abijah grew strong, married fourteen wives, and became the father of twenty-two sons and sixteen daughters.

13:22

וְיֶתֶרnow the rest דִּבְרֵיacts of אֲבִיָּהAbijah וּדְרָכָיוalong with his ways וּדְבָרָיוand his words כְּתוּבִיםwritten בְּמִדְרַשׁin the Treatise הַנָּבִיאthe prophet עִדּוֹIddo

Now the rest of the acts of Abijah, along with his ways and his words, are written in the Treatise of the Prophet Iddo.
