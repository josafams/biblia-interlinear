2 Chronicles 19

Jehoshaphat appoints judges

Open in the interlinear reader

19:1

וַיָּשָׁבreturned יְהוֹשָׁפָטJehoshaphat מֶלֶךְking יְהוּדָהJudah אֶלto בֵּיתוֹhis home בְּשָׁלוֹםsafely לִירוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem

When Jehoshaphat king of Judah had returned safely to his home in Jerusalem,

19:2

וַיֵּצֵאwent out אֶלto פָּנָיוconfront him יֵהוּאJehu בֶןson חֲנָנִיHanani הַחֹזֶהthe seer וַיֹּאמֶרand said אֶלto הַמֶּלֶךְking יְהוֹשָׁפָטJehoshaphat הֲלָרָשָׁעthe wicked לַעְזֹרhelp וּלְשֹׂנְאֵיwho hate יְהוָהLORD תֶּאֱהָבlove וּבָזֹאתbecause of this עָלֶיךָupon you קֶּצֶףwrath מִלִּפְנֵיfrom to before יְהוָהLORD

Jehu son of Hanani the seer went out to confront him and said to King Jehoshaphat, “Should you help the wicked and love those who hate the LORD? Because of this, the wrath of the LORD is upon you.

19:3

אֲבָלhowever דְּבָרִיםsome טוֹבִיםgood נִמְצְאוּfound עִמָּךְwith you כִּיfor בִעַרְתָּremoved הָאֲשֵׁרוֹתthe Asherah poles מִןfrom הָאָרֶץthe land וַהֲכִינוֹתָand have set לְבָבְךָyour heart לִדְרֹשׁon seeking הָאֱלֹהִיםGod

However, some good is found in you, for you have removed the Asherah poles from the land and have set your heart on seeking God.”

19:4

וַיֵּשֶׁבlived יְהוֹשָׁפָטJehoshaphat בִּירוּשָׁלִָםin Jerusalem וַיָּשָׁבand once again וַיֵּצֵאhe went בָעָםamong the people מִבְּאֵרfrom Beersheba שֶׁבַעBeersheba עַדto הַרhill country אֶפְרַיִםEphraim וַיְשִׁיבֵםand turned them אֶלto יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod אֲבוֹתֵיהֶםtheir fathers

Jehoshaphat lived in Jerusalem, and once again he went out among the people from Beersheba to the hill country of Ephraim and turned them back to the LORD, the God of their fathers.

19:5

וַיַּעֲמֵדhe appointed שֹׁפְטִיםjudges בָּאָרֶץin the land בְּכָלin all עָרֵיeach יְהוּדָהJudah הַבְּצֻרוֹתthe fortified לְעִירcities וָעִירand city

He appointed judges in the land, in each of the fortified cities of Judah.

19:6

וַיֹּאמֶרthen he said אֶלto הַשֹּׁפְטִיםthe judges רְאוּconsider carefully מָהwhat אַתֶּםyou עֹשִׂיםdo כִּיfor לֹאnot לְאָדָםfor man תִּשְׁפְּטוּjudging כִּיbut לַיהוָהfor the LORD וְעִמָּכֶםwho is with you בִּדְבַרwhen you render מִשְׁפָּטjudgment

Then he said to the judges, “Consider carefully what you do, for you are not judging for man, but for the LORD, who is with you when you render judgment.

19:7

וְעַתָּהand now יְהִיmay פַחַדfear יְהוָהLORD עֲלֵיכֶםupon you שִׁמְרוּcareful וַעֲשׂוּyou do כִּיfor אֵיןno עִםwith יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God עַוְלָהinjustice וּמַשֹּׂאor partiality פָנִיםpartiality וּמִקַּחor bribery שֹׁחַדbribery

And now, may the fear of the LORD be upon you. Be careful what you do, for with the LORD our God there is no injustice or partiality or bribery.”

19:8

וְגַםand also בִּירוּשָׁלִַםin Jerusalem הֶעֱמִידappointed יְהוֹשָׁפָטJehoshaphat מִןsome הַלְוִיִּםthe Levites וְהַכֹּהֲנִיםpriests וּמֵרָאשֵׁיand heads הָאָבוֹתthe families לְיִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelite to לְמִשְׁפַּטjudge יְהוָהLORD וְלָרִיבand to settle disputes וַיָּשֻׁבוּand they lived יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem

Moreover, Jehoshaphat appointed in Jerusalem some of the Levites, priests, and heads of the Israelite families to judge on behalf of the LORD and to settle disputes. And they lived in Jerusalem.

19:9

וַיְצַוhe commanded עֲלֵיהֶםon them לֵאמֹרsaying כֹּהthis תַעֲשׂוּןdo בְּיִרְאַתin the fear יְהוָהLORD בֶּאֱמוּנָהfaithfully וּבְלֵבָבand wholeheartedly שָׁלֵםwholeheartedly

He commanded them, saying, “You must serve faithfully and wholeheartedly in the fear of the LORD.

19:10

וְכָלfor every רִיבdispute אֲשֶׁרthat יָבוֹאcomes עֲלֵיכֶםon you מֵאֲחֵיכֶםfrom your brothers הַיֹּשְׁבִיםwho dwell בְּעָרֵיהֶםin their cities בֵּיןbetween דָּםbloodshed לְדָםor some other בֵּיןbetween תּוֹרָהlaw לְמִצְוָהcommandments לְחֻקִּיםstatutes וּלְמִשְׁפָּטִיםor ordinances וְהִזְהַרְתֶּםwarn אֹתָםthem וְלֹאnot יֶאְשְׁמוּincur guilt לַיהוָהbefore the LORD וְהָיָהcome קֶצֶףwrath עֲלֵיכֶםupon you וְעַלand on אֲחֵיכֶםyour brothers כֹּהthis תַעֲשׂוּןdo וְלֹאnot תֶאְשָׁמוּincur guilt

For every dispute that comes before you from your brothers who dwell in their cities—whether it regards bloodshed or some other violation of law, commandments, statutes, or ordinances—you are to warn them, so that they will not incur guilt before the LORD and wrath will not come upon you and your brothers. Do this, and you will not incur guilt.

19:11

וְהִנֵּהand saw אֲמַרְיָהוּAmariah כֹהֵןpriest הָרֹאשׁthe chief עֲלֵיכֶםover you לְכֹלin all דְּבַרpertains יְהוָהLORD וּזְבַדְיָהוּZebadiah בֶןson יִשְׁמָעֵאלIshmael הַנָּגִידthe ruler לְבֵיתthe house יְהוּדָהJudah לְכֹלin all דְּבַרpertains הַמֶּלֶךְthe king וְשֹׁטְרִיםofficers הַלְוִיִּםthe Levites לִפְנֵיכֶםbefore you חִזְקוּresolutely וַעֲשׂוּact may וִיהִיand be יְהוָהLORD עִםwith הַטּוֹבthe upright

Note that Amariah, the chief priest, will be over you in all that pertains to the LORD, and Zebadiah son of Ishmael, the ruler of the house of Judah, in all that pertains to the king. And the Levites will serve as officers before you. Act resolutely; may the LORD be with the upright!”
