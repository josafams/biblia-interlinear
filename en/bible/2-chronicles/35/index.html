2 Chronicles 35

Josiah's Passover and his death

35:1

וַיַּעַשׂthen celebrated יֹאשִׁיָּהוּJosiah בִירוּשָׁלִַםin Jerusalem פֶּסַחPassover לַיהוָהto the LORD וַיִּשְׁחֲטוּslaughtered הַפֶּסַחthe Passover lamb בְּאַרְבָּעָהon the fourteenth עָשָׂרfourteenth לַחֹדֶשׁmonth הָרִאשׁוֹןthe first

Then Josiah celebrated the Passover to the LORD in Jerusalem, and the Passover lamb was slaughtered on the fourteenth day of the first month.

He appointed the priests to their duties and encouraged them in the service of the house of the LORD.

To the Levites who taught all Israel and were holy to the LORD, Josiah said: “Put the holy ark in the temple built by Solomon son of David king of Israel. It is not to be carried around on your shoulders. Now serve the LORD your God and His people Israel.

35:4

וְהָכִונוּprepare לְבֵיתto house אֲבוֹתֵיכֶםfamilies your כְּמַחְלְקוֹתֵיכֶםdivisions according בִּכְתָבto the instructions דָּוִידDavid מֶלֶךְking יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וּבְמִכְתַּבwritten by שְׁלֹמֹהSolomon בְנוֹhis son

Prepare yourselves by families in your divisions, according to the instructions written by David king of Israel and Solomon his son.

35:5

וְעִמְדוּmoreover stand בַקֹּדֶשׁin the holy לִפְלֻגּוֹתby the divisions בֵּיתhouse הָאָבוֹתthe families לַאֲחֵיכֶםof your kinsmen בְּנֵיsons הָעָםpeople וַחֲלֻקַּתdivisions בֵּיתhouse אָבfamilies לַלְוִיִּםof the Levites

Moreover, stand in the Holy Place by the divisions of the families of your kinsmen the lay people, and by the divisions of the families of the Levites.

35:6

וְשַׁחֲטוּslaughter הַפָּסַחthe Passover lambs וְהִתְקַדְּשׁוּconsecrate וְהָכִינוּand make preparations לַאֲחֵיכֶםfor your fellow countrymen לַעֲשׂוֹתto carry כִּדְבַרword יְהוָהLORD בְּיַדin hand מֹשֶׁהMoses

Slaughter the Passover lambs, consecrate yourselves, and make preparations for your fellow countrymen to carry out the word of the LORD given by Moses.”

35:7

וַיָּרֶםand herds contributed יֹאשִׁיָּהוּJosiah לִבְנֵיto sons הָעָםhis own צֹאןflocks כְּבָשִׂיםlambs וּבְנֵיand sons עִזִּיםgoats הַכֹּלthe all לַפְּסָחִיםfor the Passover לְכָלofferings for all הַנִּמְצָאwho were present לְמִסְפַּרplus שְׁלֹשִׁים30 אֶלֶף000 וּבָקָרbulls שְׁלֹשֶׁת3 אֲלָפִים000 אֵלֶּהthese מֵרְכוּשׁfrom possessions הַמֶּלֶךְthe king

From his own flocks and herds Josiah contributed 30,000 lambs and goats plus 3,000 bulls for the Passover offerings for all the people who were present.

35:8

וְשָׂרָיוhis officials also לִנְדָבָהcontributed willingly לָעָםto the people לַכֹּהֲנִיםand priests וְלַלְוִיִּםand Levites הֵרִימוּpresented חִלְקִיָּהHilkiah וּזְכַרְיָהוּZechariah וִיחִיאֵלand Jehiel נְגִידֵיadministrators בֵּיתhouse הָאֱלֹהִיםof God לַכֹּהֲנִיםthe priests נָתְנוּgave לַפְּסָחִיםPassover אַלְפַּיִם2 וְשֵׁשׁ600 מֵאוֹת2 וּבָקָרbulls שְׁלֹשׁ300 מֵאוֹתhundreds

His officials also contributed willingly to the people and priests and Levites. Hilkiah, Zechariah, and Jehiel, the administrators of the house of God, gave the priests 2,600 Passover offerings and 300 bulls.

35:9

וְכָנַנְיָהוּConaniah וּשְׁמַעְיָהוּShemaiah וּנְתַנְאֵלand Nethanel אֶחָיוhis brothers וַחֲשַׁבְיָהוּwell as Hashabiah וִיעִיאֵלJeiel וְיוֹזָבָדand Jozabad שָׂרֵיofficers הַלְוִיִּםthe Levites הֵרִימוּdonated לַלְוִיִּםto the Levites לַפְּסָחִיםPassover חֲמֵשֶׁת5 אֲלָפִים000 offerings וּבָקָרand bulls חֲמֵשׁ500 מֵאוֹת500

Additionally, Conaniah and his brothers Shemaiah and Nethanel, as well as Hashabiah, Jeiel, and Jozabad, officers of the Levites, donated to the Levites 5,000 Passover offerings and 500 bulls.

35:10

וַתִּכּוֹןso הָעֲבוֹדָהthe service וַיַּעַמְדוּstood הַכֹּהֲנִיםthe priests עַלin עָמְדָםtheir places וְהַלְוִיִּםand the Levites עַלin מַחְלְקוֹתָםtheir divisions כְּמִצְוַתcommand הַמֶּלֶךְthe king’s

So the service was prepared; the priests stood in their places and the Levites in their divisions according to the king’s command.

35:11

וַיִּשְׁחֲטוּand they slaughtered הַפָּסַחthe Passover lambs וַיִּזְרְקוּsprinkled הַכֹּהֲנִיםthe priests the מִיָּדָםblood handed to them וְהַלְוִיִּםthe Levites מַפְשִׁיטִיםskinned

And they slaughtered the Passover lambs, while the priests sprinkled the blood handed to them and the Levites skinned the animals.

35:12

וַיָּסִירוּthey set aside הָעֹלָהthe burnt offerings לְתִתָּםto be given לְמִפְלַגּוֹתto the divisions לְבֵיתto house אָבוֹתfamilies לִבְנֵיto sons הָעָםthe people לְהַקְרִיבto offer לַיהוָהto the LORD כַּכָּתוּבas is written בְּסֵפֶרin the Book מֹשֶׁהMoses וְכֵןsame לַבָּקָרthe bulls

They set aside the burnt offerings to be given to the divisions of the families of the people to offer to the LORD, as is written in the Book of Moses; and they did the same with the bulls.

35:13

וַיְבַשְּׁלוּthey roasted הַפֶּסַחthe Passover בָּאֵשׁon the fire כַּמִּשְׁפָּטaccording to the regulation וְהַקֳּדָשִׁיםthe other holy offerings בִּשְּׁלוּboiled בַּסִּירוֹתin pots וּבַדְּוָדִיםkettles וּבַצֵּלָחוֹתand bowls וַיָּרִיצוּand quickly לְכָלthem to all בְּנֵיsons הָעָםpeople

They roasted the Passover animals on the fire according to the regulation, and they boiled the other holy offerings in pots, kettles, and bowls and quickly brought them to all the people.

35:14

וְאַחַרand after הֵכִינוּpreparations לָהֶםfor themselves וְלַכֹּהֲנִיםand for the priests כִּיfor הַכֹּהֲנִיםthe priests בְּנֵיdescendants אַהֲרֹןAaron בְּהַעֲלוֹתup הָעוֹלָהburnt offerings וְהַחֲלָבִיםand fat עַדuntil לָיְלָהnightfall וְהַלְוִיִּםso the Levites הֵכִינוּpreparations לָהֶםfor themselves וְלַכֹּהֲנִיםand for the priests בְּנֵיdescendants אַהֲרֹןAaron

Afterward, they made preparations for themselves and for the priests, since the priests, the descendants of Aaron, were offering up burnt offerings and fat until nightfall. So the Levites made preparations for themselves and for the priests, the descendants of Aaron.

35:15

וְהַמְשֹׁרֲרִיםthe singers בְּנֵיdescendants אָסָףAsaph עַלon מַעֲמָדָםtheir stations כְּמִצְוַתcommand דָּוִידDavid וְאָסָףAsaph וְהֵימָןHeman וִידֻתוּןJeduthun חוֹזֵהseer הַמֶּלֶךְthe king’s וְהַשֹּׁעֲרִיםand the gatekeepers לְשַׁעַרto gate וָשָׁעַרgate did אֵיןnot לָהֶםto them לָסוּרto leave מֵעַלfrom the עֲבֹדָתָםtheir position כִּיbecause אֲחֵיהֶםtheir fellow הַלְוִיִּםLevites הֵכִינוּpreparations לָהֶםfor them

The singers, the descendants of Asaph, were at their stations according to the command of David, Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun the king’s seer. And the gatekeepers at each gate did not need to leave their position, because their fellow Levites made preparations for them.

35:16

וַתִּכּוֹןand so כָּלentire עֲבוֹדַתservice יְהוָהLORD בַּיּוֹםon that day הַהוּאwas לַעֲשׂוֹתfor celebrating הַפֶּסַחthe Passover וְהַעֲלוֹתofferings עֹלוֹתoffering burnt עַלon מִזְבַּחaltar יְהוָהLORD כְּמִצְוַתcommand הַמֶּלֶךְof king יֹאשִׁיָּהוּJosiah

So on that day the entire service of the LORD was carried out for celebrating the Passover and offering burnt offerings on the altar of the LORD, according to the command of King Josiah.

35:17

וַיַּעֲשׂוּand did בְנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites הַנִּמְצְאִיםwho were present אֶת[obj] הַפֶּסַחalso observed the Passover בָּעֵתat that time הַהִיאthat וְאֶתand [obj] חַגfeast הַמַּצּוֹתof unleavened bread שִׁבְעַתseven יָמִיםdays

The Israelites who were present also observed the Passover at that time, as well as the Feast of Unleavened Bread for seven days.

35:18

וְלֹאno נַעֲשָׂהsuch פֶסַחPassover כָּמֹהוּlike him בְּיִשְׂרָאֵלin Israel מִימֵיsince the days שְׁמוּאֵלSamuel הַנָּבִיאthe prophet וְכָלnone מַלְכֵיkings יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael לֹאnot עָשׂוּobserved כַּפֶּסַחPassover like the אֲשֶׁרthat עָשָׂהobserved יֹאשִׁיָּהוּJosiah וְהַכֹּהֲנִיםwith the priests וְהַלְוִיִּםthe Levites וְכָלall יְהוּדָהJudah וְיִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael הַנִּמְצָאwho were present וְיוֹשְׁבֵיand the people יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem

No such Passover had been observed in Israel since the days of Samuel the prophet. None of the kings of Israel ever observed a Passover like the one that Josiah observed with the priests, the Levites, all Judah, the Israelites who were present, and the people of Jerusalem.

35:19

בִּשְׁמוֹנֶהin the eighteenth עֶשְׂרֵהeighteenth שָׁנָהyear לְמַלְכוּתof reign יֹאשִׁיָּהוּJosiah’s נַעֲשָׂהdone הַפֶּסַחthis Passover observed הַזֶּהthis

In the eighteenth year of Josiah’s reign, this Passover was observed.

35:20

אַחֲרֵיafter כָלall זֹאתthis אֲשֶׁרhad הֵכִיןset יֹאשִׁיָּהוּJosiah אֶת[obj] הַבַּיִתthe temple עָלָהmarched נְכוֹNeco מֶלֶךְking מִצְרַיִםEgypt לְהִלָּחֵםto fight בְּכַרְכְּמִישׁat Carchemish עַלon פְּרָתEuphrates וַיֵּצֵאand went לִקְרָאתוֹto confront him יֹאשִׁיָּהוּJosiah

After all this, when Josiah had set the temple in order, Neco king of Egypt marched up to fight at Carchemish by the Euphrates, and Josiah went out to confront him.

35:21

But Neco sent messengers to him, saying, “What is the issue between you and me, O king of Judah? I have not come against you today, but I am fighting another dynasty. God told me to hurry; so stop opposing God, who is with me, or He will destroy you!”

35:22

וְלֹאand not הֵסֵבturn יֹאשִׁיָּהוּJosiah פָנָיוengage מִמֶּנּוּfrom him כִּיfor לְהִלָּחֵםbattle בּוֹhim in he הִתְחַפֵּשׂdisguised וְלֹאand not שָׁמַעlisten אֶלto דִּבְרֵיwords נְכוֹNeco’s מִפִּיfrom the mouth אֱלֹהִיםGod וַיָּבֹאbut went לְהִלָּחֵםto fight בְּבִקְעַתon the plain מְגִדּוֹMegiddo

Josiah, however, did not turn away from him; instead, in order to engage him in battle, he disguised himself. He did not listen to Neco’s words from the mouth of God, but went to fight him on the Plain of Megiddo.

35:23

וַיֹּרוּthere archers הַיֹּרִיםthe shot לַמֶּלֶךְking יֹאשִׁיָּהוּJosiah וַיֹּאמֶרwho said הַמֶּלֶךְthe king לַעֲבָדָיוto his servants הַעֲבִירוּנִיme away כִּיfor הָחֳלֵיתִיbadly מְאֹדvery

There the archers shot King Josiah, who said to his servants, “Take me away, for I am badly wounded!”

35:24

So his servants took him out of his chariot, put him in his second chariot, and brought him to Jerusalem, where he died. And Josiah was buried in the tomb of his fathers, and all Judah and Jerusalem mourned for him.

35:25

Then Jeremiah lamented over Josiah, and to this day all the choirs of men and women sing laments over Josiah. They established them as a statute for Israel, and indeed they are written in the Book of Laments.

35:26

וְיֶתֶרrest דִּבְרֵיacts of יֹאשִׁיָּהוּJosiah וַחֲסָדָיוloving devotion כַּכָּתוּבis written בְּתוֹרַתin the law יְהוָהLORD

As for the rest of the acts of Josiah, along with his deeds of loving devotion according to what is written in the Law of the LORD—

his acts from beginning to end—they are indeed written in the Book of the Kings of Israel and Judah.
