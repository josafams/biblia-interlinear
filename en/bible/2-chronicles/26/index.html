Uzziah: prosperity, pride and leprosy

Uzziah was sixteen years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem fifty-two years. His mother’s name was Jecoliah; she was from Jerusalem.

And he did what was right in the eyes of the LORD, just as his father Amaziah had done.

וַיְהִיand was לִדְרֹשׁsought אֱלֹהִיםGod בִּימֵיthroughout the days זְכַרְיָהוּZechariah הַמֵּבִיןwho instructed בִּרְאֹתin the fear הָאֱלֹהִיםof God וּבִימֵיand as long דָּרְשׁוֹsought אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD הִצְלִיחוֹhim success הָאֱלֹהִיםGod gave

He sought God throughout the days of Zechariah, who instructed him in the fear of God. And as long as he sought the LORD, God gave him success.

Uzziah went out to wage war against the Philistines, and he tore down the walls of Gath, Jabneh, and Ashdod. Then he built cities near Ashdod and among the Philistines.

26:7

וַיַּעְזְרֵהוּhelped him הָאֱלֹהִיםGod עַלagainst פְּלִשְׁתִּיםPhilistines וְעַלagainst הָעַרְבִיםthe Arabs הַיֹּשְׁבִיםliving בְּגוּרin Gur בָּעַלbaal וְהַמְּעוּנִיםand against the Meunites

God helped him against the Philistines, against the Arabs living in Gur-baal, and against the Meunites.

26:8

וַיִּתְּנוּand gave הָעַמּוֹנִיםThe Ammonites מִנְחָהbrought tribute לְעֻזִּיָּהוּto Uzziah וַיֵּלֶךְand went שְׁמוֹhis fame עַדfar לְבוֹאas the border מִצְרַיִםEgypt כִּיfor הֶחֱזִיקpowerful עַדuntil לְמָעְלָהto upward toward

The Ammonites brought tribute to Uzziah, and his fame spread as far as the border of Egypt, for he had become exceedingly powerful.

26:9

Uzziah built towers in Jerusalem at the Corner Gate, the Valley Gate, and the angle in the wall, and he fortified them.

26:10

וַיִּבֶןhe built מִגְדָּלִיםtowers בַּמִּדְבָּרin the desert וַיַּחְצֹבand dug בֹּרוֹתcisterns רַבִּיםmany כִּיfor מִקְנֶהlivestock רַּבmuch הָיָהhad לוֹto him וּבַשְּׁפֵלָהin the foothills וּבַמִּישׁוֹרand in the plain אִכָּרִיםfarmers וְכֹרְמִיםand vinedressers בֶּהָרִיםin the hill country וּבַכַּרְמֶלand in the fertile כִּיfor אֹהֵבlover אֲדָמָהsoil הָיָהhad

Since he had much livestock in the foothills and in the plain, he built towers in the desert and dug many cisterns. And since he was a lover of the soil, he had farmers and vinedressers in the hill country and in the fertile fields.

Uzziah had an army ready for battle that went out to war by assigned divisions, as recorded by Jeiel the scribe and Maaseiah the officer under the direction of Hananiah, one of the royal officers.

26:12

כֹּלtotal מִסְפַּרnumber רָאשֵׁיleaders הָאָבוֹתfamily לְגִבּוֹרֵיmighty men חָיִלvalor אַלְפַּיִם2 וְשֵׁשׁ600 מֵאוֹת2

The total number of family leaders of the mighty men of valor was 2,600.

26:13

Under their authority was an army of 307,500 trained for war, a powerful force to support the king against his enemies.

26:14

וַיָּכֶןand prepared לָהֶםto them עֻזִּיָּהוּUzziah לְכָלsupplied entire הַצָּבָאthe army מָגִנִּיםshields וּרְמָחִיםspears וְכוֹבָעִיםhelmets וְשִׁרְיֹנוֹתarmor וּקְשָׁתוֹתbows וּלְאַבְנֵיand slingstones קְלָעִיםslingstones

Uzziah supplied the entire army with shields, spears, helmets, armor, bows, and slingstones.

26:15

וַיַּעַשׂmade בִּירוּשָׁלִַםin Jerusalem חִשְּׁבֹנוֹתskillfully designed מַחֲשֶׁבֶתdevices חוֹשֵׁבdesigned לִהְיוֹתto be עַלon הַמִּגְדָּלִיםthe towers וְעַלand on הַפִּנּוֹתcorners לִירוֹאto shoot בַּחִצִּיםarrows וּבָאֲבָנִיםand catapult stones גְּדֹלוֹתlarge וַיֵּצֵאand went שְׁמוֹhis fame עַדfar לְמֵרָחוֹקand wide כִּיfor הִפְלִיאwas לְהֵעָזֵרhelped עַדuntil כִּיthat חָזָקpowerful

And in Jerusalem he made skillfully designed devices to shoot arrows and catapult large stones from the towers and corners. So his fame spread far and wide, for he was helped tremendously so that he became powerful.

26:16

וּכְחֶזְקָתוֹbut when Uzziah grew powerful his גָּבַהּarrogance לִבּוֹarrogance led his עַדto לְהַשְׁחִיתown destruction וַיִּמְעַלhe was unfaithful בַּיהוָהto the LORD אֱלֹהָיוhis God וַיָּבֹאhe entered אֶלto הֵיכַלtemple יְהוָהLORD לְהַקְטִירto burn incense עַלon מִזְבַּחaltar הַקְּטֹרֶתof incense

But when Uzziah grew powerful, his arrogance led to his own destruction. He was unfaithful to the LORD his God, for he entered the temple of the LORD to burn incense on the altar of incense.

26:17

וַיָּבֹאwent אַחֲרָיוafter him עֲזַרְיָהוּAzariah הַכֹּהֵןthe priest וְעִמּוֹalong with כֹּהֲנִיםpriests לַיהוָהof the LORD שְׁמוֹנִיםeighty בְּנֵיsons חָיִלbrave

Then Azariah the priest, along with eighty brave priests of the LORD, went in after him.

26:18

They took their stand against King Uzziah and said, “Uzziah, you have no right to offer incense to the LORD. Only the priests, the descendants of Aaron, are consecrated to burn incense. Leave the sanctuary, for you have acted unfaithfully; you will not receive honor from the LORD God.”

26:19

וַיִּזְעַףand distraught עֻזִּיָּהוּUzziah וּבְיָדוֹin his hand מִקְטֶרֶתcenser לְהַקְטִירto offer incense וּבְזַעְפּוֹbut while he raged עִםagainst הַכֹּהֲנִיםthe priests וְהַצָּרַעַתleprosy זָרְחָהbroke בְמִצְחוֹon his forehead לִפְנֵיpresence הַכֹּהֲנִיםthe priests בְּבֵיתin the house יְהוָהLORD מֵעַלfrom the לְמִזְבַּחaltar הַקְּטֹרֶתof incense

Uzziah, with a censer in his hand to offer incense, was enraged. But while he raged against the priests in their presence in the house of the LORD before the altar of incense, leprosy broke out on his forehead.

26:20

When Azariah the chief priest and all the priests turned to him and saw his leprous forehead, they rushed him out. Indeed, he himself hurried to get out, because the LORD had afflicted him.

26:21

וַיְהִיwas עֻזִּיָּהוּUzziah הַמֶּלֶךְking מְצֹרָעleper עַדuntil יוֹםday מוֹתוֹhis death וַיֵּשֶׁבhe lived בֵּיתhouse הַחָפְשִׁיתisolation מְצֹרָעleprous כִּיfor נִגְזַרcut מִבֵּיתfrom the house יְהוָהLORD וְיוֹתָםJotham בְּנוֹhis son had עַלon בֵּיתpalace הַמֶּלֶךְthe royal שׁוֹפֵטgovern אֶת[obj] עַםpeople הָאָרֶץthe land

So King Uzziah was a leper until the day of his death. He lived in isolation, leprous and cut off from the house of the LORD, while his son Jotham had charge of the royal palace to govern the people of the land.

26:22

וְיֶתֶרrest דִּבְרֵיacts עֻזִּיָּהוּUzziah הָרִאשֹׁנִיםfrom beginning וְהָאֲחֲרֹנִיםto end כָּתַבrecorded יְשַׁעְיָהוּIsaiah בֶןson אָמוֹץAmoz הַנָּבִיאthe prophet

As for the rest of the acts of Uzziah, from beginning to end, they are recorded by the prophet Isaiah son of Amoz.

26:23

And Uzziah rested with his fathers and was buried near them in a field of burial that belonged to the kings; for the people said, “He was a leper.” And his son Jotham reigned in his place.
