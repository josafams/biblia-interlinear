2 Chronicles 2

The agreement with Hiram for the building

Open in the interlinear reader

2:1

וַיֹּאמֶרand said שְׁלֹמֹהSolomon לִבְנוֹתpurposed to build בַּיִתhouse לְשֵׁםfor the name יְהוָהLORD וּבַיִתpalace לְמַלְכוּתוֹroyal for himself

Now Solomon purposed to build a house for the Name of the LORD and a royal palace for himself.

2:2

וַיִּסְפֹּרso he conscripted שְׁלֹמֹהconscripted שִׁבְעִים70 אֶלֶף000 אִישׁman סַבָּלporters וּשְׁמוֹנִים80 אֶלֶף000 אִישׁ80 חֹצֵבstonecutters בָּהָרin the mountains וּמְנַצְּחִיםand 3 עֲלֵיהֶםon them שְׁלֹשֶׁת3 אֲלָפִים3 600 וְשֵׁשׁsupervisors מֵאוֹתhundreds

So he conscripted 70,000 porters, 80,000 stonecutters in the mountains, and 3,600 supervisors.

2:3

וַיִּשְׁלַחthen sent שְׁלֹמֹהSolomon אֶלto חוּרָםHiram מֶלֶךְking צֹרTyre לֵאמֹרdo for כַּאֲשֶׁרas you עָשִׂיתָdid עִםwith דָּוִידDavid אָבִיmy father וַתִּשְׁלַחwhen you sent לוֹto him אֲרָזִיםcedars לִבְנוֹתto build לוֹto him בַיִתhouse לָשֶׁבֶתto live בּוֹin

Then Solomon sent word to Hiram king of Tyre: “Do for me as you did for my father David when you sent him cedars to build himself a house to live in.

2:4

הִנֵּהbehold אֲנִיam בוֹנֶהbuild בַּיִתhouse לְשֵׁםfor the name יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהָיmy God לְהַקְדִּישׁto dedicate לוֹto him לְהַקְטִירfor burning incense לְפָנָיוbefore him קְטֹרֶתfragrant סַמִּיםfragrant וּמַעֲרֶכֶתshowbread תָּמִידcontinuously וְעֹלוֹתburnt offerings לַבֹּקֶרevery morning וְלָעֶרֶבand evening לַשַּׁבָּתוֹתon the Sabbaths וְלֶחֳדָשִׁיםnew Moons וּלְמוֹעֲדֵיand appointed feasts יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God לְעוֹלָםforever זֹאתthis עַלfor יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

Behold, I am about to build a house for the Name of the LORD my God to dedicate to Him for burning fragrant incense before Him, for displaying the showbread continuously, and for making burnt offerings every morning and evening as well as on the Sabbaths, New Moons, and appointed feasts of the LORD our God. This is ordained for Israel forever.

2:5

וְהַבַּיִתthe house אֲשֶׁרthat אֲנִיam בוֹנֶהbuilding גָּדוֹלgreat כִּיfor גָדוֹלgreater אֱלֹהֵינוּour God מִכָּלthan all הָאֱלֹהִיםgods

The house that I am building will be great, for our God is greater than all gods.

2:6

וּמִיbut who יַעֲצָרrule כֹּחַable לִבְנוֹתto build לוֹfor him בַיִתhouse כִּיfor הַשָּׁמַיִםthe heavens וּשְׁמֵיeven highest הַשָּׁמַיִםthe heavens לֹאnot יְכַלְכְּלֻהוּcontain him וּמִיwho then אֲנִיam אֲשֶׁרthat אֶבְנֶהbuild לּוֹa בַיִתhouse כִּיfor אִםif לְהַקְטִירto burn sacrifices לְפָנָיוbefore him

But who is able to build a house for Him, since the heavens, even the highest heavens, cannot contain Him? Who then am I, that I should build a house for Him, except as a place to burn sacrifices before Him?

2:7

וְעַתָּהtherefore שְׁלַחsend לִיto me אִישׁman חָכָםcraftsman skilled לַעֲשׂוֹתwork בַּזָּהָבwith gold וּבַכֶּסֶףand silver וּבַנְּחֹשֶׁתwith bronze וּבַבַּרְזֶלand iron וּבָאַרְגְּוָןand with purple וְכַרְמִילcrimson וּתְכֵלֶתand blue וְיֹדֵעַand knows לְפַתֵּחַyarn פִּתּוּחִיםengraving עִםwith הַחֲכָמִיםcraftsmen אֲשֶׁרthat עִמִּיwith my בִּיהוּדָהin Judah וּבִירוּשָׁלִַםand Jerusalem אֲשֶׁרwhom הֵכִיןprovided דָּוִידDavid אָבִיmy father

Send me, therefore, a craftsman skilled in engraving to work with gold and silver, with bronze and iron, and with purple, crimson, and blue yarn. He will work with my craftsmen in Judah and Jerusalem, whom my father David provided.

2:8

וּשְׁלַחsend לִיto me עֲצֵיalso אֲרָזִיםcedar בְּרוֹשִׁיםcypress וְאַלְגּוּמִּיםand algum מֵהַלְּבָנוֹןfrom Lebanon כִּיfor אֲנִיI יָדַעְתִּיknow אֲשֶׁרthat עֲבָדֶיךָyour servants יוֹדְעִיםskill לִכְרוֹתto cut עֲצֵיtimber לְבָנוֹןLebanon וְהִנֵּהand indeed עֲבָדַיmy servants עִםwith עֲבָדֶיךָyour servants

Send me also cedar, cypress, and algum logs from Lebanon, for I know that your servants have skill to cut timber there. And indeed, my servants will work with yours

2:9

וּלְהָכִיןto prepare לִיfor me עֵצִיםtimber לָרֹבin abundance כִּיbecause הַבַּיִתthe temple אֲשֶׁרthat אֲנִיam בוֹנֶהbuilding גָּדוֹלgreat וְהַפְלֵאand wonderful

to prepare for me timber in abundance, because the temple I am building will be great and wonderful.

2:10

וְהִנֵּהand saw לַחֹטְבִיםthe woodcutters לְכֹרְתֵיto making הָעֵצִיםthe wood נָתַתִּיpay חִטִּיםwheat מַכּוֹתground לַעֲבָדֶיךָyour servants כֹּרִיםcors עֶשְׂרִים20 אֶלֶף000 וּשְׂעֹרִיםbarley כֹּרִיםcors עֶשְׂרִים20 אָלֶף000 וְיַיִןwine and בַּתִּיםbaths עֶשְׂרִים20 אֶלֶף000 וְשֶׁמֶןolive oil בַּתִּיםbaths עֶשְׂרִיםtwenty אָלֶף000

I will pay your servants, the woodcutters, 20,000 cors of ground wheat, 20,000 cors of barley, 20,000 baths of wine, and 20,000 baths of olive oil.”

2:11

וַיֹּאמֶרthen חוּרָםHiram מֶלֶךְking צֹרTyre בִּכְתָבletter in reply וַיִּשְׁלַחreply אֶלto שְׁלֹמֹהSolomon בְּאַהֲבַתloves יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] עַמּוֹhis people he נְתָנְךָset you עֲלֵיהֶםover them מֶלֶךְking

Then Hiram king of Tyre wrote a letter in reply to Solomon: “Because the LORD loves His people, He has set you over them as king.”

2:12

וַיֹּאמֶרand added חוּרָםHiram בָּרוּךְblessed יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael אֲשֶׁרwho עָשָׂהmade אֶת[obj] הַשָּׁמַיִםthe heavens וְאֶתand [obj] הָאָרֶץthe earth אֲשֶׁרhas נָתַןgiven לְדָוִידDavid הַמֶּלֶךְking a בֵּןson חָכָםwise יוֹדֵעַknows שֵׂכֶלinsight וּבִינָהand understanding אֲשֶׁרwho יִבְנֶהbuild בַּיִתtemple לַיהוָהfor the LORD וּבַיִתpalace לְמַלְכוּתוֹroyal for himself

And Hiram added: “Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, who made the heavens and the earth! He has given King David a wise son with insight and understanding, who will build a temple for the LORD and a royal palace for himself.

2:13

וְעַתָּהso now שָׁלַחְתִּיsending אִישׁman חָכָםskillful יוֹדֵעַskillful בִּינָהendowed לְחוּרָםHuram abi אָבִיabi

So now I am sending you Huram-abi, a skillful man endowed with creativity.

2:14

בֶּןson אִשָּׁהwoman מִןfrom בְּנוֹתdaughters דָּןDan וְאָבִיוand his father אִישׁman צֹרִיTyre יוֹדֵעַhe is skilled לַעֲשׂוֹתin work בַּזָּהָבwith gold וּבַכֶּסֶףand silver בַּנְּחֹשֶׁתbronze בַּבַּרְזֶלand iron בָּאֲבָנִיםstone וּבָעֵצִיםand wood בָּאַרְגָּמָןpurple בַּתְּכֵלֶתblue וּבַבּוּץfine linen וּבַכַּרְמִילcrimson yarn וּלְפַתֵּחַand to yarn כָּלevery פִּתּוּחַkind of engraving וְלַחְשֹׁבdesign כָּלany מַחֲשָׁבֶתexecute אֲשֶׁרthat יִנָּתֶןgiven לוֹto him עִםwith חֲכָמֶיךָwork your וְחַכְמֵיcraftsmen and אֲדֹנִיmy lord דָּוִידDavid אָבִיךָyour father

He is the son of a woman from the daughters of Dan, and his father is a man of Tyre. He is skilled in work with gold and silver, bronze and iron, stone and wood, purple, blue, and crimson yarn, and fine linen. He is experienced in every kind of engraving and can execute any design that is given him. He will work with your craftsmen and with those of my lord, your father David.

2:15

וְעַתָּהnow הַחִטִּיםthe wheat וְהַשְּׂעֹרִיםbarley הַשֶּׁמֶןolive oil וְהַיַּיִןand wine אֲשֶׁרthat אָמַרpromised אֲדֹנִיmy lord יִשְׁלַחsend לַעֲבָדָיוto his servants

Now let my lord send to his servants the wheat, barley, olive oil, and wine he promised.

2:16

וַאֲנַחְנוּwe נִכְרֹתcut עֵצִיםlogs מִןfrom הַלְּבָנוֹןLebanon כְּכָלlike all צָרְכֶּךָmany you וּנְבִיאֵםfloat them לְךָto you רַפְסֹדוֹתrafts עַלon יָםsea יָפוֹJoppa וְאַתָּהthen you תַּעֲלֶהup אֹתָםthem יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem

We will cut logs from Lebanon, as many as you need, and we will float them to you as rafts by sea down to Joppa. Then you can take them up to Jerusalem.”

2:17

וַיִּסְפֹּרnumbered שְׁלֹמֹהSolomon כָּלall הָאֲנָשִׁיםthe men הַגֵּירִיםforeign אֲשֶׁרthat בְּאֶרֶץin the land יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael אַחֲרֵיfollowing הַסְּפָרthe census אֲשֶׁרhad סְפָרָםconducted and דָּוִידDavid אָבִיוhis father וַיִּמָּצְאוּthere were found מֵאָהhundred וַחֲמִשִּׁיםto be 153 אֶלֶף600 וּשְׁלֹשֶׁתand three אֲלָפִיםthousand וְשֵׁשׁand six מֵאוֹתhundreds

Solomon numbered all the foreign men in the land of Israel following the census his father David had conducted, and there were found to be 153,600 in all.

2:18

וַיַּעַשׂSolomon made מֵהֶםof them שִׁבְעִים70 אֶלֶף000 סַבָּלporters וּשְׁמֹנִים80 אֶלֶף000 חֹצֵבstonecutters בָּהָרin the mountains וּשְׁלֹשֶׁתand 3 אֲלָפִים600 וְשֵׁשׁsupervisors מֵאוֹתhundreds מְנַצְּחִיםchoirmaster לְהַעֲבִידto serve אֶת[obj] הָעָםthe people

Solomon made 70,000 of them porters, 80,000 stonecutters in the mountains, and 3,600 supervisors.
