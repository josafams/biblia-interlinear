Then Hezekiah said, “Now that you have consecrated yourselves to the LORD, come near and bring sacrifices and thank offerings to the house of the LORD.” So the assembly brought sacrifices and thank offerings, and all whose hearts were willing brought burnt offerings.

29:32

וַיְהִיand was מִסְפַּרnumber הָעֹלָהof burnt offerings אֲשֶׁרthat הֵבִיאוּbrought הַקָּהָלthe assembly בָּקָרbulls שִׁבְעִיםseventy אֵילִיםrams מֵאָהtwo hundred כְּבָשִׂיםlambs מָאתָיִםtwo hundred לְעֹלָהfor a burnt offering לַיהוָהto the LORD כָּלall אֵלֶּהthese

The number of burnt offerings the assembly brought was seventy bulls, a hundred rams, and two hundred lambs; all these were for a burnt offering to the LORD.

29:33

וְהַקֳּדָשִׁיםand the consecrated offerings בָּקָרbulls שֵׁשׁsix מֵאוֹתhundred וְצֹאןsheep שְׁלֹשֶׁתthree אֲלָפִיםthousand

And the consecrated offerings were six hundred bulls and three thousand sheep.

29:34

רַקhowever הַכֹּהֲנִיםpriests הָיוּwere לִמְעָטenough וְלֹאnot יָכְלוּcould לְהַפְשִׁיטto skin אֶת[obj] כָּלall הָעֹלוֹתthe burnt offerings וַיְּחַזְּקוּםhelped them אֲחֵיהֶםtheir brothers הַלְוִיִּםLevite עַדuntil כְּלוֹתfinished הַמְּלָאכָהthe work וְעַדand until יִתְקַדְּשׁוּconsecrated הַכֹּהֲנִיםthe priests כִּיfor הַלְוִיִּםthe Levites יִשְׁרֵיhad לֵבָבdiligent לְהִתְקַדֵּשׁin consecrating מֵהַכֹּהֲנִיםthan the priests

However, since there were not enough priests to skin all the burnt offerings, their Levite brothers helped them until the work was finished and until the priests had been consecrated. For the Levites had been more diligent in consecrating themselves than the priests had been.

29:35

וְגַםand also עֹלָהburnt offerings לָרֹבabundant along בְּחֶלְבֵיwith the fat הַשְּׁלָמִיםthe peace offerings וּבַנְּסָכִיםand the drink offerings לָעֹלָהfor the burnt offerings וַתִּכּוֹןso עֲבוֹדַתservice בֵּיתhouse יְהוָהLORD

Furthermore, the burnt offerings were abundant, along with the fat of the peace offerings and the drink offerings for the burnt offerings. So the service of the house of the LORD was established.

29:36

וַיִּשְׂמַחrejoiced יְחִזְקִיָּהוּHezekiah וְכָלand all הָעָםthe people עַלon הַהֵכִיןhad prepared הָאֱלֹהִיםGod לָעָםfor the people כִּיbecause בְּפִתְאֹםeverything הָיָהhad הַדָּבָרaccomplished so quickly

Then Hezekiah and all the people rejoiced at what God had prepared for the people, because everything had been accomplished so quickly.
