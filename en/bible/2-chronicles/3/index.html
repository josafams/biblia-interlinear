Building the temple

Then Solomon began to build the house of the LORD in Jerusalem on Mount Moriah, where the LORD had appeared to his father David. This was the place that David had prepared on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.

וַיָּחֶלSolomon began לִבְנוֹתconstruction בַּחֹדֶשׁmonth הַשֵּׁנִיsecond בַּשֵּׁנִיthe second בִּשְׁנַתyear אַרְבַּעfourth לְמַלְכוּתוֹof his reign

Solomon began construction on the second day of the second month in the fourth year of his reign.

3:3

The foundation that Solomon laid for the house of God was sixty cubits long and twenty cubits wide, according to the old standard.

3:4

וְהָאוּלָםthe portico אֲשֶׁרthat עַלon פְּנֵיfront הָאֹרֶךְthe extending עַלon פְּנֵיface רֹחַבwidth הַבַּיִתthe temple אַמּוֹתwas cubits long עֶשְׂרִיםtwenty וְהַגֹּבַהּhigh מֵאָהhundred וְעֶשְׂרִיםand twenty וַיְצַפֵּהוּhe overlaid מִפְּנִימָהthe inside זָהָבgold טָהוֹרpure

The portico at the front, extending across the width of the temple, was twenty cubits long and twenty cubits high. He overlaid the inside with pure gold.

3:5

וְאֵתand [obj] הַבַּיִתthe main הַגָּדוֹלthe great חִפָּהpaneled עֵץtrees בְּרוֹשִׁיםcypress וַיְחַפֵּהוּwhich he overlaid with זָהָבgold טוֹבfine וַיַּעַלand offered עָלָיוon him תִּמֹרִיםpalm trees וְשַׁרְשְׁרוֹתand chains

He paneled the main room with cypress, which he overlaid with fine gold and decorated with palm trees and chains.

3:6

וַיְצַףhe adorned אֶת[obj] הַבַּיִתthe temple אֶבֶןstones יְקָרָהprecious לְתִפְאָרֶתfor beauty וְהַזָּהָבand the gold זְהַבgold פַּרְוָיִםParvaim

He adorned the temple with precious stones for beauty, and its gold was from Parvaim.

3:7

וַיְחַףhe overlaid אֶת[obj] הַבַּיִתits הַקֹּרוֹתbeams הַסִּפִּיםthresholds וְקִירוֹתָיוwalls וְדַלְתוֹתָיוand doors with זָהָבgold וּפִתַּחand he carved כְּרוּבִיםcherubim עַלon הַקִּירוֹתthe walls

He overlaid its beams, thresholds, walls, and doors with gold, and he carved cherubim on the walls.

3:8

וַיַּעַשׂthen he made אֶת[obj] בֵּיתhouse קֹדֶשׁMost holy הַקֳּדָשִׁיםplace אָרְכּוֹits length עַלto פְּנֵיface רֹחַבwidth הַבַּיִתthe temple אַמּוֹתcubits long עֶשְׂרִיםtwenty וְרָחְבּוֹwide אַמּוֹתcubits עֶשְׂרִיםtwenty וַיְחַפֵּהוּand he overlaid the inside זָהָבgold טוֹבfine לְכִכָּרִיםtalents שֵׁשׁsix מֵאוֹתhundred

Then he made the Most Holy Place; its length corresponded to the width of the temple—twenty cubits long and twenty cubits wide. And he overlaid the inside with six hundred talents of fine gold.

3:9

וּמִשְׁקָלweight לְמִסְמְרוֹתnails לִשְׁקָלִיםshekels חֲמִשִּׁיםfifty זָהָבgold וְהָעֲלִיּוֹתalso the upper חִפָּהoverlaid זָהָבgold

The weight of the nails was fifty shekels of gold. He also overlaid the upper area with gold.

3:10

וַיַּעַשׂmade בְּבֵיתin house קֹדֶשׁMost holy הַקֳּדָשִׁיםthe holy כְּרוּבִיםcherubim שְׁנַיִםtwo מַעֲשֵׂהwork צַעֲצֻעִיםsculptured וַיְצַפּוּand he overlaid with אֹתָםthem זָהָבgold

In the Most Holy Place he made two cherubim of sculptured work, and he overlaid them with gold.

3:11

וְכַנְפֵיtotal wingspan הַכְּרוּבִיםthe cherubim אָרְכָּםwas אַמּוֹתwas cubits עֶשְׂרִיםtwenty כְּנַףwing הָאֶחָדthe first לְאַמּוֹתcubits חָמֵשׁfive long מַגַּעַתtouched לְקִירthe wall הַבַּיִתthe temple וְהַכָּנָףand its wing הָאַחֶרֶתother was אַמּוֹתcubits חָמֵשׁfive long מַגִּיעַtouched לִכְנַףthe wing הַכְּרוּבcherub הָאַחֵרthe other

The total wingspan of the cherubim was twenty cubits. One wing of the first cherub was five cubits long and touched the wall of the temple, and its other wing was five cubits long and touched the wing of the other cherub.

3:12

וּכְנַףwing הַכְּרוּבthe second cherub הָאֶחָדalso measured אַמּוֹתmeasured cubits חָמֵשׁfive מַגִּיעַtouched לְקִירthe wall הַבָּיִתthe temple וְהַכָּנָףwhile its wing הָאַחֶרֶתother אַמּוֹתmeasured cubits חָמֵשׁfive דְּבֵקָהtouched לִכְנַףthe wing הַכְּרוּבthe first cherub הָאַחֵרthe another

The wing of the second cherub also measured five cubits and touched the wall of the temple, while its other wing measured five cubits and touched the wing of the first cherub.

3:13

כַּנְפֵיwingspan הַכְּרוּבִיםcherubim הָאֵלֶּהof these פֹּרְשִׂיםspread אַמּוֹתwas cubits עֶשְׂרִיםtwenty וְהֵםthey עֹמְדִיםstood עַלon רַגְלֵיהֶםtheir feet וּפְנֵיהֶםfacing לַבָּיִתthe main room

So the total wingspan of these cherubim was twenty cubits. They stood on their feet, facing the main room.

3:14

וַיַּעַשׂhe made אֶת[obj] הַפָּרֹכֶתthe veil תְּכֵלֶתblue וְאַרְגָּמָןpurple וְכַרְמִילand crimson yarn וּבוּץand fine linen וַיַּעַלwith woven עָלָיוon him כְּרוּבִיםcherubim

He made the veil of blue, purple, and crimson yarn and fine linen, with cherubim woven into it.

3:15

וַיַּעַשׂhe made לִפְנֵיin front הַבַּיִתthe temple עַמּוּדִיםpillars שְׁנַיִםtwo אַמּוֹתhigh שְׁלֹשִׁיםthirty וְחָמֵשׁfive אֹרֶךְcubits וְהַצֶּפֶתwith a capital אֲשֶׁרthat עַלon רֹאשׁוֹtop אַמּוֹתmeasuring cubits חָמֵשׁfive

In front of the temple he made two pillars, which together were thirty-five cubits high, each with a capital on top measuring five cubits.

3:16

וַיַּעַשׂhe made שַׁרְשְׁרוֹתinterwoven chains בַּדְּבִירin the inner sanctuary וַיִּתֵּןand put עַלon רֹאשׁtop הָעַמֻּדִיםthe pillars וַיַּעַשׂhe made רִמּוֹנִיםpomegranates מֵאָהhundred וַיִּתֵּןand fastened בַּשַּׁרְשְׁרוֹתinto the chainwork

He made interwoven chains and put them on top of the pillars. He made a hundred pomegranates and fastened them into the chainwork.

3:17

וַיָּקֶםthen he set אֶת[obj] הָעַמּוּדִיםthe pillars עַלin פְּנֵיfront הַהֵיכָלthe temple אֶחָדone מִיָּמִיןon the south וְאֶחָדand one מֵהַשְּׂמֹאולon the north the pillar וַיִּקְרָאhe שֵׁםnamed הַיְמָנִיthe south יָכִיןJachin וְשֵׁםnamed הַשְּׂמָאלִיthe pillar בֹּעַזBoaz

Then he set up the pillars in front of the temple, one on the south and one on the north. The pillar on the south he named Jachin, and the pillar on the north he named Boaz.
