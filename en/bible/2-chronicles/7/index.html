2 Chronicles 7

Fire from heaven and God's answer

7:1

When Solomon had finished praying, fire came down from heaven and consumed the burnt offering and the sacrifices, and the glory of the LORD filled the temple.

7:2

The priests were unable to enter the house of the LORD, because the glory of the LORD had filled it.

7:3

וְכֹלwhen all בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites רֹאִיםsaw בְּרֶדֶתcoming down הָאֵשׁthe fire וּכְבוֹדand the glory יְהוָהLORD עַלon הַבָּיִתthe temple וַיִּכְרְעוּthey bowed אַפַּיִםfaces אַרְצָהground עַלon הָרִצְפָהthe pavement וַיִּשְׁתַּחֲווּand they worshiped וְהוֹדוֹתand gave thanks לַיהוָהto the LORD כִּיfor טוֹבgood כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

When all the Israelites saw the fire coming down and the glory of the LORD above the temple, they bowed down on the pavement with their faces to the ground, and they worshiped and gave thanks to the LORD: “For He is good; His loving devotion endures forever.”

7:4

וְהַמֶּלֶךְthen the king וְכָלand all הָעָםthe people זֹבְחִיםoffered זֶבַחsacrifices לִפְנֵיbefore יְהוָהLORD

Then the king and all the people offered sacrifices before the LORD.

7:5

וַיִּזְבַּחoffered הַמֶּלֶךְking שְׁלֹמֹהSolomon אֶת[obj] זֶבַחsacrifice הַבָּקָרoxen עֶשְׂרִים120 וּשְׁנַיִם22 אֶלֶף000 וְצֹאןsheep so מֵאָהhundred וְעֶשְׂרִיםand twenty אָלֶף000 וַיַּחְנְכוּdedicated אֶת[obj] בֵּיתhouse הָאֱלֹהִיםof God הַמֶּלֶךְthe king וְכָלand all הָעָםthe people

And King Solomon offered a sacrifice of 22,000 oxen and 120,000 sheep. So the king and all the people dedicated the house of God.

7:6

וְהַכֹּהֲנִיםthe priests עַלon מִשְׁמְרוֹתָםtheir posts עֹמְדִיםstood וְהַלְוִיִּםas did Levites בִּכְלֵיinstruments שִׁירmusical יְהוָהLORD אֲשֶׁרwhich עָשָׂהmade דָּוִידDavid הַמֶּלֶךְking had לְהֹדוֹתgiving thanks לַיהוָהto the LORD כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion בְּהַלֵּלpraise דָּוִידDavid בְּיָדָםin their hand וְהַכֹּהֲנִיםthe priests מַחְצְרִיםsounded נֶגְדָּםbefore them וְכָלand all יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites עֹמְדִיםstanding

The priests stood at their posts, as did the Levites with the musical instruments of the LORD, which King David had made for giving thanks to the LORD and with which David had offered praise, saying, “For His loving devotion endures forever.” Across from the Levites, the priests sounded trumpets, and all the Israelites were standing.

7:7

וַיְקַדֵּשׁthen consecrated שְׁלֹמֹהSolomon אֶת[obj] תּוֹךְmiddle הֶחָצֵרthe courtyard אֲשֶׁרthat לִפְנֵיin front בֵיתhouse יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor עָשָׂהdone שָׁםthere הָעֹלוֹתthe burnt offerings וְאֵתand [obj] חֶלְבֵיfat הַשְּׁלָמִיםthe peace offerings כִּיfor מִזְבַּחaltar הַנְּחֹשֶׁתthe bronze אֲשֶׁרhad עָשָׂהmade שְׁלֹמֹהhe לֹאnot יָכוֹלcould לְהָכִילcontain אֶת[obj] הָעֹלָהthese offerings וְאֶתand [obj] הַמִּנְחָהthe grain offering וְאֶתand [obj] הַחֲלָבִיםthe fat portions

Then Solomon consecrated the middle of the courtyard in front of the house of the LORD, and there he offered the burnt offerings and the fat of the peace offerings, since the bronze altar he had made could not contain all these offerings.

7:8

וַיַּעַשׂkept שְׁלֹמֹהSolomon אֶת[obj] הֶחָגfeast בָּעֵתat that time הַהִיאthat שִׁבְעַתseven יָמִיםdays וְכָלand all יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael עִמּוֹwith him קָהָלassembly גָּדוֹלgreat מְאֹדvery מִלְּבוֹאfrom Brook חֲמָתhamath עַדto נַחַלBrook מִצְרָיִםEgypt

So at that time Solomon and all Israel with him—a very great assembly of people from Lebo-hamath to the Brook of Egypt—kept the feast for seven days.

7:9

וַיַּעֲשׂוּheld בַּיּוֹםon the day הַשְּׁמִינִיeighth עֲצָרֶתsolemn assembly כִּיfor חֲנֻכַּתdedication הַמִּזְבֵּחַthe altar עָשׂוּlasted שִׁבְעַתseven יָמִיםdays וְהֶחָגand the feast שִׁבְעַתseven יָמִיםdays

On the eighth day they held a solemn assembly, for the dedication of the altar had lasted seven days, and the feast seven days more.

7:10

וּבְיוֹםday עֶשְׂרִיםtwenty וּשְׁלֹשָׁהthird לַחֹדֶשׁmonth הַשְּׁבִיעִיthe seventh שִׁלַּחsent אֶת[obj] הָעָםthe people לְאָהֳלֵיהֶםto their homes שְׂמֵחִיםjoyful וְטוֹבֵיand glad לֵבheart עַלfor הַטּוֹבָהthe good אֲשֶׁרthat had עָשָׂהdone יְהוָהLORD לְדָוִידfor David וְלִשְׁלֹמֹהfor Solomon וּלְיִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael עַמּוֹhis people

On the twenty-third day of the seventh month, Solomon sent the people away to their homes, joyful and glad of heart for the good things that the LORD had done for David, for Solomon, and for His people Israel.

7:11

וַיְכַלhad finished שְׁלֹמֹהSolomon אֶת[obj] בֵּיתhouse יְהוָהLORD וְאֶתand [obj] בֵּיתpalace הַמֶּלֶךְthe royal וְאֵתand [obj] כָּלall הַבָּאsuccessfully עַלin לֵבheart שְׁלֹמֹהhis לַעֲשׂוֹתto do בְּבֵיתfor the house יְהוָהLORD וּבְבֵיתוֹand for his palace הִצְלִיחַown

When Solomon had finished the house of the LORD and the royal palace, successfully carrying out all that was in his heart to do for the house of the LORD and for his own palace,

7:12

וַיֵּרָאappeared יְהוָהLORD אֶלto שְׁלֹמֹהhim בַּלָּיְלָהat night וַיֹּאמֶרand said לוֹto him שָׁמַעְתִּיheard אֶת[obj] תְּפִלָּתֶךָyour prayer וּבָחַרְתִּיand have chosen בַּמָּקוֹםthis place הַזֶּהthis לִיfor myself לְבֵיתas a house זָבַחsacrifice

the LORD appeared to him at night and said to him: “I have heard your prayer and have chosen this place for Myself as a house of sacrifice.

7:13

הֵןif אֶעֱצֹרclose הַשָּׁמַיִםthe sky וְלֹאno יִהְיֶהthere מָטָרrain וְהֵןor if אֲצַוֶּהcommand עַלon חָגָבlocust לֶאֱכוֹלto devour הָאָרֶץthe land וְאִםor if אֲשַׁלַּחsend דֶּבֶרplague בְּעַמִּיamong my people

If I close the sky so there is no rain, or if I command the locust to devour the land, or if I send a plague among My people,

7:14

וְיִכָּנְעוּhumble עַמִּיmy people אֲשֶׁרwho נִקְרָאcalled שְׁמִיmy name עֲלֵיהֶםon them וְיִתְפַּלְלוּand pray וִיבַקְשׁוּand seek פָנַיmy face וְיָשֻׁבוּand turn מִדַּרְכֵיהֶםfrom their ways הָרָעִיםwicked וַאֲנִיthen I אֶשְׁמַעhear מִןfrom הַשָּׁמַיִםheaven וְאֶסְלַחforgive לְחַטָּאתָםtheir sin וְאֶרְפָּאand heal אֶת[obj] אַרְצָםtheir land

and if My people who are called by My name humble themselves and pray and seek My face and turn from their wicked ways, then I will hear from heaven, forgive their sin, and heal their land.

7:15

עַתָּהnow עֵינַיmy eyes יִהְיוּbe פְתֻחוֹתopen וְאָזְנַיand my ears קַשֻּׁבוֹתattentive לִתְפִלַּתto the prayers הַמָּקוֹםthis place הַזֶּהthis

Now My eyes will be open and My ears attentive to the prayers offered in this place.

7:16

וְעַתָּהnow בָּחַרְתִּיchosen וְהִקְדַּשְׁתִּיand consecrated אֶת[obj] הַבַּיִתthis temple הַזֶּהthis לִהְיוֹתmay be שְׁמִיmy name שָׁםthere עַדuntil עוֹלָםforever וְהָיוּand be עֵינַיmy eyes וְלִבִּיand my heart שָׁםthere כָּלall הַיָּמִיםtime

For I have now chosen and consecrated this temple so that My Name may be there forever. My eyes and My heart will be there for all time.

7:17

וְאַתָּהyou אִםif תֵּלֵךְwalk לְפָנַיbefore me כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had הָלַךְwalked דָּוִידDavid אָבִיךָyour father וְלַעֲשׂוֹתdoing כְּכֹלall אֲשֶׁרhave צִוִּיתִיךָcommanded you וְחֻקַּיmy statutes וּמִשְׁפָּטַיand ordinances תִּשְׁמוֹרkeep

And as for you, if you walk before Me as your father David walked, doing all I have commanded you, and if you keep My statutes and ordinances,

7:18

וַהֲקִימוֹתִיwill establish אֵת[obj] כִּסֵּאthrone מַלְכוּתֶךָyour royal כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had כָּרַתִּיcovenanted with לְדָוִידDavid אָבִיךָyour father לֵאמֹרwhen I said לֹאnever יִכָּרֵתfail לְךָto have אִישׁman מוֹשֵׁלrule בְּיִשְׂרָאֵלover Israel

then I will establish your royal throne, as I covenanted with your father David when I said, ‘You will never fail to have a man to rule over Israel.’

7:19

וְאִםbut if תְּשׁוּבוּןturn אַתֶּםyou וַעֲזַבְתֶּםand forsake חֻקּוֹתַיstatutes וּמִצְוֺתַיand commandments I אֲשֶׁרhave נָתַתִּיset לִפְנֵיכֶםbefore you וַהֲלַכְתֶּםgo וַעֲבַדְתֶּםto serve אֱלֹהִיםother אֲחֵרִיםgods וְהִשְׁתַּחֲוִיתֶםand worship לָהֶםto them

But if you turn away and forsake the statutes and commandments I have set before you, and if you go off to serve and worship other gods,

7:20

then I will uproot Israel from the soil I have given them, and I will banish from My presence this temple I have sanctified for My Name. I will make it an object of scorn and ridicule among all the peoples.

7:21

And when this temple has become a heap of rubble, all who pass by it will be appalled and say, ‘Why has the LORD done such a thing to this land and to this temple?’

7:22

And others will answer, ‘Because they have forsaken the LORD, the God of their fathers, who brought them out of the land of Egypt, and have embraced other gods, worshiping and serving them—because of this, He has brought all this disaster upon them.’”
