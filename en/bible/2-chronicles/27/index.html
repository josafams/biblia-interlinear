2 Chronicles 27

The reign of Jotham

Open in the interlinear reader

27:1

בֶּןwas עֶשְׂרִיםtwenty וְחָמֵשׁfive שָׁנָהyears old יוֹתָםJotham בְּמָלְכוֹwhen he became king וְשֵׁשׁsixteen עֶשְׂרֵהsixteen שָׁנָהyears מָלַךְreigned בִּירוּשָׁלִָםin Jerusalem וְשֵׁםname אִמּוֹhis mother’s was יְרוּשָׁהJerushah בַּתdaughter צָדוֹקZadok

Jotham was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem sixteen years. His mother’s name was Jerushah daughter of Zadok.

27:2

וַיַּעַשׂand he did הַיָּשָׁרwas right בְּעֵינֵיin the eyes יְהוָהLORD כְּכֹלlike all אֲשֶׁרhad עָשָׂהdone עֻזִּיָּהוּUzziah אָבִיוhis father רַקonly לֹאnot בָאenter אֶלto הֵיכַלtemple יְהוָהLORD וְעוֹדstill הָעָםthe people behaved מַשְׁחִיתִיםcorruptly

And he did what was right in the eyes of the LORD, just as his father Uzziah had done. In addition, he did not enter the temple of the LORD. But the people still behaved corruptly.

27:3

הוּאhe בָּנָהrebuilt אֶת[obj] שַׁעַרgate בֵּיתhouse יְהוָהLORD הָעֶלְיוֹןthe upper וּבְחוֹמַתon the wall הָעֹפֶלthe hill בָּנָהworked לָרֹבof Ophel

Jotham rebuilt the Upper Gate of the house of the LORD, and he worked extensively on the wall at the hill of Ophel.

27:4

וְעָרִיםalso cities בָּנָהbuilt בְּהַרin the hill country יְהוּדָהJudah וּבֶחֳרָשִׁיםin the forests בָּנָהbuilt בִּירָנִיּוֹתfortresses וּמִגְדָּלִיםand towers

He also built cities in the hill country of Judah and fortresses and towers in the forests.

27:5

וְהוּאand he נִלְחַםwaged war עִםagainst מֶלֶךְking בְּנֵיsons עַמּוֹןAmmonites וַיֶּחֱזַקand defeated עֲלֵיהֶםon them וַיִּתְּנוּgave לוֹto him בְנֵיsons עַמּוֹןAmmonites בַּשָּׁנָהthat year הַהִיאa מֵאָהhundred כִּכַּרtalents כֶּסֶףsilver וַעֲשֶׂרֶתten אֲלָפִיםthousand כֹּרִיםcors חִטִּיםwheat וּשְׂעוֹרִיםbarley עֲשֶׂרֶתten אֲלָפִיםthousand זֹאתthis הֵשִׁיבוּpaid לוֹto him בְּנֵיsons עַמּוֹןsame וּבַשָּׁנָהyears הַשֵּׁנִיתsecond וְהַשְּׁלִשִׁיתand third

Jotham waged war against the king of the Ammonites and defeated them, and that year they gave him a hundred talents of silver, ten thousand cors of wheat, and ten thousand cors of barley. They paid him the same in the second and third years.

27:6

וַיִּתְחַזֵּקpowerful יוֹתָםJotham כִּיbecause הֵכִיןordered דְּרָכָיוhis ways לִפְנֵיbefore יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהָיוhis God

So Jotham grew powerful because he ordered his ways before the LORD his God.

27:7

וְיֶתֶרrest דִּבְרֵיwords יוֹתָםJotham וְכָלalong with all מִלְחֲמֹתָיוhis wars וּדְרָכָיוand his ways הִנָּםthey are indeed כְּתוּבִיםwritten עַלin סֵפֶרBook מַלְכֵיkings יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וִיהוּדָהand Judah

As for the rest of the acts of Jotham, along with all his wars and his ways, they are indeed written in the Book of the Kings of Israel and Judah.

27:8

בֶּןwas עֶשְׂרִיםtwenty וְחָמֵשׁfive שָׁנָהyears old הָיָהwhen בְמָלְכוֹhe became king וְשֵׁשׁsixteen עֶשְׂרֵהsixteen שָׁנָהyears מָלַךְreigned בִּירוּשָׁלִָםin Jerusalem

He was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem sixteen years.

27:9

וַיִּשְׁכַּבand rested יוֹתָםJotham עִםwith אֲבֹתָיוhis fathers וַיִּקְבְּרוּand was buried אֹתוֹhim בְּעִירin the city דָּוִידDavid וַיִּמְלֹךְand his אָחָזAhaz בְּנוֹhis son תַּחְתָּיוin place

And Jotham rested with his fathers and was buried in the City of David. And his son Ahaz reigned in his place.
