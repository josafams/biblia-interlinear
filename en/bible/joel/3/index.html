Joel 3

The nations judged in the Valley of Jehoshaphat

“Yes, in those days and at that time, when I restore Judah and Jerusalem from captivity,

3:2

I will gather all the nations and bring them down to the Valley of Jehoshaphat. There I will enter into judgment against them concerning My people, My inheritance, Israel, whom they have scattered among the nations as they divided up My land.

3:3

וְאֶלand to עַמִּיmy people יַדּוּcast גוֹרָלlots וַיִּתְּנוּthey bartered הַיֶּלֶדa boy בַּזּוֹנָהfor a prostitute וְהַיַּלְדָּהand a girl מָכְרוּsold בַיַּיִןfor wine וַיִּשְׁתּוּto drink

They cast lots for My people; they bartered a boy for a prostitute and sold a girl for wine to drink.

3:4

וְגַםand also מָהwhat אַתֶּםyou לִיto me צֹרTyre וְצִידוֹןSidon וְכֹלand all גְּלִילוֹתregions פְּלָשֶׁתPhilistia הַגְּמוּלa recompense אַתֶּםyou מְשַׁלְּמִיםrendering עָלָיagainst me וְאִםif גֹּמְלִיםretaliate אַתֶּםyou עָלַיagainst me קַלswiftly מְהֵרָהspeedily אָשִׁיבreturn גְּמֻלְכֶםyour recompense בְּרֹאשְׁכֶםupon your heads

Now what do you have against Me, O Tyre, Sidon, and all the regions of Philistia? Are you rendering against Me a recompense? If you retaliate against Me, I will swiftly and speedily return your recompense upon your heads.

3:5

אֲשֶׁרthat כַּסְפִּיmy silver וּזְהָבִיand gold לְקַחְתֶּםtook וּמַחֲמַדַּיmy treasures הַטֹּבִיםfinest הֲבֵאתֶםcarried לְהֵיכְלֵיכֶםto your temples

For you took My silver and gold and carried off My finest treasures to your temples.

3:6

וּבְנֵיthe people of יְהוּדָהJudah וּבְנֵיand sons יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem מְכַרְתֶּםsold לִבְנֵיto sons הַיְּוָנִיםGreeks send לְמַעַןto הַרְחִיקָםthem far מֵעַלfrom the גְּבוּלָםtheir homeland

You sold the people of Judah and Jerusalem to the Greeks, to send them far from their homeland.

3:7

הִנְנִיbehold I מְעִירָםrouse them מִןfrom הַמָּקוֹםthe places אֲשֶׁרwhich מְכַרְתֶּםsold אֹתָםthem שָׁמָּהthere toward וַהֲשִׁבֹתִיwill return גְמֻלְכֶםyour recompense בְּרֹאשְׁכֶםupon your heads

Behold, I will rouse them from the places to which you sold them; I will return your recompense upon your heads.

3:8

וּמָכַרְתִּיI will sell אֶת[obj] בְּנֵיכֶםyour sons וְאֶתand [obj] בְּנוֹתֵיכֶםdaughters בְּיַדinto the hands בְּנֵיsons יְהוּדָהJudah וּמְכָרוּםsell them לִשְׁבָאיִםto the Sabeans אֶלto גּוֹיnation רָחוֹקdistant כִּיfor יְהוָהLORD דִּבֵּרspoken

I will sell your sons and daughters into the hands of the people of Judah, and they will sell them to the Sabeans—to a distant nation.” Indeed, the LORD has spoken.

3:9

קִרְאוּproclaim זֹאתthis בַּגּוֹיִםamong the nations קַדְּשׁוּprepare מִלְחָמָהwar הָעִירוּrouse הַגִּבּוֹרִיםthe mighty men יִגְּשׁוּlet יַעֲלוּattack כֹּלall אַנְשֵׁיmen הַמִּלְחָמָהof war

Proclaim this among the nations: “Prepare for war; rouse the mighty men; let all the men of war advance and attack!

3:10

כֹּתּוּbeat אִתֵּיכֶםyour plowshares לַחֲרָבוֹתinto swords וּמַזְמְרֹתֵיכֶםand your pruning hooks לִרְמָחִיםinto spears הַחַלָּשׁthe weak יֹאמַרsay גִּבּוֹרstrong אָנִיam

Beat your plowshares into swords and your pruning hooks into spears. Let the weak say, ‘I am strong!’

3:11

עוּשׁוּquickly וָבֹאוּcome כָלall הַגּוֹיִםnations מִסָּבִיבsurrounding וְנִקְבָּצוּand gather שָׁמָּהthere toward הַנְחַתyourselves יְהוָהLORD גִּבּוֹרֶיךָyour mighty

Come quickly, all you surrounding nations, and gather yourselves. Bring down Your mighty ones, O LORD.

3:12

יֵעוֹרוּroused וְיַעֲלוּand advance הַגּוֹיִםthe nations אֶלto עֵמֶקValley יְהוֹשָׁפָטJehoshaphat כִּיfor שָׁםthere אֵשֵׁבsit לִשְׁפֹּטto judge אֶת[obj] כָּלall הַגּוֹיִםthe nations מִסָּבִיבon every side

Let the nations be roused and advance to the Valley of Jehoshaphat, for there I will sit down to judge all the nations on every side.

3:13

שִׁלְחוּswing מַגָּלsickle כִּיfor בָשַׁלripe קָצִירharvest בֹּאוּcome רְדוּtrample כִּיfor מָלְאָהfull גַּתwinepress הֵשִׁיקוּvats הַיְקָבִיםoverflow כִּיbecause רַבָּהgreat רָעָתָםtheir wickedness

Swing the sickle, for the harvest is ripe. Come, trample the grapes, for the winepress is full; the wine vats overflow because their wickedness is great.

3:14

הֲמוֹנִיםmultitudes הֲמוֹנִיםmultitudes בְּעֵמֶקin the valley הֶחָרוּץof decision כִּיfor קָרוֹבnear יוֹםday יְהוָהLORD בְּעֵמֶקin the valley הֶחָרוּץof decision

Multitudes, multitudes in the valley of decision! For the Day of the LORD is near in the valley of decision.

3:15

שֶׁמֶשׁsun וְיָרֵחַand moon קָדָרוּdark וְכוֹכָבִיםand the stars אָסְפוּno נָגְהָםshine

The sun and moon will grow dark, and the stars will no longer shine.

3:16

וַיהוָהLORD מִצִּיּוֹןfrom Zion יִשְׁאָגroar וּמִירוּשָׁלִַםfrom Jerusalem יִתֵּןraise קוֹלוֹhis voice וְרָעֲשׁוּwill tremble שָׁמַיִםheaven וָאָרֶץand earth וַיהוָהbut the LORD מַחֲסֶהrefuge לְעַמּוֹfor his people וּמָעוֹזstronghold לִבְנֵיfor the people of יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

The LORD will roar from Zion and raise His voice from Jerusalem; heaven and earth will tremble. But the LORD will be a refuge for His people, a stronghold for the people of Israel.

3:17

וִידַעְתֶּםknow כִּיthat אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיכֶםyour God שֹׁכֵןdwells בְּצִיּוֹןin Zion הַרmountain קָדְשִׁיmy holy וְהָיְתָהwill be יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem קֹדֶשׁholy וְזָרִיםforeigners לֹאnever יַעַבְרוּoverrun בָהּto be עוֹדlonger

Then you will know that I am the LORD your God, who dwells in Zion, My holy mountain. Jerusalem will be holy, never again to be overrun by foreigners.

3:18

וְהָיָהand be בַיּוֹםin that day הַהוּאthe will יִטְּפוּdrip הֶהָרִיםmountains עָסִיסsweet wine וְהַגְּבָעוֹתand the hills תֵּלַכְנָהflow חָלָבmilk וְכָלall אֲפִיקֵיstreams יְהוּדָהJudah יֵלְכוּrun מָיִםwater וּמַעְיָןand a spring מִבֵּיתfrom the house יְהוָהLORD יֵצֵאgo וְהִשְׁקָהto water אֶת[obj] נַחַלValley הַשִּׁטִּיםof Acacias

And in that day the mountains will drip with sweet wine, and the hills will flow with milk. All the streams of Judah will run with water, and a spring will flow from the house of the LORD to water the Valley of Acacias.

3:19

מִצְרַיִםEgypt לִשְׁמָמָהbecome desolate תִהְיֶהbecome וֶאֱדוֹםand Edom לְמִדְבַּרa desert שְׁמָמָהwasteland תִּהְיֶהbe מֵחֲמַסviolence בְּנֵיsons יְהוּדָהJudah אֲשֶׁרthat שָׁפְכוּshed דָםblood נָקִיאinnocent בְּאַרְצָםin land they

Egypt will become desolate, and Edom a desert wasteland, because of the violence done to the people of Judah, in whose land they shed innocent blood.

3:20

וִיהוּדָהBut Judah לְעוֹלָםbe forever תֵּשֵׁבinhabited וִירוּשָׁלִַםand Jerusalem לְדוֹרfrom generation וָדוֹרto generation

But Judah will be inhabited forever, and Jerusalem from generation to generation.

3:21

וְנִקֵּיתִיI will avenge דָּמָםtheir blood which לֹאnot נִקֵּיתִיavenged וַיהוָהyet for the LORD שֹׁכֵןdwells בְּצִיּוֹןin Zion

For I will avenge their blood, which I have not yet avenged.” For the LORD dwells in Zion.
