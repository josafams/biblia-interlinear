Joel יוֹאֵל

Joel starts from a concrete calamity, a plague of locusts accompanied by drought, and calls priests and people to fasting and mourning. The catastrophe becomes a sign of the “day of the LORD,” described as the invasion of an army. In response to the people's repentance, God promises to restore the harvests, pour out His Spirit on all people and judge the nations that mistreated Judah.

Structure

  1. The plague and the lament ch. 1

    Locusts and drought devastate the land; the prophet summons everyone to fasting and prayer.

  2. The day of the LORD and God's answer ch. 2

    The alarm in Zion, the call to return with all one's heart, the promise to restore the land and to pour out the Spirit.

  3. The judgment of the nations ch. 3

    The nations are gathered in the Valley of Jehoshaphat, and Judah and Jerusalem are restored.

Chapters

  1. 1 The plague of locusts and the drought
  2. 2 The day of the LORD and the Spirit poured out
  3. 3 The nations judged in the Valley of Jehoshaphat
