2 Samuel 5

Then all the tribes of Israel came to David at Hebron and said, “Here we are, your own flesh and blood.

Even in times past, while Saul was king over us, you were the one who led Israel out and brought them back. And to you the LORD said, ‘You will shepherd My people Israel, and you will be ruler over them.’”

5:3

So all the elders of Israel came to the king at Hebron, where King David made with them a covenant before the LORD. And they anointed him king over Israel.

5:4

David was thirty years old when he became king, and he reigned forty years.

5:5

בְּחֶבְרוֹןin Hebron מָלַךְreigned עַלover יְהוּדָהJudah שֶׁבַעseven שָׁנִיםyears וְשִׁשָּׁהand six חֳדָשִׁיםmonths וּבִירוּשָׁלִַםand in Jerusalem מָלַךְreigned שְׁלֹשִׁיםthirty וְשָׁלֹשׁthree שָׁנָהyears עַלover כָּלall יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וִיהוּדָהand Judah

In Hebron he reigned over Judah seven years and six months, and in Jerusalem he reigned thirty-three years over all Israel and Judah.

5:6

Now the king and his men marched to Jerusalem against the Jebusites who inhabited the land. The Jebusites said to David: “You will never get in here. Even the blind and lame can repel you.” For they thought, “David cannot get in here.”

5:7

וַיִּלְכֹּדNevertheless captured דָּוִדDavid אֵת[obj] מְצֻדַתfortress צִיּוֹןZion הִיאthat is עִירcity דָּוִדDavid

Nevertheless, David captured the fortress of Zion (that is, the City of David).

5:8

On that day he said, “Whoever attacks the Jebusites must use the water shaft to reach the lame and blind who are despised by David.” That is why it is said, “The blind and the lame will never enter the palace.”

5:9

וַיֵּשֶׁבso took דָּוִדDavid בַּמְּצֻדָהin the fortress וַיִּקְרָאand called לָהּto her עִירcity דָּוִדDavid וַיִּבֶןhe built דָּוִדDavid סָבִיבaround מִןfrom הַמִּלּוֹאthe supporting terraces וָבָיְתָהinward

So David took up residence in the fortress and called it the City of David. He built it up all the way around, from the supporting terraces inward.

5:10

וַיֵּלֶךְand went דָּוִדDavid הָלוֹךְbecame greater וְגָדוֹלgreater and greater וַיהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod צְבָאוֹתHosts עִמּוֹwith him

And David became greater and greater, for the LORD God of Hosts was with him.

5:11

וַיִּשְׁלַחsent חִירָםHiram מֶלֶךְking צֹרTyre מַלְאָכִיםenvoys אֶלto דָּוִדDavid וַעֲצֵיand wood אֲרָזִיםcedar וְחָרָשֵׁיcarpenters and עֵץlogs וְחָרָשֵׁיand carpenters אֶבֶןstone קִירstonemasons וַיִּבְנוּand they built בַיִתpalace לְדָוִדfor David

Now Hiram king of Tyre sent envoys to David, along with cedar logs, carpenters, and stonemasons, and they built a palace for David.

5:12

וַיֵּדַעand realized דָּוִדDavid כִּיthat הֱכִינוֹestablished him יְהוָהLORD לְמֶלֶךְas king עַלover יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וְכִיand if נִשֵּׂאexalted מַמְלַכְתּוֹhis kingdom בַּעֲבוּרfor the sake עַמּוֹhis people יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

And David realized that the LORD had established him as king over Israel and had exalted his kingdom for the sake of His people Israel.

5:13

וַיִּקַּחtook דָּוִדDavid עוֹדmore פִּלַגְשִׁיםconcubines וְנָשִׁיםand wives מִירוּשָׁלִַםfrom Jerusalem אַחֲרֵיafter בֹּאוֹhad arrived מֵחֶבְרוֹןfrom Hebron וַיִּוָּלְדּוּborn עוֹדmore לְדָוִדto him בָּנִיםsons וּבָנוֹתand daughters

After he had arrived from Hebron, David took more concubines and wives from Jerusalem, and more sons and daughters were born to him.

5:14

וְאֵלֶּהthese שְׁמוֹתnames הַיִּלֹּדִיםthe children born לוֹto him בִּירוּשָׁלִָםin Jerusalem שַׁמּוּעַShammua וְשׁוֹבָבShobab וְנָתָןNathan וּשְׁלֹמֹהSolomon

These are the names of the children born to him in Jerusalem: Shammua, Shobab, Nathan, Solomon,

5:15

וְיִבְחָרIbhar וֶאֱלִישׁוּעַand Elishua וְנֶפֶגNepheg וְיָפִיעַJaphia

Ibhar, Elishua, Nepheg, Japhia,

5:16

וֶאֱלִישָׁמָעElishama וְאֶלְיָדָעEliada וֶאֱלִיפָלֶטand Eliphelet

Elishama, Eliada, and Eliphelet.

5:17

When the Philistines heard that David had been anointed king over Israel, they all went in search of him; but David learned of this and went down to the stronghold.

5:18

Now the Philistines had come and spread out in the Valley of Rephaim.

So David inquired of the LORD, “Should I go up against the Philistines? Will You deliver them into my hand?” “Go,” replied the LORD, “for I will surely deliver the Philistines into your hand.”

5:20

So David went to Baal-perazim, where he defeated the Philistines and said, “Like a bursting flood, the LORD has burst out against my enemies before me.” So he called that place Baal-perazim.

5:21

וַיַּעַזְבוּPhilistines abandoned שָׁםthere אֶת[obj] עֲצַבֵּיהֶםtheir idols וַיִּשָּׂאֵםcarried them דָּוִדDavid וַאֲנָשָׁיוand his men

There the Philistines abandoned their idols, and David and his men carried them away.

Once again the Philistines came up and spread out in the Valley of Rephaim.

5:23

So David inquired of the LORD, who answered, “Do not march straight up, but circle around behind them and attack them in front of the balsam trees.

5:24

As soon as you hear the sound of marching in the tops of the balsam trees, move quickly, because this will mean that the LORD has marched out before you to strike the camp of the Philistines.”

5:25

וַיַּעַשׂso did דָּוִדDavid כֵּןso כַּאֲשֶׁרhad צִוָּהוּcommanded him יְהוָהLORD וַיַּךְand he struck אֶת[obj] פְּלִשְׁתִּיםPhilistines מִגֶּבַעfrom Gibeon עַדto בֹּאֲךָcame you גָזֶרGezer

So David did as the LORD had commanded him, and he struck down the Philistines all the way from Gibeon to Gezer.
