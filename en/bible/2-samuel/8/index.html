2 Samuel 8

David's victories and officials

Some time later, David defeated the Philistines, subdued them, and took Metheg-ammah from the hand of the Philistines.

8:2

וַיַּךְDavid also defeated אֶת[obj] מוֹאָבMoabites וַיְמַדְּדֵםand measured them בַּחֶבֶלwith a cord הַשְׁכֵּבlie אוֹתָםthem אַרְצָהground וַיְמַדֵּדhe measured שְׁנֵיtwo חֲבָלִיםlengths those לְהָמִיתto be put to death וּמְלֹאlength הַחֶבֶלthose לְהַחֲיוֹתto be spared וַתְּהִיbecame מוֹאָבMoabites לְדָוִדto David לַעֲבָדִיםbecame subject נֹשְׂאֵיbrought מִנְחָהtribute

David also defeated the Moabites, made them lie down on the ground, and measured them off with a cord. He measured off with two lengths those to be put to death, and with one length those to be spared. So the Moabites became subject to David and brought him tribute.

8:3

וַיַּךְalso defeated דָּוִדDavid אֶת[obj] הֲדַדְעֶזֶרHadadezer בֶּןson רְחֹבRehob מֶלֶךְking צוֹבָהZobah בְּלֶכְתּוֹhad marched out לְהָשִׁיבto restore יָדוֹhis along בִּנְהַרEuphrates river

David also defeated Hadadezer son of Rehob, king of Zobah, who had marched out to restore his dominion along the Euphrates River.

8:4

וַיִּלְכֹּדcaptured דָּוִדDavid מִמֶּנּוּfrom him אֶלֶףthousand וּשְׁבַעseven מֵאוֹתthousand פָּרָשִׁיםcharioteers וְעֶשְׂרִיםand twenty אֶלֶףthousand אִישׁman רַגְלִיfoot soldiers וַיְעַקֵּרand he hamstrung דָּוִדDavid אֶת[obj] כָּלall הָרֶכֶבchariots וַיּוֹתֵרhorses מִמֶּנּוּfrom us מֵאָהhundred רָכֶבchariots

David captured from him a thousand chariots, seven thousand charioteers, and twenty thousand foot soldiers, and he hamstrung all the horses except a hundred he kept for the chariots.

8:5

וַתָּבֹאcame אֲרַםArameans דַּמֶּשֶׂקDamascus לַעְזֹרto help לַהֲדַדְעֶזֶרHadadezer מֶלֶךְking צוֹבָהZobah וַיַּךְstruck דָּוִדDavid בַּאֲרָםdown עֶשְׂרִיםtwenty וּשְׁנַיִםtwo אֶלֶףthousand אִישׁmen

When the Arameans of Damascus came to help King Hadadezer of Zobah, David struck down twenty-two thousand men.

8:6

וַיָּשֶׂםthen he placed דָּוִדDavid נְצִבִיםgarrisons בַּאֲרַםin Aram דַּמֶּשֶׂקDamascus וַתְּהִיbecame אֲרָםAram לְדָוִדto David לַעֲבָדִיםsubject נוֹשְׂאֵיbrought מִנְחָהtribute וַיֹּשַׁעvictorious יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] דָּוִדDavid בְּכֹלwherever אֲשֶׁרthat הָלָךְwent

Then he placed garrisons in Aram of Damascus, and the Arameans became subject to David and brought him tribute. So the LORD made David victorious wherever he went.

8:7

וַיִּקַּחand took דָּוִדDavid אֵת[obj] שִׁלְטֵיshields הַזָּהָבthe gold אֲשֶׁרthat הָיוּbelonged אֶלto עַבְדֵיofficers הֲדַדְעָזֶרHadadezer וַיְבִיאֵםand brought them יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem

And David took the gold shields that belonged to the officers of Hadadezer and brought them to Jerusalem.

8:8

וּמִבֶּטַחfrom Betah וּמִבֵּרֹתַיBerothai עָרֵיcities הֲדַדְעָזֶרHadadezer לָקַחtook הַמֶּלֶךְking דָּוִדDavid נְחֹשֶׁתbronze הַרְבֵּהlarge מְאֹדvery

And from Betah and Berothai, cities of Hadadezer, King David took a large amount of bronze.

8:9

וַיִּשְׁמַעheard תֹּעִיToi מֶלֶךְking חֲמָתHamath כִּיthat הִכָּהdefeated דָוִדDavid אֵת[obj] כָּלentire חֵילarmy הֲדַדְעָזֶרHadadezer

When King Toi of Hamath heard that David had defeated the entire army of Hadadezer,

8:10

וַיִּשְׁלַחhe sent תֹּעִיhis אֶת[obj] יוֹרָםJoram בְּנוֹhis son אֶלto הַמֶּלֶךְking דָּוִדDavid לִשְׁאָלto greet לוֹto him לְשָׁלוֹםto peace וּלְבָרֲכוֹand bless him עַלfor אֲשֶׁרwho נִלְחַםfighting בַּהֲדַדְעֶזֶרHadadezer וַיַּכֵּהוּand defeating כִּיfor אִישׁman מִלְחֲמוֹתwar תֹּעִיToi הָיָהwas הֲדַדְעָזֶרHadadezer וּבְיָדוֹand in his hand הָיוּwere כְּלֵיarticles כֶסֶףsilver וּכְלֵיand articles זָהָבgold וּכְלֵיand articles נְחֹשֶׁתbronze

he sent his son Joram to greet King David and bless him for fighting and defeating Hadadezer, who had been at war with Toi. Joram brought with him articles of silver and gold and bronze,

8:11

גַּםand אֹתָםthem הִקְדִּישׁdedicated הַמֶּלֶךְking דָּוִדDavid לַיהוָהto the LORD עִםwith הַכֶּסֶףthe silver וְהַזָּהָבand gold אֲשֶׁרhad הִקְדִּישׁdedicated מִכָּלfrom all הַגּוֹיִםthe nations אֲשֶׁרhad כִּבֵּשׁsubdued

and King David dedicated these to the LORD, along with the silver and gold he had dedicated from all the nations he had subdued—

8:12

מֵאֲרָםfrom Edom וּמִמּוֹאָבand Moab וּמִבְּנֵיand from descendants עַמּוֹןAmmonites וּמִפְּלִשְׁתִּיםand Philistines וּמֵעֲמָלֵקand Amalekites וּמִשְּׁלַלand from the spoil הֲדַדְעֶזֶרHadadezer בֶּןson רְחֹבRehob מֶלֶךְking צוֹבָהZobah

from Edom and Moab, from the Ammonites and Philistines and Amalekites, and from the spoil of Hadadezer son of Rehob, king of Zobah.

8:13

וַיַּעַשׂand made דָּוִדDavid שֵׁםname בְּשֻׁבוֹhimself when he returned מֵהַכּוֹתוֹfrom striking אֶת[obj] אֲרָםEdomites בְּגֵיאin the Valley מֶלַחSalt שְׁמוֹנָהeighteen עָשָׂרeighteen אָלֶףthousand

And David made a name for himself when he returned from striking down eighteen thousand Edomites in the Valley of Salt.

8:14

וַיָּשֶׂםhe placed בֶּאֱדוֹםin Edom נְצִבִיםgarrisons בְּכָלthroughout אֱדוֹםEdom שָׂםmade נְצִבִיםgarrisons וַיְהִיand was כָלall אֱדוֹםEdom עֲבָדִיםsubject לְדָוִדto David וַיּוֹשַׁעvictorious יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] דָּוִדDavid בְּכֹלwherever אֲשֶׁרthat הָלָךְwent

He placed garrisons throughout Edom, and all the Edomites were subject to David. So the LORD made David victorious wherever he went.

8:15

וַיִּמְלֹךְthus reigned דָּוִדDavid עַלover כָּלall יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וַיְהִיand was דָוִדDavid עֹשֶׂהadministered מִשְׁפָּטjustice וּצְדָקָהand righteousness לְכָלfor all עַמּוֹhis people

Thus David reigned over all Israel and administered justice and righteousness for all his people:

8:16

וְיוֹאָבJoab בֶּןson צְרוּיָהZeruiah עַלover הַצָּבָאthe army וִיהוֹשָׁפָטJehoshaphat בֶּןson אֲחִילוּדAhilud מַזְכִּירrecorder

Joab son of Zeruiah was over the army; Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was the recorder;

8:17

וְצָדוֹקZadok בֶּןson אֲחִיטוּבAhitub וַאֲחִימֶלֶךְand Ahimelech בֶּןson אֶבְיָתָרAbiathar כֹּהֲנִיםpriests וּשְׂרָיָהSeraiah סוֹפֵרscribe

Zadok son of Ahitub and Ahimelech son of Abiathar were priests; Seraiah was the scribe;

8:18

וּבְנָיָהוּBenaiah בֶּןson יְהוֹיָדָעJehoiada וְהַכְּרֵתִיwas over the Cherethites וְהַפְּלֵתִיand Pelethites וּבְנֵיand sons דָוִדDavid’s כֹּהֲנִיםpriestly הָיוּwere

Benaiah son of Jehoiada was over the Cherethites and Pelethites; and David’s sons were priestly leaders.
