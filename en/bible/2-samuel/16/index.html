2 Samuel 16

Ziba, Shimei and the counsel of Ahithophel

When David had gone a little beyond the summit, Ziba the servant of Mephibosheth was there to meet him. He had a pair of saddled donkeys loaded with two hundred loaves of bread, a hundred clusters of raisins, a hundred summer fruits, and a skin of wine.

16:2

וַיֹּאמֶרasked הַמֶּלֶךְthe king אֶלto צִיבָאZiba מָהwhy אֵלֶּהthese לָּךְto you וַיֹּאמֶרreplied צִיבָאZiba הַחֲמוֹרִיםthe donkeys לְבֵיתhousehold הַמֶּלֶךְthe king’s לִרְכֹּבto ride וְהַלֶּחֶםthe bread וְהַקַּיִץand summer fruit לֶאֱכוֹלto eat הַנְּעָרִיםthe young וְהַיַּיִןand the wine לִשְׁתּוֹתto refresh הַיָּעֵףwho become exhausted בַּמִּדְבָּרin the wilderness

“Why do you have these?” asked the king. Ziba replied, “The donkeys are for the king’s household to ride, the bread and summer fruit are for the young men to eat, and the wine is to refresh those who become exhausted in the wilderness.”

16:3

וַיֹּאמֶרand said הַמֶּלֶךְthe king וְאַיֵּהwhere בֶּןgrandson אֲדֹנֶיךָyour master’s וַיֹּאמֶרasked צִיבָאZiba אֶלto הַמֶּלֶךְthe king הִנֵּהindeed יוֹשֵׁבstaying בִּירוּשָׁלִַםin Jerusalem כִּיfor אָמַרsaid הַיּוֹםtoday the יָשִׁיבוּrestore לִיto me בֵּיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael אֵת[obj] מַמְלְכוּתkingdom אָבִיmy grandfather

“Where is your master’s grandson?” asked the king. And Ziba answered, “Indeed, he is staying in Jerusalem, for he has said, ‘Today, the house of Israel will restore to me the kingdom of my grandfather.’”

16:4

וַיֹּאמֶרsaid הַמֶּלֶךְthe king לְצִבָאto Ziba הִנֵּהbehold לְךָto you כֹּלall אֲשֶׁרthat לִמְפִיto Mephibosheth בֹשֶׁתMephibosheth וַיֹּאמֶרsaid צִיבָאZiba הִשְׁתַּחֲוֵיתִיbow אֶמְצָאfind חֵןfavor בְּעֵינֶיךָin your eyes אֲדֹנִיmy lord הַמֶּלֶךְthe king

So the king said to Ziba, “All that belongs to Mephibosheth is now yours!” “I humbly bow before you,” said Ziba. “May I find favor in your eyes, my lord the king!”

16:5

וּבָאand return הַמֶּלֶךְas king דָּוִדDavid עַדuntil בַּחוּרִיםBahurim וְהִנֵּהand saw מִשָּׁםa אִישׁman יוֹצֵאout מִמִּשְׁפַּחַתfrom the family בֵּיתhouse שָׁאוּלSaul וּשְׁמוֹhis name שִׁמְעִיShimei בֶןson גֵּרָאGera יֹצֵאout יָצוֹאforth וּמְקַלֵּלcurses

As King David approached Bahurim, a man from the family of the house of Saul was just coming out. His name was Shimei son of Gera, and as he approached, he kept yelling out curses.

16:6

וַיְסַקֵּלhe threw בָּאֲבָנִיםstones at אֶת[obj] דָּוִדDavid וְאֶתand [obj] כָּלall עַבְדֵיservants הַמֶּלֶךְthe king דָּוִדDavid וְכָלand all הָעָםthe troops וְכָלand all הַגִּבֹּרִיםthe mighty men מִימִינוֹon David’s right וּמִשְּׂמֹאלוֹand left

He threw stones at David and at all the servants of the king, though the troops and all the mighty men were on David’s right and left.

16:7

וְכֹהand ever אָמַרsaid שִׁמְעִיShimei בְּקַלְלוֹcurses צֵאout צֵאout אִישׁman הַדָּמִיםof bloodshed וְאִישׁand man הַבְּלִיָּעַלworthless

And as he yelled curses, Shimei said, “Get out, get out, you worthless man of bloodshed!

16:8

הֵשִׁיבback עָלֶיךָyou for יְהוָהLORD כֹּלall דְּמֵיblood בֵיתhouse שָׁאוּלSaul אֲשֶׁרhave מָלַכְתָּreigned תַּחְתָּיוin whose place וַיִּתֵּןdelivered יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] הַמְּלוּכָהthe kingdom בְּיַדinto the hand אַבְשָׁלוֹםAbsalom בְּנֶךָyour son וְהִנְּךָsee you בְּרָעָתֶךָcome to ruin you כִּיbecause אִישׁman דָּמִיםbloodshed אָתָּהyou

The LORD has paid you back for all the blood of the house of Saul, in whose place you have reigned, and the LORD has delivered the kingdom into the hand of your son Absalom. See, you have come to ruin because you are a man of bloodshed!”

16:9

וַיֹּאמֶרsaid אֲבִישַׁיAbishai בֶּןson צְרוּיָהZeruiah אֶלto הַמֶּלֶךְthe king לָמָּהwhy יְקַלֵּלshould הַכֶּלֶבdog הַמֵּתthis dead הַזֶּהthis אֶת[obj] אֲדֹנִיmy lord הַמֶּלֶךְthe king אֶעְבְּרָהgo over נָּאlet וְאָסִירָהand cut אֶת[obj] רֹאשׁוֹhis head

Then Abishai son of Zeruiah said to the king, “Why should this dead dog curse my lord the king? Let me go over and cut off his head!”

16:10

וַיֹּאמֶרreplied הַמֶּלֶךְthe king מַהwhat לִּיto me וְלָכֶםto בְּנֵיsons צְרֻיָהZeruiah כֹּהthis יְקַלֵּלcurses כִּיbecause יְהוָהLORD אָמַרtold לוֹto him קַלֵּלcurse אֶת[obj] דָּוִדDavid וּמִיand can יֹאמַרask מַדּוּעַwhy עָשִׂיתָהdid you do כֵּןthis

But the king replied, “What have I to do with you, O sons of Zeruiah? If he curses me because the LORD told him, ‘Curse David,’ who can ask, ‘Why did you do this?’”

16:11

וַיֹּאמֶרthen said דָּוִדDavid אֶלto אֲבִישַׁיAbishai וְאֶלand to כָּלall עֲבָדָיוhis servants הִנֵּהbehold בְנִיson my אֲשֶׁרthat יָצָאflesh מִמֵּעַיand blood מְבַקֵּשׁseeks אֶת[obj] נַפְשִׁיmy life וְאַףhow much כִּיfor עַתָּהnow בֶּןBenjamite הַיְמִינִיthe Benjamite הַנִּחוּleave לוֹto him וִיקַלֵּלcurse כִּיfor אָמַרtold לוֹto him יְהוָהLORD

Then David said to Abishai and to all his servants, “Behold, my own son, my own flesh and blood, seeks my life. How much more, then, this Benjamite! Leave him alone and let him curse me, for the LORD has told him so.

16:12

אוּלַיperhaps יִרְאֶהsee יְהוָהLORD בְּעֵינִיin my sight וְהֵשִׁיבand repay יְהוָהLORD לִיto me טוֹבָהgood תַּחַתfor קִלְלָתוֹcursing הַיּוֹםtoday הַזֶּהthis

Perhaps the LORD will see my affliction and repay me with good for the cursing I receive today.”

16:13

וַיֵּלֶךְproceeded דָּוִדDavid וַאֲנָשָׁיוand his men בַּדָּרֶךְalong the road וְשִׁמְעִיas Shimei הֹלֵךְwent בְּצֵלַעalong the ridge הָהָרthe hill לְעֻמָּתוֹopposite him הָלוֹךְwent וַיְקַלֵּלhe yelled curses וַיְסַקֵּלthrew בָּאֲבָנִיםstones לְעֻמָּתוֹto his close וְעִפַּרand flung בֶּעָפָרdust at

So David and his men proceeded along the road as Shimei went along the ridge of the hill opposite him. As Shimei went, he yelled curses, threw stones, and flung dust at David.

16:14

וַיָּבֹאand came הַמֶּלֶךְthe king וְכָלand all הָעָםthe people אֲשֶׁרthat אִתּוֹwith him עֲיֵפִיםexhausted וַיִּנָּפֵשׁrefreshed שָׁםthere

Finally, the king and all the people with him arrived, exhausted. And there he refreshed himself.

16:15

וְאַבְשָׁלוֹםThen Absalom וְכָלand all הָעָםthe people אִישׁmen יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael בָּאוּcame יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem וַאֲחִיתֹפֶלand Ahithophel אִתּוֹwith him

Then Absalom and all the men of Israel came to Jerusalem, and Ahithophel was with him.

16:16

וַיְהִיand was כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had בָּאwent חוּשַׁיHushai הָאַרְכִּיthe Archite רֵעֶהfriend דָוִדDavid’s אֶלto אַבְשָׁלוֹםAbsalom וַיֹּאמֶרand said חוּשַׁיHushai אֶלto אַבְשָׁלֹםAbsalom יְחִיlive הַמֶּלֶךְthe king יְחִיlive הַמֶּלֶךְthe king

And David’s friend Hushai the Archite went to Absalom and said to him, “Long live the king! Long live the king!”

16:17

וַיֹּאמֶרreplied אַבְשָׁלוֹםAbsalom אֶלto חוּשַׁיHushai זֶהthis חַסְדְּךָloyalty you show אֶתwith רֵעֶךָyour friend לָמָּהto why לֹאnot הָלַכְתָּgo אֶתwith רֵעֶךָyour friend

“Is this the loyalty you show your friend?” Absalom replied. “Why did you not go with your friend?”

16:18

וַיֹּאמֶרanswered חוּשַׁיHushai אֶלto אַבְשָׁלֹםAbsalom לֹאnot כִּיfor אֲשֶׁרthat בָּחַרchosen יְהוָהLORD וְהָעָםby the people הַזֶּהthis וְכָלand by all אִישׁmen יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael לוֹto him אֶהְיֶהbe וְאִתּוֹand with him אֵשֵׁבremain

“Not at all,” Hushai answered. “For the one chosen by the LORD, by the people, and by all the men of Israel—his I will be, and with him I will remain.

16:19

Furthermore, whom should I serve if not the son? As I served in your father’s presence, so also I will serve in yours.”

16:20

וַיֹּאמֶרthen said אַבְשָׁלוֹםAbsalom אֶלto אֲחִיתֹפֶלAhithophel הָבוּgive לָכֶםto you עֵצָהcounsel מַהwhat should נַּעֲשֶׂהdo

Then Absalom said to Ahithophel, “Give me counsel. What should we do?”

16:21

וַיֹּאמֶרreplied אֲחִיתֹפֶלAhithophel אֶלto אַבְשָׁלֹםAbsalom בּוֹאgo אֶלto פִּלַגְשֵׁיconcubines אָבִיךָyour father’s אֲשֶׁרwhom הִנִּיחַleft לִשְׁמוֹרto keep הַבָּיִתthe palace וְשָׁמַעhears כָּלall יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael כִּיthat נִבְאַשְׁתָּstench אֶתto אָבִיךָyour father וְחָזְקוּstrengthened יְדֵיhands כָּלall אֲשֶׁרwho אִתָּךְwith you

Ahithophel replied, “Sleep with your father’s concubines, whom he has left to keep the palace. When all Israel hears that you have become a stench to your father, then the hands of all who are with you will be strengthened.”

16:22

So they pitched a tent for Absalom on the roof, and he slept with his father’s concubines in the sight of all Israel.

16:23

Now in those days the advice of Ahithophel was like the consultation of the word of God. Such was the regard that both David and Absalom had for Ahithophel’s advice.
