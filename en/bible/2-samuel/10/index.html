2 Samuel 10

War against the Ammonites and Arameans

Open in the interlinear reader

10:1

וַיְהִיsome time אַחֲרֵיlater כֵןthis וַיָּמָתdied מֶלֶךְking בְּנֵיsons עַמּוֹןAmmonites וַיִּמְלֹךְand reigned חָנוּןHanun בְּנוֹson תַּחְתָּיוunder him

Some time later, the king of the Ammonites died and was succeeded by his son Hanun.

10:2

וַיֹּאמֶרand said דָּוִדDavid אֶעֱשֶׂהshow חֶסֶדkindness עִםto חָנוּןHanun בֶּןson נָחָשׁNahash כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had עָשָׂהshowed אָבִיוhis father עִמָּדִיwith me חֶסֶדkindness וַיִּשְׁלַחso sent דָּוִדDavid לְנַחֲמוֹto console Hanun בְּיַדhis עֲבָדָיוservants אֶלto אָבִיוhis father וַיָּבֹאוּarrived עַבְדֵיservants דָוִדDavid אֶרֶץland בְּנֵיsons עַמּוֹןAmmonites

And David said, “I will show kindness to Hanun son of Nahash, just as his father showed kindness to me.” So David sent some of his servants to console Hanun concerning his father. But when they arrived in the land of the Ammonites,

10:3

וַיֹּאמְרוּsaid שָׂרֵיprinces בְנֵיsons עַמּוֹןAmmonites אֶלto חָנוּןHanun אֲדֹנֵיהֶםtheir lord הַמְכַבֵּדjust because דָּוִדDavid אֶת[obj] אָבִיךָyour father בְּעֵינֶיךָin your sight כִּיfor שָׁלַחsent לְךָto you מְנַחֲמִיםcomforters הֲלוֹאnot בַּעֲבוּרis showing חֲקוֹרexplore אֶת[obj] הָעִירthe city וּלְרַגְּלָהּspy it וּלְהָפְכָהּand overthrow it שָׁלַחsent דָּוִדDavid אֶת[obj] עֲבָדָיוhis servants אֵלֶיךָto

the princes of the Ammonites said to Hanun their lord, “Just because David has sent you comforters, do you really believe he is showing respect for your father? Has not David instead sent his servants to explore the city, spy it out, and overthrow it?”

10:4

וַיִּקַּחso took חָנוּןHanun אֶת[obj] עַבְדֵיservants דָוִדDavid’s וַיְגַלַּחshaved אֶת[obj] חֲצִיhalf זְקָנָםeach man’s beard וַיִּכְרֹתcut off אֶת[obj] מַדְוֵיהֶםtheir garments בַּחֵצִיat the hips עַדuntil שְׁתוֹתֵיהֶםhips וַיְשַׁלְּחֵםand sent them

So Hanun took David’s servants, shaved off half of each man’s beard, cut off their garments at the hips, and sent them away.

10:5

וַיַּגִּדוּreported לְדָוִדto David וַיִּשְׁלַחhe sent לִקְרָאתָםto meet men כִּיfor הָיוּhad הָאֲנָשִׁיםthoroughly נִכְלָמִיםhumiliated מְאֹדvery וַיֹּאמֶרtold הַמֶּלֶךְthe king שְׁבוּstay בִירֵחוֹin Jericho עַדuntil יְצַמַּחgrown זְקַנְכֶםyour beards וְשַׁבְתֶּםthen return

When this was reported to David, he sent messengers to meet the men, since they had been thoroughly humiliated. The king told them, “Stay in Jericho until your beards have grown back, and then return.”

10:6

וַיִּרְאוּrealized בְּנֵיsons עַמּוֹןAmmonites כִּיthat נִבְאֲשׁוּstench בְּדָוִדto David וַיִּשְׁלְחוּand sent בְנֵיsons עַמּוֹןAmmonites וַיִּשְׂכְּרוּhired אֶת[obj] אֲרַםAram בֵּיתBeth רְחוֹבrehob וְאֶתand [obj] אֲרַםAramean צוֹבָאTob עֶשְׂרִיםtwenty אֶלֶףthousand רַגְלִיfoot soldiers וְאֶתand [obj] מֶלֶךְking מַעֲכָהMaacah אֶלֶףthousand אִישׁmen וְאִישׁand man טוֹבTob שְׁנֵיםtwelve עָשָׂרtwelve אֶלֶףthousand אִישׁmen

When the Ammonites realized that they had become a stench to David, they hired twenty thousand Aramean foot soldiers from Beth-rehob and Zoba, as well as a thousand men from the king of Maacah and twelve thousand men from Tob.

10:7

וַיִּשְׁמַעhearing דָּוִדDavid וַיִּשְׁלַחsent אֶת[obj] יוֹאָבJoab וְאֵתand [obj] כָּלentire הַצָּבָאthe army הַגִּבֹּרִיםof mighty men

On hearing this, David sent Joab and the entire army of mighty men.

10:8

וַיֵּצְאוּmarched בְּנֵיsons עַמּוֹןAmmonites וַיַּעַרְכוּand arrayed מִלְחָמָהbattle פֶּתַחat the entrance הַשָּׁעַרthe city gate וַאֲרַםwhile Arameans צוֹבָאTob וּרְחוֹבand Rehob וְאִישׁand the men טוֹבTob וּמַעֲכָהand Maacah לְבַדָּםto their alone בַּשָּׂדֶהin the open country

The Ammonites marched out and arrayed themselves for battle at the entrance of the city gate, while the Arameans of Zobah and Rehob and the men of Tob and Maacah were by themselves in the open country.

10:9

וַיַּרְאsaw יוֹאָבJoab כִּיwhen הָיְתָהwas אֵלָיוto him פְּנֵיbefore הַמִּלְחָמָהthe battle מִפָּנִיםfrom face וּמֵאָחוֹרand behind וַיִּבְחַרhim he selected מִכֹּלfrom all בְּחוּרֵיbest יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וַיַּעֲרֹךְand arrayed לִקְרַאתthem against אֲרָםArameans

When Joab saw the battle lines before him and behind him, he selected some of the best men of Israel and arrayed them against the Arameans.

10:10

וְאֵתand [obj] יֶתֶרrest הָעָםthe forces נָתַןplaced בְּיַדunder אַבְשַׁיAbishai אָחִיוhis brother וַיַּעֲרֹךְwho arrayed לִקְרַאתthem against בְּנֵיsons עַמּוֹןAmmonites

And he placed the rest of the forces under the command of his brother Abishai, who arrayed them against the Ammonites.

10:11

וַיֹּאמֶרsaid אִםif תֶּחֱזַקstrong אֲרָםArameans מִמֶּנִּיfrom me וְהָיִתָהcome לִּיto my לִישׁוּעָהrescue וְאִםand if בְּנֵיsons עַמּוֹןAmmonites יֶחֱזְקוּstrong מִמְּךָfrom you וְהָלַכְתִּיcome לְהוֹשִׁיעַrescue לָךְto your

“If the Arameans are too strong for me,” said Joab, “then you will come to my rescue. And if the Ammonites are too strong for you, then I will come to your rescue.

10:12

חֲזַקstrong וְנִתְחַזַּקfight bravely בְּעַדfor עַמֵּנוּour people וּבְעַדand for עָרֵיcities אֱלֹהֵינוּour God וַיהוָהmay the LORD יַעֲשֶׂהdo הַטּוֹבis good בְּעֵינָיוin his sight

Be strong and let us fight bravely for our people and for the cities of our God. May the LORD do what is good in His sight.”

10:13

וַיִּגַּשׁso יוֹאָבJoab וְהָעָםand his troops אֲשֶׁרthat עִמּוֹwith him לַמִּלְחָמָהto fight בַּאֲרָםthe Arameans וַיָּנֻסוּwho fled מִפָּנָיוbefore him

So Joab and his troops advanced to fight the Arameans, who fled before him.

10:14

וּבְנֵיand sons עַמּוֹןAmmonites רָאוּsaw כִּיthat נָסfled אֲרָםArameans וַיָּנֻסוּhad fled they מִפְּנֵיtoo before אֲבִישַׁיAbishai וַיָּבֹאוּand they entered הָעִירthe city וַיָּשָׁבso returned יוֹאָבJoab מֵעַלfrom against בְּנֵיsons עַמּוֹןAmmonites וַיָּבֹאand came יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem

When the Ammonites saw that the Arameans had fled, they too fled before Abishai, and they entered the city. So Joab returned from fighting against the Ammonites and came to Jerusalem.

10:15

וַיַּרְאsaw אֲרָםArameans כִּיthat נִגַּףdefeated לִפְנֵיto before יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וַיֵּאָסְפוּthey regrouped יָחַדtogether

When the Arameans saw that they had been defeated by Israel, they regrouped.

10:16

וַיִּשְׁלַחsent הֲדַדְעֶזֶרHadadezer וַיֹּצֵאto bring אֶת[obj] אֲרָםArameans אֲשֶׁרthat מֵעֵבֶרfrom beyond הַנָּהָרthe Euphrates וַיָּבֹאוּand they came חֵילָםHelam וְשׁוֹבַךְwith Shobach שַׂרcommander צְבָאarmy הֲדַדְעֶזֶרHadadezer’s לִפְנֵיהֶםleading them

Hadadezer sent messengers to bring more Arameans from beyond the Euphrates, and they came to Helam with Shobach the commander of Hadadezer’s army leading them.

10:17

וַיֻּגַּדwas reported לְדָוִדto David וַיֶּאֱסֹףhe gathered אֶת[obj] כָּלall יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וַיַּעֲבֹרcrossed אֶת[obj] הַיַּרְדֵּןthe Jordan וַיָּבֹאand went חֵלָאמָהHelam וַיַּעַרְכוּarrayed אֲרָםArameans לִקְרַאתagainst דָּוִדDavid וַיִּלָּחֲמוּand fought עִמּוֹagainst him

When this was reported to David, he gathered all Israel, crossed the Jordan, and went to Helam. Then the Arameans arrayed themselves against David and fought against him.

10:18

וַיָּנָסfled אֲרָםArameans מִפְּנֵיbefore יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וַיַּהֲרֹגand killed דָּוִדDavid מֵאֲרָםfrom Aram שְׁבַעseven מֵאוֹתhundred רֶכֶבcharioteers וְאַרְבָּעִיםand forty אֶלֶףthousand פָּרָשִׁיםfoot soldiers וְאֵתand [obj] שׁוֹבַךְShobach שַׂרcommander צְבָאוֹtheir army הִכָּהstruck וַיָּמָתwho died שָׁםthere

But the Arameans fled before Israel, and David killed seven hundred charioteers and forty thousand foot soldiers. He also struck down Shobach the commander of their army, who died there.

10:19

וַיִּרְאוּsaw כָלall הַמְּלָכִיםthe kings עַבְדֵיsubject הֲדַדְעֶזֶרHadadezer כִּיthat נִגְּפוּdefeated לִפְנֵיto before יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וַיַּשְׁלִמוּthey made peace אֶתwith יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וַיַּעַבְדוּםand became subject to them וַיִּרְאוּafraid אֲרָםArameans לְהוֹשִׁיעַto help עוֹדlonger אֶת[obj] בְּנֵיsons עַמּוֹןAmmonites

When all the kings who were subject to Hadadezer saw that they had been defeated by Israel, they made peace with Israel and became subject to them. So the Arameans were afraid to help the Ammonites anymore.
