Obadiah 1

The judgment on Edom

This is the vision of Obadiah: This is what the Lord GOD says about Edom— We have heard a message from the LORD; an envoy has been sent among the nations to say, “Rise up, and let us go to battle against her!”—

1:2

הִנֵּהbehold קָטֹןsmall נְתַתִּיךָmake you בַּגּוֹיִםamong the nations בָּזוּיdespised אַתָּהyou מְאֹדvery

“Behold, I will make you small among the nations; you will be deeply despised.

1:3

זְדוֹןpride לִבְּךָyour heart הִשִּׁיאֶךָdeceived you שֹׁכְנִיdwellers בְחַגְוֵיin the clefts סֶּלַעrocks מְרוֹםheights שִׁבְתּוֹhabitation אֹמֵרsay בְּלִבּוֹin your heart מִיwho יוֹרִדֵנִיbring me down אָרֶץground

The pride of your heart has deceived you, O dwellers in the clefts of the rocks whose habitation is the heights, who say in your heart, ‘Who can bring me down to the ground?’

1:4

אִםif תַּגְבִּיהַּsoar כַּנֶּשֶׁרlike the eagle וְאִםand if בֵּיןamong כּוֹכָבִיםstars שִׂיםmake קִנֶּךָyour nest מִשָּׁםfrom there אוֹרִידְךָbring you down נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD

Though you soar like the eagle and make your nest among the stars, even from there I will bring you down,” declares the LORD.

1:5

אִםif גַּנָּבִיםthieves בָּאוּcame לְךָto you אִםif שׁוֹדְדֵיrobbers לַיְלָהnight אֵיךְhow נִדְמֵיתָהruined הֲלוֹאwould they not יִגְנְבוּsteal דַּיָּםonly what they wanted אִםif בֹּצְרִיםgrape gatherers בָּאוּcame לָךְto you הֲלוֹאwould they not יַשְׁאִירוּleave עֹלֵלוֹתgleanings

“If thieves came to you, if robbers by night— oh, how you will be ruined— would they not steal only what they wanted? If grape gatherers came to you, would they not leave some gleanings?

1:6

אֵיךְhow נֶחְפְּשׂוּbut עֵשָׂוEsau נִבְעוּpillaged מַצְפֻּנָיוhis hidden treasures sought

But how Esau will be pillaged, his hidden treasures sought out!

1:7

עַדto הַגְּבוּלthe border שִׁלְּחוּךָsent you כֹּלall אַנְשֵׁיmen בְרִיתֶךָallied with you הִשִּׁיאוּךָdeceive יָכְלוּoverpower לְךָto you אַנְשֵׁיmen שְׁלֹמֶךָpeace לַחְמְךָeat bread will יָשִׂימוּset מָזוֹרtrap תַּחְתֶּיךָfor you אֵיןwithout תְּבוּנָהawareness בּוֹin him

All the men allied with you will drive you to the border; the men at peace with you will deceive and overpower you. Those who eat your bread will set a trap for you without your awareness of it.

1:8

הֲלוֹאI not בַּיּוֹםin that day הַהוּאthat נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD וְהַאֲבַדְתִּיdestroy חֲכָמִיםwise מֵאֱדוֹםof Edom וּתְבוּנָהunderstanding מֵהַרthe mountains עֵשָׂוEsau

In that day, declares the LORD, will I not destroy the wise men of Edom and the men of understanding in the mountains of Esau?

1:9

וְחַתּוּterrified גִבּוֹרֶיךָyour mighty men תֵּימָןTeman לְמַעַןso יִכָּרֶתcut אִישׁeveryone מֵהַרthe mountains עֵשָׂוEsau מִקָּטֶלthe slaughter

Then your mighty men, O Teman, will be terrified, so that everyone in the mountains of Esau will be cut down in the slaughter.

1:10

מֵחֲמַסviolence אָחִיךָyour brother יַעֲקֹבJacob תְּכַסְּךָcovered בוּשָׁהshame וְנִכְרַתָּand cut off לְעוֹלָםforever

Because of the violence against your brother Jacob, you will be covered with shame and cut off forever.

1:11

בְּיוֹםon the day עֲמָדְךָyou stood מִנֶּגֶדfrom distance בְּיוֹםwhile שְׁבוֹתcarried זָרִיםstrangers חֵילוֹhis wealth וְנָכְרִיםand foreigners בָּאוּentered שְׁעָרָיוhis gate וְעַלfor יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem יַדּוּcast גוֹרָלlots גַּםeven אַתָּהyou כְּאַחַדjust like one מֵהֶםof them

On the day you stood aloof while strangers carried off his wealth and foreigners entered his gate and cast lots for Jerusalem, you were just like one of them.

1:12

וְאַלnot תֵּרֶאgloat בְיוֹםin that day אָחִיךָyour brother’s בְּיוֹםday נָכְרוֹmisfortune וְאַלand do not תִּשְׂמַחrejoice לִבְנֵיover the people יְהוּדָהJudah בְּיוֹםin the day אָבְדָםtheir destruction וְאַלand do not תַּגְדֵּלboast פִּיךָproudly בְּיוֹםin the day צָרָהdistress

But you should not gloat in that day, your brother’s day of misfortune, nor rejoice over the people of Judah in the day of their destruction, nor boast proudly in the day of their distress.

1:13

אַלnot תָּבוֹאenter בְשַׁעַרthe gate עַמִּיmy people בְּיוֹםin the day אֵידָםtheir disaster אַלdo not תֵּרֶאgloat גַםalso אַתָּהyou בְּרָעָתוֹover their affliction בְּיוֹםin the day אֵידוֹtheir disaster וְאַלand do not תִּשְׁלַחְנָהloot בְחֵילוֹtheir wealth בְּיוֹםin the day אֵידוֹtheir disaster

You should not enter the gate of My people in the day of their disaster, nor gloat over their affliction in the day of their disaster, nor loot their wealth in the day of their disaster.

1:14

וְאַלand do not תַּעֲמֹדstand עַלon הַפֶּרֶקthe crossroads לְהַכְרִיתto cut off אֶת[obj] פְּלִיטָיוtheir fugitives וְאַלand do not תַּסְגֵּרdeliver שְׂרִידָיוtheir survivors בְּיוֹםin the day צָרָהdistress

Nor should you stand at the crossroads to cut off their fugitives, nor deliver up their survivors in the day of their distress.

1:15

כִּיfor קָרוֹבnear יוֹםday יְהוָהLORD עַלfor כָּלall הַגּוֹיִםthe nations כַּאֲשֶׁרas you have עָשִׂיתָdone יֵעָשֶׂהdone לָּךְto you גְּמֻלְךָyour recompense יָשׁוּבreturn בְּרֹאשֶׁךָupon your own head

For the Day of the LORD is near for all the nations. As you have done, it will be done to you; your recompense will return upon your own head.

1:16

כִּיfor כַּאֲשֶׁרas you שְׁתִיתֶםdrank עַלon הַרmountain קָדְשִׁיmy holy יִשְׁתּוּdrink כָלall הַגּוֹיִםthe nations תָּמִידcontinually וְשָׁתוּthey will drink וְלָעוּand gulp וְהָיוּthey will be כְּלוֹאhad never הָיוּexisted

For as you drank on My holy mountain, so all the nations will drink continually. They will drink and gulp it down; they will be as if they had never existed.

1:17

וּבְהַרbut on Mount צִיּוֹןZion תִּהְיֶהbe פְלֵיטָהdeliverance וְהָיָהbe קֹדֶשׁholy וְיָרְשׁוּpossession בֵּיתhouse יַעֲקֹבJacob אֵת[obj] מוֹרָשֵׁיהֶםreclaim their

But on Mount Zion there will be deliverance, and it will be holy, and the house of Jacob will reclaim their possession.

1:18

וְהָיָהand be בֵיתhouse יַעֲקֹבJacob אֵשׁfire וּבֵיתand the house יוֹסֵףJoseph לֶהָבָהflame וּבֵיתbut the house עֵשָׂוEsau לְקַשׁbe stubble וְדָלְקוּablaze בָהֶםin them וַאֲכָלוּםand consume וְלֹאtherefore no יִהְיֶהremain שָׂרִידsurvivor לְבֵיתfrom the house עֵשָׂוEsau כִּיfor יְהוָהLORD דִּבֵּרspoken

Then the house of Jacob will be a blazing fire, and the house of Joseph a burning flame; but the house of Esau will be stubble— Jacob will set it ablaze and consume it. Therefore no survivor will remain from the house of Esau.” For the LORD has spoken.

1:19

וְיָרְשׁוּwill possess הַנֶּגֶבthe Negev אֶת[obj] הַרmountains עֵשָׂוEsau וְהַשְּׁפֵלָהthe foothills אֶת[obj] פְּלִשְׁתִּיםPhilistines וְיָרְשׁוּthey will occupy אֶת[obj] שְׂדֵהfields אֶפְרַיִםEphraim וְאֵתand [obj] שְׂדֵהland שֹׁמְרוֹןSamaria וּבִנְיָמִןand Benjamin אֶת[obj] הַגִּלְעָדGilead

Those from the Negev will possess the mountains of Esau; those from the foothills will possess the land of the Philistines. They will occupy the fields of Ephraim and Samaria, and Benjamin will possess Gilead.

1:20

וְגָלֻתand the exiles הַחֵלthis host הַזֶּהthis לִבְנֵיto sons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites אֲשֶׁרthat כְּנַעֲנִיםCanaanites עַדfar צָרְפַתZarephath וְגָלֻתand the exiles יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem אֲשֶׁרwho בִּסְפָרַדin Sepharad יִרְשׁוּpossess אֵת[obj] עָרֵיcities הַנֶּגֶבthe Negev

And the exiles of this host of the Israelites will possess the land of the Canaanites as far as Zarephath; and the exiles from Jerusalem who are in Sepharad will possess the cities of the Negev.

1:21

וְעָלוּwill ascend מוֹשִׁעִיםdeliverers בְּהַרMount צִיּוֹןZion לִשְׁפֹּטto rule אֶת[obj] הַרmountains עֵשָׂוEsau וְהָיְתָהbelong לַיהוָהto the LORD הַמְּלוּכָהthe kingdom

The deliverers will ascend Mount Zion to rule over the mountains of Esau. And the kingdom will belong to the LORD.
