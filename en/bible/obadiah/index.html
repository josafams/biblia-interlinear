Obadiah עֹבַדְיָה

Obadiah, the shortest book of the Hebrew Bible, is an oracle against Edom, a neighboring people descended from Esau. Edom is accused of pride and of having taken advantage of the fall of Jerusalem, plundering and handing over fugitives. The prophet announces that the day of the LORD will come upon all the nations and that the kingdom will belong to the LORD.

Structure

  1. The fall of Edom ch. 1

    Verses 1–14: Edom's pride, its announced defeat and the accusation for what it did on the day of Jerusalem's disaster.

  2. The day of the LORD ch. 1

    Verses 15–21: judgment on the nations and the restoration of the house of Jacob on Mount Zion.
