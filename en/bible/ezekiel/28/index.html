Ezekiel 28

The prince of Tyre; Sidon; a promise to Israel

Open in the interlinear reader

And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

28:2

“Son of man, tell the ruler of Tyre that this is what the Lord GOD says: Your heart is proud, and you have said, ‘I am a god; I sit in the seat of gods in the heart of the sea.’ Yet you are a man and not a god, though you have regarded your heart as that of a god.

28:3

הִנֵּהbehold חָכָםwiser אַתָּהyou מִדָּנִיֵּאלthan Daniel כָּלno סָתוּםsecret לֹאno עֲמָמוּךָhidden from you

Behold, you are wiser than Daniel; no secret is hidden from you!

28:4

בְּחָכְמָתְךָby your wisdom וּבִתְבוּנָתְךָand understanding you עָשִׂיתָgained לְּךָto you חָיִלwealth וַתַּעַשׂand amassed זָהָבgold וָכֶסֶףand silver בְּאוֹצְרוֹתֶיךָfor your treasuries

By your wisdom and understanding you have gained your wealth and amassed gold and silver for your treasuries.

28:5

בְּרֹבby great חָכְמָתְךָyour skill בִּרְכֻלָּתְךָin trading you הִרְבִּיתָincreased חֵילֶךָyour wealth וַיִּגְבַּהּproud לְבָבְךָyour heart בְּחֵילֶךָin your power

By your great skill in trading you have increased your wealth, but your heart has grown proud because of it.

28:6

לָכֵןtherefore כֹּהthis אָמַרis what אֲדֹנָיLord GOD יְהוִהsays יַעַןbecause תִּתְּךָdeliver you אֶת[obj] לְבָבְךָyour heart כְּלֵבas the heart אֱלֹהִיםgod

Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Because you regard your heart as the heart of a god,

28:7

לָכֵןto therefore הִנְנִיbehold I מֵבִיאbring עָלֶיךָagainst you זָרִיםforeigners עָרִיצֵיruthless גּוֹיִםnations וְהֵרִיקוּthey will draw חַרְבוֹתָםtheir swords עַלagainst יְפִיbeauty חָכְמָתֶךָyour wisdom וְחִלְּלוּand will defile יִפְעָתֶךָyour splendor

behold, I will bring foreigners against you, the most ruthless of nations. They will draw their swords against the beauty of your wisdom and will defile your splendor.

28:8

לַשַּׁחַתto the pit יוֹרִדוּךָbring you down וָמַתָּהdie מְמוֹתֵיviolent חָלָלviolent death בְּלֵבin the heart יַמִּיםseas

They will bring you down to the Pit, and you will die a violent death in the heart of the seas.

28:9

הֶאָמֹרthe declares תֹּאמַרsay I אֱלֹהִיםgod אָנִיam לִפְנֵיin the presence הֹרְגֶךָslay you וְאַתָּהyou אָדָםman וְלֹאnot אֵלgod בְּיַדin the hands מְחַלְלֶיךָwound you

Will you still say, ‘I am a god,’ in the presence of those who slay you? You will be only a man, not a god, in the hands of those who wound you.

28:10

מוֹתֵיdie עֲרֵלִיםuncircumcised תָּמוּתdeath בְּיַדat the hands זָרִיםforeigners כִּיfor אֲנִיI דִבַּרְתִּיspoken נְאֻםdeclares אֲדֹנָיLord יְהוִהGOD

You will die the death of the uncircumcised at the hands of foreigners. For I have spoken, declares the Lord GOD.”

28:11

וַיְהִיand was דְבַרword יְהוָהLORD אֵלַיto לֵאמֹרto saying

Again the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

28:12

בֶּןson אָדָםman שָׂאtake קִינָהlament עַלfor מֶלֶךְking צוֹרTyre וְאָמַרְתָּand tell לּוֹto him כֹּהthis אָמַרis what אֲדֹנָיLord GOD יְהוִהsays אַתָּהyou חוֹתֵםseal תָּכְנִיתperfection מָלֵאfull חָכְמָהwisdom וּכְלִילand perfect יֹפִיbeauty

“Son of man, take up a lament for the king of Tyre and tell him that this is what the Lord GOD says: ‘You were the seal of perfection, full of wisdom and perfect in beauty.

28:13

בְּעֵדֶןin Eden גַּןgarden אֱלֹהִיםGod הָיִיתָwere כָּלevery אֶבֶןstone יְקָרָהprecious מְסֻכָתֶךָadorned you אֹדֶםruby פִּטְדָהtopaz וְיָהֲלֹםand diamond תַּרְשִׁישׁberyl שֹׁהַםonyx וְיָשְׁפֵהand jasper סַפִּירsapphire נֹפֶךְturquoise וּבָרְקַתand emerald וְזָהָבgold מְלֶאכֶתwere תֻּפֶּיךָsettings וּנְקָבֶיךָmountings בָּךְin בְּיוֹםon the day הִבָּרַאֲךָyour creation כּוֹנָנוּprepared

You were in Eden, the garden of God. Every kind of precious stone adorned you: ruby, topaz, and diamond, beryl, onyx, and jasper, sapphire, turquoise, and emerald. Your mountings and settings were crafted in gold, prepared on the day of your creation.

28:14

אַתְּyou כְּרוּבcherub מִמְשַׁחanointed הַסּוֹכֵךְa guardian וּנְתַתִּיךָand given you בְּהַרmountain קֹדֶשׁholy אֱלֹהִיםGod הָיִיתָwere בְּתוֹךְamong אַבְנֵיstones אֵשׁfiery הִתְהַלָּכְתָּwalked

You were anointed as a guardian cherub, for I had ordained you. You were on the holy mountain of God; you walked among the fiery stones.

28:15

תָּמִיםblameless אַתָּהyou בִּדְרָכֶיךָin your ways מִיּוֹםfrom the day הִבָּרְאָךְyou were created עַדuntil נִמְצָאfound עַוְלָתָהwickedness בָּךְin you

From the day you were created you were blameless in your ways— until wickedness was found in you.

28:16

בְּרֹבby the vastness רְכֻלָּתְךָyour trade you מָלוּfilled תוֹכְךָyour middle חָמָסviolence וַתֶּחֱטָאand you sinned וָאֶחַלֶּלְךָyou in disgrace מֵהַרfrom the mountain אֱלֹהִיםGod וָאַבֶּדְךָand I banished you כְּרוּבcherub הַסֹּכֵךְguardian מִתּוֹךְfrom among אַבְנֵיstones אֵשׁfiery

By the vastness of your trade, you were filled with violence, and you sinned. So I drove you in disgrace from the mountain of God, and I banished you, O guardian cherub, from among the fiery stones.

28:17

גָּבַהּgrew proud לִבְּךָheart בְּיָפְיֶךָbeauty you שִׁחַתָּcorrupted חָכְמָתְךָyour wisdom עַלon יִפְעָתֶךָyour splendor עַלon אֶרֶץearth הִשְׁלַכְתִּיךָcast you לִפְנֵיbefore מְלָכִיםkings נְתַתִּיךָmade you לְרַאֲוָהa spectacle בָךְin you

Your heart grew proud of your beauty; you corrupted your wisdom because of your splendor; so I cast you to the earth; I made you a spectacle before kings.

28:18

מֵרֹבby the multitude עֲוֺנֶיךָyour iniquities בְּעֶוֶלand the dishonesty רְכֻלָּתְךָtrading you חִלַּלְתָּprofaned מִקְדָּשֶׁיךָyour sanctuaries וָאוֹצִאcome אֵשׁfire מִתּוֹכְךָfrom within you הִיאis אֲכָלַתְךָconsumed you וָאֶתֶּנְךָand gave you לְאֵפֶרto ashes עַלon הָאָרֶץthe ground לְעֵינֵיin the eyes כָּלall רֹאֶיךָsaw you

By the multitude of your iniquities and the dishonesty of your trading you have profaned your sanctuaries. So I made fire come from within you, and it consumed you. I reduced you to ashes on the ground in the eyes of all who saw you.

28:19

כָּלall יוֹדְעֶיךָknow you בָּעַמִּיםthe nations שָׁמְמוּappalled עָלֶיךָover you בַּלָּהוֹתhorrible הָיִיתָend וְאֵינְךָno עַדto עוֹלָםmore

All the nations who know you are appalled over you. You have come to a horrible end and will be no more.’”

28:20

וַיְהִיand was דְבַרword יְהוָהLORD אֵלַיto לֵאמֹרto saying

Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

28:21

בֶּןson אָדָםman שִׂיםset פָּנֶיךָyour face אֶלagainst צִידוֹןSidon וְהִנָּבֵאand prophesy עָלֶיהָagainst her

“Son of man, set your face against Sidon and prophesy against her.

28:22

וְאָמַרְתָּdeclare כֹּהthis אָמַרis what אֲדֹנָיLord GOD יְהוִהsays הִנְנִיbehold I am עָלַיִךְagainst you צִידוֹןSidon וְנִכְבַּדְתִּיglorified בְּתוֹכֵךְwithin you וְיָדְעוּthey will know כִּיthat אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD בַּעֲשׂוֹתִיwhen I execute בָהּagainst her שְׁפָטִיםjudgments וְנִקְדַּשְׁתִּיholiness בָהּin her

And you are to declare that this is what the Lord GOD says: ‘Behold, I am against you, O Sidon, and I will be glorified within you. They will know that I am the LORD when I execute judgments against her and demonstrate My holiness through her.

28:23

וְשִׁלַּחְתִּיwill send בָהּagainst her דֶּבֶרplague וָדָםand shed blood בְּחוּצוֹתֶיהָin her streets וְנִפְלַלwill fall חָלָלslain בְּתוֹכָהּwithin her בְּחֶרֶבwhile the sword עָלֶיהָagainst her מִסָּבִיבon every side וְיָדְעוּknow כִּיthat אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD

I will send a plague against her and shed blood in her streets; the slain will fall within her, while the sword is against her on every side. Then they will know that I am the LORD.

28:24

For the people of Israel will no longer face a pricking brier or a painful thorn from all around them who treat them with contempt. Then they will know that I am the Lord GOD.’

28:25

כֹּהthis אָמַרis what אֲדֹנָיLord GOD יְהוִהsays בְּקַבְּצִיwhen I gather אֶת[obj] בֵּיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael מִןfrom הָעַמִּיםthe peoples אֲשֶׁרwhom נָפֹצוּscattered בָםamong וְנִקְדַּשְׁתִּיholy בָםamong them לְעֵינֵיin the sight הַגּוֹיִםthe nations וְיָשְׁבוּdwell עַלin אַדְמָתָםtheir own land אֲשֶׁרwhich נָתַתִּיgiven לְעַבְדִּיto my servant לְיַעֲקֹבJacob

This is what the Lord GOD says: ‘When I gather the house of Israel from the peoples among whom they have been scattered, I will show Myself holy among them in the sight of the nations. Then they will dwell in their own land, which I have given to My servant Jacob.

28:26

וְיָשְׁבוּdwell עָלֶיהָon her לָבֶטַחsecurely וּבָנוּbuild בָתִּיםhouses וְנָטְעוּand plant כְרָמִיםvineyards וְיָשְׁבוּthey will dwell לָבֶטַחsecurely בַּעֲשׂוֹתִיwhen I execute שְׁפָטִיםjudgments בְּכֹלagainst all הַשָּׁאטִיםwho treat אֹתָםthem מִסְּבִיבוֹתָםaround them וְיָדְעוּknow כִּיthat אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיהֶםtheir God

And there they will dwell securely, build houses, and plant vineyards. They will dwell securely when I execute judgments against all those around them who treat them with contempt. Then they will know that I am the LORD their God.’”
