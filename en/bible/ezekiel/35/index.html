Ezekiel 35

Against Mount Seir

Open in the interlinear reader

35:1

וַיְהִיand was דְבַרword יְהוָהLORD אֵלַיto לֵאמֹרto saying

Moreover, the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

35:2

בֶּןson אָדָםman שִׂיםset פָּנֶיךָyour face עַלagainst הַרMount שֵׂעִירSeir וְהִנָּבֵאand prophesy עָלָיוagainst it

“Son of man, set your face against Mount Seir and prophesy against it,

35:3

וְאָמַרְתָּand declare לּוֹto him כֹּהthis אָמַרis what אֲדֹנָיLord GOD יְהוִהsays הִנְנִיbehold I am אֵלֶיךָagainst you הַרMount שֵׂעִירSeir וְנָטִיתִיwill stretch יָדִיmy hand עָלֶיךָagainst you וּנְתַתִּיךָand make you שְׁמָמָהdesolate וּמְשַׁמָּהwaste

and declare that this is what the Lord GOD says: Behold, I am against you, O Mount Seir. I will stretch out My hand against you and make you a desolate waste.

35:4

עָרֶיךָyour cities חָרְבָּהruins אָשִׂיםmake וְאַתָּהand you שְׁמָמָהdesolation תִהְיֶהbecome וְיָדַעְתָּknow כִּיthat אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD

I will turn your cities into ruins, and you will become a desolation. Then you will know that I am the LORD.

35:5

יַעַןbecause הֱיוֹתbe לְךָto you אֵיבַתharbored עוֹלָםancient וַתַּגֵּרand delivered אֶת[obj] בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites עַלover יְדֵיhands חָרֶבsword בְּעֵתin the time אֵידָםtheir disaster בְּעֵתat the final עֲוֺןpunishment קֵץstage

Because you harbored an ancient hatred and delivered the Israelites over to the sword in the time of their disaster at the final stage of their punishment,

35:6

לָכֵןtherefore חַיsurely אָנִיI נְאֻםdeclares אֲדֹנָיLord I יְהוִהGOD כִּיfor לְדָםto blood אֶעֶשְׂךָdo you וְדָםbloodshed and it יִרְדֲּפֶךָpursue you אִםif לֹאnot דָםbloodshed שָׂנֵאתָhate וְדָםit יִרְדֲּפֶךָpursue you

therefore as surely as I live, declares the Lord GOD, I will give you over to bloodshed and it will pursue you. Since you did not hate bloodshed, it will pursue you.

35:7

וְנָתַתִּיwill make אֶת[obj] הַרMount שֵׂעִירSeir לְשִׁמְמָהa desolate וּשְׁמָמָהwaste וְהִכְרַתִּיand will cut off מִמֶּנּוּfrom it עֹבֵרcome go וָשָׁבand go

I will make Mount Seir a desolate waste and will cut off from it those who come and go.

35:8

וּמִלֵּאתִיwill fill אֶת[obj] הָרָיוits mountains חֲלָלָיוslain גִּבְעוֹתֶיךָyour hills וְגֵאוֹתֶיךָyour valleys וְכָלand in all אֲפִיקֶיךָyour ravines חַלְלֵיkilled חֶרֶבsword יִפְּלוּfall בָהֶםon

I will fill its mountains with the slain; those killed by the sword will fall on your hills, in your valleys, and in all your ravines.

35:9

שִׁמְמוֹתdesolation עוֹלָםperpetual אֶתֶּנְךָmake you וְעָרֶיךָand your cities לֹאnot תָשֹׁבְנָהinhabited וִידַעְתֶּםknow כִּיthat אֲנִיI am יְהוָהLORD

I will make you a perpetual desolation, and your cities will not be inhabited. Then you will know that I am the LORD.

35:10

יַעַןbecause אֲמָרְךָyou have said אֶת[obj] שְׁנֵיtwo הַגּוֹיִםthese nations וְאֶתand [obj] שְׁתֵּיtwo הָאֲרָצוֹתcountries לִיto me תִהְיֶינָהbe וִירַשְׁנוּהָpossess them וַיהוָהthough the LORD שָׁםthere הָיָהwas

Because you have said, ‘These two nations and countries will be ours, and we will possess them,’ even though the LORD was there,

35:11

לָכֵןtherefore חַיsurely אָנִיI נְאֻםdeclares אֲדֹנָיLord I יְהוִהGOD וְעָשִׂיתִיwill treat כְּאַפְּךָanger וּכְקִנְאָתְךָand jealousy you אֲשֶׁרthat עָשִׂיתָהshowed מִשִּׂנְאָתֶיךָin your hatred בָּםagainst them וְנוֹדַעְתִּיknown בָםamong them כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had אֶשְׁפְּטֶךָjudge you

therefore as surely as I live, declares the Lord GOD, I will treat you according to the anger and jealousy you showed in your hatred against them, and I will make Myself known among them when I judge you.

35:12

וְיָדַעְתָּknow כִּיthat אֲנִיI יְהוָהLORD שָׁמַעְתִּיheard אֶת[obj] כָּלevery נָאָצוֹתֶיךָcontemptuous word you אֲשֶׁרthat אָמַרְתָּuttered עַלagainst הָרֵיmountains יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael לֵאמֹרsaid שָׁמֵמוּdesolate לָנוּto us נִתְּנוּgiven לְאָכְלָהto devour

Then you will know that I, the LORD, have heard every contemptuous word you uttered against the mountains of Israel when you said, ‘They are desolate; they are given to us to devour!’

35:13

וַתַּגְדִּילוּyou boasted עָלַיagainst me בְּפִיכֶםwith your mouth וְהַעְתַּרְתֶּםand multiplied עָלַיagainst me דִּבְרֵיכֶםyour words אֲנִיI שָׁמָעְתִּיheard

You boasted against Me with your mouth and multiplied your words against Me. I heard it Myself!

35:14

כֹּהthis אָמַרis what אֲדֹנָיLord GOD יְהוִהsays כִּשְׂמֹחַrejoices כָּלwhole הָאָרֶץthe earth שְׁמָמָהdesolate אֶעֱשֶׂהmake לָּךְto you

This is what the Lord GOD says: While the whole earth rejoices, I will make you desolate.

35:15

כְּשִׂמְחָתְךָas you rejoiced לְנַחְלַתwhen the inheritance בֵּיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael עַלon אֲשֶׁרthat שָׁמֵמָהdesolate כֵּןso אֶעֱשֶׂהdo לָּךְto you שְׁמָמָהdesolation תִהְיֶהbecome הַרMount שֵׂעִירSeir וְכָלall אֱדוֹםEdom כֻּלָּהּher all וְיָדְעוּknow כִּיthat אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD

As you rejoiced when the inheritance of the house of Israel became desolate, so will I do to you. You will become a desolation, O Mount Seir, and so will all of Edom. Then they will know that I am the LORD.
