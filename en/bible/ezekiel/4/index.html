Ezekiel 4

The brick and the days of the siege

4:1

“Now you, son of man, take a brick, place it before you, and draw on it the city of Jerusalem.

4:2

וְנָתַתָּהthen lay עָלֶיהָagainst it a מָצוֹרsiege וּבָנִיתָconstruct עָלֶיהָon her דָּיֵקsiege וְשָׁפַכְתָּbuild עָלֶיהָa to סֹלְלָהramp וְנָתַתָּהset עָלֶיהָagainst it מַחֲנוֹתcamps וְשִׂיםand place עָלֶיהָit on כָּרִיםrams סָבִיבaround

Then lay siege against it: Construct a siege wall, build a ramp to it, set up camps against it, and place battering rams around it on all sides.

4:3

וְאַתָּהand you קַחtake לְךָto you מַחֲבַתplate בַּרְזֶלiron וְנָתַתָּהand set אוֹתָהּher קִירwall בַּרְזֶלiron בֵּינְךָbetween yourself וּבֵיןand between הָעִירthe city וַהֲכִינֹתָהturn אֶת[obj] פָּנֶיךָyour face אֵלֶיהָto her וְהָיְתָהand be בַמָּצוֹרunder siege וְצַרְתָּand besiege עָלֶיהָon her אוֹתsign הִיאa לְבֵיתto the house יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

Then take an iron plate and set it up as an iron wall between yourself and the city. Turn your face toward it so that it is under siege, and besiege it. This will be a sign to the house of Israel.

4:4

וְאַתָּהand you שְׁכַבlie עַלon צִדְּךָside הַשְּׂמָאלִיleft side וְשַׂמְתָּand place אֶת[obj] עֲוֺןiniquity בֵּיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael עָלָיוupon מִסְפַּרnumber הַיָּמִיםof days אֲשֶׁרyou תִּשְׁכַּבlie עָלָיוon תִּשָּׂאbear אֶת[obj] עֲוֺנָםtheir iniquity

Then lie down on your left side and place the iniquity of the house of Israel upon yourself. You are to bear their iniquity for the number of days you lie on your side.

4:5

וַאֲנִיfor I נָתַתִּיassigned לְךָto you אֶת[obj] שְׁנֵיyears עֲוֺנָםtheir iniquity לְמִסְפַּרto the number יָמִיםdays שְׁלֹשׁthree מֵאוֹתhundreds וְתִשְׁעִים390 יוֹםdays וְנָשָׂאתָbear עֲוֺןiniquity בֵּיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

For I have assigned to you 390 days, according to the number of years of their iniquity. So you shall bear the iniquity of the house of Israel.

4:6

וְכִלִּיתָcompleted אֶת[obj] אֵלֶּהthese וְשָׁכַבְתָּdays lie עַלon צִדְּךָyour side הַיְמָנִיright שֵׁנִיתagain וְנָשָׂאתָand bear אֶת[obj] עֲוֺןiniquity בֵּיתhouse יְהוּדָהJudah אַרְבָּעִים40 יוֹםdays יוֹםday לַשָּׁנָהfor each year יוֹםday לַשָּׁנָהto the years נְתַתִּיוassigned לָךְto you

When you have completed these days, lie down again, but on your right side, and bear the iniquity of the house of Judah. I have assigned to you 40 days, a day for each year.

4:7

וְאֶלand to מְצוֹרsiege יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem תָּכִיןturn פָּנֶיךָyour face וּזְרֹעֲךָwith your arm חֲשׂוּפָהbared וְנִבֵּאתָand prophesy עָלֶיהָagainst it

You must turn your face toward the siege of Jerusalem with your arm bared, and prophesy against it.

4:8

וְהִנֵּהnow behold נָתַתִּיwill עָלֶיךָon you עֲבוֹתִיםtie וְלֹאand not תֵהָפֵךְturn מִצִּדְּךָfrom side אֶלto צִדֶּךָside you עַדuntil כַּלּוֹתְךָfinished יְמֵיdays מְצוּרֶךָyour siege

Now behold, I will tie you up with ropes so you cannot turn from side to side until you have finished the days of your siege.

4:9

וְאַתָּהand you קַחtake לְךָto you חִטִּיןwheat וּשְׂעֹרִיםbarley וּפוֹלbeans וַעֲדָשִׁיםlentils וְדֹחַןmillet וְכֻסְּמִיםand spelt וְנָתַתָּהput אוֹתָםthem בִּכְלִיcontainer אֶחָדsingle וְעָשִׂיתָand make אוֹתָםthem לְךָfor לְלָחֶםbread מִסְפַּרnumber הַיָּמִיםthe days אֲשֶׁרthat אַתָּהyou שׁוֹכֵבlie עַלon צִדְּךָyour side שְׁלֹשׁthree מֵאוֹתhundreds וְתִשְׁעִים390 יוֹםduring תֹּאכֲלֶנּוּeat

But take wheat, barley, beans, lentils, millet, and spelt; put them in a single container and make them into bread for yourself. This is what you are to eat during the 390 days you lie on your side.

4:10

וּמַאֲכָלְךָfood אֲשֶׁרthat תֹּאכֲלֶנּוּeat בְּמִשְׁקוֹלto weigh עֶשְׂרִיםtwenty שֶׁקֶלshekels לַיּוֹםeach day מֵעֵתfrom time עַדto עֵתtimes תֹּאכֲלֶנּוּeat it

You are to weigh out twenty shekels of food to eat each day, and you are to eat it at set times.

4:11

וּמַיִםwater בִּמְשׂוּרָהto measure תִשְׁתֶּהdrink שִׁשִּׁיתsixth הַהִיןa hin מֵעֵתat set times עַדto עֵתtime תִּשְׁתֶּהdrink

You are also to measure out a sixth of a hin of water to drink, and you are to drink it at set times.

4:12

וְעֻגַתcake שְׂעֹרִיםbarley תֹּאכֲלֶנָּהeat וְהִיאand she בְּגֶלְלֵיover dried צֵאַתexcrement הָאָדָםhuman תְּעֻגֶנָהexcrement the לְעֵינֵיהֶםin sight

And you shall eat the food as you would a barley cake, after you bake it over dried human excrement in the sight of the people.”

4:13

וַיֹּאמֶרsaid יְהוָהLORD כָּכָהthis יֹאכְלוּeat בְנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites אֶת[obj] לַחְמָםtheir bread טָמֵאdefiled בַּגּוֹיִםamong the nations אֲשֶׁרwhich אַדִּיחֵםbanish them שָׁםthere

Then the LORD said, “This is how the Israelites will eat their defiled bread among the nations to which I will banish them.”

4:14

וָאֹמַרsaid אֲהָהּah אֲדֹנָיLord I יְהוִהGOD הִנֵּהbehold נַפְשִׁיhave לֹאnever מְטֻמָּאָהdefiled וּנְבֵלָהdead וּטְרֵפָהor mauled by wild beasts לֹאnot no אָכַלְתִּיeaten מִנְּעוּרַיfrom my youth וְעַדuntil עַתָּהnow וְלֹאand not בָאentered בְּפִיmy mouth בְּשַׂרmeat פִּגּוּלever

“Ah, Lord GOD,” I said, “I have never defiled myself. From my youth until now I have not eaten anything found dead or mauled by wild beasts. No unclean meat has ever entered my mouth.”

4:15

וַיֹּאמֶרhe replied אֵלַיto me רְאֵהlook נָתַתִּיlet לְךָto you אֶת[obj] צְפִיעֵיuse הַבָּקָרcow תַּחַתinstead גֶּלְלֵיdung הָאָדָםof human וְעָשִׂיתָand make אֶת[obj] לַחְמְךָyour bread עֲלֵיהֶםover

“Look,” He replied, “I will let you use cow dung instead of human excrement, and you may bake your bread over that.”

4:16

וַיֹּאמֶרthen he told אֵלַיto me בֶּןson אָדָםman הִנְנִיI am שֹׁבֵרcut מַטֵּהsupply לֶחֶםfood בִּירוּשָׁלִַםin Jerusalem וְאָכְלוּeat לֶחֶםbread בְּמִשְׁקָלrationed by weight וּבִדְאָגָהand in despair וּמַיִםwater בִּמְשׂוּרָהby measure וּבְשִׁמָּמוֹןand in dread יִשְׁתּוּdrink

Then He told me, “Son of man, I am going to cut off the supply of food in Jerusalem. They will anxiously eat bread rationed by weight, and in despair they will drink water by measure.

4:17

לְמַעַןso יַחְסְרוּlack לֶחֶםfood וָמָיִםand water וְנָשַׁמּוּthey will be appalled אִישׁsight וְאָחִיוone another וְנָמַקּוּwasting בַּעֲוֺנָםin their iniquity

So they will lack food and water; they will be appalled at the sight of one another wasting away in their iniquity.
