The desolate land will be cultivated instead of lying desolate in the sight of all who pass through.

36:35

וְאָמְרוּsay הָאָרֶץthis land הַלֵּזוּthis הַנְּשַׁמָּהthat was desolate הָיְתָהbecome כְּגַןlike the garden עֵדֶןEden וְהֶעָרִיםthe cities הֶחֳרֵבוֹתthat were once ruined וְהַנְשַׁמּוֹתdesolate וְהַנֶּהֱרָסוֹתand destroyed are now בְּצוּרוֹתfortified יָשָׁבוּinhabited

Then they will say, ‘This land that was desolate has become like the garden of Eden. The cities that were once ruined, desolate, and destroyed are now fortified and inhabited.’

36:36

וְיָדְעוּknow הַגּוֹיִםthe nations אֲשֶׁרthat יִשָּׁאֲרוּremain סְבִיבוֹתֵיכֶםaround you כִּיthat אֲנִיI יְהוָהLORD בָּנִיתִיrebuilt הַנֶּהֱרָסוֹתwas destroyed נָטַעְתִּיreplanted הַנְּשַׁמָּהwas desolate אֲנִיI יְהוָהLORD דִּבַּרְתִּיspoken וְעָשִׂיתִיdo

Then the nations around you that remain will know that I, the LORD, have rebuilt what was destroyed, and I have replanted what was desolate. I, the LORD, have spoken, and I will do it.

36:37

כֹּהthis אָמַרis what אֲדֹנָיLord GOD יְהוִהsays עוֹדagain זֹאתI אִדָּרֵשׁwill לְבֵיתthe house יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael לַעֲשׂוֹתand do לָהֶםfor them אַרְבֶּהmultiply אֹתָםthem כַּצֹּאןlike a flock אָדָםman

This is what the Lord GOD says: Once again I will hear the plea of the house of Israel and do for them this: I will multiply their people like a flock.

36:38

כְּצֹאןflocks קָדָשִׁיםnumerous כְּצֹאןlike sheep יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem בְּמוֹעֲדֶיהָduring her appointed feasts כֵּןso תִּהְיֶינָהbe הֶעָרִיםcities הֶחֳרֵבוֹתthe ruined מְלֵאוֹתfilled צֹאןflocks אָדָםpeople וְיָדְעוּknow כִּיthat אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD

Like the numerous flocks for sacrifices at Jerusalem during her appointed feasts, so the ruined cities will be filled with flocks of people. Then they will know that I am the LORD.”
