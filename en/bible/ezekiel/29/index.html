Ezekiel 29

Against Pharaoh and Egypt

In the tenth year, on the twelfth day of the tenth month, the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

29:2

“Son of man, set your face against Pharaoh king of Egypt and prophesy against him and against all Egypt.

29:3

דַּבֵּרspeak וְאָמַרְתָּand tell כֹּהthis אָמַרis what אֲדֹנָיLord GOD יְהוִהsays הִנְנִיbehold I am עָלֶיךָagainst you פַּרְעֹהPharaoh מֶלֶךְking מִצְרַיִםEgypt הַתַּנִּיםmonster הַגָּדוֹלgreat הָרֹבֵץwho lies בְּתוֹךְamong יְאֹרָיוhis rivers אֲשֶׁרwho אָמַרsays לִיto me יְאֹרִיNile is mine וַאֲנִיI עֲשִׂיתִנִיmade it myself

Speak to him and tell him that this is what the Lord GOD says: Behold, I am against you, O Pharaoh king of Egypt, O great monster who lies among his rivers, who says, ‘The Nile is mine; I made it myself.’

29:4

וְנָתַתִּיput חַחִיםhooks בִּלְחָיֶיךָin your jaws cause וְהִדְבַּקְתִּיcling דְגַתfish יְאֹרֶיךָyour streams בְּקַשְׂקְשֹׂתֶיךָyour scales וְהַעֲלִיתִיךָwill haul you up מִתּוֹךְfrom among יְאֹרֶיךָyour rivers וְאֵתand [obj] כָּלall דְּגַתfish יְאֹרֶיךָyour streams בְּקַשְׂקְשֹׂתֶיךָyour scales תִּדְבָּקcling

But I will put hooks in your jaws and cause the fish of your streams to cling to your scales. I will haul you up out of your rivers, and all the fish of your streams will cling to your scales.

29:5

וּנְטַשְׁתִּיךָwill leave you הַמִּדְבָּרָהthe desert אוֹתְךָyou וְאֵתand [obj] כָּלall דְּגַתfish יְאֹרֶיךָstreams you עַלon פְּנֵיface הַשָּׂדֶהopen field תִּפּוֹלfall לֹאnot תֵאָסֵףtaken וְלֹאor תִקָּבֵץgathered לְחַיַּתbeasts הָאָרֶץthe earth וּלְעוֹףand the birds הַשָּׁמַיִםthe air נְתַתִּיךָgiven you לְאָכְלָהas food

I will leave you in the desert, you and all the fish of your streams. You will fall on the open field and will not be taken away or gathered for burial. I have given you as food to the beasts of the earth and the birds of the air.

29:6

וְיָדְעוּknow כָּלall יֹשְׁבֵיpeople מִצְרַיִםEgypt כִּיthat אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD יַעַןfor הֱיוֹתָםwere מִשְׁעֶנֶתstaff קָנֶהreeds לְבֵיתto the house יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

Then all the people of Egypt will know that I am the LORD. For you were only a staff of reeds to the house of Israel.

29:7

בְּתָפְשָׂםwhen Israel took בְּךָin you בַכַּףwith their hands תֵּרוֹץsplintered וּבָקַעְתָּtearing לָהֶםto them כָּלall כָּתֵףshoulders וּבְהִשָּׁעֲנָםwhen they leaned עָלֶיךָon you תִּשָּׁבֵרbroke וְהַעֲמַדְתָּwrenched לָהֶםto them כָּלall מָתְנָיִםbacks

When Israel took hold of you with their hands, you splintered, tearing all their shoulders; when they leaned on you, you broke, and their backs were wrenched.

29:8

לָכֵןtherefore כֹּהthis אָמַרis what אֲדֹנָיLord GOD יְהוִהGOD says הִנְנִיsays I מֵבִיאbring עָלַיִךְagainst you חָרֶבsword וְהִכְרַתִּיand cut off מִמֵּךְfrom you אָדָםman וּבְהֵמָהand beast

Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: I will bring a sword against you and cut off from you man and beast.

29:9

וְהָיְתָהand be אֶרֶץland מִצְרַיִםEgypt לִשְׁמָמָהbecome a desolate וְחָרְבָּהwasteland then וְיָדְעוּthey will know כִּיthat אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD יַעַןbecause אָמַרsaid יְאֹרNile לִיto me וַאֲנִיI עָשִׂיתִיmade

The land of Egypt will become a desolate wasteland. Then they will know that I am the LORD. Because you said, ‘The Nile is mine; I made it,’

29:10

לָכֵןtherefore הִנְנִיI am אֵלֶיךָagainst you וְאֶלand against יְאֹרֶיךָyour rivers וְנָתַתִּיI will אֶת[obj] אֶרֶץland מִצְרַיִםEgypt לְחָרְבוֹתdesolate חֹרֶבruin שְׁמָמָהwasteland מִמִּגְדֹּלfrom Migdol סְוֵנֵהSyene וְעַדand as far גְּבוּלborder כּוּשׁCush

therefore I am against you and against your rivers. I will turn the land of Egypt into a ruin, a desolate wasteland from Migdol to Syene, and as far as the border of Cush.

29:11

לֹאno תַעֲבָרpass בָּהּin her רֶגֶלfoot אָדָםman וְרֶגֶלand foot בְּהֵמָהbeast לֹאnot תַעֲבָרpass through בָּהּin her וְלֹאand not תֵשֵׁבuninhabited אַרְבָּעִיםforty שָׁנָהyears

No foot of man or beast will pass through, and it will be uninhabited for forty years.

29:12

וְנָתַתִּיI will make אֶת[obj] אֶרֶץland מִצְרַיִםEgypt שְׁמָמָהdesolation בְּתוֹךְamong אֲרָצוֹתlands נְשַׁמּוֹתdesolate וְעָרֶיהָand her cities בְּתוֹךְamong עָרִיםcities מָחֳרָבוֹתruined תִּהְיֶיןָwill שְׁמָמָהdesolate אַרְבָּעִיםforty שָׁנָהyears וַהֲפִצֹתִיdisperse אֶת[obj] מִצְרַיִםEgyptians בַּגּוֹיִםamong the nations וְזֵרִיתִיםand scatter them בָּאֲרָצוֹתthroughout the countries

I will make the land of Egypt a desolation among desolate lands, and her cities will lie desolate for forty years among the ruined cities. And I will disperse the Egyptians among the nations and scatter them throughout the countries.

29:13

כִּיfor כֹּהthis אָמַרis what אֲדֹנָיLord GOD יְהוִהsays מִקֵּץat the end אַרְבָּעִיםforty שָׁנָהyears אֲקַבֵּץgather אֶת[obj] מִצְרַיִםEgyptians מִןfrom הָעַמִּיםthe nations אֲשֶׁרwhich נָפֹצוּscattered שָׁמָּהthere toward

For this is what the Lord GOD says: At the end of forty years I will gather the Egyptians from the nations to which they were scattered.

29:14

וְשַׁבְתִּיI will restore אֶת[obj] שְׁבוּתfrom captivity מִצְרַיִםEgypt וַהֲשִׁבֹתִיand bring אֹתָםthem אֶרֶץland פַּתְרוֹסPathros עַלon אֶרֶץland מְכוּרָתָםtheir origin וְהָיוּbe שָׁםthere מַמְלָכָהkingdom שְׁפָלָהlowly

I will restore Egypt from captivity and bring them back to the land of Pathros, the land of their origin. There they will be a lowly kingdom.

29:15

מִןfrom הַמַּמְלָכוֹתof kingdoms תִּהְיֶהbe שְׁפָלָהbeneath וְלֹאand will never תִתְנַשֵּׂאexalt עוֹדagain עַלon הַגּוֹיִםthe nations וְהִמְעַטְתִּיםwill diminish Egypt לְבִלְתִּיto not רְדוֹתrule בַּגּוֹיִםover the nations

Egypt will be the lowliest of kingdoms and will never again exalt itself above the nations. For I will diminish Egypt so that it will never again rule over the nations.

29:16

וְלֹאwill never יִהְיֶהbe עוֹדagain לְבֵיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael לְמִבְטָחtrust for מַזְכִּירremind עָוֺןiniquity בִּפְנוֹתָםin turning אַחֲרֵיהֶםafter them וְיָדְעוּknow כִּיthat אֲנִיI am אֲדֹנָיLord יְהוִהGOD

Egypt will never again be an object of trust for the house of Israel, but will remind them of their iniquity in turning to the Egyptians. Then they will know that I am the Lord GOD.”

29:17

וַיְהִיand was בְּעֶשְׂרִיםin the twenty וָשֶׁבַעseventh שָׁנָהyear בָּרִאשׁוֹןon the first בְּאֶחָדfirst לַחֹדֶשׁthe month הָיָהwas דְבַרword יְהוָהLORD אֵלַיto לֵאמֹרto saying

In the twenty-seventh year, on the first day of the first month, the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

29:18

בֶּןson אָדָםman נְבוּכַדְרֶאצַּרNebuchadnezzar מֶלֶךְking בָּבֶלBabylon הֶעֱבִידcaused אֶת[obj] חֵילוֹhis army עֲבֹדָהlabor גְדֹלָהstrenuously אֶלagainst צֹרTyre כָּלevery רֹאשׁhead מֻקְרָחbald וְכָלand every כָּתֵףshoulder מְרוּטָהraw וְשָׂכָרwages לֹאno הָיָהwas לוֹto him וּלְחֵילוֹand army מִצֹּרfrom Tyre עַלfor הָעֲבֹדָהthe labor אֲשֶׁרthat עָבַדexpended עָלֶיהָon it

“Son of man, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon caused his army to labor strenuously against Tyre. Every head was made bald and every shoulder made raw. But he and his army received no wages from Tyre for the labor they expended on it.

29:19

לָכֵןtherefore כֹּהthis אָמַרis what אֲדֹנָיLord יְהוִהGOD הִנְנִיsays I נֹתֵןgive לִנְבוּכַדְרֶאצַּרto Nebuchadnezzar מֶלֶךְking בָּבֶלBabylon אֶת[obj] אֶרֶץland מִצְרָיִםEgypt וְנָשָׂאwill carry הֲמֹנָהּits wealth וְשָׁלַלseize שְׁלָלָהּits spoil וּבָזַזand remove בִּזָּהּits plunder this וְהָיְתָהwill be שָׂכָרwages לְחֵילוֹfor his army

Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: I will give the land of Egypt to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, who will carry off its wealth, seize its spoil, and remove its plunder. This will be the wages for his army.

29:20

פְּעֻלָּתוֹreward for his אֲשֶׁרhave עָבַדlabor בָּהּin her נָתַתִּיgiven לוֹto him אֶת[obj] אֶרֶץland מִצְרָיִםEgypt אֲשֶׁרthat עָשׂוּdone לִיfor me נְאֻםdeclares אֲדֹנָיLord יְהוִהGOD

I have given him the land of Egypt as the reward for his labor, because it was done for Me, declares the Lord GOD.

29:21

בַּיּוֹםin that day הַהוּאwill אַצְמִיחַcause קֶרֶןhorn לְבֵיתfor the house יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וּלְךָand to you אֶתֵּןwill פִּתְחוֹןopen פֶּהmouth בְּתוֹכָםto speak among them וְיָדְעוּthen they will know כִּיthat אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD

In that day I will cause a horn to sprout for the house of Israel, and I will open your mouth to speak among them. Then they will know that I am the LORD.”
