Ezekiel 6

Against the mountains of Israel

And the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

“Son of man, set your face against the mountains of Israel and prophesy against them.

6:3

וְאָמַרְתָּsay הָרֵיmountains יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael שִׁמְעוּhear דְּבַרword אֲדֹנָיLord יְהוִהGOD כֹּהthis אָמַרis what אֲדֹנָיLord GOD יְהוִהsays לֶהָרִיםto the mountains וְלַגְּבָעוֹתand hills לָאֲפִיקִיםto the ravines וְלַגֵּאָיוֹתand valleys הִנְנִיbehold me אֲנִיam מֵבִיאbring עֲלֵיכֶםagainst you חֶרֶבsword וְאִבַּדְתִּיdestroy בָּמוֹתֵיכֶםyour high places

You are to say: ‘O mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord GOD! This is what the Lord GOD says to the mountains and hills, to the ravines and valleys: I am about to bring a sword against you, and I will destroy your high places.

6:4

וְנָשַׁמּוּwill be demolished מִזְבְּחוֹתֵיכֶםyour altars וְנִשְׁבְּרוּsmashed חַמָּנֵיכֶםyour incense altars וְהִפַּלְתִּיcast חַלְלֵיכֶםyour slain לִפְנֵיbefore גִּלּוּלֵיכֶםyour idols

Your altars will be demolished and your incense altars will be smashed; and I will cast down your slain before your idols.

6:5

וְנָתַתִּיwill lay אֶת[obj] פִּגְרֵיcorpses בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites לִפְנֵיbefore גִּלּוּלֵיהֶםtheir idols וְזֵרִיתִיand scatter אֶת[obj] עַצְמוֹתֵיכֶםyour bones סְבִיבוֹתaround מִזְבְּחוֹתֵיכֶםyour altars

I will lay the corpses of the Israelites before their idols and scatter your bones around your altars.

6:6

בְּכֹלwherever מוֹשְׁבוֹתֵיכֶםwherever you live הֶעָרִיםthe cities תֶּחֱרַבְנָהlaid waste וְהַבָּמוֹתand the high places תִּישָׁמְנָהdemolished לְמַעַןso יֶחֶרְבוּlaid waste וְיֶאְשְׁמוּand desecrated מִזְבְּחוֹתֵיכֶםyour altars וְנִשְׁבְּרוּsmashed וְנִשְׁבְּתוּand obliterated גִּלּוּלֵיכֶםyour idols וְנִגְדְּעוּcut חַמָּנֵיכֶםyour incense altars down וְנִמְחוּblotted מַעֲשֵׂיכֶםyour works

Wherever you live, the cities will be laid waste and the high places will be demolished, so that your altars will be laid waste and desecrated, your idols smashed and obliterated, your incense altars cut down, and your works blotted out.

6:7

וְנָפַלwill fall חָלָלslain בְּתוֹכְכֶםamong you וִידַעְתֶּםknow כִּיthat אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD

The slain will fall among you, and you will know that I am the LORD.

6:8

וְהוֹתַרְתִּיleave בִּהְיוֹתa remnant לָכֶםfor פְּלִיטֵיescape חֶרֶבsword בַּגּוֹיִםthe nations בְּהִזָּרוֹתֵיכֶםyou are scattered among בָּאֲרָצוֹתthroughout the lands

Yet I will leave a remnant, for some of you will escape the sword when you are scattered among the nations and throughout the lands.

6:9

וְזָכְרוּremember פְלִיטֵיכֶםyour survivors אוֹתִיme בַּגּוֹיִםin the nations אֲשֶׁרwhich נִשְׁבּוּcarried captive שָׁםthere אֲשֶׁרthat נִשְׁבַּרְתִּיadulterous אֶת[obj] לִבָּםtheir hearts הַזּוֹנֶהgrieved אֲשֶׁרthat סָרturned מֵעָלַיfrom the me וְאֵתand [obj] עֵינֵיהֶםtheir eyes הַזֹּנוֹתthat lusted אַחֲרֵיafter גִּלּוּלֵיהֶםidols they וְנָקֹטּוּso will loathe בִּפְנֵיהֶםin their against אֶלto הָרָעוֹתthe evil אֲשֶׁרhave עָשׂוּdone לְכֹלfor all תּוֹעֲבֹתֵיהֶםtheir abominations

Then in the nations to which they have been carried captive, your survivors will remember Me—how I have been grieved by their adulterous hearts that turned away from Me, and by their eyes that lusted after idols. So they will loathe themselves for the evil they have done and for all their abominations.

6:10

וְיָדְעוּwill know כִּיthat אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD לֹאnot אֶלto חִנָּםvain דִּבַּרְתִּיdeclare לַעֲשׂוֹתbring לָהֶםto them הָרָעָהthis calamity הַזֹּאתthis

And they will know that I am the LORD; I did not declare in vain that I would bring this calamity upon them.

6:11

כֹּהthis אָמַרis what אֲדֹנָיLord GOD יְהוִהsays הַכֵּהclap בְכַפְּךָyour hands וּרְקַעstomp בְּרַגְלְךָyour feet וֶאֱמָרand cry אָחAlas אֶלto כָּלall תּוֹעֲבוֹתabominations רָעוֹתwicked בֵּיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael אֲשֶׁרwho בַּחֶרֶבby sword בָּרָעָבand famine וּבַדֶּבֶרand plague יִפֹּלוּfall

This is what the Lord GOD says: Clap your hands, stomp your feet, and cry out “Alas!” because of all the wicked abominations of the house of Israel, who will fall by sword and famine and plague.

6:12

הָרָחוֹקwho is far בַּדֶּבֶרby the plague יָמוּתdie וְהַקָּרוֹבhe who is near בַּחֶרֶבby the sword יִפּוֹלfall וְהַנִּשְׁאָרand he who remains וְהַנָּצוּרand the cunning בָּרָעָבby famine יָמוּתdie וְכִלֵּיתִיvent חֲמָתִיmy fury בָּםin them

He who is far off will die by the plague, he who is near will fall by the sword, and he who remains will die by famine. So I will vent My fury upon them.

6:13

וִידַעְתֶּםknow כִּיthat אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD בִּהְיוֹתin was חַלְלֵיהֶםtheir slain בְּתוֹךְlie among גִּלּוּלֵיהֶםtheir idols סְבִיבוֹתaround מִזְבְּחוֹתֵיהֶםtheir altars אֶלto כָּלevery גִּבְעָהhill רָמָהhigh בְּכֹלon all רָאשֵׁיmountaintops הֶהָרִיםthe mountaintops וְתַחַתand under כָּלevery עֵץtree רַעֲנָןgreen וְתַחַתand under כָּלall אֵלָהoak עֲבֻתָּהleafy מְקוֹםplaces אֲשֶׁרthat נָתְנוּoffered שָׁםthere רֵיחַfragrant incense נִיחֹחַpleasing לְכֹלto all גִּלּוּלֵיהֶםtheir idols

Then you will know that I am the LORD, when their slain lie among their idols around their altars, on every high hill, on all the mountaintops, and under every green tree and leafy oak—the places where they offered fragrant incense to all their idols.

6:14

וְנָטִיתִיwill stretch אֶת[obj] יָדִיmy hand עֲלֵיהֶםagainst them וְנָתַתִּיmake אֶת[obj] הָאָרֶץthe land שְׁמָמָהdesolate וּמְשַׁמָּהwaste מִמִּדְבַּרfrom the wilderness דִּבְלָתָהDiblah בְּכֹלwherever מוֹשְׁבוֹתֵיהֶםlive וְיָדְעוּknow כִּיthat אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD

I will stretch out My hand against them, and wherever they live I will make the land a desolate waste, from the wilderness to Diblah. Then they will know that I am the LORD.’”
