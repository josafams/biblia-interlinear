Ezekiel 39

The defeat of Gog

Open in the interlinear reader

39:1

וְאַתָּהas for you בֶןson אָדָםman הִנָּבֵאprophesy עַלagainst גּוֹגGog וְאָמַרְתָּand declare כֹּהthis אָמַרis what אֲדֹנָיLord GOD יְהוִהsays הִנְנִיbehold I am אֵלֶיךָagainst you גּוֹגGog נְשִׂיאprince רֹאשׁchief מֶשֶׁךְMeshech וְתֻבָלand Tubal

“As for you, O son of man, prophesy against Gog and declare that this is what the Lord GOD says: Behold, I am against you, O Gog, chief prince of Meshech and Tubal.

39:2

וְשֹׁבַבְתִּיךָwill turn you וְשִׁשֵּׁאתִיךָdrive you וְהַעֲלִיתִיךָalong bring you מִיַּרְכְּתֵיfrom the far צָפוֹןnorth וַהֲבִאוֹתִךָand send you עַלagainst הָרֵיmountains יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

I will turn you around, drive you along, bring you up from the far north, and send you against the mountains of Israel.

39:3

וְהִכֵּיתִיstrike קַשְׁתְּךָbow מִיַּדhand שְׂמֹאולֶךָyour left וְחִצֶּיךָarrows מִיַּדhand יְמִינְךָyour right אַפִּילcast

Then I will strike the bow from your left hand and dash down the arrows from your right hand.

39:4

עַלon הָרֵיmountains יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael תִּפּוֹלfall אַתָּהyou וְכָלand all אֲגַפֶּיךָyour troops וְעַמִּיםand the nations אֲשֶׁרthat אִתָּךְwith you לְעֵיטravenous צִפּוֹרbird כָּלevery כָּנָףkind וְחַיַּתand wild beast הַשָּׂדֶהthe field נְתַתִּיךָgive you לְאָכְלָהas food

On the mountains of Israel you will fall—you and all your troops and the nations with you. I will give you as food to every kind of ravenous bird and wild beast.

39:5

עַלin פְּנֵיface הַשָּׂדֶהopen field תִּפּוֹלfall כִּיfor אֲנִיI דִבַּרְתִּיspoken נְאֻםdeclares אֲדֹנָיLord יְהוִהGOD

You will fall in the open field, for I have spoken, declares the Lord GOD.

39:6

וְשִׁלַּחְתִּיI will send אֵשׁfire בְּמָגוֹגon Magog וּבְיֹשְׁבֵיdwell הָאִיִּיםthe coastlands לָבֶטַחsecurely in וְיָדְעוּknow כִּיthat אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD

I will send fire on Magog and on those who dwell securely in the coastlands, and they will know that I am the LORD.

39:7

וְאֶתand [obj] שֵׁםname קָדְשִׁיmy holy אוֹדִיעַknown בְּתוֹךְamong עַמִּיmy people יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וְלֹאand will no אַחֵלprofaned אֶת[obj] שֵׁםname קָדְשִׁיit עוֹדlonger וְיָדְעוּknow הַגּוֹיִםthe nations כִּיthat אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD קָדוֹשׁholy בְּיִשְׂרָאֵלin Israel

So I will make My holy name known among My people Israel and will no longer allow it to be profaned. Then the nations will know that I am the LORD, the Holy One in Israel.

39:8

הִנֵּהbehold בָאָהcoming וְנִהְיָתָהhappen נְאֻםdeclares אֲדֹנָיLord יְהוִהGOD הוּאis הַיּוֹםthe day אֲשֶׁרwhich דִּבַּרְתִּיspoken

Yes, it is coming, and it will surely happen, declares the Lord GOD. This is the day of which I have spoken.

39:9

וְיָצְאוּand go יֹשְׁבֵיdwell עָרֵיcities יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וּבִעֲרוּburn וְהִשִּׂיקוּkindle fires and בְּנֶשֶׁקup the weapons וּמָגֵןbucklers וְצִנָּהand shields בְּקֶשֶׁתthe bows וּבְחִצִּיםand arrows וּבְמַקֵּלthe clubs יָדhand וּבְרֹמַחand spears וּבִעֲרוּthey will use בָהֶםin them אֵשׁfuel שֶׁבַעseven שָׁנִיםyears

Then those who dwell in the cities of Israel will go out, kindle fires, and burn up the weapons—the bucklers and shields, the bows and arrows, the clubs and spears. For seven years they will use them for fuel.

39:10

וְלֹאthey will not יִשְׂאוּgather עֵצִיםwood מִןfrom הַשָּׂדֶהthe countryside וְלֹאor יַחְטְבוּcut מִןfrom הַיְּעָרִיםthe forests כִּיfor בַנֶּשֶׁקthe weapons יְבַעֲרוּuse אֵשׁfuel וְשָׁלְלוּthey will loot אֶת[obj] שֹׁלְלֵיהֶםlooted them וּבָזְזוּand plunder אֶת[obj] בֹּזְזֵיהֶםplundered them נְאֻםdeclares אֲדֹנָיLord יְהוִהGOD

They will not gather wood from the countryside or cut it from the forests, for they will use the weapons for fuel. They will loot those who looted them and plunder those who plundered them, declares the Lord GOD.

39:11

וְהָיָהand be בַיּוֹםon that day הַהוּאwill אֶתֵּןgive לְגוֹגGog מְקוֹםplace שָׁםa קֶבֶרburial בְּיִשְׂרָאֵלin Israel גֵּיValley הָעֹבְרִיםthe travelers קִדְמַתeast הַיָּםthe sea וְחֹסֶמֶתwill block הִיאis אֶת[obj] הָעֹבְרִיםthose travel וְקָבְרוּburied שָׁםthere אֶת[obj] גּוֹגGog וְאֶתand [obj] כָּלall הֲמוֹנֹהhis hordes וְקָרְאוּcalled גֵּיאValley הֲמוֹןHamon gog גּוֹגHamon

And on that day I will give Gog a burial place in Israel, the Valley of the Travelers, east of the Sea. It will block those who travel through, because Gog and all his hordes will be buried there. So it will be called the Valley of Hamon-gog.

39:12

וּקְבָרוּםburying them בֵּיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael לְמַעַןorder טַהֵרcleanse אֶת[obj] הָאָרֶץthe land שִׁבְעָהseven חֳדָשִׁיםmonths

For seven months the house of Israel will be burying them in order to cleanse the land.

39:13

וְקָבְרוּwill bury כָּלall עַםpeople הָאָרֶץthe land וְהָיָהand it will לָהֶםto them לְשֵׁםrenown יוֹםday הִכָּבְדִיmy glory נְאֻםdeclares אֲדֹנָיLord יְהוִהGOD

All the people of the land will bury them, and it will bring them renown on the day I display My glory, declares the Lord GOD.

39:14

וְאַנְשֵׁיand men תָמִידcontinually יַבְדִּילוּemployed עֹבְרִיםpass בָּאָרֶץthrough the land מְקַבְּרִיםburying אֶת[obj] הָעֹבְרִיםthe invaders אֶת[obj] הַנּוֹתָרִיםwho remain עַלon פְּנֵיface הָאָרֶץthe ground לְטַהֲרָהּto cleanse it מִקְצֵהend שִׁבְעָהseven חֳדָשִׁיםmonths יַחְקֹרוּsearch

And men will be employed to continually pass through the land to cleanse it by burying the invaders who remain on the ground. At the end of the seven months they will begin their search.

39:15

וְעָבְרוּas they pass הָעֹבְרִיםthrough בָּאָרֶץthe land וְרָאָהwho sees עֶצֶםbone אָדָםhuman וּבָנָהpillar אֶצְלוֹnext to it צִיּוּןgravediggers עַדuntil קָבְרוּburied אֹתוֹhim הַמְקַבְּרִיםburied אֶלto גֵּיאValley הֲמוֹןHamon gog גּוֹגHamon gog

As they pass through the land, anyone who sees a human bone will set up a pillar next to it, until the gravediggers have buried it in the Valley of Hamon-gog.

39:16

וְגַםeven שֶׁםnamed עִירcity הֲמוֹנָהHamonah וְטִהֲרוּcleanse הָאָרֶץthe land

(Even the city will be named Hamonah.) And so they will cleanse the land.

39:17

וְאַתָּהyou בֶןson אָדָםman כֹּהthis אָמַרis what אֲדֹנָיLord יְהֹוִהGOD אֱמֹרsays call לְצִפּוֹרbird כָּלevery כָּנָףkind וּלְכֹלand to every חַיַּתbeast הַשָּׂדֶהthe field הִקָּבְצוּassemble וָבֹאוּand come הֵאָסְפוּtogether מִסָּבִיבfrom all around עַלto זִבְחִיsacrificial אֲשֶׁרthat אֲנִיam זֹבֵחַpreparing לָכֶםfor you זֶבַחa feast גָּדוֹלgreat עַלon הָרֵיmountains יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וַאֲכַלְתֶּםwill eat בָּשָׂרflesh וּשְׁתִיתֶםand drink דָּםblood

And as for you, son of man, this is what the Lord GOD says: Call out to every kind of bird and to every beast of the field: ‘Assemble and come together from all around to the sacrificial feast that I am preparing for you, a great feast on the mountains of Israel. There you will eat flesh and drink blood.

39:18

בְּשַׂרflesh גִּבּוֹרִיםmighty תֹּאכֵלוּeat וְדַםblood נְשִׂיאֵיprinces הָאָרֶץthe earth תִּשְׁתּוּdrink אֵילִיםrams כָּרִיםlambs וְעַתּוּדִיםgoats and פָּרִיםbulls מְרִיאֵיfattened בָשָׁןBashan כֻּלָּםall

You will eat the flesh of the mighty and drink the blood of the princes of the earth as though they were rams, lambs, goats, and bulls—all the fattened animals of Bashan.

39:19

וַאֲכַלְתֶּםwill eat חֵלֶבfat לְשָׂבְעָהgorged וּשְׁתִיתֶםand drink דָּםblood לְשִׁכָּרוֹןuntil מִזִּבְחִיat the sacrifice I am אֲשֶׁרthat זָבַחְתִּיpreparing לָכֶםto you

At the sacrifice I am preparing, you will eat fat until you are gorged and drink blood until you are drunk.

39:20

וּשְׂבַעְתֶּםfill עַלon שֻׁלְחָנִיmy table סוּסhorses וָרֶכֶבand riders גִּבּוֹרmighty וְכָלevery אִישׁmen מִלְחָמָהbattle נְאֻםdeclares אֲדֹנָיLord יְהוִהGOD

And at My table you will eat your fill of horses and riders, of mighty men and warriors of every kind,’ declares the Lord GOD.

39:21

וְנָתַתִּיand give אֶת[obj] כְּבוֹדִיmy glory בַּגּוֹיִםamong the nations וְרָאוּsee כָלall הַגּוֹיִםthe nations אֶת[obj] מִשְׁפָּטִיjudgment I אֲשֶׁרthat עָשִׂיתִיexecute וְאֶתand [obj] יָדִיhand I אֲשֶׁרthat שַׂמְתִּיlay בָהֶםin them

I will display My glory among the nations, and all the nations will see the judgment that I execute and the hand that I lay upon them.

39:22

וְיָדְעוּwill know בֵּיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael כִּיthat אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיהֶםtheir God מִןfrom הַיּוֹםday הַהוּאthat forward וָהָלְאָהand beyond

From that day forward the house of Israel will know that I am the LORD their God.

39:23

וְיָדְעוּknow הַגּוֹיִםthe nations כִּיthat בַעֲוֺנָםtheir iniquity גָּלוּexile בֵיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael עַלon אֲשֶׁרthat מָעֲלוּunfaithful בִיto me וָאַסְתִּרso I hid פָּנַיmy face מֵהֶםfrom them וָאֶתְּנֵםand delivered them בְּיַדinto the hands צָרֵיהֶםtheir enemies וַיִּפְּלוּfell בַחֶרֶבby the sword כֻּלָּםthey all

And the nations will know that the house of Israel went into exile for their iniquity, because they were unfaithful to Me. So I hid My face from them and delivered them into the hands of their enemies, so that they all fell by the sword.

39:24

כְּטֻמְאָתָםaccording to their uncleanness וּכְפִשְׁעֵיהֶםand transgressions עָשִׂיתִיdealt אֹתָםthem וָאַסְתִּרand I hid פָּנַיmy face מֵהֶםfrom them

I dealt with them according to their uncleanness and transgressions, and I hid My face from them.

39:25

לָכֵןtherefore כֹּהthis אָמַרis what אֲדֹנָיLord GOD יְהוִהsays עַתָּהnow אָשִׁיבrestore אֶת[obj] שְׁבוּתfrom captivity יַעֲקֹבJacob וְרִחַמְתִּיcompassion on כָּלwhole בֵּיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וְקִנֵּאתִיjealous לְשֵׁםname קָדְשִׁיmy holy

Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Now I will restore Jacob from captivity and will have compassion on the whole house of Israel, and I will be jealous for My holy name.

39:26

וְנָשׂוּthey will forget אֶת[obj] כְּלִמָּתָםtheir disgrace וְאֶתand [obj] כָּלall מַעֲלָםtreachery they אֲשֶׁרthat מָעֲלוּcommitted בִיagainst me בְּשִׁבְתָּםwhen they dwell עַלon אַדְמָתָםtheir land לָבֶטַחsecurely in וְאֵיןwith no מַחֲרִידfrighten

They will forget their disgrace and all the treachery they committed against Me, when they dwell securely in their land, with no one to frighten them.

39:27

בְּשׁוֹבְבִיwhen I bring אוֹתָםthem מִןfrom הָעַמִּיםthe peoples וְקִבַּצְתִּיand gather אֹתָםthem מֵאַרְצוֹתlands אֹיְבֵיהֶםtheir enemies וְנִקְדַּשְׁתִּיwill show my holiness בָםin them לְעֵינֵיin the sight הַגּוֹיִםof nations רַבִּיםmany

When I bring them back from the peoples and gather them out of the lands of their enemies, I will show My holiness in them in the sight of many nations.

39:28

וְיָדְעוּknow כִּיthat אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיהֶםtheir God בְּהַגְלוֹתִיexile אֹתָםthem אֶלto הַגּוֹיִםthe nations וְכִנַּסְתִּיםregather them עַלto אַדְמָתָםtheir land וְלֹאown not אוֹתִירleaving עוֹדlonger מֵהֶםany of them שָׁםthere

Then they will know that I am the LORD their God, when I regather them to their own land, not leaving any of them behind after their exile among the nations.

39:29

וְלֹאno אַסְתִּירhide עוֹדlonger פָּנַיmy face מֵהֶםfrom them אֲשֶׁרthat שָׁפַכְתִּיpour אֶת[obj] רוּחִיmy spirit עַלon בֵּיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael נְאֻםdeclares אֲדֹנָיLord יְהוִהGOD

And I will no longer hide My face from them, for I will pour out My Spirit on the house of Israel, declares the Lord GOD.”
