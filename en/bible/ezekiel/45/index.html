Ezekiel 45

The holy portion and the prince's duties

45:1

“When you divide the land by lot as an inheritance, you are to set aside a portion for the LORD, a holy portion of the land 25,000 cubits long and 20,000 cubits wide. This entire tract of land will be holy.

45:2

Within this area there is to be a section for the sanctuary 500 cubits square, with 50 cubits around it for open land.

45:3

וּמִןfrom הַמִּדָּהthis holy הַזֹּאתportion you תָּמוֹדmeasure אֹרֶךְlength חֲמִשָּׁהfive וְעֶשְׂרִים25 אֶלֶף000 וְרֹחַבcubits and a width עֲשֶׂרֶת10 אֲלָפִים000 וּבוֹand in it יִהְיֶהbe הַמִּקְדָּשׁthe sanctuary קֹדֶשׁMost קָדָשִׁיםholy

From this holy portion, you are to measure off a length of 25,000 cubits and a width of 10,000 cubits, and in it will be the sanctuary, the Most Holy Place.

45:4

קֹדֶשׁholy מִןof הָאָרֶץthe land הוּאhe לַכֹּהֲנִיםby the priests מְשָׁרְתֵיminister הַמִּקְדָּשׁin the sanctuary יִהְיֶהbe הַקְּרֵבִיםwho draw near לְשָׁרֵתto minister before אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD וְהָיָהwill be לָהֶםa מָקוֹםplace לְבָתִּיםfor houses וּמִקְדָּשׁholy לַמִּקְדָּשׁfor the sanctuary

It will be a holy portion of the land to be used by the priests who minister in the sanctuary, who draw near to minister before the LORD. It will be a place for their houses, as well as a holy area for the sanctuary.

45:5

וַחֲמִשָּׁהadjacent area וְעֶשְׂרִיםarea 25 אֶלֶף000 אֹרֶךְcubits long וַעֲשֶׂרֶתand 10 אֲלָפִים000 רֹחַבcubits wide וְהָיָהshall belong לַלְוִיִּםto the Levites מְשָׁרְתֵיminister הַבַּיִתthe temple לָהֶםto them לַאֲחֻזָּהpossession for עֶשְׂרִיםtowns לְשָׁכֹתchambers

An adjacent area 25,000 cubits long and 10,000 cubits wide shall belong to the Levites who minister in the temple; it will be their possession for towns in which to live.

45:6

וַאֲחֻזַּתas the property הָעִירthe city תִּתְּנוּset חֲמֵשֶׁת5 אֲלָפִים000 רֹחַבwide וְאֹרֶךְcubits חֲמִשָּׁה25 וְעֶשְׂרִיםand 25 אֶלֶף000 לְעֻמַּתcubits תְּרוּמַתadjacent הַקֹּדֶשׁthe holy לְכָלwhole בֵּיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael יִהְיֶהbe

As the property of the city, you are to set aside an area 5,000 cubits wide and 25,000 cubits long, adjacent to the holy district. It will belong to the whole house of Israel.

45:7

וְלַנָּשִׂיאnow the prince מִזֶּהfrom this וּמִזֶּהand from sides לִתְרוּמַתformed by הַקֹּדֶשׁthe holy וְלַאֲחֻזַּתbordering הָעִירthe city אֶלto פְּנֵיface תְרוּמַתdistrict הַקֹּדֶשׁthe sanctuary וְאֶלand to פְּנֵיface אֲחֻזַּתproperty הָעִירthe city מִפְּאַתextending from יָםwestward יָמָּהwestern side וּמִפְּאַתside קֵדְמָהeastern side קָדִימָהeastward וְאֹרֶךְand parallel לְעֻמּוֹתparallel to אַחַדone הַחֲלָקִיםthe tribal portions מִגְּבוּלboundary יָםwestern אֶלto גְּבוּלboundary קָדִימָהeastern

Now the prince will have the area bordering each side of the area formed by the holy district and the property of the city, extending westward from the western side and eastward from the eastern side, running lengthwise from the western boundary to the eastern boundary and parallel to one of the tribal portions.

45:8

לָאָרֶץthis land יִהְיֶהwill לּוֹto him לַאֲחֻזָּהpossession בְּיִשְׂרָאֵלin Israel וְלֹאno יוֹנוּoppress עוֹדlonger נְשִׂיאַיmy princes אֶת[obj] עַמִּיmy people וְהָאָרֶץthe land יִתְּנוּgive לְבֵיתto the house יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael לְשִׁבְטֵיהֶםaccording to their tribes

This land will be his possession in Israel. And My princes will no longer oppress My people, but will give the rest of the land to the house of Israel according to their tribes.

45:9

כֹּהthis אָמַרis what אֲדֹנָיLord GOD יְהוִהsays רַבenough לָכֶםto you נְשִׂיאֵיprinces יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael חָמָסviolence וָשֹׁדand oppression הָסִירוּoppression וּמִשְׁפָּטjust וּצְדָקָהand right עֲשׂוּdo הָרִימוּstop גְרֻשֹׁתֵיכֶםdispossessing מֵעַלfrom the עַמִּיmy people נְאֻםdeclares אֲדֹנָיLord יְהוִהGOD

For this is what the Lord GOD says: ‘Enough, O princes of Israel! Cease your violence and oppression, and do what is just and right. Stop dispossessing My people, declares the Lord GOD.’

45:10

מֹאזְנֵיscales צֶדֶקhonest וְאֵיפַתephah צֶדֶקjust וּבַתbath צֶדֶקjust יְהִיmay לָכֶםto you

You must use honest scales, a just ephah, and a just bath.

45:11

הָאֵיפָהthe ephah וְהַבַּתand the bath תֹּכֶןcontain אֶחָדone יִהְיֶהbe לָשֵׂאתto carry מַעְשַׂרtenth הַחֹמֶרa homer הַבָּתbath וַעֲשִׂירִתtenth הַחֹמֶרa homer הָאֵיפָהephah אֶלto הַחֹמֶרhomer יִהְיֶהbe מַתְכֻּנְתּוֹmeasure

The ephah and the bath shall be the same quantity so that the bath will contain a tenth of a homer, and the ephah a tenth of a homer; the homer will be the standard measure for both.

45:12

וְהַשֶּׁקֶלthe shekel עֶשְׂרִיםtwenty גֵּרָהgerahs עֶשְׂרִיםtwenty שְׁקָלִיםshekels חֲמִשָּׁהfive וְעֶשְׂרִיםtwenty שְׁקָלִיםplus עֲשָׂרָהten וַחֲמִשָּׁהfifteen שֶׁקֶלshekels הַמָּנֶהequal יִהְיֶהone לָכֶםto you

The shekel will consist of twenty gerahs. Twenty shekels plus twenty-five shekels plus fifteen shekels will equal one mina.

45:13

זֹאתthis הַתְּרוּמָהthe contribution אֲשֶׁרyou תָּרִימוּoffer שִׁשִּׁיתsixth הָאֵיפָהephah מֵחֹמֶרfrom each homer הַחִטִּיםof wheat וְשִׁשִּׁיתֶםand a sixth הָאֵיפָהof an ephah מֵחֹמֶרfrom each homer הַשְּׂעֹרִיםbarley

This is the contribution you are to offer: a sixth of an ephah from each homer of wheat, and a sixth of an ephah from each homer of barley.

45:14

וְחֹקprescribed portion הַשֶּׁמֶןof oil measured הַבַּתthe bath הַשֶּׁמֶןthe oil מַעְשַׂרtenth הַבַּתa bath מִןfrom הַכֹּרeach cor עֲשֶׂרֶתten הַבַּתִּיםbaths or חֹמֶרhomer כִּיfor עֲשֶׂרֶתten הַבַּתִּיםbaths are equivalent חֹמֶרhomer

The prescribed portion of oil, measured by the bath, is a tenth of a bath from each cor (a cor consists of ten baths or one homer, since ten baths are equivalent to a homer).

45:15

וְשֶׂהand sheep אַחַתone מִןfrom הַצֹּאןeach flock מִןof הַמָּאתַיִםtwo hundred מִמַּשְׁקֵהwatered pastures יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael לְמִנְחָהfor the grain offerings וּלְעוֹלָהburnt offerings וְלִשְׁלָמִיםand peace offerings לְכַפֵּרto make atonement עֲלֵיהֶםfor the people נְאֻםdeclares אֲדֹנָיLord יְהוִהGOD

And one sheep shall be given from each flock of two hundred from the well-watered pastures of Israel. These are for the grain offerings, burnt offerings, and peace offerings, to make atonement for the people, declares the Lord GOD.

45:16

כֹּלall הָעָםthe people הָאָרֶץthe land יִהְיוּparticipate אֶלto הַתְּרוּמָהthis contribution הַזֹּאתthis לַנָּשִׂיאfor the prince בְּיִשְׂרָאֵלin Israel

All the people of the land must participate in this contribution for the prince in Israel.

45:17

וְעַלand on הַנָּשִׂיאthe prince’s יִהְיֶהbe הָעוֹלוֹתthe burnt offerings וְהַמִּנְחָהgrain וְהַנֵּסֶךְand drink offerings בַּחַגִּיםfor the feasts וּבֶחֳדָשִׁיםnew Moons וּבַשַּׁבָּתוֹתand Sabbaths בְּכָלfor all מוֹעֲדֵיappointed feasts בֵּיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael הוּאhe יַעֲשֶׂהprovide אֶת[obj] הַחַטָּאתthe sin וְאֶתand [obj] הַמִּנְחָהofferings grain וְאֶתand [obj] הָעוֹלָהofferings burnt offerings וְאֶתand [obj] הַשְּׁלָמִיםpeace offerings לְכַפֵּרto make atonement בְּעַדfor בֵּיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

And it shall be the prince’s part to provide the burnt offerings, grain offerings, and drink offerings for the feasts, New Moons, and Sabbaths—for all the appointed feasts of the house of Israel. He will provide the sin offerings, grain offerings, burnt offerings, and peace offerings to make atonement for the house of Israel.

45:18

כֹּהthis אָמַרis what אֲדֹנָיLord GOD יְהוִהsays בָּרִאשׁוֹןon the first בְּאֶחָדfirst לַחֹדֶשׁthe month תִּקַּחtake פַּרbull בֶּןyoung בָּקָרbull תָּמִיםwithout blemish וְחִטֵּאתָand purify אֶת[obj] הַמִּקְדָּשׁthe sanctuary

This is what the Lord GOD says: ‘On the first day of the first month you are to take a young bull without blemish and purify the sanctuary.

45:19

וְלָקַחtake הַכֹּהֵןpriest מִדַּםblood הַחַטָּאתthe sin offering וְנָתַןand put אֶלto מְזוּזַתdoorposts הַבַּיִתthe temple וְאֶלand to אַרְבַּעfour פִּנּוֹתcorners הָעֲזָרָהthe ledge לַמִּזְבֵּחַof the altar וְעַלand on מְזוּזַתgateposts שַׁעַרgate הֶחָצֵרcourt הַפְּנִימִיתthe inner

And the priest is to take some of the blood from the sin offering and put it on the doorposts of the temple, on the four corners of the ledge of the altar, and on the gateposts of the inner court.

45:20

וְכֵןsame תַּעֲשֶׂהdo בְּשִׁבְעָהon the seventh בַחֹדֶשׁthe month מֵאִישׁfor anyone שֹׁגֶהstrays unintentionally וּמִפֶּתִיor in ignorance וְכִפַּרְתֶּםmake atonement אֶת[obj] הַבָּיִתthe temple

You must do the same thing on the seventh day of the month for anyone who strays unintentionally or in ignorance. In this way you will make atonement for the temple.

45:21

בָּרִאשׁוֹןfirst בְּאַרְבָּעָהfourteenth עָשָׂרfourteenth יוֹםday לַחֹדֶשׁof the month יִהְיֶהare לָכֶםto observe הַפָּסַחthe Passover חָגfeast שְׁבֻעוֹתseven יָמִיםdays during מַצּוֹתunleavened bread יֵאָכֵלeaten

On the fourteenth day of the first month you are to observe the Passover, a feast of seven days, during which unleavened bread shall be eaten.

45:22

וְעָשָׂהshall provide הַנָּשִׂיאthe prince בַּיּוֹםon that day הַהוּאthat בַּעֲדוֹfor himself וּבְעַדand for כָּלall עַםpeople הָאָרֶץthe land פַּרbull חַטָּאתsin

On that day the prince shall provide a bull as a sin offering for himself and for all the people of the land.

45:23

וְשִׁבְעַתseven יְמֵיdays הֶחָגthe feast יַעֲשֶׂהprovide עוֹלָהburnt offering לַיהוָהto the LORD שִׁבְעַתseven פָּרִיםbulls וְשִׁבְעַתand seven אֵילִיםrams תְּמִימִםwithout blemish לַיּוֹםeach day שִׁבְעַתseven הַיָּמִיםduring וְחַטָּאתsin offering שְׂעִירmale עִזִּיםgoat לַיּוֹםto the day

Each day during the seven days of the feast, he shall provide seven bulls and seven rams without blemish as a burnt offering to the LORD, along with a male goat for a sin offering.

45:24

וּמִנְחָהalso provide אֵיפָהephah לַפָּרfor each bull וְאֵיפָהand an ephah לָאַיִלfor each ram יַעֲשֶׂהdo וְשֶׁמֶןolive oil הִיןhin לָאֵיפָהfor each ephah

He shall also provide as a grain offering an ephah for each bull and an ephah for each ram, along with a hin of olive oil for each ephah of grain.

45:25

בַּשְּׁבִיעִיthe seventh בַּחֲמִשָּׁהfifteenth עָשָׂרfifteenth יוֹםday לַחֹדֶשׁthat begins בֶּחָגthe feast יַעֲשֶׂהmake כָאֵלֶּהthe שִׁבְעַתseven הַיָּמִיםdays כַּחַטָּאתfor sin כָּעֹלָהofferings burnt offerings וְכַמִּנְחָהgrain offerings וְכַשָּׁמֶןand oil

During the seven days of the feast that begins on the fifteenth day of the seventh month, he is to make the same provision for sin offerings, burnt offerings, grain offerings, and oil.’
