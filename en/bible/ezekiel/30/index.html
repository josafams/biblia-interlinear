Ezekiel 30

The day of Egypt

30:1

Again the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

30:2

“Son of man, prophesy and declare that this is what the Lord GOD says: Wail, ‘Alas for that day!’

30:3

For the day is near, the Day of the LORD is near. It will be a day of clouds, a time of doom for the nations.

30:4

וּבָאָהwill come חֶרֶבsword בְּמִצְרַיִםagainst Egypt וְהָיְתָהbe חַלְחָלָהanguish בְּכוּשׁin Cush בִּנְפֹלfall חָלָלslain בְּמִצְרָיִםin Egypt וְלָקְחוּtaken הֲמוֹנָהּits wealth וְנֶהֶרְסוּtorn down יְסוֹדֹתֶיהָits foundations

A sword will come against Egypt, and there will be anguish in Cush when the slain fall in Egypt, its wealth is taken away, and its foundations are torn down.

30:5

כּוּשׁCush וּפוּטPut וְלוּדand Lud וְכָלand all הָעֶרֶבthe various peoples וְכוּבwell as Libya וּבְנֵיand the men אֶרֶץland הַבְּרִיתthe covenant אִתָּםwith Egypt בַּחֶרֶבby the sword יִפֹּלוּfall

Cush, Put, and Lud, and all the various peoples, as well as Libya and the men of the covenant land, will fall with Egypt by the sword.

30:6

כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD וְנָפְלוּwill fall סֹמְכֵיallies מִצְרַיִםEgypt וְיָרַדand went down גְּאוֹןproud עֻזָּהּher strength will collapse מִמִּגְדֹּלFrom Migdol סְוֵנֵהSyene בַּחֶרֶבby the sword יִפְּלוּfall בָהּin her נְאֻםdeclares אֲדֹנָיLord יְהוִהGOD

For this is what the LORD says: The allies of Egypt will fall, and her proud strength will collapse. From Migdol to Syene they will fall by the sword within her, declares the Lord GOD.

30:7

וְנָשַׁמּוּdesolate בְּתוֹךְamong אֲרָצוֹתlands נְשַׁמּוֹתdesolate וְעָרָיוand their cities will בְּתוֹךְlie among עָרִיםcities נַחֲרָבוֹתruined תִּהְיֶינָהbe

They will be desolate among desolate lands, and their cities will lie among ruined cities.

30:8

וְיָדְעוּwill know כִּיthat אֲנִיI am יְהוָהLORD בְּתִתִּיwhen I set אֵשׁfire בְּמִצְרַיִםto Egypt וְנִשְׁבְּרוּshattered כָּלall עֹזְרֶיהָher helpers

Then they will know that I am the LORD when I set fire to Egypt and all her helpers are shattered.

30:9

בַּיּוֹםon that day הַהוּאwill יֵצְאוּgo מַלְאָכִיםmessengers מִלְּפָנַיfrom to my before בַּצִּיםin ships לְהַחֲרִידto frighten אֶת[obj] כּוּשׁCush בֶּטַחcomplacency וְהָיְתָהwill come חַלְחָלָהanguish בָהֶםin them בְּיוֹםon the day מִצְרַיִםEgypt’s כִּיfor הִנֵּהindeed בָּאָהcoming

On that day messengers will go out from Me in ships to frighten Cush out of complacency. Anguish will come upon them on the day of Egypt’s doom. For it is indeed coming.

30:10

כֹּהthis אָמַרis what אֲדֹנָיLord GOD יְהוִהsays וְהִשְׁבַּתִּיend אֶת[obj] הֲמוֹןhordes מִצְרַיִםEgypt בְּיַדby the hand נְבוּכַדְרֶאצַּרNebuchadnezzar מֶלֶךְking בָּבֶלBabylon

This is what the Lord GOD says: I will put an end to the hordes of Egypt by the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon.

30:11

הוּאhe וְעַמּוֹand his people אִתּוֹwith him עָרִיצֵיruthless גוֹיִםnations מוּבָאִיםbrought לְשַׁחֵתto destroy הָאָרֶץthe land וְהֵרִיקוּthey will draw חַרְבוֹתָםtheir swords עַלagainst מִצְרַיִםEgypt וּמָלְאוּand fill אֶת[obj] הָאָרֶץthe land חָלָלslain

He and his people with him, the most ruthless of the nations, will be brought in to destroy the land. They will draw their swords against Egypt and fill the land with the slain.

30:12

וְנָתַתִּיwill make יְאֹרִיםstreams חָרָבָהdry וּמָכַרְתִּיand sell אֶת[obj] הָאָרֶץthe land בְּיַדin hand רָעִיםwicked וַהֲשִׁמֹּתִיwill bring desolation אֶרֶץland וּמְלֹאָהּand everything it בְּיַדhands זָרִיםforeigners אֲנִיI יְהוָהLORD דִּבַּרְתִּיspoken

I will make the streams dry up and sell the land to the wicked. By the hands of foreigners I will bring desolation upon the land and everything in it. I, the LORD, have spoken.

30:13

כֹּהthis אָמַרis what אֲדֹנָיLord GOD יְהוִהsays וְהַאֲבַדְתִּיwill destroy גִלּוּלִיםidols וְהִשְׁבַּתִּיend אֱלִילִיםimages מִנֹּףin Memphis וְנָשִׂיאprince מֵאֶרֶץfrom land מִצְרַיִםEgypt לֹאno יִהְיֶהbe עוֹדlonger וְנָתַתִּיand I will instill יִרְאָהfear בְּאֶרֶץin that land מִצְרָיִםEgypt

This is what the Lord GOD says: I will destroy the idols and put an end to the images in Memphis. There will no longer be a prince in Egypt, and I will instill fear in that land.

30:14

וַהֲשִׁמֹּתִיwill lay waste אֶת[obj] פַּתְרוֹסPathros וְנָתַתִּיset אֵשׁfire בְּצֹעַןto Zoan וְעָשִׂיתִיand execute שְׁפָטִיםjudgment בְּנֹאon Thebes

I will lay waste Pathros, set fire to Zoan, and execute judgment on Thebes.

30:15

וְשָׁפַכְתִּיwill pour חֲמָתִיmy wrath עַלon סִיןPelusium מָעוֹזstronghold מִצְרָיִםEgypt וְהִכְרַתִּיand cut off אֶת[obj] הֲמוֹןcrowds נֹאThebes

I will pour out My wrath on Pelusium, the stronghold of Egypt, and cut off the crowds of Thebes.

30:16

וְנָתַתִּיI will set אֵשׁfire בְּמִצְרַיִםto Egypt חוּלwrithe תָּחוּלanguish סִיןPelusium וְנֹאThebes תִּהְיֶהbe לְהִבָּקֵעַsplit open וְנֹףand Memphis צָרֵיdistress יוֹמָםday

I will set fire to Egypt, Pelusium will writhe in anguish, Thebes will be split open, and Memphis will face daily distress.

30:17

בַּחוּרֵיyoung men אָוֶןmen וּפִיand Pi בֶסֶתbeseth בַּחֶרֶבby the sword יִפֹּלוּfall וְהֵנָּהand they בַּשְּׁבִיwill into captivity תֵלַכְנָהgo

The young men of On and Pi-beseth will fall by the sword, and those cities will go into captivity.

30:18

וּבִתְחַפְנְחֵסin Tahpanhes חָשַׂךְdarkened הַיּוֹםthe day בְּשִׁבְרִיwhen I break שָׁםthere אֶת[obj] מֹטוֹתyoke מִצְרַיִםEgypt וְנִשְׁבַּתend בָּהּto גְּאוֹןproud עֻזָּהּstrength הִיאa עָנָןcloud יְכַסֶּנָּהcover her וּבְנוֹתֶיהָand her daughters בַּשְּׁבִיinto captivity תֵלַכְנָהgo

The day will be darkened in Tahpanhes when I break the yoke of Egypt and her proud strength comes to an end. A cloud will cover her, and her daughters will go into captivity.

30:19

וְעָשִׂיתִיexecute שְׁפָטִיםjudgment בְּמִצְרָיִםon Egypt וְיָדְעוּknow כִּיthat אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD

So I will execute judgment on Egypt, and they will know that I am the LORD.”

30:20

וַיְהִיand was בְּאַחַתin one עֶשְׂרֵהeleventh שָׁנָהyear בָּרִאשׁוֹןfirst בְּשִׁבְעָהseventh לַחֹדֶשׁthe month הָיָהwas דְבַרword יְהוָהLORD אֵלַיto לֵאמֹרto saying

In the eleventh year, on the seventh day of the first month, the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

30:21

בֶּןson אָדָםman אֶת[obj] זְרוֹעַarm פַּרְעֹהPharaoh מֶלֶךְking מִצְרַיִםEgypt שָׁבָרְתִּיbroken וְהִנֵּהsee לֹאnot חֻבְּשָׁהbound לָתֵתto give רְפֻאוֹתhealing לָשׂוּםor splinted חִתּוּלstrength לְחָבְשָׁהּsplinted לְחָזְקָהּto repair her לִתְפֹּשׂto hold בֶּחָרֶבthe sword

“Son of man, I have broken the arm of Pharaoh king of Egypt. See, it has not been bound up for healing, or splinted for strength to hold the sword.

30:22

Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Behold, I am against Pharaoh king of Egypt. I will break his arms, both the strong one and the one already broken, and will make the sword fall from his hand.

30:23

וַהֲפִצוֹתִיwill disperse אֶת[obj] מִצְרַיִםEgyptians בַּגּוֹיִםamong the nations וְזֵרִיתִםand scatter them בָּאֲרָצוֹתthroughout the lands

I will disperse the Egyptians among the nations and scatter them throughout the lands.

30:24

I will strengthen the arms of Babylon’s king and place My sword in his hand, but I will break the arms of Pharaoh, who will groan before him like a mortally wounded man.

I will strengthen the arms of Babylon’s king, but Pharaoh’s arms will fall limp. Then they will know that I am the LORD, when I place My sword in the hand of Babylon’s king, and he wields it against the land of Egypt.

30:26

וַהֲפִצוֹתִיwill disperse אֶת[obj] מִצְרַיִםEgyptians בַּגּוֹיִםamong the nations וְזֵרִיתִיand scatter אוֹתָםthem בָּאֲרָצוֹתthroughout the lands וְיָדְעוּknow כִּיthat אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD

I will disperse the Egyptians among the nations and scatter them throughout the lands. Then they will know that I am the LORD.”
