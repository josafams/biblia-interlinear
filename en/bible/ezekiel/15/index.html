Ezekiel 15

The useless wood of the vine

Open in the interlinear reader

15:1

וַיְהִיand was דְבַרword יְהוָהLORD אֵלַיto me לֵאמֹרto saying

Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

15:2

בֶּןson אָדָםman מַהhow יִּהְיֶהbe עֵץwood הַגֶּפֶןthe vine מִכָּלsurpass any עֵץtrees הַזְּמוֹרָהother branch אֲשֶׁרthat הָיָהwas בַּעֲצֵיamong the trees in הַיָּעַרthe forest

“Son of man, how does the wood of the vine surpass any other branch among the trees in the forest?

15:3

הֲיֻקַּחtaken מִמֶּנּוּfrom it עֵץwood לַעֲשׂוֹתto make לִמְלָאכָהuseful אִםor יִקְחוּmarry מִמֶּנּוּfrom it יָתֵדpeg לִתְלוֹתto hang עָלָיוon כָּלall כֶּלִיutensils

Can wood be taken from it to make something useful? Or can one make from it a peg on which to hang utensils?

15:4

הִנֵּהbehold לָאֵשׁthe fire נִתַּןcast לְאָכְלָהfor fuel אֵת[obj] שְׁנֵיboth קְצוֹתָיוends אָכְלָהdevours הָאֵשׁthe fire וְתוֹכוֹand the middle is נָחָרcharred הֲיִצְלַחuseful לִמְלָאכָהfor anything

No, it is cast into the fire for fuel. The fire devours both ends, and the middle is charred. Can it be useful for anything?

15:5

הִנֵּהbehold בִּהְיוֹתוֹwhen it was תָמִיםwhole לֹאnot יֵעָשֶׂהmade לִמְלָאכָהuseful אַףhow much כִּיfor אֵשׁfire אֲכָלַתְהוּconsumed it וַיֵּחָרand charred וְנַעֲשָׂהit עוֹדlonger לִמְלָאכָהbe useful

Even when it was whole, it could not be made useful. How much less can it ever be useful when the fire has consumed it and charred it!

15:6

לָכֵןtherefore כֹּהthis אָמַרis what אֲדֹנָיLord GOD יְהוִהsays כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had עֵץwood הַגֶּפֶןthe vine בְּעֵץamong the trees הַיַּעַרthe forest אֲשֶׁרwhich נְתַתִּיוgiven לָאֵשׁto the fire לְאָכְלָהfor fuel כֵּןso נָתַתִּיgive אֶת[obj] יֹשְׁבֵיpeople יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem

Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: ‘Like the wood of the vine among the trees of the forest, which I have given to the fire for fuel, so I will give up the people of Jerusalem.

15:7

וְנָתַתִּיset אֶת[obj] פָּנַיmy face בָּהֶםagainst them מֵהָאֵשׁescaped the fire יָצָאוּcame וְהָאֵשׁyet another fire תֹּאכְלֵםconsume them וִידַעְתֶּםknow כִּיthat אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD בְּשׂוּמִיwhen I set my אֶת[obj] פָּנַיface בָּהֶםagainst them

And I will set My face against them. Though they may have escaped the fire, yet another fire will consume them. And when I set My face against them, you will know that I am the LORD.

15:8

וְנָתַתִּיwill make אֶת[obj] הָאָרֶץthe land שְׁמָמָהdesolate יַעַןbecause מָעֲלוּacted unfaithfully מַעַלunfaithfully נְאֻםdeclares אֲדֹנָיLord יְהוִהGOD

Thus I will make the land desolate, because they have acted unfaithfully,’ declares the Lord GOD.”
