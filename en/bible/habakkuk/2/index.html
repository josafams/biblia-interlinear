Habakkuk 2

God's answer and the five woes

Open in the interlinear reader

2:1

עַלon מִשְׁמַרְתִּיmy guard אֶעֱמֹדָהpost וְאֶתְיַצְּבָהand station עַלon מָצוֹרramparts וַאֲצַפֶּהwill watch לִרְאוֹתto see מַהwhat יְדַבֶּרsay בִּיto me וּמָהand how אָשִׁיבanswer עַלon תּוֹכַחְתִּיcorrected

I will stand at my guard post and station myself on the ramparts. I will watch to see what He will say to me, and how I should answer when corrected.

2:2

וַיַּעֲנֵנִיanswered me יְהוָהLORD וַיֹּאמֶרand said כְּתוֹבwrite חָזוֹןvision וּבָאֵרand clearly inscribe עַלon הַלֻּחוֹתtablets לְמַעַןso יָרוּץrun קוֹרֵאherald בוֹwith it

Then the LORD answered me: “Write down this vision and clearly inscribe it on tablets, so that a herald may run with it.

2:3

כִּיfor עוֹדlonger חָזוֹןvision לַמּוֹעֵדawaits an appointed time וְיָפֵחַit testifies לַקֵּץof the end וְלֹאand does not יְכַזֵּבlie אִםif יִתְמַהְמָהּlingers חַכֵּהwait לוֹfor it כִּיfor בֹאsurely יָבֹאcome לֹאnot יְאַחֵרdelay

For the vision awaits an appointed time; it testifies of the end and does not lie. Though it lingers, wait for it, since it will surely come and will not delay.

2:4

הִנֵּהlook עֻפְּלָהproud לֹאnot יָשְׁרָהupright נַפְשׁוֹhis soul בּוֹin him וְצַדִּיקbut the righteous בֶּאֱמוּנָתוֹwill by faith יִחְיֶהlive

Look at the proud one; his soul is not upright— but the righteous will live by faith—

2:5

וְאַףand כִּיfor הַיַּיִןthe wine בּוֹגֵדbetrays גֶּבֶרhim יָהִירarrogant וְלֹאnever יִנְוֶהrest אֲשֶׁרthat הִרְחִיבenlarges כִּשְׁאוֹלlike Sheol נַפְשׁוֹhis appetite וְהוּאis כַמָּוֶתlike death וְלֹאhe never יִשְׂבָּעsatisfied וַיֶּאֱסֹףhe gathers אֵלָיוto himself כָּלall הַגּוֹיִםthe nations וַיִּקְבֹּץand collects אֵלָיוto him כָּלall הָעַמִּיםthe peoples

and wealth indeed betrays him. He is an arrogant man never at rest. He enlarges his appetite like Sheol, and like Death, he is never satisfied. He gathers all the nations to himself and collects all the peoples as his own.

2:6

הֲלוֹאwill not אֵלֶּהthese כֻלָּםall עָלָיוagainst him מָשָׁלspeaking יִשָּׂאוּtake וּמְלִיצָהwith mockery חִידוֹתdifficult questions לוֹto him וְיֹאמַרand derision הוֹיWoe הַמַּרְבֶּהwho amasses לֹּאnot לוֹto him עַדhow מָתַיlong וּמַכְבִּידmany עָלָיוon עַבְטִיטrich

Will not all of these take up a taunt against him, speaking with mockery and derision: ‘Woe to him who amasses what is not his and makes himself rich with many loans! How long will this go on?’

2:7

הֲלוֹאwill not פֶתַעsuddenly יָקוּמוּarise נֹשְׁכֶיךָyour creditors וְיִקְצוּdisturb מְזַעְזְעֶיךָyou awaken you וְהָיִיתָthen will become לִמְשִׁסּוֹתprey לָמוֹto them

Will not your creditors suddenly arise and those who disturb you awaken? Then you will become their prey.

2:8

כִּיbecause אַתָּהyou שַׁלּוֹתָplundered גּוֹיִםnations רַבִּיםmany יְשָׁלּוּךָplunder you כָּלall יֶתֶרremnant עַמִּיםpeople מִדְּמֵיbloodshed אָדָםman וַחֲמַסand your violence אֶרֶץland קִרְיָהcity וְכָלand all יֹשְׁבֵיdwellers בָהּin her

Because you have plundered many nations, the remnant of the people will plunder you— because of your bloodshed against man and your violence against the land, the city, and all their dwellers.

2:9

הוֹיWoe בֹּצֵעַunjust בֶּצַעunjust gain רָעevil לְבֵיתוֹhis house by לָשׂוּםto place בַּמָּרוֹםon high קִנּוֹhis nest לְהִנָּצֵלand escape מִכַּףthe hand רָעdisaster

Woe to him who builds his house by unjust gain, to place his nest on high and escape the hand of disaster!

2:10

יָעַצְתָּplotted בֹּשֶׁתshame לְבֵיתֶךָfor your house קְצוֹתcutting עַמִּיםpeoples רַבִּיםmany וְחוֹטֵאand forfeiting נַפְשֶׁךָyour life

You have plotted shame for your house by cutting off many peoples and forfeiting your life.

2:11

כִּיfor אֶבֶןstones מִקִּירfrom the wall תִּזְעָקcry out וְכָפִיסand the rafters מֵעֵץecho from יַעֲנֶנָּהwoodwork

For the stones will cry out from the wall, and the rafters will echo it from the woodwork.

2:12

הוֹיWoe בֹּנֶהbuilds עִירcity בְּדָמִיםwith bloodshed וְכוֹנֵןand establishes קִרְיָהtown בְּעַוְלָהby iniquity

Woe to him who builds a city with bloodshed and establishes a town by iniquity!

2:13

הֲלוֹאis it not הִנֵּהindeed מֵאֵתfrom the יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts וְיִיגְעוּlabor עַמִּיםpeople בְּדֵיonly feeds אֵשׁfire וּלְאֻמִּיםand the nations בְּדֵיthemselves in רִיקvain יִעָפוּweary

Is it not indeed from the LORD of Hosts that the labor of the people only feeds the fire, and the nations weary themselves in vain?

2:14

כִּיfor תִּמָּלֵאfilled הָאָרֶץthe earth לָדַעַתknowledge אֶת[obj] כְּבוֹדglory יְהוָהLORD כַּמַּיִםas the waters יְכַסּוּcover עַלon יָםsea

For the earth will be filled with the knowledge of the glory of the LORD as the waters cover the sea.

2:15

הוֹיWoe מַשְׁקֵהdrink רֵעֵהוּhis neighbors מְסַפֵּחַpouring חֲמָתְךָwineskin וְאַףand שַׁכֵּרdrunk לְמַעַןorder הַבִּיטgaze עַלon מְעוֹרֵיהֶםtheir nakedness

Woe to him who gives drink to his neighbors, pouring it from the wineskin until they are drunk, in order to gaze at their nakedness!

2:16

שָׂבַעְתָּfilled קָלוֹןshame מִכָּבוֹדinstead of glory שְׁתֵהdrink גַםtoo אַתָּהyou וְהֵעָרֵלand expose תִּסּוֹבaround עָלֶיךָto כּוֹסcup יְמִיןright hand יְהוָהLORD’s וְקִיקָלוֹןand utter disgrace עַלon כְּבוֹדֶךָyour glory

You will be filled with shame instead of glory. You too must drink and expose your uncircumcision! The cup in the LORD’s right hand will come around to you, and utter disgrace will cover your glory.

2:17

כִּיfor חֲמַסviolence לְבָנוֹןLebanon יְכַסֶּךָּoverwhelm you וְשֹׁדand the destruction בְּהֵמוֹתanimals יְחִיתַןterrify מִדְּמֵיbloodshed אָדָםmen וַחֲמַסand your violence אֶרֶץland קִרְיָהcity וְכָלand all יֹשְׁבֵיdwellers בָהּin her

For your violence against Lebanon will overwhelm you, and the destruction of animals will terrify you, because of your bloodshed against men and your violence against the land, the city, and all their dwellers.

2:18

מָהwhat הוֹעִילuse פֶּסֶלidol כִּיthat פְסָלוֹcraftsman יֹצְרוֹcarve it מַסֵּכָהimage וּמוֹרֶהteacher שָּׁקֶרlies כִּיfor בָטַחtrusts יֹצֵרmaker יִצְרוֹhis inclination עָלָיוon him לַעֲשׂוֹתmakes אֱלִילִיםidols אִלְּמִיםcannot

What use is an idol, that a craftsman should carve it— or an image, a teacher of lies? For its maker trusts in his own creation; he makes idols that cannot speak.

2:19

הוֹיWoe אֹמֵרsays לָעֵץto wood הָקִיצָהawake עוּרִיor לְאֶבֶןor to stone דּוּמָםsilent arise הוּאhe יוֹרֶהgive guidance הִנֵּהbehold הוּאis תָּפוּשׂoverlaid זָהָבgold וָכֶסֶףand silver וְכָלall רוּחַbreath אֵיןno בְּקִרְבּוֹin his among

Woe to him who says to wood, ‘Awake!’ or to silent stone, ‘Arise!’ Can it give guidance? Behold, it is overlaid with gold and silver, yet there is no breath in it at all.”

2:20

וַיהוָהbut the LORD בְּהֵיכַלtemple קָדְשׁוֹhis holy הַסsilent מִפָּנָיוbefore him כָּלall הָאָרֶץthe earth

But the LORD is in His holy temple; let all the earth be silent before Him.
