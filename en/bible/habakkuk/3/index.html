Habakkuk 3

The prayer of Habakkuk

Open in the interlinear reader

3:1

תְּפִלָּהprayer לַחֲבַקּוּקHabakkuk הַנָּבִיאthe prophet עַלto שִׁגְיֹנוֹתShigionoth

This is a prayer of Habakkuk the prophet, according to Shigionoth:

3:2

יְהוָהLORD שָׁמַעְתִּיheard שִׁמְעֲךָreport of you יָרֵאתִיawe יְהוָהLORD פָּעָלְךָyour deeds בְּקֶרֶבthese שָׁנִיםyears חַיֵּיהוּrevive them בְּקֶרֶבthem שָׁנִיםyears תּוֹדִיעַknown בְּרֹגֶזwrath רַחֵםmercy תִּזְכּוֹרremember

O LORD, I have heard the report of You; I stand in awe, O LORD, of Your deeds. Revive them in these years; make them known in these years. In Your wrath, remember mercy!

3:3

אֱלוֹהַGod מִתֵּימָןfrom Teman יָבוֹאcame וְקָדוֹשׁand the holy מֵהַרfrom Mount פָּארָןParan סֶלָהSelah כִּסָּהcovered שָׁמַיִםheavens הוֹדוֹhis glory וּתְהִלָּתוֹand his praise מָלְאָהfilled הָאָרֶץthe earth

God came from Teman, and the Holy One from Mount Paran. Selah His glory covered the heavens, and His praise filled the earth.

3:4

וְנֹגַהּradiance כָּאוֹרlike the sunlight תִּהְיֶהrays קַרְנַיִםflashed מִיָּדוֹfrom his hand לוֹto him וְשָׁםwhere חֶבְיוֹןhidden עֻזֹּהhis power

His radiance was like the sunlight; rays flashed from His hand, where His power is hidden.

3:5

לְפָנָיוbefore him יֵלֶךְwent דָּבֶרplague וְיֵצֵאand fever רֶשֶׁףfollowed לְרַגְלָיוin his steps

Plague went before Him, and fever followed in His steps.

3:6

עָמַדstood וַיְמֹדֶדand measured אֶרֶץearth רָאָהlooked וַיַּתֵּרand startled גּוֹיִםnations וַיִּתְפֹּצְצוּancient הַרְרֵיmountains עַדcrumbled שַׁחוּperpetual גִּבְעוֹתhills עוֹלָםperpetual collapsed הֲלִיכוֹתcollapsed עוֹלָםeverlasting לוֹto him

He stood and measured the earth; He looked and startled the nations; the ancient mountains crumbled; the perpetual hills collapsed. His ways are everlasting.

3:7

תַּחַתin אָוֶןdistress רָאִיתִיsaw אָהֳלֵיtents כוּשָׁןCushan יִרְגְּזוּןtrembling יְרִיעוֹתcurtains אֶרֶץland מִדְיָןMidian

I saw the tents of Cushan in distress; the curtains of Midian were trembling.

3:8

הֲבִנְהָרִיםyou at rivers חָרָהangry יְהוָהLORD אִםif בַּנְּהָרִיםagainst the streams אַפֶּךָyour wrath אִםif בַּיָּםagainst the sea עֶבְרָתֶךָrage כִּיwhen תִרְכַּבrode עַלon סוּסֶיךָyour horses מַרְכְּבֹתֶיךָyour chariots יְשׁוּעָהsalvation

Were You angry at the rivers, O LORD? Was Your wrath against the streams? Did You rage against the sea when You rode on Your horses, on Your chariots of salvation?

3:9

עֶרְיָהbrandished תֵעוֹרbrandished קַשְׁתֶּךָbow you שְׁבֻעוֹתcalled מַטּוֹתmany אֹמֶרarrows סֶלָהSelah נְהָרוֹתrivers תְּבַקַּעsplit אָרֶץearth

You brandished Your bow; You called for many arrows. Selah You split the earth with rivers.

3:10

רָאוּךָsaw you יָחִילוּquaked הָרִיםmountains זֶרֶםtorrents מַיִםwater עָבָרswept נָתַןthe roared תְּהוֹםdeep קוֹלוֹits voice רוֹםon high יָדֵיהוּits hands נָשָׂאlifted

The mountains saw You and quaked; torrents of water swept by. The deep roared with its voice and lifted its hands on high.

3:11

שֶׁמֶשׁsun יָרֵחַmoon עָמַדstood זְבֻלָהstill לְאוֹרflying חִצֶּיךָyour arrows יְהַלֵּכוּwalk לְנֹגַהּat the brightness בְּרַקshining חֲנִיתֶךָyour spear

Sun and moon stood still in their places at the flash of Your flying arrows, at the brightness of Your shining spear.

3:12

בְּזַעַםin wrath תִּצְעַדmarched אָרֶץearth בְּאַףwith fury תָּדוּשׁthreshed גּוֹיִםnations

You marched across the earth with fury; You threshed the nations in wrath.

3:13

יָצָאתָwent forth לְיֵשַׁעfor the salvation עַמֶּךָyour people לְיֵשַׁעto save אֶתwith מְשִׁיחֶךָanointed you מָחַצְתָּcrushed רֹּאשׁhead מִבֵּיתof the house רָשָׁעwicked עָרוֹתstripped יְסוֹדhead עַדto צַוָּארtoe סֶלָהSelah

You went forth for the salvation of Your people, to save Your anointed. You crushed the head of the house of the wicked and stripped him from head to toe. Selah

3:14

נָקַבְתָּpierced בְמַטָּיוown spear רֹאשׁhead פְּרָזָיוhis warriors יִסְעֲרוּstormed לַהֲפִיצֵנִיto scatter us עֲלִיצֻתָםgloating כְּמוֹas לֶאֱכֹלto devour עָנִיsecretly בַּמִּסְתָּרthe weak

With his own spear You pierced his head, when his warriors stormed out to scatter us, gloating as though ready to secretly devour the weak.

3:15

דָּרַכְתָּtrampled בַיָּםthe sea סוּסֶיךָyour horses חֹמֶרchurning מַיִםwaters רַבִּיםgreat

You trampled the sea with Your horses, churning the great waters.

3:16

שָׁמַעְתִּיheard וַתִּרְגַּזand trembled בִּטְנִיwithin my לְקוֹלat the sound צָלֲלוּquivered שְׂפָתַיlips יָבוֹאentered רָקָבdecay בַּעֲצָמַיbones I וְתַחְתַּיand under me אֶרְגָּזtrembled אֲשֶׁרthat אָנוּחַpatiently לְיוֹםfor the day צָרָהdistress לַעֲלוֹתto come לְעַםupon the people יְגוּדֶנּוּinvade us

I heard and trembled within; my lips quivered at the sound. Decay entered my bones; I trembled where I stood. Yet I must wait patiently for the day of distress to come upon the people who invade us.

3:17

כִּיfor תְאֵנָהfig tree לֹאnot תִפְרָחbud וְאֵיןand no יְבוּלfruit בַּגְּפָנִיםon the vines כִּחֵשׁcrop מַעֲשֵׂהfails זַיִתolive וּשְׁדֵמוֹתand the fields לֹאno עָשָׂהproduce אֹכֶלfood גָּזַרcut מִמִּכְלָהfrom the fold צֹאןsheep וְאֵיןand no בָּקָרcattle בָּרְפָתִיםin the stalls

Though the fig tree does not bud and no fruit is on the vines, though the olive crop fails and the fields produce no food, though the sheep are cut off from the fold and no cattle are in the stalls,

3:18

וַאֲנִיyet I בַּיהוָהin the LORD אֶעְלוֹזָהexult אָגִילָהrejoice בֵּאלֹהֵיin the God יִשְׁעִיmy salvation

yet I will exult in the LORD; I will rejoice in the God of my salvation!

3:19

יְהוִהּGOD אֲדֹנָיLord is my חֵילִיstrength וַיָּשֶׂםhe makes רַגְלַיmy feet כָּאַיָּלוֹתlike those of a deer וְעַלupon בָּמוֹתַיmakes me יַדְרִכֵנִיwalk לַמְנַצֵּחַfor the choirmaster בִּנְגִינוֹתָיwith stringed instruments

GOD the Lord is my strength; He makes my feet like those of a deer; He makes me walk upon the heights! For the choirmaster. With stringed instruments.
