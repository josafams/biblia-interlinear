Habakkuk 1

The prophet's complaint and the Chaldeans

Open in the interlinear reader

1:1

הַמַּשָׂאthe burden אֲשֶׁרthat חָזָהreceived חֲבַקּוּקHabakkuk הַנָּבִיאthe prophet

This is the burden that Habakkuk the prophet received in a vision:

1:2

עַדhow אָנָהlong יְהוָהLORD שִׁוַּעְתִּיhelp וְלֹאand not or תִשְׁמָעhear אֶזְעַקcry out אֵלֶיךָto you חָמָסviolence וְלֹאnot תוֹשִׁיעַsave

How long, O LORD, must I call for help but You do not hear, or cry out to You, “Violence!” but You do not save?

1:3

לָמָּהto why תַרְאֵנִיme see אָוֶןiniquity וְעָמָלyou tolerate תַּבִּיטwrongdoing וְשֹׁדdestruction וְחָמָסand violence לְנֶגְדִּיbefore me וַיְהִיand was רִיבstrife וּמָדוֹןongoing and conflict abounds יִשָּׂאbear

Why do You make me see iniquity? Why do You tolerate wrongdoing? Destruction and violence are before me. Strife is ongoing, and conflict abounds.

1:4

עַלon כֵּןtherefore תָּפוּגparalyzed תּוֹרָהlaw וְלֹאand never יֵצֵאgoes forth לָנֶצַחnever מִשְׁפָּטjustice כִּיfor רָשָׁעwicked מַכְתִּירhem אֶת[obj] הַצַּדִּיקthe righteous עַלon כֵּןso יֵצֵאgo מִשְׁפָּטjustice מְעֻקָּלperverted

Therefore the law is paralyzed, and justice never goes forth. For the wicked hem in the righteous, so that justice is perverted.

1:5

רְאוּlook בַגּוֹיִםat the nations וְהַבִּיטוּand observe וְהִתַּמְּהוּutterly תְּמָהוּastounded כִּיfor פֹעַלwork פֹּעֵלworkers בִּימֵיכֶםin your days that you לֹאnever תַאֲמִינוּbelieve כִּיfor יְסֻפָּרtold

“Look at the nations and observe— be utterly astounded! For I am doing a work in your days that you would never believe even if someone told you.

1:6

כִּיfor הִנְנִיbehold I am מֵקִיםraising up אֶת[obj] הַכַּשְׂדִּיםthe Chaldeans הַגּוֹיnation הַמַּרthat ruthless וְהַנִּמְהָרand impetuous which הַהוֹלֵךְthe goes לְמֶרְחֲבֵיbreadth אֶרֶץearth לָרֶשֶׁתto seize מִשְׁכָּנוֹתdwellings לֹּאnot לוֹto him

For behold, I am raising up the Chaldeans— that ruthless and impetuous nation which marches through the breadth of the earth to seize dwellings not their own.

1:7

אָיֹםdreaded וְנוֹרָאand feared הוּאhe מִמֶּנּוּfrom מִשְׁפָּטוֹjustice וּשְׂאֵתוֹand sovereignty יֵצֵאgo

They are dreaded and feared; from themselves they derive justice and sovereignty.

1:8

וְקַלּוּswifter מִנְּמֵרִיםthan leopards סוּסָיוhorses are וְחַדּוּfiercer מִזְּאֵבֵיthan wolves עֶרֶבnight וּפָשׁוּcharge פָּרָשָׁיוtheir horsemen וּפָרָשָׁיוand their cavalry מֵרָחוֹקfrom afar יָבֹאוּcomes יָעֻפוּfly כְּנֶשֶׁרlike a vulture חָשׁswooping לֶאֱכוֹלto devour

Their horses are swifter than leopards, fiercer than wolves of the night. Their horsemen charge ahead, and their cavalry comes from afar. They fly like a vulture, swooping down to devour.

1:9

כֻּלֹּהall of them לְחָמָסon violence יָבוֹאadvance מְגַמַּתhordes פְּנֵיהֶםtheir face קָדִימָהeast wind וַיֶּאֱסֹףthey gather כַּחוֹלlike sand שֶׁבִיprisoners

All of them come bent on violence; their hordes advance like the east wind; they gather prisoners like sand.

1:10

וְהוּאand he בַּמְּלָכִיםat kings יִתְקַלָּסjeered וְרֹזְנִיםand make rulers מִשְׂחָקobject לוֹto him הוּאhe לְכָלat every מִבְצָרfortress יִשְׂחָקlaugh וַיִּצְבֹּרand build up עָפָרsiege ramps וַיִּלְכְּדָהּto seize it

They scoff at kings and make rulers an object of scorn. They laugh at every fortress and build up siege ramps to seize it.

1:11

אָזthen חָלַףsweep רוּחַwind וַיַּעֲבֹרand pass וְאָשֵׁםthey are guilty זוּown כֹחוֹstrength is their לֵאלֹהוֹgod

Then they sweep by like the wind and pass on through. They are guilty; their own strength is their god.”

1:12

הֲלוֹאare not אַתָּהyou מִקֶּדֶםfrom everlasting יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהַיmy God my קְדֹשִׁיholy לֹאnot נָמוּתdie יְהוָהLORD לְמִשְׁפָּטexecute judgment שַׂמְתּוֹappointed them וְצוּרrock לְהוֹכִיחַfor correction יְסַדְתּוֹestablished them

Are You not from everlasting, O LORD, my God, my Holy One? We will not die. O LORD, You have appointed them to execute judgment; O Rock, You have established them for correction.

1:13

טְהוֹרyour עֵינַיִםeyes מֵרְאוֹתto look רָעevil וְהַבִּיטand look אֶלto עָמָלtolerate wrongdoing לֹאnot תוּכָלcannot לָמָּהso why תַבִּיטtolerate בּוֹגְדִיםfaithless תַּחֲרִישׁsilent בְּבַלַּעswallow רָשָׁעwicked צַדִּיקrighteous מִמֶּנּוּthan

Your eyes are too pure to look upon evil, and You cannot tolerate wrongdoing. So why do You tolerate the faithless? Why are You silent while the wicked swallow up those more righteous than themselves?

1:14

וַתַּעֲשֶׂהyou have made אָדָםmen כִּדְגֵיlike the fish הַיָּםthe sea כְּרֶמֶשׂlike creeping לֹאno מֹשֵׁלruler בּוֹin him

You have made men like the fish of the sea, like creeping things that have no ruler.

1:15

כֻּלֹּהall of them בְּחַכָּהwith a hook הֵעֲלָהpulls יְגֹרֵהוּcatches them בְחֶרְמוֹin his dragnet וְיַאַסְפֵהוּand gathers them בְּמִכְמַרְתּוֹin his fishing net עַלon כֵּןso יִשְׂמַחrejoices וְיָגִילhe rejoices gladly

The foe pulls all of them up with a hook; he catches them in his dragnet, and gathers them in his fishing net; so he rejoices gladly.

1:16

עַלon כֵּןtherefore יְזַבֵּחַsacrifices לְחֶרְמוֹto his dragnet וִיקַטֵּרand burns incense לְמִכְמַרְתּוֹto his fishing כִּיfor בָהֵמָּהin they שָׁמֵןthings חֶלְקוֹhis portion וּמַאֲכָלוֹand his food בְּרִאָהrich

Therefore he sacrifices to his dragnet and burns incense to his fishing net, for by these things his portion is sumptuous and his food is rich.

1:17

הַעַלfor כֵּןtherefore יָרִיקempty חֶרְמוֹhis net וְתָמִידand continue לַהֲרֹגto slay גּוֹיִםnations לֹאnot יַחְמוֹלmercy

Will he, therefore, empty his net and continue to slay nations without mercy?
