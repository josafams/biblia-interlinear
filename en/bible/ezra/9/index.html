After these things had been accomplished, the leaders approached me and said, “The people of Israel, including the priests and Levites, have not kept themselves separate from the surrounding peoples whose abominations are like those of the Canaanites, Hittites, Perizzites, Jebusites, Ammonites, Moabites, Egyptians, and Amorites.

9:2

כִּיfor נָשְׂאוּIsraelites מִבְּנֹתֵיהֶםsome of their daughters as wives לָהֶםfor themselves וְלִבְנֵיהֶםand their sons וְהִתְעָרְבוּmixed זֶרַעseed הַקֹּדֶשׁthe holy בְּעַמֵּיwith the people הָאֲרָצוֹתthe land וְיַדand hand הַשָּׂרִיםthe leaders וְהַסְּגָנִיםand officials הָיְתָהwas בַּמַּעַלin this unfaithfulness הַזֶּהthis רִאשׁוֹנָהlead

Indeed, the Israelites have taken some of their daughters as wives for themselves and their sons, so that the holy seed has been mixed with the people of the land. And the leaders and officials have taken the lead in this unfaithfulness!”

9:3

וּכְשָׁמְעִיwhen I heard אֶת[obj] הַדָּבָרthis report הַזֶּהthis קָרַעְתִּיtore אֶת[obj] בִּגְדִיmy tunic וּמְעִילִיand cloak וָאֶמְרְטָהpulled מִשְּׂעַרsome hair רֹאשִׁיmy head וּזְקָנִיand beard וָאֵשְׁבָהand sat מְשׁוֹמֵםhorror

When I heard this report, I tore my tunic and cloak, pulled out some hair from my head and beard, and sat down in horror.

9:4

וְאֵלַיand to me יֵאָסְפוּgathered כֹּלeveryone חָרֵדtrembled בְּדִבְרֵיat the words אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael עַלon מַעַלunfaithfulness הַגּוֹלָהthe exiles וַאֲנִיwhile I יֹשֵׁבsat מְשׁוֹמֵםhorror עַדuntil לְמִנְחַתoffering הָעָרֶבthe evening

Then everyone who trembled at the words of the God of Israel gathered around me because of the unfaithfulness of the exiles, while I sat there in horror until the evening offering.

9:5

וּבְמִנְחַתoffering הָעֶרֶבthe evening קַמְתִּיgot מִתַּעֲנִיתִיfrom my humiliation וּבְקָרְעִיtorn בִגְדִיmy tunic וּמְעִילִיand cloak וָאֶכְרְעָהand fell עַלon בִּרְכַּיmy knees וָאֶפְרְשָׂהspread כַפַּיmy hands אֶלto יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהָיmy God

At the evening offering, I got up from my humiliation with my tunic and cloak torn, and I fell on my knees, spread out my hands to the LORD my God,

9:6

וָאֹמְרָהand said אֱלֹהַיGod I am בֹּשְׁתִּיashamed וְנִכְלַמְתִּיand embarrassed לְהָרִיםto lift אֱלֹהַיmy God פָּנַיmy face אֵלֶיךָto you כִּיbecause עֲוֺנֹתֵינוּour iniquities רָבוּhigher לְמַעְלָהto upward toward רֹּאשׁheads וְאַשְׁמָתֵנוּand our guilt גָדְלָהgrown עַדuntil לַשָּׁמָיִםthe heavens

and said: “O my God, I am ashamed and embarrassed to lift up my face to You, my God, because our iniquities are higher than our heads, and our guilt has reached the heavens.

9:7

מִימֵיfrom the days אֲבֹתֵינוּour fathers אֲנַחְנוּwe בְּאַשְׁמָהguilt גְדֹלָהgreat עַדto הַיּוֹםthis day הַזֶּהthis וּבַעֲוֺנֹתֵינוּbecause of our iniquities נִתַּנּוּgiven אֲנַחְנוּwe מְלָכֵינוּour kings כֹהֲנֵינוּpriests בְּיַדinto the hands מַלְכֵיkings הָאֲרָצוֹתthe earth בַּחֶרֶבto the sword בַּשְּׁבִיand captivity וּבַבִּזָּהand to pillage וּבְבֹשֶׁתand humiliation פָּנִיםface כְּהַיּוֹםthis day הַזֶּהthis

From the days of our fathers to this day, our guilt has been great. Because of our iniquities, we and our kings and priests have been delivered into the hands of the kings of the earth and put to the sword and captivity, to pillage and humiliation, as we are this day.

9:8

וְעַתָּהbut now כִּמְעַטa brief רֶגַעmoment הָיְתָהcome תְחִנָּהgrace מֵאֵתfrom the יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God לְהַשְׁאִירto preserve לָנוּfor us פְּלֵיטָהremnant וְלָתֶתand to give לָנוּto us יָתֵדstake בִּמְקוֹםplace קָדְשׁוֹhis holy לְהָאִירlight to עֵינֵינוּour eyes אֱלֹהֵינוּGod וּלְתִתֵּנוּhas given us מִחְיָהnew life מְעַטlife and בְּעַבְדֻתֵנוּin our bondage

But now, for a brief moment, grace has come from the LORD our God to preserve for us a remnant and to give us a stake in His holy place. Even in our bondage, our God has given us new life and light to our eyes.

9:9

כִּיfor עֲבָדִיםslaves אֲנַחְנוּwe וּבְעַבְדֻתֵנוּin our bondage לֹאnot עֲזָבָנוּforsaken us אֱלֹהֵינוּGod has וַיַּטbut he has extended עָלֵינוּto us חֶסֶדgrace לִפְנֵיin the sight מַלְכֵיkings פָרַסPersia לָתֶתgiving לָנוּto us מִחְיָהnew life לְרוֹמֵםto rebuild אֶת[obj] בֵּיתhouse אֱלֹהֵינוּour God וּלְהַעֲמִידand repair אֶת[obj] חָרְבֹתָיוits ruins וְלָתֶתand giving לָנוּto us גָדֵרwall of protection בִּיהוּדָהin Judah וּבִירוּשָׁלִָםand Jerusalem

Though we are slaves, our God has not forsaken us in our bondage, but He has extended to us grace in the sight of the kings of Persia, giving us new life to rebuild the house of our God and repair its ruins, and giving us a wall of protection in Judah and Jerusalem.

9:10

וְעַתָּהand now מַהwhat נֹּאמַרsay אֱלֹהֵינוּour God אַחֲרֵיafter זֹאתthis כִּיfor עָזַבְנוּforsaken מִצְוֺתֶיךָcommandments

And now, our God, what can we say after this? For we have forsaken the commandments

9:11

אֲשֶׁרthat צִוִּיתָgave בְּיַדthrough עֲבָדֶיךָyour servants הַנְּבִיאִיםthe prophets לֵאמֹרsaying הָאָרֶץthe land אֲשֶׁרthat אַתֶּםyou בָּאִיםentering לְרִשְׁתָּהּto possess is a אֶרֶץland נִדָּהpolluted הִיאis בְּנִדַּתby the impurity עַמֵּיpeoples הָאֲרָצוֹתthe lands בְּתוֹעֲבֹתֵיהֶםthe abominations with which they אֲשֶׁרhave מִלְאוּהָfilled it מִפֶּהfrom end אֶלto פֶּהend בְּטֻמְאָתָםin their uncleanness

that You gave through Your servants the prophets, saying: ‘The land that you are entering to possess is a land polluted by the impurity of its peoples and the abominations with which they have filled it from end to end.

9:12

וְעַתָּהnow therefore בְּנוֹתֵיכֶםyour daughters אַלdo not תִּתְּנוּgive לִבְנֵיהֶםmarriage to their sons וּבְנֹתֵיהֶםtheir daughters אַלor תִּשְׂאוּtake לִבְנֵיכֶםfor your sons וְלֹאnever תִדְרְשׁוּseek שְׁלֹמָםtheir peace וְטוֹבָתָםor prosperity so עַדuntil עוֹלָםyou לְמַעַןmay תֶּחֶזְקוּstrong וַאֲכַלְתֶּםand may eat אֶת[obj] טוּבgood הָאָרֶץthe land וְהוֹרַשְׁתֶּםas an inheritance לִבְנֵיכֶםto your sons עַדuntil עוֹלָםforever

Now, therefore, do not give your daughters in marriage to their sons or take their daughters for your sons. Never seek their peace or prosperity, so that you may be strong and may eat the good things of the land, leaving it as an inheritance to your sons forever.’

9:13

וְאַחֲרֵיafter כָּלall הַבָּאthat has come עָלֵינוּupon us בְּמַעֲשֵׂינוּin our works הָרָעִיםevil וּבְאַשְׁמָתֵנוּand our guilt הַגְּדֹלָהgreat כִּיfor אַתָּהyou אֱלֹהֵינוּour God חָשַׂכְתָּpunished לְמַטָּהus less מֵעֲוֺנֵנוּthan our iniquities וְנָתַתָּהand have given לָּנוּto us פְּלֵיטָהremnant כָּזֹאתas this

After all that has come upon us because of our evil deeds and our great guilt (though You, our God, have punished us less than our iniquities deserve and have given us such a remnant as this),

9:14

הֲנָשׁוּבshall we again לְהָפֵרbreak מִצְוֺתֶיךָyour commandments וּלְהִתְחַתֵּןand intermarry בְּעַמֵּיwith the peoples הַתֹּעֵבוֹתabominations הָאֵלֶּהthese would הֲלוֹאnot תֶאֱנַףangry בָּנוּwith us עַדto כַּלֵּהwipe לְאֵיןno שְׁאֵרִיתremnant וּפְלֵיטָהor survivor

shall we again break Your commandments and intermarry with the peoples who commit these abominations? Would You not become so angry with us as to wipe us out, leaving no remnant or survivor?

9:15

יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael צַדִּיקrighteous אַתָּהare כִּיfor נִשְׁאַרְנוּremain פְלֵיטָהremnant כְּהַיּוֹםthis day as הַזֶּהthis הִנְנוּhere we לְפָנֶיךָare before you בְּאַשְׁמָתֵינוּin our guilt כִּיbecause אֵיןno לַעֲמוֹדcan stand לְפָנֶיךָbefore you עַלon זֹאתthis

O LORD, God of Israel, You are righteous! For we remain this day as a remnant. Here we are before You in our guilt, though because of it no one can stand before You.”
