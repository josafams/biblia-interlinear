Ezra 1

The decree of Cyrus

1:1

וּבִשְׁנַתyear אַחַתfirst לְכוֹרֶשׁof Cyrus מֶלֶךְking פָּרַסPersia לִכְלוֹתto fulfill דְּבַרword יְהוָהLORD מִפִּיspoken through יִרְמְיָהJeremiah הֵעִירstirred יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] רוּחַspirit כֹּרֶשׁCyrus מֶלֶךְking פָּרַסPersia וַיַּעֲבֶרto send קוֹלproclamation בְּכָלthroughout מַלְכוּתוֹhis kingdom וְגַםand also בְּמִכְתָּבin writing לֵאמֹרas follows

In the first year of Cyrus king of Persia, to fulfill the word of the LORD spoken through Jeremiah, the LORD stirred the spirit of Cyrus king of Persia to send a proclamation throughout his kingdom and to put it in writing as follows:

1:2

כֹּהthis אָמַרis what כֹּרֶשׁCyrus מֶלֶךְking פָּרַסPersia כֹּלall מַמְלְכוֹתkingdoms הָאָרֶץthe earth נָתַןgiven לִיto me יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod הַשָּׁמָיִםof heaven וְהוּאand he פָקַדappointed עָלַיon me לִבְנוֹתto build לוֹfor him בַיִתhouse בִּירוּשָׁלִַםat Jerusalem אֲשֶׁרthat בִּיהוּדָהin Judah

“This is what Cyrus king of Persia says: ‘The LORD, the God of heaven, who has given me all the kingdoms of the earth, has appointed me to build a house for Him at Jerusalem in Judah.

1:3

מִיwhoever בָכֶםamong you מִכָּלfrom all עַמּוֹhis people יְהִיmay אֱלֹהָיוhis God עִמּוֹwith him וְיַעַלgo לִירוּשָׁלִַםto Jerusalem אֲשֶׁרthat בִּיהוּדָהin Judah וְיִבֶןand build אֶת[obj] בֵּיתhouse יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael הוּאhe הָאֱלֹהִיםthe God אֲשֶׁרwho בִּירוּשָׁלִָםin Jerusalem

Whoever among you belongs to His people, may his God be with him, and may he go to Jerusalem in Judah and build the house of the LORD, the God of Israel; He is the God who is in Jerusalem.

1:4

וְכָלand let every הַנִּשְׁאָרsurvivor מִכָּלfrom all הַמְּקֹמוֹתwherever אֲשֶׁרthat הוּאhe גָרlives שָׁםthere יְנַשְּׂאוּהוּlift him אַנְשֵׁיmen מְקֹמוֹregion בְּכֶסֶףwith silver וּבְזָהָבgold וּבִרְכוּשׁgoods וּבִבְהֵמָהand livestock עִםwith הַנְּדָבָהa freewill offering לְבֵיתfor the house הָאֱלֹהִיםof God אֲשֶׁרthat בִּירוּשָׁלִָםin Jerusalem

And let every survivor, wherever he lives, be assisted by the men of that region with silver, gold, goods, and livestock, along with a freewill offering for the house of God in Jerusalem.’”

1:5

וַיָּקוּמוּand got רָאשֵׁיheads הָאָבוֹתthe family לִיהוּדָהof Judah וּבִנְיָמִןand Benjamin וְהַכֹּהֲנִיםalong with the priests וְהַלְוִיִּםand Levites לְכֹלeveryone הֵעִירstirred הָאֱלֹהִיםGod had אֶת[obj] רוּחוֹspirit לַעֲלוֹתto go לִבְנוֹתand rebuild אֶת[obj] בֵּיתhouse יְהוָהLORD אֲשֶׁרthat בִּירוּשָׁלִָםin Jerusalem

So the family heads of Judah and Benjamin, along with the priests and Levites—everyone whose spirit God had stirred—prepared to go up and rebuild the house of the LORD in Jerusalem.

1:6

וְכָלand all סְבִיבֹתֵיהֶםtheir neighbors חִזְּקוּsupported בִידֵיהֶםin their hands בִּכְלֵיwith articles כֶסֶףsilver בַּזָּהָבand gold בָּרְכוּשׁwith goods וּבַבְּהֵמָהand livestock וּבַמִּגְדָּנוֹתand with valuables לְבַדin addition עַלto כָּלall הִתְנַדֵּבfreewill offerings

And all their neighbors supported them with articles of silver and gold, with goods and livestock, and with valuables, in addition to all their freewill offerings.

1:7

וְהַמֶּלֶךְking also כּוֹרֶשׁCyrus הוֹצִיאbrought אֶת[obj] כְּלֵיarticles בֵיתhouse יְהוָהLORD אֲשֶׁרthat הוֹצִיאcarried נְבוּכַדְנֶצַּרNebuchadnezzar מִירוּשָׁלִַםfrom Jerusalem וַיִּתְּנֵםand placed בְּבֵיתin the temple אֱלֹהָיוhis gods

King Cyrus also brought out the articles belonging to the house of the LORD that Nebuchadnezzar had carried away from Jerusalem and placed in the temple of his gods.

1:8

וַיּוֹצִיאֵםhad them brought כּוֹרֶשׁCyrus מֶלֶךְking פָּרַסPersia עַלon יַדhand מִתְרְדָתMithredath הַגִּזְבָּרthe treasurer וַיִּסְפְּרֵםwho counted them לְשֵׁשְׁבַּצַּרto Sheshbazzar הַנָּשִׂיאthe prince לִיהוּדָהof Judah

Cyrus king of Persia had them brought out by the hand of Mithredath the treasurer, who counted them out to Sheshbazzar the prince of Judah.

1:9

וְאֵלֶּהthis מִסְפָּרָםtheir number אֲגַרְטְלֵיdishes זָהָבgold שְׁלֹשִׁים30 אֲגַרְטְלֵיdishes כֶסֶףsilver אָלֶף1 000 מַחֲלָפִיםsilver תִּשְׁעָה29 וְעֶשְׂרִיםutensils

This was the inventory: 30 gold dishes, 1,000 silver dishes, 29 silver utensils,

1:10

כְּפוֹרֵיweight זָהָבgold שְׁלֹשִׁים30 כְּפוֹרֵיbowls כֶסֶףsilver מִשְׁנִיםsecond אַרְבַּעfour מֵאוֹת1 וַעֲשָׂרָהand 1 כֵּלִיםarticles אֲחֵרִיםother אָלֶף000

30 gold bowls, 410 matching silver bowls, and 1,000 other articles.

1:11

כָּלall כֵּלִיםarticles לַזָּהָבgold וְלַכֶּסֶףand silver חֲמֵשֶׁת5 אֲלָפִים400 וְאַרְבַּע400 מֵאוֹת400 הַכֹּלall these הֶעֱלָהbrought שֵׁשְׁבַּצַּרSheshbazzar עִםwith הֵעָלוֹתwent הַגּוֹלָהthe exiles מִבָּבֶלfrom Babylon לִירוּשָׁלִָםto Jerusalem

In all, there were 5,400 gold and silver articles. Sheshbazzar brought all these along when the exiles went up from Babylon to Jerusalem.
