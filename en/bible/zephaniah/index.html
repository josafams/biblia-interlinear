Zephaniah צְפַנְיָה

Zephaniah announces the “great day of the LORD” as a day of wrath on Judah and Jerusalem, because of idolatry, violence and the indifference of those who think God does neither good nor evil. The judgment extends to the neighboring nations and to Nineveh itself. At the end, the tone changes: God will leave a humble and lowly people who trust in Him, and He will rejoice over a restored Jerusalem.

Structure

  1. The day of the LORD on Judah ch. 1

    Judgment against foreign cults and indifference in Jerusalem; the day of wrath described in strong images.

  2. Call and judgment of the nations chs. 2–3

    An invitation to seek the LORD and oracles against Philistia, Moab, Ammon, Cush and Assyria; then, against Jerusalem.

  3. Restoration ch. 3

    The peoples are purified, a humble remnant remains and Zion is called to sing.

Chapters

  1. 1 The great day of the LORD on Judah
  2. 2 A call to humility and judgment on the nations
  3. 3 Jerusalem rebuked and the remnant restored
