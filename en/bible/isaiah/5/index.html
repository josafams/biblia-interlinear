Isaiah 5

The song of the vineyard and the woes

5:1

I will sing for my beloved a song of his vineyard: My beloved had a vineyard on a very fertile hill.

5:2

וַיְעַזְּקֵהוּhe dug it וַיְסַקְּלֵהוּand cleared וַיִּטָּעֵהוּand planted the finest שֹׂרֵקvines וַיִּבֶןhe built מִגְדָּלwatchtower בְּתוֹכוֹin the middle וְגַםand also יֶקֶבwinepress חָצֵבdug בּוֹin him וַיְקַוwell waited לַעֲשׂוֹתto yield good עֲנָבִיםgrapes וַיַּעַשׂproduced בְּאֻשִׁיםsour

He dug it up and cleared the stones and planted the finest vines. He built a watchtower in the middle and dug out a winepress as well. He waited for the vineyard to yield good grapes, but the fruit it produced was sour!

5:3

וְעַתָּהand now יוֹשֵׁבdwellers יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem וְאִישׁand men יְהוּדָהJudah שִׁפְטוּjudge נָאplease בֵּינִיbetween me וּבֵיןand between כַּרְמִיmy vineyard

“And now, O dwellers of Jerusalem and men of Judah, I exhort you to judge between Me and My vineyard.

5:4

מַהwhat לַּעֲשׂוֹתdone for עוֹדmore לְכַרְמִיmy vineyard וְלֹאand not עָשִׂיתִיdid בּוֹin him מַדּוּעַwhy קִוֵּיתִיexpected לַעֲשׂוֹתsweet did עֲנָבִיםgrapes וַיַּעַשׂit bring בְּאֻשִׁיםsour fruit

What more could I have done for My vineyard than I already did for it? Why, when I expected sweet grapes, did it bring forth sour fruit?

5:5

וְעַתָּהnow אוֹדִיעָהtell נָּאplease אֶתְכֶםyou אֵת[obj] אֲשֶׁרthat אֲנִיam עֹשֶׂהdo לְכַרְמִיto my vineyard הָסֵרaway מְשׂוּכָּתוֹits hedge וְהָיָהand it will be לְבָעֵרbe consumed פָּרֹץtear גְּדֵרוֹits wall וְהָיָהbe לְמִרְמָסtrampled

Now I will tell you what I am about to do to My vineyard: I will take away its hedge, and it will be consumed; I will tear down its wall, and it will be trampled.

5:6

וַאֲשִׁיתֵהוּwill make it בָתָהwasteland לֹאnot יִזָּמֵרpruned וְלֹאand not יֵעָדֵרcultivated וְעָלָהup שָׁמִירthorns briers וָשָׁיִתthorns and וְעַלand on הֶעָבִיםthe clouds that אֲצַוֶּהcommand מֵהַמְטִירnot fall עָלָיוon it מָטָרrain

I will make it a wasteland, neither pruned nor cultivated, and thorns and briers will grow up. I will command the clouds that rain shall not fall on it.”

5:7

כִּיfor כֶרֶםvineyard יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts בֵּיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וְאִישׁand the men יְהוּדָהJudah נְטַעplant שַׁעֲשׁוּעָיוhis delight וַיְקַוhe looked לְמִשְׁפָּטfor justice וְהִנֵּהbut saw מִשְׂפָּחbloodshed לִצְדָקָהfor righteousness וְהִנֵּהand saw צְעָקָהcry

For the vineyard of the LORD of Hosts is the house of Israel, and the men of Judah are the plant of His delight. He looked for justice, but saw bloodshed; for righteousness, but heard a cry of distress.

5:8

הוֹיWoe מַגִּיעֵיadd בַיִתhouse בְּבַיִתto house שָׂדֶהfield בְשָׂדֶהto field יַקְרִיבוּjoin עַדuntil אֶפֶסno מָקוֹםplace וְהוּשַׁבְתֶּםand live לְבַדְּכֶםyou alone בְּקֶרֶבin among הָאָרֶץthe land

Woe to you who add house to house and join field to field until no place is left and you live alone in the land.

5:9

בְּאָזְנָיI heard יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts אִםif לֹאnot בָּתִּיםhouses רַבִּיםmany לְשַׁמָּהbecome desolate יִהְיוּbecome גְּדֹלִיםgreat וְטוֹבִיםmansions מֵאֵיןfrom without יוֹשֵׁבunoccupied

I heard the LORD of Hosts declare: “Surely many houses will become desolate, great mansions left unoccupied.

5:10

כִּיfor עֲשֶׂרֶתten צִמְדֵּיacres כֶרֶםvineyard יַעֲשׂוּyield בַּתbath אֶחָתone וְזֶרַעseed חֹמֶרhomer יַעֲשֶׂהdo אֵיפָהephah

For ten acres of vineyard will yield but a bath of wine, and a homer of seed only an ephah of grain.”

5:11

הוֹיWoe מַשְׁכִּימֵיrise early בַבֹּקֶרin the morning שֵׁכָרstrong drink יִרְדֹּפוּpursuit מְאַחֲרֵיlinger בַנֶּשֶׁףinto the evening יַיִןwine יַדְלִיקֵםinflamed by

Woe to those who rise early in the morning in pursuit of strong drink, who linger into the evening, to be inflamed by wine.

5:12

וְהָיָהtheir feasts כִנּוֹרlyre וָנֶבֶלand harp תֹּףtambourines וְחָלִילand flutes וָיַיִןand wine מִשְׁתֵּיהֶםthey disregard וְאֵתand [obj] פֹּעַלactions יְהוָהLORD לֹאnot יַבִּיטוּsee וּמַעֲשֵׂהwork יָדָיוhis hands לֹאnot רָאוּseen

At their feasts are the lyre and harp, tambourines and flutes and wine. They disregard the actions of the LORD and fail to see the work of His hands.

5:13

לָכֵןtherefore גָּלָהexile עַמִּיmy people מִבְּלִיfor their lack דָעַתunderstanding וּכְבוֹדוֹtheir dignitaries מְתֵיdignitaries רָעָבstarving וַהֲמוֹנוֹand their masses צִחֵהparched צָמָאthirst

Therefore My people will go into exile for their lack of understanding; their dignitaries are starving and their masses are parched with thirst.

5:14

לָכֵןtherefore הִרְחִיבָהenlarges שְּׁאוֹלSheol נַפְשָׁהּits throat וּפָעֲרָהand opens פִיהָwide its לִבְלִיto lack חֹקenormous jaws וְיָרַדand down go הֲדָרָהּZion’s nobles וַהֲמוֹנָהּand masses her וּשְׁאוֹנָהּand her uproar וְעָלֵזrevelers and carousers בָּהּin her

Therefore Sheol enlarges its throat and opens wide its enormous jaws, and down go Zion’s nobles and masses, her revelers and carousers!

5:15

וַיִּשַּׁחbrought אָדָםmankind וַיִּשְׁפַּלlow and אִישׁeach וְעֵינֵיeyes גְבֹהִיםarrogant תִּשְׁפַּלְנָהhumbled

So mankind will be brought low, and each man humbled; the arrogant will lower their eyes.

5:16

וַיִּגְבַּהexalted יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts בַּמִּשְׁפָּטby his justice וְהָאֵלand the holy God הַקָּדוֹשׁholy נִקְדָּשׁholy בִּצְדָקָהin righteousness

But the LORD of Hosts will be exalted by His justice, and the holy God will show Himself holy in righteousness.

5:17

וְרָעוּwill graze כְבָשִׂיםlambs כְּדָבְרָםas in their own pastures וְחָרְבוֹתruins מֵחִיםwealthy גָּרִיםstrangers יֹאכֵלוּfeed

Lambs will graze as in their own pastures, and strangers will feed in the ruins of the wealthy.

5:18

הוֹיWoe מֹשְׁכֵיdraw הֶעָוֺןiniquity בְּחַבְלֵיwith cords הַשָּׁוְאof deceit וְכַעֲבוֹתwith ropes הָעֲגָלָהalong cart חַטָּאָהsin

Woe to those who draw iniquity with cords of deceit and pull sin along with cart ropes,

5:19

הָאֹמְרִיםwho say יְמַהֵרhasten יָחִישָׁהhurry מַעֲשֵׂהוּhis work לְמַעַןso נִרְאֶהsee וְתִקְרַבand approaches וְתָבוֹאָהcome so עֲצַתplan קְדוֹשׁholy יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וְנֵדָעָהmay know

to those who say, “Let Him hurry and hasten His work so that we may see it! Let the plan of the Holy One of Israel come so that we may know it!”

5:20

הוֹיWoe הָאֹמְרִיםthe say לָרַעto those who call evil טוֹבgood וְלַטּוֹבand good רָעevil שָׂמִיםturn חֹשֶׁךְdarkness לְאוֹרto light וְאוֹרand light לְחֹשֶׁךְto darkness שָׂמִיםreplace מַרbitter לְמָתוֹקwith sweet וּמָתוֹקand sweet לְמָרwith bitter

Woe to those who call evil good and good evil, who turn darkness to light and light to darkness, who replace bitter with sweet and sweet with bitter.

5:21

הוֹיWoe חֲכָמִיםwise בְּעֵינֵיהֶםin their own eyes וְנֶגֶדand before פְּנֵיהֶםtheir sight נְבֹנִיםclever

Woe to those who are wise in their own eyes and clever in their own sight.

5:22

הוֹיWoe גִּבּוֹרִיםheroes לִשְׁתּוֹתin drinking יָיִןwine וְאַנְשֵׁיand men חַיִלchampions לִמְסֹךְin mixing שֵׁכָרstrong drink

Woe to those who are heroes in drinking wine and champions in mixing strong drink,

5:23

מַצְדִּיקֵיacquit רָשָׁעguilty עֵקֶבbecause שֹׁחַדbribe וְצִדְקַתjustice צַדִּיקִיםinnocent יָסִירוּremove מִמֶּנּוּof

who acquit the guilty for a bribe and deprive the innocent of justice.

5:24

לָכֵןtherefore כֶּאֱכֹלconsumes קַשׁstraw לְשׁוֹןtongue אֵשׁfire וַחֲשַׁשׁand as dry לֶהָבָהflame יִרְפֶּהtheir שָׁרְשָׁםtheir roots כַּמָּקdecay יִהְיֶהbe וּפִרְחָםand their blossoms כָּאָבָקlike dust יַעֲלֶהup כִּיfor מָאֲסוּrejected אֵת[obj] תּוֹרַתinstruction יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts וְאֵתand [obj] אִמְרַתword קְדוֹשׁholy יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael נִאֵצוּdespised

Therefore, as a tongue of fire consumes the straw, and as dry grass shrivels in the flame, so their roots will decay and their blossoms will blow away like dust; for they have rejected the instruction of the LORD of Hosts and despised the word of the Holy One of Israel.

5:25

Therefore the anger of the LORD burns against His people; His hand is raised against them to strike them down. The mountains quake, and the corpses lay like refuse in the streets. Despite all this, His anger is not turned away; His hand is still upraised.

5:26

וְנָשָׂאhe lifts נֵסbanner לַגּוֹיִםfor the nations מֵרָחוֹקdistant וְשָׁרַקand whistles לוֹfor מִקְצֵהat the ends הָאָרֶץthe earth וְהִנֵּהbehold מְהֵרָהspeedily קַלswiftly יָבוֹאcome

He lifts a banner for the distant nations and whistles for those at the ends of the earth. Behold—how speedily and swiftly they come!

5:27

אֵיןnone עָיֵףweary וְאֵיןand no כּוֹשֵׁלstumbles בּוֹin him לֹאnot יָנוּםslumbers וְלֹאor יִישָׁןsleeps וְלֹאno נִפְתַּחis אֵזוֹרbelt חֲלָצָיוloose וְלֹאand no נִתַּקbroken שְׂרוֹךְstrap נְעָלָיוsandal

None of them grows weary or stumbles; no one slumbers or sleeps. No belt is loose and no sandal strap is broken.

5:28

אֲשֶׁרthat חִצָּיוtheir arrows שְׁנוּנִיםsharpened וְכָלand all קַשְּׁתֹתָיוbows are דְּרֻכוֹתstrung פַּרְסוֹתhooves סוּסָיוhorses are כַּצַּרlike flint נֶחְשָׁבוּchariot וְגַלְגִּלָּיוwheels are כַּסּוּפָהlike a whirlwind

Their arrows are sharpened, and all their bows are strung. The hooves of their horses are like flint; their chariot wheels are like a whirlwind.

5:29

Their roaring is like that of a lion; they roar like young lions. They growl and seize their prey; they carry it away from deliverance.

5:30

In that day they will roar over it, like the roaring of the sea. If one looks over the land, he will see darkness and distress; even the light will be obscured by clouds.
