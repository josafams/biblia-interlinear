Isaiah 13

Oracle against Babylon

13:1

מַשָּׂאburden against בָּבֶלBabylon אֲשֶׁרthat חָזָהreceived יְשַׁעְיָהוּIsaiah בֶּןson אָמוֹץAmoz

This is the burden against Babylon that Isaiah son of Amoz received:

13:2

עַלon הַרhilltop נִשְׁפֶּהbarren call שְׂאוּraise נֵסbanner הָרִימוּcall קוֹלaloud לָהֶםto them הָנִיפוּwave יָדhand וְיָבֹאוּthey may enter פִּתְחֵיgates נְדִיבִיםnobles

Raise a banner on a barren hilltop; call aloud to them. Wave your hand, that they may enter the gates of the nobles.

13:3

אֲנִיI צִוֵּיתִיcommanded לִמְקֻדָּשָׁיmy sanctified גַּםeven קָרָאתִיsummoned גִבּוֹרַיmy warriors לְאַפִּיto execute my wrath עַלִּיזֵיexecute גַּאֲוָתִיmy triumph

I have commanded My sanctified ones; I have even summoned My warriors to execute My wrath and exult in My triumph.

13:4

קוֹלlisten הָמוֹןtumult בֶּהָרִיםon the mountains דְּמוּתlike עַםmultitude רָבgreat multitude קוֹלlisten שְׁאוֹןuproar מַמְלְכוֹתkingdoms גּוֹיִםnations נֶאֱסָפִיםgathered יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts מְפַקֵּדmobilizing צְבָאarmy מִלְחָמָהwar

Listen, a tumult on the mountains, like that of a great multitude! Listen, an uproar among the kingdoms, like nations gathered together! The LORD of Hosts is mobilizing an army for war.

13:5

בָּאִיםcoming מֵאֶרֶץfrom lands מֶרְחָקfaraway lands מִקְצֵהfrom the ends הַשָּׁמָיִםthe heavens יְהוָהLORD וּכְלֵיand the weapons זַעְמוֹhis wrath לְחַבֵּלto destroy כָּלwhole הָאָרֶץthe country

They are coming from faraway lands, from the ends of the heavens— the LORD and the weapons of His wrath— to destroy the whole country.

13:6

הֵילִילוּwail כִּיfor קָרוֹבnear יוֹםday יְהוָהLORD כְּשֹׁדas destruction מִשַּׁדַּיfrom the Almighty יָבוֹאcome

Wail, for the Day of the LORD is near; it will come as destruction from the Almighty.

13:7

עַלon כֵּןtherefore כָּלall יָדַיִםhands תִּרְפֶּינָהlimp וְכָלand every לְבַבheart אֱנוֹשׁman’s יִמָּסmelt

Therefore all hands will fall limp, and every man’s heart will melt.

13:8

וְנִבְהָלוּterror צִירִיםpain וַחֲבָלִיםand anguish יֹאחֵזוּןseize כַּיּוֹלֵדָהlike a woman in labor יְחִילוּןwrithe אִישׁone אֶלto רֵעֵהוּanother their יִתְמָהוּflushed פְּנֵיfaces לְהָבִיםflushed פְּנֵיהֶםfear

Terror, pain, and anguish will seize them; they will writhe like a woman in labor. They will look at one another, their faces flushed with fear.

13:9

הִנֵּהbehold יוֹםday יְהוָהLORD בָּאcoming אַכְזָרִיcruel וְעֶבְרָהwith fury וַחֲרוֹןand burning אָףanger לָשׂוּםto make הָאָרֶץthe earth לְשַׁמָּהa desolation וְחַטָּאֶיהָsinners יַשְׁמִידdestroy מִמֶּנָּהfrom her

Behold, the Day of the LORD is coming— cruel, with fury and burning anger— to make the earth a desolation and to destroy the sinners within it.

13:10

כִּיfor כוֹכְבֵיstars הַשָּׁמַיִםheaven וּכְסִילֵיהֶםand their constellations לֹאnot יָהֵלּוּgive אוֹרָםtheir light חָשַׁךְrising הַשֶּׁמֶשׁthe sun בְּצֵאתוֹin went him וְיָרֵחַand the moon לֹאnot יַגִּיהַgive אוֹרוֹits light

For the stars of heaven and their constellations will not give their light. The rising sun will be darkened, and the moon will not give its light.

13:11

וּפָקַדְתִּיwill punish עַלfor תֵּבֵלworld רָעָהevil וְעַלand for רְשָׁעִיםwicked עֲוֺנָםtheir iniquity וְהִשְׁבַּתִּיwill end גְּאוֹןhaughtiness זֵדִיםarrogant וְגַאֲוַתpride עָרִיצִיםruthless אַשְׁפִּילlow

I will punish the world for its evil and the wicked for their iniquity. I will end the haughtiness of the arrogant and lay low the pride of the ruthless.

13:12

אוֹקִירmake scarcer אֱנוֹשׁman מִפָּזthan pure gold וְאָדָםand mankind מִכֶּתֶםrarer than the gold אוֹפִירOphir

I will make man scarcer than pure gold, and mankind rarer than the gold of Ophir.

13:13

עַלon כֵּןtherefore שָׁמַיִםheavens אַרְגִּיזtremble וְתִרְעַשׁshaken הָאָרֶץthe earth מִמְּקוֹמָהּfrom its place בְּעֶבְרַתat the wrath יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts וּבְיוֹםon the day חֲרוֹןburning אַפּוֹhis anger

Therefore I will make the heavens tremble, and the earth will be shaken from its place at the wrath of the LORD of Hosts on the day of His burning anger.

13:14

וְהָיָהand be כִּצְבִיgazelle מֻדָּחhunted וּכְצֹאןlike a sheep וְאֵיןwithout מְקַבֵּץshepherd אִישׁeach אֶלto עַמּוֹhis own people יִפְנוּreturn וְאִישׁeach אֶלto אַרְצוֹhis native land יָנוּסוּflee

Like a hunted gazelle, like a sheep without a shepherd, each will return to his own people, each will flee to his native land.

13:15

כָּלwhoever הַנִּמְצָאis caught יִדָּקֵרstabbed וְכָלand whoever הַנִּסְפֶּהis captured יִפּוֹלdie בֶּחָרֶבby the sword

Whoever is caught will be stabbed, and whoever is captured will die by the sword.

13:16

וְעֹלְלֵיהֶםtheir infants יְרֻטְּשׁוּdashed pieces לְעֵינֵיהֶםto before their eyes יִשַּׁסּוּlooted בָּתֵּיהֶםtheir houses וּנְשֵׁיהֶםand their wives תִּשָּׁכַבְנָהravished

Their infants will be dashed to pieces before their eyes, their houses will be looted, and their wives will be ravished.

13:17

הִנְנִיbehold I מֵעִירstir עֲלֵיהֶםagainst them אֶת[obj] מָדָיMedes אֲשֶׁרwho כֶּסֶףsilver לֹאno יַחְשֹׁבוּregard וְזָהָבgold לֹאno יַחְפְּצוּdesire בוֹin him

Behold, I will stir up against them the Medes, who have no regard for silver and no desire for gold.

13:18

וּקְשָׁתוֹתtheir bows נְעָרִיםyoung תְּרַטַּשְׁנָהdash וּפְרִיfruit בֶטֶןwomb לֹאno יְרַחֵמוּmercy עַלon בָּנִיםsons לֹאnot תָחוּסpity עֵינָםlook

Their bows will dash young men to pieces; they will have no mercy on the fruit of the womb; they will not look with pity on the children.

13:19

וְהָיְתָהand be בָבֶלBabylon צְבִיjewel מַמְלָכוֹתkingdoms תִּפְאֶרֶתglory גְּאוֹןpride כַּשְׂדִּיםChaldeans כְּמַהְפֵּכַתoverthrown אֱלֹהִיםGod אֶת[obj] סְדֹםSodom וְאֶתand [obj] עֲמֹרָהGomorrah

And Babylon, the jewel of the kingdoms, the glory of the pride of the Chaldeans, will be overthrown by God like Sodom and Gomorrah.

13:20

לֹאnever תֵשֵׁבcontinue לָנֶצַחto forever וְלֹאand or תִשְׁכֹּןinhabited settled עַדuntil דּוֹרgeneration וָדוֹרto generation וְלֹאno יַהֵלtent שָׁםthere עֲרָבִיnomad וְרֹעִיםshepherd לֹאno יַרְבִּצוּrest שָׁםthere

She will never be inhabited or settled from generation to generation; no nomad will pitch his tent there, no shepherd will rest his flock there.

13:21

וְרָבְצוּlie שָׁםthere צִיִּיםcreatures וּמָלְאוּfill בָתֵּיהֶםhouses אֹחִיםostriches וְשָׁכְנוּwill dwell שָׁםthere בְּנוֹתher יַעֲנָהostriches וּשְׂעִירִיםand goats יְרַקְּדוּleap שָׁםthere

But desert creatures will lie down there, and howling creatures will fill her houses. Ostriches will dwell there, and wild goats will leap about.

13:22

וְעָנָהand respond אִיִּיםhyenas howl בְּאַלְמנוֹתָיוin her fortresses וְתַנִּיםand jackals בְּהֵיכְלֵיpalaces עֹנֶגluxurious Babylon’s וְקָרוֹבis לָבוֹאat hand עִתָּהּtime וְיָמֶיהָand days will לֹאnot יִמָּשֵׁכוּprolonged

Hyenas will howl in her fortresses and jackals in her luxurious palaces. Babylon’s time is at hand, and her days will not be prolonged.
