Isaiah 60

The glory of Zion among the nations

Open in the interlinear reader

60:1

קוּמִיarise אוֹרִיshine כִּיfor בָאcome אוֹרֵךְyour light וּכְבוֹדand the glory יְהוָהLORD עָלַיִךְupon you זָרָחrises

Arise, shine, for your light has come, and the glory of the LORD rises upon you.

60:2

כִּיfor הִנֵּהbehold הַחֹשֶׁךְdarkness יְכַסֶּהcovers אֶרֶץearth וַעֲרָפֶלand thick darkness לְאֻמִּיםpeoples וְעָלַיִךְupon you יִזְרַחrise יְהוָהLORD וּכְבוֹדוֹand his glory עָלַיִךְover you יֵרָאֶהappear

For behold, darkness covers the earth, and thick darkness is over the peoples; but the LORD will rise upon you, and His glory will appear over you.

60:3

וְהָלְכוּwill come גוֹיִםnations לְאוֹרֵךְto your light וּמְלָכִיםand kings לְנֹגַהּto the brightness זַרְחֵךְyour dawn

Nations will come to your light, and kings to the brightness of your dawn.

60:4

שְׂאִיlift סָבִיבaround עֵינַיִךְyour eyes וּרְאִיand look כֻּלָּםall נִקְבְּצוּgather בָאוּcome לָךְto you your בָּנַיִךְsons will come מֵרָחוֹקfrom afar יָבֹאוּcarried וּבְנֹתַיִךְand your daughters עַלon צַדarm תֵּאָמַנָהestablished

Lift up your eyes and look around: They all gather and come to you; your sons will come from afar, and your daughters will be carried on the arm.

60:5

אָזthen תִּרְאִיlook וְנָהַרְתְּand be radiant וּפָחַדtremble וְרָחַבand swell לְבָבֵךְheart כִּיbecause יֵהָפֵךְriches עָלַיִךְto you הֲמוֹןmultitude יָםsea חֵילwealth גּוֹיִםnations יָבֹאוּcome לָךְto you

Then you will look and be radiant, and your heart will tremble and swell with joy, because the riches of the sea will be brought to you, and the wealth of the nations will come to you.

60:6

שִׁפְעַתcaravans גְּמַלִּיםcamels תְּכַסֵּךְcover your בִּכְרֵיland young camels מִדְיָןMidian וְעֵיפָהand Ephah כֻּלָּםall מִשְּׁבָאfrom Sheba יָבֹאוּcome זָהָבgold וּלְבוֹנָהand frankincense יִשָּׂאוּbearing וּתְהִלֹּתpraises יְהוָהLORD יְבַשֵּׂרוּproclaiming

Caravans of camels will cover your land, young camels of Midian and Ephah, and all from Sheba will come, bearing gold and frankincense and proclaiming the praises of the LORD.

60:7

כָּלall צֹאןflocks קֵדָרKedar יִקָּבְצוּgathered לָךְto you אֵילֵיrams נְבָיוֹתNebaioth יְשָׁרְתוּנֶךְserve you יַעֲלוּgo up עַלon רָצוֹןacceptance מִזְבְּחִיmy altar וּבֵיתhouse תִּפְאַרְתִּיmy glorious אֲפָאֵרadorn

All the flocks of Kedar will be gathered to you; the rams of Nebaioth will serve you and go up on My altar with acceptance; I will adorn My glorious house.

60:8

מִיwho אֵלֶּהthese כָּעָבwho like clouds תְּעוּפֶינָהfly וְכַיּוֹנִיםlike doves אֶלto אֲרֻבֹּתֵיהֶםtheir shelters

Who are these who fly like clouds, like doves to their shelters?

60:9

כִּיfor לִיfor me אִיִּיםislands יְקַוּוּwait וָאֳנִיּוֹתwith the ships תַּרְשִׁישׁTarshish בָּרִאשֹׁנָהin the lead לְהָבִיאto bring בָנַיִךְyour children מֵרָחוֹקfrom afar כַּסְפָּםtheir silver וּזְהָבָםand gold אִתָּםwith לְשֵׁםto name יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהַיִךְyour God וְלִקְדוֹשׁthe holy יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael כִּיfor פֵאֲרָךְglorified you

Surely the islands will wait for Me, with the ships of Tarshish in the lead, to bring your children from afar, with their silver and gold, to the honor of the LORD your God, the Holy One of Israel, for He has glorified you.

60:10

וּבָנוּwill rebuild בְנֵיsons נֵכָרforeigners חֹמֹתַיִךְyour walls וּמַלְכֵיהֶםand their kings יְשָׁרְתוּנֶךְserve you כִּיfor בְקִצְפִּיin anger הִכִּיתִיךְstruck you וּבִרְצוֹנִיyet in favor I רִחַמְתִּיךְshow you mercy

Foreigners will rebuild your walls, and their kings will serve you. Although I struck you in anger, yet in favor I will show you mercy.

60:11

וּפִתְּחוּopen they שְׁעָרַיִךְyour gates תָּמִידalways יוֹמָםday וָלַיְלָהor night לֹאnever יִסָּגֵרוּshut לְהָבִיאbrought אֵלַיִךְinto you חֵילwealth גּוֹיִםnations וּמַלְכֵיהֶםwith their kings נְהוּגִיםled

Your gates will always stand open; they will never be shut, day or night, so that the wealth of the nations may be brought into you, with their kings being led in procession.

60:12

כִּיfor הַגּוֹיthe nation וְהַמַּמְלָכָהor kingdom אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot יַעַבְדוּךְserve you יֹאבֵדוּperish וְהַגּוֹיִםit will be utterly חָרֹבutterly יֶחֱרָבוּdestroyed

For the nation or kingdom that will not serve you will perish; it will be utterly destroyed.

60:13

כְּבוֹדglory הַלְּבָנוֹןof Lebanon אֵלַיִךְto you יָבוֹאcome בְּרוֹשׁcypress תִּדְהָרelm וּתְאַשּׁוּרand boxwood יַחְדָּוtogether לְפָאֵרto adorn מְקוֹםplace מִקְדָּשִׁיmy sanctuary וּמְקוֹםplace רַגְלַיmy feet אֲכַבֵּדglorify

The glory of Lebanon will come to you— its cypress, elm, and boxwood together— to adorn the place of My sanctuary, and I will glorify the place of My feet.

60:14

וְהָלְכוּand come אֵלַיִךְto you שְׁחוֹחַdowncast בְּנֵיsons מְעַנַּיִךְyour oppressors will come וְהִשְׁתַּחֲווּand bow down עַלto כַּפּוֹתfacedown רַגְלַיִךְyour feet כָּלall מְנַאֲצָיִךְreviled you וְקָרְאוּand call לָךְto you עִירcity יְהוָהLORD צִיּוֹןZion קְדוֹשׁholy יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

The sons of your oppressors will come and bow down to you; all who reviled you will fall facedown at your feet and call you the City of the LORD, Zion of the Holy One of Israel.

60:15

תַּחַתunder הֱיוֹתֵךְyou have been עֲזוּבָהforsaken וּשְׂנוּאָהand despised וְאֵיןwith no עוֹבֵרpassing וְשַׂמְתִּיךְwill make you לִגְאוֹןan pride עוֹלָםeverlasting מְשׂוֹשׂjoy דּוֹרage וָדוֹרto age

Whereas you have been forsaken and despised, with no one passing through, I will make you an everlasting pride, a joy from age to age.

60:16

וְיָנַקְתְּwill drink חֲלֵבmilk גּוֹיִםnations וְשֹׁדbreasts מְלָכִיםroyalty תִּינָקִיnurse וְיָדַעַתְּwill know כִּיthat אֲנִיI יְהוָהLORD מוֹשִׁיעֵךְyour Savior וְגֹאֲלֵךְand your Redeemer אֲבִירmighty one יַעֲקֹבJacob

You will drink the milk of nations and nurse at the breasts of royalty; you will know that I, the LORD, am your Savior and your Redeemer, the Mighty One of Jacob.

60:17

תַּחַתinstead הַנְּחֹשֶׁתof bronze אָבִיאbring זָהָבgold וְתַחַתplace הַבַּרְזֶלof iron אָבִיאbring כֶסֶףsilver וְתַחַתinstead הָעֵצִיםof wood נְחֹשֶׁתbronze וְתַחַתand instead הָאֲבָנִיםof stones בַּרְזֶלiron וְשַׂמְתִּיwill appoint פְקֻדָּתֵךְyour governor שָׁלוֹםpeace וְנֹגְשַׂיִךְyour ruler צְדָקָהrighteousness

Instead of bronze I will bring you gold; I will bring silver in place of iron, bronze instead of wood, and iron instead of stones. I will appoint peace as your governor and righteousness as your ruler.

60:18

לֹאno יִשָּׁמַעheard עוֹדlonger חָמָסviolence בְּאַרְצֵךְin your land שֹׁדruin וָשֶׁבֶרor destruction בִּגְבוּלָיִךְwithin your borders וְקָרָאתname יְשׁוּעָהsalvation חוֹמֹתַיִךְyour walls וּשְׁעָרַיִךְand your gates תְּהִלָּהpraise

No longer will violence be heard in your land, nor ruin or destruction within your borders. But you will name your walls Salvation and your gates Praise.

60:19

לֹאno יִהְיֶהbe לָּךְto you עוֹדlonger הַשֶּׁמֶשׁthe sun לְאוֹרbe light יוֹמָםby day וּלְנֹגַהּnor the brightness הַיָּרֵחַthe moon לֹאnot יָאִירshine לָךְto you וְהָיָהbe לָךְto you יְהוָהLORD לְאוֹרlight עוֹלָםeverlasting וֵאלֹהַיִךְand your God לְתִפְאַרְתֵּךְbe your splendor

No longer will the sun be your light by day, nor the brightness of the moon shine on your night; for the LORD will be your everlasting light, and your God will be your splendor.

60:20

לֹאno יָבוֹאcome עוֹדlonger שִׁמְשֵׁךְyour sun וִירֵחֵךְand your moon לֹאnot יֵאָסֵףwane כִּיfor יְהוָהLORD יִהְיֶהbe לָּךְto you לְאוֹרlight עוֹלָםeverlasting וְשָׁלְמוּand repay יְמֵיdays אֶבְלֵךְyour sorrow

Your sun will no longer set, and your moon will not wane; for the LORD will be your everlasting light, and the days of your sorrow will cease.

60:21

וְעַמֵּךְthen your people כֻּלָּםall צַדִּיקִיםrighteous לְעוֹלָםforever יִירְשׁוּpossess אָרֶץland נֵצֶרbranch מַטָּעַיmy planting מַעֲשֵׂהwork יָדַיmy hands לְהִתְפָּאֵרglorified

Then all your people will be righteous; they will possess the land forever; they are the branch of My planting, the work of My hands, so that I may be glorified.

60:22

הַקָּטֹןthe least יִהְיֶהbecome לָאֶלֶףa thousand וְהַצָּעִירand the smallest לְגוֹיa nation עָצוּםmighty אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD בְּעִתָּהּin its time אֲחִישֶׁנָּהaccomplish it quickly

The least of you will become a thousand, and the smallest a mighty nation. I am the LORD; in its time I will accomplish it quickly.
