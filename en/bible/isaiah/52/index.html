Isaiah 52

Zion awakes; the feet of the messenger

Open in the interlinear reader

52:1

עוּרִיawake עוּרִיawake לִבְשִׁיclothe עֻזֵּךְstrength O צִיּוֹןZion לִבְשִׁיput בִּגְדֵיgarments תִפְאַרְתֵּךְsplendor O יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem עִירcity הַקֹּדֶשׁholy כִּיfor לֹאno יוֹסִיףlonger יָבֹאenter בָךְthe עוֹדlonger עָרֵלuncircumcised וְטָמֵאand unclean

Awake, awake, clothe yourself with strength, O Zion! Put on your garments of splendor, O Jerusalem, holy city! For the uncircumcised and unclean will no longer enter you.

52:2

הִתְנַעֲרִיshake מֵעָפָרoff your dust קוּמִיrise שְּׁבִיsit יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem הִתְפַּתְּחִיremove מוֹסְרֵיchains צַוָּארֵךְyour neck שְׁבִיָּהcaptive בַּתdaughter צִיּוֹןZion

Shake off your dust! Rise up and sit on your throne, O Jerusalem. Remove the chains from your neck, O captive Daughter of Zion.

52:3

כִּיfor כֹהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD חִנָּםnothing נִמְכַּרְתֶּםsold וְלֹאand not בְכֶסֶףmoney תִּגָּאֵלוּredeemed

For this is what the LORD says: “You were sold for nothing, and without money you will be redeemed.”

52:4

כִּיfor כֹהthis אָמַרis what אֲדֹנָיLord GOD יְהוִהsays מִצְרַיִםEgypt יָרַדwent down עַמִּיmy people בָרִאשֹׁנָהat first לָגוּרto live שָׁםthere וְאַשּׁוּרthen Assyria בְּאֶפֶסwithout עֲשָׁקוֹoppressed them

For this is what the Lord GOD says: “At first My people went down to Egypt to live, then Assyria oppressed them without cause.

52:5

וְעַתָּהand now מַהwhat לִּיto me פֹהhere נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor לֻקַּחtaken עַמִּיmy people חִנָּםwithout cause מֹשְׁלָיוrule them יְהֵילִילוּtaunt נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD וְתָמִידcontinually כָּלall הַיּוֹםday long שְׁמִיmy name is מִנֹּאָץblasphemed

And now what have I here? declares the LORD. For My people have been taken without cause; those who rule them taunt, declares the LORD, and My name is blasphemed continually all day long.

52:6

לָכֵןtherefore יֵדַעknow עַמִּיmy people שְׁמִיmy name לָכֵןtherefore בַּיּוֹםon that day הַהוּאthat כִּיthat אֲנִיam הוּאhe הַמְדַבֵּרwho speaks הִנֵּנִיhere I am

Therefore My people will know My name; therefore they will know on that day that I am He who speaks. Here I am!”

52:7

מַהhow נָּאווּbeautiful עַלon הֶהָרִיםthe mountains רַגְלֵיfeet מְבַשֵּׂרgood news מַשְׁמִיעַproclaim שָׁלוֹםpeace מְבַשֵּׂרproclaim טוֹבgood מַשְׁמִיעַproclaim יְשׁוּעָהsalvation אֹמֵרsay לְצִיּוֹןto Zion מָלַךְreigns אֱלֹהָיִךְyour God

How beautiful on the mountains are the feet of those who bring good news, who proclaim peace, who bring good tidings, who proclaim salvation, who say to Zion, “Your God reigns!”

52:8

קוֹלlisten צֹפַיִךְyour watchmen נָשְׂאוּlift קוֹלvoices יַחְדָּוtogether יְרַנֵּנוּshout joy כִּיfor עַיִןeye בְּעַיִןevery eye יִרְאוּsee בְּשׁוּבreturns יְהוָהLORD צִיּוֹןZion

Listen! Your watchmen lift up their voices, together they shout for joy. For every eye will see when the LORD returns to Zion.

52:9

פִּצְחוּbreak forth רַנְּנוּjoy sing יַחְדָּוtogether חָרְבוֹתruins יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem כִּיfor נִחַםcomforted יְהוָהLORD עַמּוֹhis people גָּאַלredeemed יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem

Break forth in joy, sing together, O ruins of Jerusalem, for the LORD has comforted His people; He has redeemed Jerusalem.

52:10

חָשַׂףbared יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] זְרוֹעַarm קָדְשׁוֹhis holy לְעֵינֵיin the sight כָּלall הַגּוֹיִםthe nations וְרָאוּwill see כָּלall אַפְסֵיends אָרֶץearth אֵת[obj] יְשׁוּעַתsalvation אֱלֹהֵינוּour God

The LORD has bared His holy arm in the sight of all the nations; all the ends of the earth will see the salvation of our God.

52:11

סוּרוּdepart סוּרוּdepart צְאוּgo מִשָּׁםfrom there טָמֵאunclean אַלno תִּגָּעוּtouch צְאוּcome out מִתּוֹכָהּfrom her among הִבָּרוּpurify נֹשְׂאֵיcarry כְּלֵיvessels יְהוָהLORD

Depart, depart, go out from there! Touch no unclean thing; come out from it, purify yourselves, you who carry the vessels of the LORD.

52:12

כִּיfor לֹאnot בְחִפָּזוֹןin haste תֵּצֵאוּleave וּבִמְנוּסָהin a hurry לֹאnot תֵלֵכוּןflee כִּיfor הֹלֵךְgoes לִפְנֵיכֶםbefore you יְהוָהLORD וּמְאַסִּפְכֶםyour rear guard אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

For you will not leave in a hurry nor flee in haste, for the LORD goes before you, and the God of Israel is your rear guard.

52:13

הִנֵּהbehold יַשְׂכִּילprosper עַבְדִּיmy servant יָרוּםraised וְנִשָּׂאand lifted וְגָבַהּand exalted מְאֹדvery

Behold, My Servant will prosper; He will be raised and lifted up and highly exalted.

52:14

כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had שָׁמְמוּappalled עָלֶיךָon you רַבִּיםmany כֵּןso מִשְׁחַתdisfigured מֵאִישׁany מַרְאֵהוּappearance וְתֹאֲרוֹand his form מִבְּנֵיwas marred beyond אָדָםhuman

Just as many were appalled at Him— His appearance was disfigured beyond that of any man, and His form was marred beyond human likeness—

52:15

כֵּןso יַזֶּהsprinkle גּוֹיִםnations רַבִּיםmany עָלָיוon him יִקְפְּצוּshut מְלָכִיםkings פִּיהֶםtheir mouths כִּיbecause אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot סֻפַּרtold לָהֶםfor they רָאוּsee וַאֲשֶׁרhave לֹאnot שָׁמְעוּheard הִתְבּוֹנָנוּunderstand

so He will sprinkle many nations. Kings will shut their mouths because of Him. For they will see what they have not been told, and they will understand what they have not heard.
