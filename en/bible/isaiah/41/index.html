Isaiah 41

The LORD summons the nations to judgment

41:1

הַחֲרִישׁוּsilent אֵלַיto me אִיִּיםislands וּלְאֻמִּיםpeoples יַחֲלִיפוּrenew כֹחַstrength יִגְּשׁוּcome אָזthen יְדַבֵּרוּtestify יַחְדָּוtogether לַמִּשְׁפָּטfor judgment נִקְרָבָהnear

“Be silent before Me, O islands, and let the peoples renew their strength. Let them come forward and testify; let us together draw near for judgment.

Who has aroused one from the east and called him to his feet in righteousness? He hands nations over to him and subdues kings before him. He turns them to dust with his sword, to windblown chaff with his bow.

41:3

He pursues them, going on safely, hardly touching the path with his feet.

41:4

Who has performed this and carried it out, calling forth the generations from the beginning? I, the LORD—the first and the last— I am He.”

41:5

The islands see and fear; the ends of the earth tremble. They approach and come forward.

41:6

Each one helps the other and says to his brother, “Be strong!”

41:7

וַיְחַזֵּקencourages חָרָשׁcraftsman אֶת[obj] צֹרֵףgoldsmith מַחֲלִיקwields פַּטִּישׁhammer אֶת[obj] הוֹלֶםcheers פָּעַםanvil אֹמֵרsaying לַדֶּבֶקof the welding טוֹבgood הוּאhe וַיְחַזְּקֵהוּand hardened him בְמַסְמְרִיםnails לֹאnot יִמּוֹטtoppled

The craftsman encourages the goldsmith, and he who wields the hammer cheers him who strikes the anvil, saying of the welding, “It is good.” He nails it down so it will not be toppled.

41:8

וְאַתָּהbut you יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael עַבְדִּיmy servant יַעֲקֹבJacob אֲשֶׁרwhom בְּחַרְתִּיךָchosen זֶרַעdescendant אַבְרָהָםAbraham אֹהֲבִיmy friend

“But you, O Israel, My servant, Jacob, whom I have chosen, descendant of Abraham My friend—

41:9

I brought you from the ends of the earth and called you from its farthest corners. I said, ‘You are My servant.’ I have chosen and not rejected you.

41:10

אַלdo not תִּירָאfear כִּיfor עִמְּךָwith you אָנִיam אַלdo not תִּשְׁתָּעafraid כִּיfor אֲנִיam אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God אִמַּצְתִּיךָstrengthen you אַףsurely עֲזַרְתִּיךָhelp you אַףhow much תְּמַכְתִּיךָuphold you בִּימִיןwith right hand צִדְקִיrighteousness

Do not fear, for I am with you; do not be afraid, for I am your God. I will strengthen you; I will surely help you; I will uphold you with My right hand of righteousness.

41:11

הֵןbehold יֵבֹשׁוּashamed וְיִכָּלְמוּand disgraced כֹּלall הַנֶּחֱרִיםwho rage בָּךְagainst you יִהְיוּbe כְאַיִןnothing וְיֹאבְדוּand will perish אַנְשֵׁיwho רִיבֶךָcontend with you

Behold, all who rage against you will be ashamed and disgraced; those who contend with you will be reduced to nothing and will perish.

41:12

תְּבַקְשֵׁםseek them וְלֹאbut will not תִמְצָאֵםfind them אַנְשֵׁיwho מַצֻּתֶךָwage יִהְיוּcome כְאַיִןlike nothing וּכְאֶפֶסnothing אַנְשֵׁיmen מִלְחַמְתֶּךָwar against you

You will seek them but will not find them. Those who wage war against you will come to nothing.

41:13

כִּיfor אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God מַחֲזִיקhold יְמִינֶךָyour right hand הָאֹמֵרand tells לְךָto you אַלdo not תִּירָאfear אֲנִיI עֲזַרְתִּיךָhelp you

For I am the LORD your God, who takes hold of your right hand and tells you: Do not fear, I will help you.

41:14

אַלdo not תִּירְאִיfear תּוֹלַעַתworm יַעֲקֹבJacob מְתֵיfew men יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael אֲנִיI עֲזַרְתִּיךְhelp you נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD וְגֹאֲלֵךְyour Redeemer קְדוֹשׁholy יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

Do not fear, O worm of Jacob, O few men of Israel. I will help you,” declares the LORD. “Your Redeemer is the Holy One of Israel.

41:15

הִנֵּהbehold שַׂמְתִּיךְmake you לְמוֹרַגinto a threshing חָרוּץsledge חָדָשׁnew בַּעַלsharp פִּיפִיּוֹתteeth תָּדוּשׁthresh הָרִיםmountains וְתָדֹקand crush וּגְבָעוֹתhills כַּמֹּץchaff תָּשִׂיםreduce

Behold, I will make you into a threshing sledge, new and sharp, with many teeth. You will thresh the mountains and crush them, and reduce the hills to chaff.

41:16

תִּזְרֵםwinnow them וְרוּחַand a wind תִּשָּׂאֵםcarry them וּסְעָרָהgale תָּפִיץscatter אוֹתָםthem וְאַתָּהbut you תָּגִילrejoice בַּיהוָהin the LORD בִּקְדוֹשׁin the holy יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael תִּתְהַלָּלglory

You will winnow them, and a wind will carry them away; a gale will scatter them. But you will rejoice in the LORD; you will glory in the Holy One of Israel.

41:17

הָעֲנִיִּיםthe poor וְהָאֶבְיוֹנִיםand needy מְבַקְשִׁיםseek מַיִםwater וָאַיִןnone לְשׁוֹנָםtheir tongues בַּצָּמָאare with thirst נָשָׁתָּהparched אֲנִיI יְהוָהLORD אֶעֱנֵםanswer them אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael לֹאnot אֶעֶזְבֵםforsake them

The poor and needy seek water, but there is none; their tongues are parched with thirst. I, the LORD, will answer them; I, the God of Israel, will not forsake them.

41:18

אֶפְתַּחopen עַלon שְׁפָיִיםbarren heights נְהָרוֹתrivers וּבְתוֹךְand in the middle בְּקָעוֹתvalleys מַעְיָנוֹתfountains אָשִׂיםturn מִדְבָּרdesert לַאֲגַםinto a pool מַיִםwater וְאֶרֶץland צִיָּהdry לְמוֹצָאֵיinto flowing מָיִםsprings

I will open rivers on the barren heights, and fountains in the middle of the valleys. I will turn the desert into a pool of water, and the dry land into flowing springs.

41:19

אֶתֵּןwill בַּמִּדְבָּרin the wilderness אֶרֶזcedars שִׁטָּהacacias myrtles וַהֲדַסmyrtles וְעֵץand trees שָׁמֶןolive אָשִׂיםset בָּעֲרָבָהin the desert בְּרוֹשׁcypresses תִּדְהָרelms וּתְאַשּׁוּרand boxwood יַחְדָּוtogether

I will plant cedars in the wilderness, acacias, myrtles, and olive trees. I will set cypresses in the desert, elms and boxwood together,

41:20

so that all may see and know, may consider and understand, that the hand of the LORD has done this and the Holy One of Israel has created it.”

41:21

קָרְבוּpresent רִיבְכֶםyour case יֹאמַרsays יְהוָהLORD הַגִּישׁוּsubmit עֲצֻמוֹתֵיכֶםyour arguments יֹאמַרsays מֶלֶךְking יַעֲקֹבJacob

“Present your case,” says the LORD. “Submit your arguments,” says the King of Jacob.

41:22

יַגִּישׁוּcome וְיַגִּידוּand tell לָנוּto us אֵת[obj] אֲשֶׁרthat תִּקְרֶינָהhappen הָרִאשֹׁנוֹתthe former מָהwhat הֵנָּהthem הַגִּידוּtell וְנָשִׂימָהand put לִבֵּנוּwe may reflect on them וְנֵדְעָהand know אַחֲרִיתָןoutcome אוֹor הַבָּאוֹתcoming הַשְׁמִיעֻנוּannounce to us

“Let them come and tell us what will happen. Tell the former things, so that we may reflect on them and know the outcome. Or announce to us what is coming.

41:23

הַגִּידוּtell הָאֹתִיּוֹתcome לְאָחוֹרto so וְנֵדְעָהmay know כִּיthat אֱלֹהִיםgods אַתֶּםyou are אַףyes תֵּיטִיבוּgood וְתָרֵעוּor evil וְנִשְׁתָּעָהmay look וְנִרְאֶהdismay יַחְדָּוtogether

Tell us the things that are to come, so that we may know that you are gods. Yes, do something good or evil, that we may look on together in dismay.

41:24

הֵןbehold אַתֶּםyou מֵאַיִןnothing וּפָעָלְכֶםand your work מֵאָפַעno value תּוֹעֵבָהdetestable יִבְחַרchooses בָּכֶםin you

Behold, you are nothing and your work is of no value. Anyone who chooses you is detestable.

41:25

הַעִירוֹתִיraised מִצָּפוֹןfrom the north וַיַּאתcome מִמִּזְרַחfrom the east שֶׁמֶשׁeast יִקְרָאcalls בִשְׁמִיon my name וְיָבֹאhe will march סְגָנִיםrulers כְּמוֹas חֹמֶרmortar וּכְמוֹlike יוֹצֵרpotter יִרְמָסtreads טִיטclay

I have raised up one from the north, and he has come— one from the east who calls on My name. He will march over rulers as if they were mortar, like a potter who treads the clay.

41:26

Who has declared this from the beginning, so that we may know, and from times past, so that we may say: ‘He was right’? No one announced it, no one foretold it, no one heard your words.

41:27

I was the first to tell Zion: ‘Look, here they are!’ And I gave to Jerusalem a herald of good news.

41:28

When I look, there is no one; there is no counselor among them; when I ask them, they have nothing to say.

41:29

See, they are all a delusion; their works amount to nothing; their images are as empty as the wind.
