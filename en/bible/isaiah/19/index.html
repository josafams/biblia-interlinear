Isaiah 19

Judgment and future healing of Egypt

Open in the interlinear reader

19:1

מַשָּׂאburden against מִצְרָיִםEgypt הִנֵּהbehold יְהוָהLORD רֹכֵבrides עַלon עָבcloud קַלswift וּבָאhe is coming מִצְרַיִםEgypt וְנָעוּand shake אֱלִילֵיidols מִצְרַיִםEgypt מִפָּנָיוtremble before him וּלְבַבand the hearts מִצְרַיִםEgyptians יִמַּסmelt בְּקִרְבּוֹwithin them

This is the burden against Egypt: Behold, the LORD rides on a swift cloud; He is coming to Egypt. The idols of Egypt will tremble before Him, and the hearts of the Egyptians will melt within them.

19:2

וְסִכְסַכְתִּיincite מִצְרַיִםEgyptian בְּמִצְרַיִםagainst Egyptian וְנִלְחֲמוּwill fight אִישׁman בְּאָחִיוagainst brother וְאִישׁneighbor בְּרֵעֵהוּagainst neighbor עִירcity בְּעִירagainst city מַמְלָכָהkingdom בְּמַמְלָכָהagainst kingdom

“So I will incite Egyptian against Egyptian; brother will fight against brother, neighbor against neighbor, city against city, and kingdom against kingdom.

19:3

וְנָבְקָהand laid רוּחַspirit מִצְרַיִםEgyptians בְּקִרְבּוֹfrom among them וַעֲצָתוֹfrustrate their plans אֲבַלֵּעַplans וְדָרְשׁוּwill resort אֶלto הָאֱלִילִיםidols וְאֶלand to הָאִטִּיםthe dead וְאֶלto הָאֹבוֹתmediums וְאֶלand to הַיִּדְּעֹנִיםspiritists

Then the spirit of the Egyptians will be emptied out from among them, and I will frustrate their plans, so that they will resort to idols and spirits of the dead, to mediums and spiritists.

19:4

וְסִכַּרְתִּיwill deliver אֶת[obj] מִצְרַיִםEgyptians בְּיַדinto the hands אֲדֹנִיםmasters קָשֶׁהharsh וּמֶלֶךְking עַזfierce יִמְשָׁלrule בָּםin them נְאֻםdeclares הָאָדוֹןthe GOD יְהוָהLord צְבָאוֹתHosts

I will deliver the Egyptians into the hands of harsh masters, and a fierce king will rule over them,” declares the Lord GOD of Hosts.

19:5

וְנִשְּׁתוּdry מַיִםwaters מֵהַיָּםof the Nile וְנָהָרup and יֶחֱרַבriverbed וְיָבֵשׁparched and empty

The waters of the Nile will dry up, and the riverbed will be parched and empty.

19:6

וְהֶאֶזְנִיחוּcanals will stink נְהָרוֹתrivers דָּלֲלוּtrickle וְחָרְבוּand dry יְאֹרֵיstreams מָצוֹרEgypt קָנֶהreeds וָסוּףreeds and rushes קָמֵלוּwither

The canals will stink; the streams of Egypt will trickle and dry up; the reeds and rushes will wither.

19:7

עָרוֹתbulrushes עַלon יְאוֹרNile עַלon פִּיmouth יְאוֹרriver וְכֹלand all מִזְרַעfields sown along יְאוֹרNile יִיבַשׁwither נִדַּףblow וְאֵינֶנּוּand be no more

The bulrushes by the Nile, by the mouth of the river, and all the fields sown along the Nile, will wither, blow away, and be no more.

19:8

וְאָנוּthen fishermen הַדַּיָּגִיםthe fishermen וְאָבְלוּwill mourn כָּלall מַשְׁלִיכֵיcast בַיְאוֹרinto the Nile חַכָּהhook וּפֹרְשֵׂיspread מִכְמֹרֶתnets עַלon פְּנֵיface מַיִםwaters אֻמְלָלוּpine

Then the fishermen will mourn, all who cast a hook into the Nile will lament, and those who spread nets on the waters will pine away.

19:9

וּבֹשׁוּdismayed עֹבְדֵיworkers פִשְׁתִּיםflax שְׂרִיקוֹתbe וְאֹרְגִיםand the weavers חוֹרָיpale

The workers in flax will be dismayed, and the weavers of fine linen will turn pale.

19:10

וְהָיוּbe שָׁתֹתֶיהָdejected and מְדֻכָּאִיםdejected כָּלall עֹשֵׂיdo שֶׂכֶרhired אַגְמֵיsick at נָפֶשׁheart

The workers in cloth will be dejected, and all the hired workers will be sick at heart.

19:11

אַךְbut אֱוִלִיםfools שָׂרֵיprinces צֹעַןZoan חַכְמֵיwise יֹעֲצֵיcounselors פַרְעֹהPharaoh’s עֵצָהadvice נִבְעָרָהsenseless אֵיךְhow can תֹּאמְרוּsay אֶלto פַּרְעֹהPharaoh בֶּןson חֲכָמִיםwise אֲנִיam בֶּןson מַלְכֵיkings קֶדֶםeastern

The princes of Zoan are mere fools; Pharaoh’s wise counselors give senseless advice. How can you say to Pharaoh, “I am one of the wise, a son of eastern kings”?

19:12

אַיָּםwhere אֵפוֹאnow חֲכָמֶיךָyour wise וְיַגִּידוּlet tell נָאplease לָךְto you וְיֵדְעוּand reveal מַהwhat יָּעַץplanned יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts עַלagainst מִצְרָיִםEgypt

Where are your wise men now? Let them tell you and reveal what the LORD of Hosts has planned against Egypt.

19:13

נוֹאֲלוּbecome fools שָׂרֵיprinces צֹעַןZoan נִשְּׁאוּdeceived שָׂרֵיprinces נֹףMemphis הִתְעוּled astray אֶת[obj] מִצְרַיִםEgypt פִּנַּתcornerstones שְׁבָטֶיהָher tribes

The princes of Zoan have become fools; the princes of Memphis are deceived. The cornerstones of her tribes have led Egypt astray.

19:14

יְהוָהLORD מָסַךְpoured בְּקִרְבָּהּin her among רוּחַspirit עִוְעִיםconfusion וְהִתְעוּled astray אֶת[obj] מִצְרַיִםEgypt בְּכָלin all מַעֲשֵׂהוּdoes כְּהִתָּעוֹתstaggers שִׁכּוֹרdrunkard בְּקִיאוֹthrough his own vomit

The LORD has poured into her a spirit of confusion. Egypt has been led astray in all she does, as a drunkard staggers through his own vomit.

19:15

וְלֹאand not יִהְיֶהbe לְמִצְרַיִםEgypt מַעֲשֶׂהmade אֲשֶׁרthat יַעֲשֶׂהdo רֹאשׁhead וְזָנָבor tail כִּפָּהpalm וְאַגְמוֹןor reed

There is nothing Egypt can do— head or tail, palm or reed.

19:16

בַּיּוֹםin that day הַהוּאthe will יִהְיֶהbe מִצְרַיִםEgyptians כַּנָּשִׁיםlike women וְחָרַדthey will tremble וּפָחַדwith fear מִפְּנֵיfrom before תְּנוּפַתbeneath uplifted יַדhand יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts אֲשֶׁרthat הוּאhe מֵנִיףbrandishes עָלָיוagainst them

In that day the Egyptians will be like women. They will tremble with fear beneath the uplifted hand of the LORD of Hosts, when He brandishes it against them.

19:17

וְהָיְתָהand be אַדְמַתland יְהוּדָהJudah לְמִצְרַיִםbring terror לְחָגָּאto Egypt כֹּלwhenever אֲשֶׁרthat יַזְכִּירJudah אֹתָהּher אֵלָיוto him יִפְחָדtremble מִפְּנֵיfrom before עֲצַתcounsel יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts אֲשֶׁרhas הוּאhe יוֹעֵץplanned עָלָיוagainst it

The land of Judah will bring terror to Egypt; whenever Judah is mentioned, Egypt will tremble over what the LORD of Hosts has planned against it.

19:18

בַּיּוֹםin that day הַהוּאthat יִהְיוּbe חָמֵשׁfive עָרִיםcities בְּאֶרֶץin the land מִצְרַיִםEgypt מְדַבְּרוֹתspeak שְׂפַתlanguage כְּנַעַןCanaan וְנִשְׁבָּעוֹתand swear allegiance לַיהוָהto the LORD צְבָאוֹתHosts עִירcity הַהֶרֶסthe sun יֵאָמֵרcalled לְאֶחָתone of them

In that day five cities in the land of Egypt will speak the language of Canaan and swear allegiance to the LORD of Hosts. One of them will be called the City of the Sun.

19:19

בַּיּוֹםin that day הַהוּאthere will יִהְיֶהbe מִזְבֵּחַaltar לַיהוָהto the LORD בְּתוֹךְin the center אֶרֶץland מִצְרָיִםEgypt וּמַצֵּבָהand a pillar אֵצֶלnear גְּבוּלָהּher border לַיהוָהto the LORD

In that day there will be an altar to the LORD in the center of the land of Egypt, and a pillar to the LORD near her border.

19:20

וְהָיָהwill be לְאוֹתa sign וּלְעֵדand a witness לַיהוָהto the LORD צְבָאוֹתHosts בְּאֶרֶץin the land מִצְרָיִםEgypt כִּיwhen יִצְעֲקוּcry out אֶלto יְהוָהLORD מִפְּנֵיfrom before לֹחֲצִיםoppressors וְיִשְׁלַחhe will send לָהֶםto them מוֹשִׁיעַsavior וָרָבand defender וְהִצִּילָםto rescue them

It will be a sign and a witness to the LORD of Hosts in the land of Egypt. When they cry out to the LORD because of their oppressors, He will send them a savior and defender to rescue them.

19:21

וְנוֹדַעknown יְהוָהLORD לְמִצְרַיִםto Egypt וְיָדְעוּacknowledge מִצְרַיִםEgypt אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD בַּיּוֹםon that day הַהוּאwill וְעָבְדוּworship זֶבַחsacrifices וּמִנְחָהand offerings וְנָדְרוּthey will make נֵדֶרvows לַיהוָהto the LORD וְשִׁלֵּמוּand fulfill

The LORD will make Himself known to Egypt, and on that day Egypt will acknowledge the LORD. They will worship with sacrifices and offerings; they will make vows to the LORD and fulfill them.

19:22

וְנָגַףstrike יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] מִצְרַיִםEgypt נָגֹףstrike וְרָפוֹאbut heal וְשָׁבוּthey will turn עַדto יְהוָהLORD וְנֶעְתַּרprayers לָהֶםto them וּרְפָאָםand heal them

And the LORD will strike Egypt with a plague; He will strike them but heal them. They will turn to the LORD, and He will hear their prayers and heal them.

19:23

בַּיּוֹםin that day הַהוּאthere will תִּהְיֶהbe a מְסִלָּהhighway מִמִּצְרַיִםfrom Egypt אַשּׁוּרָהAssyria וּבָאgo אַשּׁוּרAssyrians בְּמִצְרַיִםwill to Egypt וּמִצְרַיִםand The Egyptians בְּאַשּׁוּרto Assyria וְעָבְדוּworship מִצְרַיִםEgyptians אֶתwith אַשּׁוּרAssyrians

In that day there will be a highway from Egypt to Assyria. The Assyrians will go to Egypt, and the Egyptians to Assyria. The Egyptians and Assyrians will worship together.

19:24

בַּיּוֹםin that day הַהוּאwill יִהְיֶהjoin יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael שְׁלִישִׁיָּהthree party alliance לְמִצְרַיִםwith Egypt וּלְאַשּׁוּרand Assyria בְּרָכָהblessing בְּקֶרֶבin among הָאָרֶץthe earth

In that day Israel will join a three-party alliance with Egypt and Assyria—a blessing upon the earth.

19:25

אֲשֶׁרthat בֵּרֲכוֹbless them יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts לֵאמֹרsaying בָּרוּךְblessed עַמִּיmy people מִצְרַיִםEgypt וּמַעֲשֵׂהhandiwork יָדַיmy hands אַשּׁוּרAssyria וְנַחֲלָתִיand my inheritance יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

The LORD of Hosts will bless them, saying, “Blessed be Egypt My people, Assyria My handiwork, and Israel My inheritance.”
