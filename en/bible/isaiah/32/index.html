Isaiah 32

The righteous king and the outpouring of the Spirit

Open in the interlinear reader

32:1

הֵןbehold לְצֶדֶקin righteousness יִמְלָךְking מֶלֶךְreign וּלְשָׂרִיםand princes לְמִשְׁפָּטjustice יָשֹׂרוּrule

Behold, a king will reign in righteousness, and princes will rule with justice.

32:2

וְהָיָהwill be אִישׁeach כְּמַחֲבֵאlike a shelter רוּחַwind וְסֵתֶרrefuge זָרֶםstorm כְּפַלְגֵיlike streams מַיִםwater בְּצָיוֹןin a dry land כְּצֵלlike the shadow סֶלַעrock כָּבֵדgreat בְּאֶרֶץland עֲיֵפָהarid

Each will be like a shelter from the wind, a refuge from the storm, like streams of water in a dry land, like the shadow of a great rock in an arid land.

32:3

וְלֹאno תִשְׁעֶינָהlonger עֵינֵיeyes רֹאִיםsee וְאָזְנֵיand the ears שֹׁמְעִיםhear תִּקְשַׁבְנָהlisten

Then the eyes of those who see will no longer be closed, and the ears of those who hear will listen.

32:4

וּלְבַבmind נִמְהָרִיםrash יָבִיןunderstand לָדָעַתwill know וּלְשׁוֹןtongue עִלְּגִיםstammering תְּמַהֵרclearly לְדַבֵּרspeak צָחוֹתfluently

The mind of the rash will know and understand, and the stammering tongue will speak clearly and fluently.

32:5

לֹאno יִקָּרֵאcalled עוֹדlonger לְנָבָלa fool נָדִיבnoble וּלְכִילַיnor a scoundrel לֹאnot יֵאָמֵרsaid שׁוֹעַrespected

No longer will a fool be called noble, nor a scoundrel be respected.

32:6

כִּיfor נָבָלfool נְבָלָהfoolishness יְדַבֵּרspeaks וְלִבּוֹhis mind יַעֲשֶׂהplots אָוֶןiniquity לַעֲשׂוֹתpractices חֹנֶףungodliness וּלְדַבֵּרand speaks אֶלto יְהוָהLORD תּוֹעָהleaves לְהָרִיקthe empty נֶפֶשׁsoul רָעֵבhungry וּמַשְׁקֶהand deprives צָמֵאthirsty יַחְסִירdrink

For a fool speaks foolishness; his mind plots iniquity. He practices ungodliness and speaks falsely about the LORD; he leaves the hungry empty and deprives the thirsty of drink.

32:7

וְכֵלַיscoundrel כֵּלָיוweapons רָעִיםdestructive הוּאhe זִמּוֹתhatches יָעָץplots לְחַבֵּלto destroy עֲנִיִּיםpoor בְּאִמְרֵיin words שֶׁקֶרlies וּבְדַבֵּרeven when the plea אֶבְיוֹןneedy מִשְׁפָּטjust

The weapons of the scoundrel are destructive; he hatches plots to destroy the poor with lies, even when the plea of the needy is just.

32:8

וְנָדִיבand prince נְדִיבוֹתnoble יָעָץhonorable וְהוּאand he עַלfor נְדִיבוֹתworthy יָקוּםcauses

But a noble man makes honorable plans; he stands up for worthy causes.

32:9

נָשִׁיםwomen שַׁאֲנַנּוֹתcomplacent קֹמְנָהstand שְׁמַעְנָהlisten קוֹלִיmy voice בָּנוֹתdaughters בֹּטחוֹתoverconfident הַאְזֵנָּהgive ear אִמְרָתִיmy word

Stand up, you complacent women; listen to me. Give ear to my word, you overconfident daughters.

32:10

יָמִיםdays עַלon שָׁנָהyear תִּרְגַּזְנָהtremble בֹּטְחוֹתsecure כִּיfor כָּלָהfinished בָצִירgrape harvest אֹסֶףharvest בְּלִיnot יָבוֹאarrive

In a little more than a year you will tremble, O secure ones. For the grape harvest will fail and the fruit harvest will not arrive.

32:11

חִרְדוּleisure שַׁאֲנַנּוֹתleisure רְגָזָהtremble בֹּטְחוֹתcomplacency פְּשֹׁטָהstrip וְעֹרָהbare וַחֲגוֹרָהand put sackcloth עַלon חֲלָצָיִםwaists

Shudder, you ladies of leisure; tremble, you daughters of complacency. Strip yourselves bare and put sackcloth around your waists.

32:12

עַלfor שָׁדַיִםbreasts סֹפְדִיםbeat עַלfor שְׂדֵיfields חֶמֶדpleasant עַלon גֶּפֶןvines פֹּרִיָּהfruitful

Beat your breasts for the pleasant fields, for the fruitful vines,

32:13

עַלfor אַדְמַתland עַמִּיmy people קוֹץthorns שָׁמִירbriers תַּעֲלֶהeven כִּיfor עַלfor כָּלevery בָּתֵּיhouse מָשׂוֹשׂmerriment קִרְיָהcity עַלִּיזָהrevelry

and for the land of my people, overgrown with thorns and briers— even for every house of merriment in this city of revelry.

32:14

כִּיfor אַרְמוֹןpalace נֻטָּשׁforsaken הֲמוֹןthe busy עִירcity עֻזָּבabandoned עֹפֶלhill וָבַחַןand the watchtower הָיָהbecome בְעַדfor מְעָרוֹתcaves עַדuntil עוֹלָםforever מְשׂוֹשׂdelight פְּרָאִיםwild donkeys מִרְעֵהpasture עֲדָרִיםflocks

For the palace will be forsaken, the busy city abandoned. The hill and the watchtower will become caves forever— the delight of wild donkeys and a pasture for flocks—

32:15

עַדuntil יֵעָרֶהpoured עָלֵינוּupon us רוּחַspirit מִמָּרוֹםfrom on high וְהָיָהand be מִדְבָּרdesert לַכַּרְמֶלbe an orchard וְהַכַּרְמֶלand the orchard לַיַּעַרa forest יֵחָשֵׁבconsidered

until the Spirit is poured out upon us from on high. Then the desert will be an orchard, and the orchard will seem like a forest.

32:16

וְשָׁכַןinhabit בַּמִּדְבָּרthe wilderness מִשְׁפָּטjustice וּצְדָקָהand righteousness בַּכַּרְמֶלin the fertile field תֵּשֵׁבdwell

Then justice will inhabit the wilderness, and righteousness will dwell in the fertile field.

32:17

וְהָיָהand be מַעֲשֵׂהwork הַצְּדָקָהof righteousness שָׁלוֹםpeace וַעֲבֹדַתservice הַצְּדָקָהrighteousness הַשְׁקֵטquiet וָבֶטַחconfidence עַדuntil עוֹלָםforever

The work of righteousness will be peace; the service of righteousness will be quiet confidence forever.

32:18

וְיָשַׁבdwell עַמִּיmy people בִּנְוֵהin pasture שָׁלוֹםpeaceful וּבְמִשְׁכְּנוֹתplace in מִבְטַחִיםsafe וּבִמְנוּחֹתrest שַׁאֲנַנּוֹתplaces

Then my people will dwell in a peaceful place, in safe and secure places of rest.

32:19

וּבָרַדbut hail level בְּרֶדֶתin down הַיָּעַרthe forest וּבַשִּׁפְלָהsink to the תִּשְׁפַּלdepths הָעִירthe city

But hail will level the forest, and the city will sink to the depths.

32:20

אַשְׁרֵיכֶםblessed זֹרְעֵיsow עַלon כָּלall מָיִםwaters מְשַׁלְּחֵיlet רֶגֶלfoot הַשּׁוֹרthe ox וְהַחֲמוֹרand donkey range

Blessed are those who sow beside abundant waters, who let the ox and donkey range freely.
