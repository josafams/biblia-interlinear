Isaiah 43

Israel redeemed, the LORD's witness

Open in the interlinear reader

43:1

וְעַתָּהnow כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD בֹּרַאֲךָcreated you יַעֲקֹבJacob וְיֹצֶרְךָformed you יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael אַלdo not תִּירָאfear כִּיfor גְאַלְתִּיךָredeemed you קָרָאתִיcalled בְשִׁמְךָby your name you לִיto me אָתָּהare

Now this is what the LORD says— He who created you, O Jacob, and He who formed you, O Israel: “Do not fear, for I have redeemed you; I have called you by your name; you are Mine!

43:2

כִּיfor תַעֲבֹרpass בַּמַּיִםthrough the waters אִתְּךָwith you אָנִיI וּבַנְּהָרוֹתthrough the rivers לֹאnot יִשְׁטְפוּךָoverwhelm you כִּיwhen תֵלֵךְwalk בְּמוֹthrough אֵשׁfire לֹאnot תִכָּוֶהscorched וְלֶהָבָהflames לֹאnot תִבְעַרablaze בָּךְin you

When you pass through the waters, I will be with you; and when you go through the rivers, they will not overwhelm you. When you walk through the fire, you will not be scorched; the flames will not set you ablaze.

43:3

כִּיfor אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God קְדוֹשׁholy יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael מוֹשִׁיעֶךָyour Savior נָתַתִּיgive כָפְרְךָyour ransom מִצְרַיִםEgypt כּוּשׁCush וּסְבָאand Seba תַּחְתֶּיךָin your place

For I am the LORD your God, the Holy One of Israel, your Savior; I give Egypt for your ransom, Cush and Seba in your place.

43:4

מֵאֲשֶׁרbecause you יָקַרְתָּprecious בְעֵינַיin my sight נִכְבַּדְתָּhonored וַאֲנִיand because I אֲהַבְתִּיךָlove you וְאֶתֵּןI will give אָדָםmen תַּחְתֶּיךָin for וּלְאֻמִּיםand nations תַּחַתplace נַפְשֶׁךָyour life

Because you are precious and honored in My sight, and because I love you, I will give men in exchange for you and nations in place of your life.

43:5

אַלdo not תִּירָאafraid כִּיfor אִתְּךָwith you אָנִיI מִמִּזְרָחfrom the east אָבִיאbring זַרְעֶךָyour offspring וּמִמַּעֲרָבfrom the west אֲקַבְּצֶךָּgather you

Do not be afraid, for I am with you; I will bring your offspring from the east and gather you from the west.

43:6

אֹמַרsay לַצָּפוֹןto the north תֵּנִיgive וּלְתֵימָןand to the south אַלdo not תִּכְלָאִיhold הָבִיאִיbring בָנַיmy sons מֵרָחוֹקfrom afar וּבְנוֹתַיand my daughters מִקְצֵהfrom the ends הָאָרֶץthe earth

I will say to the north, ‘Give them up!’ and to the south, ‘Do not hold them back!’ Bring My sons from afar, and My daughters from the ends of the earth—

43:7

כֹּלeveryone הַנִּקְרָאcalled בִשְׁמִיby my name וְלִכְבוֹדִיand for my glory בְּרָאתִיוcreated יְצַרְתִּיוformed אַףindeed and עֲשִׂיתִיוmade

everyone called by My name and created for My glory, whom I have indeed formed and made.”

43:8

הוֹצִיאbring out עַםpeople עִוֵּרblind וְעֵינַיִםeyes יֵשׁhave וְחֵרְשִׁיםdeaf וְאָזְנַיִםears but לָמוֹto them

Bring out a people who have eyes but are blind, and who have ears but are deaf.

43:9

כָּלall הַגּוֹיִםthe nations נִקְבְּצוּgather יַחְדָּוtogether וְיֵאָסְפוּassemble לְאֻמִּיםpeoples מִיwho בָהֶםamong them יַגִּידdeclare זֹאתthis וְרִאשֹׁנוֹתformer יַשְׁמִיעֻנוּproclaim to us יִתְּנוּlet them עֵדֵיהֶםpresent their witnesses וְיִצְדָּקוּvindicate them so וְיִשְׁמְעוּothers may hear וְיֹאמְרוּand say אֱמֶתtrue

All the nations gather together and the peoples assemble. Who among them can declare this, and proclaim to us the former things? Let them present their witnesses to vindicate them, so that others may hear and say, “It is true.”

43:10

אַתֶּםyou עֵדַיmy witnesses נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD וְעַבְדִּיand my servant אֲשֶׁרwhom בָּחָרְתִּיchosen לְמַעַןso תֵּדְעוּconsider וְתַאֲמִינוּand believe לִיto me וְתָבִינוּand understand כִּיthat אֲנִיam הוּאhe לְפָנַיbefore me לֹאno נוֹצַרformed אֵלgod וְאַחֲרַיand after me לֹאnot יִהְיֶהbe

“You are My witnesses,” declares the LORD, “and My servant whom I have chosen, so that you may consider and believe Me and understand that I am He. Before Me no god was formed, and after Me none will come.

43:11

אָנֹכִיI אָנֹכִיam יְהוָהLORD וְאֵיןno מִבַּלְעָדַיbut me מוֹשִׁיעַSavior

I, yes I, am the LORD, and there is no Savior but Me.

43:12

אָנֹכִיI הִגַּדְתִּיdecreed וְהוֹשַׁעְתִּיand saved וְהִשְׁמַעְתִּיand proclaimed וְאֵיןand not בָּכֶםamong you זָרforeign וְאַתֶּםso you עֵדַיmy witnesses נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD וַאֲנִיI am אֵלGod

I alone decreed and saved and proclaimed— I, and not some foreign god among you. So you are My witnesses,” declares the LORD, “that I am God.

43:13

גַּםeven מִיּוֹםfrom eternity אֲנִיam הוּאhe וְאֵיןand none מִיָּדִיout of my hand מַצִּילdeliver אֶפְעַלact וּמִיwho can יְשִׁיבֶנָּהcan reverse it

Even from eternity I am He, and none can deliver out of My hand. When I act, who can reverse it?”

43:14

כֹּהthus אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD גֹּאַלְכֶםyour Redeemer קְדוֹשׁholy יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael לְמַעַנְכֶםsake שִׁלַּחְתִּיsend בָבֶלָהBabylon וְהוֹרַדְתִּיand bring בָרִיחִיםfugitives כֻּלָּםthem all וְכַשְׂדִּיםChaldeans בָּאֳנִיּוֹתin the ships רִנָּתָםwhich they rejoice

Thus says the LORD your Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel: “For your sake, I will send to Babylon and bring them all as fugitives, even the Chaldeans, in the ships in which they rejoice.

43:15

אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD קְדוֹשְׁכֶםyour holy בּוֹרֵאCreator יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael מַלְכְּכֶםyour king

I am the LORD, your Holy One, the Creator of Israel, and your King.”

43:16

כֹּהthus אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD הַנּוֹתֵןwho makes בַּיָּםin the sea דָּרֶךְway וּבְמַיִםwaters עַזִּיםsurging נְתִיבָהpath

Thus says the LORD, who makes a way in the sea and a path through the surging waters,

43:17

הַמּוֹצִיאwho brings out רֶכֶבchariots וָסוּסand horses חַיִלarmies וְעִזּוּזand warriors יַחְדָּוtogether יִשְׁכְּבוּlie בַּלnever יָקוּמוּrise דָּעֲכוּsnuffed כַּפִּשְׁתָּהlike a wick כָבוּextinguished

who brings out the chariots and horses, the armies and warriors together, to lie down, never to rise again; to be extinguished, snuffed out like a wick:

43:18

אַלdo not תִּזְכְּרוּcall רִאשֹׁנוֹתformer וְקַדְמֹנִיּוֹתold אַלno תִּתְבֹּנָנוּattention

“Do not call to mind the former things; pay no attention to the things of old.

43:19

הִנְנִיbehold I am עֹשֶׂהdo חֲדָשָׁהnew עַתָּהnow תִצְמָחcoming הֲלוֹאdo you not תֵדָעוּהָsee it אַףindeed אָשִׂיםmake בַּמִּדְבָּרin the wilderness דֶּרֶךְway בִּישִׁמוֹןdesert נְהָרוֹתstreams

Behold, I am about to do something new; even now it is coming. Do you not see it? Indeed, I will make a way in the wilderness and streams in the desert.

43:20

תְּכַבְּדֵנִיhonor me חַיַּתbeasts הַשָּׂדֶהthe field תַּנִּיםjackals וּבְנוֹתand daughters יַעֲנָהostriches כִּיbecause נָתַתִּיprovide בַמִּדְבָּרin the wilderness מַיִםwater נְהָרוֹתrivers בִּישִׁימֹןin the desert לְהַשְׁקוֹתto give drink עַמִּיmy people בְחִירִיchosen

The beasts of the field will honor Me, the jackals and the ostriches, because I provide water in the wilderness and rivers in the desert, to give drink to My chosen people.

43:21

עַםpeople זוּI יָצַרְתִּיformed לִיfor myself תְּהִלָּתִיmy praise יְסַפֵּרוּdeclare

The people I formed for Myself will declare My praise.

43:22

וְלֹאnot אֹתִיme קָרָאתָcalled יַעֲקֹבJacob כִּיbecause יָגַעְתָּweary בִּיin me יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

But you have not called on Me, O Jacob, because you have grown weary of Me, O Israel.

43:23

לֹאnot הֵבֵיאתָbrought לִּיto me שֵׂהsheep עֹלֹתֶיךָburnt offerings וּזְבָחֶיךָwith your sacrifices לֹאnot כִבַּדְתָּנִיhonored me לֹאnot הֶעֱבַדְתִּיךָburdened you בְּמִנְחָהwith offerings וְלֹאand not הוֹגַעְתִּיךָwearied you בִּלְבוֹנָהwith frankincense

You have not brought Me sheep for burnt offerings, nor honored Me with your sacrifices. I have not burdened you with offerings, nor wearied you with frankincense.

43:24

לֹאnot קָנִיתָbought לִּיto me בַכֶּסֶףsilver קָנֶהcane וְחֵלֶבfat זְבָחֶיךָyour sacrifices לֹאnot הִרְוִיתָנִיsatisfied me אַךְbut הֶעֱבַדְתַּנִיburdened me בְּחַטֹּאותֶיךָwith your sins you הוֹגַעְתַּנִיwearied me בַּעֲוֺנֹתֶיךָwith your iniquities

You have not bought Me sweet cane with your silver, nor satisfied Me with the fat of your sacrifices. But you have burdened Me with your sins; you have wearied Me with your iniquities.

43:25

אָנֹכִיI אָנֹכִיam הוּאhe מֹחֶהblots פְשָׁעֶיךָyour transgressions לְמַעֲנִיsake וְחַטֹּאתֶיךָand your sins לֹאno אֶזְכֹּרremembers

I, yes I, am He who blots out your transgressions for My own sake and remembers your sins no more.

43:26

הַזְכִּירֵנִיremind me נִשָּׁפְטָהargue the matter יָחַדtogether סַפֵּרstate אַתָּהyou לְמַעַןmay תִּצְדָּקvindicated

Remind Me, let us argue the matter together. State your case, so that you may be vindicated.

43:27

אָבִיךָyour father הָרִאשׁוֹןfirst חָטָאsinned וּמְלִיצֶיךָand your spokesmen פָּשְׁעוּrebelled בִיagainst me

Your first father sinned, and your spokesmen rebelled against Me.

43:28

וַאֲחַלֵּלdisgrace שָׂרֵיprinces קֹדֶשׁsanctuary וְאֶתְּנָהand I will לַחֵרֶםdevote to destruction יַעֲקֹבJacob וְיִשְׂרָאֵלand Israel לְגִדּוּפִיםto reproach

So I will disgrace the princes of your sanctuary, and I will devote Jacob to destruction and Israel to reproach.”
