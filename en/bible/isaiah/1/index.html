Isaiah 1

Charges against Judah and a call to cleansing

Open in the interlinear reader

1:1

חֲזוֹןvision יְשַׁעְיָהוּIsaiah בֶןson אָמוֹץAmoz אֲשֶׁרthat חָזָהsaw עַלon יְהוּדָהJudah וִירוּשָׁלִָםand Jerusalem בִּימֵיduring the reigns עֻזִּיָּהוּUzziah יוֹתָםJotham אָחָזAhaz יְחִזְקִיָּהוּHezekiah מַלְכֵיkings יְהוּדָהJudah

This is the vision concerning Judah and Jerusalem that Isaiah son of Amoz saw during the reigns of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah.

1:2

שִׁמְעוּlisten שָׁמַיִםheavens וְהַאֲזִינִיand give ear אֶרֶץearth כִּיfor יְהוָהLORD דִּבֵּרspoken בָּנִיםchildren גִּדַּלְתִּיraised וְרוֹמַמְתִּיup וְהֵםbut they פָּשְׁעוּrebelled בִיagainst me

Listen, O heavens, and give ear, O earth, for the LORD has spoken: “I have raised children and brought them up, but they have rebelled against Me.

1:3

יָדַעknows שׁוֹרox קֹנֵהוּits master’s וַחֲמוֹרand the donkey אֵבוּסmanger בְּעָלָיוits owner יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael לֹאnot יָדַעknow עַמִּיmy people לֹאnot הִתְבּוֹנָןunderstand

The ox knows its owner, and the donkey its master’s manger, but Israel does not know; My people do not understand.”

1:4

הוֹיAlas גּוֹיnation חֹטֵאsinful עַםpeople כֶּבֶדladen עָוֺןiniquity זֶרַעbrood מְרֵעִיםevildoers בָּנִיםchildren מַשְׁחִיתִיםdepravity עָזְבוּforsaken אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD נִאֲצוּdespised אֶת[obj] קְדוֹשׁholy יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael נָזֹרוּone אָחוֹרbacks

Alas, O sinful nation, a people laden with iniquity, a brood of evildoers, children of depravity! They have forsaken the LORD; they have despised the Holy One of Israel and turned their backs on Him.

1:5

עַלon מֶהwhy תֻכּוּbeatings עוֹדmore תּוֹסִיפוּlonger סָרָהrebelling כָּלall רֹאשׁhead לָחֳלִיa massive wound וְכָלand your whole לֵבָבheart דַּוָּיafflicted

Why do you want more beatings? Why do you keep rebelling? Your head has a massive wound, and your whole heart is afflicted.

1:6

מִכַּףfrom the sole רֶגֶלfoot וְעַדto רֹאשׁhead אֵיןno בּוֹin him מְתֹםsoundness פֶּצַעwounds וְחַבּוּרָהwelts and festering וּמַכָּהwounds and טְרִיָּהsores לֹאnot זֹרוּcleansed וְלֹאand not חֻבָּשׁוּbandaged וְלֹאand not רֻכְּכָהsoothed בַּשָּׁמֶןwith oil

From the sole of your foot to the top of your head, there is no soundness— only wounds and welts and festering sores neither cleansed nor bandaged nor soothed with oil.

1:7

אַרְצְכֶםyour land שְׁמָמָהdesolate עָרֵיכֶםyour cities שְׂרֻפוֹתburned אֵשׁfire אַדְמַתְכֶםyour fields לְנֶגְדְּכֶםbefore you זָרִיםforeigners אֹכְלִיםdevour אֹתָהּher וּשְׁמָמָהdesolation כְּמַהְפֵּכַתdemolished by זָרִיםstrangers

Your land is desolate; your cities are burned with fire. Foreigners devour your fields before you— a desolation demolished by strangers.

1:8

וְנוֹתְרָהand left בַתdaughter צִיּוֹןZion כְּסֻכָּהlike a shelter בְכָרֶםin a vineyard כִּמְלוּנָהlike a shack בְמִקְשָׁהfield כְּעִירlike a city נְצוּרָהbesieged

And the Daughter of Zion is abandoned like a shelter in a vineyard, like a shack in a cucumber field, like a city besieged.

1:9

לוּלֵיunless יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts הוֹתִירleft לָנוּto us שָׂרִידsurvivors כִּמְעָטa few כִּסְדֹםlike Sodom הָיִינוּbecome לַעֲמֹרָהGomorrah דָּמִינוּresembled

Unless the LORD of Hosts had left us a few survivors, we would have become like Sodom, we would have resembled Gomorrah.

1:10

שִׁמְעוּhear דְבַרword יְהוָהLORD קְצִינֵיrulers סְדֹםSodom הַאֲזִינוּlisten תּוֹרַתinstruction אֱלֹהֵינוּour God עַםpeople עֲמֹרָהGomorrah

Hear the word of the LORD, you rulers of Sodom; listen to the instruction of our God, you people of Gomorrah!

1:11

לָמָּהwhat good לִּיto me רֹבmultitude זִבְחֵיכֶםsacrifices יֹאמַרsays יְהוָהLORD שָׂבַעְתִּיfull עֹלוֹתburnt offerings אֵילִיםrams וְחֵלֶבand the fat מְרִיאִיםfed cattle וְדַםblood פָּרִיםbulls וּכְבָשִׂיםand lambs וְעַתּוּדִיםand goats לֹאno חָפָצְתִּיdelight

“What good to Me is your multitude of sacrifices?” says the LORD. “I am full from the burnt offerings of rams and the fat of well-fed cattle; I take no delight in the blood of bulls and lambs and goats.

1:12

כִּיwhen תָבֹאוּcome לֵרָאוֹתto appear פָּנָיbefore me מִיwho בִקֵּשׁrequired זֹאתthis מִיֶּדְכֶםfrom your hand רְמֹסtrampling חֲצֵרָיmy courts

When you come to appear before Me, who has required this of you— this trampling of My courts?

1:13

לֹאno תוֹסִיפוּmore הָבִיאbring מִנְחַתofferings שָׁוְאworthless קְטֹרֶתincense תּוֹעֵבָהdetestable הִיאis לִיto me חֹדֶשׁnew Moons וְשַׁבָּתSabbaths and קְרֹאcall מִקְרָאconvocations לֹאnot אוּכַלendure אָוֶןiniquity וַעֲצָרָהin a solemn assembly

Bring your worthless offerings no more; your incense is detestable to Me— your New Moons, Sabbaths, and convocations. I cannot endure iniquity in a solemn assembly.

1:14

חָדְשֵׁיכֶםyour new Moons וּמוֹעֲדֵיכֶםand your appointed feasts שָׂנְאָהhate נַפְשִׁיthey have הָיוּbecome עָלַיto לָטֹרַחam נִלְאֵיתִיweary נְשֹׂאbearing

I hate your New Moons and your appointed feasts. They have become a burden to Me; I am weary of bearing them.

1:15

וּבְפָרִשְׂכֶםwhen you spread כַּפֵּיכֶםyour hands אַעְלִיםhide עֵינַיmy eyes מִכֶּםfrom you גַּםeven כִּיfor תַרְבּוּmultiply תְפִלָּהprayers אֵינֶנִּיI will not שֹׁמֵעַlisten יְדֵיכֶםyour hands דָּמִיםblood מָלֵאוּcovered

When you spread out your hands in prayer, I will hide My eyes from you; even though you multiply your prayers, I will not listen. Your hands are covered with blood.

1:16

רַחֲצוּwash הִזַּכּוּcleanse הָסִירוּremove רֹעַevil מַעַלְלֵיכֶםyour deeds מִנֶּגֶדfrom distance עֵינָיmy sight חִדְלוּstop הָרֵעַevil

Wash and cleanse yourselves. Remove your evil deeds from My sight. Stop doing evil!

1:17

לִמְדוּlearn הֵיטֵבright דִּרְשׁוּseek מִשְׁפָּטjustice אַשְּׁרוּcorrect חָמוֹץoppressor שִׁפְטוּdefend יָתוֹםfatherless רִיבוּplead אַלְמָנָהwidow

Learn to do right; seek justice and correct the oppressor. Defend the fatherless and plead the case of the widow.”

1:18

לְכוּcome נָאnow וְנִוָּכְחָהlet us reason יֹאמַרsays יְהוָהLORD אִםif יִהְיוּbe חֲטָאֵיכֶםyour sins כַּשָּׁנִיםlike scarlet כַּשֶּׁלֶגas snow יַלְבִּינוּwhite אִםif יַאְדִּימוּred כַתּוֹלָעas crimson כַּצֶּמֶרlike wool יִהְיוּbecome

“Come now, let us reason together,” says the LORD. “Though your sins are like scarlet, they will be as white as snow; though they are as red as crimson, they will become like wool.

1:19

אִםif תֹּאבוּwilling וּשְׁמַעְתֶּםand obedient טוּבbest הָאָרֶץthe land תֹּאכֵלוּeat

If you are willing and obedient, you will eat the best of the land.

1:20

וְאִםbut if תְּמָאֲנוּresist וּמְרִיתֶםand rebel חֶרֶבsword תְּאֻכְּלוּdevoured כִּיfor פִּיmouth יְהוָהLORD דִּבֵּרspoken

But if you resist and rebel, you will be devoured by the sword.” For the mouth of the LORD has spoken.

1:21

אֵיכָהhow הָיְתָהbecome לְזוֹנָהa harlot קִרְיָהcity נֶאֱמָנָהfaithful מְלֵאֲתִיfull מִשְׁפָּטjustice צֶדֶקrighteousness יָלִיןresided בָּהּin her וְעַתָּהbut now מְרַצְּחִיםmurderers

See how the faithful city has become a harlot! She once was full of justice; righteousness resided within her, but now only murderers!

1:22

כַּסְפֵּךְyour silver הָיָהbecome לְסִיגִיםdross סָבְאֵךְyour fine wine מָהוּלdiluted בַּמָּיִםwith water

Your silver has become dross; your fine wine is diluted with water.

1:23

שָׂרַיִךְyour rulers סוֹרְרִיםrebels וְחַבְרֵיfriends גַּנָּבִיםthieves כֻּלּוֹall אֹהֵבlove שֹׁחַדbribes וְרֹדֵףand chasing שַׁלְמֹנִיםrewards יָתוֹםfatherless לֹאnot יִשְׁפֹּטוּdefend וְרִיבand the plea אַלְמָנָהwidow לֹאnever יָבוֹאcomes אֲלֵיהֶםto them

Your rulers are rebels, friends of thieves. They all love bribes and chasing after rewards. They do not defend the fatherless, and the plea of the widow never comes before them.

1:24

לָכֵןtherefore נְאֻםdeclares הָאָדוֹןthe GOD יְהוָהLord צְבָאוֹתHosts אֲבִירmighty one יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael הוֹיWoe אֶנָּחֵםrelieved מִצָּרַיof my foes וְאִנָּקְמָהand avenge מֵאוֹיְבָיon my enemies

Therefore the Lord GOD of Hosts, the Mighty One of Israel, declares: “Ah, I will be relieved of My foes and avenge Myself on My enemies.

1:25

וְאָשִׁיבָהwill turn יָדִיmy hand עָלַיִךְagainst you וְאֶצְרֹףwill thoroughly כַּבֹּרpurge סִיגָיִךְyour dross וְאָסִירָהwill remove כָּלall בְּדִילָיִךְyour impurities

I will turn My hand against you; I will thoroughly purge your dross; I will remove all your impurities.

1:26

וְאָשִׁיבָהwill restore שֹׁפְטַיִךְyour judges כְּבָרִאשֹׁנָהas at first וְיֹעֲצַיִךְand your counselors כְּבַתְּחִלָּהas at the beginning אַחֲרֵיafter כֵןthat יִקָּרֵאcalled לָךְto you עִירcity הַצֶּדֶקrighteousness the קִרְיָהcity נֶאֱמָנָהfaithful

I will restore your judges as at first, and your counselors as at the beginning. After that you will be called the City of Righteousness, the Faithful City.”

1:27

צִיּוֹןZion בְּמִשְׁפָּטwith justice תִּפָּדֶהredeemed וְשָׁבֶיהָher repentant בִּצְדָקָהwith righteousness

Zion will be redeemed with justice, her repentant ones with righteousness.

1:28

וְשֶׁבֶרshattered פֹּשְׁעִיםrebels וְחַטָּאִיםand sinners יַחְדָּוtogether וְעֹזְבֵיforsake יְהוָהLORD יִכְלוּperish

But rebels and sinners will together be shattered, and those who forsake the LORD will perish.

1:29

כִּיfor יֵבֹשׁוּashamed מֵאֵילִיםof the sacred oaks אֲשֶׁרwhich חֲמַדְתֶּםdelighted וְתַחְפְּרוּwill be embarrassed מֵהַגַּנּוֹתby the gardens אֲשֶׁרthat בְּחַרְתֶּםchosen

Surely you will be ashamed of the sacred oaks in which you have delighted; you will be embarrassed by the gardens that you have chosen.

1:30

כִּיfor תִהְיוּbecome כְּאֵלָהlike an oak נֹבֶלֶתwithered עָלֶהָwhose leaves וּכְגַנָּהlike garden אֲשֶׁרthat מַיִםwater אֵיןwithout לָהּto her

For you will become like an oak whose leaves are withered, like a garden without water.

1:31

וְהָיָהand be הֶחָסֹןthe strong לִנְעֹרֶתbecome tinder וּפֹעֲלוֹand his work לְנִיצוֹץa spark וּבָעֲרוּwill burn שְׁנֵיהֶםboth יַחְדָּוtogether וְאֵיןwith no מְכַבֶּהquench

The strong man will become tinder and his work will be a spark; both will burn together, with no one to quench the flames.
