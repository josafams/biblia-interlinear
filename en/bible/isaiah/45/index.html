Isaiah 45

Cyrus, the LORD's anointed

Open in the interlinear reader

45:1

כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD לִמְשִׁיחוֹhis anointed לְכוֹרֶשׁto Cyrus אֲשֶׁרthat הֶחֱזַקְתִּיrepairs בִימִינוֹright hand לְרַדhave grasped to subdue לְפָנָיוbefore him גּוֹיִםnations וּמָתְנֵיto disarm מְלָכִיםkings אֲפַתֵּחַopen לִפְתֹּחַto open לְפָנָיוthe before him דְּלָתַיִםdoors וּשְׁעָרִיםgates לֹאnot יִסָּגֵרוּshut

This is what the LORD says to Cyrus His anointed, whose right hand I have grasped to subdue nations before him, to disarm kings, to open the doors before him, so that the gates will not be shut:

45:2

אֲנִיI לְפָנֶיךָwill before you אֵלֵךְgo וַהֲדוּרִיםand level אֲיַשֵּׁרmountains דַּלְתוֹתgates נְחוּשָׁהbronze אֲשַׁבֵּרbreak וּבְרִיחֵיbars בַרְזֶלiron אֲגַדֵּעַcut

“I will go before you and level the mountains; I will break down the gates of bronze and cut through the bars of iron.

45:3

וְנָתַתִּיwill give לְךָto you אוֹצְרוֹתtreasures חֹשֶׁךְdarkness וּמַטְמֻנֵיand the riches hidden מִסְתָּרִיםsecret places לְמַעַןso תֵּדַעknow כִּיthat אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD הַקּוֹרֵאcalls בְשִׁמְךָyou by name אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

I will give you the treasures of darkness and the riches hidden in secret places, so that you may know that I am the LORD, the God of Israel, who calls you by name.

45:4

לְמַעַןsake עַבְדִּיmy servant יַעֲקֹבJacob וְיִשְׂרָאֵלand Israel בְּחִירִיmy chosen וָאֶקְרָאI call לְךָto you בִּשְׁמֶךָby name אֲכַנְּךָtitle וְלֹאhave not יְדַעְתָּנִיknown me

For the sake of Jacob My servant and Israel My chosen one, I call you by name; I have given you a title of honor, though you have not known Me.

45:5

אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD וְאֵיןno עוֹדlonger זוּלָתִיbut me אֵיןno אֱלֹהִיםGod אֲאַזֶּרְךָyou for battle וְלֹאnot יְדַעְתָּנִיknown me

I am the LORD, and there is no other; there is no God but Me. I will equip you for battle, though you have not known Me,

45:6

לְמַעַןso יֵדְעוּknow מִמִּזְרַחfrom where שֶׁמֶשׁsun וּמִמַּעֲרָבָהּrises to where it sets כִּיthat אֶפֶסnone בִּלְעָדָיbut me אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD וְאֵיןno עוֹדlonger

so that all may know, from where the sun rises to where it sets, that there is none but Me; I am the LORD, and there is no other.

45:7

יוֹצֵרform אוֹרlight וּבוֹרֵאand create חֹשֶׁךְdarkness עֹשֶׂהbring שָׁלוֹםprosperity וּבוֹרֵאand create רָעcalamity אֲנִיI יְהוָהLORD עֹשֶׂהdo כָלall אֵלֶּהthese

I form the light and create the darkness; I bring prosperity and create calamity. I, the LORD, do all these things.

45:8

הַרְעִיפוּdrip שָׁמַיִםheavens מִמַּעַלfrom above וּשְׁחָקִיםskies יִזְּלוּpour צֶדֶקrighteousness תִּפְתַּחopen אֶרֶץearth וְיִפְרוּup that יֶשַׁעsalvation וּצְדָקָהmay and righteousness תַצְמִיחַsprout יַחַדmay אֲנִיI יְהוָהLORD בְּרָאתִיוcreated it

Drip down, O heavens, from above, and let the skies pour down righteousness. Let the earth open up that salvation may sprout and righteousness spring up with it; I, the LORD, have created it.

45:9

הוֹיWoe רָבwho quarrels אֶתwith יֹצְרוֹhis Maker חֶרֶשׂclay אֶתwith חַרְשֵׂיpot אֲדָמָהmany הֲיֹאמַרask חֹמֶרclay לְיֹצְרוֹthe potter מַהwhat תַּעֲשֶׂהmaking וּפָעָלְךָyour work אֵיןno יָדַיִםhands לוֹto him

Woe to him who quarrels with his Maker— one clay pot among many. Does the clay ask the potter, ‘What are you making?’ Does your work say, ‘He has no hands’?

45:10

הוֹיWoe אֹמֵרsays לְאָבto his father מַהwhat תּוֹלִידbegotten וּלְאִשָּׁהor to his mother מַהwhat תְּחִילִיןforth

Woe to him who says to his father, ‘What have you begotten?’ or to his mother, ‘What have you brought forth?’”

45:11

כֹּהthus אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD קְדוֹשׁholy יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וְיֹצְרוֹand its Maker הָאֹתִיּוֹתdare שְׁאָלוּנִיquestion me עַלon בָּנַיmy sons וְעַלin פֹּעַלwork יָדַיmy hands תְּצַוֻּנִיinstruct

Thus says the LORD, the Holy One of Israel, and its Maker: “How dare you question Me about My sons, or instruct Me in the work of My hands?

45:12

אָנֹכִיI עָשִׂיתִיmade אֶרֶץearth וְאָדָםand man עָלֶיהָupon it בָרָאתִיcreated אֲנִיI יָדַיmy hands נָטוּstretched שָׁמַיִםheavens וְכָלall צְבָאָםtheir host צִוֵּיתִיordained

It is I who made the earth and created man upon it. It was My hands that stretched out the heavens, and I ordained all their host.

45:13

אָנֹכִיI הַעִירֹתִהוּCyrus בְצֶדֶקin righteousness וְכָלall דְּרָכָיוhis ways אֲיַשֵּׁרstraight הוּאhe will יִבְנֶהrebuild עִירִיmy city וְגָלוּתִיand my exiles יְשַׁלֵּחַset לֹאnot בִמְחִירfor payment וְלֹאor בְשֹׁחַדreward אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts

I will raise up Cyrus in righteousness, and I will make all his ways straight. He will rebuild My city and set My exiles free, but not for payment or reward, says the LORD of Hosts.”

45:14

כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD יְגִיעַproducts מִצְרַיִםEgypt וּסְחַרand the merchandise כּוּשׁCush וּסְבָאִיםSabeans אַנְשֵׁיmen מִדָּהstature עָלַיִךְover to you יַעֲבֹרוּcome וְלָךְand to you יִהְיוּbe אַחֲרַיִךְbehind you יֵלֵכוּcome בַּזִּקִּיםover in chains יַעֲבֹרוּcross וְאֵלַיִךְto you יִשְׁתַּחֲוּוּbow down אֵלַיִךְto you יִתְפַּלָּלוּconfess אַךְindeed בָּךְwith you אֵלGod וְאֵיןno עוֹדlonger אֶפֶסthere אֱלֹהִיםGod

This is what the LORD says: “The products of Egypt and the merchandise of Cush, along with the Sabeans, men of stature, will come over to you and will be yours; they will trudge behind you; they will come over in chains and bow down to you. They will confess to you: ‘God is indeed with you, and there is no other; there is no other God.’”

45:15

אָכֵןtruly אַתָּהyou אֵלGod מִסְתַּתֵּרhides אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael מוֹשִׁיעַSavior

Truly You are a God who hides Himself, O God of Israel, the Savior.

45:16

בּוֹשׁוּput shame וְגַםto and נִכְלְמוּhumiliated כֻּלָּםall יַחְדָּוtogether הָלְכוּdepart בַכְּלִמָּהin disgrace חָרָשֵׁיmakers צִירִיםbeauty

They will all be put to shame and humiliated; the makers of idols will depart together in disgrace.

45:17

יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael נוֹשַׁעsaved בַּיהוָהby the LORD תְּשׁוּעַתsalvation עוֹלָמִיםeverlasting לֹאnot תֵבֹשׁוּput shame וְלֹאto or תִכָּלְמוּhumiliated עַדto עוֹלְמֵיages עַדeverlasting

But Israel will be saved by the LORD with an everlasting salvation; you will not be put to shame or humiliated, to ages everlasting.

45:18

כִּיfor כֹהthus אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD בּוֹרֵאcreated הַשָּׁמַיִםthe heavens הוּאhe הָאֱלֹהִיםis God יֹצֵרformed הָאָרֶץthe earth וְעֹשָׂהּand fashioned it הוּאhe כוֹנְנָהּestablished it לֹאnot תֹהוּempty בְרָאָהּcreate it לָשֶׁבֶתinhabited יְצָרָהּformed it אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD וְאֵיןno עוֹדlonger

For thus says the LORD, who created the heavens—He is God; He formed the earth and fashioned it; He established it; He did not create it to be empty, but formed it to be inhabited: “I am the LORD, and there is no other.

45:19

לֹאnot בַסֵּתֶרsecret from a דִּבַּרְתִּיspoken בִּמְקוֹםplace in אֶרֶץland חֹשֶׁךְdarkness לֹאnot אָמַרְתִּיsay לְזֶרַעto the descendants יַעֲקֹבJacob תֹּהוּwasteland בַקְּשׁוּנִיseek me אֲנִיI יְהוָהLORD דֹּבֵרspeak צֶדֶקtruth מַגִּידI say מֵישָׁרִיםright

I have not spoken in secret, from a place in a land of darkness. I did not say to the descendants of Jacob, ‘Seek Me in a wasteland.’ I, the LORD, speak the truth; I say what is right.

45:20

הִקָּבְצוּgather וָבֹאוּcome הִתְנַגְּשׁוּdraw near יַחְדָּוtogether פְּלִיטֵיfugitives הַגּוֹיִםfrom the nations לֹאnot יָדְעוּwho הַנֹּשְׂאִיםwho carry אֶת[obj] עֵץwood פִּסְלָםidols וּמִתְפַּלְלִיםand pray אֶלto אֵלgod לֹאnot יוֹשִׁיעַsave

Come, gather together, and draw near, you fugitives from the nations. Ignorant are those who carry idols of wood and pray to a god that cannot save.

45:21

הַגִּידוּspeak וְהַגִּישׁוּand present אַףyes יִוָּעֲצוּcounsel יַחְדָּוtogether מִיwho הִשְׁמִיעַhear זֹאתthis מִקֶּדֶםancient מֵאָזfrom time הִגִּידָהּforetold הֲלוֹאwas not אֲנִיI יְהוָהLORD וְאֵיןthere is no עוֹדlonger אֱלֹהִיםGod מִבַּלְעָדַיbut me אֵלGod צַדִּיקrighteous וּמוֹשִׁיעַand Savior אַיִןnone זוּלָתִיbut me

Speak up and present your case— yes, let them take counsel together. Who foretold this long ago? Who announced it from ancient times? Was it not I, the LORD? There is no other God but Me, a righteous God and Savior; there is none but Me.

45:22

פְּנוּturn אֵלַיto me וְהִוָּשְׁעוּand be saved כָּלall אַפְסֵיends אָרֶץearth כִּיfor אֲנִיam אֵלGod וְאֵיןno עוֹדlonger

Turn to Me and be saved, all the ends of the earth; for I am God, and there is no other.

45:23

בִּיby myself נִשְׁבַּעְתִּיsworn יָצָאgone out מִפִּיfrom my mouth צְדָקָהrighteousness דָּבָרword וְלֹאwill not יָשׁוּבrevoked כִּיfor לִיto me תִּכְרַעbow כָּלevery בֶּרֶךְknee תִּשָּׁבַעswear allegiance כָּלevery לָשׁוֹןtongue

By Myself I have sworn; truth has gone out from My mouth, a word that will not be revoked: Every knee will bow before Me, every tongue will swear allegiance.

45:24

אַךְsurely בַּיהוָהin the LORD לִיto me אָמַרsay צְדָקוֹתrighteousness וָעֹזand strength עָדָיוto him יָבוֹאcome וְיֵבֹשׁוּshame כֹּלall הַנֶּחֱרִיםwho rage בּוֹagainst him

Surely they will say of Me, ‘In the LORD alone are righteousness and strength.’” All who rage against Him will come to Him and be put to shame.

45:25

בַּיהוָהin the LORD יִצְדְּקוּjustified וְיִתְהַלְלוּand will exult כָּלall זֶרַעdescendants יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

In the LORD all descendants of Israel will be justified and will exult.
