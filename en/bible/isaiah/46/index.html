Isaiah 46

The gods of Babylon carried as burdens

Open in the interlinear reader

46:1

כָּרַעcrouches בֵּלBel קֹרֵסcrouches נְבוֹNebo הָיוּwere עֲצַבֵּיהֶםcowers their idols weigh לַחַיָּהbeasts וְלַבְּהֵמָהand cattle נְשֻׂאֹתֵיכֶםimages you עֲמוּסוֹתcarry מַשָּׂאload לַעֲיֵפָהto the weary

Bel crouches; Nebo cowers. Their idols weigh down beasts and cattle. The images you carry are burdensome, a load to the weary animal.

46:2

קָרְסוּcower כָרְעוּcrouch יַחְדָּוtogether לֹאnot יָכְלוּunable מַלֵּטrelieve מַשָּׂאburden וְנַפְשָׁםbut they themselves בַּשְּׁבִיinto captivity הָלָכָהgo

The gods cower; they crouch together, unable to relieve the burden; but they themselves go into captivity.

46:3

שִׁמְעוּlisten אֵלַיto me בֵּיתhouse יַעֲקֹבJacob וְכָלall שְׁאֵרִיתremnant בֵּיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael הַעֲמֻסִיםwho have been sustained מִנִּיfrom בֶטֶןwomb הַנְּשֻׂאִיםcarried along מִנִּיfrom רָחַםbirth

“Listen to Me, O house of Jacob, all the remnant of the house of Israel, who have been sustained from the womb, carried along since birth.

46:4

וְעַדeven to זִקְנָהold אֲנִיI הוּאhe וְעַדand to שֵיבָהgray אֲנִיI אֶסְבֹּלbear אֲנִיI עָשִׂיתִיmade וַאֲנִיand I אֶשָּׂאcarry וַאֲנִיI אֶסְבֹּלsustain וַאֲמַלֵּטand deliver

Even to your old age, I will be the same, and I will bear you up when you turn gray. I have made you, and I will carry you; I will sustain you and deliver you.

46:5

לְמִיto whom תְדַמְיוּנִיliken me וְתַשְׁווּequal וְתַמְשִׁלוּנִיcount me וְנִדְמֶהwill you compare

To whom will you liken Me or count Me equal? To whom will you compare Me, that we should be alike?

46:6

הַזָּלִיםpour זָהָבgold מִכִּיסout their bags וְכֶסֶףsilver בַּקָּנֶהon scales יִשְׁקֹלוּweigh יִשְׂכְּרוּhire צוֹרֵףgoldsmith וְיַעֲשֵׂהוּto fashion it אֵלgod יִסְגְּדוּbow אַףand יִשְׁתַּחֲוּוּdown worship

They pour out their bags of gold and weigh out silver on scales; they hire a goldsmith to fashion it into a god, so they can bow down and worship.

46:7

יִשָּׂאֻהוּlift it עַלto כָּתֵףshoulder יִסְבְּלֻהוּcarry it וְיַנִּיחֻהוּalong they set it תַחְתָּיוin its place וְיַעֲמֹדstands מִמְּקוֹמוֹfrom that spot לֹאnot יָמִישׁbudging אַףhow much יִצְעַקcry out אֵלָיוto it וְלֹאbut it does not יַעֲנֶהanswer מִצָּרָתוֹfrom troubles לֹאno יוֹשִׁיעֶנּוּsaves

They lift it to their shoulder and carry it along; they set it in its place, and there it stands, not budging from that spot. They cry out to it, but it does not answer; it saves no one from his troubles.

46:8

זִכְרוּremember זֹאתthis וְהִתְאֹשָׁשׁוּand be brave הָשִׁיבוּtake it פוֹשְׁעִיםtransgressors עַלto לֵבheart

Remember this and be brave; take it to heart, you transgressors!

46:9

זִכְרוּremember רִאשֹׁנוֹתhappened long מֵעוֹלָםlong ago כִּיfor אָנֹכִיam אֵלGod וְאֵיןno עוֹדlonger אֱלֹהִיםGod וְאֶפֶסnone כָּמוֹנִיlike me

Remember what happened long ago, for I am God, and there is no other; I am God, and there is none like Me.

46:10

מַגִּידdeclare מֵרֵאשִׁיתfrom the beginning אַחֲרִיתend וּמִקֶּדֶםand ancient times from אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot נַעֲשׂוּto come I אֹמֵרsay עֲצָתִיmy purpose תָקוּםstand וְכָלand all חֶפְצִיpleasure I אֶעֱשֶׂהaccomplish

I declare the end from the beginning, and ancient times from what is still to come. I say, ‘My purpose will stand, and all My good pleasure I will accomplish.’

46:11

קֹרֵאsummon מִמִּזְרָחfrom the east עַיִטprey מֵאֶרֶץland מֶרְחָקfar אִישׁman עֲצָתִיmy purpose אַףhow much דִּבַּרְתִּיspoken אַףhow much אֲבִיאֶנָּהbring it יָצַרְתִּיplanned אַףsurely אֶעֱשֶׂנָּהdo it

I summon a bird of prey from the east, a man for My purpose from a far-off land. Truly I have spoken, and truly I will bring it to pass. I have planned it, and I will surely do it.

46:12

שִׁמְעוּlisten אֵלַיto me אַבִּירֵיstubborn לֵבstubborn הָרְחוֹקִיםfar מִצְּדָקָהremoved from righteousness

Listen to Me, you stubborn people, far removed from righteousness:

46:13

קֵרַבְתִּיnear צִדְקָתִיmy righteousness לֹאnot תִרְחָקfar וּתְשׁוּעָתִיand my salvation לֹאnot תְאַחֵרdelayed וְנָתַתִּיwill grant בְצִיּוֹןZion תְּשׁוּעָהsalvation לְיִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael תִּפְאַרְתִּיmy splendor

I am bringing My righteousness near; it is not far away, and My salvation will not be delayed. I will grant salvation to Zion and adorn Israel with My splendor.
