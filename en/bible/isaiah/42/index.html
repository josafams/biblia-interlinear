Isaiah 42

The servant of the LORD and a new song

Open in the interlinear reader

42:1

הֵןhere עַבְדִּיmy servant whom I my אֶתְמָךְuphold בּוֹin whom בְּחִירִיchosen רָצְתָהdelights נַפְשִׁיsoul I נָתַתִּיput רוּחִיmy spirit עָלָיוon him מִשְׁפָּטjustice לַגּוֹיִםto the nations יוֹצִיאbring

“Here is My Servant, whom I uphold, My Chosen One, in whom My soul delights. I will put My Spirit on Him, and He will bring justice to the nations.

42:2

לֹאnot יִצְעַקcry out וְלֹאor יִשָּׂאraise וְלֹאand not יַשְׁמִיעַheard בַּחוּץin the streets קוֹלוֹhis voice

He will not cry out or raise His voice, nor make His voice heard in the streets.

42:3

קָנֶהreed רָצוּץbruised לֹאnot יִשְׁבּוֹרbreak וּפִשְׁתָּהand a smoldering wick כֵהָהsmoldering לֹאnot יְכַבֶּנָּהextinguish he will לֶאֱמֶתfaithfully יוֹצִיאforth מִשְׁפָּטjustice

A bruised reed He will not break and a smoldering wick He will not extinguish; He will faithfully bring forth justice.

42:4

לֹאnot יִכְהֶהweak וְלֹאor יָרוּץdiscouraged עַדuntil יָשִׂיםestablished בָּאָרֶץon the earth מִשְׁפָּטjustice וּלְתוֹרָתוֹin his law אִיִּיםislands יְיַחֵילוּhope

He will not grow weak or discouraged before He has established justice on the earth. In His law the islands will put their hope.”

42:5

כֹּהthis אָמַרis what הָאֵלGod יְהוָהLORD בּוֹרֵאcreated הַשָּׁמַיִםthe heavens וְנוֹטֵיהֶםand stretched them רֹקַעspread הָאָרֶץthe earth וְצֶאֱצָאֶיהָand its offspring נֹתֵןgives נְשָׁמָהbreath לָעָםto the people עָלֶיהָon it וְרוּחַand life לַהֹלְכִיםto those who walk בָּהּin it

This is what God the LORD says— He who created the heavens and stretched them out, who spread out the earth and its offspring, who gives breath to the people on it and life to those who walk in it:

42:6

אֲנִיI יְהוָהLORD קְרָאתִיךָcalled you בְצֶדֶקfor a righteous וְאַחְזֵקtake hold בְּיָדֶךָyour hand וְאֶצָּרְךָwill keep you וְאֶתֶּנְךָand appoint you לִבְרִיתcovenant עָםpeople לְאוֹרand a light גּוֹיִםnations

“I, the LORD, have called you for a righteous purpose, and I will take hold of your hand. I will keep you and appoint you to be a covenant for the people and a light to the nations,

42:7

לִפְקֹחַto open עֵינַיִםeyes עִוְרוֹתblind לְהוֹצִיאto bring מִמַּסְגֵּרdungeon אַסִּירprisoners מִבֵּיתhouse כֶּלֶאthose יֹשְׁבֵיsitting חֹשֶׁךְdarkness

to open the eyes of the blind, to bring prisoners out of the dungeon and those sitting in darkness out from the prison house.

42:8

אֲנִיam יְהוָהLORD הוּאthat is שְׁמִיmy name וּכְבוֹדִיmy glory לְאַחֵרto another לֹאnot אֶתֵּןyield וּתְהִלָּתִיor my praise לַפְּסִילִיםto idols

I am the LORD; that is My name! I will not yield My glory to another or My praise to idols.

42:9

הָרִאשֹׁנוֹתthe former הִנֵּהbehold בָאוּhappened וַחֲדָשׁוֹתnew אֲנִיI מַגִּידdeclare בְּטֶרֶםbefore תִּצְמַחְנָהspring אַשְׁמִיעproclaim אֶתְכֶםyou

Behold, the former things have happened, and now I declare new things. Before they spring forth I proclaim them to you.”

42:10

שִׁירוּsing לַיהוָהto the LORD שִׁירa song חָדָשׁnew תְּהִלָּתוֹhis praise מִקְצֵהfrom the ends הָאָרֶץthe earth יוֹרְדֵיgo down הַיָּםthe sea וּמְלֹאוֹand his everything אִיִּיםislands וְיֹשְׁבֵיהֶםdwell in them

Sing to the LORD a new song— His praise from the ends of the earth— you who go down to the sea, and all that is in it, you islands, and all who dwell in them.

42:11

יִשְׂאוּraise מִדְבָּרdesert וְעָרָיוand its cities חֲצֵרִיםvillages תֵּשֵׁבcontinue קֵדָרKedar יָרֹנּוּcry יֹשְׁבֵיpeople סֶלַעSela מֵרֹאשׁfrom the mountaintops הָרִיםmountaintops יִצְוָחוּcry

Let the desert and its cities raise their voices; let the villages of Kedar cry aloud. Let the people of Sela sing for joy; let them cry out from the mountaintops.

42:12

יָשִׂימוּgive לַיהוָהto the LORD כָּבוֹדglory וּתְהִלָּתוֹand his praise בָּאִיִּיםin the islands יַגִּידוּdeclare

Let them give glory to the LORD and declare His praise in the islands.

42:13

יְהוָהLORD כַּגִּבּוֹרlike a mighty יֵצֵאforth כְּאִישׁlike man מִלְחָמוֹתwarrior יָעִירstirs קִנְאָהzeal יָרִיעַshouts אַףyes יַצְרִיחַroars עַלover אֹיְבָיוhis enemies יִתְגַּבָּרprevail

The LORD goes forth like a mighty one; He stirs up His zeal like a warrior. He shouts; yes, He roars in triumph over His enemies:

42:14

הֶחֱשֵׁיתִיsilent מֵעוֹלָםfrom ages אַחֲרִישׁremained quiet אֶתְאַפָּקrestrained כַּיּוֹלֵדָהlike a woman in labor אֶפְעֶהonce אֶשֹּׁםgasp וְאֶשְׁאַףand pant יָחַדtogether

“I have kept silent from ages past; I have remained quiet and restrained. But now I will groan like a woman in labor; I will at once gasp and pant.

42:15

אַחֲרִיבwaste הָרִיםmountains וּגְבָעוֹתand hills וְכָלall עֶשְׂבָּםtheir vegetation אוֹבִישׁdry וְשַׂמְתִּיwill turn נְהָרוֹתrivers לָאִיִּיםinto dry וַאֲגַמִּיםand drain אוֹבִישׁmarshes

I will lay waste the mountains and hills and dry up all their vegetation. I will turn the rivers into dry land and drain the marshes.

42:16

וְהוֹלַכְתִּיwill lead עִוְרִיםblind בְּדֶרֶךְby a way לֹאnot יָדָעוּunfamiliar בִּנְתִיבוֹתon paths לֹאnot יָדְעוּknow אַדְרִיכֵםguide them אָשִׂיםturn מַחְשָׁךְdarkness לִפְנֵיהֶםinto before them לָאוֹרlight וּמַעֲקַשִּׁיםand rough places לְמִישׁוֹרinto level ground אֵלֶּהthese הַדְּבָרִיםthings עֲשִׂיתִםdo for them וְלֹאnot עֲזַבְתִּיםforsake them

I will lead the blind by a way they did not know; I will guide them on unfamiliar paths. I will turn darkness into light before them and rough places into level ground. These things I will do for them, and I will not forsake them.

42:17

נָסֹגוּturned אָחוֹרback יֵבֹשׁוּutter shame בֹשֶׁתshame הַבֹּטְחִיםwho trust בַּפָּסֶלin idols הָאֹמְרִיםand say לְמַסֵּכָהto molten images אַתֶּםyou אֱלֹהֵינוּour gods

But those who trust in idols and say to molten images, ‘You are our gods!’ will be turned back in utter shame.

42:18

הַחֵרְשִׁיםdeaf שְׁמָעוּlisten וְהַעִוְרִיםblind הַבִּיטוּlook לִרְאוֹתsee

Listen, you deaf ones; look, you blind ones, that you may see!

42:19

מִיwho עִוֵּרblind כִּיbut אִםor עַבְדִּיmy servant וְחֵרֵשׁor deaf כְּמַלְאָכִיlike the messenger I am אֶשְׁלָחsending מִיwho עִוֵּרblind כִּמְשֻׁלָּםlike my covenant partner וְעִוֵּרor blind כְּעֶבֶדlike the servant יְהוָהLORD

Who is blind but My servant, or deaf like the messenger I am sending? Who is blind like My covenant partner, or blind like the servant of the LORD?

42:20

רָאוֹתseeing רַבּוֹתmany וְלֹאdo not תִשְׁמֹרkeep watch פָּקוֹחַopen אָזְנַיִםears וְלֹאdo not יִשְׁמָעhear

Though seeing many things, you do not keep watch. Though your ears are open, you do not hear.”

42:21

יְהוָהLORD חָפֵץpleased לְמַעַןsake צִדְקוֹrighteousness יַגְדִּילmagnify תּוֹרָהlaw וְיַאְדִּירglorious

The LORD was pleased, for the sake of His righteousness, to magnify His law and make it glorious.

42:22

וְהוּאbut this is עַםpeople בָּזוּזplundered looted וְשָׁסוּיplundered and הָפֵחַtrapped בַּחוּרִיםtrapped in כֻּלָּםall וּבְבָתֵּיand in houses כְלָאִיםimprisoned dungeons הָחְבָּאוּhave הָיוּbecome לָבַזplunder וְאֵיןwith no מַצִּילrescue מְשִׁסָּהloot וְאֵיןno אֹמֵרsay הָשַׁבsend

But this is a people plundered and looted, all trapped in caves or imprisoned in dungeons. They have become plunder with no one to rescue them, and loot with no one to say, “Send them back!”

42:23

מִיwho בָכֶםamong you יַאֲזִיןattention זֹאתthis יַקְשִׁבlisten וְיִשְׁמַעand obey לְאָחוֹרhereafter

Who among you will pay attention to this? Who will listen and obey hereafter?

42:24

מִיwho נָתַןgave לִמְשִׁסָּהfor spoil יַעֲקֹבJacob וְיִשְׂרָאֵלand Israel לְבֹזְזִיםto the plunderers הֲלוֹאwas it not יְהוָהLORD זוּhave חָטָאנוּsinned לוֹto him וְלֹאand not אָבוּunwilling בִדְרָכָיוin his ways הָלוֹךְwalk וְלֹאnot שָׁמְעוּobey בְּתוֹרָתוֹhis law

Who gave Jacob up for spoil, and Israel to the plunderers? Was it not the LORD, against whom we have sinned? They were unwilling to walk in His ways, and they would not obey His law.

42:25

וַיִּשְׁפֹּךְso he poured עָלָיוon them his חֵמָהfurious אַפּוֹanger וֶעֱזוּזand the fierceness מִלְחָמָהbattle וַתְּלַהֲטֵהוּit enveloped them מִסָּבִיבin flames וְלֹאand not יָדָעunderstand וַתִּבְעַרit consumed בּוֹin him וְלֹאbut they did not יָשִׂיםtake עַלto לֵבheart

So He poured out on them His furious anger and the fierceness of battle. It enveloped them in flames, but they did not understand; it consumed them, but they did not take it to heart.
