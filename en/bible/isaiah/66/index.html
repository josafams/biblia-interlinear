Isaiah 66

Final judgment and the gathering of the nations

Open in the interlinear reader

66:1

כֹּהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD הַשָּׁמַיִםheaven כִּסְאִיmy throne וְהָאָרֶץand earth הֲדֹםfootstool רַגְלָיmy feet אֵיwhat זֶהthis בַיִתhouse אֲשֶׁרyou תִּבְנוּbuild לִיfor me וְאֵיor where זֶהwill מָקוֹםplace מְנוּחָתִיrepose

This is what the LORD says: “Heaven is My throne, and earth is My footstool. What kind of house will you build for Me? Or where will My place of repose be?

66:2

וְאֶתand [obj] כָּלnot אֵלֶּהthese יָדִיmy hand עָשָׂתָהmade וַיִּהְיוּand were כָלall אֵלֶּהthese נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD וְאֶלand to זֶהthis אַבִּיטesteem אֶלinto עָנִיhumble וּנְכֵהand contrite רוּחַspirit וְחָרֵדwho trembles עַלon דְּבָרִיmy word

Has not My hand made all these things? And so they came into being,” declares the LORD. “This is the one I will esteem: he who is humble and contrite in spirit, who trembles at My word.

66:3

שׁוֹחֵטslaughters הַשּׁוֹרox מַכֵּהslays אִישׁman זוֹבֵחַsacrifices הַשֶּׂהa lamb עֹרֵףneck כֶּלֶבdog’s מַעֲלֵהoffers מִנְחָהgrain offering דַּםblood חֲזִירpig’s מַזְכִּירoffers לְבֹנָהfrankincense מְבָרֵךְblesses אָוֶןidol גַּםindeed הֵמָּהthey בָּחֲרוּchosen בְּדַרְכֵיהֶםtheir own ways וּבְשִׁקּוּצֵיהֶםand in their abominations נַפְשָׁםhave חָפֵצָהdelighted

Whoever slaughters an ox is like one who slays a man; whoever sacrifices a lamb is like one who breaks a dog’s neck; whoever presents a grain offering is like one who offers pig’s blood; whoever offers frankincense is like one who blesses an idol. Indeed, they have chosen their own ways and delighted in their abominations.

66:4

גַּםeven אֲנִיI אֶבְחַרchoose בְּתַעֲלֻלֵיהֶםtheir punishment וּמְגוּרֹתָםand I will terror אָבִיאbring לָהֶםto them יַעַןbecause קָרָאתִיcalled וְאֵיןand no עוֹנֶהanswered דִּבַּרְתִּיspoke וְלֹאand no שָׁמֵעוּlistened וַיַּעֲשׂוּbut they did הָרַעevil בְּעֵינַיin my sight וּבַאֲשֶׁרand in which לֹאnot חָפַצְתִּיdelight בָּחָרוּchose

So I will choose their punishment and I will bring terror upon them, because I called and no one answered, I spoke and no one listened. But they did evil in My sight and chose that in which I did not delight.”

66:5

שִׁמְעוּhear דְּבַרword יְהוָהLORD הַחֲרֵדִיםwho tremble אֶלto דְּבָרוֹhis word אָמְרוּsaid אֲחֵיכֶםyour brothers שֹׂנְאֵיכֶםhate you מְנַדֵּיכֶםexclude you לְמַעַןbecause שְׁמִיmy name יִכְבַּדglorified יְהוָהLORD וְנִרְאֶהmay see בְשִׂמְחַתְכֶםyour joy וְהֵםbut they יֵבֹשׁוּshame

You who tremble at His word, hear the word of the LORD: “Your brothers who hate you and exclude you because of My name have said, ‘Let the LORD be glorified that we may see your joy!’ But they will be put to shame.”

66:6

קוֹלhear שָׁאוֹןuproar מֵעִירfrom the city קוֹלlisten מֵהֵיכָלfrom the temple קוֹלvoice יְהוָהLORD מְשַׁלֵּםrepaying גְּמוּלwhat לְאֹיְבָיוhis enemies

Hear the uproar from the city; listen to the voice from the temple! It is the voice of the LORD, repaying His enemies what they deserve!

66:7

בְּטֶרֶםbefore תָּחִילgave יָלָדָהbirth בְּטֶרֶםbefore יָבוֹאcome חֵבֶלpain לָהּto her וְהִמְלִיטָהshe delivered זָכָרboy

“Before she was in labor, she gave birth; before she was in pain, she delivered a boy.

66:8

מִיwho שָׁמַעheard כָּזֹאתas this מִיwho רָאָהseen כָּאֵלֶּהlike these הֲיוּחַלforth אֶרֶץcountry בְּיוֹםin a day אֶחָדone אִםor יִוָּלֵדborn גּוֹיnation פַּעַםinstant אֶחָתone כִּיfor חָלָהsoon גַּםeven יָלְדָהbirth צִיּוֹןZion אֶת[obj] בָּנֶיהָher children

Who has heard of such as this? Who has seen such things? Can a country be born in a day or a nation be delivered in an instant? Yet as soon as Zion was in labor, she gave birth to her children.

66:9

הַאֲנִיshall I אַשְׁבִּירbirth וְלֹאand not אוֹלִידdeliver יֹאמַרsays יְהוָהLORD אִםor אֲנִיI הַמּוֹלִידwho deliver וְעָצַרְתִּיclose אָמַרwomb אֱלֹהָיִךְyour God

Shall I bring a baby to the point of birth and not deliver it?” says the LORD. “Or will I who deliver close the womb?” says your God.

66:10

שִׂמְחוּglad אֶתwith יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem וְגִילוּand rejoice בָהּin her כָּלall אֹהֲבֶיהָlove her שִׂישׂוּrejoice greatly אִתָּהּwith her מָשׂוֹשׂmerriment כָּלall הַמִּתְאַבְּלִיםwho mourn עָלֶיהָover her

Be glad for Jerusalem and rejoice over her, all who love her. Rejoice greatly with her, all who mourn over her,

66:11

לְמַעַןso תִּינְקוּnurse וּשְׂבַעְתֶּםand be satisfied מִשֹּׁדbreasts תַּנְחֻמֶיהָher comforting לְמַעַןmay תָּמֹצּוּdrink deeply וְהִתְעַנַּגְתֶּםand delight מִזִּיזin glorious כְּבוֹדָהּher glorious abundance

so that you may nurse and be satisfied at her comforting breasts; you may drink deeply and delight yourselves in her glorious abundance.

66:12

כִּיfor כֹהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD הִנְנִיsays I נֹטֶהextend אֵלֶיהָto her כְּנָהָרlike a river שָׁלוֹםpeace וּכְנַחַלlike a flowing שׁוֹטֵףstream כְּבוֹדwealth גּוֹיִםnations וִינַקְתֶּםnurse and עַלon צַדarm תִּנָּשֵׂאוּcarried וְעַלand upon בִּרְכַּיִםknees תְּשָׁעֳשָׁעוּbounced

For this is what the LORD says: “I will extend peace to her like a river, and the wealth of nations like a flowing stream; you will nurse and be carried on her arm, and bounced upon her knees.

66:13

כְּאִישׁlike man אֲשֶׁרthat אִמּוֹmother תְּנַחֲמֶנּוּcomforts her son כֵּןso אָנֹכִיI אֲנַחֶמְכֶםcomfort you וּבִירוּשָׁלִַםover Jerusalem תְּנֻחָמוּconsoled

As a mother comforts her son, so will I comfort you, and you will be consoled over Jerusalem.”

66:14

וּרְאִיתֶםwhen see וְשָׂשׂwill rejoice לִבְּכֶםyou וְעַצְמוֹתֵיכֶםand your bones כַּדֶּשֶׁאlike grass תִפְרַחְנָהflourish וְנוֹדְעָהand known יַדhand יְהוָהLORD אֶתto עֲבָדָיוhis servants וְזָעַםbut his wrath אֶת[obj] אֹיְבָיוhis enemies

When you see, you will rejoice, and you will flourish like grass; then the hand of the LORD will be revealed to His servants, but His wrath will be shown to His enemies.

66:15

כִּיfor הִנֵּהbehold יְהוָהLORD בָּאֵשׁfire יָבוֹאcome וְכַסּוּפָהlike a whirlwind מַרְכְּבֹתָיוhis chariots לְהָשִׁיבto execute בְּחֵמָהwith fury אַפּוֹfury וְגַעֲרָתוֹand his rebuke בְּלַהֲבֵיwith flames אֵשׁfire

For behold, the LORD will come with fire— His chariots are like a whirlwind— to execute His anger with fury and His rebuke with flames of fire.

66:16

כִּיfor בָאֵשׁby fire יְהוָהLORD נִשְׁפָּטjudgment וּבְחַרְבּוֹand by his sword אֶתwith כָּלall בָּשָׂרflesh וְרַבּוּand many חַלְלֵיslain יְהוָהLORD

For by fire and by His sword, the LORD will execute judgment on all flesh, and many will be slain by the LORD.

66:17

הַמִּתְקַדְּשִׁיםthose who consecrate וְהַמִּטַּהֲרִיםand purify אֶלto הַגַּנּוֹתenter the groves אַחַרfollow אַחַתone בַּתָּוֶךְin the center אֹכְלֵיeat בְּשַׂרflesh הַחֲזִירof swine וְהַשֶּׁקֶץand vermin וְהָעַכְבָּרand rats יַחְדָּוtogether יָסֻפוּperish נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD

“Those who consecrate and purify themselves to enter the groves—to follow one in the center of those who eat the flesh of swine and vermin and rats—will perish together,” declares the LORD.

66:18

וְאָנֹכִיand I מַעֲשֵׂיהֶםknowing their deeds וּמַחְשְׁבֹתֵיהֶםand thoughts בָּאָהcoming לְקַבֵּץto gather אֶת[obj] כָּלall הַגּוֹיִםnations וְהַלְּשֹׁנוֹתand tongues וּבָאוּcome וְרָאוּand see אֶת[obj] כְּבוֹדִיmy glory

“And I, knowing their deeds and thoughts, am coming to gather all nations and tongues, and they will come and see My glory.

66:19

וְשַׂמְתִּיwill establish בָהֶםamong them אוֹתsign וְשִׁלַּחְתִּיsend מֵהֶםfrom among them פְּלֵיטִיםsurvivors אֶלto הַגּוֹיִםthe nations תַּרְשִׁישׁTarshish פּוּלPut וְלוּדLud מֹשְׁכֵיbearers קֶשֶׁתarchers תֻּבַלTubal וְיָוָןJavan and הָאִיִּיםthe islands הָרְחֹקִיםfar אֲשֶׁרwho לֹאnot שָׁמְעוּheard אֶת[obj] שִׁמְעִיmy fame וְלֹאor רָאוּseen אֶת[obj] כְּבוֹדִיmy glory וְהִגִּידוּproclaim אֶת[obj] כְּבוֹדִיmy glory בַּגּוֹיִםamong the nations

I will establish a sign among them, and I will send survivors from among them to the nations—to Tarshish, Put, and the archers of Lud; to Tubal, Javan, and the islands far away who have not heard of My fame or seen My glory. So they will proclaim My glory among the nations.

66:20

וְהֵבִיאוּbring אֶת[obj] כָּלall אֲחֵיכֶםyour brothers מִכָּלfrom all הַגּוֹיִםthe nations מִנְחָהgift לַיהוָהto the LORD בַּסּוּסִיםon horses וּבָרֶכֶבand chariots וּבַצַּבִּיםand wagons וּבַפְּרָדִיםon mules וּבַכִּרְכָּרוֹתand camels עַלto הַרmountain קָדְשִׁיmy holy יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had יָבִיאוּbring בְנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites אֶת[obj] הַמִּנְחָהoffering בִּכְלִיvessel טָהוֹרclean בֵּיתhouse יְהוָהLORD

And they will bring all your brothers from all the nations as a gift to the LORD on horses and chariots and wagons, on mules and camels, to My holy mountain Jerusalem,” says the LORD, “just as the Israelites bring an offering in a clean vessel to the house of the LORD.”

66:21

וְגַםand I will מֵהֶםsome of them אֶקַּחselect לַכֹּהֲנִיםas priests לַלְוִיִּםLevites אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD

“And I will select some of them as priests and Levites,” says the LORD.

66:22

כִּיfor כַאֲשֶׁרlike had הַשָּׁמַיִםthe heavens הַחֳדָשִׁיםnew וְהָאָרֶץand the earth הַחֲדָשָׁהnew אֲשֶׁרwhich אֲנִיI עֹשֶׂהmake עֹמְדִיםendure לְפָנַיbefore me נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD כֵּןso יַעֲמֹדendure זַרְעֲכֶםyour descendants וְשִׁמְכֶםand your name

“For just as the new heavens and the new earth, which I will make, will endure before Me,” declares the LORD, “so your descendants and your name will endure.

66:23

וְהָיָהand be מִדֵּיfrom one חֹדֶשׁnew בְּחָדְשׁוֹmoon to another וּמִדֵּיand from one שַׁבָּתSabbath בְּשַׁבַּתּוֹin his Sabbath יָבוֹאcome כָלall בָּשָׂרmankind לְהִשְׁתַּחֲוֺתto worship לְפָנַיbefore me אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD

From one New Moon to another and from one Sabbath to another, all mankind will come to worship before Me,” says the LORD.

66:24

וְיָצְאוּthey go forth וְרָאוּthey will see בְּפִגְרֵיthe corpses הָאֲנָשִׁיםthe men הַפֹּשְׁעִיםwho have rebelled בִּיagainst me כִּיfor תוֹלַעְתָּםtheir worm לֹאnever תָמוּתdie וְאִשָּׁםtheir fire לֹאnever תִכְבֶּהquenched וְהָיוּbe a דֵרָאוֹןhorror לְכָלto all בָּשָׂרmankind

“As they go forth, they will see the corpses of the men who have rebelled against Me; for their worm will never die, their fire will never be quenched, and they will be a horror to all mankind.”
