Isaiah 35

The desert blooms; the way of holiness

Open in the interlinear reader

35:1

יְשֻׂשׂוּםglad מִדְבָּרwilderness וְצִיָּהdesert וְתָגֵלwill rejoice עֲרָבָהdesert וְתִפְרַחand blossom כַּחֲבַצָּלֶתlike a rose

The wilderness and the land will be glad; the desert will rejoice and blossom like a rose.

35:2

פָּרֹחַflourish תִּפְרַחbloom profusely וְתָגֵלand rejoice אַףwith גִּילַתjoy singing וְרַנֵּןjoy and כְּבוֹדglory הַלְּבָנוֹןof Lebanon נִתַּןgiven לָהּto it הֲדַרsplendor הַכַּרְמֶלCarmel וְהַשָּׁרוֹןand Sharon הֵמָּהthey יִרְאוּsee כְבוֹדglory יְהוָהLORD הֲדַרsplendor אֱלֹהֵינוּour God

It will bloom profusely and rejoice with joy and singing. The glory of Lebanon will be given to it, the splendor of Carmel and Sharon. They will see the glory of the LORD, the splendor of our God.

35:3

חַזְּקוּstrengthen יָדַיִםhands רָפוֹתlimp וּבִרְכַּיִםknees כֹּשְׁלוֹתfeeble אַמֵּצוּmighty

Strengthen the limp hands and steady the feeble knees!

35:4

אִמְרוּsay לְנִמְהֲרֵיanxious לֵבhearts חִזְקוּstrong אַלdo not תִּירָאוּfear הִנֵּהbehold אֱלֹהֵיכֶםyour God נָקָםvengeance יָבוֹאcome גְּמוּלretribution אֱלֹהִיםGod הוּאhe יָבוֹאcome וְיֹשַׁעֲכֶםto save you

Say to those with anxious hearts: “Be strong, do not fear! Behold, your God will come with vengeance. With divine retribution He will come to save you.”

35:5

אָזthen תִּפָּקַחְנָהopened עֵינֵיeyes עִוְרִיםblind וְאָזְנֵיand the ears חֵרְשִׁיםdeaf תִּפָּתַחְנָהunstopped

Then the eyes of the blind will be opened and the ears of the deaf unstopped.

35:6

אָזthen יְדַלֵּגleap כָּאַיָּלlike a deer פִּסֵּחַlame וְתָרֹןjoy לְשׁוֹןtongue אִלֵּםmute כִּיfor נִבְקְעוּgush בַמִּדְבָּרin the wilderness מַיִםwaters וּנְחָלִיםand streams בָּעֲרָבָהin the desert

Then the lame will leap like a deer and the mute tongue will shout for joy. For waters will gush forth in the wilderness, and streams in the desert.

35:7

וְהָיָהand be הַשָּׁרָבthe parched לַאֲגַםbecome a pool וְצִמָּאוֹןthirsty לְמַבּוּעֵיsprings מָיִםwater בִּנְוֵהin the haunt תַנִּיםjackals רִבְצָהּwhere once lay there will חָצִירgrass לְקָנֶהand reeds וָגֹמֶאand papyrus

The parched ground will become a pool, the thirsty land springs of water. In the haunt where jackals once lay, there will be grass and reeds and papyrus.

35:8

וְהָיָהand there שָׁםa מַסְלוּלhighway וָדֶרֶךְway וְדֶרֶךְand way הַקֹּדֶשׁholiness יִקָּרֵאcalled לָהּto her לֹאnot יַעַבְרֶנּוּtravel it טָמֵאunclean וְהוּאwill לָמוֹto them הֹלֵךְwalk דֶּרֶךְway וֶאֱוִילִיםand fools לֹאnot יִתְעוּstray onto

And there will be a highway called the Way of Holiness. The unclean will not travel it— only those who walk in the Way— and fools will not stray onto it.

35:9

לֹאno יִהְיֶהbe שָׁםthere אַרְיֵהlion וּפְרִיץand vicious חַיּוֹתvicious beast בַּלno יַעֲלֶנָּהgo up on it לֹאnot תִמָּצֵאfound שָׁםthere וְהָלְכוּwalk גְּאוּלִיםredeemed

No lion will be there, and no vicious beast will go up on it. Such will not be found there, but the redeemed will walk upon it.

35:10

וּפְדוּיֵיso the redeemed יְהוָהLORD יְשֻׁבוּןreturn וּבָאוּand enter צִיּוֹןZion בְּרִנָּהwith singing וְשִׂמְחַתgladness עוֹלָםeverlasting עַלon רֹאשָׁםtheir head שָׂשׂוֹןjoy gladness וְשִׂמְחָהand joy יַשִּׂיגוּovertake וְנָסוּflee יָגוֹןsorrow וַאֲנָחָהand sighing

So the redeemed of the LORD will return and enter Zion with singing, crowned with everlasting joy. Gladness and joy will overtake them, and sorrow and sighing will flee.
