Isaiah 2

The mountain of the LORD and pride brought low

Open in the interlinear reader

2:1

הַדָּבָרthe message אֲשֶׁרthat חָזָהrevealed יְשַׁעְיָהוּIsaiah בֶּןson אָמוֹץAmoz עַלon יְהוּדָהJudah וִירוּשָׁלִָםand Jerusalem

This is the message that was revealed to Isaiah son of Amoz concerning Judah and Jerusalem:

2:2

וְהָיָהand be בְּאַחֲרִיתin last הַיָּמִיםthe days נָכוֹןestablished יִהְיֶהbe הַרmountain בֵּיתhouse יְהוָהLORD בְּרֹאשׁas the chief הֶהָרִיםthe mountains וְנִשָּׂאwill be raised מִגְּבָעוֹתabove the hills וְנָהֲרוּstream אֵלָיוto it כָּלall הַגּוֹיִםnations

In the last days the mountain of the house of the LORD will be established as the chief of the mountains; it will be raised above the hills, and all nations will stream to it.

2:3

וְהָלְכוּcome עַמִּיםpeoples רַבִּיםmany וְאָמְרוּand say לְכוּcome וְנַעֲלֶהus go up אֶלto הַרmountain יְהוָהLORD אֶלto בֵּיתhouse אֱלֹהֵיGod יַעֲקֹבJacob וְיֹרֵנוּhe will teach us מִדְּרָכָיוhis ways וְנֵלְכָהwalk בְּאֹרְחֹתָיוin his paths כִּיfor מִצִּיּוֹןfrom Zion תֵּצֵאgo תוֹרָהlaw וּדְבַרand the word יְהוָהLORD מִירוּשָׁלִָםfrom Jerusalem

And many peoples will come and say: “Come, let us go up to the mountain of the LORD, to the house of the God of Jacob. He will teach us His ways so that we may walk in His paths.” For the law will go forth from Zion, and the word of the LORD from Jerusalem.

2:4

וְשָׁפַטjudge בֵּיןbetween הַגּוֹיִםthe nations וְהוֹכִיחַand arbitrate לְעַמִּיםfor peoples רַבִּיםmany וְכִתְּתוּthey will beat חַרְבוֹתָםtheir swords לְאִתִּיםinto plowshares וַחֲנִיתוֹתֵיהֶםand their spears לְמַזְמֵרוֹתinto pruning hooks לֹאno יִשָּׂאtake גוֹיnation אֶלagainst גּוֹיnation חֶרֶבsword וְלֹאand not יִלְמְדוּtrain עוֹדlonger מִלְחָמָהwar

Then He will judge between the nations and arbitrate for many peoples. They will beat their swords into plowshares and their spears into pruning hooks. Nation will no longer take up the sword against nation, nor train anymore for war.

2:5

בֵּיתhouse יַעֲקֹבJacob לְכוּlet וְנֵלְכָהus walk בְּאוֹרin the light יְהוָהLORD

Come, O house of Jacob, let us walk in the light of the LORD.

2:6

כִּיfor נָטַשְׁתָּהabandoned עַמְּךָyour people בֵּיתhouse יַעֲקֹבJacob כִּיbecause מָלְאוּfilled מִקֶּדֶםinfluences from the east וְעֹנְנִיםthey are soothsayers כַּפְּלִשְׁתִּיםlike the Philistines וּבְיַלְדֵיwith the children נָכְרִיםforeigners יַשְׂפִּיקוּstrike hands

For You have abandoned Your people, the house of Jacob, because they are filled with influences from the east; they are soothsayers like the Philistines; they strike hands with the children of foreigners.

2:7

וַתִּמָּלֵאfull אַרְצוֹland כֶּסֶףsilver וְזָהָבand gold וְאֵיןwith no קֵצֶהlimit לְאֹצְרֹתָיוto their treasures their וַתִּמָּלֵאfull אַרְצוֹland סוּסִיםhorses וְאֵיןwith no קֵצֶהlimit לְמַרְכְּבֹתָיוto their chariots

Their land is full of silver and gold, with no limit to their treasures; their land is full of horses, with no limit to their chariots.

2:8

וַתִּמָּלֵאfull אַרְצוֹland אֱלִילִיםidols לְמַעֲשֵׂהto the work יָדָיוtheir hands יִשְׁתַּחֲווּbow down לַאֲשֶׁרto whom עָשׂוּmade אֶצְבְּעֹתָיוtheir fingers

Their land is full of idols; they bow down to the work of their hands, to what their fingers have made.

2:9

וַיִּשַּׁחbrought אָדָםmankind וַיִּשְׁפַּלlow and אִישׁman וְאַלdo not תִּשָּׂאforgive לָהֶםto them

So mankind is brought low, and man is humbled— do not forgive them!

2:10

בּוֹאgo בַצּוּרinto the rocks וְהִטָּמֵןand hide בֶּעָפָרin the dust מִפְּנֵיfrom before פַּחַדterror יְהוָהLORD וּמֵהֲדַרand the splendor גְּאֹנוֹhis majesty

Go into the rocks and hide in the dust from the terror of the LORD and the splendor of His majesty.

2:11

עֵינֵיlook גַּבְהוּתproud אָדָםman שָׁפֵלhumbled וְשַׁחhumbled and רוּםloftiness אֲנָשִׁיםmen וְנִשְׂגַּבwill exalted יְהוָהLORD לְבַדּוֹalone be בַּיּוֹםin that day הַהוּאthat

The proud look of man will be humbled, and the loftiness of men brought low; the LORD alone will be exalted in that day.

2:12

כִּיfor יוֹםday לַיהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts עַלagainst כָּלall גֵּאֶהproud וָרָםand lofty וְעַלagainst כָּלall נִשָּׂאexalted וְשָׁפֵלwill be humbled

For the Day of the LORD of Hosts will come against all the proud and lofty, against all that is exalted— it will be humbled—

2:13

וְעַלagainst כָּלall אַרְזֵיcedars הַלְּבָנוֹןof Lebanon הָרָמִיםlofty וְהַנִּשָּׂאִיםand lifted וְעַלagainst כָּלall אַלּוֹנֵיoaks הַבָּשָׁןof Bashan

against all the cedars of Lebanon, lofty and lifted up, against all the oaks of Bashan,

2:14

וְעַלagainst כָּלall הֶהָרִיםthe mountains הָרָמִיםtall וְעַלagainst כָּלall הַגְּבָעוֹתthe high hills הַנִּשָּׂאוֹתthe raised

against all the tall mountains, against all the high hills,

2:15

וְעַלagainst כָּלevery מִגְדָּלtower גָּבֹהַhigh וְעַלagainst כָּלevery חוֹמָהwall בְצוּרָהfortified

against every high tower, against every fortified wall,

2:16

וְעַלagainst כָּלevery אֳנִיּוֹתship תַּרְשִׁישׁTarshish וְעַלand against כָּלevery שְׂכִיּוֹתstately הַחֶמְדָּהvessel

against every ship of Tarshish, and against every stately vessel.

2:17

וְשַׁחand humbled גַּבְהוּתpride הָאָדָםof man וְשָׁפֵלlow and רוּםloftiness אֲנָשִׁיםmen וְנִשְׂגַּבexalted יְהוָהLORD לְבַדּוֹalone will be בַּיּוֹםin that day הַהוּאthat

So the pride of man will be brought low, and the loftiness of men will be humbled; the LORD alone will be exalted in that day,

2:18

וְהָאֱלִילִיםand the idols כָּלִילcompletely יַחֲלֹףvanish

and the idols will vanish completely.

2:19

וּבָאוּwill flee בִּמְעָרוֹתto caves in צֻרִיםrocks וּבִמְחִלּוֹתand holes in עָפָרground מִפְּנֵיfrom before פַּחַדterror יְהוָהLORD וּמֵהֲדַרand from the splendor גְּאוֹנוֹhis majesty בְּקוּמוֹwhen he rises לַעֲרֹץto shake הָאָרֶץthe earth

Men will flee to caves in the rocks and holes in the ground, away from the terror of the LORD and from the splendor of His majesty, when He rises to shake the earth.

2:20

בַּיּוֹםin that day הַהוּאmen will יַשְׁלִיךְcast הָאָדָםthe man אֵת[obj] אֱלִילֵיidols כַסְפּוֹsilver וְאֵתand [obj] אֱלִילֵיidols זְהָבוֹgold אֲשֶׁרthat עָשׂוּmade לוֹto him לְהִשְׁתַּחֲוֺתto worship לַחְפֹּרto the moles פֵּרוֹתbats וְלָעֲטַלֵּפִיםand bats

In that day men will cast away to the moles and bats their idols of silver and gold— the idols they made to worship.

2:21

לָבוֹאflee to בְּנִקְרוֹתcaverns in הַצֻּרִיםthe rocks וּבִסְעִפֵיand crevices in הַסְּלָעִיםthe cliffs מִפְּנֵיfrom before פַּחַדterror יְהוָהLORD וּמֵהֲדַרand from the splendor גְּאוֹנוֹhis majesty בְּקוּמוֹwhen he rises לַעֲרֹץto shake הָאָרֶץthe earth

They will flee to caverns in the rocks and crevices in the cliffs, away from the terror of the LORD and from the splendor of His majesty, when He rises to shake the earth.

2:22

חִדְלוּno לָכֶםto you מִןfrom הָאָדָםman אֲשֶׁרwho נְשָׁמָהbreath בְּאַפּוֹin his nostrils כִּיfor בַמֶּהwhat נֶחְשָׁבaccount הוּאis he

Put no more trust in man, who has only the breath in his nostrils. Of what account is he?
