Isaiah 51

Awakening and comfort for Zion

Open in the interlinear reader

51:1

שִׁמְעוּlisten אֵלַיto me רֹדְפֵיpursue צֶדֶקrighteousness מְבַקְשֵׁיseek יְהוָהLORD הַבִּיטוּlook אֶלto צוּרrock חֻצַּבְתֶּםcut וְאֶלand to מַקֶּבֶתquarry בּוֹרfrom which נֻקַּרְתֶּםhewn

“Listen to Me, you who pursue righteousness, you who seek the LORD: Look to the rock from which you were cut, and to the quarry from which you were hewn.

51:2

הַבִּיטוּlook אֶלto אַבְרָהָםAbraham אֲבִיכֶםyour father וְאֶלand to שָׂרָהSarah תְּחוֹלֶלְכֶםgave you birth כִּיwhen אֶחָדone קְרָאתִיוcalled him וַאֲבָרְכֵהוּthen I blessed him וְאַרְבֵּהוּand multiplied him

Look to Abraham your father, and to Sarah who gave you birth. When I called him, he was but one; then I blessed him and multiplied him.

51:3

כִּיfor נִחַםcomfort יְהוָהLORD צִיּוֹןZion נִחַםcompassion כָּלall חָרְבֹתֶיהָher ruins וַיָּשֶׂםmake מִדְבָּרָהּher wilderness כְּעֵדֶןlike Eden וְעַרְבָתָהּand her desert כְּגַןlike the garden יְהוָהLORD שָׂשׂוֹןjoy וְשִׂמְחָהand gladness יִמָּצֵאfound בָהּin her תּוֹדָהthanksgiving וְקוֹלand melodious זִמְרָהsong

For the LORD will comfort Zion and will look with compassion on all her ruins; He will make her wilderness like Eden and her desert like the garden of the LORD. Joy and gladness will be found in her, thanksgiving and melodious song.

51:4

הַקְשִׁיבוּpay attention אֵלַיto me עַמִּיmy people וּלְאוּמִּיand listen אֵלַיto me הַאֲזִינוּnation כִּיfor תוֹרָהlaw מֵאִתִּיfrom the me תֵצֵאgo וּמִשְׁפָּטִיand my justice לְאוֹרbecome a light עַמִּיםnations אַרְגִּיעַquickly

Pay attention to Me, My people, and listen to Me, My nation; for a law will go out from Me, and My justice will become a light to the nations; I will bring it about quickly.

51:5

קָרוֹבnear צִדְקִיmy righteousness יָצָאdraws יִשְׁעִיmy salvation וּזְרֹעַיand my arms עַמִּיםnations יִשְׁפֹּטוּjustice אֵלַיto אִיִּיםislands יְקַוּוּwait וְאֶלand to זְרֹעִיmy arm יְיַחֵלוּןhope

My righteousness draws near, My salvation is on the way, and My arms will bring justice to the nations. The islands will look for Me and wait in hope for My arm.

51:6

שְׂאוּlift לַשָּׁמַיִםto the heavens עֵינֵיכֶםyour eyes וְהַבִּיטוּand look אֶלto הָאָרֶץthe earth מִתַּחַתbelow כִּיfor שָׁמַיִםheavens כֶּעָשָׁןlike smoke נִמְלָחוּvanish וְהָאָרֶץthe earth כַּבֶּגֶדlike a garment תִּבְלֶהwear וְיֹשְׁבֶיהָand its people כְּמוֹlike כֵןgnats יְמוּתוּןdie וִישׁוּעָתִיbut my salvation לְעוֹלָםwill forever תִּהְיֶהlast וְצִדְקָתִיand my righteousness לֹאnever תֵחָתfail

Lift up your eyes to the heavens, and look at the earth below; for the heavens will vanish like smoke, the earth will wear out like a garment, and its people will die like gnats. But My salvation will last forever, and My righteousness will never fail.

51:7

שִׁמְעוּlisten אֵלַיto me יֹדְעֵיknow צֶדֶקright עַםpeople תּוֹרָתִיmy law בְלִבָּםin your hearts אַלdo not תִּירְאוּfear חֶרְפַּתscorn אֱנוֹשׁmen וּמִגִּדֻּפֹתָםby their insults אַלdo not תֵּחָתּוּbroken

Listen to Me, you who know what is right, you people with My law in your hearts: Do not fear the scorn of men; do not be broken by their insults.

51:8

כִּיfor כַבֶּגֶדlike a garment יֹאכְלֵםdevour them עָשׁmoth וְכַצֶּמֶרlike wool יֹאכְלֵםeat them סָסwool וְצִדְקָתִיbut my righteousness לְעוֹלָםwill forever תִּהְיֶהlast וִישׁוּעָתִיmy salvation לְדוֹרall דּוֹרִיםthrough generations

For the moth will devour them like a garment, and the worm will eat them like wool. But My righteousness will last forever, My salvation through all generations.”

51:9

עוּרִיawake עוּרִיawake לִבְשִׁיput עֹזstrength זְרוֹעַarm יְהוָהLORD עוּרִיwake כִּימֵיas in days קֶדֶםpast דֹּרוֹתgenerations עוֹלָמִיםold הֲלוֹאwas it not אַתְּyou הִיאis הַמַּחְצֶבֶתwho cut רַהַבRahab מְחוֹלֶלֶתpierced תַּנִּיןdragon

Awake, awake, put on strength, O arm of the LORD. Wake up as in days past, as in generations of old. Was it not You who cut Rahab to pieces, who pierced through the dragon?

51:10

הֲלוֹאwas it not אַתְּyou הִיאis הַמַּחֲרֶבֶתdried up the יָםsea מֵיwaters תְּהוֹםdeep רַבָּהgreat הַשָּׂמָהwho made מַעֲמַקֵּיdepths יָםsea דֶּרֶךְroad לַעֲבֹרto cross גְּאוּלִיםredeemed

Was it not You who dried up the sea, the waters of the great deep, who made a road in the depths of the sea for the redeemed to cross over?

51:11

וּפְדוּיֵיso the redeemed יְהוָהLORD יְשׁוּבוּןreturn וּבָאוּand enter צִיּוֹןZion בְּרִנָּהwith singing וְשִׂמְחַתcrowned with עוֹלָםeverlasting עַלon רֹאשָׁםtheir head שָׂשׂוֹןjoy וְשִׂמְחָהgladness and יַשִּׂיגוּןovertake נָסוּflee יָגוֹןsorrow וַאֲנָחָהand sighing

So the redeemed of the LORD will return and enter Zion with singing, crowned with everlasting joy. Gladness and joy will overtake them, and sorrow and sighing will flee.

51:12

אָנֹכִיI אָנֹכִיam הוּאhe מְנַחֶמְכֶםcomforts you מִיwho אַתְּyou וַתִּירְאִיafraid מֵאֱנוֹשׁof mortal יָמוּתdie וּמִבֶּןof a son אָדָםman חָצִירgrass יִנָּתֵןwithers

“I, even I, am He who comforts you. Why should you be afraid of mortal man, of a son of man who withers like grass?

51:13

וַתִּשְׁכַּחforgotten יְהוָהLORD עֹשֶׂךָyour Maker נוֹטֶהstretched שָׁמַיִםheavens וְיֹסֵדand laid the foundations אָרֶץearth וַתְּפַחֵדterror תָּמִידalways כָּלall הַיּוֹםday long מִפְּנֵיfrom before חֲמַתfury הַמֵּצִיקthe oppressor כַּאֲשֶׁרwho כּוֹנֵןbent לְהַשְׁחִיתon destruction וְאַיֵּהbut where חֲמַתfury הַמֵּצִיקthe oppressor

But you have forgotten the LORD, your Maker, who stretched out the heavens and laid the foundations of the earth. You live in terror all day long because of the fury of the oppressor who is bent on destruction. But where is the fury of the oppressor?

51:14

מִהַרsoon צֹעֶהfreed לְהִפָּתֵחַbe freed he וְלֹאwill not יָמוּתdie לַשַּׁחַתin the dungeon וְלֹאnot יֶחְסַרlacking לַחְמוֹhis bread

The captive will soon be freed; he will not die in the dungeon, and his bread will not be lacking.

51:15

וְאָנֹכִיfor I am יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God רֹגַעstirs הַיָּםthe sea וַיֶּהֱמוּroar גַּלָּיוits waves יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts שְׁמוֹhis name

For I am the LORD your God who stirs up the sea so that its waves roar— the LORD of Hosts is His name.

51:16

וָאָשִׂיםI have put דְּבָרַיmy words בְּפִיךָin your mouth וּבְצֵלwith the shadow יָדִיmy hand כִּסִּיתִיךָcovered you לִנְטֹעַto establish שָׁמַיִםheavens וְלִיסֹדto found אָרֶץearth וְלֵאמֹרand to say לְצִיּוֹןto Zion עַמִּיmy people אָתָּהyou are

I have put My words in your mouth, and covered you with the shadow of My hand, to establish the heavens, to found the earth, and to say to Zion, ‘You are My people.’”

51:17

הִתְעוֹרְרִיawake הִתְעוֹרְרִיawake קוּמִיrise יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem אֲשֶׁרwho שָׁתִיתdrunk מִיַּדfrom the hand יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] כּוֹסcup חֲמָתוֹhis fury אֶת[obj] קֻבַּעַתgoblet כּוֹסcup הַתַּרְעֵלָהdregs the that makes שָׁתִיתstagger מָצִיתdrained

Awake, awake! Rise up, O Jerusalem, you who have drunk from the hand of the LORD the cup of His fury; you who have drained the goblet to the dregs— the cup that makes men stagger.

51:18

אֵיןno מְנַהֵלguide לָהּto her מִכָּלamong all בָּנִיםsons יָלָדָהbore וְאֵיןthere is no מַחֲזִיקtake בְּיָדָהּto her hand מִכָּלamong all בָּנִיםsons גִּדֵּלָהhonored

Among all the sons she bore, there is no one to guide her; among all the sons she brought up, there is no one to take her hand.

51:19

שְׁתַּיִםpairs הֵנָּהthese קֹרְאֹתַיִךְbefallen you מִיwho יָנוּדgrieve לָךְfor you הַשֹּׁדdevastation וְהַשֶּׁבֶרand destruction וְהָרָעָבfamine וְהַחֶרֶבand sword מִיcan אֲנַחֲמֵךְcomfort you

These pairs have befallen you: devastation and destruction, famine and sword. Who will grieve for you? Who can comfort you?

51:20

בָּנַיִךְyour sons עֻלְּפוּfainted שָׁכְבוּlie בְּרֹאשׁat the head כָּלevery חוּצוֹתstreet כְּתוֹאlike an antelope מִכְמָרnet הַמְלֵאִיםare full חֲמַתwrath יְהוָהLORD גַּעֲרַתrebuke אֱלֹהָיִךְyour God

Your sons have fainted; they lie at the head of every street, like an antelope in a net. They are full of the wrath of the LORD, the rebuke of your God.

51:21

לָכֵןtherefore שִׁמְעִיhear נָאnow זֹאתthis עֲנִיָּהafflicted וּשְׁכֻרַתdrunken וְלֹאbut not מִיָּיִןwith wine

Therefore now hear this, you afflicted one, drunken, but not with wine.

51:22

כֹּהthus אָמַרsays אֲדֹנַיִךְyour Lord יְהוָהLORD וֵאלֹהַיִךְeven your God יָרִיבwho עַמּוֹhis people הִנֵּהsee לָקַחְתִּיremoved מִיָּדֵךְfrom your hand אֶת[obj] כּוֹסcup הַתַּרְעֵלָהof staggering אֶת[obj] קֻבַּעַתgoblet כּוֹסcup חֲמָתִיmy fury לֹאnot תוֹסִיפִיnever לִשְׁתּוֹתָהּdrink עוֹדagain

Thus says your Lord, the LORD, even your God, who defends His people: “See, I have removed from your hand the cup of staggering. From that goblet, the cup of My fury, you will never drink again.

51:23

וְשַׂמְתִּיהָwill place it בְּיַדin the hands מוֹגַיִךְyour tormentors אֲשֶׁרwho אָמְרוּtold לְנַפְשֵׁךְyou שְׁחִיdown וְנַעֲבֹרָהwalk וַתָּשִׂימִיmade כָאָרֶץlike the ground גֵּוֵךְyour back וְכַחוּץlike a street לַעֹבְרִיםto be traversed

I will place it in the hands of your tormentors, who told you: ‘Lie down, so we can walk over you,’ so that you made your back like the ground, like a street to be traversed.”
