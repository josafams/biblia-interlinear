Isaiah 44

The only God and the folly of idols

Open in the interlinear reader

44:1

וְעַתָּהbut now שְׁמַעlisten יַעֲקֹבJacob עַבְדִּיmy servant וְיִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael בָּחַרְתִּיchosen בוֹin him

But now listen, O Jacob My servant, Israel, whom I have chosen.

44:2

כֹּהthis אָמַרis יְהוָהLORD עֹשֶׂךָyour Maker וְיֹצֶרְךָwho formed you מִבֶּטֶןfrom the womb יַעְזְרֶךָּhelp you אַלdo not תִּירָאafraid עַבְדִּיmy servant יַעֲקֹבJacob וִישֻׁרוּןJeshurun בָּחַרְתִּיchosen בוֹin him

This is the word of the LORD, your Maker, who formed you from the womb and who will help you: “Do not be afraid, O Jacob My servant, Jeshurun, whom I have chosen.

44:3

כִּיfor אֶצָּקpour מַיִםwater עַלon צָמֵאthirsty וְנֹזְלִיםand currents עַלon יַבָּשָׁהdry ground אֶצֹּקpour רוּחִיmy spirit עַלon זַרְעֶךָyour descendants וּבִרְכָתִיand my blessing עַלon צֶאֱצָאֶיךָyour offspring

For I will pour water on the thirsty land, and currents on the dry ground. I will pour out My Spirit on your descendants, and My blessing on your offspring.

44:4

וְצָמְחוּthey will sprout בְּבֵיןamong the חָצִירgrass כַּעֲרָבִיםlike willows עַלon יִבְלֵיflowing streams מָיִםflowing

They will sprout among the grass like willows by flowing streams.

44:5

זֶהthis יֹאמַרsay לַיהוָהLORD אָנִיI וְזֶהand this יִקְרָאcall בְשֵׁםby the name יַעֲקֹבJacob וְזֶהand this יִכְתֹּבwrite יָדוֹhis hand לַיהוָהthe LORD’s וּבְשֵׁםthe name יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael יְכַנֶּהflatter

One will say, ‘I belong to the LORD,’ another will call himself by the name of Jacob, and still another will write on his hand, ‘The LORD’s,’ and will take the name of Israel.”

44:6

כֹּהthus אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD מֶלֶךְking יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וְגֹאֲלוֹand Redeemer יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts אֲנִיam רִאשׁוֹןfirst וַאֲנִיand I am אַחֲרוֹןlast וּמִבַּלְעָדַיbut me אֵיןno אֱלֹהִיםGod

Thus says the LORD, the King and Redeemer of Israel, the LORD of Hosts: “I am the first and I am the last, and there is no God but Me.

44:7

וּמִיwho then כָמוֹנִיlike me יִקְרָאcalls וְיַגִּידֶהָhim declare וְיַעְרְכֶהָhis case לִיto me מִשּׂוּמִיsince I established עַםpeople עוֹלָםancient וְאֹתִיּוֹתcome וַאֲשֶׁרand what תָּבֹאנָהtake יַגִּידוּdeclare לָמוֹto

Who then is like Me? Let him say so! Let him declare his case before Me, since I established an ancient people. Let him foretell the things to come, and what is to take place.

44:8

אַלdo not תִּפְחֲדוּtremble וְאַלor תִּרְהוּfear הֲלֹאhave not מֵאָזfrom time הִשְׁמַעְתִּיךָtold you וְהִגַּדְתִּיand declared וְאַתֶּםyou עֵדָיmy witnesses הֲיֵשׁis there אֱלוֹהַּGod מִבַּלְעָדַיany but me וְאֵיןthere is no צוּרrock בַּלnot יָדָעְתִּיknow

Do not tremble or fear. Have I not told you and declared it long ago? You are My witnesses! Is there any God but Me? There is no other Rock; I know not one.”

44:9

יֹצְרֵיmakers פֶסֶלidols כֻּלָּםall תֹּהוּnothing וַחֲמוּדֵיהֶםtreasure are בַּלnot יוֹעִילוּworthless וְעֵדֵיהֶםtheir witnesses הֵׄמָּׄהׄthey בַּלnot יִרְאוּsee וּבַלand no יֵדְעוּcomprehend לְמַעַןso to יֵבֹשׁוּput shame

All makers of idols are nothing, and the things they treasure are worthless. Their witnesses fail to see or comprehend, so they are put to shame.

44:10

מִיwho יָצַרfashions אֵלgod וּפֶסֶלidol נָסָךְcasts לְבִלְתִּיto not הוֹעִילnothing

Who fashions a god or casts an idol which profits him nothing?

44:11

הֵןbehold כָּלall חֲבֵרָיוhis companions יֵבֹשׁוּput shame וְחָרָשִׁיםcraftsmen הֵמָּהare מֵאָדָםhuman יִתְקַבְּצוּassemble כֻלָּםall יַעֲמֹדוּstand יִפְחֲדוּterror יֵבֹשׁוּshame יָחַדall

Behold, all his companions will be put to shame, for the craftsmen themselves are only human. Let them all assemble and take their stand; they will all be brought to terror and shame.

44:12

חָרַשׁblacksmith בַּרְזֶלtakes מַעֲצָדtool labors וּפָעַלand labors בַּפֶּחָםhammers forges וּבַמַּקָּבוֹתcoals יִצְּרֵהוּfashions וַיִּפְעָלֵהוּand practices him בִּזְרוֹעַwith strong arms כֹּחוֹhis strong גַּםeven רָעֵבhungry וְאֵיןand no כֹּחַstrength לֹאnot שָׁתָהdrink מַיִםwater וַיִּיעָףand grows faint

The blacksmith takes a tool and labors over the coals; he fashions an idol with hammers and forges it with his strong arms. Yet he grows hungry and loses his strength; he fails to drink water and grows faint.

44:13

חָרַשׁwoodworker עֵצִיםextends נָטָהout קָוmeasuring line יְתָאֲרֵהוּmarks it בַשֶּׂרֶדwith a stylus יַעֲשֵׂהוּhe בַּמַּקְצֻעוֹתshapes וּבַמְּחוּגָהwith chisels יְתָאֳרֵהוּoutlines וַיַּעֲשֵׂהוּand made him כְּתַבְנִיתlikeness אִישׁman כְּתִפְאֶרֶתglory אָדָםman לָשֶׁבֶתdwell בָּיִתshrine

The woodworker extends a measuring line; he marks it out with a stylus; he shapes it with chisels and outlines it with a compass. He fashions it in the likeness of man, like man in all his glory, that it may dwell in a shrine.

44:14

לִכְרָתcuts לוֹto him אֲרָזִיםcedars וַיִּקַּחor retrieves תִּרְזָהcypress וְאַלּוֹןor oak lets וַיְאַמֶּץstrong לוֹto him בַּעֲצֵיamong the trees יָעַרforest נָטַעplants אֹרֶןlaurel וְגֶשֶׁםand the rain יְגַדֵּלgrow

He cuts down cedars or retrieves a cypress or oak. He lets it grow strong among the trees of the forest. He plants a laurel, and the rain makes it grow.

44:15

וְהָיָהit serves לְאָדָםfor man לְבָעֵרas fuel וַיִּקַּחhe takes מֵהֶםsome וַיָּחָםwarm himself and אַףhow much יַשִּׂיקkindles a fire וְאָפָהand bakes לָחֶםbread אַףeven יִפְעַלfashions אֵלgod וַיִּשְׁתָּחוּand worships עָשָׂהוּmakes פֶסֶלidol וַיִּסְגָּדand bows לָמוֹto

It serves as fuel for man. He takes some of it to warm himself, and he kindles a fire and bakes his bread; he even fashions it into a god and worships it; he makes an idol and bows down to it.

44:16

חֶצְיוֹhalf of it שָׂרַףburns בְּמוֹwith אֵשׁfire עַלon חֶצְיוֹhalf he בָּשָׂרmeat יֹאכֵלeats יִצְלֶהroast צָלִיroast וְיִשְׂבָּעand is satisfied אַףindeed יָחֹםwarms וְיֹאמַרand says הֶאָחah חַמּוֹתִיwarm רָאִיתִיsee אוּרfire

He burns half of it in the fire, and he roasts meat on that half. He eats the roast and is satisfied. Indeed, he warms himself and says, “Ah! I am warm; I see the fire.”

44:17

וּשְׁאֵרִיתוֹrest he לְאֵלa god עָשָׂהmakes לְפִסְלוֹhis graven image he יִסְגָּדbows לוֹdown to it וְיִשְׁתַּחוּand worships וְיִתְפַּלֵּלhe prays אֵלָיוto it וְיֹאמַרand says הַצִּילֵנִיsave me כִּיfor אֵלִיmy god אָתָּהyou are

From the rest he makes a god, his graven image. He bows down to it and worships; he prays to it and says, “Save me, for you are my god.”

44:18

לֹאnot יָדְעוּcomprehend וְלֹאor יָבִינוּdiscern כִּיfor טַחshut מֵרְאוֹתcannot see עֵינֵיהֶםtheir eyes מֵהַשְׂכִּילcannot understand לִבֹּתָםclosed their minds

They do not comprehend or discern, for He has shut their eyes so they cannot see and closed their minds so they cannot understand.

44:19

וְלֹאand no יָשִׁיבconsiders אֶלto לִבּוֹhis heart וְלֹאno דַעַתknowledge וְלֹאor תְבוּנָהinsight לֵאמֹרto say חֶצְיוֹhalf of it שָׂרַפְתִּיburned בְמוֹwith אֵשׁfire וְאַףand אָפִיתִיbaked עַלon גֶּחָלָיוits coals לֶחֶםbread אֶצְלֶהroasted בָשָׂרmeat וְאֹכֵלand I ate וְיִתְרוֹwith the rest of it לְתוֹעֵבָהdetestable אֶעֱשֶׂהmake לְבוּלto a block עֵץwood אֶסְגּוֹדbow

And no one considers in his heart, no one has the knowledge or insight to say, “I burned half of it in the fire, and I baked bread on its coals; I roasted meat and I ate. Shall I make something detestable with the rest of it? Shall I bow down to a block of wood?”

44:20

רֹעֶהfeeds אֵפֶרashes לֵבheart הוּתַלdeluded הִטָּהוּhim astray וְלֹאand not יַצִּילdeliver אֶת[obj] נַפְשׁוֹhimself וְלֹאor יֹאמַרsay הֲלוֹאis not שֶׁקֶרthing בִּימִינִיin my right hand

He feeds on ashes. His deluded heart has led him astray, and he cannot deliver himself or say, “Is not this thing in my right hand a lie?”

44:21

זְכָרremember אֵלֶּהthese יַעֲקֹבJacob וְיִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael כִּיfor עַבְדִּיmy servant אָתָּהyou יְצַרְתִּיךָmade עֶבֶדservant לִיto me אַתָּהyou יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael לֹאnever תִנָּשֵׁנִיforget you

Remember these things, O Jacob, for you are My servant, O Israel. I have made you, and you are My servant; O Israel, I will never forget you.

44:22

מָחִיתִיblotted כָעָבlike a cloud פְּשָׁעֶיךָyour transgressions וְכֶעָנָןlike a mist חַטֹּאותֶיךָyour sins שׁוּבָהreturn אֵלַיto me כִּיfor גְאַלְתִּיךָredeemed you

I have blotted out your transgressions like a cloud, and your sins like a mist. Return to Me, for I have redeemed you.

44:23

רָנּוּsing שָׁמַיִםheavens כִּיfor עָשָׂהdone יְהוָהLORD הָרִיעוּshout aloud תַּחְתִּיּוֹתdepths אָרֶץearth פִּצְחוּbreak forth הָרִיםmountains רִנָּהsong יַעַרforests וְכָלand all עֵץtrees בּוֹin him כִּיfor גָאַלredeemed יְהוָהLORD יַעֲקֹבJacob וּבְיִשְׂרָאֵלin Israel יִתְפָּאָרglory

Sing for joy, O heavens, for the LORD has done this; shout aloud, O depths of the earth. Break forth in song, O mountains, you forests and all your trees. For the LORD has redeemed Jacob, and revealed His glory in Israel.

44:24

כֹּהthus אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD גֹּאֲלֶךָyour Redeemer וְיֹצֶרְךָwho formed you מִבָּטֶןfrom the womb אָנֹכִיI am יְהוָהLORD עֹשֶׂהmade כֹּלall נֹטֶהstretched שָׁמַיִםheavens לְבַדִּיalone רֹקַעspread הָאָרֶץthe earth מֵאִתִּיfrom the me

Thus says the LORD, your Redeemer who formed you from the womb: “I am the LORD, who has made all things, who alone stretched out the heavens, who by Myself spread out the earth,

44:25

מֵפֵרfoils אֹתוֹתsigns בַּדִּיםfalse prophets וְקֹסְמִיםdiviners יְהוֹלֵלfools מֵשִׁיבconfounds חֲכָמִיםwise אָחוֹרturns וְדַעְתָּםand their knowledge into יְשַׂכֵּלnonsense

who foils the signs of false prophets and makes fools of diviners, who confounds the wise and turns their knowledge into nonsense,

44:26

מֵקִיםconfirms דְּבַרmessage עַבְדּוֹhis servant וַעֲצַתcounsel מַלְאָכָיוhis messengers יַשְׁלִיםwho הָאֹמֵרwho says לִירוּשָׁלִַםof Jerusalem תּוּשָׁבinhabited וּלְעָרֵיand of the cities יְהוּדָהJudah תִּבָּנֶינָהrebuilt וְחָרְבוֹתֶיהָand I will their ruins אֲקוֹמֵםrestore

who confirms the message of His servant and fulfills the counsel of His messengers, who says of Jerusalem, ‘She will be inhabited,’ and of the cities of Judah, ‘They will be rebuilt, and I will restore their ruins,’

44:27

הָאֹמֵרwho says לַצּוּלָהto the depths חֳרָבִיdry וְנַהֲרֹתַיִךְup your currents אוֹבִישׁdry

who says to the depths of the sea, ‘Be dry, and I will dry up your currents,’

44:28

הָאֹמֵרwho says לְכוֹרֶשׁof Cyrus רֹעִיmy shepherd וְכָלwill all חֶפְצִיI desire יַשְׁלִםfulfill וְלֵאמֹרwho says לִירוּשָׁלִַםof Jerusalem תִּבָּנֶהrebuilt וְהֵיכָלtemple תִּוָּסֵדfoundation

who says of Cyrus, ‘My shepherd will fulfill all that I desire,’ who says of Jerusalem, ‘She will be rebuilt,’ and of the temple, ‘Let its foundation be laid.’”
