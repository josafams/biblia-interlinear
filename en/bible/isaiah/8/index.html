Isaiah 8

Maher-shalal-hash-baz and the Assyrian invasion

Then the LORD said to me, “Take a large scroll and write on it with an ordinary stylus: Maher-shalal-hash-baz.

And I will appoint for Myself trustworthy witnesses—Uriah the priest and Zechariah son of Jeberekiah.”

And I had relations with the prophetess, and she conceived and gave birth to a son. The LORD said to me, “Name him Maher-shalal-hash-baz.

8:4

For before the boy knows how to cry ‘Father’ or ‘Mother,’ the wealth of Damascus and the plunder of Samaria will be carried off by the king of Assyria.”

8:5

וַיֹּסֶףand again יְהוָהLORD דַּבֵּרspoke אֵלַיto me עוֹדlonger לֵאמֹרto saying

And the LORD spoke to me further:

8:6

“Because this people has rejected the gently flowing waters of Shiloah and rejoiced in Rezin and the son of Remaliah,

8:7

וְלָכֵןand to therefore הִנֵּהsurely אֲדֹנָיLord will מַעֲלֶהbring עֲלֵיהֶםagainst them אֶת[obj] מֵיfloodwaters הַנָּהָרthe Euphrates הָעֲצוּמִיםthe mighty וְהָרַבִּיםand the many אֶת[obj] מֶלֶךְking אַשּׁוּרAssyria וְאֶתand [obj] כָּלall כְּבוֹדוֹhis pomp it וְעָלָהwill overflow עַלon כָּלall אֲפִיקָיוits channels וְהָלַךְand way עַלon כָּלall גְּדוֹתָיוoverrun its banks

the Lord will surely bring against them the mighty floodwaters of the Euphrates— the king of Assyria and all his pomp. It will overflow its channels and overrun its banks.

8:8

וְחָלַףwill pour בִּיהוּדָהinto Judah שָׁטַףswirling sweeping וְעָבַרand over עַדto צַוָּארneck יַגִּיעַreaching וְהָיָהand be מֻטּוֹתspreading כְּנָפָיוstreams מְלֹאcover רֹחַבentire אַרְצְךָyour land O עִמָּנוּImmanuel אֵלImmanuel

It will pour into Judah, swirling and sweeping over it, reaching up to the neck; its spreading streams will cover your entire land, O Immanuel!

8:9

רֹעוּhuddle עַמִּיםpeoples וָחֹתּוּand be shattered וְהַאֲזִינוּpay attention כֹּלall מֶרְחַקֵּיdistant אָרֶץlands הִתְאַזְּרוּprepare for battle וָחֹתּוּand be shattered הִתְאַזְּרוּprepare for battle וָחֹתּוּand be shattered

Huddle together, O peoples, and be shattered; pay attention, all you distant lands; prepare for battle, and be shattered; prepare for battle, and be shattered!

8:10

עֻצוּdevise עֵצָהplan וְתֻפָרthwarted דַּבְּרוּstate דָבָרproposal וְלֹאnot יָקוּםhappen כִּיfor עִמָּנוּwith us אֵלGod

Devise a plan, but it will be thwarted; state a proposal, but it will not happen. For God is with us.”

8:11

כִּיfor כֹהthis אָמַרis what יְהוָהLORD אֵלַיto me כְּחֶזְקַתwith strong הַיָּדa hand וְיִסְּרֵנִיinstructing me מִלֶּכֶתnot to walk בְּדֶרֶךְin the way הָעָםthis people הַזֶּהthis לֵאמֹרto saying

For this is what the LORD has spoken to me with a strong hand, instructing me not to walk in the way of this people:

8:12

לֹאnot תֹאמְרוּןcall קֶשֶׁרconspiracy לְכֹלeverything אֲשֶׁרthat יֹאמַרsays הָעָםthese people הַזֶּהthis קָשֶׁרconspiracy וְאֶתand [obj] מוֹרָאוֹhis fear dread לֹאnot תִירְאוּfear וְלֹאnot תַעֲרִיצוּdread

“Do not call conspiracy everything these people regard as conspiracy. Do not fear what they fear; do not live in dread.

8:13

אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts אֹתוֹhim תַקְדִּישׁוּholy וְהוּאonly he מוֹרַאֲכֶםyou shall regard וְהוּאand he מַעֲרִצְכֶםfeared

The LORD of Hosts is the One you shall regard as holy. Only He should be feared; only He should be dreaded.

8:14

וְהָיָהbe לְמִקְדָּשׁa sanctuary וּלְאֶבֶןa stone נֶגֶףstumbling וּלְצוּרand a rock מִכְשׁוֹלoffense לִשְׁנֵיboth בָתֵּיhouses יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael לְפַחtrap וּלְמוֹקֵשׁa and a snare לְיוֹשֵׁבdwellers יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem

And He will be a sanctuary— but to both houses of Israel a stone of stumbling and a rock of offense, to the dwellers of Jerusalem a trap and a snare.

8:15

וְכָשְׁלוּwill stumble בָםin them רַבִּיםmany וְנָפְלוּwill fall וְנִשְׁבָּרוּand be broken וְנוֹקְשׁוּensnared וְנִלְכָּדוּand captured

Many will stumble over these; they will fall and be broken; they will be ensnared and captured.”

8:16

צוֹרbind תְּעוּדָהtestimony חֲתוֹםseal תּוֹרָהlaw בְּלִמֻּדָיamong my disciples

Bind up the testimony and seal the law among my disciples.

8:17

וְחִכִּיתִיwill wait לַיהוָהfor the LORD הַמַּסְתִּירwho is hiding פָּנָיוhis face מִבֵּיתfrom the house יַעֲקֹבJacob וְקִוֵּיתִיtrust לוֹto him

I will wait for the LORD, who is hiding His face from the house of Jacob. I will put my trust in Him.

8:18

הִנֵּהhere אָנֹכִיam וְהַיְלָדִיםand the children אֲשֶׁרhas נָתַןgiven לִיto me יְהוָהLORD לְאֹתוֹתas signs וּלְמוֹפְתִיםand symbols בְּיִשְׂרָאֵלin Israel מֵעִםfrom presence יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts הַשֹּׁכֵןwho dwells בְּהַרon Mount צִיּוֹןZion

Here am I, and the children the LORD has given me as signs and symbols in Israel from the LORD of Hosts, who dwells on Mount Zion.

8:19

וְכִיwhen יֹאמְרוּtell אֲלֵיכֶםyou to דִּרְשׁוּconsult אֶלto הָאֹבוֹתthe spirits וְאֶלand to הַיִּדְּעֹנִיםthe spiritists הַמְצַפְצְפִיםwho whisper וְהַמַּהְגִּיםand mutter הֲלוֹאnot עַםpeople אֶלto אֱלֹהָיוGod instead why יִדְרֹשׁconsult בְּעַדbehalf הַחַיִּיםthe living אֶלto הַמֵּתִיםthe dead

When men tell you to consult the spirits of the dead and the spiritists who whisper and mutter, shouldn’t a people consult their God instead? Why consult the dead on behalf of the living?

8:20

לְתוֹרָהto the law וְלִתְעוּדָהand to the testimony אִםif לֹאnot יֹאמְרוּspeak כַּדָּבָרaccording to this word הַזֶּהthis אֲשֶׁרhave אֵיןno לוֹto him שָׁחַרdawn

To the law and to the testimony! If they do not speak according to this word, they have no light of dawn.

8:21

וְעָבַרthey will roam בָּהּthe land נִקְשֶׁהdejected וְרָעֵבand hungry וְהָיָהwill become כִיwhen יִרְעַבhungry וְהִתְקַצַּףthey are famished וְקִלֵּלthey will curse בְּמַלְכּוֹtheir king their וּבֵאלֹהָיוand their God וּפָנָהand looking לְמָעְלָהupward

They will roam the land, dejected and hungry. When they are famished, they will become enraged; and looking upward, they will curse their king and their God.

8:22

וְאֶלto אֶרֶץearth יַבִּיטlook וְהִנֵּהand see צָרָהdistress וַחֲשֵׁכָהand darkness מְעוּףdimness צוּקָהanguish וַאֲפֵלָהgloom מְנֻדָּחdriven

Then they will look to the earth and see only distress and darkness and the gloom of anguish. And they will be driven into utter darkness.
