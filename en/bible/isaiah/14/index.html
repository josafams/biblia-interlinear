Isaiah 14

The fall of the king of Babylon; Assyria and Philistia

For the LORD will have compassion on Jacob; once again He will choose Israel and settle them in their own land. The foreigner will join them and unite with the house of Jacob.

The nations will escort Israel and bring it to its homeland. Then the house of Israel will possess the nations as menservants and maidservants in the LORD’s land. They will make captives of their captors and rule over their oppressors.

14:3

וְהָיָהand be בְּיוֹםon the day הָנִיחַrest יְהוָהLORD לְךָto you מֵעָצְבְּךָfrom your pain וּמֵרָגְזֶךָand torment וּמִןand from הָעֲבֹדָהthe service הַקָּשָׁהhard אֲשֶׁרwhich עֻבַּדlabor בָּךְin you

On the day that the LORD gives you rest from your pain and torment, and from the hard labor into which you were forced,

14:4

you will sing this song of contempt against the king of Babylon: How the oppressor has ceased, and how his fury has ended!

14:5

The LORD has broken the staff of the wicked, the scepter of the rulers.

14:6

מַכֶּהstruck עַמִּיםpeoples בְּעֶבְרָהin anger מַכַּתunceasing בִּלְתִּיno סָרָהunceasing רֹדֶהsubdued בָאַףthe in rage גּוֹיִםnations מֻרְדָּףpersecuted בְּלִיwithout חָשָׂךְpersecution

It struck the peoples in anger with unceasing blows; it subdued the nations in rage with relentless persecution.

14:7

All the earth is at peace and at rest; they break out in song.

14:8

גַּםeven בְּרוֹשִׁיםcypresses שָׂמְחוּexult לְךָto you אַרְזֵיcedars לְבָנוֹןLebanon מֵאָזfrom time שָׁכַבְתָּlaid לֹאno יַעֲלֶהup הַכֹּרֵתwoodcutter עָלֵינוּagainst us

Even the cypresses and cedars of Lebanon exult over you: “Since you have been laid low, no woodcutter comes against us.”

14:9

שְׁאוֹלSheol מִתַּחַתbeneath רָגְזָהeager לְךָto you לִקְרַאתmeet בּוֹאֶךָyour arrival עוֹרֵרstirs לְךָto you רְפָאִיםspirits כָּלall עַתּוּדֵיrulers אָרֶץearth הֵקִיםrise מִכִּסְאוֹתָםfrom their thrones כֹּלall מַלְכֵיkings גוֹיִםnations

Sheol beneath is eager to meet you upon your arrival. It stirs the spirits of the dead to greet you— all the rulers of the earth. It makes all the kings of the nations rise from their thrones.

14:10

כֻּלָּםthey will all יַעֲנוּrespond וְיֹאמְרוּyou saying אֵלֶיךָto you גַּםtoo אַתָּהyou חֻלֵּיתָweak כָמוֹנוּas we אֵלֵינוּto us נִמְשָׁלְתָּbecome like

They will all respond to you, saying, “You too have become weak, as we are; you have become like us!”

14:11

הוּרַדbrought down שְׁאוֹלSheol גְּאוֹנֶךָalong הֶמְיַתmaggots נְבָלֶיךָyour harps תַּחְתֶּיךָunder you יֻצַּעbed רִמָּהworms וּמְכַסֶּיךָand your covering entrails תּוֹלֵעָהblanket

Your pomp has been brought down to Sheol, along with the music of your harps. Maggots are your bed and worms your blanket.

14:12

אֵיךְhow נָפַלְתָּfallen מִשָּׁמַיִםfrom heaven הֵילֵלday star בֶּןson שָׁחַרdawn נִגְדַּעְתָּcut לָאָרֶץto the ground חוֹלֵשׁdestroyer עַלon גּוֹיִםnations

How you have fallen from heaven, O day star, son of the dawn! You have been cut down to the ground, O destroyer of nations.

14:13

וְאַתָּהyou אָמַרְתָּsaid בִלְבָבְךָin your heart הַשָּׁמַיִםthe heavens אֶעֱלֶהascend מִמַּעַלabove לְכוֹכְבֵיthe stars אֵלGod אָרִיםraise כִּסְאִיmy throne וְאֵשֵׁבwill sit בְּהַרon the mount מוֹעֵדassembly בְּיַרְכְּתֵיin the far צָפוֹןnorth

You said in your heart: “I will ascend to the heavens; I will raise my throne above the stars of God. I will sit on the mount of assembly, in the far reaches of the north.

14:14

אֶעֱלֶהascend עַלon בָּמֳתֵיtops עָבclouds אֶדַּמֶּהlike לְעֶלְיוֹןthe Most high

I will ascend above the tops of the clouds; I will make myself like the Most High.”

14:15

אַךְbut אֶלto שְׁאוֹלSheol תּוּרָדbrought down אֶלto יַרְכְּתֵיlowest depths בוֹרpit

But you will be brought down to Sheol, to the lowest depths of the Pit.

14:16

רֹאֶיךָsee you אֵלֶיךָto you יַשְׁגִּיחוּstare אֵלֶיךָto you יִתְבּוֹנָנוּponder fate הֲזֶהis this הָאִישׁthe man מַרְגִּיזshook הָאָרֶץwho the earth מַרְעִישׁtremble מַמְלָכוֹתkingdoms

Those who see you will stare; they will ponder your fate: “Is this the man who shook the earth and made the kingdoms tremble,

14:17

שָׂםturned תֵּבֵלworld כַּמִּדְבָּרinto a desert וְעָרָיוand its cities הָרָסdestroyed אֲסִירָיוcaptives לֹאnot פָתַחreturn בָּיְתָהhomes

who turned the world into a desert and destroyed its cities, who refused to let the captives return to their homes?”

14:18

כָּלall מַלְכֵיkings גוֹיִםnations כֻּלָּםtheir all שָׁכְבוּlie בְכָבוֹדin state אִישׁeach בְּבֵיתוֹin his own tomb

All the kings of the nations lie in state, each in his own tomb.

14:19

וְאַתָּהbut you הָשְׁלַכְתָּcast מִקִּבְרְךָout of your grave כְּנֵצֶרbranch נִתְעָבrejected לְבוּשׁcovered הֲרֻגִיםslain מְטֹעֲנֵיthrust through חָרֶבsword יוֹרְדֵיdumped אֶלinto אַבְנֵיa rocky בוֹרpit כְּפֶגֶרlike a carcass underfoot מוּבָסtrampled

But you are cast out of your grave like a rejected branch, covered by those slain with the sword, and dumped into a rocky pit like a carcass trampled underfoot.

14:20

לֹאnot תֵחַדjoin אִתָּםwith them בִּקְבוּרָהin burial כִּיfor אַרְצְךָyour land שִׁחַתָּdestroyed עַמְּךָyour own people הָרָגְתָּslaughtered לֹאnever יִקָּרֵאagain לְעוֹלָםbe mentioned זֶרַעoffspring מְרֵעִיםwicked

You will not join them in burial, since you have destroyed your land and slaughtered your own people. The offspring of the wicked will never again be mentioned.

14:21

הָכִינוּprepare לְבָנָיוto his sons מַטְבֵּחַslaughter בַּעֲוֺןfor the iniquities אֲבוֹתָםtheir forefathers they בַּלnever יָקֻמוּrise וְיָרְשׁוּto possess אָרֶץland וּמָלְאוּor cover פְנֵיface תֵבֵלearth עָרִיםcities

Prepare a place to slaughter his sons for the iniquities of their forefathers. They will never rise up to possess a land or cover the earth with their cities.

14:22

וְקַמְתִּיwill rise עֲלֵיהֶםagainst them נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts וְהִכְרַתִּיwill cut off לְבָבֶלfrom Babylon שֵׁםname וּשְׁאָרand her remnant וְנִיןher offspring וָנֶכֶדand her posterity נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD

“I will rise up against them,” declares the LORD of Hosts. “I will cut off from Babylon her name and her remnant, her offspring and her posterity,” declares the LORD.

14:23

וְשַׂמְתִּיהָwill make her לְמוֹרַשׁfor owls קִפֹּדscreech owl וְאַגְמֵיand for swamplands מָיִםwater וְטֵאטֵאתִיהָwill sweep her בְּמַטְאֲטֵאwith the broom הַשְׁמֵדdestruction נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts

“I will make her a place for owls and for swamplands; I will sweep her away with the broom of destruction,” declares the LORD of Hosts.

14:24

נִשְׁבַּעsworn יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts לֵאמֹרhas אִםif לֹאnot כַּאֲשֶׁרlike have דִּמִּיתִיplanned כֵּןso הָיָתָהbe וְכַאֲשֶׁרit as I have יָעַצְתִּיpurposed הִיאwill תָקוּםstand

The LORD of Hosts has sworn: “Surely, as I have planned, so will it be; as I have purposed, so will it stand.

14:25

לִשְׁבֹּרwill break אַשּׁוּרAssyria בְּאַרְצִיin my land וְעַלon הָרַיmy mountain אֲבוּסֶנּוּtrample him וְסָרwill be taken מֵעֲלֵיהֶםfrom the them עֻלּוֹhis yoke וְסֻבֳּלוֹand his burden מֵעַלfrom the שִׁכְמוֹtheir shoulders יָסוּרremoved

I will break Assyria in My land; I will trample him on My mountain. His yoke will be taken off My people, and his burden removed from their shoulders.”

14:26

זֹאתthis הָעֵצָהthe plan הַיְּעוּצָהdevised עַלfor כָּלwhole הָאָרֶץthe earth וְזֹאתand this הַיָּדthe hand הַנְּטוּיָהstretched עַלover כָּלall הַגּוֹיִםthe nations

This is the plan devised for the whole earth, and this is the hand stretched out over all the nations.

14:27

כִּיfor יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts יָעָץpurposed וּמִיand who יָפֵרthwart וְיָדוֹhim his hand הַנְּטוּיָהis outstretched וּמִיso who יְשִׁיבֶנָּהturn it

The LORD of Hosts has purposed, and who can thwart Him? His hand is outstretched, so who can turn it back?

14:28

בִּשְׁנַתin the year מוֹתdied הַמֶּלֶךְthat king אָחָזAhaz הָיָהwas הַמַּשָּׂאthis burden הַזֶּהthis

In the year that King Ahaz died, this burden was received:

14:29

אַלdo not תִּשְׂמְחִיrejoice פְלֶשֶׁתPhilistines כֻּלֵּךְall you כִּיthat נִשְׁבַּרbroken שֵׁבֶטrod מַכֵּךְstruck you כִּיfor מִשֹּׁרֶשׁfrom the root נָחָשׁsnake יֵצֵאspring צֶפַעviper וּפִרְיוֹits egg שָׂרָףserpent מְעוֹפֵףflying

Do not rejoice, all you Philistines, that the rod that struck you is broken. For a viper will spring from the root of the snake, and a flying serpent from its egg.

14:30

וְרָעוּpasture בְּכוֹרֵיfirstborn דַלִּיםpoor וְאֶבְיוֹנִיםand the needy לָבֶטַחin safety יִרְבָּצוּlie down וְהֵמַתִּיkill בָרָעָבby famine שָׁרְשֵׁךְyour root וּשְׁאֵרִיתֵךְand your remnant יַהֲרֹגslain

Then the firstborn of the poor will find pasture, and the needy will lie down in safety, but I will kill your root by famine, and your remnant will be slain.

14:31

הֵילִילִיwail שַׁעַרgate זַעֲקִיcry out עִירcity נָמוֹגmelt פְּלֶשֶׁתPhilistines כֻּלֵּךְall you כִּיfor מִצָּפוֹןfrom the north עָשָׁןsmoke בָּאcomes וְאֵיןno בּוֹדֵדstragglers בְּמוֹעָדָיוin its ranks

Wail, O gate! Cry out, O city! Melt away, all you Philistines! For a cloud of smoke comes from the north, and there are no stragglers in its ranks.

14:32

וּמַהwhat יַּעֲנֶהanswer מַלְאֲכֵיenvoys גוֹיnation כִּיthat יְהוָהLORD יִסַּדfounded צִיּוֹןZion וּבָהּand in her יֶחֱסוּrefuge עֲנִיֵּיafflicted עַמּוֹhis people

What answer will be given to the envoys of that nation? “The LORD has founded Zion, where His afflicted people will find refuge.”
