Isaiah 47

The fall of the daughter of Babylon

“Go down and sit in the dust, O Virgin Daughter of Babylon. Sit on the ground without a throne, O Daughter of Chaldea! For you will no longer be called tender or delicate.

47:2

Take millstones and grind flour; remove your veil; strip off your skirt, bare your thigh, and wade through the streams.

47:3

תִּגָּלuncovered עֶרְוָתֵךְyour nakedness גַּםand תֵּרָאֶהappear חֶרְפָּתֵךְyour shame נָקָםvengeance אֶקָּחtake וְלֹאno אֶפְגַּעspare אָדָםone

Your nakedness will be uncovered and your shame will be exposed. I will take vengeance; I will spare no one.”

47:4

גֹּאֲלֵנוּour Redeemer יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts שְׁמוֹhis name קְדוֹשׁholy יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

Our Redeemer—the LORD of Hosts is His name— is the Holy One of Israel.

47:5

“Sit in silence and go into darkness, O Daughter of Chaldea. For you will no longer be called the queen of kingdoms.

47:6

קָצַפְתִּיangry עַלon עַמִּיmy people I חִלַּלְתִּיprofaned נַחֲלָתִיmy heritage וָאֶתְּנֵםand I placed them בְּיָדֵךְunder your control לֹאno שַׂמְתְּthem לָהֶםto them רַחֲמִיםmercy עַלon זָקֵןelderly הִכְבַּדְתְּheavy עֻלֵּךְyoke מְאֹדvery

I was angry with My people; I profaned My heritage, and I placed them under your control. You showed them no mercy; even on the elderly you laid a most heavy yoke.

47:7

וַתֹּאמְרִיyou said לְעוֹלָםforever אֶהְיֶהbe גְבָרֶתqueen עַדuntil לֹאnot שַׂמְתְּtake אֵלֶּהthese עַלto לִבֵּךְheart לֹאor זָכַרְתְּconsider אַחֲרִיתָהּtheir outcome

You said, ‘I will be queen forever.’ You did not take these things to heart or consider their outcome.

47:8

וְעַתָּהso now שִׁמְעִיhear זֹאתthis עֲדִינָהlover of luxury הַיּוֹשֶׁבֶתwho sits לָבֶטַחsecurely הָאֹמְרָהwho says בִּלְבָבָהּin her heart אֲנִיam וְאַפְסִיnone me עוֹדlonger לֹאnever אֵשֵׁבsit אַלְמָנָהwidow וְלֹאor אֵדַעknow שְׁכוֹלloss

So now hear this, O lover of luxury who sits securely, who says to herself, ‘I am, and there is none besides me. I will never be a widow or know the loss of children.’

47:9

וְתָבֹאנָהwill overtake לָּךְto you שְׁתֵּיtwo אֵלֶּהthese רֶגַעmoment בְּיוֹםday אֶחָדsingle שְׁכוֹלloss of children וְאַלְמֹןand widowhood כְּתֻמָּםfull בָּאוּcome עָלַיִךְupon you בְּרֹבmany כְּשָׁפַיִךְyour sorceries בְּעָצְמַתpotency חֲבָרַיִךְyour spells מְאֹדvery

These two things will overtake you in a moment, in a single day: loss of children, and widowhood. They will come upon you in full measure, in spite of your many sorceries and the potency of your spells.

47:10

וַתִּבְטְחִיwere secure בְרָעָתֵךְin your wickedness you אָמַרְתְּsaid אֵיןno רֹאָנִיsees me חָכְמָתֵךְyour wisdom וְדַעְתֵּךְand knowledge you הִיאis שׁוֹבְבָתֶךְastray you וַתֹּאמְרִיtold בְלִבֵּךְled אֲנִיam וְאַפְסִיnone me עוֹדlonger

You were secure in your wickedness; you said, ‘No one sees me.’ Your wisdom and knowledge led you astray; you told yourself, ‘I am, and there is none besides me.’

47:11

וּבָאcome עָלַיִךְupon you רָעָהdisaster לֹאnot תֵדְעִיknow שַׁחְרָהּcharm it וְתִפֹּלcalamity עָלַיִךְon you הֹוָהdisaster לֹאnot תוּכְלִיunable כַּפְּרָהּward off וְתָבֹאand come עָלַיִךְto you פִּתְאֹםsuddenly שׁוֹאָהdevastation לֹאnot תֵדָעִיhappen

But disaster will come upon you; you will not know how to charm it away. A calamity will befall you that you will be unable to ward off. Devastation will happen to you suddenly and unexpectedly.

47:12

עִמְדִיstand נָאplease בַחֲבָרַיִךְwith your spells וּבְרֹבand with many כְּשָׁפַיִךְyour sorceries בַּאֲשֶׁרwith which יָגַעַתְּwearied מִנְּעוּרָיִךְfrom your youth אוּלַיperhaps תּוּכְלִיyou הוֹעִילsucceed אוּלַיperhaps תַּעֲרוֹצִיterror

So take your stand with your spells and with your many sorceries, with which you have wearied yourself from your youth. Perhaps you will succeed; perhaps you will inspire terror!

47:13

נִלְאֵיתwearied בְּרֹבby your many עֲצָתָיִךְcounselors יַעַמְדוּforward נָאnow וְיוֹשִׁיעֻךְand save you your הֹבְרֵיastrologers שָׁמַיִםastrologers הַחֹזִיםwho observe בַּכּוֹכָבִיםthe stars מוֹדִיעִםwho לֶחֳדָשִׁיםwho monthly מֵאֲשֶׁרfrom had יָבֹאוּpredict עָלָיִךְon you

You are wearied by your many counselors; let them come forward now and save you— your astrologers who observe the stars, who monthly predict your fate.

47:14

Surely they are like stubble; the fire will burn them up. They cannot deliver themselves from the power of the flame. There will be no coals to warm them or fire to sit beside.

47:15

כֵּןthis הָיוּare לָךְto you אֲשֶׁרwhom יָגָעַתְּlabored סֹחֲרַיִךְtraded מִנְּעוּרַיִךְfrom youth אִישׁeach לְעֶבְרוֹhis own direction תָּעוּstrays אֵיןnot מוֹשִׁיעֵךְsave you

This is what they are to you— those with whom you have labored and traded from youth— each one strays in his own direction; not one of them can save you.
