Isaiah 15

Lament over Moab

Open in the interlinear reader

15:1

מַשָּׂאburden against מוֹאָבMoab כִּיfor בְּלֵילin night שֻׁדַּדruined עָרAr מוֹאָבMoab נִדְמָהdestroyed כִּיfor בְּלֵילin a night שֻׁדַּדdevastated destroyed קִירKir מוֹאָבMoab נִדְמָהperish

This is the burden against Moab: Ar in Moab is ruined, destroyed in a night! Kir in Moab is devastated, destroyed in a night!

15:2

עָלָהgoes הַבַּיִתto its temple וְדִיבֹןDibon הַבָּמוֹתits high places לְבֶכִיto weep עַלon נְבוֹNebo וְעַלwell as over מֵידְבָאMedeba מוֹאָבMoab יְיֵלִילwails בְּכָלevery רֹאשָׁיוhead is קָרְחָהshaved כָּלevery זָקָןbeard גְּרוּעָהoff

Dibon goes up to its temple to weep at its high places. Moab wails over Nebo, as well as over Medeba. Every head is shaved, every beard is cut off.

15:3

בְּחוּצֹתָיוin its streets חָגְרוּwear שָׂקsackcloth עַלon גַּגּוֹתֶיהָrooftops וּבִרְחֹבֹתֶיהָand in the public squares they כֻּלֹּהall יְיֵלִילwail יֹרֵדfalling down בַּבֶּכִיdown weeping

In its streets they wear sackcloth; on the rooftops and in the public squares they all wail, falling down weeping.

15:4

וַתִּזְעַקcry out חֶשְׁבּוֹןHeshbon וְאֶלְעָלֵהand Elealeh עַדfar יַהַץJahaz נִשְׁמַעheard קוֹלָםtheir voices עַלon כֵּןtherefore חֲלֻצֵיthe soldiers מוֹאָבMoab יָרִיעוּcry נַפְשׁוֹtheir souls יָרְעָהtremble לּוֹto him

Heshbon and Elealeh cry out; their voices are heard as far as Jahaz. Therefore the soldiers of Moab cry out; their souls tremble within.

15:5

לִבִּיmy heart לְמוֹאָבover Moab יִזְעָקcries out בְּרִיחֶהָher fugitives עַדfar צֹעַרZoar עֶגְלַתEglath שְׁלִשִׁיָּהshelishiyah כִּיfor מַעֲלֵהslope הַלּוּחִיתof Luhith בִּבְכִיwith weeping יַעֲלֶהascend בּוֹon כִּיfor דֶּרֶךְroad חוֹרֹנַיִםHoronaim זַעֲקַתlament שֶׁבֶרdestruction יְעֹעֵרוּawaken

My heart cries out over Moab; her fugitives flee as far as Zoar, as far as Eglath-shelishiyah. With weeping they ascend the slope of Luhith; they lament their destruction on the road to Horonaim.

15:6

כִּיfor מֵיwaters נִמְרִיםNimrim מְשַׁמּוֹתdried יִהְיוּbe כִּיfor יָבֵשׁwithered חָצִירgrass כָּלָהfinished דֶשֶׁאvegetation יֶרֶקgreenery לֹאno הָיָהwas

The waters of Nimrim are dried up, and the grass is withered; the vegetation is gone, and the greenery is no more.

15:7

עַלon כֵּןso יִתְרָהwealth עָשָׂהcarry וּפְקֻדָּתָםtheir and belongings עַלover נַחַלBrook הָעֲרָבִיםthe willows יִשָּׂאוּםbear them

So they carry their wealth and belongings over the Brook of the Willows.

15:8

כִּיfor הִקִּיפָהechoes הַזְּעָקָהtheir outcry אֶת[obj] גְּבוּלborder מוֹאָבMoab עַדto אֶגְלַיִםEglaim יִלְלָתָהּwailing וּבְאֵרreaches אֵילִיםBeer יִלְלָתָהּelim

For their outcry echoes to the border of Moab. Their wailing reaches Eglaim; it is heard in Beer-elim.

15:9

כִּיfor מֵיwaters דִימוֹןDimon מָלְאוּfull דָםblood כִּיbut אָשִׁיתbring עַלupon דִּימוֹןDimon נוֹסָפוֹתmore לִפְלֵיטַתfugitives מוֹאָבMoab אַרְיֵהlion וְלִשְׁאֵרִיתremnant אֲדָמָהland

The waters of Dimon are full of blood, but I will bring more upon Dimon— a lion upon the fugitives of Moab and upon the remnant of the land.
