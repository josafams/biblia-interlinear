Isaiah 39

The envoys from Babylon

Open in the interlinear reader

39:1

בָּעֵתat that time הַהִואthat שָׁלַחsent מְרֹדַךְmerodach baladan בַּלְאֲדָןMerodach בֶּןson בַּלְאֲדָןBaladan מֶלֶךְking בָּבֶלBabylon סְפָרִיםletters וּמִנְחָהand a gift אֶלto חִזְקִיָּהוּHezekiah וַיִּשְׁמַעhe heard כִּיfor חָלָהillness וַיֶּחֱזָקand recovery

At that time Merodach-baladan son of Baladan king of Babylon sent letters and a gift to Hezekiah, for he had heard about Hezekiah’s illness and recovery.

39:2

וַיִּשְׂמַחand welcomed עֲלֵיהֶםon them חִזְקִיָּהוּHezekiah וַיַּרְאֵםand showed them אֶת[obj] בֵּיתhouse נְכֹתוֹhis treasure אֶת[obj] הַכֶּסֶףthe silver וְאֶתand [obj] הַזָּהָבthe gold וְאֶתand [obj] הַבְּשָׂמִיםthe spices וְאֵתand [obj] הַשֶּׁמֶןthe precious oil הַטּוֹבwell וְאֵתand [obj] כָּלentire בֵּיתarmory כֵּלָיוarmory וְאֵתand [obj] כָּלall אֲשֶׁרthat נִמְצָאfound בְּאֹצְרֹתָיוin his storehouses לֹאnot הָיָהwas דָבָרnothing אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot הֶרְאָםshow them חִזְקִיָּהוּHezekiah בְּבֵיתוֹin his palace וּבְכָלor in all מֶמְשַׁלְתּוֹhis dominion

And Hezekiah welcomed the envoys gladly and showed them what was in his treasure house—the silver, the gold, the spices, and the precious oil, as well as his entire armory—all that was found in his storehouses. There was nothing in his palace or in all his dominion that Hezekiah did not show them.

39:3

וַיָּבֹאwent יְשַׁעְיָהוּIsaiah הַנָּבִיאthe prophet אֶלto הַמֶּלֶךְking חִזְקִיָּהוּHezekiah וַיֹּאמֶרand asked אֵלָיוto him מָהwhat אָמְרוּsay הָאֲנָשִׁיםthose men הָאֵלֶּהthese וּמֵאַיִןand from where יָבֹאוּcome אֵלֶיךָto you וַיֹּאמֶרreplied חִזְקִיָּהוּHezekiah מֵאֶרֶץland רְחוֹקָהdistant בָּאוּcame אֵלַיto me מִבָּבֶלBabylon

Then the prophet Isaiah went to King Hezekiah and asked, “Where did those men come from, and what did they say to you?” “They came to me from a distant land,” Hezekiah replied, “from Babylon.”

39:4

וַיֹּאמֶרIsaiah asked מָהwhat רָאוּseen בְּבֵיתֶךָin your palace וַיֹּאמֶרanswered חִזְקִיָּהוּHezekiah אֵת[obj] כָּלeverything אֲשֶׁרhave בְּבֵיתִיin my palace רָאוּseen לֹאnot הָיָהthere דָבָרnothing אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot הִרְאִיתִיםshow them בְּאוֹצְרֹתָיamong my treasures

“What have they seen in your palace?” Isaiah asked. “They have seen everything in my palace,” answered Hezekiah. “There is nothing among my treasures that I did not show them.”

39:5

וַיֹּאמֶרthen said יְשַׁעְיָהוּIsaiah אֶלto חִזְקִיָּהוּHezekiah שְׁמַעhear דְּבַרword יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts

Then Isaiah said to Hezekiah, “Hear the word of the LORD of Hosts:

39:6

הִנֵּהsurely יָמִיםtime בָּאִיםcome וְנִשָּׂאwhen כָּלeverything אֲשֶׁרthat בְּבֵיתֶךָin your palace וַאֲשֶׁרand all that אָצְרוּstored אֲבֹתֶיךָyour fathers עַדuntil הַיּוֹםthis day הַזֶּהthis בָּבֶלBabylon לֹאnot יִוָּתֵרleft דָּבָרword אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD

The time will surely come when everything in your palace and all that your fathers have stored up until this day will be carried off to Babylon. Nothing will be left, says the LORD.

39:7

וּמִבָּנֶיךָand some your descendants your אֲשֶׁרthat יֵצְאוּgo מִמְּךָof אֲשֶׁרthat תּוֹלִידflesh יִקָּחוּtaken וְהָיוּbe סָרִיסִיםeunuchs בְּהֵיכַלin the palace מֶלֶךְking בָּבֶלBabylon

And some of your descendants, your own flesh and blood, will be taken away to be eunuchs in the palace of the king of Babylon.”

39:8

וַיֹּאמֶרbut said חִזְקִיָּהוּHezekiah אֶלto יְשַׁעְיָהוּIsaiah טוֹבgood דְּבַרword יְהוָהLORD אֲשֶׁרthat דִּבַּרְתָּspoken וַיֹּאמֶרhe thought כִּיfor יִהְיֶהbe שָׁלוֹםpeace וֶאֱמֶתand security בְּיָמָיin my lifetime

But Hezekiah said to Isaiah, “The word of the LORD that you have spoken is good.” For he thought, “At least there will be peace and security in my lifetime.”
