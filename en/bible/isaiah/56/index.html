Isaiah 56

Foreigners and eunuchs in the house of prayer

This is what the LORD says: “Maintain justice and do what is right, for My salvation is coming soon, and My righteousness will be revealed.

56:2

Blessed is the man who does this, and the son of man who holds it fast, who keeps the Sabbath without profaning it and keeps his hand from doing any evil.”

56:3

Let no foreigner who has joined himself to the LORD say, “The LORD will utterly exclude me from His people.” And let the eunuch not say, “I am but a dry tree.”

56:4

For this is what the LORD says: “To the eunuchs who keep My Sabbaths, who choose what pleases Me and hold fast to My covenant—

56:5

וְנָתַתִּיwill give לָהֶםto them בְּבֵיתִיin my house וּבְחוֹמֹתַיand within my walls יָדmemorial וָשֵׁםand a name טוֹבbetter מִבָּנִיםsons וּמִבָּנוֹתand daughters שֵׁםname עוֹלָםeverlasting אֶתֶּןgive לוֹto him אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot יִכָּרֵתcut off

I will give them, in My house and within My walls, a memorial and a name better than that of sons and daughters. I will give them an everlasting name that will not be cut off.

56:6

וּבְנֵיand sons הַנֵּכָרforeigners הַנִּלְוִיםwho join עַלto יְהוָהLORD לְשָׁרְתוֹto minister to him וּלְאַהֲבָהto love אֶת[obj] שֵׁםname יְהוָהLORD לִהְיוֹתand to be לוֹto him לַעֲבָדִיםservants כָּלall שֹׁמֵרkeep שַׁבָּתSabbath מֵחַלְּלוֹwithout profaning it וּמַחֲזִיקִיםand who hold fast בִּבְרִיתִיto my covenant

And the foreigners who join themselves to the LORD to minister to Him, to love the name of the LORD, and to be His servants— all who keep the Sabbath without profaning it and who hold fast to My covenant—

56:7

I will bring them to My holy mountain and make them joyful in My house of prayer. Their burnt offerings and sacrifices will be accepted on My altar, for My house will be called a house of prayer for all the nations.”

56:8

נְאֻםdeclares אֲדֹנָיLord יְהוִהGOD מְקַבֵּץgathers נִדְחֵיdispersed יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael עוֹדstill אֲקַבֵּץgather עָלָיוto them לְנִקְבָּצָיוthose already gathered

Thus declares the Lord GOD, who gathers the dispersed of Israel: “I will gather to them still others besides those already gathered.”

56:9

כֹּלall חַיְתוֹbeasts שָׂדָיfield אֵתָיוּgreedily לֶאֱכֹלeat כָּלall חַיְתוֹbeasts בַּיָּעַרthe forest

Come, all you beasts of the field; eat greedily, all you beasts of the forest.

56:10

צֹפָיוIsrael’s watchmen are עִוְרִיםblind כֻּלָּםthey all לֹאnot יָדָעוּoblivious כֻּלָּםall כְּלָבִיםdogs אִלְּמִיםmute לֹאnot יוּכְלוּcannot לִנְבֹּחַbark הֹזִיםdreamers שֹׁכְבִיםlying אֹהֲבֵיloving לָנוּםto slumber

Israel’s watchmen are blind, they are all oblivious; they are all mute dogs, they cannot bark; they are dreamers lying around, loving to slumber.

56:11

וְהַכְּלָבִיםdogs עַזֵּיravenous נֶפֶשׁthey לֹאnever יָדְעוּknow שָׂבְעָהsatisfied וְהֵמָּהthey are רֹעִיםshepherds לֹאno יָדְעוּknow הָבִיןdiscernment כֻּלָּםall לְדַרְכָּםto own way פָּנוּturn אִישׁeach לְבִצְעוֹown gain מִקָּצֵהוּone seeking his

Like ravenous dogs, they are never satisfied. They are shepherds with no discernment; they all turn to their own way, each one seeking his own gain:

56:12

אֵתָיוּcome אֶקְחָהget יַיִןwine וְנִסְבְּאָהlet us שֵׁכָרstrong drink וְהָיָהbe כָזֶהlike this יוֹםtoday מָחָרtomorrow גָּדוֹלfar יֶתֶרbetter מְאֹדvery

“Come, let me get the wine, let us imbibe the strong drink, and tomorrow will be like today, only far better!”
