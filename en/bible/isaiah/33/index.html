Isaiah 33

Woe to the destroyer; Zion secure

Open in the interlinear reader

33:1

Woe to you, O destroyer never destroyed, O traitor never betrayed! When you have finished destroying, you will be destroyed. When you have finished betraying, you will be betrayed.

33:2

יְהוָהLORD חָנֵּנוּgracious to us לְךָfor you קִוִּינוּwait הֱיֵהbe זְרֹעָםstrength לַבְּקָרִיםevery morning אַףand יְשׁוּעָתֵנוּour salvation בְּעֵתin time צָרָהtrouble

O LORD, be gracious to us! We wait for You. Be our strength every morning and our salvation in time of trouble.

33:3

מִקּוֹלthunder of voice הָמוֹןarmy נָדְדוּflee עַמִּיםpeoples מֵרוֹמְמֻתֶךָfrom lifting up of self נָפְצוּscatter גּוֹיִםnations

The peoples flee the thunder of Your voice; the nations scatter when You rise.

33:4

וְאֻסַּףgathered שְׁלַלְכֶםyour spoil אֹסֶףnations הֶחָסִילlocusts כְּמַשַּׁקswarm גֵּבִיםsweep שׁוֹקֵקsweep בּוֹin him

Your spoil, O nations, is gathered as by locusts; like a swarm of locusts men sweep over it.

33:5

נִשְׂגָּבexalted יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor שֹׁכֵןdwells מָרוֹםhigh מִלֵּאfilled צִיּוֹןZion מִשְׁפָּטjustice וּצְדָקָהand righteousness

The LORD is exalted, for He dwells on high; He has filled Zion with justice and righteousness.

33:6

וְהָיָהhe will be אֱמוּנַתsure עִתֶּיךָyour times חֹסֶןstorehouse יְשׁוּעֹתsalvation חָכְמַתwisdom וָדָעַתand knowledge יִרְאַתfear יְהוָהLORD הִיאis אוֹצָרוֹtreasure

He will be the sure foundation for your times, a storehouse of salvation, wisdom, and knowledge. The fear of the LORD is Zion’s treasure.

33:7

הֵןbehold אֶרְאֶלָּםvaliant צָעֲקוּcry חֻצָהstreets מַלְאֲכֵיenvoys שָׁלוֹםpeace מַרbitterly יִבְכָּיוּןweep

Behold, their valiant ones cry aloud in the streets; the envoys of peace weep bitterly.

33:8

נָשַׁמּוּdeserted מְסִלּוֹתtravel שָׁבַתceased עֹבֵרpass אֹרַחbeen הֵפֵרbroken בְּרִיתtreaty מָאַסdespised עָרִיםhuman לֹאnot חָשַׁבdisregarded אֱנוֹשׁlife

The highways are deserted; travel has ceased. The treaty has been broken, the witnesses are despised, and human life is disregarded.

33:9

אָבַלmourns אֻמְלְלָהlanguishes אָרֶץland הֶחְפִּירashamed לְבָנוֹןLebanon קָמַלdecayed הָיָהwas הַשָּׁרוֹןSharon כָּעֲרָבָהlike a desert וְנֹעֵרshake off their בָּשָׁןBashan וְכַרְמֶלand Carmel

The land mourns and languishes; Lebanon is ashamed and decayed. Sharon is like a desert; Bashan and Carmel shake off their leaves.

33:10

עַתָּהnow אָקוּםarise יֹאמַרsays יְהוָהLORD עַתָּהnow אֵרוֹמָםlift עַתָּהnow אֶנָּשֵׂאexalted

“Now I will arise,” says the LORD. “Now I will lift Myself up. Now I will be exalted.

33:11

תַּהֲרוּconceive חֲשַׁשׁchaff תֵּלְדוּbirth קַשׁstubble רוּחֲכֶםyour breath אֵשׁfire תֹּאכַלְכֶםconsume you

You conceive chaff; you give birth to stubble. Your breath is a fire that will consume you.

33:12

וְהָיוּand be עַמִּיםpeoples מִשְׂרְפוֹתburned שִׂידashes קוֹצִיםthorns כְּסוּחִיםcut בָּאֵשׁin the fire יִצַּתּוּablaze

The peoples will be burned to ashes, like thorns cut down and set ablaze.

33:13

שִׁמְעוּhear רְחוֹקִיםfar אֲשֶׁרthat עָשִׂיתִיdone וּדְעוּacknowledge קְרוֹבִיםnear גְּבֻרָתִיmy might

You who are far off, hear what I have done; you who are near, acknowledge My might.”

33:14

פָּחֲדוּafraid בְצִיּוֹןin Zion חַטָּאִיםsinners אָחֲזָהgrips רְעָדָהtrembling חֲנֵפִיםungodly מִיwho יָגוּרdwell לָנוּto us אֵשׁfire אוֹכֵלָהconsuming מִיwho יָגוּרdwell לָנוּto us מוֹקְדֵיflames עוֹלָםeverlasting

The sinners in Zion are afraid; trembling grips the ungodly: “Who of us can dwell with a consuming fire? Who of us can dwell with everlasting flames?”

33:15

הֹלֵךְwalks צְדָקוֹתrighteously וְדֹבֵרand speaks מֵישָׁרִיםsincerity מֹאֵסrefuses בְּבֶצַעgain מַעֲשַׁקּוֹתextortion נֹעֵרnever כַּפָּיוwhose hand מִתְּמֹךְtakes בַּשֹּׁחַדtakes a bribe אֹטֵםstops אָזְנוֹhis ears מִשְּׁמֹעַagainst murderous דָּמִיםplots וְעֹצֵםand shuts עֵינָיוhis eyes מֵרְאוֹתtightly בְּרָעagainst evil

He who walks righteously and speaks with sincerity, who refuses gain from extortion, whose hand never takes a bribe, who stops his ears against murderous plots and shuts his eyes tightly against evil—

33:16

הוּאhe מְרוֹמִיםheights יִשְׁכֹּןdwell מְצָדוֹתfortress סְלָעִיםmountain מִשְׂגַּבּוֹrefuge his לַחְמוֹfood will be נִתָּןprovided מֵימָיוhis water נֶאֱמָנִיםassured

he will dwell on the heights; the mountain fortress will be his refuge; his food will be provided and his water assured.

33:17

מֶלֶךְking בְּיָפְיוֹin his beauty and תֶּחֱזֶינָהbehold עֵינֶיךָyour eyes תִּרְאֶינָהsee אֶרֶץland מַרְחַקִּיםstretches afar

Your eyes will see the King in His beauty and behold a land that stretches afar.

33:18

לִבְּךָyour mind יֶהְגֶּהponder אֵימָהformer terror אַיֵּהwhere סֹפֵרtallies אַיֵּהwhere שֹׁקֵלweighs אַיֵּהwhere סֹפֵרcounts אֶת[obj] הַמִּגְדָּלִיםthe towers

Your mind will ponder the former terror: “Where is he who tallies? Where is he who weighs? Where is he who counts the towers?”

33:19

אֶת[obj] עַםpeople נוֹעָזlonger לֹאno תִרְאֶהsee עַםpeople עִמְקֵיwhose שָׂפָהspeech מִשְּׁמוֹעַis unintelligible נִלְעַגunintelligible לָשׁוֹןlanguage אֵיןno בִּינָהunderstand

You will no longer see the insolent, a people whose speech is unintelligible, who stammer in a language you cannot understand.

33:20

חֲזֵהlook צִיּוֹןZion קִרְיַתcity מוֹעֲדֵנוּour appointed feasts עֵינֶיךָyour eyes תִרְאֶינָהsee יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem נָוֶהpasture שַׁאֲנָןpeaceful אֹהֶלtent בַּלnot יִצְעָןbe taken down בַּלnot יִסַּעwander יְתֵדֹתָיוits tent pegs לָנֶצַחto forever וְכָלnor will any חֲבָלָיוits cords בַּלbe יִנָּתֵקוּbroken

Look upon Zion, the city of our appointed feasts. Your eyes will see Jerusalem, a peaceful pasture, a tent that does not wander; its tent pegs will not be pulled up, nor will any of its cords be broken.

33:21

כִּיbut אִםif שָׁםthere אַדִּירmajestic יְהוָהLORD לָנוּfor us מְקוֹםplace נְהָרִיםrivers יְאֹרִיםand canals רַחֲבֵיwide יָדָיִםwhere בַּלno תֵּלֶךְgalley בּוֹwith אֳנִיoars שַׁיִטrow וְצִיvessel אַדִּירmajestic לֹאno יַעַבְרֶנּוּpass

But there the Majestic One, our LORD, will be for us a place of rivers and wide canals, where no galley with oars will row, and no majestic vessel will pass.

33:22

כִּיfor יְהוָהLORD שֹׁפְטֵנוּour judge יְהוָהLORD מְחֹקְקֵנוּour lawgiver יְהוָהLORD מַלְכֵּנוּour king it הוּאis יוֹשִׁיעֵנוּsave us

For the LORD is our Judge, the LORD is our lawgiver, the LORD is our King. It is He who will save us.

33:23

נִטְּשׁוּslack חֲבָלָיִךְyour ropes בַּלcannot יְחַזְּקוּharden כֵןsecure תָּרְנָםmast בַּלnot פָּרְשׂוּspread נֵסsail אָזthen חֻלַּקdivided עַדabundance שָׁלָלspoils מַרְבֶּהeven פִּסְחִיםlame בָּזְזוּcarry off בַזplunder

Your ropes are slack; they cannot secure the mast or spread the sail. Then an abundance of spoils will be divided, and even the lame will carry off plunder.

33:24

וּבַלand no יֹאמַרsay שָׁכֵןZion חָלִיתִיsick הָעָםthe people הַיֹּשֵׁבwho dwell בָּהּin her נְשֻׂאforgiven עָוֺןiniquity

And no resident of Zion will say, “I am sick.” The people who dwell there will be forgiven of iniquity.
