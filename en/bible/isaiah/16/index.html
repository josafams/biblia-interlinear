Isaiah 16

The fugitives of Moab and a new lament

Open in the interlinear reader

Send the tribute lambs to the ruler of the land, from Sela in the desert to the mount of Daughter Zion.

16:2

Like fluttering birds pushed out of the nest, so are the daughters of Moab at the fords of the Arnon:

16:3

הָבִיאִיbring עֵצָהcounsel עֲשׂוּdo פְלִילָהrender שִׁיתִיdecision כַלַּיִלas night צִלֵּךְshade בְּתוֹךְin among צָהֳרָיִםnoonday סַתְּרִיhide נִדָּחִיםdark נֹדֵדrefugees אַלdo not תְּגַלִּיbetray

“Give us counsel; render a decision. Shelter us at noonday with shade as dark as night. Hide the refugees; do not betray the one who flees.

16:4

יָגוּרוּstay בָךְwith you נִדָּחַיmy fugitives מוֹאָבMoab הֱוִיbe סֵתֶרrefuge לָמוֹfor מִפְּנֵיfrom before שׁוֹדֵדdestroyer כִּיwhen אָפֵסgone הַמֵּץthe oppressor כָּלָהhas gone שֹׁדdestruction תַּמּוּceased רֹמֵסvanished מִןfrom הָאָרֶץthe land

Let my fugitives stay with you; be a refuge for Moab from the destroyer.” When the oppressor has gone, destruction has ceased, and the oppressors have vanished from the land,

16:5

וְהוּכַןwill be established בַּחֶסֶדin loving devotion כִּסֵּאthrone וְיָשַׁבwill sit עָלָיוon it בֶּאֱמֶתin faithfulness בְּאֹהֶלin the tent דָּוִדDavid שֹׁפֵטjudge וְדֹרֵשׁseeking מִשְׁפָּטjustice וּמְהִרand hastening צֶדֶקrighteousness

in loving devotion a throne will be established in the tent of David. A judge seeking justice and hastening righteousness will sit on it in faithfulness.

16:6

שָׁמַעְנוּheard גְאוֹןpomposity מוֹאָבMoab’s גֵּאpomposity מְאֹדvery גַּאֲוָתוֹpride וּגְאוֹנוֹpride and conceit his וְעֶבְרָתוֹbut his boasting לֹאnot כֵןis בַּדָּיוempty

We have heard of Moab’s pomposity, his exceeding pride and conceit, his overflowing arrogance. But his boasting is empty.

16:7

לָכֵןtherefore יְיֵלִילwail מוֹאָבMoab לְמוֹאָבfor Moab כֻּלֹּהhis all יְיֵלִילwail לַאֲשִׁישֵׁיfor the raisin קִירcakes חֲרֶשֶׂתKir תֶּהְגּוּmoan אַךְbut נְכָאִיםstricken

Therefore let Moab wail; let them wail together for Moab. Moan for the raisin cakes of Kir-hareseth, you who are utterly stricken.

16:8

כִּיfor שַׁדְמוֹתfields חֶשְׁבּוֹןHeshbon אֻמְלָלwithered גֶּפֶןgrapevines שִׂבְמָהSibmah בַּעֲלֵיrulers גוֹיִםnations הָלְמוּtrampled שְׂרוּקֶּיהָtrampled its choicest עַדfar יַעְזֵרJazer נָגָעוּreached תָּעוּspread toward מִדְבָּרdesert שְׁלֻחוֹתֶיהָtheir shoots had נִטְּשׁוּspread עָבְרוּpassed יָםsea

For the fields of Heshbon have withered, along with the grapevines of Sibmah. The rulers of the nations have trampled its choicest vines, which had reached as far as Jazer and spread toward the desert. Their shoots had spread out and passed over the sea.

16:9

עַלon כֵּןso אֶבְכֶּהweep בִּבְכִיweep with יַעְזֵרJazer גֶּפֶןvines שִׂבְמָהSibmah אֲרַיָּוֶךְdrench דִּמְעָתִיmy tears חֶשְׁבּוֹןHeshbon וְאֶלְעָלֵהand Elealeh כִּיfor עַלon קֵיצֵךְyour summer fruit וְעַלover קְצִירֵךְyour harvest הֵידָדshouts נָפָלfallen

So I weep with Jazer for the vines of Sibmah; I drench Heshbon and Elealeh with my tears. Triumphant shouts have fallen silent over your summer fruit and your harvest.

16:10

וְנֶאֱסַףand gathered שִׂמְחָהjoy וָגִילand gladness מִןfrom הַכַּרְמֶלthe orchard וּבַכְּרָמִיםin the vineyards לֹאno יְרֻנָּןsings לֹאor יְרֹעָעshouts יַיִןwine בַּיְקָבִיםin the winepresses לֹאno יִדְרֹךְtramples הַדֹּרֵךְthe treads הֵידָדgrapes הִשְׁבַּתִּיend

Joy and gladness are removed from the orchard; no one sings or shouts in the vineyards. No one tramples the grapes in the winepresses; I have put an end to the cheering.

16:11

עַלon כֵּןtherefore מֵעַיmy heart לְמוֹאָבfor Moab כַּכִּנּוֹרlike a harp יֶהֱמוּlaments וְקִרְבִּיmy inmost לְקִירfor heres חָרֶשׂKir

Therefore my heart laments for Moab like a harp, my inmost being for Kir-heres.

16:12

וְהָיָהand be כִיwhen נִרְאָהappears כִּיwhen נִלְאָהwearies מוֹאָבMoab עַלon הַבָּמָהthe high place וּבָאand enters אֶלto מִקְדָּשׁוֹhis sanctuary לְהִתְפַּלֵּלto pray וְלֹאhim no יוּכָלgood

When Moab appears on the high place, when he wearies himself and enters his sanctuary to pray, it will do him no good.

16:13

זֶהthis הַדָּבָרthe message אֲשֶׁרthat דִּבֶּרspoke יְהוָהLORD אֶלto מוֹאָבMoab מֵאָזfrom time

This is the message that the LORD spoke earlier concerning Moab.

16:14

וְעַתָּהand now דִּבֶּרspoken יְהוָהLORD לֵאמֹרsays בְּשָׁלֹשׁin three שָׁנִיםyears כִּשְׁנֵיyears שָׂכִירhired worker וְנִקְלָהcounts כְּבוֹדsplendor מוֹאָבMoab’s בְּכֹלwith all הֶהָמוֹןher הָרָבher many וּשְׁאָרleft מְעַטfew מִזְעָרfew לוֹאnot כַבִּירfeeble

And now the LORD says, “In three years, as a hired worker counts the years, Moab’s splendor will become an object of contempt, with all her many people. And those who are left will be few and feeble.”
