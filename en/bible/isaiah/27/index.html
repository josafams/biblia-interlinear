Isaiah 27

Leviathan defeated and the vineyard restored

Open in the interlinear reader

27:1

בַּיּוֹםin that day הַהוּאthat יִפְקֹדattend יְהוָהLORD בְּחַרְבוֹsword הַקָּשָׁהsharp וְהַגְּדוֹלָהgreat וְהַחֲזָקָהand mighty עַלon לִוְיָתָןLeviathan נָחָשׁserpent בָּרִחַserpent וְעַלand on לִוְיָתָןLeviathan נָחָשׁserpent עֲקַלָּתוֹןcoiling וְהָרַגslay אֶת[obj] הַתַּנִּיןthe dragon אֲשֶׁרthat בַּיָּםthe sea

In that day the LORD will take His sharp, great, and mighty sword, and bring judgment on Leviathan the fleeing serpent—Leviathan the coiling serpent—and He will slay the dragon of the sea.

27:2

בַּיּוֹםin that day הַהוּאthat כֶּרֶםvineyard חֶמֶדfruitful עַנּוּsing לָהּto her

In that day: “Sing about a fruitful vineyard.

27:3

אֲנִיI יְהוָהLORD נֹצְרָהּguard לִרְגָעִיםkeeper אַשְׁקֶנָּהwater it פֶּןor יִפְקֹדI עָלֶיהָon her לַיְלָהnight וָיוֹםand day אֶצֳּרֶנָּהdisturb it

I, the LORD, am its keeper; I water it continually. I guard it night and day so no one can disturb it;

27:4

חֵמָהam angry אֵיןnot לִיto me מִיwho יִתְּנֵנִיonly שָׁמִירbriers confronted שַׁיִתbriers בַּמִּלְחָמָהtrample אֶפְשְׂעָהgo בָהּin her אֲצִיתֶנָּהburn them יָּחַדtogether

I am not angry. If only thorns and briers confronted Me, I would march and trample them, I would burn them to the ground.

27:5

אוֹor יַחֲזֵקclaim בְּמָעוּזִּיto my protection יַעֲשֶׂהmake שָׁלוֹםpeace לִיto me שָׁלוֹםpeace יַעֲשֶׂהmake לִּיto me

Or let them lay claim to My protection; let them make peace with Me— yes, let them make peace with Me.”

27:6

הַבָּאִיםto come יַשְׁרֵשׁroot יַעֲקֹבJacob יָצִיץbud וּפָרַחand blossom יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וּמָלְאוּand fill פְנֵיface תֵבֵלwhole world תְּנוּבָהfruit

In the days to come, Jacob will take root. Israel will bud and blossom and fill the whole world with fruit.

27:7

הַכְּמַכַּתIsrael as מַכֵּהוּstruck הִכָּהוּstruck אִםif כְּהֶרֶגoppressors הֲרֻגָיוslain him הֹרָגkilled

Has the LORD struck Israel as He struck her oppressors? Was she killed like those who slayed her?

27:8

בְּסַּאסְּאָהby warfare בְּשַׁלְחָהּexile תְּרִיבֶנָּהcontended הָגָהremoved בְּרוּחוֹwind הַקָּשָׁהfierce as בְּיוֹםon the day קָדִיםeast wind

By warfare and exile You contended with her and removed her with a fierce wind, as on the day the east wind blows.

27:9

Therefore Jacob’s guilt will be atoned for, and the full fruit of the removal of his sin will be this: When he makes all the altar stones like crushed bits of chalk, no Asherah poles or incense altars will remain standing.

27:10

כִּיfor עִירcity בְּצוּרָהfortified בָּדָדlies נָוֶהdeserted מְשֻׁלָּחhomestead וְנֶעֱזָבabandoned כַּמִּדְבָּרa wilderness שָׁםthere יִרְעֶהgraze עֵגֶלcalves וְשָׁםand there יִרְבָּץlie down וְכִלָּהthey strip סְעִפֶיהָits branches bare

For the fortified city lies deserted— a homestead abandoned, a wilderness forsaken. There the calves graze, and there they lie down; they strip its branches bare.

27:11

בִּיבֹשׁdry קְצִירָהּits limbs תִּשָּׁבַרְנָהbroken נָשִׁיםwomen בָּאוֹתcome מְאִירוֹתuse אוֹתָהּher כִּיfor לֹאnot עַםpeople בִּינוֹתunderstanding הוּאis עַלon כֵּןtherefore לֹאno יְרַחֲמֶנּוּcompassion on עֹשֵׂהוּMaker וְיֹצְרוֹand their Creator shows them לֹאno יְחֻנֶּנּוּfavor

When its limbs are dry, they are broken off. Women come and use them for kindling; for this is a people without understanding. Therefore their Maker has no compassion on them, and their Creator shows them no favor.

27:12

וְהָיָהand be בַּיּוֹםin that day הַהוּאthe will יַחְבֹּטthresh יְהוָהLORD מִשִּׁבֹּלֶתfrom the flowing הַנָּהָרthe Euphrates עַדto נַחַלWadi מִצְרָיִםEgypt וְאַתֶּםand you תְּלֻקְּטוּgathered לְאַחַדone by אֶחָדone בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites

In that day the LORD will thresh from the flowing Euphrates to the Wadi of Egypt, and you, O Israelites, will be gathered one by one.

27:13

וְהָיָהand be בַּיּוֹםin that day הַהוּאa יִתָּקַעsound בְּשׁוֹפָרram’s horn גָּדוֹלgreat וּבָאוּand come הָאֹבְדִיםwho were perishing בְּאֶרֶץin land אַשּׁוּרAssyria וְהַנִּדָּחִיםwho were exiles בְּאֶרֶץin land מִצְרָיִםEgypt וְהִשְׁתַּחֲווּworship לַיהוָהthe LORD בְּהַרmountain הַקֹּדֶשׁthe holy בִּירוּשָׁלִָםin Jerusalem

And in that day a great ram’s horn will sound, and those who were perishing in Assyria will come forth with those who were exiles in Egypt. And they will worship the LORD on the holy mountain in Jerusalem.
