Isaiah 55

An invitation to the waters and the effective word

55:1

הוֹיWoe כָּלall צָמֵאthirsty לְכוּcome לַמַּיִםto the waters וַאֲשֶׁרand you אֵיןwithout לוֹto him כָּסֶףmoney לְכוּcome שִׁבְרוּbuy וֶאֱכֹלוּand eat וּלְכוּcome שִׁבְרוּbuy בְּלוֹאin without כֶסֶףmoney וּבְלוֹאand in not מְחִירcost יַיִןwine וְחָלָבand milk

“Come, all you who are thirsty, come to the waters; and you without money, come, buy, and eat! Come, buy wine and milk without money and without cost!

55:2

לָמָּהwhy תִשְׁקְלוּspend כֶסֶףmoney בְּלוֹאnot לֶחֶםbread וִיגִיעֲכֶםand your labor בְּלוֹאnot לְשָׂבְעָהsatisfy שִׁמְעוּlisten שָׁמוֹעַcarefully אֵלַיto me וְאִכְלוּand eat טוֹבgood וְתִתְעַנַּגdelight בַּדֶּשֶׁןin the richest נַפְשְׁכֶםyour soul

Why spend money on that which is not bread, and your labor on that which does not satisfy? Listen carefully to Me, and eat what is good, and your soul will delight in the richest of foods.

55:3

הַטּוּincline אָזְנְכֶםyour ear וּלְכוּand come אֵלַיto me שִׁמְעוּlisten וּתְחִיlive נַפְשְׁכֶםyour soul וְאֶכְרְתָהwill make לָכֶםto you בְּרִיתcovenant עוֹלָםeverlasting חַסְדֵיloving devotion דָוִדDavid הַנֶּאֱמָנִיםpromised

Incline your ear and come to Me; listen, so that your soul may live. I will make with you an everlasting covenant— My loving devotion promised to David.

55:4

הֵןbehold עֵדwitness לְאוּמִּיםnations נְתַתִּיוmade him נָגִידleader וּמְצַוֵּהand commander לְאֻמִּיםpeoples

Behold, I have made him a witness to the nations, a leader and commander of the peoples.

55:5

הֵןsurely גּוֹיnation לֹאnot תֵדַעknow תִּקְרָאsummon וְגוֹיand nations לֹאnot יְדָעוּךָknow you אֵלֶיךָto you יָרוּצוּrun לְמַעַןso יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God וְלִקְדוֹשׁthe holy יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael כִּיfor פֵאֲרָךְglory on you

Surely you will summon a nation you do not know, and nations who do not know you will run to you. For the LORD your God, the Holy One of Israel, has bestowed glory on you.”

55:6

דִּרְשׁוּseek יְהוָהLORD בְּהִמָּצְאוֹfound קְרָאֻהוּcall him בִּהְיוֹתוֹon while he קָרוֹבnear

Seek the LORD while He may be found; call on Him while He is near.

55:7

יַעֲזֹבforsake רָשָׁעwicked דַּרְכּוֹhis own way וְאִישׁman אָוֶןunrighteous his מַחְשְׁבֹתָיוthoughts let him וְיָשֹׁבreturn אֶלto יְהוָהLORD וִירַחֲמֵהוּhave compassion וְאֶלand to אֱלֹהֵינוּour God כִּיfor יַרְבֶּהhe לִסְלוֹחַpardon

Let the wicked man forsake his own way and the unrighteous man his own thoughts; let him return to the LORD, that He may have compassion, and to our God, for He will freely pardon.

55:8

כִּיfor לֹאnot מַחְשְׁבוֹתַיmy thoughts מַחְשְׁבוֹתֵיכֶםyour thoughts וְלֹאand not דַרְכֵיכֶםyour ways דְּרָכָיways my ways נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD

“For My thoughts are not your thoughts, neither are your ways My ways,” declares the LORD.

55:9

כִּיfor גָבְהוּhigher שָׁמַיִםheavens מֵאָרֶץthan the earth כֵּןso גָּבְהוּhigher דְרָכַיmy ways מִדַּרְכֵיכֶםthan your ways וּמַחְשְׁבֹתַיand my thoughts מִמַּחְשְׁבֹתֵיכֶםthan your thoughts

“For as the heavens are higher than the earth, so My ways are higher than your ways and My thoughts than your thoughts.

55:10

כִּיfor כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had יֵרֵדgo down הַגֶּשֶׁםrain וְהַשֶּׁלֶגand snow מִןfrom הַשָּׁמַיִםheaven וְשָׁמָּהand there toward לֹאnot יָשׁוּבreturn כִּיfor אִםif הִרְוָהwatering אֶת[obj] הָאָרֶץthe earth וְהוֹלִידָהּmaking it bud וְהִצְמִיחָהּand sprout וְנָתַןand providing זֶרַעseed לַזֹּרֵעַto sow וְלֶחֶםand food לָאֹכֵלto eat

For just as rain and snow fall from heaven and do not return without watering the earth, making it bud and sprout, and providing seed to sow and food to eat,

55:11

כֵּןso יִהְיֶהbe דְבָרִיmy word אֲשֶׁרthat יֵצֵאproceeds מִפִּיfrom my mouth לֹאnot יָשׁוּבreturn אֵלַיto me רֵיקָםempty כִּיbut אִםif עָשָׂהaccomplish אֶת[obj] אֲשֶׁרthat חָפַצְתִּיplease וְהִצְלִיחַprosper אֲשֶׁרthat שְׁלַחְתִּיוsend it

so My word that proceeds from My mouth will not return to Me empty, but it will accomplish what I please, and it will prosper where I send it.

55:12

כִּיfor בְשִׂמְחָהwith joy תֵצֵאוּforth וּבְשָׁלוֹםin peace תּוּבָלוּןled הֶהָרִיםthe mountains וְהַגְּבָעוֹתand hills יִפְצְחוּburst לִפְנֵיכֶםinto before you רִנָּהsong וְכָלand all עֲצֵיtrees הַשָּׂדֶהthe field יִמְחֲאוּclap כָףhands

You will indeed go out with joy and be led forth in peace; the mountains and hills will burst into song before you, and all the trees of the field will clap their hands.

55:13

תַּחַתinstead הַנַּעֲצוּץthe thornbush יַעֲלֶהup בְרוֹשׁcypress וְתַחַתand instead הַסִּרְפַּדof the brier יַעֲלֶהup הֲדַסmyrtle וְהָיָהand be לַיהוָהLORD לְשֵׁםname for לְאוֹתsign עוֹלָםeverlasting לֹאnever יִכָּרֵתdestroyed

Instead of the thornbush, a cypress will grow, and instead of the brier, a myrtle will spring up; they will make a name for the LORD, an everlasting sign, never to be destroyed.”
