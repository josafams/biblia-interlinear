Isaiah 64

A plea for God to rend the heavens

If only You would rend the heavens and come down, so that mountains would quake at Your presence,

64:2

כִּקְדֹחַas kindles אֵשׁfire הֲמָסִיםbrushwood מַיִםwater תִּבְעֶהboil אֵשׁboil לְהוֹדִיעַknown שִׁמְךָyour name לְצָרֶיךָto your enemies מִפָּנֶיךָat your presence גּוֹיִםnations יִרְגָּזוּtremble

as fire kindles the brushwood and causes the water to boil, to make Your name known to Your enemies, so that the nations will tremble at Your presence!

64:3

בַּעֲשׂוֹתְךָwhen you did נוֹרָאוֹתawesome לֹאnot נְקַוֶּהexpect יָרַדְתָּdown מִפָּנֶיךָat your presence הָרִיםmountains נָזֹלּוּtrembled

When You did awesome works that we did not expect, You came down, and the mountains trembled at Your presence.

64:4

וּמֵעוֹלָםfrom ancient לֹאno שָׁמְעוּheard לֹאno הֶאֱזִינוּear עַיִןeye לֹאno רָאָתָהseen אֱלֹהִיםGod זוּלָתְךָbesides you יַעֲשֶׂהacts לִמְחַכֵּהwait לוֹfor him

From ancient times no one has heard, no ear has perceived, no eye has seen any God besides You, who acts on behalf of those who wait for Him.

64:5

You welcome those who gladly do right, who remember Your ways. Surely You were angry, for we sinned. How can we be saved if we remain in our sins?

64:6

וַנְּהִיbecome כַטָּמֵאlike something unclean כֻּלָּנוּall our וּכְבֶגֶדlike filthy עִדִּיםrags כָּלall צִדְקֹתֵינוּrighteous וַנָּבֶלwither כֶּעָלֶהlike a leaf כֻּלָּנוּour all וַעֲוֺנֵנוּand iniquities כָּרוּחַlike the wind יִשָּׂאֻנוּcarry us

Each of us has become like something unclean, and all our righteous acts are like filthy rags; we all wither like a leaf, and our iniquities carry us away like the wind.

64:7

וְאֵיןno קוֹרֵאcalls בְשִׁמְךָon your name מִתְעוֹרֵרor strives לְהַחֲזִיקto take hold בָּךְin you כִּיfor הִסְתַּרְתָּhidden פָנֶיךָyour face מִמֶּנּוּfrom us וַתְּמוּגֵנוּand delivered us בְּיַדinto the hand עֲוֺנֵנוּour iniquity

No one calls on Your name or strives to take hold of You. For You have hidden Your face from us and delivered us into the hand of our iniquity.

64:8

וְעַתָּהbut now יְהוָהLORD אָבִינוּour father אָתָּהyou are אֲנַחְנוּwe הַחֹמֶרthe clay וְאַתָּהand you יֹצְרֵנוּpotter we וּמַעֲשֵׂהwork יָדְךָyour hand כֻּלָּנוּall the

But now, O LORD, You are our Father; we are the clay, and You are the potter; we are all the work of Your hand.

64:9

אַלdo not תִּקְצֹףangry יְהוָהLORD עַדuntil מְאֹדvery וְאַלdo not לָעַדforever תִּזְכֹּרremember עָוֺןiniquity הֵןbehold הַבֶּטlook נָאplease עַמְּךָyour people כֻלָּנוּall

Do not be angry, O LORD, beyond measure; do not remember our iniquity forever. Oh, look upon us, we pray; we are all Your people!

64:10

עָרֵיcities קָדְשְׁךָyour holy הָיוּbecome מִדְבָּרwilderness צִיּוֹןZion מִדְבָּרwasteland הָיָתָהbecome יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem שְׁמָמָהdesolation

Your holy cities have become a wilderness. Zion has become a wasteland and Jerusalem a desolation.

64:11

Our holy and beautiful temple, where our fathers praised You, has been burned with fire, and all that was dear to us lies in ruins.

64:12

הַעַלfor אֵלֶּהthis תִתְאַפַּקO יְהוָהLORD תֶּחֱשֶׁהsilent וּתְעַנֵּנוּand afflict us עַדuntil מְאֹדvery

After all this, O LORD, will You restrain Yourself? Will You keep silent and afflict us beyond measure?
