Isaiah 59

The sin that separates; the Redeemer comes

Open in the interlinear reader

59:1

הֵןsurely לֹאnot קָצְרָהtoo short יַדhand יְהוָהLORD מֵהוֹשִׁיעַto save וְלֹאand not כָבְדָהdull אָזְנוֹhis ear מִשְּׁמוֹעַto hear

Surely the arm of the LORD is not too short to save, nor His ear too dull to hear.

59:2

כִּיbut אִםif עֲוֺנֹתֵיכֶםyour iniquities הָיוּwere מַבְדִּלִיםbarriers בֵּינֵכֶםbetween you לְבֵיןto between אֱלֹהֵיכֶםyour God וְחַטֹּאותֵיכֶםand your sins הִסְתִּירוּhidden פָנִיםface מִכֶּםfrom of you מִשְּׁמוֹעַnot hear

But your iniquities have built barriers between you and your God, and your sins have hidden His face from you, so that He does not hear.

59:3

כִּיfor כַפֵּיכֶםyour hands נְגֹאֲלוּstained בַדָּםwith blood וְאֶצְבְּעוֹתֵיכֶםand your fingers בֶּעָוֺןwith iniquity שִׂפְתוֹתֵיכֶםyour lips דִּבְּרוּspoken שֶׁקֶרlies לְשׁוֹנְכֶםyour tongue עַוְלָהinjustice תֶהְגֶּהmutters

For your hands are stained with blood, and your fingers with iniquity; your lips have spoken lies, and your tongue mutters injustice.

59:4

אֵיןno קֹרֵאcalls בְצֶדֶקfor justice וְאֵיןno נִשְׁפָּטpleads בֶּאֱמוּנָהhonestly בָּטוֹחַrely עַלon תֹּהוּempty וְדַבֶּרthey tell שָׁוְאlies הָרוֹconceive עָמָלmischief וְהוֹלֵידand give birth אָוֶןiniquity

No one calls for justice; no one pleads his case honestly. They rely on empty pleas; they tell lies; they conceive mischief and give birth to iniquity.

59:5

בֵּיצֵיeggs צִפְעוֹנִיvipers בִּקֵּעוּhatch וְקוּרֵיand weave עַכָּבִישׁspider’s web יֶאֱרֹגוּweave הָאֹכֵלwhoever eats מִבֵּיצֵיהֶםtheir eggs יָמוּתdie וְהַזּוּרֶהcrack תִּבָּקַעopen אֶפְעֶהviper

They hatch the eggs of vipers and weave a spider’s web. Whoever eats their eggs will die; crack one open, and a viper is hatched.

59:6

קוּרֵיהֶםtheir cobwebs לֹאnot יִהְיוּbe לְבֶגֶדinto clothing וְלֹאand not יִתְכַּסּוּcover בְּמַעֲשֵׂיהֶםwith their works מַעֲשֵׂיהֶםtheir works מַעֲשֵׂיdeeds אָוֶןsinful וּפֹעַלdeeds and חָמָסviolence בְּכַפֵּיהֶםin their hands

Their cobwebs cannot be made into clothing, and they cannot cover themselves with their works. Their deeds are sinful deeds, and acts of violence are in their hands.

59:7

רַגְלֵיהֶםtheir feet לָרַעto evil יָרֻצוּrun וִימַהֲרוּthey are swift לִשְׁפֹּךְto shed דָּםblood נָקִיinnocent מַחְשְׁבוֹתֵיהֶםtheir thoughts are מַחְשְׁבוֹתthoughts אָוֶןsinful שֹׁדruin וָשֶׁבֶרand destruction בִּמְסִלּוֹתָםin their wake

Their feet run to evil; they are swift to shed innocent blood. Their thoughts are sinful thoughts; ruin and destruction lie in their wake.

59:8

דֶּרֶךְway שָׁלוֹםpeace לֹאnot יָדָעוּknown וְאֵיןno מִשְׁפָּטjustice בְּמַעְגְּלוֹתָםin their tracks נְתִיבוֹתֵיהֶםpaths עִקְּשׁוּcrooked לָהֶםto them כֹּלno דֹּרֵךְtreads בָּהּon them לֹאnot יָדַעknow שָׁלוֹםpeace

The way of peace they have not known, and there is no justice in their tracks. They have turned them into crooked paths; no one who treads on them will know peace.

59:9

עַלon כֵּןtherefore רָחַקfar מִשְׁפָּטjustice מִמֶּנּוּfrom us וְלֹאnot תַשִּׂיגֵנוּreach us צְדָקָהrighteousness נְקַוֶּהhope לָאוֹרfor light וְהִנֵּהbut there חֹשֶׁךְdarkness לִנְגֹהוֹתfor brightness בָּאֲפֵלוֹתin gloom נְהַלֵּךְwalk

Therefore justice is far from us, and righteousness does not reach us. We hope for light, but there is darkness; for brightness, but we walk in gloom.

59:10

נְגַשְׁשָׁהwe כַעִוְרִיםlike the blind קִירwall וּכְאֵיןlike those without עֵינַיִםeyes נְגַשֵּׁשָׁהwe כָּשַׁלְנוּstumble בַצָּהֳרַיִםat midday כַּנֶּשֶׁףas in the twilight בָּאַשְׁמַנִּיםamong the vigorous כַּמֵּתִיםlike the dead

Like the blind, we feel our way along the wall, groping like those without eyes. We stumble at midday as in the twilight; among the vigorous we are like the dead.

59:11

נֶהֱמֶהgrowl כַדֻּבִּיםlike bears כֻּלָּנוּwe all וְכַיּוֹנִיםand like doves הָגֹהmoan נֶהְגֶּהwe נְקַוֶּהhope לַמִּשְׁפָּטfor justice וָאַיִןbut find none לִישׁוּעָהfor salvation רָחֲקָהfar מִמֶּנּוּfrom us

We all growl like bears and moan like doves. We hope for justice, but find none, for salvation, but it is far from us.

59:12

כִּיfor רַבּוּmultiplied פְשָׁעֵינוּour transgressions נֶגְדֶּךָbefore you וְחַטֹּאותֵינוּand our sins עָנְתָהtestify בָּנוּagainst us כִּיfor פְשָׁעֵינוּour transgressions אִתָּנוּwith us וַעֲוֺנֹתֵינוּand we our iniquities יְדַעֲנוּםknow

For our transgressions are multiplied before You, and our sins testify against us. Our transgressions are indeed with us, and we know our iniquities:

59:13

פָּשֹׁעַrebelling וְכַחֵשׁand denying בַּיהוָהthe LORD וְנָסוֹגturning מֵאַחַרfrom following אֱלֹהֵינוּour God דַּבֶּרspeaking עֹשֶׁקoppression וְסָרָהand revolt הֹרוֹconceiving וְהֹגוֹand uttering מִלֵּבfrom the heart דִּבְרֵיwords שָׁקֶרlies

rebelling and denying the LORD, turning away from our God, speaking oppression and revolt, conceiving and uttering lies from the heart.

59:14

וְהֻסַּגturned אָחוֹרaway מִשְׁפָּטjustice וּצְדָקָהand righteousness מֵרָחוֹקat a distance תַּעֲמֹדstands כִּיfor כָשְׁלָהstumbled בָרְחוֹבin the public square אֱמֶתtruth וּנְכֹחָהand honesty לֹאnot תוּכַלcannot לָבוֹאenter

So justice is turned away, and righteousness stands at a distance. For truth has stumbled in the public square, and honesty cannot enter.

59:15

וַתְּהִיand was הָאֱמֶתtruth נֶעְדֶּרֶתmissing וְסָרand whoever turns מֵרָעfrom evil מִשְׁתּוֹלֵלprey וַיַּרְאlooked יְהוָהLORD וַיֵּרַעand was displeased בְּעֵינָיוin his sight כִּיfor אֵיןno מִשְׁפָּטjustice

Truth is missing, and whoever turns from evil becomes prey. The LORD looked and was displeased that there was no justice.

59:16

וַיַּרְאhe saw כִּיthat אֵיןno אִישׁman וַיִּשְׁתּוֹמֵםhe was amazed כִּיthat אֵיןno מַפְגִּיעַintercede וַתּוֹשַׁעbrought לוֹto him זְרֹעוֹarm וְצִדְקָתוֹand own righteousness הִיאis סְמָכָתְהוּsustained him

He saw that there was no man; He was amazed that there was no one to intercede. So His own arm brought salvation, and His own righteousness sustained Him.

59:17

וַיִּלְבַּשׁhe put צְדָקָהrighteousness כַּשִּׁרְיָןlike a breastplate וְכוֹבַעand the helmet יְשׁוּעָהsalvation בְּרֹאשׁוֹon his head וַיִּלְבַּשׁhe put בִּגְדֵיgarments נָקָםvengeance תִּלְבֹּשֶׁתwrapped וַיַּעַטand wrapped כַּמְעִילin a cloak קִנְאָהzeal

He put on righteousness like a breastplate, and the helmet of salvation on His head; He put on garments of vengeance and wrapped Himself in a cloak of zeal.

59:18

כְּעַלaccording to גְּמֻלוֹתretribution כְּעַלlike against יְשַׁלֵּםrepay חֵמָהfury לְצָרָיוfoes גְּמוּלrecompense לְאֹיְבָיוenemies to his לָאִיִּיםto the islands גְּמוּלwhat יְשַׁלֵּםrestitution

So He will repay according to their deeds: fury to His enemies, retribution to His foes, and recompense to the islands.

59:19

וְיִירְאוּfear מִמַּעֲרָבsets אֶת[obj] שֵׁםname יְהוָהLORD וּמִמִּזְרַחwhere שֶׁמֶשׁsun אֶת[obj] כְּבוֹדוֹhis glory כִּיfor יָבוֹאcome כַנָּהָרlike a flood צָרraging driven רוּחַbreath יְהוָהLORD נֹסְסָהdriven בוֹby

So shall they fear the name of the LORD where the sun sets, and His glory where it rises. For He will come like a raging flood, driven by the breath of the LORD.

59:20

וּבָאwill come לְצִיּוֹןto Zion גּוֹאֵלRedeemer וּלְשָׁבֵיturn פֶשַׁעtransgression בְּיַעֲקֹבin Jacob נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD

“The Redeemer will come to Zion, to those in Jacob who turn from transgression,” declares the LORD.

59:21

וַאֲנִיand I זֹאתthis בְּרִיתִיmy covenant אוֹתָםthem אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD רוּחִיmy spirit אֲשֶׁרthat עָלֶיךָon you וּדְבָרַיand my words אֲשֶׁרthat שַׂמְתִּיput בְּפִיךָin your mouth לֹאnot יָמוּשׁוּdepart מִפִּיךָfrom your mouth וּמִפִּיor from the mouths זַרְעֲךָyour children וּמִפִּיand from mouth זֶרַעgrandchildren זַרְעֲךָyour descendants אָמַרsays יְהוָהLORD מֵעַתָּהfrom now on וְעַדand to עוֹלָםforevermore

“As for Me, this is My covenant with them,” says the LORD. “My Spirit will not depart from you, and My words that I have put in your mouth will not depart from your mouth or from the mouths of your children and grandchildren, from now on and forevermore,” says the LORD.
