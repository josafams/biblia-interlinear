Romans 3

All have sinned; righteousness through faith in Christ

Open in the interlinear reader

3:1

What, then, is the advantage of being a Jew? Or what is the value of circumcision?

3:2

Much in every way. First of all, they have been entrusted with the very words of God.

3:3

What if some did not have faith? Will their lack of faith nullify God’s faithfulness?

3:4

Certainly not! Let God be true and every man a liar. As it is written: “So that You may be proved right when You speak and victorious when You judge.”

3:5

εἰif δὲbut ἡthe ἀδικίαunrighteousness ἡμῶνus θεοῦGod δικαιοσύνηνrighteousness συνίστησιν,highlights τίwhat ἐροῦμεν;say μὴdo ἄδικοςunjust ὁthe θεὸςGod ὁthe ἐπιφέρωνinflict τὴνhis ὀργήν;wrath κατὰin ἄνθρωπονhuman λέγω.terms

But if our unrighteousness highlights the righteousness of God, what shall we say? That God is unjust to inflict His wrath on us? I am speaking in human terms.

3:6

μὴnot γένοιτο·not ἐπεὶcase πῶςhow κρινεῖjudge ὁthe θεὸςGod τὸνthe κόσμον;world

Certainly not! In that case, how could God judge the world?

3:7

εἰif δὲbut ἡthe ἀλήθειαfalsehood accentuates τοῦthe θεοῦGod’s ἐνin τῷthe ἐμῷmy own ψεύσματιincrease ἐπερίσσευσενabound εἰςto τὴνhis δόξανglory αὐτοῦ,Him τίwhy ἔτιstill κἀγὼI ὡςas ἁμαρτωλὸςsinner κρίνομαι,condemned

However, if my falsehood accentuates God’s truthfulness, to the increase of His glory, why am I still condemned as a sinner?

3:8

καὶand μὴnot καθὼςas βλασφημούμεθαabuse καὶand καθώςas φασίνsay τινεςsome ἡμᾶςus λέγεινclaim ὅτιthat Ποιήσωμενdo τὰthe κακὰevil ἵναthat ἔλθῃcomes τὰthe ἀγαθά;good ὧνwhose τὸthe κρίμαresult condemnation ἔνδικόνdeserved ἐστιν.is

Why not say, as some slanderously claim that we say, “Let us do evil that good may result”? Their condemnation is deserved!

3:9

Τίwhat οὖν;then προεχόμεθα;better οὐnot πάντως,at all προῃτιασάμεθαcharge γὰρfor ἸουδαίουςJews τεand καὶand ἝλληναςGreeks alike πάνταςall ὑφ’under ἁμαρτίανsin εἶναι,be

What then? Are we any better? Not at all. For we have already made the charge that Jews and Greeks alike are all under sin.

3:10

καθὼςas γέγραπταιwritten ὅτιis Οὐκno ἔστινone δίκαιοςrighteous οὐδὲnot even εἷς,one

As it is written: “There is no one righteous, not even one.

3:11

οὐκno ἔστινone ὁwho συνίων,understands οὐκno ἔστινone ὁwho ἐκζητῶνseeks τὸνthe θεόν·God

There is no one who understands, no one who seeks God.

3:12

πάντεςall ἐξέκλιναν,turned ἅμαtogether ἠχρεώθησαν·worthless οὐκno ἔστινone ποιῶνdoes χρηστότητα,good οὐκnot ἔστινis ἕωςto ἑνός.one

All have turned away, they have together become worthless; there is no one who does good, not even one.”

3:13

“Their throats are open graves; their tongues practice deceit.” “The venom of vipers is on their lips.”

3:14

“Their mouths are full of cursing and bitterness.”

3:15

“Their feet are swift to shed blood;

3:16

σύντριμμαruin καὶand ταλαιπωρίαmisery lie ἐνin ταῖςthe ὁδοῖςwake αὐτῶν,them

ruin and misery lie in their wake,

3:17

καὶand ὁδὸνway εἰρήνηςpeace οὐκnot ἔγνωσαν.known

and the way of peace they have not known.”

3:18

οὐκno ἔστινis φόβοςfear θεοῦGod ἀπέναντιbefore τῶνthe ὀφθαλμῶνeyes αὐτῶν.them

“There is no fear of God before their eyes.”

3:19

Οἴδαμενknow δὲnow ὅτιthat ὅσαwhatever ὁthe νόμοςlaw λέγειsays τοῖςthe ἐνin τῷthe νόμῳlaw λαλεῖ,speaks ἵναso that πᾶνevery στόμαmouth φραγῇsilenced καὶand ὑπόδικοςanswerable to γένηταιbecome πᾶςwhole ὁthe κόσμοςworld τῷthe θεῷ·God

Now we know that whatever the law says, it says to those who are under the law, so that every mouth may be silenced and the whole world held accountable to God.

3:20

Therefore no one will be justified in His sight by works of the law. For the law merely brings awareness of sin.

But now, apart from the law, the righteousness of God has been revealed, as attested by the Law and the Prophets.

3:22

δικαιοσύνηrighteousness δὲbut θεοῦGod διὰthrough πίστεωςfaith ἸησοῦJesus Χριστοῦ,Christ εἰςto πάνταςall τοὺςwho πιστεύοντας,believe οὐno γάρis ἐστινno διαστολή.distinction

And this righteousness from God comes through faith in Jesus Christ to all who believe. There is no distinction,

3:23

πάντεςall γὰρfor ἥμαρτονsinned καὶand ὑστεροῦνταιshort τῆςthe δόξηςglory τοῦof θεοῦ,God

for all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God,

3:24

δικαιούμενοιjustified δωρεὰνfreely τῇthe αὐτοῦHim χάριτιgrace διὰthrough τῆςthe ἀπολυτρώσεωςredemption τῆςthe ἐνin ΧριστῷChrist Ἰησοῦ·Jesus

and are justified freely by His grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus.

3:25

ὃνwhom προέθετοatoning ὁthe θεὸςGod ἱλαστήριονpresented διὰthrough πίστεωςfaith ἐνin τῷhis αὐτοῦHim αἵματιblood εἰςin to ἔνδειξινdemonstrate τῆςhis δικαιοσύνηςrighteousness αὐτοῦHim διὰbecause τὴνhis πάρεσινforbearance τῶνthe προγεγονότωνpassed ἁμαρτημάτωνsins

God presented Him as the atoning sacrifice through faith in His blood, in order to demonstrate His righteousness, because in His forbearance He had passed over the sins committed beforehand.

3:26

ἐνin τῇthe ἀνοχῇdid τοῦthe θεοῦ,God πρὸςto τὴνthe ἔνδειξινdemonstrate τῆςhis δικαιοσύνηςrighteousness αὐτοῦHim ἐνat τῷthe νῦνpresent καιρῷ,time εἰςto τὸthe εἶναιbe αὐτὸνHim δίκαιονjust καὶand δικαιοῦνταjustify τὸνthe ἐκfrom πίστεωςfaith Ἰησοῦ.Jesus

He did this to demonstrate His righteousness at the present time, so as to be just and to justify the one who has faith in Jesus.

3:27

Where, then, is boasting? It is excluded. On what principle? On that of works? No, but on that of faith.

3:28

For we maintain that a man is justified by faith apart from works of the law.

3:29

Is God the God of Jews only? Is He not the God of Gentiles too? Yes, of Gentiles too,

3:30

since there is only one God, who will justify the circumcised by faith and the uncircumcised through that same faith.

3:31

Do we, then, nullify the law by this faith? Certainly not! Instead, we uphold the law.
