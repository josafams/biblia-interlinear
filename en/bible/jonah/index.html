Jonah יוֹנָה

Unlike the other prophets, Jonah is almost entirely narrative. Sent to preach against Nineveh, the Assyrian capital and Israel's enemy, the prophet flees by ship, is thrown into the sea and swallowed by a great fish. After praying and being returned to land, he preaches in the city, which repents and is spared. The book ends with Jonah angry at God's mercy and with a question that goes unanswered.

Structure

  1. The flight chs. 1–2

    Jonah flees to Tarshish, the storm overtakes him, the sailors throw him into the sea and he prays from the belly of the fish.

  2. The mission and the reaction chs. 3–4

    Nineveh hears the message and repents; God spares the city, and Jonah argues with God because of it.

Chapters
