Numbers 6

The Nazirite vow and the priestly blessing

And the LORD said to Moses,

“Speak to the Israelites and tell them that if a man or woman makes a special vow, the vow of a Nazirite, to separate himself to the LORD,

6:3

מִיַּיִןfrom wine וְשֵׁכָרand strong drink יַזִּירis חֹמֶץabstain יַיִןwine וְחֹמֶץvinegar שֵׁכָרdrink לֹאnot יִשְׁתֶּהdrink וְכָלany מִשְׁרַתgrape עֲנָבִיםjuice לֹאnot יִשְׁתֶּהdrink וַעֲנָבִיםgrapes לַחִיםfresh וִיבֵשִׁיםor raisins לֹאor יֹאכֵלeat

he is to abstain from wine and strong drink. He must not drink vinegar made from wine or strong drink, and he must not drink any grape juice or eat fresh grapes or raisins.

6:4

כֹּלall יְמֵיdays נִזְרוֹhis separation he מִכֹּלanything אֲשֶׁרthat יֵעָשֶׂהdone מִגֶּפֶןfrom grapevine הַיַּיִןthe grapevine מֵחַרְצַנִּיםseeds וְעַדand to זָגskins לֹאnot יֹאכֵלeat

All the days of his separation, he is not to eat anything that comes from the grapevine, not even the seeds or skins.

6:5

כָּלentire יְמֵיperiod נֶדֶרvow נִזְרוֹseparation תַּעַרrazor לֹאno יַעֲבֹרpass עַלover רֹאשׁוֹhis head he עַדuntil מְלֹאתcomplete הַיָּמִםthe time אֲשֶׁרthat יַזִּירseparation לַיהוָהto the LORD קָדֹשׁholy יִהְיֶהbe גַּדֵּלgrow פֶּרַעlong שְׂעַרhair רֹאשׁוֹhis head

For the entire period of his vow of separation, no razor shall pass over his head. He must be holy until the time of his separation to the LORD is complete; he must let the hair of his head grow long.

6:6

כָּלthroughout יְמֵיdays הַזִּירוֹhis separation לַיהוָהto the LORD עַלon נֶפֶשׁdead מֵתmust לֹאnot יָבֹאgo

Throughout the days of his separation to the LORD, he must not go near a dead body.

6:7

לְאָבִיוhis father וּלְאִמּוֹor mother לְאָחִיוor brother וּלְאַחֹתוֹor sister לֹאnot יִטַּמָּאdefile לָהֶםto בְּמֹתָםdie כִּיbecause נֵזֶרconsecration אֱלֹהָיוhis God עַלupon רֹאשׁוֹhis head

Even if his father or mother or brother or sister should die, he is not to defile himself, because the crown of consecration to his God is upon his head.

6:8

כֹּלthroughout יְמֵיtime נִזְרוֹhis separation he קָדֹשׁholy הוּאis לַיהוָהto the LORD

Throughout the time of his separation, he is holy to the LORD.

6:9

וְכִיif יָמוּתdie מֵתdies עָלָיוin his בְּפֶתַעin presence פִּתְאֹםpresence וְטִמֵּאand defiles רֹאשׁhead נִזְרוֹhair he וְגִלַּחmust shave רֹאשׁוֹhis head בְּיוֹםon the day טָהֳרָתוֹhis cleansing בַּיּוֹםday הַשְּׁבִיעִיthe seventh יְגַלְּחֶנּוּshave him

If someone suddenly dies in his presence and defiles his consecrated head of hair, he must shave his head on the day of his cleansing—the seventh day.

6:10

וּבַיּוֹםon the day הַשְּׁמִינִיeighth יָבִאbring שְׁתֵּיtwo תֹרִיםturtledoves אוֹor שְׁנֵיtwo בְּנֵיyoung יוֹנָהpigeons אֶלto הַכֹּהֵןthe priest אֶלto פֶּתַחentrance אֹהֶלTent מוֹעֵדMeeting

On the eighth day he must bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons to the priest at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.

6:11

וְעָשָׂהand does הַכֹּהֵןthe priest אֶחָדone לְחַטָּאתas a sin offering וְאֶחָדand the other לְעֹלָהas a burnt offering וְכִפֶּרto make atonement עָלָיוfor him מֵאֲשֶׁרbecause he has חָטָאsinned עַלon הַנָּפֶשׁthe dead body וְקִדַּשׁhe must consecrate אֶת[obj] רֹאשׁוֹhis head again בַּיּוֹםon that day הַהוּאthat

And the priest is to offer one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering to make atonement for him, because he has sinned by being in the presence of the dead body. On that day he must consecrate his head again.

6:12

וְהִזִּירhe must לַיהוָהto the LORD אֶת[obj] יְמֵיtime נִזְרוֹseparation וְהֵבִיאand bring כֶּבֶשׂmale lamb בֶּןyear old שְׁנָתוֹold לְאָשָׁםas a guilt offering וְהַיָּמִיםbut the preceding days הָרִאשֹׁנִיםcounted יִפְּלוּfall כִּיbecause טָמֵאdefiled נִזְרוֹhis separation

He must rededicate his time of separation to the LORD and bring a year-old male lamb as a guilt offering. But the preceding days shall not be counted, because his separation was defiled.

6:13

וְזֹאתnow this תּוֹרַתlaw הַנָּזִירthe Nazirite בְּיוֹםwhen his time מְלֹאתcomplete יְמֵיdays נִזְרוֹseparation יָבִיאbrought אֹתוֹhim אֶלto פֶּתַחentrance אֹהֶלTent מוֹעֵדMeeting

Now this is the law of the Nazirite when his time of separation is complete: He must be brought to the entrance to the Tent of Meeting,

6:14

וְהִקְרִיבpresent אֶת[obj] קָרְבָּנוֹoffering לַיהוָהto the LORD כֶּבֶשׂmale lamb בֶּןold שְׁנָתוֹyear old תָמִיםunblemished אֶחָדone לְעֹלָהas burnt offering וְכַבְשָׂהlamb אַחַתone בַּתold female שְׁנָתָהּan year old תְּמִימָהunblemished לְחַטָּאתas a sin offering וְאַיִלram אֶחָדone תָּמִיםunblemished לִשְׁלָמִיםas peace offering

and he is to present an offering to the LORD of an unblemished year-old male lamb as a burnt offering, an unblemished year-old female lamb as a sin offering, and an unblemished ram as a peace offering—

6:15

וְסַלbasket מַצּוֹתunleavened סֹלֶתfine flour חַלֹּתcakes בְּלוּלֹתmixed בַּשֶּׁמֶןwith oil וּרְקִיקֵיand wafers מַצּוֹתunleavened מְשֻׁחִיםcoated בַּשָּׁמֶןwith oil וּמִנְחָתָםtheir grain וְנִסְכֵּיהֶםofferings and drink offerings

together with their grain offerings and drink offerings—and a basket of unleavened cakes made from fine flour mixed with oil and unleavened wafers coated with oil.

6:16

וְהִקְרִיבpresent הַכֹּהֵןthe priest לִפְנֵיbefore יְהוָהLORD וְעָשָׂהand make אֶת[obj] חַטָּאתוֹsin offering וְאֶתand [obj] עֹלָתוֹburnt offering

The priest is to present all these before the LORD and make the sin offering and the burnt offering.

6:17

וְאֶתand [obj] הָאַיִלthe ram יַעֲשֶׂהoffer זֶבַחsacrifice שְׁלָמִיםpeace offering לַיהוָהto the LORD עַלon סַלbasket הַמַּצּוֹתof unleavened bread וְעָשָׂהoffer הַכֹּהֵןthe priest אֶת[obj] מִנְחָתוֹaccompanying grain offering וְאֶתand [obj] נִסְכּוֹdrink offering

He shall also offer the ram as a peace offering to the LORD, along with the basket of unleavened bread. And the priest is to offer the accompanying grain offering and drink offering.

6:18

וְגִלַּחshave הַנָּזִירthe Nazirite פֶּתַחentrance אֹהֶלTent מוֹעֵדMeeting אֶת[obj] רֹאשׁhead נִזְרוֹhis consecrated וְלָקַחtake אֶת[obj] שְׂעַרhair רֹאשׁhead נִזְרוֹhis separation וְנָתַןand put עַלon הָאֵשׁthe fire אֲשֶׁרthat תַּחַתunder זֶבַחsacrifice הַשְּׁלָמִיםthe peace offering

Then at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting, the Nazirite is to shave his consecrated head, take the hair, and put it on the fire under the peace offering.

6:19

וְלָקַחtake הַכֹּהֵןthe priest אֶת[obj] הַזְּרֹעַthe shoulder בְּשֵׁלָהboiled shoulder מִןfrom הָאַיִלthe ram וְחַלַּתcake מַצָּהunleavened אַחַתone מִןfrom הַסַּלthe basket וּרְקִיקwafer מַצָּהunleavened אֶחָדone וְנָתַןand put עַלon כַּפֵּיhands הַנָּזִירthe Nazirite אַחַרafter הִתְגַּלְּחוֹshaved אֶת[obj] נִזְרוֹconsecration

And the priest is to take the boiled shoulder from the ram, one unleavened cake from the basket, and one unleavened wafer, and put them into the hands of the Nazirite who has just shaved the hair of his consecration.

6:20

וְהֵנִיףthen wave אוֹתָםthem הַכֹּהֵןthe priest תְּנוּפָהas wave offering לִפְנֵיbefore יְהוָהLORD קֹדֶשׁholy הוּאis לַכֹּהֵןfor the priest עַלin חֲזֵהbreast הַתְּנוּפָהthe wave offering וְעַלand on שׁוֹקthigh הַתְּרוּמָהthat was presented וְאַחַרafter יִשְׁתֶּהdrink הַנָּזִירthe Nazirite יָיִןwine

The priest shall then wave them as a wave offering before the LORD. This is a holy portion for the priest, in addition to the breast of the wave offering and the thigh that was presented. After that, the Nazirite may drink wine.

6:21

זֹאתthis תּוֹרַתlaw הַנָּזִירthe Nazirite אֲשֶׁרwho יִדֹּרvows קָרְבָּנוֹhis offering לַיהוָהto the LORD עַלfor נִזְרוֹhis separation מִלְּבַדin addition to אֲשֶׁרthat תַּשִּׂיגafford יָדוֹelse כְּפִיfulfill נִדְרוֹhis vow אֲשֶׁרthat יִדֹּרvow כֵּןso יַעֲשֶׂהmakes עַלto תּוֹרַתlaw נִזְרוֹhis separation

This is the law of the Nazirite who vows his offering to the LORD for his separation, in addition to whatever else he can afford; he must fulfill whatever vow he makes, according to the law of his separation.”

“Tell Aaron and his sons: This is how you are to bless the Israelites. Say to them:

‘May the LORD bless you and keep you;

may the LORD cause His face to shine upon you and be gracious to you;

6:26

יִשָּׂאlift יְהוָהLORD פָּנָיוhis countenance אֵלֶיךָto you וְיָשֵׂםand give לְךָto you שָׁלוֹםpeace

may the LORD lift up His countenance toward you and give you peace.’

So they shall put My name on the Israelites, and I will bless them.”
