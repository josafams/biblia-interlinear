Numbers 13

The twelve spies in Canaan

And the LORD said to Moses,

13:2

“Send out for yourself men to spy out the land of Canaan, which I am giving to the Israelites. From each of their fathers’ tribes send one man who is a leader among them.”

So at the consent of the LORD, Moses sent them out from the Wilderness of Paran. All the men were leaders of the Israelites,

13:4

וְאֵלֶּהand these שְׁמוֹתָםtheir names לְמַטֵּהfrom the tribe רְאוּבֵןReuben שַׁמּוּעַShammua בֶּןson זַכּוּרZaccur

and these were their names: From the tribe of Reuben, Shammua son of Zaccur;

13:5

לְמַטֵּהfrom the tribe שִׁמְעוֹןSimeon שָׁפָטShaphat בֶּןson חוֹרִיHori

from the tribe of Simeon, Shaphat son of Hori;

13:6

לְמַטֵּהfrom the tribe יְהוּדָהJudah כָּלֵבCaleb בֶּןson יְפֻנֶּהJephunneh

from the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh;

13:7

לְמַטֵּהfrom the tribe יִשָּׂשכָרIssachar יִגְאָלIgal בֶּןson יוֹסֵףJoseph

from the tribe of Issachar, Igal son of Joseph;

13:8

לְמַטֵּהfrom the tribe אֶפְרָיִםEphraim הוֹשֵׁעַHoshea בִּןson נוּןNun

from the tribe of Ephraim, Hoshea son of Nun;

13:9

לְמַטֵּהfrom the tribe בִנְיָמִןBenjamin פַּלְטִיPalti בֶּןson רָפוּאRaphu

from the tribe of Benjamin, Palti son of Raphu;

13:10

לְמַטֵּהfrom the tribe זְבוּלֻןZebulun גַּדִּיאֵלGaddiel בֶּןson סוֹדִיSodi

from the tribe of Zebulun, Gaddiel son of Sodi;

13:11

לְמַטֵּהa tribe יוֹסֵףJoseph לְמַטֵּהtribe מְנַשֶּׁהManasseh גַּדִּיGaddi בֶּןson סוּסִיSusi

from the tribe of Manasseh (a tribe of Joseph), Gaddi son of Susi;

13:12

לְמַטֵּהfrom the tribe דָןDan עַמִּיאֵלAmmiel בֶּןson גְּמַלִּיGemalli

from the tribe of Dan, Ammiel son of Gemalli;

13:13

לְמַטֵּהthe tribe אָשֵׁרAsher סְתוּרSethur בֶּןson מִיכָאֵלMichael

from the tribe of Asher, Sethur son of Michael;

13:14

לְמַטֵּהfrom the tribe נַפְתָּלִיNaphtali נַחְבִּיNahbi בֶּןson וָפְסִיVophsi

from the tribe of Naphtali, Nahbi son of Vophsi;

13:15

לְמַטֵּהtribe גָדGad גְּאוּאֵלGeuel בֶּןson מָכִיMachi

and from the tribe of Gad, Geuel son of Machi.

13:16

אֵלֶּהthese שְׁמוֹתnames הָאֲנָשִׁיםthe men אֲשֶׁרthat שָׁלַחsent מֹשֶׁהMoses לָתוּרto spy אֶת[obj] הָאָרֶץthe land וַיִּקְרָאand gave מֹשֶׁהMoses לְהוֹשֵׁעַgave to Hoshea בִּןson נוּןNun יְהוֹשֻׁעַJoshua

These were the names of the men Moses sent to spy out the land; and Moses gave to Hoshea son of Nun the name Joshua.

13:17

וַיִּשְׁלַחwhen sent אֹתָםthem מֹשֶׁהMoses לָתוּרto spy אֶת[obj] אֶרֶץland כְּנָעַןCanaan וַיֹּאמֶרhe told אֲלֵהֶםto them עֲלוּgo up זֶהthis בַּנֶּגֶבthrough the Negev וַעֲלִיתֶםand go up אֶת[obj] הָהָרthe hill country

When Moses sent them to spy out the land of Canaan, he told them, “Go up through the Negev and into the hill country.

13:18

וּרְאִיתֶםsee אֶת[obj] הָאָרֶץthe land מַהwhat הִואis וְאֶתand [obj] הָעָםwhether people הַיֹּשֵׁבlike עָלֶיהָon her הֶחָזָקstrong הוּאhe הֲרָפֶהor weak הַמְעַטfew הוּאhe אִםor רָבmany

See what the land is like and whether its people are strong or weak, few or many.

13:19

וּמָהand what הָאָרֶץthe land אֲשֶׁרthat הוּאhe יֹשֵׁבlive בָּהּin her הֲטוֹבָהgood הִואis אִםor רָעָהbad וּמָהand what הֶעָרִיםthe cities אֲשֶׁרthat הוּאhe יוֹשֵׁבdwell בָּהֵנָּהin are הַבְּמַחֲנִיםcamps אִםor בְּמִבְצָרִיםfortifications

Is the land where they live good or bad? Are the cities where they dwell open camps or fortifications?

13:20

וּמָהand what הָאָרֶץthe soil הַשְּׁמֵנָהfertile הִואis אִםor רָזָהunproductive הֲיֵשׁare there בָּהּin it עֵץtrees אִםor אַיִןnot וְהִתְחַזַּקְתֶּםcourageous וּלְקַחְתֶּםand bring מִפְּרִיfruit הָאָרֶץthe land וְהַיָּמִיםwas the season יְמֵיfor the first בִּכּוּרֵיripe עֲנָבִיםgrapes

Is the soil fertile or unproductive? Are there trees in it or not? Be courageous, and bring back some of the fruit of the land.” (It was the season for the first ripe grapes.)

13:21

וַיַּעֲלוּwent up וַיָּתֻרוּand spied אֶת[obj] הָאָרֶץthe land מִמִּדְבַּרfrom the wilderness צִןZin עַדfar רְחֹבRehob לְבֹאtoward חֲמָתLebo hamath

So they went up and spied out the land from the Wilderness of Zin as far as Rehob, toward Lebo-hamath.

13:22

וַיַּעֲלוּthey went בַנֶּגֶבthrough the Negev וַיָּבֹאand came עַדto חֶבְרוֹןHebron וְשָׁםwhere אֲחִימַןAhiman שֵׁשַׁיSheshai וְתַלְמַיand Talmai יְלִידֵיdescendants הָעֲנָקAnak וְחֶבְרוֹןdwelled שֶׁבַעseven שָׁנִיםyears נִבְנְתָהbuilt לִפְנֵיbefore צֹעַןZoan מִצְרָיִםEgypt

They went up through the Negev and came to Hebron, where Ahiman, Sheshai, and Talmai, the descendants of Anak, dwelled. It had been built seven years before Zoan in Egypt.

13:23

וַיָּבֹאוּwhen they came עַדto נַחַלValley אֶשְׁכֹּלEshcol וַיִּכְרְתוּthey cut מִשָּׁםdown a זְמוֹרָהbranch וְאֶשְׁכּוֹלcluster עֲנָבִיםgrapes אֶחָדsingle וַיִּשָּׂאֻהוּthey carried בַמּוֹטon a pole בִּשְׁנָיִםtwo וּמִןalso some הָרִמֹּנִיםtook pomegranates וּמִןand from הַתְּאֵנִיםfigs

When they came to the Valley of Eshcol, they cut down a branch with a single cluster of grapes, which they carried on a pole between two men. They also took some pomegranates and figs.

13:24

לַמָּקוֹםthat place הַהוּאwas קָרָאcalled נַחַלValley אֶשְׁכּוֹלEshcol עַלon אֹדוֹתabout הָאֶשְׁכּוֹלthe cluster of grapes אֲשֶׁרthat כָּרְתוּcut מִשָּׁםthere בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites

Because of the cluster of grapes the Israelites cut there, that place was called the Valley of Eshcol.

13:25

וַיָּשֻׁבוּreturned מִתּוּרfrom spying הָאָרֶץthe land מִקֵּץafter אַרְבָּעִיםforty יוֹםdays

After forty days the men returned from spying out the land,

13:26

וַיֵּלְכוּand went וַיָּבֹאוּthey back אֶלto מֹשֶׁהMoses וְאֶלand to אַהֲרֹןAaron וְאֶלand to כָּלwhole עֲדַתcongregation בְּנֵיsons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael אֶלto מִדְבַּרwilderness פָּארָןParan קָדֵשָׁהat Kadesh וַיָּשִׁיבוּthey brought back אוֹתָםthem דָּבָרreport וְאֶתand [obj] כָּלwhole הָעֵדָהthe congregation וַיַּרְאוּםand showed them אֶת[obj] פְּרִיfruit הָאָרֶץthe land

and they went back to Moses, Aaron, and the whole congregation of Israel in the Wilderness of Paran at Kadesh. They brought back a report for the whole congregation and showed them the fruit of the land.

13:27

וַיְסַפְּרוּand they לוֹto Moses וַיֹּאמְרוּwe בָּאנוּwent אֶלinto הָאָרֶץthe land אֲשֶׁרwhich שְׁלַחְתָּנוּsent us וְגַםand indeed זָבַתflowing חָלָבmilk וּדְבַשׁand honey הִואis וְזֶהhere some פִּרְיָהּits fruit

And they gave this account to Moses: “We went into the land to which you sent us, and indeed, it is flowing with milk and honey. Here is some of its fruit!

13:28

אֶפֶסNevertheless כִּיfor עַזstrong הָעָםthe people הַיֹּשֵׁבliving בָּאָרֶץin the land וְהֶעָרִיםand the cities בְּצֻרוֹתfortified גְּדֹלֹתlarge מְאֹדvery וְגַםeven יְלִדֵיdescendants הָעֲנָקof Anak רָאִינוּsaw שָׁםthere

Nevertheless, the people living in the land are strong, and the cities are large and fortified. We even saw the descendants of Anak there.

13:29

עֲמָלֵקAmalekites יוֹשֵׁבlive בְּאֶרֶץin the land הַנֶּגֶבthe Negev וְהַחִתִּיHittites וְהַיְבוּסִיJebusites וְהָאֱמֹרִיand Amorites יוֹשֵׁבlive בָּהָרin the hill country וְהַכְּנַעֲנִיand Canaanites יֹשֵׁבlive עַלon הַיָּםthe sea וְעַלand on יַדalong הַיַּרְדֵּןJordan

The Amalekites live in the land of the Negev; the Hittites, Jebusites, and Amorites live in the hill country; and the Canaanites live by the sea and along the Jordan.”

13:30

וַיַּהַסthen quieted כָּלֵבCaleb אֶת[obj] הָעָםthe people אֶלto מֹשֶׁהMoses וַיֹּאמֶרand said עָלֹהgo נַעֲלֶהup וְיָרַשְׁנוּand take possession אֹתָהּher כִּיfor יָכוֹלwe נוּכַלcan certainly conquer לָהּto her

Then Caleb quieted the people before Moses and said, “We must go up and take possession of the land, for we can certainly conquer it!”

13:31

וְהָאֲנָשִׁיםbut the men אֲשֶׁרhad עָלוּgone up עִמּוֹwith him אָמְרוּreplied לֹאnot נוּכַלwe cannot לַעֲלוֹתgo up אֶלagainst הָעָםthe people כִּיfor חָזָקstronger הוּאhe מִמֶּנּוּthan we

But the men who had gone up with him replied, “We cannot go up against the people, for they are stronger than we are!”

13:32

So they gave the Israelites a bad report about the land that they had spied out: “The land we explored devours its inhabitants, and all the people we saw there are great in stature.

13:33

וְשָׁםthere רָאִינוּsaw אֶת[obj] הַנְּפִילִיםthe Nephilim בְּנֵיdescendants עֲנָקAnak מִןfrom הַנְּפִלִיםthe Nephilim וַנְּהִיand was בְעֵינֵינוּour own sight כַּחֲגָבִיםlike grasshoppers וְכֵןsame הָיִינוּbecome בְּעֵינֵיהֶםseemed

We even saw the Nephilim there—the descendants of Anak that come from the Nephilim! We seemed like grasshoppers in our own sight, and we must have seemed the same to them!”
