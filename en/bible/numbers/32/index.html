וַיֵּלְכוּwent בְּנֵיdescendants מָכִירMachir בֶּןson מְנַשֶּׁהManasseh גִּלְעָדָהto Gilead וַיִּלְכְּדֻהָcaptured it וַיּוֹרֶשׁand drove אֶת[obj] הָאֱמֹרִיAmorites אֲשֶׁרwho בָּהּin her

The descendants of Machir son of Manasseh went to Gilead, captured it, and drove out the Amorites who were there.

32:40

וַיִּתֵּןso gave מֹשֶׁהMoses אֶת[obj] הַגִּלְעָדGilead לְמָכִירMachir בֶּןson מְנַשֶּׁהManasseh וַיֵּשֶׁבand they settled בָּהּin her

So Moses gave Gilead to the clan of Machir son of Manasseh, and they settled there.

32:41

וְיָאִירJair בֶּןdescendant מְנַשֶּׁהManasseh הָלַךְwent וַיִּלְכֹּדand captured אֶת[obj] חַוֺּתֵיהֶםtheir villages וַיִּקְרָאand called אֶתְהֶןthem חַוֺּתHavvoth יָאִירHavvoth

Jair, a descendant of Manasseh, went and captured their villages and called them Havvoth-jair.

32:42

וְנֹבַחAnd Nobah הָלַךְwent וַיִּלְכֹּדand captured אֶת[obj] קְנָתKenath וְאֶתand [obj] בְּנֹתֶיהָits villages וַיִּקְרָאand called לָהto her נֹבַחNobah בִּשְׁמוֹafter his own name

And Nobah went and captured Kenath and its villages and called it Nobah, after his own name.
