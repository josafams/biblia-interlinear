Psalms 116

Thanksgiving of one who escaped death

Open in the interlinear reader

116:1

אָהַבְתִּיlove כִּיfor יִשְׁמַעheard יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] קוֹלִיvoice my תַּחֲנוּנָיappeal for mercy

I love the LORD, for He has heard my voice— my appeal for mercy.

116:2

כִּיbecause הִטָּהinclined אָזְנוֹhis ear לִיto me וּבְיָמַיlong as I אֶקְרָאcall

Because He has inclined His ear to me, I will call on Him as long as I live.

116:3

אֲפָפוּנִיentangled me חֶבְלֵיropes מָוֶתdeath וּמְצָרֵיanguish שְׁאוֹלSheol מְצָאוּנִיovercame me צָרָהtrouble וְיָגוֹןand sorrow אֶמְצָאfind

The ropes of death entangled me; the anguish of Sheol overcame me; I was confronted by trouble and sorrow.

116:4

וּבְשֵׁםon the name יְהוָהLORD אֶקְרָאcalled אָנָּהO יְהוָהLORD מַלְּטָהdeliver נַפְשִׁיmy soul

Then I called on the name of the LORD: “O LORD, deliver my soul!”

116:5

חַנּוּןgracious יְהֹוָהLORD וְצַדִּיקand righteous וֵאלֹהֵינוּour God מְרַחֵםcompassion

The LORD is gracious and righteous; our God is full of compassion.

116:6

שֹׁמֵרpreserves פְּתָאיִםsimplehearted יְהֹוָהLORD דַּלּוֹתִיhelpless וְלִיand to me יְהוֹשִׁיעַsaved

The LORD preserves the simplehearted; I was helpless, and He saved me.

116:7

שׁוּבִיreturn נַפְשִׁיmy soul לִמְנוּחָיְכִיto your rest כִּיfor יְהוָהLORD גָּמַלgood עָלָיְכִיto you

Return to your rest, O my soul, for the LORD has been good to you.

116:8

כִּיfor חִלַּצְתָּdelivered נַפְשִׁיmy soul מִמָּוֶתfrom death אֶת[obj] עֵינִיmy eyes מִןfrom דִּמְעָהtears אֶת[obj] רַגְלִיmy feet מִדֶּחִיfrom stumbling

For You have delivered my soul from death, my eyes from tears, my feet from stumbling.

116:9

אֶתְהַלֵּךְwalk לִפְנֵיbefore יְהוָהLORD בְּאַרְצוֹתin the land הַחַיִּיםthe living

I will walk before the LORD in the land of the living.

116:10

הֶאֱמַנְתִּיbelieved כִּיfor אֲדַבֵּרsaid אֲנִיam עָנִיתִיafflicted מְאֹדgreatly

I believed, therefore I said, “I am greatly afflicted.”

116:11

אֲנִיI אָמַרְתִּיsaid בְחָפְזִיin my alarm כָּלall הָאָדָםmen כֹּזֵבliars

In my alarm I said, “All men are liars!”

116:12

מָהhow אָשִׁיבrepay לַיהוָהthe LORD for כָּלall תַּגְמוּלוֹהִיhis goodness עָלָיto me

How can I repay the LORD for all His goodness to me?

116:13

כּוֹסcup יְשׁוּעוֹתsalvation אֶשָּׂאlift וּבְשֵׁםand on the name יְהוָהLORD אֶקְרָאcall

I will lift the cup of salvation and call on the name of the LORD.

116:14

נְדָרַיfulfill my vows לַיהוָהto the LORD אֲשַׁלֵּםfulfill נֶגְדָהpresence נָּאplease לְכָלof all עַמּוֹhis people

I will fulfill my vows to the LORD in the presence of all His people.

116:15

יָקָרprecious בְּעֵינֵיin the sight יְהוָהLORD הַמָּוְתָהthe death לַחֲסִידָיוof his saints

Precious in the sight of the LORD is the death of His saints.

116:16

אָנָּהtruly O יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor אֲנִיam עַבְדֶּךָyour servant אֲנִיam עַבְדְּךָservant בֶּןson אֲמָתֶךָmaidservant you פִּתַּחְתָּbroken לְמוֹסֵרָיmy bonds

Truly, O LORD, I am Your servant; I am Your servant, the son of Your maidservant; You have broken my bonds.

116:17

לְךָto you אֶזְבַּחoffer זֶבַחsacrifice תּוֹדָהthanksgiving וּבְשֵׁםand on the name יְהוָהLORD אֶקְרָאcall

I will offer to You a sacrifice of thanksgiving and call on the name of the LORD.

116:18

נְדָרַיfulfill my vows לַיהוָהto the LORD אֲשַׁלֵּםfulfill נֶגְדָהpresence נָּאplease לְכָלof all עַמּוֹhis people

I will fulfill my vows to the LORD in the presence of all His people,

116:19

בְּחַצְרוֹתin the courts בֵּיתhouse יְהוָהLORD’s בְּתוֹכֵכִיin your midst יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem הַלְלוּHallelujah יָהּHallelujah

in the courts of the LORD’s house, in your midst, O Jerusalem. Hallelujah!
