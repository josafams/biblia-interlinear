Psalms 110

The priest-king at God's right hand

Open in the interlinear reader

110:1

לְדָוִדDavid מִזְמוֹרPsalm נְאֻםdeclares יְהוָהLORD לַאדֹנִיto my Lord שֵׁבsit לִימִינִיat my right hand עַדuntil אָשִׁיתmake אֹיְבֶיךָyour enemies הֲדֹםfootstool לְרַגְלֶיךָyour feet

A Psalm of David. The LORD said to my Lord: “Sit at My right hand until I make Your enemies a footstool for Your feet.”

110:2

מַטֵּהscepter עֻזְּךָyour mighty scepter יִשְׁלַחextends יְהוָהLORD מִצִּיּוֹןfrom Zion רְדֵהrule בְּקֶרֶבin the midst אֹיְבֶיךָyour enemies

The LORD extends Your mighty scepter from Zion: “Rule in the midst of Your enemies.”

110:3

עַמְּךָyour people נְדָבֹתwilling בְּיוֹםon your day חֵילֶךָof battle arrayed בְּהַדְרֵיin splendor קֹדֶשׁholy מֵרֶחֶםfrom the womb מִשְׁחָרdawn לְךָto you טַלdew יַלְדֻתֶיךָyour youth

Your people shall be willing on Your day of battle. Arrayed in holy splendor, from the womb of the dawn, to You belongs the dew of Your youth.

110:4

נִשְׁבַּעsworn יְהוָהLORD וְלֹאand will not יִנָּחֵםchange אַתָּהyou כֹהֵןpriest לְעוֹלָםforever עַלin דִּבְרָתִיbefore מַלְכִּיorder צֶדֶקMelchizedek

The LORD has sworn and will not change His mind: “You are a priest forever in the order of Melchizedek.”

110:5

אֲדֹנָיLord is עַלon יְמִינְךָyour right hand מָחַץcrush בְּיוֹםin the day אַפּוֹhis wrath מְלָכִיםkings

The Lord is at Your right hand; He will crush kings in the day of His wrath.

110:6

יָדִיןjudge בַּגּוֹיִםthe nations מָלֵאheaping גְוִיּוֹתdead מָחַץcrush רֹאשׁleaders עַלon אֶרֶץfar and רַבָּהwide

He will judge the nations, heaping up the dead; He will crush the leaders far and wide.

110:7

מִנַּחַלfrom the brook בַּדֶּרֶךְby the road יִשְׁתֶּהdrink עַלon כֵּןtherefore יָרִיםlift רֹאשׁhead

He will drink from the brook by the road; therefore He will lift up His head.
