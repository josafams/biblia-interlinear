Psalms 109

A cry against unjust accusers

Open in the interlinear reader

109:1

לַמְנַצֵּחַfor the choirmaster לְדָוִדDavid מִזְמוֹרPsalm אֱלֹהֵיGod תְהִלָּתִיmy praise אַלnot תֶּחֱרַשׁsilent

For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. O God of my praise, be not silent.

109:2

כִּיfor פִיmouth רָשָׁעwicked וּפִיand mouths מִרְמָהdeceitful עָלַיagainst me פָּתָחוּopen דִּבְּרוּspeak אִתִּיagainst me לְשׁוֹןtongues שָׁקֶרlying

For wicked and deceitful mouths open against me; they speak against me with lying tongues.

109:3

וְדִבְרֵיwords and שִׂנְאָהhateful סְבָבוּנִיsurround me וַיִּלָּחֲמוּנִיattack me חִנָּםwithout cause

They surround me with hateful words and attack me without cause.

109:4

תַּחַתin אַהֲבָתִיmy love יִשְׂטְנוּנִיaccuse me וַאֲנִיbut I am תְפִלָּהprayer

In return for my love they accuse me, but I am a man of prayer.

109:5

וַיָּשִׂימוּthey repay עָלַיon me רָעָהevil תַּחַתfor טוֹבָהgood וְשִׂנְאָהand hatred תַּחַתfor אַהֲבָתִיmy love

They repay me evil for good, and hatred for my love.

109:6

הַפְקֵדset עָלָיוover him רָשָׁעwicked וְשָׂטָןlet an accuser יַעֲמֹדstand עַלon יְמִינוֹhis right hand

Set over him a wicked man; let an accuser stand at his right hand.

109:7

בְּהִשָּׁפְטוֹtried let him יֵצֵאgo רָשָׁעguilty וּתְפִלָּתוֹand may his prayer תִּהְיֶהbe לַחֲטָאָהregarded as sin

When he is tried, let him be found guilty, and may his prayer be regarded as sin.

109:8

יִהְיוּmay יָמָיוhis days מְעַטִּיםfew פְּקֻדָּתוֹhis position יִקַּחtake אַחֵרanother

May his days be few; may another take his position.

109:9

יִהְיוּmay בָנָיוhis children יְתוֹמִיםfatherless וְאִשְׁתּוֹand his wife אַלְמָנָהwidow

May his children be fatherless and his wife a widow.

109:10

וְנוֹעַand shake יָנוּעוּwander בָנָיוhis children וְשִׁאֵלוּas beggars וְדָרְשׁוּseeking מֵחָרְבוֹתֵיהֶםfar from their ruined homes

May his children wander as beggars, seeking sustenance far from their ruined homes.

109:11

יְנַקֵּשׁseize נוֹשֶׁהcreditor לְכָלall אֲשֶׁרthat לוֹto him וְיָבֹזּוּand plunder זָרִיםstrangers יְגִיעוֹhis labor

May the creditor seize all he owns, and strangers plunder the fruits of his labor.

109:12

אַלno יְהִיmay לוֹto מֹשֵׁךְextend חָסֶדkindness וְאַלand no יְהִיone חוֹנֵןfavor לִיתוֹמָיוto his fatherless

May there be no one to extend kindness to him, and no one to favor his fatherless children.

109:13

יְהִיmay אַחֲרִיתוֹhis descendants לְהַכְרִיתbe cut off בְּדוֹרfrom the next אַחֵרgeneration יִמַּחblotted שְׁמָםtheir name

May his descendants be cut off; may their name be blotted out from the next generation.

109:14

יִזָּכֵרremembered עֲוֺןiniquity אֲבֹתָיוhis fathers אֶלto יְהוָהLORD וְחַטַּאתand the sin אִמּוֹhis mother אַלdo not תִּמָּחblotted

May the iniquity of his fathers be remembered before the LORD, and the sin of his mother never be blotted out.

109:15

יִהְיוּremain נֶגֶדbefore יְהוָהLORD תָּמִידalways וְיַכְרֵתhe may cut off מֵאֶרֶץfrom the earth זִכְרָםtheir memory

May their sins always remain before the LORD, that He may cut off their memory from the earth.

109:16

יַעַןfor אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnever זָכַרthought עֲשׂוֹתshow חָסֶדkindness וַיִּרְדֹּףbut pursued אִישׁman עָנִיpoor וְאֶבְיוֹןand needy וְנִכְאֵהand brokenhearted לֵבָבbrokenhearted לְמוֹתֵתto their death

For he never thought to show kindness, but pursued the poor and needy and brokenhearted, even to their death.

109:17

וַיֶּאֱהַבhe loved קְלָלָהcursing וַתְּבוֹאֵהוּmay וְלֹאand not חָפֵץdelight בִּבְרָכָהblessing in וַתִּרְחַקfar מִמֶּנּוּfrom him

The cursing that he loved, may it fall on him; the blessing in which he refused to delight, may it be far from him.

109:18

וַיִּלְבַּשׁhe wore קְלָלָהcursing כְּמַדּוֹlike a coat וַתָּבֹאand came כַמַּיִםlike water בְּקִרְבּוֹin his among וְכַשֶּׁמֶןlike oil בְּעַצְמוֹתָיוhis bones

The cursing that he wore like a coat, may it soak into his body like water, and into his bones like oil.

109:19

תְּהִיbe לוֹto him כְּבֶגֶדlike a robe יַעְטֶהwrapped וּלְמֵזַחa belt תָּמִידforever יַחְגְּרֶהָtied around him

May it be like a robe wrapped about him, like a belt tied forever around him.

109:20

זֹאתthis פְּעֻלַּתreward שֹׂטְנַיmy accusers מֵאֵתthe יְהוָהLORD’s וְהַדֹּבְרִיםto those who speak רָעevil עַלagainst נַפְשִׁיmy soul

May this be the LORD’s reward to my accusers, to those who speak evil against me.

109:21

וְאַתָּהbut you יְהוִהGOD אֲדֹנָיLord עֲשֵׂהdeal אִתִּיwith לְמַעַןsake שְׁמֶךָyour name כִּיfor טוֹבgoodness חַסְדְּךָyour loving devotion הַצִּילֵנִיdeliver me

But You, O GOD, the Lord, deal kindly with me for the sake of Your name; deliver me by the goodness of Your loving devotion.

109:22

כִּיfor עָנִיpoor וְאֶבְיוֹןand needy אָנֹכִיam וְלִבִּיmy heart חָלַלwounded בְּקִרְבִּיwithin me

For I am poor and needy; my heart is wounded within me.

109:23

כְּצֵלlike a lengthening shadow כִּנְטוֹתוֹfading נֶהֱלָכְתִּיgo נִנְעַרְתִּיshaken כָּאַרְבֶּהlike a locust

I am fading away like a lengthening shadow; I am shaken off like a locust.

109:24

בִּרְכַּיmy knees כָּשְׁלוּweak מִצּוֹםfrom fasting וּבְשָׂרִיand my body כָּחַשׁgrows lean מִשָּׁמֶןand gaunt

My knees are weak from fasting, and my body grows lean and gaunt.

109:25

וַאֲנִיI הָיִיתִיam חֶרְפָּהscorn לָהֶםto יִרְאוּנִיsee me יְנִיעוּןshake רֹאשָׁםtheir heads

I am an object of scorn to my accusers; when they see me, they shake their heads.

109:26

עָזְרֵנִיhelp me יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהָיmy God הוֹשִׁיעֵנִיsave me כְחַסְדֶּךָaccording to your loving devotion

Help me, O LORD my God; save me according to Your loving devotion.

109:27

וְיֵדְעוּlet them know כִּיthat יָדְךָyour hand זֹּאתthis אַתָּהyou יְהוָהLORD עֲשִׂיתָהּdone it

Let them know that this is Your hand, that You, O LORD, have done it.

109:28

יְקַלְלוּcurse הֵמָּהthey וְאַתָּהyou תְבָרֵךְbless קָמוּrise וַיֵּבֹשׁוּshame וְעַבְדְּךָbut your servant יִשְׂמָחrejoice

Though they curse, You will bless. When they rise up, they will be put to shame, but Your servant will rejoice.

109:29

יִלְבְּשׁוּclothed שׂוֹטְנַיmy accusers כְּלִמָּהdisgrace וְיַעֲטוּthey wear כַמְעִילlike a robe בָּשְׁתָּםtheir shame

May my accusers be clothed with disgrace; may they wear their shame like a robe.

109:30

אוֹדֶהthank יְהוָהLORD מְאֹדvery בְּפִיwith my mouth I וּבְתוֹךְin the presence רַבִּיםmany אֲהַלְלֶנּוּpraise him

With my mouth I will thank the LORD profusely; I will praise Him in the presence of many.

109:31

כִּיfor יַעֲמֹדstands לִימִיןat the right hand אֶבְיוֹןneedy לְהוֹשִׁיעַto save מִשֹּׁפְטֵיfrom the condemners נַפְשׁוֹhis soul

For He stands at the right hand of the needy one, to save him from the condemners of his soul.
