Psalms 32

The blessedness of the forgiven

Open in the interlinear reader

32:1

לְדָוִדof David מַשְׂכִּילMaskil אַשְׁרֵיblessed נְשׂוּיforgiven פֶּשַׁעsins כְּסוּיcovered חֲטָאָהtransgressions

Of David. A Maskil. Blessed is he whose transgressions are forgiven, whose sins are covered.

32:2

אַשְׁרֵיblessed אָדָםman לֹאnot יַחְשֹׁבcount יְהוָהLORD לוֹto him עָוֺןiniquity וְאֵיןthere is no בְּרוּחוֹhim in whose spirit רְמִיָּהdeceit

Blessed is the man whose iniquity the LORD does not count against him, in whose spirit there is no deceit.

32:3

כִּיwhen הֶחֱרַשְׁתִּיsilent בָּלוּkept עֲצָמָיmy bones בְּשַׁאֲגָתִיfrom my groaning כָּלall הַיּוֹםday long

When I kept silent, my bones became brittle from my groaning all day long.

32:4

כִּיfor יוֹמָםday וָלַיְלָהand night תִּכְבַּדheavy עָלַיupon me יָדֶךָyour hand נֶהְפַּךְturned לְשַׁדִּיdrained בְּחַרְבֹנֵיheat קַיִץsummer סֶלָהSelah

For day and night Your hand was heavy upon me; my strength was drained as in the summer heat. Selah

32:5

חַטָּאתִיacknowledged my sin אוֹדִיעֲךָyou וַעֲוֺנִיmy iniquity לֹאnot כִסִּיתִיhide אָמַרְתִּיsaid אוֹדֶהconfess עֲלֵיon פְשָׁעַיmy transgressions לַיהוָהto the LORD וְאַתָּהand you נָשָׂאתָforgave עֲוֺןguilt חַטָּאתִיmy sin סֶלָהSelah

Then I acknowledged my sin to You and did not hide my iniquity. I said, “I will confess my transgressions to the LORD,” and You forgave the guilt of my sin. Selah

32:6

עַלon זֹאתthis יִתְפַּלֵּלpray כָּלall חָסִידgodly אֵלֶיךָto you while you לְעֵתto time מְצֹאfound רַקsurely לְשֵׁטֶףrise מַיִםwaters רַבִּיםgreat אֵלָיוto him לֹאnot יַגִּיעוּcome near

Therefore let all the godly pray to You while You may be found. Surely when great waters rise, they will not come near.

32:7

אַתָּהyou סֵתֶרhiding לִיto me מִצַּרfrom trouble תִּצְּרֵנִיprotect me רָנֵּיsongs פַלֵּטdeliverance תְּסוֹבְבֵנִיsurround me סֶלָהSelah

You are my hiding place. You protect me from trouble; You surround me with songs of deliverance. Selah

32:8

אַשְׂכִּילְךָinstruct you וְאוֹרְךָand teach you בְּדֶרֶךְthe way זוּyou should תֵלֵךְgo אִיעֲצָהcounsel עָלֶיךָover you עֵינִיwatch

I will instruct you and teach you the way you should go; I will give you counsel and watch over you.

32:9

אַלdo not תִּהְיוּbe כְּסוּסlike the horse כְּפֶרֶדor mule אֵיןno הָבִיןunderstanding בְּמֶתֶגcontrolled with bit וָרֶסֶןand bridle עֶדְיוֹhis jewelry לִבְלוֹםto be held in בַּלnot קְרֹבcome אֵלֶיךָto you

Do not be like the horse or mule, which have no understanding; they must be controlled with bit and bridle to make them come to you.

32:10

רַבִּיםmany מַכְאוֹבִיםsorrows לָרָשָׁעof the wicked וְהַבּוֹטֵחַwho trusts בַּיהוָהin the LORD חֶסֶדloving devotion יְסוֹבְבֶנּוּsurrounds him

Many are the sorrows of the wicked, but loving devotion surrounds him who trusts in the LORD.

32:11

שִׂמְחוּglad בַיהוָהin the LORD וְגִילוּand rejoice צַדִּיקִיםrighteous וְהַרְנִינוּshout for joy כָּלall יִשְׁרֵיupright לֵבheart

Be glad in the LORD and rejoice, O righteous ones; shout for joy, all you upright in heart.
