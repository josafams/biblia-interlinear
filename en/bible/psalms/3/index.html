Psalms 3

Trust while fleeing from Absalom

Open in the interlinear reader

A Psalm of David, when he fled from his son Absalom

3:1

יְהוָהLORD מָהhow רַבּוּincreased צָרָיmy foes רַבִּיםmany קָמִיםrise עָלָיagainst me

O LORD, how my foes have increased! How many rise up against me!

3:2

רַבִּיםmany אֹמְרִיםsay לְנַפְשִׁיto my soul אֵיןnot יְשׁוּעָתָהdeliver לּוֹto him בֵאלֹהִיםGod סֶלָהSelah

Many say of me, “God will not deliver him.” Selah

3:3

וְאַתָּהbut you יְהוָהLORD מָגֵןshield בַּעֲדִיaround me כְּבוֹדִיmy glory וּמֵרִיםlifts רֹאשִׁיmy head

But You, O LORD, are a shield around me, my glory, and the One who lifts my head.

3:4

קוֹלִיmy voice אֶלto יְהוָהLORD אֶקְרָאcry aloud וַיַּעֲנֵנִיand he answers me מֵהַרmountain קָדְשׁוֹhis holy סֶלָהSelah

To the LORD I cry aloud, and He answers me from His holy mountain. Selah

3:5

אֲנִיI שָׁכַבְתִּיlie וָאִישָׁנָהand sleep הֱקִיצוֹתִיwake כִּיfor יְהוָהLORD יִסְמְכֵנִיsustains me

I lie down and sleep; I wake again, for the LORD sustains me.

3:6

לֹאnot אִירָאfear מֵרִבְבוֹתthe myriads עָםpeople אֲשֶׁרthat סָבִיבevery שָׁתוּset עָלָיagainst me

I will not fear the myriads set against me on every side.

3:7

קוּמָהarise יְהוָהLORD הוֹשִׁיעֵנִיsave me אֱלֹהַיmy God כִּיfor הִכִּיתָstrike אֶת[obj] כָּלall אֹיְבַיmy enemies לֶחִיjaw שִׁנֵּיteeth רְשָׁעִיםwicked שִׁבַּרְתָּbreak

Arise, O LORD! Save me, O my God! Strike all my enemies on the jaw; break the teeth of the wicked.

3:8

לַיהוָהto LORD הַיְשׁוּעָהsalvation עַלon עַמְּךָpeople בִרְכָתֶךָblessing סֶּלָהSelah

Salvation belongs to the LORD; may Your blessing be on Your people. Selah
