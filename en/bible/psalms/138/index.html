Psalms 138

Thanksgiving before the kings of the earth

Open in the interlinear reader

138:1

לְדָוִדof David אוֹדְךָgive you thanks בְכָלwith all לִבִּיmy heart נֶגֶדbefore אֱלֹהִיםgods אֲזַמְּרֶךָּsing your praises

Of David. I give You thanks with all my heart; before the gods I sing Your praises.

138:2

אֶשְׁתַּחֲוֶהbow down אֶלto הֵיכַלtemple קָדְשְׁךָyour holy וְאוֹדֶהand give thanks אֶת[obj] שְׁמֶךָyour name עַלfor חַסְדְּךָyour loving devotion your וְעַלand on אֲמִתֶּךָfaithfulness you כִּיfor הִגְדַּלְתָּexalted עַלon כָּלall שִׁמְךָyour name and your אִמְרָתֶךָword

I bow down toward Your holy temple and give thanks to Your name for Your loving devotion and Your faithfulness; You have exalted Your name and Your word above all else.

138:3

בְּיוֹםon the day קָרָאתִיcalled וַתַּעֲנֵנִיyou answered me תַּרְהִבֵנִיemboldened me בְנַפְשִׁיand my soul עֹזstrengthened

On the day I called, You answered me; You emboldened me and strengthened my soul.

138:4

יוֹדוּךָgive you thanks יְהוָהLORD כָּלall מַלְכֵיkings אָרֶץearth כִּיwhen שָׁמְעוּhear אִמְרֵיwords פִיךָyour mouth

All the kings of the earth will give You thanks, O LORD, when they hear the words of Your mouth.

138:5

וְיָשִׁירוּthey will sing בְּדַרְכֵיthe ways יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor גָדוֹלgreat כְּבוֹדglory יְהוָהLORD

They will sing of the ways of the LORD, for the glory of the LORD is great.

138:6

כִּיfor רָםhigh יְהוָהLORD וְשָׁפָלlowly יִרְאֶהattends וְגָבֹהַּbut the proud מִמֶּרְחָקfrom afar יְיֵדָעknows

Though the LORD is on high, He attends to the lowly; but the proud He knows from afar.

138:7

אִםif אֵלֵךְwalk בְּקֶרֶבin the midst צָרָהtrouble תְּחַיֵּנִיpreserve me עַלon אַףanger אֹיְבַיmy foes תִּשְׁלַחextend יָדֶךָyour hand וְתוֹשִׁיעֵנִיsaves me יְמִינֶךָyour right hand

If I walk in the midst of trouble, You preserve me from the anger of my foes; You extend Your hand, and Your right hand saves me.

138:8

יְהוָהLORD יִגְמֹרpurpose בַּעֲדִיfor me יְהוָהLORD חַסְדְּךָyour loving devotion לְעוֹלָםendures forever מַעֲשֵׂיworks יָדֶיךָyour hands אַלdo not תֶּרֶףabandon

The LORD will fulfill His purpose for me. O LORD, Your loving devotion endures forever— do not abandon the works of Your hands.
