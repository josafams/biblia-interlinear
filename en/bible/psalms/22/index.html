Psalms 22

From forsakenness to praise in the assembly

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. To the tune of “The Doe of the Dawn.” A Psalm of David

22:1

אֵלִיmy God אֵלִיmy God לָמָהwhy עֲזַבְתָּנִיforsaken me רָחוֹקfar מִישׁוּעָתִיfrom saving דִּבְרֵיwords שַׁאֲגָתִיgroaning

My God, my God, why have You forsaken me? Why are You so far from saving me, so far from my words of groaning?

22:2

אֱלֹהַיmy God אֶקְרָאcry יוֹמָםby day וְלֹאnot תַעֲנֶהanswer וְלַיְלָהand by night וְלֹאno דוּמִיָּהrest לִיto me

I cry out by day, O my God, but You do not answer, and by night, but I have no rest.

22:3

וְאַתָּהyet you קָדוֹשׁholy יוֹשֵׁבenthroned תְּהִלּוֹתpraises יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

Yet You are holy, enthroned on the praises of Israel.

22:4

בְּךָin you בָּטְחוּtrusted אֲבֹתֵינוּour fathers בָּטְחוּtrusted וַתְּפַלְּטֵמוֹand you delivered them

In You our fathers trusted; they trusted and You delivered them.

22:5

אֵלֶיךָto you זָעֲקוּcried out וְנִמְלָטוּand were set בְּךָin you בָטְחוּtrusted וְלֹאand were not בוֹשׁוּdisappointed

They cried out to You and were set free; they trusted in You and were not disappointed.

22:6

וְאָנֹכִיbut I am תוֹלַעַתworm וְלֹאand not אִישׁman חֶרְפַּתscorned אָדָםmen וּבְזוּיand despised עָםpeople

But I am a worm and not a man, scorned by men and despised by the people.

22:7

כָּלall רֹאַיsee me יַלְעִגוּmock לִיto me יַפְטִירוּsneer בְשָׂפָהin speech יָנִיעוּshake רֹאשׁheads

All who see me mock me; they sneer and shake their heads:

22:8

גֹּלtrusts אֶלto יְהוָהLORD יְפַלְּטֵהוּdeliver him יַצִּילֵהוּrescue him כִּיfor חָפֵץdelights בּוֹin him

“He trusts in the LORD, let the LORD deliver him; let the LORD rescue him, since He delights in him.”

22:9

כִּיfor אַתָּהyou גֹחִיbrought me forth מִבָּטֶןfrom the womb מַבְטִיחִיme secure עַלon שְׁדֵיbreast אִמִּיmy mother’s

Yet You brought me forth from the womb; You made me secure at my mother’s breast.

22:10

עָלֶיךָupon you הָשְׁלַכְתִּיcast מֵרָחֶםfrom birth מִבֶּטֶןwomb אִמִּיmy mother’s אֵלִיmy God אָתָּהyou

From birth I was cast upon You; from my mother’s womb You have been my God.

22:11

אַלnot תִּרְחַקfar מִמֶּנִּיfrom me כִּיfor צָרָהtrouble קְרוֹבָהnear כִּיfor אֵיןno עוֹזֵרhelp

Be not far from me, for trouble is near and there is no one to help.

22:12

סְבָבוּנִיsurround me פָּרִיםbulls רַבִּיםmany אַבִּירֵיstrong bulls בָשָׁןBashan כִּתְּרוּנִיencircle me

Many bulls surround me; strong bulls of Bashan encircle me.

22:13

פָּצוּopen עָלַיagainst me פִּיהֶםtheir jaws אַרְיֵהlions טֹרֵףmaul וְשֹׁאֵגroar and

They open their jaws against me like lions that roar and maul.

22:14

כַּמַּיִםlike water נִשְׁפַּכְתִּיpoured וְהִתְפָּרְדוּand Abram כָּלall עַצְמוֹתָיmy bones הָיָהare לִבִּיmy heart כַּדּוֹנָגlike wax it melts נָמֵסmelts בְּתוֹךְwithin מֵעָיwithin me

I am poured out like water, and all my bones are disjointed. My heart is like wax; it melts away within me.

22:15

יָבֵשׁdried כַּחֶרֶשׂlike a potsherd כֹּחִיmy strength וּלְשׁוֹנִיand my tongue מֻדְבָּקsticks מַלְקוֹחָיroof of my וְלַעֲפַרin the dust מָוֶתdeath תִּשְׁפְּתֵנִיmouth

My strength is dried up like a potsherd, and my tongue sticks to the roof of my mouth. You lay me in the dust of death.

22:16

כִּיfor סְבָבוּנִיsurround me כְּלָבִיםdogs עֲדַתband מְרֵעִיםevil הִקִּיפוּנִיencircles me כָּאֲרִיthey have pierced יָדַיmy hands וְרַגְלָיand feet

For dogs surround me; a band of evil men encircles me; they have pierced my hands and feet.

22:17

אֲסַפֵּרcount כָּלall עַצְמוֹתָיmy bones הֵמָּהthey יַבִּיטוּstare יִרְאוּgloat בִיin me

I can count all my bones; they stare and gloat over me.

22:18

יְחַלְּקוּdivide בְגָדַיmy garments לָהֶםto them וְעַלfor לְבוּשִׁיmy clothing יַפִּילוּcast גוֹרָלlots

They divide my garments among them and cast lots for my clothing.

22:19

וְאַתָּהbut you יְהוָהLORD אַלnot תִּרְחָקfar אֱיָלוּתִיmy strength לְעֶזְרָתִיto help me חוּשָׁהquickly

But You, O LORD, be not far off; O my Strength, come quickly to help me.

22:20

הַצִּילָהdeliver מֵחֶרֶבfrom the sword נַפְשִׁיmy soul מִיַּדfrom the power כֶּלֶבdogs יְחִידָתִיmy precious

Deliver my soul from the sword, my precious life from the power of wild dogs.

22:21

הוֹשִׁיעֵנִיsave me מִפִּיfrom the mouth אַרְיֵהlion וּמִקַּרְנֵיat the horns רֵמִיםwild oxen עֲנִיתָנִיanswered me

Save me from the mouth of the lion; at the horns of the wild oxen You have answered me!

22:22

אֲסַפְּרָהproclaim שִׁמְךָyour name לְאֶחָיto my brothers בְּתוֹךְin among קָהָלassembly אֲהַלְלֶךָּpraise you

I will proclaim Your name to my brothers; I will praise You in the assembly.

22:23

יִרְאֵיfear יְהוָהLORD הַלְלוּהוּpraise him כָּלall זֶרַעdescendants יַעֲקֹבJacob כַּבְּדוּהוּhonor him וְגוּרוּrevere מִמֶּנּוּfrom us כָּלall זֶרַעoffspring יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

You who fear the LORD, praise Him! All descendants of Jacob, honor Him! All offspring of Israel, revere Him!

22:24

כִּיfor לֹאnot בָזָהdespised וְלֹאor שִׁקַּץdetested עֱנוּתtorment עָנִיafflicted וְלֹאhe has not הִסְתִּירhidden פָּנָיוhis face מִמֶּנּוּfrom him וּבְשַׁוְּעוֹcry for help אֵלָיוto his שָׁמֵעַhas

For He has not despised or detested the torment of the afflicted. He has not hidden His face from him, but has attended to his cry for help.

22:25

מֵאִתְּךָfrom the you תְהִלָּתִיmy praise בְּקָהָלassembly רָבgreat נְדָרַיfulfill my vows אֲשַׁלֵּםfulfill נֶגֶדbefore יְרֵאָיוfear

My praise for You resounds in the great assembly; I will fulfill my vows before those who fear You.

22:26

יֹאכְלוּeat עֲנָוִיםpoor וְיִשְׂבָּעוּand be satisfied יְהַלְלוּpraise יְהוָהLORD דֹּרְשָׁיוseek יְחִיlive לְבַבְכֶםyour hearts לָעַדforever

The poor will eat and be satisfied; those who seek the LORD will praise Him. May your hearts live forever!

22:27

יִזְכְּרוּremember וְיָשֻׁבוּand turn אֶלto יְהוָהLORD כָּלall אַפְסֵיends אָרֶץearth וְיִשְׁתַּחֲווּbow down לְפָנֶיךָbefore כָּלall מִשְׁפְּחוֹתfamilies גּוֹיִםnations

All the ends of the earth will remember and turn to the LORD. All the families of the nations will bow down before Him.

22:28

כִּיfor לַיהוָהto the LORD הַמְּלוּכָהdominion וּמֹשֵׁלand he rules בַּגּוֹיִםover the nations

For dominion belongs to the LORD and He rules over the nations.

22:29

אָכְלוּfeast וַיִּשְׁתַּחֲוּוּand worship כָּלall דִּשְׁנֵיrich אֶרֶץearth לְפָנָיוbefore him יִכְרְעוּkneel כָּלall יוֹרְדֵיdown עָפָרdust וְנַפְשׁוֹtheir lives לֹאnot חִיָּהpreserve

All the rich of the earth will feast and worship; all who go down to the dust will kneel before Him— even those unable to preserve their lives.

22:30

זֶרַעposterity יַעַבְדֶנּוּserve him יְסֻפַּרdeclare לַאדֹנָיthe Lord לַדּוֹרto a new generation

Posterity will serve Him; they will declare the Lord to a new generation.

22:31

יָבֹאוּcome וְיַגִּידוּand proclaim צִדְקָתוֹhis righteousness לְעַםto a people נוֹלָדunborn כִּיfor עָשָׂהdone

They will come and proclaim His righteousness to a people yet unborn— all that He has done.
