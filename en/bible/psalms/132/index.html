Psalms 132

David, the ark and the choice of Zion

Open in the interlinear reader

132:1

שִׁירsong הַמַּעֲלוֹתof ascents זְכוֹרremember יְהוָהLORD לְדָוִדDavid אֵת[obj] כָּלall עֻנּוֹתוֹhardships he endured

A song of ascents. O LORD, remember on behalf of David all the hardships he endured,

132:2

אֲשֶׁרthat נִשְׁבַּעswore an oath לַיהוָהto the LORD נָדַרvowed לַאֲבִירto the mighty one יַעֲקֹבJacob

how he swore an oath to the LORD, and vowed to the Mighty One of Jacob:

132:3

אִםnot אָבֹאenter בְּאֹהֶלin Tent בֵּיתִיmy house אִםor אֶעֱלֶהgo עַלon עֶרֶשׂbed יְצוּעָיmy bed

“I will not enter my house or get into my bed,

132:4

אִםnot אֶתֵּןgive שְׁנַתsleep לְעֵינָיto my eyes לְעַפְעַפַּיmy eyelids תְּנוּמָהslumber

I will not give sleep to my eyes or slumber to my eyelids,

132:5

עַדuntil אֶמְצָאfind מָקוֹםplace לַיהוָהfor the LORD מִשְׁכָּנוֹתdwelling לַאֲבִירfor the mighty one יַעֲקֹבJacob

until I find a place for the LORD, a dwelling for the Mighty One of Jacob.”

132:6

הִנֵּהbehold שְׁמַעֲנוּהָheard that the ark בְאֶפְרָתָהwas in Ephrathah מְצָאנוּהָfound it בִּשְׂדֵיin the fields יָעַרJaar

We heard that the ark was in Ephrathah; we found it in the fields of Jaar.

132:7

נָבוֹאָהgo לְמִשְׁכְּנוֹתָיוto his dwelling נִשְׁתַּחֲוֶהworship לַהֲדֹםat footstool רַגְלָיוhis feet

Let us go to His dwelling place; let us worship at His footstool.

132:8

קוּמָהarise יְהוָהLORD לִמְנוּחָתֶךָto your resting place אַתָּהyou וַאֲרוֹןand the ark עֻזֶּךָyour strength

Arise, O LORD, to Your resting place, You and the ark of Your strength.

132:9

כֹּהֲנֶיךָyour priests יִלְבְּשׁוּclothed צֶדֶקrighteousness וַחֲסִידֶיךָand your saints יְרַנֵּנוּjoy

May Your priests be clothed with righteousness, and Your saints shout for joy.

132:10

בַּעֲבוּרfor the sake דָּוִדDavid עַבְדֶּךָyour servant אַלdo not תָּשֵׁבreject פְּנֵיface מְשִׁיחֶךָyour anointed

For the sake of Your servant David, do not reject Your anointed one.

132:11

נִשְׁבַּעswore יְהוָהLORD לְדָוִדto David אֱמֶתpromise לֹאnot יָשׁוּבrevoke מִמֶּנָּהone of מִפְּרִיdescendants בִטְנְךָyour womb אָשִׁיתplace לְכִסֵּאon throne לָךְto you

The LORD swore an oath to David, a promise He will not revoke: “One of your descendants I will place on your throne.

132:12

אִםif יִשְׁמְרוּkeep בָנֶיךָyour sons בְּרִיתִיmy covenant וְעֵדֹתִיand the testimony I זוֹthis אֲלַמְּדֵםteach them גַּםalso בְּנֵיהֶםtheir sons עֲדֵיforever עַדever יֵשְׁבוּsit לְכִסֵּאon throne לָךְto you

If your sons keep My covenant and the testimony I will teach them, then their sons will also sit on your throne forever and ever.”

132:13

כִּיfor בָחַרchosen יְהוָהLORD בְּצִיּוֹןZion אִוָּהּdesired it לְמוֹשָׁבfor home לוֹto him

For the LORD has chosen Zion; He has desired it for His home:

132:14

זֹאתthis מְנוּחָתִיmy resting place עֲדֵיforever עַדever פֹּהhere אֵשֵׁבdwell כִּיfor אִוִּתִיהָdesired this

“This is My resting place forever and ever; here I will dwell, for I have desired this home.

132:15

צֵידָהּprovisions בָּרֵךְbless אֲבָרֵךְbless אֶבְיוֹנֶיהָher poor אַשְׂבִּיעַsatisfy לָחֶםbread

I will bless her with abundant provisions; I will satisfy her poor with bread.

132:16

וְכֹהֲנֶיהָwill her priests אַלְבִּישׁclothe יֶשַׁעsalvation וַחֲסִידֶיהָand her saints רַנֵּןsing יְרַנֵּנוּjoy

I will clothe her priests with salvation, and her saints will sing out in joy.

132:17

שָׁםthere אַצְמִיחַgrow קֶרֶןhorn לְדָוִדfor David עָרַכְתִּיprepared נֵרlamp לִמְשִׁיחִיfor my anointed

There I will make a horn grow for David; I have prepared a lamp for My anointed one.

132:18

אוֹיְבָיוhis enemies אַלְבִּישׁclothe בֹּשֶׁתshame וְעָלָיוupon him יָצִיץgleam נִזְרוֹcrown

I will clothe his enemies with shame, but the crown upon him will gleam.”
