Psalms 141

A prayer not to give in to evil

Open in the interlinear reader

141:1

מִזְמוֹרPsalm לְדָוִדof David יְהוָהLORD קְרָאתִיךָcall upon you חוּשָׁהquickly לִּיto me הַאֲזִינָהhear קוֹלִיmy voice בְּקָרְאִיwhen I call לָךְto you

A Psalm of David. I call upon You, O LORD; come quickly to me. Hear my voice when I call to You.

141:2

תִּכּוֹןmy תְּפִלָּתִיmy prayer קְטֹרֶתincense לְפָנֶיךָbefore you מַשְׂאַתuplifted כַּפַּיhands מִנְחַתoffering עָרֶבevening

May my prayer be set before You like incense, my uplifted hands like the evening offering.

141:3

שִׁיתָהset יְהוָהLORD שָׁמְרָהguard לְפִיover my mouth נִצְּרָהkeep watch עַלon דַּלdoor שְׂפָתָיmy lips

Set a guard, O LORD, over my mouth; keep watch at the door of my lips.

141:4

אַלdo תַּטnot לִבִּיmy heart לְדָבָרthing רָעevil לְהִתְעוֹלֵלor take part עֲלִלוֹתpart works בְּרֶשַׁעwickedness אֶתwith אִישִׁיםmen פֹּעֲלֵיdo אָוֶןiniquity וּבַלlet me not אֶלְחַםfeast בְּמַנְעַמֵּיהֶםon their delicacies

Do not let my heart be drawn to any evil thing or take part in works of wickedness with men who do iniquity; let me not feast on their delicacies.

141:5

יֶהֶלְמֵנִיstrike me צַדִּיקrighteous חֶסֶדloving devotion וְיוֹכִיחֵנִיrebuke שֶׁמֶןoil רֹאשׁhead אַלnot יָנִיrefuse רֹאשִׁיmy head כִּיfor עוֹדlonger וּתְפִלָּתִיmy prayer בְּרָעוֹתֵיהֶםagainst the deeds

Let the righteous man strike me; let his rebuke be an act of loving devotion. It is oil for my head; let me not refuse it. For my prayer is ever against the deeds of the wicked.

141:6

נִשְׁמְטוּthrown בִידֵיin hands סֶלַעcliffs שֹׁפְטֵיהֶםtheir rulers וְשָׁמְעוּlisten אֲמָרַיmy words כִּיfor נָעֵמוּpleasant

When their rulers are thrown down from the cliffs, the people will listen to my words, for they are pleasant.

141:7

כְּמוֹas פֹלֵחַplows וּבֹקֵעַand breaks בָּאָרֶץup the soil נִפְזְרוּscattered עֲצָמֵינוּour bones לְפִיat the mouth שְׁאוֹלSheol

As when one plows and breaks up the soil, so our bones have been scattered at the mouth of Sheol.

141:8

כִּיbut אֵלֶיךָto you יְהֹוִהGOD אֲדֹנָיLord עֵינָיeyes are fixed בְּכָהin you חָסִיתִיseek refuge אַלdo not תְּעַרleave נַפְשִׁיmy soul

But my eyes are fixed on You, O GOD the Lord. In You I seek refuge; do not leave my soul defenseless.

141:9

שָׁמְרֵנִיkeep me מִידֵיfrom hands פַחsnares יָקְשׁוּlaid לִיfor me וּמֹקְשׁוֹתlures פֹּעֲלֵיevildoers אָוֶןevildoers

Keep me from the snares they have laid for me, and from the lures of evildoers.

141:10

יִפְּלוּfall בְמַכְמֹרָיוinto their own nets רְשָׁעִיםwicked יַחַדtogether אָנֹכִיI עַדuntil אֶעֱבוֹרpass

Let the wicked fall into their own nets, while I pass by in safety.
