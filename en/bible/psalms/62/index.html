Psalms 62

In God alone the soul finds rest

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. According to Jeduthun. A Psalm of David

62:1

אַךְbut אֶלto אֱלֹהִיםGod דּוּמִיָּהrest נַפְשִׁיmy soul מִמֶּנּוּfrom him יְשׁוּעָתִיmy salvation

In God alone my soul finds rest; my salvation comes from Him.

62:2

אַךְbut הוּאis צוּרִיmy rock וִישׁוּעָתִיand my salvation מִשְׂגַּבִּיfortress I לֹאnever אֶמּוֹטshaken רַבָּהgreat

He alone is my rock and my salvation. He is my fortress; I will never be shaken.

62:3

עַדhow אָנָהlong will you תְּהוֹתְתוּthreaten עַלon אִישׁa man תְּרָצְּחוּmurder כֻלְּכֶםall of you כְּקִירlike a leaning נָטוּיleaning גָּדֵרwall הַדְּחוּיָהor a tottering fence

How long will you threaten a man? Will all of you throw him down like a leaning wall or a tottering fence?

62:4

אַךְbut מִשְּׂאֵתוֹfrom his lofty יָעֲצוּlofty לְהַדִּיחַto cast יִרְצוּdelight כָזָבlies בְּפִיוwith their mouths יְבָרֵכוּbless וּבְקִרְבָּםbut inwardly they יְקַלְלוּcurse סֶלָהSelah

They fully intend to cast him down from his lofty perch; they delight in lies; with their mouths they bless, but inwardly they curse. Selah

62:5

אַךְbut לֵאלֹהִיםin God דּוֹמִּיrest נַפְשִׁיmy soul כִּיfor מִמֶּנּוּfrom him תִּקְוָתִיmy hope

Rest in God alone, O my soul, for my hope comes from Him.

62:6

אַךְbut הוּאis צוּרִיmy rock וִישׁוּעָתִיand my salvation מִשְׂגַּבִּיfortress I לֹאnot אֶמּוֹטshaken

He alone is my rock and my salvation; He is my fortress; I will not be shaken.

62:7

עַלon אֱלֹהִיםGod יִשְׁעִיmy salvation my וּכְבוֹדִיand honor צוּרrock עֻזִּיstrong my מַחְסִיrefuge בֵּאלֹהִיםin God

My salvation and my honor rest on God, my strong rock; my refuge is in God.

62:8

בִּטְחוּtrust בוֹin him בְכָלat all עֵתtimes עָםpeople שִׁפְכוּpour לְפָנָיוbefore him לְבַבְכֶםyour hearts אֱלֹהִיםGod מַחֲסֶהrefuge לָּנוּto us סֶלָהSelah

Trust in Him at all times, O people; pour out your hearts before Him. God is our refuge. Selah

62:9

אַךְbut הֶבֶלvapor בְּנֵיsons אָדָםmen כָּזָבexalted בְּנֵיsons אִישׁman בְּמֹאזְנַיִםweighed on לַעֲלוֹתscale הֵמָּהthey מֵהֶבֶלa vapor יָחַדtogether

Lowborn men are but a vapor, the exalted but a lie. Weighed on the scale, they go up; together they are but a vapor.

62:10

אַלno תִּבְטְחוּtrust בְעֹשֶׁקin extortion וּבְגָזֵלor hope in stolen אַלor תֶּהְבָּלוּfalse חַיִלgoods כִּיif יָנוּבriches increase אַלdo not תָּשִׁיתוּset לֵבheart

Place no trust in extortion, or false hope in stolen goods. If your riches increase, do not set your heart upon them.

62:11

אַחַתonce דִּבֶּרspoken אֱלֹהִיםGod שְׁתַּיִםtwice זוּhave שָׁמָעְתִּיheard כִּיthat עֹזpower לֵאלֹהִיםto God

God has spoken once; I have heard this twice: that power belongs to God,

62:12

וּלְךָto you אֲדֹנָיLord חָסֶדloving devotion כִּיfor אַתָּהyou תְשַׁלֵּםrepay לְאִישׁeach man כְּמַעֲשֵׂהוּaccording to his deeds

and loving devotion to You, O Lord. For You will repay each man according to his deeds.
