Psalms 21

Gratitude for the king's victories

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David

21:1

יְהוָהLORD בְּעָזְּךָin your strength יִשְׂמַחrejoices מֶלֶךְking וּבִישׁוּעָתְךָhe in your salvation מַהhow יָּגֶלexults מְאֹדgreatly

O LORD, the king rejoices in Your strength. How greatly he exults in Your salvation!

21:2

תַּאֲוַתdesire לִבּוֹhis heart’s נָתַתָּהgranted לּוֹto him וַאֲרֶשֶׁתrequest שְׂפָתָיוhis lips בַּלnot מָנַעְתָּwithheld סֶּלָהSelah

You have granted his heart’s desire and have not withheld the request of his lips. Selah

21:3

כִּיfor תְקַדְּמֶנּוּwelcomed him בִּרְכוֹתblessings טוֹבrich תָּשִׁיתplaced לְרֹאשׁוֹon his head עֲטֶרֶתcrown פָּזpure gold

For You welcomed him with rich blessings; You placed on his head a crown of pure gold.

21:4

חַיִּיםlife שָׁאַלasked מִמְּךָfrom you נָתַתָּהgranted לּוֹto him אֹרֶךְlength יָמִיםdays עוֹלָםforever וָעֶדand ever

He asked You for life, and You granted it— length of days, forever and ever.

21:5

גָּדוֹלgreat כְּבוֹדוֹhis glory בִּישׁוּעָתֶךָin your salvation you הוֹדsplendor וְהָדָרand majesty תְּשַׁוֶּהbestow עָלָיוon him

Great is his glory in Your salvation; You bestow on him splendor and majesty.

21:6

כִּיfor תְשִׁיתֵהוּgrant him בְרָכוֹתblessings לָעַדforever תְּחַדֵּהוּcheer him בְשִׂמְחָהwith joy אֶתwith פָּנֶיךָyour presence

For You grant him blessings forever; You cheer him with joy in Your presence.

21:7

כִּיfor הַמֶּלֶךְthe king בֹּטֵחַtrusts בַּיהוָהin the LORD וּבְחֶסֶדthrough the loving devotion עֶלְיוֹןMost high בַּלnot יִמּוֹטshaken

For the king trusts in the LORD; through the loving devotion of the Most High, he will not be shaken.

21:8

תִּמְצָאapprehend יָדְךָyour hand לְכָלall אֹיְבֶיךָenemies your יְמִינְךָright hand will תִּמְצָאseize שֹׂנְאֶיךָhate you

Your hand will apprehend all Your enemies; Your right hand will seize those who hate You.

21:9

תְּשִׁיתֵמוֹplace them כְּתַנּוּרa furnace אֵשׁfiery לְעֵתat the time פָּנֶיךָyour appearing יְהוָהLORD בְּאַפּוֹin his wrath יְבַלְּעֵםengulf them וְתֹאכְלֵםconsume them אֵשׁfire

You will place them in a fiery furnace at the time of Your appearing. In His wrath the LORD will engulf them, and the fire will consume them.

21:10

פִּרְיָמוֹtheir descendants מֵאֶרֶץfrom the earth תְּאַבֵּדwipe וְזַרְעָםand their offspring מִבְּנֵיfrom the sons אָדָםmen

You will wipe their descendants from the earth, and their offspring from the sons of men.

21:11

כִּיfor נָטוּintend עָלֶיךָon you רָעָהharm חָשְׁבוּdevise מְזִמָּהschemes בַּלnot יוּכָלוּprevail

Though they intend You harm, the schemes they devise will not prevail.

21:12

כִּיfor תְּשִׁיתֵמוֹput them שֶׁכֶםflight בְּמֵיתָרֶיךָwhen your bow תְּכוֹנֵןis trained עַלupon פְּנֵיהֶםtheir face

For You will put them to flight when Your bow is trained upon them.

21:13

רוּמָהexalted יְהוָהLORD בְּעֻזֶּךָin your strength נָשִׁירָהsing וּנְזַמְּרָהand praise גְּבוּרָתֶךָyour power

Be exalted, O LORD, in Your strength; we will sing and praise Your power.
