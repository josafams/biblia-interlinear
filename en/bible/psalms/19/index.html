Psalms 19

Creation and the Law reveal God

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David

19:1

הַשָּׁמַיִםthe heavens מְסַפְּרִיםdeclare כְּבוֹדglory אֵלGod וּמַעֲשֵׂהwork יָדָיוhis hands מַגִּידproclaim הָרָקִיעַthe skies

The heavens declare the glory of God; the skies proclaim the work of His hands.

19:2

יוֹםday לְיוֹםafter day יַבִּיעַpour אֹמֶרspeech וְלַיְלָהnight after לְּלַיְלָהafter night יְחַוֶּהreveal דָּעַתknowledge

Day after day they pour forth speech; night after night they reveal knowledge.

19:3

אֵיןwithout אֹמֶרspeech וְאֵיןor without דְּבָרִיםlanguage בְּלִיwithout נִשְׁמָעheard קוֹלָםsound

Without speech or language, without a sound to be heard,

19:4

בְּכָלinto all הָאָרֶץthe earth יָצָאgone out קַוָּםtheir voice וּבִקְצֵהto the ends תֵבֵלworld מִלֵּיהֶםtheir words לַשֶּׁמֶשׁfor the sun שָׂםpitched a אֹהֶלtent בָּהֶםin them

their voice has gone out into all the earth, their words to the ends of the world. In the heavens He has pitched a tent for the sun.

19:5

וְהוּאa כְּחָתָןlike bridegroom יֹצֵאemerging מֵחֻפָּתוֹfrom his chamber יָשִׂישׂchampion כְּגִבּוֹרrejoicing לָרוּץto run אֹרַחcourse

Like a bridegroom emerging from his chamber, like a champion rejoicing to run his course,

19:6

מִקְצֵהat one end הַשָּׁמַיִםthe heavens מוֹצָאוֹit rises וּתְקוּפָתוֹand runs its עַלto קְצוֹתָםcircuit וְאֵיןnothing נִסְתָּרdeprived מֵחַמָּתוֹof its warmth

it rises at one end of the heavens and runs its circuit to the other; nothing is deprived of its warmth.

19:7

תּוֹרַתlaw יְהוָהLORD תְּמִימָהperfect מְשִׁיבַתreviving נָפֶשׁsoul עֵדוּתtestimony יְהוָהLORD נֶאֱמָנָהtrustworthy מַחְכִּימַתwise פֶּתִיsimple

The Law of the LORD is perfect, reviving the soul; the testimony of the LORD is trustworthy, making wise the simple.

19:8

פִּקּוּדֵיprecepts יְהוָהLORD יְשָׁרִיםright מְשַׂמְּחֵיbringing joy לֵבheart מִצְוַתcommandments יְהוָהLORD בָּרָהradiant מְאִירַתlight עֵינָיִםeyes

The precepts of the LORD are right, bringing joy to the heart; the commandments of the LORD are radiant, giving light to the eyes.

19:9

יִרְאַתfear יְהוָהLORD טְהוֹרָהpure עוֹמֶדֶתenduring לָעַדforever מִשְׁפְּטֵיjudgments יְהוָהLORD אֱמֶתtrue צָדְקוּrighteous יַחְדָּוtogether

The fear of the LORD is pure, enduring forever; the judgments of the LORD are true, being altogether righteous.

19:10

הַנֶּחֱמָדִיםare more precious מִזָּהָבthan gold וּמִפַּזthan pure gold רָבmuch וּמְתוּקִיםthey are sweeter מִדְּבַשׁthan honey than וְנֹפֶתhoney צוּפִיםcomb

They are more precious than gold, than much pure gold; they are sweeter than honey, than honey from the comb.

19:11

גַּםindeed עַבְדְּךָyour servant נִזְהָרwarned בָּהֶםby them בְּשָׁמְרָםin keeping them עֵקֶבreward רָבgreat

By them indeed Your servant is warned; in keeping them is great reward.

19:12

שְׁגִיאוֹתwho מִיwho can יָבִיןdiscern מִנִּסְתָּרוֹתfrom my hidden נַקֵּנִיfaults

Who can discern his own errors? Cleanse me from my hidden faults.

19:13

גַּםalso מִזֵּדִיםfrom willful חֲשֹׂךְsins עַבְדֶּךָyour servant אַלnot יִמְשְׁלוּrule בִיin me אָזthen אֵיתָםblameless וְנִקֵּיתִיand cleansed מִפֶּשַׁעof transgression רָבgreat

Keep Your servant also from willful sins; may they not rule over me. Then I will be blameless and cleansed of great transgression.

19:14

יִהְיוּmay לְרָצוֹןto acceptable אִמְרֵיwords פִיmy mouth וְהֶגְיוֹןand the meditation לִבִּיmy heart לְפָנֶיךָin your sight יְהוָהLORD צוּרִיmy rock וְגֹאֲלִיand my Redeemer

May the words of my mouth and the meditation of my heart be pleasing in Your sight, O LORD, my Rock and my Redeemer.
