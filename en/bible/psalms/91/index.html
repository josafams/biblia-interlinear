Psalms 91

Protection in the shadow of the Most High

Open in the interlinear reader

91:1

יֹשֵׁבdwells בְּסֵתֶרin the shelter עֶלְיוֹןMost high בְּצֵלabide in the shadow שַׁדַּיAlmighty יִתְלוֹנָןnight

He who dwells in the shelter of the Most High will abide in the shadow of the Almighty.

91:2

אֹמַרsay לַיהוָהto the LORD מַחְסִיmy refuge my וּמְצוּדָתִיand fortress my אֱלֹהַיGod אֶבְטַחtrust בּוֹin whom

I will say to the LORD, “You are my refuge and my fortress, my God, in whom I trust.”

91:3

כִּיfor הוּאhe will יַצִּילְךָdeliver you מִפַּחfrom the snare יָקוּשׁfowler מִדֶּבֶרplague הַוּוֹתdeadly

Surely He will deliver you from the snare of the fowler, and from the deadly plague.

91:4

בְּאֶבְרָתוֹhis feathers יָסֶךְcover לָךְto you וְתַחַתunder כְּנָפָיוhis wings תֶּחְסֶהrefuge צִנָּהshield וְסֹחֵרָהand rampart אֲמִתּוֹhis faithfulness

He will cover you with His feathers; under His wings you will find refuge; His faithfulness is a shield and rampart.

91:5

לֹאnot תִירָאfear מִפַּחַדthe terror לָיְלָהnight מֵחֵץnor the arrow יָעוּףflies יוֹמָםby day

You will not fear the terror of the night, nor the arrow that flies by day,

91:6

מִדֶּבֶרstalks בָּאֹפֶלin the darkness יַהֲלֹךְgo מִקֶּטֶבnor the calamity יָשׁוּדdestroys צָהֳרָיִםnoon

nor the pestilence that stalks in the darkness, nor the calamity that destroys at noon.

91:7

יִפֹּלfall מִצִּדְּךָat your side אֶלֶףthousand וּרְבָבָהand ten thousand מִימִינֶךָat your right hand אֵלֶיךָto you לֹאno יִגָּשׁcome near

Though a thousand may fall at your side, and ten thousand at your right hand, no harm will come near you.

91:8

רַקonly בְּעֵינֶיךָwith your eyes תַבִּיטsee וְשִׁלֻּמַתand witness רְשָׁעִיםwicked תִּרְאֶהsee

You will only see it with your eyes and witness the punishment of the wicked.

91:9

כִּיbecause אַתָּהyou יְהוָהLORD מַחְסִיmy refuge עֶלְיוֹןMost high שַׂמְתָּmade מְעוֹנֶךָyour dwelling

Because you have made the LORD your dwelling— my refuge, the Most High—

91:10

לֹאno תְאֻנֶּהbefall אֵלֶיךָto you רָעָהevil וְנֶגַעplague לֹאno יִקְרַבapproach בְּאָהֳלֶךָyour tent

no evil will befall you, no plague will approach your tent.

91:11

כִּיfor מַלְאָכָיוhis angels יְצַוֶּהcommand לָּךְto you לִשְׁמָרְךָto guard you בְּכָלin all דְּרָכֶיךָyour ways

For He will command His angels concerning you to guard you in all your ways.

91:12

עַלin כַּפַּיִםhands יִשָּׂאוּנְךָlift you פֶּןnot תִּגֹּףstrike בָּאֶבֶןagainst a stone רַגְלֶךָyour foot

They will lift you up in their hands, so that you will not strike your foot against a stone.

91:13

עַלon שַׁחַלlion וָפֶתֶןand cobra תִּדְרֹךְtread תִּרְמֹסtrample כְּפִירyoung lion וְתַנִּיןand serpent

You will tread on the lion and cobra; you will trample the young lion and serpent.

91:14

כִּיbecause בִיin me חָשַׁקloves וַאֲפַלְּטֵהוּwill deliver him אֲשַׂגְּבֵהוּprotect him כִּיbecause יָדַעknows שְׁמִיmy name

“Because he loves Me, I will deliver him; because he knows My name, I will protect him.

91:15

יִקְרָאֵנִיcalls וְאֶעֱנֵהוּI will answer him עִמּוֹwith him אָנֹכִיI בְצָרָהin trouble אֲחַלְּצֵהוּdeliver him וַאֲכַבְּדֵהוּand honor him

When he calls out to Me, I will answer him; I will be with him in trouble. I will deliver him and honor him.

91:16

אֹרֶךְlong יָמִיםlife אַשְׂבִּיעֵהוּsatisfy him וְאַרְאֵהוּand show him בִּישׁוּעָתִיmy salvation

With long life I will satisfy him and show him My salvation.”
