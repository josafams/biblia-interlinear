Psalms 131

The soul quieted like a child

Open in the interlinear reader

131:1

שִׁירsong הַמַּעֲלוֹתof ascents לְדָוִדof David יְהוָהLORD לֹאnot גָבַהּproud לִבִּיmy heart וְלֹאnot רָמוּhaughty עֵינַיmy eyes וְלֹאI do not הִלַּכְתִּיaspire בִּגְדֹלוֹתto great וּבְנִפְלָאוֹתor matters lofty מִמֶּנִּיfrom me

A song of ascents. Of David. My heart is not proud, O LORD, my eyes are not haughty. I do not aspire to great things or matters too lofty for me.

131:2

אִםif לֹאnot שִׁוִּיתִיstilled וְדוֹמַמְתִּיand quieted נַפְשִׁיmy soul כְּגָמֻלlike a weaned עֲלֵיon אִמּוֹhis mother כַּגָּמֻלlike a weaned עָלַיon me נַפְשִׁיsoul within me

Surely I have stilled and quieted my soul; like a weaned child with his mother, like a weaned child is my soul within me.

131:3

יַחֵלput your hope יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael אֶלto יְהוָהLORD מֵעַתָּהboth now וְעַדand to עוֹלָםforevermore

O Israel, put your hope in the LORD, both now and forevermore.
