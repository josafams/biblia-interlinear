Psalms 56

Trust in God in the midst of fear

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. To the tune of “A Dove on Distant Oaks.” A Miktam of David, when the Philistines seized him in Gath

56:1

חָנֵּנִיmerciful to me אֱלֹהִיםGod כִּיfor שְׁאָפַנִיhounding me אֱנוֹשׁmen כָּלall הַיּוֹםday לֹחֵםattack יִלְחָצֵנִיpress

Be merciful to me, O God, for men are hounding me; all day they press their attack.

56:2

שָׁאֲפוּpursue שׁוֹרְרַיenemies me כָּלall הַיּוֹםday long כִּיfor רַבִּיםmany לֹחֲמִיםassail לִיto me מָרוֹםproudly

My enemies pursue me all day long, for many proudly assail me.

56:3

יוֹםday אִירָאafraid אֲנִיam I אֵלֶיךָto you אֶבְטָחtrust

When I am afraid, I put my trust in You.

56:4

בֵּאלֹהִיםin God אֲהַלֵּלwhose דְּבָרוֹword בֵּאלֹהִיםin God בָּטַחְתִּיtrust לֹאnot אִירָאafraid מַהwhat יַּעֲשֶׂהdo בָשָׂרflesh לִיto me

In God, whose word I praise— in God I trust. I will not be afraid. What can man do to me?

56:5

כָּלall הַיּוֹםday long דְּבָרַיmy words יְעַצֵּבוּtwist עָלַיon me כָּלall מַחְשְׁבֹתָםtheir thoughts are לָרָעon my demise

All day long they twist my words; all their thoughts are on my demise.

56:6

יָגוּרוּconspire יִצְפּוֹנוּconspire they lurk הֵמָּהthey עֲקֵבַיmy steps יִשְׁמֹרוּwatch כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had קִוּוּwait נַפְשִׁיmy life

They conspire, they lurk, they watch my steps while they wait to take my life.

56:7

עַלin אָוֶןsin פַּלֶּטescape לָמוֹto them בְּאַףin your anger עַמִּיםnations הוֹרֵדcast down אֱלֹהִיםGod

In spite of such sin, will they escape? In Your anger, O God, cast down the nations.

56:8

נֹדִיaccount of my סָפַרְתָּהwanderings אָתָּהyou שִׂימָהput דִמְעָתִיmy tears בְנֹאדֶךָin your bottle הֲלֹאare they not בְּסִפְרָתֶךָin your book

You have taken account of my wanderings. Put my tears in Your bottle— are they not in Your book?

56:9

אָזthen יָשׁוּבוּwill אוֹיְבַיmy enemies אָחוֹרretreat בְּיוֹםon the day אֶקְרָאcry זֶהthis יָדַעְתִּיknow כִּיthat אֱלֹהִיםGod לִיto me

Then my enemies will retreat on the day I cry for help. By this I will know that God is on my side.

56:10

בֵּאלֹהִיםin God אֲהַלֵּלwhose דָּבָרword בַּיהוָהin the LORD אֲהַלֵּלwhose דָּבָרword

In God, whose word I praise, in the LORD, whose word I praise,

56:11

בֵּאלֹהִיםin God בָּטַחְתִּיtrust לֹאnot אִירָאafraid מַהwhat יַּעֲשֶׂהdo אָדָםman לִיto me

in God I trust; I will not be afraid. What can man do to me?

56:12

עָלַיupon me אֱלֹהִיםGod נְדָרֶיךָyour vows אֲשַׁלֵּםrender תּוֹדֹתthank offerings לָךְto you

Your vows are upon me, O God; I will render thank offerings to You.

56:13

כִּיfor הִצַּלְתָּdelivered נַפְשִׁיmy soul מִמָּוֶתfrom death הֲלֹאnot רַגְלַיmy feet מִדֶּחִיfrom stumbling לְהִתְהַלֵּךְthat I may walk לִפְנֵיbefore אֱלֹהִיםGod בְּאוֹרin the light הַחַיִּיםof life

For You have delivered my soul from death, and my feet from stumbling, that I may walk before God in the light of life.
