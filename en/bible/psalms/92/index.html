Psalms 92

Praise for the Sabbath day

A Psalm. A song for the Sabbath day

92:1

טוֹבgood לְהֹדוֹתto praise לַיהוָהthe LORD וּלְזַמֵּרand to sing praises לְשִׁמְךָto your name O עֶלְיוֹןMost high

It is good to praise the LORD, and to sing praises to Your name, O Most High,

92:2

לְהַגִּידto proclaim בַּבֹּקֶרin the morning חַסְדֶּךָyour loving devotion וֶאֱמוּנָתְךָand your faithfulness בַּלֵּילוֹתat night

to proclaim Your loving devotion in the morning and Your faithfulness at night

92:3

עֲלֵיon עָשׂוֹרten וַעֲלֵיand on נָבֶלstringed harp עֲלֵיon הִגָּיוֹןmelody בְּכִנּוֹרthe lyre

with the ten-stringed harp and the melody of the lyre.

92:4

כִּיfor שִׂמַּחְתַּנִיme glad יְהוָהLORD בְּפָעֳלֶךָby your deeds בְּמַעֲשֵׂיat the works יָדֶיךָyour hands אֲרַנֵּןjoy

For You, O LORD, have made me glad by Your deeds; I sing for joy at the works of Your hands.

92:5

מַהhow גָּדְלוּgreat מַעֲשֶׂיךָyour works יְהוָהLORD מְאֹדvery עָמְקוּdeep מַחְשְׁבֹתֶיךָyour thoughts

How great are Your works, O LORD, how deep are Your thoughts!

92:6

אִישׁman בַּעַרsenseless לֹאnot יֵדָעknow וּכְסִילand a fool לֹאnot יָבִיןunderstand אֶת[obj] זֹאתthis

A senseless man does not know, and a fool does not understand,

92:7

בִּפְרֹחַsprout רְשָׁעִיםwicked כְּמוֹlike עֵשֶׂבgrass וַיָּצִיצוּflourish כָּלall פֹּעֲלֵיevildoers אָוֶןevildoers לְהִשָּׁמְדָםdestroyed עֲדֵיforever עַדuntil

that though the wicked sprout like grass, and all evildoers flourish, they will be forever destroyed.

92:8

וְאַתָּהbut you מָרוֹםexalted לְעֹלָםforever יְהוָהLORD

But You, O LORD, are exalted forever!

92:9

כִּיfor הִנֵּהsurely אֹיְבֶיךָyour enemies O יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor הִנֵּהbehold אֹיְבֶיךָyour enemies יֹאבֵדוּperish יִתְפָּרְדוּscattered כָּלall פֹּעֲלֵיevildoers אָוֶןevildoers

For surely Your enemies, O LORD, surely Your enemies will perish; all evildoers will be scattered.

92:10

וַתָּרֶםexalted כִּרְאֵיםwild ox קַרְנִיmy horn בַּלֹּתִיfine בְּשֶׁמֶןwith oil רַעֲנָןanointed

But You have exalted my horn like that of a wild ox; with fine oil I have been anointed.

92:11

וַתַּבֵּטsee עֵינִיmy eyes בְּשׁוּרָיthe downfall of my בַּקָּמִיםfoes עָלַיon me מְרֵעִיםwicked תִּשְׁמַעְנָהhear אָזְנָיears

My eyes see the downfall of my enemies; my ears hear the wailing of my wicked foes.

92:12

צַדִּיקrighteous כַּתָּמָרlike a palm tree יִפְרָחlike כְּאֶרֶזlike a cedar בַּלְּבָנוֹןin Lebanon יִשְׂגֶּהflourish

The righteous will flourish like a palm tree, and grow like a cedar in Lebanon.

92:13

שְׁתוּלִיםplanted בְּבֵיתin the house יְהוָהLORD בְּחַצְרוֹתin the courts אֱלֹהֵינוּour God יַפְרִיחוּflourish

Planted in the house of the LORD, they will flourish in the courts of our God.

92:14

עוֹדstill יְנוּבוּןfruit בְּשֵׂיבָהin old age דְּשֵׁנִיםhealthy וְרַעֲנַנִּיםand green יִהְיוּremain

In old age they will still bear fruit; healthy and green they will remain,

92:15

לְהַגִּידto proclaim כִּיfor יָשָׁרupright יְהוָהLORD צוּרִיmy rock וְלֹאno עַוְלָתָהis unrighteousness בּוֹin him

to proclaim, “The LORD is upright; He is my Rock, and in Him there is no unrighteousness.”
