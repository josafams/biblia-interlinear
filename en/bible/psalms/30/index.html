Psalms 30

Praise for healing and restoration

Open in the interlinear reader

A Psalm. A song for the dedication of the temple. Of David

30:1

אֲרוֹמִמְךָexalt you יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor דִלִּיתָנִיlifted me וְלֹאand have not שִׂמַּחְתָּrejoice אֹיְבַיmy foes לִיto

I will exalt You, O LORD, for You have lifted me up and have not allowed my foes to rejoice over me.

30:2

יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהָיmy God שִׁוַּעְתִּיcried אֵלֶיךָto you וַתִּרְפָּאֵנִיand you healed me

O LORD my God, I cried to You for help, and You healed me.

30:3

יְהוָהLORD הֶעֱלִיתָup מִןfrom שְׁאוֹלSheol נַפְשִׁיpulled me חִיִּיתַנִיspared me מִיָּרְדִיfrom descending בוֹרpit

O LORD, You pulled me up from Sheol; You spared me from descending into the Pit.

30:4

זַמְּרוּsing לַיהוָהto the LORD חֲסִידָיוhis saints וְהוֹדוּand praise לְזֵכֶרname קָדְשׁוֹhis holy

Sing to the LORD, O you His saints, and praise His holy name.

30:5

כִּיfor רֶגַעfleeting בְּאַפּוֹhis anger חַיִּיםlifetime בִּרְצוֹנוֹhis favor בָּעֶרֶבthe night יָלִיןstay בֶּכִיweeping וְלַבֹּקֶרin the morning רִנָּהjoy

For His anger is fleeting, but His favor lasts a lifetime. Weeping may stay the night, but joy comes in the morning.

30:6

וַאֲנִיI אָמַרְתִּיsaid בְשַׁלְוִיin prosperity בַּלnever אֶמּוֹטshaken לְעוֹלָםto forever

In prosperity I said, “I will never be shaken.”

30:7

יְהוָהLORD בִּרְצוֹנְךָfavored me you הֶעֱמַדְתָּהstand לְהַרְרִיmy mountain עֹזstrong הִסְתַּרְתָּhid פָנֶיךָyour face הָיִיתִיwas נִבְהָלdismayed

O LORD, You favored me; You made my mountain stand strong. When You hid Your face, I was dismayed.

30:8

אֵלֶיךָto you יְהוָהLORD אֶקְרָאcalled וְאֶלand to אֲדֹנָיmy Lord אֶתְחַנָּןmercy

To You, O LORD, I called, and I begged my Lord for mercy:

30:9

מַהwhat בֶּצַעgain בְּדָמִיin my bloodshed בְּרִדְתִּיin my descent אֶלto שָׁחַתpit הֲיוֹדְךָpraise you עָפָרdust הֲיַגִּידwill it proclaim אֲמִתֶּךָyour faithfulness

“What gain is there in my bloodshed, in my descent to the Pit? Will the dust praise You? Will it proclaim Your faithfulness?

30:10

שְׁמַעhear יְהוָהLORD וְחָנֵּנִיand have mercy O יְהוָהLORD הֱיֵהbe עֹזֵרhelper לִיto me

Hear me, O LORD, and have mercy; O LORD, be my helper.”

30:11

הָפַכְתָּturned מִסְפְּדִיmy mourning לְמָחוֹלinto dancing לִיto me פִּתַּחְתָּpeeled שַׂקִּיmy sackcloth וַתְּאַזְּרֵנִיand clothed me שִׂמְחָהjoy

You turned my mourning into dancing; You peeled off my sackcloth and clothed me with joy,

30:12

לְמַעַןmay יְזַמֶּרְךָsing your praises כָבוֹדglory וְלֹאand not יִדֹּםsilent יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהַיmy God I לְעוֹלָםforever אוֹדֶךָּgive thanks

that my heart may sing Your praises and not be silent. O LORD my God, I will give thanks forever.
