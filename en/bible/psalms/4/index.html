Psalms 4

Peace to sleep in the midst of distress

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. With stringed instruments. A Psalm of David

4:1

בְּקָרְאִיme when I call עֲנֵנִיanswer אֱלֹהֵיGod צִדְקִיmy righteousness בַּצָּרdistress הִרְחַבְתָּrelieved לִּיto me חָנֵּנִיshow me grace וּשְׁמַעand hear תְּפִלָּתִיmy prayer

Answer me when I call, O God of my righteousness! You have relieved my distress; show me grace and hear my prayer.

4:2

בְּנֵיsons אִישׁmen עַדuntil מֶהhow כְבוֹדִיmy honor לִכְלִמָּהmaligned תֶּאֱהָבוּןlove רִיקvanity תְּבַקְשׁוּseek after כָזָבlies סֶלָהSelah

How long, O men, will my honor be maligned? How long will you love vanity and seek after lies? Selah

4:3

וּדְעוּknow כִּיthat הִפְלָהbut יְהוָהLORD חָסִידgodly לוֹfor himself יְהוָהLORD יִשְׁמַעhears בְּקָרְאִיwhen I call אֵלָיוto him

Know that the LORD has set apart the godly for Himself; the LORD hears when I call to Him.

4:4

רִגְזוּangry וְאַלyet do not תֶּחֱטָאוּsin אִמְרוּsay בִלְבַבְכֶםheart עַלon מִשְׁכַּבְכֶםbed your וְדֹמּוּand be still סֶלָהSelah

Be angry, yet do not sin; on your bed, search your heart and be still. Selah

4:5

זִבְחוּoffer זִבְחֵיsacrifices צֶדֶקrighteous וּבִטְחוּand trust אֶלto יְהוָהLORD

Offer the sacrifices of the righteous and trust in the LORD.

4:6

רַבִּיםmany אֹמְרִיםask מִיwho can יַרְאֵנוּshow us טוֹבgood נְסָהshine עָלֵינוּupon us אוֹרlight פָּנֶיךָyour face יְהוָהLORD

Many ask, “Who can show us the good?” Shine the light of Your face upon us, O LORD.

4:7

נָתַתָּהgave שִׂמְחָהjoy בְלִבִּיmy heart with מֵעֵתthan when דְּגָנָםgrain וְתִירוֹשָׁםand new wine רָבּוּabound

You have filled my heart with more joy than when grain and new wine abound.

4:8

בְּשָׁלוֹםin peace יַחְדָּוtogether אֶשְׁכְּבָהlie וְאִישָׁןand sleep כִּיfor אַתָּהyou יְהוָהLORD לְבָדָדalone לָבֶטַחin safety תּוֹשִׁיבֵנִיme dwell

I will lie down and sleep in peace, for You alone, O LORD, make me dwell in safety.
