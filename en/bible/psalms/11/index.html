Psalms 11

Refuge in the LORD when the foundations shake

Open in the interlinear reader

11:1

לַמְנַצֵּחַfor the choirmaster לְדָוִדof David בַּיהוָהin the LORD חָסִיתִיrefuge אֵיךְhow תֹּאמְרוּsay לְנַפְשִׁיto my soul נוּדִיflee הַרְכֶםyour mountain צִפּוֹרbird

For the choirmaster. Of David. In the LORD I take refuge. How then can you say to me: “Flee like a bird to your mountain!

11:2

כִּיfor הִנֵּהbehold הָרְשָׁעִיםthe wicked יִדְרְכוּןbend קֶשֶׁתbows כּוֹנְנוּset חִצָּםtheir arrow עַלon יֶתֶרstring לִירוֹתto shoot בְּמוֹwith אֹפֶלshadows לְיִשְׁרֵיat the upright לֵבheart

For behold, the wicked bend their bows. They set their arrow on the string to shoot from the shadows at the upright in heart.

11:3

כִּיif הַשָּׁתוֹתthe foundations יֵהָרֵסוּןdestroyed צַדִּיקrighteous מַהwhat פָּעָלdo

If the foundations are destroyed, what can the righteous do?”

11:4

יְהוָהLORD בְּהֵיכַלtemple קָדְשׁוֹhis holy יְהוָהLORD בַּשָּׁמַיִםon his heavenly כִּסְאוֹthrone his עֵינָיוeyes are יֶחֱזוּwatching closely עַפְעַפָּיוwatching יִבְחֲנוּexamine בְּנֵיsons אָדָםmen

The LORD is in His holy temple; the LORD is on His heavenly throne. His eyes are watching closely; they examine the sons of men.

11:5

יְהוָהLORD צַדִּיקrighteous יִבְחָןtests וְרָשָׁעand the wicked וְאֹהֵבlover חָמָסviolence שָׂנְאָהhates נַפְשׁוֹhis soul

The LORD tests the righteous and the wicked; His soul hates the lover of violence.

11:6

יַמְטֵרrain עַלon רְשָׁעִיםwicked פַּחִיםfiery אֵשׁcoals וְגָפְרִיתand sulfur וְרוּחַwind זִלְעָפוֹתscorching מְנָתportion כּוֹסָםbe their

On the wicked He will rain down fiery coals and sulfur; a scorching wind will be their portion.

11:7

כִּיfor צַדִּיקrighteous יְהוָהLORD צְדָקוֹתjustice אָהֵבloves יָשָׁרupright יֶחֱזוּsee פָנֵימוֹhis face

For the LORD is righteous; He loves justice. The upright will see His face.
