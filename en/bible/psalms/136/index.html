Psalms 136

A litany of thanks for creation and history

Open in the interlinear reader

136:1

הוֹדוּgive thanks לַיהוָהto the LORD כִּיfor טוֹבgood כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good. His loving devotion endures forever.

136:2

הוֹדוּgive thanks לֵאלֹהֵיto the God הָאֱלֹהִיםgods כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

Give thanks to the God of gods. His loving devotion endures forever.

136:3

הוֹדוּgive thanks לַאֲדֹנֵיto the Lord הָאֲדֹנִיםof lords כִּיfor לְעֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

Give thanks to the Lord of lords. His loving devotion endures forever.

136:4

לְעֹשֵׂהdoes נִפְלָאוֹתwonders גְּדֹלוֹתgreat לְבַדּוֹalone כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹloving devotion

He alone does great wonders. His loving devotion endures forever.

136:5

לְעֹשֵׂהmade הַשָּׁמַיִםthe heavens בִּתְבוּנָהinsight כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

By His insight He made the heavens. His loving devotion endures forever.

136:6

לְרֹקַעspread הָאָרֶץthe earth עַלupon הַמָּיִםthe waters כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

He spread out the earth upon the waters. His loving devotion endures forever.

136:7

לְעֹשֵׂהmade אוֹרִיםlights גְּדֹלִיםgreat כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

He made the great lights— His loving devotion endures forever.

136:8

אֶת[obj] הַשֶּׁמֶשׁthe sun לְמֶמְשֶׁלֶתto rule בַּיּוֹםthe day כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

the sun to rule the day, His loving devotion endures forever.

136:9

אֶת[obj] הַיָּרֵחַthe moon וְכוֹכָבִיםand stars לְמֶמְשְׁלוֹתto govern בַּלָּיְלָהthe night כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

the moon and stars to govern the night. His loving devotion endures forever.

136:10

לְמַכֵּהstruck מִצְרַיִםEgypt בִּבְכוֹרֵיהֶםdown the firstborn כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

He struck down the firstborn of Egypt His loving devotion endures forever.

136:11

וַיּוֹצֵאand brought יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael מִתּוֹכָםfrom among them כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

and brought Israel out from among them His loving devotion endures forever.

136:12

בְּיָדhand חֲזָקָהmighty וּבִזְרוֹעַand an arm נְטוּיָהoutstretched כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

with a mighty hand and an outstretched arm. His loving devotion endures forever.

136:13

לְגֹזֵרdivided יַםsea סוּףRed sea לִגְזָרִיםin two כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

He divided the Red Sea in two His loving devotion endures forever.

136:14

וְהֶעֱבִירand led יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael בְּתוֹכוֹthrough the midst his כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹloving devotion

and led Israel through the midst, His loving devotion endures forever.

136:15

וְנִעֵרbut swept פַּרְעֹהPharaoh וְחֵילוֹand his army בְיַםsea סוּףRed sea כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

but swept Pharaoh and his army into the Red Sea. His loving devotion endures forever.

136:16

לְמוֹלִיךְled עַמּוֹhis people בַּמִּדְבָּרthrough the wilderness כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

He led His people through the wilderness. His loving devotion endures forever.

136:17

לְמַכֵּהstruck מְלָכִיםkings גְּדֹלִיםgreat כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

He struck down great kings His loving devotion endures forever.

136:18

וַיַּהֲרֹגand slaughtered מְלָכִיםkings אַדִּירִיםmighty כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

and slaughtered mighty kings— His loving devotion endures forever.

136:19

לְסִיחוֹןSihon מֶלֶךְking הָאֱמֹרִיthe Amorites כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

Sihon king of the Amorites His loving devotion endures forever.

136:20

וּלְעוֹגand Og מֶלֶךְking הַבָּשָׁןof Bashan כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

and Og king of Bashan— His loving devotion endures forever.

136:21

וְנָתַןand he gave אַרְצָםtheir land לְנַחֲלָהas an inheritance כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

and He gave their land as an inheritance, His loving devotion endures forever.

136:22

נַחֲלָהheritage לְיִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael עַבְדּוֹhis servant כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

a heritage to His servant Israel. His loving devotion endures forever.

136:23

שֶׁבְּשִׁפְלֵנוּhe remembered us זָכַרremembered לָנוּto us כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

He remembered us in our low estate His loving devotion endures forever.

136:24

וַיִּפְרְקֵנוּand freed us מִצָּרֵינוּfrom our enemies כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

and freed us from our enemies. His loving devotion endures forever.

136:25

נֹתֵןgives לֶחֶםfood לְכָלto every בָּשָׂרcreature כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

He gives food to every creature. His loving devotion endures forever.

136:26

הוֹדוּgive thanks לְאֵלto the God הַשָּׁמָיִםof heaven כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

Give thanks to the God of heaven! His loving devotion endures forever.
