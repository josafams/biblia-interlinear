Psalms 1

The two ways: the righteous and the wicked

Open in the interlinear reader

1:1

אַשְׁרֵיblessed הָאִישׁthe man אֲשֶׁרwho לֹאnot הָלַךְwalk בַּעֲצַתin the counsel רְשָׁעִיםwicked וּבְדֶרֶךְon the path חַטָּאִיםsinners לֹאnot עָמָדset וּבְמוֹשַׁבin the seat לֵצִיםmockers לֹאor יָשָׁבsit

Blessed is the man who does not walk in the counsel of the wicked, or set foot on the path of sinners, or sit in the seat of mockers.

1:2

כִּיbut אִםif בְּתוֹרַתin the law יְהוָהLORD חֶפְצוֹhis delight וּבְתוֹרָתוֹand on his law יֶהְגֶּהmeditates יוֹמָםday וָלָיְלָהand night

But his delight is in the Law of the LORD, and on His law he meditates day and night.

1:3

וְהָיָהand be כְּעֵץlike a tree שָׁתוּלplanted עַלon פַּלְגֵיstreams מָיִםwater אֲשֶׁרthat פִּרְיוֹits fruit יִתֵּןgive בְּעִתּוֹin season whose וְעָלֵהוּleaf does לֹאnot יִבּוֹלwither וְכֹלall אֲשֶׁרwho יַעֲשֶׂהdoes יַצְלִיחַprospers

He is like a tree planted by streams of water, yielding its fruit in season, whose leaf does not wither, and who prospers in all he does.

1:4

לֹאnot כֵןso הָרְשָׁעִיםthe wicked כִּיfor אִםif כַּמֹּץlike chaff אֲשֶׁרthat תִּדְּפֶנּוּdriven off by רוּחַwind

Not so the wicked! For they are like chaff driven off by the wind.

1:5

עַלon כֵּןtherefore לֹאnot יָקֻמוּstand רְשָׁעִיםwicked בַּמִּשְׁפָּטin the judgment וְחַטָּאִיםnor sinners בַּעֲדַתin the assembly צַדִּיקִיםrighteous

Therefore the wicked will not stand in the judgment, nor sinners in the assembly of the righteous.

1:6

כִּיfor יוֹדֵעַthe guards יְהוָהLORD דֶּרֶךְpath צַדִּיקִיםrighteous וְדֶרֶךְbut the way רְשָׁעִיםwicked תֹּאבֵדperish

For the LORD guards the path of the righteous, but the way of the wicked will perish.
