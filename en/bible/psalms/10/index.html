Psalms 10

A complaint against the arrogance of the wicked

Open in the interlinear reader

10:1

לָמָהwhy יְהוָהLORD תַּעֲמֹדstand בְּרָחוֹקfar תַּעְלִיםhide לְעִתּוֹתin times בַּצָּרָהtrouble

Why, O LORD, do You stand far off? Why do You hide in times of trouble?

10:2

בְּגַאֲוַתin pride רָשָׁעwicked יִדְלַקpursue עָנִיneedy יִתָּפְשׂוּcaught בִּמְזִמּוֹתin the schemes זוּthey חָשָׁבוּdevise

In pride the wicked pursue the needy; let them be caught in the schemes they devise.

10:3

כִּיfor הִלֵּלboasts רָשָׁעwicked עַלin תַּאֲוַתcravings נַפְשׁוֹhis heart וּבֹצֵעַgreedy and בֵּרֵךְblesses נִאֵץreviles יְהוָהLORD

For the wicked man boasts in the cravings of his heart; he blesses the greedy and reviles the LORD.

10:4

רָשָׁעwicked כְּגֹבַהּin his pride אַפּוֹman does בַּלnot יִדְרֹשׁseek אֵיןno אֱלֹהִיםGod כָּלall מְזִמּוֹתָיוhis schemes

In his pride the wicked man does not seek Him; in all his schemes there is no God.

10:5

יָחִילוּsecure דְרָכָיוhis ways בְּכָלat all עֵתtimes מָרוֹםlofty מִשְׁפָּטֶיךָyour judgments מִנֶּגְדּוֹfrom distance him כָּלall צוֹרְרָיוhis foes יָפִיחַsneers בָּהֶםin them

He is secure in his ways at all times; Your lofty judgments are far from him; he sneers at all his foes.

10:6

אָמַרsays בְּלִבּוֹto himself בַּלnot אֶמּוֹטmoved לְדֹרfrom age וָדֹרto age אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot בְרָעdistress

He says to himself, “I will not be moved; from age to age I am free of distress.”

10:7

אָלָהcursing פִּיהוּhis mouth מָלֵאfull וּמִרְמוֹתdeceit וָתֹךְand violence תַּחַתunder לְשׁוֹנוֹhis tongue עָמָלtrouble וָאָוֶןand malice

His mouth is full of cursing, deceit, and violence; trouble and malice are under his tongue.

10:8

יֵשֵׁבwait בְּמַאְרַבin near חֲצֵרִיםvillages בַּמִּסְתָּרִיםin ambush יַהֲרֹגslays נָקִיinnocent עֵינָיוhis eyes watch לְחֵלְכָהin stealth יִצְפֹּנוּhelpless

He lies in wait near the villages; in ambush he slays the innocent; his eyes watch in stealth for the helpless.

10:9

יֶאֱרֹבlies wait בַּמִּסְתָּרin the ambush כְּאַרְיֵהlike a lion בְסֻכֹּהin a thicket he יֶאֱרֹבlurks לַחֲטוֹףlurks to seize עָנִיoppressed יַחְטֹףcatches עָנִיlowly בְּמָשְׁכוֹin draw him בְרִשְׁתּוֹhis net

He lies in wait like a lion in a thicket; he lurks to seize the oppressed; he catches the lowly in his net.

10:10

יִדְכֶּהcrushed יָשֹׁחַhapless וְנָפַלfall בַּעֲצוּמָיוprey to his חֵילstrength כָּאִיםstrength

They are crushed and beaten down; the hapless fall prey to his strength.

10:11

אָמַרsays בְּלִבּוֹto himself שָׁכַחforgotten אֵלGod הִסְתִּירhides פָּנָיוhis face בַּלnever רָאָהsees לָנֶצַחto forever

He says to himself, “God has forgotten; He hides His face and never sees.”

10:12

קוּמָהarise יְהוָהLORD אֵלGod נְשָׂאlift יָדֶךָyour hand אַלdo not תִּשְׁכַּחforget עֲנָוִיםhelpless

Arise, O LORD! Lift up Your hand, O God! Do not forget the helpless.

10:13

עַלon מֶהwhy נִאֵץhas רָשָׁעwicked אֱלֹהִיםGod אָמַרsays בְּלִבּוֹto himself לֹאnever תִּדְרֹשׁcall

Why has the wicked man renounced God? He says to himself, “You will never call me to account.”

10:14

רָאִתָהregarded כִּיbut אַתָּהyou עָמָלtrouble וָכַעַסand grief תַּבִּיטsee לָתֵתto repay בְּיָדֶךָby your hand עָלֶיךָto you יַעֲזֹבvictim חֵלֶכָהentrusts יָתוֹםfatherless אַתָּהyou are הָיִיתָare the עוֹזֵרhelper

But You have regarded trouble and grief; You see to repay it by Your hand. The victim entrusts himself to You; You are the helper of the fatherless.

10:15

שְׁבֹרbreak זְרוֹעַarm רָשָׁעwicked וָרָעand evildoer תִּדְרוֹשׁcall רִשְׁעוֹhis wickedness בַלbe תִּמְצָאfound

Break the arm of the wicked and evildoer; call him to account for his wickedness until none is left to be found.

10:16

יְהוָהLORD מֶלֶךְking עוֹלָםforever וָעֶדand ever אָבְדוּperish גוֹיִםnations מֵאַרְצוֹfrom his land

The LORD is King forever and ever; the nations perish from His land.

10:17

תַּאֲוַתdesire עֲנָוִיםhumble שָׁמַעְתָּheard יְהוָהLORD תָּכִיןstrengthen לִבָּםtheir hearts תַּקְשִׁיבincline אָזְנֶךָyour ear

You have heard, O LORD, the desire of the humble; You will strengthen their hearts. You will incline Your ear,

10:18

לִשְׁפֹּטto vindicate יָתוֹםfatherless וָדָךְand oppressed בַּלno יוֹסִיףmore עוֹדmore לַעֲרֹץstrike terror אֱנוֹשׁmen מִןof הָאָרֶץthe earth

to vindicate the fatherless and oppressed, that the men of the earth may strike terror no more.
