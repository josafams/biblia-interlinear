Psalms 128

The blessing of a God-fearing home

Open in the interlinear reader

128:1

שִׁירsong הַמַּעֲלוֹתof ascents אַשְׁרֵיblessed כָּלall יְרֵאfear יְהוָהLORD הַהֹלֵךְwho walk בִּדְרָכָיוin his ways

A song of ascents. Blessed are all who fear the LORD, who walk in His ways!

128:2

יְגִיעַlabor כַּפֶּיךָblessings כִּיfor תֹאכֵלeat אַשְׁרֶיךָfruit of your וְטוֹבand prosperity לָךְto you

For when you eat the fruit of your labor, blessings and prosperity will be yours.

Your wife will be like a fruitful vine flourishing within your house, your sons like olive shoots sitting around your table.

128:4

הִנֵּהbehold כִיfor כֵןthis יְבֹרַךְblessing גָּבֶרman יְרֵאfears יְהוָהLORD

In this way indeed shall blessing come to the man who fears the LORD.

128:5

יְבָרֶכְךָbless you יְהוָהLORD מִצִּיּוֹןfrom Zion וּרְאֵהmay see בְּטוּבthe prosperity יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem כֹּלall יְמֵיdays חַיֶּיךָyour life

May the LORD bless you from Zion, that you may see the prosperity of Jerusalem all the days of your life,

128:6

וּרְאֵהmay see בָנִיםchildren’s לְבָנֶיךָyour children שָׁלוֹםpeace עַלupon יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

that you may see your children’s children. Peace be upon Israel!
