Psalms 106

Israel's unfaithfulness and divine patience

Open in the interlinear reader

106:1

הַלְלוּHallelujah יָהּHallelujah הוֹדוּgive thanks לַיהוָהto the LORD כִּיfor טוֹבgood כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

Hallelujah! Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good; His loving devotion endures forever.

106:2

מִיwho can יְמַלֵּלdescribe גְּבוּרוֹתmighty יְהוָהLORD יַשְׁמִיעַproclaim כָּלor תְּהִלָּתוֹhis praise

Who can describe the mighty acts of the LORD or fully proclaim His praise?

106:3

אַשְׁרֵיblessed שֹׁמְרֵיuphold מִשְׁפָּטjustice עֹשֵׂהpractice צְדָקָהrighteousness בְכָלat all עֵתtimes

Blessed are those who uphold justice, who practice righteousness at all times.

106:4

זָכְרֵנִיremember me יְהוָהLORD בִּרְצוֹןin your favor עַמֶּךָyour people פָּקְדֵנִיvisit me בִּישׁוּעָתֶךָwith your salvation

Remember me, O LORD, in Your favor to Your people; visit me with Your salvation,

106:5

לִרְאוֹתmay see בְּטוֹבַתthe prosperity בְּחִירֶיךָyour chosen לִשְׂמֹחַand rejoice בְּשִׂמְחַתin the gladness גּוֹיֶךָyour nation לְהִתְהַלֵּלand give glory עִםwith נַחֲלָתֶךָyour inheritance

that I may see the prosperity of Your chosen ones, and rejoice in the gladness of Your nation, and give glory with Your inheritance.

106:6

חָטָאנוּsinned עִםwith אֲבוֹתֵינוּour fathers we הֶעֱוִינוּacted הִרְשָׁעְנוּwickedly

We have sinned like our fathers; we have done wrong and acted wickedly.

106:7

אֲבוֹתֵינוּour fathers בְמִצְרַיִםin Egypt לֹאnot הִשְׂכִּילוּgrasp נִפְלְאוֹתֶיךָyour wonders לֹאor זָכְרוּremember אֶת[obj] רֹבabundant חֲסָדֶיךָyour kindness וַיַּמְרוּbut they rebelled עַלon יָםsea בְּיַםsea סוּףRed sea

Our fathers in Egypt did not grasp Your wonders or remember Your abundant kindness; but they rebelled by the sea, there at the Red Sea.

106:8

וַיּוֹשִׁיעֵםyet he saved them לְמַעַןsake שְׁמוֹhis name לְהוֹדִיעַknown אֶת[obj] גְּבוּרָתוֹhis power

Yet He saved them for the sake of His name, to make His power known.

106:9

וַיִּגְעַרhe rebuked בְּיַםthe Red סוּףsea וַיֶּחֱרָבand it dried וַיּוֹלִיכֵםhe led them בַּתְּהֹמוֹתthrough the depths כַּמִּדְבָּרas through a desert

He rebuked the Red Sea, and it dried up; He led them through the depths as through a desert.

106:10

וַיּוֹשִׁיעֵםhe saved them מִיַּדfrom the hand שׂוֹנֵאhated וַיִּגְאָלֵםhe redeemed them מִיַּדfrom the hand אוֹיֵבenemy

He saved them from the hand that hated them; He redeemed them from the hand of the enemy.

106:11

וַיְכַסּוּcovered מַיִםwaters צָרֵיהֶםtheir foes אֶחָדone מֵהֶםof them לֹאnot נוֹתָרremained

The waters covered their foes; not one of them remained.

106:12

וַיַּאֲמִינוּthen they believed בִדְבָרָיוhis promises and יָשִׁירוּsang תְּהִלָּתוֹhis praise

Then they believed His promises and sang His praise.

106:13

מִהֲרוּsoon שָׁכְחוּforgot מַעֲשָׂיוhis works and לֹאnot חִכּוּwait לַעֲצָתוֹfor his counsel

Yet they soon forgot His works and failed to wait for His counsel.

106:14

וַיִּתְאַוּוּthey craved תַאֲוָהintensely בַּמִּדְבָּרin the wilderness וַיְנַסּוּand tested אֵלGod בִּישִׁימוֹןin the desert

They craved intensely in the wilderness and tested God in the desert.

106:15

וַיִּתֵּןso he granted לָהֶםto them שֶׁאֱלָתָםrequest וַיְשַׁלַּחbut sent רָזוֹןwasting disease בְּנַפְשָׁםdisease upon them

So He granted their request, but sent a wasting disease upon them.

106:16

וַיְקַנְאוּthey envied לְמֹשֶׁהMoses בַּמַּחֲנֶהin the camp לְאַהֲרֹןas Aaron קְדוֹשׁholy יְהוָהLORD

In the camp they envied Moses, as well as Aaron, the holy one of the LORD.

106:17

תִּפְתַּחopened אֶרֶץearth וַתִּבְלַעand swallowed דָּתָןDathan וַתְּכַסit covered עַלon עֲדַתassembly אֲבִירָםAbiram

The earth opened up and swallowed Dathan; it covered the assembly of Abiram.

106:18

וַתִּבְעַרthen blazed אֵשׁfire בַּעֲדָתָםthrough their company לֶהָבָהflames תְּלַהֵטconsumed רְשָׁעִיםwicked

Then fire blazed through their company; flames consumed the wicked.

106:19

יַעֲשׂוּmade עֵגֶלcalf בְּחֹרֵבat Horeb וַיִּשְׁתַּחֲווּand worshiped לְמַסֵּכָהa molten image

At Horeb they made a calf and worshiped a molten image.

106:20

וַיָּמִירוּthey exchanged אֶת[obj] כְּבוֹדָםtheir glory בְּתַבְנִיתfor the image שׁוֹרox אֹכֵלeating עֵשֶׂבgrass

They exchanged their Glory for the image of a grass-eating ox.

106:21

שָׁכְחוּforgot אֵלGod מוֹשִׁיעָםtheir Savior עֹשֶׂהdid גְדֹלוֹתgreat בְּמִצְרָיִםin Egypt

They forgot God their Savior, who did great things in Egypt,

106:22

נִפְלָאוֹתwondrous works בְּאֶרֶץin the land חָםHam נוֹרָאוֹתawesome deeds עַלon יַםRed סוּףsea

wondrous works in the land of Ham, and awesome deeds by the Red Sea.

106:23

וַיֹּאמֶרso he said לְהַשְׁמִידָםwould destroy them לוּלֵיhad not מֹשֶׁהMoses בְחִירוֹhis chosen one עָמַדstood בַּפֶּרֶץin the breach לְפָנָיוbefore him לְהָשִׁיבto divert חֲמָתוֹhis wrath מֵהַשְׁחִיתfrom destroying

So He said He would destroy them— had not Moses His chosen one stood before Him in the breach to divert His wrath from destroying them.

106:24

וַיִּמְאֲסוּthey despised בְּאֶרֶץthe land חֶמְדָּהpleasant לֹאnot הֶאֱמִינוּbelieve לִדְבָרוֹhis promise

They despised the pleasant land; they did not believe His promise.

106:25

וַיֵּרָגְנוּthey grumbled בְאָהֳלֵיהֶםin their tents לֹאnot שָׁמְעוּlisten בְּקוֹלto the voice יְהוָהLORD

They grumbled in their tents and did not listen to the voice of the LORD.

106:26

וַיִּשָּׂאso he raised יָדוֹhis hand לָהֶםto them לְהַפִּילto cast אוֹתָםthem בַּמִּדְבָּרin the wilderness

So He raised His hand and swore to cast them down in the wilderness,

106:27

וּלְהַפִּילto disperse זַרְעָםtheir offspring בַּגּוֹיִםamong the nations וּלְזָרוֹתָםand scatter them בָּאֲרָצוֹתthroughout the lands

to disperse their offspring among the nations and scatter them throughout the lands.

106:28

וַיִּצָּמְדוּthey yoked לְבַעַלto Baal פְּעוֹרBaal וַיֹּאכְלוּand ate זִבְחֵיsacrifices מֵתִיםlifeless

They yoked themselves to Baal of Peor and ate sacrifices offered to lifeless gods.

106:29

וַיַּכְעִיסוּso they provoked בְּמַעַלְלֵיהֶםwith their deeds וַתִּפְרָץbroke בָּםamong them מַגֵּפָהplague

So they provoked the LORD to anger with their deeds, and a plague broke out among them.

106:30

וַיַּעֲמֹדbut stood פִּינְחָסPhinehas וַיְפַלֵּלand intervened וַתֵּעָצַרand halted הַמַּגֵּפָהthe plague was restrained

But Phinehas stood and intervened, and the plague was restrained.

106:31

וַתֵּחָשֶׁבwas credited לוֹto him לִצְדָקָהas righteousness לְדֹרfor endless וָדֹרgenerations come עַדto עוֹלָםcome

It was credited to him as righteousness for endless generations to come.

106:32

וַיַּקְצִיפוּand angry עַלon מֵיwaters מְרִיבָהMeribah וַיֵּרַעand trouble לְמֹשֶׁהto Moses בַּעֲבוּרָםbecause of them

At the waters of Meribah they angered the LORD, and trouble came to Moses because of them.

106:33

כִּיfor הִמְרוּrebelled against אֶת[obj] רוּחוֹhis spirit and וַיְבַטֵּאand Moses spoke rashly בִּשְׂפָתָיוwith his lips

For they rebelled against His Spirit, and Moses spoke rashly with his lips.

106:34

לֹאnot הִשְׁמִידוּdestroy אֶת[obj] הָעַמִּיםthe peoples אֲשֶׁרhad אָמַרcommanded יְהוָהLORD לָהֶםto them

They did not destroy the peoples as the LORD had commanded them,

106:35

וַיִּתְעָרְבוּbut they mingled בַגּוֹיִםwith the nations וַיִּלְמְדוּand adopted מַעֲשֵׂיהֶםtheir customs

but they mingled with the nations and adopted their customs.

106:36

וַיַּעַבְדוּthey worshiped אֶת[obj] עֲצַבֵּיהֶםtheir idols which וַיִּהְיוּbecame לָהֶםto them לְמוֹקֵשׁa snare

They worshiped their idols, which became a snare to them.

106:37

וַיִּזְבְּחוּthey sacrificed אֶת[obj] בְּנֵיהֶםtheir sons וְאֶתand [obj] בְּנוֹתֵיהֶםtheir daughters לַשֵּׁדִיםto demons

They sacrificed their sons and their daughters to demons.

106:38

וַיִּשְׁפְּכוּthey shed דָםblood נָקִיinnocent דַּםblood בְּנֵיהֶםtheir sons וּבְנוֹתֵיהֶםand daughters they אֲשֶׁרwhom זִבְּחוּsacrificed לַעֲצַבֵּיto the idols כְנָעַןCanaan וַתֶּחֱנַףpolluted הָאָרֶץthe land בַּדָּמִיםwith blood

They shed innocent blood— the blood of their sons and daughters, whom they sacrificed to the idols of Canaan, and the land was polluted with blood.

106:39

וַיִּטְמְאוּthey defiled בְמַעֲשֵׂיהֶםby their actions וַיִּזְנוּand prostituted themselves בְּמַעַלְלֵיהֶםby their deeds

They defiled themselves by their actions and prostituted themselves by their deeds.

106:40

וַיִּחַרburned אַףanger יְהוָהLORD בְּעַמּוֹagainst his people וַיְתָעֵבand he abhorred אֶת[obj] נַחֲלָתוֹhis own inheritance

So the anger of the LORD burned against His people, and He abhorred His own inheritance.

106:41

וַיִּתְּנֵםhe delivered them בְּיַדinto the hand גּוֹיִםnations וַיִּמְשְׁלוּruled בָהֶםin them שֹׂנְאֵיהֶםhated them

He delivered them into the hand of the nations, and those who hated them ruled over them.

106:42

וַיִּלְחָצוּםtheir oppressed אוֹיְבֵיהֶםenemies them וַיִּכָּנְעוּand subdued תַּחַתunder יָדָםtheir hand

Their enemies oppressed them, and subdued them under their hand.

106:43

פְּעָמִיםtimes רַבּוֹתmany יַצִּילֵםrescued them וְהֵמָּהbut they יַמְרוּbent rebellion בַעֲצָתָםon rebellion וַיָּמֹכּוּand sank בַּעֲוֺנָםin their iniquity

Many times He rescued them, but they were bent on rebellion and sank down in their iniquity.

106:44

וַיַּרְאnote בַּצַּרof their distress לָהֶםto them בְּשָׁמְעוֹheard אֶת[obj] רִנָּתָםtheir cry

Nevertheless He heard their cry; He took note of their distress.

106:45

וַיִּזְכֹּרand he remembered לָהֶםto them בְּרִיתוֹhis covenant them וַיִּנָּחֵםand relented כְּרֹבby the abundance חֲסָדָיוhis loving devotion

And He remembered His covenant with them, and relented by the abundance of His loving devotion.

106:46

וַיִּתֵּןhe made אוֹתָםthem לְרַחֲמִיםobjects of compassion לִפְנֵיto before כָּלall שׁוֹבֵיהֶםheld them captive

He made them objects of compassion to all who held them captive.

106:47

הוֹשִׁיעֵנוּsave us יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God וְקַבְּצֵנוּand gather us מִןfrom הַגּוֹיִםthe nations לְהֹדוֹתgive thanks לְשֵׁםname קָדְשֶׁךָyour holy לְהִשְׁתַּבֵּחַmay glory בִּתְהִלָּתֶךָin your praise

Save us, O LORD our God, and gather us from the nations, that we may give thanks to Your holy name, that we may glory in Your praise.

106:48

בָּרוּךְblessed יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael מִןfrom הָעוֹלָםeverlasting וְעַדto הָעוֹלָםeverlasting וְאָמַרlet כָּלall הָעָםthe people אָמֵןsay Amen הַלְלוּHallelujah יָהּHallelujah

Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting. Let all the people say, “Amen!” Hallelujah!
