Psalms 98

The King comes to judge with justice

Open in the interlinear reader

98:1

מִזְמוֹרPsalm שִׁירוּsing לַיהוָהto the LORD שִׁירa song חָדָשׁnew כִּיfor נִפְלָאוֹתwonders עָשָׂהdone הוֹשִׁיעָהvictory לּוֹto him יְמִינוֹright hand וּזְרוֹעַand arm קָדְשׁוֹgained him

A Psalm. Sing to the LORD a new song, for He has done wonders; His right hand and holy arm have gained Him the victory.

98:2

הוֹדִיעַknown יְהוָהLORD יְשׁוּעָתוֹhis salvation and לְעֵינֵיto sight הַגּוֹיִםthe nations גִּלָּהrevealed צִדְקָתוֹhis righteousness

The LORD has proclaimed His salvation and revealed His righteousness to the nations.

98:3

זָכַרremembered חַסְדּוֹhis love וֶאֱמוּנָתוֹand faithfulness לְבֵיתto the house יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael רָאוּseen כָלall אַפְסֵיends אָרֶץearth אֵת[obj] יְשׁוּעַתsalvation אֱלֹהֵינוּour God

He has remembered His love and faithfulness to the house of Israel; all the ends of the earth have seen the salvation of our God.

98:4

הָרִיעוּjoyful noise לַיהוָהto the LORD כָּלall הָאָרֶץthe earth פִּצְחוּbreak forth וְרַנְּנוּlet your cry וְזַמֵּרוּand sing praises

Make a joyful noise to the LORD, all the earth; break forth—let your cry ring out, and sing praises!

98:5

זַמְּרוּsing praises לַיהוָהto the LORD בְּכִנּוֹרwith the lyre בְּכִנּוֹרharp וְקוֹלmelodious with זִמְרָהsong

Sing praises to the LORD with the lyre, in melodious song with the harp.

98:6

בַּחֲצֹצְרוֹתwith trumpets וְקוֹלand the blast שׁוֹפָרram’s horn הָרִיעוּshout joy לִפְנֵיfor before הַמֶּלֶךְking יְהוָהLORD

With trumpets and the blast of the ram’s horn shout for joy before the LORD, the King.

98:7

יִרְעַםlet הַיָּםthe sea וּמְלֹאוֹfills it תֵּבֵלworld וְיֹשְׁבֵיdwell בָהּin it

Let the sea resound, and all that fills it, the world, and all who dwell in it.

98:8

נְהָרוֹתrivers יִמְחֲאוּclap כָףhands יַחַדtogether הָרִיםmountains יְרַנֵּנוּsing

Let the rivers clap their hands, let the mountains sing together for joy

98:9

לִפְנֵיbefore יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor בָאcomes לִשְׁפֹּטto judge הָאָרֶץthe earth יִשְׁפֹּטjudge תֵּבֵלworld בְּצֶדֶקwith righteousness וְעַמִּיםand the peoples בְּמֵישָׁרִיםwith equity

before the LORD, for He comes to judge the earth. He will judge the world with righteousness and the peoples with equity.
