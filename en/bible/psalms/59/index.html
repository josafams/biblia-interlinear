Psalms 59

Enemies prowling the city at night

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. To the tune of “Do Not Destroy.” A Miktam of David, when Saul sent men to watch David’s house in order to kill him

59:1

הַצִּילֵנִיdeliver me מֵאֹיְבַיfrom my enemies O my אֱלֹהָיGod מִּמִתְקוֹמְמַיrise תְּשַׂגְּבֵנִיprotect

Deliver me from my enemies, O my God; protect me from those who rise against me.

59:2

הַצִּילֵנִיdeliver me מִפֹּעֲלֵיfrom workers אָוֶןiniquity וּמֵאַנְשֵׁיfrom men דָמִיםbloodshed הוֹשִׁיעֵנִיsave me

Deliver me from workers of iniquity, and save me from men of bloodshed.

59:3

כִּיfor הִנֵּהsee אָרְבוּlie wait לְנַפְשִׁיin for me fierce יָגוּרוּconspire עָלַיagainst me עַזִיםfierce לֹאno פִשְׁעִיtransgression וְלֹאor חַטָּאתִיsin יְהוָהLORD

See how they lie in wait for me. Fierce men conspire against me for no transgression or sin of my own, O LORD.

59:4

בְּלִיno עָוֺןfault יְרוּצוּןmove וְיִכּוֹנָנוּarise עוּרָהarise לִקְרָאתִיto help me וּרְאֵהand take notice

For no fault of my own, they move swiftly to attack me. Arise to help me, and take notice.

59:5

וְאַתָּהand you יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהִיםGod צְבָאוֹתHosts אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael הָקִיצָהrouse לִפְקֹדto punish כָּלall הַגּוֹיִםthe nations אַלno תָּחֹןshow mercy כָּלall בֹּגְדֵיtraitors אָוֶןwicked סֶלָהSelah

O LORD God of Hosts, the God of Israel, rouse Yourself to punish all the nations; show no mercy to the wicked traitors. Selah

59:6

יָשׁוּבוּreturn לָעֶרֶבin the evening יֶהֱמוּsnarling כַכָּלֶבlike dogs prowling וִיסוֹבְבוּand around עִירcity

They return in the evening, snarling like dogs and prowling around the city.

59:7

הִנֵּהsee יַבִּיעוּןspew בְּפִיהֶםfrom their mouths חֲרָבוֹתsharp בְּשִׂפְתוֹתֵיהֶםfrom their lips כִּיfor מִיwho can שֹׁמֵעַhear

See what they spew from their mouths— sharp words from their lips: “For who can hear us?”

59:8

וְאַתָּהbut you יְהוָהLORD תִּשְׂחַקlaugh לָמוֹto them תִּלְעַגscoff לְכָלat all גּוֹיִםnations

But You, O LORD, laugh at them; You scoff at all the nations.

59:9

עֻזּוֹstrength אֵלֶיךָto you אֶשְׁמֹרָהwatch כִּיfor אֱלֹהִיםGod מִשְׂגַּבִּיmy fortress

I will keep watch for You, O my strength, because You, O God, are my fortress.

59:10

אֱלֹהֵיGod חַסְדִּיloving devotion יְקַדְּמֵנִיmeet me אֱלֹהִיםGod יַרְאֵנִיme stare בְשֹׁרְרָיdown my foes

My God of loving devotion will come to meet me; God will let me stare down my foes.

59:11

אַלdo not תַּהַרְגֵםkill them פֶּןor יִשְׁכְּחוּforget עַמִּיmy people הֲנִיעֵמוֹscatter them בְחֵילְךָby your power וְהוֹרִידֵמוֹand bring them down מָגִנֵּנוּour shield אֲדֹנָיLord

Do not kill them, or my people will forget. Scatter them by Your power, and bring them down, O Lord, our shield.

59:12

חַטַּאתsins פִּימוֹtheir mouths and דְּבַרwords שְׂפָתֵימוֹtheir lips let them וְיִלָּכְדוּtrapped בִגְאוֹנָםin their pride וּמֵאָלָהin the curses וּמִכַּחַשׁand lies יְסַפֵּרוּthey utter

By the sins of their mouths and the words of their lips, let them be trapped in their pride, in the curses and lies they utter.

59:13

כַּלֵּהconsume בְחֵמָהin wrath כַּלֵּהconsume וְאֵינֵמוֹthem till they are no וְיֵדְעוּknown כִּיthat אֱלֹהִיםGod מֹשֵׁלrules בְּיַעֲקֹבover Jacob לְאַפְסֵיto the ends הָאָרֶץthe earth סֶלָהSelah

Consume them in wrath; consume them till they are no more, so it may be known to the ends of the earth that God rules over Jacob. Selah

59:14

וְיָשׁוּבוּthey return לָעֶרֶבin the evening יֶהֱמוּsnarling כַכָּלֶבlike dogs prowling וִיסוֹבְבוּand around עִירcity

They return in the evening, snarling like dogs and prowling around the city.

59:15

הֵמָּהthey יְנִיעוּןscavenge לֶאֱכֹלfor food אִםif לֹאnot יִשְׂבְּעוּsatisfied וַיָּלִינוּand growl

They scavenge for food, and growl if they are not satisfied.

59:16

וַאֲנִיbut I אָשִׁירsing עֻזֶּךָyour strength וַאֲרַנֵּןand proclaim לַבֹּקֶרin the morning חַסְדֶּךָyour loving devotion כִּיfor הָיִיתָare מִשְׂגָּבfortress לִיto me וּמָנוֹסrefuge בְּיוֹםin times צַרtrouble לִיto me

But I will sing of Your strength and proclaim Your loving devotion in the morning. For You are my fortress, my refuge in times of trouble.

59:17

עֻזִּיmy strength אֵלֶיךָto you אֲזַמֵּרָהsing praises כִּיfor אֱלֹהִיםGod מִשְׂגַּבִּיfortress my אֱלֹהֵיGod חַסְדִּיloving devotion

To You, O my strength, I sing praises, for You, O God, are my fortress, my God of loving devotion.
