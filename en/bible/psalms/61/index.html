Psalms 61

A prayer from the ends of the earth

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. With stringed instruments. Of David

61:1

שִׁמְעָהhear אֱלֹהִיםGod רִנָּתִיmy cry הַקְשִׁיבָהattend תְּפִלָּתִיmy prayer

Hear my cry, O God; attend to my prayer.

61:2

מִקְצֵהfrom the ends הָאָרֶץthe earth אֵלֶיךָto you אֶקְרָאcall בַּעֲטֹףfaint לִבִּיmy heart בְּצוּרto the rock יָרוּםhigher מִמֶּנִּיthan תַנְחֵנִיlead

From the ends of the earth I call out to You whenever my heart is faint. Lead me to the rock that is higher than I.

61:3

כִּיfor הָיִיתָbeen מַחְסֶהrefuge לִיmy a מִגְדַּלtower עֹזstrength מִפְּנֵיagainst אוֹיֵבenemy

For You have been my refuge, a tower of strength against the enemy.

61:4

אָגוּרָהdwell בְאָהָלְךָin your tent עוֹלָמִיםforever אֶחֱסֶהrefuge בְסֵתֶרin the shelter כְּנָפֶיךָyour wings סֶּלָהSelah

Let me dwell in Your tent forever and take refuge in the shelter of Your wings. Selah

61:5

כִּיfor אַתָּהyou אֱלֹהִיםGod שָׁמַעְתָּheard לִנְדָרָיmy vows נָתַתָּgiven יְרֻשַּׁתinheritance יִרְאֵיfear שְׁמֶךָyour name

For You have heard my vows, O God; You have given me the inheritance reserved for those who fear Your name.

61:6

יָמִיםdays עַלon יְמֵיlife מֶלֶךְking’s תּוֹסִיףincrease שְׁנוֹתָיוhis years כְּמוֹlike דֹרmany וָדֹרgenerations

Increase the days of the king’s life; may his years span many generations.

61:7

יֵשֵׁבsit enthroned עוֹלָםforever לִפְנֵיpresence appoint אֱלֹהִיםGod’s חֶסֶדloving devotion וֶאֱמֶתand your faithfulness מַןnumbered יִנְצְרֻהוּguard him

May he sit enthroned in God’s presence forever; appoint Your loving devotion and Your faithfulness to guard him.

61:8

כֵּןso אֲזַמְּרָהsing praise שִׁמְךָyour name לָעַדever לְשַׁלְּמִיand fulfill my נְדָרַיvows יוֹםday יוֹםday

Then I will ever sing praise to Your name and fulfill my vows day by day.
