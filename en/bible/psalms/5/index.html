Psalms 5

Morning prayer against deceivers

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster, to be accompanied by flutes. A Psalm of David

5:1

אֲמָרַיmy words הַאֲזִינָהgive ear יְהוָהLORD בִּינָהconsider הֲגִיגִיmy groaning

Give ear to my words, O LORD; consider my groaning.

5:2

הַקְשִׁיבָהattend לְקוֹלto the sound שַׁוְעִיmy cry מַלְכִּיmy king וֵאלֹהָיand my God כִּיfor אֵלֶיךָto you אֶתְפַּלָּלpray

Attend to the sound of my cry, my King and my God, for to You I pray.

5:3

יְהוָהLORD בֹּקֶרmorning תִּשְׁמַעhear קוֹלִיmy voice בֹּקֶרdaybreak אֶעֱרָךְplea לְךָto you וַאֲצַפֶּהand wait

In the morning, O LORD, You hear my voice; at daybreak I lay my plea before You and wait in expectation.

5:4

כִּיfor לֹאnot אֵלGod חָפֵץdelights רֶשַׁעwickedness אָתָּהyou לֹאno יְגֻרְךָdwell with you רָעevil

For You are not a God who delights in wickedness; no evil can dwell with You.

5:5

לֹאnot יִתְיַצְּבוּstand הוֹלְלִיםpraise לְנֶגֶדto before עֵינֶיךָpresence you שָׂנֵאתָhate כָּלall פֹּעֲלֵיworkers אָוֶןiniquity

The boastful cannot stand in Your presence; You hate all workers of iniquity.

5:6

תְּאַבֵּדdestroy דֹּבְרֵיtell כָזָבlies אִישׁman דָּמִיםbloodshed וּמִרְמָהand deceit יְתָעֵבabhors יְהוָהLORD

You destroy those who tell lies; the LORD abhors the man of bloodshed and deceit.

5:7

וַאֲנִיbut I בְּרֹבby the abundance חַסְדְּךָyour loving devotion אָבוֹאenter בֵיתֶךָhouse אֶשְׁתַּחֲוֶהbow down אֶלto הֵיכַלtemple קָדְשְׁךָyour holy בְּיִרְאָתֶךָin reverence I will

But I will enter Your house by the abundance of Your loving devotion; in reverence I will bow down toward Your holy temple.

5:8

יְהוָהLORD נְחֵנִיlead me בְצִדְקָתֶךָin your righteousness לְמַעַןbecause שׁוֹרְרָיmy enemies הַיְשַׁרmake straight לְפָנַיbefore me דַּרְכֶּךָyour way

Lead me, O LORD, in Your righteousness because of my enemies; make straight Your way before me.

5:9

כִּיfor אֵיןnot בְּפִיהוּspeak can be נְכוֹנָהtrusted קִרְבָּםwithin them הַוּוֹתdestruction קֶבֶרgraves פָּתוּחַopen גְּרוֹנָםtheir throats לְשׁוֹנָםtheir tongues practice יַחֲלִיקוּןdeceit

For not a word they speak can be trusted; destruction lies within them. Their throats are open graves; their tongues practice deceit.

5:10

הַאֲשִׁימֵםdeclare them guilty אֱלֹהִיםGod יִפְּלוּfall מִמֹּעֲצוֹתֵיהֶםby their devices בְּרֹבmany פִּשְׁעֵיהֶםtheir transgressions הַדִּיחֵמוֹdrive them כִּיfor מָרוּrebelled בָךְagainst you

Declare them guilty, O God; let them fall by their own devices. Drive them out for their many transgressions, for they have rebelled against You.

5:11

וְיִשְׂמְחוּbut let כָלall חוֹסֵיrefuge בָךְin you לְעוֹלָםever for יְרַנֵּנוּshout joy וְתָסֵךְmay you shelter עָלֵימוֹon them וְיַעְלְצוּmay rejoice בְךָin you אֹהֲבֵיlove שְׁמֶךָname

But let all who take refuge in You rejoice; let them ever shout for joy. May You shelter them, that those who love Your name may rejoice in You.

5:12

כִּיfor אַתָּהyou תְּבָרֵךְbless צַדִּיקrighteous יְהוָהLORD כַּצִּנָּהwith the shield רָצוֹןfavor תַּעְטְרֶנּוּsurround them

For surely You, O LORD, bless the righteous; You surround them with the shield of Your favor.
