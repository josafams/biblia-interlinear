Psalms 113

God lifts up the humble

Open in the interlinear reader

113:1

הַלְלוּHallelujah give יָהּHallelujah הַלְלוּpraise עַבְדֵיservants יְהוָהLORD הַלְלוּpraise אֶת[obj] שֵׁםname יְהוָהLORD

Hallelujah! Give praise, O servants of the LORD; praise the name of the LORD.

113:2

יְהִיbe שֵׁםname יְהוָהLORD מְבֹרָךְblessed מֵעַתָּהboth now וְעַדand to עוֹלָםforevermore

Blessed be the name of the LORD both now and forevermore.

113:3

מִמִּזְרַחfrom where שֶׁמֶשׁsun עַדto מְבוֹאוֹwhere it sets מְהֻלָּלpraised שֵׁםname יְהוָהLORD

From where the sun rises to where it sets, the name of the LORD is praised.

113:4

רָםexalted עַלover כָּלall גּוֹיִםnations יְהוָהLORD עַלon הַשָּׁמַיִםthe heavens כְּבוֹדוֹhis glory

The LORD is exalted over all the nations, His glory above the heavens.

113:5

מִיwho כַּיהוָהlike the LORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God הַמַּגְבִּיהִיhigh לָשָׁבֶתenthroned on

Who is like the LORD our God, the One enthroned on high?

113:6

הַמַּשְׁפִּילִיhumbles לִרְאוֹתto behold בַּשָּׁמַיִםthe heavens וּבָאָרֶץand the earth

He humbles Himself to behold the heavens and the earth.

113:7

מְקִימִיraises מֵעָפָרfrom the dust דָּלpoor מֵאַשְׁפֹּתfrom the dump יָרִיםlifts אֶבְיוֹןneedy

He raises the poor from the dust and lifts the needy from the dump

113:8

לְהוֹשִׁיבִיto seat עִםwith נְדִיבִיםnobles עִםwith נְדִיבֵיprinces עַמּוֹhis people

to seat them with nobles, with the princes of His people.

113:9

מוֹשִׁיבִיsettles עֲקֶרֶתbarren woman הַבַּיִתher home אֵםmother הַבָּנִיםher children שְׂמֵחָהjoyful הַלְלוּHallelujah יָהּHallelujah

He settles the barren woman in her home as a joyful mother to her children. Hallelujah!
