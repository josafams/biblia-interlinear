Psalms 145

An acrostic hymn to the greatness of the King

Open in the interlinear reader

145:1

תְּהִלָּהpraise לְדָוִדDavid אֲרוֹמִמְךָexalt you אֱלוֹהַיmy God הַמֶּלֶךְand king וַאֲבָרֲכָהwill bless שִׁמְךָyour name לְעוֹלָםforever וָעֶדand ever

A Psalm of praise. Of David. I will exalt You, my God and King; I will bless Your name forever and ever.

145:2

בְּכָלevery יוֹםday אֲבָרֲכֶךָּbless you וַאֲהַלְלָהpraise שִׁמְךָyour name לְעוֹלָםforever וָעֶדand ever

Every day I will bless You, and I will praise Your name forever and ever.

145:3

גָּדוֹלgreat יְהוָהLORD וּמְהֻלָּלpraised מְאֹדgreatly וְלִגְדֻלָּתוֹhis greatness is אֵיןno חֵקֶרunsearchable

Great is the LORD and greatly to be praised; His greatness is unsearchable.

145:4

דּוֹרgeneration לְדוֹרto the next יְשַׁבַּחcommend מַעֲשֶׂיךָyour works וּגְבוּרֹתֶיךָyour mighty acts יַגִּידוּproclaim

One generation will commend Your works to the next, and will proclaim Your mighty acts—

145:5

הֲדַרsplendor כְּבוֹדglorious הוֹדֶךָsplendor of your majesty וְדִבְרֵיand words נִפְלְאוֹתֶיךָyour wondrous works אָשִׂיחָהmeditate

the glorious splendor of Your majesty. And I will meditate on Your wondrous works.

145:6

וֶעֱזוּזthey will proclaim נוֹרְאֹתֶיךָyour awesome deeds יֹאמֵרוּsay וּגְדוּלָּתְךָyour greatness אֲסַפְּרֶנָּהdeclare

They will proclaim the power of Your awesome deeds, and I will declare Your greatness.

145:7

זֵכֶרextol רַבabundant טוּבְךָyour goodness יַבִּיעוּfame וְצִדְקָתְךָyour righteousness יְרַנֵּנוּsing joyfully

They will extol the fame of Your abundant goodness and sing joyfully of Your righteousness.

145:8

חַנּוּןgracious וְרַחוּםand compassionate יְהוָהLORD אֶרֶךְslow אַפַּיִםanger וּגְדָלand abounding חָסֶדloving devotion

The LORD is gracious and compassionate, slow to anger and abounding in loving devotion.

145:9

טוֹבgood יְהוָהLORD לַכֹּלto all וְרַחֲמָיוhis compassion עַלon כָּלall מַעֲשָׂיוmade

The LORD is good to all; His compassion rests on all He has made.

145:10

יוֹדוּךָgive you thanks יְהוָהLORD כָּלall מַעֲשֶׂיךָmade וַחֲסִידֶיךָand your saints will יְבָרֲכוּכָהbless you

All You have made will give You thanks, O LORD, and Your saints will bless You.

145:11

כְּבוֹדglory מַלְכוּתְךָyour kingdom יֹאמֵרוּtell וּגְבוּרָתְךָyour might יְדַבֵּרוּspeak

They will tell of the glory of Your kingdom and speak of Your might,

145:12

לְהוֹדִיעַto make known לִבְנֵיto sons הָאָדָםmen גְּבוּרֹתָיוmighty acts וּכְבוֹדand the glorious הֲדַרsplendor מַלְכוּתוֹkingdom

to make known to men Your mighty acts and the glorious splendor of Your kingdom.

145:13

מַלְכוּתְךָkingdom מַלְכוּתkingdom כָּלall עֹלָמִיםeverlasting וּמֶמְשֶׁלְתְּךָand your dominion בְּכָלthrough all דּוֹרgenerations וָדוֹרall

Your kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and Your dominion endures through all generations. The LORD is faithful in all His words and kind in all His actions.

145:14

סוֹמֵךְupholds יְהוָהLORD לְכָלall הַנֹּפְלִיםwho fall וְזוֹקֵףand lifts לְכָלall הַכְּפוּפִיםwho are bowed

The LORD upholds all who fall and lifts up all who are bowed down.

145:15

עֵינֵיeyes כֹלall אֵלֶיךָto you יְשַׂבֵּרוּlook וְאַתָּהand you נוֹתֵןgive לָהֶםto them אֶת[obj] אָכְלָםtheir food בְּעִתּוֹin season

The eyes of all look to You, and You give them their food in season.

145:16

פּוֹתֵחַopen אֶת[obj] יָדֶךָyour hand וּמַשְׂבִּיעַand satisfy לְכָלdesire of every חַיliving רָצוֹןthing

You open Your hand and satisfy the desire of every living thing.

145:17

צַדִּיקrighteous יְהוָהLORD בְּכָלin all דְּרָכָיוhis ways וְחָסִידand kind בְּכָלin all מַעֲשָׂיוhis deeds

The LORD is righteous in all His ways and kind in all His deeds.

145:18

קָרוֹבnear יְהוָהLORD לְכָלto all קֹרְאָיוcall on him לְכֹלto all אֲשֶׁרwho יִקְרָאֻהוּcall בֶאֱמֶתin truth

The LORD is near to all who call on Him, to all who call out to Him in truth.

145:19

רְצוֹןfulfills יְרֵאָיוfear him יַעֲשֶׂהhe וְאֶתand [obj] שַׁוְעָתָםtheir cry יִשְׁמַעhears וְיוֹשִׁיעֵםand saves them

He fulfills the desires of those who fear Him; He hears their cry and saves them.

145:20

שׁוֹמֵרpreserves יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] כָּלall אֹהֲבָיוlove him וְאֵתand [obj] כָּלall הָרְשָׁעִיםthe wicked יַשְׁמִידdestroy

The LORD preserves all who love Him, but all the wicked He will destroy.

145:21

תְּהִלַּתpraise יְהוָהLORD יְדַבֶּרdeclare פִּיmy mouth וִיבָרֵךְbless כָּלevery בָּשָׂרcreature שֵׁםname קָדְשׁוֹhis holy לְעוֹלָםforever וָעֶדand ever

My mouth will declare the praise of the LORD; let every creature bless His holy name forever and ever.
