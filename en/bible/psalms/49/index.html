Psalms 49

Wealth does not rescue from death

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. A Psalm of the sons of Korah

49:1

שִׁמְעוּhear זֹאתthis כָּלall הָעַמִּיםpeoples הַאֲזִינוּlisten כָּלall יֹשְׁבֵיinhabitants חָלֶדworld

Hear this, all you peoples; listen, all inhabitants of the world,

49:2

גַּםboth בְּנֵיlow אָדָםlow גַּםand בְּנֵיsons אִישׁman יַחַדalike עָשִׁירrich וְאֶבְיוֹןand poor

both low and high, rich and poor alike.

49:3

פִּיmy mouth יְדַבֵּרimpart חָכְמוֹתwisdom וְהָגוּתand the meditation לִבִּיmy heart תְבוּנוֹתunderstanding

My mouth will impart wisdom, and the meditation of my heart will bring understanding.

49:4

אַטֶּהincline לְמָשָׁלto a proverb אָזְנִיmy ear אֶפְתַּחexpress בְּכִנּוֹרwith the harp חִידָתִיmy riddle

I will incline my ear to a proverb; I will express my riddle with the harp:

49:5

לָמָּהto should אִירָאfear בִּימֵיin times רָעtrouble עֲוֺןwicked usurpers עֲקֵבַיusurpers יְסוּבֵּנִיsurround me

Why should I fear in times of trouble, when wicked usurpers surround me?

49:6

הַבֹּטְחִיםtrust עַלin חֵילָםtheir wealth וּבְרֹבand in great עָשְׁרָםtheir riches יִתְהַלָּלוּboast

They trust in their wealth and boast in their great riches.

49:7

אָחbrother לֹאor פָדֹהredeem יִפְדֶּהpossibly redeem אִישׁman לֹאno יִתֵּןpay לֵאלֹהִיםto God כָּפְרוֹhis ransom

No man can possibly redeem his brother or pay his ransom to God.

49:8

וְיֵקַרhis פִּדְיוֹןredemption נַפְשָׁםsoul וְחָדַלcostly and לְעוֹלָםnever

For the redemption of his soul is costly, and never can payment suffice,

49:9

וִיחִיhe should live עוֹדlonger לָנֶצַחon forever לֹאnot יִרְאֶהsee הַשָּׁחַתdecay

that he should live on forever and not see decay.

49:10

כִּיfor יִרְאֶהclear חֲכָמִיםwise יָמוּתוּdie יַחַדtogether כְּסִילfoolish וָבַעַרand the senseless יֹאבֵדוּperish וְעָזְבוּand leave לַאֲחֵרִיםto others חֵילָםtheir wealth

For it is clear that wise men die, and the foolish and the senseless both perish and leave their wealth to others.

49:11

קִרְבָּםtheir graves בָּתֵּימוֹtheir homes לְעוֹלָםeternal מִשְׁכְּנֹתָםtheir dwellings לְדֹרfor endless וָדֹרgenerations even though קָרְאוּcall בִשְׁמוֹתָםtheir lands were their עֲלֵיon אֲדָמוֹתnamesakes

Their graves are their eternal homes— their dwellings for endless generations— even though their lands were their namesakes.

49:12

וְאָדָםbut a man בִּיקָרdespite his wealth בַּלcannot יָלִיןendure נִמְשַׁלlike כַּבְּהֵמוֹתlike the beasts נִדְמוּperish

But a man, despite his wealth, cannot endure; he is like the beasts that perish.

49:13

זֶהthis דַרְכָּםfate כֵּסֶלconfident לָמוֹto them וְאַחֲרֵיהֶםand after them בְּפִיהֶםendorse their יִרְצוּsayings סֶלָהSelah

This is the fate of the self-confident and their followers who endorse their sayings. Selah

49:14

כַּצֹּאןlike sheep לִשְׁאוֹלfor Sheol שַׁתּוּthey מָוֶתdeath יִרְעֵםtheir shepherd וַיִּרְדּוּwill rule בָםin them יְשָׁרִיםupright לַבֹּקֶרin the morning וְצוּרָםand their form לְבַלּוֹתwill decay שְׁאוֹלSheol מִזְּבֻלfar from lofty לוֹto him

Like sheep they are destined for Sheol. Death will be their shepherd. The upright will rule them in the morning, and their form will decay in Sheol, far from their lofty abode.

49:15

אַךְbut אֱלֹהִיםGod יִפְדֶּהredeem נַפְשִׁיmy life מִיַּדfrom hand שְׁאוֹלSheol כִּיfor יִקָּחֵנִיtake me סֶלָהSelah

But God will redeem my life from Sheol, for He will surely take me to Himself. Selah

49:16

אַלdo not תִּירָאamazed כִּיwhen יַעֲשִׁרrich אִישׁman כִּיwhen יִרְבֶּהincreases כְּבוֹדsplendor בֵּיתוֹhis house

Do not be amazed when a man grows rich, when the splendor of his house increases.

49:17

כִּיfor לֹאnot בְמוֹתוֹwhen he dies he יִקַּחcarry הַכֹּלnothing לֹאnot יֵרֵדdown אַחֲרָיוfollow him כְּבוֹדוֹabundance

For when he dies, he will carry nothing away; his abundance will not follow him down.

49:18

כִּיfor נַפְשׁוֹhis soul בְּחַיָּיוin his lifetime יְבָרֵךְblesses וְיוֹדֻךָand men praise you כִּיwhen תֵיטִיבprosper לָךְto you

Though in his lifetime he blesses his soul— and men praise you when you prosper—

49:19

תָּבוֹאcome עַדuntil דּוֹרgeneration אֲבוֹתָיוhis fathers עַדuntil נֵצַחnever לֹאnever יִרְאוּsee אוֹרlight

he will join the generation of his fathers, who will never see the light of day.

49:20

אָדָםman בִּיקָרhas riches וְלֹאand not יָבִיןunderstanding נִמְשַׁלlike כַּבְּהֵמוֹתlike the beasts נִדְמוּperish

A man who has riches without understanding is like the beasts that perish.
