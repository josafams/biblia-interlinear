Psalms 120

Living among those who hate peace

Open in the interlinear reader

120:1

שִׁירsong הַמַּעֲלוֹתof ascents אֶלto יְהוָהLORD בַּצָּרָתָהin my distress לִּיto me קָרָאתִיcried וַיַּעֲנֵנִיand he answered me

A song of ascents. In my distress I cried to the LORD, and He answered me.

120:2

יְהוָהLORD הַצִּילָהdeliver נַפְשִׁיmy soul מִשְּׂפַתfrom lips שֶׁקֶרlying מִלָּשׁוֹןtongue רְמִיָּהdeceitful

Deliver my soul, O LORD, from lying lips and a deceitful tongue.

120:3

מַהwhat יִּתֵּןwill לְךָto you וּמַהand what יֹּסִיףadded לָךְto you לָשׁוֹןtongue רְמִיָּהdeceitful

What will He do to you, and what will be added to you, O deceitful tongue?

120:4

חִצֵּיarrows גִבּוֹרwarrior שְׁנוּנִיםsharp עִםwith גַּחֲלֵיburning coals רְתָמִיםbroom tree

Sharp arrows will come from the warrior, with burning coals of the broom tree!

120:5

אוֹיָהWoe לִיto me כִּיthat גַרְתִּיdwell מֶשֶׁךְMeshech שָׁכַנְתִּיlive עִםamong אָהֳלֵיtents קֵדָרKedar

Woe to me that I dwell in Meshech, that I live among the tents of Kedar!

120:6

רַבַּתtoo שָׁכְנָהdwelt לָּהּto her נַפְשִׁיhave I עִםamong שׂוֹנֵאhate שָׁלוֹםpeace

Too long have I dwelt among those who hate peace.

120:7

אֲנִיI am שָׁלוֹםpeace וְכִיbut when אֲדַבֵּרspeak הֵמָּהthey לַמִּלְחָמָהwar

I am in favor of peace; but when I speak, they want war.
