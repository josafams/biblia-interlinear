Psalms 111

An acrostic of the works of the LORD

Open in the interlinear reader

111:1

הַלְלוּHallelujah יָהּHallelujah אוֹדֶהthanks יְהוָהLORD בְּכָלwith all לֵבָבheart בְּסוֹדin the council יְשָׁרִיםupright וְעֵדָהassembly

Hallelujah! I will give thanks to the LORD with all my heart in the council of the upright and in the assembly.

111:2

גְּדֹלִיםgreat מַעֲשֵׂיworks יְהוָהLORD דְּרוּשִׁיםpondered לְכָלby all חֶפְצֵיהֶםdelight in them

Great are the works of the LORD; they are pondered by all who delight in them.

111:3

הוֹדsplendid וְהָדָרand majestic פָּעֳלוֹhis work וְצִדְקָתוֹhis righteousness עֹמֶדֶתendures לָעַדendures forever

Splendid and majestic is His work; His righteousness endures forever.

111:4

זֵכֶרremembered עָשָׂהcaused לְנִפְלְאֹתָיוhis wonders to חַנּוּןgracious וְרַחוּםand compassionate יְהוָהLORD

He has caused His wonders to be remembered; the LORD is gracious and compassionate.

111:5

טֶרֶףfood נָתַןprovides for לִירֵאָיוfear him יִזְכֹּרremembers לְעוֹלָםforever בְּרִיתוֹhis covenant

He provides food for those who fear Him; He remembers His covenant forever.

111:6

כֹּחַpower מַעֲשָׂיוhis works הִגִּידshown לְעַמּוֹpeople לָתֵתby giving לָהֶםto them נַחֲלַתinheritance גּוֹיִםnations

He has shown His people the power of His works by giving them the inheritance of the nations.

111:7

מַעֲשֵׂיworks יָדָיוhis hands אֱמֶתtruth וּמִשְׁפָּטand justice נֶאֱמָנִיםtrustworthy כָּלall פִּקּוּדָיוhis precepts

The works of His hands are truth and justice; all His precepts are trustworthy.

111:8

סְמוּכִיםupheld לָעַדforever ever לְעוֹלָםforever and עֲשׂוּיִםenacted בֶּאֱמֶתin truth וְיָשָׁרand uprightness

They are upheld forever and ever, enacted in truth and uprightness.

111:9

פְּדוּתredemption שָׁלַחsent לְעַמּוֹto his people he צִוָּהordained לְעוֹלָםforever בְּרִיתוֹhis covenant קָדוֹשׁholy וְנוֹרָאand awesome שְׁמוֹhis name

He has sent redemption to His people; He has ordained His covenant forever; holy and awesome is His name.

111:10

רֵאשִׁיתbeginning חָכְמָהwisdom יִרְאַתfear יְהוָהLORD שֵׂכֶלprecepts gain understanding טוֹבrich לְכָלall עֹשֵׂיהֶםfollow תְּהִלָּתוֹhis praise עֹמֶדֶתendures לָעַדendures forever

The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom; all who follow His precepts gain rich understanding. His praise endures forever!
