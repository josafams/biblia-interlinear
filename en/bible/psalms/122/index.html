Psalms 122

The joy of arriving in Jerusalem

Open in the interlinear reader

122:1

שִׁירsong הַמַּעֲלוֹתof ascents לְדָוִדof David שָׂמַחְתִּיglad בְּאֹמְרִיםwhen they said לִיto me בֵּיתhouse יְהוָהLORD נֵלֵךְgo

A song of ascents. Of David. I was glad when they said to me, “Let us go to the house of the LORD.”

122:2

עֹמְדוֹתstanding הָיוּwere רַגְלֵינוּour feet בִּשְׁעָרַיִךְin your gates O יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem

Our feet are standing in your gates, O Jerusalem.

122:3

יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem הַבְּנוּיָהis built כְּעִירas a city שֶׁחֻבְּרָהunited לָּהּto her יַחְדָּוtogether

Jerusalem is built up as a city united together,

122:4

שֶׁשָּׁםwhere the עָלוּgo up שְׁבָטִיםtribes שִׁבְטֵיtribes יָהּLORD עֵדוּתtestimony לְיִשְׂרָאֵלfor Israel לְהֹדוֹתto give thanks לְשֵׁםto the name יְהוָהLORD

where the tribes go up, the tribes of the LORD, as a testimony for Israel, to give thanks to the name of the LORD.

122:5

כִּיfor שָׁמָּהthere יָשְׁבוּlived כִסְאוֹתthrones לְמִשְׁפָּטof judgment כִּסְאוֹתthrones לְבֵיתthe house דָּוִידDavid

For there the thrones of judgment stand, the thrones of the house of David.

122:6

שַׁאֲלוּpray שְׁלוֹםpeace יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem יִשְׁלָיוּprosper אֹהֲבָיִךְlove you

Pray for the peace of Jerusalem: “May those who love you prosper.

122:7

יְהִיbe שָׁלוֹםpeace בְּחֵילֵךְwithin your walls שַׁלְוָהprosperity בְּאַרְמְנוֹתָיִךְinside your fortresses

May there be peace within your walls, and prosperity inside your fortresses.”

122:8

לְמַעַןsake אַחַיmy brothers וְרֵעָיand friends I אֲדַבְּרָהsay נָּאplease שָׁלוֹםpeace בָּךְin you

For the sake of my brothers and friends, I will say, “Peace be within you.”

122:9

לְמַעַןsake בֵּיתhouse יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God אֲבַקְשָׁהseek טוֹבprosperity לָךְto you

For the sake of the house of the LORD our God, I will seek your prosperity.
