Psalms 16

God as inheritance and joy

Open in the interlinear reader

16:1

מִכְתָּםMiktam לְדָוִדof David שָׁמְרֵנִיpreserve me אֵלGod כִּיfor חָסִיתִיrefuge בָךְin you

A Miktam of David. Preserve me, O God, for in You I take refuge.

16:2

אָמַרְתְּsaid לַיהוָהto the LORD אֲדֹנָיmy Lord אָתָּהyou טוֹבָתִיI have good בַּלno עָלֶיךָon you

I said to the LORD, “You are my Lord; apart from You I have no good thing.”

16:3

לִקְדוֹשִׁיםsaints אֲשֶׁרthat בָּאָרֶץin the land הֵמָּהthey וְאַדִּירֵיwhom כָּלall חֶפְצִיmy delight בָםin

As for the saints in the land, they are the excellence in whom all my delight resides.

16:4

יִרְבּוּmultiply עַצְּבוֹתָםsorrows אַחֵרother מָהָרוּchase בַּלnot אַסִּיךְpour נִסְכֵּיהֶםtheir libations מִדָּםof blood וּבַלand no אֶשָּׂאlift אֶת[obj] שְׁמוֹתָםtheir names עַלon שְׂפָתָיmy lips

Sorrows will multiply to those who chase other gods. I will not pour out their libations of blood, or speak their names with my lips.

16:5

יְהוָהLORD מְנָתchosen חֶלְקִיmy chosen portion וְכוֹסִיand my cup אַתָּהyou תּוֹמִיךְsecure גּוֹרָלִיmy lot

The LORD is my chosen portion and my cup; You have made my lot secure.

16:6

חֲבָלִיםlines נָפְלוּfallen לִיto me בַּנְּעִמִיםin pleasant אַףsurely נַחֲלָתinheritance שָׁפְרָהdelightful עָלָיon me

The lines of my boundary have fallen in pleasant places; surely my inheritance is delightful.

16:7

אֲבָרֵךְbless אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD אֲשֶׁרwho יְעָצָנִיcounsels me אַףeven לֵילוֹתnight יִסְּרוּנִיmy instructs כִלְיוֹתָיconscience me

I will bless the LORD who counsels me; even at night my conscience instructs me.

16:8

שִׁוִּיתִיset יְהוָהLORD לְנֶגְדִּיbefore me תָמִידalways כִּיbecause מִימִינִיat my right hand בַּלnot אֶמּוֹטshaken

I have set the LORD always before me. Because He is at my right hand, I will not be shaken.

16:9

לָכֵןtherefore שָׂמַחglad לִבִּיmy heart וַיָּגֶלrejoices כְּבוֹדִיmy tongue אַףalso בְּשָׂרִיmy body יִשְׁכֹּןdwell לָבֶטַחsecurely

Therefore my heart is glad and my tongue rejoices; my body also will dwell securely.

16:10

כִּיfor לֹאnot תַעֲזֹבabandon נַפְשִׁיmy soul לִשְׁאוֹלto Sheol לֹאnot תִתֵּןlet חֲסִידְךָyour holy לִרְאוֹתone see שָׁחַתdecay

For You will not abandon my soul to Sheol, nor will You let Your Holy One see decay.

16:11

תּוֹדִיעֵנִיknown to me אֹרַחpath חַיִּיםlife שֹׂבַעfill שְׂמָחוֹתjoy אֶתwith פָּנֶיךָyour presence נְעִמוֹתpleasures בִּימִינְךָat your right hand נֶצַחeternal

You have made known to me the path of life; You will fill me with joy in Your presence, with eternal pleasures at Your right hand.
