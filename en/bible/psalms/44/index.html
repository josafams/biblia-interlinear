Psalms 44

The people's lament after defeat

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. A Maskil of the sons of Korah

44:1

אֱלֹהִיםGod בְּאָזְנֵינוּwith our ears שָׁמַעְנוּheard אֲבוֹתֵינוּour fathers סִפְּרוּtold לָנוּto us פֹּעַלwork פָּעַלְתָּdid בִימֵיהֶםin their days בִּימֵיin the days קֶדֶםold

We have heard with our ears, O God; our fathers have told us the work You did in their days, in the days of old.

44:2

אַתָּהyou יָדְךָhand you גּוֹיִםnations הוֹרַשְׁתָּdrove וַתִּטָּעֵםand planted our fathers תָּרַעcrushed לְאֻמִּיםpeoples וַתְּשַׁלְּחֵםand cast them

With Your hand You drove out the nations and planted our fathers there; You crushed the peoples and cast them out.

44:3

כִּיfor לֹאnot בְחַרְבָּםby their sword that they יָרְשׁוּtook אָרֶץland וּזְרוֹעָםtheir arm did לֹאnot הוֹשִׁיעָהvictory לָּמוֹto them כִּיfor יְמִינְךָyour right hand וּזְרוֹעֲךָyour arm וְאוֹרand the light פָּנֶיךָyour face כִּיbecause רְצִיתָםfavored them

For it was not by their sword that they took the land; their arm did not bring them victory. It was by Your right hand, Your arm, and the light of Your face, because You favored them.

44:4

אַתָּהyou הוּאhe מַלְכִּיmy king O אֱלֹהִיםGod צַוֵּהordains יְשׁוּעוֹתvictories יַעֲקֹבJacob

You are my King, O God, who ordains victories for Jacob.

44:5

בְּךָin you צָרֵינוּwe our foes נְנַגֵּחַrepel בְּשִׁמְךָthrough your name נָבוּסtrample קָמֵינוּour enemies

Through You we repel our foes; through Your name we trample our enemies.

44:6

כִּיfor לֹאnot בְקַשְׁתִּיin my bow אֶבְטָחtrust וְחַרְבִּיnor does my sword לֹאnot תוֹשִׁיעֵנִיsave me

For I do not trust in my bow, nor does my sword save me.

44:7

כִּיfor הוֹשַׁעְתָּנוּsave us מִצָּרֵינוּfrom our enemies וּמְשַׂנְאֵינוּwho hate us הֱבִישׁוֹתָshame

For You save us from our enemies; You put those who hate us to shame.

44:8

בֵּאלֹהִיםin God הִלַּלְנוּboasted כָלall הַיּוֹםday long וְשִׁמְךָand your name לְעוֹלָםwill forever נוֹדֶהpraise סֶלָהSelah

In God we have boasted all day long, and Your name we will praise forever. Selah

44:9

אַףhow much זָנַחְתָּrejected וַתַּכְלִימֵנוּand humbled us וְלֹאno תֵצֵאgo forth בְּצִבְאוֹתֵינוּwith our armies

But You have rejected and humbled us; You no longer go forth with our armies.

44:10

תְּשִׁיבֵנוּmade us אָחוֹרretreat מִנִּיfrom צָרfoe וּמְשַׂנְאֵינוּhate us שָׁסוּplundered לָמוֹto them

You have made us retreat from the foe, and those who hate us have plundered us.

44:11

תִּתְּנֵנוּgiven us כְּצֹאןas sheep מַאֲכָלdevoured וּבַגּוֹיִםamong the nations זֵרִיתָנוּscattered us

You have given us up as sheep to be devoured; You have scattered us among the nations.

44:12

תִּמְכֹּרsell עַמְּךָyour people בְלֹאin without הוֹןgain וְלֹאno רִבִּיתָprofit בִּמְחִירֵיהֶםfrom their sale

You sell Your people for nothing; no profit do You gain from their sale.

44:13

תְּשִׂימֵנוּmade us חֶרְפָּהreproach לִשְׁכֵנֵינוּto our neighbors לַעַגmockery וָקֶלֶסand derision לִסְבִיבוֹתֵינוּto those around us

You have made us a reproach to our neighbors, a mockery and derision to those around us.

44:14

תְּשִׂימֵנוּmade us מָשָׁלbyword בַּגּוֹיִםamong the nations מְנוֹדlaughingstock רֹאשׁamong the בַּלpeoples אֻמִּיםpeoples

You have made us a byword among the nations, a laughingstock among the peoples.

44:15

כָּלall הַיּוֹםday long כְּלִמָּתִיmy disgrace נֶגְדִּיbefore me וּבֹשֶׁתand shame פָּנַיface כִּסָּתְנִיcovered my

All day long my disgrace is before me, and shame has covered my face,

44:16

מִקּוֹלat the voice מְחָרֵףscorner וּמְגַדֵּףand reviler מִפְּנֵיfrom before אוֹיֵבenemy וּמִתְנַקֵּםbent on revenge

at the voice of the scorner and reviler, because of the enemy, bent on revenge.

44:17

כָּלall זֹאתthis בָּאַתְנוּcome upon us וְלֹאnot שְׁכַחֲנוּךָforgotten you וְלֹאor שִׁקַּרְנוּbetrayed בִּבְרִיתֶךָyour covenant

All this has come upon us, though we have not forgotten You or betrayed Your covenant.

44:18

לֹאnot נָסוֹגturned אָחוֹרback לִבֵּנוּour hearts וַתֵּטnot אֲשֻׁרֵינוּour steps מִנִּיfrom אָרְחֶךָyour path

Our hearts have not turned back; our steps have not strayed from Your path.

44:19

כִּיbut דִכִּיתָנוּcrushed us בִּמְקוֹםin the lair תַּנִּיםjackals וַתְּכַסhave covered עָלֵינוּon us בְצַלְמָוֶתwith deepest darkness

But You have crushed us in the lair of jackals; You have covered us with deepest darkness.

44:20

אִםif שָׁכַחְנוּforgotten שֵׁםname אֱלֹהֵינוּour God וַנִּפְרֹשׂor spread כַּפֵּינוּour hands לְאֵלgod זָרforeign

If we had forgotten the name of our God or spread out our hands to a foreign god,

44:21

הֲלֹאwould not אֱלֹהִיםGod יַחֲקָרdiscovered זֹאתthis כִּיfor הוּאhe יֹדֵעַknows תַּעֲלֻמוֹתsecrets לֵבheart

would not God have discovered, since He knows the secrets of the heart?

44:22

כִּיfor עָלֶיךָfor הֹרַגְנוּdeath כָלall הַיּוֹםday long נֶחְשַׁבְנוּconsidered כְּצֹאןas sheep טִבְחָהslaughtered

Yet for Your sake we face death all day long; we are considered as sheep to be slaughtered.

44:23

עוּרָהwake לָמָּהwhy תִישַׁןsleeping אֲדֹנָיLord הָקִיצָהarise אַלdo not תִּזְנַחreject לָנֶצַחus forever

Wake up, O Lord! Why are You sleeping? Arise! Do not reject us forever.

44:24

לָמָּהto why פָנֶיךָyour face תַסְתִּירhide תִּשְׁכַּחforget עָנְיֵנוּour affliction וְלַחֲצֵנוּand oppression

Why do You hide Your face and forget our affliction and oppression?

44:25

כִּיfor שָׁחָהsunk לֶעָפָרto the dust נַפְשֵׁנוּour soul דָּבְקָהcling לָאָרֶץto the earth בִּטְנֵנוּour bodies

For our soul has sunk to the dust; our bodies cling to the earth.

44:26

קוּמָהrise עֶזְרָתָהhelp לָּנוּto us וּפְדֵנוּredeem us לְמַעַןso חַסְדֶּךָyour loving devotion

Rise up; be our help! Redeem us on account of Your loving devotion.
