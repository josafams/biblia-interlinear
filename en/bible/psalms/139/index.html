Psalms 139

God knows and surrounds the psalmist

Open in the interlinear reader

139:1

לַמְנַצֵּחַfor the choirmaster לְדָוִדDavid מִזְמוֹרPsalm יְהוָהLORD חֲקַרְתַּנִיsearched me וַתֵּדָעand known

For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. O LORD, You have searched me and known me.

139:2

אַתָּהyou יָדַעְתָּknow שִׁבְתִּיsit וְקוּמִיand when I rise בַּנְתָּהunderstand לְרֵעִיmy thoughts מֵרָחוֹקfrom afar

You know when I sit and when I rise; You understand my thoughts from afar.

139:3

אָרְחִיmy path my וְרִבְעִיsearch זֵרִיתָdown וְכָלall דְּרָכַיmy ways הִסְכַּנְתָּהaware

You search out my path and my lying down; You are aware of all my ways.

139:4

כִּיfor אֵיןno מִלָּהword בִּלְשׁוֹנִיon my tongue הֵןbehold יְהוָהLORD יָדַעְתָּknow כֻלָּהּall about it

Even before a word is on my tongue, You know all about it, O LORD.

139:5

אָחוֹרbehind וָקֶדֶםand before צַרְתָּנִיhem me וַתָּשֶׁתyou have laid עָלַיupon me כַּפֶּכָהyour hand

You hem me in behind and before; You have laid Your hand upon me.

139:6

פְּלִיאָהsuch דַעַתknowledge מִמֶּנִּיfrom me נִשְׂגְּבָהlofty לֹאnot אוּכַלme attain לָהּto attain

Such knowledge is too wonderful for me, too lofty for me to attain.

139:7

אָנָהwhere אֵלֵךְgo מֵרוּחֶךָescape your spirit וְאָנָהwhere מִפָּנֶיךָfrom your presence אֶבְרָחflee

Where can I go to escape Your Spirit? Where can I flee from Your presence?

139:8

אִםif אֶסַּקascend שָׁמַיִםheavens שָׁםthere אָתָּהyou are וְאַצִּיעָהbed שְּׁאוֹלSheol הִנֶּךָּif

If I ascend to the heavens, You are there; if I make my bed in Sheol, You are there.

139:9

אֶשָּׂאrise כַנְפֵיwings שָׁחַרdawn אֶשְׁכְּנָהsettle בְּאַחֲרִיתby the farthest יָםsea

If I rise on the wings of the dawn, if I settle by the farthest sea,

139:10

גַּםeven שָׁםthere יָדְךָyour hand תַנְחֵנִיguide me וְתֹאחֲזֵנִיwill hold me יְמִינֶךָyour right hand

even there Your hand will guide me; Your right hand will hold me fast.

139:11

וָאֹמַרI say אַךְsurely חֹשֶׁךְdarkness יְשׁוּפֵנִיhide me וְלַיְלָהnight אוֹרlight בַּעֲדֵנִיaround me

If I say, “Surely the darkness will hide me, and the light become night around me”—

139:12

גַּםeven חֹשֶׁךְdarkness לֹאnot יַחְשִׁיךְdark מִמֶּךָfrom you וְלַיְלָהbut the night כַּיּוֹםlike the day יָאִירshines כַּחֲשֵׁיכָהfor darkness כָּאוֹרָהas light

even the darkness is not dark to You, but the night shines like the day, for darkness is as light to You.

139:13

כִּיfor אַתָּהyou קָנִיתָformed כִלְיֹתָיmy inmost תְּסֻכֵּנִיknit me together בְּבֶטֶןwomb אִמִּיmy mother’s

For You formed my inmost being; You knit me together in my mother’s womb.

139:14

אוֹדְךָpraise you עַלfor כִּיfor נוֹרָאוֹתfearfully נִפְלֵיתִיwonderfully נִפְלָאִיםmarvelous מַעֲשֶׂיךָyour works וְנַפְשִׁיand my soul יֹדַעַתknow מְאֹדvery

I praise You, for I am fearfully and wonderfully made. Marvelous are Your works, and I know this very well.

139:15

לֹאnot נִכְחַדhidden עָצְמִיmy frame מִמֶּךָּfrom you אֲשֶׁרthat עֻשֵּׂיתִיmade בַסֵּתֶרin secret רֻקַּמְתִּיwoven בְּתַחְתִּיּוֹתin the depths אָרֶץearth

My frame was not hidden from You when I was made in secret, when I was woven together in the depths of the earth.

139:16

גָּלְמִיmy unformed body רָאוּsaw עֵינֶיךָyour eyes וְעַלin סִפְרְךָyour book כֻּלָּםall יִכָּתֵבוּwritten יָמִיםdays יֻצָּרוּordained וְלוֹand for אֶחָדone בָּהֶםin them

Your eyes saw my unformed body; all my days were written in Your book and ordained for me before one of them came to be.

139:17

וְלִיto me מַהhow יָּקְרוּprecious רֵעֶיךָyour thoughts אֵלGod מֶהhow עָצְמוּvast רָאשֵׁיהֶםtheir sum

How precious to me are Your thoughts, O God, how vast is their sum!

139:18

אֶסְפְּרֵםcount them מֵחוֹלof sand יִרְבּוּןmultiplied הֱקִיצֹתִיawake וְעוֹדִיI am still עִמָּךְwith you

If I were to count them, they would outnumber the grains of sand; and when I awake, I am still with You.

139:19

אִםif תִּקְטֹלslay אֱלוֹהַּGod רָשָׁעwicked וְאַנְשֵׁיmen דָמִיםbloodthirsty סוּרוּaway מֶנִּיfrom of me

O God, that You would slay the wicked— away from me, you bloodthirsty men—

139:20

אֲשֶׁרwho יֹאמְרֻךָspeak of you לִמְזִמָּהenemies נָשֻׂאtake לַשָּׁוְאyour name in vain עָרֶיךָdeceitfully your

who speak of You deceitfully; Your enemies take Your name in vain.

139:21

הֲלוֹאdo I not מְשַׂנְאֶיךָhate יְהוָהLORD אֶשְׂנָאhate וּבִתְקוֹמְמֶיךָrise against you אֶתְקוֹטָטdetest

Do I not hate those who hate You, O LORD, and detest those who rise against You?

139:22

תַּכְלִיתperfect שִׂנְאָהhatred שְׂנֵאתִיםhate them לְאוֹיְבִיםenemies הָיוּwere לִיto me

I hate them with perfect hatred; I count them as my enemies.

139:23

חָקְרֵנִיsearch me אֵלGod וְדַעand know לְבָבִיmy heart בְּחָנֵנִיtest me וְדַעand know שַׂרְעַפָּיmy concerns

Search me, O God, and know my heart; test me and know my concerns.

139:24

וּרְאֵהsee אִםif דֶּרֶךְway עֹצֶבoffensive בִּיin me וּנְחֵנִיlead me בְּדֶרֶךְin the way עוֹלָםeverlasting

See if there is any offensive way in me; lead me in the way everlasting.
