Psalms 6

The plea of a sick and weary man

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. With stringed instruments, according to Sheminith. A Psalm of David

6:1

יְהוָהLORD אַלdo not בְּאַפְּךָin your anger תוֹכִיחֵנִיrebuke me וְאַלor בַּחֲמָתְךָin your wrath תְיַסְּרֵנִיdiscipline me

O LORD, do not rebuke me in Your anger or discipline me in Your wrath.

6:2

חָנֵּנִיmerciful to me יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor אֻמְלַלfrail אָנִיam רְפָאֵנִיheal me יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor נִבְהֲלוּagony עֲצָמָיmy bones

Be merciful to me, O LORD, for I am frail; heal me, O LORD, for my bones are in agony.

6:3

וְנַפְשִׁיmy soul נִבְהֲלָהdeeply מְאֹדvery וְאַתָּהand you יְהוָהLORD עַדuntil מָתָיhow

My soul is deeply distressed. How long, O LORD, how long?

6:4

שׁוּבָהturn יְהוָהLORD חַלְּצָהdeliver נַפְשִׁיmy soul הוֹשִׁיעֵנִיsave me לְמַעַןbecause חַסְדֶּךָyour loving devotion

Turn, O LORD, and deliver my soul; save me because of Your loving devotion.

6:5

כִּיfor אֵיןno בַּמָּוֶתdeath זִכְרֶךָmention of you בִּשְׁאוֹלSheol מִיwho can יוֹדֶהpraise לָּךְto you

For there is no mention of You in death; who can praise You from Sheol?

6:6

יָגַעְתִּיweary בְּאַנְחָתִיfrom groaning אַשְׂחֶהflood בְכָלall לַיְלָהnight מִטָּתִיmy bed בְּדִמְעָתִיwith weeping and drench my עַרְשִׂיcouch אַמְסֶהtears

I am weary from groaning; all night I flood my bed with weeping and drench my couch with tears.

6:7

עָשְׁשָׁהfail מִכַּעַסfrom grief עֵינִיmy eyes עָתְקָהdim בְּכָלbecause of all צוֹרְרָיmy foes

My eyes fail from grief; they grow dim because of all my foes.

6:8

סוּרוּdepart מִמֶּנִּיfrom me כָּלall פֹּעֲלֵיworkers אָוֶןiniquity כִּיfor שָׁמַעheard יְהוָהLORD קוֹלvoice בִּכְיִיmy weeping

Depart from me, all you workers of iniquity, for the LORD has heard my weeping.

6:9

שָׁמַעheard יְהוָהLORD תְּחִנָּתִיmy cry יְהוָהLORD תְּפִלָּתִיmy prayer יִקָּחaccepts

The LORD has heard my cry for mercy; the LORD accepts my prayer.

6:10

יֵבֹשׁוּashamed וְיִבָּהֲלוּand dismayed מְאֹדvery כָּלall אֹיְבָיmy enemies יָשֻׁבוּwill יֵבֹשׁוּsudden רָגַעmoment

All my enemies will be ashamed and dismayed; they will turn back in sudden disgrace.
