Psalms 105

God's faithfulness from the patriarchs to the exodus

Open in the interlinear reader

105:1

הוֹדוּgive thanks לַיהוָהto the LORD קִרְאוּcall בִּשְׁמוֹupon his name הוֹדִיעוּknown בָעַמִּיםamong the nations עֲלִילוֹתָיוhis deeds

Give thanks to the LORD, call upon His name; make known His deeds among the nations.

105:2

שִׁירוּsing לוֹto him זַמְּרוּsing praises לוֹto him שִׂיחוּtell בְּכָלall נִפְלְאוֹתָיוhis wonders

Sing to Him, sing praises to Him; tell of all His wonders.

105:3

הִתְהַלְלוּglory בְּשֵׁםname קָדְשׁוֹhis holy יִשְׂמַחrejoice לֵבhearts מְבַקְשֵׁיseek יְהוָהLORD

Glory in His holy name; let the hearts of those who seek the LORD rejoice.

105:4

דִּרְשׁוּseek יְהוָהLORD וְעֻזּוֹand his strength בַּקְּשׁוּseek פָנָיוhis face תָּמִידalways

Seek out the LORD and His strength; seek His face always.

105:5

זִכְרוּremember נִפְלְאוֹתָיוwonders he אֲשֶׁרhas עָשָׂהdone מֹפְתָיוhis marvels וּמִשְׁפְּטֵיand the judgments פִיוpronounced

Remember the wonders He has done, His marvels, and the judgments He has pronounced,

105:6

זֶרַעoffspring אַבְרָהָםAbraham עַבְדּוֹhis servant בְּנֵיsons יַעֲקֹבJacob בְּחִירָיוhis chosen

O offspring of His servant Abraham, O sons of Jacob, His chosen ones.

105:7

הוּאis יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God בְּכָלthroughout הָאָרֶץthe earth מִשְׁפָּטָיוhis judgments

He is the LORD our God; His judgments carry throughout the earth.

105:8

זָכַרremembers לְעוֹלָםforever בְּרִיתוֹhis covenant דָּבָרword צִוָּהordained לְאֶלֶףfor a thousand דּוֹרgenerations

He remembers His covenant forever, the word He ordained for a thousand generations—

105:9

אֲשֶׁרthat כָּרַתcovenant made אֶתwith אַבְרָהָםAbraham וּשְׁבוּעָתוֹand the oath he swore לְיִשְׂחָקto Isaac

the covenant He made with Abraham, and the oath He swore to Isaac.

105:10

וַיַּעֲמִידֶהָhe confirmed it לְיַעֲקֹבto Jacob לְחֹקas a decree לְיִשְׂרָאֵלto Israel בְּרִיתcovenant עוֹלָםeverlasting

He confirmed it to Jacob as a decree, to Israel as an everlasting covenant:

105:11

לֵאמֹרI לְךָto you אֶתֵּןgive the אֶת[obj] אֶרֶץland כְּנָעַןCanaan חֶבֶלportion נַחֲלַתְכֶםyour inheritance

“I will give you the land of Canaan as the portion of your inheritance.”

105:12

בִּהְיוֹתָםwhen they were מְתֵיfew מִסְפָּרnumber כִּמְעַטfew וְגָרִיםand strangers בָּהּin the land

When they were few in number, few indeed, and strangers in the land,

105:13

וַיִּתְהַלְּכוּthey wandered מִגּוֹיfrom nation אֶלto גּוֹיnation מִמַּמְלָכָהfrom one kingdom אֶלto עַםpeople אַחֵרanother

they wandered from nation to nation, from one kingdom to another.

105:14

לֹאno הִנִּיחַlet אָדָםman לְעָשְׁקָםoppress them וַיּוֹכַחhe rebuked עֲלֵיהֶםon their מְלָכִיםkings

He let no man oppress them; He rebuked kings on their behalf:

105:15

אַלdo not תִּגְּעוּtouch בִמְשִׁיחָיmy anointed וְלִנְבִיאַיto my prophets אַלdo תָּרֵעוּharm

“Do not touch My anointed ones! Do no harm to My prophets!”

105:16

וַיִּקְרָאhe called רָעָבfamine עַלon הָאָרֶץthe land כָּלall מַטֵּהcut לֶחֶםfood שָׁבָרsupplies

He called down famine on the land and cut off all their supplies of food.

105:17

שָׁלַחsent לִפְנֵיהֶםa before them אִישׁman לְעֶבֶדas a slave נִמְכַּרsold יוֹסֵףJoseph

He sent a man before them— Joseph, sold as a slave.

105:18

עִנּוּbruised בַכֶּבֶלwith shackles רַגְלוֹhis feet בַּרְזֶלplaced בָּאָהcome נַפְשׁוֹhis neck

They bruised his feet with shackles and placed his neck in irons,

105:19

עַדuntil עֵתprediction בֹּאcame דְבָרוֹtrue and אִמְרַתword יְהוָהLORD צְרָפָתְהוּproved him

until his prediction came true and the word of the LORD proved him right.

105:20

שָׁלַחsent מֶלֶךְking וַיַּתִּירֵהוּand released him מֹשֵׁלruler עַמִּיםpeoples וַיְפַתְּחֵהוּand opened him

The king sent and released him; the ruler of peoples set him free.

105:21

שָׂמוֹmade him אָדוֹןmaster לְבֵיתוֹof his household וּמֹשֵׁלruler בְּכָלover all קִנְיָנוֹhis substance

He made him master of his household, ruler over all his substance,

105:22

לֶאְסֹרto instruct שָׂרָיוhis princes בְּנַפְשׁוֹas he pleased וּזְקֵנָיוand teach his elders יְחַכֵּםwisdom

to instruct his princes as he pleased and teach his elders wisdom.

105:23

וַיָּבֹאthen entered יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael מִצְרָיִםEgypt וְיַעֲקֹבJacob גָּרdwelt בְּאֶרֶץin the land חָםHam

Then Israel entered Egypt; Jacob dwelt in the land of Ham.

105:24

וַיֶּפֶרfruitful אֶת[obj] עַמּוֹhis people מְאֹדvery וַיַּעֲצִמֵהוּnumerous their מִצָּרָיוthan foes

And the LORD made His people very fruitful, more numerous than their foes,

105:25

הָפַךְwhose לִבָּםhearts לִשְׂנֹאto hate עַמּוֹhis people לְהִתְנַכֵּלto conspire בַּעֲבָדָיוagainst his servants

whose hearts He turned to hate His people, to conspire against His servants.

105:26

שָׁלַחsent מֹשֶׁהMoses עַבְדּוֹhis servant and אַהֲרֹןAaron אֲשֶׁרwhom בָּחַרchosen בּוֹin him

He sent Moses His servant, and Aaron, whom He had chosen.

105:27

שָׂמוּperformed בָםamong them דִּבְרֵיwords אֹתוֹתָיוhis miraculous signs וּמֹפְתִיםand wonders בְּאֶרֶץin the land חָםHam

They performed His miraculous signs among them, and wonders in the land of Ham.

105:28

שָׁלַחsent חֹשֶׁךְdarkness וַיַּחְשִׁךְand it became dark וְלֹאand not מָרוּdefied אֶת[obj] דְּבָרוֹhis words

He sent darkness, and it became dark— yet they defied His words.

105:29

הָפַךְturned אֶת[obj] מֵימֵיהֶםtheir waters לְדָםto blood וַיָּמֶתdie אֶת[obj] דְּגָתָםcaused their fish

He turned their waters to blood and caused their fish to die.

105:30

שָׁרַץteemed אַרְצָםtheir land צְפַרְדְּעִיםfrogs בְּחַדְרֵיchambers מַלְכֵיהֶםtheir royal

Their land teemed with frogs, even in their royal chambers.

105:31

אָמַרspoke וַיָּבֹאand insects עָרֹבswarmed כִּנִּיםgnats בְּכָלthroughout גְּבוּלָםtheir country

He spoke, and insects swarmed— gnats throughout their country.

105:32

נָתַןgave גִּשְׁמֵיהֶםrain בָּרָדhail אֵשׁlightning לֶהָבוֹתthroughout בְּאַרְצָםthroughout their land

He gave them hail for rain, with lightning throughout their land.

105:33

וַיַּךְhe struck גַּפְנָםtheir vines וּתְאֵנָתָםand fig trees וַיְשַׁבֵּרand shattered עֵץtrees גְּבוּלָםtheir country

He struck their vines and fig trees and shattered the trees of their country.

105:34

אָמַרspoke וַיָּבֹאcame אַרְבֶּהlocusts וְיֶלֶקyoung locusts וְאֵיןwithout מִסְפָּרnumber

He spoke, and the locusts came— young locusts without number.

105:35

וַיֹּאכַלthey devoured כָּלevery עֵשֶׂבplant בְּאַרְצָםin their land וַיֹּאכַלand consumed פְּרִיproduce אַדְמָתָםtheir soil

They devoured every plant in their land and consumed the produce of their soil.

105:36

וַיַּךְthen he struck כָּלall בְּכוֹרfirstborn בְּאַרְצָםin their land רֵאשִׁיתfirstfruits לְכָלof all אוֹנָםtheir vigor

Then He struck all the firstborn in their land, the firstfruits of all their vigor.

105:37

וַיּוֹצִיאֵםhe brought Israel בְּכֶסֶףwith silver וְזָהָבand gold וְאֵיןand none בִּשְׁבָטָיוamong his tribes כּוֹשֵׁלstumbled

He brought Israel out with silver and gold, and none among His tribes stumbled.

105:38

שָׂמַחglad מִצְרַיִםEgypt בְּצֵאתָםwhen they departed כִּיfor נָפַלfallen פַּחְדָּםdread עֲלֵיהֶםon them

Egypt was glad when they departed, for the dread of Israel had fallen on them.

105:39

פָּרַשׂspread עָנָןcloud לְמָסָךְas a covering וְאֵשׁand a fire לְהָאִירto light לָיְלָהnight

He spread a cloud as a covering and a fire to light up the night.

105:40

שָׁאַלasked וַיָּבֵאand he brought שְׂלָוquail וְלֶחֶםbread שָׁמַיִםheaven יַשְׂבִּיעֵםsatisfied them

They asked, and He brought quail and satisfied them with the bread of heaven.

105:41

פָּתַחopened צוּרrock וַיָּזוּבוּand gushed מָיִםwater הָלְכוּwalked בַּצִּיּוֹתin the desert נָהָרriver

He opened a rock, and water gushed out; it flowed like a river in the desert.

105:42

כִּיfor זָכַרremembered אֶת[obj] דְּבַרpromise קָדְשׁוֹhis holy אֶת[obj] אַבְרָהָםAbraham עַבְדּוֹhis servant

For He remembered His holy promise to Abraham His servant.

105:43

וַיּוֹצִאhe brought עַמּוֹhis people בְשָׂשׂוֹןjoy בְּרִנָּהwith shouts אֶת[obj] בְּחִירָיוhis chosen

He brought forth His people with rejoicing, His chosen with shouts of joy.

105:44

וַיִּתֵּןhe gave לָהֶםto them אַרְצוֹתlands גּוֹיִםnations וַעֲמַלlabor לְאֻמִּיםmight יִירָשׁוּinherit

He gave them the lands of the nations, that they might inherit the fruit of others’ labor,

105:45

בַּעֲבוּרmight יִשְׁמְרוּkeep חֻקָּיוhis statutes וְתוֹרֹתָיוand his laws יִנְצֹרוּobey הַלְלוּHallelujah יָהּHallelujah

that they might keep His statutes and obey His laws. Hallelujah!
