Psalms 50

God judges the worship of His people

Open in the interlinear reader

50:1

מִזְמוֹרPsalm לְאָסָףof Asaph אֵלmighty אֱלֹהִיםGod יְהוָהLORD דִּבֶּרspeaks וַיִּקְרָאand summons אָרֶץearth מִמִּזְרַחfrom where שֶׁמֶשׁsun עַדto מְבֹאוֹwhere it sets

A Psalm of Asaph. The Mighty One, God the LORD, speaks and summons the earth from where the sun rises to where it sets.

50:2

מִצִּיּוֹןFrom Zion מִכְלַלperfect יֹפִיbeauty אֱלֹהִיםGod הוֹפִיעַshines forth

From Zion, perfect in beauty, God shines forth.

50:3

יָבֹאapproaches אֱלֹהֵינוּour God וְאַלand will not יֶחֱרַשׁsilent אֵשׁfire לְפָנָיוprecedes him תֹּאכֵלconsuming וּסְבִיבָיוaround him נִשְׂעֲרָהtempest מְאֹדvery

Our God approaches and will not be silent! Consuming fire precedes Him, and a tempest rages around Him.

50:4

יִקְרָאsummons אֶלto הַשָּׁמַיִםthe heavens מֵעָלabove וְאֶלand to הָאָרֶץthe earth לָדִיןthat he may judge עַמּוֹhis people

He summons the heavens above, and the earth, that He may judge His people:

50:5

אִסְפוּgather לִיto me חֲסִידָיmy saints כֹּרְתֵיmade בְרִיתִיcovenant with me עֲלֵיon זָבַחsacrifice

“Gather to Me My saints, who made a covenant with Me by sacrifice.”

50:6

וַיַּגִּידוּproclaim שָׁמַיִםheavens צִדְקוֹhis righteousness כִּיfor אֱלֹהִיםGod שֹׁפֵטjudge הוּאis סֶלָהSelah

And the heavens proclaim His righteousness, for God Himself is Judge. Selah

50:7

שִׁמְעָהhear עַמִּיmy people וַאֲדַבֵּרָהspeak יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וְאָעִידָהtestify בָּךְagainst you אֱלֹהִיםGod אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God אָנֹכִיam

“Hear, O My people, and I will speak, O Israel, and I will testify against you: I am God, your God.

50:8

לֹאnot עַלfor זְבָחֶיךָyou your sacrifices אוֹכִיחֶךָrebuke וְעוֹלֹתֶיךָand your burnt offerings לְנֶגְדִּיare before me תָמִידever

I do not rebuke you for your sacrifices, and your burnt offerings are ever before Me.

50:9

לֹאno אֶקַּחtake מִבֵּיתְךָfrom your house פָרbull מִמִּכְלְאֹתֶיךָfrom your stall עַתּוּדִיםgoats

I have no need for a bull from your stall or goats from your pens,

50:10

כִּיfor לִיto me כָלevery חַיְתוֹbeast יָעַרforest בְּהֵמוֹתcattle בְּהַרְרֵיhills אָלֶףthousand

for every beast of the forest is Mine— the cattle on a thousand hills.

50:11

יָדַעְתִּיknow כָּלevery עוֹףbird הָרִיםmountains וְזִיזand the creatures שָׂדַיfield עִמָּדִיwith me

I know every bird in the mountains, and the creatures of the field are Mine.

50:12

אִםif אֶרְעַבhungry לֹאnot אֹמַרtell לָךְto you כִּיfor לִיto me תֵבֵלworld וּמְלֹאָהּand the fullness thereof

If I were hungry, I would not tell you, for the world is Mine, and the fullness thereof.

50:13

הַאוֹכַלdo I eat בְּשַׂרflesh אַבִּירִיםbulls וְדַםblood עַתּוּדִיםgoats אֶשְׁתֶּהdrink

Do I eat the flesh of bulls, or drink the blood of goats?

50:14

זְבַחsacrifice לֵאלֹהִיםto God תּוֹדָהthank וְשַׁלֵּםand fulfill לְעֶלְיוֹןto the Most high נְדָרֶיךָyour vows

Sacrifice a thank offering to God, and fulfill your vows to the Most High.

50:15

וּקְרָאֵנִיcall upon me בְּיוֹםin the day צָרָהtrouble אֲחַלֶּצְךָdeliver you וּתְכַבְּדֵנִיhonor me

Call upon Me in the day of trouble; I will deliver you, and you will honor Me.”

50:16

וְלָרָשָׁעto the wicked however אָמַרsays אֱלֹהִיםGod מַהwhat לְּךָto you לְסַפֵּרto recite חֻקָּיmy statutes וַתִּשָּׂאand to bear בְרִיתִיmy covenant עֲלֵיon פִיךָyour lips

To the wicked, however, God says, “What right have you to recite My statutes and to bear My covenant on your lips?

50:17

וְאַתָּהfor you שָׂנֵאתָhate מוּסָרinstruction וַתַּשְׁלֵךְand cast דְּבָרַיmy words אַחֲרֶיךָbehind you

For you hate My instruction and cast My words behind you.

50:18

אִםif רָאִיתָsee גַנָּבthief וַתִּרֶץyou befriend עִמּוֹwith him וְעִםwith מְנָאֲפִיםadulterers חֶלְקֶךָyour lot

When you see a thief, you befriend him, and throw in your lot with adulterers.

50:19

פִּיךָyour mouth שָׁלַחְתָּunleash בְרָעָהfor evil וּלְשׁוֹנְךָand your tongue תַּצְמִידunharness מִרְמָהdeceit

You unleash your mouth for evil and unharness your tongue for deceit.

50:20

תֵּשֵׁבsit בְּאָחִיךָmalign your brother you תְדַבֵּרslander בְּבֶןown son אִמְּךָyour mother’s תִּתֶּןgive דֹּפִיslanderest

You sit and malign your brother; you slander your own mother’s son.

50:21

אֵלֶּהthese עָשִׂיתָdone וְהֶחֱרַשְׁתִּיsilent דִּמִּיתָthought הֱיוֹתwas אֶהְיֶהjust כָמוֹךָlike you אוֹכִיחֲךָrebuke you וְאֶעֶרְכָהand accuse לְעֵינֶיךָto your face

You have done these things, and I kept silent; you thought I was just like you. But now I rebuke you and accuse you to your face.

50:22

בִּינוּconsider נָאnow זֹאתthis שֹׁכְחֵיforget אֱלוֹהַּGod פֶּןlest אֶטְרֹףtear וְאֵיןwith no מַצִּילrescue

Now consider this, you who forget God, lest I tear you to pieces, with no one to rescue you:

50:23

זֹבֵחַsacrifices תּוֹדָהthank יְכַבְּדָנְנִיhonors me וְשָׂםand set דֶּרֶךְway אַרְאֶנּוּshow בְּיֵשַׁעthe salvation אֱלֹהִיםGod

He who sacrifices a thank offering honors Me, and to him who rights his way, I will show the salvation of God.”
