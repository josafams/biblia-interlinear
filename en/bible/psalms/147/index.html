Psalms 147

God rebuilds Jerusalem and rules nature

Open in the interlinear reader

147:1

הַלְלוּHallelujah יָהּHallelujah כִּיfor טוֹבgood זַמְּרָהsing praises אֱלֹהֵינוּour God כִּיfor נָעִיםpleasant נָאוָהlovely תְהִלָּהpraise

Hallelujah! How good it is to sing praises to our God, how pleasant and lovely to praise Him!

147:2

בּוֹנֵהbuilds יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem יְהוָהLORD נִדְחֵיexiles יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael יְכַנֵּסgathers

The LORD builds up Jerusalem; He gathers the exiles of Israel.

147:3

הָרֹפֵאheals לִשְׁבוּרֵיthe brokenhearted לֵבbrokenhearted וּמְחַבֵּשׁand binds לְעַצְּבוֹתָםtheir wounds

He heals the brokenhearted and binds up their wounds.

147:4

מוֹנֶהdetermines מִסְפָּרnumber לַכּוֹכָבִיםof the stars לְכֻלָּםthem each by שֵׁמוֹתname יִקְרָאcalls

He determines the number of the stars; He calls them each by name.

147:5

גָּדוֹלgreat אֲדוֹנֵינוּour Lord וְרַבand mighty כֹּחַpower לִתְבוּנָתוֹhis understanding אֵיןno מִסְפָּרlimit

Great is our Lord, and mighty in power; His understanding has no limit.

147:6

מְעוֹדֵדsustains עֲנָוִיםhumble יְהוָהLORD מַשְׁפִּילcasts רְשָׁעִיםwicked עֲדֵיto אָרֶץground

The LORD sustains the humble, but casts the wicked to the ground.

147:7

עֱנוּsing לַיהוָהto the LORD בְּתוֹדָהwith thanksgiving זַמְּרוּmake music לֵאלֹהֵינוּto our God בְכִנּוֹרon the harp

Sing to the LORD with thanksgiving; make music on the harp to our God,

147:8

הַמְכַסֶּהwho covers the שָׁמַיִםsky בְּעָבִיםwith clouds הַמֵּכִיןwho prepares לָאָרֶץfor the earth מָטָרrain הַמַּצְמִיחַwho makes הָרִיםhills חָצִירgrass

who covers the sky with clouds, who prepares rain for the earth, who makes grass to grow on the hills.

147:9

נוֹתֵןprovides לִבְהֵמָהfor the animals לַחְמָהּfood לִבְנֵיfor the young עֹרֵבravens אֲשֶׁרthat יִקְרָאוּcall

He provides food for the animals, and for the young ravens when they call.

147:10

לֹאnot בִגְבוּרַתin the strength הַסּוּסthe horse יֶחְפָּץtakes לֹאno בְשׁוֹקֵיin the legpower הָאִישׁthe man יִרְצֶהpleasure

He does not delight in the strength of the horse; He takes no pleasure in the legpower of the man.

147:11

רוֹצֶהpleased יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] יְרֵאָיוfear him אֶת[obj] הַמְיַחֲלִיםwho hope לְחַסְדּוֹin his loving devotion

The LORD is pleased with those who fear Him, who hope in His loving devotion.

147:12

שַׁבְּחִיexalt יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD הַלְלִיpraise אֱלֹהַיִךְyour God צִיּוֹןZion

Exalt the LORD, O Jerusalem; praise your God, O Zion!

147:13

כִּיfor חִזַּקstrengthens בְּרִיחֵיbars שְׁעָרָיִךְyour gates בֵּרַךְblesses בָּנַיִךְchildren בְּקִרְבֵּךְwithin you

For He strengthens the bars of your gates and blesses the children within you.

147:14

הַשָּׂםmakes גְּבוּלֵךְyour borders שָׁלוֹםpeace חֵלֶבfinest חִטִּיםwheat יַשְׂבִּיעֵךְfills you

He makes peace at your borders; He fills you with the finest wheat.

147:15

הַשֹּׁלֵחַsends אִמְרָתוֹforth his אָרֶץearth עַדto מְהֵרָהruns יָרוּץswiftly דְּבָרוֹcommand

He sends forth His command to the earth; His word runs swiftly.

147:16

הַנֹּתֵןthe gives שֶׁלֶגsnow כַּצָּמֶרlike wool כְּפוֹרfrost כָּאֵפֶרlike ashes יְפַזֵּרscatters

He spreads the snow like wool; He scatters the frost like ashes;

147:17

מַשְׁלִיךְcasts קַרְחוֹhis hail כְפִתִּיםlike pebbles לִפְנֵיto before קָרָתוֹhis cold מִיwho can יַעֲמֹדwithstand

He casts forth His hail like pebbles. Who can withstand His icy blast?

147:18

יִשְׁלַחsends דְּבָרוֹhis word וְיַמְסֵםand melts them יַשֵּׁבunleashes רוּחוֹhis winds and יִזְּלוּflow מָיִםwaters

He sends forth His word and melts them; He unleashes His winds, and the waters flow.

147:19

מַגִּידdeclares דְּבָרָיוhis word לְיַעֲקֹבto Jacob חֻקָּיוhis statutes וּמִשְׁפָּטָיוand judgments לְיִשְׂרָאֵלto Israel

He declares His word to Jacob, His statutes and judgments to Israel.

147:20

לֹאnot עָשָׂהdone כֵןthis לְכָלfor no גּוֹיnation וּמִשְׁפָּטִיםjudgments בַּלnot יְדָעוּםknow הַלְלוּHallelujah יָהּHallelujah

He has done this for no other nation; they do not know His judgments. Hallelujah!
