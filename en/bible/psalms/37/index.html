Psalms 37

Patience before the prosperity of the wicked

Open in the interlinear reader

37:1

לְדָוִדof David אַלdo not תִּתְחַרfret בַּמְּרֵעִיםover those who evil אַלdo תְּקַנֵּאenvy בְּעֹשֵׂיdo עַוְלָהwrong

Of David. Do not fret over those who do evil; do not envy those who do wrong.

37:2

כִּיfor כֶחָצִירlike grass מְהֵרָהquickly יִמָּלוּwither וּכְיֶרֶקand wilt like דֶּשֶׁאtender יִבּוֹלוּןplants

For they wither quickly like grass and wilt like tender plants.

37:3

בְּטַחtrust בַּיהוָהin the LORD וַעֲשֵׂהand do טוֹבgood שְׁכָןdwell אֶרֶץland וּרְעֵהand cultivate אֱמוּנָהfaithfulness

Trust in the LORD and do good; dwell in the land and cultivate faithfulness.

37:4

וְהִתְעַנַּגdelight עַלin יְהוָהLORD וְיִתֶּןand he will give לְךָto you מִשְׁאֲלֹתdesires לִבֶּךָyour heart

Delight yourself in the LORD, and He will give you the desires of your heart.

37:5

גּוֹלcommit עַלto יְהוָהLORD דַּרְכֶּךָyour way וּבְטַחtrust עָלָיוin him וְהוּאand he יַעֲשֶׂהdo

Commit your way to the LORD; trust in Him, and He will do it.

37:6

וְהוֹצִיאhe will bring forth כָאוֹרlike the dawn צִדְקֶךָyour righteousness וּמִשְׁפָּטֶךָyour justice כַּצָּהֳרָיִםlike the noonday sun

He will bring forth your righteousness like the dawn, your justice like the noonday sun.

37:7

דּוֹםstill לַיהוָהbefore the LORD וְהִתְחוֹלֵלand wait patiently לוֹfor him אַלdo not תִּתְחַרfret בְּמַצְלִיחַwhen men prosper דַּרְכּוֹtheir ways בְּאִישׁwhen they עֹשֶׂהcarry מְזִמּוֹתschemes

Be still before the LORD and wait patiently for Him; do not fret when men prosper in their ways, when they carry out wicked schemes.

37:8

הֶרֶףrefrain מֵאַףfrom anger וַעֲזֹבand abandon חֵמָהwrath אַלdo not תִּתְחַרfret אַךְonly לְהָרֵעַbring harm

Refrain from anger and abandon wrath; do not fret—it can only bring harm.

37:9

כִּיfor מְרֵעִיםevildoers יִכָּרֵתוּןcut off וְקֹוֵיhope יְהוָהLORD הֵמָּהthey יִירְשׁוּinherit אָרֶץland

For the evildoers will be cut off, but those who hope in the LORD will inherit the land.

37:10

וְעוֹדwhile מְעַטlittle וְאֵיןno רָשָׁעwicked וְהִתְבּוֹנַנְתָּthough you look עַלfor מְקוֹמוֹhis place וְאֵינֶנּוּthey will not be found

Yet a little while, and the wicked will be no more; though you look for them, they will not be found.

37:11

וַעֲנָוִיםbut the meek יִירְשׁוּinherit אָרֶץland וְהִתְעַנְּגוּand delight עַלin רֹבabundant שָׁלוֹםprosperity

But the meek will inherit the land and delight in abundant prosperity.

37:12

זֹמֵםscheme רָשָׁעwicked לַצַּדִּיקagainst the righteous וְחֹרֵקand gnash עָלָיוon him שִׁנָּיוteeth them

The wicked scheme against the righteous and gnash their teeth at them,

37:13

אֲדֹנָיLord יִשְׂחַקlaughs לוֹto him כִּיthat רָאָהseeing כִּיfor יָבֹאcoming יוֹמוֹday

but the Lord laughs, seeing that their day is coming.

37:14

חֶרֶבsword פָּתְחוּdrawn רְשָׁעִיםwicked וְדָרְכוּand bent קַשְׁתָּםbow לְהַפִּילto bring down עָנִיpoor וְאֶבְיוֹןand needy לִטְבוֹחַto slay יִשְׁרֵיupright דָרֶךְways

The wicked have drawn the sword and bent the bow to bring down the poor and needy, to slay those whose ways are upright.

37:15

חַרְבָּםtheir swords תָּבוֹאpierce בְלִבָּםown hearts וְקַשְּׁתוֹתָםand their bows will תִּשָּׁבַרְנָהbroken

But their swords will pierce their own hearts, and their bows will be broken.

37:16

טוֹבbetter מְעַטlittle לַצַּדִּיקof the righteous מֵהֲמוֹןthan the abundance רְשָׁעִיםwicked רַבִּיםmany

Better is the little of the righteous than the abundance of many who are wicked.

37:17

כִּיfor זְרוֹעוֹתarms רְשָׁעִיםwicked תִּשָּׁבַרְנָהbroken וְסוֹמֵךְupholds צַדִּיקִיםrighteous יְהוָהLORD

For the arms of the wicked will be broken, but the LORD upholds the righteous.

37:18

יוֹדֵעַknows יְהוָהLORD יְמֵיdays תְמִימִםblameless וְנַחֲלָתָםand their inheritance לְעוֹלָםwill forever תִּהְיֶהlast

The LORD knows the days of the blameless, and their inheritance will last forever.

37:19

לֹאnot יֵבֹשׁוּashamed בְּעֵתin the time רָעָהevil וּבִימֵיand in the days רְעָבוֹןfamine יִשְׂבָּעוּsatisfied

In the time of evil they will not be ashamed, and in the days of famine they will be satisfied.

37:20

כִּיbut רְשָׁעִיםwicked יֹאבֵדוּperish וְאֹיְבֵיand enemies יְהוָהLORD כִּיקַרlike the glory כָּרִיםfields כָּלוּvanish בֶעָשָׁןlike smoke כָּלוּfade

But the wicked and enemies of the LORD will perish like the glory of the fields. They will vanish; like smoke they will fade away.

37:21

לֹוֶהborrow רָשָׁעwicked וְלֹאand do not יְשַׁלֵּםrepay וְצַדִּיקbut the righteous חוֹנֵןgracious וְנוֹתֵןand giving

The wicked borrow and do not repay, but the righteous are gracious and giving.

37:22

כִּיfor מְבֹרָכָיוblesses will יִירְשׁוּinherit אָרֶץland וּמְקֻלָּלָיוbut the cursed will be יִכָּרֵתוּdestroyed

Surely those He blesses will inherit the land, but the cursed will be destroyed.

37:23

מֵיְהוָהby the LORD מִצְעֲדֵיsteps גֶבֶרman כּוֹנָנוּordered וְדַרְכּוֹhis journey יֶחְפָּץdelight

The steps of a man are ordered by the LORD who takes delight in his journey.

37:24

כִּיfor יִפֹּלfalls לֹאnot יוּטָלoverwhelmed כִּיfor יְהוָהLORD סוֹמֵךְholding יָדוֹhis hand

Though he falls, he will not be overwhelmed, for the LORD is holding his hand.

37:25

נַעַרyoung הָיִיתִיam גַּםeven זָקַנְתִּיold וְלֹאyet never רָאִיתִיseen צַדִּיקrighteous נֶעֱזָבabandoned וְזַרְעוֹor their children מְבַקֶּשׁbegging for לָחֶםbread

I once was young and now am old, yet never have I seen the righteous abandoned or their children begging for bread.

37:26

כָּלall הַיּוֹםthe day חוֹנֵןgenerous quick וּמַלְוֶהlend וְזַרְעוֹand their children לִבְרָכָהa blessing

They are ever generous and quick to lend, and their children are a blessing.

37:27

סוּרturn מֵרָעfrom evil וַעֲשֵׂהand do טוֹבgood וּשְׁכֹןabide לְעוֹלָםforever

Turn away from evil and do good, so that you will abide forever.

37:28

כִּיfor יְהוָהLORD אֹהֵבloves מִשְׁפָּטjustice וְלֹאand will not יַעֲזֹבforsake אֶת[obj] חֲסִידָיוhis saints לְעוֹלָםare forever נִשְׁמָרוּpreserved וְזֶרַעbut the offspring רְשָׁעִיםwicked נִכְרָתcut off

For the LORD loves justice and will not forsake His saints. They are preserved forever, but the offspring of the wicked will be cut off.

37:29

צַדִּיקִיםrighteous יִירְשׁוּinherit אָרֶץland וְיִשְׁכְּנוּand dwell לָעַדin forever עָלֶיהָon her

The righteous will inherit the land and dwell in it forever.

37:30

פִּיmouth צַדִּיקrighteous יֶהְגֶּהutters חָכְמָהwisdom וּלְשׁוֹנוֹand his tongue תְּדַבֵּרspeaks מִשְׁפָּטjustice

The mouth of the righteous man utters wisdom, and his tongue speaks justice.

37:31

תּוֹרַתlaw אֱלֹהָיוhis God בְּלִבּוֹin his heart לֹאnot תִמְעַדfalter אֲשֻׁרָיוhis steps

The law of his God is in his heart; his steps do not falter.

37:32

צוֹפֶהwait רָשָׁעwicked לַצַּדִּיקfor the righteous וּמְבַקֵּשׁand seek לַהֲמִיתוֹto slay them

Though the wicked lie in wait for the righteous, and seek to slay them,

37:33

יְהוָהLORD לֹאnot יַעַזְבֶנּוּleave them בְיָדוֹin their power וְלֹאor יַרְשִׁיעֶנּוּthem be condemned בְּהִשָּׁפְטוֹunder judgment

the LORD will not leave them in their power or let them be condemned under judgment.

37:34

קַוֵּהwait אֶלto יְהוָהLORD וּשְׁמֹרand keep דַּרְכּוֹhis way וִירוֹמִמְךָraise you לָרֶשֶׁתto inherit אָרֶץland בְּהִכָּרֵתcut off רְשָׁעִיםwicked תִּרְאֶהsee

Wait for the LORD and keep His way, and He will raise you up to inherit the land. When the wicked are cut off, you will see it.

37:35

רָאִיתִיseen רָשָׁעwicked עָרִיץruthless וּמִתְעָרֶהflourishing כְּאֶזְרָחnative רַעֲנָןtree

I have seen a wicked, ruthless man flourishing like a well-rooted native tree,

37:36

וַיַּעֲבֹרyet he passed וְהִנֵּהand was אֵינֶנּוּno וָאֲבַקְשֵׁהוּthough I searched he וְלֹאnot נִמְצָאfound

yet he passed away and was no more; though I searched, he could not be found.

37:37

שְׁמָרconsider תָּםblameless וּרְאֵהand observe יָשָׁרupright כִּיfor אַחֲרִיתposterity לְאִישׁawaits the man שָׁלוֹםpeace

Consider the blameless and observe the upright, for posterity awaits the man of peace.

37:38

וּפֹשְׁעִיםbut the transgressors נִשְׁמְדוּdestroyed יַחְדָּוall אַחֲרִיתfuture רְשָׁעִיםwicked נִכְרָתָהcut off

But the transgressors will all be destroyed; the future of the wicked will be cut off.

37:39

וּתְשׁוּעַתsalvation צַדִּיקִיםrighteous מֵיְהוָהfrom the LORD מָעוּזָּםtheir stronghold בְּעֵתin time צָרָהtrouble

The salvation of the righteous is from the LORD; He is their stronghold in time of trouble.

37:40

וַיַּעְזְרֵםhelps יְהוָהLORD וַיְפַלְּטֵםand delivers them יְפַלְּטֵםrescues מֵרְשָׁעִיםfrom the wicked וְיוֹשִׁיעֵםand saves כִּיbecause חָסוּrefuge בוֹin him

The LORD helps and delivers them; He rescues and saves them from the wicked, because they take refuge in Him.
