Psalms 96

A new song for all the earth

Open in the interlinear reader

96:1

שִׁירוּsing לַיהוָהto the LORD שִׁירa song חָדָשׁnew שִׁירוּsing לַיהוָהto the LORD כָּלall הָאָרֶץthe earth

Sing to the LORD a new song; sing to the LORD, all the earth.

96:2

שִׁירוּsing לַיהוָהto the LORD בָּרֲכוּbless שְׁמוֹhis name בַּשְּׂרוּproclaim מִיּוֹםafter day לְיוֹםday יְשׁוּעָתוֹhis salvation

Sing to the LORD, bless His name; proclaim His salvation day after day.

96:3

סַפְּרוּdeclare בַגּוֹיִםamong the nations כְּבוֹדוֹhis glory בְּכָלamong all הָעַמִּיםpeoples נִפְלְאוֹתָיוhis wonderful deeds

Declare His glory among the nations, His wonderful deeds among all peoples.

96:4

כִּיfor גָדוֹלgreat יְהוָהLORD וּמְהֻלָּלpraised מְאֹדgreatly נוֹרָאfeared הוּאhe עַלon כָּלall אֱלֹהִיםgods

For great is the LORD, and greatly to be praised; He is to be feared above all gods.

96:5

כִּיfor כָּלall אֱלֹהֵיgods הָעַמִּיםthe nations אֱלִילִיםidols וַיהוָהLORD שָׁמַיִםheavens עָשָׂהmade

For all the gods of the nations are idols, but it is the LORD who made the heavens.

96:6

הוֹדsplendor וְהָדָרand majesty לְפָנָיוare before him עֹזstrength וְתִפְאֶרֶתand beauty בְּמִקְדָּשׁוֹfill his sanctuary

Splendor and majesty are before Him; strength and beauty fill His sanctuary.

96:7

הָבוּascribe לַיהוָהto the LORD מִשְׁפְּחוֹתfamilies עַמִּיםnations הָבוּascribe לַיהוָהto the LORD כָּבוֹדglory וָעֹזand strength

Ascribe to the LORD, O families of the nations, ascribe to the LORD glory and strength.

96:8

הָבוּascribe לַיהוָהto the LORD כְּבוֹדglory שְׁמוֹhis name שְׂאוּbring מִנְחָהoffering וּבֹאוּand enter לְחַצְרוֹתָיוhis courts

Ascribe to the LORD the glory due His name; bring an offering and enter His courts.

96:9

הִשְׁתַּחֲווּworship לַיהוָהthe LORD בְּהַדְרַתin the splendor קֹדֶשׁholiness חִילוּtremble מִפָּנָיוbefore him כָּלall הָאָרֶץthe earth

Worship the LORD in the splendor of His holiness; tremble before Him, all the earth.

96:10

אִמְרוּdeclare בַגּוֹיִםamong the nations יְהוָהLORD מָלָךְreigns אַףhow much תִּכּוֹןestablished תֵּבֵלworld בַּלcannot תִּמּוֹטmoved יָדִיןjudge עַמִּיםpeoples בְּמֵישָׁרִיםwith equity

Declare among the nations: “The LORD reigns!” The world is firmly established; it cannot be moved; He will judge the peoples with equity.

96:11

יִשְׂמְחוּlet הַשָּׁמַיִםthe heavens וְתָגֵלglad and הָאָרֶץthe earth יִרְעַםlet הַיָּםthe sea וּמְלֹאוֹfills it

Let the heavens be glad and the earth rejoice; let the sea resound, and all that fills it.

96:12

יַעֲלֹזexult שָׂדַיfields וְכָלand all אֲשֶׁרthat בּוֹin them אָזthen יְרַנְּנוּsing כָּלall עֲצֵיtrees יָעַרforest

Let the fields exult, and all that is in them. Then all the trees of the forest will sing for joy

96:13

לִפְנֵיbefore יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor בָאcoming כִּיfor בָאcoming לִשְׁפֹּטto judge הָאָרֶץthe earth יִשְׁפֹּטjudge תֵּבֵלworld בְּצֶדֶקin righteousness וְעַמִּיםand the peoples בֶּאֱמוּנָתוֹin his faithfulness

before the LORD, for He is coming— He is coming to judge the earth. He will judge the world in righteousness and the peoples in His faithfulness.
