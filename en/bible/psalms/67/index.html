Psalms 67

A blessing so that the peoples praise

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. With stringed instruments. A Psalm. A song

67:1

אֱלֹהִיםGod יְחָנֵּנוּgracious to us וִיבָרְכֵנוּand bless us יָאֵרshine פָּנָיוhis face אִתָּנוּto סֶלָהSelah

May God be gracious to us and bless us, and cause His face to shine upon us, Selah

67:2

לָדַעַתknown בָּאָרֶץon earth דַּרְכֶּךָyour ways בְּכָלamong all גּוֹיִםnations יְשׁוּעָתֶךָyour salvation

that Your ways may be known on earth, Your salvation among all nations.

67:3

יוֹדוּךָpraise you עַמִּיםpeoples אֱלֹהִיםGod יוֹדוּךָpraise you עַמִּיםpeoples כֻּלָּםall

Let the peoples praise You, O God; let all the peoples praise You.

67:4

יִשְׂמְחוּglad וִירַנְּנוּand sing לְאֻמִּיםnations כִּיfor תִשְׁפֹּטjudge עַמִּיםpeoples מִישׁוֹרjustly וּלְאֻמִּיםnations בָּאָרֶץthe earth תַּנְחֵםlead סֶלָהSelah

Let the nations be glad and sing for joy, for You judge the peoples justly and lead the nations of the earth. Selah

67:5

יוֹדוּךָpraise you עַמִּיםpeoples אֱלֹהִיםGod יוֹדוּךָpraise you עַמִּיםpeoples כֻּלָּםall

Let the peoples praise You, O God; let all the peoples praise You.

67:6

אֶרֶץearth נָתְנָהgave יְבוּלָהּyielded its harvest יְבָרְכֵנוּour blesses אֱלֹהִיםGod אֱלֹהֵינוּour God

The earth has yielded its harvest; God, our God, blesses us.

67:7

יְבָרְכֵנוּblesses us אֱלֹהִיםGod וְיִירְאוּshall fear אֹתוֹhim כָּלall אַפְסֵיends אָרֶץearth

God blesses us, that all the ends of the earth shall fear Him.
