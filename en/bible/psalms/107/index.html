Psalms 107

Redeemed from four dangers

Open in the interlinear reader

107:1

הֹדוּgive thanks לַיהוָהto the LORD כִּיfor טוֹבgood כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good; His loving devotion endures forever.

107:2

יֹאמְרוּlet גְּאוּלֵיredeemed יְהוָהLORD אֲשֶׁרwhom גְּאָלָםredeemed מִיַּדfrom the hand צָרenemy

Let the redeemed of the LORD say so, whom He has redeemed from the hand of the enemy

107:3

וּמֵאֲרָצוֹתand from the lands קִבְּצָםgathered מִמִּזְרָחfrom east וּמִמַּעֲרָבand west מִצָּפוֹןfrom north וּמִיָּםand south

and gathered from the lands, from east and west, from north and south.

107:4

תָּעוּwandered בַמִּדְבָּרin desert בִּישִׁימוֹןdesert wastelands דָּרֶךְpath עִירcity מוֹשָׁבwhere לֹאno מָצָאוּfinding

Some wandered in desert wastelands, finding no path to a city in which to dwell.

107:5

רְעֵבִיםhungry גַּםand צְמֵאִיםthirsty נַפְשָׁםtheir soul בָּהֶםin them תִּתְעַטָּףfainted

They were hungry and thirsty; their soul fainted within them.

107:6

וַיִּצְעֲקוּthen they cried out אֶלto יְהוָהLORD בַּצַּרin their trouble לָהֶםto them מִמְּצוּקוֹתֵיהֶםfrom distress יַצִּילֵםdelivered them

Then they cried out to the LORD in their trouble, and He delivered them from their distress.

107:7

וַיַּדְרִיכֵםhe led them בְּדֶרֶךְpath יְשָׁרָהstraight לָלֶכֶתto go אֶלto עִירcity מוֹשָׁבwhere

He led them on a straight path to reach a city where they could live.

107:8

יוֹדוּgive thanks לַיהוָהto the LORD חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion וְנִפְלְאוֹתָיוand his wonders לִבְנֵיto the sons אָדָםmen

Let them give thanks to the LORD for His loving devotion and His wonders to the sons of men.

107:9

כִּיfor הִשְׂבִּיעַsatisfies נֶפֶשׁlife שֹׁקֵקָהthirsty וְנֶפֶשׁand anyone רְעֵבָהhungry מִלֵּאfills טוֹבgood

For He satisfies the thirsty and fills the hungry with good things.

107:10

יֹשְׁבֵיsat חֹשֶׁךְdarkness וְצַלְמָוֶתshadow of death אֲסִירֵיprisoners עֳנִיaffliction וּבַרְזֶלand chains

Some sat in darkness and in the shadow of death, prisoners in affliction and chains,

107:11

כִּיbecause הִמְרוּrebelled against אִמְרֵיwords אֵלGod וַעֲצַתcounsel עֶלְיוֹןMost high נָאָצוּdespised

because they rebelled against the words of God and despised the counsel of the Most High.

107:12

וַיַּכְנַעhe humbled בֶּעָמָלwith hard labor לִבָּםtheir hearts כָּשְׁלוּstumbled וְאֵיןno עֹזֵרhelp

He humbled their hearts with hard labor; they stumbled, and there was no one to help.

107:13

וַיִּזְעֲקוּthen they cried out אֶלto יְהוָהLORD בַּצַּרin their trouble לָהֶםto them מִמְּצֻקוֹתֵיהֶםthem from distress יוֹשִׁיעֵםsaved

Then they cried out to the LORD in their trouble, and He saved them from their distress.

107:14

יוֹצִיאֵםbrought them מֵחֹשֶׁךְout of darkness וְצַלְמָוֶתand the shadow of death וּמוֹסְרוֹתֵיהֶםand broke their chains יְנַתֵּקaway

He brought them out of darkness and the shadow of death and broke away their chains.

107:15

יוֹדוּgive thanks לַיהוָהto the LORD חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion וְנִפְלְאוֹתָיוand his wonders לִבְנֵיto the sons אָדָםmen

Let them give thanks to the LORD for His loving devotion and His wonders to the sons of men.

107:16

כִּיfor שִׁבַּרbroken דַּלְתוֹתgates נְחֹשֶׁתbronze וּבְרִיחֵיbars בַרְזֶלiron גִּדֵּעַcut

For He has broken down the gates of bronze and cut through the bars of iron.

107:17

אֱוִלִיםfools מִדֶּרֶךְways פִּשְׁעָםtheir rebellious וּמֵעֲוֺנֹתֵיהֶםand through their iniquities יִתְעַנּוּsuffered

Fools, in their rebellious ways, and through their iniquities, suffered affliction.

107:18

כָּלall אֹכֶלfood תְּתַעֵבloathed נַפְשָׁםtheir soul וַיַּגִּיעוּand drew near עַדto שַׁעֲרֵיgates מָוֶתdeath

They loathed all food and drew near to the gates of death.

107:19

וַיִּזְעֲקוּthen they cried out אֶלto יְהוָהLORD בַּצַּרin their trouble לָהֶםto them מִמְּצֻקוֹתֵיהֶםfrom distress יוֹשִׁיעֵםsaved them

Then they cried out to the LORD in their trouble, and He saved them from their distress.

107:20

יִשְׁלַחsent דְּבָרוֹhis word וְיִרְפָּאֵםand healed them וִימַלֵּטhe rescued מִשְּׁחִיתוֹתָםthem from the pit

He sent forth His word and healed them; He rescued them from the Pit.

107:21

יוֹדוּgive thanks לַיהוָהto the LORD חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion וְנִפְלְאוֹתָיוand his wonders לִבְנֵיto the sons אָדָםmen

Let them give thanks to the LORD for His loving devotion and His wonders to the sons of men.

107:22

וְיִזְבְּחוּlet them offer זִבְחֵיsacrifices תוֹדָהthanksgiving וִיסַפְּרוּand declare מַעֲשָׂיוhis works בְּרִנָּהwith rejoicing

Let them offer sacrifices of thanksgiving and declare His works with rejoicing.

107:23

יוֹרְדֵיwent הַיָּםto sea בָּאֳנִיּוֹתin ships עֹשֵׂיconducting מְלָאכָהtrade בְּמַיִםwaters רַבִּיםmighty

Others went out to sea in ships, conducting trade on the mighty waters.

107:24

הֵמָּהthey רָאוּsaw מַעֲשֵׂיworks יְהוָהLORD וְנִפְלְאוֹתָיוand his wonders בִּמְצוּלָהin the deep

They saw the works of the LORD, and His wonders in the deep.

107:25

וַיֹּאמֶרhe spoke וַיַּעֲמֵדand raised רוּחַspirit סְעָרָהtempest וַתְּרוֹמֵםthat lifted גַּלָּיוwaves

For He spoke and raised a tempest that lifted the waves of the sea.

107:26

יַעֲלוּup שָׁמַיִםheavens יֵרְדוּsunk תְהוֹמוֹתdepths נַפְשָׁםtheir soul בְּרָעָהmelted in תִתְמוֹגָגcourage

They mounted up to the heavens, then sunk to the depths; their courage melted in their anguish.

107:27

יָחוֹגּוּreeled וְיָנוּעוּand staggered כַּשִּׁכּוֹרlike drunkards וְכָלand all חָכְמָתָםtheir skill תִּתְבַּלָּעuseless

They reeled and staggered like drunkards, and all their skill was useless.

107:28

וַיִּצְעֲקוּthen they cried out אֶלto יְהוָהLORD בַּצַּרin their trouble לָהֶםto them וּמִמְּצוּקֹתֵיהֶםout of their distress יוֹצִיאֵםbrought them

Then they cried out to the LORD in their trouble, and He brought them out of their distress.

107:29

יָקֵםcalmed סְעָרָהstorm לִדְמָמָהto a whisper וַיֶּחֱשׁוּsea גַּלֵּיהֶםwaves

He calmed the storm to a whisper, and the waves of the sea were hushed.

107:30

וַיִּשְׂמְחוּthey rejoiced כִיfor יִשְׁתֹּקוּsilence וַיַּנְחֵםand he guided them אֶלto מְחוֹזharbor חֶפְצָםthey desired

They rejoiced in the silence, and He guided them to the harbor they desired.

107:31

יוֹדוּgive thanks לַיהוָהto the LORD חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion וְנִפְלְאוֹתָיוand his wonders לִבְנֵיto the sons אָדָםmen

Let them give thanks to the LORD for His loving devotion and His wonders to the sons of men.

107:32

וִירֹמְמוּהוּlet them exalt him בִּקְהַלin the assembly עָםpeople וּבְמוֹשַׁבin the council זְקֵנִיםelders יְהַלְלוּהוּpraise him

Let them exalt Him in the assembly of the people and praise Him in the council of the elders.

107:33

יָשֵׂםturns נְהָרוֹתrivers לְמִדְבָּרinto deserts וּמֹצָאֵיsprings מַיִםwater לְצִמָּאוֹןinto thirsty ground

He turns rivers into deserts, springs of water into thirsty ground,

107:34

אֶרֶץland פְּרִיfruitful לִמְלֵחָהsalt מֵרָעַתwickedness יֹשְׁבֵיdwellers בָהּin her

and fruitful land into fields of salt, because of the wickedness of its dwellers.

107:35

יָשֵׂםturns מִדְבָּרdesert לַאֲגַםinto pools מַיִםwater וְאֶרֶץland צִיָּהdry לְמֹצָאֵיinto flowing מָיִםsprings

He turns a desert into pools of water and a dry land into flowing springs.

107:36

וַיּוֹשֶׁבsettle שָׁםthere רְעֵבִיםhungry וַיְכוֹנְנוּthey may establish עִירcity מוֹשָׁבwhere

He causes the hungry to settle there, that they may establish a city in which to dwell.

107:37

וַיִּזְרְעוּthey sow שָׂדוֹתfields וַיִּטְּעוּand plant כְרָמִיםvineyards וַיַּעֲשׂוּthat yield פְּרִיfruitful תְבוּאָהharvest

They sow fields and plant vineyards that yield a fruitful harvest.

107:38

וַיְבָרֲכֵםhe blesses them וַיִּרְבּוּand they multiply מְאֹדgreatly וּבְהֶמְתָּםtheir herds לֹאnot יַמְעִיטdiminish

He blesses them, and they multiply greatly; He does not let their herds diminish.

107:39

וַיִּמְעֲטוּdecreased וַיָּשֹׁחוּand humbled מֵעֹצֶרby oppression רָעָהevil וְיָגוֹןand sorrow

When they are decreased and humbled by oppression, evil, and sorrow,

107:40

שֹׁפֵךְpours בּוּזcontempt עַלon נְדִיבִיםnobles וַיַּתְעֵםand makes them wander בְּתֹהוּin a trackless לֹאnot דָרֶךְwasteland

He pours out contempt on the nobles and makes them wander in a trackless wasteland.

107:41

וַיְשַׂגֵּבbut he lifts אֶבְיוֹןneedy מֵעוֹנִיfrom affliction וַיָּשֶׂםand increases כַּצֹּאןtheir like flocks מִשְׁפָּחוֹתfamilies

But He lifts the needy from affliction and increases their families like flocks.

107:42

יִרְאוּsee יְשָׁרִיםupright וְיִשְׂמָחוּand rejoice וְכָלand all עַוְלָהiniquity קָפְצָהshuts פִּיהָits mouth

The upright see and rejoice, and all iniquity shuts its mouth.

107:43

מִיwho חָכָםwise וְיִשְׁמָרpay heed אֵלֶּהthese וְיִתְבּוֹנְנוּand consider חַסְדֵיloving devotion יְהוָהLORD

Let him who is wise pay heed to these things and consider the loving devotion of the LORD.
