Psalms 70

An urgent plea for help

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. Of David. To bring remembrance

70:1

אֱלֹהִיםGod לְהַצִּילֵנִיto deliver me יְהוָהLORD לְעֶזְרָתִיto help me חוּשָׁהhurry

Make haste, O God, to deliver me! Hurry, O LORD, to help me!

70:2

יֵבֹשׁוּashamed וְיַחְפְּרוּand confounded מְבַקְשֵׁיseek נַפְשִׁיmy life יִסֹּגוּwho אָחוֹרrepelled וְיִכָּלְמוּand humiliated חֲפֵצֵיwish רָעָתִיme harm

May those who seek my life be ashamed and confounded; may those who wish me harm be repelled and humiliated.

70:3

יָשׁוּבוּretreat עַלon עֵקֶבbecause בָּשְׁתָּםtheir shame הָאֹמְרִיםwho say הֶאָחaha הֶאָחaha

May those who say, “Aha, aha!” retreat because of their shame.

70:4

יָשִׂישׂוּrejoice וְיִשְׂמְחוּand be glad בְּךָin you כָּלall מְבַקְשֶׁיךָseek you וְיֹאמְרוּsay תָמִידalways יִגְדַּלmagnified אֱלֹהִיםGod אֹהֲבֵיlove יְשׁוּעָתֶךָyour salvation

May all who seek You rejoice and be glad in You; may those who love Your salvation always say, “Let God be magnified!”

70:5

וַאֲנִיbut I am עָנִיpoor וְאֶבְיוֹןand needy אֱלֹהִיםGod חוּשָׁהhurry לִּיto me עֶזְרִיmy help וּמְפַלְטִיand my deliverer אַתָּהyou יְהוָהLORD אַלdo not תְּאַחַרdelay

But I am poor and needy; hurry to me, O God. You are my help and my deliverer; O LORD, do not delay.
