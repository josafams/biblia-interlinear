Psalms 129

Israel afflicted but not defeated

Open in the interlinear reader

129:1

שִׁירsong הַמַּעֲלוֹתof ascents רַבַּתmany צְרָרוּנִיpersecuted me מִנְּעוּרַיfrom my youth יֹאמַרdeclare נָאlet now יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

A song of ascents. Many a time they have persecuted me from my youth— let Israel now declare—

129:2

רַבַּתmany צְרָרוּנִיpersecuted me מִנְּעוּרָיfrom my youth גַּםhave לֹאnot יָכְלוּprevailed לִיto me

many a time they have persecuted me from my youth, but they have not prevailed against me.

129:3

עַלover גַּבִּיplowmen חָרְשׁוּplowed חֹרְשִׁיםfurrows הֶאֱרִיכוּlong לְמַעֲנִיתָםtheir furrows

The plowmen plowed over my back; they made their furrows long.

129:4

יְהוָהLORD צַדִּיקrighteous קִצֵּץcut עֲבוֹתcords רְשָׁעִיםwicked

The LORD is righteous; He has cut me from the cords of the wicked.

129:5

יֵבֹשׁוּshame וְיִסֹּגוּturned אָחוֹרback כֹּלall שֹׂנְאֵיhate צִיּוֹןZion

May all who hate Zion be turned back in shame.

129:6

יִהְיוּbe כַּחֲצִירlike grass גַּגּוֹתrooftops שֶׁקַּדְמַתwhich withers שָׁלַףbefore יָבֵשׁwithers

May they be like grass on the rooftops, which withers before it can grow,

129:7

שֶׁלֹּאwho not מִלֵּאfill כַפּוֹhands קוֹצֵרreaper וְחִצְנוֹor the arms מְעַמֵּרsheaves

unable to fill the hands of the reaper, or the arms of the binder of sheaves.

129:8

וְלֹאand not אָמְרוּsay הָעֹבְרִיםwho pass בִּרְכַּתblessing יְהוָהLORD אֲלֵיכֶםto you בֵּרַכְנוּbless אֶתְכֶםyou בְּשֵׁםin the name יְהוָהLORD

May none who pass by say to them, “The blessing of the LORD be on you; we bless you in the name of the LORD.”
