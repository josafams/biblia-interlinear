Psalms 58

Against unjust judges

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. To the tune of “Do Not Destroy.” A Miktam of David

58:1

הַאֻמְנָםdo you indeed אֵלֶםjustly צֶדֶקjustly תְּדַבֵּרוּןspeak מֵישָׁרִיםuprightly תִּשְׁפְּטוּjudge בְּנֵיsons אָדָםmen

Do you indeed speak justly, O rulers? Do you judge uprightly, O sons of men?

58:2

אַףhow much בְּלֵבin your hearts עוֹלֹתinjustice תִּפְעָלוּןdevise בָּאָרֶץon the earth חֲמַסviolence יְדֵיכֶםhands you תְּפַלֵּסוּןmete

No, in your hearts you devise injustice; with your hands you mete out violence on the earth.

58:3

זֹרוּestranged רְשָׁעִיםwicked מֵרָחֶםfrom the womb תָּעוּastray מִבֶּטֶןfrom birth דֹּבְרֵיtell כָזָבliars

The wicked are estranged from the womb; the liars go astray from birth.

58:4

חֲמַתvenom לָמוֹto them כִּדְמוּתlike likeness חֲמַתvenom נָחָשׁsnake כְּמוֹlike פֶתֶןcobra חֵרֵשׁcobra that יַאְטֵםshuts אָזְנוֹits ears

Their venom is like the venom of a snake, like a cobra that shuts its ears,

58:5

אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot יִשְׁמַעhear לְקוֹלto voice מְלַחֲשִׁיםtune חוֹבֵרcharmer חֲבָרִיםweaves מְחֻכָּםskillfully

refusing to hear the tune of the charmer who skillfully weaves his spell.

58:6

אֱלֹהִיםGod הֲרָסshatter שִׁנֵּימוֹtheir teeth בְּפִימוֹin their mouths מַלְתְּעוֹתfangs כְּפִירִיםlions נְתֹץtear יְהוָהLORD

O God, shatter their teeth in their mouths; O LORD, tear out the fangs of the lions.

58:7

יִמָּאֲסוּvanish כְמוֹlike מַיִםwater יִתְהַלְּכוּruns לָמוֹto them יִדְרֹךְdraw חִצָּיוtheir arrows כְּמוֹlike יִתְמֹלָלוּblunted

May they vanish like water that runs off; when they draw the bow, may their arrows be blunted.

58:8

כְּמוֹlike שַׁבְּלוּלslug תֶּמֶסdissolves יַהֲלֹךְgo נֵפֶלstillborn child אֵשֶׁתwoman’s בַּלnever חָזוּsee שָׁמֶשׁsun

Like a slug that dissolves in its slime, like a woman’s stillborn child, may they never see the sun.

58:9

בְּטֶרֶםbefore יָבִינוּfeel סִּירֹתֵיכֶםyour pots אָטָדthornbush כְּמוֹlike חַיthorns כְּמוֹlike חָרוֹןburning יִשְׂעָרֶנּוּsweep them

Before your pots can feel the burning thorns— whether green or dry— He will sweep them away.

58:10

יִשְׂמַחrejoice צַדִּיקrighteous כִּיwhen חָזָהsee נָקָםavenged פְּעָמָיוtheir feet יִרְחַץwash בְּדַםin the blood הָרָשָׁעthe wicked

The righteous will rejoice when they see they are avenged; they will wash their feet in the blood of the wicked.

58:11

וְיֹאמַרsay אָדָםmen אַךְsurely פְּרִיreward לַצַּדִּיקfor the righteous אַךְsurely יֵשׁthere אֱלֹהִיםGod שֹׁפְטִיםjudges בָּאָרֶץthe earth

Then men will say, “There is surely a reward for the righteous! There is surely a God who judges the earth!”
