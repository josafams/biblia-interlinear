Psalms 103

The mercy and forgiveness of God

Open in the interlinear reader

103:1

לְדָוִדof David בָּרֲכִיbless נַפְשִׁיmy soul אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD וְכָלall קְרָבַיwithin me אֶת[obj] שֵׁםname קָדְשׁוֹbless his holy

Of David. Bless the LORD, O my soul; all that is within me, bless His holy name.

103:2

בָּרֲכִיbless נַפְשִׁיmy soul אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD וְאַלand do not תִּשְׁכְּחִיforget כָּלall גְּמוּלָיוhis kind deeds

Bless the LORD, O my soul, and do not forget all His kind deeds—

103:3

הַסֹּלֵחַwho forgives לְכָלall עֲוֺנֵכִיyour iniquities הָרֹפֵאand heals לְכָלall תַּחֲלֻאָיְכִיyour diseases

He who forgives all your iniquities and heals all your diseases,

103:4

הַגּוֹאֵלwho redeems מִשַּׁחַתfrom the pit חַיָּיְכִיyour life הַמְעַטְּרֵכִיand crowns you חֶסֶדloving devotion וְרַחֲמִיםand compassion

who redeems your life from the Pit and crowns you with loving devotion and compassion,

103:5

הַמַּשְׂבִּיַעwho satisfies בַּטּוֹבwith good עֶדְיֵךְthings תִּתְחַדֵּשׁrenewed כַּנֶּשֶׁרlike the eagle’s נְעוּרָיְכִיyouth

who satisfies you with good things, so that your youth is renewed like the eagle’s.

103:6

עֹשֵׂהexecutes צְדָקוֹתrighteousness יְהוָהLORD וּמִשְׁפָּטִיםand justice לְכָלfor all עֲשׁוּקִיםoppressed

The LORD executes righteousness and justice for all the oppressed.

103:7

יוֹדִיעַknown דְּרָכָיוhis ways לְמֹשֶׁהto Moses לִבְנֵיto the people יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael עֲלִילוֹתָיוhis deeds

He made known His ways to Moses, His deeds to the people of Israel.

103:8

רַחוּםcompassionate וְחַנּוּןand gracious יְהוָהLORD אֶרֶךְslow אַפַּיִםanger וְרַבabounding חָסֶדloving devotion

The LORD is compassionate and gracious, slow to anger, abounding in loving devotion.

103:9

לֹאnot לָנֶצַחalways יָרִיבaccuse וְלֹאand not לְעוֹלָםforever יִטּוֹרharbor

He will not always accuse us, nor harbor His anger forever.

103:10

לֹאnot כַחֲטָאֵינוּaccording to our sins עָשָׂהdealt לָנוּto us וְלֹאor כַעֲוֺנֹתֵינוּiniquities גָּמַלrepaid עָלֵינוּto our

He has not dealt with us according to our sins or repaid us according to our iniquities.

103:11

כִּיfor כִגְבֹהַּas high שָׁמַיִםheavens עַלon הָאָרֶץthe earth גָּבַרgreat חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion עַלfor יְרֵאָיוfear him

For as high as the heavens are above the earth, so great is His loving devotion for those who fear Him.

103:12

כִּרְחֹקas far מִזְרָחeast מִמַּעֲרָבfrom the west הִרְחִיקfar מִמֶּנּוּfrom us אֶת[obj] פְּשָׁעֵינוּtransgressions from us

As far as the east is from the west, so far has He removed our transgressions from us.

103:13

כְּרַחֵםcompassion אָבfather עַלon בָּנִיםchildren רִחַםcompassion יְהוָהLORD עַלon יְרֵאָיוfear him

As a father has compassion on his children, so the LORD has compassion on those who fear Him.

103:14

כִּיfor הוּאhe יָדַעknows יִצְרֵנוּour frame זָכוּרmindful כִּיthat עָפָרdust אֲנָחְנוּwe

For He knows our frame; He is mindful that we are dust.

103:15

אֱנוֹשׁman כֶּחָצִירlike grass יָמָיוhis days כְּצִיץblooms like a flower הַשָּׂדֶהthe field כֵּןso יָצִיץsprings

As for man, his days are like grass— he blooms like a flower of the field;

103:16

כִּיwhen רוּחַwind עָבְרָהpasses בּוֹin him וְאֵינֶנּוּand no him וְלֹאno יַכִּירֶנּוּremembers it עוֹדmore מְקוֹמוֹplace

when the wind passes over, it vanishes, and its place remembers it no more.

103:17

וְחֶסֶדloving devotion יְהוָהLORD מֵעוֹלָםfrom everlasting וְעַדto עוֹלָםeverlasting עַלto יְרֵאָיוfear him וְצִדְקָתוֹand his righteousness לִבְנֵיto their children’s בָנִיםchildren

But from everlasting to everlasting the loving devotion of the LORD extends to those who fear Him, and His righteousness to their children’s children—

103:18

לְשֹׁמְרֵיkeep בְרִיתוֹhis covenant וּלְזֹכְרֵיand remember פִקֻּדָיוhis precepts לַעֲשׂוֹתָםto obey

to those who keep His covenant and remember to obey His precepts.

103:19

יְהוָהLORD בַּשָּׁמַיִםin heaven הֵכִיןestablished כִּסְאוֹhis throne וּמַלְכוּתוֹand his kingdom בַּכֹּלover all מָשָׁלָהrules

The LORD has established His throne in heaven, and His kingdom rules over all.

103:20

בָּרֲכוּbless יְהוָהLORD מַלְאָכָיוhis angels גִּבֹּרֵיmighty כֹחַstrength עֹשֵׂיcarry דְבָרוֹhis word לִשְׁמֹעַhearken to בְּקוֹלvoice דְּבָרוֹhis command

Bless the LORD, all His angels mighty in strength who carry out His word, who hearken to the voice of His command.

103:21

בָּרֲכוּbless יְהוָהLORD כָּלall צְבָאָיוhis hosts מְשָׁרְתָיוservants who עֹשֵׂיdo רְצוֹנוֹhis delight

Bless the LORD, all His hosts, you servants who do His will.

103:22

בָּרֲכוּbless יְהוָהLORD כָּלall מַעֲשָׂיוhis works בְּכָלin all מְקֹמוֹתplaces מֶמְשַׁלְתּוֹhis dominion בָּרֲכִיbless נַפְשִׁיmy soul אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD

Bless the LORD, all His works in all places of His dominion. Bless the LORD, O my soul!
