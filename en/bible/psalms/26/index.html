Psalms 26

A man of integrity asks to be examined

Open in the interlinear reader

26:1

לְדָוִדof David שָׁפְטֵנִיvindicate me יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor אֲנִיI בְּתֻמִּיwith integrity I הָלַכְתִּיwalked וּבַיהוָהin the LORD בָּטַחְתִּיtrusted לֹאnot אֶמְעָדwavering

Of David. Vindicate me, O LORD! For I have walked with integrity; I have trusted in the LORD without wavering.

26:2

בְּחָנֵנִיtest me יְהוָהLORD וְנַסֵּנִיand try me צָרְפָהexamine כִלְיוֹתַיmy heart וְלִבִּיheart and

Test me, O LORD, and try me; examine my heart and mind.

26:3

כִּיfor חַסְדְּךָyour loving devotion לְנֶגֶדbefore עֵינָיmy eyes וְהִתְהַלַּכְתִּיwalked בַּאֲמִתֶּךָin your truth

For Your loving devotion is before my eyes, and I have walked in Your truth.

26:4

לֹאnot יָשַׁבְתִּיsit עִםwith מְתֵיmen שָׁוְאdeceitful וְעִםwith נַעֲלָמִיםhypocrites לֹאnot אָבוֹאcome

I do not sit with deceitful men, nor keep company with hypocrites.

26:5

שָׂנֵאתִיhate קְהַלmob מְרֵעִיםevildoers וְעִםwith רְשָׁעִיםwicked לֹאnot אֵשֵׁבsit

I hate the mob of evildoers, and refuse to sit with the wicked.

26:6

אֶרְחַץwash בְּנִקָּיוֹןin innocence כַּפָּיmy hands וַאֲסֹבְבָהmay go אֶת[obj] מִזְבַּחֲךָyour altar יְהוָהLORD

I wash my hands in innocence that I may go about Your altar, O LORD,

26:7

לַשְׁמִעַto raise בְּקוֹלvoice in תּוֹדָהthanksgiving וּלְסַפֵּרand declare כָּלall נִפְלְאוֹתֶיךָyour wonderful works

to raise my voice in thanksgiving and declare all Your wonderful works.

26:8

יְהוָהLORD אָהַבְתִּיlove מְעוֹןwhere בֵּיתֶךָhouse you וּמְקוֹםdwell מִשְׁכַּןwhere כְּבוֹדֶךָyour glory

O LORD, I love the house where You dwell, the place where Your glory resides.

26:9

אַלdo not תֶּאֱסֹףtake עִםwith חַטָּאִיםsinners נַפְשִׁיmy soul וְעִםwith אַנְשֵׁיmen דָמִיםbloodshed חַיָּיmy life

Do not take my soul away with sinners, or my life with men of bloodshed,

26:10

אֲשֶׁרthat בִּידֵיהֶםin hands are wicked זִמָּהschemes וִימִינָםwhose right hands are מָלְאָהfull שֹּׁחַדbribes

in whose hands are wicked schemes, whose right hands are full of bribes.

26:11

וַאֲנִיbut I בְּתֻמִּיwill with integrity אֵלֵךְwalk פְּדֵנִיredeem me וְחָנֵּנִיand be merciful to me

But I will walk with integrity; redeem me and be merciful to me.

26:12

רַגְלִיmy feet עָמְדָהstand בְמִישׁוֹרon level ground בְּמַקְהֵלִיםin the congregations אֲבָרֵךְbless יְהוָהLORD

My feet stand on level ground; in the congregations I will bless the LORD.
