Psalms 66

All the earth invited to praise

Open in the interlinear reader

66:1

לַמְנַצֵּחַfor the choirmaster שִׁירsong מִזְמוֹרPsalm הָרִיעוּjoyful noise לֵאלֹהִיםto God כָּלall הָאָרֶץthe earth

For the choirmaster. A song. A Psalm. Make a joyful noise to God, all the earth!

66:2

זַמְּרוּsing כְבוֹדglory שְׁמוֹhis name שִׂימוּmake כָבוֹדglorious תְּהִלָּתוֹhis praise

Sing the glory of His name; make His praise glorious.

66:3

אִמְרוּsay לֵאלֹהִיםto God מַהhow נּוֹרָאawesome מַעֲשֶׂיךָyour deeds בְּרֹבgreat עֻזְּךָyour power יְכַחֲשׁוּcower לְךָto you אֹיְבֶיךָenemies

Say to God, “How awesome are Your deeds! So great is Your power that Your enemies cower before You.

66:4

כָּלall הָאָרֶץthe earth יִשְׁתַּחֲווּbows down לְךָto you וִיזַמְּרוּthey sing לָךְto you יְזַמְּרוּsing שִׁמְךָyour name סֶלָהSelah

All the earth bows down to You; they sing praise to You; they sing praise to Your name.” Selah

66:5

לְכוּcome וּרְאוּand see מִפְעֲלוֹתworks אֱלֹהִיםGod נוֹרָאawesome עֲלִילָהdeeds עַלon בְּנֵיsons אָדָםmankind

Come and see the works of God; how awesome are His deeds toward mankind.

66:6

הָפַךְturned יָםsea לְיַבָּשָׁהinto dry land בַּנָּהָרthrough the waters יַעַבְרוּpassed בְרָגֶלon foot שָׁםthere נִשְׂמְחָהrejoiced בּוֹin him

He turned the sea into dry land; they passed through the waters on foot; there we rejoiced in Him.

66:7

מֹשֵׁלrules בִּגְבוּרָתוֹby his power עוֹלָםforever עֵינָיוhis eyes בַּגּוֹיִםthe nations תִּצְפֶּינָהwatch הַסּוֹרְרִיםthe rebellious אַלdo not יָרוּמוּexalt לָמוֹto them סֶלָהSelah

He rules forever by His power; His eyes watch the nations. Do not let the rebellious exalt themselves. Selah

66:8

בָּרְכוּbless עַמִּיםpeoples אֱלֹהֵינוּour God וְהַשְׁמִיעוּheard קוֹלsound תְּהִלָּתוֹhis praise

Bless our God, O peoples; let the sound of His praise be heard.

66:9

הַשָּׂםhe נַפְשֵׁנוּpreserves our בַּחַיִּיםlives וְלֹאand not נָתַןgiven לַמּוֹטkeeps רַגְלֵנוּour feet

He preserves our lives and keeps our feet from slipping.

66:10

כִּיfor בְחַנְתָּנוּtested us אֱלֹהִיםGod צְרַפְתָּנוּrefined us כִּצְרָףlike refined כָּסֶףsilver

For You, O God, have tested us; You have refined us like silver.

66:11

הֲבֵאתָנוּled us בַמְּצוּדָהinto the net שַׂמְתָּlaid מוּעָקָהburdens בְמָתְנֵינוּon our backs

You led us into the net; You laid burdens on our backs.

66:12

הִרְכַּבְתָּride אֱנוֹשׁmen לְרֹאשֵׁנוּover our heads we בָּאנוּwent בָאֵשׁthrough fire וּבַמַּיִםand water וַתּוֹצִיאֵנוּbut you brought us לָרְוָיָהinto abundance

You let men ride over our heads; we went through fire and water, but You brought us into abundance.

66:13

אָבוֹאenter בֵיתְךָyour house בְעוֹלוֹתwith burnt offerings אֲשַׁלֵּםfulfill לְךָto you נְדָרָיmy vows

I will enter Your house with burnt offerings; I will fulfill my vows to You—

66:14

אֲשֶׁרthat פָּצוּvows שְׂפָתָיmy lips וְדִבֶּרpromised and פִּיmy mouth בַּצַּרin distress לִיto me

the vows that my lips promised and my mouth spoke in my distress.

66:15

עֹלוֹתburnt מֵחִיםfatlings אַעֲלֶהoffer לָּךְto you עִםwith קְטֹרֶתfragrant אֵילִיםrams אֶעֱשֶׂהoffer בָקָרbulls עִםwith עַתּוּדִיםgoats סֶלָהSelah

I will offer You fatlings as burnt offerings, with the fragrant smoke of rams; I will offer bulls and goats. Selah

66:16

לְכוּcome שִׁמְעוּlisten וַאֲסַפְּרָהdeclare כָּלall יִרְאֵיfear אֱלֹהִיםGod אֲשֶׁרthat עָשָׂהdone לְנַפְשִׁיto my soul

Come and listen, all you who fear God, and I will declare what He has done for me.

66:17

אֵלָיוto him פִּיmy mouth קָרָאתִיcried וְרוֹמַםand praised תַּחַתunder לְשׁוֹנִיmy tongue

I cried out to Him with my mouth and praised Him with my tongue.

66:18

אָוֶןcherished iniquity אִםif רָאִיתִיhad בְלִבִּיin my heart לֹאnot יִשְׁמַעlistened אֲדֹנָיLord would

If I had cherished iniquity in my heart, the Lord would not have listened.

66:19

אָכֵןsurely שָׁמַעheard אֱלֹהִיםGod הִקְשִׁיבattended בְּקוֹלto the sound תְּפִלָּתִיmy prayer

But God has surely heard; He has attended to the sound of my prayer.

66:20

בָּרוּךְblessed אֱלֹהִיםGod אֲשֶׁרwho לֹאnot הֵסִירrejected תְּפִלָּתִיmy prayer וְחַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion מֵאִתִּיfrom the me

Blessed be God, who has not rejected my prayer or withheld from me His loving devotion!
