Psalms 14

The folly of those who deny God

Open in the interlinear reader

14:1

לַמְנַצֵּחַfor the choirmaster לְדָוִדof David אָמַרsays נָבָלfool בְּלִבּוֹin his heart אֵיןno אֱלֹהִיםGod הִשְׁחִיתוּcorrupt הִתְעִיבוּvile עֲלִילָהacts אֵיןno עֹשֵׂהdoes טוֹבgood

For the choirmaster. Of David. The fool says in his heart, “There is no God.” They are corrupt; their acts are vile. There is no one who does good.

14:2

יְהוָהLORD מִשָּׁמַיִםfrom heaven הִשְׁקִיףlooks down עַלupon בְּנֵיsons אָדָםmen לִרְאוֹתto see הֲיֵשׁif any מַשְׂכִּילunderstand דֹּרֵשׁseek אֶת[obj] אֱלֹהִיםGod

The LORD looks down from heaven upon the sons of men to see if any understand, if any seek God.

14:3

הַכֹּלall סָרturned יַחְדָּוtogether נֶאֱלָחוּbecome corrupt אֵיןno עֹשֵׂהdoes טוֹבgood אֵיןnot גַּםeven אֶחָדone

All have turned away, they have together become corrupt; there is no one who does good, not even one.

14:4

הֲלֹאnever יָדְעוּlearn כָּלall פֹּעֲלֵיworkers אָוֶןiniquity אֹכְלֵיdevour עַמִּיmy people אָכְלוּlike לֶחֶםbread יְהוָהLORD לֹאnot קָרָאוּcall

Will the workers of iniquity never learn? They devour my people like bread; they refuse to call upon the LORD.

14:5

שָׁםthere פָּחֲדוּoverwhelmed פָחַדdread כִּיfor אֱלֹהִיםGod בְּדוֹרin the company צַדִּיקrighteous

There they are, overwhelmed with dread, for God is in the company of the righteous.

14:6

עֲצַתplans עָנִיoppressed תָבִישׁוּyet כִּיfor יְהוָהLORD מַחְסֵהוּtheir shelter

You sinners frustrate the plans of the oppressed, yet the LORD is their shelter.

14:7

מִיwho יִתֵּןgive מִצִּיּוֹןfrom Zion יְשׁוּעַתsalvation יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael בְּשׁוּבwould come יְהוָהLORD שְׁבוּתrestores captive עַמּוֹhis people יָגֵלlet rejoice יַעֲקֹבJacob יִשְׂמַחrejoice let יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

Oh, that the salvation of Israel would come from Zion! When the LORD restores His captive people, let Jacob rejoice, let Israel be glad!
