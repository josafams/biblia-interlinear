Psalms 81

Call to the feast and appeal to obedience

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. According to Gittith. Of Asaph

81:1

הַרְנִינוּsing joy לֵאלֹהִיםfor to God עוּזֵּנוּour strength הָרִיעוּjoyful noise לֵאלֹהֵיto the God יַעֲקֹבJacob

Sing for joy to God our strength; make a joyful noise to the God of Jacob.

81:2

שְׂאוּlift זִמְרָהsong strike וּתְנוּand give תֹףtambourine כִּנּוֹרlyre נָעִיםsweet עִםwith נָבֶלharp

Lift up a song, strike the tambourine, play the sweet-sounding harp and lyre.

81:3

תִּקְעוּsound בַחֹדֶשׁat the new שׁוֹפָרram’s horn בַּכֵּסֶהat the full moon לְיוֹםon the day חַגֵּנוּour feast

Sound the ram’s horn at the New Moon, and at the full moon on the day of our Feast.

81:4

כִּיfor חֹקstatute לְיִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael הוּאhe מִשְׁפָּטordinance לֵאלֹהֵיGod יַעֲקֹבJacob

For this is a statute for Israel, an ordinance of the God of Jacob.

81:5

עֵדוּתtestimony בִּיהוֹסֵףfor Joseph שָׂמוֹJoseph he בְּצֵאתוֹwhen went out עַלover אֶרֶץland מִצְרָיִםEgypt שְׂפַתlanguage לֹאnot יָדַעְתִּיunfamiliar אֶשְׁמָעheard

He ordained it as a testimony for Joseph when he went out over the land of Egypt, where I heard an unfamiliar language:

81:6

הֲסִירוֹתִיremoved מִסֵּבֶלrelieved שִׁכְמוֹhis shoulder כַּפָּיוhis hands were freed מִדּוּדfrom the basket תַּעֲבֹרְנָהcross

“I relieved his shoulder of the burden; his hands were freed from the basket.

81:7

בַּצָּרָהin distress קָרָאתָcalled וָאֲחַלְּצֶךָּand I rescued you אֶעֶנְךָanswered you בְּסֵתֶרfrom the cloud רַעַםthunder אֶבְחָנְךָtested you עַלon מֵיwaters מְרִיבָהMeribah סֶלָהSelah

You called out in distress, and I rescued you; I answered you from the cloud of thunder; I tested you at the waters of Meribah. Selah

81:8

שְׁמַעhear עַמִּיmy people וְאָעִידָהwarn בָּךְin you יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael אִםif תִּשְׁמַעlisten לִיto me

Hear, O My people, and I will warn you: O Israel, if only you would listen to Me!

81:9

לֹאno יִהְיֶהbe בְךָamong you אֵלgod זָרstrange וְלֹאand not תִשְׁתַּחֲוֶהbow לְאֵלgod נֵכָרforeign

There must be no strange god among you, nor shall you bow to a foreign god.

81:10

אָנֹכִיam יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God הַמַּעַלְךָwho brought you מֵאֶרֶץfrom land מִצְרָיִםEgypt הַרְחֶבopen wide פִּיךָyour mouth וַאֲמַלְאֵהוּfill it

I am the LORD your God, who brought you up out of Egypt. Open wide your mouth, and I will fill it.

81:11

וְלֹאwould not שָׁמַעlisten עַמִּיmy people לְקוֹלִיto my voice וְיִשְׂרָאֵלand Israel לֹאnot אָבָהwould obey לִיto me

But My people would not listen to Me, and Israel would not obey Me.

81:12

וָאֲשַׁלְּחֵהוּso I gave them בִּשְׁרִירוּתto stubborn לִבָּםhearts יֵלְכוּfollow בְּמוֹעֲצוֹתֵיהֶםtheir own devices

So I gave them up to their stubborn hearts to follow their own devices.

81:13

לוּif only עַמִּיmy people שֹׁמֵעַlisten לִיto me יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael בִּדְרָכַיwould my ways יְהַלֵּכוּfollow

If only My people would listen to Me, if Israel would follow My ways,

81:14

כִּמְעַטsoon I would אוֹיְבֵיהֶםtheir enemies אַכְנִיעַsubdue וְעַלagainst צָרֵיהֶםtheir foes אָשִׁיבturn יָדִיmy hand

how soon I would subdue their enemies and turn My hand against their foes!

81:15

מְשַׂנְאֵיhate יְהוָהLORD יְכַחֲשׁוּfeign לוֹto him וִיהִיlast עִתָּםtheir doom לְעוֹלָםwould forever

Those who hate the LORD would feign obedience, and their doom would last forever.

81:16

וַיַּאֲכִילֵהוּbut I would feed מֵחֵלֶבthe finest חִטָּהwheat וּמִצּוּרwith from the rock דְּבַשׁhoney אַשְׂבִּיעֶךָsatisfy you

But I would feed you the finest wheat; with honey from the rock I would satisfy you.”
