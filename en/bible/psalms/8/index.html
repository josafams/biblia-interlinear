Psalms 8

The glory of God and human dignity

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. According to Gittith. A Psalm of David

8:1

יְהוָהLORD אֲדֹנֵינוּour Lord מָהhow אַדִּירmajestic שִׁמְךָyour name בְּכָלin all הָאָרֶץthe earth אֲשֶׁרhave תְּנָהset הוֹדְךָyour glory עַלon הַשָּׁמָיִםthe heavens

O LORD, our Lord, how majestic is Your name in all the earth! You have set Your glory above the heavens.

8:2

מִפִּיfrom the mouths עוֹלְלִיםchildren וְיֹנְקִיםand infants יִסַּדְתָּordained עֹזpraise לְמַעַןso צוֹרְרֶיךָyour adversaries לְהַשְׁבִּיתto silence אוֹיֵבenemy וּמִתְנַקֵּםand avenger

From the mouths of children and infants You have ordained praise on account of Your adversaries, to silence the enemy and avenger.

8:3

כִּיwhen אֶרְאֶהbehold שָׁמֶיךָyour heavens מַעֲשֵׂיwork אֶצְבְּעֹתֶיךָyour fingers יָרֵחַmoon וְכוֹכָבִיםand the stars אֲשֶׁרwhich כּוֹנָנְתָּהset

When I behold Your heavens, the work of Your fingers, the moon and the stars, which You have set in place—

8:4

מָהwhat אֱנוֹשׁman כִּיthat תִזְכְּרֶנּוּmindful of him וּבֶןor the son אָדָםman כִּיthat תִפְקְדֶנּוּcare for him

what is man that You are mindful of him, or the son of man that You care for him?

8:5

וַתְּחַסְּרֵהוּmade him מְּעַטlittle מֵאֱלֹהִיםthan the angels וְכָבוֹדwith glory וְהָדָרand honor תְּעַטְּרֵהוּcrowned him

You made him a little lower than the angels; You crowned him with glory and honor.

8:6

תַּמְשִׁילֵהוּhim ruler בְּמַעֲשֵׂיthe works יָדֶיךָhands you כֹּלeverything שַׁתָּהplaced תַחַתunder רַגְלָיוhis feet

You made him ruler of the works of Your hands; You have placed everything under his feet:

8:7

צֹנֶהsheep וַאֲלָפִיםand oxen כֻּלָּםall וְגַםand even בַּהֲמוֹתbeasts שָׂדָיfield

all sheep and oxen, and even the beasts of the field,

8:8

צִפּוֹרbirds שָׁמַיִםair וּדְגֵיand the fish הַיָּםthe sea עֹבֵרswim אָרְחוֹתpaths יַמִּיםseas

the birds of the air and the fish of the sea, all that swim the paths of the seas.

8:9

יְהוָהLORD אֲדֹנֵינוּLORD our Lord מָהhow אַדִּירmajestic שִׁמְךָyour name בְּכָלin all הָאָרֶץthe earth

O LORD, our Lord, how majestic is Your name in all the earth!
