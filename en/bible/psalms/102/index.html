Psalms 102

The lament of the afflicted and hope for Zion

Open in the interlinear reader

A prayer of one who is afflicted, when he grows faint and pours out his lament before the LORD

102:1

יְהוָהLORD שִׁמְעָהhear תְפִלָּתִיmy prayer וְשַׁוְעָתִיmy cry for help אֵלֶיךָto you תָבוֹאcome

Hear my prayer, O LORD; let my cry for help come before You.

102:2

אַלdo not תַּסְתֵּרhide פָּנֶיךָyour face מִמֶּנִּיfrom me בְּיוֹםin day צַרdistress לִיto me הַטֵּהincline אֵלַיto me אָזְנֶךָyour ear בְּיוֹםin day אֶקְרָאcall מַהֵרquickly עֲנֵנִיanswer me

Do not hide Your face from me in my day of distress. Incline Your ear to me; answer me quickly when I call.

102:3

כִּיfor כָלוּvanish בְעָשָׁןlike smoke יָמָיmy days וְעַצְמוֹתַיand my bones כְּמוֹlike קֵדglowing נִחָרוּburn

For my days vanish like smoke, and my bones burn like glowing embers.

102:4

הוּכָּהafflicted כָעֵשֶׂבlike grass וַיִּבַשׁand withered לִבִּיmy heart כִּיfor שָׁכַחְתִּיforget מֵאֲכֹלto eat לַחְמִיmy bread

My heart is afflicted, and withered like grass; I even forget to eat my bread.

102:5

מִקּוֹלthrough loud אַנְחָתִיgroaning my דָּבְקָהclings עַצְמִיbones לִבְשָׂרִיflesh to my

Through my loud groaning my flesh clings to my bones.

102:6

דָּמִיתִיlike לִקְאַתa owl מִדְבָּרdesert הָיִיתִיam כְּכוֹסlike an owl חֳרָבוֹתruins

I am like a desert owl, like an owl among the ruins.

102:7

שָׁקַדְתִּיlie awake וָאֶהְיֶהI am כְּצִפּוֹרbird בּוֹדֵדlone עַלon גָּגhousetop

I lie awake; I am like a lone bird on a housetop.

102:8

כָּלall הַיּוֹםday long חֵרְפוּנִיmy taunt אוֹיְבָיenemies me מְהוֹלָלַיridicule me בִּיin me נִשְׁבָּעוּcurse

All day long my enemies taunt me; they ridicule me and curse me.

102:9

כִּיfor אֵפֶרashes כַּלֶּחֶםlike bread אָכָלְתִּיeaten וְשִׁקֻּוַיand mixed my בִּבְכִיdrink with tears מָסָכְתִּיmixed

For I have eaten ashes like bread and mixed my drink with tears

102:10

מִפְּנֵיfrom before זַעַמְךָyour indignation וְקִצְפֶּךָand wrath you כִּיfor נְשָׂאתַנִיpicked me וַתַּשְׁלִיכֵנִיand cast me

because of Your indignation and wrath, for You have picked me up and cast me aside.

102:11

יָמַיmy days כְּצֵלlike lengthening נָטוּיshadows וַאֲנִיand I כָּעֵשֶׂבlike grass אִיבָשׁwither

My days are like lengthening shadows, and I wither away like grass.

102:12

וְאַתָּהbut you יְהוָהLORD לְעוֹלָםforever תֵּשֵׁבsit enthroned וְזִכְרְךָyour renown לְדֹרendures to all וָדֹרgenerations

But You, O LORD, sit enthroned forever; Your renown endures to all generations.

102:13

אַתָּהyou תָקוּםrise תְּרַחֵםcompassion on צִיּוֹןZion כִּיfor עֵתtime לְחֶנְנָהּto show her favor כִּיfor בָאcome מוֹעֵדappointed time

You will rise up and have compassion on Zion, for it is time to show her favor— the appointed time has come.

102:14

כִּיfor רָצוּdelight עֲבָדֶיךָyour servants אֶת[obj] אֲבָנֶיהָher stones וְאֶתand [obj] עֲפָרָהּher dust יְחֹנֵנוּpity

For Your servants delight in her stones and take pity on her dust.

102:15

וְיִירְאוּfear גוֹיִםnations אֶת[obj] שֵׁםname יְהוָהLORD וְכָלand all מַלְכֵיkings הָאָרֶץthe earth אֶת[obj] כְּבוֹדֶךָyour glory

So the nations will fear the name of the LORD, and all the kings of the earth will fear Your glory.

102:16

כִּיfor בָנָהrebuild יְהוָהLORD צִיּוֹןZion נִרְאָהappeared בִּכְבוֹדוֹin his glory

For the LORD will rebuild Zion; He has appeared in His glory.

102:17

פָּנָהturn אֶלto תְּפִלַּתprayer הָעַרְעָרthe destitute וְלֹאhe will not בָזָהdespise אֶת[obj] תְּפִלָּתָםtheir prayer

He will turn toward the prayer of the destitute; He will not despise their prayer.

102:18

תִּכָּתֶבwritten זֹאתthis לְדוֹרfor the generation אַחֲרוֹןcome וְעַםpeople נִבְרָאcreated יְהַלֶּלpraise יָהּLORD

Let this be written for the generation to come, so that a people not yet created may praise the LORD.

102:19

כִּיfor הִשְׁקִיףlooked down מִמְּרוֹםfrom the heights קָדְשׁוֹhis sanctuary יְהוָהLORD מִשָּׁמַיִםfrom heaven אֶלto אֶרֶץearth הִבִּיטgazed

For He looked down from the heights of His sanctuary; the LORD gazed out from heaven to earth

102:20

לִשְׁמֹעַto hear אֶנְקַתgroaning אָסִירprisoner’s לְפַתֵּחַto release בְּנֵיthose תְמוּתָהcondemned

to hear a prisoner’s groaning, to release those condemned to death,

102:21

לְסַפֵּרmay proclaim בְּצִיּוֹןin Zion שֵׁםname יְהוָהLORD וּתְהִלָּתוֹand praise him בִּירוּשָׁלִָםin Jerusalem

that they may proclaim the name of the LORD in Zion and praise Him in Jerusalem,

102:22

בְּהִקָּבֵץassemble עַמִּיםpeoples יַחְדָּוtogether וּמַמְלָכוֹתand kingdoms לַעֲבֹדto serve אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD

when peoples and kingdoms assemble to serve the LORD.

102:23

עִנָּהbroken בַדֶּרֶךְon the way כֹּחִיmy strength קִצַּרshort יָמָיmy days

He has broken my strength on the way; He has cut short my days.

102:24

אֹמַרsay אֵלִיmy God אַלdo not תַּעֲלֵנִיlead up me בַּחֲצִיin the midst יָמָיmy days בְּדוֹרthrough all דּוֹרִיםgenerations שְׁנוֹתֶיךָyour years

I say: “O my God, do not take me in the midst of my days! Your years go on through all generations.

102:25

לְפָנִיםto formerly הָאָרֶץearth יָסַדְתָּfoundations וּמַעֲשֵׂהwork יָדֶיךָyour hands שָׁמָיִםheavens

In the beginning You laid the foundations of the earth, and the heavens are the work of Your hands.

102:26

הֵמָּהthey יֹאבֵדוּperish וְאַתָּהbut you תַעֲמֹדremain וְכֻלָּםthey will all כַּבֶּגֶדlike a garment יִבְלוּwear כַּלְּבוּשׁlike clothing תַּחֲלִיפֵםchange them וְיַחֲלֹפוּand they will

They will perish, but You remain; they will all wear out like a garment. Like clothing You will change them, and they will be passed on.

102:27

וְאַתָּהbut you הוּאhe וּשְׁנוֹתֶיךָand your years לֹאnever יִתָּמּוּend

But You remain the same, and Your years will never end.

102:28

בְּנֵיchildren עֲבָדֶיךָyour servants יִשְׁכּוֹנוּdwell וְזַרְעָםand their descendants לְפָנֶיךָbefore you יִכּוֹןestablished

The children of Your servants will dwell securely, and their descendants will be established before You.”
