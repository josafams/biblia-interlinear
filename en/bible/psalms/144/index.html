Psalms 144

The king asks for victory and prosperity

Open in the interlinear reader

144:1

לְדָוִדDavid בָּרוּךְblessed יְהוָהLORD צוּרִיmy rock הַמְלַמֵּדwho trains יָדַיmy hands לַקְרָבfor war אֶצְבְּעוֹתַיmy fingers לַמִּלְחָמָהfor battle

Of David. Blessed be the LORD, my Rock, who trains my hands for war, my fingers for battle.

144:2

חַסְדִּיlove my וּמְצוּדָתִיand fortress my מִשְׂגַּבִּיstronghold וּמְפַלְטִיand my deliverer לִיto me מָגִנִּיshield וּבוֹin whom חָסִיתִיrefuge הָרוֹדֵדwho subdues עַמִּיpeoples תַחְתָּיunder me

He is my steadfast love and my fortress, my stronghold and my deliverer. He is my shield, in whom I take refuge, who subdues peoples under me.

144:3

יְהוָהLORD מָהwhat אָדָםman וַתֵּדָעֵהוּand knew him בֶּןson אֱנוֹשׁman וַתְּחַשְּׁבֵהוּthink of him

O LORD, what is man, that You regard him, the son of man that You think of him?

144:4

אָדָםman לַהֶבֶלa breath דָּמָהlike יָמָיוhis days כְּצֵלshadow עוֹבֵרpassing

Man is like a breath; his days are like a passing shadow.

144:5

יְהוָהLORD הַטpart שָׁמֶיךָyour heavens O וְתֵרֵדand come down גַּעtouch בֶּהָרִיםthe mountains וְיֶעֱשָׁנוּthey may smoke

Part Your heavens, O LORD, and come down; touch the mountains, that they may smoke.

144:6

בְּרוֹקflash בָּרָקlightning וּתְפִיצֵםand scatter them שְׁלַחsend חִצֶּיךָshoot your arrows וּתְהֻמֵּםand rout them

Flash forth Your lightning and scatter them; shoot Your arrows and rout them.

144:7

שְׁלַחreach יָדֶיךָyour hands מִמָּרוֹםfrom on high פְּצֵנִיset me וְהַצִּילֵנִיand rescue me מִמַּיִםfrom the deep waters רַבִּיםmany מִיַּדfrom the grasp בְּנֵיsons נֵכָרforeigners

Reach down from on high; set me free and rescue me from the deep waters, from the grasp of foreigners,

144:8

אֲשֶׁרthat פִּיהֶםmouths דִּבֶּרspeak שָׁוְאfalsehood וִימִינָםwhose right יְמִיןhands שָׁקֶרdeceitful

whose mouths speak falsehood, whose right hands are deceitful.

144:9

אֱלֹהִיםGod שִׁירsong חָדָשׁnew אָשִׁירָהsing לָּךְto you בְּנֵבֶלon a harp עָשׂוֹרten אֲזַמְּרָהstrings לָּךְto you

I will sing to You a new song, O God; on a harp of ten strings I will make music to You—

144:10

הַנּוֹתֵןwho gives תְּשׁוּעָהvictory לַמְּלָכִיםto kings הַפּוֹצֶהwho frees אֶת[obj] דָּוִדDavid עַבְדּוֹhis servant מֵחֶרֶבsword רָעָהdeadly

to Him who gives victory to kings, who frees His servant David from the deadly sword.

144:11

פְּצֵנִיset me וְהַצִּילֵנִיand rescue me מִיַּדfrom the grasp בְּנֵיsons נֵכָרforeigners אֲשֶׁרthat פִּיהֶםmouths דִּבֶּרspeak שָׁוְאfalsehood וִימִינָםwhose right יְמִיןhands שָׁקֶרdeceitful

Set me free and rescue me from the grasp of foreigners, whose mouths speak falsehood, whose right hands are deceitful.

144:12

אֲשֶׁרthat בָּנֵינוּour sons כִּנְטִעִיםlike plants מְגֻדָּלִיםnurtured בִּנְעוּרֵיהֶםin their youth בְּנוֹתֵינוּour daughters כְזָוִיֹּתlike corner מְחֻטָּבוֹתpillars carved תַּבְנִיתcarved הֵיכָלpalace

Then our sons will be like plants nurtured in their youth, our daughters like corner pillars carved to adorn a palace.

144:13

מְזָוֵינוּour storehouses מְלֵאִיםfull מְפִיקִיםsupplying מִזַּןall manner אֶלto זַןproduce צֹאונֵנוּour flocks מַאֲלִיפוֹתthousands מְרֻבָּבוֹתtens of thousands בְּחוּצוֹתֵינוּin our fields

Our storehouses will be full, supplying all manner of produce; our flocks will bring forth thousands, tens of thousands in our fields.

144:14

אַלּוּפֵינוּour oxen מְסֻבָּלִיםbear אֵיןno פֶּרֶץbreach וְאֵיןno יוֹצֵאתgoing וְאֵיןand no צְוָחָהcry בִּרְחֹבֹתֵינוּour streets

Our oxen will bear great loads. There will be no breach in the walls, no going into captivity, and no cry of lament in our streets.

144:15

אַשְׁרֵיblessed הָעָםthe people שֶׁכָּכָהwhom this is לּוֹto him אַשְׁרֵיblessed הָעָםthe people שֶׁיֲהוָהLORD אֱלֹהָיוGod

Blessed are the people of whom this is so; blessed are the people whose God is the LORD.
