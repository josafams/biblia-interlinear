Psalms 64

Against conspirators with sharp tongues

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David

64:1

שְׁמַעhear אֱלֹהִיםGod קוֹלִיmy voice בְשִׂיחִיcomplaint מִפַּחַדfrom dread אוֹיֵבenemy תִּצֹּרpreserve חַיָּיmy life

Hear, O God, my voice of complaint; preserve my life from dread of the enemy.

64:2

תַּסְתִּירֵנִיhide me מִסּוֹדfrom the scheming מְרֵעִיםwicked מֵרִגְשַׁתfrom the mob פֹּעֲלֵיworkers אָוֶןiniquity

Hide me from the scheming of the wicked, from the mob of workers of iniquity,

64:3

אֲשֶׁרwho שָׁנְנוּsharpen כַחֶרֶבlike swords לְשׁוֹנָםtheir tongues דָּרְכוּaim חִצָּםarrows דָּבָרwords מָרbitter

who sharpen their tongues like swords and aim their bitter words like arrows,

64:4

לִירוֹתambushing בַּמִּסְתָּרִיםthe innocent in תָּםseclusion פִּתְאֹםsuddenly יֹרֻהוּshooting וְלֹאand not יִירָאוּfear

ambushing the innocent in seclusion, shooting suddenly, without fear.

64:5

יְחַזְּקוּhold fast לָמוֹto their דָּבָרword רָעevil יְסַפְּרוּspeak לִטְמוֹןof hiding מוֹקְשִׁיםsnares אָמְרוּsay מִיwho יִרְאֶהsee לָּמוֹto them

They hold fast to their evil purpose; they speak of hiding their snares. “Who will see them?” they say.

64:6

יַחְפְּשׂוּdevise עוֹלֹתinjustice תַּמְנוּfinished חֵפֶשׂperfected מְחֻפָּשׂsecret וְקֶרֶבinner אִישׁman וְלֵבand the heart עָמֹקmysterious

They devise injustice and say, “We have perfected a secret plan.” For the inner man and the heart are mysterious.

64:7

וַיֹּרֵםshoot them אֱלֹהִיםGod חֵץarrows פִּתְאוֹםsuddenly הָיוּbe מַכּוֹתָםwounded

But God will shoot them with arrows; suddenly they will be wounded.

64:8

וַיַּכְשִׁילוּהוּto stumble their עָלֵימוֹagainst לְשׁוֹנָםtongues יִתְנֹדֲדוּwho כָּלall רֹאֵהsee בָםin them

They will be made to stumble, their own tongues turned against them. All who see will shake their heads.

64:9

וַיִּירְאוּfear כָּלall אָדָםmankind וַיַּגִּידוּand proclaim פֹּעַלwork אֱלֹהִיםGod וּמַעֲשֵׂהוּwhat he הִשְׂכִּילוּponder

Then all mankind will fear and proclaim the work of God; so they will ponder what He has done.

64:10

יִשְׂמַחrejoice צַדִּיקrighteous בַּיהוָהin the LORD וְחָסָהand take refuge בוֹin him וְיִתְהַלְלוּexult כָּלall יִשְׁרֵיupright לֵבheart

Let the righteous rejoice in the LORD and take refuge in Him; let all the upright in heart exult.
