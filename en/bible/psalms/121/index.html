Psalms 121

The LORD keeps the pilgrim

Open in the interlinear reader

121:1

שִׁירsong לַמַּעֲלוֹתof ascents אֶשָּׂאlift עֵינַיmy eyes אֶלto הֶהָרִיםthe hills מֵאַיִןfrom where יָבֹאcome עֶזְרִיmy help

A song of ascents. I lift up my eyes to the hills. From where does my help come?

121:2

עֶזְרִיmy help מֵעִםfrom presence יְהוָהLORD עֹשֵׂהMaker שָׁמַיִםheaven וָאָרֶץand earth

My help comes from the LORD, the Maker of heaven and earth.

121:3

אַלnot יִתֵּןallow לַמּוֹטto slip רַגְלֶךָyour foot אַלnot יָנוּםslumber שֹׁמְרֶךָyour Protector

He will not allow your foot to slip; your Protector will not slumber.

121:4

הִנֵּהbehold לֹאnot יָנוּםslumber וְלֹאand not יִישָׁןsleep שׁוֹמֵרProtector יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

Behold, the Protector of Israel will neither slumber nor sleep.

121:5

יְהוָהLORD שֹׁמְרֶךָyour keeper יְהוָהLORD צִלְּךָshade your עַלon יַדhand יְמִינֶךָright

The LORD is your keeper; the LORD is the shade on your right hand.

121:6

יוֹמָםby day הַשֶּׁמֶשׁthe sun לֹאnot יַכֶּכָּהstrike you וְיָרֵחַnor the moon בַּלָּיְלָהby night

The sun will not strike you by day, nor the moon by night.

121:7

יְהוָהLORD יִשְׁמָרְךָguard you מִכָּלfrom all רָעevil יִשְׁמֹרpreserve אֶת[obj] נַפְשֶׁךָyour soul

The LORD will guard you from all evil; He will preserve your soul.

121:8

יְהוָהLORD יִשְׁמָרwatch צֵאתְךָyour coming וּבוֹאֶךָcoming and going מֵעַתָּהboth now וְעַדand to עוֹלָםforevermore

The LORD will watch over your coming and going, both now and forevermore.
