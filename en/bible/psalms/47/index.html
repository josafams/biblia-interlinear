Psalms 47

God, king of all the earth

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. A Psalm of the sons of Korah

47:1

כָּלall הָעַמִּיםpeoples תִּקְעוּclap כָףhands הָרִיעוּshout לֵאלֹהִיםunto God בְּקוֹלwith a voice רִנָּהtriumph

Clap your hands, all you peoples; shout unto God with a voice of triumph.

47:2

כִּיfor יְהוָהLORD עֶלְיוֹןMost high נוֹרָאawesome מֶלֶךְking גָּדוֹלgreat עַלover כָּלall הָאָרֶץthe earth

How awesome is the LORD Most High, the great King over all the earth!

47:3

יַדְבֵּרsubdues עַמִּיםnations תַּחְתֵּינוּbeneath us וּלְאֻמִּיםand peoples תַּחַתunder רַגְלֵינוּour feet

He subdues nations beneath us, and peoples under our feet.

47:4

יִבְחַרchooses לָנוּto us אֶת[obj] נַחֲלָתֵנוּinheritance for us אֶת[obj] גְּאוֹןpride יַעֲקֹבJacob אֲשֶׁרwhom אָהֵבloves סֶלָהSelah

He chooses our inheritance for us, the pride of Jacob, whom He loves. Selah

47:5

עָלָהascended אֱלֹהִיםGod בִּתְרוּעָהamid shouts יְהֹוָהLORD בְּקוֹלwith the sound שׁוֹפָרhorn

God has ascended amid shouts of joy, the LORD with the sound of the horn.

47:6

זַמְּרוּsing אֱלֹהִיםGod זַמֵּרוּsing זַמְּרוּpraises sing לְמַלְכֵּנוּto our king זַמֵּרוּsing praises

Sing praises to God, sing praises; sing praises to our King, sing praises!

47:7

כִּיfor מֶלֶךְking כָּלall הָאָרֶץthe earth אֱלֹהִיםGod זַמְּרוּsing praises מַשְׂכִּילprofound

For God is King of all the earth; sing profound praises to Him.

47:8

מָלַךְreigns אֱלֹהִיםGod עַלover גּוֹיִםnations אֱלֹהִיםGod יָשַׁבseated עַלon כִּסֵּאthrone קָדְשׁוֹhis holy

God reigns over the nations; God is seated on His holy throne.

47:9

נְדִיבֵיnobles עַמִּיםnations נֶאֱסָפוּassembled עַםpeople אֱלֹהֵיGod אַבְרָהָםAbraham כִּיfor לֵאלֹהִיםbelong to God מָגִנֵּיshields אֶרֶץearth מְאֹדvery נַעֲלָהexalted

The nobles of the nations have assembled as the people of the God of Abraham; for the shields of the earth belong to God; He is highly exalted.
