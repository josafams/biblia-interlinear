Psalms 101

The king's commitment to integrity

Open in the interlinear reader

101:1

לְדָוִדDavid מִזְמוֹרPsalm חֶסֶדloving devotion וּמִשְׁפָּטand justice אָשִׁירָהsing לְךָto יְהוָהLORD אֲזַמֵּרָהsing praises

A Psalm of David. I will sing of Your loving devotion and justice; to You, O LORD, I will sing praises.

101:2

אַשְׂכִּילָהponder בְּדֶרֶךְthe way תָּמִיםblameless מָתַיwhen will תָּבוֹאcome אֵלָיto me אֶתְהַלֵּךְwalk בְּתָםintegrity לְבָבִיheart בְּקֶרֶבin among בֵּיתִיhouse

I will ponder the way that is blameless— when will You come to me? I will walk in my house with integrity of heart.

101:3

לֹאno אָשִׁיתset לְנֶגֶדbefore עֵינַיmy eyes דְּבַרthing בְּלִיָּעַלworthless עֲשֹׂהwork סֵטִיםthose שָׂנֵאתִיhate לֹאnot יִדְבַּקcling בִּיto me

I will set no worthless thing before my eyes. I hate the work of those who fall away; it shall not cling to me.

101:4

לֵבָבheart עִקֵּשׁperverse יָסוּרdepart מִמֶּנִּיfrom me רָעevil לֹאnot אֵדָעknow

A perverse heart shall depart from me; I will know nothing of evil.

101:5

מְלָשְׁנִיslanders בַסֵּתֶרin secret רֵעֵהוּhis neighbor אוֹתוֹhim אַצְמִיתsilence גְּבַהּhaughty עֵינַיִםeyes וּרְחַבand a proud לֵבָבheart אֹתוֹhim לֹאnot אוּכָלendure

Whoever slanders his neighbor in secret, I will put to silence; the one with haughty eyes and a proud heart, I will not endure.

101:6

עֵינַיmy eyes בְּנֶאֶמְנֵיthe faithful אֶרֶץland לָשֶׁבֶתdwell עִמָּדִיwith me הֹלֵךְwalks בְּדֶרֶךְin the way תָּמִיםintegrity הוּאhe יְשָׁרְתֵנִיminister to me

My eyes favor the faithful of the land, that they may dwell with me; he who walks in the way of integrity shall minister to me.

101:7

לֹאno יֵשֵׁבdwell בְּקֶרֶבin among בֵּיתִיmy house עֹשֵׂהpractices רְמִיָּהdeceit דֹּבֵרtells שְׁקָרִיםlies לֹאno יִכּוֹןstand לְנֶגֶדin presence עֵינָיmy presence

No one who practices deceit shall dwell in my house; no one who tells lies shall stand in my presence.

101:8

לַבְּקָרִיםevery morning אַצְמִיתremove כָּלall רִשְׁעֵיwicked אָרֶץland לְהַכְרִיתthat I may cut off מֵעִירfrom the city יְהוָהLORD כָּלevery פֹּעֲלֵיevildoer אָוֶןiniquity

Every morning I will remove all the wicked of the land, that I may cut off every evildoer from the city of the LORD.
