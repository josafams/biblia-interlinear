Psalms 135

Praise to the God who chose Israel

Open in the interlinear reader

135:1

הַלְלוּHallelujah יָהּHallelujah הַלְלוּpraise אֶת[obj] שֵׁםname יְהוָהLORD הַלְלוּpraise עַבְדֵיservants יְהוָהLORD

Hallelujah! Praise the name of the LORD. Give praise, O servants of the LORD,

135:2

שֶׁעֹמְדִיםwho stand בְּבֵיתin the house יְהוָהLORD בְּחַצְרוֹתin the courts בֵּיתhouse אֱלֹהֵינוּour God

who stand in the house of the LORD, in the courts of the house of our God.

135:3

הַלְלוּHallelujah יָהּHallelujah כִּיfor טוֹבgood יְהוָהLORD זַמְּרוּsing praises לִשְׁמוֹto his name כִּיfor נָעִיםlovely

Hallelujah, for the LORD is good; sing praises to His name, for it is lovely.

135:4

כִּיfor יַעֲקֹבJacob בָּחַרchosen לוֹto him יָהּLORD יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael לִסְגֻלָּתוֹas his treasured possession

For the LORD has chosen Jacob as His own, Israel as His treasured possession.

135:5

כִּיfor אֲנִיI יָדַעְתִּיknow כִּיthat גָדוֹלgreat יְהוָהLORD וַאֲדֹנֵינוּour Lord מִכָּלabove all אֱלֹהִיםgods

For I know that the LORD is great; our Lord is above all gods.

135:6

כֹּלall אֲשֶׁרthat חָפֵץpleases יְהוָהLORD עָשָׂהdoes בַּשָּׁמַיִםin the heavens וּבָאָרֶץand on the earth בַּיַּמִּיםin the seas וְכָלand in all תְּהוֹמוֹתdepths

The LORD does all that pleases Him in the heavens and on the earth, in the seas and in all their depths.

135:7

מַעֲלֶהcauses נְשִׂאִיםclouds מִקְצֵהfrom the ends הָאָרֶץthe earth בְּרָקִיםlightning לַמָּטָרthe rain עָשָׂהdone מוֹצֵאforth רוּחַwind מֵאוֹצְרוֹתָיוfrom his storehouses

He causes the clouds to rise from the ends of the earth. He generates the lightning with the rain and brings forth the wind from His storehouses.

135:8

שֶׁהִכָּהhe struck בְּכוֹרֵיfirstborn מִצְרָיִםEgypt מֵאָדָםboth man עַדuntil בְּהֵמָהbeast

He struck down the firstborn of Egypt, of both man and beast.

135:9

שָׁלַחsent אֹתוֹתsigns וּמֹפְתִיםand wonders בְּתוֹכֵכִיinto your midst O מִצְרָיִםEgypt בְּפַרְעֹהagainst Pharaoh וּבְכָלand all עֲבָדָיוhis servants

He sent signs and wonders into your midst, O Egypt, against Pharaoh and all his servants.

135:10

שֶׁהִכָּהhe struck גּוֹיִםnations רַבִּיםmany וְהָרַגand slaughtered מְלָכִיםkings עֲצוּמִיםmighty

He struck down many nations and slaughtered mighty kings:

135:11

לְסִיחוֹןSihon מֶלֶךְking הָאֱמֹרִיthe Amorites וּלְעוֹגOg מֶלֶךְking הַבָּשָׁןof Bashan וּלְכֹלand all מַמְלְכוֹתkings כְּנָעַןCanaan

Sihon king of the Amorites, Og king of Bashan, and all the kings of Canaan.

135:12

וְנָתַןhe gave אַרְצָםtheir land נַחֲלָהinheritance נַחֲלָהheritage לְיִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael עַמּוֹhis people

He gave their land as an inheritance, as a heritage to His people Israel.

135:13

יְהוָהLORD שִׁמְךָyour name לְעוֹלָםendures forever יְהוָהLORD זִכְרְךָyour renown לְדֹרall וָדֹרgenerations

Your name, O LORD, endures forever, Your renown, O LORD, through all generations.

135:14

כִּיfor יָדִיןvindicate יְהוָהLORD עַמּוֹhis people וְעַלon עֲבָדָיוhis servants יִתְנֶחָםcompassion

For the LORD will vindicate His people and will have compassion on His servants.

135:15

עֲצַבֵּיidols הַגּוֹיִםthe nations כֶּסֶףsilver וְזָהָבand gold מַעֲשֵׂהmade יְדֵיhands אָדָםmen

The idols of the nations are silver and gold, made by the hands of men.

135:16

פֶּהmouths לָהֶםto them וְלֹאand not יְדַבֵּרוּspeak עֵינַיִםeyes לָהֶםto them וְלֹאand not יִרְאוּsee

They have mouths, but cannot speak; they have eyes, but cannot see;

135:17

אָזְנַיִםears לָהֶםto them וְלֹאand not יַאֲזִינוּhear אַףhow much אֵיןno יֶשׁthere רוּחַbreath בְּפִיהֶםin their mouths

they have ears, but cannot hear; nor is there breath in their mouths.

135:18

כְּמוֹהֶםthem like them יִהְיוּbecome עֹשֵׂיהֶםdo כֹּלall אֲשֶׁרwho בֹּטֵחַtrust בָּהֶםin them

Those who make them become like them, as do all who trust in them.

135:19

בֵּיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael בָּרֲכוּbless אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD בֵּיתhouse אַהֲרֹןAaron בָּרֲכוּbless אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD

O house of Israel, bless the LORD; O house of Aaron, bless the LORD;

135:20

בֵּיתhouse הַלֵּוִיLevi בָּרֲכוּbless אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD יִרְאֵיfear יְהוָהLORD בָּרֲכוּbless אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD

O house of Levi, bless the LORD; you who fear the LORD, bless the LORD!

135:21

בָּרוּךְblessed יְהוָהLORD מִצִּיּוֹןfrom Zion שֹׁכֵןdwells יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem הַלְלוּHallelujah יָהּHallelujah

Blessed be the LORD from Zion— He who dwells in Jerusalem. Hallelujah!
