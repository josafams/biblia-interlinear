Psalms 94

An appeal to the Judge of the earth

Open in the interlinear reader

94:1

אֵלGod נְקָמוֹתvengeance יְהוָהLORD אֵלGod נְקָמוֹתvengeance הוֹפִיַעshine forth

O LORD, God of vengeance, O God of vengeance, shine forth.

94:2

הִנָּשֵׂאrise שֹׁפֵטjudge הָאָרֶץthe earth הָשֵׁבrender גְּמוּלreward עַלto גֵּאִיםproud

Rise up, O Judge of the earth; render a reward to the proud.

94:3

עַדhow מָתַיlong רְשָׁעִיםwicked יְהוָהLORD עַדhow מָתַיlong will רְשָׁעִיםwicked יַעֲלֹזוּexult

How long will the wicked, O LORD, how long will the wicked exult?

94:4

יַבִּיעוּpour יְדַבְּרוּspeak עָתָקarrogant words יִתְאַמְּרוּwords כָּלall פֹּעֲלֵיworkers אָוֶןiniquity boast

They pour out arrogant words; all workers of iniquity boast.

94:5

עַמְּךָyour people יְהוָהLORD יְדַכְּאוּcrush וְנַחֲלָתְךָthey your heritage יְעַנּוּoppress

They crush Your people, O LORD; they oppress Your heritage.

94:6

אַלְמָנָהwidow וְגֵרand the foreigner יַהֲרֹגוּkill וִיתוֹמִיםfatherless יְרַצֵּחוּmurder

They kill the widow and the foreigner; they murder the fatherless.

94:7

וַיֹּאמְרוּthey say לֹאnot יִרְאֶהsee יָּהּLORD וְלֹאpays no יָבִיןheed אֱלֹהֵיGod יַעֲקֹבJacob

They say, “The LORD does not see; the God of Jacob pays no heed.”

94:8

בִּינוּnotice בֹּעֲרִיםsenseless בָּעָםamong the people וּכְסִילִיםfools מָתַיwhen תַּשְׂכִּילוּwise

Take notice, O senseless among the people! O fools, when will you be wise?

94:9

הֲנֹטַעhe who affixed אֹזֶןear הֲלֹאcan he not יִשְׁמָעhear אִםif יֹצֵרformed עַיִןeye הֲלֹאcan he not יַבִּיטsee

He who affixed the ear, can He not hear? He who formed the eye, can He not see?

94:10

הֲיֹסֵרhe who admonishes גּוֹיִםnations הֲלֹאdoes he not יוֹכִיחַdiscipline הַמְלַמֵּדwho teaches אָדָםman דָּעַתknowledge

He who admonishes the nations, does He not discipline? He who teaches man, does He lack knowledge?

94:11

יְהוָהLORD יֹדֵעַknows מַחְשְׁבוֹתthoughts אָדָםman כִּיthat הֵמָּהthey הָבֶלfutile

The LORD knows the thoughts of man, that they are futile.

94:12

אַשְׁרֵיblessed הַגֶּבֶרthe man אֲשֶׁרyou תְּיַסְּרֶנּוּdiscipline יָּהּLORD וּמִתּוֹרָתְךָand from your law תְלַמְּדֶנּוּteach

Blessed is the man You discipline, O LORD, and teach from Your law,

94:13

לְהַשְׁקִיטto grant לוֹto him מִימֵיfrom days רָעtrouble עַדuntil יִכָּרֶהdug לָרָשָׁעfor the wicked שָׁחַתpit

to grant him relief from days of trouble, until a pit is dug for the wicked.

94:14

כִּיfor לֹאnot יִטֹּשׁforsake יְהוָהLORD עַמּוֹhis people וְנַחֲלָתוֹhis heritage לֹאnever יַעֲזֹבabandon

For the LORD will not forsake His people; He will never abandon His heritage.

94:15

כִּיfor עַדuntil צֶדֶקrighteous יָשׁוּבagain מִשְׁפָּטjudgment וְאַחֲרָיוfollow it כָּלall יִשְׁרֵיupright לֵבheart

Surely judgment will again be righteous, and all the upright in heart will follow it.

94:16

מִיwho יָקוּםrise לִיfor me עִםagainst מְרֵעִיםwicked מִיwho יִתְיַצֵּבstand לִיfor me עִםagainst פֹּעֲלֵיworkers אָוֶןiniquity

Who will rise up for me against the wicked? Who will stand for me against the workers of iniquity?

94:17

לוּלֵיunless יְהוָהLORD עֶזְרָתָהhelper לִּיto me כִּמְעַטsoon שָׁכְנָהdwelt דוּמָהsilence נַפְשִׁיmy soul

Unless the LORD had been my helper, I would soon have dwelt in the abode of silence.

94:18

אִםif אָמַרְתִּיsay מָטָהslipping רַגְלִיmy foot חַסְדְּךָyour loving devotion O יְהוָהLORD יִסְעָדֵנִיsupports me

If I say, “My foot is slipping,” Your loving devotion, O LORD, supports me.

94:19

בְּרֹבwhen anxiety שַׂרְעַפַּיoverwhelms me בְּקִרְבִּיin my among תַּנְחוּמֶיךָyour consolation יְשַׁעַשְׁעוּdelights נַפְשִׁיmy soul

When anxiety overwhelms me, Your consolation delights my soul.

94:20

הַיְחָבְרְךָa corrupt כִּסֵּאthrone הַוּוֹתally יֹצֵרdevising עָמָלmischief עֲלֵיon חֹקdecree

Can a corrupt throne be Your ally— one devising mischief by decree?

94:21

יָגוֹדּוּband עַלagainst נֶפֶשׁsoul צַדִּיקrighteous וְדָםand condemn נָקִיinnocent יַרְשִׁיעוּcondemn

They band together against the righteous and condemn the innocent to death.

94:22

וַיְהִיand was יְהוָהLORD לִיto me לְמִשְׂגָּבstronghold וֵאלֹהַיand my God is my לְצוּרrock מַחְסִיrefuge

But the LORD has been my stronghold, and my God is my rock of refuge.

94:23

וַיָּשֶׁבhe will bring עֲלֵיהֶםupon them אֶת[obj] אוֹנָםtheir iniquity וּבְרָעָתָםtheir wickedness יַצְמִיתֵםdestroy them יַצְמִיתֵםdestroy them יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God

He will bring upon them their own iniquity and destroy them for their wickedness. The LORD our God will destroy them.
