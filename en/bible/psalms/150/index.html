Psalms 150

Final praise with every instrument

Open in the interlinear reader

150:1

הַלְלוּHallelujah יָהּHallelujah הַלְלוּpraise אֵלGod בְּקָדְשׁוֹin his sanctuary הַלְלוּהוּpraise him בִּרְקִיעַheavens עֻזּוֹhis mighty

Hallelujah! Praise God in His sanctuary. Praise Him in His mighty heavens.

150:2

הַלְלוּהוּpraise him בִגְבוּרֹתָיוfor his mighty acts הַלְלוּהוּpraise him כְּרֹבexcellent גֻּדְלוֹhis greatness

Praise Him for His mighty acts; praise Him for His excellent greatness.

150:3

הַלְלוּהוּpraise him בְּתֵקַעwith the sound שׁוֹפָרhorn הַלְלוּהוּpraise him בְּנֵבֶלwith the harp וְכִנּוֹרand lyre

Praise Him with the sound of the horn; praise Him with the harp and lyre.

150:4

הַלְלוּהוּpraise him בְתֹףwith tambourine וּמָחוֹלand dancing הַלְלוּהוּpraise him בְּמִנִּיםwith strings וְעוּגָבand flute

Praise Him with tambourine and dancing; praise Him with strings and flute.

150:5

הַלְלוּהוּpraise him בְצִלְצְלֵיwith cymbals שָׁמַעclashing הַלְלוּהוּpraise him בְּצִלְצְלֵיwith cymbals תְרוּעָהresounding

Praise Him with clashing cymbals; praise Him with resounding cymbals.

150:6

כֹּלeverything הַנְּשָׁמָהthat has breath תְּהַלֵּלpraise יָהּLORD הַלְלוּHallelujah יָהּHallelujah

Let everything that has breath praise the LORD! Hallelujah!
