Psalms 34

An acrostic testimony of deliverance

Open in the interlinear reader

Of David, when he pretended to be insane before Abimelech, so that the king drove him away

34:1

אֲבָרֲכָהbless אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD בְּכָלat all עֵתtimes תָּמִידalways תְּהִלָּתוֹhis praise בְּפִיon my lips

I will bless the LORD at all times; His praise will always be on my lips.

34:2

בַּיהוָהin the LORD תִּתְהַלֵּלboasts נַפְשִׁיmy soul יִשְׁמְעוּhear עֲנָוִיםoppressed וְיִשְׂמָחוּand rejoice

My soul boasts in the LORD; let the oppressed hear and rejoice.

34:3

גַּדְּלוּmagnify לַיהוָהthe LORD אִתִּיwith me וּנְרוֹמְמָהlet us exalt שְׁמוֹhis name יַחְדָּוtogether

Magnify the LORD with me; let us exalt His name together.

34:4

דָּרַשְׁתִּיsought אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD וְעָנָנִיand he answered me וּמִכָּלfrom all מְגוּרוֹתַיmy fears הִצִּילָנִיdelivered me

I sought the LORD, and He answered me; He delivered me from all my fears.

34:5

הִבִּיטוּlook אֵלָיוto him וְנָהָרוּradiant joy וּפְנֵיהֶםwith their faces אַלdo not יֶחְפָּרוּashamed

Those who look to Him are radiant with joy; their faces shall never be ashamed.

34:6

זֶהthis עָנִיpoor קָרָאcalled וַיהוָהand the LORD שָׁמֵעַheard וּמִכָּלfrom all צָרוֹתָיוtroubles הוֹשִׁיעוֹsaved him

This poor man called out, and the LORD heard him; He saved him from all his troubles.

34:7

חֹנֶהencamps מַלְאַךְangel יְהוָהLORD סָבִיבaround לִירֵאָיוthose who fear him וַיְחַלְּצֵםand he delivers them

The angel of the LORD encamps around those who fear Him, and he delivers them.

34:8

טַעֲמוּtaste וּרְאוּand see כִּיthat טוֹבgood יְהוָהLORD אַשְׁרֵיblessed הַגֶּבֶרthe man who takes יֶחֱסֶהrefuge בּוֹin him

Taste and see that the LORD is good; blessed is the man who takes refuge in Him!

34:9

יְראוּfear אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD קְדֹשָׁיוhis saints כִּיfor אֵיןno מַחְסוֹרnothing לִירֵאָיוfear him

Fear the LORD, you His saints, for those who fear Him lack nothing.

34:10

כְּפִירִיםyoung lions רָשׁוּlacking וְרָעֵבוּand hungry וְדֹרְשֵׁיseek יְהוָהLORD לֹאno יַחְסְרוּlack כָלno טוֹבgood

Young lions go lacking and hungry, but those who seek the LORD lack no good thing.

34:11

לְכוּcome בָנִיםchildren שִׁמְעוּlisten לִיto me יִרְאַתfear יְהוָהLORD אֲלַמֶּדְכֶםteach you

Come, children, listen to me; I will teach you the fear of the LORD.

34:12

מִיwho הָאִישׁthe man הֶחָפֵץwho delights חַיִּיםlife אֹהֵבdesires יָמִיםdays לִרְאוֹתto see טוֹבgood

Who is the man who delights in life, who desires to see good days?

34:13

נְצֹרkeep לְשׁוֹנְךָyour tongue מֵרָעfrom evil וּשְׂפָתֶיךָand your lips מִדַּבֵּרfrom speech מִרְמָהdeceitful

Keep your tongue from evil and your lips from deceitful speech.

34:14

סוּרturn מֵרָעfrom evil וַעֲשֵׂהand do טוֹבgood בַּקֵּשׁseek שָׁלוֹםpeace וְרָדְפֵהוּand pursue it

Turn away from evil and do good; seek peace and pursue it.

34:15

עֵינֵיeyes יְהוָהLORD אֶלto צַדִּיקִיםrighteous וְאָזְנָיוand his ears are אֶלto שַׁוְעָתָםinclined their cry

The eyes of the LORD are on the righteous, and His ears are inclined to their cry.

34:16

פְּנֵיface יְהוָהLORD בְּעֹשֵׂיdo רָעevil לְהַכְרִיתto wipe מֵאֶרֶץfrom the earth זִכְרָםmemory of them

But the face of the LORD is against those who do evil, to wipe out all memory of them from the earth.

34:17

צָעֲקוּrighteous cry וַיהוָהand the LORD שָׁמֵעַhears וּמִכָּלfrom all צָרוֹתָםtheir troubles הִצִּילָםdelivers

The righteous cry out, and the LORD hears; He delivers them from all their troubles.

34:18

קָרוֹבnear יְהוָהLORD לְנִשְׁבְּרֵיto the brokenhearted לֵבbrokenhearted וְאֶתand [obj] דַּכְּאֵיcontrite רוּחַspirit יוֹשִׁיעַsaves

The LORD is near to the brokenhearted; He saves the contrite in spirit.

34:19

רַבּוֹתmany רָעוֹתafflictions צַדִּיקrighteous וּמִכֻּלָּםfrom them all יַצִּילֶנּוּdelivers him יְהוָהLORD

Many are the afflictions of the righteous, but the LORD delivers him from them all.

34:20

שֹׁמֵרprotects כָּלall עַצְמוֹתָיוhis bones אַחַתone מֵהֵנָּהof them לֹאnot נִשְׁבָּרָהbroken

He protects all his bones; not one of them will be broken.

34:21

תְּמוֹתֵתslay רָשָׁעwicked רָעָהevil וְשֹׂנְאֵיand the haters צַדִּיקrighteous יֶאְשָׁמוּcondemned

Evil will slay the wicked, and the haters of the righteous will be condemned.

34:22

פּוֹדֶהredeems יְהוָהLORD נֶפֶשׁredeems עֲבָדָיוhis servants וְלֹאand not יֶאְשְׁמוּcondemned כָּלnone הַחֹסִיםwho take refuge בּוֹin him

The LORD redeems His servants, and none who take refuge in Him will be condemned.
