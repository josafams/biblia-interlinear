Psalms 140

Protection against the violent

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David

140:1

חַלְּצֵנִיrescue me יְהוָהLORD מֵאָדָםfrom men רָעevil מֵאִישׁfrom men חֲמָסִיםviolence תִּנְצְרֵנִיprotect me

Rescue me, O LORD, from evil men. Protect me from men of violence,

140:2

אֲשֶׁרwho חָשְׁבוּdevise רָעוֹתevil בְּלֵבin their hearts כָּלall יוֹםday יָגוּרוּstir מִלְחָמוֹתwar

who devise evil in their hearts and stir up war all day long.

140:3

שָׁנֲנוּsharpen לְשׁוֹנָםtheir tongues כְּמוֹlike נָחָשׁsnakes חֲמַתvenom עַכְשׁוּבvipers תַּחַתunder שְׂפָתֵימוֹtheir lips סֶלָהSelah

They sharpen their tongues like snakes; the venom of vipers is on their lips. Selah

140:4

שָׁמְרֵנִיguard me יְהוָהLORD מִידֵיfrom the hands רָשָׁעwicked מֵאִישׁfrom men חֲמָסִיםviolence תִּנְצְרֵנִיkeep me אֲשֶׁרwho חָשְׁבוּscheme לִדְחוֹתto make stumble פְּעָמָיme stumble

Guard me, O LORD, from the hands of the wicked. Keep me safe from men of violence who scheme to make me stumble.

140:5

טָמְנוּhide גֵאִיםproud פַּחsnare לִיfor me וַחֲבָלִיםcords פָּרְשׂוּspread רֶשֶׁתnet לְיַדto beside מַעְגָּלpath מֹקְשִׁיםlures שָׁתוּset לִיfor me סֶלָהSelah

The proud hide a snare for me; the cords of their net are spread along the path, and lures are set out for me. Selah

140:6

אָמַרְתִּיsay לַיהוָהto the LORD אֵלִיmy God אָתָּהyou are הַאֲזִינָהhear O יְהוָהLORD קוֹלcry תַּחֲנוּנָיhelp

I say to the LORD, “You are my God.” Hear, O LORD, my cry for help.

140:7

יְהֹוִהGOD אֲדֹנָיLord עֹזstrength יְשׁוּעָתִיmy salvation you סַכֹּתָהshield לְרֹאשִׁיmy head בְּיוֹםin the day נָשֶׁקbattle

O GOD the Lord, the strength of my salvation, You shield my head in the day of battle.

140:8

אַלnot תִּתֵּןgrant יְהוָהLORD מַאֲוַיֵּיdesires רָשָׁעwicked זְמָמוֹpromote their אַלdo not תָּפֵקevil יָרוּמוּexalted סֶלָהSelah

Grant not, O LORD, the desires of the wicked; do not promote their evil plans, lest they be exalted. Selah

140:9

רֹאשׁheads מְסִבָּיsurround me עֲמַלtrouble שְׂפָתֵימוֹtheir lips יְכַסֵּמוֹcovered

May the heads of those who surround me be covered in the trouble their lips have caused.

140:10

יִמּוֹטוּfall עֲלֵיהֶםon them גֶּחָלִיםburning coals בָּאֵשׁinto the fire יַפִּלֵםthrown בְּמַהֲמֹרוֹתinto the miry pits בַּלnever יָקוּמוּrise again

May burning coals fall on them; may they be thrown into the fire, into the miry pits, never to rise again.

140:11

אִישׁman לָשׁוֹןslanderer בַּלno יִכּוֹןestablished בָּאָרֶץin the land אִישׁman חָמָסviolence רָעcalamity יְצוּדֶנּוּhunt לְמַדְחֵפֹתdown

May no slanderer be established in the land; may calamity hunt down the man of violence.

140:12

יָדַעְתִּיknow כִּיthat יַעֲשֶׂהdo יְהוָהLORD דִּיןupholds justice עָנִיpoor מִשְׁפַּטcause אֶבְיֹנִיםneedy

I know that the LORD upholds justice for the poor and defends the cause of the needy.

140:13

אַךְsurely צַדִּיקִיםrighteous יוֹדוּpraise לִשְׁמֶךָyour name יֵשְׁבוּdwell יְשָׁרִיםupright אֶתwith פָּנֶיךָyour presence

Surely the righteous will praise Your name; the upright will dwell in Your presence.
