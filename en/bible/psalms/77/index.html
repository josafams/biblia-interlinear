Psalms 77

Remembering the exodus in a night of distress

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. According to Jeduthun. A Psalm of Asaph

77:1

קוֹלִיmy voice אֶלto אֱלֹהִיםGod וְאֶצְעָקָהI cried קוֹלִיaloud אֶלto אֱלֹהִיםGod וְהַאֲזִיןto hear אֵלָיto me

I cried out to God; I cried aloud to God to hear me.

77:2

בְּיוֹםin the day צָרָתִיtrouble I אֲדֹנָיLord דָּרָשְׁתִּיsought יָדִיthrough לַיְלָהnight נִגְּרָהoutstretched וְלֹאdid not תָפוּגweary מֵאֲנָהrefused הִנָּחֵםcomforted נַפְשִׁיmy soul

In the day of trouble I sought the Lord; through the night my outstretched hands did not grow weary; my soul refused to be comforted.

77:3

אֶזְכְּרָהremembered אֱלֹהִיםGod וְאֶהֱמָיָהand I groaned אָשִׂיחָהmused וְתִתְעַטֵּףfaint רוּחִיmy spirit סֶלָהSelah

I remembered You, O God, and I groaned; I mused and my spirit grew faint. Selah

77:4

אָחַזְתָּseized שְׁמֻרוֹתclosing עֵינָיmy eyes נִפְעַמְתִּיtroubled וְלֹאand not אֲדַבֵּרspeak

You have kept my eyes from closing; I am too troubled to speak.

77:5

חִשַּׁבְתִּיconsidered יָמִיםdays מִקֶּדֶםof old שְׁנוֹתyears עוֹלָמִיםlong

I considered the days of old, the years long in the past.

77:6

אֶזְכְּרָהremembered נְגִינָתִיmy song בַּלָּיְלָהat night עִםwith לְבָבִיmy heart I אָשִׂיחָהmused וַיְחַפֵּשׂpondered רוּחִיmy spirit

At night I remembered my song; in my heart I mused, and my spirit pondered:

77:7

הַלְעוֹלָמִיםforever יִזְנַחspurn אֲדֹנָיLord us וְלֹאand never יֹסִיףmore לִרְצוֹתhis favor עוֹדagain

“Will the Lord spurn us forever and never show His favor again?

77:8

הֶאָפֵסgone לָנֶצַחforever חַסְדּוֹloving devotion גָּמַרpromise אֹמֶרfailed לְדֹרfor all וָדֹרtime

Is His loving devotion gone forever? Has His promise failed for all time?

77:9

הֲשָׁכַחhas forgotten חַנּוֹתgracious אֵלGod אִםif קָפַץshut בְּאַףanger רַחֲמָיוhis compassion סֶלָהSelah

Has God forgotten to be gracious? Has His anger shut off His compassion?” Selah

77:10

וָאֹמַרso I said חַלּוֹתִיam grieved הִיאthat שְׁנוֹתyears יְמִיןright hand עֶלְיוֹןMost high

So I said, “I am grieved that the right hand of the Most High has changed.”

77:11

אֶזְכּוֹרremember מַעַלְלֵיworks יָהּLORD כִּיfor אֶזְכְּרָהremember מִקֶּדֶםof old פִּלְאֶךָyour wonders

I will remember the works of the LORD; yes, I will remember Your wonders of old.

77:12

וְהָגִיתִיwill reflect בְכָלon all פָּעֳלֶךָmighty וּבַעֲלִילוֹתֶיךָand your deeds אָשִׂיחָהponder

I will reflect on all You have done and ponder Your mighty deeds.

77:13

אֱלֹהִיםGod בַּקֹּדֶשׁis holy דַּרְכֶּךָyour way O מִיwhat אֵלgod גָּדוֹלgreat כֵּאלֹהִיםas our God

Your way, O God, is holy. What god is so great as our God?

77:14

אַתָּהyou הָאֵלthe God עֹשֵׂהworks פֶלֶאwonders הוֹדַעְתָּyou בָעַמִּיםamong the peoples עֻזֶּךָyour strength

You are the God who works wonders; You display Your strength among the peoples.

77:15

גָּאַלְתָּredeemed בִּזְרוֹעַwith power עַמֶּךָyour people בְּנֵיsons יַעֲקֹבJacob וְיוֹסֵףand Joseph סֶלָהSelah

With power You redeemed Your people, the sons of Jacob and Joseph. Selah

77:16

רָאוּךָsaw you מַּיִםwaters אֱלֹהִיםGod רָאוּךָsaw you מַּיִםwaters יָחִילוּswirled אַףeven יִרְגְּזוּshaken תְהֹמוֹתdepths

The waters saw You, O God; the waters saw You and swirled; even the depths were shaken.

77:17

זֹרְמוּpoured מַיִםwater עָבוֹתclouds קוֹלthunder נָתְנוּresounded שְׁחָקִיםskies אַףwith חֲצָצֶיךָyour arrows יִתְהַלָּכוּwalk

The clouds poured down water; the skies resounded with thunder; Your arrows flashed back and forth.

77:18

קוֹלvoice רַעַמְךָyour thunder resounded בַּגַּלְגַּלin the whirlwind הֵאִירוּlit בְרָקִיםlightning תֵּבֵלworld רָגְזָהtrembled וַתִּרְעַשׁtrembled and quaked הָאָרֶץthe earth

Your thunder resounded in the whirlwind; the lightning lit up the world; the earth trembled and quaked.

77:19

בַּיָּםthrough the sea דַּרְכֶּךָyour path וּשְׁבִילְךָand your leaving roads בְּמַיִםwaters רַבִּיםmighty וְעִקְּבוֹתֶיךָbut your footprints לֹאnot נֹדָעוּfound

Your path led through the sea, Your way through the mighty waters, but Your footprints were not to be found.

77:20

נָחִיתָled כַצֹּאןlike a flock עַמֶּךָyour people בְּיַדby the hand מֹשֶׁהMoses וְאַהֲרֹןand Aaron

You led Your people like a flock by the hand of Moses and Aaron.
