Psalms 40

Deliverance, praise and a new cry for help

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David

40:1

קַוֺּהwaited קִוִּיתִיpatiently for יְהוָהLORD וַיֵּטhe inclined אֵלַיto me וַיִּשְׁמַעand heard שַׁוְעָתִיmy cry

I waited patiently for the LORD; He inclined to me and heard my cry.

40:2

וַיַּעֲלֵנִיhe lifted me מִבּוֹרfrom the pit שָׁאוֹןdespair מִטִּיטclay הַיָּוֵןthe miry וַיָּקֶםhe set עַלupon סֶלַעrock רַגְלַיmy feet כּוֹנֵןfootsteps אֲשֻׁרָיmy footsteps firm

He lifted me up from the pit of despair, out of the miry clay; He set my feet upon a rock, and made my footsteps firm.

40:3

וַיִּתֵּןhe put בְּפִיin my mouth שִׁירsong חָדָשׁnew תְּהִלָּהpraise לֵאלֹהֵינוּto our God יִרְאוּsee רַבִּיםmany וְיִירָאוּand fear וְיִבְטְחוּtrust בַּיהוָהin the LORD

He put a new song in my mouth, a hymn of praise to our God. Many will see and fear and put their trust in the LORD.

40:4

אַשְׁרֵיblessed הַגֶּבֶרthe man אֲשֶׁרwho שָׂםmade יְהֹוָהLORD מִבְטַחוֹhis trust וְלֹאwho has not פָנָהturned אֶלto רְהָבִיםproud וְשָׂטֵיnor to those כָזָבfalsehood

Blessed is the man who has made the LORD his trust, who has not turned to the proud, nor to those who lapse into falsehood.

40:5

רַבּוֹתmany עָשִׂיתָdone אַתָּהyou יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהַיmy God נִפְלְאֹתֶיךָwonders you וּמַחְשְׁבֹתֶיךָand the plans you אֵלֵינוּto us אֵיןnone עֲרֹךְcompare אֵלֶיךָto you אַגִּידָהproclaim וַאֲדַבֵּרָהand declare עָצְמוּmore מִסַּפֵּרcount

Many, O LORD my God, are the wonders You have done, and the plans You have for us— none can compare to You— if I proclaim and declare them, they are more than I can count.

40:6

זֶבַחsacrifice וּמִנְחָהand offering לֹאnot חָפַצְתָּdesire אָזְנַיִםears כָּרִיתָopened לִּיto me עוֹלָהburnt offerings וַחֲטָאָהand sin לֹאnot שָׁאָלְתָּrequire

Sacrifice and offering You did not desire, but my ears You have opened. Burnt offerings and sin offerings You did not require.

40:7

אָזthen אָמַרְתִּיsaid הִנֵּהhere בָאתִיcome בִּמְגִלַּתin the scroll סֵפֶרscroll כָּתוּבwritten עָלָיon me

Then I said, “Here I am, I have come— it is written about me in the scroll:

40:8

לַעֲשׂוֹתto do רְצוֹנְךָdelight אֱלֹהַיmy God חָפָצְתִּיdelight וְתוֹרָתְךָyour law בְּתוֹךְis within מֵעָיmy heart

I delight to do Your will, O my God; Your law is within my heart.”

40:9

בִּשַּׂרְתִּיproclaim צֶדֶקrighteousness בְּקָהָלassembly רָבgreat הִנֵּהbehold שְׂפָתַיmy lips לֹאnot אֶכְלָאseal יְהוָהLORD אַתָּהyou יָדָעְתָּknow

I proclaim righteousness in the great assembly; behold, I do not seal my lips, as You, O LORD, do know.

40:10

צִדְקָתְךָyour righteousness לֹאnot כִסִּיתִיcovered בְּתוֹךְin among לִבִּיmy heart I אֱמוּנָתְךָyour faithfulness וּתְשׁוּעָתְךָand salvation אָמָרְתִּיdeclared לֹאnot כִחַדְתִּיconcealed חַסְדְּךָyour loving devotion וַאֲמִתְּךָand faithfulness לְקָהָלassembly רָבgreat

I have not covered up Your righteousness in my heart; I have declared Your faithfulness and salvation; I have not concealed Your loving devotion and faithfulness from the great assembly.

40:11

אַתָּהyou יְהוָהLORD לֹאnot תִכְלָאwithhold רַחֲמֶיךָyour mercy מִמֶּנִּיfrom me חַסְדְּךָyour loving devotion וַאֲמִתְּךָand faithfulness will תָּמִידalways יִצְּרוּנִיguard me

O LORD, do not withhold Your mercy from me; Your loving devotion and faithfulness will always guard me.

40:12

כִּיfor אָפְפוּsurround עָלַיon me רָעוֹתevils עַדuntil אֵיןwithout מִסְפָּרnumber הִשִּׂיגוּנִיovertaken עֲוֺנֹתַיsins וְלֹאand not יָכֹלְתִּיcannot לִרְאוֹתsee עָצְמוּmore מִשַּׂעֲרוֹתthan the hairs רֹאשִׁיmy head וְלִבִּיand my heart עֲזָבָנִיhas

For evils without number surround me; my sins have overtaken me, so that I cannot see. They are more than the hairs of my head, and my heart has failed within me.

40:13

רְצֵהpleased יְהוָהLORD לְהַצִּילֵנִיto deliver me יְהוָהLORD לְעֶזְרָתִיto help me חוּשָׁהhurry

Be pleased, O LORD, to deliver me; hurry, O LORD, to help me.

40:14

יֵבֹשׁוּashamed וְיַחְפְּרוּand confounded יַחַדmay מְבַקְשֵׁיseek נַפְשִׁיmy life לִסְפּוֹתָהּto stoke her יִסֹּגוּwho אָחוֹרrepelled וְיִכָּלְמוּand humiliated חֲפֵצֵיwish רָעָתִיme harm

May those who seek my life be ashamed and confounded; may those who wish me harm be repelled and humiliated.

40:15

יָשֹׁמּוּappalled עַלon עֵקֶבown בָּשְׁתָּםshame הָאֹמְרִיםwho say לִיto me הֶאָחaha הֶאָחaha

May those who say to me, “Aha, aha!” be appalled at their own shame.

40:16

יָשִׂישׂוּrejoice וְיִשְׂמְחוּand be glad בְּךָin you כָּלall מְבַקְשֶׁיךָseek you יֹאמְרוּsay תָמִידalways יִגְדַּלmagnified יְהוָהLORD אֹהֲבֵיlove תְּשׁוּעָתֶךָyour salvation

May all who seek You rejoice and be glad in You; may those who love Your salvation always say, “The LORD be magnified!”

40:17

וַאֲנִיbut I am עָנִיpoor וְאֶבְיוֹןand needy אֲדֹנָיLord יַחֲשָׁבthink לִיto me עֶזְרָתִיmy helper וּמְפַלְטִיand deliverer my אַתָּהyou אֱלֹהַיGod אַלdo not תְּאַחַרdelay

But I am poor and needy; may the Lord think of me. You are my helper and deliverer; O my God, do not delay.
