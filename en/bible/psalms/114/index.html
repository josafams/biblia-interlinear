Psalms 114

Nature trembles before the exodus

Open in the interlinear reader

114:1

בְּצֵאתwhen departed יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael מִמִּצְרָיִםfrom Egypt בֵּיתhouse יַעֲקֹבJacob מֵעַםfrom a people לֹעֵזtongue

When Israel departed from Egypt, the house of Jacob from a people of foreign tongue,

114:2

הָיְתָהbecame יְהוּדָהJudah לְקָדְשׁוֹbecame God’s sanctuary יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael מַמְשְׁלוֹתָיוhis dominion

Judah became God’s sanctuary, and Israel His dominion.

114:3

הַיָּםthe sea רָאָהobserved וַיָּנֹסand fled הַיַּרְדֵּןJordan יִסֹּבturned לְאָחוֹרback

The sea observed and fled; the Jordan turned back;

114:4

הֶהָרִיםthe mountains רָקְדוּskipped כְאֵילִיםlike rams גְּבָעוֹתhills כִּבְנֵיlike sons צֹאןlambs

the mountains skipped like rams, the hills like lambs.

114:5

מַהwhy לְּךָto you הַיָּםsea כִּיthat תָנוּסfled הַיַּרְדֵּןJordan תִּסֹּבturned לְאָחוֹרback

Why was it, O sea, that you fled, O Jordan, that you turned back,

114:6

הֶהָרִיםmountains תִּרְקְדוּskipped כְאֵילִיםlike rams גְּבָעוֹתhills כִּבְנֵיlike sons צֹאןlambs

O mountains, that you skipped like rams, O hills, like lambs?

114:7

מִלִּפְנֵיat the presence אָדוֹןLord חוּלִיtremble אָרֶץearth מִלִּפְנֵיat the presence אֱלוֹהַּGod יַעֲקֹבJacob

Tremble, O earth, at the presence of the Lord, at the presence of the God of Jacob,

114:8

הַהֹפְכִיwho turned הַצּוּרthe rock אֲגַםpool מָיִםwater חַלָּמִישׁflint לְמַעְיְנוֹinto a fountain מָיִםwater

who turned the rock into a pool, the flint into a fountain of water!
