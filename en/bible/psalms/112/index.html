Psalms 112

An acrostic of the life of one who fears God

Open in the interlinear reader

112:1

הַלְלוּHallelujah יָהּHallelujah אַשְׁרֵיblessed אִישׁman יָרֵאfears אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD בְּמִצְוֺתָיוin his commandments חָפֵץdelights מְאֹדgreatly

Hallelujah! Blessed is the man who fears the LORD, who greatly delights in His commandments.

112:2

גִּבּוֹרmighty בָּאָרֶץin the land יִהְיֶהbe זַרְעוֹhis descendants דּוֹרgeneration יְשָׁרִיםupright יְבֹרָךְblessed

His descendants will be mighty in the land; the generation of the upright will be blessed.

112:3

הוֹןwealth וָעֹשֶׁרand riches בְּבֵיתוֹin his house וְצִדְקָתוֹand his righteousness עֹמֶדֶתendures לָעַדendures forever

Wealth and riches are in his house, and his righteousness endures forever.

112:4

זָרַחdawns בַּחֹשֶׁךְin the darkness אוֹרlight לַיְשָׁרִיםfor the upright חַנּוּןgracious וְרַחוּםcompassionate וְצַדִּיקand righteous

Light dawns in the darkness for the upright— for the gracious, compassionate, and righteous.

112:5

טוֹבwell אִישׁman חוֹנֵןgenerous וּמַלְוֶהand lends יְכַלְכֵּלfreely דְּבָרָיוaffairs are guided בְּמִשְׁפָּטby justice

It is well with the man who is generous and lends freely, whose affairs are guided by justice.

112:6

כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםnever לֹאnot יִמּוֹטshaken לְזֵכֶרremembered עוֹלָםforever יִהְיֶהbe צַדִּיקrighteous

Surely he will never be shaken; the righteous man will be remembered forever.

112:7

מִשְּׁמוּעָהnews רָעָהbad לֹאnot יִירָאfear נָכוֹןis steadfast לִבּוֹhis heart בָּטֻחַtrusting בַּיהוָהin the LORD

He does not fear bad news; his heart is steadfast, trusting in the LORD.

112:8

סָמוּךְhis לִבּוֹhis heart לֹאnot יִירָאfear עַדuntil אֲשֶׁרthat יִרְאֶהlooks בְצָרָיוin triumph on his foes

His heart is assured; he does not fear, until he looks in triumph on his foes.

112:9

פִּזַּרscattered abroad נָתַןgifts לָאֶבְיוֹנִיםto the poor צִדְקָתוֹhis righteousness עֹמֶדֶתendures לָעַדendures forever קַרְנוֹhis horn will תָּרוּםlifted high בְּכָבוֹדin honor

He has scattered abroad his gifts to the poor; his righteousness endures forever; his horn will be lifted high in honor.

112:10

רָשָׁעwicked יִרְאֶהsee וְכָעָסand be grieved שִׁנָּיוhis teeth יַחֲרֹקgnash וְנָמָסand waste תַּאֲוַתdesires רְשָׁעִיםwicked תֹּאבֵדperish

The wicked man will see and be grieved; he will gnash his teeth and waste away; the desires of the wicked will perish.
