Psalms 90

The eternal God and fleeting life

Open in the interlinear reader

90:1

תְּפִלָּהprayer לְמֹשֶׁהof Moses אִישׁman הָאֱלֹהִיםGod אֲדֹנָיLord have מָעוֹןdwelling אַתָּהyou הָיִיתָbeen לָּנוּto us בְּדֹרthrough all וָדֹרgenerations

A prayer of Moses the man of God. Lord, You have been our dwelling place through all generations.

90:2

בְּטֶרֶםbefore הָרִיםmountains יֻלָּדוּborn וַתְּחוֹלֵלforth אֶרֶץearth וְתֵבֵלand the world וּמֵעוֹלָםfrom everlasting עַדto עוֹלָםeverlasting אַתָּהyou אֵלGod

Before the mountains were born or You brought forth the earth and the world, from everlasting to everlasting You are God.

90:3

תָּשֵׁבreturn אֱנוֹשׁman עַדto דַּכָּאdust וַתֹּאמֶרsaying שׁוּבוּreturn בְנֵיsons אָדָםmortals

You return man to dust, saying, “Return, O sons of mortals.”

90:4

כִּיfor אֶלֶףthousand שָׁנִיםyears בְּעֵינֶיךָin your sight כְּיוֹםa day אֶתְמוֹלpasses כִּיthat יַעֲבֹרpasses וְאַשְׁמוּרָהor a watch בַלָּיְלָהthe night

For in Your sight a thousand years are but a day that passes, or a watch of the night.

90:5

זְרַמְתָּםwhisk them שֵׁנָהsleep יִהְיוּbe בַּבֹּקֶרthe morning כֶּחָצִירlike the grass יַחֲלֹףnew

You whisk them away in their sleep; they are like the new grass of the morning—

90:6

בַּבֹּקֶרin the morning יָצִיץsprings וְחָלָףnew but לָעֶרֶבby evening יְמוֹלֵלfades וְיָבֵשׁand withers

in the morning it springs up new, but by evening it fades and withers.

90:7

כִּיfor כָלִינוּconsumed בְאַפֶּךָby your anger וּבַחֲמָתְךָand by your wrath נִבְהָלְנוּterrified

For we are consumed by Your anger and terrified by Your wrath.

90:8

שַׁתָּהset עֲוֺנֹתֵינוּour iniquities לְנֶגְדֶּךָbefore you עֲלֻמֵנוּour secret לִמְאוֹרin the light פָּנֶיךָyour presence

You have set our iniquities before You, our secret sins in the light of Your presence.

90:9

כִּיfor כָלall יָמֵינוּour days פָּנוּdecline בְעֶבְרָתֶךָin your fury כִּלִּינוּfinish שָׁנֵינוּour years כְמוֹlike הֶגֶהsigh

For all our days decline in Your fury; we finish our years with a sigh.

90:10

יְמֵיdays שְׁנוֹתֵינוּour years בָהֶםin them שִׁבְעִיםseventy שָׁנָהyears וְאִםor if בִּגְבוּרֹתstrong שְׁמוֹנִיםeighty שָׁנָהyears וְרָהְבָּםyet their pride עָמָלlabor וָאָוֶןand sorrow כִּיfor גָזpass חִישׁquickly pass וַנָּעֻפָהand we fly

The length of our days is seventy years— or eighty if we are strong— yet their pride is but labor and sorrow, for they quickly pass, and we fly away.

90:11

מִיwho יוֹדֵעַknows עֹזpower אַפֶּךָyour anger your וּכְיִרְאָתְךָmatches the fear עֶבְרָתֶךָwrath

Who knows the power of Your anger? Your wrath matches the fear You are due.

90:12

לִמְנוֹתto number יָמֵינוּour days כֵּןso הוֹדַעteach וְנָבִאmay present לְבַבheart חָכְמָהwisdom

So teach us to number our days, that we may present a heart of wisdom.

90:13

שׁוּבָהreturn יְהוָהLORD עַדhow מָתָיlong וְהִנָּחֵםcompassion עַלon עֲבָדֶיךָyour servants

Return, O LORD! How long will it be? Have compassion on Your servants.

90:14

שַׂבְּעֵנוּsatisfy us בַבֹּקֶרin the morning חַסְדֶּךָyour loving devotion וּנְרַנְּנָהmay sing joy וְנִשְׂמְחָהand be glad בְּכָלall יָמֵינוּour days

Satisfy us in the morning with Your loving devotion, that we may sing for joy and be glad all our days.

90:15

שַׂמְּחֵנוּus glad כִּימוֹתas many days עִנִּיתָנוּafflicted us שְׁנוֹתyears רָאִינוּseen רָעָהevil

Make us glad for as many days as You have afflicted us, for as many years as we have seen evil.

90:16

יֵרָאֶהshown אֶלto עֲבָדֶיךָservants פָעֳלֶךָwork וַהֲדָרְךָand your splendor עַלto בְּנֵיהֶםtheir children

May Your work be shown to Your servants, and Your splendor to their children.

90:17

וִיהִיand be נֹעַםfavor אֲדֹנָיLord our אֱלֹהֵינוּour God עָלֵינוּupon us וּמַעֲשֵׂהwork יָדֵינוּour hands כּוֹנְנָהestablish עָלֵינוּfor us וּמַעֲשֵׂהwork יָדֵינוּour hands כּוֹנְנֵהוּestablish

May the favor of the Lord our God rest upon us; establish for us the work of our hands— yes, establish the work of our hands!
