Psalms 51

Confession and a plea for a pure heart

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. When Nathan the prophet came to him after his adultery with Bathsheba

51:1

חָנֵּנִיmercy on me אֱלֹהִיםGod כְּחַסְדֶּךָaccording to your loving devotion כְּרֹבgreat רַחֲמֶיךָyour compassion מְחֵהblot פְשָׁעָיmy transgressions

Have mercy on me, O God, according to Your loving devotion; according to Your great compassion, blot out my transgressions.

51:2

הֶרֶבclean כַּבְּסֵנִיwash me מֵעֲוֺנִיof my iniquity וּמֵחַטָּאתִיand me from sin טַהֲרֵנִיcleanse

Wash me clean of my iniquity and cleanse me from my sin.

51:3

כִּיfor פְשָׁעַיmy transgressions אֲנִיI אֵדָעknow וְחַטָּאתִיand my sin נֶגְדִּיbefore me תָמִידalways

For I know my transgressions, and my sin is always before me.

51:4

לְךָto you לְבַדְּךָyou only have חָטָאתִיsinned וְהָרַעevil בְּעֵינֶיךָin your sight עָשִׂיתִיdone לְמַעַןmay תִּצְדַּקproved right בְּדָבְרֶךָwhen you speak תִּזְכֶּהblameless בְשָׁפְטֶךָwhen you judge

Against You, You only, have I sinned and done what is evil in Your sight, so that You may be proved right when You speak and blameless when You judge.

51:5

הֵןsurely בְּעָווֹןin iniquity חוֹלָלְתִּיforth וּבְחֵטְאI was sinful יֶחֱמַתְנִיwhen my אִמִּיmother conceived me

Surely I was brought forth in iniquity; I was sinful when my mother conceived me.

51:6

הֵןsurely אֱמֶתtruth חָפַצְתָּdesire בַטֻּחוֹתin the inmost וּבְסָתֻםbeing חָכְמָהwisdom תוֹדִיעֵנִיteach me

Surely You desire truth in the inmost being; You teach me wisdom in the inmost place.

51:7

תְּחַטְּאֵנִיpurify me בְאֵזוֹבwith hyssop וְאֶטְהָרclean תְּכַבְּסֵנִיwash me וּמִשֶּׁלֶגwhiter than snow אַלְבִּיןwhiter

Purify me with hyssop, and I will be clean; wash me, and I will be whiter than snow.

51:8

תַּשְׁמִיעֵנִיme hear שָׂשׂוֹןjoy וְשִׂמְחָהand gladness תָּגֵלְנָהrejoice עֲצָמוֹתbones דִּכִּיתָcrushed

Let me hear joy and gladness; let the bones You have crushed rejoice.

51:9

הַסְתֵּרhide פָּנֶיךָyour face מֵחֲטָאָיfrom my sins וְכָלall עֲוֺנֹתַיmy iniquities מְחֵהblot

Hide Your face from my sins and blot out all my iniquities.

51:10

לֵבheart טָהוֹרclean בְּרָאcreate לִיto me אֱלֹהִיםGod וְרוּחַspirit נָכוֹןright חַדֵּשׁrenew בְּקִרְבִּיwithin me

Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me.

51:11

אַלnot תַּשְׁלִיכֵנִיcast me מִלְּפָנֶיךָaway from your presence וְרוּחַspirit קָדְשְׁךָyour holy אַלnot תִּקַּחtake מִמֶּנִּיfrom me

Cast me not away from Your presence; take not Your Holy Spirit from me.

51:12

הָשִׁיבָהrestore לִּיto me שְׂשׂוֹןjoy יִשְׁעֶךָyour salvation וְרוּחַspirit נְדִיבָהwilling תִסְמְכֵנִיsustain me

Restore to me the joy of Your salvation, and sustain me with a willing spirit.

51:13

אֲלַמְּדָהteach פֹשְׁעִיםtransgressors דְּרָכֶיךָyour ways וְחַטָּאִיםand sinners אֵלֶיךָto you יָשׁוּבוּreturn

Then I will teach transgressors Your ways, and sinners will return to You.

51:14

הַצִּילֵנִיdeliver me מִדָּמִיםfrom bloodguilt אֱלֹהִיםGod אֱלֹהֵיGod תְּשׁוּעָתִיmy salvation תְּרַנֵּןsing לְשׁוֹנִיmy tongue צִדְקָתֶךָyour righteousness

Deliver me from bloodguilt, O God, the God of my salvation, and my tongue will sing of Your righteousness.

51:15

אֲדֹנָיLord my שְׂפָתַיlips my תִּפְתָּחopen וּפִיand mouth will יַגִּידdeclare תְּהִלָּתֶךָyour praise

O Lord, open my lips, and my mouth will declare Your praise.

51:16

כִּיfor לֹאnot תַחְפֹּץdelight זֶבַחsacrifice וְאֶתֵּנָהbring עוֹלָהburnt offerings לֹאno תִרְצֶהpleasure

For You do not delight in sacrifice, or I would bring it; You take no pleasure in burnt offerings.

51:17

זִבְחֵיsacrifices אֱלֹהִיםGod רוּחַspirit נִשְׁבָּרָהbroken לֵבheart נִשְׁבָּרbroken וְנִדְכֶּהand a contrite אֱלֹהִיםGod לֹאnot תִבְזֶהdespise

The sacrifices of God are a broken spirit; a broken and a contrite heart, O God, You will not despise.

51:18

הֵיטִיבָהgood בִרְצוֹנְךָin your pleasure cause אֶת[obj] צִיּוֹןZion תִּבְנֶהbuild חוֹמוֹתwalls יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem

In Your good pleasure, cause Zion to prosper; build up the walls of Jerusalem.

51:19

אָזthen תַּחְפֹּץdelight זִבְחֵיsacrifices צֶדֶקrighteous עוֹלָהburnt offerings וְכָלִילin whole אָזthen יַעֲלוּoffered עַלon מִזְבַּחֲךָyour altar פָרִיםbulls

Then You will delight in righteous sacrifices, in whole burnt offerings; then bulls will be offered on Your altar.
