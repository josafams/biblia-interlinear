Psalms 27

Trust and longing for the house of the LORD

Open in the interlinear reader

27:1

לְדָוִדof David יְהוָהLORD אוֹרִיmy light וְיִשְׁעִיand my salvation מִמִּיwhom אִירָאfear יְהוָהLORD מָעוֹזstronghold חַיַּיmy life מִמִּיwhom אֶפְחָדdread

Of David. The LORD is my light and my salvation— whom shall I fear? The LORD is the stronghold of my life— whom shall I dread?

27:2

בִּקְרֹבwhen the came עָלַיupon me מְרֵעִיםwicked לֶאֱכֹלto devour אֶת[obj] בְּשָׂרִיmy flesh צָרַיenemies וְאֹיְבַיmy enemies and foes לִיto me הֵמָּהthey כָשְׁלוּstumbled וְנָפָלוּand fell

When the wicked came upon me to devour my flesh, my enemies and foes stumbled and fell.

27:3

אִםif תַּחֲנֶהencamps עָלַיon me מַחֲנֶהarmy לֹאnot יִירָאfear לִבִּיmy heart אִםif תָּקוּםbreaks עָלַיagainst me מִלְחָמָהwar בְּזֹאתin this אֲנִיI בוֹטֵחַtrust

Though an army encamps around me, my heart will not fear; though a war breaks out against me, I will keep my trust.

27:4

אַחַתone שָׁאַלְתִּיasked מֵאֵתof the יְהוָהLORD אוֹתָהּher אֲבַקֵּשׁdesire שִׁבְתִּיdwell בְּבֵיתin the house יְהוָהLORD כָּלall יְמֵיdays חַיַּיmy life לַחֲזוֹתto gaze בְּנֹעַםon the beauty יְהוָהLORD וּלְבַקֵּרand seek בְּהֵיכָלוֹin his temple

One thing I have asked of the LORD; this is what I desire: to dwell in the house of the LORD all the days of my life, to gaze on the beauty of the LORD and seek Him in His temple.

27:5

כִּיfor יִצְפְּנֵנִיconceal me בְּסֻכֹּהunder the cover of his בְּיוֹםin the day רָעָהtrouble יַסְתִּרֵנִיhide me בְּסֵתֶרin his shelter אָהֳלוֹtent he בְּצוּרupon a rock יְרוֹמְמֵנִיset me high

For in the day of trouble He will hide me in His shelter; He will conceal me under the cover of His tent; He will set me high upon a rock.

27:6

וְעַתָּהand now יָרוּםhigh רֹאשִׁיmy head עַלon אֹיְבַיmy enemies סְבִיבוֹתַיaround me וְאֶזְבְּחָהI will offer בְאָהֳלוֹat his tabernacle זִבְחֵיsacrifices תְרוּעָהshouts אָשִׁירָהsing וַאֲזַמְּרָהand make music לַיהוָהto the LORD

Then my head will be held high above my enemies around me. At His tabernacle I will offer sacrifices with shouts of joy; I will sing and make music to the LORD.

27:7

שְׁמַעhear יְהוָהLORD קוֹלִיmy voice I אֶקְרָאcall וְחָנֵּנִיmerciful וַעֲנֵנִיand answer me

Hear, O LORD, my voice when I call; be merciful and answer me.

27:8

לְךָto you אָמַרsaid לִבִּיmy heart בַּקְּשׁוּseek פָנָיface אֶת[obj] פָּנֶיךָyour face O יְהוָהLORD אֲבַקֵּשׁseek

My heart said, “Seek His face.” Your face, O LORD, I will seek.

27:9

אַלnot תַּסְתֵּרhide פָּנֶיךָyour face מִמֶּנִּיfrom me אַלdo not תַּטturn בְּאַףin anger עַבְדֶּךָyour servant עֶזְרָתִיmy helper הָיִיתָbeen אַלdo not תִּטְּשֵׁנִיleave me וְאַלor תַּעַזְבֵנִיforsake me אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׁעִיmy salvation

Hide not Your face from me, nor turn away Your servant in anger. You have been my helper; do not leave me or forsake me, O God of my salvation.

27:10

כִּיfor אָבִיmy father וְאִמִּיand mother עֲזָבוּנִיforsake me וַיהוָהLORD יַאַסְפֵנִיreceive me

Though my father and mother forsake me, the LORD will receive me.

27:11

הוֹרֵנִיteach me יְהוָהLORD דַּרְכֶּךָyour way וּנְחֵנִיand lead me בְּאֹרַחpath מִישׁוֹרlevel לְמַעַןbecause שׁוֹרְרָיmy oppressors

Teach me Your way, O LORD, and lead me on a level path, because of my oppressors.

27:12

אַלdo not תִּתְּנֵנִיhand me בְּנֶפֶשׁin life צָרָיmy foes כִּיfor קָמוּrise בִיagainst me עֵדֵיwitnesses שֶׁקֶרfalse וִיפֵחַbreathing חָמָסviolence

Do not hand me over to the will of my foes, for false witnesses rise up against me, breathing out violence.

27:13

לׅׄוּלֵׅׄאׅׄstill הֶאֱמַנְתִּיcertain לִרְאוֹתto see בְּטוּבthe goodness יְהוָהLORD בְּאֶרֶץin the land חַיִּיםliving

Still I am certain to see the goodness of the LORD in the land of the living.

27:14

קַוֵּהwait אֶלto יְהוָהLORD חֲזַקstrong וְיַאֲמֵץand courageous לִבֶּךָyour heart וְקַוֵּהwait patiently אֶלto יְהוָהLORD

Wait patiently for the LORD; be strong and courageous. Wait patiently for the LORD!
