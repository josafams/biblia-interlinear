Psalms 29

The voice of the LORD in the storm

Open in the interlinear reader

29:1

מִזְמוֹרPsalm לְדָוִדof David הָבוּascribe לַיהוָהto LORD בְּנֵיsons אֵלִיםheavenly beings הָבוּascribe לַיהוָהto the LORD כָּבוֹדglory וָעֹזand strength

A Psalm of David. Ascribe to the LORD, O heavenly beings, ascribe to the LORD glory and strength.

29:2

הָבוּascribe לַיהוָהto the LORD כְּבוֹדglory שְׁמוֹhis name הִשְׁתַּחֲווּworship לַיהוָהthe LORD בְּהַדְרַתin the splendor קֹדֶשׁholiness

Ascribe to the LORD the glory due His name; worship the LORD in the splendor of His holiness.

29:3

קוֹלvoice יְהוָהLORD עַלover הַמָּיִםthe waters אֵלGod הַכָּבוֹדglory הִרְעִיםthunders יְהוָהLORD עַלover מַיִםwaters רַבִּיםmany

The voice of the LORD is over the waters; the God of glory thunders; the LORD is heard over many waters.

29:4

קוֹלvoice יְהוָהLORD בַּכֹּחַis powerful קוֹלvoice יְהוָהLORD בֶּהָדָרis majestic

The voice of the LORD is powerful; the voice of the LORD is majestic.

29:5

קוֹלvoice יְהוָהLORD שֹׁבֵרbreaks אֲרָזִיםcedars וַיְשַׁבֵּרshatters יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] אַרְזֵיcedars הַלְּבָנוֹןof Lebanon

The voice of the LORD breaks the cedars; the LORD shatters the cedars of Lebanon.

29:6

וַיַּרְקִידֵםskip כְּמוֹlike עֵגֶלcalf לְבָנוֹןLebanon וְשִׂרְיֹןand Sirion כְּמוֹlike בֶןyoung רְאֵמִיםwild ox

He makes Lebanon skip like a calf, and Sirion like a young wild ox.

29:7

קוֹלvoice יְהוָהLORD חֹצֵבstrikes לַהֲבוֹתflames אֵשׁfire

The voice of the LORD strikes with flames of fire.

29:8

קוֹלvoice יְהוָהLORD יָחִילshakes מִדְבָּרwilderness יָחִילshakes יְהוָהLORD מִדְבַּרwilderness קָדֵשׁKadesh

The voice of the LORD shakes the wilderness; the LORD shakes the Wilderness of Kadesh.

29:9

קוֹלvoice יְהוָהLORD יְחוֹלֵלtwists אַיָּלוֹתoaks וַיֶּחֱשֹׂףand strips יְעָרוֹתforests וּבְהֵיכָלוֹand in his temple כֻּלּוֹall אֹמֵרcry כָּבוֹדglory

The voice of the LORD twists the oaks and strips the forests bare. And in His temple all cry, “Glory!”

29:10

יְהוָהLORD לַמַּבּוּלover the flood יָשָׁבsits וַיֵּשֶׁבenthroned יְהוָהLORD מֶלֶךְking לְעוֹלָםforever

The LORD sits enthroned over the flood; the LORD is enthroned as King forever.

29:11

יְהוָהLORD עֹזstrength לְעַמּוֹhis people יִתֵּןgives יְהוָהLORD יְבָרֵךְblesses אֶת[obj] עַמּוֹhis people בַשָּׁלוֹםwith peace

The LORD gives His people strength; the LORD blesses His people with peace.
