For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David

20:1

יַעַנְךָanswer you יְהוָהLORD בְּיוֹםin the day צָרָהtrouble יְשַׂגֶּבְךָprotect you שֵׁםname אֱלֹהֵיGod יַעֲקֹבJacob

May the LORD answer you in the day of trouble; may the name of the God of Jacob protect you.

20:2

יִשְׁלַחsend עֶזְרְךָyou help מִקֹּדֶשׁfrom the sanctuary וּמִצִּיּוֹןfrom Zion יִסְעָדֶךָּsustain you

May He send you help from the sanctuary and sustain you from Zion.

20:3

יִזְכֹּרremember כָּלall מִנְחֹתֶךָyour gifts וְעוֹלָתְךָyour burnt offerings יְדַשְּׁנֶהofferings סֶלָהSelah

May He remember all your gifts and look favorably on your burnt offerings. Selah

20:4

יִתֶּןgive לְךָto you כִלְבָבֶךָyour heart וְכָלand make all עֲצָתְךָyour plans יְמַלֵּאsucceed

May He give you the desires of your heart and make all your plans succeed.

20:5

נְרַנְּנָהjoy בִּישׁוּעָתֶךָat your victory וּבְשֵׁםin the name אֱלֹהֵינוּour God נִדְגֹּלbanner יְמַלֵּאraise יְהוָהLORD כָּלall מִשְׁאֲלוֹתֶיךָgrant your petitions

May we shout for joy at your victory and raise a banner in the name of our God. May the LORD grant all your petitions.

20:6

עַתָּהnow יָדַעְתִּיknow כִּיthat הוֹשִׁיעַsaves יְהוָהLORD מְשִׁיחוֹhis anointed he יַעֲנֵהוּanswers him מִשְּׁמֵיheaven קָדְשׁוֹhis holy בִּגְבֻרוֹתpower יֵשַׁעsaving יְמִינוֹhis right hand

Now I know that the LORD saves His anointed; He answers him from His holy heaven with the saving power of His right hand.

20:7

אֵלֶּהthese בָרֶכֶבtrust in chariots וְאֵלֶּהand these בַסּוּסִיםin horses וַאֲנַחְנוּbut we בְּשֵׁםtrust in the name יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God נַזְכִּירinvoke

Some trust in chariots and others in horses, but we trust in the name of the LORD our God.

20:8

הֵמָּהthey כָּרְעוּcollapse וְנָפָלוּand fall וַאֲנַחְנוּbut we קַּמְנוּrise וַנִּתְעוֹדָדand firm

They collapse and fall, but we rise up and stand firm.

20:9

יְהוָהLORD הוֹשִׁיעָהsave הַמֶּלֶךְthe king יַעֲנֵנוּanswer us בְיוֹםon the day קָרְאֵנוּwe call

O LORD, save the king. Answer us on the day we call.
