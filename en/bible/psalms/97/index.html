Psalms 97

God's reign makes the earth rejoice

Open in the interlinear reader

97:1

יְהוָהLORD מָלָךְreigns תָּגֵלlet הָאָרֶץthe earth יִשְׂמְחוּglad אִיִּיםshores רַבִּיםdistant

The LORD reigns, let the earth rejoice; let the distant shores be glad.

97:2

עָנָןclouds וַעֲרָפֶלand darkness סְבִיבָיוsurround him צֶדֶקrighteousness וּמִשְׁפָּטand justice מְכוֹןfoundation כִּסְאוֹhis throne’s

Clouds and darkness surround Him; righteousness and justice are His throne’s foundation.

97:3

אֵשׁfire לְפָנָיוgoes before him תֵּלֵךְgo וּתְלַהֵטand consumes סָבִיבevery צָרָיוhis foes

Fire goes before Him and consumes His foes on every side.

97:4

הֵאִירוּilluminates בְרָקָיוhis lightning תֵּבֵלworld רָאֲתָהsees וַתָּחֵלand trembles הָאָרֶץthe earth

His lightning illuminates the world; the earth sees and trembles.

97:5

הָרִיםmountains כַּדּוֹנַגlike wax נָמַסּוּmelt מִלִּפְנֵיat the presence יְהוָהLORD מִלִּפְנֵיbefore אֲדוֹןLord כָּלall הָאָרֶץthe earth

The mountains melt like wax at the presence of the LORD, before the Lord of all the earth.

97:6

הִגִּידוּproclaim הַשָּׁמַיִםthe heavens צִדְקוֹhis righteousness וְרָאוּsee כָלall הָעַמִּיםthe peoples כְּבוֹדוֹhis glory

The heavens proclaim His righteousness; all the peoples see His glory.

97:7

יֵבֹשׁוּshame כָּלall עֹבְדֵיworshipers פֶסֶלimages הַמִּתְהַלְלִיםwho boast בָּאֱלִילִיםin idols הִשְׁתַּחֲווּworship לוֹto him כָּלall אֱלֹהִיםgods

All worshipers of images are put to shame— those who boast in idols. Worship Him, all you gods!

97:8

שָׁמְעָהhears וַתִּשְׂמַחand rejoices צִיּוֹןZion וַתָּגֵלְנָהrejoices and בְּנוֹתtowns יְהוּדָהJudah לְמַעַןbecause מִשְׁפָּטֶיךָyour judgments יְהוָהLORD

Zion hears and rejoices, and the towns of Judah exult because of Your judgments, O LORD.

97:9

כִּיfor אַתָּהyou יְהוָהLORD עֶלְיוֹןMost high עַלover כָּלall הָאָרֶץthe earth מְאֹדvery נַעֲלֵיתָlifted עַלon כָּלall אֱלֹהִיםgods

For You, O LORD, are Most High over all the earth; You are exalted far above all gods.

97:10

אֹהֲבֵיlove יְהוָהLORD שִׂנְאוּhate רָעevil שֹׁמֵרpreserves נַפְשׁוֹתsouls חֲסִידָיוhis saints he מִיַּדfrom the hand רְשָׁעִיםwicked יַצִּילֵםdelivers them

Hate evil, O you who love the LORD! He preserves the souls of His saints; He delivers them from the hand of the wicked.

97:11

אוֹרlight זָרֻעַshines לַצַּדִּיקon the righteous וּלְיִשְׁרֵיon the upright לֵבheart שִׂמְחָהgladness

Light shines on the righteous, gladness on the upright in heart.

97:12

שִׂמְחוּrejoice צַדִּיקִיםrighteous בַּיהוָהin the LORD וְהוֹדוּand praise לְזֵכֶרname קָדְשׁוֹhis holy

Rejoice in the LORD, you righteous ones, and praise His holy name.
