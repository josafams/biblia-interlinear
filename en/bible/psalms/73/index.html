Psalms 73

Envy of the wicked and the sanctuary

Open in the interlinear reader

73:1

מִזְמוֹרPsalm לְאָסָףof Asaph אַךְsurely טוֹבgood לְיִשְׂרָאֵלto Israel אֱלֹהִיםGod לְבָרֵיpure לֵבָבheart

A Psalm of Asaph. Surely God is good to Israel, to those who are pure in heart.

73:2

וַאֲנִיand I כִּמְעַטhad almost נָטָיוּstumbled רַגְלָיme my feet כְּאַיִןlike nothing שֻׁפְּכוּslipped אֲשֻׁרָיmy steps

But as for me, my feet had almost stumbled; my steps had nearly slipped.

73:3

כִּיfor קִנֵּאתִיenvied בַּהוֹלְלִיםthe arrogant when שְׁלוֹםprosperity רְשָׁעִיםwicked אֶרְאֶהsaw

For I envied the arrogant when I saw the prosperity of the wicked.

73:4

כִּיfor אֵיןno חַרְצֻבּוֹתstruggle לְמוֹתָםin death their וּבָרִיאwell fed אוּלָםbodies

They have no struggle in their death; their bodies are well-fed.

73:5

בַּעֲמַלburdens אֱנוֹשׁothers אֵינֵמוֹno them וְעִםand with אָדָםmen לֹאnot יְנֻגָּעוּafflicted

They are free of the burdens others carry; they are not afflicted like other men.

73:6

לָכֵןtherefore עֲנָקַתְמוֹtheir necklace גַאֲוָהpride יַעֲטָףnecklace שִׁיתgarment חָמָסviolence לָמוֹto them

Therefore pride is their necklace; a garment of violence covers them.

73:7

יָצָאcame מֵחֵלֶבfrom their prosperity proceeds עֵינֵמוֹiniquity the imaginations עָבְרוּcrossed מַשְׂכִּיּוֹתrun wild לֵבָבhearts

From their prosperity proceeds iniquity; the imaginations of their hearts run wild.

73:8

יָמִיקוּmock וִידַבְּרוּand speak בְרָעwith malice עֹשֶׁקoppression מִמָּרוֹםwith arrogance יְדַבֵּרוּthreaten

They mock and speak with malice; with arrogance they threaten oppression.

73:9

שַׁתּוּthey בַשָּׁמַיִםagainst the heavens פִּיהֶםtheir mouths וּלְשׁוֹנָםand their tongues תִּהֲלַךְstrut בָּאָרֶץacross the earth

They set their mouths against the heavens, and their tongues strut across the earth.

73:10

לָכֵןso יָשׁוּבreturn עַמּוֹpeople to הֲלֹםthis וּמֵיwaters מָלֵאdrink יִמָּצוּabundance לָמוֹto them

So their people return to this place and drink up waters in abundance.

73:11

וְאָמְרוּsay אֵיכָהhow can יָדַעknow אֵלGod וְיֵשׁhave דֵּעָהknowledge בְעֶלְיוֹןthe Most high

The wicked say, “How can God know? Does the Most High have knowledge?”

73:12

הִנֵּהbehold אֵלֶּהthese רְשָׁעִיםwicked וְשַׁלְוֵיcarefree עוֹלָםalways הִשְׂגּוּcarefree חָיִלwealth

Behold, these are the wicked— always carefree as they increase their wealth.

73:13

אַךְsurely רִיקvain זִכִּיתִיpure לְבָבִיmy heart וָאֶרְחַץI have washed בְּנִקָּיוֹןin innocence כַּפָּיmy hands

Surely in vain I have kept my heart pure; in innocence I have washed my hands.

73:14

וָאֱהִיam נָגוּעַafflicted כָּלall הַיּוֹםday long וְתוֹכַחְתִּיand punished לַבְּקָרִיםevery morning

For I am afflicted all day long and punished every morning.

73:15

אִםif אָמַרְתִּיsaid אֲסַפְּרָהhad כְמוֹlike הִנֵּהbehold דוֹרwould בָּנֶיךָyour children בָגָדְתִּיbetrayed

If I had said, “I will speak this way,” then I would have betrayed Your children.

73:16

וָאֲחַשְּׁבָהwhen I tried לָדַעַתto understand זֹאתthis עָמָלtroublesome הוּאwas בְעֵינָיin my sight

When I tried to understand all this, it was troublesome in my sight

73:17

עַדuntil אָבוֹאentered אֶלto מִקְדְּשֵׁיsanctuary אֵלGod’s אָבִינָהdiscerned לְאַחֲרִיתָםtheir end

until I entered God’s sanctuary; then I discerned their end.

73:18

אַךְsurely בַּחֲלָקוֹתon slick ground תָּשִׁיתset לָמוֹto them הִפַּלְתָּםcast them לְמַשּׁוּאוֹתinto ruin

Surely You set them on slick ground; You cast them down into ruin.

73:19

אֵיךְhow הָיוּare לְשַׁמָּהare laid waste כְרָגַעcompletely swept סָפוּaway תַמּוּcompletely מִןfrom בַּלָּהוֹתterrors

How suddenly they are laid waste, completely swept away by terrors!

73:20

כַּחֲלוֹםdream מֵהָקִיץwaking from אֲדֹנָיLord בָּעִירawaken צַלְמָםtheir form תִּבְזֶהdespise

Like one waking from a dream, so You, O Lord, awaken and despise their form.

73:21

כִּיwhen יִתְחַמֵּץleavened לְבָבִיmy heart was grieved וְכִלְיוֹתַיand I was pierced אֶשְׁתּוֹנָןsharpen

When my heart was grieved and I was pierced within,

73:22

וַאֲנִיI בַעַרsenseless ignorant וְלֹאand not אֵדָעbrute בְּהֵמוֹתbeast הָיִיתִיwas עִמָּךְwith you

I was senseless and ignorant; I was a brute beast before You.

73:23

וַאֲנִיyet I am תָמִידalways עִמָּךְwith you אָחַזְתָּhold בְּיַדhand יְמִינִיmy right

Yet I am always with You; You hold my right hand.

73:24

בַּעֲצָתְךָwith your counsel תַנְחֵנִיguide me וְאַחַרand later כָּבוֹדglory תִּקָּחֵנִיreceive me

You guide me with Your counsel, and later receive me in glory.

73:25

מִיwhom לִיto me בַשָּׁמָיִםin heaven וְעִמְּךָand with you לֹאno חָפַצְתִּיdesire בָאָרֶץon earth

Whom have I in heaven but You? And on earth I desire no one besides You.

73:26

כָּלָהmy שְׁאֵרִיmy flesh my וּלְבָבִיand heart צוּרstrength לְבָבִיmy heart וְחֶלְקִיmy portion אֱלֹהִיםGod לְעוֹלָםforever

My flesh and my heart may fail, but God is the strength of my heart and my portion forever.

73:27

כִּיfor הִנֵּהsurely רְחֵקֶיךָperish you יֹאבֵדוּperish הִצְמַתָּהdestroy כָּלall זוֹנֶהunfaithful מִמֶּךָּfrom you

Those far from You will surely perish; You destroy all who are unfaithful to You.

73:28

וַאֲנִיI קִרֲבַתnear אֱלֹהִיםGod לִיfor me טוֹבgood שַׁתִּיmade בַּאדֹנָיLord my יְהֹוִהGOD מַחְסִיrefuge that I לְסַפֵּרmay proclaim כָּלall מַלְאֲכוֹתֶיךָyour works

But as for me, it is good to draw near to God. I have made the Lord GOD my refuge, that I may proclaim all Your works.
