Psalms 46

God, the refuge of the city amid chaos

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. Of the sons of Korah. According to Alamoth. A song

46:1

אֱלֹהִיםGod לָנוּto us מַחֲסֶהrefuge וָעֹזand strength עֶזְרָהhelp בְצָרוֹתtrouble נִמְצָאpresent מְאֹדvery

God is our refuge and strength, an ever-present help in times of trouble.

46:2

עַלon כֵּןtherefore לֹאnot נִירָאfear בְּהָמִירthough אָרֶץearth וּבְמוֹטtoppled הָרִיםmountains בְּלֵבinto the depths יַמִּיםseas

Therefore we will not fear, though the earth is transformed and the mountains are toppled into the depths of the seas,

46:3

יֶהֱמוּroar יֶחְמְרוּfoam מֵימָיוwaters יִרְעֲשׁוּquake הָרִיםmountains בְּגַאֲוָתוֹin the surge סֶלָהSelah

though their waters roar and foam and the mountains quake in the surge. Selah

46:4

נָהָרriver פְּלָגָיוwhose streams יְשַׂמְּחוּdelight עִירcity אֱלֹהִיםGod קְדֹשׁholy מִשְׁכְּנֵיplace where עֶלְיוֹןMost high

There is a river whose streams delight the city of God, the holy place where the Most High dwells.

46:5

אֱלֹהִיםGod בְּקִרְבָּהּis within her בַּלnot תִּמּוֹטmoved יַעְזְרֶהָhelp her אֱלֹהִיםGod לִפְנוֹתwhen dawns בֹּקֶרmorning

God is within her; she will not be moved. God will help her when morning dawns.

46:6

הָמוּrage גוֹיִםnations מָטוּcrumble מַמְלָכוֹתkingdoms נָתַןcrumble בְּקוֹלוֹwhen he lifts his voice תָּמוּגmelts אָרֶץearth

Nations rage, kingdoms crumble; the earth melts when He lifts His voice.

46:7

יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts עִמָּנוּwith מִשְׂגָּבfortress לָנוּto us אֱלֹהֵיGod יַעֲקֹבJacob סֶלָהSelah

The LORD of Hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our fortress. Selah

46:8

לְכוּcome חֲזוּsee מִפְעֲלוֹתworks יְהוָהLORD אֲשֶׁרwho שָׂםwho שַׁמּוֹתdevastation בָּאָרֶץupon the earth

Come, see the works of the LORD, who brings devastation upon the earth.

46:9

מַשְׁבִּיתcease מִלְחָמוֹתwars עַדto קְצֵהthroughout הָאָרֶץthe earth קֶשֶׁתbow יְשַׁבֵּרshatters וְקִצֵּץand shatters חֲנִיתspear עֲגָלוֹתshields יִשְׂרֹףshields בָּאֵשׁin the fire

He makes wars to cease throughout the earth; He breaks the bow and shatters the spear; He burns the shields in the fire.

46:10

הַרְפּוּstill וּדְעוּand know כִּיthat אָנֹכִיam אֱלֹהִיםGod אָרוּםexalted בַּגּוֹיִםamong the nations אָרוּםexalted בָּאָרֶץover the earth

“Be still and know that I am God; I will be exalted among the nations, I will be exalted over the earth.”

46:11

יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts עִמָּנוּwith us מִשְׂגָּבfortress לָנוּto us אֱלֹהֵיGod יַעֲקֹבJacob סֶלָהSelah

The LORD of Hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our fortress. Selah
