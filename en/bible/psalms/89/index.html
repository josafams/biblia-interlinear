Psalms 89

The covenant with David in crisis

Open in the interlinear reader

A Maskil of Ethan the Ezrahite

89:1

חַסְדֵיloving devotion יְהוָהLORD עוֹלָםforever אָשִׁירָהsing לְדֹרto all וָדֹרgenerations אוֹדִיעַproclaim אֱמוּנָתְךָyour faithfulness בְּפִיwith my mouth

I will sing of the loving devotion of the LORD forever; with my mouth I will proclaim Your faithfulness to all generations.

89:2

כִּיfor אָמַרְתִּיsaid עוֹלָםforever חֶסֶדloving devotion יִבָּנֶהbuilt שָׁמַיִםheavens תָּכִןestablish אֱמוּנָתְךָyour faithfulness בָהֶםin them

For I have said, “Loving devotion is built up forever; in the heavens You establish Your faithfulness.”

89:3

כָּרַתִּיmade בְרִיתcovenant לִבְחִירִיwith my chosen נִשְׁבַּעְתִּיsworn לְדָוִדto David עַבְדִּיmy servant

You said, “I have made a covenant with My chosen one, I have sworn to David My servant:

89:4

עַדuntil עוֹלָםforever אָכִיןestablish זַרְעֶךָyour offspring וּבָנִיתִיand build לְדֹרfor all generations וָדוֹרand generations כִּסְאֲךָyour throne סֶלָהSelah

‘I will establish your offspring forever and build up your throne for all generations.’” Selah

89:5

וְיוֹדוּpraise שָׁמַיִםheavens פִּלְאֲךָyour wonders יְהוָהLORD אַףhow much אֱמוּנָתְךָyour faithfulness בִּקְהַלin the assembly קְדֹשִׁיםholy

The heavens praise Your wonders, O LORD— Your faithfulness as well— in the assembly of the holy ones.

89:6

כִּיfor מִיwho בַשַּׁחַקin the skies יַעֲרֹךְcompare לַיהוָהwith the LORD יִדְמֶהlike לַיהוָהthe LORD בִּבְנֵיin sons אֵלִיםheavenly beings

For who in the skies can compare with the LORD? Who among the heavenly beings is like the LORD?

89:7

אֵלGod נַעֲרָץgreatly בְּסוֹדin the council קְדֹשִׁיםholy רַבָּהfeared וְנוֹרָאand awesome עַלon כָּלall סְבִיבָיוsurround him

In the council of the holy ones, God is greatly feared, and awesome above all who surround Him.

89:8

יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod צְבָאוֹתHosts מִיwho כָמוֹךָlike you חֲסִיןmighty יָהּLORD וֶאֱמוּנָתְךָyour faithfulness סְבִיבוֹתֶיךָsurrounds you

O LORD God of Hosts, who is like You? O mighty LORD, Your faithfulness surrounds You.

89:9

אַתָּהyou מוֹשֵׁלrule בְּגֵאוּתthe raging הַיָּםsea בְּשׂוֹאwhen its mount גַלָּיוits waves אַתָּהyou תְשַׁבְּחֵםstill them

You rule the raging sea; when its waves mount up, You still them.

89:10

אַתָּהyou דִכִּאתָcrushed כֶחָלָלlike a carcass רָהַבRahab בִּזְרוֹעַarm עֻזְּךָmighty פִּזַּרְתָּscattered אוֹיְבֶיךָenemies your

You crushed Rahab like a carcass; You scattered Your enemies with Your mighty arm.

89:11

לְךָto you שָׁמַיִםheavens אַףalso לְךָto you אָרֶץearth תֵּבֵלearth וּמְלֹאָהּand its fullness אַתָּהyou יְסַדְתָּםfounded

The heavens are Yours, and also the earth. The earth and its fullness You founded.

89:12

צָפוֹןnorth וְיָמִיןand south אַתָּהyou בְרָאתָםcreated תָּבוֹרTabor וְחֶרְמוֹןand Hermon בְּשִׁמְךָat your name יְרַנֵּנוּjoy

North and south You created; Tabor and Hermon shout for joy at Your name.

89:13

לְךָto you זְרוֹעַarm עִםwith גְּבוּרָהstrong תָּעֹזstrengthened יָדְךָhand תָּרוּםexalted יְמִינֶךָhand your right

Mighty is Your arm; strong is Your hand. Your right hand is exalted.

89:14

צֶדֶקrighteousness וּמִשְׁפָּטand justice מְכוֹןfoundation כִּסְאֶךָyour throne חֶסֶדloving devotion וֶאֱמֶתand faithfulness יְקַדְּמוּgo פָנֶיךָbefore you

Righteousness and justice are the foundation of Your throne; loving devotion and faithfulness go before You.

89:15

אַשְׁרֵיblessed הָעָםthe people יוֹדְעֵיknow תְרוּעָהjoyful sound יְהוָהLORD בְּאוֹרin the light פָּנֶיךָyour presence יְהַלֵּכוּןwalk

Blessed are those who know the joyful sound, who walk, O LORD, in the light of Your presence.

89:16

בְּשִׁמְךָin your name יְגִילוּןrejoice כָּלall הַיּוֹםday long וּבְצִדְקָתְךָand in your righteousness יָרוּמוּexult

They rejoice in Your name all day long, and in Your righteousness they exult.

89:17

כִּיfor תִפְאֶרֶתglory עֻזָּמוֹtheir strength אָתָּהyou וּבִרְצֹנְךָand by your favor תָּרוּםexalted קַרְנֵנוּour horn

For You are the glory of their strength, and by Your favor our horn is exalted.

89:18

כִּיfor לַיהוָהto the LORD מָגִנֵּנוּour shield וְלִקְדוֹשׁto the holy יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael מַלְכֵּנוּour king

Surely our shield belongs to the LORD, and our king to the Holy One of Israel.

89:19

אָזthen דִּבַּרְתָּspoke בְחָזוֹןin a vision לַחֲסִידֶיךָto your godly וַתֹּאמֶרyou said שִׁוִּיתִיbestowed עֵזֶרhelp עַלon גִּבּוֹרwarrior הֲרִימוֹתִיexalted בָחוּרchosen מֵעָםfrom the people

You once spoke in a vision; to Your godly ones You said, “I have bestowed help on a warrior; I have exalted one chosen from the people.

89:20

מָצָאתִיfound דָּוִדDavid עַבְדִּיmy servant בְּשֶׁמֶןoil קָדְשִׁיsacred I מְשַׁחְתִּיוanointed him

I have found My servant David; with My sacred oil I have anointed him.

89:21

אֲשֶׁרthat יָדִיmy hand תִּכּוֹןsustain עִמּוֹwith him אַףsurely זְרוֹעִיmy arm תְאַמְּצֶנּוּstrengthen him

My hand will sustain him; surely My arm will strengthen him.

89:22

לֹאno יַשִּׁאdebtor אוֹיֵבenemy בּוֹin him וּבֶןwill exact עַוְלָהtribute wicked לֹאno יְעַנֶּנּוּoppress him

No enemy will exact tribute; no wicked man will oppress him.

89:23

וְכַתּוֹתִיwill crush מִפָּנָיוhis before צָרָיוfoes him וּמְשַׂנְאָיוhate him אֶגּוֹףstrike

I will crush his foes before him and strike down those who hate him.

89:24

וֶאֶמוּנָתִיmy faithfulness וְחַסְדִּיand loving devotion will עִמּוֹwith him וּבִשְׁמִיand through my name תָּרוּםexalted קַרְנוֹhis horn

My faithfulness and loving devotion will be with him, and through My name his horn will be exalted.

89:25

וְשַׂמְתִּיwill set בַיָּםover the sea יָדוֹhis hand וּבַנְּהָרוֹתupon the rivers יְמִינוֹhis right hand

I will set his hand over the sea, and his right hand upon the rivers.

89:26

הוּאhe יִקְרָאֵנִיcall אָבִיfather אָתָּהyou אֵלִיmy God וְצוּרrock יְשׁוּעָתִיmy salvation

He will call to Me, ‘You are my Father, my God, the Rock of my salvation.’

89:27

אַףindeed אָנִיI בְּכוֹרfirstborn אֶתְּנֵהוּappoint him עֶלְיוֹןhighest לְמַלְכֵיof the kings אָרֶץearth

I will indeed appoint him as My firstborn, the highest of the kings of the earth.

89:28

לְעוֹלָםwill forever אֶשְׁמָרpreserve לוֹfor him חַסְדִּיmy loving devotion וּבְרִיתִיand my covenant נֶאֱמֶנֶתstand לוֹto him

I will forever preserve My loving devotion for him, and My covenant with him will stand fast.

89:29

וְשַׂמְתִּיwill establish לָעַדhis forever זַרְעוֹline his וְכִסְאוֹthrone כִּימֵיas long שָׁמָיִםheavens

I will establish his line forever, his throne as long as the heavens endure.

89:30

אִםif יַעַזְבוּforsake בָנָיוhis sons תּוֹרָתִיmy law וּבְמִשְׁפָּטַיin my judgments לֹאnot יֵלֵכוּןwalk

If his sons forsake My law and do not walk in My judgments,

89:31

אִםif חֻקֹּתַיmy statutes יְחַלֵּלוּviolate וּמִצְוֺתַיmy commandments לֹאnot יִשְׁמֹרוּkeep

if they violate My statutes and fail to keep My commandments,

89:32

וּפָקַדְתִּיwill attend בְשֵׁבֶטrod פִּשְׁעָםtheir transgression וּבִנְגָעִיםwith stripes עֲוֺנָםtheir iniquity

I will attend to their transgression with the rod, and to their iniquity with stripes.

89:33

וְחַסְדִּיmy loving devotion לֹאnot אָפִירwithdraw מֵעִמּוֹfrom presence him וְלֹאand not אֲשַׁקֵּרbetray בֶּאֱמוּנָתִיmy faithfulness

But I will not withdraw My loving devotion from him, nor ever betray My faithfulness.

89:34

לֹאnot אֲחַלֵּלviolate בְּרִיתִיmy covenant וּמוֹצָאutterance שְׂפָתַיmy lips לֹאnot אֲשַׁנֶּהalter

I will not violate My covenant or alter the utterance of My lips.

89:35

אַחַתonce נִשְׁבַּעְתִּיsworn בְקָדְשִׁיby my holiness אִםnot לְדָוִדto David אֲכַזֵּבlie

Once and for all I have sworn by My holiness— I will not lie to David—

89:36

זַרְעוֹhis offspring לְעוֹלָםendure forever יִהְיֶהbe וְכִסְאוֹand his throne כַשֶּׁמֶשׁlike the sun נֶגְדִּיbefore me

his offspring shall endure forever, and his throne before Me like the sun,

89:37

כְּיָרֵחַlike the moon יִכּוֹןestablished עוֹלָםforever וְעֵדwitness בַּשַּׁחַקin the sky נֶאֱמָןfaithful סֶלָהSelah

like the moon, established forever, a faithful witness in the sky.” Selah

89:38

וְאַתָּהnow however you זָנַחְתָּspurned וַתִּמְאָסand rejected הִתְעַבַּרְתָּenraged עִםwith מְשִׁיחֶךָyour anointed

Now, however, You have spurned and rejected him; You are enraged by Your anointed one.

89:39

נֵאַרְתָּהrenounced בְּרִיתcovenant עַבְדֶּךָyour servant חִלַּלְתָּsullied לָאָרֶץin the dust נִזְרוֹhis crown

You have renounced the covenant with Your servant and sullied his crown in the dust.

89:40

פָּרַצְתָּbroken כָלall גְּדֵרֹתָיוhis walls שַׂמְתָּhave מִבְצָרָיוhis strongholds מְחִתָּהrubble

You have broken down all his walls; You have reduced his strongholds to rubble.

89:41

שַׁסֻּהוּplunder him כָּלall עֹבְרֵיpass דָרֶךְway הָיָהbecome חֶרְפָּהreproach לִשְׁכֵנָיוto his neighbors

All who pass by plunder him; he has become a reproach to his neighbors.

89:42

הֲרִימוֹתָexalted יְמִיןright hand צָרָיוhis foes הִשְׂמַחְתָּrejoice כָּלall אוֹיְבָיוhis enemies

You have exalted the right hand of his foes; You have made all his enemies rejoice.

89:43

אַףhow much תָּשִׁיבreturn צוּרedge חַרְבּוֹhis sword וְלֹאand have not הֲקֵימֹתוֹsustained him בַּמִּלְחָמָהin battle

You have bent the edge of his sword and have not sustained him in battle.

89:44

הִשְׁבַּתָּyou מִטְּהָרוֹended his splendor וְכִסְאוֹand cast his throne לָאָרֶץto the ground מִגַּרְתָּהtossed

You have ended his splendor and cast his throne to the ground.

89:45

הִקְצַרְתָּshort יְמֵיdays עֲלוּמָיוhis youth הֶעֱטִיתָcovered עָלָיוon him בּוּשָׁהshame סֶלָהSelah

You have cut short the days of his youth; You have covered him with shame. Selah

89:46

עַדuntil מָהhow יְהוָהLORD תִּסָּתֵרhide לָנֶצַחyourself forever תִּבְעַרburning כְּמוֹlike אֵשׁfire חֲמָתֶךָyour wrath

How long, O LORD? Will You hide Yourself forever? Will Your wrath keep burning like fire?

89:47

זְכָרremember אֲנִיI מֶהwhat חָלֶדlifespan עַלfor מַהwhat שָּׁוְאfutility בָּרָאתָcreated כָלall בְּנֵיsons אָדָםmen

Remember the briefness of my lifespan! For what futility You have created all men!

89:48

מִיwhat גֶבֶרman יִחְיֶהlive וְלֹאand never יִרְאֶהsee מָּוֶתdeath יְמַלֵּטdeliver נַפְשׁוֹhis soul מִיַּדfrom the power שְׁאוֹלSheol סֶלָהSelah

What man can live and never see death? Can he deliver his soul from the power of Sheol? Selah

89:49

אַיֵּהwhere חֲסָדֶיךָyour loving devotion הָרִאשֹׁנִיםof old אֲדֹנָיLord is נִשְׁבַּעְתָּswore לְדָוִדto David בֶּאֱמוּנָתֶךָyou faithfully

Where, O Lord, is Your loving devotion of old, which You faithfully swore to David?

89:50

זְכֹרremember אֲדֹנָיLord חֶרְפַּתreproach עֲבָדֶיךָyour servants שְׂאֵתִיbear my בְחֵיקִיin heart כָּלall רַבִּיםmany עַמִּיםpeople

Remember, O Lord, the reproach of Your servants, which I bear in my heart from so many people—

89:51

אֲשֶׁרhave חֵרְפוּtaunted אוֹיְבֶיךָyour enemies יְהוָהLORD אֲשֶׁרhave חֵרְפוּmocked עִקְּבוֹתevery step מְשִׁיחֶךָyour anointed

how Your enemies have taunted, O LORD, and have mocked every step of Your anointed one!

89:52

בָּרוּךְblessed יְהוָהLORD לְעוֹלָםforever אָמֵןAmen וְאָמֵןand amen

Blessed be the LORD forever! Amen and amen.
