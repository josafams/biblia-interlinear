Psalms 142

A cry from the cave

Open in the interlinear reader

A Maskil of David, when he was in the cave. A prayer

142:1

קוֹלִיI aloud אֶלto יְהוָהLORD אֶזְעָקcry קוֹלִיmy voice אֶלto יְהוָהLORD אֶתְחַנָּןmercy

I cry aloud to the LORD; I lift my voice to the LORD for mercy.

142:2

אֶשְׁפֹּךְpour לְפָנָיוbefore שִׂיחִיmy complaint צָרָתִיmy trouble לְפָנָיוto his before אַגִּידreveal

I pour out my complaint before Him; I reveal my trouble to Him.

142:3

בְּהִתְעַטֵּףgrows faint within עָלַיon me רוּחִיspirit וְאַתָּהyou יָדַעְתָּknow נְתִיבָתִיmy way בְּאֹרַחalong the path זוּI אֲהַלֵּךְtravel טָמְנוּhidden פַחsnare לִיfor me

Although my spirit grows faint within me, You know my way. Along the path I travel they have hidden a snare for me.

142:4

הַבֵּיטlook יָמִיןright וּרְאֵהand see וְאֵיןno לִיto me מַכִּירattends אָבַדperished מָנוֹסrefuge מִמֶּנִּיfrom me אֵיןno דּוֹרֵשׁcares לְנַפְשִׁיfor my soul

Look to my right and see; no one attends to me. There is no refuge for me; no one cares for my soul.

142:5

זָעַקְתִּיcry אֵלֶיךָto you יְהוָהLORD אָמַרְתִּיsaid אַתָּהyou מַחְסִיmy refuge חֶלְקִיmy portion בְּאֶרֶץin the land הַחַיִּיםthe living

I cry to You, O LORD: “You are my refuge, my portion in the land of the living.”

142:6

הַקְשִׁיבָהlisten אֶלto רִנָּתִיmy cry כִּיfor דַלּוֹתִיbrought מְאֹדvery הַצִּילֵנִיrescue me מֵרֹדְפַיfrom my pursuers כִּיfor אָמְצוּstrong מִמֶּנִּיfrom me

Listen to my cry, for I am brought quite low. Rescue me from my pursuers, for they are too strong for me.

142:7

הוֹצִיאָהfree מִמַּסְגֵּרfrom prison נַפְשִׁיmy soul לְהוֹדוֹתmay praise אֶת[obj] שְׁמֶךָyour name בִּיthe יַכְתִּרוּgather צַדִּיקִיםrighteous כִּיbecause תִגְמֹלgoodness עָלָיto me

Free my soul from prison, that I may praise Your name. The righteous will gather around me because of Your goodness to me.
