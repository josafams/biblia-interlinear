Psalms 80

A plea to restore the vine of Israel

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. To the tune of “The Lilies of the Covenant.” A Psalm of Asaph

80:1

רֹעֵהshepherd יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael הַאֲזִינָהhear נֹהֵגleads כַּצֹּאןlike a flock יוֹסֵףJoseph יֹשֵׁבsit הַכְּרוּבִיםenthroned between the cherubim הוֹפִיעָהshine forth

Hear us, O Shepherd of Israel, who leads Joseph like a flock; You who sit enthroned between the cherubim, shine forth

80:2

לִפְנֵיbefore אֶפְרַיִםEphraim וּבִנְיָמִןBenjamin וּמְנַשֶּׁהand Manasseh עוֹרְרָהrally אֶת[obj] גְּבוּרָתֶךָyour mighty power וּלְכָהand come לִישֻׁעָתָהto save לָּנוּto us

before Ephraim, Benjamin, and Manasseh. Rally Your mighty power and come to save us.

80:3

אֱלֹהִיםGod הֲשִׁיבֵנוּrestore us וְהָאֵרand cause פָּנֶיךָyour face וְנִוָּשֵׁעָהsaved

Restore us, O God, and cause Your face to shine upon us, that we may be saved.

80:4

יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהִיםGod צְבָאוֹתHosts עַדhow מָתַיlong עָשַׁנְתָּanger smolder בִּתְפִלַּתagainst the prayers עַמֶּךָyour people

O LORD God of Hosts, how long will Your anger smolder against the prayers of Your people?

80:5

הֶאֱכַלְתָּםfed them לֶחֶםbread דִּמְעָהtears וַתַּשְׁקֵמוֹand made them drink בִּדְמָעוֹתtears שָׁלִישׁmeasure

You fed them with the bread of tears and made them drink the full measure of their tears.

80:6

תְּשִׂימֵנוּmake us מָדוֹןcontend לִשְׁכֵנֵינוּwith our neighbors our וְאֹיְבֵינוּenemies us יִלְעֲגוּmock לָמוֹto them

You make us contend with our neighbors; our enemies mock us.

80:7

אֱלֹהִיםGod צְבָאוֹתHosts הֲשִׁיבֵנוּrestore us וְהָאֵרand cause פָּנֶיךָyour face וְנִוָּשֵׁעָהmay be saved

Restore us, O God of Hosts, and cause Your face to shine upon us, that we may be saved.

80:8

גֶּפֶןvine מִמִּצְרַיִםfrom Egypt תַּסִּיעַout the תְּגָרֵשׁdrove גּוֹיִםnations וַתִּטָּעֶהָand transplanted it

You uprooted a vine from Egypt; You drove out the nations and transplanted it.

80:9

פִּנִּיתָcleared לְפָנֶיהָto her before וַתַּשְׁרֵשׁand took root שָׁרָשֶׁיהָit root וַתְּמַלֵּאand filled אָרֶץland

You cleared the ground for it, and it took root and filled the land.

80:10

כָּסּוּcovered הָרִיםmountains צִלָּהּits shade וַעֲנָפֶיהָits branches אַרְזֵיcedars אֵלmighty

The mountains were covered by its shade, and the mighty cedars with its branches.

80:11

תְּשַׁלַּחsent קְצִירֶהָits branches עַדto יָםsea וְאֶלand to נָהָרriver יוֹנְקוֹתֶיהָits shoots

It sent out its branches to the Sea, and its shoots toward the River.

80:12

לָמָּהto why פָּרַצְתָּbroken גְדֵרֶיהָits walls וְאָרוּהָpick its fruit כָּלall עֹבְרֵיpass דָרֶךְway

Why have You broken down its walls, so that all who pass by pick its fruit?

80:13

יְכַרְסְמֶנָּהravages it חֲזִירboar מִיָּעַרfrom the forest וְזִיזand the creatures שָׂדַיfield יִרְעֶנָּהfeed upon it

The boar from the forest ravages it, and the creatures of the field feed upon it.

80:14

אֱלֹהִיםGod צְבָאוֹתHosts שׁוּבreturn נָאplease הַבֵּטlook מִשָּׁמַיִםfrom heaven וּרְאֵהand see וּפְקֹדattend גֶּפֶןvine זֹאתthis

Return, O God of Hosts, we pray! Look down from heaven and see! Attend to this vine—

80:15

וְכַנָּהroot אֲשֶׁרhas נָטְעָהplanted יְמִינֶךָyour right hand וְעַלand on בֵּןson אִמַּצְתָּהraised לָּךְfor yourself

the root Your right hand has planted, the son You have raised up for Yourself.

80:16

שְׂרֻפָהvine בָאֵשׁbeen down כְּסוּחָהcut מִגַּעֲרַתat the rebuke פָּנֶיךָyour countenance יֹאבֵדוּperish

Your vine has been cut down and burned; they perish at the rebuke of Your countenance.

80:17

תְּהִיbe יָדְךָyour hand עַלupon אִישׁman יְמִינֶךָyour right hand עַלon בֶּןson אָדָםman אִמַּצְתָּraised לָּךְfor yourself

Let Your hand be upon the man at Your right hand, on the son of man You have raised up for Yourself.

80:18

וְלֹאnot נָסוֹגturn מִמֶּךָּfrom you תְּחַיֵּנוּrevive us וּבְשִׁמְךָon your name נִקְרָאcall

Then we will not turn away from You; revive us, and we will call on Your name.

80:19

יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהִיםGod צְבָאוֹתHosts הֲשִׁיבֵנוּrestore us הָאֵרshine פָּנֶיךָyour face וְנִוָּשֵׁעָהmay be saved

Restore us, O LORD God of Hosts; cause Your face to shine upon us, that we may be saved.
