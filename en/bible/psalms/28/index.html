Psalms 28

A cry heard and thanksgiving

Open in the interlinear reader

28:1

לְדָוִדof David אֵלֶיךָto you יְהוָהLORD אֶקְרָאcall צוּרִיmy rock אַלnot תֶּחֱרַשׁdeaf מִמֶּנִּיfrom me פֶּןor תֶּחֱשֶׁהsilent מִמֶּנִּיfrom me וְנִמְשַׁלְתִּיwill be like עִםto יוֹרְדֵיdescending בוֹרpit

Of David. To You, O LORD, I call; be not deaf to me, O my Rock. For if You remain silent, I will be like those descending to the Pit.

28:2

שְׁמַעhear קוֹלcry תַּחֲנוּנַיmercy בְּשַׁוְּעִיwhen I call אֵלֶיךָto you בְּנָשְׂאִיwhen I lift up my יָדַיhands אֶלto דְּבִירsanctuary קָדְשֶׁךָyour holy

Hear my cry for mercy when I call to You for help, when I lift up my hands toward Your holy sanctuary.

28:3

אַלdo not תִּמְשְׁכֵנִיdrag me עִםwith רְשָׁעִיםwicked וְעִםand with פֹּעֲלֵיworkers אָוֶןiniquity דֹּבְרֵיspeak שָׁלוֹםpeace עִםto רֵעֵיהֶםtheir neighbors וְרָעָהwhile malice בִּלְבָבָםin their hearts

Do not drag me away with the wicked, and with the workers of iniquity, who speak peace to their neighbors while malice is in their hearts.

28:4

תֶּןrepay לָהֶםto them כְּפָעֳלָםaccording to their deeds וּכְרֹעַevil מַעַלְלֵיהֶםtheir works כְּמַעֲשֵׂהwhat יְדֵיהֶםtheir hands תֵּןrepay לָהֶםfor הָשֵׁבback גְּמוּלָםthem what they deserve לָהֶםto them

Repay them according to their deeds and for their works of evil. Repay them for what their hands have done; bring back on them what they deserve.

28:5

כִּיfor לֹאno יָבִינוּregard אֶלto פְּעֻלֹּתworks יְהוָהLORD וְאֶלand to מַעֲשֵׂהwhat יָדָיוhis hands יֶהֶרְסֵםtear them down וְלֹאand never יִבְנֵםrebuild them

Since they show no regard for the works of the LORD or what His hands have done, He will tear them down and never rebuild them.

28:6

בָּרוּךְblessed יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor שָׁמַעheard קוֹלcry תַּחֲנוּנָיmercy

Blessed be the LORD, for He has heard my cry for mercy.

28:7

יְהוָהLORD עֻזִּיmy strength וּמָגִנִּיand my shield my בּוֹin him בָטַחtrusts לִבִּיheart וְנֶעֱזָרְתִּיhelped וַיַּעֲלֹזrejoices לִבִּיmy heart וּמִשִּׁירִיwith my song אֲהוֹדֶנּוּgive thanks to him

The LORD is my strength and my shield; my heart trusts in Him, and I am helped. Therefore my heart rejoices, and I give thanks to Him with my song.

28:8

יְהוָהLORD עֹזstrength לָמוֹto them וּמָעוֹזstronghold יְשׁוּעוֹתsalvation מְשִׁיחוֹhis anointed הוּאhe

The LORD is the strength of His people, a stronghold of salvation for His anointed.

28:9

הוֹשִׁיעָהsave אֶת[obj] עַמֶּךָyour people וּבָרֵךְand bless אֶת[obj] נַחֲלָתֶךָyour inheritance וּרְעֵםshepherd them וְנַשְּׂאֵםand carry them עַדuntil הָעוֹלָםforever

Save Your people and bless Your inheritance; shepherd them and carry them forever.
