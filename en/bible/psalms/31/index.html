Psalms 31

A life committed into God's hands

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David

31:1

בְּךָin you יְהוָהLORD חָסִיתִיrefuge אַלdo not אֵבוֹשָׁהput shame לְעוֹלָםput to בְּצִדְקָתְךָby your righteousness פַלְּטֵנִיsave me

In You, O LORD, I have taken refuge; let me never be put to shame; save me by Your righteousness.

31:2

הַטֵּהincline אֵלַיto me אָזְנְךָyour ear מְהֵרָהquickly הַצִּילֵנִיmy rescue הֱיֵהbe לִיto me לְצוּרrock מָעוֹזrefuge לְבֵיתto house מְצוּדוֹתstronghold לְהוֹשִׁיעֵנִיof my deliverance

Incline Your ear to me; come quickly to my rescue. Be my rock of refuge, the stronghold of my deliverance.

31:3

כִּיfor סַלְעִיmy rock וּמְצוּדָתִיand my fortress אָתָּהyou וּלְמַעַןsake שִׁמְךָyour name תַּנְחֵנִיlead me וּתְנַהֲלֵנִיand guide me

For You are my rock and my fortress; lead me and guide me for the sake of Your name.

31:4

תּוֹצִיאֵנִיfree me מֵרֶשֶׁתfrom the net זוּyou טָמְנוּlaid לִיfor me כִּיfor אַתָּהyou מָעוּזִּיmy refuge

You free me from the net laid out for me, for You are my refuge.

31:5

בְּיָדְךָinto your hands אַפְקִידI רוּחִיmy spirit פָּדִיתָהredeemed אוֹתִיme יְהוָהLORD אֵלGod אֱמֶתtruth

Into Your hands I commit my spirit; You have redeemed me, O LORD, God of truth.

31:6

שָׂנֵאתִיhate הַשֹּׁמְרִיםthose who cling הַבְלֵיworthless שָׁוְאworthless idols וַאֲנִיI אֶלto יְהוָהLORD בָּטָחְתִּיtrust

I hate those who cling to worthless idols, but in the LORD I trust.

31:7

אָגִילָהglad וְאֶשְׂמְחָהand rejoice בְּחַסְדֶּךָin your loving devotion for you אֲשֶׁרhave רָאִיתָseen אֶת[obj] עָנְיִיmy affliction יָדַעְתָּknown בְּצָרוֹתthe anguish נַפְשִׁיmy soul

I will be glad and rejoice in Your loving devotion, for You have seen my affliction; You have known the anguish of my soul.

31:8

וְלֹאyou have not הִסְגַּרְתַּנִיdelivered me בְּיַדin hand אוֹיֵבenemy הֶעֱמַדְתָּset בַמֶּרְחָבin the open רַגְלָיmy feet

You have not delivered me to the enemy; You have set my feet in the open.

31:9

חָנֵּנִיmerciful to me יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor צַרdistress לִיto me עָשְׁשָׁהfail בְכַעַסfrom sorrow עֵינִיeyes נַפְשִׁיsoul וּבִטְנִיand body

Be merciful to me, O LORD, for I am in distress; my eyes fail from sorrow, my soul and body as well.

31:10

כִּיfor כָלוּconsumed בְיָגוֹןwith grief חַיַּיmy life וּשְׁנוֹתַיand my years בַּאֲנָחָהwith groaning כָּשַׁלdrained בַּעֲוֺנִיmy iniquity כֹחִיstrength וַעֲצָמַיand my bones עָשֵׁשׁוּwasting

For my life is consumed with grief and my years with groaning; my iniquity has drained my strength, and my bones are wasting away.

31:11

מִכָּלamong all צֹרְרַיmy enemies הָיִיתִיam חֶרְפָּהdisgrace וְלִשֲׁכֵנַיand among my neighbors מְאֹדvery וּפַחַדI am dreaded לִמְיֻדָּעָיby my friends רֹאַיsee me בַּחוּץon the street נָדְדוּflee מִמֶּנִּיfrom me

Among all my enemies I am a disgrace, and among my neighbors even more. I am dreaded by my friends— they flee when they see me on the street.

31:12

נִשְׁכַּחְתִּיforgotten כְּמֵתlike a dead מִלֵּבout of mind הָיִיתִיam כִּכְלִיvessel אֹבֵדbroken

I am forgotten like a dead man, out of mind. I am like a broken vessel.

31:13

כִּיfor שָׁמַעְתִּיhear דִּבַּתslander רַבִּיםmany מָגוֹרterror מִסָּבִיבon every side בְּהִוָּסְדָםthey conspire יַחַדtogether עָלַיagainst me לָקַחַתto take נַפְשִׁיmy life זָמָמוּplanned

For I hear the slander of many; there is terror on every side. They conspire against me and plot to take my life.

31:14

וַאֲנִיbut I עָלֶיךָin you בָטַחְתִּיtrust יְהוָהLORD אָמַרְתִּיsay אֱלֹהַיmy God אָתָּהyou are

But I trust in You, O LORD; I say, “You are my God.”

31:15

בְּיָדְךָin your hands עִתֹּתָיmy times הַצִּילֵנִיdeliver me מִיַּדfrom hand אוֹיְבַיmy enemies וּמֵרֹדְפָיpursue me

My times are in Your hands; deliver me from my enemies and from those who pursue me.

31:16

הָאִירָהshine פָנֶיךָyour face עַלon עַבְדֶּךָyour servant הוֹשִׁיעֵנִיsave me בְחַסְדֶּךָby your loving devotion

Make Your face shine on Your servant; save me by Your loving devotion.

31:17

יְהוָהLORD אַלnot אֵבוֹשָׁהme כִּיfor קְרָאתִיךָcalled on you יֵבֹשׁוּshame רְשָׁעִיםwicked יִדְּמוּsilent לִשְׁאוֹלin Sheol

O LORD, let me not be ashamed, for I have called on You. Let the wicked be put to shame; let them lie silent in Sheol.

31:18

תֵּאָלַמְנָהsilenced שִׂפְתֵיlips שָׁקֶרlying הַדֹּבְרוֹתthat speak עַלon צַדִּיקrighteous עָתָקarrogance בְּגַאֲוָהpride וָבוּזand contempt

May lying lips be silenced— lips that speak with arrogance against the righteous, full of pride and contempt.

31:19

מָהhow רַבgreat טוּבְךָyour goodness you אֲשֶׁרwhich have צָפַנְתָּlaid up לִּירֵאֶיךָfear you פָּעַלְתָּbestowed לַחֹסִיםon those who take refuge בָּךְin you נֶגֶדbefore בְּנֵיsons אָדָםmen

How great is Your goodness which You have laid up for those who fear You, which You have bestowed before the sons of men on those who take refuge in You!

31:20

תַּסְתִּירֵםhide them בְּסֵתֶרin the secret פָּנֶיךָyour presence מֵרֻכְסֵיfrom the schemes אִישׁmen תִּצְפְּנֵםconceal them בְּסֻכָּהin your shelter מֵרִיבfrom accusing לְשֹׁנוֹתtongues

You hide them in the secret place of Your presence from the schemes of men. You conceal them in Your shelter from accusing tongues.

31:21

בָּרוּךְblessed יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor הִפְלִיאhe חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion לִיto me בְּעִירcity מָצוֹרunder siege

Blessed be the LORD, for He has shown me His loving devotion in a city under siege.

31:22

וַאֲנִיI אָמַרְתִּיsaid בְחָפְזִיin my alarm נִגְרַזְתִּיalarm מִנֶּגֶדfrom distance עֵינֶיךָyour sight you אָכֵןbut שָׁמַעְתָּheard קוֹלplea תַּחֲנוּנַיmercy I בְּשַׁוְּעִיhelp אֵלֶיךָto you

In my alarm I said, “I am cut off from Your sight!” But You heard my plea for mercy when I called to You for help.

31:23

אֶהֱבוּlove אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD כָּלall חֲסִידָיוhis saints אֱמוּנִיםfaithful נֹצֵרpreserves יְהוָהLORD וּמְשַׁלֵּםbut fully repays עַלon יֶתֶרstring עֹשֵׂהMaker גַאֲוָהarrogant

Love the LORD, all His saints. The LORD preserves the faithful, but fully repays the arrogant.

31:24

חִזְקוּstrong וְיַאֲמֵץand courageous לְבַבְכֶםyour heart כָּלall הַמְיַחֲלִיםwho hope לַיהוָהin the LORD

Be strong and courageous, all you who hope in the LORD.
