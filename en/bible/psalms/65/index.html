Psalms 65

God forgives and fills the earth with harvests

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. A song

65:1

לְךָto you דֻמִיָּהawaits תְהִלָּהpraise אֱלֹהִיםGod בְּצִיּוֹןin Zion וּלְךָto you יְשֻׁלַּםfulfilled נֶדֶרvows

Praise awaits You, O God, in Zion; to You our vows will be fulfilled.

65:2

שֹׁמֵעַlisten תְּפִלָּהprayer עָדֶיךָto כָּלall בָּשָׂרpeople יָבֹאוּcome

O You who listen to prayer, all people will come to You.

65:3

דִּבְרֵיwords עֲוֺנֹתiniquities גָּבְרוּprevail מֶנִּיfrom me פְּשָׁעֵינוּour transgressions אַתָּהyou תְכַפְּרֵםatone

When iniquities prevail against me, You atone for our transgressions.

65:4

אַשְׁרֵיblessed תִּבְחַרchoose וּתְקָרֵבand bring near יִשְׁכֹּןdwell חֲצֵרֶיךָyour courts נִשְׂבְּעָהfilled בְּטוּבwith the goodness בֵּיתֶךָyour house קְדֹשׁholiness הֵיכָלֶךָyour temple

Blessed is the one You choose and bring near to dwell in Your courts! We are filled with the goodness of Your house, the holiness of Your temple.

65:5

נוֹרָאוֹתawesome deeds בְּצֶדֶקrighteousness תַּעֲנֵנוּanswer us אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׁעֵנוּour salvation מִבְטָחhope כָּלall קַצְוֵיends אֶרֶץearth וְיָםfarthest רְחֹקִיםseas

With awesome deeds of righteousness You answer us, O God of our salvation, the hope of all the ends of the earth and of the farthest seas.

65:6

מֵכִיןformed הָרִיםmountains בְּכֹחוֹby your power having girded נֶאְזָרgirded yourself בִּגְבוּרָהwith might

You formed the mountains by Your power, having girded Yourself with might.

65:7

מַשְׁבִּיחַstilled שְׁאוֹןroaring יַמִּיםseas שְׁאוֹןpounding גַּלֵּיהֶםtheir waves וַהֲמוֹןand the tumult לְאֻמִּיםnations

You stilled the roaring of the seas, the pounding of their waves, and the tumult of the nations.

65:8

וַיִּירְאוּfear יֹשְׁבֵיlive קְצָוֺתfar מֵאוֹתֹתֶיךָaway your wonders מוֹצָאֵיmake בֹקֶרdawn וָעֶרֶבand sunset תַּרְנִיןshout for joy

Those who live far away fear Your wonders; You make the dawn and sunset shout for joy.

65:9

פָּקַדְתָּattend הָאָרֶץthe earth וַתְּשֹׁקְקֶהָand water it רַבַּתabundance תַּעְשְׁרֶנָּהenrich it פֶּלֶגstreams אֱלֹהִיםGod מָלֵאfull מָיִםwater תָּכִיןprepare דְּגָנָםgrain כִּיfor כֵןthis תְּכִינֶהָbuild her

You attend to the earth and water it; with abundance You enrich it. The streams of God are full of water, for You prepare our grain by providing for the earth.

65:10

תְּלָמֶיהָsoak its furrows רַוֵּהlevel נַחֵתridges גְּדוּדֶיהָsoften it בִּרְבִיבִיםwith showers תְּמֹגְגֶנָּהmelt her צִמְחָהּand its growth תְּבָרֵךְbless

You soak its furrows and level its ridges; You soften it with showers and bless its growth.

65:11

עִטַּרְתָּcrown שְׁנַתyear טוֹבָתֶךָyour bounty וּמַעְגָּלֶיךָand your paths יִרְעֲפוּןoverflow דָּשֶׁןplenty

You crown the year with Your bounty, and Your paths overflow with plenty.

65:12

יִרְעֲפוּoverflow נְאוֹתpastures מִדְבָּרwilderness וְגִילwith joy גְּבָעוֹתhills תַּחְגֹּרְנָהrobed

The pastures of the wilderness overflow; the hills are robed with joy.

65:13

לָבְשׁוּclothed כָרִיםpastures הַצֹּאןflocks וַעֲמָקִיםand the valleys יַעַטְפוּdecked בָרgrain יִתְרוֹעֲעוּshout in triumph אַףindeed יָשִׁירוּsing

The pastures are clothed with flocks, and the valleys are decked with grain. They shout in triumph; indeed, they sing.
