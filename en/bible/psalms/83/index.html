Psalms 83

Against the alliance of enemy nations

Open in the interlinear reader

A song. A Psalm of Asaph

83:1

אֱלֹהִיםGod אַלnot דֳּמִיsilent לָךְto you אַלnot תֶּחֱרַשׁspeechless וְאַלnot תִּשְׁקֹטstill אֵלGod

O God, be not silent; be not speechless; be not still, O God.

83:2

כִּיfor הִנֵּהsee אוֹיְבֶיךָyour enemies יֶהֱמָיוּןrage וּמְשַׂנְאֶיךָhow your foes נָשְׂאוּreared רֹאשׁheads

See how Your enemies rage, how Your foes have reared their heads.

83:3

עַלagainst עַמְּךָyour people יַעֲרִימוּcunning סוֹדscheme וְיִתְיָעֲצוּand conspire עַלagainst צְפוּנֶיךָthose you cherish

With cunning they scheme against Your people and conspire against those You cherish,

83:4

אָמְרוּsaying לְכוּcome וְנַכְחִידֵםlet us erase them מִגּוֹיa nation וְלֹאno יִזָּכֵרremembered שֵׁםname יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael עוֹדmore

saying, “Come, let us erase them as a nation; may the name of Israel be remembered no more.”

83:5

כִּיfor נוֹעֲצוּcounselor לֵבmind יַחְדָּוtogether עָלֶיךָagainst you בְּרִיתform יִכְרֹתוּalliance

For with one mind they plot together, they form an alliance against You—

83:6

אָהֳלֵיtents אֱדוֹםEdom וְיִשְׁמְעֵאלִיםand the Ishmaelites מוֹאָבMoab וְהַגְרִיםand the Hagrites

the tents of Edom and the Ishmaelites, of Moab and the Hagrites,

83:7

גְּבָלGebal וְעַמּוֹןAmmon וַעֲמָלֵקand Amalek פְּלֶשֶׁתPhilistia עִםwith יֹשְׁבֵיpeople צוֹרTyre

of Gebal, Ammon, and Amalek, of Philistia with the people of Tyre.

83:8

גַּםeven אַשּׁוּרAssyria נִלְוָהjoined עִמָּםwith them הָיוּlending זְרוֹעַstrength לִבְנֵיto the sons לוֹטLot סֶלָהSelah

Even Assyria has joined them, lending strength to the sons of Lot. Selah

83:9

עֲשֵׂהdo לָהֶםto them כְּמִדְיָןMidian כְּסִיסְרָאas to Sisera כְיָבִיןJabin בְּנַחַלat the river קִישׁוֹןKishon

Do to them as You did to Midian, as to Sisera and Jabin at the River Kishon,

83:10

נִשְׁמְדוּperished בְעֵיןin Endor דֹּארEndor הָיוּbecame דֹּמֶןdung לָאֲדָמָהon the ground

who perished at Endor and became like dung on the ground.

83:11

שִׁיתֵמוֹmake their נְדִיבֵמוֹnobles כְּעֹרֵבlike Oreb וְכִזְאֵבand Zeeb וּכְזֶבַחZebah וּכְצַלְמֻנָּעlike and Zalmunna כָּלall נְסִיכֵמוֹtheir princes

Make their nobles like Oreb and Zeeb, and all their princes like Zebah and Zalmunna,

83:12

אֲשֶׁרwho אָמְרוּsaid נִירֲשָׁהpossess לָּנוּfor ourselves אֵת[obj] נְאוֹתpastures אֱלֹהִיםGod

who said, “Let us possess for ourselves the pastures of God.”

83:13

אֱלֹהַיmy God שִׁיתֵמוֹmake them כַגַּלְגַּלlike tumbleweed כְּקַשׁlike chaff לִפְנֵיbefore רוּחַwind

Make them like tumbleweed, O my God, like chaff before the wind.

83:14

כְּאֵשׁas fire תִּבְעַרconsumes יָעַרforest וּכְלֶהָבָהas a flame תְּלַהֵטsets הָרִיםmountains

As fire consumes a forest, as a flame sets the mountains ablaze,

83:15

כֵּןso תִּרְדְּפֵםpursue them בְּסַעֲרֶךָwith your tempest וּבְסוּפָתְךָwith your storm תְבַהֲלֵםterrify them

so pursue them with Your tempest, and terrify them with Your storm.

83:16

מַלֵּאcover פְנֵיהֶםtheir faces קָלוֹןshame וִיבַקְשׁוּthey may seek שִׁמְךָyour name O יְהוָהLORD

Cover their faces with shame, that they may seek Your name, O LORD.

83:17

יֵבֹשׁוּashamed וְיִבָּהֲלוּand terrified עֲדֵיever עַדuntil וְיַחְפְּרוּdisgrace וְיֹאבֵדוּthey perish

May they be ever ashamed and terrified; may they perish in disgrace.

83:18

וְיֵדְעוּmay they know כִּיthat אַתָּהyou שִׁמְךָname is יְהוָהLORD לְבַדֶּךָalone עֶלְיוֹןMost high עַלover כָּלall הָאָרֶץthe earth

May they know that You alone, whose name is the LORD, are Most High over all the earth.
