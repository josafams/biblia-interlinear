Psalms 93

The LORD reigns above the waters

Open in the interlinear reader

93:1

יְהוָהLORD מָלָךְreigns גֵּאוּתrobed majesty לָבֵשׁclothed לָבֵשׁclothed יְהוָהLORD עֹזstrength הִתְאַזָּרarmed אַףindeed תִּכּוֹןestablished תֵּבֵלworld בַּלcannot תִּמּוֹטmoved

The LORD reigns! He is robed in majesty; the LORD has clothed and armed Himself with strength. The world indeed is firmly established; it cannot be moved.

93:2

נָכוֹןestablished כִּסְאֲךָyour throne מֵאָזfrom time מֵעוֹלָםfrom all eternity אָתָּהyou

Your throne was established long ago; You are from all eternity.

93:3

נָשְׂאוּrisen נְהָרוֹתfloodwaters יְהוָהLORD נָשְׂאוּraised נְהָרוֹתrivers קוֹלָםtheir voice יִשְׂאוּlift נְהָרוֹתseas up pounding דָּכְיָםtheir waves

The floodwaters have risen, O LORD; the rivers have raised their voice; the seas lift up their pounding waves.

93:4

מִקֹּלוֹתabove the roar מַיִםwaters רַבִּיםmany אַדִּירִיםmighty מִשְׁבְּרֵיbreakers יָםsea אַדִּירmajestic בַּמָּרוֹםthe on high יְהוָהLORD

Above the roar of many waters— the mighty breakers of the sea— the LORD on high is majestic.

93:5

עֵדֹתֶיךָyour testimonies נֶאֶמְנוּconfirmed מְאֹדvery לְבֵיתְךָyour house O נַאֲוָהadorns קֹדֶשׁholiness יְהוָהLORD לְאֹרֶךְfor all יָמִיםdays

Your testimonies are fully confirmed; holiness adorns Your house, O LORD, for all the days to come.
