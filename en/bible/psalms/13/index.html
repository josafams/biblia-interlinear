Psalms 13

From long complaint to trust

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David

13:1

עַדhow אָנָהlong יְהוָהLORD תִּשְׁכָּחֵנִיforget me נֶצַחforever עַדhow אָנָהlong will you תַּסְתִּירhide אֶת[obj] פָּנֶיךָyour face מִמֶּנִּיfrom me

How long, O LORD? Will You forget me forever? How long will You hide Your face from me?

13:2

עַדhow אָנָהlong אָשִׁיתbring עֵצוֹתwrestle בְּנַפְשִׁיin my soul יָגוֹןsorrow בִּלְבָבִיin my heart יוֹמָםday עַדhow אָנָהlong יָרוּםdominate אֹיְבִיmy enemy עָלָיon me

How long must I wrestle in my soul, with sorrow in my heart each day? How long will my enemy dominate me?

13:3

הַבִּיטָהconsider עֲנֵנִיme and respond יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהָיmy God הָאִירָהlight עֵינַיmy eyes פֶּןlest אִישַׁןsleep הַמָּוֶתin death

Consider me and respond, O LORD my God. Give light to my eyes, lest I sleep in death,

13:4

פֶּןlest יֹאמַרsay אֹיְבִיmy enemy יְכָלְתִּיוovercome him צָרַיmy foes יָגִילוּrejoice כִּיwhen אֶמּוֹטfall

lest my enemy say, “I have overcome him,” and my foes rejoice when I fall.

13:5

וַאֲנִיbut I בְּחַסְדְּךָin your loving devotion בָטַחְתִּיtrusted יָגֵלrejoice לִבִּיmy heart בִּישׁוּעָתֶךָin your salvation

But I have trusted in Your loving devotion; my heart will rejoice in Your salvation.

13:6

אָשִׁירָהsing לַיהוָהto the LORD כִּיfor גָמַלgood עָלָיto me

I will sing to the LORD, for He has been good to me.
