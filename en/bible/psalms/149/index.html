Psalms 149

The praise and victory of the faithful

Open in the interlinear reader

149:1

הַלְלוּHallelujah יָהּHallelujah שִׁירוּsing לַיהוָהto the LORD שִׁירa song חָדָשׁnew תְּהִלָּתוֹhis praise בִּקְהַלin the assembly חֲסִידִיםgodly

Hallelujah! Sing to the LORD a new song— His praise in the assembly of the godly.

149:2

יִשְׂמַחlet rejoice יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael בְּעֹשָׂיוin their Maker בְּנֵיchildren צִיּוֹןZion יָגִילוּrejoice בְמַלְכָּםin their king

Let Israel rejoice in their Maker; let the children of Zion rejoice in their King.

149:3

יְהַלְלוּpraise שְׁמוֹhis name בְמָחוֹלwith dancing בְּתֹףwith tambourine וְכִנּוֹרand harp יְזַמְּרוּmake music לוֹto him

Let them praise His name with dancing, and make music to Him with tambourine and harp.

149:4

כִּיfor רוֹצֶהpleasure יְהוָהLORD בְּעַמּוֹhis people he יְפָאֵרadorns עֲנָוִיםafflicted בִּישׁוּעָהwith salvation

For the LORD takes pleasure in His people; He adorns the afflicted with salvation.

149:5

יַעְלְזוּexult חֲסִידִיםsaints בְּכָבוֹדin glory יְרַנְּנוּjoy עַלupon מִשְׁכְּבוֹתָםtheir beds

Let the saints exult in glory; let them shout for joy upon their beds.

149:6

רוֹמְמוֹתpraises אֵלGod בִּגְרוֹנָםin their mouths וְחֶרֶבsword פִּיפִיּוֹתdouble edged בְּיָדָםin their hands

May the high praises of God be in their mouths, and a double-edged sword in their hands,

149:7

לַעֲשׂוֹתto inflict נְקָמָהvengeance בַּגּוֹיִםon the nations תּוֹכֵחֹתpunishment בַּלnot אֻמִּיםpeoples

to inflict vengeance on the nations and punishment on the peoples,

149:8

לֶאְסֹרto bind מַלְכֵיהֶםtheir kings בְּזִקִּיםwith chains וְנִכְבְּדֵיהֶםand their nobles בְּכַבְלֵיwith shackles בַרְזֶלiron

to bind their kings with chains and their nobles with shackles of iron,

149:9

לַעֲשׂוֹתto execute בָּהֶםagainst them מִשְׁפָּטjudgment כָּתוּבwritten הָדָרhonor הוּאis לְכָלfor all חֲסִידָיוhis saints הַלְלוּHallelujah יָהּHallelujah

to execute the judgment written against them. This honor is for all His saints. Hallelujah!
