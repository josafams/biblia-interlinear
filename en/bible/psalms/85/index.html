Psalms 85

Restoration: righteousness and peace meet

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. A Psalm of the sons of Korah

85:1

רָצִיתָfavor יְהוָהLORD אַרְצֶךָland שַׁבְתָּrestored שְׁבִיתfrom captivity יַעֲקֹבJacob

You showed favor to Your land, O LORD; You restored Jacob from captivity.

85:2

נָשָׂאתָforgave עֲוֺןiniquity עַמֶּךָpeople you כִּסִּיתָcovered כָלall חַטָּאתָםtheir sin סֶלָהSelah

You forgave the iniquity of Your people; You covered all their sin. Selah

85:3

אָסַפְתָּwithheld כָלall עֶבְרָתֶךָfury you הֱשִׁיבוֹתָturned מֵחֲרוֹןfrom burning אַפֶּךָyour anger

You withheld all Your fury; You turned from Your burning anger.

85:4

שׁוּבֵנוּrestore us אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׁעֵנוּour salvation וְהָפֵרand put כַּעַסְךָaway your displeasure עִמָּנוּwith us

Restore us, O God of our salvation, and put away Your displeasure toward us.

85:5

הַלְעוֹלָםforever תֶּאֱנַףangry בָּנוּwith us תִּמְשֹׁךְdraw אַפְּךָyour anger לְדֹרto all generations וָדֹרand generations

Will You be angry with us forever? Will You draw out Your anger to all generations?

85:6

הֲלֹאwill not אַתָּהyou תָּשׁוּבreturn תְּחַיֵּנוּrevive us וְעַמְּךָagain that your people יִשְׂמְחוּrejoice בָךְin you

Will You not revive us again, that Your people may rejoice in You?

85:7

הַרְאֵנוּshow us יְהוָהLORD חַסְדֶּךָyour loving devotion וְיֶשְׁעֲךָyour salvation תִּתֶּןgrant לָנוּto us

Show us Your loving devotion, O LORD, and grant us Your salvation.

85:8

אֶשְׁמְעָהlisten מַהwhat יְדַבֵּרsay הָאֵלGod יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor יְדַבֵּרspeak שָׁלוֹםpeace אֶלto עַמּוֹhis people וְאֶלand to חֲסִידָיוhis saints וְאַלhe will not יָשׁוּבוּreturn לְכִסְלָהto folly

I will listen to what God the LORD will say; for He will surely speak peace to His people and His saints; He will not let them return to folly.

85:9

אַךְsurely קָרוֹבnear לִירֵאָיוfear him יִשְׁעוֹsalvation לִשְׁכֹּןmay dwell כָּבוֹדglory בְּאַרְצֵנוּin our land

Surely His salvation is near to those who fear Him, that His glory may dwell in our land.

85:10

חֶסֶדloving devotion וֶאֱמֶתand faithfulness נִפְגָּשׁוּjoined צֶדֶקrighteousness וְשָׁלוֹםand peace נָשָׁקוּkissed

Loving devotion and faithfulness have joined together; righteousness and peace have kissed.

85:11

אֱמֶתfaithfulness מֵאֶרֶץfrom the earth תִּצְמָחsprouts וְצֶדֶקand righteousness מִשָּׁמַיִםfrom heaven נִשְׁקָףlooks down

Faithfulness sprouts from the earth, and righteousness looks down from heaven.

85:12

גַּםindeed יְהוָהLORD יִתֵּןprovide הַטּוֹבis good וְאַרְצֵנוּand our land תִּתֵּןwill יְבוּלָהּyield its increase

The LORD will indeed provide what is good, and our land will yield its increase.

85:13

צֶדֶקrighteousness לְפָנָיוwill before him יְהַלֵּךְgo וְיָשֵׂםto prepare לְדֶרֶךְto the way פְּעָמָיוhis steps

Righteousness will go before Him to prepare the way for His steps.
