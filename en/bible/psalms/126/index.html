Psalms 126

The joy of Zion's restoration

Open in the interlinear reader

126:1

שִׁירsong הַמַּעֲלוֹתof ascents בְּשׁוּבrestored יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] שִׁיבַתcaptives צִיּוֹןZion הָיִינוּwere כְּחֹלְמִיםlike dreamers

A song of ascents. When the LORD restored the captives of Zion, we were like dreamers.

126:2

אָזthen יִמָּלֵאfilled שְׂחוֹקlaughter פִּינוּour mouths וּלְשׁוֹנֵנוּwith our tongues רִנָּהshouts of joy אָזthen יֹאמְרוּsaid בַגּוֹיִםamong the nations הִגְדִּילgreat יְהוָהLORD לַעֲשׂוֹתhas done עִםwith אֵלֶּהthese

Then our mouths were filled with laughter, our tongues with shouts of joy. Then it was said among the nations, “The LORD has done great things for them.”

126:3

הִגְדִּילgreat יְהוָהLORD לַעֲשׂוֹתdone עִמָּנוּwith הָיִינוּare שְׂמֵחִיםjoy

The LORD has done great things for us; we are filled with joy.

126:4

שׁוּבָהrestore יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] שְׁבִיתֵנוּour captives כַּאֲפִיקִיםlike streams בַּנֶּגֶבin the Negev

Restore our captives, O LORD, like streams in the Negev.

126:5

הַזֹּרְעִיםthose who sow בְּדִמְעָהin tears בְּרִנָּהwith shouts יִקְצֹרוּreap

Those who sow in tears will reap with shouts of joy.

126:6

הָלוֹךְgoes יֵלֵךְout וּבָכֹהweeping נֹשֵׂאbearing מֶשֶׁךְtrail הַזָּרַעof seed בֹּאsurely return יָבוֹאcome בְרִנָּהwith shouts of joy נֹשֵׂאcarrying אֲלֻמֹּתָיוsheaves of grain

He who goes out weeping, bearing a trail of seed, will surely return with shouts of joy, carrying sheaves of grain.
