Psalms 54

Help in the face of the Ziphites' betrayal

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. With stringed instruments. A Maskil of David. When the Ziphites went to Saul and said, “Is David not hiding among us?”

54:1

אֱלֹהִיםGod בְּשִׁמְךָby your name הוֹשִׁיעֵנִיsave me וּבִגְבוּרָתְךָby your might תְדִינֵנִיvindicate me

Save me, O God, by Your name, and vindicate me by Your might!

54:2

אֱלֹהִיםGod שְׁמַעhear תְּפִלָּתִיmy prayer הַאֲזִינָהlisten לְאִמְרֵיto the words פִיmy mouth

Hear my prayer, O God; listen to the words of my mouth.

54:3

כִּיfor זָרִיםstrangers קָמוּrise עָלַיagainst me וְעָרִיצִיםand ruthless בִּקְשׁוּseek נַפְשִׁיmy life לֹאno שָׂמוּregard אֱלֹהִיםGod לְנֶגְדָּםto before them סֶלָהSelah

For strangers rise up against me, and ruthless men seek my life— men with no regard for God. Selah

54:4

הִנֵּהsurely אֱלֹהִיםGod עֹזֵרhelper לִיto me אֲדֹנָיLord is בְּסֹמְכֵיthe sustainer נַפְשִׁיmy soul

Surely God is my helper; the Lord is the sustainer of my soul.

54:5

יָשִׁיבwill הָרַעevil לְשֹׁרְרָיreward my enemies בַּאֲמִתְּךָyour faithfulness הַצְמִיתֵםdestroy them

He will reward my enemies with evil. In Your faithfulness, destroy them.

54:6

בִּנְדָבָהfreely אֶזְבְּחָהsacrifice לָּךְto you אוֹדֶהpraise שִּׁמְךָyour name יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor טוֹבgood

Freely I will sacrifice to You; I will praise Your name, O LORD, for it is good.

54:7

כִּיfor מִכָּלfrom every צָרָהtrouble הִצִּילָנִיdelivered me וּבְאֹיְבַיmy foes רָאֲתָהstared עֵינִיmy eyes

For He has delivered me from every trouble, and my eyes have stared down my foes.
