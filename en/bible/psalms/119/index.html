Psalms 119

An acrostic meditation on the Law of God

Open in the interlinear reader

119:1

אַשְׁרֵיblessed תְמִימֵיblameless דָרֶךְway הַהֹלְכִיםwho walk בְּתוֹרַתin the law יְהוָהLORD

Blessed are those whose way is blameless, who walk in the Law of the LORD.

119:2

אַשְׁרֵיblessed נֹצְרֵיkeep עֵדֹתָיוtestimonies and בְּכָלwith all לֵבheart יִדְרְשׁוּהוּseek him

Blessed are those who keep His testimonies and seek Him with all their heart.

119:3

אַףhow much לֹאno פָעֲלוּdo עַוְלָהiniquity בִּדְרָכָיוin his ways הָלָכוּwalk

They do no iniquity; they walk in His ways.

119:4

אַתָּהyou צִוִּיתָהordained פִקֻּדֶיךָyour precepts לִשְׁמֹרshould keep מְאֹדvery

You have ordained Your precepts, that we should keep them diligently.

119:5

אַחֲלַיoh יִכֹּנוּmy דְרָכָיmy ways לִשְׁמֹרto keeping חֻקֶּיךָyour statutes

Oh, that my ways were committed to keeping Your statutes!

119:6

אָזthen לֹאnot אֵבוֹשׁashamed בְּהַבִּיטִיwhen I consider אֶלto כָּלall מִצְוֺתֶיךָyour commandments

Then I would not be ashamed when I consider all Your commandments.

119:7

אוֹדְךָpraise you בְּיֹשֶׁרwith an upright לֵבָבheart בְּלָמְדִיwhen I learn מִשְׁפְּטֵיjudgments צִדְקֶךָyour righteous

I will praise You with an upright heart when I learn Your righteous judgments.

119:8

אֶת[obj] חֻקֶּיךָyour statutes אֶשְׁמֹרkeep אַלdo not תַּעַזְבֵנִיforsake me עַדuntil מְאֹדvery

I will keep Your statutes; do not utterly forsake me.

119:9

בַּמֶּהhow יְזַכֶּהcan נַּעַרyoung אֶת[obj] אָרְחוֹhis way לִשְׁמֹרby guarding כִּדְבָרֶךָaccording to your word

How can a young man keep his way pure? By guarding it according to Your word.

119:10

בְּכָלwith all לִבִּיmy heart I דְרַשְׁתִּיךָsought you אַלdo not תַּשְׁגֵּנִיme stray מִמִּצְוֺתֶיךָfrom your commandments

With all my heart I have sought You; do not let me stray from Your commandments.

119:11

בְּלִבִּיin my heart צָפַנְתִּיhidden אִמְרָתֶךָyour word לְמַעַןso לֹאnot אֶחֱטָאsin לָךְto you

I have hidden Your word in my heart that I might not sin against You.

119:12

בָּרוּךְblessed אַתָּהyou יְהוָהLORD לַמְּדֵנִיteach me חֻקֶּיךָyour statutes

Blessed are You, O LORD; teach me Your statutes.

119:13

בִּשְׂפָתַיwith my lips סִפַּרְתִּיproclaim כֹּלall מִשְׁפְּטֵיjudgments פִיךָyour mouth

With my lips I proclaim all the judgments of Your mouth.

119:14

בְּדֶרֶךְin the way עֵדְוֺתֶיךָyour testimonies שַׂשְׂתִּיrejoice כְּעַלas in כָּלall הוֹןriches

I rejoice in the way of Your testimonies as much as in all riches.

119:15

בְּפִקֻּדֶיךָon your precepts אָשִׂיחָהmeditate וְאַבִּיטָהand regard אֹרְחֹתֶיךָyour ways

I will meditate on Your precepts and regard Your ways.

119:16

בְּחֻקֹּתֶיךָin your statutes אֶשְׁתַּעֲשָׁעdelight לֹאnot אֶשְׁכַּחforget דְּבָרֶךָyour word

I will delight in Your statutes; I will not forget Your word.

119:17

גְּמֹלbountifully עַלon עַבְדְּךָyour servant אֶחְיֶהlive וְאֶשְׁמְרָהand keep דְבָרֶךָyour word

Deal bountifully with Your servant, that I may live and keep Your word.

119:18

גַּלopen עֵינַיmy eyes that I וְאַבִּיטָהmay see נִפְלָאוֹתwondrous מִתּוֹרָתֶךָfrom your law

Open my eyes that I may see wondrous things from Your law.

119:19

גֵּרstranger אָנֹכִיam בָאָרֶץon the earth אַלdo not תַּסְתֵּרhide מִמֶּנִּיfrom me מִצְוֺתֶיךָyour commandments

I am a stranger on the earth; do not hide Your commandments from me.

119:20

גָּרְסָהground נַפְשִׁיmy soul לְתַאֲבָהconsumed with longing for אֶלto מִשְׁפָּטֶיךָyour judgments בְכָלat all עֵתtimes

My soul is consumed with longing for Your judgments at all times.

119:21

גָּעַרְתָּrebuke זֵדִיםarrogant אֲרוּרִיםcursed הַשֹּׁגִיםwho stray מִמִּצְוֺתֶיךָfrom your commandments

You rebuke the arrogant— the cursed who stray from Your commandments.

119:22

גַּלremove מֵעָלַיfrom the me חֶרְפָּהscorn וָבוּזand contempt כִּיfor עֵדֹתֶיךָyour testimonies נָצָרְתִּיobey

Remove my scorn and contempt, for I have kept Your testimonies.

119:23

גַּםeven יָשְׁבוּsit שָׂרִיםrulers בִּיin me נִדְבָּרוּslander עַבְדְּךָyour servant יָשִׂיחַmeditates בְּחֻקֶּיךָon your statutes

Though rulers sit and slander me, Your servant meditates on Your statutes.

119:24

גַּםindeed עֵדֹתֶיךָyour testimonies שַׁעֲשֻׁעָיmy delight אַנְשֵׁיthey עֲצָתִיmy counselors

Your testimonies are indeed my delight; they are my counselors.

119:25

דָּבְקָהcleaves לֶעָפָרto the dust נַפְשִׁיmy soul חַיֵּנִיrevive me כִּדְבָרֶךָaccording to your word

My soul cleaves to the dust; revive me according to Your word.

119:26

דְּרָכַיmy ways סִפַּרְתִּיrecounted וַתַּעֲנֵנִיand you answered me לַמְּדֵנִיteach me חֻקֶּיךָyour statutes

I recounted my ways, and You answered me; teach me Your statutes.

119:27

דֶּרֶךְway פִּקּוּדֶיךָyour precepts הֲבִינֵנִיclear to me וְאָשִׂיחָהmeditate בְּנִפְלְאוֹתֶיךָon your wonders

Make clear to me the way of Your precepts; then I will meditate on Your wonders.

119:28

דָּלְפָהmelts נַפְשִׁיmy soul מִתּוּגָהwith sorrow קַיְּמֵנִיstrengthen me כִּדְבָרֶךָaccording to your word

My soul melts with sorrow; strengthen me according to Your word.

119:29

דֶּרֶךְpath שֶׁקֶרdeceit הָסֵרremove מִמֶּנִּיme from וְתוֹרָתְךָyour law חָנֵּנִיgraciously grant me

Remove me from the path of deceit and graciously grant me Your law.

119:30

דֶּרֶךְway אֱמוּנָהtruth בָחָרְתִּיchosen מִשְׁפָּטֶיךָyour ordinances שִׁוִּיתִיset

I have chosen the way of truth; I have set Your ordinances before me.

119:31

דָּבַקְתִּיcling בְעֵדְוֺתֶיךָto your testimonies יְהוָהLORD אַלnot תְּבִישֵׁנִיshame

I cling to Your testimonies, O LORD; let me not be put to shame.

119:32

דֶּרֶךְpath מִצְוֺתֶיךָyour commandments אָרוּץrun כִּיfor תַרְחִיבenlarge לִבִּיmy heart

I run in the path of Your commandments, for You will enlarge my heart.

119:33

הוֹרֵנִיteach me יְהוָהLORD דֶּרֶךְway חֻקֶּיךָyour statutes וְאֶצְּרֶנָּהkeep them עֵקֶבend

Teach me, O LORD, the way of Your statutes, and I will keep them to the end.

119:34

הֲבִינֵנִיgive me understanding וְאֶצְּרָהmay obey תוֹרָתֶךָyour law וְאֶשְׁמְרֶנָּהand follow it בְכָלwith all לֵבheart

Give me understanding that I may obey Your law, and follow it with all my heart.

119:35

הַדְרִיכֵנִיdirect me בִּנְתִיבin the path מִצְוֺתֶיךָyour commandments כִּיfor בוֹin him חָפָצְתִּיdelight

Direct me in the path of Your commandments, for there I find delight.

119:36

הַטturn לִבִּיmy heart אֶלto עֵדְוֺתֶיךָyour testimonies וְאַלand not אֶלto בָּצַעcovetous gain

Turn my heart to Your testimonies and not to covetous gain.

119:37

הַעֲבֵרturn עֵינַיmy eyes מֵרְאוֹתfrom see שָׁוְאworthless בִּדְרָכֶךָin your ways חַיֵּנִיrevive me

Turn my eyes away from worthless things; revive me with Your word.

119:38

הָקֵםestablish לְעַבְדְּךָyour to your servant אִמְרָתֶךָword אֲשֶׁרthat לְיִרְאָתֶךָto produce reverence for you

Establish Your word to Your servant, to produce reverence for You.

119:39

הַעֲבֵרturn חֶרְפָּתִיdisgrace I אֲשֶׁרthat יָגֹרְתִּיdread כִּיfor מִשְׁפָּטֶיךָyour judgments טוֹבִיםgood

Turn away the disgrace I dread, for Your judgments are good.

119:40

הִנֵּהbehold תָּאַבְתִּיlong לְפִקֻּדֶיךָfor your precepts בְּצִדְקָתְךָin your righteousness חַיֵּנִיrevive me

How I long for Your precepts! Revive me in Your righteousness.

119:41

וִיבֹאֻנִיcome to me חֲסָדֶךָyour loving devotion יְהוָהLORD תְּשׁוּעָתְךָyour salvation כְּאִמְרָתֶךָaccording to your promise

May Your loving devotion come to me, O LORD, Your salvation, according to Your promise.

119:42

וְאֶעֱנֶהanswer חֹרְפִיhim who taunts דָבָרword כִּיfor בָטַחְתִּיtrust בִּדְבָרֶךָin your word

Then I can answer him who taunts, for I trust in Your word.

119:43

וְאַלand do not תַּצֵּלtake מִפִּיfrom my mouth דְבַרword אֱמֶתtruth עַדuntil מְאֹדvery כִּיfor לְמִשְׁפָּטֶךָin your judgments יִחָלְתִּיhope

Never take Your word of truth from my mouth, for I hope in Your judgments.

119:44

וְאֶשְׁמְרָהobey תוֹרָתְךָyour law תָמִידalways לְעוֹלָםforever וָעֶדand ever

I will always obey Your law, forever and ever.

119:45

וְאֶתְהַלְּכָהwalk בָרְחָבָהin freedom כִּיfor פִקֻּדֶיךָyour precepts דָרָשְׁתִּיsought

And I will walk in freedom, for I have sought Your precepts.

119:46

וַאֲדַבְּרָהI will speak בְעֵדֹתֶיךָyour testimonies נֶגֶדbefore מְלָכִיםkings וְלֹאnot אֵבוֹשׁashamed

I will speak of Your testimonies before kings, and I will not be ashamed.

119:47

וְאֶשְׁתַּעֲשַׁעI delight בְּמִצְוֺתֶיךָin your commandments אֲשֶׁרthat אָהָבְתִּיlove

I delight in Your commandments because I love them.

119:48

וְאֶשָּׂאI lift כַפַּיmy hands אֶלto מִצְוֺתֶיךָyour commandments אֲשֶׁרwhich אָהָבְתִּיlove וְאָשִׂיחָהand I meditate בְחֻקֶּיךָon your statutes

I lift up my hands to Your commandments, which I love, and I meditate on Your statutes.

119:49

זְכֹרremember דָּבָרword לְעַבְדֶּךָto your servant עַלupon אֲשֶׁרwhich יִחַלְתָּנִיgiven me hope

Remember Your word to Your servant, upon which You have given me hope.

119:50

זֹאתthis נֶחָמָתִיmy comfort בְעָנְיִיin affliction כִּיthat אִמְרָתְךָyour promise חִיָּתְנִיgiven me life

This is my comfort in affliction, that Your promise has given me life.

119:51

זֵדִיםarrogant הֱלִיצֻנִיderide me עַדuntil מְאֹדvery מִתּוֹרָתְךָfrom your law לֹאnot נָטִיתִיturn

The arrogant utterly deride me, but I do not turn from Your law.

119:52

זָכַרְתִּיremember מִשְׁפָּטֶיךָyour judgments מֵעוֹלָםof old יְהוָהLORD וָאֶתְנֶחָםcomfort

I remember Your judgments of old, O LORD, and in them I find comfort.

119:53

זַלְעָפָהrage אֲחָזַתְנִיhold of me מֵרְשָׁעִיםwicked עֹזְבֵיreject תּוֹרָתֶךָyour law

Rage has taken hold of me because of the wicked who reject Your law.

119:54

זְמִרוֹתsongs הָיוּwere לִיto me חֻקֶּיךָyour statutes בְּבֵיתin the house מְגוּרָיmy pilgrimage

Your statutes are songs to me in the house of my pilgrimage.

119:55

זָכַרְתִּיremember בַלַּיְלָהin the night שִׁמְךָyour name יְהוָהLORD וָאֶשְׁמְרָהmay keep תּוֹרָתֶךָyour law

In the night, O LORD, I remember Your name, that I may keep Your law.

119:56

זֹאתthis הָיְתָהwas לִּיto me כִּיfor פִקֻּדֶיךָI your precepts נָצָרְתִּיobey

This is my practice, for I obey Your precepts.

119:57

חֶלְקִיmy portion יְהוָהLORD אָמַרְתִּיpromised לִשְׁמֹרto keep דְּבָרֶיךָyour words

The LORD is my portion; I have promised to keep Your words.

119:58

חִלִּיתִיsought פָנֶיךָyour face בְכָלwith all לֵבheart חָנֵּנִיgracious to me כְּאִמְרָתֶךָaccording to your promise

I have sought Your face with all my heart; be gracious to me according to Your promise.

119:59

חִשַּׁבְתִּיconsidered דְרָכָיmy ways וָאָשִׁיבָהand turned רַגְלַיmy steps אֶלto עֵדֹתֶיךָyour testimonies

I considered my ways and turned my steps to Your testimonies.

119:60

חַשְׁתִּיhurried וְלֹאand not הִתְמַהְמָהְתִּיhesitating לִשְׁמֹרto keep מִצְוֺתֶיךָyour commandments

I hurried without hesitating to keep Your commandments.

119:61

חֶבְלֵיropes רְשָׁעִיםwicked עִוְּדֻנִיbind me תּוֹרָתְךָyour law לֹאnot שָׁכָחְתִּיforget

Though the ropes of the wicked bind me, I do not forget Your law.

119:62

חֲצוֹתmidnight לַיְלָהnight אָקוּםrise לְהוֹדוֹתto give you thanks לָךְfor your עַלon מִשְׁפְּטֵיjudgments צִדְקֶךָyour righteous

At midnight I rise to give You thanks for Your righteous judgments.

119:63

חָבֵרfriend אָנִיam לְכָלto all אֲשֶׁרwho יְרֵאוּךָfear you וּלְשֹׁמְרֵיkeep פִּקּוּדֶיךָyour precepts

I am a friend to all who fear You, and to those who keep Your precepts.

119:64

חַסְדְּךָyour loving devotion יְהוָהLORD מָלְאָהfilled הָאָרֶץthe earth חֻקֶּיךָyour statutes לַמְּדֵנִיteach me

The earth is filled with Your loving devotion, O LORD; teach me Your statutes.

119:65

טוֹבgood עָשִׂיתָdone עִםto עַבְדְּךָyour servant יְהוָהLORD כִּדְבָרֶךָaccording to your word

You are good to Your servant, O LORD, according to Your word.

119:66

טוּבgood טַעַםjudgment וָדַעַתand knowledge לַמְּדֵנִיteach me כִּיfor בְמִצְוֺתֶיךָin your commandments הֶאֱמָנְתִּיbelieve

Teach me good judgment and knowledge, for I believe in Your commandments.

119:67

טֶרֶםbefore אֶעֱנֶהafflicted אֲנִיI שֹׁגֵגastray וְעַתָּהbut now אִמְרָתְךָyour word שָׁמָרְתִּיkeep

Before I was afflicted, I went astray; but now I keep Your word.

119:68

טוֹבgood אַתָּהyou וּמֵטִיבgood לַמְּדֵנִיteach me חֻקֶּיךָyour statutes

You are good, and You do what is good; teach me Your statutes.

119:69

טָפְלוּsmeared עָלַיon me שֶׁקֶרlies זֵדִיםarrogant אֲנִיI בְּכָלwith all לֵבheart אֱצֹּרkeep פִּקּוּדֶיךָyour precepts

Though the arrogant have smeared me with lies, I keep Your precepts with all my heart.

119:70

טָפַשׁhard כַּחֵלֶבand callous לִבָּםtheir hearts אֲנִיI תּוֹרָתְךָyour law שִׁעֲשָׁעְתִּיdelight

Their hearts are hard and callous, but I delight in Your law.

119:71

טוֹבgood לִיfor me כִיthat עֻנֵּיתִיafflicted לְמַעַןso אֶלְמַדlearn חֻקֶּיךָyour statutes

It was good for me to be afflicted, that I might learn Your statutes.

119:72

טוֹבprecious לִיto me תוֹרַתlaw פִּיךָyour mouth מֵאַלְפֵיthan thousands זָהָבgold וָכָסֶףand silver

The law from Your mouth is more precious to me than thousands of pieces of gold and silver.

119:73

יָדֶיךָyour hands עָשׂוּנִיmade me וַיְכוֹנְנוּנִיand fashioned me הֲבִינֵנִיgive me understanding וְאֶלְמְדָהto learn מִצְוֺתֶיךָyour commandments

I call to You; save me, that I may keep Your testimonies.

119:147

קִדַּמְתִּיrise בַנֶּשֶׁףbefore dawn וָאֲשַׁוֵּעָהand cry help לִדְבָרְךָfor in your word יִחָלְתִּיhope

I rise before dawn and cry for help; in Your word I have put my hope.

119:148

קִדְּמוּanticipate עֵינַיmy eyes אַשְׁמֻרוֹתwatches לָשִׂיחַmay meditate בְּאִמְרָתֶךָon your word

My eyes anticipate the watches of night, that I may meditate on Your word.

119:149

קוֹלִיmy voice שִׁמְעָהhear כְחַסְדֶּךָaccording to your loving devotion יְהוָהLORD כְּמִשְׁפָּטֶךָaccording to your justice חַיֵּנִיme life

Hear my voice, O LORD, according to Your loving devotion; give me life according to Your justice.

119:150

קָרְבוּnear רֹדְפֵיfollow זִמָּהwickedness מִתּוֹרָתְךָyour law רָחָקוּfar

Those who follow after wickedness draw near; they are far from Your law.

119:151

קָרוֹבnear אַתָּהyou יְהוָהLORD וְכָלand all מִצְוֺתֶיךָyour commandments אֱמֶתtrue

You are near, O LORD, and all Your commandments are true.

119:152

קֶדֶםlong יָדַעְתִּיlearned מֵעֵדֹתֶיךָfrom your testimonies כִּיthat לְעוֹלָםforever יְסַדְתָּםestablished them

Long ago I learned from Your testimonies that You have established them forever.

119:153

רְאֵהlook עָנְיִיmy affliction וְחַלְּצֵנִיand rescue me כִּיfor תוֹרָתְךָyour law לֹאnot שָׁכָחְתִּיforgotten

Look upon my affliction and rescue me, for I have not forgotten Your law.

119:154

רִיבָהdefend רִיבִיmy cause וּגְאָלֵנִיand redeem me לְאִמְרָתְךָaccording to your word חַיֵּנִיrevive me

Defend my cause and redeem me; revive me according to Your word.

119:155

רָחוֹקfar מֵרְשָׁעִיםfrom the wicked יְשׁוּעָהsalvation כִּיbecause חֻקֶּיךָyour statutes לֹאnot דָרָשׁוּseek

Salvation is far from the wicked because they do not seek Your statutes.

119:156

רַחֲמֶיךָyour mercies רַבִּיםgreat יְהוָהLORD כְּמִשְׁפָּטֶיךָaccording to your ordinances חַיֵּנִיrevive me

Great are Your mercies, O LORD; revive me according to Your ordinances.

119:157

רַבִּיםmany רֹדְפַיmy persecutors וְצָרָיand foes מֵעֵדְוֺתֶיךָfrom your testimonies לֹאnot נָטִיתִיturned

Though my persecutors and foes are many, I have not turned from Your testimonies.

119:158

רָאִיתִיlook בֹגְדִיםfaithless וָאֶתְקוֹטָטָהwith loathing אֲשֶׁרthat אִמְרָתְךָyour word לֹאnot שָׁמָרוּkeep

I look on the faithless with loathing because they do not keep Your word.

119:159

רְאֵהconsider כִּיfor פִקּוּדֶיךָI your precepts אָהָבְתִּיlove יְהוָהLORD כְּחַסְדְּךָaccording to your loving devotion חַיֵּנִיme life

Consider how I love Your precepts, O LORD; give me life according to Your loving devotion.

119:160

רֹאשׁhead דְּבָרְךָyour word אֱמֶתtruth וּלְעוֹלָםendure forever כָּלall מִשְׁפַּטjudgments צִדְקֶךָyour righteous

The entirety of Your word is truth, and all Your righteous judgments endure forever.

119:161

שָׂרִיםrulers רְדָפוּנִיpersecute me חִנָּםwithout cause וּמִדְּבָרְךָyour word פָּחַדfears לִבִּיmy heart

Rulers persecute me without cause, but my heart fears only Your word.

119:162

שָׂשׂrejoice אָנֹכִיI עַלin אִמְרָתֶךָyour promise כְּמוֹצֵאfinds שָׁלָלspoil רָבgreat

I rejoice in Your promise like one who finds great spoil.

119:163

שֶׁקֶרfalsehood שָׂנֵאתִיhate וַאֲתַעֵבָהand abhor תּוֹרָתְךָyour law אָהָבְתִּיlove

I hate and abhor falsehood, but Your law I love.

119:164

שֶׁבַעseven בַּיּוֹםtimes a day הִלַּלְתִּיךָpraise you עַלfor מִשְׁפְּטֵיjudgments צִדְקֶךָyour righteous

Seven times a day I praise You for Your righteous judgments.

119:165

שָׁלוֹםpeace רָבabundant לְאֹהֲבֵיlove תוֹרָתֶךָyour law וְאֵיןnothing לָמוֹto them מִכְשׁוֹלstumble

Abundant peace belongs to those who love Your law; nothing can make them stumble.

119:166

שִׂבַּרְתִּיwait לִישׁוּעָתְךָfor your salvation O יְהוָהLORD וּמִצְוֺתֶיךָyour commandments עָשִׂיתִיcarry

I wait for Your salvation, O LORD, and I carry out Your commandments.

119:167

שָׁמְרָהobey נַפְשִׁיmy soul עֵדֹתֶיךָyour testimonies וָאֹהֲבֵםand love them מְאֹדgreatly

I obey Your testimonies and love them greatly.

119:168

שָׁמַרְתִּיobey פִקּוּדֶיךָyour precepts וְעֵדֹתֶיךָand your testimonies כִּיfor כָלall דְּרָכַיmy ways נֶגְדֶּךָbefore you

I obey Your precepts and Your testimonies, for all my ways are before You.

119:169

תִּקְרַבcome רִנָּתִיmy cry לְפָנֶיךָbefore you יְהוָהLORD כִּדְבָרְךָaccording to your word הֲבִינֵנִיgive me understanding

May my cry come before You, O LORD; give me understanding according to Your word.

119:170

תָּבוֹאcome תְּחִנָּתִיmay my plea לְפָנֶיךָbefore you כְּאִמְרָתְךָaccording to your promise הַצִּילֵנִיrescue me

May my plea come before You; rescue me according to Your promise.

119:171

תַּבַּעְנָהpour שְׂפָתַיmy lips תְּהִלָּהpraise כִּיfor תְלַמְּדֵנִיteach me חֻקֶּיךָyour statutes

My lips pour forth praise, for You teach me Your statutes.

119:172

תַּעַןanswer לְשׁוֹנִיmy tongue אִמְרָתֶךָyour word כִּיfor כָלall מִצְוֺתֶיךָyour commandments צֶּדֶקrighteous

My tongue sings of Your word, for all Your commandments are righteous.

119:173

תְּהִיmay יָדְךָyour hand לְעָזְרֵנִיto help me כִּיfor פִקּוּדֶיךָyour precepts בָחָרְתִּיchosen

May Your hand be ready to help me, for I have chosen Your precepts.

119:174

תָּאַבְתִּיlong לִישׁוּעָתְךָfor your salvation O יְהוָהLORD וְתוֹרָתְךָand your law שַׁעֲשֻׁעָיmy delight

I long for Your salvation, O LORD, and Your law is my delight.

119:175

תְּחִיlive נַפְשִׁיlet me וּתְהַלְלֶךָּto praise you וּמִשְׁפָּטֶךָmay your judgments יַעֲזְרֻנִיsustain me

Let me live to praise You; may Your judgments sustain me.

119:176

תָּעִיתִיstrayed כְּשֶׂהsheep אֹבֵדlost בַּקֵּשׁseek עַבְדֶּךָyour servant כִּיfor מִצְוֺתֶיךָyour commandments לֹאnot שָׁכָחְתִּיforgotten

I have strayed like a lost sheep; seek Your servant, for I have not forgotten Your commandments.
