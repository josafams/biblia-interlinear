Psalms 25

An acrostic prayer for guidance and forgiveness

Open in the interlinear reader

25:1

לְדָוִדof David אֵלֶיךָto you יְהוָהLORD נַפְשִׁיmy soul אֶשָּׂאlift

Of David. To You, O LORD, I lift up my soul;

25:2

אֱלֹהַיmy God בְּךָin you בָטַחְתִּיtrust אַלdo not do אֵבוֹשָׁהshame אַלnot יַעַלְצוּlet אֹיְבַיmy enemies לִיto me

in You, my God, I trust. Do not let me be put to shame; do not let my enemies exult over me.

25:3

גַּםeven כָּלnone קֹוֶיךָwait for you לֹאnot יֵבֹשׁוּbe יֵבֹשׁוּput הַבּוֹגְדִיםwho are faithless רֵיקָםwithout cause

Surely none who wait for You will be put to shame; but those who are faithless without cause will be disgraced.

25:4

דְּרָכֶיךָyour ways יְהוָהLORD הוֹדִיעֵנִיshow me אֹרְחוֹתֶיךָyour paths לַמְּדֵנִיteach me

Show me Your ways, O LORD; teach me Your paths.

25:5

הַדְרִיכֵנִיguide me בַאֲמִתֶּךָin your truth וְלַמְּדֵנִיand teach me כִּיfor אַתָּהyou אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׁעִיmy salvation אוֹתְךָyou קִוִּיתִיwait כָּלall הַיּוֹםday

Guide me in Your truth and teach me, for You are the God of my salvation; all day long I wait for You.

25:6

זְכֹרremember רַחֲמֶיךָO your compassion יְהוָהLORD וַחֲסָדֶיךָand loving devotion כִּיfor מֵעוֹלָםfrom age הֵמָּהthey

Remember, O LORD, Your compassion and loving devotion, for they are from age to age.

25:7

חַטֹּאותsins נְעוּרַיmy youth וּפְשָׁעַיnor my rebellious אַלnot תִּזְכֹּרremember כְּחַסְדְּךָaccording to your loving devotion זְכָרremember לִיto me אַתָּהyou לְמַעַןbecause טוּבְךָyour goodness O יְהוָהLORD

Remember not the sins of my youth, nor my rebellious acts; remember me according to Your loving devotion, because of Your goodness, O LORD.

25:8

טוֹבgood וְיָשָׁרand upright יְהוָהLORD עַלon כֵּןtherefore יוֹרֶהshows חַטָּאִיםsinners בַּדָּרֶךְthe way

Good and upright is the LORD; therefore He shows sinners the way.

25:9

יַדְרֵךְguides עֲנָוִיםhumble בַּמִּשְׁפָּטin what is right וִילַמֵּדand teaches עֲנָוִיםthem דַּרְכּוֹhis way

He guides the humble in what is right and teaches them His way.

25:10

כָּלall אָרְחוֹתways יְהוָהLORD’s חֶסֶדloving וֶאֱמֶתand faithful לְנֹצְרֵיkeep בְרִיתוֹhis covenant וְעֵדֹתָיוand his decrees

All the LORD’s ways are loving and faithful to those who keep His covenant and His decrees.

25:11

לְמַעַןsake שִׁמְךָyour name יְהוָהLORD וְסָלַחְתָּforgive לַעֲוֺנִיmy iniquity כִּיfor רַבgreat הוּאis

For the sake of Your name, O LORD, forgive my iniquity, for it is great.

25:12

מִיwho זֶהis הָאִישׁthe man יְרֵאfears יְהוָהLORD יוֹרֶנּוּinstruct him בְּדֶרֶךְin the path יִבְחָרchosen

Who is the man who fears the LORD? He will instruct him in the path chosen for him.

25:13

נַפְשׁוֹhis soul בְּטוֹבin prosperity תָּלִיןdwell וְזַרְעוֹand his descendants will יִירַשׁinherit אָרֶץland

His soul will dwell in prosperity, and his descendants will inherit the land.

25:14

סוֹדthe confides יְהוָהLORD לִירֵאָיוfear him וּבְרִיתוֹand his covenant לְהוֹדִיעָםreveals

The LORD confides in those who fear Him, and reveals His covenant to them.

25:15

עֵינַיmy eyes תָּמִידalways אֶלto יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor הוּאhe יוֹצִיאfree מֵרֶשֶׁתfrom the mesh רַגְלָיmy feet

My eyes are always on the LORD, for He will free my feet from the mesh.

25:16

פְּנֵהturn אֵלַיto me וְחָנֵּנִיand be gracious I כִּיfor יָחִידlonely וְעָנִיand afflicted אָנִיam

Turn to me and be gracious, for I am lonely and afflicted.

25:17

צָרוֹתtroubles לְבָבִיmy heart הִרְחִיבוּincrease מִמְּצוּקוֹתַיfrom my distress הוֹצִיאֵנִיfree me

The troubles of my heart increase; free me from my distress.

25:18

רְאֵהconsider עָנְיִיmy affliction וַעֲמָלִיand trouble וְשָׂאand take לְכָלall חַטֹּאותָיmy sins

Consider my affliction and trouble, and take away all my sins.

25:19

רְאֵהconsider אוֹיְבַיmy enemies כִּיfor רָבּוּmany וְשִׂנְאַתhatred חָמָסvicious שְׂנֵאוּנִיhate me

Consider my enemies, for they are many, and they hate me with vicious hatred.

25:20

שָׁמְרָהguard נַפְשִׁיmy soul וְהַצִּילֵנִיand deliver me אַלnot אֵבוֹשׁshame כִּיfor חָסִיתִיrefuge בָךְin you

Guard my soul and deliver me; let me not be put to shame, for I take refuge in You.

25:21

תֹּםintegrity וָיֹשֶׁרand uprightness יִצְּרוּנִיpreserve me כִּיbecause קִוִּיתִיךָwait for you

May integrity and uprightness preserve me, because I wait for You.

25:22

פְּדֵהredeem אֱלֹהִיםGod אֶת[obj] יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael מִכֹּלfrom all צָרוֹתָיוits distress

Redeem Israel, O God, from all its distress.
