Psalms 48

Zion, the city of the great King

Open in the interlinear reader

A song. A Psalm of the sons of Korah

48:1

גָּדוֹלgreat יְהוָהLORD וּמְהֻלָּלpraised מְאֹדgreatly בְּעִירin the city אֱלֹהֵינוּour God הַרmountain קָדְשׁוֹhis holy

Great is the LORD, and greatly to be praised in the city of our God, His holy mountain.

48:2

יְפֵהbeautiful נוֹףloftiness מְשׂוֹשׂjoy כָּלall הָאָרֶץthe earth הַרMount צִיּוֹןZion יַרְכְּתֵיpeaks צָפוֹןZaphon קִרְיַתcity מֶלֶךְking רָבgreat

Beautiful in loftiness, the joy of all the earth, like the peaks of Zaphon is Mount Zion, the city of the great King.

48:3

אֱלֹהִיםGod בְּאַרְמְנוֹתֶיהָin her citadels נוֹדַעhe לְמִשְׂגָּבfortress

God is in her citadels; He has shown Himself to be a fortress.

48:4

כִּיfor הִנֵּהbehold הַמְּלָכִיםthe kings נוֹעֲדוּassembled עָבְרוּadvanced יַחְדָּוtogether

For behold, the kings assembled; they all advanced together.

48:5

הֵמָּהthey רָאוּsaw כֵּןso תָּמָהוּastounded נִבְהֲלוּfled נֶחְפָּזוּterror

They saw and were astounded; they fled in terror.

48:6

רְעָדָהtrembling אֲחָזָתַםseized them שָׁםthere חִילanguish כַּיּוֹלֵדָהlike a woman in labor

Trembling seized them there, anguish like a woman in labor.

48:7

בְּרוּחַwith a wind קָדִיםeast תְּשַׁבֵּרwrecked אֳנִיּוֹתships תַּרְשִׁישׁTarshish

With a wind from the east You wrecked the ships of Tarshish.

48:8

כַּאֲשֶׁרas we have שָׁמַעְנוּheard כֵּןso רָאִינוּseen בְּעִירin the city יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts בְּעִירin the city אֱלֹהֵינוּour God אֱלֹהִיםGod יְכוֹנְנֶהָestablish her עַדuntil עוֹלָםforever סֶלָהSelah

As we have heard, so we have seen in the city of the LORD of Hosts, in the city of our God: God will establish her forever. Selah

48:9

דִּמִּינוּO אֱלֹהִיםGod חַסְדֶּךָyour loving devotion בְּקֶרֶבwithin הֵיכָלֶךָyour temple

Within Your temple, O God, we contemplate Your loving devotion.

48:10

כְּשִׁמְךָyour name אֱלֹהִיםGod כֵּןso תְּהִלָּתְךָyour praise reaches עַלto קַצְוֵיends אֶרֶץearth צֶדֶקrighteousness מָלְאָהfull יְמִינֶךָyour right hand

Your name, O God, like Your praise, reaches to the ends of the earth; Your right hand is full of righteousness.

48:11

יִשְׂמַחglad הַרMount צִיּוֹןZion תָּגֵלְנָהrejoice בְּנוֹתdaughters יְהוּדָהJudah לְמַעַןso מִשְׁפָּטֶיךָyour judgments

Mount Zion is glad, the daughters of Judah rejoice, on account of Your judgments.

48:12

סֹבּוּmarch around צִיּוֹןZion וְהַקִּיפוּהָencircle her סִפְרוּcount מִגְדָּלֶיהָher towers

March around Zion, encircle her, count her towers,

48:13

שִׁיתוּconsider לִבְּכֶםyour heart לְחֵילָהher ramparts פַּסְּגוּramparts אַרְמְנוֹתֶיהָtour her citadels לְמַעַןmay תְּסַפְּרוּtell לְדוֹרthe generation אַחֲרוֹןnext

consider her ramparts, tour her citadels, that you may tell the next generation.

48:14

כִּיfor זֶהthis אֱלֹהִיםGod אֱלֹהֵינוּour God עוֹלָםforever וָעֶדand ever הוּאhe יְנַהֲגֵנוּour guide עַלon מוּתdeath

For this God is our God forever and ever; He will be our guide even till death.
