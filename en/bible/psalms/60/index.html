Psalms 60

Lament after defeat and a divine promise

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. To the tune of “The Lily of the Covenant.” A Miktam of David for instruction. When he fought Aram-naharaim and Aram-zobah, and Joab returned and struck down 12,000 Edomites in the Valley of Salt

60:1

אֱלֹהִיםGod זְנַחְתָּנוּrejected us פְרַצְתָּנוּbroken us אָנַפְתָּangry תְּשׁוֹבֵבrestore לָנוּto us

You have rejected us, O God; You have broken us; You have been angry; restore us!

60:2

הִרְעַשְׁתָּהshaken אֶרֶץland פְּצַמְתָּהּtorn it רְפָהheal שְׁבָרֶיהָits fractures כִיfor מָטָהquaking

You have shaken the land and torn it open. Heal its fractures, for it is quaking.

60:3

הִרְאִיתָהshown עַמְּךָyour people קָשָׁהhardship הִשְׁקִיתָנוּmade us drink יַיִןwine תַּרְעֵלָהstaggered

You have shown Your people hardship; we are staggered from the wine You made us drink.

60:4

נָתַתָּהraised לִּירֵאֶיךָfear you נֵּסbanner לְהִתְנוֹסֵסmay flee מִפְּנֵיfrom before קֹשֶׁטbow סֶלָהSelah

You have raised a banner for those who fear You, that they may flee the bow. Selah

60:5

לְמַעַןmay יֵחָלְצוּןdelivered יְדִידֶיךָbeloved הוֹשִׁיעָהsave יְמִינְךָyour right hand that your וַעֲנֵנִיbe

Respond and save us with Your right hand, that Your beloved may be delivered.

60:6

אֱלֹהִיםGod דִּבֶּרspoken בְּקָדְשׁוֹfrom his sanctuary אֶעְלֹזָהtriumph אֲחַלְּקָהparcel שְׁכֶםShechem וְעֵמֶקValley סֻכּוֹתSuccoth אֲמַדֵּדapportion

God has spoken from His sanctuary: “I will triumph! I will parcel out Shechem and apportion the Valley of Succoth.

60:7

לִיto me גִלְעָדGilead וְלִיand to me מְנַשֶּׁהManasseh וְאֶפְרַיִםEphraim מָעוֹזis רֹאשִׁיmy helmet יְהוּדָהJudah מְחֹקְקִיmy scepter

Gilead is Mine, and Manasseh is Mine; Ephraim is My helmet, Judah is My scepter.

60:8

מוֹאָבMoab סִירis רַחְצִיmy washbasin עַלupon אֱדוֹםEdom אַשְׁלִיךְI נַעֲלִיtoss my sandal עָלַיover I פְּלֶשֶׁתPhilistia הִתְרֹעָעִיshout

Moab is My washbasin; upon Edom I toss My sandal; over Philistia I shout in triumph.”

60:9

מִיwho יֹבִלֵנִיbring me עִירcity מָצוֹרfortified מִיwho נָחַנִיlead me עַדto אֱדוֹםEdom

Who will bring me to the fortified city? Who will lead me to Edom?

60:10

הֲלֹאyou not אַתָּהyou אֱלֹהִיםGod זְנַחְתָּנוּrejected us וְלֹאwill you no תֵצֵאmarch אֱלֹהִיםGod בְּצִבְאוֹתֵינוּwith our armies

Have You not rejected us, O God? Will You no longer march out, O God, with our armies?

60:11

הָבָהgive לָּנוּto us עֶזְרָתaid מִצָּרagainst the enemy וְשָׁוְאworthless תְּשׁוּעַתhelp אָדָםman

Give us aid against the enemy, for the help of man is worthless.

60:12

בֵּאלֹהִיםwith God נַעֲשֶׂהperform חָיִלvalor וְהוּאand he יָבוּסtrample צָרֵינוּour enemies

With God we will perform with valor, and He will trample our enemies.
