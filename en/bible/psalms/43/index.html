Psalms 43

A plea for light to return to the altar

Open in the interlinear reader

43:1

שָׁפְטֵנִיvindicate me אֱלֹהִיםGod וְרִיבָהand plead רִיבִיmy case מִגּוֹיnation לֹאnot חָסִידungodly מֵאִישׁmen מִרְמָהdeceitful וְעַוְלָהand unjust תְפַלְּטֵנִיdeliver me

Vindicate me, O God, and plead my case against an ungodly nation; deliver me from deceitful and unjust men.

43:2

כִּיfor אַתָּהyou אֱלֹהֵיGod מָעוּזִּיmy refuge לָמָהwhy זְנַחְתָּנִיrejected me לָמָּהwhy קֹדֵרI אֶתְהַלֵּךְwalk בְּלַחַץenemy’s oppression אוֹיֵבbecause

For You are the God of my refuge. Why have You rejected me? Why must I walk in sorrow because of the enemy’s oppression?

43:3

שְׁלַחsend אוֹרְךָyour light וַאֲמִתְּךָand your truth הֵמָּהthem יַנְחוּנִיlead me יְבִיאוּנִיbring me אֶלto הַרmountain קָדְשְׁךָyour holy וְאֶלand to מִשְׁכְּנוֹתֶיךָplace where you

Send out Your light and Your truth; let them lead me. Let them bring me to Your holy mountain, and to the place where You dwell.

43:4

וְאָבוֹאָהgo אֶלto מִזְבַּחaltar אֱלֹהִיםGod אֶלto אֵלGod שִׂמְחַתgreatest גִּילִיmy greatest joy I וְאוֹדְךָwill praise you בְכִנּוֹרwith the harp אֱלֹהִיםGod אֱלֹהָיGod my God

Then I will go to the altar of God, to God, my greatest joy. I will praise You with the harp, O God, my God.

43:5

מַהwhy תִּשְׁתּוֹחֲחִיdowncast נַפְשִׁיmy soul וּמַהwhy תֶּהֱמִיunease עָלָיon me הוֹחִילִיput your hope לֵאלֹהִיםin God כִּיfor עוֹדlonger אוֹדֶנּוּpraise him יְשׁוּעֹתSavior פָּנַיmy face וֵאלֹהָיand my God

Why are you downcast, O my soul? Why the unease within me? Put your hope in God, for I will yet praise Him, my Savior and my God.
