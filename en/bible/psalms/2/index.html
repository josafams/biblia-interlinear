Psalms 2

The anointed king and the rebellious nations

Open in the interlinear reader

2:1

לָמָּהto why רָגְשׁוּrage גוֹיִםnations וּלְאֻמִּיםand the peoples יֶהְגּוּplot רִיקvain

Why do the nations rage and the peoples plot in vain?

2:2

יִתְיַצְּבוּstand מַלְכֵיkings אֶרֶץearth וְרוֹזְנִיםand the rulers נוֹסְדוּgather יָחַדtogether עַלagainst יְהוָהLORD וְעַלand against מְשִׁיחוֹhis anointed

The kings of the earth take their stand and the rulers gather together, against the LORD and against His Anointed One:

2:3

נְנַתְּקָהbreak אֶת[obj] מוֹסְרוֹתֵימוֹtheir chains וְנַשְׁלִיכָהand cast מִמֶּנּוּfrom us עֲבֹתֵימוֹtheir cords

“Let us break Their chains and cast away Their cords.”

2:4

יוֹשֵׁבenthroned בַּשָּׁמַיִםin heaven יִשְׂחָקlaughs אֲדֹנָיLord יִלְעַגtaunts לָמוֹto them

The One enthroned in heaven laughs; the Lord taunts them.

2:5

אָזthen יְדַבֵּרrebukes אֵלֵימוֹto them בְאַפּוֹin his anger וּבַחֲרוֹנוֹin his fury יְבַהֲלֵמוֹterrifies them

Then He rebukes them in His anger, and terrifies them in His fury:

2:6

וַאֲנִיI נָסַכְתִּיinstalled מַלְכִּיmy king עַלon צִיּוֹןZion הַרmountain קָדְשִׁיmy holy

“I have installed My King on Zion, upon My holy mountain.”

2:7

אֲסַפְּרָהproclaim אֶלto חֹקdecree יְהוָהLORD אָמַרspoken אֵלַיto me בְּנִיmy son אַתָּהyou אֲנִיI הַיּוֹםtoday יְלִדְתִּיךָbecome your father

I will proclaim the decree spoken to Me by the LORD: “You are My Son; today I have become Your Father.

2:8

שְׁאַלask מִמֶּנִּיfrom me וְאֶתְּנָהmake גוֹיִםnations נַחֲלָתֶךָyour inheritance וַאֲחֻזָּתְךָyour possession אַפְסֵיends אָרֶץearth

Ask Me, and I will make the nations Your inheritance, the ends of the earth Your possession.

2:9

תְּרֹעֵםwill break them בְּשֵׁבֶטscepter בַּרְזֶלiron כִּכְלִיlike article יוֹצֵרpottery תְּנַפְּצֵםshatter them

You will break them with an iron scepter; You will shatter them like pottery.”

2:10

וְעַתָּהtherefore מְלָכִיםkings הַשְׂכִּילוּwise הִוָּסְרוּadmonished שֹׁפְטֵיjudges אָרֶץearth

Therefore be wise, O kings; be admonished, O judges of the earth.

2:11

עִבְדוּserve אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD בְּיִרְאָהwith fear וְגִילוּand rejoice בִּרְעָדָהwith trembling

Serve the LORD with fear, and rejoice with trembling.

2:12

נַשְּׁקוּkiss בַרson פֶּןlest יֶאֱנַףangry וְתֹאבְדוּand you perish דֶרֶךְrebellion כִּיwhen יִבְעַרrebellion כִּמְעַטan instant אַפּוֹhis wrath אַשְׁרֵיblessed כָּלall חוֹסֵיrefuge בוֹin him

Kiss the Son, lest He be angry and you perish in your rebellion, when His wrath ignites in an instant. Blessed are all who take refuge in Him.
