Psalms 87

Zion, the birthplace of the peoples

Open in the interlinear reader

87:1

לִבְנֵיsons קֹרַחKorah מִזְמוֹרPsalm שִׁירsong יְסוּדָתוֹfounded his בְּהַרְרֵיmountains קֹדֶשׁholy

A Psalm of the sons of Korah. A song. He has founded His city on the holy mountains.

87:2

אֹהֵבloves יְהוָהLORD שַׁעֲרֵיgates צִיּוֹןZion מִכֹּלthan all מִשְׁכְּנוֹתdwellings יַעֲקֹבJacob

The LORD loves the gates of Zion more than all the dwellings of Jacob.

87:3

נִכְבָּדוֹתglorious מְדֻבָּרascribed בָּךְto you עִירcity הָאֱלֹהִיםof God סֶלָהSelah

Glorious things are ascribed to you, O city of God. Selah

87:4

אַזְכִּירmention רַהַבRahab וּבָבֶלand Babylon לְיֹדְעָיknow me הִנֵּהbehold פְלֶשֶׁתPhilistia וְצוֹרTyre and עִםwith כּוּשׁCush זֶהthis יֻלַּדborn שָׁםthere

“I will mention Rahab and Babylon among those who know Me— along with Philistia, Tyre, and Cush— when I say, ‘This one was born in Zion.’”

87:5

וּלֲצִיּוֹןZion יֵאָמַרsaid אִישׁone וְאִישׁand that one יֻלַּדborn בָּהּin her וְהוּאand he יְכוֹנְנֶהָestablish her עֶלְיוֹןMost high

And it will be said of Zion: “This one and that one were born in her, and the Most High Himself will establish her.”

87:6

יְהוָהLORD יִסְפֹּרrecord בִּכְתוֹבrecord in עַמִּיםpeoples זֶהthis יֻלַּדborn שָׁםthere סֶלָהSelah

The LORD will record in the register of the peoples: “This one was born in Zion.” Selah

87:7

וְשָׁרִיםsingers and כְּחֹלְלִיםproclaim כָּלall מַעְיָנַיmy springs בָּךְin you

Singers and pipers will proclaim, “All my springs of joy are in You.”
