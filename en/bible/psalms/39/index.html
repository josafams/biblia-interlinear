Psalms 39

The brevity of human life

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. For Jeduthun. A Psalm of David

39:1

אָמַרְתִּיsaid אֶשְׁמְרָהwatch דְרָכַיmy ways מֵחֲטוֹאnot sin בִלְשׁוֹנִיwith my tongue אֶשְׁמְרָהguard לְפִיmy mouth מַחְסוֹםmuzzle בְּעֹדin to רָשָׁעwicked לְנֶגְדִּיto before me

I said, “I will watch my ways so that I will not sin with my tongue; I will guard my mouth with a muzzle as long as the wicked are present.”

39:2

נֶאֱלַמְתִּיspeechless דוּמִיָּהremained הֶחֱשֵׁיתִיsilent מִטּוֹבfrom speaking good וּכְאֵבִיand my sorrow נֶעְכָּרstirred

I was speechless and still; I remained silent, even from speaking good, and my sorrow was stirred.

39:3

חַםhot לִבִּיmy heart בְּקִרְבִּיwithin me בַּהֲגִיגִיas I mused תִבְעַרfire אֵשׁburned דִּבַּרְתִּיspoke בִּלְשׁוֹנִיwith my tongue

My heart grew hot within me; as I mused, the fire burned. Then I spoke with my tongue:

39:4

הוֹדִיעֵנִיshow me יְהוָהLORD קִצִּיmy end וּמִדַּתand the measure יָמַיdays let me מַהwhat הִיאis אֵדְעָהknow מֶהhow חָדֵלfleeting אָנִיI

“Show me, O LORD, my end and the measure of my days. Let me know how fleeting my life is.

39:5

הִנֵּהindeed טְפָחוֹתhandbreadth thick נָתַתָּהmade יָמַיmy days וְחֶלְדִּיand my lifetime כְאַיִןas nothing נֶגְדֶּךָbefore you אַךְbut כָּלeach הֶבֶלbreath כָּלall אָדָםman נִצָּבstanding סֶלָהSelah

You, indeed, have made my days as handbreadths, and my lifetime as nothing before You. Truly each man at his best exists as but a breath. Selah

39:6

אַךְsurely בְּצֶלֶםevery יִתְהַלֶּךְgoes אִישׁman אַךְsurely הֶבֶלvain יֶהֱמָיוּןbustles יִצְבֹּרheaps וְלֹאnot יֵדַעknowing מִיwho אֹסְפָםhaul them

Surely every man goes about like a phantom; surely he bustles in vain; he heaps up riches not knowing who will haul them away.

39:7

וְעַתָּהand now מַהwhat קִּוִּיתִיwait אֲדֹנָיLord תּוֹחַלְתִּיhope is לְךָto you הִיאis

And now, O Lord, for what do I wait? My hope is in You.

39:8

מִכָּלfrom all פְּשָׁעַיmy transgressions הַצִּילֵנִיdeliver me חֶרְפַּתreproach נָבָלfools אַלdo not תְּשִׂימֵנִיmake me

Deliver me from all my transgressions; do not make me the reproach of fools.

39:9

נֶאֱלַמְתִּיmute לֹאnot אֶפְתַּחopen פִּיmy mouth כִּיbecause אַתָּהyou עָשִׂיתָdone

I have become mute; I do not open my mouth because of what You have done.

39:10

הָסֵרremove מֵעָלַיfrom the me נִגְעֶךָyour scourge מִתִּגְרַתby the force יָדְךָyour hand אֲנִיam כָלִיתִיperishing

Remove Your scourge from me; I am perishing by the force of Your hand.

39:11

בְּתוֹכָחוֹתdiscipline עַלfor עָוֺןiniquity יִסַּרְתָּcorrect אִישׁman וַתֶּמֶסconsuming כָּעָשׁlike a moth חֲמוּדוֹholds dear אַךְsurely הֶבֶלvapor כָּלeach אָדָםman סֶלָהSelah

You discipline and correct a man for his iniquity, consuming like a moth what he holds dear; surely each man is but a vapor. Selah

39:12

שִׁמְעָהhear תְפִלָּתִיmy prayer יְהוָהLORD וְשַׁוְעָתִיmy cry הַאֲזִינָהgive ear אֶלto דִּמְעָתִיmy weeping אַלdo not תֶּחֱרַשׁdeaf כִּיfor גֵרforeigner אָנֹכִיam עִמָּךְwith you תּוֹשָׁבstranger כְּכָלlike all אֲבוֹתָיmy fathers

Hear my prayer, O LORD, and give ear to my cry for help; do not be deaf to my weeping. For I am a foreigner dwelling with You, a stranger like all my fathers.

39:13

הָשַׁעturn מִמֶּנִּיfrom me וְאַבְלִיגָהmay again be cheered בְּטֶרֶםbefore אֵלֵךְdepart וְאֵינֶנִּיand am no

Turn Your gaze away from me, that I may again be cheered before I depart and am no more.”
