Psalms 12

False words and the pure word of God

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. According to Sheminith. A Psalm of David

12:1

הוֹשִׁיעָהhelp יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor גָמַרpurpose חָסִידgodly כִּיfor פַסּוּno more אֱמוּנִיםfaithful מִבְּנֵיfrom among אָדָםmen

Help, O LORD, for the godly are no more; the faithful have vanished from among men.

12:2

שָׁוְאlie יְדַבְּרוּspeak אִישׁone אֶתwith רֵעֵהוּanother שְׂפַתlips חֲלָקוֹתflattering בְּלֵבin hearts וָלֵבheart יְדַבֵּרוּdouble

They lie to one another; they speak with flattering lips and a double heart.

12:3

יַכְרֵתcut off יְהוָהLORD כָּלall שִׂפְתֵיlips חֲלָקוֹתflattering לָשׁוֹןtongue מְדַבֶּרֶתspeaking גְּדֹלוֹתgreat

May the LORD cut off all flattering lips and every boastful tongue.

12:4

אֲשֶׁרthat אָמְרוּsay לִלְשֹׁנֵנוּtongues we נַגְבִּירprevail שְׂפָתֵינוּour lips אִתָּנוּwith our מִיwho אָדוֹןmaster לָנוּto us

They say, “With our tongues we will prevail. We own our lips—who can be our master?”

12:5

מִשֹּׁדfor the cause עֲנִיִּיםoppressed מֵאַנְקַתgroaning אֶבְיוֹנִיםneedy עַתָּהnow אָקוּםarise יֹאמַרsays יְהוָהLORD אָשִׁיתbring בְּיֵשַׁעsafety יָפִיחַyearns לוֹto him

“For the cause of the oppressed and for the groaning of the needy, I will now arise,” says the LORD. “I will bring safety to him who yearns.”

12:6

אִמֲרוֹתwords יְהוָהLORD אֲמָרוֹתflawless טְהֹרוֹתlike כֶּסֶףsilver צָרוּףrefined בַּעֲלִילin a furnace לָאָרֶץto the ground מְזֻקָּקpurified שִׁבְעָתָיִםsevenfold

The words of the LORD are flawless, like silver refined in a furnace, like gold purified sevenfold.

12:7

אַתָּהyou יְהוָהLORD תִּשְׁמְרֵםkeep us תִּצְּרֶנּוּguard מִןfrom הַדּוֹרthis generation זוּyou לְעוֹלָםforever

You, O LORD, will keep us; You will forever guard us from this generation.

12:8

סָבִיבaround רְשָׁעִיםwicked יִתְהַלָּכוּןwander כְּרֻםexalted זֻלּוּתfreely vileness לִבְנֵיamong אָדָםmen

The wicked wander freely, and vileness is exalted among men.
