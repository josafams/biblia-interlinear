Psalms 74

Lament for the ruined sanctuary

Open in the interlinear reader

74:1

מַשְׂכִּילMaskil לְאָסָףAsaph לָמָהwhy אֱלֹהִיםGod זָנַחְתָּrejected לָנֶצַחforever יֶעְשַׁןsmolder אַפְּךָyour anger בְּצֹאןagainst the sheep מַרְעִיתֶךָyour pasture

A Maskil of Asaph. Why have You rejected us forever, O God? Why does Your anger smolder against the sheep of Your pasture?

74:2

זְכֹרremember עֲדָתְךָcongregation which you קָנִיתָpurchased קֶּדֶםlong ago גָּאַלְתָּredeemed שֵׁבֶטtribe נַחֲלָתֶךָyour inheritance הַרMount צִיּוֹןZion זֶהyou שָׁכַנְתָּdwell בּוֹin him

Remember Your congregation, which You purchased long ago and redeemed as the tribe of Your inheritance— Mount Zion where You dwell.

74:3

הָרִימָהturn פְעָמֶיךָyour steps לְמַשֻּׁאוֹתto the everlasting ruins נֶצַחeverlasting כָּלeverything הֵרַעdestroyed אוֹיֵבenemy בַּקֹּדֶשׁin the sanctuary

Turn Your steps to the everlasting ruins, to everything in the sanctuary the enemy has destroyed.

74:4

שָׁאֲגוּroared צֹרְרֶיךָyour foes בְּקֶרֶבwithin מוֹעֲדֶךָyour meeting שָׂמוּplace אוֹתֹתָםunfurled their banners אֹתוֹתsigns

Your foes have roared within Your meeting place; they have unfurled their banners as signs,

74:5

יִוָּדַעmen כְּמֵבִיאlike bring לְמָעְלָהto upward toward בִּסֲבָךְin a thicket עֵץtrees קַרְדֻּמּוֹתaxes

like men wielding axes in a thicket of trees

74:6

וְעַתָּהand now פִּתּוּחֶיהָsmashing all the יָּחַדtogether בְּכַשִּׁילwith hatchets וְכֵילַפֹּתhatchets and יַהֲלֹמוּןpicks

and smashing all the carvings with hatchets and picks.

74:7

שִׁלְחוּsent בָאֵשׁthey have burned מִקְדָּשֶׁךָyour sanctuary לָאָרֶץto the ground חִלְּלוּdefiled מִשְׁכַּןdwelling שְׁמֶךָyour name

They have burned Your sanctuary to the ground; they have defiled the dwelling place of Your Name.

74:8

אָמְרוּsaid בְלִבָּםin their hearts נִינָםcrush them יָחַדcompletely שָׂרְפוּburned כָלevery מוֹעֲדֵיmet אֵלGod בָּאָרֶץin the land

They said in their hearts, “We will crush them completely.” They burned down every place where God met us in the land.

74:9

אוֹתֹתֵינוּsigns for us לֹאno רָאִינוּsee אֵיןno עוֹדlonger נָבִיאprophet וְלֹאand not אִתָּנוּwith us יֹדֵעַknows עַדuntil מָהhow

There are no signs for us to see. There is no longer any prophet. And none of us knows how long this will last.

74:10

עַדhow מָתַיlong אֱלֹהִיםGod יְחָרֶףtaunt צָרfoe יְנָאֵץrevile אוֹיֵבenemy שִׁמְךָyour name לָנֶצַחforever

How long, O God, will the enemy taunt You? Will the foe revile Your name forever?

74:11

לָמָּהto why תָשִׁיבwithdraw יָדְךָhand וִימִינֶךָyour strong right מִקֶּרֶבfrom among חֵיקְךָstretch כַלֵּהdestroy

Why do You withdraw Your strong right hand? Stretch it out to destroy them!

74:12

וֵאלֹהִיםyet God מַלְכִּיmy king מִקֶּדֶםfrom ancient פֹּעֵלworking יְשׁוּעוֹתsalvation בְּקֶרֶבin among הָאָרֶץthe earth

Yet God is my King from ancient times, working salvation on the earth.

74:13

אַתָּהyou פוֹרַרְתָּdivided בְעָזְּךָby your strength you יָםsea שִׁבַּרְתָּsmashed רָאשֵׁיheads תַנִּינִיםdragons עַלon הַמָּיִםthe sea

You divided the sea by Your strength; You smashed the heads of the dragons of the sea;

74:14

אַתָּהyou רִצַּצְתָּcrushed רָאשֵׁיheads לִוְיָתָןLeviathan תִּתְּנֶנּוּyou him מַאֲכָלfed לְעָםto people לְצִיִּיםdesert

You crushed the heads of Leviathan; You fed him to the creatures of the desert.

74:15

אַתָּהyou בָקַעְתָּbroke open מַעְיָןfountain וָנָחַלand the flood אַתָּהyou הוֹבַשְׁתָּdried נַהֲרוֹתrivers אֵיתָןflowing

You broke open the fountain and the flood; You dried up the ever-flowing rivers.

74:16

לְךָto you יוֹםday אַףalso לְךָto you לָיְלָהnight אַתָּהyou הֲכִינוֹתָestablished מָאוֹרmoon וָשָׁמֶשׁand the sun

The day is Yours, and also the night; You established the moon and the sun.

74:17

אַתָּהyou הִצַּבְתָּset כָּלall גְּבוּלוֹתboundaries אָרֶץearth קַיִץsummer וָחֹרֶףand winter אַתָּהyou יְצַרְתָּםmade

You set all the boundaries of the earth; You made the summer and winter.

74:18

זְכָרremember זֹאתthis אוֹיֵבenemy חֵרֵףmocked יְהוָהLORD וְעַםpeople נָבָלfoolish נִאֲצוּhas שְׁמֶךָyour name

Remember how the enemy has mocked You, O LORD, how a foolish people has spurned Your name.

74:19

אַלdo not תִּתֵּןdeliver לְחַיַּתto beasts נֶפֶשׁsoul תּוֹרֶךָyour dove חַיַּתlives עֲנִיֶּיךָyour afflicted אַלdo not תִּשְׁכַּחforget לָנֶצַחforever

Do not deliver the soul of Your dove to beasts; do not forget the lives of Your afflicted forever.

74:20

הַבֵּטconsider לַבְּרִיתyour covenant כִּיfor מָלְאוּfill מַחֲשַׁכֵּיdark אֶרֶץland נְאוֹתhaunts חָמָסviolence

Consider Your covenant, for haunts of violence fill the dark places of the land.

74:21

אַלnot יָשֹׁבretreat דַּךְoppressed נִכְלָםshame עָנִיpoor וְאֶבְיוֹןand needy יְהַלְלוּpraise שְׁמֶךָyour name

Do not let the oppressed retreat in shame; may the poor and needy praise Your name.

74:22

קוּמָהrise אֱלֹהִיםGod רִיבָהdefend רִיבֶךָyour cause זְכֹרremember חֶרְפָּתְךָmocks you מִנִּיfrom נָבָלfool כָּלall הַיּוֹםday

Rise up, O God; defend Your cause! Remember how the fool mocks You all day long.

74:23

אַלdo not תִּשְׁכַּחdisregard קוֹלclamor צֹרְרֶיךָyour adversaries שְׁאוֹןuproar קָמֶיךָyour enemies עֹלֶהascends תָמִידcontinually

Do not disregard the clamor of Your adversaries, the uproar of Your enemies that ascends continually.
