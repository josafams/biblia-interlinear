Psalms 35

A plea for defense against ungrateful accusers

Open in the interlinear reader

35:1

לְדָוִדof David רִיבָהcontend יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] יְרִיבַיmy opponents לְחַםfight אֶת[obj] לֹחֲמָיfight against me

Of David. Contend with my opponents, O LORD; fight against those who fight against me.

35:2

הַחֲזֵקyour מָגֵןshield וְצִנָּהand buckler וְקוּמָהarise and come בְּעֶזְרָתִיto my aid

Take up Your shield and buckler; arise and come to my aid.

35:3

וְהָרֵקdraw חֲנִיתspear וּסְגֹרand javelin לִקְרַאתagainst רֹדְפָיmy pursuers אֱמֹרsay לְנַפְשִׁיto my soul יְשֻׁעָתֵךְyour salvation אָנִיam

Draw the spear and javelin against my pursuers; say to my soul: “I am your salvation.”

35:4

יֵבֹשׁוּdisgraced put וְיִכָּלְמוּdisgraced and מְבַקְשֵׁיseek נַפְשִׁיmy life יִסֹּגוּdriven אָחוֹרback וְיַחְפְּרוּand confounded חֹשְׁבֵיplan רָעָתִיharm me

May those who seek my life be disgraced and put to shame; may those who plan to harm me be driven back and confounded.

35:5

יִהְיוּbe כְּמֹץlike chaff לִפְנֵיto before רוּחַwind וּמַלְאַךְas the angel יְהוָהLORD דּוֹחֶהdrives

May they be like chaff in the wind, as the angel of the LORD drives them away.

35:6

יְהִיmay דַרְכָּםtheir path חֹשֶׁךְdark וַחֲלַקְלַקּוֹתand slick וּמַלְאַךְas the angel יְהוָהLORD רֹדְפָםpursues

May their path be dark and slick, as the angel of the LORD pursues.

35:7

כִּיfor חִנָּםwithout cause טָמְנוּlaid לִיfor me שַׁחַתpit רִשְׁתָּםtheir net חִנָּםwithout reason חָפְרוּdug לְנַפְשִׁיfor my soul

For without cause they laid their net for me; without reason they dug a pit for my soul.

35:8

תְּבוֹאֵהוּmay שׁוֹאָהruin לֹאnot יֵדָעsurprise וְרִשְׁתּוֹmay the net אֲשֶׁרthat טָמַןhid תִּלְכְּדוֹensnare them בְּשׁוֹאָהmay יִפָּלfall בָּהּin her

May ruin befall them by surprise; may the net they hid ensnare them; may they fall into the hazard they created.

35:9

וְנַפְשִׁיthen my soul תָּגִילrejoice בַּיהוָהin the LORD תָּשִׂישׂexult בִּישׁוּעָתוֹin his salvation

Then my soul will rejoice in the LORD and exult in His salvation.

35:10

כָּלall עַצְמוֹתַיmy bones תֹּאמַרְנָהexclaim יְהוָהLORD מִיwho כָמוֹךָlike you מַצִּילdelivers עָנִיafflicted מֵחָזָקfrom the aggressor מִמֶּנּוּfrom us וְעָנִיpoor וְאֶבְיוֹןand needy מִגֹּזְלוֹfrom the robber

All my bones will exclaim, “Who is like You, O LORD, who delivers the afflicted from the aggressor, the poor and needy from the robber?”

35:11

יְקוּמוּןhostile עֵדֵיwitnesses חָמָסforward אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot יָדַעְתִּיknow יִשְׁאָלוּנִיcharges I

Hostile witnesses come forward; they make charges I know nothing about.

35:12

יְשַׁלְּמוּנִיrepay me רָעָהevil תַּחַתfor טוֹבָהgood שְׁכוֹלbereavement לְנַפְשִׁיof my soul

They repay me evil for good, to the bereavement of my soul.

35:13

וַאֲנִיI בַּחֲלוֹתָםwhen they were ill לְבוּשִׁיill שָׂקsackcloth עִנֵּיתִיhumbled בַצּוֹםwith fasting נַפְשִׁיbut וּתְפִלָּתִיbut my prayers עַלon חֵיקִיunanswered תָשׁוּבreturned

Yet when they were ill, I put on sackcloth; I humbled myself with fasting, but my prayers returned unanswered.

35:14

כְּרֵעַfriend כְּאָחor brother לִיfor my הִתְהַלָּכְתִּיwalked כַּאֲבֶלgrief like one mourning אֵםmother קֹדֵרhis שַׁחוֹתִיbowed

I paced about as for my friend or brother; I was bowed down with grief, like one mourning for his mother.

35:15

וּבְצַלְעִיbut when I stumbled שָׂמְחוּstumbled וְנֶאֱסָפוּthey assembled נֶאֶסְפוּtogether עָלַיagainst me נֵכִיםassailants וְלֹאI did not יָדַעְתִּיknow קָרְעוּslandered וְלֹאand not דָמּוּceasing

But when I stumbled, they assembled in glee; they gathered together against me. Assailants I did not know slandered me without ceasing.

35:16

בְּחַנְפֵיlike godless לַעֲגֵיjesters מָעוֹגfeast חָרֹקteeth עָלַיon me שִׁנֵּימוֹtheir teeth

Like godless jesters at a feast, they gnashed their teeth at me.

35:17

אֲדֹנָיLord will כַּמָּהlike how תִּרְאֶהlook הָשִׁיבָהrescue נַפְשִׁיmy soul מִשֹּׁאֵיהֶםlife מִכְּפִירִיםfrom these lions יְחִידָתִיravages my precious

How long, O Lord, will You look on? Rescue my soul from their ravages, my precious life from these lions.

35:18

אוֹדְךָgive you thanks בְּקָהָלassembly רָבgreat בְּעַםamong people עָצוּםmany אֲהַלְלֶךָּpraise you

Then I will give You thanks in the great assembly; I will praise You among many people.

35:19

אַלnot יִשְׂמְחוּgloat לִיto me אֹיְבַיenemies שֶׁקֶרwithout שֹׂנְאַיhate me חִנָּםwithout יִקְרְצוּreason wink עָיִןmalice

Let not my enemies gloat over me without cause, nor those who hate me without reason wink in malice.

35:20

כִּיfor לֹאnot שָׁלוֹםpeace יְדַבֵּרוּspeak וְעַלagainst רִגְעֵיschemes אֶרֶץland דִּבְרֵיwords מִרְמוֹתdeceitful יַחֲשֹׁבוּןdevise

For they do not speak peace, but they devise deceitful schemes against those who live quietly in the land.

35:21

וַיַּרְחִיבוּthey gape עָלַיon me פִּיהֶםtheir mouth אָמְרוּsay הֶאָחaha הֶאָחaha רָאֲתָהseen עֵינֵינוּour eyes

They gape at me and say, “Aha, aha! Our eyes have seen!”

35:22

רָאִיתָהseen יְהוָהLORD אַלnot תֶּחֱרַשׁsilent אֲדֹנָיLord אֲלnot תִּרְחַקfar מִמֶּנִּיfrom me

O LORD, You have seen it; be not silent. O Lord, be not far from me.

35:23

הָעִירָהawake וְהָקִיצָהawake and rise לְמִשְׁפָּטִיto my justice אֱלֹהַיGod וַאדֹנָיand my Lord לְרִיבִיmy cause

Awake and rise to my defense, to my cause, my God and my Lord!

35:24

שָׁפְטֵנִיvindicate me כְצִדְקְךָby your righteousness יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהָיmy God וְאַלand do not יִשְׂמְחוּgloat לִיto me

Vindicate me by Your righteousness, O LORD my God, and do not let them gloat over me.

35:25

אַלnot יֹאמְרוּsay בְלִבָּםin their hearts הֶאָחaha נַפְשֵׁנוּjust what we wanted אַלnot יֹאמְרוּsay בִּלַּעֲנוּהוּswallowed him

Let them not say in their hearts, “Aha, just what we wanted!” Let them not say, “We have swallowed him up!”

35:26

יֵבֹשׁוּashamed וְיַחְפְּרוּand confounded יַחְדָּוmay שְׂמֵחֵיgloat רָעָתִיmy distress יִלְבְּשׁוּclothed בֹשֶׁתshame וּכְלִמָּהand reproach הַמַּגְדִּילִיםthose who exalt עָלָיover me

May those who gloat in my distress be ashamed and confounded; may those who exalt themselves over me be clothed in shame and reproach.

35:27

יָרֹנּוּjoy וְיִשְׂמְחוּjoy and חֲפֵצֵיfavor צִדְקִיfavor my vindication וְיֹאמְרוּthey say תָמִידalways יִגְדַּלexalted יְהוָהLORD הֶחָפֵץwho delights שְׁלוֹםwell עַבְדּוֹhis servant’s

May those who favor my vindication shout for joy and gladness; may they always say, “Exalted be the LORD who delights in His servant’s well-being.”

35:28

וּלְשׁוֹנִיthen my tongue תֶּהְגֶּהproclaim צִדְקֶךָyour righteousness כָּלall הַיּוֹםday תְּהִלָּתֶךָyour praises

Then my tongue will proclaim Your righteousness and Your praises all day long.
