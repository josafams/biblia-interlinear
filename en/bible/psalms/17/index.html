Psalms 17

The appeal of an innocent man pursued

Open in the interlinear reader

17:1

תְּפִלָּהprayer לְדָוִדof David שִׁמְעָהhear יְהוָהLORD צֶדֶקrighteous הַקְשִׁיבָהlisten רִנָּתִיmy cry הַאֲזִינָהgive ear תְפִלָּתִיmy prayer בְּלֹאin without שִׂפְתֵיlips מִרְמָהdeceit

A prayer of David. Hear, O LORD, my righteous plea; listen to my cry. Give ear to my prayer— it comes from lips free of deceit.

17:2

מִלְּפָנֶיךָyour presence מִשְׁפָּטִיmy vindication יֵצֵאcome עֵינֶיךָyour eyes תֶּחֱזֶינָהsee מֵישָׁרִיםright

May my vindication come from Your presence; may Your eyes see what is right.

17:3

בָּחַנְתָּtried לִבִּיheart פָּקַדְתָּvisited לַּיְלָהnight צְרַפְתַּנִיtested me בַלno תִּמְצָאfound זַמֹּתִיevil בַּלnot יַעֲבָרsin פִּיmy mouth

You have tried my heart; You have visited me in the night. You have tested me and found no evil; I have resolved not to sin with my mouth.

17:4

לִפְעֻלּוֹתfor the deeds אָדָםmen בִּדְבַרby the word שְׂפָתֶיךָyour lips אֲנִיI שָׁמַרְתִּיhave avoided אָרְחוֹתways פָּרִיץviolent

As for the deeds of men— by the word of Your lips I have avoided the ways of the violent.

17:5

תָּמֹךְheld אֲשֻׁרַיmy steps בְּמַעְגְּלוֹתֶיךָto your paths בַּלnot נָמוֹטּוּslipped פְעָמָיmy feet

My steps have held to Your paths; my feet have not slipped.

17:6

אֲנִיI קְרָאתִיךָcall on you O כִיfor תַעֲנֵנִיanswer me אֵלGod הַטincline אָזְנְךָyour ear לִיto me שְׁמַעhear אִמְרָתִיmy words

I call on You, O God, for You will answer me. Incline Your ear to me; hear my words.

17:7

הַפְלֵהwonders חֲסָדֶיךָloving devotion you מוֹשִׁיעַsave חוֹסִיםseek refuge מִמִּתְקוֹמְמִיםfrom their foes בִּימִינֶךָby your right hand

Show the wonders of Your loving devotion, You who save by Your right hand those who seek refuge from their foes.

17:8

שָׁמְרֵנִיkeep me כְּאִישׁוֹןas the apple בַּתdaughter עָיִןeye בְּצֵלin the shadow כְּנָפֶיךָyour wings תַּסְתִּירֵנִיhide me

Keep me as the apple of Your eye; hide me in the shadow of Your wings

17:9

מִפְּנֵיfrom before רְשָׁעִיםwicked זוּassail שַׁדּוּנִיassail me אֹיְבַיmy mortal enemies בְּנֶפֶשׁwho יַקִּיפוּsurround עָלָיon me

from the wicked who assail me, from my mortal enemies who surround me.

17:10

חֶלְבָּמוֹtheir callous hearts סָּגְרוּclosed פִּימוֹtheir mouths דִּבְּרוּspeak בְגֵאוּתwith arrogance

They have closed their callous hearts; their mouths speak with arrogance.

17:11

אַשֻּׁרֵינוּtracked us עַתָּהnow סְבָבוּנוּsurround us עֵינֵיהֶםtheir eyes יָשִׁיתוּset לִנְטוֹתto cast בָּאָרֶץto the ground

They have tracked us down, and now surround us; their eyes are set to cast us to the ground,

17:12

דִּמְיֹנוֹlike כְּאַרְיֵהlike a lion יִכְסוֹףgreedy לִטְרוֹףfor prey וְכִכְפִירlike a young lion יֹשֵׁבlurking בְּמִסְתָּרִיםin ambush

like a lion greedy for prey, like a young lion lurking in ambush.

17:13

קוּמָהarise יְהוָהLORD קַדְּמָהconfront פָנָיוhis face הַכְרִיעֵהוּknees פַּלְּטָהdeliver נַפְשִׁיmy soul מֵרָשָׁעfrom the wicked חַרְבֶּךָyour sword

Arise, O LORD, confront them! Bring them to their knees; deliver me from the wicked by Your sword,

17:14

מִמְתִיםfrom such men יָדְךָyour hand יְהוָהLORD מִמְתִיםfrom men מֵחֶלֶדof the world חֶלְקָםportion בַּחַיִּיםin this life וּצְפוּנְךָyour treasured תְּמַלֵּאfill בִטְנָםbellies יִשְׂבְּעוּsatisfy בָנִיםsons וְהִנִּיחוּso they leave יִתְרָםtheir abundance לְעוֹלְלֵיהֶםto their children

from such men, O LORD, by Your hand— from men of the world whose portion is in this life. May You fill the bellies of Your treasured ones and satisfy their sons, so they leave their abundance to their children.

17:15

אֲנִיI בְּצֶדֶקin righteousness אֶחֱזֶהbehold פָנֶיךָyour face אֶשְׂבְּעָהsatisfied בְהָקִיץawake תְּמוּנָתֶךָyour presence

As for me, I will behold Your face in righteousness; when I awake, I will be satisfied in Your presence.
