Psalms 88

Lament without comfort in the face of death

Open in the interlinear reader

A song. A Psalm of the sons of Korah. For the choirmaster. According to Mahalath Leannoth. A Maskil of Heman the Ezrahite

88:1

יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod יְשׁוּעָתִיmy salvation יוֹםday צָעַקְתִּיcry out בַלַּיְלָהand night נֶגְדֶּךָbefore you

O LORD, the God of my salvation, day and night I cry out before You.

88:2

תָּבוֹאcome לְפָנֶיךָbefore you תְּפִלָּתִיmy prayer הַטֵּהincline אָזְנְךָyour ear לְרִנָּתִיto my cry

May my prayer come before You; incline Your ear to my cry.

88:3

כִּיfor שָׂבְעָהfull בְרָעוֹתtroubles נַפְשִׁיmy soul וְחַיַּיand my life לִשְׁאוֹלto Sheol הִגִּיעוּnear

For my soul is full of troubles, and my life draws near to Sheol.

88:4

נֶחְשַׁבְתִּיcounted עִםamong יוֹרְדֵיdescending בוֹרpit הָיִיתִיam כְּגֶבֶרlike a man אֵיןwithout אֱיָלstrength

I am counted among those descending to the Pit. I am like a man without strength.

88:5

בַּמֵּתִיםamong the dead חָפְשִׁיforsaken כְּמוֹlike חֲלָלִיםslain שֹׁכְבֵיlie קֶבֶרgrave אֲשֶׁרwhom לֹאno זְכַרְתָּםremember עוֹדmore וְהֵמָּהwho are מִיָּדְךָfrom your care נִגְזָרוּcut

I am forsaken among the dead, like the slain who lie in the grave, whom You remember no more, who are cut off from Your care.

88:6

שַׁתַּנִיlaid me בְּבוֹרpit תַּחְתִּיּוֹתlowest בְּמַחֲשַׁכִּיםin the darkest בִּמְצֹלוֹתdepths

You have laid me in the lowest Pit, in the darkest of the depths.

88:7

עָלַיupon me סָמְכָהweighs heavily חֲמָתֶךָyour wrath וְכָלall מִשְׁבָּרֶיךָyour waves עִנִּיתָsubmerged סֶּלָהSelah

Your wrath weighs heavily upon me; all Your waves have submerged me. Selah

88:8

הִרְחַקְתָּremoved מְיֻדָּעַיmy friends מִמֶּנִּיfrom me שַׁתַּנִיmade me תוֹעֵבוֹתrepulsive לָמוֹto them כָּלֻאconfined וְלֹאand not אֵצֵאescape

You have removed my friends from me; You have made me repulsive to them; I am confined and cannot escape.

88:9

עֵינִיmy eyes דָאֲבָהdim מִנִּיfrom עֹנִיgrief קְרָאתִיךָcall יְהוָהLORD בְּכָלin all יוֹםdaily שִׁטַּחְתִּיspread out אֵלֶיךָto you כַפָּיmy hands

My eyes grow dim with grief. I call to You daily, O LORD; I spread out my hands to You.

88:10

הֲלַמֵּתִיםfor the dead תַּעֲשֶׂהwork פֶּלֶאwonders אִםif רְפָאִיםdeparted spirits יָקוּמוּrise יוֹדוּךָpraise you סֶּלָהSelah

Do You work wonders for the dead? Do departed spirits rise up to praise You? Selah

88:11

הַיְסֻפַּרproclaimed בַּקֶּבֶרin the grave חַסְדֶּךָyour loving devotion אֱמוּנָתְךָyour faithfulness בָּאֲבַדּוֹןin Abaddon

Can Your loving devotion be proclaimed in the grave, Your faithfulness in Abaddon?

88:12

הֲיִוָּדַעknown בַּחֹשֶׁךְin the darkness פִּלְאֶךָyour wonders וְצִדְקָתְךָor your righteousness בְּאֶרֶץin the land נְשִׁיָּהoblivion

Will Your wonders be known in the darkness, or Your righteousness in the land of oblivion?

88:13

וַאֲנִיI אֵלֶיךָto you יְהוָהLORD שִׁוַּעְתִּיcry help וּבַבֹּקֶרfor in the morning תְּפִלָּתִיmy prayer תְקַדְּמֶךָּbefore you

But to You, O LORD, I cry for help; in the morning my prayer comes before You.

88:14

לָמָהwhy יְהוָהLORD תִּזְנַחreject נַפְשִׁיyou me תַּסְתִּירhide פָּנֶיךָyour face מִמֶּנִּיfrom me

Why, O LORD, do You reject me? Why do You hide Your face from me?

88:15

עָנִיafflicted אֲנִיI וְגֹוֵעַand near death מִנֹּעַרfrom my youth נָשָׂאתִיswore אֵמֶיךָyour terrors אָפוּנָהdespair

From my youth I was afflicted and near death. I have borne Your terrors; I am in despair.

88:16

עָלַיover me עָבְרוּswept חֲרוֹנֶיךָyour wrath בִּעוּתֶיךָyour terrors צִמְּתוּתֻנִיdestroyed me

Your wrath has swept over me; Your terrors have destroyed me.

88:17

סַבּוּנִיthey engulf me כַמַּיִםlike water כָּלall הַיּוֹםday long הִקִּיפוּenclose עָלַיme on every יָחַדtogether

All day long they engulf me like water; they enclose me on every side.

88:18

הִרְחַקְתָּremoved מִמֶּנִּיfrom me אֹהֵבbeloved וָרֵעַand friend מְיֻדָּעַיclosest מַחְשָׁךְdarkness

You have removed my beloved and my friend; darkness is my closest companion.
