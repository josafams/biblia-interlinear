Psalms 76

God victorious in Zion

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. With stringed instruments. A Psalm of Asaph. A song

76:1

נוֹדָעknown בִּיהוּדָהin Judah אֱלֹהִיםGod בְּיִשְׂרָאֵלin Israel גָּדוֹלgreat שְׁמוֹhis name

God is known in Judah; His name is great in Israel.

76:2

וַיְהִיand was בְשָׁלֵםin Salem סֻכּוֹtent וּמְעוֹנָתוֹdwelling בְצִיּוֹןin Zion

His tent is in Salem, His dwelling place in Zion.

76:3

שָׁמָּהthere שִׁבַּרshattered רִשְׁפֵיflaming קָשֶׁתarrows מָגֵןshield וְחֶרֶבand sword וּמִלְחָמָהand weapons of war סֶלָהSelah

There He shattered the flaming arrows, the shield and sword and weapons of war. Selah

76:4

נָאוֹרlight אַתָּהyou אַדִּירmajestic מֵהַרְרֵיthan mountains טָרֶףgame

You are resplendent with light, more majestic than mountains filled with game.

76:5

אֶשְׁתּוֹלְלוּvaliant אַבִּירֵיplundered לֵבheart נָמוּlast שְׁנָתָםsleep their וְלֹאand not מָצְאוּfind כָלno אַנְשֵׁיmen חַיִלmight יְדֵיהֶםlift a hand

The valiant lie plundered; they sleep their last sleep. No men of might could lift a hand.

76:6

מִגַּעֲרָתְךָat your rebuke אֱלֹהֵיGod יַעֲקֹבJacob נִרְדָּםboth וְרֶכֶבand rider lie stunned וָסוּסhorse

At Your rebuke, O God of Jacob, both horse and rider lie stunned.

76:7

אַתָּהyou נוֹרָאfeared אַתָּהyou וּמִיwho יַעֲמֹדstand לְפָנֶיךָbefore מֵאָזfrom time אַפֶּךָangry

You alone are to be feared. When You are angry, who can stand before You?

76:8

מִשָּׁמַיִםfrom heaven הִשְׁמַעְתָּpronounced דִּיןjudgment אֶרֶץearth יָרְאָהfeared וְשָׁקָטָהand was still

From heaven You pronounced judgment, and the earth feared and was still

76:9

בְּקוּםwhen rose לַמִּשְׁפָּטto judge אֱלֹהִיםGod לְהוֹשִׁיעַto save כָּלall עַנְוֵיlowly אֶרֶץearth סֶלָהSelah

when God rose up to judge, to save all the lowly of the earth. Selah

76:10

כִּיfor חֲמַתwrath אָדָםman תּוֹדֶךָּpraise you שְׁאֵרִיתsurvivors חֵמֹתwrath תַּחְגֹּרclothe

Even the wrath of man shall praise You; with the survivors of wrath You will clothe Yourself.

76:11

נִדֲרוּmake וְשַׁלְּמוּand fulfill לַיהוָהto the LORD אֱלֹהֵיכֶםyour God כָּלall סְבִיבָיוneighboring יוֹבִילוּlands bring שַׁיtribute לַמּוֹרָאfeared

Make and fulfill your vows to the LORD your God; let all the neighboring lands bring tribute to Him who is to be feared.

76:12

יִבְצֹרbreaks רוּחַspirits נְגִידִיםprinces נוֹרָאfeared לְמַלְכֵיby the kings אָרֶץearth

He breaks the spirits of princes; He is feared by the kings of the earth.
