Psalms 23

The LORD as shepherd and host

Open in the interlinear reader

23:1

מִזְמוֹרPsalm לְדָוִדof David יְהוָהLORD רֹעִיmy shepherd לֹאnot אֶחְסָרwant

A Psalm of David. The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want.

23:2

בִּנְאוֹתin pastures דֶּשֶׁאgreen יַרְבִּיצֵנִיme lie down עַלon מֵיwaters מְנֻחוֹתquiet יְנַהֲלֵנִיleads me

He makes me lie down in green pastures; He leads me beside quiet waters.

23:3

נַפְשִׁיmy soul יְשׁוֹבֵבrestores יַנְחֵנִיguides me בְמַעְגְּלֵיin the paths צֶדֶקrighteousness לְמַעַןsake שְׁמוֹhis name

He restores my soul; He guides me in the paths of righteousness for the sake of His name.

23:4

גַּםeven כִּיfor אֵלֵךְwalk בְּגֵיאthrough the valley צַלְמָוֶתshadow לֹאno אִירָאfear רָעevil כִּיfor אַתָּהyou עִמָּדִיwith me שִׁבְטְךָyour rod וּמִשְׁעַנְתֶּךָand your staff הֵמָּהthey יְנַחֲמֻנִיcomfort me

Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil, for You are with me; Your rod and Your staff, they comfort me.

23:5

תַּעֲרֹךְprepare לְפָנַיpresence שֻׁלְחָןtable נֶגֶדbefore צֹרְרָיmy enemies דִּשַּׁנְתָּanoint בַשֶּׁמֶןoil רֹאשִׁיmy head כּוֹסִיmy cup רְוָיָהoverflows

You prepare a table before me in the presence of my enemies. You anoint my head with oil; my cup overflows.

23:6

אַךְsurely טוֹבgoodness וָחֶסֶדand mercy יִרְדְּפוּנִיfollow me כָּלall יְמֵיdays חַיָּיmy life וְשַׁבְתִּיdwell בְּבֵיתin the house יְהוָהLORD לְאֹרֶךְforever יָמִיםforever

Surely goodness and mercy will follow me all the days of my life, and I will dwell in the house of the LORD forever.
