Psalms 9

Praise to the God who judges the nations

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. To the tune of “The Death of the Son.” A Psalm of David

9:1

אוֹדֶהgive thanks יְהוָהLORD בְּכָלwith all לִבִּיmy heart אֲסַפְּרָהrecount כָּלall נִפְלְאוֹתֶיךָyour wonders

I will give thanks to the LORD with all my heart; I will recount all Your wonders.

9:2

אֶשְׂמְחָהglad וְאֶעֶלְצָהand rejoice בָךְin you אֲזַמְּרָהsing praise שִׁמְךָyour name O עֶלְיוֹןMost high

I will be glad and rejoice in You; I will sing praise to Your name, O Most High.

9:3

בְּשׁוּבretreat אוֹיְבַיmy enemies אָחוֹרretreat יִכָּשְׁלוּstumble וְיֹאבְדוּand perish מִפָּנֶיךָbefore you

When my enemies retreat, they stumble and perish before You.

9:4

כִּיfor עָשִׂיתָdone מִשְׁפָּטִיupheld my just וְדִינִיcause you יָשַׁבְתָּsit לְכִסֵּאon your throne שׁוֹפֵטjudging צֶדֶקrighteously

For You have upheld my just cause; You sit on Your throne judging righteously.

9:5

גָּעַרְתָּrebuked גוֹיִםnations אִבַּדְתָּdestroyed רָשָׁעwicked שְׁמָםtheir name מָחִיתָerased לְעוֹלָםforever וָעֶדand ever

You have rebuked the nations; You have destroyed the wicked; You have erased their name forever and ever.

9:6

הָאוֹיֵבthe enemy תַּמּוּcompletely חֳרָבוֹתruin לָנֶצַחcome to eternal וְעָרִיםcities נָתַשְׁתָּuprooted אָבַדtheir זִכְרָםmemory of them הֵמָּהthem

The enemy has come to eternal ruin, and You have uprooted their cities; the very memory of them has vanished.

9:7

וַיהוָהbut the LORD לְעוֹלָםforever יֵשֵׁבabides כּוֹנֵןestablished לַמִּשְׁפָּטfor judgment כִּסְאוֹhis throne

But the LORD abides forever; He has established His throne for judgment.

9:8

וְהוּאhe יִשְׁפֹּטjudges תֵּבֵלworld בְּצֶדֶקwith justice יָדִיןgoverns לְאֻמִּיםpeople בְּמֵישָׁרִיםwith equity

He judges the world with justice; He governs the people with equity.

9:9

וִיהִיand be יְהוָהLORD מִשְׂגָּבrefuge לַדָּךְfor the oppressed מִשְׂגָּבstronghold לְעִתּוֹתin times בַּצָּרָהtrouble

The LORD is a refuge for the oppressed, a stronghold in times of trouble.

9:10

וְיִבְטְחוּtrust בְךָin יוֹדְעֵיknow שְׁמֶךָname כִּיfor לֹאnot עָזַבְתָּforsaken דֹרְשֶׁיךָseek you יְהוָהLORD

Those who know Your name trust in You, for You, O LORD, have not forsaken those who seek You.

9:11

זַמְּרוּsing praises לַיהוָהto the LORD יֹשֵׁבdwells צִיּוֹןZion הַגִּידוּproclaim בָעַמִּיםamong the nations עֲלִילוֹתָיוhis deeds

Sing praises to the LORD, who dwells in Zion; proclaim His deeds among the nations.

9:12

כִּיfor דֹרֵשׁavenger דָּמִיםbloodshed אוֹתָםthem זָכָרremembers לֹאnot שָׁכַחignore צַעֲקַתcry עֲנָוִיםafflicted

For the Avenger of bloodshed remembers; He does not ignore the cry of the afflicted.

9:13

חָנְנֵנִיmerciful to me יְהוָהLORD רְאֵהsee עָנְיִיhow my מִשֹּׂנְאָיenemies afflict me מְרוֹמְמִיlift me מִשַּׁעֲרֵיfrom the gates מָוֶתdeath

Be merciful to me, O LORD; see how my enemies afflict me! Lift me up from the gates of death,

9:14

לְמַעַןmay אֲסַפְּרָהdeclare כָּלall תְּהִלָּתֶיךָyour praises בְּשַׁעֲרֵיwithin the gates בַתdaughter צִיּוֹןZion אָגִילָהrejoice בִּישׁוּעָתֶךָin your salvation

that I may declare all Your praises— that within the gates of Daughter Zion I may rejoice in Your salvation.

9:15

טָבְעוּfallen גוֹיִםnations בְּשַׁחַתinto a pit עָשׂוּmaking בְּרֶשֶׁתin the net זוּhave טָמָנוּhidden נִלְכְּדָהcaught רַגְלָםtheir feet

The nations have fallen into a pit of their making; their feet are caught in the net they have hidden.

9:16

נוֹדַעknown יְהוָהLORD מִשְׁפָּטjustice עָשָׂהhe בְּפֹעַלby the work כַּפָּיוtheir hands נוֹקֵשׁensnared רָשָׁעwicked הִגָּיוֹןhiggaion סֶלָהSelah

The LORD is known by the justice He brings; the wicked are ensnared by the work of their hands. Higgaion Selah

9:17

יָשׁוּבוּreturn רְשָׁעִיםwicked לִשְׁאוֹלָהto Sheol כָּלall גּוֹיִםnations שְׁכֵחֵיforget אֱלֹהִיםGod

The wicked will return to Sheol— all the nations who forget God.

9:18

כִּיfor לֹאnot לָנֶצַחalways יִשָּׁכַחforgotten אֶבְיוֹןneedy תִּקְוַתhope עֲנִיִּיםoppressed תֹּאבַדdashed לָעַדforever

For the needy will not always be forgotten; nor the hope of the oppressed forever dashed.

9:19

קוּמָהrise יְהוָהLORD אַלdo not יָעֹזman אֱנוֹשׁprevail יִשָּׁפְטוּjudged גוֹיִםnations עַלin פָּנֶיךָyour presence

Rise up, O LORD, do not let man prevail; let the nations be judged in Your presence.

9:20

שִׁיתָהlay יְהוָהLORD מוֹרָהterror לָהֶםto them יֵדְעוּknow גוֹיִםnations אֱנוֹשׁmen הֵמָּהare סֶּלָהSelah

Lay terror upon them, O LORD; let the nations know they are but men. Selah
