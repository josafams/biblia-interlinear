Psalms 143

No one is righteous before God

Open in the interlinear reader

143:1

מִזְמוֹרPsalm לְדָוִדof David יְהוָהLORD שְׁמַעhear תְּפִלָּתִיmy prayer הַאֲזִינָהgive ear אֶלto תַּחֲנוּנַיmy plea בֶּאֱמֻנָתְךָin your faithfulness עֲנֵנִיanswer me בְּצִדְקָתֶךָin your righteousness

A Psalm of David. O LORD, hear my prayer. In Your faithfulness, give ear to my plea; in Your righteousness, answer me.

143:2

וְאַלdo not תָּבוֹאbring בְמִשְׁפָּטinto judgment אֶתwith עַבְדֶּךָyour servant כִּיfor לֹאno יִצְדַּקrighteous לְפָנֶיךָbefore you כָלall חָיalive

Do not bring Your servant into judgment, for no one alive is righteous before You.

143:3

כִּיfor רָדַףpursued אוֹיֵבenemy נַפְשִׁיmy soul דִּכָּאcrushing לָאָרֶץto the ground חַיָּתִיmy life הוֹשִׁיבַנִיme dwell בְמַחֲשַׁכִּיםin darkness כְּמֵתֵיdead עוֹלָםlong

For the enemy has pursued my soul, crushing my life to the ground, making me dwell in darkness like those long since dead.

143:4

וַתִּתְעַטֵּףgrows faint עָלַיon me רוּחִיspirit בְּתוֹכִיinside יִשְׁתּוֹמֵםdismayed לִבִּיheart

My spirit grows faint within me; my heart is dismayed inside me.

143:5

זָכַרְתִּיremember יָמִיםdays מִקֶּדֶםof old הָגִיתִיmeditate בְכָלon all פָּעֳלֶךָwork בְּמַעֲשֵׂהworks יָדֶיךָyour hands אֲשׂוֹחֵחַconsider

I remember the days of old; I meditate on all Your works; I consider the work of Your hands.

143:6

פֵּרַשְׂתִּיstretch יָדַיmy hands אֵלֶיךָto you נַפְשִׁיmy soul כְּאֶרֶץland עֲיֵפָהthirsts לְךָfor you סֶלָהSelah

I stretch out my hands to You; my soul thirsts for You like a parched land. Selah

143:7

מַהֵרquickly עֲנֵנִיanswer me יְהוָהLORD כָּלְתָהmy רוּחִיspirit fails אַלdo not תַּסְתֵּרhide פָּנֶיךָyour face מִמֶּנִּיfrom me וְנִמְשַׁלְתִּיlike עִםto יֹרְדֵיdescend בוֹרpit

Answer me quickly, O LORD; my spirit fails. Do not hide Your face from me, or I will be like those who descend to the Pit.

143:8

הַשְׁמִיעֵנִיme hear בַבֹּקֶרin the morning חַסְדֶּךָyour loving devotion כִּיfor בְךָin you בָטָחְתִּיtrust הוֹדִיעֵנִיteach me דֶּרֶךְway זוּI should אֵלֵךְwalk כִּיfor אֵלֶיךָto you נָשָׂאתִיlift נַפְשִׁיmy soul

Let me hear Your loving devotion in the morning, for I have put my trust in You. Teach me the way I should walk, for to You I lift up my soul.

143:9

הַצִּילֵנִיdeliver me מֵאֹיְבַיfrom my enemies יְהוָהLORD אֵלֶיךָto you כִסִּתִיrefuge

Deliver me from my enemies, O LORD; I flee to You for refuge.

143:10

לַמְּדֵנִיteach me לַעֲשׂוֹתto do רְצוֹנֶךָyour delight כִּיfor אַתָּהyou אֱלוֹהָיmy God רוּחֲךָyour spirit טוֹבָהgood תַּנְחֵנִיlead me בְּאֶרֶץon ground מִישׁוֹרlevel

Teach me to do Your will, for You are my God. May Your good Spirit lead me on level ground.

143:11

לְמַעַןsake שִׁמְךָyour name O יְהוָהLORD תְּחַיֵּנִיrevive me בְּצִדְקָתְךָin your righteousness תוֹצִיאbring מִצָּרָהout of trouble נַפְשִׁיmy soul

For the sake of Your name, O LORD, revive me. In Your righteousness, bring my soul out of trouble.

143:12

וּבְחַסְדְּךָand in your loving devotion תַּצְמִיתcut אֹיְבָיmy enemies וְהַאֲבַדְתָּdestroy כָּלall צֹרֲרֵיafflict נַפְשִׁיwho me כִּיfor אֲנִיam עַבְדֶּךָyour servant

And in Your loving devotion, cut off my enemies. Destroy all who afflict me, for I am Your servant.
