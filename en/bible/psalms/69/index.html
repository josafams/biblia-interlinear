Psalms 69

Sinking in waters and insults

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. To the tune of “Lilies.” Of David

69:1

הוֹשִׁיעֵנִיsave me אֱלֹהִיםGod כִּיfor בָאוּcome מַיִםwaters עַדto נָפֶשׁneck

Save me, O God, for the waters are up to my neck.

69:2

טָבַעְתִּיsunk בִּיוֵןinto the miry מְצוּלָהdepths וְאֵיןno מָעֳמָדfooting בָּאתִיcome בְמַעֲמַקֵּיinto deep מַיִםwaters וְשִׁבֹּלֶתwhere the engulfs שְׁטָפָתְנִיflood me

I have sunk into the miry depths, where there is no footing; I have drifted into deep waters, where the flood engulfs me.

69:3

יָגַעְתִּיweary בְקָרְאִיfrom my crying my נִחַרparched גְּרוֹנִיthroat כָּלוּfail עֵינַיeyes מְיַחֵלlooking לֵאלֹהָיfor my God

I am weary from my crying; my throat is parched. My eyes fail, looking for my God.

69:4

רַבּוּmany מִשַּׂעֲרוֹתhairs of רֹאשִׁיmy head שֹׂנְאַיhate me חִנָּםwithout עָצְמוּcause מַצְמִיתַיdestroy me אֹיְבַיmy enemies שֶׁקֶרreason אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot גָזַלְתִּיsteal אָזthen אָשִׁיבrepay

Those who hate me without cause outnumber the hairs of my head; many are those who would destroy me— my enemies for no reason. Though I did not steal, I must repay.

69:5

אֱלֹהִיםGod אַתָּהyou יָדַעְתָּknow לְאִוַּלְתִּיmy folly וְאַשְׁמוֹתַיand my guilt מִמְּךָfrom you לֹאnot נִכְחָדוּhidden

You know my folly, O God, and my guilt is not hidden from You.

69:6

אַלnot יֵבֹשׁוּashamed בִיin me קֹוֶיךָhope in you אֲדֹנָיLord יְהוִהGOD צְבָאוֹתHosts אַלnot יִכָּלְמוּdishonored בִיin me מְבַקְשֶׁיךָseek you אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

May those who hope in You not be ashamed through me, O Lord GOD of Hosts; may those who seek You not be dishonored through me, O God of Israel.

69:7

כִּיfor עָלֶיךָfor נָשָׂאתִיendured חֶרְפָּהscorn כִּסְּתָהcovered כְלִמָּהshame פָנָיmy face

For I have endured scorn for Your sake, and shame has covered my face.

69:8

מוּזָרstranger הָיִיתִיbecome לְאֶחָיto my brothers וְנָכְרִיand a foreigner לִבְנֵיsons אִמִּיmy mother’s

I have become a stranger to my brothers and a foreigner to my mother’s sons,

69:9

כִּיbecause קִנְאַתzeal בֵּיתְךָyour house אֲכָלָתְנִיconsumed me וְחֶרְפּוֹתand the insults חוֹרְפֶיךָinsult you נָפְלוּfallen עָלָיon me

because zeal for Your house has consumed me, and the insults of those who insult You have fallen on me.

69:10

וָאֶבְכֶּהI wept and בַצּוֹםand fasted נַפְשִׁיbut וַתְּהִיand was לַחֲרָפוֹתreproach לִיto me

I wept and fasted, but it brought me reproach.

69:11

וָאֶתְּנָהI made לְבוּשִׁיmy clothing שָׂקsackcloth וָאֱהִיand I was לָהֶםto them לְמָשָׁלsport to

I made sackcloth my clothing, and I was sport to them.

69:12

יָשִׂיחוּthose בִיin me יֹשְׁבֵיsit שָׁעַרgate וּנְגִינוֹתsong שׁוֹתֵיdrunkards שֵׁכָרstrong drink

Those who sit at the gate mock me, and I am the song of drunkards.

69:13

וַאֲנִיand I תְפִלָּתִיmy prayer לְךָto you יְהוָהLORD עֵתtime רָצוֹןfavor אֱלֹהִיםGod בְּרָבin your abundant חַסְדֶּךָloving devotion O עֲנֵנִיanswer me בֶּאֱמֶתwith sure יִשְׁעֶךָyour salvation

But my prayer to You, O LORD, is for a time of favor. In Your abundant loving devotion, O God, answer me with Your sure salvation.

69:14

הַצִּילֵנִיrescue me מִטִּיטfrom the mire וְאַלand do not אֶטְבָּעָהsink אִנָּצְלָהdeliver מִשֹּׂנְאַיfrom my foes וּמִמַּעֲמַקֵּיand out of the deep מָיִםwaters

Rescue me from the mire and do not let me sink; deliver me from my foes and out of the deep waters.

69:15

אַלdo not תִּשְׁטְפֵנִיfloods engulf me שִׁבֹּלֶתthe engulf מַיִםfloods וְאַלor תִּבְלָעֵנִיswallow me מְצוּלָהdepths וְאַלlet not תֶּאְטַרpit עָלַיover me בְּאֵרclose פִּיהָits mouth

Do not let the floods engulf me or the depths swallow me up; let not the Pit close its mouth over me.

69:16

עֲנֵנִיanswer me יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor טוֹבgood חַסְדֶּךָyour loving devotion כְּרֹבgreat רַחֲמֶיךָyour compassion פְּנֵהturn אֵלָיto me

Answer me, O LORD, for Your loving devotion is good; turn to me in keeping with Your great compassion.

69:17

וְאַלnot תַּסְתֵּרhide פָּנֶיךָyour face מֵעַבְדֶּךָfrom your servant כִּיfor צַרdistress לִיto me מַהֵרquickly עֲנֵנִיanswer me

Hide not Your face from Your servant, for I am in distress. Answer me quickly!

69:18

קָרְבָהnear אֶלto נַפְשִׁיmy soul גְאָלָהּand redeem לְמַעַןbecause אֹיְבַיfoes פְּדֵנִיredeem ransom me

Draw near to my soul and redeem me; ransom me because of my foes.

69:19

אַתָּהyou יָדַעְתָּknow חֶרְפָּתִיmy reproach וּבָשְׁתִּיmy shame וּכְלִמָּתִיand disgrace נֶגְדְּךָbefore you כָּלall צוֹרְרָיmy adversaries

You know my reproach, my shame and disgrace. All my adversaries are before You.

69:20

חֶרְפָּהinsults שָׁבְרָהbroken לִבִּיmy heart וָאָנוּשָׁהdespair וָאֲקַוֶּהI looked לָנוּדfor sympathy וָאַיִןnone וְלַמְנַחֲמִיםfor comforters וְלֹאno מָצָאתִיfound

Insults have broken my heart, and I am in despair. I looked for sympathy, but there was none, for comforters, but I found no one.

69:21

וַיִּתְּנוּand gave בְּבָרוּתִיpoisoned my food with רֹאשׁgall וְלִצְמָאִיto quench my thirst יַשְׁקוּנִיgave me חֹמֶץvinegar

They poisoned my food with gall and gave me vinegar to quench my thirst.

69:22

יְהִיbecome שֻׁלְחָנָםtheir table לִפְנֵיהֶםto their before לְפָחsnare וְלִשְׁלוֹמִיםa retribution לְמוֹקֵשׁand a trap

May their table become a snare; may it be a retribution and a trap.

69:23

תֶּחְשַׁכְנָהdarkened עֵינֵיהֶםtheir eyes מֵרְאוֹתcannot see וּמָתְנֵיהֶםand their backs תָּמִידforever הַמְעַדbent forever

May their eyes be darkened so they cannot see, and their backs be bent forever.

69:24

שְׁפָךְpour עֲלֵיהֶםupon them זַעְמֶךָyour wrath וַחֲרוֹןburning אַפְּךָyour anger יַשִּׂיגֵםovertake them

Pour out Your wrath upon them, and let Your burning anger overtake them.

69:25

תְּהִיmay טִירָתָםtheir place נְשַׁמָּהdeserted בְּאָהֳלֵיהֶםin their tents אַלno יְהִיbe יֹשֵׁבdwell

May their place be deserted; let there be no one to dwell in their tents.

69:26

כִּיfor אַתָּהyou אֲשֶׁרthat הִכִּיתָstruck רָדָפוּpersecute וְאֶלand to מַכְאוֹבpain חֲלָלֶיךָyou wounded יְסַפֵּרוּrecount

For they persecute the one You struck and recount the pain of those You wounded.

69:27

תְּנָהadd עָוֺןiniquity עַלto עֲוֺנָםtheir iniquity וְאַלlet them not יָבֹאוּshare בְּצִדְקָתֶךָin your righteousness

Add iniquity to their iniquity; let them not share in Your righteousness.

69:28

יִמָּחוּblotted מִסֵּפֶרBook חַיִּיםlife וְעִםwith צַדִּיקִיםrighteous אַלnot יִכָּתֵבוּlisted

May they be blotted out of the Book of Life and not listed with the righteous.

69:29

וַאֲנִיbut I am עָנִיdistress וְכוֹאֵבpain and יְשׁוּעָתְךָyour salvation אֱלֹהִיםGod תְּשַׂגְּבֵנִיprotect me

But I am in pain and distress; let Your salvation protect me, O God.

69:30

אֲהַלְלָהpraise שֵׁםname אֱלֹהִיםGod’s בְּשִׁירin song וַאֲגַדְּלֶנּוּand exalt him בְתוֹדָהwith thanksgiving

I will praise God’s name in song and exalt Him with thanksgiving.

69:31

וְתִיטַבplease לַיהוָהthe LORD מִשּׁוֹרthan an ox פָּרbull מַקְרִןhorns מַפְרִיסhooves

And this will please the LORD more than an ox, more than a bull with horns and hooves.

69:32

רָאוּsee עֲנָוִיםhumble יִשְׂמָחוּrejoice דֹּרְשֵׁיseek אֱלֹהִיםGod וִיחִיrevived לְבַבְכֶםyour hearts

The humble will see and rejoice. You who seek God, let your hearts be revived!

69:33

כִּיfor שֹׁמֵעַlistens אֶלto אֶבְיוֹנִיםneedy יְהוָהLORD וְאֶתand [obj] אֲסִירָיוhis captive לֹאnot בָזָהdespise

For the LORD listens to the needy and does not despise His captive people.

69:34

יְהַלְלוּהוּpraise him שָׁמַיִםheaven וָאָרֶץand earth יַמִּיםseas וְכָלand everything רֹמֵשׂmoves בָּםin them

Let heaven and earth praise Him, the seas and everything that moves in them.

69:35

כִּיfor אֱלֹהִיםGod יוֹשִׁיעַsave צִיּוֹןZion וְיִבְנֶהand rebuild עָרֵיcities יְהוּדָהJudah וְיָשְׁבוּthey may dwell שָׁםthere וִירֵשׁוּהָand possess it

For God will save Zion and rebuild the cities of Judah, that they may dwell there and possess it.

69:36

וְזֶרַעdescendants עֲבָדָיוhis servants יִנְחָלוּהָinherit it וְאֹהֲבֵיlove שְׁמוֹhis name יִשְׁכְּנוּsettle בָהּin it

The descendants of His servants will inherit it, and those who love His name will settle in it.
