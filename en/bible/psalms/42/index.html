Psalms 42

Longing for God far from the sanctuary

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. A Maskil of the sons of Korah

42:1

כְּאַיָּלas the deer תַּעֲרֹגpants עַלfor אֲפִיקֵיstreams מָיִםwater כֵּןso נַפְשִׁיmy soul תַעֲרֹגlongs אֵלֶיךָto you אֱלֹהִיםGod

As the deer pants for streams of water, so my soul longs after You, O God.

42:2

צָמְאָהthirsts נַפְשִׁיmy soul לֵאלֹהִיםfor God לְאֵלthe God חָיliving מָתַיwhen אָבוֹאcome וְאֵרָאֶהand appear פְּנֵיpresence אֱלֹהִיםGod’s

My soul thirsts for God, the living God. When shall I come and appear in God’s presence?

42:3

הָיְתָהwas לִּיto me דִמְעָתִיtears לֶחֶםfood יוֹמָםday וָלָיְלָהand night בֶּאֱמֹרwhile men ask אֵלַיto me כָּלall הַיּוֹםday long אַיֵּהwhere אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God

My tears have been my food both day and night, while men ask me all day long, “Where is your God?”

42:4

אֵלֶּהthese אֶזְכְּרָהmind as וְאֶשְׁפְּכָהas I pour עָלַיon me נַפְשִׁיsoul I כִּיfor אֶעֱבֹרwalked בַּסָּךְwith the multitude leading אֶדַּדֵּםleading the procession עַדto בֵּיתhouse אֱלֹהִיםGod בְּקוֹלin voice רִנָּהshouts of joy וְתוֹדָהand thanksgiving הָמוֹןpraise חוֹגֵגcelebrate

These things come to mind as I pour out my soul: how I walked with the multitude, leading the procession to the house of God with shouts of joy and praise.

42:5

מַהwhy תִּשְׁתּוֹחֲחִיdowncast נַפְשִׁיmy soul וַתֶּהֱמִיwhy the unease עָלָיon me הוֹחִילִיput your hope לֵאלֹהִיםin God כִּיfor עוֹדlonger אוֹדֶנּוּpraise him יְשׁוּעוֹתsalvation פָּנָיוhis presence

Why are you downcast, O my soul? Why the unease within me? Put your hope in God, for I will yet praise Him for the salvation of His presence.

42:6

אֱלֹהַיmy God עָלַיon me נַפְשִׁיsoul תִשְׁתּוֹחָחdespairs עַלon כֵּןtherefore אֶזְכָּרְךָremember you מֵאֶרֶץfrom the land יַרְדֵּןJordan וְחֶרְמוֹנִיםand the peaks מֵהַרfrom Mount מִצְעָרMizar

O my God, my soul despairs within me. Therefore I remember You from the land of Jordan and the peaks of Hermon— even from Mount Mizar.

42:7

תְּהוֹםdeep אֶלto תְּהוֹםdeep קוֹרֵאcalls לְקוֹלin the roar צִנּוֹרֶיךָyour waterfalls כָּלall מִשְׁבָּרֶיךָyour breakers וְגַלֶּיךָand waves have rolled עָלַיover me עָבָרוּcrossed

Deep calls to deep in the roar of Your waterfalls; all Your breakers and waves have rolled over me.

42:8

יוֹמָםby day יְצַוֶּהdecrees יְהוָהLORD חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion וּבַלַּיְלָהand at night שִׁירוֹhis song עִמִּיwith me תְּפִלָּהprayer לְאֵלto the God חַיָּיmy life

The LORD decrees His loving devotion by day, and at night His song is with me as a prayer to the God of my life.

42:9

אוֹמְרָהsay לְאֵלto God סַלְעִיmy rock לָמָהto why שְׁכַחְתָּנִיforgotten me לָמָּהwhy קֹדֵרI אֵלֵךְwalk בְּלַחַץenemy’s oppression אוֹיֵבbecause

I say to God my Rock, “Why have You forgotten me? Why must I walk in sorrow because of the enemy’s oppression?”

42:10

בְּרֶצַחlike the crushing בְּעַצְמוֹתַיmy bones חֵרְפוּנִיmy taunt צוֹרְרָיenemies me בְּאָמְרָםwhile they say אֵלַיto me כָּלall הַיּוֹםday אַיֵּהwhere אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God

Like the crushing of my bones, my enemies taunt me, while they say to me all day long, “Where is your God?”

42:11

מַהwhy תִּשְׁתּוֹחֲחִיdowncast נַפְשִׁיmy soul וּמַהwhy תֶּהֱמִיunease עָלָיon me הוֹחִילִיput your hope לֵאלֹהִיםin God כִּיfor עוֹדlonger אוֹדֶנּוּpraise him יְשׁוּעֹתSavior פָּנַיmy face וֵאלֹהָיand my God

Why are you downcast, O my soul? Why the unease within me? Put your hope in God, for I will yet praise Him, my Savior and my God.
