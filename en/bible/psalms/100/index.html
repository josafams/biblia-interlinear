Psalms 100

All the earth serving with gladness

Open in the interlinear reader

100:1

מִזְמוֹרPsalm לְתוֹדָהof thanksgiving הָרִיעוּjoyful noise לַיהוָהto the LORD כָּלall הָאָרֶץthe earth

A Psalm of thanksgiving. Make a joyful noise to the LORD, all the earth.

100:2

עִבְדוּserve אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD בְּשִׂמְחָהwith gladness בֹּאוּcome לְפָנָיוinto his presence בִּרְנָנָהwith joyful

Serve the LORD with gladness; come into His presence with joyful songs.

100:3

דְּעוּknow כִּיthat יְהוָהLORD הוּאis אֱלֹהִיםGod הוּאis עָשָׂנוּmade us וְלוֹand to him אֲנַחְנוּwe עַמּוֹhis people וְצֹאןand the sheep מַרְעִיתוֹhis pasture

Know that the LORD is God. It is He who made us, and we are His; we are His people, and the sheep of His pasture.

100:4

בֹּאוּenter שְׁעָרָיוhis gates בְּתוֹדָהwith thanksgiving חֲצֵרֹתָיוhis courts בִּתְהִלָּהwith praise הוֹדוּgive thanks לוֹto him בָּרֲכוּbless שְׁמוֹhis name

Enter His gates with thanksgiving and His courts with praise; give thanks to Him and bless His name.

100:5

כִּיfor טוֹבgood יְהֹוָהLORD לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion וְעַדto דֹּרcontinues all וָדֹרgenerations אֱמוּנָתוֹhis faithfulness

For the LORD is good, and His loving devotion endures forever; His faithfulness continues to all generations.
