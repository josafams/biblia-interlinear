Psalms 75

God judges at the appointed time

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster: To the tune of “Do Not Destroy.” A Psalm of Asaph. A song

75:1

הוֹדִינוּgive thanks לְּךָto you אֱלֹהִיםGod הוֹדִינוּgive thanks וְקָרוֹבnear שְׁמֶךָyour name סִפְּרוּdeclare נִפְלְאוֹתֶיךָyour wondrous works

We give thanks to You, O God; we give thanks, for Your Name is near. The people declare Your wondrous works.

75:2

כִּיwhen אֶקַּחchoose מוֹעֵדtime אֲנִיI מֵישָׁרִיםfairly אֶשְׁפֹּטjudge

“When I choose a time, I will judge fairly.

75:3

נְמֹגִיםwhen אֶרֶץearth וְכָלand all יֹשְׁבֶיהָdwellers quake it אָנֹכִיI תִכַּנְתִּיbear עַמּוּדֶיהָits pillars סֶּלָהSelah

When the earth and all its dwellers quake, it is I who bear up its pillars. Selah

75:4

אָמַרְתִּיsay לַהוֹלְלִיםto the proud אַלdo not תָּהֹלּוּboast וְלָרְשָׁעִיםand to the wicked אַלdo not תָּרִימוּlift קָרֶןhorn

I say to the proud, ‘Do not boast,’ and to the wicked, ‘Do not lift up your horn.

75:5

אַלdo not תָּרִימוּlift לַמָּרוֹםagainst heaven or קַרְנְכֶםyour horn תְּדַבְּרוּspeak בְצַוָּארneck עָתָקoutstretched

Do not lift up your horn against heaven or speak with an outstretched neck.’”

75:6

כִּיfor לֹאnot מִמּוֹצָאcomes neither וּמִמַּעֲרָבfrom east west וְלֹאand not מִמִּדְבַּרdesert הָרִיםlift

For exaltation comes neither from east nor west, nor out of the desert,

75:7

כִּיbut אֱלֹהִיםGod שֹׁפֵטjudges זֶהthis יַשְׁפִּילdown וְזֶהand this יָרִיםexalts

but it is God who judges; He brings down one and exalts another.

75:8

כִּיfor כוֹסcup בְּיַדin the hand יְהוָהLORD וְיַיִןwine חָמַרmixed מָלֵאfull מֶסֶךְfoaming וַיַּגֵּרwith he pours מִזֶּהthe אַךְbut שְׁמָרֶיהָcup יִמְצוּhis יִשְׁתּוּdrink כֹּלall רִשְׁעֵיwicked אָרֶץearth

For a cup is in the hand of the LORD, full of foaming wine mixed with spices. He pours from His cup, and all the wicked of the earth drink it down to the dregs.

75:9

וַאֲנִיbut I אַגִּידproclaim לְעֹלָםhim forever אֲזַמְּרָהsing praise לֵאלֹהֵיto the God יַעֲקֹבJacob

But I will proclaim Him forever; I will sing praise to the God of Jacob.

75:10

וְכָלall קַרְנֵיhorns רְשָׁעִיםwicked אֲגַדֵּעַcut תְּרוֹמַמְנָהexalted קַרְנוֹתhorns צַדִּיקrighteous

“All the horns of the wicked I will cut off, but the horns of the righteous will be exalted.”
