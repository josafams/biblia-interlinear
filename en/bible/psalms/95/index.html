Psalms 95

A call to worship and obedience

Open in the interlinear reader

95:1

לְכוּcome נְרַנְּנָהsing joy לַיהוָהfor to the LORD נָרִיעָהshout לְצוּרto the rock יִשְׁעֵנוּour salvation

Come, let us sing for joy to the LORD; let us shout to the Rock of our salvation!

95:2

נְקַדְּמָהenter פָנָיוhis presence בְּתוֹדָהwith thanksgiving בִּזְמִרוֹתin song נָרִיעַjoyful noise לוֹto him

Let us enter His presence with thanksgiving; let us make a joyful noise to Him in song.

95:3

כִּיfor אֵלGod גָּדוֹלgreat יְהוָהLORD וּמֶלֶךְking גָּדוֹלgreat עַלon כָּלall אֱלֹהִיםgods

For the LORD is a great God, a great King above all gods.

95:4

אֲשֶׁרthat בְּיָדוֹin his hand מֶחְקְרֵיdepths אָרֶץearth וְתוֹעֲפוֹתpeaks הָרִיםmountain לוֹto

In His hand are the depths of the earth, and the mountain peaks belong to Him.

95:5

אֲשֶׁרthat לוֹhis for הַיָּםthe sea וְהוּאfor he עָשָׂהוּmade it וְיַבֶּשֶׁתdry יָדָיוhis hands יָצָרוּformed

The sea is His, for He made it, and His hands formed the dry land.

95:6

בֹּאוּcome נִשְׁתַּחֲוֶהworship וְנִכְרָעָהand bow נִבְרְכָהkneel לִפְנֵיbefore יְהוָהLORD עֹשֵׂנוּour Maker

O come, let us worship and bow down; let us kneel before the LORD our Maker.

95:7

כִּיfor הוּאhe is אֱלֹהֵינוּour God וַאֲנַחְנוּand we עַםpeople מַרְעִיתוֹhis pasture וְצֹאןsheep יָדוֹunder his הַיּוֹםtoday אִםif בְּקֹלוֹhis voice תִשְׁמָעוּhear

For He is our God, and we are the people of His pasture, the sheep under His care. Today, if you hear His voice,

95:8

אַלdo not תַּקְשׁוּharden לְבַבְכֶםyour hearts you כִּמְרִיבָהas did at Meribah כְּיוֹםin the day מַסָּהMassah בַּמִּדְבָּרin the wilderness

do not harden your hearts as you did at Meribah, in the day at Massah in the wilderness,

95:9

אֲשֶׁרthat נִסּוּנִיtested אֲבוֹתֵיכֶםyour fathers בְּחָנוּנִיtried me גַּםeven רָאוּseen פָעֳלִיmy work

where your fathers tested and tried Me, though they had seen My work.

95:10

אַרְבָּעִיםforty שָׁנָהyears אָקוּטangry בְּדוֹרwith that generation וָאֹמַרand I said עַםpeople תֹּעֵיastray לֵבָבhearts הֵםthey וְהֵםand they לֹאnot יָדְעוּknown דְרָכָיmy ways

For forty years I was angry with that generation, and I said, “They are a people whose hearts go astray, and they have not known My ways.”

95:11

אֲשֶׁרthat נִשְׁבַּעְתִּיswore oath בְאַפִּיon in my anger אִםif יְבֹאוּןenter אֶלto מְנוּחָתִיmy rest

So I swore on oath in My anger, “They shall never enter My rest.”
