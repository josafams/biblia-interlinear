Psalms 7

An appeal to God the judge against persecutors

Open in the interlinear reader

A Shiggaion of David, which he sang to the LORD concerning the words of Cush, a Benjamite

7:1

יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהַיmy God I בְּךָin you חָסִיתִיrefuge הוֹשִׁיעֵנִיsave me מִכָּלfrom all רֹדְפַיmy pursuers וְהַצִּילֵנִיand deliver me

O LORD my God, I take refuge in You; save me and deliver me from all my pursuers,

7:2

פֶּןor יִטְרֹףpieces כְּאַרְיֵהlike a lion נַפְשִׁיsoul פֹּרֵקtear וְאֵיןno מַצִּילrescue

or they will shred my soul like a lion and tear me to pieces with no one to rescue me.

7:3

יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהַיmy God אִםif עָשִׂיתִיdone זֹאתthis אִםif יֶשׁthere עָוֶלinjustice בְּכַפָּיon my hands

O LORD my God, if I have done this, if injustice is on my hands,

7:4

אִםif גָּמַלְתִּיrewarded שׁוֹלְמִיmy ally רָעevil וָאֲחַלְּצָהI have plundered צוֹרְרִיmy foe רֵיקָםwithout cause

if I have rewarded my ally with evil, if I have plundered my foe without cause,

7:5

יִרַדֹּףpursue אוֹיֵבenemy נַפְשִׁיme וְיַשֵּׂגand overtake וְיִרְמֹסhe trample לָאָרֶץto the ground חַיָּיleave וּכְבוֹדִיand my honor לֶעָפָרin the dust יַשְׁכֵּןdwell סֶלָהSelah

then may my enemy pursue me and overtake me; may he trample me to the ground and leave my honor in the dust. Selah

7:6

קוּמָהarise יְהוָהLORD בְּאַפֶּךָin your anger הִנָּשֵׂאrise בְּעַבְרוֹתagainst the fury צוֹרְרָיmy enemies וְעוּרָהawake אֵלַיto me מִשְׁפָּטjudgment צִוִּיתָordain

Arise, O LORD, in Your anger; rise up against the fury of my enemies. Awake, my God, and ordain judgment.

7:7

וַעֲדַתlet the assembled לְאֻמִּיםpeoples תְּסוֹבְבֶךָּaround וְעָלֶיהָseat over them לַמָּרוֹםon high שׁוּבָהback

Let the assembled peoples gather around You; take Your seat over them on high.

7:8

יְהוָהLORD יָדִיןjudges עַמִּיםpeoples שָׁפְטֵנִיvindicate me יְהוָהLORD כְּצִדְקִיaccording to my righteousness וּכְתֻמִּיand integrity עָלָיon me

The LORD judges the peoples; vindicate me, O LORD, according to my righteousness and integrity.

7:9

יִגְמָרend נָאplease רַעevil רְשָׁעִיםwicked וּתְכוֹנֵןbut establish צַדִּיקrighteous וּבֹחֵןwho searches לִבּוֹתhearts וּכְלָיוֹתand minds אֱלֹהִיםGod צַדִּיקrighteous

Put an end to the evil of the wicked, but establish the righteous, O righteous God who searches hearts and minds.

7:10

מָגִנִּיmy shield עַלon אֱלֹהִיםGod מוֹשִׁיעַsaves יִשְׁרֵיupright לֵבheart

My shield is with God, who saves the upright in heart.

7:11

אֱלֹהִיםGod שׁוֹפֵטjudge צַדִּיקrighteous וְאֵלand a God זֹעֵםfeels indignation בְּכָלindignation each יוֹםday

God is a righteous judge and a God who feels indignation each day.

7:12

אִםif לֹאnot יָשׁוּבrepent חַרְבּוֹhis sword יִלְטוֹשׁsharpen קַשְׁתּוֹhis bow דָרַךְbent strung וַיְכוֹנְנֶהָbent and

If one does not repent, God will sharpen His sword; He has bent and strung His bow.

7:13

וְלוֹand to him הֵכִיןprepared כְּלֵיweapons מָוֶתdeadly חִצָּיוordains his arrows לְדֹלְקִיםwith fire יִפְעָלdo

He has prepared His deadly weapons; He ordains His arrows with fire.

7:14

הִנֵּהbehold יְחַבֶּלwicked אָוֶןevil וְהָרָהhe conceives עָמָלtrouble וְיָלַדand births שָׁקֶרfalsehood

Behold, the wicked man travails with evil; he conceives trouble and births falsehood.

7:15

בּוֹרhas כָּרָהdug וַיַּחְפְּרֵהוּhole and hollowed it וַיִּפֹּלhe has fallen בְּשַׁחַתinto a pit יִפְעָלmaking

He has dug a hole and hollowed it out; he has fallen into a pit of his own making.

7:16

יָשׁוּבrecoils עֲמָלוֹhis trouble בְרֹאשׁוֹin his head וְעַלon קָדְקֳדוֹhead חֲמָסוֹviolence יֵרֵדfalls

His trouble recoils on himself, and his violence falls on his own head.

7:17

אוֹדֶהthank יְהוָהLORD כְּצִדְקוֹhis righteousness וַאֲזַמְּרָהand sing praise שֵׁםname יְהוָהLORD עֶלְיוֹןMost high

I will thank the LORD for His righteousness and sing praise to the name of the LORD Most High.
