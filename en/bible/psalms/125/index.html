Psalms 125

Firm as Mount Zion

Open in the interlinear reader

125:1

שִׁירsong הַמַּעֲלוֹתof ascents הַבֹּטְחִיםwho trust בַּיהוָהin the LORD כְּהַרlike Mount צִיּוֹןZion לֹאnot יִמּוֹטmoved לְעוֹלָםforever יֵשֵׁבabides

A song of ascents. Those who trust in the LORD are like Mount Zion. It cannot be moved; it abides forever.

125:2

יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem הָרִיםmountains סָבִיבsurround לָהּto her וַיהוָהso the LORD סָבִיבsurrounds לְעַמּוֹhis people מֵעַתָּהboth now וְעַדand to עוֹלָםforevermore

As the mountains surround Jerusalem, so the LORD surrounds His people, both now and forevermore.

125:3

כִּיfor לֹאnot יָנוּחַrest שֵׁבֶטscepter הָרֶשַׁעthe wicked עַלupon גּוֹרַלallotted הַצַּדִּיקִיםthe righteous לְמַעַןso לֹאnot יִשְׁלְחוּput הַצַּדִּיקִיםthat the righteous בְּעַוְלָתָהto injustice יְדֵיהֶםtheir hands

For the scepter of the wicked will not rest upon the land allotted to the righteous, so that the righteous will not put forth their hands to injustice.

125:4

הֵיטִיבָהgood יְהוָהLORD לַטּוֹבִיםto those who are good וְלִישָׁרִיםand to the upright בְּלִבּוֹתָםin heart

Do good, O LORD, to those who are good, and to the upright in heart.

125:5

וְהַמַּטִּיםbut those who turn עַקַלְקַלּוֹתָםcrooked יוֹלִיכֵםtake them יְהוָהLORD אֶתwith פֹּעֲלֵיbanish הָאָוֶןthe evildoers שָׁלוֹםpeace עַלupon יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

But those who turn to crooked ways the LORD will banish with the evildoers. Peace be upon Israel.
