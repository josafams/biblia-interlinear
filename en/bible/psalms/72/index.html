Psalms 72

The righteous reign of the king

Open in the interlinear reader

72:1

לִשְׁלֹמֹהof Solomon אֱלֹהִיםGod מִשְׁפָּטֶיךָyour justice לְמֶלֶךְking with תֵּןendow וְצִדְקָתְךָyour righteousness לְבֶןthe son מֶלֶךְking

Of Solomon. Endow the king with Your justice, O God, and the son of the king with Your righteousness.

72:2

יָדִיןjudge עַמְּךָyour people בְצֶדֶקwith righteousness וַעֲנִיֶּיךָand your afflicted בְמִשְׁפָּטwith justice

May he judge Your people with righteousness and Your afflicted with justice.

72:3

יִשְׂאוּbring הָרִיםmountains שָׁלוֹםpeace לָעָםto the people וּגְבָעוֹתand the hills בִּצְדָקָהrighteousness

May the mountains bring peace to the people, and the hills bring righteousness.

72:4

יִשְׁפֹּטvindicate עֲנִיֵּיafflicted עָםpeople יוֹשִׁיעַsave לִבְנֵיthe children אֶבְיוֹןneedy וִידַכֵּאand crush עוֹשֵׁקoppressor

May he vindicate the afflicted among the people; may he save the children of the needy and crush the oppressor.

72:5

יִירָאוּךָfear עִםwith שָׁמֶשׁsun וְלִפְנֵיand to before יָרֵחַmoon דּוֹרall דּוֹרִיםgenerations

May they fear him as long as the sun shines, as long as the moon remains, through all generations.

72:6

יֵרֵדfalls כְּמָטָרlike rain עַלon גֵּזfreshly cut grass spring כִּרְבִיבִיםlike showers זַרְזִיףwater אָרֶץearth

May he be like rain that falls on freshly cut grass, like spring showers that water the earth.

72:7

יִפְרַחflourish בְּיָמָיוin his days צַדִּיקrighteous וְרֹבand abound שָׁלוֹםprosperity עַדuntil בְּלִיno יָרֵחַmoon

May the righteous flourish in his days and prosperity abound, until the moon is no more.

72:8

וְיֵרְדְּmay he rule מִיָּםfrom sea עַדto יָםsea וּמִנָּהָרand from the Euphrates עַדto אַפְסֵיends אָרֶץearth

May he rule from sea to sea, and from the Euphrates to the ends of the earth.

72:9

לְפָנָיוbefore him יִכְרְעוּbow צִיִּיםnomads וְאֹיְבָיוand his enemies עָפָרdust יְלַחֵכוּlick

May the nomads bow before him, and his enemies lick the dust.

72:10

מַלְכֵיkings תַרְשִׁישׁTarshish וְאִיִּיםand distant shores מִנְחָהtribute יָשִׁיבוּbring מַלְכֵיkings שְׁבָאSheba וּסְבָאand Seba אֶשְׁכָּרgifts יַקְרִיבוּoffer

May the kings of Tarshish and distant shores bring tribute; may the kings of Sheba and Seba offer gifts.

72:11

וְיִשְׁתַּחֲווּbow down לוֹto him כָלall מְלָכִיםkings כָּלall גּוֹיִםnations יַעַבְדוּהוּserve him

May all kings bow down to him and all nations serve him.

72:12

כִּיfor יַצִּילdeliver אֶבְיוֹןneedy מְשַׁוֵּעַcry וְעָנִיand the afflicted וְאֵיןwho have no עֹזֵרhelper לוֹto him

For he will deliver the needy who cry out and the afflicted who have no helper.

72:13

יָחֹסpity עַלon דַּלpoor וְאֶבְיוֹןand needy וְנַפְשׁוֹתlives אֶבְיוֹנִיםoppressed יוֹשִׁיעַsave

He will take pity on the poor and needy and save the lives of the oppressed.

72:14

מִתּוֹךְfrom oppression וּמֵחָמָסand violence יִגְאַלredeem נַפְשָׁםwill them וְיֵיקַרprecious דָּמָםtheir blood בְּעֵינָיוin his sight

He will redeem them from oppression and violence, for their blood is precious in his sight.

72:15

וִיחִיhe live וְיִתֶּןgiven לוֹto him מִזְּהַבgold from שְׁבָאSheba וְיִתְפַּלֵּלpray בַּעֲדוֹfor him תָמִידever כָּלall הַיּוֹםday long יְבָרֲכֶנְהוּbless him

Long may he live! May gold from Sheba be given him. May people ever pray for him; may they bless him all day long.

72:16

יְהִיbe פִסַּתabundance בַּרgrain בָּאָרֶץin the land בְּרֹאשׁsway atop הָרִיםhills יִרְעַשׁflourish כַּלְּבָנוֹןLebanon פִּרְיוֹits fruit וְיָצִיצוּand springs מֵעִירfrom city כְּעֵשֶׂבlike the grass הָאָרֶץthe field

May there be an abundance of grain in the land; may it sway atop the hills. May its fruit trees flourish like the forests of Lebanon, and its people like the grass of the field.

72:17

יְהִיmay שְׁמוֹhis name לְעוֹלָםendure forever לִפְנֵיto before שֶׁמֶשׁsun יִנּוֹןshines שְׁמוֹhis name וְיִתְבָּרְכוּblessed בוֹin him כָּלall גּוֹיִםnations יְאַשְּׁרוּהוּcall him blessed

May his name endure forever; may his name continue as long as the sun shines. In him may all nations be blessed; may they call him blessed.

72:18

בָּרוּךְblessed יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהִיםGod אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael עֹשֵׂהdoes נִפְלָאוֹתmarvelous deeds לְבַדּוֹalone

Blessed be the LORD God, the God of Israel, who alone does marvelous deeds.

72:19

וּבָרוּךְand blessed שֵׁםname כְּבוֹדוֹhis glorious לְעוֹלָםforever וְיִמָּלֵאfilled כְבוֹדוֹhis glory אֶת[obj] כֹּלall הָאָרֶץthe earth אָמֵןAmen וְאָמֵןand amen

And blessed be His glorious name forever; may all the earth be filled with His glory. Amen and amen.

72:20

כָּלּוּthus תְפִלּוֹתprayers דָּוִדDavid בֶּןson יִשָׁיJesse

Thus conclude the prayers of David son of Jesse.
