Psalms 71

The prayer of one who has trusted since youth

Open in the interlinear reader

71:1

בְּךָin you יְהוָהLORD חָסִיתִיrefuge אַלdo not אֵבוֹשָׁהput shame לְעוֹלָםput to

In You, O LORD, I have taken refuge; let me never be put to shame.

71:2

בְּצִדְקָתְךָin your justice תַּצִּילֵנִיrescue וּתְפַלְּטֵנִיand deliver me הַטֵּהincline אֵלַיto me אָזְנְךָyour ear וְהוֹשִׁיעֵנִיand save me

In Your justice, rescue and deliver me; incline Your ear and save me.

71:3

הֱיֵהbe לִיto me לְצוּרrock of refuge מָעוֹןwhere לָבוֹאcan go תָּמִידalways צִוִּיתָcommand לְהוֹשִׁיעֵנִיto save me כִּיfor סַלְעִיmy rock וּמְצוּדָתִיand my fortress אָתָּהyou

Be my rock of refuge, where I can always go. Give the command to save me, for You are my rock and my fortress.

71:4

אֱלֹהַיme O my God פַּלְּטֵנִיdeliver מִיַּדfrom the hand רָשָׁעwicked מִכַּףfrom the grasp מְעַוֵּלunjust וְחוֹמֵץand ruthless

Deliver me, O my God, from the hand of the wicked, from the grasp of the unjust and ruthless.

71:5

כִּיfor אַתָּהyou תִקְוָתִיmy hope אֲדֹנָיLord יְהוִהGOD מִבְטַחִיmy confidence מִנְּעוּרָיfrom my youth

For You are my hope, O Lord GOD, my confidence from my youth.

71:6

עָלֶיךָon you נִסְמַכְתִּיleaned מִבֶּטֶןsince birth מִמְּעֵיwomb אִמִּיmy mother’s אַתָּהyou גוֹזִיpulled me בְּךָin you תְהִלָּתִיmy praise is תָמִידalways

I have leaned on You since birth; You pulled me from my mother’s womb. My praise is always for You.

71:7

כְּמוֹפֵתa portent הָיִיתִיbecome לְרַבִּיםto many וְאַתָּהbut you מַחֲסִיmy refuge עֹזstrong

I have become a portent to many, but You are my strong refuge.

71:8

יִמָּלֵאfilled פִיmy mouth תְּהִלָּתֶךָyour praise כָּלall הַיּוֹםday תִּפְאַרְתֶּךָyour splendor

My mouth is filled with Your praise and with Your splendor all day long.

71:9

אַלdo not תַּשְׁלִיכֵנִיdiscard me לְעֵתin my old זִקְנָהage כִּכְלוֹתwhen my כֹּחִיstrength fails אַלdo not תַּעַזְבֵנִיforsake me

Do not discard me in my old age; do not forsake me when my strength fails.

71:10

כִּיfor אָמְרוּspeak אוֹיְבַיmy enemies לִיto me וְשֹׁמְרֵיand those who נַפְשִׁיmy life נוֹעֲצוּconspire יַחְדָּוtogether

For my enemies speak against me, and those who lie in wait for my life conspire,

71:11

לֵאמֹרsaying אֱלֹהִיםGod עֲזָבוֹforsaken him רִדְפוּpursue וְתִפְשׂוּהוּand seize him כִּיfor אֵיןno מַצִּילrescue

saying, “God has forsaken him; pursue him and seize him, for there is no one to rescue him.”

71:12

אֱלֹהִיםGod אַלnot תִּרְחַקfar מִמֶּנִּיfrom me אֱלֹהַיmy God לְעֶזְרָתִיto help me חוּשָׁהhurry

Be not far from me, O God. Hurry, O my God, to help me.

71:13

יֵבֹשׁוּashamed יִכְלוּconsumed שֹׂטְנֵיaccusers נַפְשִׁיmy soul יַעֲטוּcovered חֶרְפָּהscorn וּכְלִמָּהand disgrace מְבַקְשֵׁיseek רָעָתִיmy harm

May the accusers of my soul be ashamed and consumed; may those who seek my harm be covered with scorn and disgrace.

71:14

וַאֲנִיbut I תָּמִידalways אֲיַחֵלhope וְהוֹסַפְתִּיmore עַלon כָּלall תְּהִלָּתֶךָpraise you

But I will always hope and will praise You more and more.

71:15

פִּיmy mouth יְסַפֵּרdeclare צִדְקָתֶךָyour righteousness כָּלall הַיּוֹםday תְּשׁוּעָתֶךָyour salvation כִּיfor לֹאnot יָדַעְתִּיknow סְפֹרוֹתfull measure

My mouth will declare Your righteousness and Your salvation all day long, though I cannot know their full measure.

71:16

אָבוֹאenter בִּגְבֻרוֹתin the strength אֲדֹנָיLord I יְהוִהGOD אַזְכִּירproclaim צִדְקָתְךָyour righteousness לְבַדֶּךָyours alone

I will enter in the strength of the Lord GOD; I will proclaim Your righteousness—Yours alone.

71:17

אֱלֹהִיםGod לִמַּדְתַּנִיtaught me מִנְּעוּרָיfrom my youth וְעַדand to הֵנָּהthis אַגִּידproclaim נִפְלְאוֹתֶיךָyour marvelous deeds

O God, You have taught me from my youth, and to this day I proclaim Your marvelous deeds.

71:18

וְגַםeven when עַדuntil זִקְנָהold וְשֵׂיבָהand gray אֱלֹהִיםGod אַלdo not תַּעַזְבֵנִיforsake me עַדuntil אַגִּידproclaim זְרוֹעֲךָyour power לְדוֹרto the next generation לְכָלto all יָבוֹאcome גְּבוּרָתֶךָyour might

Even when I am old and gray, do not forsake me, O God, until I proclaim Your power to the next generation, Your might to all who are to come.

71:19

וְצִדְקָתְךָyour righteousness reaches אֱלֹהִיםGod עַדto מָרוֹםheavens אֲשֶׁרwho עָשִׂיתָdone גְדֹלוֹתgreat אֱלֹהִיםGod מִיwho כָמוֹךָlike you

Your righteousness reaches to the heavens, O God, You who have done great things. Who, O God, is like You?

71:20

אֲשֶׁרhave הִרְאִיתַנִיshown me צָרוֹתtroubles רַבּוֹתmany וְרָעוֹתtroubles and misfortunes תָּשׁוּבwill תְּחַיֵּינִיrevive me וּמִתְּהֹמוֹתagain even from the depths הָאָרֶץthe earth תָּשׁוּבback תַּעֲלֵנִיbring me up

Though You have shown me many troubles and misfortunes, You will revive me once again. Even from the depths of the earth You will bring me back up.

71:21

תֶּרֶבincrease גְּדֻלָּתִיmy honor וְתִסֹּבand circumference תְּנַחֲמֵנִיcomfort me

You will increase my honor and comfort me once again.

71:22

גַּםeven אֲנִיI אוֹדְךָpraise you בִכְלִיin jar נֶבֶלharp אֲמִתְּךָyour faithfulness אֱלֹהָיmy God I אֲזַמְּרָהsing praise לְךָto you בְכִנּוֹרwith the lyre קְדוֹשׁholy יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

So I will praise You with the harp for Your faithfulness, O my God; I will sing praise to You with the lyre, O Holy One of Israel.

71:23

תְּרַנֵּנָּהshout שְׂפָתַיmy lips כִּיwhen אֲזַמְּרָהsing praise לָּךְto you וְנַפְשִׁיalong with my soul אֲשֶׁרwhich פָּדִיתָredeemed

When I sing praise to You my lips will shout for joy, along with my soul, which You have redeemed.

71:24

גַּםindeed לְשׁוֹנִיmy tongue כָּלall הַיּוֹםday תֶּהְגֶּהproclaim צִדְקָתֶךָyour righteousness כִּיfor בֹשׁוּthose כִיfor חָפְרוּwho מְבַקְשֵׁיseek רָעָתִיmy harm

My tongue will indeed proclaim Your righteousness all day long, for those who seek my harm are disgraced and confounded.
