Psalms 68

God's victorious march to the sanctuary

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. A song

68:1

יָקוּםarises אֱלֹהִיםGod יָפוּצוּscattered אוֹיְבָיוhis enemies וְיָנוּסוּand those מְשַׂנְאָיוhate him מִפָּנָיוflee his presence

God arises. His enemies are scattered, and those who hate Him flee His presence.

68:2

כְּהִנְדֹּףblown עָשָׁןsmoke תִּנְדֹּףdrive כְּהִמֵּסas melts דּוֹנַגwax מִפְּנֵיmelts before אֵשׁfire יֹאבְדוּperish רְשָׁעִיםwicked מִפְּנֵיin the presence אֱלֹהִיםGod

As smoke is blown away, You will drive them out; as wax melts before the fire, the wicked will perish in the presence of God.

68:3

וְצַדִּיקִיםbut the righteous יִשְׂמְחוּglad יַעַלְצוּrejoice לִפְנֵיbefore אֱלֹהִיםGod וְיָשִׂישׂוּthey will celebrate בְשִׂמְחָהwith joy

But the righteous will be glad and rejoice before God; they will celebrate with joy.

68:4

שִׁירוּsing לֵאלֹהִיםto God זַמְּרוּsing praises שְׁמוֹname him סֹלּוּexalt לָרֹכֵבhim who rides בָּעֲרָבוֹתon the clouds בְּיָהּLORD שְׁמוֹhis name וְעִלְזוּand rejoice לְפָנָיוbefore him

Sing to God! Sing praises to His name. Exalt Him who rides on the clouds— His name is the LORD— and rejoice before Him.

68:5

אֲבִיfather יְתוֹמִיםfatherless וְדַיַּןand a defender אַלְמָנוֹתwidows אֱלֹהִיםGod בִּמְעוֹןhabitation קָדְשׁוֹhis holy

A father of the fatherless, and a defender of the widows, is God in His holy habitation.

68:6

אֱלֹהִיםGod מוֹשִׁיבsettles יְחִידִיםlonely בַּיְתָהfamilies he leads מוֹצִיאout אֲסִירִיםprisoners בַּכּוֹשָׁרוֹתto prosperity אַךְbut סוֹרֲרִיםrebellious שָׁכְנוּdwell צְחִיחָהsun

God settles the lonely in families; He leads the prisoners out to prosperity, but the rebellious dwell in a sun-scorched land.

68:7

אֱלֹהִיםGod בְּצֵאתְךָwhen you went לִפְנֵיout before עַמֶּךָyour people בְּצַעְדְּךָwhen you marched בִישִׁימוֹןthrough the wasteland סֶלָהSelah

O God, when You went out before Your people, when You marched through the wasteland, Selah

68:8

אֶרֶץearth רָעָשָׁהshook אַףand שָׁמַיִםheavens נָטְפוּpoured מִפְּנֵיbefore אֱלֹהִיםGod זֶהthis סִינַיSinai מִפְּנֵיon before אֱלֹהִיםGod אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

the earth shook and the heavens poured down rain before God, the One on Sinai, before God, the God of Israel.

68:9

גֶּשֶׁםrain נְדָבוֹתsent abundant תָּנִיףO אֱלֹהִיםGod נַחֲלָתְךָyour inheritance וְנִלְאָהweary אַתָּהyou כוֹנַנְתָּהּrefreshed

You sent abundant rain, O God; You refreshed Your weary inheritance.

68:10

חַיָּתְךָyour flock יָשְׁבוּsettled בָהּin her תָּכִיןprovided בְּטוֹבָתְךָyour bounty you לֶעָנִיfor the poor אֱלֹהִיםGod

Your flock settled therein; O God, from Your bounty You provided for the poor.

68:11

אֲדֹנָיLord יִתֶּןgives אֹמֶרcommand הַמְבַשְּׂרוֹתproclaim צָבָאit רָבgreat

The Lord gives the command; a great company of women proclaim it:

68:12

מַלְכֵיkings צְבָאוֹתarmies יִדֹּדוּןflee יִדֹּדוּןhaste וּנְוַתshe who waits בַּיִתhome תְּחַלֵּקdivides שָׁלָלplunder

“Kings and their armies flee in haste; she who waits at home divides the plunder.

68:13

אִםif תִּשְׁכְּבוּןlie בֵּיןamong שְׁפַתָּיִםsheepfolds כַּנְפֵיwings יוֹנָהdove נֶחְפָּהcovered בַכֶּסֶףwith silver וְאֶבְרוֹתֶיהָand her feathers בִּירַקְרַקwith shimmering חָרוּץgold

Though you lie down among the sheepfolds, the wings of the dove are covered with silver, and her feathers with shimmering gold.”

68:14

בְּפָרֵשׂscattered שַׁדַּיAlmighty מְלָכִיםkings בָּהּin the land it תַּשְׁלֵגsnow בְּצַלְמוֹןfalling on Zalmon

When the Almighty scattered the kings in the land, it was like the snow falling on Zalmon.

68:15

הַרmountain אֱלֹהִיםGod הַרMount בָּשָׁןBashan הַרmountain גַּבְנֻנִּיםmany peaks הַרMount בָּשָׁןBashan

A mountain of God is Mount Bashan; a mountain of many peaks is Mount Bashan.

68:16

לָמָּהwhy תְּרַצְּדוּןenvy הָרִיםmountains גַּבְנֻנִּיםmany peaks הָהָרthis is the mountain חָמַדchose אֱלֹהִיםGod לְשִׁבְתּוֹfor his dwelling אַףsurely יְהוָהLORD יִשְׁכֹּןdwell לָנֶצַחforever

Why do you gaze in envy, O mountains of many peaks? This is the mountain God chose for His dwelling, where the LORD will surely dwell forever.

68:17

רֶכֶבchariots אֱלֹהִיםGod רִבֹּתַיִםtens אַלְפֵיthousands שִׁנְאָןthousands אֲדֹנָיLord is בָםin סִינַיSinai בַּקֹּדֶשׁhis sanctuary

The chariots of God are tens of thousands— thousands of thousands are they; the Lord is in His sanctuary as He was at Sinai.

68:18

עָלִיתָascended לַמָּרוֹםon high שָׁבִיתָcaptives שֶּׁבִיled captives לָקַחְתָּreceived מַתָּנוֹתgifts בָּאָדָםfrom men וְאַףeven סוֹרְרִיםrebellious לִשְׁכֹּןdwell יָהּLORD אֱלֹהִיםGod

You have ascended on high; You have led captives away. You have received gifts from men, even from the rebellious, that the LORD God may dwell there.

68:19

בָּרוּךְblessed אֲדֹנָיLord יוֹםday יוֹםdaily יַעֲמָסbears לָנוּto us הָאֵלthe God יְשׁוּעָתֵנוּour salvation סֶלָהSelah

Blessed be the Lord, who daily bears our burden, the God of our salvation. Selah

68:20

הָאֵלGod לָנוּa אֵלGod לְמוֹשָׁעוֹתof deliverance וְלֵיהוִהthe GOD אֲדֹנָיLord לַמָּוֶתfrom death תּוֹצָאוֹתrescuer

Our God is a God of deliverance; the Lord GOD is our rescuer from death.

68:21

אַךְsurely אֱלֹהִיםGod יִמְחַץcrush רֹאשׁheads אֹיְבָיוhis enemies קָדְקֹדcrowns שֵׂעָרhairy מִתְהַלֵּךְpersist בַּאֲשָׁמָיוin guilty ways

Surely God will crush the heads of His enemies, the hairy crowns of those who persist in guilty ways.

68:22

אָמַרsaid אֲדֹנָיLord I מִבָּשָׁןfrom Bashan אָשִׁיבwill אָשִׁיבbring מִמְּצֻלוֹתfrom the depths יָםsea

The Lord said, “I will retrieve them from Bashan, I will bring them up from the depths of the sea,

68:23

לְמַעַןmay תִּמְחַץdipped רַגְלְךָyour foot בְּדָםin the blood לְשׁוֹןtongues כְּלָבֶיךָdogs מֵאֹיְבִיםfoes מִנֵּהוּhis from

that your foot may be dipped in the blood of your foes— the tongues of your dogs in the same.”

68:24

רָאוּseen הֲלִיכוֹתֶיךָyour procession אֱלֹהִיםGod הֲלִיכוֹתmarch אֵלִיmy God מַלְכִּיking בַקֹּדֶשׁinto the sanctuary

They have seen Your procession, O God— the march of my God and King into the sanctuary.

68:25

קִדְּמוּsingers שָׁרִיםmusicians אַחַרfollow נֹגְנִיםplaying בְּתוֹךְafter among עֲלָמוֹתmaidens תּוֹפֵפוֹתtambourines

The singers lead the way, the musicians follow after, among the maidens playing tambourines.

68:26

בְּמַקְהֵלוֹתcongregation בָּרְכוּbless אֱלֹהִיםGod יְהוָהLORD מִמְּקוֹרfrom the fountain יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

Bless God in the great congregation; bless the LORD from the fountain of Israel.

68:27

שָׁםthere בִּנְיָמִןBenjamin צָעִירyoungest רֹדֵםruling them שָׂרֵיprinces יְהוּדָהJudah רִגְמָתָםtheir company שָׂרֵיprinces זְבֻלוּןZebulun שָׂרֵיcommanders נַפְתָּלִיNaphtali

There is Benjamin, the youngest, ruling them, the princes of Judah in their company, the princes of Zebulun and of Naphtali.

68:28

צִוָּהsummon אֱלֹהֶיךָGod עֻזֶּךָyour strength עוּזָּהpower אֱלֹהִיםGod זוּhave פָּעַלְתָּexerted לָּנוּto us

Summon Your power, O God; show Your strength, O God, which You have exerted on our behalf.

68:29

מֵהֵיכָלֶךָbecause of your temple עַלon יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem לְךָto you יוֹבִילוּbring מְלָכִיםkings שָׁיgifts

Because of Your temple at Jerusalem kings will bring You gifts.

68:30

גְּעַרrebuke חַיַּתbeast קָנֶהreeds עֲדַתherd אַבִּירִיםbulls בְּעֶגְלֵיamong the calves עַמִּיםnations מִתְרַפֵּסsubmits בְּרַצֵּיbringing bars כָסֶףsilver בִּזַּרscatter עַמִּיםnations קְרָבוֹתwar יֶחְפָּצוּdelight

Rebuke the beast in the reeds, the herd of bulls among the calves of the nations, until it submits, bringing bars of silver. Scatter the nations who delight in war.

68:31

יֶאֱתָיוּenvoys חַשְׁמַנִּיםarrive מִנִּיfrom מִצְרָיִםEgypt כּוּשׁCush תָּרִיץstretch יָדָיוhands לֵאלֹהִיםto God

Envoys will arrive from Egypt; Cush will stretch out her hands to God.

68:32

מַמְלְכוֹתkingdoms הָאָרֶץthe earth שִׁירוּsing לֵאלֹהִיםto God זַמְּרוּpraises אֲדֹנָיLord סֶלָהSelah

Sing to God, O kingdoms of the earth; sing praises to the Lord— Selah

68:33

לָרֹכֵבto him who rides בִּשְׁמֵיupon the highest שְׁמֵיheavens קֶדֶםold הֵןbehold יִתֵּןgive בְּקוֹלוֹin his voice קוֹלvoice resounds עֹזmighty

to Him who rides upon the highest heavens of old; behold, His mighty voice resounds.

68:34

תְּנוּascribe עֹזpower לֵאלֹהִיםto God עַלover יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael גַּאֲוָתוֹwhose majesty וְעֻזּוֹwhose strength is בַּשְּׁחָקִיםin the skies

Ascribe the power to God, whose majesty is over Israel, whose strength is in the skies.

68:35

נוֹרָאawesome אֱלֹהִיםGod מִמִּקְדָּשֶׁיךָin your sanctuary אֵלGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael הוּאhe נֹתֵןgives עֹזstrength power וְתַעֲצֻמוֹתand power לָעָםto his people בָּרוּךְblessed אֱלֹהִיםGod

O God, You are awesome in Your sanctuary; the God of Israel Himself gives strength and power to His people. Blessed be God!
