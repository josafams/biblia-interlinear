Psalms 53

A new version of the psalm on the fool

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. According to Mahalath. A Maskil of David

53:1

אָמַרsays נָבָלfool בְּלִבּוֹin his heart אֵיןno אֱלֹהִיםGod הִשְׁחִיתוּcorrupt וְהִתְעִיבוּvile עָוֶלthere is אֵיןno עֹשֵׂהdoes טוֹבgood

The fool says in his heart, “There is no God.” They are corrupt; their ways are vile. There is no one who does good.

53:2

אֱלֹהִיםGod מִשָּׁמַיִםfrom heaven הִשְׁקִיףlooks down עַלupon בְּנֵיsons אָדָםmen לִרְאוֹתto see הֲיֵשׁif any מַשְׂכִּילunderstand דֹּרֵשׁseek אֶת[obj] אֱלֹהִיםGod

God looks down from heaven upon the sons of men to see if any understand, if any seek God.

53:3

כֻּלּוֹall סָגturned יַחְדָּוtogether נֶאֱלָחוּbecome corrupt אֵיןno עֹשֵׂהdoes טוֹבgood אֵיןnot גַּםeven אֶחָדone

All have turned away, they have together become corrupt; there is no one who does good, not even one.

53:4

הֲלֹאnever יָדְעוּlearn פֹּעֲלֵיworkers אָוֶןiniquity אֹכְלֵיdevour עַמִּיmy people אָכְלוּlike לֶחֶםbread אֱלֹהִיםGod לֹאnot קָרָאוּcall

Will the workers of iniquity never learn? They devour my people like bread; they refuse to call upon God.

53:5

שָׁםthere פָּחֲדוּoverwhelmed פַחַדdread לֹאnot הָיָהwas פָחַדfear כִּיfor אֱלֹהִיםGod פִּזַּרscattered עַצְמוֹתbones חֹנָךְbesieged you הֱבִשֹׁתָהput כִּיfor אֱלֹהִיםGod מְאָסָםdespised them

There they are, overwhelmed with dread, where there was nothing to fear. For God has scattered the bones of those who besieged you. You put them to shame, for God has despised them.

53:6

מִיwho יִתֵּןgive מִצִּיּוֹןfrom Zion יְשֻׁעוֹתsalvation יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael בְּשׁוּבwould come אֱלֹהִיםGod שְׁבוּתrestores captive עַמּוֹhis people יָגֵלlet rejoice יַעֲקֹבJacob יִשְׂמַחlet יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

Oh, that the salvation of Israel would come from Zion! When God restores His captive people, let Jacob rejoice, let Israel be glad!
