Psalms 18

The king's thanksgiving for victory

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. Of David the servant of the LORD, who sang this song to the LORD on the day the LORD had delivered him from the hand of all his enemies and from the hand of Saul. He said

18:1

וַיֹּאמַרI אֶרְחָמְךָlove you יְהוָהLORD חִזְקִיmy strength

I love You, O LORD, my strength.

18:2

יְהוָהLORD סַלְעִיrock my וּמְצוּדָתִיfortress my וּמְפַלְטִיand deliverer my אֵלִיGod is my צוּרִיrock אֶחֱסֶהrefuge בּוֹin whom מָגִנִּיmy shield וְקֶרֶןand the horn יִשְׁעִיmy salvation מִשְׂגַּבִּיmy stronghold

The LORD is my rock, my fortress, and my deliverer. My God is my rock, in whom I take refuge, my shield, and the horn of my salvation, my stronghold.

18:3

מְהֻלָּלworthy אֶקְרָאcall יְהוָהLORD וּמִןfrom אֹיְבַיmy enemies אִוָּשֵׁעַsaved

I will call upon the LORD, who is worthy to be praised; so shall I be saved from my enemies.

18:4

אֲפָפוּנִיencompassed me חֶבְלֵיcords מָוֶתdeath וְנַחֲלֵיtorrents בְלִיַּעַלoverwhelmed יְבַעֲתוּנִיchaos overwhelmed me

The cords of death encompassed me; the torrents of chaos overwhelmed me.

18:5

חֶבְלֵיcords שְׁאוֹלSheol סְבָבוּנִיentangled me קִדְּמוּנִיconfronted me מוֹקְשֵׁיsnares מָוֶתdeath

The cords of Sheol entangled me; the snares of death confronted me.

18:6

בַּצַּרin my distress לִיto me אֶקְרָאcalled יְהוָהLORD וְאֶלI cried to אֱלֹהַיmy God אֲשַׁוֵּעַhelp יִשְׁמַעheard מֵהֵיכָלוֹfrom his temple קוֹלִיmy voice וְשַׁוְעָתִיand my cry לְפָנָיוto his before תָּבוֹאreached בְאָזְנָיוhis ears

In my distress I called upon the LORD; I cried to my God for help. From His temple He heard my voice, and my cry for His help reached His ears.

18:7

וַתִּגְעַשׁshook וַתִּרְעַשׁshook and quaked הָאָרֶץthe earth וּמוֹסְדֵיand the foundations הָרִיםmountains יִרְגָּזוּtrembled וַיִּתְגָּעֲשׁוּthey were shaken כִּיbecause חָרָהburned לוֹto him

Then the earth shook and quaked, and the foundations of the mountains trembled; they were shaken because He burned with anger.

18:8

עָלָהrose עָשָׁןsmoke בְּאַפּוֹfrom his nostrils וְאֵשׁand consuming fire מִפִּיוfrom his mouth תֹּאכֵלglowing גֶּחָלִיםglowing coals forth בָּעֲרוּblazed מִמֶּנּוּfrom him

Smoke rose from His nostrils, and consuming fire came from His mouth; glowing coals blazed forth.

18:9

וַיֵּטhe parted שָׁמַיִםheavens וַיֵּרַדand came down וַעֲרָפֶלwith dark clouds תַּחַתbeneath רַגְלָיוhis feet

He parted the heavens and came down with dark clouds beneath His feet.

18:10

וַיִּרְכַּבhe mounted עַלon כְּרוּבcherub וַיָּעֹףand flew וַיֵּדֶאhe soared עַלon כַּנְפֵיwings רוּחַwind

He mounted a cherub and flew; He soared on the wings of the wind.

18:11

יָשֶׁתmade חֹשֶׁךְdarkness סִתְרוֹhis hiding סְבִיבוֹתָיוaround him סֻכָּתוֹcanopy חֶשְׁכַתplace מַיִםstorm עָבֵיstorm clouds שְׁחָקִיםclouds

He made darkness His hiding place, and storm clouds a canopy around Him.

18:12

מִנֹּגַהּfrom the brightness נֶגְדּוֹpresence his עָבָיוclouds עָבְרוּadvanced בָּרָדhailstones וְגַחֲלֵיand coals אֵשׁfire

From the brightness of His presence His clouds advanced— hailstones and coals of fire.

18:13

וַיַּרְעֵםthundered בַּשָּׁמַיִםfrom heaven יְהוָהLORD וְעֶלְיוֹןMost high יִתֵּןresounded קֹלוֹvoice בָּרָדhailstones וְגַחֲלֵיand coals אֵשׁfire

The LORD thundered from heaven; the voice of the Most High resounded— hailstones and coals of fire.

18:14

וַיִּשְׁלַחhe shot חִצָּיוhis arrows וַיְפִיצֵםand scattered the foes וּבְרָקִיםhe lightning רָבhurled וַיְהֻמֵּםand routed them

He shot His arrows and scattered the foes; He hurled lightning and routed them.

18:15

וַיֵּרָאוּappeared אֲפִיקֵיchannels מַיִםsea וַיִּגָּלוּexposed מוֹסְדוֹתfoundations תֵּבֵלworld מִגַּעֲרָתְךָat your rebuke יְהוָהLORD מִנִּשְׁמַתat the blast רוּחַbreath אַפֶּךָyour nostrils

The channels of the sea appeared, and the foundations of the world were exposed, at Your rebuke, O LORD, at the blast of the breath of Your nostrils.

18:16

יִשְׁלַחreached מִמָּרוֹםfrom on high יִקָּחֵנִיtook יַמְשֵׁנִיdrew me מִמַּיִםout of deep waters רַבִּיםmany

He reached down from on high and took hold of me; He drew me out of deep waters.

18:17

יַצִּילֵנִיrescued me מֵאֹיְבִיfrom my enemy עָזpowerful וּמִשֹּׂנְאַיfrom foes כִּיfor אָמְצוּmighty מִמֶּנִּיfrom me

He rescued me from my powerful enemy, from foes too mighty for me.

18:18

יְקַדְּמוּנִיconfronted me בְיוֹםin day אֵידִיcalamity וַיְהִיand was יְהוָהLORD לְמִשְׁעָןsupport לִיto me

They confronted me in my day of calamity, but the LORD was my support.

18:19

וַיּוֹצִיאֵנִיhe brought me לַמֶּרְחָבinto the open יְחַלְּצֵנִיrescued me כִּיbecause חָפֵץdelighted בִּיin me

He brought me out into the open; He rescued me because He delighted in me.

18:20

יִגְמְלֵנִיrewarded me יְהוָהLORD כְּצִדְקִיaccording to my righteousness כְּבֹרcleanness יָדַיmy hands יָשִׁיבrepay לִיto

The LORD has rewarded me according to my righteousness; He has repaid me according to the cleanness of my hands.

18:21

כִּיfor שָׁמַרְתִּיkept דַּרְכֵיways יְהוָהLORD וְלֹאand have not רָשַׁעְתִּיwickedly departed מֵאֱלֹהָיfrom my God

For I have kept the ways of the LORD and have not wickedly departed from my God.

18:22

כִּיfor כָלall מִשְׁפָּטָיוhis ordinances לְנֶגְדִּיare before me וְחֻקֹּתָיוhis statutes לֹאnot אָסִירdisregarded מֶנִּיfrom me

For all His ordinances are before me; I have not disregarded His statutes.

18:23

וָאֱהִיbeen תָמִיםblameless עִמּוֹwith him וָאֶשְׁתַּמֵּרand kept מֵעֲוֺנִיmyself from iniquity

And I have been blameless before Him and kept myself from iniquity.

18:24

וַיָּשֶׁבrepaid יְהוָהLORD לִיme to כְצִדְקִיaccording my righteousness כְּבֹרcleanness יָדַיmy hands לְנֶגֶדto before עֵינָיוhis sight

So the LORD has repaid me according to my righteousness, according to the cleanness of my hands in His sight.

18:25

עִםto חָסִידfaithful תִּתְחַסָּדshow עִםwith גְּבַרyourself תָּמִיםblameless תִּתַּמָּםblameless

To the faithful You show Yourself faithful, to the blameless You show Yourself blameless;

18:26

עִםto נָבָרpure תִּתְבָּרָרpure וְעִםbut to עִקֵּשׁcrooked תִּתְפַּתָּלyourself

to the pure You show Yourself pure, but to the crooked You show Yourself shrewd.

18:27

כִּיfor אַתָּהyou עַםpeople עָנִיafflicted תוֹשִׁיעַsave וְעֵינַיִםeyes רָמוֹתhaughty תַּשְׁפִּילhumble

For You save an afflicted people, but You humble those with haughty eyes.

18:28

כִּיfor אַתָּהyou תָּאִירlight נֵרִיlamp my יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהַיGod יַגִּיהַּlights חָשְׁכִּיmy darkness

For You, O LORD, light my lamp; my God lights up my darkness.

18:29

כִּיfor בְךָin you אָרֻץcharge גְּדוּדarmy וּבֵאלֹהַיand with my God אֲדַלֶּגcan שׁוּרwall

For in You I can charge an army, and with my God I can scale a wall.

18:30

הָאֵלGod תָּמִיםperfect דַּרְכּוֹhis way אִמְרַתword יְהוָהLORD צְרוּפָהflawless מָגֵןshield הוּאis לְכֹלto all הַחֹסִיםwho take refuge בּוֹin him

As for God, His way is perfect; the word of the LORD is flawless. He is a shield to all who take refuge in Him.

18:31

כִּיfor מִיwho אֱלוֹהַּGod מִבַּלְעֲדֵיbesides the יְהוָהLORD וּמִיand who צוּרrock זוּלָתִיexcept our אֱלֹהֵינוּour God

For who is God besides the LORD? And who is the Rock except our God?

18:32

הָאֵלit is God הַמְאַזְּרֵנִיwho arms me חָיִלstrength וַיִּתֵּןand gave תָּמִיםmakes דַּרְכִּיmy way

It is God who arms me with strength and makes my way clear.

18:33

מְשַׁוֶּהmakes רַגְלַיmy feet כָּאַיָּלוֹתdeer וְעַלupon בָּמֹתַיstations me יַעֲמִידֵנִיstations

He makes my feet like those of a deer and stations me upon the heights.

18:34

מְלַמֵּדtrains יָדַיmy hands לַמִּלְחָמָהfor battle וְנִחֲתָהbend קֶשֶׁתbow נְחוּשָׁהbronze זְרוֹעֹתָיmy arms

He trains my hands for battle; my arms can bend a bow of bronze.

18:35

וַתִּתֶּןhave given לִיto me מָגֵןshield יִשְׁעֶךָsalvation your וִימִינְךָright hand תִסְעָדֵנִיupholds me וְעַנְוַתְךָand your gentleness תַרְבֵּנִיgentleness exalts me

You have given me Your shield of salvation; Your right hand upholds me, and Your gentleness exalts me.

18:36

תַּרְחִיבbroaden צַעֲדִיpath תַחְתָּיbeneath me וְלֹאdo not מָעֲדוּankles קַרְסֻלָּיmy ankles

You broaden the path beneath me so that my ankles do not give way.

18:37

אֶרְדּוֹףpursued אוֹיְבַיmy enemies וְאַשִּׂיגֵםand overtook them וְלֹאI did not אָשׁוּבturn back עַדuntil כַּלּוֹתָםconsumed

I pursued my enemies and overtook them; I did not turn back until they were consumed.

18:38

אֶמְחָצֵםcrushed them וְלֹאnot יֻכְלוּcould קוּםrise יִפְּלוּfallen תַּחַתunder רַגְלָיmy feet

I crushed them so they could not rise; they have fallen under my feet.

18:39

וַתְּאַזְּרֵנִיyou have armed me חַיִלstrength לַמִּלְחָמָהfor battle תַּכְרִיעַsubdued קָמַיmy foes תַּחְתָּיbeneath me

You have armed me with strength for battle; You have subdued my foes beneath me.

18:40

וְאֹיְבַיmy enemies נָתַתָּהretreat לִּיto me עֹרֶףput וּמְשַׂנְאַיwho hated me אַצְמִיתֵםhated

You have made my enemies retreat before me; I put an end to those who hated me.

18:41

יְשַׁוְּעוּcried וְאֵיןwas no מוֹשִׁיעַsave עַלto יְהוָהLORD וְלֹאnot עָנָםanswer

They cried for help, but there was no one to save them— to the LORD, but He did not answer.

18:42

וְאֶשְׁחָקֵםI ground them כְּעָפָרas dust עַלin פְּנֵיface רוּחַwind כְּטִיטlike mud חוּצוֹתstreets אֲרִיקֵםtrampled them

I ground them as dust in the face of the wind; I trampled them like mud in the streets.

18:43

תְּפַלְּטֵנִיdelivered me מֵרִיבֵיfrom the strife עָםpeople תְּשִׂימֵנִיmade me לְרֹאשׁhead גּוֹיִםnations עַםpeople לֹאnot יָדַעְתִּיknown יַעַבְדוּנִיserve me

You have delivered me from the strife of the people; You have made me the head of nations; a people I had not known shall serve me.

18:44

לְשֵׁמַעwhen they hear אֹזֶןme יִשָּׁמְעוּobey לִיto me בְּנֵיsons נֵכָרforeigners יְכַחֲשׁוּcower לִיto me

When they hear me, they obey me; foreigners cower before me.

18:45

Foreigners lose heart and come trembling from their strongholds.

18:46

חַיlives יְהוָהLORD וּבָרוּךְand blessed צוּרִיmy rock וְיָרוּםexalted אֱלוֹהֵיGod יִשְׁעִיmy salvation

The LORD lives, and blessed be my Rock! And may the God of my salvation be exalted—

18:47

הָאֵלthe God הַנּוֹתֵןthe gives נְקָמוֹתavenges לִיto me וַיַּדְבֵּרand subdues עַמִּיםnations תַּחְתָּיbeneath me

the God who avenges me and subdues nations beneath me,

18:48

מְפַלְּטִיdelivers me מֵאֹיְבָיfrom my enemies אַףhow much מִןfrom קָמַיme my foes תְּרוֹמְמֵנִיexalt מֵאִישׁfrom men חָמָסviolent תַּצִּילֵנִיrescue me

who delivers me from my enemies. You exalt me above my foes; You rescue me from violent men.

18:49

Therefore I will praise You, O LORD, among the nations; I will sing praises to Your name.

18:50

Great salvation He brings to His king. He shows loving devotion to His anointed, to David and his descendants forever.
