Psalms 84

Longing for the courts of the LORD

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. According to Gittith. A Psalm of the sons of Korah

84:1

מַהhow יְּדִידוֹתlovely מִשְׁכְּנוֹתֶיךָyour dwelling יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts

How lovely is Your dwelling place, O LORD of Hosts!

84:2

נִכְסְפָהlongs וְגַםeven כָּלְתָהfaints נַפְשִׁיmy soul לְחַצְרוֹתfor the courts יְהוָהLORD לִבִּיmy heart וּבְשָׂרִיand my flesh יְרַנְּנוּcry אֶלto אֵלGod חָיliving

My soul longs, even faints, for the courts of the LORD; my heart and my flesh cry out for the living God.

84:3

גַּםeven צִפּוֹרsparrow מָצְאָהfound בַיִתhome וּדְרוֹרand the swallow קֵןnest לָהּfor herself אֲשֶׁרthat שָׁתָהplaces אֶפְרֹחֶיהָplaces her young אֶת[obj] מִזְבְּחוֹתֶיךָyour altars O יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts מַלְכִּיmy king וֵאלֹהָיand my God

Even the sparrow has found a home, and the swallow a nest for herself, where she places her young near Your altars, O LORD of Hosts, my King and my God.

84:4

אַשְׁרֵיblessed יוֹשְׁבֵיdwell בֵיתֶךָyour house עוֹדlonger יְהַלְלוּךָpraising you סֶּלָהSelah

How blessed are those who dwell in Your house! They are ever praising You. Selah

84:5

אַשְׁרֵיblessed אָדָםman עוֹזstrength לוֹto him בָךְin you מְסִלּוֹתwhose בִּלְבָבָםwhose hearts are

Blessed are those whose strength is in You, whose hearts are set on pilgrimage.

84:6

עֹבְרֵיpass בְּעֵמֶקthrough the Valley הַבָּכָאof Baca מַעְיָןsprings יְשִׁיתוּהוּmake it גַּםeven בְּרָכוֹתblessings יַעְטֶהrain covers מוֹרֶהautumn

As they pass through the Valley of Baca, they make it a place of springs; even the autumn rain covers it with pools.

84:7

יֵלְכוּgo מֵחַיִלfrom strength אֶלto חָיִלstrength יֵרָאֶהappears אֶלto אֱלֹהִיםGod בְּצִיּוֹןin Zion

They go from strength to strength, until each appears before God in Zion.

84:8

יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהִיםGod צְבָאוֹתHosts שִׁמְעָהhear תְפִלָּתִיmy prayer הַאֲזִינָהgive ear אֱלֹהֵיGod יַעֲקֹבJacob סֶלָהSelah

O LORD God of Hosts, hear my prayer; give ear, O God of Jacob. Selah

84:9

מָגִנֵּנוּour shield רְאֵהnotice אֱלֹהִיםGod וְהַבֵּטand look פְּנֵיface מְשִׁיחֶךָyour anointed

Take notice of our shield, O God, and look with favor on the face of Your anointed.

84:10

כִּיfor טוֹבbetter יוֹםday בַּחֲצֵרֶיךָin your courts מֵאָלֶףthan a thousand בָּחַרְתִּיrather הִסְתּוֹפֵףdoorkeeper בְּבֵיתin the house אֱלֹהַיmy God מִדּוּרthan dwell בְּאָהֳלֵיin the tents רֶשַׁעwicked

For better is one day in Your courts than a thousand elsewhere. I would rather be a doorkeeper in the house of my God than dwell in the tents of the wicked.

84:11

כִּיfor שֶׁמֶשׁsun וּמָגֵןand a shield יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהִיםGod חֵןgrace וְכָבוֹדand glory יִתֵּןhe יְהוָהLORD לֹאno יִמְנַעwithholds טוֹבgood לַהֹלְכִיםfrom those who walk בְּתָמִיםwith integrity

For the LORD God is a sun and a shield; the LORD gives grace and glory; He withholds no good thing from those who walk with integrity.

84:12

יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts אַשְׁרֵיhow blessed אָדָםman בֹּטֵחַtrusts בָּךְin you

O LORD of Hosts, how blessed is the man who trusts in You!
