Psalms 63

Thirsting for God in the wilderness

Open in the interlinear reader

A Psalm of David, when he was in the Wilderness of Judah

63:1

אֱלֹהִיםGod אֵלִיmy God אַתָּהyou אֲשַׁחֲרֶךָּseek you צָמְאָהthirsts לְךָfor you נַפְשִׁיmy soul כָּמַהּyearns לְךָfor you בְשָׂרִיmy body בְּאֶרֶץland צִיָּהdry וְעָיֵףand weary בְּלִיwithout מָיִםwater

O God, You are my God. Earnestly I seek You; my soul thirsts for You. My body yearns for You in a dry and weary land without water.

63:2

כֵּןso בַּקֹּדֶשׁin the sanctuary חֲזִיתִיךָseen you לִרְאוֹתand beheld עֻזְּךָyour power וּכְבוֹדֶךָand glory

So I have seen You in the sanctuary and beheld Your power and glory.

63:3

כִּיbecause טוֹבbetter חַסְדְּךָyour loving devotion מֵחַיִּיםthan life שְׂפָתַיmy lips יְשַׁבְּחוּנְךָglorify you

Because Your loving devotion is better than life, my lips will glorify You.

63:4

כֵּןso אֲבָרֶכְךָbless you בְחַיָּיI live בְּשִׁמְךָin your name אֶשָּׂאlift כַפָּיmy hands

So I will bless You as long as I live; in Your name I will lift my hands.

63:5

כְּמוֹas חֵלֶבfat וָדֶשֶׁןwith the richest תִּשְׂבַּעsatisfied נַפְשִׁיmy soul וְשִׂפְתֵיwith lips רְנָנוֹתjoyful יְהַלֶּלpraise פִּיmy mouth

My soul is satisfied as with the richest of foods; with joyful lips my mouth will praise You.

63:6

אִםif זְכַרְתִּיךָremember you עַלon יְצוּעָיmy bed I בְּאַשְׁמֻרוֹתthrough the watches אֶהְגֶּהthink בָּךְin you

When I remember You on my bed, I think of You through the watches of the night.

63:7

כִּיfor הָיִיתָare עֶזְרָתָהhelp לִּיto me וּבְצֵלin the shadow כְּנָפֶיךָyour wings אֲרַנֵּןjoy

For You are my help; I will sing for joy in the shadow of Your wings.

63:8

דָּבְקָהclings נַפְשִׁיmy soul אַחֲרֶיךָafter you בִּיin me תָּמְכָהupholds יְמִינֶךָyour right hand

My soul clings to You; Your right hand upholds me.

63:9

וְהֵמָּהand they לְשׁוֹאָהto destroy יְבַקְשׁוּseek נַפְשִׁיmy life יָבֹאוּgo בְּתַחְתִּיּוֹתinto the depths הָאָרֶץthe earth

But those who seek my life to destroy it will go into the depths of the earth.

63:10

יַגִּירֻהוּfall עַלto יְדֵיpower חָרֶבsword מְנָתportion שֻׁעָלִיםfoxes יִהְיוּbecome

They will fall to the power of the sword; they will become a portion for foxes.

63:11

וְהַמֶּלֶךְbut the king יִשְׂמַחrejoice בֵּאלֹהִיםin God יִתְהַלֵּלexult כָּלall הַנִּשְׁבָּעwho swear בּוֹby him כִּיfor יִסָּכֵרdeliver פִּיmouths דוֹבְרֵיliars שָׁקֶרshut

But the king will rejoice in God; all who swear by Him will exult, for the mouths of liars will be shut.
