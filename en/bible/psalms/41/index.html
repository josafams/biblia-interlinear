Psalms 41

Sick and betrayed, trusting in God

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David

41:1

אַשְׁרֵיblessed מַשְׂכִּילcares אֶלto דָּלpoor בְּיוֹםin the day רָעָהtrouble יְמַלְּטֵהוּdeliver him יְהוָהLORD

Blessed is the one who cares for the poor; the LORD will deliver him in the day of trouble.

41:2

יְהוָהLORD יִשְׁמְרֵהוּprotect וִיחַיֵּהוּand preserve him וְאֻשַּׁרhe will bless בָּאָרֶץin the land וְאַלand do not תִּתְּנֵהוּsurrender him בְּנֶפֶשׁto the will אֹיְבָיוhis foes

The LORD will protect and preserve him; He will bless him in the land and refuse to surrender him to the will of his foes.

41:3

יְהוָהLORD יִסְעָדֶנּוּsustain him עַלon עֶרֶשׂbed דְּוָיillness כָּלall מִשְׁכָּבוֹhim his bed הָפַכְתָּrestore בְחָלְיוֹsickness

The LORD will sustain him on his bed of illness and restore him from his bed of sickness.

41:4

אֲנִיI אָמַרְתִּיsaid יְהוָהLORD חָנֵּנִיgracious to me רְפָאָהheal נַפְשִׁיmy soul כִּיfor חָטָאתִיsinned לָךְto you

I said, “O LORD, be gracious to me; heal me, for I have sinned against You.”

41:5

אוֹיְבַיmy enemies יֹאמְרוּsay רַעmalice לִיto me מָתַיwhen will יָמוּתdie וְאָבַדand perish שְׁמוֹforgotten

My enemies say with malice: “When will he die and be forgotten?”

41:6

וְאִםand if בָּאcome לִרְאוֹתvisitor שָׁוְאfalsehood יְדַבֵּרspeaks לִבּוֹheart יִקְבָּץgathers אָוֶןslander לוֹto him יֵצֵאout spreads לַחוּץand it abroad יְדַבֵּרspeaks

My visitor speaks falsehood; he gathers slander in his heart; he goes out and spreads it abroad.

41:7

יַחַדtogether עָלַיme against יִתְלַחֲשׁוּwhisper כָּלall שֹׂנְאָיhate עָלַיon me יַחְשְׁבוּimagine רָעָהworst לִיfor me

All who hate me whisper against me; they imagine the worst for me:

41:8

דְּבַרdisease בְּלִיַּעַלvile יָצוּקpoured בּוֹin him וַאֲשֶׁרand what שָׁכַבlies לֹאnot יוֹסִיףnever לָקוּםget up from

“A vile disease has been poured into him; he will never get up from where he lies!”

41:9

גַּםeven אִישׁman שְׁלוֹמִיmy close friend אֲשֶׁרwhom בָּטַחְתִּיtrusted בוֹthe one who אוֹכֵלshared לַחְמִיmy bread הִגְדִּילlifted עָלַיagainst me עָקֵבheel

Even my close friend whom I trusted, the one who shared my bread, has lifted up his heel against me.

41:10

וְאַתָּהbut you יְהוָהLORD חָנֵּנִיgracious to me וַהֲקִימֵנִיand raise me וַאֲשַׁלְּמָהmay repay לָהֶםto them

But You, O LORD, be gracious to me and raise me up, that I may repay them.

41:11

בְּזֹאתby this יָדַעְתִּיknow כִּיthat חָפַצְתָּdelight בִּיin me כִּיfor לֹאnot יָרִיעַtriumph אֹיְבִיmy enemy עָלָיover me

By this I know that You delight in me, for my enemy does not triumph over me.

41:12

וַאֲנִיand I בְּתֻמִּיin my integrity תָּמַכְתָּuphold בִּיin me וַתַּצִּיבֵנִיand set me לְפָנֶיךָin your presence לְעוֹלָםforever

In my integrity You uphold me and set me in Your presence forever.

41:13

בָּרוּךְblessed יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיGod יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael מֵהָעוֹלָםfrom everlasting וְעַדto הָעוֹלָםeverlasting אָמֵןAmen וְאָמֵןand Amen

Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting. Amen and Amen.
