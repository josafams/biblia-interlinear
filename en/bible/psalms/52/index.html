Psalms 52

The end of the mighty who trusts in deceit

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. A Maskil of David. After Doeg the Edomite went to Saul and told him, “David has gone to the house of Ahimelech.”

52:1

מַהwhy תִּתְהַלֵּלboast בְּרָעָהevil הַגִּבּוֹרmighty man the חֶסֶדloving devotion אֵלGod כָּלall הַיּוֹםday long

Why do you boast of evil, O mighty man? The loving devotion of God endures all day long.

52:2

הַוּוֹתdestruction תַּחְשֹׁבdevises לְשׁוֹנֶךָyour tongue כְּתַעַרrazor מְלֻטָּשׁsharpened עֹשֵׂהworker רְמִיָּהdeceit

Your tongue devises destruction like a sharpened razor, O worker of deceit.

52:3

אָהַבְתָּlove רָּעevil מִטּוֹבthan good שֶׁקֶרfalsehood מִדַּבֵּרthan speaking צֶדֶקtruth סֶלָהSelah

You love evil more than good, falsehood more than speaking truth. Selah

52:4

אָהַבְתָּlove כָלevery דִּבְרֵיword בָלַעdevours לְשׁוֹןtongue מִרְמָהdeceitful

You love every word that devours, O deceitful tongue.

52:5

גַּםeven אֵלGod יִתָּצְךָbring you down לָנֶצַחto everlasting יַחְתְּךָsnatch you וְיִסָּחֲךָand tear you מֵאֹהֶלfrom your tent וְשֵׁרֶשְׁךָhe will uproot you מֵאֶרֶץfrom the land חַיִּיםliving סֶלָהSelah

Surely God will bring you down to everlasting ruin; He will snatch you up and tear you away from your tent; He will uproot you from the land of the living. Selah

52:6

וְיִרְאוּwill see צַדִּיקִיםrighteous וְיִירָאוּand fear וְעָלָיוand on him יִשְׂחָקוּmock

The righteous will see and fear; they will mock the evildoer, saying,

52:7

הִנֵּהlook הַגֶּבֶרat the man לֹאnot יָשִׂיםmake אֱלֹהִיםGod מָעוּזּוֹhis refuge וַיִּבְטַחbut trusted בְּרֹבin the abundance עָשְׁרוֹhis wealth יָעֹזstrengthened בְּהַוָּתוֹby destruction

“Look at the man who did not make God his refuge, but trusted in the abundance of his wealth and strengthened himself by destruction.”

52:8

וַאֲנִיbut I am כְּזַיִתlike an olive tree רַעֲנָןflourishing בְּבֵיתin the house אֱלֹהִיםGod בָּטַחְתִּיtrust בְחֶסֶדin the loving devotion אֱלֹהִיםGod עוֹלָםforever וָעֶדand ever

But I am like an olive tree flourishing in the house of God; I trust in the loving devotion of God forever and ever.

52:9

אוֹדְךָpraise you לְעוֹלָםforever כִּיbecause עָשִׂיתָdone וַאֲקַוֶּהit I will wait שִׁמְךָyour name כִיfor טוֹבgood נֶגֶדpresence חֲסִידֶיךָyour saints

I will praise You forever, because You have done it. I will wait on Your name— for it is good— in the presence of Your saints.
