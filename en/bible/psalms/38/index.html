Psalms 38

Sickness, guilt and abandonment

Open in the interlinear reader

A Psalm of David, for remembrance

38:1

יְהוָהLORD אַלdo not בְּקֶצְפְּךָin your anger תוֹכִיחֵנִיrebuke me וּבַחֲמָתְךָin your wrath תְיַסְּרֵנִיdiscipline me

O LORD, do not rebuke me in Your anger or discipline me in Your wrath.

38:2

כִּיfor חִצֶּיךָyour arrows נִחֲתוּpierced בִיin me וַתִּנְחַתdeeply and עָלַיon me יָדֶךָyour hand

For Your arrows have pierced me deeply, and Your hand has pressed down on me.

38:3

אֵיןno מְתֹםsoundness בִּבְשָׂרִיin my body מִפְּנֵיfrom before זַעְמֶךָyour anger אֵיןno שָׁלוֹםrest בַּעֲצָמַיrest in my bones מִפְּנֵיfrom before חַטָּאתִיmy sin

There is no soundness in my body because of Your anger; there is no rest in my bones because of my sin.

38:4

כִּיfor עֲוֺנֹתַיmy iniquities עָבְרוּoverwhelmed רֹאשִׁיmy head כְּמַשָּׂאa burden כָבֵדtoo יִכְבְּדוּheavy מִמֶּנִּיfrom me

For my iniquities have overwhelmed me; they are a burden too heavy to bear.

38:5

הִבְאִישׁוּwounds נָמַקּוּfoul חַבּוּרֹתָיfestering מִפְּנֵיsinful אִוַּלְתִּיmy folly

My wounds are foul and festering because of my sinful folly.

38:6

נַעֲוֵיתִיbent שַׁחֹתִיbrought עַדuntil מְאֹדvery כָּלall הַיּוֹםday קֹדֵרI הִלָּכְתִּיgo about

I am bent and brought low; all day long I go about mourning.

38:7

כִּיfor כְסָלַיmy loins מָלְאוּfull נִקְלֶהburning pain וְאֵיןand no מְתֹםsoundness בִּבְשָׂרִיin my body

For my loins are full of burning pain, and no soundness remains in my body.

38:8

נְפוּגוֹתִיnumb וְנִדְכֵּיתִיand crushed עַדuntil מְאֹדvery שָׁאַגְתִּיgroan מִנַּהֲמַתanguish לִבִּיheart

I am numb and badly crushed; I groan in anguish of heart.

38:9

אֲדֹנָיLord my נֶגְדְּךָbefore you כָלevery תַּאֲוָתִיdesire וְאַנְחָתִיgroaning is מִמְּךָfrom you לֹאnot נִסְתָּרָהhidden

O Lord, my every desire is before You; my groaning is not hidden from You.

38:10

לִבִּיmy heart סְחַרְחַרpounds עֲזָבַנִיforsaken me כֹחִיstrength fails וְאוֹרand even light עֵינַיmy eyes גַּםeven הֵםthey אֵיןno אִתִּיthe

My heart pounds, my strength fails, and even the light of my eyes has faded.

38:11

אֹהֲבַיmy beloved וְרֵעַיand friends my מִנֶּגֶדfrom distance נִגְעִיdisease יַעֲמֹדוּstand וּקְרוֹבַיand my kinsmen מֵרָחֹקat a distance עָמָדוּstand

My beloved and friends shun my disease, and my kinsmen stand at a distance.

38:12

וַיְנַקְשׁוּthose מְבַקְשֵׁיseek נַפְשִׁיmy life וְדֹרְשֵׁיsnares those who רָעָתִיme harm דִּבְּרוּspeak הַוּוֹתdestruction וּמִרְמוֹתplotting deceit כָּלall הַיּוֹםday יֶהְגּוּlong

Those who seek my life lay snares; those who wish me harm speak destruction, plotting deceit all day long.

38:13

וַאֲנִיand I כְחֵרֵשׁlike a deaf לֹאnot אֶשְׁמָעhear וּכְאִלֵּםand like a mute לֹאnot יִפְתַּחopen פִּיוmouth

But like a deaf man, I do not hear; and like a mute man, I do not open my mouth.

38:14

וָאֱהִיI am כְּאִישׁlike a man אֲשֶׁרwho לֹאnot שֹׁמֵעַhear וְאֵיןno בְּפִיוmouth offers תּוֹכָחוֹתreply

I am like a man who cannot hear, whose mouth offers no reply.

38:15

כִּיfor לְךָfor you יְהוָהLORD הוֹחָלְתִּיwait אַתָּהyou תַעֲנֶהanswer אֲדֹנָיLord my אֱלֹהָיGod

I wait for You, O LORD; You will answer, O Lord my God.

38:16

כִּיfor אָמַרְתִּיsaid פֶּןnot יִשְׂמְחוּgloat לִיto me בְּמוֹטwhen my רַגְלִיfoot עָלַיon me הִגְדִּילוּslips

For I said, “Let them not gloat over me— those who taunt me when my foot slips.”

38:17

כִּיfor אֲנִיam לְצֶלַעto fall נָכוֹןready וּמַכְאוֹבִיand my pain נֶגְדִּיbefore me תָמִידever

For I am ready to fall, and my pain is ever with me.

38:18

כִּיfor עֲוֺנִיmy iniquity אַגִּידconfess אֶדְאַגtroubled מֵחַטָּאתִיby my sin

Yes, I confess my iniquity; I am troubled by my sin.

38:19

וְאֹיְבַיmy enemies חַיִּיםwithout עָצֵמוּwithout cause וְרַבּוּand many שֹׂנְאַיhate me שָׁקֶרwithout

Many are my enemies without cause, and many hate me without reason.

38:20

וּמְשַׁלְּמֵיwho repay רָעָהevil תַּחַתunder טוֹבָהgood יִשְׂטְנוּנִיattack me תַּחַתfor רָדְפִיpursuing טוֹבgood

Those who repay my good with evil attack me for pursuing the good.

38:21

אַלdo not תַּעַזְבֵנִיforsake me יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהַיmy God אַלnot תִּרְחַקfar מִמֶּנִּיfrom me

Do not forsake me, O LORD; be not far from me, O my God.

38:22

חוּשָׁהquickly לְעֶזְרָתִיto help me אֲדֹנָיLord my תְּשׁוּעָתִיSavior

Come quickly to help me, O Lord my Savior.
