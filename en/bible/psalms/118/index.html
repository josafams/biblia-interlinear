Psalms 118

Thanksgiving and the rejected stone

Open in the interlinear reader

118:1

הוֹדוּgive thanks לַיהוָהto the LORD כִּיfor טוֹבgood כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good; His loving devotion endures forever.

118:2

יֹאמַרsay נָאlet יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

Let Israel say, “His loving devotion endures forever.”

118:3

יֹאמְרוּsay נָאlet בֵיתhouse אַהֲרֹןAaron כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

Let the house of Aaron say, “His loving devotion endures forever.”

118:4

יֹאמְרוּsay נָאlet יִרְאֵיfear יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

Let those who fear the LORD say, “His loving devotion endures forever.”

118:5

מִןfrom הַמֵּצַרmy distress קָרָאתִיcalled יָּהּLORD עָנָנִיanswered בַמֶּרְחָבin the open יָהּfree

In my distress I called to the LORD, and He answered and set me free.

118:6

יְהוָהLORD לִיto me לֹאnot אִירָאafraid מַהwhat יַּעֲשֶׂהdo לִיto me אָדָםman

The LORD is on my side; I will not be afraid. What can man do to me?

118:7

יְהוָהLORD לִיto me בְּעֹזְרָיmy helper וַאֲנִיtherefore I אֶרְאֶהlook בְשֹׂנְאָיhate me

The LORD is on my side; He is my helper. Therefore I will look in triumph on those who hate me.

118:8

טוֹבbetter לַחֲסוֹתto take refuge בַּיהוָהin the LORD מִבְּטֹחַthan to trust בָּאָדָםin man

It is better to take refuge in the LORD than to trust in man.

118:9

טוֹבbetter לַחֲסוֹתto take refuge בַּיהוָהin the LORD מִבְּטֹחַthan to trust בִּנְדִיבִיםin princes

It is better to take refuge in the LORD than to trust in princes.

118:10

כָּלall גּוֹיִםnations סְבָבוּנִיsurrounded me בְּשֵׁםin the name יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor אֲמִילַםcut them

All the nations surrounded me, but in the name of the LORD I cut them off.

118:11

סַבּוּנִיsurrounded me גַםalso סְבָבוּנִיsurround me בְּשֵׁםin the name יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor אֲמִילַםcut them

They surrounded me on every side, but in the name of the LORD I cut them off.

118:12

סַבּוּנִיaround me כִדְבוֹרִיםlike bees דֹּעֲכוּextinguished כְּאֵשׁlike burning קוֹצִיםthorns בְּשֵׁםin the name יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor אֲמִילַםcut them

They swarmed around me like bees, but they were extinguished like burning thorns; in the name of the LORD I cut them off.

118:13

דַּחֹהthrown דְחִיתַנִיpushed לִנְפֹּלfalling וַיהוָהbut the LORD עֲזָרָנִיhelped me

I was pushed so hard I was falling, but the LORD helped me.

118:14

עָזִּיmy strength my וְזִמְרָתsong and יָהּLORD וַיְהִיbecome לִיto me לִישׁוּעָהsalvation

The LORD is my strength and my song, and He has become my salvation.

118:15

קוֹלresound רִנָּהshouts of joy וִישׁוּעָהand salvation בְּאָהֳלֵיin the tents צַדִּיקִיםrighteous יְמִיןright hand יְהוָהLORD עֹשָׂהperforms חָיִלvalor

Shouts of joy and salvation resound in the tents of the righteous: “The right hand of the LORD performs with valor!

118:16

יְמִיןright hand יְהוָהLORD רוֹמֵמָהexalted יְמִיןright hand יְהוָהLORD עֹשָׂהperforms חָיִלvalor

The right hand of the LORD is exalted! The right hand of the LORD performs with valor!”

118:17

לֹאnot אָמוּתdie כִּיbut אֶחְיֶהlive וַאֲסַפֵּרand proclaim מַעֲשֵׂיwhat יָהּLORD

I will not die, but I will live and proclaim what the LORD has done.

118:18

יַסֹּרdiscipline יִסְּרַנִּיdisciplined me severely יָּהּLORD וְלַמָּוֶתover to death לֹאnot נְתָנָנִיgiven me

The LORD disciplined me severely, but He has not given me over to death.

118:19

פִּתְחוּopen לִיto me שַׁעֲרֵיgates צֶדֶקrighteousness אָבֹאenter בָםin them אוֹדֶהgive thanks יָהּLORD

Open to me the gates of righteousness, that I may enter and give thanks to the LORD.

118:20

זֶהthis הַשַּׁעַרthe gate לַיהוָהof the LORD צַדִּיקִיםrighteous יָבֹאוּenter בוֹin him

This is the gate of the LORD; the righteous shall enter through it.

118:21

אוֹדְךָgive you thanks כִּיfor עֲנִיתָנִיanswered me וַתְּהִיbecome לִיto me לִישׁוּעָהsalvation

I will give You thanks, for You have answered me, and You have become my salvation.

118:22

אֶבֶןstone מָאֲסוּrejected הַבּוֹנִיםthe builders הָיְתָהbecome לְרֹאשׁto head פִּנָּהcornerstone

The stone the builders rejected has become the cornerstone.

118:23

מֵאֵתfrom the יְהוָהLORD הָיְתָהwas זֹּאתthis הִיאis נִפְלָאתmarvelous בְּעֵינֵינוּin our eyes

This is from the LORD, and it is marvelous in our eyes.

118:24

זֶהthis הַיּוֹםthe day עָשָׂהmade יְהוָהLORD נָגִילָהrejoice וְנִשְׂמְחָהand be glad בוֹin it

This is the day that the LORD has made; we will rejoice and be glad in it.

118:25

אָנָּאO יְהוָהLORD הוֹשִׁיעָהsave נָּאplease אָנָּאO יְהוָהLORD הַצְלִיחָהcause נָּאplease

O LORD, save us, we pray. We beseech You, O LORD, cause us to prosper!

118:26

בָּרוּךְblessed הַבָּאwho comes בְּשֵׁםin the name יְהוָהLORD בֵּרַכְנוּכֶםbless you מִבֵּיתfrom the house יְהוָהLORD

Blessed is he who comes in the name of the LORD. From the house of the LORD we bless you.

118:27

אֵלGod יְהוָהLORD וַיָּאֶרlight shine לָנוּto אִסְרוּbind חַגfestal sacrifice בַּעֲבֹתִיםwith cords עַדto קַרְנוֹתhorns הַמִּזְבֵּחַthe altar

The LORD is God; He has made His light to shine upon us. Bind the festal sacrifice with cords to the horns of the altar.

118:28

אֵלִיmy God אַתָּהare וְאוֹדֶךָּgive you thanks you אֱלֹהַיmy God אֲרוֹמְמֶךָּexalt you

You are my God, and I will give You thanks. You are my God, and I will exalt You.

118:29

הוֹדוּgive thanks לַיהוָהto the LORD כִּיfor טוֹבgood כִּיfor לְעוֹלָםendures forever חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion

Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good; His loving devotion endures forever.
