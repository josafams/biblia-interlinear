Psalms 86

The prayer of a poor and needy servant

Open in the interlinear reader

86:1

תְּפִלָּהprayer לְדָוִדof David הַטֵּהincline יְהוָהLORD אָזְנְךָyour ear עֲנֵנִיanswer me כִּיfor עָנִיpoor וְאֶבְיוֹןand needy אָנִיam

A prayer of David. Incline Your ear, O LORD, and answer me, for I am poor and needy.

86:2

שָׁמְרָהpreserve נַפְשִׁיmy soul כִּיfor חָסִידgodly אָנִיam הוֹשַׁעsave עַבְדְּךָyour servant אַתָּהyou אֱלֹהַיmy God הַבּוֹטֵחַwho trusts אֵלֶיךָto you

Preserve my soul, for I am godly. You are my God; save Your servant who trusts in You.

86:3

חָנֵּנִיmerciful to me אֲדֹנָיLord I כִּיfor אֵלֶיךָto you אֶקְרָאcall כָּלall הַיּוֹםday long

Be merciful to me, O Lord, for I call to You all day long.

86:4

שַׂמֵּחַjoy נֶפֶשׁsoul עַבְדֶּךָyour servant כִּיfor אֵלֶיךָto you אֲדֹנָיLord I נַפְשִׁיmy soul אֶשָּׂאlift

Bring joy to Your servant, for to You, O Lord, I lift up my soul.

86:5

כִּיfor אַתָּהyou אֲדֹנָיLord טוֹבkind וְסַלָּחand forgiving וְרַבrich חֶסֶדloving devotion לְכָלto all קֹרְאֶיךָcall on you

For You, O Lord, are kind and forgiving, rich in loving devotion to all who call on You.

86:6

הַאֲזִינָהhear יְהוָהLORD תְּפִלָּתִיmy prayer וְהַקְשִׁיבָהand attend בְּקוֹלto plea תַּחֲנוּנוֹתָיmercy

Hear my prayer, O LORD, and attend to my plea for mercy.

86:7

בְּיוֹםin the day צָרָתִיmy distress אֶקְרָאֶךָּcall on you כִּיbecause תַעֲנֵנִיanswer me

In the day of my distress I call on You, because You answer me.

86:8

אֵיןnone כָּמוֹךָlike you בָאֱלֹהִיםamong the gods אֲדֹנָיO וְאֵיןand no כְּמַעֲשֶׂיךָworks like yours

O Lord, there is none like You among the gods, nor any works like Yours.

86:9

כָּלall גּוֹיִםnations אֲשֶׁרhave עָשִׂיתָmade יָבוֹאוּcome וְיִשְׁתַּחֲווּand bow לְפָנֶיךָbefore you O אֲדֹנָיLord וִיכַבְּדוּglorify לִשְׁמֶךָyour name

All the nations You have made will come and bow before You, O Lord, and they will glorify Your name.

86:10

כִּיfor גָדוֹלgreat אַתָּהyou are וְעֹשֵׂהand perform נִפְלָאוֹתwonders אַתָּהyou are אֱלֹהִיםGod לְבַדֶּךָalone are

For You are great and perform wonders; You alone are God.

86:11

הוֹרֵנִיteach me יְהוָהLORD דַּרְכֶּךָyour way אֲהַלֵּךְwalk בַּאֲמִתֶּךָin your truth give יַחֵדundivided לְבָבִיheart לְיִרְאָהmay fear שְׁמֶךָyour name

Teach me Your way, O LORD, that I may walk in Your truth. Give me an undivided heart, that I may fear Your name.

86:12

אוֹדְךָpraise you אֲדֹנָיLord my אֱלֹהַיGod בְּכָלwith all לְבָבִיheart I וַאֲכַבְּדָהwill glorify שִׁמְךָyour name לְעוֹלָםforever

I will praise You, O Lord my God, with all my heart; I will glorify Your name forever.

86:13

כִּיfor חַסְדְּךָyour loving devotion גָּדוֹלgreat עָלָיto me וְהִצַּלְתָּdelivered נַפְשִׁיhave me מִשְּׁאוֹלSheol תַּחְתִּיָּהdepths

For great is Your loving devotion to me; You have delivered me from the depths of Sheol.

86:14

אֱלֹהִיםGod זֵדִיםarrogant קָמוּrise עָלַיagainst me וַעֲדַתband עָרִיצִיםruthless בִּקְשׁוּseeks נַפְשִׁיmy life וְלֹאwith no שָׂמוּךָregard you לְנֶגְדָּםto before them

The arrogant rise against me, O God; a band of ruthless men seeks my life, with no regard for You.

86:15

וְאַתָּהbut you אֲדֹנָיLord אֵלGod רַחוּםcompassionate וְחַנּוּןand gracious אֶרֶךְslow אַפַּיִםanger וְרַבabounding חֶסֶדloving devotion וֶאֱמֶתand faithfulness

But You, O Lord, are a compassionate and gracious God, slow to anger, abounding in loving devotion and faithfulness.

86:16

פְּנֵהturn אֵלַיto me וְחָנֵּנִיand have mercy תְּנָהgrant עֻזְּךָyour strength לְעַבְדֶּךָto your servant וְהוֹשִׁיעָהsave לְבֶןthe son אֲמָתֶךָyour maidservant

Turn to me and have mercy; grant Your strength to Your servant; save the son of Your maidservant.

86:17

עֲשֵׂהshow עִמִּיwith me אוֹתsign לְטוֹבָהyour goodness וְיִרְאוּmay see שֹׂנְאַיmy enemies וְיֵבֹשׁוּand be ashamed כִּיfor אַתָּהyou יְהוָהLORD עֲזַרְתַּנִיhelped me וְנִחַמְתָּנִיand comforted me

Show me a sign of Your goodness, that my enemies may see and be ashamed; for You, O LORD, have helped me and comforted me.
