Psalms 137

The lament of the exiles in Babylon

Open in the interlinear reader

137:1

עַלon נַהֲרוֹתrivers בָּבֶלBabylon שָׁםthere יָשַׁבְנוּsat גַּםand בָּכִינוּwept בְּזָכְרֵנוּwhen we remembered אֶת[obj] צִיּוֹןZion

By the rivers of Babylon we sat and wept when we remembered Zion.

137:2

עַלon עֲרָבִיםwillows בְּתוֹכָהּin her among תָּלִינוּhung כִּנֹּרוֹתֵינוּour harps

There on the willows we hung our harps,

137:3

כִּיfor שָׁםthere שְׁאֵלוּנוּcounsel us שׁוֹבֵינוּcaptors דִּבְרֵיrequested שִׁירsong וְתוֹלָלֵינוּour tormentors שִׂמְחָהjoy שִׁירוּsing לָנוּto us מִשִּׁירa song צִיּוֹןZion

for there our captors requested a song; our tormentors demanded songs of joy: “Sing us a song of Zion.”

137:4

אֵיךְhow can נָשִׁירsing אֶת[obj] שִׁירsong יְהוָהLORD עַלin אַדְמַתland נֵכָרforeign

How can we sing a song of the LORD in a foreign land?

137:5

אִםif אֶשְׁכָּחֵךְforget you יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem תִּשְׁכַּחcease יְמִינִיmy right hand

If I forget you, O Jerusalem, may my right hand cease to function.

137:6

תִּדְבַּקcling לְשׁוֹנִיtongue לְחִכִּיto the roof of my mouth אִםif לֹאnot אֶזְכְּרֵכִיremember you אִםif לֹאnot אַעֲלֶהexalt אֶת[obj] יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem עַלon רֹאשׁgreatest שִׂמְחָתִיmy greatest joy

May my tongue cling to the roof of my mouth if I do not remember you, if I do not exalt Jerusalem as my greatest joy!

137:7

זְכֹרremember יְהוָהLORD לִבְנֵיthe sons אֱדוֹםEdom אֵת[obj] יוֹםday יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem הָאֹמְרִיםit they said עָרוּpoured עָרוּtear עַדto הַיְסוֹדits foundations בָּהּin her

Remember, O LORD, the sons of Edom on the day Jerusalem fell: “Destroy it,” they said, “tear it down to its foundations!”

137:8

בַּתdaughter בָּבֶלBabylon הַשְּׁדוּדָהdoomed to destruction אַשְׁרֵיblessed שֶׁיְשַׁלֶּםwho repays לָךְto you אֶת[obj] גְּמוּלֵךְrepays שֶׁגָּמַלְתְּdone לָנוּto us

O Daughter of Babylon, doomed to destruction, blessed is he who repays you as you have done to us.

137:9

אַשְׁרֵיblessed שֶׁיֹּאחֵזseizes וְנִפֵּץand dashes אֶת[obj] עֹלָלַיִךְyour infants אֶלagainst הַסָּלַעthe rocks

Blessed is he who seizes your infants and dashes them against the rocks.
