Psalms 45

A song for the king's wedding

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. To the tune of “The Lilies.” A Maskil of the sons of Korah. A love song

45:1

רָחַשׁstirred לִבִּיmy heart דָּבָרword טוֹבnoble אֹמֵרtheme אָנִיI מַעֲשַׂיrecite my לְמֶלֶךְto the king לְשׁוֹנִיmy tongue עֵטpen סוֹפֵרskillful מָהִירwriter

My heart is stirred by a noble theme as I recite my verses to the king; my tongue is the pen of a skillful writer.

45:2

יָפְיָפִיתָhandsome מִבְּנֵיfrom descendants אָדָםmen הוּצַקanointed חֵןgrace בְּשְׂפְתוֹתֶיךָyour lips since עַלon כֵּןso בֵּרַכְךָblessed you אֱלֹהִיםGod לְעוֹלָםforever

You are the most handsome of men; grace has anointed your lips, since God has blessed you forever.

45:3

חֲגוֹרstrap חַרְבְּךָyour sword your עַלon יָרֵךְside גִּבּוֹרmighty warrior הוֹדְךָappear in your majesty וַהֲדָרֶךָand splendor

Strap your sword at your side, O mighty warrior; appear in your majesty and splendor.

45:4

וַהֲדָרְךָin your splendor צְלַחforth רְכַבride עַלon דְּבַרword אֱמֶתtruth וְעַנְוָהand humility צֶדֶקjustice וְתוֹרְךָand teach you נוֹרָאוֹתawesome deeds יְמִינֶךָright hand show your

In your splendor ride forth in victory on behalf of truth and humility and justice; may your right hand show your awesome deeds.

45:5

חִצֶּיךָyour arrows שְׁנוּנִיםpierce עַמִּיםnations תַּחְתֶּיךָbeneath your יִפְּלוּfall בְּלֵבthe hearts אוֹיְבֵיfoes הַמֶּלֶךְthe king’s

Your arrows pierce the hearts of the king’s foes; the nations fall beneath your feet.

45:6

כִּסְאֲךָyour throne אֱלֹהִיםGod עוֹלָםforever וָעֶדand ever שֵׁבֶטtribe מִישֹׁרjustice שֵׁבֶטscepter מַלְכוּתֶךָyour kingdom

Your throne, O God, endures forever and ever, and justice is the scepter of Your kingdom.

45:7

אָהַבְתָּloved צֶּדֶקrighteousness וַתִּשְׂנָאand hated רֶשַׁעwickedness עַלon כֵּןtherefore מְשָׁחֲךָanointed you אֱלֹהִיםGod אֱלֹהֶיךָGod שֶׁמֶןoil שָׂשׂוֹןjoy מֵחֲבֵרֶיךָabove your companions

You have loved righteousness and hated wickedness; therefore God, your God, has anointed you above your companions with the oil of joy.

45:8

מֹרmyrrh וַאֲהָלוֹתand aloes קְצִיעוֹתcassia כָּלall בִּגְדֹתֶיךָyour garments מִןfrom הֵיכְלֵיpalaces שֵׁןivory מִנִּיharps שִׂמְּחוּךָyou glad

All your garments are fragrant with myrrh and aloes and cassia; from palaces of ivory the harps make you glad.

45:9

בְּנוֹתdaughters מְלָכִיםkings בְּיִקְּרוֹתֶיךָamong your honored נִצְּבָהwomen שֵׁגַלqueen לִימִינְךָstands at your right hand בְּכֶתֶםadorned with the gold אוֹפִירOphir

The daughters of kings are among your honored women; the queen stands at your right hand, adorned with the gold of Ophir.

45:10

שִׁמְעִיlisten בַתdaughter וּרְאִיconsider וְהַטִּיand incline אָזְנֵךְyour ear וְשִׁכְחִיforget עַמֵּךְyour people וּבֵיתhouse אָבִיךְyour father’s

Listen, O daughter! Consider and incline your ear: Forget your people and your father’s house,

45:11

וְיִתְאָוdesire הַמֶּלֶךְthe king יָפְיֵךְyour beauty כִּיfor הוּאis אֲדֹנַיִךְyour lord וְהִשְׁתַּחֲוִיbow לוֹto him

and the king will desire your beauty; bow to him, for he is your lord.

45:12

וּבַתdaughter צֹרTyre בְּמִנְחָהwith a gift פָּנַיִךְyour face יְחַלּוּseek favor עֲשִׁירֵיfavor עָםpeople

The Daughter of Tyre will come with a gift; men of wealth will seek your favor.

45:13

כָּלall כְּבוּדָּהglorious בַתprincess מֶלֶךְking פְּנִימָהin מִמִּשְׁבְּצוֹתembroidered זָהָבgold לְבוּשָׁהּchamber her

All glorious is the princess in her chamber; her gown is embroidered with gold.

45:14

לִרְקָמוֹתin colorful garments תּוּבַלled לַמֶּלֶךְto the king בְּתוּלוֹתvirgin אַחֲרֶיהָafter her רֵעוֹתֶיהָcompanions are מוּבָאוֹתbrought לָךְto you

In colorful garments she is led to the king; her virgin companions are brought before you.

45:15

תּוּבַלְנָהled בִּשְׂמָחֹתin with joy וָגִילand gladness תְּבֹאֶינָהenter בְּהֵיכַלthe palace מֶלֶךְking

They are led in with joy and gladness; they enter the palace of the king.

45:16

תַּחַתunder אֲבֹתֶיךָfathers you יִהְיוּwill בָנֶיךָsons תְּשִׁיתֵמוֹmake them לְשָׂרִיםprinces בְּכָלthroughout הָאָרֶץthe land

Your sons will succeed your fathers; you will make them princes throughout the land.

45:17

אַזְכִּירָהcommemorate שִׁמְךָyour name בְּכָלthrough all דֹּרgenerations וָדֹרand generations עַלon כֵּןtherefore עַמִּיםnations יְהוֹדֻךָpraise you לְעֹלָםforever וָעֶדand ever

I will commemorate your name through all generations; therefore the nations will praise you forever and ever.
