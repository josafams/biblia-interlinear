Psalms 24

The entrance of the King of glory

Open in the interlinear reader

24:1

לְדָוִדDavid מִזְמוֹרPsalm לַיהוָהLORD’s הָאָרֶץthe earth is the וּמְלוֹאָהּand the fullness thereof the תֵּבֵלworld וְיֹשְׁבֵיdwell בָהּin her

A Psalm of David. The earth is the LORD’s, and the fullness thereof, the world and all who dwell therein.

24:2

כִּיfor הוּאhe עַלupon יַמִּיםseas יְסָדָהּfounded it וְעַלupon נְהָרוֹתwaters יְכוֹנְנֶהָestablished it

For He has founded it upon the seas and established it upon the waters.

24:3

מִיwho יַעֲלֶהascend בְהַרthe hill יְהוָהLORD וּמִיwho יָקוּםstand בִּמְקוֹםplace קָדְשׁוֹhis holy

Who may ascend the hill of the LORD? Who may stand in His holy place?

24:4

נְקִיclean כַפַּיִםhands וּבַרand a pure לֵבָבheart אֲשֶׁרwho לֹאnot נָשָׂאlift לַשָּׁוְאhis to an idol נַפְשִׁיsoul וְלֹאor נִשְׁבַּעswear לְמִרְמָהdeceitfully

He who has clean hands and a pure heart, who does not lift up his soul to an idol or swear deceitfully.

24:5

יִשָּׂאbear בְרָכָהblessing מֵאֵתfrom the יְהוָהLORD וּצְדָקָהand vindication מֵאֱלֹהֵיfrom the God יִשְׁעוֹhis salvation

He will receive blessing from the LORD and vindication from the God of his salvation.

24:6

זֶהthis דּוֹרgeneration דֹּרְשָׁיוseek him מְבַקְשֵׁיseek פָנֶיךָyour face יַעֲקֹבJacob סֶלָהSelah

Such is the generation of those who seek Him, who seek Your face, O God of Jacob. Selah

24:7

שְׂאוּlift שְׁעָרִיםgates רָאשֵׁיכֶםyour heads וְהִנָּשְׂאוּlifted פִּתְחֵיdoors עוֹלָםancient וְיָבוֹאmay enter מֶלֶךְking הַכָּבוֹדglory

Lift up your heads, O gates! Be lifted up, O ancient doors, that the King of Glory may enter!

24:8

מִיwho זֶהthis מֶלֶךְking הַכָּבוֹדof glory יְהוָהLORD עִזּוּזstrong וְגִבּוֹרand mighty יְהוָהLORD גִּבּוֹרmighty מִלְחָמָהbattle

Who is this King of Glory? The LORD strong and mighty, the LORD mighty in battle.

24:9

שְׂאוּlift שְׁעָרִיםgates רָאשֵׁיכֶםyour heads וּשְׂאוּlifted פִּתְחֵיdoors עוֹלָםancient וְיָבֹאenter מֶלֶךְking הַכָּבוֹדof glory

Lift up your heads, O gates! Be lifted up, O ancient doors, that the King of Glory may enter!

24:10

מִיwho הוּאhe זֶהthis מֶלֶךְking הַכָּבוֹדof glory יְהוָהLORD צְבָאוֹתHosts הוּאis מֶלֶךְking הַכָּבוֹדof glory סֶלָהSelah

Who is He, this King of Glory? The LORD of Hosts— He is the King of Glory. Selah
