Psalms 115

The living God and lifeless idols

Open in the interlinear reader

115:1

לֹאnot לָנוּto us יְהוָהLORD לֹאnot לָנוּto us כִּיbut לְשִׁמְךָto your name תֵּןgive כָּבוֹדglory עַלon חַסְדְּךָyour loving devotion עַלon אֲמִתֶּךָyour faithfulness

Not to us, O LORD, not to us, but to Your name be the glory, because of Your loving devotion, because of Your faithfulness.

115:2

לָמָּהto should יֹאמְרוּsay הַגּוֹיִםthe nations אַיֵּהwhere נָאplease אֱלֹהֵיהֶםtheir God

Why should the nations say, “Where is their God?”

115:3

וֵאלֹהֵינוּour God בַשָּׁמָיִםin heaven כֹּלall אֲשֶׁרthat חָפֵץpleases עָשָׂהdone

Our God is in heaven; He does as He pleases.

115:4

עֲצַבֵּיהֶםtheir idols are כֶּסֶףsilver וְזָהָבand gold מַעֲשֵׂהmade יְדֵיhands אָדָםmen

Their idols are silver and gold, made by the hands of men.

115:5

פֶּהmouths לָהֶםto them וְלֹאand not יְדַבֵּרוּspeak עֵינַיִםeyes לָהֶםto them וְלֹאand not יִרְאוּsee

They have mouths, but cannot speak; they have eyes, but cannot see;

115:6

אָזְנַיִםears לָהֶםto them וְלֹאand not יִשְׁמָעוּhear אַףanger לָהֶםto them וְלֹאand not יְרִיחוּןsmell

they have ears, but cannot hear; they have noses, but cannot smell;

115:7

יְדֵיהֶםthey have hands וְלֹאand not יְמִישׁוּןfeel רַגְלֵיהֶםthey have feet וְלֹאand not יְהַלֵּכוּwalk לֹאnot יֶהְגּוּclear בִּגְרוֹנָםtheir throats

they have hands, but cannot feel; they have feet, but cannot walk; they cannot even clear their throats.

115:8

כְּמוֹהֶםlike them יִהְיוּbecome עֹשֵׂיהֶםmake them כֹּלall אֲשֶׁרwho בֹּטֵחַtrust בָּהֶםin them

Those who make them become like them, as do all who trust in them.

115:9

יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael בְּטַחtrust בַּיהוָהin the LORD עֶזְרָםtheir help וּמָגִנָּםand shield הוּאis

O Israel, trust in the LORD! He is their help and shield.

115:10

בֵּיתhouse אַהֲרֹןAaron בִּטְחוּtrust בַיהוָהin the LORD עֶזְרָםtheir help וּמָגִנָּםand shield הוּאis

O house of Aaron, trust in the LORD! He is their help and shield.

115:11

יִרְאֵיfear יְהוָהLORD בִּטְחוּtrust בַיהוָהin the LORD עֶזְרָםtheir help וּמָגִנָּםand shield הוּאis

You who fear the LORD, trust in the LORD! He is their help and shield.

115:12

יְהוָהLORD זְכָרָנוּmindful of us יְבָרֵךְbless יְבָרֵךְbless אֶת[obj] בֵּיתhouse יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael יְבָרֵךְbless אֶת[obj] בֵּיתhouse אַהֲרֹןAaron

The LORD is mindful of us; He will bless us. He will bless the house of Israel; He will bless the house of Aaron;

115:13

יְבָרֵךְbless יִרְאֵיfear יְהוָהLORD הַקְּטַנִּיםthe small עִםwith הַגְּדֹלִיםgreat alike

He will bless those who fear the LORD— small and great alike.

115:14

יֹסֵףincrease יְהוָהLORD עֲלֵיכֶםon you עֲלֵיכֶםon you וְעַלand on בְּנֵיכֶםyour children

May the LORD give you increase, both you and your children.

115:15

בְּרוּכִיםblessed אַתֶּםyou לַיהוָהby the LORD עֹשֵׂהMaker שָׁמַיִםheaven וָאָרֶץand earth

May you be blessed by the LORD, the Maker of heaven and earth.

115:16

הַשָּׁמַיִםthe highest שָׁמַיִםheavens לַיהוָהbelong to the LORD וְהָאָרֶץbut the earth נָתַןgiven לִבְנֵיto sons אָדָםmankind

The highest heavens belong to the LORD, but the earth He has given to mankind.

115:17

לֹאnot הַמֵּתִיםthe dead יְהַלְלוּpraise יָהּLORD וְלֹאand not כָּלany יֹרְדֵיdescend דוּמָהsilence

It is not the dead who praise the LORD, nor any who descend into silence.

115:18

וַאֲנַחְנוּwe נְבָרֵךְbless יָהּLORD מֵעַתָּהboth now וְעַדand to עוֹלָםforevermore הַלְלוּHallelujah יָהּHallelujah

But it is we who will bless the LORD, both now and forevermore. Hallelujah!
