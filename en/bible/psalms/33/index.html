Psalms 33

Praise to the Creator and Lord of history

Open in the interlinear reader

33:1

רַנְּנוּrejoice צַדִּיקִיםrighteous בַּיהוָהin the LORD לַיְשָׁרִיםfor the upright נָאוָהlovely תְהִלָּהpraise

Rejoice in the LORD, O righteous ones; it is fitting for the upright to praise Him.

33:2

הוֹדוּpraise לַיהוָהthe LORD בְּכִנּוֹרwith the harp בְּנֵבֶלin skin עָשׂוֹרten זַמְּרוּmake music לוֹto him

Praise the LORD with the harp; make music to Him with ten strings.

33:3

שִׁירוּsing לוֹto him שִׁירa song חָדָשׁnew הֵיטִיבוּskillfully נַגֵּןplay skillfully בִּתְרוּעָהwith a shout

Sing to Him a new song; play skillfully with a shout of joy.

33:4

כִּיfor יָשָׁרupright דְּבַרword יְהוָהLORD וְכָלand all מַעֲשֵׂהוּhis work בֶּאֱמוּנָהis trustworthy

For the word of the LORD is upright, and all His work is trustworthy.

33:5

אֹהֵבloves צְדָקָהrighteousness וּמִשְׁפָּטand justice חֶסֶדloving devotion יְהוָהLORD מָלְאָהfull הָאָרֶץthe earth

The LORD loves righteousness and justice; the earth is full of His loving devotion.

33:6

בִּדְבַרby the word יְהוָהLORD שָׁמַיִםheavens נַעֲשׂוּmade וּבְרוּחַby the breath פִּיוhis mouth כָּלall צְבָאָםstars

By the word of the LORD the heavens were made, and all the stars by the breath of His mouth.

33:7

כֹּנֵסpiles כַּנֵּדup the מֵיwaters הַיָּםthe sea נֹתֵןputs בְּאֹצָרוֹתinto storehouses תְּהוֹמוֹתdepths

He piles up the waters of the sea; He puts the depths into storehouses.

33:8

יִירְאוּfear מֵיְהוָהthe LORD כָּלall הָאָרֶץthe earth מִמֶּנּוּof יָגוּרוּrevere כָּלall יֹשְׁבֵיpeople תֵבֵלworld

Let all the earth fear the LORD; let all the people of the world revere Him.

33:9

כִּיfor הוּאhe אָמַרspoke וַיֶּהִיand it came הוּאhe צִוָּהcommanded וַיַּעֲמֹדand it stood

For He spoke, and it came to be; He commanded, and it stood firm.

33:10

יְהוָהLORD הֵפִירfrustrates עֲצַתplans גּוֹיִםnations הֵנִיאthwarts מַחְשְׁבוֹתdevices עַמִּיםpeoples

The LORD frustrates the plans of the nations; He thwarts the devices of the peoples.

33:11

עֲצַתcounsel יְהוָהLORD לְעוֹלָםforever תַּעֲמֹדstands מַחְשְׁבוֹתpurposes לִבּוֹhis heart לְדֹרto all וָדֹרgenerations

The counsel of the LORD stands forever, the purposes of His heart to all generations.

33:12

אַשְׁרֵיblessed הַגּוֹיthe nation אֲשֶׁרthat יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהָיוGod הָעָםthe people בָּחַרchosen לְנַחֲלָהas inheritance לוֹto him

Blessed is the nation whose God is the LORD, the people He has chosen as His inheritance!

33:13

מִשָּׁמַיִםfrom heaven הִבִּיטlooks יְהוָהLORD רָאָהsees אֶת[obj] כָּלall בְּנֵיsons הָאָדָםof men

The LORD looks down from heaven; He sees all the sons of men.

33:14

מִמְּכוֹןplace שִׁבְתּוֹhis dwelling הִשְׁגִּיחַgazes אֶלto כָּלall יֹשְׁבֵיinhabit הָאָרֶץthe earth

From His dwelling place He gazes on all who inhabit the earth.

33:15

הַיֹּצֵרshapes the יַחַדtogether לִבָּםhearts הַמֵּבִיןeach he considers אֶלto כָּלall מַעֲשֵׂיהֶםtheir works

He shapes the hearts of each; He considers all their works.

33:16

אֵיןno הַמֶּלֶךְking נוֹשָׁעsaved בְּרָבby his vast חָיִלarmy גִּבּוֹרwarrior לֹאno יִנָּצֵלdelivered בְּרָבby his great כֹּחַstrength

No king is saved by his vast army; no warrior is delivered by his great strength.

33:17

שֶׁקֶרvain הַסּוּסhorse לִתְשׁוּעָהhope for salvation וּבְרֹבeven great חֵילוֹits strength לֹאnot יְמַלֵּטsave

A horse is a vain hope for salvation; even its great strength cannot save.

33:18

הִנֵּהsurely עֵיןeyes יְהוָהLORD אֶלto יְרֵאָיוfear him לַמְיַחֲלִיםon those whose hope לְחַסְדּוֹin his loving devotion

Surely the eyes of the LORD are on those who fear Him, on those whose hope is in His loving devotion

33:19

לְהַצִּילto deliver מִמָּוֶתfrom death נַפְשָׁםtheir soul וּלְחַיּוֹתָםkeep them alive בָּרָעָבin famine

to deliver them from death and keep them alive in famine.

33:20

נַפְשֵׁנוּour soul חִכְּתָהwaits לַיהוָהfor the LORD עֶזְרֵנוּour help וּמָגִנֵּנוּand our shield הוּאis

Our soul waits for the LORD; He is our help and our shield.

33:21

כִּיfor בוֹin him יִשְׂמַחrejoice לִבֵּנוּour hearts כִּיfor בְשֵׁםname קָדְשׁוֹhis holy בָטָחְנוּtrust

For our hearts rejoice in Him, since we trust in His holy name.

33:22

יְהִיmay חַסְדְּךָyour loving devotion יְהוָהLORD עָלֵינוּon us כַּאֲשֶׁרlike had יִחַלְנוּput our hope לָךְto you

May Your loving devotion rest on us, O LORD, as we put our hope in You.
