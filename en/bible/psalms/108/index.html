Psalms 108

Praise and confidence in victory

Open in the interlinear reader

A song. A Psalm of David

108:1

נָכוֹןis steadfast לִבִּיmy heart אֱלֹהִיםGod אָשִׁירָהsing וַאֲזַמְּרָהand make music אַףwith כְּבוֹדִיall my

My heart is steadfast, O God; I will sing and make music with all my being.

108:2

עוּרָהawake הַנֵּבֶלharp וְכִנּוֹרand lyre אָעִירָהawaken שָּׁחַרdawn

Awake, O harp and lyre! I will awaken the dawn.

108:3

אוֹדְךָpraise you בָעַמִּיםamong the nations יְהוָהLORD וַאֲזַמֶּרְךָwill sing your praises בַּלnot אֻמִּיםpeoples

I will praise You, O LORD, among the nations; I will sing Your praises among the peoples.

108:4

כִּיfor גָדוֹלgreat מֵעַלfrom the שָׁמַיִםheavens חַסְדֶּךָyour loving devotion וְעַדto שְׁחָקִיםreaches אֲמִתֶּךָyour faithfulness

For Your loving devotion extends beyond the heavens, and Your faithfulness reaches to the clouds.

108:5

רוּמָהexalted עַלon שָׁמַיִםheavens אֱלֹהִיםGod וְעַלand on כָּלall הָאָרֶץthe earth כְּבוֹדֶךָyour glory

Be exalted, O God, above the heavens; may Your glory cover all the earth.

108:6

לְמַעַןmay יֵחָלְצוּןdelivered יְדִידֶיךָyour beloved הוֹשִׁיעָהsave יְמִינְךָyour right hand וַעֲנֵנִיrespond and us

Respond and save us with Your right hand, that Your beloved may be delivered.

108:7

אֱלֹהִיםGod דִּבֶּרspoken בְּקָדְשׁוֹfrom his sanctuary אֶעְלֹזָהtriumph אֲחַלְּקָהparcel שְׁכֶםShechem וְעֵמֶקValley סֻכּוֹתSuccoth אֲמַדֵּדapportion

God has spoken from His sanctuary: “I will triumph! I will parcel out Shechem and apportion the Valley of Succoth.

108:8

לִיto me גִלְעָדGilead לִיto me מְנַשֶּׁהManasseh וְאֶפְרַיִםEphraim מָעוֹזis רֹאשִׁיmy helmet יְהוּדָהJudah מְחֹקְקִיmy scepter

Gilead is Mine, and Manasseh is Mine; Ephraim is My helmet, Judah is My scepter.

108:9

מוֹאָבMoab סִירis רַחְצִיmy washbasin עַלupon אֱדוֹםEdom אַשְׁלִיךְtoss נַעֲלִיmy sandal עֲלֵיover פְלֶשֶׁתPhilistia אֶתְרוֹעָעshout

Moab is My washbasin; upon Edom I toss My sandal; over Philistia I shout in triumph.”

108:10

מִיwho יֹבִלֵנִיbring me עִירcity מִבְצָרfortified מִיwho נָחַנִיlead me עַדto אֱדוֹםEdom

Who will bring me to the fortified city? Who will lead me to Edom?

108:11

הֲלֹאyou not אֱלֹהִיםGod זְנַחְתָּנוּrejected us וְלֹאwill you no תֵצֵאmarch אֱלֹהִיםGod בְּצִבְאֹתֵינוּwith our armies

Have You not rejected us, O God? Will You no longer march out, O God, with our armies?

108:12

הָבָהgive לָּנוּto us עֶזְרָתaid מִצָּרagainst the enemy וְשָׁוְאworthless תְּשׁוּעַתhelp אָדָםman

Give us aid against the enemy, for the help of man is worthless.

108:13

בֵּאלֹהִיםwith God נַעֲשֶׂהperform חָיִלvalor וְהוּאand he יָבוּסtrample צָרֵינוּour enemies

With God we will perform with valor, and He will trample our enemies.
