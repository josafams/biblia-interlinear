Psalms 99

The holy King of Israel

Open in the interlinear reader

99:1

יְהוָהLORD מָלָךְreigns יִרְגְּזוּtremble עַמִּיםnations יֹשֵׁבenthroned כְּרוּבִיםcherubim תָּנוּטabove הָאָרֶץthe earth

The LORD reigns; let the nations tremble! He is enthroned above the cherubim; let the earth quake!

99:2

יְהוָהLORD בְּצִיּוֹןin Zion גָּדוֹלgreat וְרָםhe exalted הוּאis עַלon כָּלall הָעַמִּיםthe peoples

Great is the LORD in Zion; He is exalted above all the peoples.

99:3

יוֹדוּpraise שִׁמְךָname גָּדוֹלgreat וְנוֹרָאand awesome קָדוֹשׁholy הוּאis

Let them praise Your great and awesome name— He is holy!

99:4

וְעֹזmighty מֶלֶךְking מִשְׁפָּטjustice אָהֵבloves אַתָּהyou כּוֹנַנְתָּestablished מֵישָׁרִיםequity מִשְׁפָּטjustice וּצְדָקָהand righteousness בְּיַעֲקֹבin Jacob אַתָּהyou עָשִׂיתָexercised

The mighty King loves justice. You have established equity; You have exercised justice and righteousness in Jacob.

99:5

רוֹמְמוּexalt יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God וְהִשְׁתַּחֲווּand worship לַהֲדֹםat footstool רַגְלָיוhis feet קָדוֹשׁholy הוּאis

Exalt the LORD our God, and worship at His footstool; He is holy!

99:6

מֹשֶׁהMoses וְאַהֲרֹןand Aaron בְּכֹהֲנָיוamong his priests וּשְׁמוּאֵלSamuel בְּקֹרְאֵיcalled שְׁמוֹhis name קֹרִאיםcalled אֶלto יְהוָהLORD וְהוּאand he יַעֲנֵםanswered

Moses and Aaron were among His priests; Samuel was among those who called on His name. They called to the LORD and He answered.

99:7

בְּעַמּוּדfrom the pillar עָנָןcloud יְדַבֵּרspoke אֲלֵיהֶםto them שָׁמְרוּkept עֵדֹתָיוhis decrees וְחֹקand the statutes נָתַןgave לָמוֹto them

He spoke to them from the pillar of cloud; they kept His decrees and the statutes He gave them.

99:8

יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God אַתָּהyou עֲנִיתָםanswered them אֵלGod נֹשֵׂאforgiving הָיִיתָwere לָהֶםto them וְנֹקֵםyet an avenger עַלon עֲלִילוֹתָםtheir misdeeds

O LORD our God, You answered them. You were a forgiving God to them, yet an avenger of their misdeeds.

99:9

רוֹמְמוּexalt יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God וְהִשְׁתַּחֲווּand worship לְהַרmountain קָדְשׁוֹhis holy כִּיfor קָדוֹשׁholy יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵינוּour God

Exalt the LORD our God and worship at His holy mountain, for the LORD our God is holy.
