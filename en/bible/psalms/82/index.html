Psalms 82

God judges the gods

Open in the interlinear reader

82:1

מִזְמוֹרPsalm לְאָסָףof Asaph אֱלֹהִיםGod נִצָּבpresides בַּעֲדַתassembly אֵלrenders בְּקֶרֶבamong אֱלֹהִיםgods יִשְׁפֹּטjudgment

A Psalm of Asaph. God presides in the divine assembly; He renders judgment among the gods:

82:2

עַדhow מָתַיlong תִּשְׁפְּטוּjudge עָוֶלunjustly וּפְנֵיand show partiality רְשָׁעִיםwicked תִּשְׂאוּbear סֶלָהSelah

“How long will you judge unjustly and show partiality to the wicked? Selah

82:3

שִׁפְטוּdefend דַלweak וְיָתוֹםand fatherless עָנִיafflicted oppressed וָרָשׁand oppressed הַצְדִּיקוּrights

Defend the cause of the weak and fatherless; uphold the rights of the afflicted and oppressed.

82:4

פַּלְּטוּsave דַלweak וְאֶבְיוֹןand needy מִיַּדfrom the hand רְשָׁעִיםwicked הַצִּילוּrescue

Rescue the weak and needy; save them from the hand of the wicked.

82:5

לֹאnot יָדְעוּknow וְלֹאor יָבִינוּunderstand בַּחֲשֵׁכָהin the darkness יִתְהַלָּכוּwander יִמּוֹטוּshaken כָּלall מוֹסְדֵיfoundations אָרֶץearth

They do not know or understand; they wander in the darkness; all the foundations of the earth are shaken.

82:6

אֲנִיI אָמַרְתִּיsaid אֱלֹהִיםgods אַתֶּםyou וּבְנֵיsons עֶלְיוֹןMost high כֻּלְּכֶםall

I have said, ‘You are gods; you are all sons of the Most High.’

82:7

אָכֵןbut כְּאָדָםlike mortals תְּמוּתוּןdie וּכְאַחַדand like one הַשָּׂרִיםrulers תִּפֹּלוּfall

But like mortals you will die, and like rulers you will fall.”

82:8

קוּמָהarise אֱלֹהִיםGod שָׁפְטָהjudge הָאָרֶץthe earth כִּיfor אַתָּהare תִנְחַלinheritance בְּכָלall הַגּוֹיִםthe nations

Arise, O God, judge the earth, for all the nations are Your inheritance.
