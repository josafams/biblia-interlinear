Psalms 124

The people escape like a bird from the snare

Open in the interlinear reader

שִׁירsong הַמַּעֲלוֹתof ascents לְדָוִדof David לוּלֵיif יְהוָהLORD שֶׁהָיָהnot been לָנוּto us יֹאמַרdeclare נָאlet now יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

A song of ascents. Of David. If the LORD had not been on our side— let Israel now declare—

if the LORD had not been on our side when men attacked us,

אֲזַיthen חַיִּיםalive בְּלָעוּנוּswallowed us בַּחֲרוֹתwhen anger אַפָּםtheir anger flared בָּנוּagainst us

when their anger flared against us, then they would have swallowed us alive,

אֲזַיthen הַמַּיִםthe floods שְׁטָפוּנוּengulfed us נַחְלָהtorrent עָבַרoverwhelmed עַלon נַפְשֵׁנוּhave us

then the floods would have engulfed us, then the torrent would have overwhelmed us,

then the raging waters would have swept us away.

124:6

Blessed be the LORD, who has not given us as prey to their teeth.

124:7

We have escaped like a bird from the snare of the fowler; the net is torn, and we have slipped away.

124:8

עֶזְרֵנוּour help בְּשֵׁםin the name יְהוָהLORD עֹשֵׂהMaker שָׁמַיִםheaven וָאָרֶץand earth

Our help is in the name of the LORD, the Maker of heaven and earth.
