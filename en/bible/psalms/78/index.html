Psalms 78

Lessons from the history of Israel

Open in the interlinear reader

78:1

מַשְׂכִּילMaskil לְאָסָףof Asaph הַאֲזִינָהgive ear O עַמִּיmy people תּוֹרָתִיmy instruction הַטּוּincline אָזְנְכֶםyour ear לְאִמְרֵיto the words פִיmy mouth

A Maskil of Asaph. Give ear, O my people, to my instruction; listen to the words of my mouth.

78:2

אֶפְתְּחָהopen בְמָשָׁלin parables פִּיmy mouth אַבִּיעָהutter חִידוֹתhidden מִנִּיfrom קֶדֶםbeginning

I will open my mouth in parables; I will utter things hidden from the beginning,

78:3

אֲשֶׁרthat שָׁמַעְנוּheard וַנֵּדָעֵםand known וַאֲבוֹתֵינוּand our fathers סִפְּרוּrelayed לָנוּto us

that we have heard and known and our fathers have relayed to us.

78:4

לֹאnot נְכַחֵדhide מִבְּנֵיהֶםfrom their children לְדוֹרgeneration אַחֲרוֹןnext מְסַפְּרִיםdeclare תְּהִלּוֹתpraises יְהוָהLORD וֶעֱזוּזוֹand his might וְנִפְלְאוֹתָיוand the wonders he אֲשֶׁרhas עָשָׂהperformed

We will not hide them from their children, but will declare to the next generation the praises of the LORD and His might, and the wonders He has performed.

78:5

וַיָּקֶםhe established עֵדוּתtestimony בְּיַעֲקֹבin Jacob וְתוֹרָהlaw שָׂםappointed בְּיִשְׂרָאֵלin Israel אֲשֶׁרwhich צִוָּהcommanded אֶת[obj] אֲבוֹתֵינוּour fathers לְהוֹדִיעָםto teach their לִבְנֵיהֶםto children

For He established a testimony in Jacob and appointed a law in Israel, which He commanded our fathers to teach to their children,

78:6

לְמַעַןso יֵדְעוּknow דּוֹרgeneration would אַחֲרוֹןthem בָּנִיםchildren יִוָּלֵדוּborn יָקֻמוּarise וִיסַפְּרוּand tell לִבְנֵיהֶםtheir own children

that the coming generation would know them— even children yet to be born— to arise and tell their own children

78:7

וְיָשִׂימוּput בֵאלֹהִיםin God כִּסְלָםtheir confidence וְלֹאnot יִשְׁכְּחוּforgetting מַעַלְלֵיworks אֵלhis וּמִצְוֺתָיוbut his commandments יִנְצֹרוּpreserve

that they should put their confidence in God, not forgetting His works, but keeping His commandments.

78:8

וְלֹאnot יִהְיוּbe כַּאֲבוֹתָםlike their fathers דּוֹרgeneration סוֹרֵרstubborn וּמֹרֶהand rebellious דּוֹרgeneration לֹאnot הֵכִיןloyal לִבּוֹheart was וְלֹאwas not נֶאֶמְנָהfaithful אֶתto אֵלGod רוּחוֹspirit was

Then they will not be like their fathers, a stubborn and rebellious generation, whose heart was not loyal, whose spirit was not faithful to God.

78:9

בְּנֵיsons אֶפְרַיִםEphraim נוֹשְׁקֵיarchers רוֹמֵיarchers קָשֶׁתarchers הָפְכוּturned בְּיוֹםon the day קְרָבbattle

The archers of Ephraim turned back on the day of battle.

78:10

לֹאnot שָׁמְרוּkeep בְּרִיתcovenant אֱלֹהִיםGod’s וּבְתוֹרָתוֹby his law מֵאֲנוּrefused לָלֶכֶתto go

They failed to keep God’s covenant and refused to live by His law.

78:11

וַיִּשְׁכְּחוּthey forgot עֲלִילוֹתָיוwhat he וְנִפְלְאוֹתָיוwonders he אֲשֶׁרhad הֶרְאָםshown them

They forgot what He had done, the wonders He had shown them.

78:12

נֶגֶדbefore אֲבוֹתָםtheir fathers עָשָׂהworked פֶלֶאwonders בְּאֶרֶץin the land מִצְרַיִםEgypt שְׂדֵהregion צֹעַןZoan

He worked wonders before their fathers in the land of Egypt, in the region of Zoan.

78:13

בָּקַעsplit יָםsea וַיַּעֲבִירֵםand brought them through וַיַּצֶּבhe set מַיִםwaters כְּמוֹlike נֵדwall

He split the sea and brought them through; He set the waters upright like a wall.

78:14

וַיַּנְחֵםhe led them בֶּעָנָןwith a cloud יוֹמָםby day וְכָלall הַלַּיְלָהnight בְּאוֹרwith a light אֵשׁfire

He led them with a cloud by day and with a light of fire all night.

78:15

יְבַקַּעsplit צֻרִיםrocks בַּמִּדְבָּרin the wilderness וַיַּשְׁקְand gave them drink כִּתְהֹמוֹתas the seas רַבָּהabundant

He split the rocks in the wilderness and gave them drink as abundant as the seas.

78:16

וַיּוֹצִאhe brought נוֹזְלִיםstreams מִסָּלַעfrom the stone וַיּוֹרֶדdown כַּנְּהָרוֹתlike rivers מָיִםwater

He brought streams from the stone and made water flow down like rivers.

78:17

וַיּוֹסִיפוּbut they continued עוֹדlonger לַחֲטֹאto sin לוֹto him לַמְרוֹתrebelling עֶלְיוֹןMost high בַּצִּיָּהin the desert

But they continued to sin against Him, rebelling in the desert against the Most High.

78:18

וַיְנַסּוּthey willfully tested אֵלGod בִּלְבָבָםin their heart לִשְׁאָלdemanding אֹכֶלfood לְנַפְשָׁםthey craved

They willfully tested God by demanding the food they craved.

78:19

וַיְדַבְּרוּthey spoke בֵּאלֹהִיםagainst God אָמְרוּsaying הֲיוּכַלcan אֵלGod לַעֲרֹךְprepare שֻׁלְחָןtable בַּמִּדְבָּרin the wilderness

They spoke against God, saying, “Can God really prepare a table in the wilderness?

78:20

הֵןbehold הִכָּהstruck צוּרrock וַיָּזוּבוּgushed מַיִםwater וּנְחָלִיםand torrents raged יִשְׁטֹפוּtorrents הֲגַםcan he also לֶחֶםbread יוּכַלcannot תֵּתgive אִםor יָכִיןpiles שְׁאֵרmeat לְעַמּוֹsupply his people

When He struck the rock, water gushed out and torrents raged. But can He also give bread or supply His people with meat?”

78:21

לָכֵןtherefore שָׁמַעheard יְהוָהLORD וַיִּתְעַבָּרwrath וְאֵשׁso a fire נִשְּׂקָהkindled בְיַעֲקֹבagainst Jacob וְגַםand also אַףanger עָלָהflared בְיִשְׂרָאֵלagainst Israel

Therefore the LORD heard and was filled with wrath; so a fire was kindled against Jacob, and His anger flared against Israel,

78:22

כִּיbecause לֹאnot הֶאֱמִינוּbelieve בֵּאלֹהִיםGod וְלֹאor בָטְחוּrely בִּישׁוּעָתוֹon his salvation

because they did not believe God or rely on His salvation.

78:23

וַיְצַוyet he commanded שְׁחָקִיםclouds מִמָּעַלabove וְדַלְתֵיdoors שָׁמַיִםheavens פָּתָחopened

Yet He commanded the clouds above and opened the doors of the heavens.

78:24

וַיַּמְטֵרhe rained עֲלֵיהֶםfor them מָןmanna לֶאֱכֹלto eat וּדְגַןgrain שָׁמַיִםheaven נָתַןgave לָמוֹto them

He rained down manna for them to eat; He gave them grain from heaven.

78:25

לֶחֶםbread אַבִּירִיםbulls אָכַלate אִישׁman צֵידָהangels שָׁלַחsent לָהֶםto them לָשֹׂבַעin abundance

Man ate the bread of angels; He sent them food in abundance.

78:26

יַסַּעstirred קָדִיםeast wind בַּשָּׁמָיִםfrom the heavens וַיְנַהֵגand drove בְּעֻזּוֹby his might תֵימָןsouth wind

He stirred the east wind from the heavens and drove the south wind by His might.

78:27

וַיַּמְטֵרhe rained עֲלֵיהֶםon them כֶּעָפָרlike dust שְׁאֵרmeat וּכְחוֹלlike the sand יַמִּיםsea עוֹףbirds כָּנָףwinged

He rained meat on them like dust, and winged birds like the sand of the sea.

78:28

וַיַּפֵּלhe felled בְּקֶרֶבin the midst מַחֲנֵהוּtheir camp סָבִיבaround לְמִשְׁכְּנֹתָיוtheir dwellings

He felled them in the midst of their camp, all around their dwellings.

78:29

וַיֹּאכְלוּso they ate וַיִּשְׂבְּעוּfilled מְאֹדvery וְתַאֲוָתָםfor he gave them יָבִאbring לָהֶםto them

So they ate and were well filled, for He gave them what they craved.

78:30

לֹאnot זָרוּfilled מִתַּאֲוָתָםhad their desire עוֹדstill אָכְלָםfood בְּפִיהֶםin mouths

Yet before they had filled their desire, with the food still in their mouths,

78:31

וְאַףanger אֱלֹהִיםGod’s עָלָהflared בָהֶםagainst them וַיַּהֲרֹגand he put בְּמִשְׁמַנֵּיהֶםdeath their strongest וּבַחוּרֵיyoung men יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael הִכְרִיעַsubdued

God’s anger flared against them, and He put to death their strongest and subdued the young men of Israel.

78:32

בְּכָלall זֹאתthis חָטְאוּsinning עוֹדlonger וְלֹאand not הֶאֱמִינוּbelieve בְּנִפְלְאוֹתָיוdespite his wonderful works

In spite of all this, they kept on sinning; despite His wonderful works, they did not believe.

78:33

וַיְכַלso he ended בַּהֶבֶלin futility יְמֵיהֶםtheir days וּשְׁנוֹתָםand their years בַּבֶּהָלָהin sudden terror

So He ended their days in futility, and their years in sudden terror.

78:34

אִםif הֲרָגָםslew them וּדְרָשׁוּהוּthey would seek him וְשָׁבוּthey repented וְשִׁחֲרוּand searched אֵלGod

When He slew them, they would seek Him; they repented and searched for God.

78:35

וַיִּזְכְּרוּand they remembered כִּיthat אֱלֹהִיםGod צוּרָםtheir rock וְאֵלthat God עֶלְיוֹןMost high גֹּאֲלָםtheir Redeemer

And they remembered that God was their Rock, that God Most High was their Redeemer.

78:36

וַיְפַתּוּהוּbut they deceived him בְּפִיהֶםwith their mouths וּבִלְשׁוֹנָםwith their tongues יְכַזְּבוּlied לוֹto him

But they deceived Him with their mouths, and lied to Him with their tongues.

78:37

וְלִבָּםtheir hearts were לֹאnot נָכוֹןdisloyal עִמּוֹto him וְלֹאand not נֶאֶמְנוּunfaithful בִּבְרִיתוֹto his covenant

Their hearts were disloyal to Him, and they were unfaithful to His covenant.

78:38

וְהוּאand he was רַחוּםcompassionate יְכַפֵּרforgave עָוֺןiniquity וְלֹאand not יַשְׁחִיתdestroy וְהִרְבָּהhe often לְהָשִׁיבrestrained אַפּוֹhis anger וְלֹאand not יָעִירstirs כָּלfull חֲמָתוֹunleash his wrath

And yet He was compassionate; He forgave their iniquity and did not destroy them. He often restrained His anger and did not unleash His full wrath.

78:39

וַיִּזְכֹּרhe remembered כִּיthat בָשָׂרflesh הֵמָּהthey רוּחַpassing breeze הוֹלֵךְwalks וְלֹאthat does not יָשׁוּבreturn

He remembered that they were but flesh, a passing breeze that does not return.

78:40

כַּמָּהhow they יַמְרוּהוּoften disobeyed him בַמִּדְבָּרin the wilderness יַעֲצִיבוּהוּgrieved him בִּישִׁימוֹןin the desert

How often they disobeyed Him in the wilderness and grieved Him in the desert!

78:41

וַיָּשׁוּבוּagain and again וַיְנַסּוּthey tested אֵלGod וּקְדוֹשׁholy יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael הִתְווּprovoked

Again and again they tested God and provoked the Holy One of Israel.

78:42

לֹאnot זָכְרוּremember אֶת[obj] יָדוֹhis power יוֹםday אֲשֶׁרthat פָּדָםredeemed them מִנִּיfrom צָרadversary

They did not remember His power— the day He redeemed them from the adversary,

78:43

אֲשֶׁרthat שָׂםperformed בְּמִצְרַיִםin Egypt אֹתוֹתָיוhis signs וּמוֹפְתָיוand his wonders בִּשְׂדֵהin the fields צֹעַןZoan

when He performed His signs in Egypt and His wonders in the fields of Zoan.

78:44

וַיַּהֲפֹךְhe turned לְדָםto blood יְאֹרֵיהֶםtheir rivers וְנֹזְלֵיהֶםand from their streams בַּלnot יִשְׁתָּיוּןdrink

He turned their rivers to blood, and from their streams they could not drink.

78:45

יְשַׁלַּחsent בָּהֶםin them עָרֹבflies וַיֹּאכְלֵםthat devoured them וּצְפַרְדֵּעַand frogs וַתַּשְׁחִיתֵםthat devastated them

He sent swarms of flies that devoured them, and frogs that devastated them.

78:46

וַיִּתֵּןhe gave לֶחָסִילto the grasshopper יְבוּלָםfruit of their וִיגִיעָםlabor לָאַרְבֶּהto the locust

He gave their crops to the grasshopper, the fruit of their labor to the locust.

78:47

יַהֲרֹגkilled בַּבָּרָדwith hailstones גַּפְנָםtheir vines וְשִׁקְמוֹתָםand their sycamore figs בַּחֲנָמַלwith sleet

He killed their vines with hailstones and their sycamore-figs with sleet.

78:48

וַיַּסְגֵּרhe abandoned לַבָּרָדto the hail בְּעִירָםlivestock וּמִקְנֵיהֶםand their livestock לָרְשָׁפִיםto bolts of lightning

He abandoned their cattle to the hail and their livestock to bolts of lightning.

78:49

יְשַׁלַּחunleashed בָּםagainst them חֲרוֹןwrath אַפּוֹhis anger עֶבְרָהfury וָזַעַםindignation וְצָרָהand calamity מִשְׁלַחַתdestroying מַלְאֲכֵיangels רָעִיםwicked

He unleashed His fury against them, wrath, indignation, and calamity— a band of destroying angels.

78:50

יְפַלֵּסcleared נָתִיבpath לְאַפּוֹfor his anger he לֹאnot חָשַׂךְspare מִמָּוֶתfrom death נַפְשָׁםtheir soul וְחַיָּתָםbut their lives לַדֶּבֶרto the plague הִסְגִּירdelivered

He cleared a path for His anger; He did not spare them from death but delivered their lives to the plague.

78:51

וַיַּךְhe struck כָּלall בְּכוֹרfirstborn בְּמִצְרָיִםEgypt רֵאשִׁיתfirstfruits אוֹנִיםvirility בְּאָהֳלֵיin the tents חָםHam

He struck all the firstborn of Egypt, the virility in the tents of Ham.

78:52

וַיַּסַּעhe led כַּצֹּאןlike sheep עַמּוֹhis people וַיְנַהֲגֵםand guided them כַּעֵדֶרlike a flock בַּמִּדְבָּרin the wilderness

He led out His people like sheep and guided them like a flock in the wilderness.

78:53

וַיַּנְחֵםhe led them לָבֶטַחsafely וְלֹאnot פָחָדוּfear וְאֶתand [obj] אוֹיְבֵיהֶםtheir enemies כִּסָּהengulfed הַיָּםthe sea

He led them safely, so they did not fear, but the sea engulfed their enemies.

78:54

וַיְבִיאֵםhe brought them אֶלto גְּבוּלland קָדְשׁוֹhis holy הַרmountain זֶהthe קָנְתָהacquired יְמִינוֹhis right hand had

He brought them to His holy land, to the mountain His right hand had acquired.

78:55

וַיְגָרֶשׁhe drove מִפְּנֵיהֶםout before them גּוֹיִםnations וַיַּפִּילֵםand apportioned their בְּחֶבֶלin region נַחֲלָהinheritance וַיַּשְׁכֵּןhe settled בְּאָהֳלֵיהֶםin their tents שִׁבְטֵיtribes יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

He drove out nations before them and apportioned their inheritance; He settled the tribes of Israel in their tents.

78:56

וַיְנַסּוּbut they tested וַיַּמְרוּand disobeyed אֶת[obj] אֱלֹהִיםGod עֶלְיוֹןMost high וְעֵדוֹתָיוhis decrees לֹאnot שָׁמָרוּkeep

But they tested and disobeyed God Most High, for they did not keep His decrees.

78:57

וַיִּסֹּגוּthey turned וַיִּבְגְּדוּand were faithless כַּאֲבוֹתָםlike their fathers נֶהְפְּכוּtwisted כְּקֶשֶׁתbow רְמִיָּהfaulty

They turned back and were faithless like their fathers, twisted like a faulty bow.

78:58

וַיַּכְעִיסוּהוּthey enraged him בְּבָמוֹתָםwith their high places וּבִפְסִילֵיהֶםwith their idols יַקְנִיאוּהוּprovoked his jealousy

They enraged Him with their high places and provoked His jealousy with their idols.

78:59

שָׁמַעhearing אֱלֹהִיםGod וַיִּתְעַבָּרwas furious וַיִּמְאַסand rejected מְאֹדvery בְּיִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

On hearing it, God was furious and rejected Israel completely.

78:60

וַיִּטֹּשׁhe abandoned מִשְׁכַּןtabernacle שִׁלוֹShiloh אֹהֶלtent שִׁכֵּןpitched בָּאָדָםhad among men

He abandoned the tabernacle of Shiloh, the tent He had pitched among men.

78:61

וַיִּתֵּןhe delivered לַשְּׁבִיto captivity עֻזּוֹhis strength וְתִפְאַרְתּוֹand his splendor בְיַדto the hand צָרadversary

He delivered His strength to captivity, and His splendor to the hand of the adversary.

78:62

וַיַּסְגֵּרhe surrendered לַחֶרֶבto the sword עַמּוֹhis people וּבְנַחֲלָתוֹby his heritage הִתְעַבָּרenraged

He surrendered His people to the sword because He was enraged by His heritage.

78:63

בַּחוּרָיוhis young men אָכְלָהconsumed אֵשׁfire וּבְתוּלֹתָיוand their maidens לֹאnot הוּלָּלוּwedding songs

Fire consumed His young men, and their maidens were left without wedding songs.

78:64

כֹּהֲנָיוhis priests בַּחֶרֶבby the sword נָפָלוּfell וְאַלְמְנֹתָיוbut their widows לֹאnot תִבְכֶּינָהlament

His priests fell by the sword, but their widows could not lament.

78:65

וַיִּקַץawoke כְּיָשֵׁןas from sleep אֲדֹנָיLord כְּגִבּוֹרlike a mighty warrior מִתְרוֹנֵןwarrior overcome מִיָּיִןby wine

Then the Lord awoke as from sleep, like a mighty warrior overcome by wine.

78:66

וַיַּךְhe beat צָרָיוhis foes אָחוֹרback חֶרְפַּתshame עוֹלָםeverlasting נָתַןput לָמוֹthem to

He beat back His foes; He put them to everlasting shame.

78:67

וַיִּמְאַסhe rejected בְּאֹהֶלthe tent יוֹסֵףJoseph וּבְשֵׁבֶטtribe אֶפְרַיִםEphraim לֹאnot בָחָרchosen

He rejected the tent of Joseph and refused the tribe of Ephraim.

78:68

וַיִּבְחַרbut he chose אֶת[obj] שֵׁבֶטtribe יְהוּדָהJudah אֶת[obj] הַרMount צִיּוֹןZion אֲשֶׁרwhich אָהֵבloved

But He chose the tribe of Judah, Mount Zion, which He loved.

78:69

וַיִּבֶןhe built כְּמוֹlike רָמִיםheights מִקְדָּשׁוֹhis sanctuary כְּאֶרֶץlike the earth יְסָדָהּestablished לְעוֹלָםforever

He built His sanctuary like the heights, like the earth He has established forever.

78:70

וַיִּבְחַרhe chose בְּדָוִדDavid עַבְדּוֹhis servant וַיִּקָּחֵהוּand took him מִמִּכְלְאֹתfrom the sheepfolds צֹאןsheepfolds

He chose David His servant and took him from the sheepfolds;

78:71

מֵאַחַרfrom following עָלוֹתtending הֱבִיאוֹbrought him לִרְעוֹתto be shepherd בְּיַעֲקֹבJacob עַמּוֹhis people וּבְיִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael נַחֲלָתוֹhis inheritance

from tending the ewes He brought him to be shepherd of His people Jacob, of Israel His inheritance.

78:72

וַיִּרְעֵםso David shepherded them כְּתֹםwith integrity לְבָבוֹheart וּבִתְבוּנוֹתwith skillful כַּפָּיוhands יַנְחֵםguided them

So David shepherded them with integrity of heart and guided them with skillful hands.
