Psalms 36

Human wickedness and the goodness of God

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David, the servant of the LORD

36:1

נְאֻםoracle פֶּשַׁעtransgression לָרָשָׁעof the wicked בְּקֶרֶבin among לִבִּיmy heart אֵיןno פַּחַדfear אֱלֹהִיםGod לְנֶגֶדbefore עֵינָיוhis eyes

An oracle is in my heart regarding the transgression of the wicked man: There is no fear of God before his eyes.

36:2

כִּיfor הֶחֱלִיקconceit אֵלָיוto בְּעֵינָיוeyes לִמְצֹאdetect עֲוֺנוֹown sin לִשְׂנֹאor hate

For his eyes are too full of conceit to detect or hate his own sin.

36:3

דִּבְרֵיwords פִיוhis mouth אָוֶןwicked וּמִרְמָהand deceitful חָדַלceased לְהַשְׂכִּילto be wise לְהֵיטִיבand well

The words of his mouth are wicked and deceitful; he has ceased to be wise and well-doing.

36:4

אָוֶןwickedness יַחְשֹׁבplots עַלon מִשְׁכָּבוֹbed he יִתְיַצֵּבsets עַלon דֶּרֶךְpath לֹאnot טוֹבgood רָעevil לֹאnot יִמְאָסreject

Even on his bed he plots wickedness; he sets himself on a path that is not good; he fails to reject evil.

36:5

יְהוָהLORD בְּהַשָּׁמַיִםto the heavens חַסְדֶּךָyour loving devotion אֱמוּנָתְךָyour faithfulness עַדto שְׁחָקִיםclouds

Your loving devotion, O LORD, reaches to the heavens, Your faithfulness to the clouds.

36:6

צִדְקָתְךָyour righteousness כְּהַרְרֵיmountains אֵלhighest מִשְׁפָּטֶךָyour judgments תְּהוֹםdeepest רַבָּהgreat אָדָםman וּבְהֵמָהand beast תוֹשִׁיעַsea יְהוָהLORD

Your righteousness is like the highest mountains; Your judgments are like the deepest sea. O LORD, You preserve man and beast.

36:7

מַהhow יָּקָרprecious חַסְדְּךָyour loving devotion אֱלֹהִיםGod וּבְנֵיthat the children אָדָםmen בְּצֵלin the shadow כְּנָפֶיךָyour wings יֶחֱסָיוּןrefuge

How precious is Your loving devotion, O God, that the children of men take refuge in the shadow of Your wings!

36:8

יִרְוְיֻןfeast מִדֶּשֶׁןon the abundance בֵּיתֶךָyour house וְנַחַלriver עֲדָנֶיךָdelights תַשְׁקֵםgive them drink

They feast on the abundance of Your house, and You give them drink from Your river of delights.

36:9

כִּיfor עִמְּךָwith you מְקוֹרfountain חַיִּיםlife בְּאוֹרְךָin your light נִרְאֶהsee אוֹרlight

For with You is the fountain of life; in Your light we see light.

36:10

מְשֹׁךְextend חַסְדְּךָyour loving devotion לְיֹדְעֶיךָknow you וְצִדְקָתְךָand your righteousness לְיִשְׁרֵיto the upright לֵבheart

Extend Your loving devotion to those who know You, and Your righteousness to the upright in heart.

36:11

אַלnot תְּבוֹאֵנִיcome against me רֶגֶלfoot גַּאֲוָהproud וְיַדnor the hand רְשָׁעִיםwicked אַלdo not תְּנִדֵנִיdrive me

Let not the foot of the proud come against me, nor the hand of the wicked drive me away.

36:12

שָׁםthere נָפְלוּfallen פֹּעֲלֵיevildoers אָוֶןevildoers דֹּחוּthrown וְלֹאand not יָכְלוּunable קוּםrise

There the evildoers lie fallen, thrown down and unable to rise.
