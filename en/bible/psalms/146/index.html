Psalms 146

Trust in God, not in princes

Open in the interlinear reader

146:1

הַלְלוּHallelujah יָהּHallelujah הַלְלִיpraise נַפְשִׁיmy soul אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD

Hallelujah! Praise the LORD, O my soul.

146:2

אֲהַלְלָהpraise יְהוָהLORD בְּחַיָּיmy life אֲזַמְּרָהsing praises לֵאלֹהַיto my God בְּעוֹדִיwhile

I will praise the LORD all my life; I will sing praises to my God while I have my being.

146:3

אַלnot תִּבְטְחוּtrust בִנְדִיבִיםin princes בְּבֶןin mortal אָדָםman שֶׁאֵיןwho no לוֹto him תְשׁוּעָהsave

Put not your trust in princes, in mortal man, who cannot save.

146:4

תֵּצֵאwhen רוּחוֹhis spirit יָשֻׁבreturns לְאַדְמָתוֹto the ground בַּיּוֹםon that very day הַהוּאthat אָבְדוּperish עֶשְׁתֹּנֹתָיוhis plans

When his spirit departs, he returns to the ground; on that very day his plans perish.

146:5

אַשְׁרֵיblessed שֶׁאֵלGod יַעֲקֹבJacob בְּעֶזְרוֹhelp שִׂבְרוֹhope is עַלin יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהָיוhis God

Blessed is he whose help is the God of Jacob, whose hope is in the LORD his God,

146:6

עֹשֶׂהMaker שָׁמַיִםheaven וָאָרֶץand earth אֶת[obj] הַיָּםthe sea וְאֶתand [obj] כָּלeverything אֲשֶׁרthat בָּםin them הַשֹּׁמֵרremains אֱמֶתfaithful לְעוֹלָםforever

the Maker of heaven and earth, the sea, and everything in them. He remains faithful forever.

146:7

עֹשֶׂהexecutes מִשְׁפָּטjustice לָעֲשׁוּקִיםfor the oppressed נֹתֵןgives לֶחֶםfood לָרְעֵבִיםto the hungry יְהוָהLORD מַתִּירsets אֲסוּרִיםprisoners

He executes justice for the oppressed and gives food to the hungry. The LORD sets the prisoners free,

146:8

יְהוָהLORD פֹּקֵחַopens עִוְרִיםblind יְהוָהLORD זֹקֵףlifts כְּפוּפִיםweighed יְהוָהLORD אֹהֵבloves צַדִּיקִיםrighteous

the LORD opens the eyes of the blind, the LORD lifts those who are weighed down, the LORD loves the righteous.

146:9

יְהוָהLORD שֹׁמֵרprotects אֶת[obj] גֵּרִיםforeigners יָתוֹםfatherless וְאַלְמָנָהand the widow יְעוֹדֵדsustains וְדֶרֶךְbut the ways רְשָׁעִיםwicked יְעַוֵּתfrustrates

The LORD protects foreigners; He sustains the fatherless and the widow, but the ways of the wicked He frustrates.

146:10

יִמְלֹךְreigns יְהוָהLORD לְעוֹלָםforever אֱלֹהַיִךְyour God O צִיּוֹןZion לְדֹרfor all וָדֹרgenerations הַלְלוּHallelujah יָהּHallelujah

The LORD reigns forever, your God, O Zion, for all generations. Hallelujah!
