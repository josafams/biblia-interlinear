Psalms 55

Betrayed by a close companion

Open in the interlinear reader

For the choirmaster. With stringed instruments. A Maskil of David

55:1

הַאֲזִינָהlisten אֱלֹהִיםGod תְּפִלָּתִיmy prayer וְאַלand do not תִּתְעַלַּםignore מִתְּחִנָּתִיnot my plea

Listen to my prayer, O God, and do not ignore my plea.

55:2

הַקְשִׁיבָהattend לִּיto me וַעֲנֵנִיand answer me אָרִידrestless בְּשִׂיחִיin my complaint וְאָהִימָהand distraught

Attend to me and answer me. I am restless in my complaint, and distraught

55:3

מִקּוֹלat the voice אוֹיֵבenemy מִפְּנֵיfrom before עָקַתpressure רָשָׁעwicked כִּיfor יָמִיטוּrelease עָלַיupon me אָוֶןdisaster וּבְאַףin their anger יִשְׂטְמוּנִיrevile me

at the voice of the enemy, at the pressure of the wicked. For they release disaster upon me and revile me in their anger.

55:4

לִבִּיmy heart יָחִילmurmurs בְּקִרְבִּיwithin me וְאֵימוֹתand the terrors מָוֶתdeath נָפְלוּassail עָלָיon me

My heart murmurs within me, and the terrors of death assail me.

55:5

יִרְאָהfear וָרַעַדand trembling יָבֹאgrip בִיin me וַתְּכַסֵּנִיoverwhelmed me פַּלָּצוּתhorror

Fear and trembling grip me, and horror has overwhelmed me.

55:6

וָאֹמַרI said מִיwho יִתֶּןgive לִּיto me אֵבֶרwings כַּיּוֹנָהlike a dove אָעוּפָהfly וְאֶשְׁכֹּנָהand find rest

I said, “Oh, that I had wings like a dove! I would fly away and find rest.

55:7

הִנֵּהbehold אַרְחִיקfar נְדֹדflee אָלִיןwould בַּמִּדְבָּרin the wilderness סֶלָהSelah

How far away I would flee! In the wilderness I would remain. Selah

55:8

אָחִישָׁהhurry מִפְלָטescape לִיto my מֵרוּחַshelter סֹעָהfar מִסָּעַרtempest

I would hurry to my shelter, far from this raging tempest.”

55:9

בַּלַּעconfuse אֲדֹנָיLord פַּלַּגconfound לְשׁוֹנָםtheir speech כִּיfor רָאִיתִיsee חָמָסviolence וְרִיבand strife בָּעִירin the city

O Lord, confuse and confound their speech, for I see violence and strife in the city.

55:10

יוֹמָםday וָלַיְלָהand night יְסוֹבְבֻהָthey encircle the עַלon חוֹמֹתֶיהָwalls וְאָוֶןwhile malice וְעָמָלand trouble בְּקִרְבָּהּlie within

Day and night they encircle the walls, while malice and trouble lie within.

55:11

הַוּוֹתdestruction בְּקִרְבָּהּis within וְלֹאnever יָמִישׁleave מֵרְחֹבָהּthe streets תֹּךְoppression וּמִרְמָהand deceit

Destruction is within; oppression and deceit never leave the streets.

55:12

כִּיfor לֹאnot אוֹיֵבenemy יְחָרְפֵנִיinsults me וְאֶשָּׂאI could endure לֹאnot מְשַׂנְאִיrises עָלַיagainst me הִגְדִּילgreat וְאֶסָּתֵרI could hide מִמֶּנּוּfrom him

For it is not an enemy who insults me; that I could endure. It is not a foe who rises against me; from him I could hide.

55:13

וְאַתָּהyou אֱנוֹשׁman כְּעֶרְכִּיlike my valuation אַלּוּפִיmy chiefs וּמְיֻדָּעִיcompanion and close friend

But it is you, a man like myself, my companion and close friend.

55:14

אֲשֶׁרthat יַחְדָּוtogether נַמְתִּיקsweet סוֹדfellowship בְּבֵיתinto the house אֱלֹהִיםGod נְהַלֵּךְwalked בְּרָגֶשׁwith the crowd

We shared sweet fellowship together; we walked with the crowd into the house of God.

55:15

יַשִּׁיlet seize מָוֶתdeath עָלֵימוֹon them יֵרְדוּgo down שְׁאוֹלSheol חַיִּיםalive כִּיfor רָעוֹתevil בִּמְגוּרָםhomes בְּקִרְבָּםin their among

Let death seize them by surprise; let them go down to Sheol alive, for evil is with them in their homes.

55:16

אֲנִיI אֶלto אֱלֹהִיםGod אֶקְרָאcall וַיהוָהand the LORD יוֹשִׁיעֵנִיsaves me

But I call to God, and the LORD saves me.

55:17

עֶרֶבnight וָבֹקֶרmorning and I וְצָהֳרַיִםnoon אָשִׂיחָהcry וְאֶהֱמֶהin distress וַיִּשְׁמַעand he hears קוֹלִיmy voice

Morning, noon, and night, I cry out in distress, and He hears my voice.

55:18

פָּדָהredeems בְשָׁלוֹםin peace נַפְשִׁיmy soul מִקֲּרָבfrom the battle לִיto me כִּיfor בְרַבִּיםthough many הָיוּwere עִמָּדִיwith me

He redeems my soul in peace from the battle waged against me, even though many oppose me.

55:19

יִשְׁמַעhear אֵלGod וְיַעֲנֵםand humiliate them וְיֹשֵׁבenthroned קֶדֶםages סֶלָהSelah אֲשֶׁרthat אֵיןnot חֲלִיפוֹתchange לָמוֹto them וְלֹאhave no יָרְאוּfear אֱלֹהִיםGod

God will hear and humiliate them— the One enthroned for the ages— Selah because they do not change and they have no fear of God.

55:20

שָׁלַחcompanion attacks יָדָיוattacks his בִּשְׁלֹמָיוfriends he חִלֵּלviolates בְּרִיתוֹhis covenant

My companion attacks his friends; he violates his covenant.

55:21

חָלְקוּbutter מַחְמָאֹתsmooth פִּיוhis speech וּקֲרָבbut war לִבּוֹis in his רַכּוּheart דְבָרָיוhis words מִשֶּׁמֶןthan oil וְהֵמָּהyet they פְתִחוֹתswords unsheathed

His speech is smooth as butter, but war is in his heart. His words are softer than oil, yet they are swords unsheathed.

55:22

הַשְׁלֵךְcast עַלupon יְהוָהLORD יְהָבְךָyour burden וְהוּאand he יְכַלְכְּלֶךָsustain you לֹאnever יִתֵּןlet לְעוֹלָםbe מוֹטshaken לַצַּדִּיקthe righteous

Cast your burden upon the LORD and He will sustain you; He will never let the righteous be shaken.

55:23

וְאַתָּהbut you אֱלֹהִיםGod תּוֹרִדֵםbring them down לִבְאֵרdestruction שַׁחַתpit אַנְשֵׁיmen דָמִיםbloodshed וּמִרְמָהand deceit לֹאnot יֶחֱצוּhalf יְמֵיהֶםtheir days וַאֲנִיbut I אֶבְטַחtrust בָּךְin you

But You, O God, will bring them down to the Pit of destruction; men of bloodshed and deceit will not live out half their days. But I will trust in You.
