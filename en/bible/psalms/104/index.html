Psalms 104

A hymn to the Creator who sustains life

Open in the interlinear reader

104:1

בָּרֲכִיbless נַפְשִׁיmy soul אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהַיmy God גָּדַלְתָּgreat מְּאֹדvery הוֹדsplendor וְהָדָרand majesty לָבָשְׁתָּclothed

Bless the LORD, O my soul! O LORD my God, You are very great; You are clothed with splendor and majesty.

104:2

עֹטֶהwraps אוֹרlight כַּשַּׂלְמָהas with a garment נוֹטֶהstretches שָׁמַיִםheavens כַּיְרִיעָהlike a tent

He wraps Himself in light as with a garment; He stretches out the heavens like a tent,

104:3

הַמְקָרֶהlaying the beams בַמַּיִםin the waters עֲלִיּוֹתָיוhis chambers הַשָּׂםmaking the עָבִיםclouds רְכוּבוֹhis chariot הַמְהַלֵּךְchariot walking עַלon כַּנְפֵיwings רוּחַwind

laying the beams of His chambers in the waters above, making the clouds His chariot, walking on the wings of the wind.

104:4

עֹשֶׂהmakes מַלְאָכָיוhis messengers רוּחוֹתwinds מְשָׁרְתָיוhis servants אֵשׁfire לֹהֵטflames

He makes the winds His messengers, flames of fire His servants.

104:5

יָסַדset אֶרֶץearth עַלon מְכוֹנֶיהָits foundations בַּלnever תִּמּוֹטmoved עוֹלָםforever וָעֶדand ever

He set the earth on its foundations, never to be moved.

104:6

תְּהוֹםdeep כַּלְּבוּשׁlike a garment כִּסִּיתוֹcovered it עַלon הָרִיםmountains יַעַמְדוּstood מָיִםwaters

You covered it with the deep like a garment; the waters stood above the mountains.

104:7

מִןfrom גַּעֲרָתְךָyour rebuke יְנוּסוּןfled מִןfrom קוֹלsound רַעַמְךָyour thunder יֵחָפֵזוּןhurried

At Your rebuke the waters fled; at the sound of Your thunder they hurried away—

104:8

יַעֲלוּrose הָרִיםmountains יֵרְדוּsank בְקָעוֹתvalleys אֶלto מְקוֹםplace זֶהyou יָסַדְתָּassigned לָהֶםfor them

the mountains rose and the valleys sank to the place You assigned for them—

104:9

גְּבוּלboundary שַׂמְתָּset בַּלcannot יַעֲבֹרוּןcross בַּלnever יְשׁוּבוּןagain לְכַסּוֹתcover הָאָרֶץthe earth

You set a boundary they cannot cross, that they may never again cover the earth.

104:10

הַמְשַׁלֵּחַsends מַעְיָנִיםsprings בַּנְּחָלִיםin the valleys בֵּיןbetween הָרִיםmountains יְהַלֵּכוּןflow

He sends forth springs in the valleys; they flow between the mountains.

104:11

יַשְׁקוּgive drink כָּלevery חַיְתוֹbeast שָׂדָיfield יִשְׁבְּרוּquench פְרָאִיםwild donkeys צְמָאָםquench their thirst

They give drink to every beast of the field; the wild donkeys quench their thirst.

104:12

עֲלֵיהֶםon them עוֹףbirds הַשָּׁמַיִםthe air יִשְׁכּוֹןnest מִבֵּיןamong עֳפָאיִםsing יִתְּנוּgive קוֹלbranches

The birds of the air nest beside the springs; they sing among the branches.

104:13

מַשְׁקֶהwaters הָרִיםmountains מֵעֲלִיּוֹתָיוfrom his chambers מִפְּרִיby the fruit מַעֲשֶׂיךָworks תִּשְׂבַּעsatisfied הָאָרֶץthe earth

He waters the mountains from His chambers; the earth is satisfied by the fruit of His works.

104:14

מַצְמִיחַgrow חָצִירgrass לַבְּהֵמָהfor the livestock וְעֵשֶׂבand provides crops לַעֲבֹדַתto service הָאָדָםman לְהוֹצִיאto cultivate bringing forth לֶחֶםfood מִןfrom הָאָרֶץthe earth

He makes the grass grow for the livestock and provides crops for man to cultivate, bringing forth food from the earth:

104:15

וְיַיִןwine יְשַׂמַּחgladdens לְבַבheart אֱנוֹשׁman לְהַצְהִילthat makes פָּנִיםface מִשָּׁמֶןto shine וְלֶחֶםand bread לְבַבheart אֱנוֹשׁsustains יִסְעָדhis

wine that gladdens the heart of man, oil that makes his face to shine, and bread that sustains his heart.

104:16

יִשְׂבְּעוּfill עֲצֵיtrees יְהוָהLORD אַרְזֵיcedars לְבָנוֹןLebanon אֲשֶׁרthat נָטָעplanted

The trees of the LORD have their fill, the cedars of Lebanon that He planted,

104:17

אֲשֶׁרthat שָׁםwhere צִפֳּרִיםbirds יְקַנֵּנוּnests חֲסִידָהstork בְּרוֹשִׁיםcypresses בֵּיתָהּher home

where the birds build their nests; the stork makes her home in the cypresses.

104:18

הָרִיםmountains הַגְּבֹהִיםthe high לַיְּעֵלִיםfor the wild goats סְלָעִיםcliffs מַחְסֶהrefuge לַשְׁפַנִּיםfor the rock badgers

The high mountains are for the wild goats, the cliffs a refuge for the rock badgers.

104:19

עָשָׂהmade יָרֵחַmoon לְמוֹעֲדִיםto mark שֶׁמֶשׁsun יָדַעknows מְבוֹאוֹset

He made the moon to mark the seasons; the sun knows when to set.

104:20

תָּשֶׁתbring חֹשֶׁךְdarkness וִיהִיand it becomes לָיְלָהnight בּוֹin him תִרְמֹשׂprowl כָּלall חַיְתוֹbeasts יָעַרforest

You bring darkness, and it becomes night, when all the beasts of the forest prowl.

104:21

הַכְּפִירִיםthe young lions שֹׁאֲגִיםroar לַטָּרֶףfor their prey וּלְבַקֵּשׁand seek מֵאֵלfrom God אָכְלָםtheir food

The young lions roar for their prey and seek their food from God.

104:22

תִּזְרַחrises הַשֶּׁמֶשׁthe sun יֵאָסֵפוּןwithdraw וְאֶלand to מְעוֹנֹתָםtheir dens יִרְבָּצוּןlie down

The sun rises, and they withdraw; they lie down in their dens.

104:23

יֵצֵאgoes forth אָדָםman לְפָעֳלוֹto his work וְלַעֲבֹדָתוֹand to his labor עֲדֵיuntil עָרֶבevening

Man goes forth to his work and to his labor until evening.

104:24

מָהhow רַבּוּmany מַעֲשֶׂיךָyour works O יְהוָהLORD כֻּלָּםthem all בְּחָכְמָהin wisdom עָשִׂיתָmade מָלְאָהfull הָאָרֶץthe earth קִנְיָנֶךָyour creatures

How many are Your works, O LORD! In wisdom You have made them all; the earth is full of Your creatures.

104:25

זֶהhere הַיָּםthe sea גָּדוֹלvast וּרְחַבand wide יָדָיִםhands שָׁםthere רֶמֶשׂteeming creatures וְאֵיןand no מִסְפָּרnumber חַיּוֹתliving קְטַנּוֹתsmall עִםwith גְּדֹלוֹתgreat

Here is the sea, vast and wide, teeming with creatures beyond number, living things both great and small.

104:26

שָׁםthere אֳנִיּוֹתships יְהַלֵּכוּןwalk לִוְיָתָןLeviathan זֶהyou יָצַרְתָּformed לְשַׂחֶקto frolic בּוֹin him

There the ships pass, and Leviathan, which You formed to frolic there.

104:27

כֻּלָּםall creatures אֵלֶיךָto you יְשַׂבֵּרוּןlook לָתֵתto give אָכְלָםtheir food בְּעִתּוֹin due season

All creatures look to You to give them their food in due season.

104:28

תִּתֵּןgive לָהֶםto them יִלְקֹטוּןgather תִּפְתַּחopen יָדְךָyour hand יִשְׂבְּעוּןsatisfied טוֹבgood

When You give it to them, they gather it up; when You open Your hand, they are satisfied with good things.

104:29

תַּסְתִּירhide פָּנֶיךָyour face יִבָּהֵלוּןterrified תֹּסֵףtake רוּחָםbreath they יִגְוָעוּןdie וְאֶלand to עֲפָרָםdust יְשׁוּבוּןreturn

When You hide Your face, they are terrified; when You take away their breath, they die and return to dust.

104:30

תְּשַׁלַּחsend רוּחֲךָyour spirit יִבָּרֵאוּןcreated וּתְחַדֵּשׁand you renew פְּנֵיface אֲדָמָהearth

When You send Your Spirit, they are created, and You renew the face of the earth.

104:31

יְהִיmay כְבוֹדglory יְהוָהLORD לְעוֹלָםendure forever יִשְׂמַחmay יְהוָהLORD בְּמַעֲשָׂיוin his works

May the glory of the LORD endure forever; may the LORD rejoice in His works.

104:32

הַמַּבִּיטlooks לָאָרֶץon the earth וַתִּרְעָדand it trembles יִגַּעtouches בֶּהָרִיםthe mountains וְיֶעֱשָׁנוּand they smolder

He looks on the earth, and it trembles; He touches the mountains, and they smolder.

104:33

אָשִׁירָהsing לַיהוָהto the LORD בְּחַיָּיmy life I אֲזַמְּרָהsing praise לֵאלֹהַיto my God בְּעוֹדִיwhile

I will sing to the LORD all my life; I will sing praise to my God while I have my being.

104:34

יֶעֱרַבpleasing עָלָיוto him שִׂיחִיmy meditation אָנֹכִיI אֶשְׂמַחrejoice בַּיהוָהin the LORD

May my meditation be pleasing to Him, for I rejoice in the LORD.

104:35

יִתַּמּוּvanish חַטָּאִיםsinners מִןfrom הָאָרֶץthe earth וּרְשָׁעִיםand the wicked עוֹדmore אֵינָםno בָּרֲכִיbless נַפְשִׁיmy soul אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD הַלְלוּHallelujah יָהּHallelujah

May sinners vanish from the earth and the wicked be no more. Bless the LORD, O my soul. Hallelujah!
