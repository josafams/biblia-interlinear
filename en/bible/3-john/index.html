3 John Ἰωάννου Γʹ

3 John is a personal note from “the elder” to his beloved Gaius, praised for welcoming brothers who traveled to proclaim the gospel. The elder criticizes Diotrephes, who wants to rule the church, does not receive the envoys and expels those who welcome them, and recommends Demetrius. It is the shortest book of the Bible in number of words in the original Greek.

Structure

  1. Greeting ch. 1

    Wishes for good health and joy at knowing that Gaius walks in the truth (1:1–4).

  2. Gaius's hospitality ch. 1

    Gaius welcomes the traveling brothers; supporting them is to be a fellow worker for the truth (1:5–8).

  3. Diotrephes and Demetrius ch. 1

    Diotrephes rejects the elder and the envoys; Demetrius is well spoken of by everyone (1:9–12).

  4. Farewell ch. 1

    The elder hopes to see Gaius soon and sends greetings from the friends (1:13–14).
