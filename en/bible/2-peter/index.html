2 Peter Πέτρου Βʹ

2 Peter has the form of a testament: the apostle, knowing that his death is near (1:14), leaves a last reminder to the faithful. He asks them to grow in a chain of virtues that runs from faith to love, recalls that the message about Christ is not based on myths but on the testimony of those who saw His glory on the mountain, and on the word of the prophets. He harshly denounces false teachers who exploit the community and live without restraint, and answers those who mocked the delay of the Lord's coming: for God, a thousand years are like a day, and the delay is patience to give time for repentance.

Structure

  1. Growing and remembering ch. 1

    God has given everything for a life of godliness; faith must be accompanied by virtue, knowledge, self-control, perseverance, godliness, brotherly kindness and love; Peter recalls the transfiguration and the divine origin of prophecy.

  2. The false teachers ch. 2

    As there were false prophets, there will be false teachers; the examples of the angels who sinned, the flood and Sodom show that God judges the ungodly and rescues the righteous, like Noah and Lot.

  3. The promise of His coming ch. 3

    The scoffers ask about the promised coming; God is not slow, but patient; the day of the Lord will come like a thief, and new heavens and a new earth are awaited; Paul's letters are mentioned as hard to understand.

Chapters

  1. 1 Growing in faith; witness and prophecy
  2. 2 False teachers and their judgment
  3. 3 The scoffers and the day of the Lord
