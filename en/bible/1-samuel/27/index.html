1 Samuel 27

David among the Philistines, at Ziklag

Open in the interlinear reader

27:1

וַיֹּאמֶרhowever said דָּוִדDavid אֶלto לִבּוֹhimself עַתָּהnow אֶסָּפֶהswept יוֹםdays אֶחָדone בְּיַדby the hand שָׁאוּלSaul אֵיןnothing לִיto me טוֹבbetter כִּיfor הִמָּלֵטthan אִמָּלֵטescape אֶלto אֶרֶץland פְּלִשְׁתִּיםPhilistines וְנוֹאַשׁstop מִמֶּנִּיfrom me שָׁאוּלSaul לְבַקְשֵׁנִיsearching for me עוֹדlonger בְּכָלall over גְּבוּלborder יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וְנִמְלַטְתִּיand I will slip מִיָּדוֹout of his hand

David, however, said to himself, “One of these days now I will be swept away by the hand of Saul. There is nothing better for me than to escape to the land of the Philistines. Then Saul will stop searching for me all over Israel, and I will slip out of his hand.”

27:2

וַיָּקָםso set דָּוִדDavid וַיַּעֲבֹרand went הוּאhe וְשֵׁשׁsix מֵאוֹתhundred אִישׁmen אֲשֶׁרthat עִמּוֹwith אֶלto אָכִישׁAchish בֶּןson מָעוֹךְMaoch מֶלֶךְking גַּתGath

So David set out with his six hundred men and went to Achish son of Maoch, the king of Gath.

27:3

וַיֵּשֶׁבsettled דָּוִדDavid עִםwith אָכִישׁAchish בְּגַתin Gath הוּאhe וַאֲנָשָׁיוeach אִישׁman וּבֵיתוֹhad family with him דָּוִדDavid וּשְׁתֵּיhad two נָשָׁיוhis wives אֲחִינֹעַםAhinoam הַיִּזְרְעֵאלִיתof Jezreel וַאֲבִיגַיִלand Abigail אֵשֶׁתwidow נָבָלNabal הַכַּרְמְלִיתof Carmel the

David and his men settled in Gath with Achish. Each man had his family with him, and David had his two wives: Ahinoam of Jezreel and Abigail of Carmel, the widow of Nabal.

27:4

וַיֻּגַּדand reported לְשָׁאוּלwhen Saul כִּיthat בָרַחfled דָּוִדDavid גַּתGath וְלֹאhe no יָסַףagain עוֹדlonger לְבַקְשׁוֹsearched for him

And when Saul learned that David had fled to Gath, he no longer searched for him.

27:5

וַיֹּאמֶרthen said דָּוִדDavid אֶלto אָכִישׁAchish אִםif נָאI מָצָאתִיfound חֵןfavor בְּעֵינֶיךָin your eyes יִתְּנוּlet לִיa מָקוֹםplace בְּאַחַתin one עָרֵיtowns הַשָּׂדֶהthe outlying וְאֵשְׁבָהlive שָּׁםthere וְלָמָּהfor why should יֵשֵׁבlive עַבְדְּךָyour servant בְּעִירcity הַמַּמְלָכָהthe royal עִמָּךְwith you

Then David said to Achish, “If I have found favor in your eyes, let me be assigned a place in one of the outlying towns, so I can live there. For why should your servant live in the royal city with you?”

27:6

וַיִּתֶּןgave לוֹto him אָכִישׁAchish בַּיּוֹםthat day הַהוּאthat אֶת[obj] צִקְלָגZiklag לָכֵןand to this הָיְתָהwas צִקְלַגZiklag לְמַלְכֵיto the kings יְהוּדָהJudah עַדuntil הַיּוֹםthis day הַזֶּהthis

That day Achish gave him Ziklag, and to this day it still belongs to the kings of Judah.

27:7

וַיְהִיand was מִסְפַּרnumber הַיָּמִיםthe time אֲשֶׁרthat יָשַׁבlived דָּוִדDavid בִּשְׂדֵהin territory פְלִשְׁתִּיםPhilistine יָמִיםyear וְאַרְבָּעָהand four חֳדָשִׁיםmonths

And the time that David lived in Philistine territory amounted to a year and four months.

27:8

וַיַּעַלwent דָּוִדDavid וַאֲנָשָׁיוand his men וַיִּפְשְׁטוּand raided אֶלto הַגְּשׁוּרִיthe Geshurites וְהַגִּזְרִיthe Girzites וְהָעֲמָלֵקִיand the Amalekites כִּיfor הֵנָּהthese יֹשְׁבוֹתinhabited הָאָרֶץthe land אֲשֶׁרthat מֵעוֹלָםfrom ancient בּוֹאֲךָcame you שׁוּרָהShur וְעַדand to אֶרֶץland מִצְרָיִםEgypt

Now David and his men went up and raided the Geshurites, the Girzites, and the Amalekites. (From ancient times these people had inhabited the land extending to Shur and Egypt.)

27:9

וְהִכָּהwhenever attacked דָוִדDavid אֶת[obj] הָאָרֶץa territory וְלֹאand not יְחַיֶּהleave אִישׁman וְאִשָּׁהor woman וְלָקַחbut he took צֹאןflocks וּבָקָרand herds וַחֲמֹרִיםdonkeys וּגְמַלִּיםcamels וּבְגָדִיםand clothing וַיָּשָׁבreturn וַיָּבֹאand came אֶלto אָכִישׁAchish

Whenever David attacked a territory, he did not leave a man or woman alive, but he took the flocks and herds, the donkeys, camels, and clothing. Then he would return to Achish,

27:10

וַיֹּאמֶרwho would ask אָכִישׁWhat אַלdo not פְּשַׁטְתֶּםraided הַיּוֹםtoday וַיֹּאמֶרreply דָּוִדDavid עַלon נֶגֶבNegev יְהוּדָהJudah וְעַלand on נֶגֶבNegev הַיַּרְחְמְאֵלִיof Jerahmeel וְאֶלand to נֶגֶבNegev הַקֵּינִיKenites

who would ask him, “What have you raided today?” And David would reply, “The Negev of Judah,” or “The Negev of Jerahmeel,” or “The Negev of the Kenites.”

27:11

וְאִישׁman וְאִשָּׁהor woman לֹאnot יְחַיֶּהleave דָוִדDavid לְהָבִיאto be brought גַתGath לֵאמֹרfor he said פֶּןotherwise יַגִּדוּreport עָלֵינוּon us לֵאמֹרsaying is כֹּהthis what עָשָׂהdid דָוִדDavid וְכֹהand this מִשְׁפָּטוֹcustom כָּלwhole הַיָּמִיםthe time אֲשֶׁרthat יָשַׁבlived בִּשְׂדֵהin territory פְלִשְׁתִּיםPhilistine

David did not leave a man or woman alive to be brought to Gath, for he said, “Otherwise they will report us, saying, ‘This is what David did.’” And this was David’s custom the whole time he lived in Philistine territory.

27:12

וַיַּאֲמֵןso trusted אָכִישׁAchish בְּדָוִדDavid לֵאמֹרthinking since הַבְאֵשׁutter הִבְאִישׁstench בְּעַמּוֹto his people בְיִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וְהָיָהhe will be לִיto me לְעֶבֶדservant עוֹלָםforever

So Achish trusted David, thinking, “Since he has made himself an utter stench to his people Israel, he will be my servant forever.”
