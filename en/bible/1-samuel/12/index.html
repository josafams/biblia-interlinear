Then Samuel said to all Israel, “I have listened to your voice in all that you have said to me, and I have set over you a king.

Now here is the king walking before you, and I am old and gray, and my sons are here with you. I have walked before you from my youth until this day.

Here I am. Bear witness against me before the LORD and before His anointed: Whose ox or donkey have I taken? Whom have I cheated or oppressed? From whose hand have I accepted a bribe and closed my eyes? Tell me, and I will restore it to you.”

12:4

וַיֹּאמְרוּyou have לֹאnot עֲשַׁקְתָּנוּwronged us וְלֹאor רַצּוֹתָנוּoppressed us וְלֹאand not לָקַחְתָּtaken מִיַּדfrom the hand אִישׁman מְאוּמָהanything

“You have not wronged us or oppressed us,” they replied, “nor have you taken anything from the hand of man.”

12:5

וַיֹּאמֶרSamuel said אֲלֵיהֶםto them עֵדwitness יְהוָהLORD בָּכֶםagainst you וְעֵדwitness מְשִׁיחוֹhis anointed הַיּוֹםa today הַזֶּהthat you כִּיfor לֹאnot מְצָאתֶםfound בְּיָדִיin my hand מְאוּמָהanything וַיֹּאמֶרthey replied עֵדwitness

Samuel said to them, “The LORD is a witness against you, and His anointed is a witness today, that you have not found anything in my hand.” “He is a witness,” they replied.

12:6

וַיֹּאמֶרthen said שְׁמוּאֵלSamuel אֶלto הָעָםthe people יְהוָהLORD אֲשֶׁרwho עָשָׂהappointed אֶת[obj] מֹשֶׁהMoses וְאֶתand [obj] אַהֲרֹןAaron וַאֲשֶׁרand who הֶעֱלָהbrought אֶת[obj] אֲבֹתֵיכֶםyour fathers מֵאֶרֶץland מִצְרָיִםEgypt

Then Samuel said to the people, “The LORD is the One who appointed Moses and Aaron, and who brought your fathers up out of the land of Egypt.

12:7

וְעַתָּהnow הִתְיַצְּבוּpresent וְאִשָּׁפְטָהmay confront אִתְּכֶםwith you לִפְנֵיbefore יְהוָהLORD אֵת[obj] כָּלall צִדְקוֹתrighteous יְהוָהacts אֲשֶׁרhas עָשָׂהdone אִתְּכֶםwith you וְאֶתand with אֲבוֹתֵיכֶםyour fathers

Now present yourselves, so that I may confront you before the LORD with all the righteous acts He has done for you and your fathers.

12:8

When Jacob went to Egypt, your fathers cried out to the LORD, and He sent them Moses and Aaron, who brought your fathers out of Egypt and settled them in this place.

12:9

וַיִּשְׁכְּחוּbut they forgot אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיהֶםtheir God וַיִּמְכֹּרand he sold אֹתָםthem בְּיַדinto the hand סִיסְרָאSisera שַׂרcommander צְבָאarmy חָצוֹרHazor וּבְיַדand into the hands פְּלִשְׁתִּיםPhilistines וּבְיַדand in hand מֶלֶךְking מוֹאָבMoab וַיִּלָּחֲמוּwho fought בָּםagainst them

But they forgot the LORD their God, and He sold them into the hand of Sisera the commander of the army of Hazor, and into the hands of the Philistines and the king of Moab, who fought against them.

12:10

וַיִּזְעֲקוּthen they cried out אֶלto יְהוָהLORD וַיֹּאמְרוּand said חָטָאנוּsinned כִּיfor עָזַבְנוּforsaken אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD וַנַּעֲבֹדand served אֶת[obj] הַבְּעָלִיםthe Baals וְאֶתand [obj] הָעַשְׁתָּרוֹתAshtoreths וְעַתָּהnow הַצִּילֵנוּdeliver us מִיַּדfrom the hands אֹיְבֵינוּour enemies that we וְנַעַבְדֶךָּmay serve you

Then they cried out to the LORD and said, ‘We have sinned, for we have forsaken the LORD and served the Baals and Ashtoreths. Now deliver us from the hands of our enemies, that we may serve You.’

12:11

וַיִּשְׁלַחsent יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] יְרֻבַּעַלJerubbaal וְאֶתand [obj] בְּדָןBarak וְאֶתand [obj] יִפְתָּחJephthah וְאֶתand [obj] שְׁמוּאֵלSamuel וַיַּצֵּלand he delivered אֶתְכֶםyou מִיַּדfrom the hands אֹיְבֵיכֶםyour enemies מִסָּבִיבon every side וַתֵּשְׁבוּand you dwelt בֶּטַחsecurely

So the LORD sent Jerubbaal, Barak, Jephthah, and Samuel, and He delivered you from the hands of your enemies on every side, and you dwelt securely.

12:12

וַתִּרְאוּsaw כִּיthat נָחָשׁNahash מֶלֶךְking בְּנֵיsons עַמּוֹןAmmonites בָּאmoving עֲלֵיכֶםagainst you וַתֹּאמְרוּyou said לִיto me לֹאno כִּיfor מֶלֶךְking יִמְלֹךְrule עָלֵינוּover us וַיהוָהeven though the LORD אֱלֹהֵיכֶםGod מַלְכְּכֶםyour king

But when you saw that Nahash king of the Ammonites was moving against you, you said to me, ‘No, we must have a king to rule over us’—even though the LORD your God was your king.

12:13

וְעַתָּהnow הִנֵּהhere הַמֶּלֶךְthe king אֲשֶׁרhave בְּחַרְתֶּםchosen אֲשֶׁרyou שְׁאֶלְתֶּםrequested וְהִנֵּהbehold נָתַןplaced יְהוָהLORD עֲלֵיכֶםover you מֶלֶךְking

Now here is the king you have chosen, the one you requested. Behold, the LORD has placed a king over you.

12:14

אִםif תִּירְאוּfear אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD וַעֲבַדְתֶּםand serve אֹתוֹhim וּשְׁמַעְתֶּםand obey בְּקֹלוֹhis voice וְלֹאnot תַמְרוּrebel against אֶת[obj] פִּיcommand יְהוָהLORD וִהְיִתֶםand be גַּםboth אַתֶּםyou וְגַםand also הַמֶּלֶךְthe king אֲשֶׁרwho מָלַךְreigns עֲלֵיכֶםover you אַחַרfollow יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֵיכֶםyour God

If you fear the LORD and serve Him and obey His voice, and if you do not rebel against the command of the LORD, and if both you and the king who reigns over you follow the LORD your God, then all will be well.

12:15

וְאִםbut if לֹאnot תִשְׁמְעוּdisobey בְּקוֹלin voice יְהוָהLORD וּמְרִיתֶםand rebel against אֶת[obj] פִּיcommand יְהוָהLORD וְהָיְתָהand be יַדhand יְהוָהLORD בָּכֶםagainst you וּבַאֲבֹתֵיכֶםagainst your fathers

But if you disobey the LORD and rebel against His command, then the hand of the LORD will be against you as it was against your fathers.

12:16

גַּםeven עַתָּהtherefore הִתְיַצְּבוּstand וּרְאוּand see אֶת[obj] הַדָּבָרthis thing הַגָּדוֹלgreat that הַזֶּהthis אֲשֶׁרthat יְהוָהLORD עֹשֶׂהdo לְעֵינֵיכֶםbefore your eyes

Now, therefore, stand and see this great thing that the LORD will do before your eyes.

12:17

הֲלוֹאis it not קְצִירharvest חִטִּיםwheat הַיּוֹםtoday אֶקְרָאcall אֶלto יְהוָהLORD וְיִתֵּןto send קֹלוֹתthunder וּמָטָרand rain וּדְעוּknow וּרְאוּand see כִּיfor רָעַתְכֶםevil you רַבָּהgreat אֲשֶׁרhave עֲשִׂיתֶםcommitted בְּעֵינֵיin the sight יְהוָהLORD לִשְׁאוֹלby asking לָכֶםfor מֶלֶךְking

Is it not the wheat harvest today? I will call on the LORD to send thunder and rain, so that you will know and see what a great evil you have committed in the sight of the LORD by asking for a king.”

12:18

וַיִּקְרָאso called שְׁמוּאֵלSamuel אֶלto יְהוָהLORD וַיִּתֵּןsent יְהוָהLORD קֹלֹתthunder וּמָטָרand rain בַּיּוֹםon that day הַהוּאa וַיִּירָאfeared כָלall הָעָםthe people מְאֹדgreatly אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD וְאֶתand [obj] שְׁמוּאֵלSamuel

So Samuel called to the LORD, and on that day the LORD sent thunder and rain. As a result, all the people greatly feared the LORD and Samuel.

12:19

וַיֹּאמְרוּthey pleaded כָלall הָעָםthe people אֶלto שְׁמוּאֵלSamuel הִתְפַּלֵּלpray בְּעַדfor עֲבָדֶיךָyour servants אֶלto יְהוָהLORD אֱלֹהֶיךָyour God וְאַלnot נָמוּתdie כִּיfor יָסַפְנוּadded עַלto כָּלall חַטֹּאתֵינוּour sins רָעָהevil לִשְׁאֹלasking לָנוּfor מֶלֶךְking

They pleaded with Samuel, “Pray to the LORD your God for your servants so that we will not die! For we have added to all our sins the evil of asking for a king.”

12:20

וַיֹּאמֶרreplied even שְׁמוּאֵלSamuel אֶלto הָעָםthough אַלdo not תִּירָאוּafraid אַתֶּםyou עֲשִׂיתֶםcommitted אֵת[obj] כָּלall הָרָעָהthis evil הַזֹּאתthis אַךְbut אַלnot תָּסוּרוּturn aside מֵאַחֲרֵיfrom following יְהוָהLORD וַעֲבַדְתֶּםbut serve אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD בְּכָלwith all לְבַבְכֶםyour heart

“Do not be afraid,” Samuel replied. “Even though you have committed all this evil, do not turn aside from following the LORD, but serve the LORD with all your heart.

12:21

וְלֹאdo not תָּסוּרוּturn aside כִּיfor אַחֲרֵיafter הַתֹּהוּworthless אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot יוֹעִילוּprofit וְלֹאyou or יַצִּילוּdeliver כִּיfor תֹהוּempty הֵמָּהthey are

Do not turn aside after worthless things that cannot profit you or deliver you, for they are empty.

12:22

כִּיfor לֹאnot יִטֹּשׁabandon יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] עַמּוֹhis people בַּעֲבוּרthe sake שְׁמוֹhis name הַגָּדוֹלgreat the כִּיbecause הוֹאִילpleased יְהוָהLORD לַעֲשׂוֹתto make אֶתְכֶםyou לוֹto him לְעָםown

Indeed, for the sake of His great name, the LORD will not abandon His people, because He was pleased to make you His own.

12:23

גַּםeven אָנֹכִיI חָלִילָהfar לִּיto me מֵחֲטֹאshould sin לַיהוָהagainst the LORD מֵחֲדֹלby ceasing לְהִתְפַּלֵּלto pray בַּעַדְכֶםfor you וְהוֹרֵיתִיteach אֶתְכֶםyou בְּדֶרֶךְway הַטּוֹבָהthe good וְהַיְשָׁרָהand right

As for me, far be it from me that I should sin against the LORD by ceasing to pray for you. And I will continue to teach you the good and right way.

12:24

אַךְbut יְראוּfear אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD וַעֲבַדְתֶּםand serve אֹתוֹhim בֶּאֱמֶתfaithfully בְּכָלwith all לְבַבְכֶםyour heart כִּיfor רְאוּconsider אֵת[obj] אֲשֶׁרthat הִגְדִּלgreat עִמָּכֶםwith you

Above all, fear the LORD and serve Him faithfully with all your heart; consider what great things He has done for you.

12:25

וְאִםbut if הָרֵעַyou persist תָּרֵעוּevil גַּםboth אַתֶּםyou גַּםand מַלְכְּכֶםyour king תִּסָּפוּswept

But if you persist in doing evil, both you and your king will be swept away.”
