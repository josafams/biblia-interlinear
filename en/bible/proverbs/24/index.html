Proverbs 24

More sayings of the wise; the sluggard's field

Open in the interlinear reader

24:1

אַלdo not תְּקַנֵּאenvy בְּאַנְשֵׁיmen רָעָהwicked וְאַלor תִּתְאָיוdesire לִהְיוֹתto be אִתָּםwith them

Do not envy wicked men or desire their company;

24:2

כִּיfor שֹׁדviolence יֶהְגֶּהdevise לִבָּםtheir hearts וְעָמָלtrouble שִׂפְתֵיהֶםtheir lips תְּדַבֵּרְנָהdeclare

for their hearts devise violence, and their lips declare trouble.

24:3

בְּחָכְמָהby wisdom יִבָּנֶהbuilt בָּיִתhouse וּבִתְבוּנָהand by understanding יִתְכּוֹנָןestablished

By wisdom a house is built and by understanding it is established;

24:4

וּבְדַעַתthrough knowledge חֲדָרִיםrooms יִמָּלְאוּfilled כָּלevery הוֹןwealth יָקָרprecious וְנָעִיםand beautiful treasure

through knowledge its rooms are filled with every precious and beautiful treasure.

24:5

גֶּבֶרman חָכָםwise בַּעוֹזis strong וְאִישׁand a man דַּעַתknowledge מְאַמֶּץenhances כֹּחַstrength

A wise man is strong, and a man of knowledge enhances his strength.

24:6

כִּיfor בְתַחְבֻּלוֹתwith sound guidance תַּעֲשֶׂהwage לְּךָto you מִלְחָמָהwar וּתְשׁוּעָהand victory בְּרֹבin a multitude יוֹעֵץcounselors

Only with sound guidance should you wage war, and victory lies in a multitude of counselors.

24:7

רָאמוֹתhigh לֶאֱוִילfor a fool חָכְמוֹתwisdom בַּשַּׁעַרin the meeting לֹאnot יִפְתַּחopen פִּיהוּhis mouth

Wisdom is too high for a fool; he does not open his mouth in the meeting place.

24:8

מְחַשֵּׁבplots לְהָרֵעַevil לוֹto him בַּעַלhusband מְזִמּוֹתschemer יִקְרָאוּcalled

He who plots evil will be called a schemer.

24:9

זִמַּתscheme אִוֶּלֶתfoolish חַטָּאתsin וְתוֹעֲבַתdetestable לְאָדָםto men לֵץmocker

A foolish scheme is sin, and a mocker is detestable to men.

24:10

הִתְרַפִּיתָfaint בְּיוֹםin the day צָרָהdistress צַרsmall כֹּחֶכָהyour strength

If you faint in the day of distress, how small is your strength!

24:11

הַצֵּלrescue לְקֻחִיםled לַמָּוֶתto death וּמָטִיםstumbling לַהֶרֶגtoward the slaughter אִםif תַּחְשׂוֹךְrestrain

Rescue those being led away to death, and restrain those stumbling toward the slaughter.

24:12

כִּיif תֹאמַרsay הֵןbehold לֹאnot יָדַעְנוּknow זֶהthis הֲלֹאdoes not תֹכֵןweighs לִבּוֹתhearts הוּאhe יָבִיןconsider וְנֹצֵרwho guards נַפְשְׁךָyour life הוּאwill יֵדָעknow וְהֵשִׁיבwill he not repay לְאָדָםa man כְּפָעֳלוֹaccording to his deeds

If you say, “Behold, we did not know about this,” does not He who weighs hearts consider it? Does not the One who guards your life know? Will He not repay a man according to his deeds?

24:13

אֱכָלeat בְּנִיmy son דְבַשׁhoney כִּיfor טוֹבgood וְנֹפֶתand the honeycomb מָתוֹקsweet עַלto חִכֶּךָyour taste

Eat honey, my son, for it is good, and the honeycomb is sweet to your taste.

24:14

כֵּןtherefore דְּעֶהknow חָכְמָהwisdom לְנַפְשֶׁךָsweet to your soul אִםif מָצָאתָfind וְיֵשׁit there אַחֲרִיתfuture וְתִקְוָתְךָand your hope לֹאnever תִכָּרֵתcut off

Know therefore that wisdom is sweet to your soul. If you find it, there is a future for you, and your hope will never be cut off.

24:15

אַלdo not תֶּאֱרֹבlie in wait רָשָׁעwicked לִנְוֵהdwelling צַדִּיקrighteous אַלdo not תְּשַׁדֵּדdestroy רִבְצוֹhis resting place

Do not lie in wait, O wicked man, near the dwelling of the righteous; do not destroy his resting place.

24:16

כִּיfor שֶׁבַעseven יִפּוֹלfall צַדִּיקrighteous וָקָםhe still gets up וּרְשָׁעִיםbut the wicked יִכָּשְׁלוּstumble בְרָעָהin bad

For though a righteous man may fall seven times, he still gets up; but the wicked stumble in bad times.

24:17

בִּנְפֹלfalls אוֹיִבְךָyour enemy אַלdo not תִּשְׂמָחgloat וּבִכָּשְׁלוֹwhen he stumbles אַלdo not יָגֵלrejoice לִבֶּךָyour heart

Do not gloat when your enemy falls, and do not let your heart rejoice when he stumbles,

24:18

פֶּןor יִרְאֶהsee יְהוָהLORD וְרַעwill and בְּעֵינָיוin his sight וְהֵשִׁיבand turn מֵעָלָיוfrom the him אַפּוֹwrath

or the LORD will see and disapprove, and turn His wrath away from him.

24:19

אַלdo not תִּתְחַרfret בַּמְּרֵעִיםover evildoers אַלdo not תְּקַנֵּאenvious בָּרְשָׁעִיםthe wicked

Do not fret over evildoers, and do not be envious of the wicked.

24:20

כִּיfor לֹאno תִהְיֶהbe אַחֲרִיתfuture לָרָעfor the evil נֵרlamp רְשָׁעִיםwicked יִדְעָךְextinguished

For the evil man has no future; the lamp of the wicked will be extinguished.

24:21

יְרָאfear אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD בְּנִיmy son וָמֶלֶךְand the king עִםwith שׁוֹנִיםdifferent אַלdo not תִּתְעָרָבassociate

My son, fear the LORD and the king, and do not associate with the rebellious.

24:22

כִּיfor פִתְאֹםsudden יָקוּםstand אֵידָםdestruction וּפִידruin שְׁנֵיהֶםtheir two מִיcan יוֹדֵעַknows

For they will bring sudden destruction. Who knows what ruin they can bring?

24:23

גַּםalso אֵלֶּהthese לַחֲכָמִיםwise to הַכֵּרshow partiality פָּנִיםpartiality בְּמִשְׁפָּטin judgment בַּלnot טוֹבgood

These also are sayings of the wise: To show partiality in judgment is not good.

24:24

אֹמֵרtells לְרָשָׁעthe guilty צַדִּיקinnocent אָתָּהyou are יִקְּבֻהוּcurse him עַמִּיםpeoples יִזְעָמוּהוּdenounce him לְאֻמִּיםnations

Whoever tells the guilty, “You are innocent”— peoples will curse him, and nations will denounce him;

24:25

וְלַמּוֹכִיחִיםwith those who convict יִנְעָםguilty וַעֲלֵיהֶםupon them תָּבוֹאcome בִרְכַּתblessing טוֹבrich

but it will go well with those who convict the guilty, and rich blessing will come upon them.

24:26

שְׂפָתַיִםlips יִשָּׁקkiss מֵשִׁיבhonest דְּבָרִיםanswer נְכֹחִיםgiven

An honest answer given is like a kiss on the lips.

24:27

הָכֵןcomplete בַּחוּץoutdoor מְלַאכְתֶּךָyour work וְעַתְּדָהּand prepare בַּשָּׂדֶהfield לָךְto you אַחַרafter וּבָנִיתָmay build בֵיתֶךָyour house

Complete your outdoor work and prepare your field; after that, you may build your house.

24:28

אַלdo not תְּהִיbe עֵדtestify חִנָּםwithout cause בְּרֵעֶךָagainst your neighbor וַהֲפִתִּיתָand do not deceive בִּשְׂפָתֶיךָwith your lips

Do not testify against your neighbor without cause, and do not deceive with your lips.

24:29

אַלdo not תֹּאמַרsay כַּאֲשֶׁרas he has עָשָׂהdone לִיto me כֵּןso אֶעֱשֶׂהdo לּוֹto him אָשִׁיבrepay לָאִישׁthe man כְּפָעֳלוֹaccording to his work

Do not say, “I will do to him as he has done to me; I will repay the man according to his work.”

24:30

עַלon שְׂדֵהfield אִישׁman עָצֵלslacker עָבַרְתִּיtransgressed וְעַלand on כֶּרֶםvineyard אָדָםman חֲסַרlacking לֵבjudgment

I went past the field of a slacker and by the vineyard of a man lacking judgment.

24:31

וְהִנֵּהand saw עָלָהup כֻלּוֹeverywhere had קִמְּשֹׂנִיםthistles כָּסּוּcovered פָנָיוhis face חֲרֻלִּיםground וְגֶדֶרwall אֲבָנָיוstone was נֶהֱרָסָהbroken

Thorns had grown up everywhere, thistles had covered the ground, and the stone wall was broken down.

24:32

וָאֶחֱזֶהobserved and took אָנֹכִיI אָשִׁיתobserved לִבִּיheart I רָאִיתִיlooked לָקַחְתִּיreceived מוּסָרinstruction

I observed and took it to heart; I looked and received instruction:

24:33

מְעַטlittle שֵׁנוֹתsleep מְעַטlittle תְּנוּמוֹתslumber מְעַטlittle חִבֻּקfolding יָדַיִםhands לִשְׁכָּבto rest

A little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to rest,

24:34

וּבָאcome מִתְהַלֵּךְwalking רֵישֶׁךָpoverty וּמַחְסֹרֶיךָand need כְּאִישׁlike man מָגֵןbandit

and poverty will come upon you like a robber, and need like a bandit.
