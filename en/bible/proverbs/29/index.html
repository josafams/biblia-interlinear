Proverbs 29

Just rule, discipline and trust in the LORD

Open in the interlinear reader

29:1

אִישׁman תּוֹכָחוֹתreproof מַקְשֶׁהstiff עֹרֶףnecked פֶּתַעsuddenly יִשָּׁבֵרshattered וְאֵיןand no מַרְפֵּאrecovery

A man who remains stiff-necked after much reproof will suddenly be shattered beyond recovery.

29:2

בִּרְבוֹתflourish צַדִּיקִיםrighteous flourish יִשְׂמַחrejoice הָעָםthe people וּבִמְשֹׁלrule רָשָׁעwicked יֵאָנַחgroan עָםpeople

When the righteous flourish, the people rejoice, but when the wicked rule, the people groan.

29:3

אִישׁman אֹהֵבloves חָכְמָהwisdom יְשַׂמַּחjoy אָבִיוhis father וְרֹעֶהbut a companion זוֹנוֹתprostitutes יְאַבֶּדsquanders הוֹןwealth

A man who loves wisdom brings joy to his father, but a companion of prostitutes squanders his wealth.

29:4

מֶלֶךְking בְּמִשְׁפָּטby justice יַעֲמִידstability אָרֶץland וְאִישׁbut a man תְּרוּמוֹתexacts יֶהֶרְסֶנָּהtribute demolishes it

By justice a king brings stability to the land, but a man who exacts tribute demolishes it.

29:5

גֶּבֶרman מַחֲלִיקflatters עַלon רֵעֵהוּhis neighbor רֶשֶׁתnet פּוֹרֵשׂspreads עַלfor פְּעָמָיוhis feet

A man who flatters his neighbor spreads a net for his feet.

29:6

בְּפֶשַׁעin rebellious אִישׁman רָעevil מוֹקֵשׁcaught וְצַדִּיקbut a righteous יָרוּןsings וְשָׂמֵחַand rejoices

An evil man is caught by his own sin, but a righteous one sings and rejoices.

29:7

יֹדֵעַconsider צַדִּיקrighteous דִּיןcause דַּלִּיםpoor רָשָׁעwicked לֹאno יָבִיןregard דָּעַתconcerns

The righteous consider the cause of the poor, but the wicked have no regard for such concerns.

29:8

אַנְשֵׁיmen לָצוֹןmockers יָפִיחוּinflame קִרְיָהcity וַחֲכָמִיםbut the wise יָשִׁיבוּturn אָףanger

Mockers inflame a city, but the wise turn away anger.

29:9

אִישׁman חָכָםwise goes נִשְׁפָּטcourt אֶתwith אִישׁman אֱוִילfool וְרָגַזwill be raving וְשָׂחַקand laughing וְאֵיןwith no נָחַתresolution

If a wise man goes to court with a fool, there will be raving and laughing with no resolution.

29:10

אַנְשֵׁיmen דָמִיםbloodshed יִשְׂנְאוּhate תָםblameless וִישָׁרִיםbut the upright יְבַקְשׁוּseek נַפְשׁוֹhis life

Men of bloodshed hate a blameless man, but the upright care for his life.

29:11

כָּלall רוּחוֹhis anger יוֹצִיאvents כְסִילfool וְחָכָםbut a wise בְּאָחוֹרback יְשַׁבְּחֶנָּהholds it

A fool vents all his anger, but a wise man holds it back.

29:12

מֹשֵׁלruler מַקְשִׁיבlistens עַלto דְּבַרword שָׁקֶרlies כָּלall מְשָׁרְתָיוhis officials רְשָׁעִיםwicked

If a ruler listens to lies, all his officials will be wicked.

29:13

רָשׁpoor וְאִישׁman and תְּכָכִיםoppressor נִפְגָּשׁוּcommon מֵאִירlight עֵינֵיeyes שְׁנֵיהֶםboth יְהוָהLORD

The poor man and the oppressor have this in common: The LORD gives light to the eyes of both.

29:14

מֶלֶךְking שׁוֹפֵטjudges בֶּאֱמֶתwith fairness דַּלִּיםpoor כִּסְאוֹhis throne לָעַדbe forever יִכּוֹןestablished

A king who judges the poor with fairness— his throne will be established forever.

29:15

שֵׁבֶטrod וְתוֹכַחַתcorrection יִתֵּןimparts חָכְמָהwisdom וְנַעַרbut a child מְשֻׁלָּחsent מֵבִישׁdisgraces אִמּוֹhis mother

A rod of correction imparts wisdom, but a child left to himself disgraces his mother.

29:16

בִּרְבוֹתin multiplied רְשָׁעִיםwicked יִרְבֶּהthrive פָּשַׁעrebellion וְצַדִּיקִיםbut the righteous בְּמַפַּלְתָּםwill their downfall יִרְאוּsee

When the wicked thrive, rebellion increases; but the righteous will see their downfall.

29:17

יַסֵּרdiscipline בִּנְךָyour son וִינִיחֶךָyou rest וְיִתֵּןhe will bring מַעֲדַנִּיםdelight לְנַפְשֶׁךָto your soul

Discipline your son, and he will give you rest; he will bring delight to your soul.

29:18

בְּאֵיןno חָזוֹןvision יִפָּרַעrestraint עָםpeople וְשֹׁמֵרkeeps תּוֹרָהlaw אַשְׁרֵהוּblessed is he

Where there is no vision, the people cast off restraint; but blessed is he who keeps the Law.

29:19

בִּדְבָרִיםby words לֹאnot יִוָּסֶרcorrected עָבֶדservant כִּיfor יָבִיןunderstands וְאֵיןhe will not מַעֲנֶהrespond

A servant cannot be corrected by words alone; though he understands, he will not respond.

29:20

חָזִיתָsee אִישׁman אָץspeaks haste בִּדְבָרָיוis more תִּקְוָהhope לִכְסִילfor a fool מִמֶּנּוּthan for him

Do you see a man who speaks in haste? There is more hope for a fool than for him.

29:21

מְפַנֵּקpampered מִנֹּעַרfrom youth עַבְדּוֹservant וְאַחֲרִיתוֹend יִהְיֶהbe מָנוֹןgrief

A servant pampered from his youth will bring grief in the end.

29:22

אִישׁman אַףanger יְגָרֶהstirs מָדוֹןdissension וּבַעַלand husband חֵמָהhot רַבmany פָּשַׁעtransgression

An angry man stirs up dissension, and a hot-tempered man abounds in transgression.

29:23

גַּאֲוַתpride אָדָםman’s תַּשְׁפִּילֶנּוּbring him low וּשְׁפַלbut a humble רוּחַspirit יִתְמֹךְobtain כָּבוֹדhonor

A man’s pride will bring him low, but a humble spirit will obtain honor.

29:24

חוֹלֵקpartner עִםto גַּנָּבthief שׂוֹנֵאhates נַפְשׁוֹown soul he אָלָהoath יִשְׁמַעhear וְלֹאbut does not יַגִּידtestify

A partner to a thief hates his own soul; he receives the oath, but does not testify.

29:25

חֶרְדַּתfear אָדָםman יִתֵּןset מוֹקֵשׁsnare וּבוֹטֵחַbut whoever trusts בַּיהוָהin the LORD יְשֻׂגָּבhigh

The fear of man is a snare, but whoever trusts in the LORD is set securely on high.

29:26

רַבִּיםmany מְבַקְשִׁיםseek פְּנֵיfavor מוֹשֵׁלruler’s וּמֵיְהוָהfrom the LORD מִשְׁפַּטjustice אִישׁman

Many seek the ruler’s favor, but a man receives justice from the LORD.

29:27

תּוֹעֲבַתdetestable צַדִּיקִיםrighteous אִישׁman עָוֶלunjust וְתוֹעֲבַתdetestable רָשָׁעwicked יְשַׁרupright דָּרֶךְway

An unjust man is detestable to the righteous, and one whose way is upright is detestable to the wicked.
