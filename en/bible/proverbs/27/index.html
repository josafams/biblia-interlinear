Proverbs 27

Sincere friendship and care for the flock

Open in the interlinear reader

27:1

אַלdo not תִּתְהַלֵּלboast בְּיוֹםin day מָחָרtomorrow כִּיfor לֹאnot תֵדַעknow מַהwhat יֵּלֶדbirth יוֹםday

Do not boast about tomorrow, for you do not know what a day may bring.

27:2

יְהַלֶּלְךָpraise you זָרoutsider וְלֹאand not פִיךָyour own mouth נָכְרִיstranger וְאַלand not שְׂפָתֶיךָyour own lips

Let another praise you, and not your own mouth— a stranger, and not your own lips.

27:3

כֹּבֶדstress אֶבֶןstone וְנֵטֶלheavy and הַחוֹלsand is a וְכַעַסbut aggravation אֱוִילfool כָּבֵדoutweighs מִשְּׁנֵיהֶםthem both

A stone is heavy and sand is a burden, but aggravation from a fool outweighs them both.

27:4

אַכְזְרִיּוּתcruel חֵמָהwrath וְשֶׁטֶףcruel and אָףanger וּמִיbut who can יַעֲמֹדwithstand לִפְנֵיto before קִנְאָהjealousy

Wrath is cruel and anger is like a flood, but who can withstand jealousy?

27:5

טוֹבָהbetter תּוֹכַחַתrebuke מְגֻלָּהopen מֵאַהֲבָהthan love מְסֻתָּרֶתconcealed

Better an open rebuke than love that is concealed.

27:6

נֶאֱמָנִיםfaithful פִּצְעֵיwounds אוֹהֵבfriend וְנַעְתָּרוֹתfaithful but נְשִׁיקוֹתkisses שׂוֹנֵאenemy

The wounds of a friend are faithful, but the kisses of an enemy are deceitful.

27:7

נֶפֶשׁsoul שְׂבֵעָהfull תָּבוּסloathes נֹפֶתhoney וְנֶפֶשׁsoul רְעֵבָהhungry כָּלany מַרbitter מָתוֹקsweet

The soul that is full loathes honey, but to a hungry soul, any bitter thing is sweet.

27:8

כְּצִפּוֹרlike a bird נוֹדֶדֶתstrays מִןfrom קִנָּהּits nest כֵּןso אִישׁman נוֹדֵדwanders מִמְּקוֹמוֹfrom his home

Like a bird that strays from its nest is a man who wanders from his home.

27:9

שֶׁמֶןoil וּקְטֹרֶתand incense יְשַׂמַּחjoy לֵבheart וּמֶתֶקand the sweetness רֵעֵהוּfriend is מֵעֲצַתcounsel נָפֶשׁsoul

Oil and incense bring joy to the heart, and the sweetness of a friend is counsel to the soul.

27:10

רֵעֲךָyour friend וְרֵעַfriend אָבִיךָyour father’s אַלdo not תַּעֲזֹבforsake וּבֵיתhouse אָחִיךָyour brother’s אַלdo not תָּבוֹאgo בְּיוֹםin the day אֵידֶךָyour calamity טוֹבbetter שָׁכֵןneighbor קָרוֹבnearby מֵאָחthan a brother רָחוֹקfar

Do not forsake your friend or your father’s friend, and do not go to your brother’s house in the day of your calamity; better a neighbor nearby than a brother far away.

27:11

חֲכַםwise בְּנִיmy son וְשַׂמַּחand bring joy לִבִּיmy heart וְאָשִׁיבָהanswer חֹרְפִיtaunts me דָבָרword

Be wise, my son, and bring joy to my heart, so that I can answer him who taunts me.

27:12

עָרוּםprudent רָאָהsee רָעָהdanger נִסְתָּרcover פְּתָאיִםsimple עָבְרוּgoing נֶעֱנָשׁוּpenalty

The prudent see danger and take cover; but the simple keep going and pay the penalty.

27:13

קַחtake בִּגְדוֹgarment of him כִּיfor עָרַבsecurity זָרposts וּבְעַדfor נָכְרִיָּהforeigner חַבְלֵהוּcollateral if it

Take the garment of him who posts security for a stranger; get collateral if it is for a foreigner.

27:14

מְבָרֵךְblesses רֵעֵהוּhis neighbor בְּקוֹלvoice גָּדוֹלloud בַּבֹּקֶרin the morning הַשְׁכֵּיםearly קְלָלָהcurse תֵּחָשֶׁבcounted לוֹto him

If one blesses his neighbor with a loud voice early in the morning, it will be counted to him as a curse.

27:15

דֶּלֶףconstant טוֹרֵדdripping בְּיוֹםday סַגְרִירrainy וְאֵשֶׁתwoman מִדְיָנִיםcontentious נִשְׁתָּוָהalike

A constant dripping on a rainy day and a contentious woman are alike—

27:16

צֹפְנֶיהָrestraining her צָפַןtreasured רוּחַwind וְשֶׁמֶןor oil יְמִינוֹright hand יִקְרָאcalls

restraining her is like holding back the wind or grasping oil with one’s right hand.

27:17

בַּרְזֶלiron בְּבַרְזֶלas iron יָחַדsharpens וְאִישׁso one man יַחַדsharpens פְּנֵיface רֵעֵהוּanother

As iron sharpens iron, so one man sharpens another.

27:18

נֹצֵרtends תְּאֵנָהfig tree יֹאכַלeat פִּרְיָהּits fruit וְשֹׁמֵרand he who אֲדֹנָיוhis master יְכֻבָּדhonored

Whoever tends a fig tree will eat its fruit, and he who looks after his master will be honored.

27:19

כַּמַּיִםas water הַפָּנִיםthe presence לַפָּנִיםthe face כֵּןso לֵבheart הָאָדָםreflects the true לָאָדָםman

As water reflects the face, so the heart reflects the true man.

27:20

שְׁאוֹלSheol וַאֲבַדּוֹand Abaddon לֹאnever תִשְׂבַּעְנָהsatisfied וְעֵינֵיso the eyes הָאָדָםof man לֹאnever תִשְׂבַּעְנָהsatisfied

Sheol and Abaddon are never satisfied; so the eyes of man are never satisfied.

27:21

מַצְרֵףcrucible לַכֶּסֶףfor silver וְכוּרand a furnace לַזָּהָבfor gold וְאִישׁbut a man לְפִיto mouth מַהֲלָלוֹpraise accorded him

A crucible for silver and a furnace for gold, but a man is tested by the praise accorded him.

27:22

אִםif תִּכְתּוֹשׁgrind אֶת[obj] הָאֱוִילa fool בַּמַּכְתֵּשׁlike grain בְּתוֹךְin among הָרִיפוֹתmortar בַּעֱלִיand a pestle לֹאnot תָסוּרdepart מֵעָלָיוfrom the him אִוַּלְתּוֹfolly

Though you grind a fool like grain with mortar and a pestle, yet his folly will not depart from him.

27:23

יָדֹעַsure תֵּדַעknow פְּנֵיthe state צֹאנֶךָyour flocks שִׁיתpay close לִבְּךָattention your לַעֲדָרִיםto herds

Be sure to know the state of your flocks, and pay close attention to your herds;

27:24

כִּיfor לֹאnot לְעוֹלָםforever חֹסֶןnor וְאִםand if נֵזֶרcrown לְדוֹרendure to every וָדוֹרgeneration

for riches are not forever, nor does a crown endure to every generation.

27:25

גָּלָהremoved חָצִירnew וְנִרְאָהappears דֶשֶׁאgrowth וְנֶאֶסְפוּgathered עִשְּׂבוֹתgrain הָרִיםhills

When hay is removed and new growth appears and the grain from the hills is gathered,

27:26

כְּבָשִׂיםlambs לִלְבוּשֶׁךָprovide you with clothing וּמְחִירprice שָׂדֶהfield עַתּוּדִיםgoats

the lambs will provide you with clothing, and the goats with the price of a field.

27:27

וְדֵיwill have plenty חֲלֵבmilk עִזִּיםgoats לְלַחְמְךָto feed you לְלֶחֶםfood for בֵּיתֶךָyour household וְחַיִּיםand nourishment לְנַעֲרוֹתֶיךָfor your maidservants

You will have plenty of goats’ milk to feed you— food for your household and nourishment for your maidservants.
