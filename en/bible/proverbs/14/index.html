Proverbs 14

The wise and the foolish; righteousness exalts a nation

Open in the interlinear reader

14:1

חַכְמוֹתwise נָשִׁיםwoman בָּנְתָהbuilds בֵיתָהּher house וְאִוֶּלֶתbut a foolish בְּיָדֶיהָher own hands תֶהֶרְסֶנּוּtears it down

Every wise woman builds her house, but a foolish one tears it down with her own hands.

14:2

הוֹלֵךְwalks בְּיָשְׁרוֹin uprightness יְרֵאfears יְהוָהLORD וּנְלוֹזdevious דְּרָכָיוhis ways בּוֹזֵהוּdespises him

He who walks in uprightness fears the LORD, but the one who is devious in his ways despises Him.

14:3

בְּפִיspeech אֱוִילfool חֹטֶרbrings גַּאֲוָהproud וְשִׂפְתֵיbut the lips חֲכָמִיםwise תִּשְׁמוּרֵםprotect them

The proud speech of a fool brings a rod to his back, but the lips of the wise protect them.

14:4

בְּאֵיןno אֲלָפִיםoxen אֵבוּסmanger בָּרempty וְרָבbut an abundant תְּבוּאוֹתharvest בְּכֹחַthrough the strength שׁוֹרox

Where there are no oxen, the manger is empty, but an abundant harvest comes through the strength of the ox.

14:5

עֵדwitness אֱמוּנִיםhonest לֹאnot יְכַזֵּבdeceive וְיָפִיחַpours כְּזָבִיםpours עֵדwitness שָׁקֶרlies

An honest witness does not deceive, but a dishonest witness pours forth lies.

14:6

בִּקֶּשׁseeks לֵץmocker חָכְמָהwisdom וָאָיִןand finds none וְדַעַתbut knowledge לְנָבוֹןto discerning נָקָלcurse

A mocker seeks wisdom and finds none, but knowledge comes easily to the discerning.

14:7

לֵךְaway מִנֶּגֶדfrom distance לְאִישׁa man כְּסִילfoolish וּבַלwill no יָדַעְתָּgain שִׂפְתֵיspeech דָעַתknowledge

Stay away from a foolish man; you will gain no knowledge from his speech.

14:8

חָכְמַתwisdom עָרוּםprudent הָבִיןdiscern דַּרְכּוֹhis way וְאִוֶּלֶתbut the folly כְּסִילִיםfools מִרְמָהdeceives

The wisdom of the prudent is to discern his way, but the folly of fools deceives them.

14:9

אֱוִלִיםfools יָלִיץmock אָשָׁםamends goodwill וּבֵיןamong יְשָׁרִיםupright רָצוֹןfavor

Fools mock the making of amends, but goodwill is found among the upright.

14:10

לֵבheart יוֹדֵעַknows מָרַּתbitterness נַפְשׁוֹits own וּבְשִׂמְחָתוֹin its joy לֹאno יִתְעָרַבand זָרstranger

The heart knows its own bitterness, and no stranger shares in its joy.

14:11

בֵּיתhouse רְשָׁעִיםwicked יִשָּׁמֵדdestroyed וְאֹהֶלbut the tent יְשָׁרִיםupright יַפְרִיחַflourish

The house of the wicked will be destroyed, but the tent of the upright will flourish.

14:12

יֵשׁthere דֶּרֶךְway יָשָׁרright לִפְנֵיseems to אִישׁman וְאַחֲרִיתָהּbut its end דַּרְכֵיway מָוֶתdeath

There is a way that seems right to a man, but its end is the way of death.

14:13

גַּםeven בִּשְׂחוֹקin laughter יִכְאַבpain לֵבheart וְאַחֲרִיתָהּend in שִׂמְחָהjoy תוּגָהsorrow

Even in laughter the heart may ache, and joy may end in sorrow.

14:14

מִדְּרָכָיוhis own ways יִשְׂבַּעfill סוּגbackslider לֵבheart וּמֵעָלָיוand from the him אִישׁman טוֹבgood

The backslider in heart receives the fill of his own ways, but a good man is rewarded for his ways.

14:15

פֶּתִיsimple יַאֲמִיןbelieves לְכָלevery דָּבָרword וְעָרוּםbut the prudent יָבִיןwatches לַאֲשֻׁרוֹhis steps

The simple man believes every word, but the prudent man watches his steps.

14:16

חָכָםwise יָרֵאfears וְסָרand turns מֵרָעfrom evil וּכְסִילbut a fool מִתְעַבֵּרcareless וּבוֹטֵחַand reckless

A wise man fears and turns from evil, but a fool is careless and reckless.

14:17

קְצַרquick tempered אַפַּיִםman יַעֲשֶׂהacts אִוֶּלֶתfoolishly וְאִישׁman מְזִמּוֹתdevious יִשָּׂנֵאhated

A quick-tempered man acts foolishly, and a devious man is hated.

14:18

נָחֲלוּinherit פְתָאיִםsimple אִוֶּלֶתfolly וַעֲרוּמִיםbut the prudent יַכְתִּרוּcrowned דָעַתknowledge

The simple inherit folly, but the prudent are crowned with knowledge.

14:19

שַׁחוּthe bow רָעִיםevil לִפְנֵיbefore טוֹבִיםgood וּרְשָׁעִיםand the wicked עַלon שַׁעֲרֵיgates צַדִּיקrighteous

The evil bow before the good, and the wicked at the gates of the righteous.

14:20

גַּםeven לְרֵעֵהוּby his neighbor יִשָּׂנֵאhated רָשׁpoor וְאֹהֲבֵיlove עָשִׁירrich רַבִּיםmany

The poor man is hated even by his neighbor, but many are those who love the rich.

14:21

בָּזdespises לְרֵעֵהוּhis neighbor חוֹטֵאsins וּמְחוֹנֵןshows kindness עֲנָוִיםpoor אַשְׁרָיוblessed is he who

He who despises his neighbor sins, but blessed is he who shows kindness to the poor.

14:22

הֲלוֹאdo not יִתְעוּastray חֹרְשֵׁיcontrive רָעevil וְחֶסֶדloving devotion וֶאֱמֶתand faithfulness חֹרְשֵׁיplan טוֹבgoodness

Do not those who contrive evil go astray? But those who plan goodness find loving devotion and faithfulness.

14:23

בְּכָלin all עֶצֶבlabor יִהְיֶהthere מוֹתָרprofit וּדְבַרbut mere talk שְׂפָתַיִםleads אַךְonly לְמַחְסוֹרto poverty

There is profit in all labor, but mere talk leads only to poverty.

14:24

עֲטֶרֶתcrown חֲכָמִיםwise עָשְׁרָםtheir wealth אִוֶּלֶתeffort כְּסִילִיםfools אִוֶּלֶתfolly

The crown of the wise is their wealth, but the effort of fools is folly.

14:25

מַצִּילsaves נְפָשׁוֹתlives עֵדa witness אֱמֶתtruthful וְיָפִחַbut one who utters כְּזָבִיםutters lies מִרְמָהdeceitful

A truthful witness saves lives, but one who utters lies is deceitful.

14:26

בְּיִרְאַתfears יְהוָהLORD מִבְטַחconfidence עֹזstrength וּלְבָנָיוand his children shall יִהְיֶהbe מַחְסֶהrefuge

He who fears the LORD is secure in confidence, and his children shall have a place of refuge.

14:27

יִרְאַתfear יְהוָהLORD מְקוֹרfountain חַיִּיםlife לָסוּרturning מִמֹּקְשֵׁיfrom the snares מָוֶתdeath

The fear of the LORD is a fountain of life, turning a man from the snares of death.

14:28

בְּרָבa large עָםpeople הַדְרַתsplendor מֶלֶךְking’s וּבְאֶפֶסbut a lack לְאֹםprince’s מְחִתַּתruin רָזוֹןprince’s

A large population is a king’s splendor, but a lack of subjects is a prince’s ruin.

14:29

אֶרֶךְpatient אַפַּיִםman רַבgreat תְּבוּנָהunderstanding וּקְצַרbut a quick tempered רוּחַpromotes מֵרִיםpromotes אִוֶּלֶתfolly

A patient man has great understanding, but a quick-tempered man promotes folly.

14:30

חַיֵּיlife בְשָׂרִיםbody לֵבheart מַרְפֵּאtranquil וּרְקַבbut rots עֲצָמוֹתbones קִנְאָהenvy

A tranquil heart is life to the body, but envy rots the bones.

14:31

עֹשֵׁקoppresses דָּלpoor חֵרֵףtaunts עֹשֵׂהוּMaker וּמְכַבְּדוֹhonors him חֹנֵןkind אֶבְיוֹןneedy

Whoever oppresses the poor taunts their Maker, but whoever is kind to the needy honors Him.

14:32

בְּרָעָתוֹwicked יִדָּחֶהthrown רָשָׁעsin וְחֹסֶהrefuge בְמוֹתוֹin death צַדִּיקrighteous

The wicked man is thrown down by his own sin, but the righteous man has a refuge even in death.

14:33

בְּלֵבin the heart נָבוֹןdiscerning תָּנוּחַrests חָכְמָהwisdom וּבְקֶרֶבeven among כְּסִילִיםfools תִּוָּדֵעַknown

Wisdom rests in the heart of the discerning; even among fools she is known.

14:34

צְדָקָהrighteousness תְרוֹמֵםexalts גּוֹיnation וְחֶסֶדdisgrace לְאֻמִּיםpeople חַטָּאתsin

Righteousness exalts a nation, but sin is a disgrace to any people.

14:35

רְצוֹןdelights מֶלֶךְking לְעֶבֶדservant מַשְׂכִּילwise וְעֶבְרָתוֹbut his anger falls תִּהְיֶהbe מֵבִישׁshameful

A king delights in a wise servant, but his anger falls on the shameful.
