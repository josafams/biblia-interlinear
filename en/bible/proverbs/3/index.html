Proverbs 3

Trust in the LORD and the worth of wisdom

Open in the interlinear reader

3:1

בְּנִיmy son תּוֹרָתִיmy teaching אַלdo not תִּשְׁכָּחforget וּמִצְוֺתַיmy commandments יִצֹּרkeep לִבֶּךָyour heart

My son, do not forget my teaching, but let your heart keep my commandments;

3:2

כִּיfor אֹרֶךְlength יָמִיםdays וּשְׁנוֹתyears חַיִּיםlife וְשָׁלוֹםand peace יוֹסִיפוּadd לָךְto your

for they will add length to your days, years and peace to your life.

3:3

חֶסֶדloving devotion וֶאֱמֶתor faithfulness אַלdo not יַעַזְבֻךָleave you קָשְׁרֵםbind them עַלon גַּרְגְּרוֹתֶיךָyour neck כָּתְבֵםwrite them עַלon לוּחַtablet לִבֶּךָyour heart

Never let loving devotion or faithfulness leave you; bind them around your neck, write them on the tablet of your heart.

3:4

וּמְצָאfind חֵןfavor וְשֵׂכֶלand regard טוֹבhigh בְּעֵינֵיin the sight אֱלֹהִיםGod וְאָדָםand man

Then you will find favor and high regard in the sight of God and man.

3:5

בְּטַחtrust אֶלto יְהוָהLORD בְּכָלwith all לִבֶּךָyour heart וְאֶלand to בִּינָתְךָyour understanding אַלnot תִּשָּׁעֵןlean

Trust in the LORD with all your heart, and lean not on your own understanding;

3:6

בְּכָלin all דְּרָכֶיךָyour ways דָעֵהוּacknowledge him וְהוּאand he יְיַשֵּׁרstraight אֹרְחֹתֶיךָyour paths

in all your ways acknowledge Him, and He will make your paths straight.

3:7

אַלnot תְּהִיbe חָכָםwise בְּעֵינֶיךָin your own eyes יְרָאfear אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD וְסוּרand turn מֵרָעfrom evil

Be not wise in your own eyes; fear the LORD and turn away from evil.

3:8

רִפְאוּתhealing תְּהִיthis לְשָׁרֶּךָyour body וְשִׁקּוּיand refreshment לְעַצְמוֹתֶיךָto your bones

This will bring healing to your body and refreshment to your bones.

3:9

כַּבֵּדhonor אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD מֵהוֹנֶךָwith your wealth וּמֵרֵאשִׁיתand with the firstfruits כָּלall תְּבוּאָתֶךָyour harvest

Honor the LORD with your wealth and with the firstfruits of all your harvest;

3:10

וְיִמָּלְאוּfilled אֲסָמֶיךָyour barns שָׂבָעplenty וְתִירוֹשׁnew wine יְקָבֶיךָyour vats יִפְרֹצוּoverflow

then your barns will be filled with plenty, and your vats will overflow with new wine.

3:11

מוּסַרdiscipline יְהוָהLORD בְּנִיmy son אַלdo not תִּמְאָסreject וְאַלand do not תָּקֹץloathe בְּתוֹכַחְתּוֹhis rebuke

My son, do not reject the discipline of the LORD, and do not loathe His rebuke;

3:12

כִּיfor אֶת[obj] אֲשֶׁרthat יֶאֱהַבloves יְהוָהLORD יוֹכִיחַdisciplines וּכְאָבfather אֶת[obj] בֵּןson יִרְצֶהdelights

for the LORD disciplines the one He loves, as does a father the son in whom he delights.

3:13

אַשְׁרֵיblessed אָדָםman מָצָאfinds חָכְמָהwisdom וְאָדָםman יָפִיקacquires תְּבוּנָהunderstanding

Blessed is the man who finds wisdom, the man who acquires understanding,

3:14

כִּיfor טוֹבbetter סַחְרָהּher gain מִסְּחַרprofitable than כָּסֶףsilver וּמֵחָרוּץthan fine gold תְּבוּאָתָהּher gain is

for she is more profitable than silver, and her gain is better than fine gold.

3:15

יְקָרָהprecious הִיאis מִפְּנִינִיםthan rubies וְכָלnothing חֲפָצֶיךָyou desire לֹאnot יִשְׁווּcompares בָהּwith her

She is more precious than rubies; nothing you desire compares with her.

3:16

אֹרֶךְlong יָמִיםlife בִּימִינָהּin her right hand בִּשְׂמֹאולָהּin her left עֹשֶׁרriches וְכָבוֹדand honor

Long life is in her right hand; in her left hand are riches and honor.

3:17

דְּרָכֶיהָall her דַרְכֵיways נֹעַםpleasant וְכָלand all נְתִיבוֹתֶיהָher paths שָׁלוֹםpeaceful

All her ways are pleasant, and all her paths are peaceful.

3:18

עֵץtree חַיִּיםlife הִיאis לַמַּחֲזִיקִיםto those who embrace בָּהּin her וְתֹמְכֶיהָlay מְאֻשָּׁרblessed

She is a tree of life to those who embrace her, and those who lay hold of her are blessed.

3:19

יְהוָהLORD בְּחָכְמָהby wisdom יָסַדfounded אָרֶץearth כּוֹנֵןestablished שָׁמַיִםheavens בִּתְבוּנָהby understanding

The LORD founded the earth by wisdom and established the heavens by understanding.

3:20

בְּדַעְתּוֹby his knowledge תְּהוֹמוֹתwatery depths נִבְקָעוּopen וּשְׁחָקִיםand the clouds יִרְעֲפוּdripped טָלdew

By His knowledge the watery depths were broken open, and the clouds dripped with dew.

3:21

בְּנִיmy son אַלdo not יָלֻזוּlose מֵעֵינֶיךָsight נְצֹרpreserve תֻּשִׁיָּהsound judgment וּמְזִמָּהand discernment

My son, do not lose sight of this: Preserve sound judgment and discernment.

3:22

וְיִהְיוּthey will be חַיִּיםlife לְנַפְשֶׁךָto your soul וְחֵןand adornment לְגַרְגְּרֹתֶיךָto your neck

They will be life to your soul and adornment to your neck.

3:23

אָזthen תֵּלֵךְgo לָבֶטַחsafety דַּרְכֶּךָyour way וְרַגְלְךָand your foot לֹאnot תִגּוֹףstumble

Then you will go on your way in safety, and your foot will not stumble.

3:24

אִםif תִּשְׁכַּבlie לֹאnot תִפְחָדafraid וְשָׁכַבְתָּwhen you rest וְעָרְבָהwill be sweet שְׁנָתֶךָyour sleep

When you lie down, you will not be afraid; when you rest, your sleep will be sweet.

3:25

אַלdo not תִּירָאfear מִפַּחַדnot fear danger פִּתְאֹםsudden וּמִשֹּׁאַתor the ruin רְשָׁעִיםwicked כִּיthat תָבֹאcome

Do not fear sudden danger or the ruin that overtakes the wicked,

3:26

כִּיfor יְהוָהLORD יִהְיֶהbe בְכִסְלֶךָyour confidence וְשָׁמַרand will keep רַגְלְךָyour foot מִלָּכֶדfrom the snare

for the LORD will be your confidence and will keep your foot from the snare.

3:27

אַלdo not תִּמְנַעwithhold טוֹבgood מִבְּעָלָיוfrom the deserving when it בִּהְיוֹתin was לְאֵלpower יָדְךָyour power לַעֲשׂוֹתto act

Do not withhold good from the deserving when it is within your power to act.

3:28

אַלdo not תֹּאמַרtell לְרֵעֲךָyour neighbor לֵךְcome וָשׁוּבback וּמָחָרtomorrow and I אֶתֵּןprovide וְיֵשׁhave אִתָּךְthe means

Do not tell your neighbor, “Come back tomorrow and I will provide”— when you already have the means.

3:29

אַלdo not תַּחֲרֹשׁdevise עַלagainst רֵעֲךָyour neighbor רָעָהevil וְהוּאand he יוֹשֵׁבdwells לָבֶטַחbeside אִתָּךְwith you

Do not devise evil against your neighbor, for he trustfully dwells beside you.

3:30

אַלdo not תָּרִיבaccuse עִםwith אָדָםman חִנָּםwithout cause אִםif לֹאno גְמָלְךָdone you רָעָהharm

Do not accuse a man without cause, when he has done you no harm.

3:31

אַלdo not תְּקַנֵּאenvy בְּאִישׁa man חָמָסviolent וְאַלor תִּבְחַרchoose בְּכָלany דְּרָכָיוhis ways

Do not envy a violent man or choose any of his ways;

3:32

כִּיfor תוֹעֲבַתdetests יְהוָהLORD נָלוֹזperverse וְאֶתto יְשָׁרִיםupright סוֹדוֹhis friends

for the LORD detests the perverse, but He is a friend to the upright.

3:33

מְאֵרַתcurse יְהוָהLORD בְּבֵיתon the house רָשָׁעwicked וּנְוֵהhome צַדִּיקִיםrighteous יְבָרֵךְblesses

The curse of the LORD is on the house of the wicked, but He blesses the home of the righteous.

3:34

אִםif לַלֵּצִיםmocks the הוּאhe יָלִיץmockers וְלַעֲנָוִיםto the humble יִתֶּןgives חֵןgrace

He mocks the mockers, but gives grace to the humble.

3:35

כָּבוֹדhonor חֲכָמִיםwise יִנְחָלוּinherit וּכְסִילִיםbut fools מֵרִיםheld קָלוֹןshame

The wise will inherit honor, but fools are held up to shame.
