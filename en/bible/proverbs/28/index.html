Proverbs 28

Those who keep the law and those who forsake it

Open in the interlinear reader

28:1

נָסוּflee וְאֵיןwhen no רֹדֵףpursues רָשָׁעwicked וְצַדִּיקִיםbut the righteous כִּכְפִירlion יִבְטָחbold

The wicked flee when no one pursues, but the righteous are as bold as a lion.

28:2

בְּפֶשַׁעin rebellion אֶרֶץland רַבִּיםmany שָׂרֶיהָrulers a וּבְאָדָםbut a man מֵבִיןunderstanding יֹדֵעַknowledge כֵּןso יַאֲרִיךְmaintains

A land in rebellion has many rulers, but a man of understanding and knowledge maintains order.

28:3

גֶּבֶרa destitute רָשׁleader וְעֹשֵׁקwho oppresses דַּלִּיםpoor מָטָרrain סֹחֵףdriving וְאֵיןleaves no לָחֶםfood

A destitute leader who oppresses the poor is like a driving rain that leaves no food.

28:4

עֹזְבֵיforsake תוֹרָהlaw יְהַלְלוּpraise רָשָׁעwicked וְשֹׁמְרֵיkeep תוֹרָהlaw יִתְגָּרוּresist בָםin them

Those who forsake the law praise the wicked, but those who keep the law resist them.

28:5

אַנְשֵׁיmen רָעevil לֹאnot יָבִינוּunderstand מִשְׁפָּטjustice וּמְבַקְשֵׁיseek יְהוָהLORD יָבִינוּcomprehend כֹלfully

Evil men do not understand justice, but those who seek the LORD comprehend fully.

28:6

טוֹבbetter רָשׁpoor הוֹלֵךְwalks בְּתֻמּוֹwith integrity מֵעִקֵּשׁperverse דְּרָכַיִםways וְהוּאand he עָשִׁירrich

Better a poor man who walks with integrity than a rich man whose ways are perverse.

28:7

נוֹצֵרkeeps תּוֹרָהlaw בֵּןson מֵבִיןdiscerning וְרֹעֶהbut a companion זוֹלְלִיםgluttons יַכְלִיםdisgraces אָבִיוhis father

A discerning son keeps the law, but a companion of gluttons disgraces his father.

28:8

מַרְבֶּהincreases הוֹנוֹhis wealth בְּנֶשֶׁךְby interest usury וְתַרְבִּיתinterest and לְחוֹנֵןkind דַּלִּיםpoor יִקְבְּצֶנּוּlays it

He who increases his wealth by interest and usury lays it up for one who is kind to the poor.

28:9

מֵסִירturns אָזְנוֹhis ear מִשְּׁמֹעַfrom hearing תּוֹרָהlaw גַּםeven תְּפִלָּתוֹhis prayer תּוֹעֵבָהdetestable

Whoever turns his ear away from hearing the law, even his prayer is detestable.

28:10

מַשְׁגֶּהleads יְשָׁרִיםupright בְּדֶרֶךְalong the path רָעevil בִּשְׁחוּתוֹinto his own pit הוּאwill יִפּוֹלfall וּתְמִימִיםbut the blameless יִנְחֲלוּinherit טוֹבgood

He who leads the upright along the path of evil will fall into his own pit, but the blameless will inherit what is good.

28:11

חָכָםwise בְּעֵינָיוin his eyes אִישׁman עָשִׁירrich וְדַלbut a poor מֵבִיןdiscernment יַחְקְרֶנּוּsees through him

A rich man is wise in his own eyes, but a poor man with discernment sees through him.

28:12

בַּעֲלֹץtriumph צַדִּיקִיםrighteous רַבָּהgreat תִפְאָרֶתglory וּבְקוּםrise רְשָׁעִיםwicked יְחֻפַּשׂhide אָדָםmen

When the righteous triumph, there is great glory, but when the wicked rise, men hide themselves.

28:13

מְכַסֶּהconceals פְשָׁעָיוhis sins will לֹאnot יַצְלִיחַprosper וּמוֹדֶהbut whoever confesses וְעֹזֵבand renounces יְרֻחָםmercy

He who conceals his sins will not prosper, but whoever confesses and renounces them will find mercy.

28:14

אַשְׁרֵיblessed אָדָםman מְפַחֵדreverent תָּמִידalways וּמַקְשֶׁהhardens לִבּוֹhis heart יִפּוֹלfalls בְּרָעָהinto trouble

Blessed is the man who is always reverent, but he who hardens his heart falls into trouble.

28:15

אֲרִיlion נֹהֵםroaring וְדֹבbear שׁוֹקֵקcharging מֹשֵׁלruler רָשָׁעwicked עַלover עַםpeople דָּלhelpless

Like a roaring lion or a charging bear is a wicked ruler over a helpless people.

28:16

נָגִידleader חֲסַרlacks תְּבוּנוֹתjudgment וְרַבalso a great מַעֲשַׁקּוֹתoppressor שֹׂנֵאhates בֶצַעdishonest profit יַאֲרִיךְprolong יָמִיםdays

A leader who lacks judgment is also a great oppressor, but he who hates dishonest profit will prolong his days.

28:17

אָדָםman עָשֻׁקburdened בְּדַםby bloodguilt נָפֶשׁwill עַדuntil בּוֹרpit יָנוּסflee אַלno יִתְמְכוּsupport בוֹin him

A man burdened by bloodguilt will flee into the Pit; let no one support him.

28:18

הוֹלֵךְwalks תָּמִיםintegrity יִוָּשֵׁעַsafe וְנֶעְקַשׁperverse דְּרָכַיִםways יִפּוֹלfall בְּאֶחָתin one

He who walks with integrity will be kept safe, but whoever is perverse in his ways will suddenly fall.

28:19

עֹבֵדworks אַדְמָתוֹhis land יִשְׂבַּעplenty לָחֶםfood וּמְרַדֵּףbut whoever chases רֵקִיםchases fantasies יִשְׂבַּעfill רִישׁpoverty

The one who works his land will have plenty of food, but whoever chases fantasies will have his fill of poverty.

28:20

אִישׁman אֱמוּנוֹתfaithful רַבabound בְּרָכוֹתblessings וְאָץbut one eager לְהַעֲשִׁירto be rich לֹאnot יִנָּקֶהgo unpunished

A faithful man will abound with blessings, but one eager to be rich will not go unpunished.

28:21

הַכֵּרshow partiality פָּנִיםpartiality לֹאnot טוֹבgood וְעַלfor פַּתpiece לֶחֶםbread יִפְשַׁעwrong גָּבֶרman

To show partiality is not good, yet a man will do wrong for a piece of bread.

28:22

נִבֳהָלhastens לַהוֹןafter wealth אִישׁman רַעstingy עָיִןeye וְלֹאand does not יֵדַעknow כִּיthat חֶסֶרpoverty יְבֹאֶנּוּawaits him

A stingy man hastens after wealth and does not know that poverty awaits him.

28:23

מוֹכִיחַrebukes אָדָםman אַחֲרַיlater חֵןfavor יִמְצָאfind מִמַּחֲלִיקflatters לָשׁוֹןtongue

He who rebukes a man will later find more favor than one who flatters with his tongue.

28:24

גּוֹזֵלrobs אָבִיוhis father וְאִמּוֹor mother saying it וְאֹמֵרit is אֵיןnot פָּשַׁעwrong חָבֵרcompanion הוּאis לְאִישׁto the man מַשְׁחִיתdestroys

He who robs his father or mother, saying, “It is not wrong,” is a companion to the man who destroys.

28:25

רְחַבgreedy נֶפֶשׁman יְגָרֶהstirs מָדוֹןstrife וּבוֹטֵחַtrusts עַלin יְהוָהLORD יְדֻשָּׁןprosper

A greedy man stirs up strife, but he who trusts in the LORD will prosper.

28:26

בּוֹטֵחַtrusts בְּלִבּוֹin himself הוּאis כְסִילfool וְהוֹלֵךְwalks בְּחָכְמָהin wisdom הוּאhe יִמָּלֵטsafe

He who trusts in himself is a fool, but one who walks in wisdom will be safe.

28:27

נוֹתֵןgives לָרָשׁto the poor אֵיןnot מַחְסוֹרneed וּמַעְלִיםhides עֵינָיוhis eyes will רַבmany מְאֵרוֹתcurses

Whoever gives to the poor will not be in need, but he who hides his eyes will receive many curses.

28:28

בְּקוּםcome רְשָׁעִיםwicked יִסָּתֵרhide אָדָםpeople וּבְאָבְדָםbut when they perish יִרְבּוּflourish צַדִּיקִיםrighteous flourish

When the wicked come to power, people hide themselves; but when they perish, the righteous flourish.
