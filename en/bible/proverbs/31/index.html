Proverbs 31

Lemuel and the praise of the noble wife

Open in the interlinear reader

31:1

דִּבְרֵיwords לְמוּאֵלLemuel מֶלֶךְking מַשָּׂאburden אֲשֶׁרthat יִסְּרַתּוּhis taught him אִמּוֹmother

These are the words of King Lemuel—the burden that his mother taught him:

31:2

מַהwhat בְּרִיmy son וּמַהwhat בַּרson בִּטְנִיmy womb וּמֶהwhat בַּרson נְדָרָיmy vows

What shall I say, O my son? What, O son of my womb? What, O son of my vows?

31:3

אַלdo not תִּתֵּןspend לַנָּשִׁיםon women חֵילֶךָyour strength וּדְרָכֶיךָand your ways לַמְחוֹתthose who ruin מְלָכִיןkings

Do not spend your strength on women or your vigor on those who ruin kings.

31:4

אַלnot לַמְלָכִיםfor kings לְמוֹאֵלLemuel אַלnot לַמְלָכִיםfor kings שְׁתוֹdrink יָיִןwine וּלְרוֹזְנִיםor for rulers אֵיwhere שֵׁכָרstrong drink

It is not for kings, O Lemuel, it is not for kings to drink wine, or for rulers to crave strong drink,

31:5

פֶּןlest יִשְׁתֶּהdrink וְיִשְׁכַּחand forget מְחֻקָּקdecreed וִישַׁנֶּהdepriving דִּיןjustice כָּלall בְּנֵיsons עֹנִיoppressed

lest they drink and forget what is decreed, depriving all the oppressed of justice.

31:6

תְּנוּgive שֵׁכָרstrong drink לְאוֹבֵדperishing וְיַיִןand wine לְמָרֵיto the bitter נָפֶשׁsoul

Give strong drink to one who is perishing, and wine to the bitter in soul.

31:7

יִשְׁתֶּהdrink וְיִשְׁכַּחand forget רִישׁוֹhis poverty וַעֲמָלוֹand his misery לֹאno יִזְכָּרremember עוֹדmore

Let him drink and forget his poverty, and remember his misery no more.

31:8

פְּתַחopen פִּיךָyour mouth לְאִלֵּםno voice אֶלto דִּיןcause כָּלall בְּנֵיsons חֲלוֹףdispossessed

Open your mouth for those with no voice, for the cause of all the dispossessed.

31:9

פְּתַחopen פִּיךָyour mouth שְׁפָטjudge צֶדֶקrighteously וְדִיןand defend עָנִיpoor וְאֶבְיוֹןand needy

Open your mouth, judge righteously, and defend the cause of the poor and needy.

31:10

אֵשֶׁתwife חַיִלnoble character מִיwho יִמְצָאfind וְרָחֹקshe is far מִפְּנִינִיםmore than rubies מִכְרָהּprecious

A wife of noble character, who can find? She is far more precious than rubies.

31:11

בָּטַחtrusts בָּהּin לֵבheart בַּעְלָהּhusband וְשָׁלָלand he lacks לֹאnot יֶחְסָרvalue

The heart of her husband trusts in her, and he lacks nothing of value.

31:12

גְּמָלַתְהוּshe brings him טוֹבgood וְלֹאand not רָעharm כֹּלall יְמֵיdays חַיֶּיהher life

She brings him good and not harm all the days of her life.

31:13

דָּרְשָׁהselects צֶמֶרwool וּפִשְׁתִּיםand flax וַתַּעַשׂand works בְּחֵפֶץwith eager כַּפֶּיהָhands

She selects wool and flax and works with eager hands.

31:14

הָיְתָהwas כָּאֳנִיּוֹתships סוֹחֵרmerchant מִמֶּרְחָקfrom afar תָּבִיאbringing לַחְמָהּher food

She is like the merchant ships, bringing her food from afar.

31:15

וַתָּקָםshe rises בְּעוֹדstill לַיְלָהnight וַתִּתֵּןto provide טֶרֶףfood לְבֵיתָהּfor her household וְחֹקand portions לְנַעֲרֹתֶיהָfor her maidservants

She rises while it is still night to provide food for her household and portions for her maidservants.

31:16

זָמְמָהappraises שָׂדֶהfield וַתִּקָּחֵהוּand buys it מִפְּרִיfrom her earnings כַפֶּיהָher hands נָטְעָהplants כָּרֶםvineyard

She appraises a field and buys it; from her earnings she plants a vineyard.

31:17

חָגְרָהgirds herself בְעוֹזwith strength מָתְנֶיהָshows וַתְּאַמֵּץstrong זְרֹעוֹתֶיהָher arms

She girds herself with strength and shows that her arms are strong.

31:18

טָעֲמָהsees כִּיthat טוֹבgood סַחְרָהּher gain לֹאnot יִכְבֶּהextinguished בַלַּיְלָהat night נֵרָהּher lamp

She sees that her gain is good, and her lamp is not extinguished at night.

31:19

יָדֶיהָher hands שִׁלְּחָהout בַכִּישׁוֹרto the distaff grasps וְכַפֶּיהָspindle with her תָּמְכוּuphold פָלֶךְfingers

She stretches out her hands to the distaff and grasps the spindle with her fingers.

31:20

כַּפָּהּopens her פָּרְשָׂהarms לֶעָנִיto the poor וְיָדֶיהָher hands שִׁלְּחָהreaches out לָאֶבְיוֹןto the needy

She opens her arms to the poor and reaches out her hands to the needy.

31:21

לֹאno תִירָאfear לְבֵיתָהּfor her household מִשָּׁלֶגhousehold כִּיfor כָלall בֵּיתָהּher house לָבֻשׁclothed שָׁנִיםscarlet

When it snows, she has no fear for her household, for they are all clothed in scarlet.

31:22

מַרְבַדִּיםcoverings עָשְׂתָהmakes לָּהּfor her שֵׁשׁfine linen וְאַרְגָּמָןand purple לְבוּשָׁהּbed her clothing

She makes coverings for her bed; her clothing is fine linen and purple.

31:23

נוֹדָעknown בַּשְּׁעָרִיםat the city gate בַּעְלָהּher husband בְּשִׁבְתּוֹwhere he sits עִםamong זִקְנֵיelders אָרֶץland

Her husband is known at the city gate, where he sits among the elders of the land.

31:24

סָדִיןlinen garments עָשְׂתָהmakes וַתִּמְכֹּרand sells וַחֲגוֹרshe delivers sashes נָתְנָהgave לַכְּנַעֲנִיto the merchants

She makes linen garments and sells them; she delivers sashes to the merchants.

31:25

עֹזstrength וְהָדָרand honor לְבוּשָׁהּher clothing וַתִּשְׂחַקlaugh לְיוֹםat the days אַחֲרוֹןcome

Strength and honor are her clothing, and she can laugh at the days to come.

31:26

פִּיהָshe her mouth פָּתְחָהopens בְחָכְמָהwith wisdom וְתוֹרַתand instruction חֶסֶדfaithful עַלon לְשׁוֹנָהּher tongue

She opens her mouth with wisdom, and faithful instruction is on her tongue.

31:27

צוֹפִיָּהwatches הֲלִיכוֹתaffairs בֵּיתָהּher household וְלֶחֶםbread עַצְלוּתidleness לֹאnot תֹאכֵלeat

She watches over the affairs of her household and does not eat the bread of idleness.

31:28

קָמוּrise בָנֶיהָher children וַיְאַשְּׁרוּהָand call her blessed בַּעְלָהּher husband וַיְהַלְלָהּpraises her as well

Her children rise up and call her blessed; her husband praises her as well:

31:29

רַבּוֹתmany בָּנוֹתdaughters עָשׂוּdone חָיִלnoble וְאַתְּbut you עָלִיתsurpass עַלon כֻּלָּנָהthem all

“Many daughters have done noble things, but you surpass them all!”

31:30

שֶׁקֶרdeceptive הַחֵןcharm וְהֶבֶלfleeting הַיֹּפִיbeauty אִשָּׁהwoman יִרְאַתfears יְהוָהLORD הִיאis תִתְהַלָּלpraised

Charm is deceptive and beauty is fleeting, but a woman who fears the LORD is to be praised.

31:31

תְּנוּgive לָהּto her מִפְּרִיthe fruit יָדֶיהָher hands וִיהַלְלוּהָpraise her בַשְּׁעָרִיםat the gates מַעֲשֶׂיהָher works

Give her the fruit of her hands, and let her works praise her at the gates.
