Proverbs 12

Truthful words and diligent work

Open in the interlinear reader

12:1

אֹהֵבloves מוּסָרdiscipline אֹהֵבloves דָּעַתknowledge וְשֹׂנֵאhates תוֹכַחַתcorrection בָּעַרstupid

Whoever loves discipline loves knowledge, but he who hates correction is stupid.

12:2

טוֹבgood יָפִיקobtains רָצוֹןfavor מֵיְהוָהfrom the LORD וְאִישׁman מְזִמּוֹתdevises יַרְשִׁיעַevil

The good man obtains favor from the LORD, but the LORD condemns a man who devises evil.

12:3

לֹאnot יִכּוֹןestablished אָדָםman בְּרֶשַׁעthrough wickedness וְשֹׁרֶשׁuprooted צַדִּיקִיםrighteous בַּלcannot יִמּוֹטbe

A man cannot be established through wickedness, but the righteous cannot be uprooted.

12:4

אֵשֶׁתwife חַיִלnoble עֲטֶרֶתcrown בַּעְלָהּher husband’s וּכְרָקָבlike decay בְּעַצְמוֹתָיוin his bones מְבִישָׁהshame

A wife of noble character is her husband’s crown, but she who causes shame is like decay in his bones.

12:5

מַחְשְׁבוֹתplans צַדִּיקִיםrighteous מִשְׁפָּטjust תַּחְבֻּלוֹתcounsel רְשָׁעִיםwicked מִרְמָהdeceit

The plans of the righteous are just, but the counsel of the wicked leads to deceit.

12:6

דִּבְרֵיwords רְשָׁעִיםwicked אֱרָבlie in wait דָּםblood וּפִיbut the speech יְשָׁרִיםupright יַצִּילֵםrescues them

The words of the wicked lie in wait for blood, but the speech of the upright rescues them.

12:7

הָפוֹךְoverthrown רְשָׁעִיםwicked וְאֵינָםand no them וּבֵיתbut the house צַדִּיקִיםrighteous יַעֲמֹדstand

The wicked are overthrown and perish, but the house of the righteous will stand.

12:8

לְפִיaccording שִׂכְלוֹhis wisdom יְהֻלַּלpraised אִישׁman וְנַעֲוֵהbut a twisted לֵבmind יִהְיֶהbe לָבוּזdespised

A man is praised according to his wisdom, but a twisted mind is despised.

12:9

טוֹבbetter נִקְלֶהlightly esteemed וְעֶבֶדservant לוֹto מִמְּתַכַּבֵּדself important וַחֲסַרbut lack לָחֶםfood

Better to be lightly esteemed yet have a servant, than to be self-important but lack food.

12:10

יוֹדֵעַknows צַדִּיקrighteous נֶפֶשׁlife בְּהֶמְתּוֹhis animal וְרַחֲמֵיmercies רְשָׁעִיםwicked אַכְזָרִיcruelty

A righteous man regards the life of his animal, but the tender mercies of the wicked are only cruelty.

12:11

עֹבֵדworks אַדְמָתוֹhis land יִשְׂבַּעplenty לָחֶםfood וּמְרַדֵּףbut whoever chases רֵיקִיםfantasies חֲסַרlacks לֵבjudgment

The one who works his land will have plenty of food, but whoever chases fantasies lacks judgment.

12:12

חָמַדdesire רָשָׁעwicked מְצוֹדplunder רָעִיםevil וְשֹׁרֶשׁbut the root צַדִּיקִיםrighteous יִתֵּןflourishes

The wicked desire the plunder of evil men, but the root of the righteous flourishes.

12:13

בְּפֶשַׁעby his rebellious שְׂפָתַיִםspeech מוֹקֵשׁtrapped רָעevil וַיֵּצֵאescapes מִצָּרָהfrom trouble צַדִּיקrighteous

An evil man is trapped by his rebellious speech, but a righteous man escapes from trouble.

12:14

מִפְּרִיby fruitful פִיspeech אִישׁman יִשְׂבַּעfilled טוֹבgood וּגְמוּלand the work יְדֵיhands אָדָםman יָשִׁיבreturns לוֹto him

By fruitful speech a man is filled with good things, and the work of his hands returns to him.

12:15

דֶּרֶךְway אֱוִילfool יָשָׁרright בְּעֵינָיוin his own eyes וְשֹׁמֵעַlistens לְעֵצָהto counsel חָכָםwise

The way of a fool is right in his own eyes, but a wise man listens to counsel.

12:16

אֱוִילfool’s בַּיּוֹםat once יִוָּדַעknown כַּעְסוֹanger וְכֹסֶהbut a prudent קָלוֹןoverlooks עָרוּםinsult

A fool’s anger is known at once, but a prudent man overlooks an insult.

12:17

יָפִיחַspeaks אֱמוּנָהtruth יַגִּידdeclares צֶדֶקright וְעֵדbut a witness שְׁקָרִיםfalse speaks מִרְמָהdeceit

He who speaks the truth declares what is right, but a false witness speaks deceit.

12:18

יֵשׁthere בּוֹטֶהrashly כְּמַדְקְרוֹתlike a piercing חָרֶבsword וּלְשׁוֹןbut the tongue חֲכָמִיםwise מַרְפֵּאhealing

Speaking rashly is like a piercing sword, but the tongue of the wise brings healing.

12:19

שְׂפַתlips אֱמֶתtruthful תִּכּוֹןendure לָעַדendure forever וְעַדand to אַרְגִּיעָהlasts לְשׁוֹןtongue שָׁקֶרlying

Truthful lips endure forever, but a lying tongue lasts only a moment.

12:20

מִרְמָהdeceit בְּלֶבin the hearts חֹרְשֵׁיdevise רָעevil וּלְיֹעֲצֵיbut the counselors שָׁלוֹםpeace שִׂמְחָהjoy

Deceit is in the hearts of those who devise evil, but the counselors of peace have joy.

12:21

לֹאno יְאֻנֶּהharm לַצַּדִּיקbefalls the righteous כָּלall אָוֶןiniquity וּרְשָׁעִיםbut the wicked מָלְאוּfilled רָעtrouble

No harm befalls the righteous, but the wicked are filled with trouble.

12:22

תּוֹעֲבַתdetestable יְהוָהLORD שִׂפְתֵיlips שָׁקֶרlying וְעֹשֵׂיdeal אֱמוּנָהfaithfully רְצוֹנוֹhis delight

Lying lips are detestable to the LORD, but those who deal faithfully are His delight.

12:23

אָדָםman עָרוּםshrewd כֹּסֶהkeeps דָּעַתknowledge וְלֵבheart כְּסִילִיםfoolish יִקְרָאproclaims אִוֶּלֶתfolly

A shrewd man keeps his knowledge to himself, but a foolish heart proclaims its folly.

12:24

יַדhand חָרוּצִיםdiligent תִּמְשׁוֹלrule ורְמִיָּהbut laziness תִּהְיֶהends לָמַסin forced labor

The hand of the diligent will rule, but laziness ends in forced labor.

12:25

דְּאָגָהanxiety בְלֶבheart אִישׁman יַשְׁחֶנָּהweighs down וְדָבָרword טוֹבgood יְשַׂמְּחֶנָּהcheers it

Anxiety weighs down the heart of a man, but a good word cheers it up.

12:26

יָתֵרcautious מֵרֵעֵהוּfriendship צַדִּיקrighteous וְדֶרֶךְbut the ways רְשָׁעִיםwicked תַּתְעֵםlead them astray

A righteous man is cautious in friendship, but the ways of the wicked lead them astray.

12:27

לֹאnot יַחֲרֹךְlazy רְמִיָּהroast צֵידוֹhis game וְהוֹןbut a diligent אָדָםman יָקָרprizes חָרוּץpossession

A lazy man does not roast his game, but a diligent man prizes his possession.

12:28

בְּאֹרַחin the path צְדָקָהrighteousness חַיִּיםlife וְדֶרֶךְbut another path נְתִיבָהleads אַלdo not מָוֶתdeath

There is life in the path of righteousness, but another path leads to death.
