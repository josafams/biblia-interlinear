Proverbs 15

The gentle answer and the eyes of the LORD

Open in the interlinear reader

15:1

מַעֲנֶהanswer רַּךְgentle יָשִׁיבturns חֵמָהwrath וּדְבַרword עֶצֶבharsh stirs יַעֲלֶהup אָףanger

A gentle answer turns away wrath, but a harsh word stirs up anger.

15:2

לְשׁוֹןtongue חֲכָמִיםwise תֵּיטִיבcommends דָּעַתknowledge וּפִיbut the mouth כְסִילִיםfool יַבִּיעַspouts אִוֶּלֶתfolly

The tongue of the wise commends knowledge, but the mouth of the fool spouts folly.

15:3

בְּכָלin every מָקוֹםplace עֵינֵיeyes יְהוָהLORD צֹפוֹתobserving רָעִיםevil וטוֹבִיםand the good

The eyes of the LORD are in every place, observing the evil and the good.

15:4

מַרְפֵּאsoothing לָשׁוֹןtongue עֵץtree חַיִּיםlife וְסֶלֶףbut a perverse בָּהּin her שֶׁבֶרcrushes בְּרוּחַthe spirit

A soothing tongue is a tree of life, but a perverse tongue crushes the spirit.

15:5

אֱוִילfool יִנְאַץrejects מוּסַרdiscipline אָבִיוhis father’s וְשֹׁמֵרbut whoever heeds תּוֹכַחַתcorrection יַעְרִםprudent

A fool rejects his father’s discipline, but whoever heeds correction is prudent.

15:6

בֵּיתhouse צַדִּיקrighteous חֹסֶןtreasure רָבgreat וּבִתְבוּאַתbut the income רָשָׁעwicked נֶעְכָּרֶתtrouble

The house of the righteous has great treasure, but the income of the wicked is trouble.

15:7

שִׂפְתֵיlips חֲכָמִיםwise יְזָרוּspread דָעַתknowledge וְלֵבhearts כְּסִילִיםfools לֹאnot כֵןso

The lips of the wise spread knowledge, but not so the hearts of fools.

15:8

זֶבַחsacrifice רְשָׁעִיםwicked תּוֹעֲבַתdetestable יְהוָהLORD וּתְפִלַּתbut the prayer יְשָׁרִיםupright רְצוֹנוֹhis delight

The sacrifice of the wicked is detestable to the LORD, but the prayer of the upright is His delight.

15:9

תּוֹעֲבַתdetests יְהוָהLORD דֶּרֶךְway רָשָׁעwicked וּמְרַדֵּףpursue צְדָקָהrighteousness יֶאֱהָבloves

The LORD detests the way of the wicked, but He loves those who pursue righteousness.

15:10

מוּסָרdiscipline רָעis harsh לְעֹזֵבfor him who leaves אֹרַחpath שׂוֹנֵאhates תוֹכַחַתcorrection יָמוּתdie

Discipline is harsh for him who leaves the path; he who hates correction will die.

15:11

שְׁאוֹלSheol וַאֲבַדּוֹןand Abaddon נֶגֶדbefore יְהוָהLORD אַףhow much כִּיfor לִבּוֹתhearts בְּנֵיsons אָדָםmen

Sheol and Abaddon lie open before the LORD— how much more the hearts of men!

15:12

לֹאnot יֶאֱהַבlove לֵץmocker הוֹכֵחַdoes לוֹto אֶלto חֲכָמִיםreproved לֹאnot יֵלֵךְgo

A mocker does not love to be reproved, nor will he consult the wise.

15:13

לֵבheart שָׂמֵחַjoyful יֵיטִבcheerful פָּנִיםcountenance וּבְעַצְּבַתbut sorrow לֵבheart רוּחַspirit נְכֵאָהcrushes

A joyful heart makes a cheerful countenance, but sorrow of the heart crushes the spirit.

15:14

לֵבheart נָבוֹןdiscerning יְבַקֶּשׁseeks דָּעַתknowledge וּפִיbut the mouth כְסִילִיםfool יִרְעֶהfeeds אִוֶּלֶתfolly

A discerning heart seeks knowledge, but the mouth of a fool feeds on folly.

15:15

כָּלall יְמֵיdays עָנִיoppressed רָעִיםbad וְטוֹבbut a cheerful לֵבheart מִשְׁתֶּהfeast תָמִידcontinual

All the days of the oppressed are bad, but a cheerful heart has a continual feast.

15:16

טוֹבbetter מְעַטlittle בְּיִרְאַתwith the fear יְהוָהLORD מֵאוֹצָרthan treasure רָבgreat וּמְהוּמָהwith turmoil בוֹin him

Better a little with the fear of the LORD than great treasure with turmoil.

15:17

טוֹבbetter אֲרֻחַתdish יָרָקvegetables וְאַהֲבָהlove שָׁםthere מִשּׁוֹרox אָבוּסfattened וְשִׂנְאָהwith hatred בוֹin him

Better a dish of vegetables where there is love than a fattened ox with hatred.

15:18

אִישׁman חֵמָהhot יְגָרֶהtempered stirs מָדוֹןup strife וְאֶרֶךslow אַפַּיִםanger יַשְׁקִיטcalms רִיבdispute

A hot-tempered man stirs up strife, but he who is slow to anger calms dispute.

15:19

דֶּרֶךְway עָצֵלslacker כִּמְשֻׂכַתlike a hedge חָדֶקthorns וְאֹרַחbut the path יְשָׁרִיםupright סְלֻלָהhighway

The way of the slacker is like a hedge of thorns, but the path of the upright is a highway.

15:20

בֵּןson חָכָםwise יְשַׂמַּחjoy אָבfather וּכְסִילbut a foolish אָדָםman בּוֹזֶהdespises אִמּוֹhis mother

A wise son brings joy to his father, but a foolish man despises his mother.

15:21

אִוֶּלֶתfolly שִׂמְחָהjoy לַחֲסַרlacks לֵבjudgment וְאִישׁbut a man תְּבוּנָהunderstanding יְיַשֶׁרwalks a straight לָכֶתpath

Folly is joy to one who lacks judgment, but a man of understanding walks a straight path.

15:22

הָפֵרfail מַחֲשָׁבוֹתplans בְּאֵיןin no סוֹדcounsel וּבְרֹבbut with many יוֹעֲצִיםadvisers תָּקוּםsucceed

Plans fail for lack of counsel, but with many advisers they succeed.

15:23

שִׂמְחָהjoy לָאִישׁa man בְּמַעֲנֵהfitting reply פִיוhis mouth וְדָבָרword בְּעִתּוֹtimely מַהhow טּוֹבgood

A man takes joy in a fitting reply— and how good is a timely word!

15:24

אֹרַחpath חַיִּיםlife לְמַעְלָהleads upward לְמַשְׂכִּילfor the wise לְמַעַןmay סוּרhe avoid מִשְּׁאוֹלto Sheol מָטָּהdown

The path of life leads upward for the wise, that he may avoid going down to Sheol.

15:25

בֵּיתhouse גֵּאִיםproud יִסַּחtears יְהוָהLORD וְיַצֵּבbut he protects גְּבוּלboundaries אַלְמָנָהwidow

The LORD tears down the house of the proud, but He protects the boundaries of the widow.

15:26

תּוֹעֲבַתdetests יְהוָהLORD מַחְשְׁבוֹתthoughts רָעwicked וּטְהֹרִיםpure אִמְרֵיwords נֹעַםpleasant

The LORD detests the thoughts of the wicked, but the words of the pure are pleasant to Him.

15:27

עֹכֵרtrouble בֵּיתוֹhis household בּוֹצֵעַgreedy בָּצַעunjust gain וְשׂוֹנֵאhates מַתָּנֹתbribes יִחְיֶהlive

He who is greedy for unjust gain brings trouble on his household, but he who hates bribes will live.

15:28

לֵבheart צַדִּיקrighteous יֶהְגֶּהponders לַעֲנוֹתto answer וּפִיbut the mouth רְשָׁעִיםwicked יַבִּיעַblurts רָעוֹתevil

The heart of the righteous ponders how to answer, but the mouth of the wicked blurts out evil.

15:29

רָחוֹקfar יְהוָהLORD מֵרְשָׁעִיםfrom the wicked וּתְפִלַּתprayer צַדִּיקִיםrighteous יִשְׁמָעhears

The LORD is far from the wicked, but He hears the prayer of the righteous.

15:30

מְאוֹרlight עֵינַיִםeyes יְשַׂמַּחcheers לֵבheart שְׁמוּעָהnews טוֹבָהgood תְּדַשֶּׁןnourishes עָצֶםbones

The light of the eyes cheers the heart, and good news nourishes the bones.

15:31

אֹזֶןwho to שֹׁמַעַתlistens תּוֹכַחַתreproof חַיִּיםlife בְּקֶרֶבwill among חֲכָמִיםwise תָּלִיןdwell

He who listens to life-giving reproof will dwell among the wise.

15:32

פּוֹרֵעַignores מוּסָרdiscipline מוֹאֵסdespises נַפְשׁוֹhimself וְשׁוֹמֵעַbut whoever heeds תּוֹכַחַתcorrection קוֹנֶהgains לֵּבunderstanding

He who ignores discipline despises himself, but whoever heeds correction gains understanding.

15:33

יִרְאַתfear יְהוָהLORD מוּסַרinstruction חָכְמָהwisdom וְלִפְנֵיbefore כָבוֹדhonor עֲנָוָהhumility

The fear of the LORD is the instruction of wisdom, and humility comes before honor.
