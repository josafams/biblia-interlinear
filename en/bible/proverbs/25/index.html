Proverbs 25

Proverbs copied by the men of Hezekiah

Open in the interlinear reader

25:1

גַּםeven אֵלֶּהthese מִשְׁלֵיproverbs שְׁלֹמֹהSolomon אֲשֶׁרwhich הֶעְתִּיקוּcopied אַנְשֵׁיmen חִזְקִיָּהHezekiah מֶלֶךְking יְהוּדָהJudah

These are additional proverbs of Solomon, which were copied by the men of Hezekiah king of Judah:

25:2

כְּבֹדglory אֱלֹהִיםGod הַסְתֵּרconceal דָּבָרmatter וּכְבֹדand the glory מְלָכִיםkings חֲקֹרsearch דָּבָרword

It is the glory of God to conceal a matter and the glory of kings to search it out.

25:3

שָׁמַיִםheavens לָרוּםare high וָאָרֶץand the earth לָעֹמֶקis deep וְלֵבso the hearts מְלָכִיםkings אֵיןno חֵקֶרsearched

As the heavens are high and the earth is deep, so the hearts of kings cannot be searched.

25:4

הָגוֹremove סִיגִיםdross מִכָּסֶףfrom the silver וַיֵּצֵאforth לַצֹּרֵףfor a silversmith כֶּלִיvessel

Remove the dross from the silver, and a vessel for a silversmith will come forth.

25:5

הָגוֹremove רָשָׁעwicked לִפְנֵיpresence מֶלֶךְking’s וְיִכּוֹןestablished בַּצֶּדֶקin righteousness כִּסְאוֹhis throne

Remove the wicked from the king’s presence, and his throne will be established in righteousness.

25:6

אַלdo not תִּתְהַדַּרexalt לִפְנֵיin the presence מֶלֶךְking וּבִמְקוֹםin the place גְּדֹלִיםgreat אַלdo not תַּעֲמֹדstand

Do not exalt yourself in the presence of the king, and do not stand in the place of great men;

25:7

כִּיfor טוֹבbetter אֲמָרsays לְךָto you עֲלֵהcome up הֵנָּהhere מֵהַשְׁפִּילְךָthan that you should לִפְנֵיin the presence נָדִיבprince אֲשֶׁרthat רָאוּseen עֵינֶיךָyour own eyes

for it is better that he says to you, “Come up here!” than that you should be demoted in the presence of the prince. Even what you have seen with your own eyes,

25:8

אַלdo not תֵּצֵאbring לָרִבhastily to מַהֵרcourt פֶּןotherwise מַהwhat תַּעֲשֶׂהdo בְּאַחֲרִיתָהּin the end בְּהַכְלִיםshame אֹתְךָyou רֵעֶךָneighbor puts you

do not bring hastily to court. Otherwise, what will you do in the end when your neighbor puts you to shame?

25:9

רִיבְךָargue your רִיבcase אֶתwith רֵעֶךָyour neighbor וְסוֹדanother’s אַחֵרconfidence אַלdo not תְּגָלbetraying

Argue your case with your neighbor without betraying another’s confidence,

25:10

פֶּןlest may יְחַסֶּדְךָdisgrace you שֹׁמֵעַhears וְדִבָּתְךָand your infamy לֹאnever תָשׁוּבreturn

lest the one who hears may disgrace you, and your infamy never go away.

25:11

תַּפּוּחֵיapples זָהָבgold בְּמַשְׂכִּיּוֹתin settings כָּסֶףsilver דָּבָרword דָּבֻרspoken עַלon אָפְנָיוfitly

A word fitly spoken is like apples of gold in settings of silver.

25:12

נֶזֶםearring זָהָבgold וַחֲלִיor an ornament כָתֶםfine gold מוֹכִיחַrebuke חָכָםwise עַלto אֹזֶןear שֹׁמָעַתlistening

Like an earring of gold or an ornament of fine gold is a wise man’s rebuke to a listening ear.

25:13

כְּצִנַּתlike the cold שֶׁלֶגsnow בְּיוֹםin the time קָצִירharvest צִירmessenger נֶאֱמָןtrustworthy לְשֹׁלְחָיוsend him וְנֶפֶשׁsoul אֲדֹנָיוhis masters יָשִׁיבrefreshes

Like the cold of snow in the time of harvest is a trustworthy messenger to those who send him; he refreshes the soul of his masters.

25:14

נְשִׂיאִיםclouds וְרוּחַand wind וְגֶשֶׁםrain אָיִןwithout אִישׁman מִתְהַלֵּלboasts בְּמַתַּתgifts never שָׁקֶרnever given

Like clouds and wind without rain is the man who boasts of gifts never given.

25:15

בְּאֹרֶךְthrough patience אַפַּיִםpatience יְפֻתֶּהruler קָצִיןpersuaded וְלָשׁוֹןtongue רַכָּהgentle תִּשְׁבָּרbreak גָּרֶםbone

Through patience a ruler can be persuaded, and a gentle tongue can break a bone.

25:16

דְּבַשׁhoney מָצָאתָfind אֱכֹלeat דַּיֶּךָּjust what you פֶּןlest תִּשְׂבָּעֶנּוּmuch וַהֲקֵאתוֹand vomit it

If you find honey, eat just what you need, lest you have too much and vomit it up.

25:17

הֹקַרseldom רַגְלְךָset foot your מִבֵּיתhouse רֵעֶךָneighbor’s פֶּןlest יִשְׂבָּעֲךָgrow weary וּשְׂנֵאֶךָand hate you

Seldom set foot in your neighbor’s house, lest he grow weary and hate you.

25:18

מֵפִיץclub וְחֶרֶבor sword וְחֵץor arrow שָׁנוּןsharp אִישׁman עֹנֶהis בְרֵעֵהוּagainst his neighbor עֵדbears witness שָׁקֶרfalse

Like a club or sword or sharp arrow is a man who bears false witness against his neighbor.

25:19

שֵׁןtooth רֹעָהbroken וְרֶגֶלor a foot מוּעָדֶתjoint מִבְטָחconfidence בּוֹגֵדfaithless בְּיוֹםin time צָרָהtrouble

Like a broken tooth or a foot out of joint is confidence in a faithless man in time of trouble.

25:20

מַעֲדֶהremoves בֶּגֶדgarment בְּיוֹםday קָרָהcold or poured חֹמֶץvinegar עַלon נָתֶרwound וְשָׁרone who בַּשִּׁרִיםsongs עַלto לֶבheart רָעheavy

Like one who removes a garment on a cold day or vinegar poured on a wound is one who sings songs to a heavy heart.

25:21

אִםif רָעֵבhungry שֹׂנַאֲךָyour enemy הַאֲכִלֵהוּeat לָחֶםfood וְאִםand if צָמֵאthirsty הַשְׁקֵהוּgive him מָיִםwater

If your enemy is hungry, give him food to eat, and if he is thirsty, give him water to drink.

25:22

כִּיfor גֶחָלִיםburning coals אַתָּהyou חֹתֶהheap עַלon רֹאשׁוֹhis head וַיהוָהand the LORD יְשַׁלֶּםreward לָךְto you

For in so doing, you will heap burning coals on his head, and the LORD will reward you.

25:23

רוּחַwind צָפוֹןnorth תְּחוֹלֵלforth גָּשֶׁםrain וּפָנִיםso a backbiting נִזְעָמִיםangry לְשׁוֹןtongue סָתֶרlooks

As the north wind brings forth rain, so a backbiting tongue brings angry looks.

25:24

טוֹבbetter שֶׁבֶתlive עַלon פִּנַּתcorner גָּגroof מֵאֵשֶׁתwife מִדְיָנִיםquarrelsome וּבֵיתhouse with חָבֶרspells

Better to live on a corner of the roof than to share a house with a quarrelsome wife.

25:25

מַיִםwater קָרִיםcold עַלto נֶפֶשׁsoul עֲיֵפָהweary וּשְׁמוּעָהnews טוֹבָהgood מֵאֶרֶץland מֶרְחָקdistant

Like cold water to a weary soul is good news from a distant land.

25:26

מַעְיָןspring נִרְפָּשׂmuddied וּמָקוֹרor a polluted מָשְׁחָתwell צַדִּיקrighteous מָטgives לִפְנֵיto before רָשָׁעwicked

Like a muddied spring or a polluted well is a righteous man who gives way to the wicked.

25:27

אָכֹלeat דְּבַשׁhoney הַרְבּוֹתmuch לֹאnot טוֹבgood וְחֵקֶרor to search כְּבֹדָםone’s own כָּבוֹדglory

It is not good to eat too much honey or to search out one’s own glory.

25:28

עִירcity פְּרוּצָהbroken אֵיןno חוֹמָהwalls אִישׁman אֲשֶׁרwho אֵיןnot מַעְצָרcontrol לְרוּחוֹcontrol his temper

Like a city whose walls are broken down is a man who does not control his temper.
