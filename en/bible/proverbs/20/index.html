Proverbs 20

Wine, honest weights and a man's steps

Open in the interlinear reader

20:1

לֵץmocker הַיַּיןwine is a הֹמֶהloud שֵׁכָרstrong drink וְכָלand whoever שֹׁגֶהastray בּוֹby them לֹאnot יֶחְכָּםwise

Wine is a mocker, strong drink is a brawler, and whoever is led astray by them is not wise.

20:2

נַהַםroar כַּכְּפִירlike the roar אֵימַתterror מֶלֶךְking מִתְעַבְּרוֹprovokes him forfeits his חוֹטֵאown נַפְשׁוֹlife

The terror of a king is like the roar of a lion; whoever provokes him forfeits his own life.

20:3

כָּבוֹדhonorable לָאִישׁfor a man שֶׁבֶתresolve מֵרִיבa dispute וְכָלbut any אֱוִילfool יִתְגַּלָּעquarrel

It is honorable for a man to resolve a dispute, but any fool will quarrel.

20:4

מֵחֹרֶףthe does עָצֵלslacker לֹאnot יַחֲרֹשׁplow וְשָׁאַלseason בַּקָּצִירharvest וָאָיִןbut nothing

The slacker does not plow in season; at harvest time he looks, but nothing is there.

20:5

מַיִםwaters עֲמֻקִּיםdeep עֵצָהintentions בְלֶבa heart אִישׁman’s וְאִישׁbut a man תְּבוּנָהunderstanding יִדְלֶנָּהdraws them

The intentions of a man’s heart are deep waters, but a man of understanding draws them out.

20:6

רָבmany אָדָםman יִקְרָאproclaims אִישׁhis חַסְדּוֹhis loving devotion וְאִישׁman אֱמוּנִיםtrustworthy מִיwho can יִמְצָאfind

Many a man proclaims his loving devotion, but who can find a trustworthy man?

20:7

מִתְהַלֵּךְwalks בְּתֻמּוֹwith integrity צַדִּיקrighteous אַשְׁרֵיblessed בָנָיוhis children אַחֲרָיוafter him

The righteous man walks with integrity; blessed are his children after him.

20:8

מֶלֶךְking יוֹשֵׁבsits עַלon כִּסֵּאthrone דִיןjudge מְזָרֶהsifts בְעֵינָיוwith his eyes כָּלall רָעevil

A king who sits on a throne to judge sifts out all evil with his eyes.

20:9

מִיwho can יֹאמַרsay זִכִּיתִיpure לִבִּיheart טָהַרְתִּיcleansed מֵחַטָּאתִיfrom my sin

Who can say, “I have kept my heart pure; I am cleansed from my sin”?

20:10

אֶבֶןstone וָאֶבֶןweights and unequal אֵיפָהdiffering וְאֵיפָהmeasures תּוֹעֲבַתdetestable יְהוָהLORD גַּםeven שְׁנֵיהֶםboth are

Differing weights and unequal measures— both are detestable to the LORD.

20:11

גַּםeven בְּמַעֲלָלָיוby his actions יִתְנַכֶּרman נָעַרyoung אִםif זַךְpure וְאִםand if יָשָׁרupright פָּעֳלוֹhis conduct

Even a young man is known by his actions— whether his conduct is pure and upright.

20:12

אֹזֶןears שֹׁמַעַתhear וְעַיִןand eyes רֹאָהsee יְהוָהLORD עָשָׂהmade גַםalso שְׁנֵיהֶםthem both

Ears that hear and eyes that see— the LORD has made them both.

20:13

אַלdo not תֶּאֱהַבlove שֵׁנָהsleep פֶּןor תִּוָּרֵשׁpoor פְּקַחopen עֵינֶיךָeyes and you שְׂבַעplenty לָחֶםfood

Do not love sleep, or you will grow poor; open your eyes, and you will have plenty of food.

20:14

רַעworthless רַעworthless יֹאמַרsays הַקּוֹנֶהthe buyer וְאֹזֵלway לוֹto him אָזhe יִתְהַלָּלgloats

“Worthless, worthless!” says the buyer, but on the way out, he gloats.

20:15

יֵשׁthere זָהָבgold וְרָבabundance פְּנִינִיםrubies וּכְלִיrare יְקָרtreasure שִׂפְתֵיlips דָעַתknowledge

There is an abundance of gold and rubies, but lips of knowledge are a rare treasure.

20:16

לְקַחtake בִּגְדוֹgarment כִּיfor עָרַבsecurity זָרposts וּבְעַדfor נָכְרִיָּהforeigner חַבְלֵהוּcollateral if it

Take the garment of the one who posts security for a stranger; get collateral if it is for a foreigner.

20:17

עָרֵבsweet לָאִישׁto a man לֶחֶםfood שָׁקֶרfraud וְאַחַרbut later יִמָּלֵאfull פִיהוּhis mouth חָצָץgravel

Food gained by fraud is sweet to a man, but later his mouth is full of gravel.

20:18

מַחֲשָׁבוֹתplans בְּעֵצָהplans by consultation תִכּוֹןset וּבְתַחְבֻּלוֹתunder sound guidance עֲשֵׂהwage מִלְחָמָהwar

Set plans by consultation, and wage war under sound guidance.

20:19

גּוֹלֶהreveals סּוֹדsecrets הוֹלֵךְwalks רָכִילconstant gossip avoid וּלְפֹתֶהwho babbles שְׂפָתָיוhis lips לֹאnot תִתְעָרָבsecurity

He who reveals secrets is a constant gossip; avoid the one who babbles with his lips.

20:20

מְקַלֵּלcurses אָבִיוhis father וְאִמּוֹor mother his יִדְעַךְextinguished נֵרוֹlamp will be בֶּאֱשׁוּןin deepest חֹשֶׁךְdarkness

Whoever curses his father or mother, his lamp will be extinguished in deepest darkness.

20:21

נַחֲלָהinheritance מְבֹהֶלֶתquickly בָּרִאשֹׁנָהin the first וְאַחֲרִיתָהּend לֹאnot תְבֹרָךְblessed

An inheritance gained quickly will not be blessed in the end.

20:22

אַלdo not תֹּאמַרsay אֲשַׁלְּמָהavenge רָעevil קַוֵּהwait לַיהוָהon the LORD וְיֹשַׁעsave לָךְto you

Do not say, “I will avenge this evil!” Wait on the LORD, and He will save you.

20:23

תּוֹעֲבַתdetestable יְהוָהLORD אֶבֶןunequal וָאָבֶןweights וּמֹאזְנֵיdishonest מִרְמָהscales לֹאno טוֹבgood

Unequal weights are detestable to the LORD, and dishonest scales are no good.

20:24

מֵיהוָהfrom the LORD מִצְעֲדֵיsteps גָבֶרa man’s וְאָדָםanyone מַהhow יָּבִיןunderstand דַּרְכּוֹhis own way

A man’s steps are from the LORD, so how can anyone understand his own way?

20:25

מוֹקֵשׁtrap אָדָםman יָלַעdedicate קֹדֶשׁsomething rashly וְאַחַרonly later נְדָרִיםvows לְבַקֵּרto reconsider

It is a trap for a man to dedicate something rashly, only later to reconsider his vows.

20:26

מְזָרֶהseparates רְשָׁעִיםwicked מֶלֶךְking חָכָםwise וַיָּשֶׁבand drives עֲלֵיהֶםover them אוֹפָןwheel

A wise king separates out the wicked and drives the threshing wheel over them.

20:27

נֵרlamp יְהוָהLORD נִשְׁמַתspirit אָדָםman חֹפֵשׂsearching כָּלall חַדְרֵיinmost בָטֶןbeing

The spirit of a man is the lamp of the LORD, searching out his inmost being.

20:28

חֶסֶדloving devotion וֶאֱמֶתand faithfulness יִצְּרוּpreserve מֶלֶךְking וְסָעַדand refresh בַּחֶסֶדby these he maintains כִּסְאוֹhis throne

Loving devotion and faithfulness preserve a king; by these he maintains his throne.

20:29

תִּפְאֶרֶתglory בַּחוּרִיםyoung men כֹּחָםtheir strength וַהֲדַרsplendor זְקֵנִיםold שֵׂיבָהgray hair

The glory of young men is their strength, and gray hair is the splendor of the old.

20:30

חַבֻּרוֹתlashes פֶּצַעwounds תַּמְרוּקscour בְּרָעevil וּמַכּוֹתand beatings חַדְרֵיcleanse the inmost בָטֶןinmost parts

Lashes and wounds scour evil, and beatings cleanse the inmost parts.
