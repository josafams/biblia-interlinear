Proverbs 5

Faithfulness to the wife of your youth

Open in the interlinear reader

5:1

בְּנִיmy son לְחָכְמָתִיmy wisdom הַקְשִׁיבָהpay attention לִתְבוּנָתִיmy insight הַטincline אָזְנֶךָyour ear

My son, pay attention to my wisdom; incline your ear to my insight,

5:2

לִשְׁמֹרmay maintain מְזִמּוֹתdiscretion וְדַעַתknowledge שְׂפָתֶיךָyour lips יִנְצֹרוּpreserve

that you may maintain discretion and your lips may preserve knowledge.

5:3

כִּיfor נֹפֶתhoney תִּטֹּפְנָהdrip שִׂפְתֵיlips זָרָהforbidden וְחָלָקsmoother מִשֶּׁמֶןthan oil חִכָּהּher speech

Though the lips of the forbidden woman drip honey and her speech is smoother than oil,

5:4

וְאַחֲרִיתָהּin the end she מָרָהbitter כַלַּעֲנָהas wormwood חַדָּהsharp כְּחֶרֶבsword פִּיּוֹתedged

in the end she is bitter as wormwood, sharp as a double-edged sword.

5:5

רַגְלֶיהָher feet יֹרְדוֹתgo down מָוֶתdeath שְׁאוֹלSheol צְעָדֶיהָher steps יִתְמֹכוּstraight

Her feet go down to death; her steps lead straight to Sheol.

5:6

אֹרַחpath חַיִּיםlife פֶּןnot תְּפַלֵּסconsider נָעוּwandered מַעְגְּלֹתֶיהָher ways לֹאnot תֵדָעknow

She does not consider the path of life; she does not know that her ways are unstable.

5:7

וְעַתָּהso now בָנִיםsons שִׁמְעוּlisten לִיto me וְאַלand do not תָּסוּרוּturn aside מֵאִמְרֵיfrom the words פִיmy mouth

So now, my sons, listen to me, and do not turn aside from the words of my mouth.

5:8

הַרְחֵקfar מֵעָלֶיהָfrom the her דַרְכֶּךָyour path וְאַלdo not תִּקְרַבnear אֶלto פֶּתַחdoor בֵּיתָהּher house

Keep your path far from her; do not go near the door of her house,

5:9

פֶּןlest תִּתֵּןvigor לַאֲחֵרִיםto others הוֹדֶךָconcede your וּשְׁנֹתֶיךָand your years לְאַכְזָרִיcruel

lest you concede your vigor to others, and your years to one who is cruel;

5:10

פֶּןlest יִשְׂבְּעוּfeast זָרִיםstrangers כֹּחֶךָyour wealth וַעֲצָבֶיךָand your labors enrich בְּבֵיתthe house נָכְרִיforeigner

lest strangers feast on your wealth, and your labors enrich the house of a foreigner.

5:11

וְנָהַמְתָּwill groan בְאַחֲרִיתֶךָend of your בִּכְלוֹתin finished בְּשָׂרְךָyour body וּשְׁאֵרֶךָyour flesh and

At the end of your life you will groan when your flesh and your body are spent,

5:12

וְאָמַרְתָּsay אֵיךְhow שָׂנֵאתִיhated מוּסָרdiscipline וְתוֹכַחַתreproof נָאַץdespised לִבִּיmy heart

and you will say, “How I hated discipline, and my heart despised reproof!

5:13

וְלֹאand not שָׁמַעְתִּיlisten בְּקוֹלto the voice מוֹרָיmentors וְלִמְלַמְּדַיteachers לֹאor הִטִּיתִיincline אָזְנִיmy ear

I did not listen to the voice of my teachers or incline my ear to my mentors.

5:14

כִּמְעַטI הָיִיתִיam בְכָלutter רָעruin בְּתוֹךְin the midst קָהָלwhole וְעֵדָהassembly

I am on the brink of utter ruin in the midst of the whole assembly.”

5:15

שְׁתֵהdrink מַיִםwater מִבּוֹרֶךָfrom your own cistern וְנֹזְלִיםand running water מִתּוֹךְfrom among בְּאֵרֶךָyour own well

Drink water from your own cistern, and running water from your own well.

5:16

יָפוּצוּwhy מַעְיְנֹתֶיךָyour springs חוּצָהflow בָּרְחֹבוֹתin the public squares פַּלְגֵיstreams מָיִםwater

Why should your springs flow in the streets, your streams of water in the public squares?

5:17

יִהְיוּbe לְךָto you לְבַדֶּךָyours alone וְאֵיןand no לְזָרִיםto be shared strangers אִתָּךְwith

Let them be yours alone, never to be shared with strangers.

5:18

יְהִיmay מְקוֹרְךָyour fountain בָרוּךְblessed וּשְׂמַחrejoice מֵאֵשֶׁתin the wife נְעוּרֶךָyour youth

May your fountain be blessed, and may you rejoice in the wife of your youth:

5:19

אַיֶּלֶתloving doe אֲהָבִיםgraceful וְיַעֲלַתand roe חֵןfawn דַּדֶּיהָher breasts יְרַוֻּךָbreasts satisfy you בְכָלalways עֵתtime בְּאַהֲבָתָהּby her love תִּשְׁגֶּהcaptivated תָמִידforever

A loving doe, a graceful fawn— may her breasts satisfy you always; may you be captivated by her love forever.

5:20

וְלָמָּהwhy תִשְׁגֶּהcaptivated בְנִיmy son בְזָרָהby an adulteress וּתְחַבֵּקor embrace חֵקbosom נָכְרִיָּהstranger

Why be captivated, my son, by an adulteress, or embrace the bosom of a stranger?

5:21

כִּיfor נֹכַחopposite עֵינֵיeyes יְהוָהLORD דַּרְכֵיways אִישׁman’s וְכָלall מַעְגְּלֹתָיוhis paths מְפַלֵּסexamines

For a man’s ways are before the eyes of the LORD, and the LORD examines all his paths.

5:22

עַווֹנוֹתָיוiniquities יִלְכְּדֻנוֹentrap him אֶת[obj] הָרָשָׁעa wicked וּבְחַבְלֵיthe cords חַטָּאתוֹsin him יִתָּמֵךְentangle

The iniquities of a wicked man entrap him; the cords of his sin entangle him.

5:23

הוּאhe יָמוּתdies בְּאֵיןin no מוּסָרdiscipline וּבְרֹבown great אִוַּלְתּוֹfolly יִשְׁגֶּהastray

He dies for lack of discipline, led astray by his own great folly.
