Proverbs 19

The honest poor and the counsel of the LORD

Open in the interlinear reader

19:1

טוֹבbetter רָשׁpoor הוֹלֵךְwalks בְּתֻמּוֹwith integrity מֵעִקֵּשׁperverse שְׂפָתָיוwhose lips וְהוּאa כְסִילfool

Better a poor man who walks with integrity than a fool whose lips are perverse.

19:2

גַּםeven בְּלֹאin without דַעַתknowledge נֶפֶשׁzeal לֹאno טוֹבgood וְאָץhurries בְּרַגְלַיִםfootsteps חוֹטֵאmisses

Even zeal is no good without knowledge, and he who hurries his footsteps misses the mark.

19:3

אִוֶּלֶתfolly אָדָםman’s תְּסַלֵּףsubverts דַּרְכּוֹhis way וְעַלagainst יְהוָהLORD יִזְעַףrages לִבּוֹhis heart

A man’s own folly subverts his way, yet his heart rages against the LORD.

19:4

הוֹןwealth יֹסִיףattracts רֵעִיםfriends רַבִּיםmany וְדָלbut a poor מֵרֵעהוּhis friend יִפָּרֵדdeserted

Wealth attracts many friends, but a poor man is deserted by his friend.

19:5

עֵדa witness שְׁקָרִיםfalse לֹאnot יִנָּקֶהgo unpunished וְיָפִיחַand one who utters כְּזָבִיםutters lies לֹאnot יִמָּלֵטescape

A false witness will not go unpunished, and one who utters lies will not escape.

19:6

רַבִּיםmany יְחַלּוּseek פְנֵיfavor נָדִיבprince וְכָלand everyone הָרֵעַa friend לְאִישׁto man מַתָּןgift giver

Many seek the favor of the prince, and everyone is a friend of the gift giver.

19:7

כָּלall אֲחֵיbrothers רָשׁpoor שְׂנֵאֻהוּhate him אַףhow much כִּיfor מְרֵעֵהוּhis friends רָחֲקוּavoid מִמֶּנּוּfrom us מְרַדֵּףpursue אֲמָרִיםpleading לוֹto הֵמָּהthey

All the brothers of a poor man hate him— how much more do his friends avoid him! He may pursue them with pleading, but they are nowhere to be found.

19:8

קֹנֶהacquires לֵּבwisdom himself אֹהֵבloves נַפְשׁוֹhimself one who שֹׁמֵרwho safeguards תְּבוּנָהunderstanding לִמְצֹאwill find טוֹבsuccess

He who acquires wisdom loves himself; one who safeguards understanding will find success.

19:9

עֵדa witness שְׁקָרִיםfalse לֹאnot יִנָּקֶהgo unpunished וְיָפִיחַand one who כְּזָבִיםlies יֹאבֵדperish

A false witness will not go unpunished, and one who pours out lies will perish.

19:10

לֹאnot נָאוֶהunseemly לִכְסִילfor a fool תַּעֲנוּגdelights אַףhow כִּיfor לְעֶבֶדa slave מְשֹׁלrule בְּשָׂרִיםover princes

Luxury is unseemly for a fool— how much worse for a slave to rule over princes!

19:11

שֵׂכֶלinsight אָדָםman’s הֶאֱרִיךְgives אַפּוֹhim patience וְתִפאַרְתּוֹand his virtue עֲבֹרvirtue עַלto פָּשַׁעoffense

A man’s insight gives him patience, and his virtue is to overlook an offense.

19:12

נַהַםroar כַּכְּפִירlion זַעַףrage מֶלֶךְking’s וּכְטַלlike dew עַלon עֵשֶׂבgrass רְצוֹנוֹhis favor

A king’s rage is like the roar of a lion, but his favor is like dew on the grass.

19:13

הַוֺּתruin לְאָבִיוhis father’s בֵּןson כְּסִילfoolish וְדֶלֶףconstant טֹרֵדdripping מִדְיְנֵיquarrelsome אִשָּׁהwife

A foolish son is his father’s ruin, and a quarrelsome wife is like a constant dripping.

19:14

בַּיִתhouses וָהוֹןand wealth נַחֲלַתinherited אָבוֹתfathers וּמֵיְהוָהfrom the LORD אִשָּׁהwife מַשְׂכָּלֶתprudent

Houses and wealth are inherited from fathers, but a prudent wife is from the LORD.

19:15

עַצְלָהlaziness תַּפִּילcast תַּרְדֵּמָהdeep sleep וְנֶפֶשׁsoul רְמִיָּהidle תִרְעָבhunger

Laziness brings on deep sleep, and an idle soul will suffer hunger.

19:16

שֹׁמֵרkeeps מִצְוָהcommandment שֹׁמֵרpreserves נַפְשׁוֹhis soul בּוֹזֵהcareless דְרָכָיוhis ways will יָמוּתdie

He who keeps a commandment preserves his soul, but he who is careless in his ways will die.

19:17

מַלְוֵהloan יְהוָהLORD חוֹנֵןkindness דָּלpoor וּגְמֻלוֹand his recompense יְשַׁלֶּםrepay לוֹto him

Kindness to the poor is a loan to the LORD, and He will repay the lender.

19:18

יַסֵּרdiscipline בִּנְךָyour son כִּיfor that יֵשׁthere תִּקְוָהhope וְאֶלto הֲמִיתוֹhis death אַלdo not תִּשָּׂאparty נַפְשֶׁךָbe

Discipline your son, for in that there is hope; do not be party to his death.

19:19

גְּדָלgreat חֵמָהanger נֹשֵׂאpay עֹנֶשׁpenalty כִּיif אִםif תַּצִּילrescue וְעוֹדso again תּוֹסִףadd

A man of great anger must pay the penalty; if you rescue him, you will have to do so again.

19:20

שְׁמַעlisten עֵצָהcounsel וְקַבֵּלand accept מוּסָרdiscipline לְמַעַןmay תֶּחְכַּםwise בְּאַחֲרִיתֶךָrest of your days

Listen to counsel and accept discipline, that you may be wise the rest of your days.

19:21

רַבּוֹתmany מַחֲשָׁבוֹתplans בְּלֶבheart אִישׁman’s וַעֲצַתbut the purpose יְהוָהLORD הִיאwill תָקוּםprevail

Many plans are in a man’s heart, but the purpose of the LORD will prevail.

19:22

תַּאֲוַתdesire אָדָםman חַסְדּוֹloving devotion וְטוֹבbetter רָשׁpoor מֵאִישׁfrom men כָּזָבliar

The desire of a man is loving devotion; better to be poor than a liar.

19:23

יִרְאַתfear יְהוָהLORD לְחַיִּיםto life וְשָׂבֵעַone יָלִיןrest בַּלnot יִפָּקֶדvisitation רָעharm

The fear of the LORD leads to life, that one may rest content, without visitation from harm.

19:24

טָמַןburies עָצֵלslacker יָדוֹhis hand בַּצַּלָּחַתin the dish גַּםwill אֶלto פִּיהוּhis mouth לֹאnot יְשִׁיבֶנָּהbring it

The slacker buries his hand in the dish; he will not even bring it back to his mouth.

19:25

לֵץmocker תַּכֶּהstrike וּפֶתִיand the simple יַעְרִםbeware וְהוֹכִיחַrebuke לְנָבוֹןdiscerning יָבִיןgain דָּעַתknowledge

Strike a mocker, and the simple will beware; rebuke the discerning man, and he will gain knowledge.

19:26

מְשַׁדֶּדassaults אָבfather יַבְרִיחַevicts אֵםmother בֵּןson מֵבִישׁshame וּמַחְפִּירand disgrace

He who assaults his father or evicts his mother is a son who brings shame and disgrace.

19:27

חַדַלcease בְּנִיmy son לִשְׁמֹעַto hear מוּסָרinstruction לִשְׁגוֹתwill stray מֵאִמְרֵיfrom the words דָעַתknowledge

If you cease to hear instruction, my son, you will stray from the words of knowledge.

19:28

עֵדa witness בְּלִיַּעַלcorrupt יָלִיץmocks מִשְׁפָּטjustice וּפִיmouth רְשָׁעִיםwicked יְבַלַּעswallows אָוֶןiniquity

A corrupt witness mocks justice, and a wicked mouth swallows iniquity.

19:29

נָכוֹנוּprepared לַלֵּצִיםfor mockers שְׁפָטִיםjudgments וּמַהֲלֻמוֹתand beatings לְגֵוfor the backs כְּסִילִיםfools

Judgments are prepared for mockers, and beatings for the backs of fools.
