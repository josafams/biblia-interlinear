Proverbs 8

Wisdom speaks and recalls the creation

Open in the interlinear reader

8:1

הֲלֹאdoes not חָכְמָהwisdom תִקְרָאcall וּתְבוּנָהand understanding תִּתֵּןraise קוֹלָהּher voice

Does not wisdom call out, and understanding raise her voice?

8:2

בְּרֹאשׁoverlooking מְרוֹמִיםheights עֲלֵיon דָרֶךְroad בֵּיתhouse נְתִיבוֹתcrossroads נִצָּבָהstand

On the heights overlooking the road, at the crossroads she takes her stand.

8:3

לְיַדbeside שְׁעָרִיםgates לְפִיto mouth קָרֶתcity מְבוֹאentrance פְתָחִיםat entrances תָּרֹנָּהshe cries

Beside the gates to the city, at the entrances she cries out:

8:4

אֲלֵיכֶםto you אִישִׁיםmen אֶקְרָאcall וְקוֹלִיand my cry אֶלto בְּנֵיsons אָדָםmen

“To you, O men, I call out, and my cry is to the sons of men.

8:5

הָבִינוּlearn פְתָאיִםsimple עָרְמָהshrewd וּכְסִילִיםfools הָבִינוּgain לֵבunderstanding

O simple ones, learn to be shrewd; O fools, gain understanding.

8:6

שִׁמְעוּlisten כִּיfor נְגִידִיםof noble אֲדַבֵּרspeak וּמִפְתַּחand the opening שְׂפָתַיmy lips מֵישָׁרִיםright

Listen, for I speak of noble things, and the opening of my lips will reveal right.

8:7

כִּיfor אֱמֶתtruth יֶהְגֶּהspeak חִכִּיmy mouth וְתוֹעֲבַתdetestable שְׂפָתַיmy lips רֶשַׁעwickedness

For my mouth will speak the truth, and wickedness is detestable to my lips.

8:8

בְּצֶדֶקrighteous כָּלall אִמְרֵיwords פִיmy mouth אֵיןnone בָּהֶםin them נִפְתָּלcrooked וְעִקֵּשׁor perverse

All the words of my mouth are righteous; none are crooked or perverse.

8:9

כֻּלָּםthey are all נְכֹחִיםplain לַמֵּבִיןto the discerning וִישָׁרִיםand upright לְמֹצְאֵיfind דָעַתknowledge

They are all plain to the discerning, and upright to those who find knowledge.

8:10

קְחוּreceive מוּסָרִיmy instruction וְאַלand do not כָּסֶףsilver וְדַעַתand knowledge מֵחָרוּץthan pure gold נִבְחָרrather

Receive my instruction instead of silver, and knowledge rather than pure gold.

8:11

כִּיfor טוֹבָהprecious חָכְמָהwisdom מִפְּנִינִיםthan rubies וְכָלand nothing חֲפָצִיםdesire לֹאnot יִשְׁווּcompares בָהּwith her

For wisdom is more precious than rubies, and nothing you desire compares with her.

8:12

אֲנִיI חָכְמָהwisdom שָׁכַנְתִּיdwell עָרְמָהprudence וְדַעַתknowledge מְזִמּוֹתdiscretion אֶמְצָאfind

I, wisdom, dwell together with prudence, and I find knowledge and discretion.

8:13

יִרְאַתfear יְהוָהLORD שְׂנֹאתhate רָעevil גֵּאָהarrogant וְגָאוֹןarrogant pride וְדֶרֶךְconduct רָעevil וּפִיand speech תַהְפֻּכוֹתperverse שָׂנֵאתִיhate

To fear the LORD is to hate evil; I hate arrogant pride, evil conduct, and perverse speech.

8:14

לִיto me עֵצָהcounsel וְתוּשִׁיָּהand sound judgment אֲנִיI בִינָהinsight לִיto me גְבוּרָהstrength

Counsel and sound judgment are mine; I have insight and strength.

8:15

בִּיby me מְלָכִיםkings יִמְלֹכוּreign וְרוֹזְנִיםand rulers יְחֹקְקוּenact צֶדֶקjust

By me kings reign, and rulers enact just laws;

8:16

בִּיby me שָׂרִיםprinces יָשֹׂרוּrule וּנְדִיבִיםand nobles כָּלall שֹׁפְטֵיgovern צֶדֶקjustly

By me princes rule, and all nobles who govern justly.

8:17

אֲנִיI אֹהֲבַיlove me אֵהָבlove וּמְשַׁחֲרַיseek me early יִמְצָאֻנְנִיfind me

I love those who love me, and those who seek me early shall find me.

8:18

עֹשֶׁרriches וְכָבוֹדand honor אִתִּיwith me הוֹןwealth עָתֵקenduring וּצְדָקָהand righteousness

With me are riches and honor, enduring wealth and righteousness.

8:19

טוֹבbetter פִּרְיִיmy fruit מֵחָרוּץthan gold וּמִפָּזpure gold וּתְבוּאָתִיand my harvest מִכֶּסֶףsurpasses silver נִבְחָרchoice

My fruit is better than gold, pure gold, and my harvest surpasses choice silver.

8:20

בְּאֹרַחin the way צְדָקָהrighteousness אֲהַלֵּךwalk בְּתוֹךְin among נְתִיבוֹתpaths מִשְׁפָּטjustice

I walk in the way of righteousness, along the paths of justice,

8:21

לְהַנְחִילbestowing wealth אֹהֲבַיlove me יֵשׁthere וְאֹצְרֹתֵיהֶםand making their treasuries אֲמַלֵּאfull

bestowing wealth on those who love me and making their treasuries full.

8:22

יְהוָהLORD קָנָנִיcreated me רֵאשִׁיתfirst דַּרְכּוֹhis course קֶדֶםbefore מִפְעָלָיוhis works מֵאָזfrom time

The LORD created me as His first course, before His works of old.

8:23

מֵעוֹלָםfrom everlasting נִסַּכְתִּיestablished מֵרֹאשׁfrom the beginning מִקַּדְמֵיbefore אָרֶץearth

From everlasting I was established, from the beginning, before the earth began.

8:24

בְּאֵיןno תְּהֹמוֹתwatery depths חוֹלָלְתִּיforth בְּאֵיןwhen no מַעְיָנוֹתsprings נִכְבַּדֵּיoverflowing מָיִםwater

When there were no watery depths, I was brought forth, when no springs were overflowing with water.

8:25

בְּטֶרֶםbefore הָרִיםmountains הָטְבָּעוּsettled לִפְנֵיbefore גְבָעוֹתhills חוֹלָלְתִּיforth

Before the mountains were settled, before the hills, I was brought forth,

8:26

עַדuntil לֹאnot עָשָׂהmade אֶרֶץland וְחוּצוֹתor fields וְרֹאשׁand head עָפְרוֹתdust תֵּבֵלearth

before He made the land or fields, or any of the dust of the earth.

8:27

בַּהֲכִינוֹwhen he established שָׁמַיִםheavens שָׁםthere אָנִיI בְּחוּקוֹwhen he inscribed חוּגcircle עַלon פְּנֵיface תְהוֹםdeep

I was there when He established the heavens, when He inscribed a circle on the face of the deep,

8:28

בְּאַמְּצוֹwhen he established שְׁחָקִיםclouds מִמָּעַלabove בַּעֲזוֹזwhen the fountains עִינוֹתsprings תְּהוֹםdeep

when He established the clouds above, when the fountains of the deep gushed forth,

8:29

בְּשׂוּמוֹwhen he set לַיָּםfor the sea חֻקּוֹboundary וּמַיִםwaters לֹאnot יַעַבְרוּsurpass פִיוhis command בְּחוּקוֹwhen he marked out מוֹסְדֵיfoundations אָרֶץearth

when He set a boundary for the sea, so that the waters would not surpass His command, when He marked out the foundations of the earth.

8:30

וָאֶהְיֶהthen I was אֶצְלוֹhis side אָמוֹןside וָאֶהְיֶהand been שַׁעֲשֻׁעִיםdelight יוֹםday יוֹםday מְשַׂחֶקֶתrejoicing לְפָנָיוhis presence בְּכָלalways in עֵתalways

Then I was a skilled craftsman at His side, and His delight day by day, rejoicing always in His presence.

8:31

מְשַׂחֶקֶתrejoicing בְּתֵבֵלin whole אַרְצוֹhis world וְשַׁעֲשֻׁעַיdelighting אֶתthe בְּנֵיsons אָדָםmen

I was rejoicing in His whole world, delighting together in the sons of men.

8:32

וְעַתָּהnow therefore בָנִיםsons שִׁמְעוּlisten לִיto me וְאַשְׁרֵיfor blessed דְּרָכַיmy ways יִשְׁמֹרוּkeep

Now therefore, my sons, listen to me, for blessed are those who keep my ways.

8:33

שִׁמְעוּlisten מוּסָרinstruction וַחֲכָמוּand be wise וְאַלdo not תִּפְרָעוּignore

Listen to instruction and be wise; do not ignore it.

8:34

אַשְׁרֵיblessed אָדָםman שֹׁמֵעַlistens לִיto me לִשְׁקֹדwatching עַלon דַּלְתֹתַיmy doors יוֹםdaily יוֹםday לִשְׁמֹרwaiting מְזוּזֹתposts פְּתָחָיmy doorway

Blessed is the man who listens to me, watching daily at my doors, waiting at the posts of my doorway.

8:35

כִּיfor מֹצְאִיfinds me מָצָאfinds חַיִּיםlife וַיָּפֶקand obtains רָצוֹןfavor מֵיְהוָהof the LORD

For whoever finds me finds life and obtains the favor of the LORD.

8:36

וְחֹטְאִיfails חֹמֵסharms נַפְשׁוֹhimself כָּלall מְשַׂנְאַיhate me אָהֲבוּlove מָוֶתdeath

But he who fails to find me harms himself; all who hate me love death.”
