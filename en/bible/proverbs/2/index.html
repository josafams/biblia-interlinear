Proverbs 2

Seeking wisdom like hidden treasure

Open in the interlinear reader

2:1

בְּנִיmy son אִםif תִּקַּחaccept אֲמָרָיmy words וּמִצְוֺתַיand my commandments תִּצְפֹּןhide אִתָּךְwith you

My son, if you accept my words and hide my commandments within you,

2:2

לְהַקְשִׁיבto attentiveness לַחָכְמָהto wisdom אָזְנֶךָyou your ear תַּטֶּהincline לִבְּךָyour heart לַתְּבוּנָהto understanding

if you incline your ear to wisdom and direct your heart to understanding,

2:3

כִּיif אִםif לַבִּינָהtruly תִקְרָאcall לַתְּבוּנָהto understanding תִּתֵּןlift קוֹלֶךָyour voice

if you truly call out to insight and lift your voice to understanding,

2:4

אִםif תְּבַקְשֶׁנָּהseek it כַכָּסֶףlike silver וְכַמַּטְמוֹנִיםlike hidden treasure תַּחְפְּשֶׂנָּהsearch it

if you seek it like silver and search it out like hidden treasure,

2:5

אָזthen תָּבִיןdiscern יִרְאַתfear יְהוָהLORD וְדַעַתknowledge אֱלֹהִיםGod תִּמְצָאdiscover

then you will discern the fear of the LORD and discover the knowledge of God.

2:6

כִּיfor יְהוָהLORD יִתֵּןgives חָכְמָהwisdom מִפִּיוfrom his mouth דַּעַתknowledge וּתְבוּנָהand understanding

For the LORD gives wisdom; from His mouth come knowledge and understanding.

2:7

יִצְפֹּןstores לַיְשָׁרִיםfor the upright תּוּשִׁיָּהsound wisdom מָגֵןshield לְהֹלְכֵיwalk תֹםintegrity

He stores up sound wisdom for the upright; He is a shield to those who walk with integrity,

2:8

לִנְצֹרto guard אָרְחוֹתpaths מִשְׁפָּטjustice וְדֶרֶךְway חֲסִידָיוhis saints יִשְׁמֹרprotect

to guard the paths of justice and protect the way of His saints.

2:9

אָזthen תָּבִיןdiscern צֶדֶקrighteousness וּמִשְׁפָּטand justice וּמֵישָׁרִיםand equity כָּלevery מַעְגַּלpath טוֹבgood

Then you will discern righteousness and justice and equity—every good path.

2:10

כִּיfor תָבוֹאenter חָכְמָהwisdom בְלִבֶּךָyour heart וְדַעַתand knowledge לְנַפְשְׁךָwill your soul יִנְעָםdelight

For wisdom will enter your heart, and knowledge will delight your soul.

2:11

מְזִמָּהdiscretion תִּשְׁמֹרwatch עָלֶיךָover you תְּבוּנָהunderstanding תִנְצְרֶכָּהguard you

Discretion will watch over you, and understanding will guard you,

2:12

לְהַצִּילְךָto deliver you מִדֶּרֶךְfrom the way רָעevil מֵאִישׁfrom the man מְדַבֵּרspeaks תַּהְפֻּכוֹתperversity

to deliver you from the way of evil, from the man who speaks perversity,

2:13

הַעֹזְבִיםwho leave אָרְחוֹתpaths יֹשֶׁרstraight לָלֶכֶתto walk בְּדַרְכֵיin the ways חֹשֶׁךְdarkness

from those who leave the straight paths to walk in the ways of darkness,

2:14

הַשְּׂמֵחִיםwho enjoy לַעֲשׂוֹתdoing רָעevil יָגִילוּrejoice בְּתַהְפֻּכוֹתin the twistedness רָעevil

from those who enjoy doing evil and rejoice in the twistedness of evil,

2:15

אֲשֶׁרthat אָרְחֹתֵיהֶםpaths are עִקְּשִׁיםcrooked וּנְלוֹזִיםdevious בְּמַעְגְּלוֹתָםwhose ways are

whose paths are crooked and whose ways are devious.

2:16

לְהַצִּילְךָwill rescue you מֵאִשָּׁהwoman זָרָהforbidden מִנָּכְרִיָּהfrom the stranger אֲמָרֶיהָwith words הֶחֱלִיקָהseductive

It will rescue you from the forbidden woman, from the stranger with seductive words

2:17

הַעֹזֶבֶתwho abandons אַלּוּףpartner נְעוּרֶיהָher youth וְאֶתand [obj] בְּרִיתcovenant אֱלֹהֶיהָher God שָׁכֵחָהforgets

who abandons the partner of her youth and forgets the covenant of her God.

2:18

כִּיfor שָׁחָהsinks אֶלto מָוֶתdeath בֵּיתָהּher house וְאֶלto רְפָאִיםdeparted spirits מַעְגְּלֹתֶיהָher tracks

For her house sinks down to death, and her tracks to the departed spirits.

2:19

כָּלnone בָּאֶיהָgo to her לֹאnot יְשׁוּבוּןreturn וְלֹאor יַשִּׂיגוּnegotiate אָרְחוֹתpaths חַיִּיםlife

None who go to her return or negotiate the paths of life.

2:20

לְמַעַןso תֵּלֵךְfollow בְּדֶרֶךְin the ways טוֹבִיםgood וְאָרְחוֹתpaths צַדִּיקִיםrighteous תִּשְׁמֹרkeep

So you will follow in the ways of the good, and keep to the paths of the righteous.

2:21

כִּיfor יְשָׁרִיםupright יִשְׁכְּנוּinhabit אָרֶץland וּתְמִימִיםand the blameless יִוָּתְרוּremain בָהּin it

For the upright will inhabit the land, and the blameless will remain in it;

2:22

וּרְשָׁעִיםbut the wicked מֵאֶרֶץfrom the land יִכָּרֵתוּcut off וּבוֹגְדִיםand the unfaithful יִסְּחוּuprooted מִמֶּנָּהfrom her

but the wicked will be cut off from the land, and the unfaithful will be uprooted.
