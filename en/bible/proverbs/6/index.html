Proverbs 6

Pledges, laziness and what the LORD hates

Open in the interlinear reader

6:1

בְּנִיmy son אִםif עָרַבְתָּsecurity לְרֵעֶךָfor neighbor if you תָּקַעְתָּpledge לַזָּרwith a stranger כַּפֶּיךָhands

My son, if you have put up security for your neighbor, if you have struck hands in pledge with a stranger,

6:2

נוֹקַשְׁתָּtrapped בְאִמְרֵיby the words פִיךָyour lips נִלְכַּדְתָּensnared בְּאִמְרֵיby the words פִיךָyour mouth

if you have been trapped by the words of your lips, ensnared by the words of your mouth,

6:3

עֲשֵׂהdo זֹאתthis אֵפוֹאthen בְּנִיmy son וְהִנָּצֵלto free yourself כִּיfor בָאתָcome בְכַףhands רֵעֶךָyour neighbor’s לֵךְgo הִתְרַפֵּסhumble וּרְהַבplea רֵעֶיךָyour neighbor

then do this, my son, to free yourself, for you have fallen into your neighbor’s hands: Go, humble yourself, and press your plea with your neighbor.

6:4

אַלno תִּתֵּןallow שֵׁנָהsleep לְעֵינֶיךָto your eyes וּתְנוּמָהor slumber לְעַפְעַפֶּיךָto your eyelids

Allow no sleep to your eyes or slumber to your eyelids.

6:5

הִנָּצֵלfree yourself כִּצְבִיlike a gazelle מִיָּדfrom the hand וּכְצִפּוֹרhunter like a bird מִיַּדfrom the snare יָקוּשׁfowler

Free yourself, like a gazelle from the hand of the hunter, like a bird from the snare of the fowler.

6:6

לֵךְwalk אֶלto נְמָלָהmanner עָצֵלslacker רְאֵהobserve דְרָכֶיהָits ways וַחֲכָםand become wise

Walk in the manner of the ant, O slacker; observe its ways and become wise.

6:7

אֲשֶׁרthat אֵיןwithout לָהּa קָצִיןcommander שֹׁטֵרoverseer וּמֹשֵׁלor ruler

Without a commander, without an overseer or ruler,

6:8

תָּכִיןprepares בַּקַּיִץits in summer לַחְמָהּprovisions אָגְרָהgathers בַקָּצִירat harvest מַאֲכָלָהּits food

it prepares its provisions in summer; it gathers its food at harvest.

6:9

עַדhow מָתַיlong עָצֵלslacker תִּשְׁכָּבlie מָתַיwhen will תָּקוּםget מִשְּׁנָתֶךָfrom your sleep

How long will you lie there, O slacker? When will you get up from your sleep?

6:10

מְעַטlittle שֵׁנוֹתsleep מְעַטlittle תְּנוּמוֹתslumber מְעַטlittle חִבֻּקfolding יָדַיִםhands לִשְׁכָּבto rest

A little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to rest,

6:11

וּבָאand come כִמְהַלֵּךְcome רֵאשֶׁךָupon you וּמַחְסֹרְךָand need כְּאִישׁlike man מָגֵןbandit

and poverty will come upon you like a robber, and need like a bandit.

6:12

אָדָםperson בְּלִיַּעַלworthless אִישׁperson אָוֶןwicked הוֹלֵךְwalks עִקְּשׁוּתperverse פֶּהmouth

A worthless person, a wicked man, walks with a perverse mouth,

6:13

קֹרֵץwinking בְּעֵינָוhis eyes מֹלֵלspeaking בְּרַגְלָוwith his feet and מֹרֶהpointing בְּאֶצְבְּעֹתָיוwith his fingers

winking his eyes, speaking with his feet, and pointing with his fingers.

6:14

תַּהְפֻּכוֹתdeceit בְּלִבּוֹin his heart he חֹרֵשׁdevises רָעevil בְּכָלin all עֵתcontinually מִדְיָנִיםsows יְשַׁלֵּחַdiscord

With deceit in his heart he devises evil; he continually sows discord.

6:15

עַלupon כֵּןtherefore פִּתְאֹםsuddenly יָבוֹאcome אֵידוֹcalamity פֶּתַעinstant יִשָּׁבֵרshattered וְאֵיןand no מַרְפֵּאrecovery

Therefore calamity will come upon him suddenly; in an instant he will be shattered beyond recovery.

6:16

שֶׁשׁsix הֵנָּהare שָׂנֵאhates יְהוָהLORD וְשֶׁבַעseven תּוֹעֲבַתdetestable נַפְשׁוֹhis soul

There are six things that the LORD hates, seven that are detestable to Him:

6:17

עֵינַיִםeyes רָמוֹתhaughty לְשׁוֹןtongue שָׁקֶרlying וְיָדַיִםhands שֹׁפְכוֹתshed דָּםblood נָקִיinnocent

haughty eyes, a lying tongue, hands that shed innocent blood,

6:18

לֵבheart חֹרֵשׁdevises מַחְשְׁבוֹתschemes אָוֶןwicked רַגְלַיִםfeet מְמַהֲרוֹתswiftly לָרוּץrun to לָרָעָהevil

a heart that devises wicked schemes, feet that run swiftly to evil,

6:19

יָפִיחַsneers כְּזָבִיםfalse עֵדwitness שָׁקֶרfalse וּמְשַׁלֵּחַdiscord מְדָנִיםdiscord בֵּיןamong אַחִיםbrothers

a false witness who gives false testimony, and one who stirs up discord among brothers.

6:20

נְצֹרkeep בְּנִיmy son מִצְוַתcommandment אָבִיךָyour father’s וְאַלand do not תִּטֹּשׁforsake תּוֹרַתteaching אִמֶּךָyour mother’s

My son, keep your father’s commandment, and do not forsake your mother’s teaching.

6:21

קָשְׁרֵםbind them עַלupon לִבְּךָyour heart תָמִידalways עָנְדֵםtie them עַלon גַּרְגְּרֹתֶךָyour neck

Bind them always upon your heart; tie them around your neck.

6:22

בְּהִתְהַלֶּכְךָwhen you walk תַּנְחֶהguide אֹתָךְyou בְּשָׁכְבְּךָwhen you lie תִּשְׁמֹרwatch עָלֶיךָover you וַהֲקִיצוֹתָwhen you awake הִיאwill תְשִׂיחֶךָspeak to you

When you walk, they will guide you; when you lie down, they will watch over you; when you awake, they will speak to you.

6:23

כִּיfor נֵרlamp מִצְוָהcommandment וְתוֹרָהthis teaching אוֹרlight וְדֶרֶךְway חַיִּיםlife תּוֹכְחוֹתreproofs מוּסָרdiscipline

For this commandment is a lamp, this teaching is a light, and the reproofs of discipline are the way to life,

6:24

לִשְׁמָרְךָto keep you מֵאֵשֶׁתwoman רָעevil מֵחֶלְקַתfrom the smooth לָשׁוֹןtongue נָכְרִיָּהadulteress

to keep you from the evil woman, from the smooth tongue of the adulteress.

6:25

אַלdo not תַּחְמֹדlust יָפְיָהּher beauty בִּלְבָבֶךָin your heart וְאַלor תִּקָּחֲךָcaptivate you בְּעַפְעַפֶּיהָwith her eyes

Do not lust in your heart for her beauty or let her captivate you with her eyes.

6:26

כִּיfor בְעַדfor אִשָּׁהwoman זוֹנָהprostitute עַדuntil כִּכַּרpoverty לָחֶםpoverty וְאֵשֶׁתand the adulteress אִישׁyour נֶפֶשׁlife יְקָרָהpreys תָצוּדhunt

For the levy of the prostitute is poverty, and the adulteress preys upon your very life.

6:27

הֲיַחְתֶּהruin snatch אִישׁman אֵשׁfire בְּחֵיקוֹembrace וּבְגָדָיוand his clothes לֹאnot תִשָּׂרַפְנָהburned

Can a man embrace fire and his clothes not be burned?

6:28

אִםif יְהַלֵּךְwalk אִישׁman עַלon הַגֶּחָלִיםcoals וְרַגְלָיוhis feet לֹאnot תִכָּוֶינָהscorching

Can a man walk on hot coals without scorching his feet?

6:29

כֵּןso הַבָּאwho sleeps אֶלto אֵשֶׁתwife רֵעֵהוּanother man’s לֹאno יִנָּקֶהgo unpunished כָּלall הַנֹּגֵעַwho touches בָּהּin her

So is he who sleeps with another man’s wife; no one who touches her will go unpunished.

6:30

לֹאnot יָבוּזוּdespise לַגַּנָּבthe thief כִּיif יִגְנוֹבsteals לְמַלֵּאto satisfy נַפְשׁוֹsatisfy his כִּיfor יִרְעָבhunger

Men do not despise the thief if he steals to satisfy his hunger.

6:31

וְנִמְצָאyet if caught יְשַׁלֵּםpay שִׁבְעָתָיִםsevenfold אֶת[obj] כָּלall הוֹןwealth בֵּיתוֹhis house יִתֵּןgive

Yet if caught, he must pay sevenfold; he must give up all the wealth of his house.

6:32

נֹאֵףcommits adultery אִשָּׁהwoman חֲסַרlacks לֵבjudgment מַשְׁחִיתdestroys נַפְשׁוֹhimself הוּאhe יַעֲשֶׂנָּהdo her

He who commits adultery lacks judgment; whoever does so destroys himself.

6:33

נֶגַעwounds וְקָלוֹןand dishonor יִמְצָאbefall וְחֶרְפָּתוֹand his reproach לֹאnever תִמָּחֶהwiped

Wounds and dishonor will befall him, and his reproach will never be wiped away.

6:34

כִּיfor קִנְאָהjealousy חֲמַתenrages גָּבֶרhusband וְלֹאno יַחְמוֹלmercy בְּיוֹםin the day נָקָםvengeance

For jealousy enrages a husband, and he will show no mercy in the day of vengeance.

6:35

לֹאnot יִשָּׂאappeased פְּנֵיface כָלany כֹּפֶרransom וְלֹאor יֹאבֶהpersuaded כִּיfor תַרְבֶּהlavish gifts שֹׁחַדlavish

He will not be appeased by any ransom, or persuaded by lavish gifts.
