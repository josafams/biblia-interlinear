Proverbs 10

Proverbs of Solomon: the righteous and the wicked

Open in the interlinear reader

10:1

מִשְׁלֵיproverbs שְׁלֹמֹהSolomon בֵּןson חָכָםwise יְשַׂמַּחjoy אָבfather וּבֵןson כְּסִילfoolish תּוּגַתgrief אִמּוֹhis mother

The proverbs of Solomon: A wise son brings joy to his father, but a foolish son grief to his mother.

10:2

לֹאnot יוֹעִילוּprofit אוֹצְרוֹתtreasures רֶשַׁעgotten וּצְדָקָהbut righteousness תַּצִּילdeliverance מִמָּוֶתfrom death

Ill-gotten treasures profit nothing, but righteousness brings deliverance from death.

10:3

לֹאnot יַרְעִיבhungry יְהוָהLORD נֶפֶשׁdoes צַדִּיקrighteous וְהַוַּתbut he denies רְשָׁעִיםwicked יֶהְדֹּףcraving

The LORD does not let the righteous go hungry, but He denies the craving of the wicked.

10:4

רָאשׁpoor עֹשֶׂהmake כַףhands רְמִיָּהidle וְיַדbut hands חָרוּצִיםdiligent תַּעֲשִׁירwealth

Idle hands make one poor, but diligent hands bring wealth.

10:5

אֹגֵרgathers בַּקַּיִץin summer בֵּןson מַשְׂכִּילwise נִרְדָּםsleeps בַּקָּצִירduring harvest בֵּןson מֵבִישׁdisgraceful

He who gathers in summer is a wise son, but he who sleeps during harvest is a disgraceful son.

10:6

בְּרָכוֹתblessings לְרֹאשׁon the head צַדִּיקrighteous וּפִיbut the mouth רְשָׁעִיםwicked יְכַסֶּהconceals חָמָסviolence

Blessings are on the head of the righteous, but the mouth of the wicked conceals violence.

10:7

זֵכֶרmemory צַדִּיקrighteous לִבְרָכָהa blessing וְשֵׁםbut the name רְשָׁעִיםwicked יִרְקָבrot

The memory of the righteous is a blessing, but the name of the wicked will rot.

10:8

חֲכַםwise לֵבheart יִקַּחreceive מִצְוֺתcommandments וֶאֱוִילbut foolish שְׂפָתַיִםlips יִלָּבֵטruin

A wise heart will receive commandments, but foolish lips will come to ruin.

10:9

הוֹלֵךְwalks בַּתֹּםin integrity יֵלֶךְwalks בֶּטַחsecurely וּמְעַקֵּשׁperverts דְּרָכָיוhis ways יִוָּדֵעַfound

He who walks in integrity walks securely, but he who perverts his ways will be found out.

10:10

קֹרֵץwinks עַיִןeye יִתֵּןcauses עַצָּבֶתgrief וֶאֱוִילand foolish שְׂפָתַיִםlips יִלָּבֵטruin

He who winks the eye causes grief, and foolish lips will come to ruin.

10:11

מְקוֹרfountain חַיִּיםlife פִּיmouth צַדִּיקrighteous וּפִיbut the mouth רְשָׁעִיםwicked יְכַסֶּהconceals חָמָסviolence

The mouth of the righteous is a fountain of life, but the mouth of the wicked conceals violence.

10:12

שִׂנְאָהhatred תְּעוֹרֵרstirs מְדָנִיםdissension וְעַלand on כָּלall פְּשָׁעִיםtransgressions תְּכַסֶּהcovers אַהֲבָהlove

Hatred stirs up dissension, but love covers all transgressions.

10:13

בְּשִׂפְתֵיon the lips נָבוֹןdiscerning תִּמָּצֵאfound חָכְמָהwisdom וְשֵׁבֶטbut a rod לְגֵוfor the back חֲסַרlacks לֵבjudgment

Wisdom is found on the lips of the discerning, but a rod is for the back of him who lacks judgment.

10:14

חֲכָמִיםwise יִצְפְּנוּstore up דָעַתknowledge וּפִיbut the mouth אֱוִילfool מְחִתָּהinvites קְרֹבָהinvites destruction

The wise store up knowledge, but the mouth of the fool invites destruction.

10:15

הוֹןwealth עָשִׁירrich קִרְיַתfortified עֻזּוֹhis fortified city מְחִתַּתruin דַּלִּיםpoor רֵישָׁםpoverty

The wealth of the rich man is his fortified city, but poverty is the ruin of the poor.

10:16

פְּעֻלַּתlabor צַדִּיקrighteous לְחַיִּיםto life תְּבוּאַתgain רָשָׁעwicked לְחַטָּאתpunishment

The labor of the righteous leads to life, but the gain of the wicked brings punishment.

10:17

אֹרַחpath לְחַיִּיםto life שׁוֹמֵרwhoever מוּסָרheeds instruction וְעוֹזֵבignores תּוֹכַחַתreproof מַתְעֶהastray

Whoever heeds instruction is on the path to life, but he who ignores reproof goes astray.

10:18

מְכַסֶּהconceals שִׂנְאָהhatred שִׂפְתֵיlips שָׁקֶרlying וּמוֹצִאand whoever spreads דִבָּהslander הוּאis כְסִילfool

The one who conceals hatred has lying lips, and whoever spreads slander is a fool.

10:19

בְּרֹבmany דְּבָרִיםwords לֹאnot יֶחְדַּלunavoidable פָּשַׁעunavoidable וְחֹשֵׂךְrestrains שְׂפָתָיוhis lips מַשְׂכִּילwise

When words are many, sin is unavoidable, but he who restrains his lips is wise.

10:20

כֶּסֶףsilver נִבְחָרchoice לְשׁוֹןtongue צַדִּיקrighteous לֵבheart רְשָׁעִיםwicked כִּמְעָטlittle

The tongue of the righteous is choice silver, but the heart of the wicked has little worth.

10:21

שִׂפְתֵיlips צַדִּיקrighteous יִרְעוּfeed רַבִּיםmany וֶאֱוִילִיםbut fools בַּחֲסַרfor lack לֵבjudgment יָמוּתוּdie

The lips of the righteous feed many, but fools die for lack of judgment.

10:22

בִּרְכַּתblessing יְהוָהLORD הִיאhe תַעֲשִׁירenriches וְלֹאno יוֹסִףadds עֶצֶבsorrow עִמָּהּto it

The blessing of the LORD enriches, and He adds no sorrow to it.

10:23

כִּשְׂחוֹקdelights לִכְסִילfool in עֲשׂוֹתshameful זִמָּהconduct וְחָכְמָהwisdom לְאִישׁa man תְּבוּנָהunderstanding

The fool delights in shameful conduct, but a man of understanding has wisdom.

10:24

מְגוֹרַתdreads רָשָׁעwicked הִיאwill תְבוֹאֶנּוּovertake him וְתַאֲוַתbut the desire צַדִּיקִיםrighteous יִתֵּןgranted

What the wicked man dreads will overtake him, but the desire of the righteous will be granted.

10:25

כַּעֲבוֹרpasses סוּפָהwhirlwind וְאֵיןno רָשָׁעwicked וְצַדִּיקbut the righteous יְסוֹדsecure עוֹלָםforever

When the whirlwind passes, the wicked are no more, but the righteous are secure forever.

10:26

כַּחֹמֶץlike vinegar לַשִּׁנַּיִםto the teeth וְכֶעָשָׁןand smoke לָעֵינָיִםto the eyes כֵּןso הֶעָצֵלthe slacker לְשֹׁלְחָיוsend him

Like vinegar to the teeth and smoke to the eyes, so is the slacker to those who send him.

10:27

יִרְאַתfear יְהוָהLORD תּוֹסִיףprolongs יָמִיםlife וּשְׁנוֹתbut the years רְשָׁעִיםwicked תִּקְצֹרְנָהshort

The fear of the LORD prolongs life, but the years of the wicked will be cut short.

10:28

תּוֹחֶלֶתhope צַדִּיקִיםrighteous שִׂמְחָהjoy וְתִקְוַתbut the expectations רְשָׁעִיםwicked תֹּאבֵדperish

The hope of the righteous is joy, but the expectations of the wicked will perish.

10:29

מָעוֹזrefuge לַתֹּםto the upright דֶּרֶךְway יְהוָהLORD וּמְחִתָּהbut destruction awaits לְפֹעֲלֵיdo אָוֶןevil

The way of the LORD is a refuge to the upright, but destruction awaits those who do evil.

10:30

צַדִּיקrighteous לְעוֹלָםwill never בַּלbe יִמּוֹטshaken וּרְשָׁעִיםbut the wicked לֹאnot יִשְׁכְּנוּinhabit אָרֶץland

The righteous will never be shaken, but the wicked will not inhabit the land.

10:31

פִּיmouth צַדִּיקrighteous יָנוּבforth חָכְמָהwisdom וּלְשׁוֹןtongue תַּהְפֻּכוֹתperverse תִּכָּרֵתcut

The mouth of the righteous brings forth wisdom, but a perverse tongue will be cut out.

10:32

שִׂפְתֵיlips צַדִּיקrighteous יֵדְעוּןknow רָצוֹןfitting וּפִיbut the mouth רְשָׁעִיםwicked תַּהְפֻּכוֹתperverse

The lips of the righteous know what is fitting, but the mouth of the wicked is perverse.
