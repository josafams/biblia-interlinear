Proverbs 30

Agur: the limits of knowledge and numerical sayings

Open in the interlinear reader

30:1

דִּבְרֵיwords אָגוּרAgur בִּןson יָקֶהJakeh הַמַּשָּׂאthe burden נְאֻםdeclared הַגֶּבֶרthis man לְאִיתִיאֵלto Ithiel לְאִיתִיאֵלto Ithiel וְאֻכָלGod and worn

These are the words of Agur son of Jakeh—the burden that this man declared to Ithiel: “I am weary, O God, and worn out.

30:2

כִּיfor בַעַרI אָנֹכִיam מֵאִישׁof men וְלֹאand not בִינַתunderstanding אָדָםman לִיto me

Surely I am the most ignorant of men, and I lack the understanding of a man.

30:3

וְלֹאI have not לָמַדְתִּיlearned חָכְמָהwisdom וְדַעַתno knowledge קְדֹשִׁיםholy אֵדָעknow

I have not learned wisdom, and I have no knowledge of the Holy One.

30:4

מִיwho עָלָהascended שָׁמַיִםheaven וַיֵּרַדand come down מִיwho אָסַףgathered רוּחַwind בְּחָפְנָיוin his hands מִיwho צָרַרbound מַיִםwaters בַּשִּׂמְלָהin his cloak מִיwho הֵקִיםestablished כָּלall אַפְסֵיends אָרֶץearth מַהwhat שְּׁמוֹhis name וּמַהand what שֶּׁםname בְּנוֹhis son כִּיfor תֵדָעknow

Who has ascended to heaven and come down? Who has gathered the wind in His hands? Who has bound up the waters in His cloak? Who has established all the ends of the earth? What is His name, and what is the name of His Son— surely you know!

30:5

כָּלevery אִמְרַתword אֱלוֹהַּGod צְרוּפָהflawless מָגֵןshield הוּאa לַחֹסִיםto those who take refuge בּוֹin him

Every word of God is flawless; He is a shield to those who take refuge in Him.

30:6

אַלdo not תּוֹסְףְּadd עַלto דְּבָרָיוhis words פֶּןlest יוֹכִיחַrebuke בְּךָin you וְנִכְזָבְתָּand prove

Do not add to His words, lest He rebuke you and prove you a liar.

30:7

שְׁתַּיִםtwo שָׁאַלְתִּיask מֵאִתָּךְfrom the you אַלdo not תִּמְנַעrefuse מִמֶּנִּיfrom me בְּטֶרֶםbefore אָמוּתdie

Two things I ask of You— do not refuse me before I die:

30:8

שָׁוְאfalsehood וּדְבַרand words כָּזָבdeceitful הַרְחֵקfar מִמֶּנִּיfrom me רֵאשׁpoverty וָעֹשֶׁרnor riches אַלdo not תִּתֶּןgive לִיto me הַטְרִיפֵנִיfeed me לֶחֶםbread חֻקִּיmy portion

Keep falsehood and deceitful words far from me. Give me neither poverty nor riches; feed me with the bread that is my portion.

30:9

פֶּןotherwise אֶשְׂבַּעmuch וְכִחַשְׁתִּיand deny וְאָמַרְתִּיyou saying מִיwho יְהוָהLORD וּפֶןor אִוָּרֵשׁpoor וְגָנַבְתִּיand steal וְתָפַשְׂתִּיprofaning שֵׁםname אֱלֹהָיmy God

Otherwise, I may have too much and deny You, saying, ‘Who is the LORD?’ Or I may become poor and steal, profaning the name of my God.

30:10

אַלdo not תַּלְשֵׁןslander עֶבֶדservant אֶלto אֲדֹנָיוhis master פֶּןor יְקַלֶּלְךָcurse you וְאָשָׁמְתָּguilt

Do not slander a servant to his master, or he will curse you, and you will bear the guilt.

30:11

דּוֹרgeneration אָבִיוtheir fathers יְקַלֵּלcurse וְאֶתand [obj] אִמּוֹtheir mothers לֹאnot יְבָרֵךְbless

There is a generation of those who curse their fathers and do not bless their mothers.

30:12

דּוֹרgeneration טָהוֹרpure בְּעֵינָיוin their own eyes וּמִצֹּאָתוֹof their filth לֹאnot רֻחָץunwashed

There is a generation of those who are pure in their own eyes and yet unwashed of their filth.

30:13

דּוֹרgeneration מָהhow רָמוּhaughty עֵינָיוtheir eyes וְעַפְעַפָּיוand pretentious are their יִנָּשֵׂאוּglances

There is a generation—how haughty are their eyes and pretentious are their glances—

30:14

דּוֹרgeneration חֲרָבוֹתswords שִׁנָּיוteeth וּמַאֲכָלוֹתand whose jaws מְתַלְּעֹתָיוjaws are knives לֶאֱכֹלdevouring עֲנִיִּיםoppressed מֵאֶרֶץfrom the earth וְאֶבְיוֹנִיםand the needy מֵאָדָםfrom among men

there is a generation whose teeth are swords and whose jaws are knives, devouring the oppressed from the earth and the needy from among men.

30:15

לַעֲלוּקָהthe leech שְׁתֵּיtwo בָנוֹתdaughters הַבgive הַבgive שָׁלוֹשׁthree הֵנָּהare לֹאnever תִשְׂבַּעְנָהsatisfied אַרְבַּעfour לֹאnever אָמְרוּsay הוֹןenough

The leech has two daughters: Give and Give. There are three things that are never satisfied, four that never say, ‘Enough!’:

30:16

שְׁאוֹלSheol וְעֹצֶרbarren רָחַםwomb אֶרֶץland לֹאnever שָׂבְעָהsatisfied מַּיִםwater וְאֵשׁand fire לֹאnever אָמְרָהsays הוֹןenough

Sheol, the barren womb, land never satisfied with water, and fire that never says, ‘Enough!’

30:17

עַיִןeye תִּלְעַגmocks לְאָבa father וְתָבוּזand scorns לִיקֲּהַתobedience to אֵםmother יִקְּרוּהָpluck it עֹרְבֵיravens נַחַלvalley וְיֹאכְלוּהָdevour it בְנֵיyoung נָשֶׁרvultures

As for the eye that mocks a father and scorns obedience to a mother, may the ravens of the valley pluck it out and young vultures devour it.

30:18

שְׁלֹשָׁהthree הֵמָּהare נִפְלְאוּwonderful מִמֶּנִּיfrom me וְאַרְבָּעָהfour לֹאnot יְדַעְתִּיםunderstand

There are three things too wonderful for me, four that I cannot understand:

30:19

דֶּרֶךְway הַנֶּשֶׁרof an eagle בַּשָּׁמַיִםin the sky דֶּרֶךְway נָחָשׁsnake עֲלֵיon צוּרrock דֶּרֶךְway אֳנִיָּהship בְלֶבin heart יָםsea וְדֶרֶךְand the way גֶּבֶרman בְּעַלְמָהwith a maiden

the way of an eagle in the sky, the way of a snake on a rock, the way of a ship at sea, and the way of a man with a maiden.

30:20

כֵּןthis דֶּרֶךְway אִשָּׁהshe מְנָאָפֶתadulteress אָכְלָהeats וּמָחֲתָהand wipes פִיהָher mouth וְאָמְרָהand says לֹאnot פָעַלְתִּיdone אָוֶןwrong

This is the way of an adulteress: She eats and wipes her mouth and says, ‘I have done nothing wrong.’

30:21

תַּחַתunder שָׁלוֹשׁthree רָגְזָהthings אֶרֶץearth וְתַחַתunder אַרְבַּעfour לֹאnot תוּכַלcannot שְׂאֵתbear

Under three things the earth trembles, under four it cannot bear up:

30:22

תַּחַתunder עֶבֶדservant כִּיfor יִמְלוֹךְking וְנָבָלfool כִּיfor יִשְׂבַּעfilled לָחֶםfood

a servant who becomes king, a fool who is filled with food,

30:23

תַּחַתunder שְׂנוּאָהunloved כִּיfor תִבָּעֵלmarries וְשִׁפְחָהand a maidservant כִּיfor תִירַשׁsupplants גְּבִרְתָּהּher mistress

an unloved woman who marries, and a maidservant who supplants her mistress.

30:24

אַרְבָּעָהfour הֵםare קְטַנֵּיsmall אָרֶץearth וְהֵמָּהyet they חֲכָמִיםexceedingly מְחֻכָּמִיםwise

Four things on earth are small, yet they are exceedingly wise:

30:25

הַנְּמָלִיםthe ants עַםcreatures לֹאnot עָזstrength וַיָּכִינוּyet they store up בַקַּיִץin the summer לַחְמָםtheir food

The ants are creatures of little strength, yet they store up their food in the summer;

30:26

שְׁפַנִּיםrock badgers עַםcreatures לֹאnot עָצוּםpower וַיָּשִׂימוּyet they make בַסֶּלַעin the rocks בֵּיתָםtheir homes

the rock badgers are creatures of little power, yet they make their homes in the rocks;

30:27

מֶלֶךְking אֵיןno לָאַרְבֶּהthe locusts וַיֵּצֵאand went חֹצֵץadvance כֻּלּוֹall

the locusts have no king, yet they all advance in formation;

30:28

שְׂמָמִיתlizard בְּיָדַיִםin one’s hands תְּתַפֵּשׂcaught וְהִיאyet it is בְּהֵיכְלֵיin the palaces מֶלֶךְkings

and the lizard can be caught in one’s hands, yet it is found in the palaces of kings.

30:29

שְׁלֹשָׁהthree הֵמָּהare מֵיטִיבֵיstately צָעַדstride וְאַרְבָּעָהand four מֵיטִבֵיimpressive לָכֶתwalk

There are three things that are stately in their stride, and four that are impressive in their walk:

30:30

לַיִשׁlion גִּבּוֹרmighty בַּבְּהֵמָהamong beasts וְלֹאand not יָשׁוּבretreat מִפְּנֵיbefore כֹלanything

a lion, mighty among beasts, refusing to retreat before anything;

30:31

זַרְזִירstrutting מָתְנַיִםrooster אוֹa תָיִשׁgoat וּמֶלֶךְand a king אַלְקוּםarmy עִמּוֹwith

a strutting rooster; a he-goat; and a king with his army around him.

30:32

אִםif נָבַלְתָּfoolishly בְהִתְנַשֵּׂאexalted וְאִםor if זַמּוֹתָplotted evil יָדhand לְפֶהover your mouth

If you have foolishly exalted yourself or if you have plotted evil, put your hand over your mouth.

30:33

כִּיfor מִיץchurning חָלָבmilk יוֹצִיאyields חֶמְאָהbutter וּמִיץand the twisting אַףnose יוֹצִיאdraws דָםblood וּמִיץso the stirring אַפַּיִםanger יוֹצִיאforth רִיבstrife

For as the churning of milk yields butter, and the twisting of the nose draws blood, so the stirring of anger brings forth strife.”
