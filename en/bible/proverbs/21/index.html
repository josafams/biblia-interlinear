Proverbs 21

The king's heart in the hand of the LORD

Open in the interlinear reader

21:1

פַּלְגֵיwaterway מַיִםwater לֶבheart מֶלֶךְking’s בְּיַדin the hand יְהוָהLORD עַלon כָּלdirects אֲשֶׁרthat יַחְפֹּץpleases יַטֶּנּוּdeny him

The king’s heart is a waterway in the hand of the LORD; He directs it where He pleases.

21:2

כָּלall דֶּרֶךְways אִישׁman’s יָשָׁרright בְּעֵינָיוin his sight וְתֹכֵןweighs לִבּוֹתheart יְהוָהLORD

All a man’s ways seem right to him, but the LORD weighs the heart.

21:3

עֲשֹׂהdo צְדָקָהrighteousness וּמִשְׁפָּטand justice נִבְחָרdesirable לַיהוָהto the LORD מִזָּבַחthan sacrifice

To do righteousness and justice is more desirable to the LORD than sacrifice.

21:4

רוּםhaughty עֵינַיִםeyes וּרְחַבand a proud לֵבheart נִרguides רְשָׁעִיםwicked חַטָּאתsin

Haughty eyes and a proud heart— the guides of the wicked—are sin.

21:5

מַחְשְׁבוֹתplans חָרוּץdiligent אַךְsurely לְמוֹתָרbring plenty וְכָלand all אָץhaste אַךְbut לְמַחְסוֹרto poverty

The plans of the diligent bring plenty, as surely as haste leads to poverty.

21:6

פֹּעַלfortune אוֹצָרוֹתfortune בִּלְשׁוֹןtongue שָׁקֶרlying הֶבֶלvanishing נִדָּףmist מְבַקְשֵׁיvanishing מָוֶתdeadly

Making a fortune by a lying tongue is a vanishing mist, a deadly pursuit.

21:7

שֹׁדviolence רְשָׁעִיםwicked יְגוֹרֵםsweep them כִּיbecause מֵאֲנוּrefuse לַעֲשׂוֹתto do מִשְׁפָּטjust

The violence of the wicked will sweep them away because they refuse to do what is just.

21:8

הֲפַכְפַּךְguilty דֶּרֶךְway אִישׁman וָזָרcrooked וְזַךְinnocent יָשָׁרupright פָּעֳלוֹhis work

The way of a guilty man is crooked, but the conduct of the innocent is upright.

21:9

טוֹבbetter לָשֶׁבֶתto live עַלon פִּנַּתcorner גָּגroof מֵאֵשֶׁתwife מִדְיָנִיםquarrelsome וּבֵיתhouse with חָבֶרspells

Better to live on a corner of the roof than to share a house with a quarrelsome wife.

21:10

נֶפֶשׁsoul רָשָׁעwicked אִוְּתָהcraves רָעevil לֹאno יֻחַןfavor בְּעֵינָיוin his eyes רֵעֵהוּneighbor

The soul of the wicked man craves evil; his neighbor finds no favor in his eyes.

21:11

בַּעְנָשׁpunished לֵץmocker יֶחְכַּםwisdom פֶּתִיsimple וּבְהַשְׂכִּילand when a wise לְחָכָםinstructed יִקַּחacquires דָּעַתknowledge

When a mocker is punished, the simple gain wisdom; and when a wise man is instructed, he acquires knowledge.

21:12

מַשְׂכִּילconsiders צַדִּיקrighteous לְבֵיתthe house רָשָׁעwicked מְסַלֵּףbrings רְשָׁעִיםwicked לָרָעto ruin

The Righteous One considers the house of the wicked and brings the wicked to ruin.

21:13

אֹטֵםshuts אָזְנוֹhis ears מִזַּעֲקַתto the cry דָּלpoor גַּםtoo הוּאhe יִקְרָאcry וְלֹאand receive no יֵעָנֶהanswer

Whoever shuts his ears to the cry of the poor, he too shall cry out and receive no answer.

21:14

מַתָּןgift בַּסֵּתֶרin secret יִכְפֶּהsoothes אָףanger וְשֹׁחַדbribe בַּחֵקa covert חֵמָהwrath עַזָּהpacifies great

A gift in secret soothes anger, and a covert bribe pacifies great wrath.

21:15

שִׂמְחָהjoy לַצַּדִּיקto the righteous עֲשׂוֹתexecuted מִשְׁפָּטjustice וּמְחִתָּהbut a terror לְפֹעֲלֵיto the workers אָוֶןiniquity

Justice executed is a joy to the righteous, but a terror to the workers of iniquity.

21:16

אָדָםman תּוֹעֶהstrays מִדֶּרֶךְfrom the path הַשְׂכֵּלunderstanding בִּקְהַלin the assembly רְפָאִיםdead יָנוּחַrest

The man who strays from the path of understanding will rest in the assembly of the dead.

21:17

אִישׁwho מַחְסוֹרwho אֹהֵבloves שִׂמְחָהpleasure אֹהֵבloves יַיִןwine וָשֶׁמֶןand oil לֹאnever יַעֲשִׁירrich

He who loves pleasure will become poor; the one who loves wine and oil will never be rich.

21:18

כֹּפֶרransom לַצַּדִּיקfor the righteous רָשָׁעwicked וְתַחַתfor יְשָׁרִיםupright בּוֹגֵדfaithless

The wicked become a ransom for the righteous, and the faithless for the upright.

21:19

טוֹבbetter שֶׁבֶתlive בְּאֶרֶץin land מִדְבָּרdesert מֵאֵשֶׁתwife מִדְיָנִיםcontentious וָכָעַסand ill

Better to live in the desert than with a contentious and ill-tempered wife.

21:20

אוֹצָרtreasures נֶחְמָדprecious וָשֶׁמֶןand oil בִּנְוֵהin the dwelling חָכָםwise וּכְסִילbut a foolish אָדָםman יְבַלְּעֶנּוּconsumes them

Precious treasures and oil are in the dwelling of the wise, but a foolish man consumes them.

21:21

רֹדֵףpursues צְדָקָהrighteousness וָחָסֶדand loving devotion יִמְצָאfinds חַיִּיםlife צְדָקָהrighteousness וְכָבוֹדand honor

He who pursues righteousness and loving devotion finds life, righteousness, and honor.

21:22

עִירcity גִּבֹּרִיםmighty עָלָהpulls חָכָםwise וַיֹּרֶדand down עֹזstronghold מִבְטֶחָהtrust

A wise man scales the city of the mighty and pulls down the stronghold in which they trust.

21:23

שֹׁמֵרguards פִּיוhis mouth וּלְשׁוֹנוֹand tongue שֹׁמֵרkeeps מִצָּרוֹתfrom distress נַפְשׁוֹhis soul

He who guards his mouth and tongue keeps his soul from distress.

21:24

זֵדproud יָהִירarrogant לֵץmocker שְׁמוֹname עוֹשֶׂהacts בְּעֶבְרַתwith excessive זָדוֹןpride

Mocker is the name of the proud and arrogant man— of him who acts with excessive pride.

21:25

תַּאֲוַתcraving עָצֵלslacker תְּמִיתֶנּוּkills him כִּיbecause מֵאֲנוּrefuse יָדָיוhis hands לַעֲשׂוֹתto work

The craving of the slacker kills him because his hands refuse to work.

21:26

כָּלall הַיּוֹםday הִתְאַוָּהlong תַאֲוָהcovets more וְצַדִּיקbut the righteous יִתֵּןgive וְלֹאand not יַחְשֹׂךְrestraint

All day long he covets more, but the righteous give without restraint.

21:27

זֶבַחsacrifice רְשָׁעִיםwicked תּוֹעֵבָהdetestable אַףhow much כִּיwhen בְזִמָּהwith ill יְבִיאֶנּוּbrought

The sacrifice of the wicked is detestable— how much more so when brought with ill intent!

21:28

עֵדa witness כְּזָבִיםlying יֹאבֵדperish וְאִישׁbut the man שׁוֹמֵעַlistens לָנֶצַחto truth יְדַבֵּרspeak

A lying witness will perish, but the man who listens to truth will speak forever.

21:29

הֵעֵזhardens אִישׁman רָשָׁעwicked בְּפָנָיוhis face וְיָשָׁרbut the upright הוּאhe יָבִיןsure דַּרְכּוֹhis way

A wicked man hardens his face, but the upright man makes his way sure.

21:30

אֵיןno חָכְמָהwisdom וְאֵיןno תְּבוּנָהunderstanding וְאֵיןno עֵצָהcounsel לְנֶגֶדto before יְהוָהLORD

There is no wisdom, no understanding, no counsel that can prevail against the LORD.

21:31

סוּסhorse מוּכָןprepared לְיוֹםfor the day מִלְחָמָהbattle וְלַיהוָהLORD הַתְּשׁוּעָהvictory

A horse is prepared for the day of battle, but victory is of the LORD.
