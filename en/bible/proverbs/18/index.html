Proverbs 18

The power of the tongue; the name of the LORD

Open in the interlinear reader

18:1

לְתַאֲוָהdesires יְבַקֵּשׁrebels נִפְרָדrebels בְּכָלagainst all תּוּשִׁיָּהsound judgment יִתְגַּלָּעsound

He who isolates himself pursues selfish desires; he rebels against all sound judgment.

18:2

לֹאnot יַחְפֹּץdelight כְּסִילfool בִּתְבוּנָהin understanding כִּיbut אִםif בְּהִתְגַּלּוֹתin airing לִבּוֹhis opinions

A fool does not delight in understanding, but only in airing his opinions.

18:3

בְּבוֹאin came רָשָׁעwicked בָּאcomes גַםwell בּוּזcontempt וְעִםand with קָלוֹןshame חֶרְפָּהdisgrace

With a wicked man comes contempt as well, and shame is accompanied by disgrace.

18:4

מַיִםwaters עֲמֻקִּיםdeep דִּבְרֵיwords פִיmouth אִישׁman’s נַחַלbrook נֹבֵעַbubbling מְקוֹרfountain חָכְמָהwisdom

The words of a man’s mouth are deep waters; the fountain of wisdom is a bubbling brook.

18:5

שְׂאֵתshowing partiality פְּנֵיpartiality רָשָׁעwicked לֹאnot טוֹבgood לְהַטּוֹתdepriving צַדִּיקinnocent בַּמִּשְׁפָּטof justice

Showing partiality to the wicked is not good, nor is depriving the innocent of justice.

18:6

שִׂפְתֵיlips כְסִילfool’s יָבֹאוּbring בְרִיבhim strife וּפִיוand his mouth לְמַהֲלֻמוֹתinvites יִקְרָאbeating

A fool’s lips bring him strife, and his mouth invites a beating.

18:7

פִּיmouth כְסִילfool’s מְחִתָּהruin לוֹto him וּשְׂפָתָיוand lips מוֹקֵשׁsnare נַפְשׁוֹhis soul

A fool’s mouth is his ruin, and his lips are a snare to his soul.

18:8

דִּבְרֵיwords נִרְגָּןgossip כְּמִתְלַהֲמִיםlike choice morsels וְהֵםand they יָרְדוּgo down חַדְרֵיinto the inmost בָטֶןinmost being

The words of a gossip are like choice morsels that go down into the inmost being.

18:9

גַּםeven מִתְרַפֶּהslothful בִמְלַאכְתּוֹin his work אָחbrother הוּאis לְבַעַלto him who מַשְׁחִיתdestroys

Whoever is slothful in his work is brother to him who destroys.

18:10

מִגְדַּלtower עֹזstrong שֵׁםname יְהוָהLORD בּוֹin him יָרוּץrun צַדִּיקrighteous וְנִשְׂגָּבand are safe

The name of the LORD is a strong tower; the righteous run to it and are safe.

18:11

הוֹןwealth עָשִׁירrich קִרְיַתfortified עֻזּוֹhis city וּכְחוֹמָהwall נִשְׂגָּבָהhigh בְּמַשְׂכִּיתוֹin his imagination

A rich man’s wealth is his fortified city; it is like a high wall in his imagination.

18:12

לִפְנֵיbefore שֶׁבֶרdownfall יִגְבַּהּproud לֵבheart אִישׁman’s וְלִפְנֵיbefore כָבוֹדhonor עֲנָוָהhumility

Before his downfall a man’s heart is proud, but humility comes before honor.

18:13

מֵשִׁיבanswers דָּבָרmatter בְּטֶרֶםbefore יִשְׁמָעhears אִוֶּלֶתfolly הִיאis לוֹto him וּכְלִמָּהand disgrace

He who answers a matter before he hears it— this is folly and disgrace to him.

18:14

רוּחַspirit אִישׁman יְכַלְכֵּלendure מַחֲלֵהוּhis sickness וְרוּחַspirit נְכֵאָהbroken מִיwho יִשָּׂאֶנָּהsurvive a

The spirit of a man can endure his sickness, but who can survive a broken spirit?

18:15

לֵבheart נָבוֹןdiscerning יִקְנֶהacquires דָּעַתknowledge וְאֹזֶןand the ear חֲכָמִיםwise תְּבַקֶּשׁseeks דָּעַתout

The heart of the discerning acquires knowledge, and the ear of the wise seeks it out.

18:16

מַתָּןgift opens אָדָםman’s יַרְחִיבdoors לוֹfor him וְלִפְנֵיand brings him before גְדֹלִיםgreat יַנְחֶנּוּmen

A man’s gift opens doors for him, and brings him before great men.

18:17

צַדִּיקseems הָרִאשׁוֹןthe first בְּרִיבוֹto state his case וּבָאuntil comes רֵעֵהוּanother וַחֲקָרוֹand cross examines him

The first to state his case seems right until another comes and cross-examines him.

18:18

מִדְיָנִיםcasting יַשְׁבִּיתremove הַגּוֹרָלthe lot וּבֵיןand between עֲצוּמִיםstrong יַפְרִידseparates opponents

Casting the lot ends quarrels and separates strong opponents.

18:19

אָחbrother נִפְשָׁעoffended מִקִּרְיַתcity עֹזfortified וּמִדְיָנִיםand disputes כִּבְרִיחַlike the bars אַרְמוֹןcastle

An offended brother is harder to win than a fortified city, and disputes are like the bars of a castle.

18:20

מִפְּרִיfrom the fruit פִיmouth אִישׁman’s תִּשְׂבַּעfilled בִּטְנוֹbelly תְּבוּאַתharvest שְׂפָתָיוhis lips he יִשְׂבָּעsatisfied

From the fruit of his mouth a man’s belly is filled; with the harvest from his lips he is satisfied.

18:21

מָוֶתdeath וְחַיִּיםlife and בְּיַדin the power לָשׁוֹןtongue וְאֹהֲבֶיהָlove it יֹאכַלeat פִּרְיָהּits fruit

Life and death are in the power of the tongue, and those who love it will eat its fruit.

18:22

מָצָאfinds אִשָּׁהwife מָצָאfinds טוֹבgood וַיָּפֶקand obtains רָצוֹןfavor מֵיְהוָהfrom the LORD

He who finds a wife finds a good thing and obtains favor from the LORD.

18:23

תַּחֲנוּנִיםmercy יְדַבֶּרspeaks רָשׁpoor וְעָשִׁירbut the rich יַעֲנֶהanswers עַזּוֹתharshly

The poor man pleads for mercy, but the rich man answers harshly.

18:24

אִישׁman רֵעִיםcompanions לְהִתְרֹעֵעַcome to ruin וְיֵשׁbut there אֹהֵבfriend דָּבֵקstays closer מֵאָחthan a brother

A man of many companions may come to ruin, but there is a friend who stays closer than a brother.
