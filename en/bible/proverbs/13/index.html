Proverbs 13

Correction, honest wealth and good company

Open in the interlinear reader

13:1

בֵּןson חָכָםwise מוּסַרdiscipline אָבfather’s וְלֵץbut a mocker לֹאnot שָׁמַעlisten גְּעָרָהrebuke

A wise son heeds his father’s discipline, but a mocker does not listen to rebuke.

13:2

מִפְּרִיfrom the fruit פִיlips אִישׁman יֹאכַלenjoys טוֹבgood וְנֶפֶשׁbut the desire בֹּגְדִיםfaithless חָמָסviolence

From the fruit of his lips a man enjoys good things, but the desire of the faithless is violence.

13:3

נֹצֵרguards פִּיוhis mouth שֹׁמֵרprotects נַפְשׁוֹhis life פֹּשֵׂקopens שְׂפָתָיוlips his own מְחִתָּהinvites לוֹto him

He who guards his mouth protects his life, but the one who opens his lips invites his own ruin.

13:4

מִתְאַוָּהcraves וָאַיִןyet has nothing נַפְשׁוֹhis soul עָצֵלslacker וְנֶפֶשׁbut the soul חָרֻצִיםdiligent תְּדֻשָּׁןfully satisfied

The slacker craves yet has nothing, but the soul of the diligent is fully satisfied.

13:5

דְּבַרword שֶׁקֶרfalsehood יִשְׂנָאhate צַדִּיקrighteous וְרָשָׁעbut the wicked יַבְאִישׁshame וְיַחְפִּירand disgrace

The righteous hate falsehood, but the wicked bring shame and disgrace.

13:6

צְדָקָהrighteousness תִּצֹּרguards תָּםintegrity דָּרֶךְway וְרִשְׁעָהbut wickedness תְּסַלֵּףundermines חַטָּאתsinner

Righteousness guards the man of integrity, but wickedness undermines the sinner.

13:7

יֵשׁthere מִתְעַשֵּׁרrich וְאֵיןbut has nothing כֹּלpretends מִתְרוֹשֵׁשׁpoor וְהוֹןwealth רָבgreat

One pretends to be rich, but has nothing; another pretends to be poor, yet has great wealth.

13:8

כֹּפֶרransom נֶפֶשׁlife אִישׁman’s עָשְׁרוֹriches וְרָשׁbut a poor לֹאno שָׁמַעhears גְּעָרָהthreat

Riches may ransom a man’s life, but a poor man hears no threat.

13:9

אוֹרlight צַדִּיקִיםrighteous יִשְׂמָחshines וְנֵרbrightly but the lamp רְשָׁעִיםwicked יִדְעָךְextinguished

The light of the righteous shines brightly, but the lamp of the wicked is extinguished.

13:10

רַקonly בְּזָדוֹןarrogance יִתֵּןgive מַצָּהstrife וְאֶתwith נוֹעָצִיםadvised חָכְמָהwisdom

Arrogance leads only to strife, but wisdom is with the well-advised.

13:11

הוֹןdishonest wealth מֵהֶבֶלdwindle יִמְעָטdwindle וְקֹבֵץearned עַלon יָדthrough יַרְבֶּהmultiplied

Dishonest wealth will dwindle, but what is earned through hard work will be multiplied.

13:12

תּוֹחֶלֶתhope מְמֻשָּׁכָהdeferred מַחֲלָהsick לֵבheart וְעֵץtree חַיִּיםlife תַּאֲוָהdesire בָאָהfulfilled

Hope deferred makes the heart sick, but desire fulfilled is a tree of life.

13:13

בָּזdespises לְדָבָרinstruction will pay יֵחָבֶלpenalty לוֹto him וִירֵאrespects מִצְוָהcommand הוּאwill יְשֻׁלָּםrewarded

He who despises instruction will pay the penalty, but the one who respects a command will be rewarded.

13:14

תּוֹרַתteaching חָכָםwise מְקוֹרfountain חַיִּיםlife לָסוּרturning מִמֹּקְשֵׁיfrom the snares מָוֶתdeath

The teaching of the wise is a fountain of life, turning one from the snares of death.

13:15

שֵׂכֶלunderstanding טוֹבgood יִתֶּןwins חֵןfavor וְדֶרֶךְbut the way בֹּגְדִיםfaithless אֵיתָןdifficult

Good understanding wins favor, but the way of the faithless is difficult.

13:16

כָּלevery עָרוּםprudent יַעֲשֶׂהacts בְדָעַתwith knowledge וּכְסִילbut a fool יִפְרֹשׂdisplays אִוֶּלֶתfolly

Every prudent man acts with knowledge, but a fool displays his folly.

13:17

מַלְאָךְmessenger רָשָׁעwicked יִפֹּלfalls בְּרָעinto trouble וְצִירenvoy אֱמוּנִיםfaithful מַרְפֵּאhealing

A wicked messenger falls into trouble, but a faithful envoy brings healing.

13:18

רֵישׁpoverty וְקָלוֹןand shame פּוֹרֵעַignores מוּסָרdiscipline וְשׁוֹמֵרbut whoever heeds תּוֹכַחַתcorrection יְכֻבָּדhonored

Poverty and shame come to him who ignores discipline, but whoever heeds correction is honored.

13:19

תַּאֲוָהdesire נִהְיָהfulfilled תֶּעֱרַבsweet לְנָפֶשׁto the soul וְתוֹעֲבַתdetestable כְּסִילִיםfools סוּרturning מֵרָעfrom evil

Desire fulfilled is sweet to the soul, but turning from evil is detestable to fools.

13:20

הוֹלֵךְwalks אֶתwith חֲכָמִיםwise יֶחְכָּםwise וְרֹעֶהbut the companion כְסִילִיםfools יֵרוֹעַdestroyed

He who walks with the wise will become wise, but the companion of fools will be destroyed.

13:21

חַטָּאִיםsinners תְּרַדֵּףpursues רָעָהdisaster וְאֶתand [obj] צַדִּיקִיםrighteous יְשַׁלֶּםreward טוֹבprosperity

Disaster pursues sinners, but prosperity is the reward of the righteous.

13:22

טוֹבgood יַנְחִילinheritance בְּנֵיchildren’s בָנִיםchildren וְצָפוּןbut the sinner’s לַצַּדִּיקto the righteous חֵילwealth חוֹטֵאpassed

A good man leaves an inheritance to his children’s children, but the sinner’s wealth is passed to the righteous.

13:23

רָבabundant אֹכֶלfood נִירfallow ground רָאשִׁיםpoor וְיֵשׁand there נִסְפֶּהswept בְּלֹאin without מִשְׁפָּטjustice

Abundant food is in the fallow ground of the poor, but without justice it is swept away.

13:24

חוֹשֵׂךְspares שִׁבְטוֹrod his שׂוֹנֵאhates בְנוֹson וְאֹהֲבוֹloves him שִׁחֲרוֹdisciplines him מוּסָרdiligently

He who spares the rod hates his son, but he who loves him disciplines him diligently.

13:25

צַדִּיקrighteous אֹכֵלeats לְשֹׂבַעto heart’s נַפְשׁוֹhis content וּבֶטֶןbut the stomach רְשָׁעִיםwicked תֶּחְסָרempty

A righteous man eats to his heart’s content, but the stomach of the wicked is empty.
