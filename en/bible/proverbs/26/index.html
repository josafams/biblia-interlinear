Proverbs 26

The fool, the sluggard and the deceiver

Open in the interlinear reader

26:1

כַּשֶּׁלֶגlike snow בַּקַּיִץin summer וְכַמָּטָרand rain בַּקָּצִירat harvest כֵּןso לֹאnot נָאוֶהbefit לִכְסִילa fool כָּבוֹדhonor

Like snow in summer and rain at harvest, honor does not befit a fool.

26:2

כַּצִּפּוֹרlike a sparrow לָנוּדfluttering sparrow or כַּדְּרוֹרor darting swallow לָעוּףan undeserved כֵּןso קִלְלַתcurse חִנָּםdoes לוֹto תָבֹאcome

Like a fluttering sparrow or darting swallow, an undeserved curse does not come to rest.

26:3

שׁוֹטwhip לַסּוּסfor the horse מֶתֶגbridle לַחֲמוֹרfor the donkey וְשֵׁבֶטand a rod לְגֵוfor the backs כְּסִילִיםfools

A whip for the horse, a bridle for the donkey, and a rod for the backs of fools!

26:4

אַלdo not תַּעַןanswer כְּסִילfool כְּאִוַּלְתּוֹaccording to his folly פֶּןor תִּשְׁוֶהyourself לּוֹto him גַםwill אָתָּהyou

Do not answer a fool according to his folly, or you yourself will be like him.

26:5

עֲנֵהanswer כְסִילfool כְּאִוַּלְתּוֹaccording to his folly פֶּןlest יִהְיֶהbecome חָכָםwise בְּעֵינָיוin his own eyes

Answer a fool according to his folly, lest he become wise in his own eyes.

26:6

מְקַצֶּהcutting רַגְלַיִםfeet חָמָסviolence שֹׁתֶהdrinking שֹׁלֵחַsending דְּבָרִיםmessage בְּיַדby the hand כְּסִילfool

Like cutting off one’s own feet or drinking violence is the sending of a message by the hand of a fool.

26:7

דַּלְיוּhanging שֹׁקַיִםlegs limp מִפִּסֵּחַlike lame וּמָשָׁלproverb בְּפִיin the mouth כְסִילִיםfool

Like lame legs hanging limp is a proverb in the mouth of a fool.

26:8

כִּצְרוֹרlike binding אֶבֶןstone בְּמַרְגֵּמָהinto a sling כֵּןso נוֹתֵןgiving לִכְסִילto a fool כָּבוֹדhonor

Like binding a stone into a sling is the giving of honor to a fool.

26:9

חוֹחַfalls עָלָהfalls בְיַדinto the hand שִׁכּוֹרdrunkard וּמָשָׁלproverb בְּפִיin the mouth כְסִילִיםfool

Like a thorn that falls into the hand of a drunkard is a proverb in the mouth of a fool.

26:10

רַבarcher מְחוֹלֵלwounds כֹּלrandom וְשֹׂכֵרwho hires כְּסִילfool וְשֹׂכֵרand hire עֹבְרִיםpasserby

Like an archer who wounds at random is he who hires a fool or passerby.

26:11

כְּכֶלֶבas a dog שָׁבreturns עַלto קֵאוֹits vomit so כְּסִילfool שׁוֹנֶהrepeats בְאִוַּלְתּוֹhis folly

As a dog returns to its vomit, so a fool repeats his folly.

26:12

רָאִיתָsee אִישׁman חָכָםwise בְּעֵינָיוin his own eyes תִּקְוָהhope לִכְסִילfor a fool מִמֶּנּוּthan for him

Do you see a man who is wise in his own eyes? There is more hope for a fool than for him.

26:13

אָמַרsays עָצֵלslacker שַׁחַלlion בַּדָּרֶךְin the road אֲרִיlion בֵּיןbetween הָרְחֹבוֹתthe public square

The slacker says, “A lion is in the road! A fierce lion roams the public square!”

26:14

הַדֶּלֶתa door תִּסּוֹבturns עַלon צִירָהּits hinges וְעָצֵלso the slacker עַלon מִטָּתוֹturns his bed

As a door turns on its hinges, so the slacker turns on his bed.

26:15

טָמַןburies עָצֵלslacker יָדוֹhis hand בַּצַּלָּחַתin the dish נִלְאָהwearies לַהֲשִׁיבָהּto bring it אֶלto פִּיוhis mouth

The slacker buries his hand in the dish; it wearies him to bring it back to his mouth.

26:16

חָכָםwiser עָצֵלslacker בְּעֵינָיוin his own eyes מִשִּׁבְעָהthan seven מְשִׁיבֵיanswer טָעַםdiscreetly

The slacker is wiser in his own eyes than seven men who answer discreetly.

26:17

מַחֲזִיקgrabs בְּאָזְנֵיby the ears כָלֶבdog עֹבֵרpasserby מִתְעַבֵּרmeddles a עַלin רִיבquarrel לֹּאnot לוֹto him

Like one who grabs a dog by the ears is a passerby who meddles in a quarrel not his own.

26:18

כְּמִתְלַהְלֵהַּlike a madman הַיֹּרֶהshooting זִקִּיםfirebrands חִצִּיםarrows וָמָוֶתand deadly

Like a madman shooting firebrands and deadly arrows,

26:19

כֵּןso אִישׁman רִמָּהdeceives אֶת[obj] רֵעֵהוּhis neighbor וְאָמַרand says הֲלֹאnot מְשַׂחֵקjoking אָנִיI

so is the man who deceives his neighbor and says, “I was only joking!”

26:20

בְּאֶפֶסwithout עֵצִיםwood תִּכְבֶּהgoes אֵשׁfire וּבְאֵיןwithout נִרְגָּןgossip יִשְׁתֹּקceases מָדוֹןconflict

Without wood, a fire goes out; without gossip, a conflict ceases.

26:21

פֶּחָםcharcoal לְגֶחָלִיםfor embers וְעֵצִיםand wood לְאֵשׁfor fire וְאִישׁso is a man מִדְיָנִיםquarrelsome לְחַרְחַרfor kindling רִיבstrife

Like charcoal for embers and wood for fire, so is a quarrelsome man for kindling strife.

26:22

דִּבְרֵיwords נִרְגָּןgossip כְּמִתְלַהֲמִיםlike choice morsels וְהֵםand they יָרְדוּgo down חַדְרֵיinto the inmost בָטֶןinmost being

The words of a gossip are like choice morsels that go down into the inmost being.

26:23

כֶּסֶףglaze סִיגִיםglaze covering מְצֻפֶּהcovering עַלon חָרֶשׂvessel שְׂפָתַיִםlips דֹּלְקִיםburning וְלֶבheart רָעwicked

Like glaze covering an earthen vessel are burning lips and a wicked heart.

26:24

בִּשְׂפָתָיוwith his speech יִנָּכֵרhateful man שׂוֹנֵאdisguises וּבְקִרְבּוֹin his heart יָשִׁיתlays מִרְמָהdeceit

A hateful man disguises himself with his speech, but he lays up deceit in his heart.

26:25

כִּיwhen יְחַנֵּןgraciously קוֹלוֹspeaks אַלdo not תַּאֲמֶןbelieve בּוֹin him כִּיfor שֶׁבַעseven תּוֹעֵבוֹתabominations בְּלִבּוֹfill his heart

When he speaks graciously, do not believe him, for seven abominations fill his heart.

26:26

תִּכַּסֶּהthough שִׂנְאָהhatred בְּמַשָּׁאוֹןconcealed by deception תִּגָּלֶהexposed רָעָתוֹhis wickedness בְקָהָלin the assembly

Though his hatred is concealed by deception, his wickedness will be exposed in the assembly.

26:27

כֹּרֶהdigs שַּׁחַתpit בָּהּin her יִפֹּלfall וְגֹלֵלroll אֶבֶןstone אֵלָיוto him תָּשׁוּבback

He who digs a pit will fall into it, and he who rolls a stone will have it roll back on him.

26:28

לְשׁוֹןtongue שֶׁקֶרlying יִשְׂנָאhates דַכָּיוit crushes וּפֶהmouth חָלָקflattering causes יַעֲשֶׂהdo מִדְחֶהruin

A lying tongue hates those it crushes, and a flattering mouth causes ruin.
