Proverbs 23

At the table of the powerful; the evils of wine

Open in the interlinear reader

23:1

כִּיwhen תֵשֵׁבsit לִלְחוֹםto dine אֶתwith מוֹשֵׁלruler בִּיןconsider תָּבִיןcarefully אֶת[obj] אֲשֶׁרthat לְפָנֶיךָset before you

When you sit down to dine with a ruler, consider carefully what is set before you,

23:2

וְשַׂמְתָּand put שַׂכִּיןknife בְּלֹעֶךָto your throat אִםif בַּעַלhusband נֶפֶשׁappetite אָתָּהyou

and put a knife to your throat if you possess a great appetite.

23:3

אַלdo not תִּתְאָוcrave לְמַטְעַמּוֹתָיוhis delicacies for וְהוּאfor that לֶחֶםfood כְּזָבִיםdeceptive

Do not crave his delicacies, for that food is deceptive.

23:4

אַלdo not תִּיגַעwear לְהַעֲשִׁירto get rich מִבִּינָתְךָwise enough to restrain yourself חֲדָלstop

Do not wear yourself out to get rich; be wise enough to restrain yourself.

23:5

הֲתָעִיףyou glance עֵינֶיךָyour eyes בּוֹin him וְאֵינֶנּוּand no him כִּיfor עָשֹׂהbrought יַעֲשֶׂהmakes לּוֹto him כְנָפַיִםwings כְּנֶשֶׁרlike an eagle יָעוּףflies הַשָּׁמָיִםthe sky

When you glance at wealth, it disappears, for it makes wings for itself and flies like an eagle to the sky.

23:6

אַלdo not תִּלְחַםeat אֶת[obj] לֶחֶםbread רַעstingy עָיִןman do וְאַלand not תִּתְאָיוcrave לְמַטְעַמֹּתָיוhis delicacies

Do not eat the bread of a stingy man, and do not crave his delicacies;

23:7

כִּיfor כְּמוֹlike שָׁעַרtrack inwardly counting בְּנַפְשׁוֹthe cost כֶּןthis הוּאhe אֱכֹלeat וּשְׁתֵהand drink יֹאמַרsays לָךְto you וְלִבּוֹbut his heart is בַּלnot עִמָּךְwith you

for he is keeping track, inwardly counting the cost. “Eat and drink,” he says to you, but his heart is not with you.

23:8

פִּתְּךָlittle אָכַלְתָּeaten תְקִיאֶנָּהvomit וְשִׁחַתָּand waste דְּבָרֶיךָyour words הַנְּעִימִיםpleasant

You will vomit up what little you have eaten and waste your pleasant words.

23:9

בְּאָזְנֵיin hearing כְסִילfool אַלdo not תְּדַבֵּרspeak כִּיfor יָבוּזdespise לְשֵׂכֶלthe wisdom מִלֶּיךָyour words

Do not speak to a fool, for he will despise the wisdom of your words.

23:10

אַלdo not תַּסֵּגmove גְּבוּלboundary עוֹלָםancient וּבִשְׂדֵיor on the fields יְתוֹמִיםfatherless אַלdo not תָּבֹאenter

Do not move an ancient boundary stone or encroach on the fields of the fatherless,

23:11

כִּיfor גֹאֲלָםtheir Redeemer חָזָקstrong הוּאhe will יָרִיבtake אֶת[obj] רִיבָםtheir case אִתָּךְagainst you

for their Redeemer is strong; He will take up their case against you.

23:12

הָבִיאָהbring לַמּוּסָרto instruction לִבֶּךָapply your heart וְאָזְנֶךָand your ears לְאִמְרֵיto words דָעַתknowledge

Apply your heart to instruction and your ears to words of knowledge.

23:13

אַלdo not תִּמְנַעwithhold מִנַּעַרfrom a child מוּסָרdiscipline כִּיfor תַכֶּנּוּstrike him בַשֵּׁבֶטwith a rod לֹאnot יָמוּתdie

Do not withhold discipline from a child; although you strike him with a rod, he will not die.

23:14

אַתָּהyou בַּשֵּׁבֶטwith a rod תַּכֶּנּוּstrike him וְנַפְשׁוֹhis soul מִשְּׁאוֹלfrom Sheol תַּצִּילdeliver

Strike him with a rod, and you will deliver his soul from Sheol.

23:15

בְּנִיmy son אִםif חָכַםwise לִבֶּךָyour heart יִשְׂמַחrejoice לִבִּיmy own heart גַםindeed אָנִיI

My son, if your heart is wise, my own heart will indeed rejoice.

23:16

וְתַעְלֹזְנָהwill rejoice כִלְיוֹתָיmy inmost בְּדַבֵּרspeak שְׂפָתֶיךָyour lips מֵישָׁרִיםright

My inmost being will rejoice when your lips speak what is right.

23:17

אַלdo not יְקַנֵּאenvy לִבְּךָyour heart בַּחַטָּאִיםsinners כִּיbut אִםif בְּיִרְאַתin fear יְהוָהLORD כָּלalways הַיּוֹםthe day

Do not let your heart envy sinners, but always continue in the fear of the LORD.

23:18

כִּיfor אִםif יֵשׁthere אַחֲרִיתfuture וְתִקְוָתְךָand your hope לֹאnot תִכָּרֵתcut off

For surely there is a future, and your hope will not be cut off.

23:19

שְׁמַעlisten אַתָּהyou בְנִיmy son וַחֲכָםand be wise וְאַשֵּׁרand guide בַּדֶּרֶךְon the right course לִבֶּךָyour heart

Listen, my son, and be wise, and guide your heart on the right course.

23:20

אַלdo not תְּהִיjoin בְסֹבְאֵיdrink too much יָיִןwine בְּזֹלֲלֵיor gorge בָשָׂרmeat לָמוֹto them

Do not join those who drink too much wine or gorge themselves on meat.

23:21

כִּיfor סֹבֵאdrunkard וְזוֹלֵלand the glutton יִוָּרֵשׁpoverty וּקְרָעִיםand drowsiness תַּלְבִּישׁclothe נוּמָהrags

For the drunkard and the glutton will come to poverty, and drowsiness will clothe them in rags.

23:22

שְׁמַעlisten לְאָבִיךָto your father זֶהthis יְלָדֶךָgave you וְאַלand do not תָּבוּזdespise כִּיwhen זָקְנָהold אִמֶּךָyour mother

Listen to your father who gave you life, and do not despise your mother when she is old.

23:23

אֱמֶתtruth קְנֵהinvest וְאַלand do not תִּמְכֹּרsell חָכְמָהwisdom וּמוּסָרand instruction וּבִינָהand understanding

Invest in truth and never sell it— in wisdom and instruction and understanding.

23:24

גִּילgreatly יָגִילrejoice אֲבִיfather צַדִּיקrighteous וְיוֹלֵדson חָכָםwise יִשְׂמַחrejoice בּוֹin rejoice him

The father of a righteous man will greatly rejoice, and he who fathers a wise son will delight in him.

23:25

יִשְׂמַחmay אָבִיךָyour father וְאִמֶּךָand mother וְתָגֵלglad and יוֹלַדְתֶּךָgave you birth

May your father and mother be glad, and may she who gave you birth rejoice!

23:26

תְּנָהgive בְנִיson me לִבְּךָyour heart לִיto me וְעֵינֶיךָand let your eyes דְּרָכַיmy ways תִּצֹּרְנָהdelight

My son, give me your heart, and let your eyes delight in my ways.

23:27

כִּיfor שׁוּחָהpit עֲמֻקָּהdeep זוֹנָהprostitute וּבְאֵרwell צָרָהnarrow נָכְרִיָּהadulteress

For a prostitute is a deep pit, and an adulteress is a narrow well.

23:28

אַףhow much הִיאa כְּחֶתֶףlike robber תֶּאֱרֹבwait וּבוֹגְדִיםfaithless בְּאָדָםamong men תּוֹסִףagain

Like a robber she lies in wait and multiplies the faithless among men.

23:29

לְמִיwho אוֹיwoe לְמִיwho אֲבוֹיsorrow לְמִיto who מִדְיָנִיםcontentions לְמִיwho שִׂיחַcomplaints לְמִיwho פְּצָעִיםwounds חִנָּםneedless לְמִיwho חַכְלִלוּתbloodshot עֵינָיִםeyes

Who has woe? Who has sorrow? Who has contentions? Who has complaints? Who has needless wounds? Who has bloodshot eyes?

23:30

לַמְאַחֲרִיםthose who linger עַלover הַיָּיִןwine לַבָּאִיםwho go לַחְקֹרto taste מִמְסָךְmixed drinks

Those who linger over wine, who go to taste mixed drinks.

23:31

אַלdo not תֵּרֶאgaze יַיִןwine כִּיfor יִתְאַדָּםred כִּיwhen יִתֵּןgive בַּכּוֹסin the cup עֵינוֹhis eyes יִתְהַלֵּךְgoes בְּמֵישָׁרִיםdown smoothly

Do not gaze at wine while it is red, when it sparkles in the cup and goes down smoothly.

23:32

אַחֲרִיתוֹend it כְּנָחָשׁlike a snake יִשָּׁךְbites וּכְצִפְעֹנִיand like a viper יַפְרִשׁstings

In the end it bites like a snake and stings like a viper.

23:33

עֵינֶיךָyour eyes יִרְאוּsee זָרוֹתstrange וְלִבְּךָand your mind יְדַבֵּרutter תַּהְפֻּכוֹתperversities

Your eyes will see strange things, and your mind will utter perversities.

23:34

וְהָיִיתָwill be כְּשֹׁכֵבlike one sleeping בְּלֶבon the high יָםseas וּכְשֹׁכֵבor lying בְּרֹאשׁon the top חִבֵּלmast

You will be like one sleeping on the high seas or lying on the top of a mast:

23:35

הִכּוּנִיstruck me בַלno חָלִיתִיpain הֲלָמוּנִיbeat me בַּלnot יָדָעְתִּיknow מָתַיwhen אָקִיץwake אוֹסִיףagain אֲבַקְשֶׁנּוּsearch for another עוֹדlonger

“They struck me, but I feel no pain! They beat me, but I did not know it! When can I wake up to search for another drink?”
