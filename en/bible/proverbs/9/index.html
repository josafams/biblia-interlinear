Proverbs 9

Two banquets: Wisdom and Folly

Open in the interlinear reader

9:1

חָכְמוֹתwisdom בָּנְתָהbuilt בֵיתָהּher house she חָצְבָהcarved עַמּוּדֶיהָher pillars שִׁבְעָהseven

Wisdom has built her house; she has carved out her seven pillars.

9:2

טָבְחָהprepared טִבְחָהּher meat מָסְכָהmixed יֵינָהּher wine she אַףalso עָרְכָהset שֻׁלְחָנָהּher table

She has prepared her meat and mixed her wine; she has also set her table.

9:3

שָׁלְחָהsent נַעֲרֹתֶיהָher maidservants she תִקְרָאshe calls עַלon גַּפֵּיalone מְרֹמֵיheights קָרֶתcity

She has sent out her maidservants; she calls out from the heights of the city.

9:4

מִיwhoever פֶתִיsimple יָסֻרturn הֵנָּהhere חֲסַרlacks לֵבjudgment אָמְרָהsays לּוֹto him

“Whoever is simple, let him turn in here!” she says to him who lacks judgment.

9:5

לְכוּcome לַחֲמוּeat בְלַחֲמִיmy bread וּשְׁתוּand drink בְּיַיִןthe wine מָסָכְתִּיmixed

“Come, eat my bread and drink the wine I have mixed.

9:6

עִזְבוּleave פְתָאיִםfolly וִחְיוּwill live וְאִשְׁרוּwalk בְּדֶרֶךְin the way בִּינָהunderstanding

Leave your folly behind, and you will live; walk in the way of understanding.”

9:7

יֹסֵרcorrects לֵץmocker לֹקֵחַtake לוֹto him קָלוֹןshame וּמוֹכִיחַhe who rebukes לְרָשָׁעa wicked מוּמוֹtaints himself

He who corrects a mocker brings shame on himself; he who rebukes a wicked man taints himself.

9:8

אַלdo not תּוֹכַחrebuke לֵץmocker פֶּןor יִשְׂנָאֶךָּhate you הוֹכַחrebuke לְחָכָםa wise וְיֶאֱהָבֶךָּlove you

Do not rebuke a mocker, or he will hate you; rebuke a wise man, and he will love you.

9:9

תֵּןinstruct לְחָכָםa wise וְיֶחְכַּםwiser עוֹדstill הוֹדַעteach לְצַדִּיקa righteous וְיוֹסֶףincrease לֶקַחlearning

Instruct a wise man, and he will be wiser still; teach a righteous man, and he will increase his learning.

9:10

תְּחִלַּתbeginning חָכְמָהwisdom יִרְאַתfear יְהוָהLORD וְדַעַתand knowledge קְדֹשִׁיםholy בִּינָהunderstanding

The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom, and knowledge of the Holy One is understanding.

9:11

כִּיfor בִיin me יִרְבּוּmultiplied יָמֶיךָyour days וְיוֹסִיפוּadded לְּךָto your שְׁנוֹתyears חַיִּיםlife

For through wisdom your days will be multiplied, and years will be added to your life.

9:12

אִםif חָכַמְתָּwise חָכַמְתָּwise לָּךְto וְלַצְתָּscoff לְבַדְּךָyou alone will תִשָּׂאbear

If you are wise, you are wise to your own advantage; but if you scoff, you alone will bear the consequences.

9:13

אֵשֶׁתwoman כְּסִילוּתnamed folly הֹמִיָּהloud פְּתַיּוּתshe is naive וּבַלand no יָדְעָהknows מָּהwhat

The woman named Folly is loud; she is naive and knows nothing.

9:14

וְיָשְׁבָהshe sits לְפֶתַחat the door בֵּיתָהּher house עַלon כִּסֵּאseat מְרֹמֵיheights קָרֶתcity

She sits at the door of her house, on a seat in the heights of the city,

9:15

לִקְרֹאcalling לְעֹבְרֵיpass דָרֶךְway הַמְיַשְּׁרִיםwho make אֹרְחוֹתָםtheir paths

calling out to those who pass by, who make their paths straight.

9:16

מִיwhoever פֶתִיsimple יָסֻרturn הֵנָּהhere וַחֲסַרlacks לֵבjudgment וְאָמְרָהsays לּוֹto him

“Whoever is simple, let him turn in here!” she says to him who lacks judgment.

9:17

מַיִםwater גְּנוּבִיםstolen יִמְתָּקוּsweet וְלֶחֶםand bread סְתָרִיםsecret יִנְעָםtasty

“Stolen water is sweet, and bread eaten in secret is tasty!”

9:18

וְלֹאnot יָדַעknow כִּיthat רְפָאִיםdead שָׁםthere בְּעִמְקֵיin the depths שְׁאוֹלSheol קְרֻאֶיהָher guests

But they do not know that the dead are there, that her guests are in the depths of Sheol.
