Proverbs 11

Honest scales, generosity and integrity

Open in the interlinear reader

11:1

מֹאזְנֵיdishonest מִרְמָהscales תּוֹעֲבַתabomination יְהוָהLORD וְאֶבֶןweight שְׁלֵמָהaccurate רְצוֹנוֹhis delight

Dishonest scales are an abomination to the LORD, but an accurate weight is His delight.

11:2

בָּאwhen זָדוֹןpride וַיָּבֹאcomes קָלוֹןdisgrace וְאֶתbut with צְנוּעִיםhumility חָכְמָהwisdom

When pride comes, disgrace follows, but with humility comes wisdom.

11:3

תֻּמַּתintegrity יְשָׁרִיםupright תַּנְחֵםguides them וְסֶלֶףbut the perversity בּוֹגְדִיםfaithless יְשָׁדֵּםdestroys them

The integrity of the upright guides them, but the perversity of the faithless destroys them.

11:4

לֹאnot יוֹעִילworthless הוֹןriches בְּיוֹםin the day עֶבְרָהwrath וּצְדָקָהbut righteousness תַּצִּילdeliverance מִמָּוֶתfrom death

Riches are worthless in the day of wrath, but righteousness brings deliverance from death.

11:5

צִדְקַתrighteousness תָּמִיםblameless תְּיַשֵּׁרdirects דַּרְכּוֹtheir path וּבְרִשְׁעָתוֹtheir own wickedness יִפֹּלfall רָשָׁעwicked

The righteousness of the blameless directs their path, but the wicked fall by their own wickedness.

11:6

צִדְקַתrighteousness יְשָׁרִיםupright תַּצִּילֵםdelivers them וּבְהַוַּתown desires בֹּגְדִיםfaithless יִלָּכֵדוּtrapped

The righteousness of the upright delivers them, but the faithless are trapped by their own desires.

11:7

בְּמוֹתdies אָדָםman רָשָׁעwicked תֹּאבַדperishes תִּקְוָהhope וְתוֹחֶלֶתand the hope אוֹנִיםhis אָבָדָהstrength

When the wicked man dies, his hope perishes, and the hope of his strength vanishes.

11:8

צַדִּיקrighteous מִצָּרָהfrom trouble נֶחֱלָץdelivered וַיָּבֹאand came רָשָׁעwicked תַּחְתָּיוhis place

The righteous man is delivered from trouble; in his place the wicked man goes in.

11:9

בְּפֶהwith his mouth חָנֵףungodly יַשְׁחִתdestroys רֵעֵהוּhis neighbor וּבְדַעַתbut through knowledge צַדִּיקִיםrighteous יֵחָלֵצוּrescued

With his mouth the ungodly man destroys his neighbor, but through knowledge the righteous are rescued.

11:10

בְּטוּבthrive צַדִּיקִיםrighteous תַּעֲלֹץrejoices קִרְיָהcity וּבַאֲבֹדperish רְשָׁעִיםwicked רִנָּהshouts

When the righteous thrive, the city rejoices, and when the wicked perish, there are shouts of joy.

11:11

בְּבִרְכַּתby the blessing יְשָׁרִיםupright תָּרוּםexalted קָרֶתcity וּבְפִיbut by the mouth רְשָׁעִיםwicked תֵּהָרֵסtorn down

By the blessing of the upright a city is built up, but by the mouth of the wicked it is torn down.

11:12

בָּזshows contempt לְרֵעֵהוּfor his neighbor חֲסַרlacks לֵבjudgment וְאִישׁbut a man תְּבוּנוֹתunderstanding יַחֲרִישׁsilent

Whoever shows contempt for his neighbor lacks judgment, but a man of understanding remains silent.

11:13

הוֹלֵךְwalks רָכִילgossip מְגַלֶּהreveals סּוֹדsecret וְנֶאֱמַןbut trustworthy רוּחַa person מְכַסֶּהkeeps דָבָרconfidence

A gossip reveals a secret, but a trustworthy person keeps a confidence.

11:14

בְּאֵיןin no תַּחְבֻּלוֹתguidance יִפָּלfalls עָםnation וּתְשׁוּעָהdeliverance בְּרֹבwith many יוֹעֵץcounselors

For lack of guidance, a nation falls, but with many counselors comes deliverance.

11:15

רַעevil יֵרוֹעַputs כִּיfor עָרַבsecurity זָרstranger וְשֹׂנֵאhates תֹקְעִיםindebtedness בּוֹטֵחַsecure

He who puts up security for a stranger will surely suffer, but the one who hates indebtedness is secure.

11:16

אֵשֶׁתa woman חֵןgracious תִּתְמֹךְattains כָּבוֹדhonor וְעָרִיצִיםbut ruthless יִתְמְכוּgain עֹשֶׁרwealth

A gracious woman attains honor, but ruthless men gain only wealth.

11:17

גֹּמֵלkind נַפְשׁוֹhimself אִישׁman חָסֶדbenefits וְעֹכֵרtrouble שְׁאֵרוֹhis close relative אַכְזָרִיcruel

A kind man benefits himself, but a cruel man brings trouble on himself.

11:18

רָשָׁעwicked עֹשֶׂהdoing פְעֻלַּתearns empty שָׁקֶרempty wage וְזֹרֵעַsows צְדָקָהrighteousness שֶׂכֶרreaps אֱמֶתtrue

The wicked man earns an empty wage, but he who sows righteousness reaps a true reward.

11:19

כֵּןso צְדָקָהrighteousness לְחַיִּיםto life וּמְרַדֵּףbut the pursuit רָעָהevil לְמוֹתוֹdeath

Genuine righteousness leads to life, but the pursuit of evil brings death.

11:20

תּוֹעֲבַתabomination יְהוָהLORD עִקְּשֵׁיperverse לֵבheart וּרְצוֹנוֹhis delight תְּמִימֵיblameless דָרֶךְwalk

The perverse in heart are an abomination to the LORD, but the blameless in their walk are His delight.

11:21

יָדassured לְיָדto hand לֹאnot יִנָּקֶהgo unpunished רָּעwicked וְזֶרַעbut the offspring צַדִּיקִיםrighteous נִמְלָטescape

Be assured that the wicked will not go unpunished, but the offspring of the righteous will escape.

11:22

נֶזֶםring זָהָבgold בְּאַףsnout חֲזִירpig’s אִשָּׁהwoman יָפָהbeautiful וְסָרַתwho lacks טָעַםdiscretion

Like a gold ring in a pig’s snout is a beautiful woman who lacks discretion.

11:23

תַּאֲוַתdesire צַדִּיקִיםrighteous אַךְonly טוֹבgood תִּקְוַתhope רְשָׁעִיםwicked עֶבְרָהwrath

The desire of the righteous leads only to good, but the hope of the wicked brings wrath.

11:24

יֵשׁthere מְפַזֵּרgains וְנוֹסָףyet even more עוֹדlonger וְחוֹשֵׂךְwithholds מִיֹּשֶׁרis right אַךְonly לְמַחְסוֹרto become poor

One gives freely, yet gains even more; another withholds what is right, only to become poor.

11:25

נֶפֶשׁsoul בְּרָכָהgenerous תְדֻשָּׁןprosper וּמַרְוֶהrefreshes others גַּםwill הוּאhe יוֹרֶאrefreshed

A generous soul will prosper, and he who refreshes others will himself be refreshed.

11:26

מֹנֵעַhoarder בָּרgrain יִקְּבֻהוּcurse לְאוֹםpeople וּבְרָכָהbut blessing לְרֹאשׁwill crown the מַשְׁבִּירsells

The people will curse the hoarder of grain, but blessing will crown the one who sells it.

11:27

שֹׁחֵרsearches טוֹבgood יְבַקֵּשׁfinds רָצוֹןfavor וְדֹרֵשׁseeks רָעָהevil תְבוֹאֶנּוּcome to him

He who searches out good finds favor, but evil will come to him who seeks it.

11:28

בּוֹטֵחַtrusts בְּעָשְׁרוֹin his riches הוּאwill יִפֹּלfall וְכֶעָלֶהthrive like צַדִּיקִיםrighteous יִפְרָחוּblossom

He who trusts in his riches will fall, but the righteous will thrive like foliage.

11:29

עוֹכֵרtrouble בֵּיתוֹhis house יִנְחַלinherit רוּחַwind וְעֶבֶדservant אֱוִילfool לַחֲכַםto the wise לֵבheart

He who brings trouble on his house will inherit the wind, and the fool will be servant to the wise of heart.

11:30

פְּרִיfruit צַדִּיקrighteous עֵץtree חַיִּיםlife וְלֹקֵחַwins נְפָשׂוֹתsouls חָכָםwise

The fruit of the righteous is a tree of life, and he who wins souls is wise.

11:31

הֵןif צַדִּיקrighteous בָּאָרֶץon earth יְשֻׁלָּםfulfilled אַףhow כִּיfor רָשָׁעungodly וְחוֹטֵאand the sinner

If the righteous receive their due on earth, how much more the ungodly and the sinner!
