Proverbs 4

A father teaches to guard the heart

Open in the interlinear reader

4:1

שִׁמְעוּlisten בָנִיםsons מוּסַרinstruction אָבfather’s וְהַקְשִׁיבוּpay attention and לָדַעַתand gain בִּינָהunderstanding

Listen, my sons, to a father’s instruction; pay attention and gain understanding.

4:2

כִּיfor לֶקַחsound טוֹבgood נָתַתִּיgive לָכֶםto you תּוֹרָתִיteaching אַלdo not תַּעֲזֹבוּabandon

For I give you sound teaching; do not abandon my directive.

4:3

כִּיwhen בֵןson הָיִיתִיwas לְאָבִיto my father רַךְtender וְיָחִידand the only child לִפְנֵיto before אִמִּיmy mother

When I was a son to my father, tender and the only child of my mother,

4:4

וַיֹּרֵנִיhe taught me וַיֹּאמֶרand said לִיto me יִתְמָךְlay דְּבָרַיmy words לִבֶּךָyour heart שְׁמֹרkeep מִצְוֺתַיmy commands וֶחְיֵהlive

he taught me and said, “Let your heart lay hold of my words; keep my commands and you will live.

4:5

קְנֵהget חָכְמָהwisdom קְנֵהget בִינָהunderstanding אַלdo not תִּשְׁכַּחforget וְאַלor תֵּטturn מֵאִמְרֵיwords פִיmy or

Get wisdom, get understanding; do not forget my words or turn from them.

4:6

אַלdo not תַּעַזְבֶהָforsake wisdom she וְתִשְׁמְרֶךָּpreserve you אֱהָבֶהָlove her וְתִצְּרֶךָּguard you

Do not forsake wisdom, and she will preserve you; love her, and she will guard you.

4:7

רֵאשִׁיתsupreme חָכְמָהwisdom קְנֵהacquire חָכְמָהwisdom וּבְכָלand whatever קִנְיָנְךָyour possessions קְנֵהacquire בִינָהunderstanding

Wisdom is supreme; so acquire wisdom. And whatever you may acquire, gain understanding.

4:8

סַלְסְלֶהָprize her וּתְרוֹמְמֶךָּexalt you תְּכַבֵּדְךָhonor you כִּיif תְחַבְּקֶנָּהembrace her

Prize her, and she will exalt you; if you embrace her, she will honor you.

4:9

תִּתֵּןset לְרֹאשְׁךָyour head לִוְיַתgarland חֵןgrace עֲטֶרֶתcrown תִּפְאֶרֶתbeauty תְּמַגְּנֶךָּpresent you

She will set a garland of grace on your head; she will present you with a crown of beauty.”

4:10

שְׁמַעlisten בְּנִיmy son וְקַחand receive אֲמָרָיmy words וְיִרְבּוּmany לְךָto you שְׁנוֹתyears חַיִּיםlife

Listen, my son, and receive my words, and the years of your life will be many.

4:11

בְּדֶרֶךְin the way חָכְמָהwisdom הֹרֵתִיךָlead הִדְרַכְתִּיךָguide בְּמַעְגְּלֵיon paths יֹשֶׁרstraight

I will guide you in the way of wisdom; I will lead you on straight paths.

4:12

בְּלֶכְתְּךָwhen you walk your לֹאnot יֵצַרimpeded צַעֲדֶךָsteps וְאִםand if תָּרוּץrun לֹאnot תִכָּשֵׁלstumble

When you walk, your steps will not be impeded; when you run, you will not stumble.

4:13

הַחֲזֵקhold בַּמּוּסָרon to instruction אַלdo not תֶּרֶףlet נִצְּרֶהָguard it כִּיfor הִיאis חַיֶּיךָyour life

Hold on to instruction; do not let go. Guard it, for it is your life.

4:14

בְּאֹרַחon the path רְשָׁעִיםwicked אַלdo not תָּבֹאenter וְאַלor תְּאַשֵּׁרwalk בְּדֶרֶךְin the way רָעִיםevildoers

Do not set foot on the path of the wicked or walk in the way of evildoers.

4:15

פְּרָעֵהוּavoid it אַלdo not תַּעֲבָרtravel בּוֹon it שְׂטֵהturn מֵעָלָיוfrom the him וַעֲבוֹרand pass

Avoid it; do not travel on it. Turn from it and pass on by.

4:16

כִּיfor לֹאnot יִשְׁנוּsleep אִםif לֹאnot יָרֵעוּevil וְנִגְזְלָהthey are deprived שְׁנָתָםslumber until they אִםif לֹאnot יַכְשִׁילוּfall

For they cannot sleep unless they do evil; they are deprived of slumber until they make someone fall.

4:17

כִּיfor לָחֲמוּeat לֶחֶםbread רֶשַׁעwickedness וְיֵיןwine חֲמָסִיםviolence יִשְׁתּוּdrink

For they eat the bread of wickedness and drink the wine of violence.

4:18

וְאֹרַחpath צַדִּיקִיםrighteous כְּאוֹרdawn נֹגַהּshining הוֹלֵךְwalks וָאוֹרbrighter and brighter עַדuntil נְכוֹןmidday הַיּוֹםthe day

The path of the righteous is like the first gleam of dawn, shining brighter and brighter until midday.

4:19

דֶּרֶךְway רְשָׁעִיםwicked כָּאֲפֵלָהlike the darkest gloom לֹאnot יָדְעוּknow בַּמֶּהwhat יִכָּשֵׁלוּstumble

But the way of the wicked is like the darkest gloom; they do not know what makes them stumble.

4:20

בְּנִיmy son לִדְבָרַיmy sayings הַקְשִׁיבָהattention לַאֲמָרַיto my words הַטincline אָזְנֶךָyour ear

My son, pay attention to my words; incline your ear to my sayings.

4:21

אַלdo not יַלִּיזוּlose מֵעֵינֶיךָsight שָׁמְרֵםkeep them בְּתוֹךְwithin לְבָבֶךָyour heart

Do not lose sight of them; keep them within your heart.

4:22

כִּיfor חַיִּיםlife הֵםthey לְמֹצְאֵיהֶםfind them וּלְכָלand to the whole בְּשָׂרוֹbody מַרְפֵּאhealth

For they are life to those who find them, and health to the whole body.

4:23

מִכָּלwith all מִשְׁמָרdiligence נְצֹרguard לִבֶּךָyour heart כִּיfor מִמֶּנּוּfrom it תּוֹצְאוֹתsprings חַיִּיםlife

Guard your heart with all diligence, for from it flow springs of life.

4:24

הָסֵרaway deception מִמְּךָfrom your עִקְּשׁוּתperverse פֶּהspeech וּלְזוּתlips שְׂפָתַיִםlips הַרְחֵקfar מִמֶּךָּyour from

Put away deception from your mouth; keep your lips from perverse speech.

4:25

עֵינֶיךָyour eyes לְנֹכַחto opposite יַבִּיטוּlook וְעַפְעַפֶּיךָforward fix your יַיְשִׁרוּstraight נֶגְדֶּךָbefore you

Let your eyes look forward; fix your gaze straight ahead.

4:26

פַּלֵּסlevel מַעְגַּלpath רַגְלֶךָyour feet וְכָלand all דְּרָכֶיךָyour ways יִכֹּנוּsure

Make a level path for your feet, and all your ways will be sure.

4:27

אַלdo not תֵּטswerve יָמִיןright וּשְׂמֹאולleft הָסֵרturn רַגְלְךָyour feet מֵרָעfrom evil

Do not swerve to the right or to the left; turn your feet away from evil.
