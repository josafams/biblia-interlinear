Proverbs 16

Man plans, the LORD directs

Open in the interlinear reader

16:1

לְאָדָםbelong to man מַעַרְכֵיplans לֵבheart וּמֵיְהוָהfrom the LORD מַעֲנֵהreply לָשׁוֹןtongue

The plans of the heart belong to man, but the reply of the tongue is from the LORD.

16:2

כָּלall דַּרְכֵיways אִישׁman’s זַךְpure בְּעֵינָיוin his own eyes his וְתֹכֵןweighed רוּחוֹתwinds יְהוָהLORD

All a man’s ways are pure in his own eyes, but his motives are weighed out by the LORD.

16:3

גֹּלcommit אֶלto יְהוָהLORD מַעֲשֶׂיךָyour works וְיִכֹּנוּand prepare מַחְשְׁבֹתֶיךָyour plans

Commit your works to the LORD and your plans will be achieved.

16:4

כֹּלeverything פָּעַלthings יְהוָהLORD לַמַּעֲנֵהוּfor his purpose וְגַםeven רָשָׁעwicked לְיוֹםfor the day רָעָהdisaster

The LORD has made everything for His purpose— even the wicked for the day of disaster.

16:5

תּוֹעֲבַתdetestable יְהוָהLORD כָּלeveryone גְּבַהּproud לֵבheart יָדhand לְיָדassured לֹאnot יִנָּקֶהgo unpunished

Everyone who is proud in heart is detestable to the LORD; be assured that he will not go unpunished.

16:6

בְּחֶסֶדby loving devotion וֶאֱמֶתand faithfulness יְכֻפַּרatoned עָוֺןiniquity וּבְיִרְאַתand by the fear יְהוָהLORD סוּרturns aside מֵרָעfrom evil

By loving devotion and faithfulness iniquity is atoned for, and by the fear of the LORD one turns aside from evil.

16:7

בִּרְצוֹתplease יְהוָהLORD דַּרְכֵיways אִישׁman’s גַּםeven אוֹיְבָיוenemies live יַשְׁלִםpeace אִתּוֹwith him

When a man’s ways please the LORD, He makes even the man’s enemies live at peace with him.

16:8

טוֹבbetter מְעַטlittle בִּצְדָקָהwith righteousness מֵרֹבthan great תְּבוּאוֹתgain בְּלֹאin without מִשְׁפָּטinjustice

Better a little with righteousness than great gain with injustice.

16:9

לֵבheart אָדָםman’s יְחַשֵּׁבplans דַּרְכּוֹhis course וַיהוָהbut the LORD יָכִיןdetermines צַעֲדוֹhis steps

A man’s heart plans his course, but the LORD determines his steps.

16:10

קֶסֶםverdict עַלon שִׂפְתֵיlips מֶלֶךְking בְּמִשְׁפָּטjustice לֹאnot יִמְעַלbetray פִּיוhis mouth

A divine verdict is on the lips of a king; his mouth must not betray justice.

16:11

פֶּלֶסhonest וּמֹאזְנֵיscales and מִשְׁפָּטbalances לַיהוָהfrom the LORD מַעֲשֵׂהוּhis work כָּלall אַבְנֵיweights כִיסbag

Honest scales and balances are from the LORD; all the weights in the bag are His concern.

16:12

תּוֹעֲבַתdetestable מְלָכִיםkings עֲשׂוֹתbehavior רֶשַׁעwicked כִּיfor בִצְדָקָהthrough righteousness יִכּוֹןestablished כִּסֵּאthrone

Wicked behavior is detestable to kings, for a throne is established through righteousness.

16:13

רְצוֹןdelight מְלָכִיםking’s שִׂפְתֵיlips צֶדֶקrighteous וְדֹבֵרspeaks יְשָׁרִיםhonestly יֶאֱהָבbeloved

Righteous lips are a king’s delight, and he who speaks honestly is beloved.

16:14

חֲמַתwrath מֶלֶךְking מַלְאֲכֵיmessenger מָוֶתdeath וְאִישׁman חָכָםwise יְכַפְּרֶנָּהpacify it

The wrath of a king is a messenger of death, but a wise man will pacify it.

16:15

בְּאוֹרbrightens פְּנֵיface מֶלֶךְking’s חַיִּיםlife וּרְצוֹנוֹhis favor is כְּעָבcloud מַלְקוֹשׁspring

When a king’s face brightens, there is life; his favor is like a rain cloud in spring.

16:16

קְנֹהacquire חָכְמָהwisdom מַהhow טּוֹבmuch better מֵחָרוּץthan gold וּקְנוֹתto gain בִּינָהunderstanding נִבְחָרdesirable מִכָּסֶףthan silver

How much better to acquire wisdom than gold! To gain understanding is more desirable than silver.

16:17

מְסִלַּתhighway יְשָׁרִיםupright סוּרaway מֵרָעfrom evil שֹׁמֵרprotects נַפְשׁוֹhis life נֹצֵרguards דַּרְכּוֹway

The highway of the upright leads away from evil; he who guards his way protects his life.

16:18

לִפְנֵיgoes before שֶׁבֶרdestruction גָּאוֹןpride וְלִפְנֵיbefore כִשָּׁלוֹןhaughty גֹּבַהּhaughty רוּחַspirit

Pride goes before destruction, and a haughty spirit before a fall.

16:19

טוֹבbetter שְׁפַלlowly רוּחַspirit אֶתwith עֲנָוִיםhumble מֵחַלֵּקthan to divide שָׁלָלspoil אֶתwith גֵּאִיםproud

It is better to be lowly in spirit among the humble than to divide the spoil with the proud.

16:20

מַשְׂכִּילwhoever עַלon דָּבָרinstruction יִמְצָאfind טוֹבsuccess וּבוֹטֵחַtrusts בַּיהוָהin the LORD אַשְׁרָיוblessed is he

Whoever heeds instruction will find success, and blessed is he who trusts in the LORD.

16:21

לַחֲכַםthe wise לֵבheart יִקָּרֵאcalled נָבוֹןdiscerning וּמֶתֶקand pleasant שְׂפָתַיִםspeech יֹסִיףpromotes לֶקַחinstruction

The wise in heart are called discerning, and pleasant speech promotes instruction.

16:22

מְקוֹרfountain חַיִּיםlife שֵׂכֶלunderstanding בְּעָלָיוits possessor וּמוּסַרbut the discipline אֱוִלִיםfools אִוֶּלֶתfolly

Understanding is a fountain of life to its possessor, but the discipline of fools is folly.

16:23

לֵבheart חָכָםwise יַשְׂכִּילinstructs פִּיהוּhis mouth וְעַלto שְׂפָתָיוhis lips יֹסִיףadds לֶקַחpersuasiveness

The heart of the wise man instructs his mouth and adds persuasiveness to his lips.

16:24

צוּףare דְּבַשׁhoneycomb אִמְרֵיwords נֹעַםpleasant מָתוֹקsweet לַנֶּפֶשׁto the soul וּמַרְפֵּאand healing לָעָצֶםto the bones

Pleasant words are a honeycomb, sweet to the soul and healing to the bones.

16:25

יֵשׁthere דֶּרֶךְway יָשָׁרright לִפְנֵיseems to אִישׁman וְאַחֲרִיתָהּbut its end דַּרְכֵיway מָוֶתdeath

There is a way that seems right to a man, but its end is the way of death.

16:26

נֶפֶשׁappetite עָמֵלworker’s עָמְלָהworks לּוֹfor him כִּיbecause אָכַףhunger עָלָיוon him פִּיהוּdrives him onward

A worker’s appetite works for him because his hunger drives him onward.

16:27

אִישׁa man בְּלִיַּעַלworthless כֹּרֶהdigs רָעָהevil וְעַלand on שְׂפָתוֹhis speech is כְּאֵשׁfire צָרָבֶתscorching

A worthless man digs up evil, and his speech is like a scorching fire.

16:28

אִישׁa man תַּהְפֻּכוֹתperverse יְשַׁלַּחspreads מָדוֹןdissension וְנִרְגָּןand a gossip מַפְרִידdivides אַלּוּףclose friends

A perverse man spreads dissension, and a gossip divides close friends.

16:29

אִישׁman חָמָסviolent יְפַתֶּהentices רֵעֵהוּhis neighbor וְהוֹלִיכוֹand leads him בְּדֶרֶךְa path לֹאnot טוֹבgood

A violent man entices his neighbor and leads him down a path that is not good.

16:30

עֹצֶהwinks עֵינָיוhis eye לַחְשֹׁבdevises תַּהְפֻּכוֹתperversity קֹרֵץpurses שְׂפָתָיוhis lips is כִּלָּהbent רָעָהevil

He who winks his eye devises perversity; he who purses his lips is bent on evil.

16:31

עֲטֶרֶתcrown תִּפְאֶרֶתglory שֵׂיבָהgray hair בְּדֶרֶךְalong the path צְדָקָהrighteousness תִּמָּצֵאfound

Gray hair is a crown of glory; it is attained along the path of righteousness.

16:32

טוֹבbetter אֶרֶךְslow אַפַּיִםanger מִגִּבּוֹרthan a warrior וּמֹשֵׁלcontrols בְּרוּחוֹhis temper מִלֹּכֵדcaptures עִירcity

He who is slow to anger is better than a warrior, and he who controls his temper is greater than one who captures a city.

16:33

בַּחֵיקinto the lap יוּטַלcast אֶת[obj] הַגּוֹרָלthe lot וּמֵיְהוָהfrom the LORD כָּלevery מִשְׁפָּטוֹits decision

The lot is cast into the lap, but its every decision is from the LORD.
