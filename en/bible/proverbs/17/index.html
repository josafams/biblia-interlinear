Proverbs 17

Peace at home, friendship and silence

Open in the interlinear reader

17:1

טוֹבbetter פַּתmorsel חֲרֵבָהdry וְשַׁלְוָהquietness בָהּin מִבַּיִתthan house מָלֵאfull זִבְחֵיfeasting רִיבstrife

Better a dry morsel in quietness than a house full of feasting with strife.

17:2

עֶבֶדservant מַשְׂכִּילwise יִמְשֹׁלrule בְּבֵןson מֵבִישׁdisgraceful וּבְתוֹךְand in among אַחִיםbrothers יַחֲלֹקshare נַחֲלָהinheritance

A wise servant will rule over a disgraceful son and share his inheritance as one of the brothers.

17:3

מַצְרֵףcrucible לַכֶּסֶףfor silver וְכוּרand a furnace לַזָּהָבfor gold וּבֹחֵןtester לִבּוֹתhearts יְהוָהLORD

A crucible for silver and a furnace for gold, but the LORD is the tester of hearts.

17:4

מֵרַעwicked מַקְשִׁיבlistens עַלto שְׂפַתlips אָוֶןevil שֶׁקֶרliar מֵזִיןear עַלto לְשׁוֹןtongue הַוֺּתdestructive

A wicked man listens to evil lips; a liar gives ear to a destructive tongue.

17:5

לֹעֵגmocks לָרָשׁthe poor חֵרֵףinsults עֹשֵׂהוּMaker שָׂמֵחַwhoever gloats לְאֵידover calamity לֹאnot יִנָּקֶהgo unpunished

He who mocks the poor insults their Maker; whoever gloats over calamity will not go unpunished.

17:6

עֲטֶרֶתcrown זְקֵנִיםaged בְּנֵיgrandchildren בָנִיםsons וְתִפְאֶרֶתand the glory בָּנִיםson אֲבוֹתָםfather

Grandchildren are the crown of the aged, and the glory of a son is his father.

17:7

לֹאnot נָאוָהunfit לְנָבָלfor a fool שְׂפַתbank יֶתֶרstring אַףhow כִּיfor לְנָדִיבto a ruler שְׂפַתlips שָׁקֶרlying

Eloquent words are unfit for a fool; how much worse are lying lips to a ruler!

17:8

אֶבֶןstone חֵןcharm הַשֹּׁחַדbribe is a בְּעֵינֵיin sight בְעָלָיוits giver אֶלto כָּלwherever אֲשֶׁרthat יִפְנֶהturns יַשְׂכִּילsucceeds

A bribe is a charm to its giver; wherever he turns, he succeeds.

17:9

מְכַסֶּהconceals פֶּשַׁעoffense מְבַקֵּשׁpromotes אַהֲבָהlove וְשֹׁנֶהbrings בְדָבָרin word מַפְרִידseparates אַלּוּףfriends

Whoever conceals an offense promotes love, but he who brings it up separates friends.

17:10

תֵּחַתcuts גְּעָרָהrebuke בְמֵבִיןdiscernment מֵהַכּוֹתlashes כְּסִילfool מֵאָהhundred

A rebuke cuts into a man of discernment deeper than a hundred lashes cut into a fool.

17:11

אַךְonly מְרִיrebellion יְבַקֶּשׁseeks רָעevil וּמַלְאָךְmessenger אַכְזָרִיcruel יְשֻׁלַּחsent בּוֹagainst him

An evil man seeks only rebellion; a cruel messenger will be sent against him.

17:12

פָּגוֹשׁmeet דֹּבbear שַׁכּוּלrobbed בְּאִישׁher וְאַלand do not כְּסִילfool בְּאִוַּלְתּוֹin his folly

It is better to meet a bear robbed of her cubs than a fool in his folly.

17:13

מֵשִׁיבreturns רָעָהevil תַּחַתfor טוֹבָהgood לֹאnever תָמוּשׁleave רָעָהevil מִבֵּיתוֹhis house

If anyone returns evil for good, evil will never leave his house.

17:14

פּוֹטֵרrelease מַיִםflood רֵאשִׁיתabandon מָדוֹןstart וְלִפְנֵיbefore הִתְגַּלַּעquarrel הָרִיבthe dispute נְטוֹשׁbreaks

To start a quarrel is to release a flood; so abandon the dispute before it breaks out.

17:15

מַצְדִּיקacquitting רָשָׁעguilty וּמַרְשִׁיעַand condemning צַדִּיקrighteous תּוֹעֲבַתdetestable יְהוָהLORD גַּםboth שְׁנֵיהֶםboth are

Acquitting the guilty and condemning the righteous— both are detestable to the LORD.

17:16

לָמָּהto should זֶּהhave מְחִירmoney בְּיַדin his hand כְּסִילfool לִקְנוֹתbuying חָכְמָהwisdom וְלֶבwith intention אָיִןno

Why should the fool have money in his hand with no intention of buying wisdom?

17:17

בְּכָלat all עֵתtimes אֹהֵבloves הָרֵעַa friend וְאָחand a brother לְצָרָהfor adversity יִוָּלֵדborn

A friend loves at all times, and a brother is born for adversity.

17:18

אָדָםman חֲסַרlacking לֵבjudgment תּוֹקֵעַstrikes כָּףhands עֹרֵבpledge עֲרֻבָּהsecurity לִפְנֵיto before רֵעֵהוּhis neighbor

A man lacking judgment strikes hands in pledge and puts up security for his neighbor.

17:19

אֹהֵבloves פֶּשַׁעtransgression אֹהֵבloves מַצָּהstrife מַגְבִּיהַּhigh פִּתְחוֹbuilds his gate מְבַקֶּשׁinvites שָׁבֶרdestruction

He who loves transgression loves strife; he who builds his gate high invites destruction.

17:20

עִקֶּשׁperverse לֵבheart לֹאno יִמְצָאfinds טוֹבgood וְנֶהְפָּךְand he whose בִּלְשׁוֹנוֹtongue is deceitful יִפּוֹלfalls בְּרָעָהinto trouble

The one with a perverse heart finds no good, and he whose tongue is deceitful falls into trouble.

17:21

יֹלֵדfathers כְּסִילfool לְתוּגָהgrief לוֹto his וְלֹאno יִשְׂמַחjoy אֲבִיfather נָבָלfool

A man fathers a fool to his own grief; the father of a fool has no joy.

17:22

לֵבheart שָׂמֵחַjoyful יֵיטִבgood גֵּהָהmedicine וְרוּחַspirit נְכֵאָהbroken תְּיַבֶּשׁdries גָּרֶםbones

A joyful heart is good medicine, but a broken spirit dries up the bones.

17:23

שֹׁחַדbribe מֵחֵיקcovert רָשָׁעwicked יִקָּחtakes לְהַטּוֹתto subvert אָרְחוֹתcourse מִשְׁפָּטjustice

A wicked man takes a covert bribe to subvert the course of justice.

17:24

אֶתwith פְּנֵיface מֵבִיןdiscerning חָכְמָהwisdom וְעֵינֵיbut the eyes כְסִילfool בִּקְצֵהto the ends אָרֶץearth

Wisdom is the focus of the discerning, but the eyes of a fool wander to the ends of the earth.

17:25

כַּעַסgrief לְאָבִיוto his father בֵּןson כְּסִילfoolish וּמֶמֶרand bitterness לְיוֹלַדְתּוֹbore him

A foolish son brings grief to his father and bitterness to her who bore him.

17:26

גַּםeven עֲנוֹשׁpunish לַצַּדִּיקto the innocent לֹאnot or טוֹבgood לְהַכּוֹתto flog נְדִיבִיםnoble עַלfor יֹשֶׁרhonesty

It is surely not good to punish the innocent or to flog a noble for his honesty.

17:27

חוֹשֵׂךְrestrains אֲמָרָיוhis words and יוֹדֵעַknows דָּעַתknowledge יְקַרcalm רוּחַspirit אִישׁman תְּבוּנָהunderstanding

A man of knowledge restrains his words, and a man of understanding maintains a calm spirit.

17:28

גַּםeven אֱוִילfool מַחֲרִישׁsilent חָכָםwise יֵחָשֵׁבconsidered אֹטֵםkeeps שְׂפָתָיוhis lips נָבוֹןdiscerning

Even a fool is considered wise if he keeps silent, and discerning when he holds his tongue.
