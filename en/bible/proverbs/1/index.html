Proverbs 1

The purpose of the book; Wisdom calls in the streets

Open in the interlinear reader

1:1

מִשְׁלֵיproverbs שְׁלֹמֹהSolomon בֶןson דָּוִדDavid מֶלֶךְking יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

These are the proverbs of Solomon son of David, king of Israel,

1:2

לָדַעַתfor gaining חָכְמָהwisdom וּמוּסָרand discipline לְהָבִיןfor comprehending אִמְרֵיwords בִינָהinsight

for gaining wisdom and discipline, for comprehending words of insight,

1:3

לָקַחַתfor receiving מוּסַרinstruction הַשְׂכֵּלwise צֶדֶקrighteousness וּמִשְׁפָּטjustice וּמֵישָׁרִיםand equity

and for receiving instruction in wise living and in righteousness, justice, and equity.

1:4

לָתֵתto impart לִפְתָאיִםto the simple עָרְמָהprudence לְנַעַרto the young דַּעַתknowledge וּמְזִמָּהand discretion

To impart prudence to the simple and knowledge and discretion to the young,

1:5

יִשְׁמַעlisten חָכָםwise וְיוֹסֶףand gain לֶקַחinstruction וְנָבוֹןand the discerning תַּחְבֻּלוֹתcounsel יִקְנֶהacquire

let the wise listen and gain instruction, and the discerning acquire wise counsel

1:6

לְהָבִיןby understanding מָשָׁלproverbs parables וּמְלִיצָהand parables דִּבְרֵיsayings חֲכָמִיםwise וְחִידֹתָםand riddles

by understanding the proverbs and parables, the sayings and riddles of the wise.

1:7

יִרְאַתfear יְהוָהLORD רֵאשִׁיתbeginning דָּעַתknowledge חָכְמָהwisdom וּמוּסָרand discipline אֱוִילִיםfools בָּזוּdespise

The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge, but fools despise wisdom and discipline.

1:8

שְׁמַעlisten בְּנִיmy son מוּסַרinstruction אָבִיךָyour father’s וְאַלand do not תִּטֹּשׁforsake תּוֹרַתteaching אִמֶּךָyour mother

Listen, my son, to your father’s instruction, and do not forsake the teaching of your mother.

1:9

כִּיfor לִוְיַתgarland חֵןgrace הֵםthey לְרֹאשֶׁךָon your head וַעֲנָקִיםand a pendant לְגַרְגְּרֹתֶיךָaround your neck

For they are a garland of grace on your head and a pendant around your neck.

1:10

בְּנִיmy son אִםif יְפַתּוּךָentice you חַטָּאִיםsinners אַלdo not תֹּבֵאyield

My son, if sinners entice you, do not yield to them.

1:11

אִםif יֹאמְרוּsay לְכָהcome אִתָּנוּwith us נֶאֶרְבָהlie wait לְדָםfor blood נִצְפְּנָהhide לְנָקִיinnocent חִנָּםwithout cause

If they say, “Come along, let us lie in wait for blood, let us ambush the innocent without cause,

1:12

נִבְלָעֵםswallow them כִּשְׁאוֹלlike Sheol חַיִּיםalive וּתְמִימִיםand whole כְּיוֹרְדֵיlike those descending בוֹרpit

let us swallow them alive like Sheol, and whole like those descending into the Pit.

1:13

כָּלall הוֹןmanner יָקָרprecious נִמְצָאfind נְמַלֵּאfill בָתֵּינוּour houses שָׁלָלplunder

We will find all manner of precious goods; we will fill our houses with plunder.

1:14

גּוֹרָלְךָyour lot תַּפִּילthrow בְּתוֹכֵנוּin our among כִּיסshare אֶחָדone יִהְיֶהpurse לְכֻלָּנוּus all

Throw in your lot with us; let us all share one purse”—

1:15

בְּנִיmy son אַלdo not תֵּלֵךְwalk בְּדֶרֶךְthe road אִתָּםwith them מְנַעor רַגְלְךָset foot upon מִנְּתִיבָתָםtheir path

my son, do not walk the road with them or set foot upon their path.

1:16

כִּיfor רַגְלֵיהֶםtheir feet לָרַעto evil יָרוּצוּrun וִימַהֲרוּswift לִשְׁפָּךְto shed דָּםblood

For their feet run to evil, and they are swift to shed blood.

1:17

כִּיfor חִנָּםfutile מְזֹרָהspread הָרָשֶׁתthe net בְּעֵינֵיsee כָלany בַּעַלwhere כָּנָףbird

How futile it is to spread the net where any bird can see it!

1:18

וְהֵםbut they לְדָמָםtheir own blood they יֶאֱרֹבוּambush יִצְפְּנוּhelpless לְנַפְשֹׁתָםtheir own lives

But they lie in wait for their own blood; they ambush their own lives.

1:19

כֵּןso אָרְחוֹתfate כָּלall בֹּצֵעַgreedy בָּצַעunjust gain אֶת[obj] נֶפֶשׁlives בְּעָלָיוits possessors יִקָּחpossessors

Such is the fate of all who are greedy, whose unjust gain takes the lives of its possessors.

1:20

חָכְמוֹתwisdom בַּחוּץin the street תָּרֹנָּהshe lifts בָּרְחֹבוֹתin the square תִּתֵּןwill קוֹלָהּher voice

Wisdom calls out in the street, she lifts her voice in the square;

1:21

בְּרֹאשׁin the main הֹמִיּוֹתconcourse cries תִּקְרָאshe aloud בְּפִתְחֵיin doors שְׁעָרִיםgates בָּעִירthe city אֲמָרֶיהָmakes her תֹאמֵרspeech

in the main concourse she cries aloud, at the city gates she makes her speech:

1:22

עַדhow מָתַיhow long פְּתָיִםsimple תְּאֵהֲבוּlove פֶתִיsimple וְלֵצִיםand mockers לָצוֹןscoffers חָמְדוּdelight לָהֶםto them וּכְסִילִיםand fools יִשְׂנְאוּhate דָעַתknowledge

“How long, O simple ones, will you love your simple ways? How long will scoffers delight in their scorn and fools hate knowledge?

1:23

תָּשׁוּבוּrepented לְתוֹכַחְתִּיhad at my rebuke הִנֵּהsurely אַבִּיעָהpoured לָכֶםto you רוּחִיmy spirit אוֹדִיעָהknown דְבָרַיmy words אֶתְכֶםyou

If you had repented at my rebuke, then surely I would have poured out my spirit on you; I would have made my words known to you.

1:24

יַעַןbecause קָרָאתִיcall וַתְּמָאֵנוּrefused נָטִיתִיoutstretched יָדִיhand וְאֵיןno מַקְשִׁיבtook

Because you refused my call, and no one took my outstretched hand,

1:25

וַתִּפְרְעוּbecause you neglected כָלall עֲצָתִיmy counsel וְתוֹכַחְתִּיmy correction לֹאnot אֲבִיתֶםwanted

because you neglected all my counsel, and wanted none of my correction,

1:26

גַּםeven אֲנִיI בְּאֵידְכֶםyour calamity אֶשְׂחָקmock אֶלְעַגsneer בְּבֹאwhen strikes פַחְדְּכֶםterror you

in turn I will mock your calamity; I will sneer when terror strikes you,

1:27

בְּבֹאcomes כְשׁוֹאָהlike a storm פַּחְדְּכֶםyour dread וְאֵידְכֶםand your destruction כְּסוּפָהlike a whirlwind יֶאֱתֶהpast בְּבֹאin came עֲלֵיכֶםon you צָרָהdistress anguish וְצוּקָהand anguish overwhelm

when your dread comes like a storm, and your destruction like a whirlwind, when distress and anguish overwhelm you.

1:28

אָזthen יִקְרָאֻנְנִיcall on me וְלֹאnot אֶעֱנֶהanswer יְשַׁחֲרֻנְנִיearnestly seek me וְלֹאbut will not יִמְצָאֻנְנִיfind me

Then they will call on me, but I will not answer; they will earnestly seek me, but will not find me.

1:29

תַּחַתfor כִּיfor שָׂנְאוּhated דָעַתknowledge וְיִרְאַתfear יְהֹוָהLORD לֹאnot בָחָרוּchose

For they hated knowledge and chose not to fear the LORD.

1:30

לֹאnot אָבוּaccepted לַעֲצָתִיof my counsel נָאֲצוּdespised כָּלall תּוֹכַחְתִּיmy reproof

They accepted none of my counsel; they despised all my reproof.

1:31

וְיֹאכְלוּeat מִפְּרִיthe fruit דַרְכָּםtheir own way וּמִמֹּעֲצֹתֵיהֶםwith their own devices יִשְׂבָּעוּfilled

So they will eat the fruit of their own way, and be filled with their own devices.

1:32

כִּיfor מְשׁוּבַתwaywardness פְּתָיִםsimple תַּהַרְגֵםslay them וְשַׁלְוַתand the complacency כְּסִילִיםfools תְּאַבְּדֵםdestroy them

For the waywardness of the simple will slay them, and the complacency of fools will destroy them.

1:33

וְשֹׁמֵעַbut whoever listens לִיto me יִשְׁכָּןdwell בֶּטַחsafety וְשַׁאֲנַןsecure מִפַּחַדfrom the fear רָעָהevil

But whoever listens to me will dwell in safety, secure from the fear of evil.”
