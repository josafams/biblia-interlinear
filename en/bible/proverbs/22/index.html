Proverbs 22

A good name; the words of the wise begin

Open in the interlinear reader

22:1

נִבְחָרdesirable שֵׁםname מֵעֹשֶׁרthan riches רָבgreat מִכֶּסֶףthan silver וּמִזָּהָבand gold חֵןfavor טוֹבbetter

A good name is more desirable than great riches; favor is better than silver and gold.

22:2

עָשִׁירrich וָרָשׁand the poor נִפְגָּשׁוּcommon עֹשֵׂהMaker כֻלָּםthem all יְהוָהLORD

The rich and the poor have this in common: The LORD is Maker of them all.

22:3

עָרוּםprudent רָאָהsee רָעָהdanger וְנִסְתָּרand take cover וּפְתָיִיםbut the simple עָבְרוּgoing וְנֶעֱנָשׁוּand suffer

The prudent see danger and take cover, but the simple keep going and suffer the consequences.

22:4

עֵקֶבrewards עֲנָוָהhumility יִרְאַתfear יְהוָהLORD עֹשֶׁרwealth וְכָבוֹדand honor וְחַיִּיםand life

The rewards of humility and the fear of the LORD are wealth and honor and life.

22:5

צִנִּיםthorns פַּחִיםsnares בְּדֶרֶךְon the path עִקֵּשׁperverse שׁוֹמֵרguards נַפְשׁוֹhis soul יִרְחַקstays far מֵהֶםfrom them

Thorns and snares lie on the path of the perverse; he who guards his soul stays far from them.

22:6

חֲנֹךְtrain לַנַּעַרa child עַלin פִּיmouth דַרְכּוֹway he גַּםand כִּיwhen יַזְקִיןold לֹאnot יָסוּרdepart מִמֶּנָּהfrom it

Train up a child in the way he should go, and when he is old he will not depart from it.

22:7

עָשִׁירrich בְּרָשִׁיםover the poor יִמְשׁוֹלrule וְעֶבֶדslave לֹוֶהborrower לְאִישׁto man מַלְוֶהlender

The rich rule over the poor, and the borrower is slave to the lender.

22:8

זוֹרֵעַsows עַוְלָהinjustice יִקְצָרreap אָוֶןdisaster וְשֵׁבֶטand the rod עֶבְרָתוֹhis fury יִכְלֶהdestroyed

He who sows injustice will reap disaster, and the rod of his fury will be destroyed.

22:9

טוֹבgenerous עַיִןman הוּאwill יְבֹרָךְblessed כִּיfor נָתַןhe מִלַּחְמוֹshares his bread לַדָּלwith the poor

A generous man will be blessed, for he shares his bread with the poor.

22:10

גָּרֵשׁdrive לֵץmocker וְיֵצֵאdepart מָדוֹןconflict וְיִשְׁבֹּתcease דִּיןquarreling וְקָלוֹןand insults

Drive out the mocker, and conflict will depart; even quarreling and insults will cease.

22:11

אֹהֵבloves טְהָרpure לֵבheart חֵןgracious שְׂפָתָיוlips will רֵעֵהוּfriend מֶלֶךְking

He who loves a pure heart and gracious lips will have the king for a friend.

22:12

עֵינֵיeyes יְהוָהLORD’s נָצְרוּkeep דָעַתknowledge וַיְסַלֵּףbut he frustrates דִּבְרֵיwords בֹגֵדfaithless

The LORD’s eyes keep watch over knowledge, but He frustrates the words of the faithless.

22:13

אָמַרsays עָצֵלslacker אֲרִיlion בַחוּץoutside בְּתוֹךְslain רְחֹבוֹתstreets אֵרָצֵחַmurder

The slacker says, “There is a lion outside! I will be slain in the streets!”

22:14

שׁוּחָהpit עֲמֻקָּהdeep פִּיmouth זָרוֹתadulteress זְעוּםwrath יְהוָהLORD יִפָּלfall שָׁםthere

The mouth of an adulteress is a deep pit; he who is under the wrath of the LORD will fall into it.

22:15

אִוֶּלֶתfoolishness קְשׁוּרָהbound בְלֶבin the heart נָעַרchild שֵׁבֶטrod מוּסָרdiscipline יַרְחִיקֶנָּהdrives it far מִמֶּנּוּfrom him

Foolishness is bound up in the heart of a child, but the rod of discipline drives it far from him.

22:16

עֹשֵׁקoppressing דָּלpoor לְהַרְבּוֹתto enrich לוֹto him נֹתֵןgiving לְעָשִׁירto the rich אַךְsurely לְמַחְסוֹרto poverty

Oppressing the poor to enrich oneself or giving gifts to the rich will surely lead to poverty.

22:17

הַטincline אָזְנְךָyour ear וּשְׁמַעand hear דִּבְרֵיwords חֲכָמִיםwise וְלִבְּךָapply your mind תָּשִׁיתmind לְדַעְתִּיto my knowledge

Incline your ear and hear the words of the wise— apply your mind to my knowledge—

22:18

כִּיfor נָעִיםpleasing כִּיwhen תִשְׁמְרֵםkeep them בְּבִטְנֶךָwithin you יִכֹּנוּestablished יַחְדָּוtogether עַלon שְׂפָתֶיךָyour lips

for it is pleasing when you keep them within you and they are constantly on your lips.

22:19

לִהְיוֹתmay be בַּיהוָהin the LORD מִבְטַחֶךָyour trust הוֹדַעְתִּיךָinstruct you הַיּוֹםtoday אַףyes אָתָּהyou

So that your trust may be in the LORD, I instruct you today—yes, you.

22:20

הֲלֹאhave I not כָתַבְתִּיwritten לְךָfor you שָׁלִישִׁיםthirty בְּמוֹעֵצֹתsayings about counsel וָדָעַתand knowledge

Have I not written for you thirty sayings about counsel and knowledge,

22:21

לְהוֹדִיעֲךָto show you קֹשְׁטְreliable אִמְרֵיreliable words אֱמֶתtrue לְהָשִׁיבanswer אֲמָרִיםsoundly אֱמֶתthose לְשֹׁלְחֶיךָsent you

to show you true and reliable words, that you may soundly answer those who sent you?

22:22

אַלdo not תִּגְזָלrob דָּלpoor כִּיbecause דַלpoor הוּאis וְאַלand do not תְּדַכֵּאcrush עָנִיafflicted בַשָּׁעַרat the gate

Do not rob a poor man because he is poor, and do not crush the afflicted at the gate,

22:23

כִּיfor יְהוָהLORD יָרִיבtake רִיבָםtheir case וְקָבַעand will plunder אֶת[obj] קֹבְעֵיהֶםrob them נָפֶשׁwho

for the LORD will take up their case and will plunder those who rob them.

22:24

אַלdo not תִּתְרַעfriends אֶתwith בַּעַלangry אָףangry man וְאֶתwith אִישׁman חֵמוֹתhot tempered לֹאnot תָבוֹאassociate

Do not make friends with an angry man, and do not associate with a hot-tempered man,

22:25

פֶּןor תֶּאֱלַףlearn אֹרְחֹתָיוhis ways וְלָקַחְתָּand entangle מוֹקֵשׁsnare לְנַפְשֶׁךָyourself in

or you may learn his ways and entangle yourself in a snare.

22:26

אַלdo not תְּהִיbe בְתֹקְעֵיone who gives כָףpledges בַּעֹרְבִיםwho puts up security מַשָּׁאוֹתdebts

Do not be one who gives pledges, who puts up security for debts.

22:27

אִםif אֵיןnothing לְךָto you לְשַׁלֵּםto pay לָמָּהwhy should יִקַּחtaken מִשְׁכָּבְךָyour bed מִתַּחְתֶּיךָfrom under you

If you have nothing with which to pay, why should your bed be taken from under you?

22:28

אַלdo not תַּסֵּגmove גְּבוּלboundary עוֹלָםancient אֲשֶׁרwhich עָשׂוּdone אֲבוֹתֶיךָyour fathers

Do not move an ancient boundary stone which your fathers have placed.

22:29

חָזִיתָsee אִישׁman מָהִירskilled בִּמְלַאכְתּוֹin his work לִפְנֵיin the presence מְלָכִיםkings יִתְיַצָּבstand בַּלnot יִתְיַצֵּבstand לִפְנֵיbefore חֲשֻׁכִּיםobscure

Do you see a man skilled in his work? He will be stationed in the presence of kings; he will not stand before obscure men.
