Proverbs 7

A young man seduced at nightfall

Open in the interlinear reader

7:1

בְּנִיmy son שְׁמֹרkeep אֲמָרָיmy words וּמִצְוֺתַיand my commandments תִּצְפֹּןtreasure אִתָּךְwith you

My son, keep my words and treasure my commandments within you.

7:2

שְׁמֹרkeep מִצְוֺתַיmy commandments וֶחְיֵהand live וְתוֹרָתִיguard my כְּאִישׁוֹןteachings as the apple עֵינֶיךָyour eye

Keep my commandments and live; guard my teachings as the apple of your eye.

7:3

קָשְׁרֵםtie them עַלto אֶצְבְּעֹתֶיךָyour fingers כָּתְבֵםwrite them עַלon לוּחַtablet לִבֶּךָyour heart

Tie them to your fingers; write them on the tablet of your heart.

7:4

אֱמֹרsay לַחָכְמָהto wisdom אֲחֹתִיmy sister אָתְּyou are וּמֹדָעand call לַבִּינָהunderstanding תִקְרָאcall

Say to wisdom, “You are my sister,” and call understanding your kinsman,

7:5

לִשְׁמָרְךָkeep you מֵאִשָּׁהfrom woman זָרָהadulteress מִנָּכְרִיָּהfrom the stranger אֲמָרֶיהָwith words הֶחֱלִיקָהseductive

that they may keep you from the adulteress, from the stranger with seductive words.

7:6

כִּיfor בְּחַלּוֹןat the window בֵּיתִיmy house בְּעַדthrough אֶשְׁנַבִּיlattice נִשְׁקָפְתִּיlooked

For at the window of my house I looked through the lattice.

7:7

וָאֵרֶאI saw בַפְּתָאיִםamong the simple אָבִינָהnoticed בַבָּנִיםin the sons נַעַרyouths חֲסַרlacking לֵבjudgment

I saw among the simple, I noticed among the youths, a young man lacking judgment,

7:8

עֹבֵרcrossing בַּשּׁוּקthe street אֵצֶלnear פִּנָּהּher corner וְדֶרֶךְroad בֵּיתָהּher house יִצְעָדbranches

crossing the street near her corner, strolling down the road to her house,

7:9

בְּנֶשֶׁףat twilight בְּעֶרֶבtwilight as יוֹםday בְּאִישׁוֹןfading into לַיְלָהnight וַאֲפֵלָהand gloom

at twilight, as the day was fading into the dark of the night.

7:10

וְהִנֵּהand saw אִשָּׁהwoman לִקְרָאתוֹto meet him שִׁיתattire זוֹנָהharlot וּנְצֻרַתand cunning לֵבheart

Then a woman came out to meet him, with the attire of a harlot and cunning of heart.

7:11

הֹמִיָּהloud הִיאis וְסֹרָרֶתand defiant בְּבֵיתָהּat home לֹאnot יִשְׁכְּנוּremain רַגְלֶיהָfeet

She is loud and defiant; her feet do not remain at home.

7:12

פַּעַםnow בַּחוּץin the street פַּעַםnow בָּרְחֹבוֹתin the squares וְאֵצֶלand beside כָּלevery פִּנָּהcorner תֶאֱרֹבlurks

Now in the street, now in the squares, she lurks at every corner.

7:13

וְהֶחֱזִיקָהshe seizes בּוֹin him וְנָשְׁקָהand kisses לּוֹto him הֵעֵזָהbrazenly פָנֶיהָher face וַתֹּאמַרsays לוֹto him

She seizes him and kisses him; she brazenly says to him:

7:14

זִבְחֵיsacrifices שְׁלָמִיםpeace offerings עָלָיon me הַיּוֹםtoday שִׁלַּמְתִּיpaid נְדָרָיmy vows

“I have made my peace offerings; today I have paid my vows.

7:15

עַלon כֵּןso יָצָאתִיcame out לִקְרָאתֶךָto meet you לְשַׁחֵרsought פָּנֶיךָsought you וָאֶמְצָאֶךָּfound you

So I came out to meet you; I sought you, and I have found you.

7:16

מַרְבַדִּיםcoverings רָבַדְתִּיdecked עַרְשִׂיmy bed חֲטֻבוֹתcolored אֵטוּןlinen מִצְרָיִםEgypt

I have decked my bed with coverings, with colored linen from Egypt.

7:17

נַפְתִּיperfumed מִשְׁכָּבִיmy bed מֹרmyrrh אֲהָלִיםaloes וְקִנָּמוֹןand with cinnamon

I have perfumed my bed with myrrh, with aloes, and with cinnamon.

7:18

לְכָהcome נִרְוֶהfill דֹדִיםlove עַדuntil הַבֹּקֶרmorning נִתְעַלְּסָהdelight loving בָּאֳהָבִיםin loving caresses

Come, let us take our fill of love till morning. Let us delight in loving caresses!

7:19

כִּיfor אֵיןnot הָאִישׁhusband בְּבֵיתוֹat home he הָלַךְgone בְּדֶרֶךְjourney מֵרָחוֹקa long

For my husband is not at home; he has gone on a long journey.

7:20

צְרוֹרbag הַכֶּסֶףof money לָקַחtook בְּיָדוֹin his hand לְיוֹםfull הַכֵּסֶאtill the moon יָבֹאreturn בֵיתוֹhis house

He took with him a bag of money and will not return till the moon is full.”

7:21

הִטַּתּוּentices him בְּרֹבwith her great לִקְחָהּpersuasion בְּחֵלֶקwith flattering שְׂפָתֶיהָlips she תַּדִּיחֶנּוּlures him

With her great persuasion she entices him; with her flattering lips she lures him.

7:22

הוֹלֵךְwalks אַחֲרֶיהָafter her פִּתְאֹםimpulse כְּשׁוֹרlike an ox אֶלto טָבַחslaughter יָבוֹאcome וּכְעֶכֶסlike a deer אֶלinto מוּסַרbounding אֱוִילtrap

He follows her on impulse, like an ox going to the slaughter, like a deer bounding into a trap,

7:23

עַדuntil יְפַלַּחpierces חֵץarrow כְּבֵדוֹhis liver כְּמַהֵרdarting צִפּוֹרbird אֶלinto פָּחsnare וְלֹאnot יָדַעknowing כִּיfor בְנַפְשׁוֹcost him life הוּאhe

until an arrow pierces his liver, like a bird darting into a snare— not knowing it will cost him his life.

7:24

וְעַתָּהnow בָנִיםsons שִׁמְעוּlisten לִיto me וְהַקְשִׁיבוּand attend לְאִמְרֵיto the words פִיmy mouth

Now, my sons, listen to me, and attend to the words of my mouth.

7:25

אַלdo יֵשְׂטְturn אֶלto דְּרָכֶיהָher ways לִבֶּךָyour heart אַלdo not תֵּתַעstray בִּנְתִיבוֹתֶיהָinto her paths

Do not let your heart turn aside to her ways; do not stray into her paths.

7:26

כִּיfor רַבִּיםmany חֲלָלִיםslain הִפִּילָהdown וַעֲצֻמִיםmany כָּלnumber הֲרֻגֶיהָdeath her

For she has brought many down to death; her slain are many in number.

7:27

דַּרְכֵיroad שְׁאוֹלSheol בֵּיתָהּher house יֹרְדוֹתdescending אֶלto חַדְרֵיchambers מָוֶתdeath

Her house is the road to Sheol, descending to the chambers of death.
