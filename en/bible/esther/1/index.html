Esther 1

Xerxes' banquet and Vashti's refusal

This is what happened in the days of Xerxes, who reigned over 127 provinces from India to Cush.

In those days King Xerxes sat on his royal throne in the citadel of Susa.

In the third year of his reign, Xerxes held a feast for all his officials and servants. The military leaders of Persia and Media were there, along with the nobles and princes of the provinces.

And for a full 180 days he displayed the glorious riches of his kingdom and the magnificent splendor of his greatness.

At the end of this time, in the garden court of the royal palace, the king held a seven-day feast for all the people in the citadel of Susa, from the least to the greatest.

חוּרwhite כַּרְפַּסwhite וּתְכֵלֶתand blue אָחוּזfastened בְּחַבְלֵיwith cords בוּץlinen וְאַרְגָּמָןand purple עַלon גְּלִילֵיrings כֶסֶףsilver וְעַמּוּדֵיpillars שֵׁשׁmarble מִטּוֹתcouches זָהָבgold וָכֶסֶףand silver עַלon רִצְפַתmosaic pavement בַּהַטporphyry וָשֵׁשׁmarble וְדַרmother of pearl וְסֹחָרֶתand other costly

Hangings of white and blue linen were fastened with cords of fine white and purple material to silver rings on the marble pillars. Gold and silver couches were arranged on a mosaic pavement of porphyry, marble, mother-of-pearl, and other costly stones.

1:7

וְהַשְׁקוֹתbeverages were בִּכְלֵיgoblets זָהָבgold וְכֵלִיםand articles מִכֵּלִיםdesign שׁוֹנִיםdifferent וְיֵיןwine מַלְכוּתroyal flowed רָבgreat כְּיַדbounty הַמֶּלֶךְthe king’s

Beverages were served in an array of goblets of gold, each with a different design, and the royal wine flowed freely, according to the king’s bounty.

1:8

וְהַשְּׁתִיָּהby order כַדָּתlimit אֵיןno אֹנֵסlimit כִּיfor כֵןthis יִסַּדdrinking הַמֶּלֶךְthe king עַלon כָּלevery רַבofficial בֵּיתוֹhis household לַעֲשׂוֹתwhatever כִּרְצוֹןhe desired אִישׁeach וָאִישׁman

By order of the king, no limit was placed on the drinking, and every official of his household was to serve each man whatever he desired.

1:9

גַּםalso וַשְׁתִּיVashti gave הַמַּלְכָּהqueen עָשְׂתָהdone מִשְׁתֵּהbanquet נָשִׁיםwomen בֵּיתpalace הַמַּלְכוּתroyal אֲשֶׁרthat לַמֶּלֶךְof king אֲחַשְׁוֵרוֹשׁXerxes

Queen Vashti also gave a banquet for the women in the royal palace of King Xerxes.

1:10

בַּיּוֹםon the day הַשְּׁבִיעִיseventh כְּטוֹבmerry לֵבheart הַמֶּלֶךְthe king’s בַּיָּיִןwith wine אָמַרordered לִמְהוּמָןMehuman בִּזְּתָאMehuman חַרְבוֹנָאHarbona בִּגְתָאBigtha וַאֲבַגְתָאAbagtha זֵתַרZethar וְכַרְכַּסand Carkas שִׁבְעַתseven הַסָּרִיסִיםthe eunuchs הַמְשָׁרְתִיםwho served אֶתwith פְּנֵיface הַמֶּלֶךְthe king אֲחַשְׁוֵרוֹשׁXerxes

On the seventh day, when the king’s heart was merry with wine, he ordered the seven eunuchs who served him—Mehuman, Biztha, Harbona, Bigtha, Abagtha, Zethar, and Carkas—

לְהָבִיאto bring אֶת[obj] וַשְׁתִּיVashti הַמַּלְכָּהqueen לִפְנֵיbefore הַמֶּלֶךְhim בְּכֶתֶרcrown מַלְכוּתroyal לְהַרְאוֹתto display הָעַמִּיםthe people וְהַשָּׂרִיםand officials אֶת[obj] יָפְיָהּher beauty כִּיfor טוֹבַתwas מַרְאֶהbeautiful הִיאis

to bring Queen Vashti before him, wearing her royal crown, to display her beauty to the people and officials. For she was beautiful to behold.

1:12

וַתְּמָאֵןhowever refused הַמַּלְכָּהqueen וַשְׁתִּיVashti לָבוֹאto come בִּדְבַרcommand הַמֶּלֶךְthe king’s אֲשֶׁרthat בְּיַדby his הַסָּרִיסִיםeunuchs וַיִּקְצֹףand angry הַמֶּלֶךְthe king מְאֹדvery וַחֲמָתוֹand his anger בָּעֲרָהburned בוֹin him

Queen Vashti, however, refused to come at the king’s command brought by his eunuchs. And the king became furious, and his anger burned within him.

1:13

וַיֹּאמֶרthen הַמֶּלֶךְthe king לַחֲכָמִיםconsulted the wise יֹדְעֵיknew הָעִתִּיםthe times כִּיfor כֵןthis דְּבַרword הַמֶּלֶךְfor him לִפְנֵיto confer כָּלall יֹדְעֵיknow דָּתlaw וָדִיןand justice

Then the king consulted the wise men who knew the times, for it was customary for him to confer with the experts in law and justice.

1:14

וְהַקָּרֹבand the near אֵלָיוto him כַּרְשְׁנָאclosest שֵׁתָרclosest advisors אַדְמָתָאAdmatha תַרְשִׁישׁTarshish מֶרֶסMeres מַרְסְנָאMarsena מְמוּכָןMemucan שִׁבְעַתseven שָׂרֵיprinces פָּרַסPersia וּמָדַיand Media רֹאֵיhad פְּנֵיaccess הַמֶּלֶךְthe king הַיֹּשְׁבִיםand ranked רִאשֹׁנָהhighest בַּמַּלְכוּתin the kingdom

His closest advisors were Carshena, Shethar, Admatha, Tarshish, Meres, Marsena, and Memucan, the seven princes of Persia and Media who had personal access to the king and ranked highest in the kingdom.

1:15

כְּדָתaccording to law מַהwhat לַּעֲשׂוֹתshould be done בַּמַּלְכָּהwith queen וַשְׁתִּיVashti עַלon אֲשֶׁרthat לֹאnot עָשְׂתָהobey אֶת[obj] מַאֲמַרcommand הַמֶּלֶךְof king אֲחַשְׁוֵרוֹשׁXerxes בְּיַדin hand הַסָּרִיסִיםthe eunuchs

“According to law,” he asked, “what should be done with Queen Vashti, since she refused to obey the command of King Xerxes delivered by the eunuchs?”

1:16

וַיֹּאמֶרreplied מְמוּכָןMemucan לִפְנֵיin the presence הַמֶּלֶךְthe king וְהַשָּׂרִיםand his princes לֹאnot עַלon הַמֶּלֶךְthe king לְבַדּוֹonly עָוְתָהwronged וַשְׁתִּיVashti הַמַּלְכָּהqueen כִּיbut עַלon כָּלall הַשָּׂרִיםthe princes וְעַלand on כָּלall הָעַמִּיםthe peoples אֲשֶׁרthat בְּכָלin all מְדִינוֹתprovinces הַמֶּלֶךְking אֲחַשְׁוֵרוֹשׁXerxes

And in the presence of the king and his princes, Memucan replied, “Queen Vashti has wronged not only the king, but all the princes and the peoples in all the provinces of King Xerxes.

1:17

כִּיfor יֵצֵאgo דְבַרconduct הַמַּלְכָּהthe queen will become known עַלto כָּלall הַנָּשִׁיםwomen לְהַבְזוֹתto despise בַּעְלֵיהֶןcausing them בְּעֵינֵיהֶןdespise their husbands בְּאָמְרָםand say הַמֶּלֶךְking אֲחַשְׁוֵרוֹשׁXerxes אָמַרordered לְהָבִיאto be brought אֶת[obj] וַשְׁתִּיVashti הַמַּלְכָּהqueen לְפָנָיוbefore him וְלֹאnot בָאָהcome

For the conduct of the queen will become known to all women, causing them to despise their husbands and say, ‘King Xerxes ordered Queen Vashti to be brought before him, but she did not come.’

1:18

וְהַיּוֹםthis very day הַזֶּהthis תֹּאמַרְנָהsay שָׂרוֹתnoble פָּרַסPersia וּמָדַיand Media אֲשֶׁרwho שָׁמְעוּheard אֶת[obj] דְּבַרabout הַמַּלְכָּהthe queen’s conduct לְכֹלto all שָׂרֵיofficials הַמֶּלֶךְthe king’s וּכְדַיresulting in much בִּזָּיוֹןcontempt וָקָצֶףand wrath

This very day the noble women of Persia and Media who have heard about the queen’s conduct will say the same thing to all the king’s officials, resulting in much contempt and wrath.

So if it pleases the king, let him issue a royal decree, and let it be recorded in the laws of Persia and Media so that it cannot be repealed, that Vashti shall never again enter the presence of King Xerxes, and that her royal position shall be given to a woman better than she.

1:20

וְנִשְׁמַעwill be heard פִּתְגָםedict הַמֶּלֶךְthe king אֲשֶׁרthat יַעֲשֶׂהdo בְּכָלin all מַלְכוּתוֹhis kingdom כִּיfor רַבָּהvast הִיאis וְכָלand so all הַנָּשִׁיםwomen יִתְּנוּwill יְקָרhonor לְבַעְלֵיהֶןtheir husbands לְמִגָּדוֹלgreatest וְעַדto קָטָןleast

The edict the king issues will be heard throughout his vast kingdom—and so all women, from the least to the greatest, will honor their husbands.”

1:21

וַיִּיטַבwere pleased הַדָּבָרthis counsel בְּעֵינֵיin sight הַמֶּלֶךְthe king וְהַשָּׂרִיםand his princes וַיַּעַשׂdid הַמֶּלֶךְthe king כִּדְבַרas had מְמוּכָןMemucan advised

The king and his princes were pleased with this counsel; so the king did as Memucan had advised.

He sent letters to all the provinces of the kingdom, to each province in its own script and to each people in their own language, proclaiming that every man should be master of his own household.
