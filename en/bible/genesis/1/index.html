Genesis 1

The creation

In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth.

Now the earth was formless and void, and darkness was over the surface of the deep. And the Spirit of God was hovering over the surface of the waters.

1:3

וַיֹּאמֶרand said אֱלֹהִיםGod יְהִיbe אוֹרlight וַיְהִיand there was אוֹרlight

And God said, “Let there be light,” and there was light.

1:4

וַיַּרְאand saw אֱלֹהִיםGod אֶת[obj] הָאוֹרthe light כִּיfor טוֹבgood וַיַּבְדֵּלand separated אֱלֹהִיםhe בֵּיןbetween הָאוֹרthe light וּבֵיןand between הַחֹשֶׁךְthe darkness

And God saw that the light was good, and He separated the light from the darkness.

1:5

וַיִּקְרָאcalled אֱלֹהִיםGod לָאוֹרthe light יוֹםday וְלַחֹשֶׁךְand the darkness קָרָאcalled לָיְלָהnight וַיְהִיand there was עֶרֶבevening וַיְהִיand there was בֹקֶרmorning יוֹםday אֶחָדfirst

God called the light “day,” and the darkness He called “night.” And there was evening, and there was morning—the first day.

1:6

וַיֹּאמֶרand said אֱלֹהִיםGod יְהִיthere רָקִיעַexpanse בְּתוֹךְbetween הַמָּיִםthe waters וִיהִיand be מַבְדִּילseparate בֵּיןbetween מַיִםwaters לָמָיִםwaters

And God said, “Let there be an expanse between the waters, to separate the waters from the waters.”

1:7

וַיַּעַשׂso made אֱלֹהִיםGod אֶת[obj] הָרָקִיעַthe expanse וַיַּבְדֵּלand separated בֵּיןbetween הַמַּיִםthe waters אֲשֶׁרthat מִתַּחַתbeneath it from לָרָקִיעַto the expanse וּבֵיןand between הַמַּיִםthe waters אֲשֶׁרthat מֵעַלfrom the לָרָקִיעַto the expanse וַיְהִיand it was כֵןso

So God made the expanse and separated the waters beneath it from the waters above. And it was so.

1:8

וַיִּקְרָאcalled אֱלֹהִיםGod לָרָקִיעַthe expanse שָׁמָיִםsky וַיְהִיand there was עֶרֶבevening וַיְהִיand there was בֹקֶרmorning יוֹםday שֵׁנִיsecond

God called the expanse “sky.” And there was evening, and there was morning—the second day.

1:9

וַיֹּאמֶרand said אֱלֹהִיםGod יִקָּווּlet הַמַּיִםthe waters מִתַּחַתunder הַשָּׁמַיִםthe sky אֶלinto מָקוֹםplace אֶחָדone וְתֵרָאֶהappear הַיַּבָּשָׁהthe dry land וַיְהִיand it was כֵןso

And God said, “Let the waters under the sky be gathered into one place, so that the dry land may appear.” And it was so.

1:10

וַיִּקְרָאcalled אֱלֹהִיםGod לַיַּבָּשָׁהthe dry land אֶרֶץland earth וּלְמִקְוֵהand the gathering הַמַּיִםof waters קָרָאcalled יַמִּיםseas וַיַּרְאand saw אֱלֹהִיםGod כִּיfor טוֹבgood

God called the dry land “earth,” and the gathering of waters He called “seas.” And God saw that it was good.

1:11

וַיֹּאמֶרthen said אֱלֹהִיםGod תַּדְשֵׁאlet הָאָרֶץthe earth דֶּשֶׁאvegetation עֵשֶׂבbearing plants מַזְרִיעַbearing זֶרַעseed עֵץtrees פְּרִיfruit עֹשֶׂהbearing פְּרִיfruit לְמִינוֹkind אֲשֶׁרthat זַרְעוֹseed בוֹin him עַלto הָאָרֶץthe land וַיְהִיand it was כֵןso

Then God said, “Let the earth bring forth vegetation: seed-bearing plants and fruit trees, each bearing fruit with seed according to its kind.” And it was so.

1:12

וַתּוֹצֵאand brought הָאָרֶץthe earth דֶּשֶׁאproduced vegetation עֵשֶׂבbearing plants מַזְרִיעַbearing זֶרַעseed לְמִינֵהוּaccording to their kinds וְעֵץand trees עֹשֶׂהbearing פְּרִיfruit אֲשֶׁרthat זַרְעוֹseed בוֹin him לְמִינֵהוּto kinds וַיַּרְאand saw אֱלֹהִיםGod כִּיthat טוֹבgood

The earth produced vegetation: seed-bearing plants according to their kinds and trees bearing fruit with seed according to their kinds. And God saw that it was good.

1:13

וַיְהִיand there was עֶרֶבevening וַיְהִיand there was בֹקֶרmorning יוֹםday שְׁלִישִׁיthird

And there was evening, and there was morning—the third day.

1:14

וַיֹּאמֶרand said אֱלֹהִיםGod יְהִיbe מְאֹרֹתlights בִּרְקִיעַin the expanse הַשָּׁמַיִםthe sky לְהַבְדִּילto distinguish בֵּיןbetween הַיּוֹםthe day וּבֵיןand between הַלָּיְלָהthe night וְהָיוּbe לְאֹתֹתsigns וּלְמוֹעֲדִיםto mark the seasons וּלְיָמִיםand days וְשָׁנִיםand years

And God said, “Let there be lights in the expanse of the sky to distinguish between the day and the night, and let them be signs to mark the seasons and days and years.

1:15

וְהָיוּserve לִמְאוֹרֹתas lights בִּרְקִיעַin the expanse הַשָּׁמַיִםthe sky לְהָאִירto shine עַלupon הָאָרֶץthe earth וַיְהִיand it was כֵןso

And let them serve as lights in the expanse of the sky to shine upon the earth.” And it was so.

1:16

וַיַּעַשׂmade אֱלֹהִיםGod אֶת[obj] שְׁנֵיtwo הַמְּאֹרֹתlights הַגְּדֹלִיםgreat אֶת[obj] הַמָּאוֹרlight הַגָּדֹלthe greater לְמֶמְשֶׁלֶתto rule הַיּוֹםthe day וְאֶתand [obj] הַמָּאוֹרthe light הַקָּטֹןlesser לְמֶמְשֶׁלֶתto rule הַלַּיְלָהthe night וְאֵתand [obj] הַכּוֹכָבִיםthe stars

God made two great lights: the greater light to rule the day and the lesser light to rule the night. And He made the stars as well.

1:17

וַיִּתֵּןset אֹתָםthem אֱלֹהִיםlights בִּרְקִיעַin the expanse הַשָּׁמָיִםthe sky לְהָאִירto shine עַלupon הָאָרֶץthe earth

God set these lights in the expanse of the sky to shine upon the earth,

1:18

וְלִמְשֹׁלto preside בַּיּוֹםover the day וּבַלַּיְלָהand the night וּלֲהַבְדִּילand to separate בֵּיןbetween הָאוֹרthe light וּבֵיןand between הַחֹשֶׁךְthe darkness וַיַּרְאand saw אֱלֹהִיםGod כִּיfor טוֹבgood

to preside over the day and the night, and to separate the light from the darkness. And God saw that it was good.

1:19

וַיְהִיand there was עֶרֶבevening וַיְהִיand there was בֹקֶרmorning יוֹםday רְבִיעִיfourth

And there was evening, and there was morning—the fourth day.

1:20

וַיֹּאמֶרand said אֱלֹהִיםGod יִשְׁרְצוּlet הַמַּיִםthe waters שֶׁרֶץteem נֶפֶשׁliving חַיָּהcreatures וְעוֹףand let birds יְעוֹפֵףfly עַלon הָאָרֶץthe earth עַלin פְּנֵיopen רְקִיעַexpanse הַשָּׁמָיִםthe sky

And God said, “Let the waters teem with living creatures, and let birds fly above the earth in the open expanse of the sky.”

1:21

וַיִּבְרָאso created אֱלֹהִיםGod אֶת[obj] הַתַּנִּינִםsea creatures הַגְּדֹלִיםthe great וְאֵתand [obj] כָּלevery נֶפֶשׁliving הַחַיָּהthe live הָרֹמֶשֶׂתthat moves אֲשֶׁרwhich שָׁרְצוּteemed הַמַּיִםthe waters לְמִינֵהֶםaccording to their kinds וְאֵתand [obj] כָּלevery עוֹףbird כָּנָףflight לְמִינֵהוּafter its kind וַיַּרְאand saw אֱלֹהִיםGod כִּיthat טוֹבgood

So God created the great sea creatures and every living thing that moves, with which the waters teemed according to their kinds, and every bird of flight after its kind. And God saw that it was good.

1:22

וַיְבָרֶךְthen blessed אֹתָםthem אֱלֹהִיםGod לֵאמֹרand said פְּרוּfruitful וּרְבוּand multiply וּמִלְאוּand fill אֶת[obj] הַמַּיִםthe waters בַּיַּמִּיםthe seas וְהָעוֹףand let birds יִרֶבmultiply בָּאָרֶץon the earth

Then God blessed them and said, “Be fruitful and multiply and fill the waters of the seas, and let birds multiply on the earth.”

1:23

וַיְהִיand there was עֶרֶבevening וַיְהִיand there was בֹקֶרmorning יוֹםday חֲמִישִׁיfifth

And there was evening, and there was morning—the fifth day.

1:24

וַיֹּאמֶרand said אֱלֹהִיםGod תּוֹצֵאbring forth הָאָרֶץthe earth נֶפֶשׁliving חַיָּהcreatures לְמִינָהּaccording to their kinds land בְּהֵמָהlivestock וָרֶמֶשׂland crawlers וְחַיְתוֹand beasts אֶרֶץearth לְמִינָהּaccording to their kinds it וַיְהִיand was כֵןso

And God said, “Let the earth bring forth living creatures according to their kinds: livestock, land crawlers, and beasts of the earth according to their kinds.” And it was so.

1:25

וַיַּעַשׂmade אֱלֹהִיםGod אֶת[obj] חַיַּתbeasts הָאָרֶץthe earth לְמִינָהּaccording to their kinds וְאֶתand [obj] הַבְּהֵמָהthe livestock לְמִינָהּaccording to their kinds and וְאֵתand [obj] כָּלeverything רֶמֶשׂcrawls הָאֲדָמָהthe earth לְמִינֵהוּaccording to its kind וַיַּרְאand saw אֱלֹהִיםGod כִּיfor טוֹבgood

God made the beasts of the earth according to their kinds, the livestock according to their kinds, and everything that crawls upon the earth according to its kind. And God saw that it was good.

1:26

וַיֹּאמֶרthen said אֱלֹהִיםGod נַעֲשֶׂהmake אָדָםman בְּצַלְמֵנוּin our image כִּדְמוּתֵנוּour likeness וְיִרְדּוּto rule בִדְגַתover the fish הַיָּםof the sea וּבְעוֹףand the birds הַשָּׁמַיִםthe air וּבַבְּהֵמָהover the livestock וּבְכָלand over all הָאָרֶץthe earth וּבְכָלand every הָרֶמֶשׂcreature הָרֹמֵשׂthat crawls עַלupon הָאָרֶץit

Then God said, “Let Us make man in Our image, after Our likeness, to rule over the fish of the sea and the birds of the air, over the livestock, and over all the earth itself and every creature that crawls upon it.”

1:27

וַיִּבְרָאso created אֱלֹהִיםGod אֶת[obj] הָאָדָםman בְּצַלְמוֹin his own image בְּצֶלֶםin the image אֱלֹהִיםGod בָּרָאcreated אֹתוֹhim זָכָרmale וּנְקֵבָהand female בָּרָאcreated אֹתָםthem

So God created man in His own image; in the image of God He created him; male and female He created them.

1:28

וַיְבָרֶךְblessed אֹתָםthem אֱלֹהִיםGod וַיֹּאמֶרand said לָהֶםto them אֱלֹהִיםGod פְּרוּfruitful וּרְבוּand multiply וּמִלְאוּand fill אֶת[obj] הָאָרֶץthe earth וְכִבְשֻׁהָand subdue it וּרְדוּrule בִּדְגַתover the fish הַיָּםthe sea וּבְעוֹףand the birds הַשָּׁמַיִםthe air וּבְכָלand every חַיָּהcreature הָרֹמֶשֶׂתthat crawls עַלupon הָאָרֶץthe earth

God blessed them and said to them, “Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the earth and subdue it; rule over the fish of the sea and the birds of the air and every creature that crawls upon the earth.”

1:29

וַיֹּאמֶרthen said אֱלֹהִיםGod הִנֵּהbehold נָתַתִּיgiven לָכֶםto you אֶת[obj] כָּלevery עֵשֶׂבbearing זֹרֵעַbearing plant זֶרַעseed אֲשֶׁרthat עַלon פְּנֵיface כָלall הָאָרֶץthe earth וְאֶתand [obj] כָּלevery הָעֵץtree אֲשֶׁרthat בּוֹin him פְרִיfruit עֵץtree זֹרֵעַcontains זָרַעseed לָכֶםto you יִהְיֶהbe לְאָכְלָהfor food

Then God said, “Behold, I have given you every seed-bearing plant on the face of all the earth, and every tree whose fruit contains seed. They will be yours for food.

1:30

וּלְכָלand to every חַיַּתbeast הָאָרֶץthe earth וּלְכָלand every עוֹףbird הַשָּׁמַיִםthe air וּלְכֹלand every רוֹמֵשׂcreature that crawls עַלupon הָאָרֶץthe earth אֲשֶׁרthat בּוֹin it נֶפֶשׁbreath חַיָּהlife אֶת[obj] כָּלevery יֶרֶקgreen עֵשֶׂבplant לְאָכְלָהfor food וַיְהִיand it was כֵןso

And to every beast of the earth and every bird of the air and every creature that crawls upon the earth—everything that has the breath of life in it—I have given every green plant for food.” And it was so.

1:31

וַיַּרְאand looked אֱלֹהִיםGod אֶת[obj] כָּלall אֲשֶׁרthat had עָשָׂהmade וְהִנֵּהand indeed טוֹבgood מְאֹדvery וַיְהִיand there was עֶרֶבevening וַיְהִיand there was בֹקֶרmorning יוֹםday הַשִּׁשִּׁיthe sixth

And God looked upon all that He had made, and indeed, it was very good. And there was evening, and there was morning—the sixth day.
