But while he was still alive, Abraham gave gifts to the sons of his concubines and sent them away from his son Isaac to the land of the east.

And at a ripe old age he breathed his last and died, old and contented, and was gathered to his people.

His sons Isaac and Ishmael buried him in the cave of Machpelah near Mamre, in the field of Ephron son of Zohar the Hittite.

הַשָּׂדֶהfield אֲשֶׁרthat had קָנָהbought אַבְרָהָםAbraham מֵאֵתfrom the בְּנֵיsons חֵתHittites שָׁמָּהthere קֻבַּרburied אַבְרָהָםAbraham וְשָׂרָהSarah אִשְׁתּוֹhis wife

This was the field that Abraham had bought from the Hittites. Abraham was buried there with his wife Sarah.

25:11

After Abraham’s death, God blessed his son Isaac, who lived near Beer-lahai-roi.

25:12

וְאֵלֶּהthis תֹּלְדֹתaccount יִשְׁמָעֵאלIshmael בֶּןson אַבְרָהָםAbraham’s אֲשֶׁרwhom יָלְדָהbore הָגָרHagar הַמִּצְרִיתthe Egyptian שִׁפְחַתmaidservant שָׂרָהSarah’s לְאַבְרָהָםto Abraham

This is the account of Abraham’s son Ishmael, whom Hagar the Egyptian, Sarah’s maidservant, bore to Abraham.

25:13

וְאֵלֶּהthese שְׁמוֹתnames בְּנֵיsons יִשְׁמָעֵאלIshmael בִּשְׁמֹתָםin their names לְתוֹלְדֹתָםtheir birth בְּכֹרfirstborn יִשְׁמָעֵאלIshmael נְבָיֹתNebaioth וְקֵדָרthen Kedar וְאַדְבְּאֵלAdbeel וּמִבְשָׂםMibsam

These are the names of the sons of Ishmael in the order of their birth: Nebaioth the firstborn of Ishmael, then Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam,

25:14

וּמִשְׁמָעMishma וְדוּמָהDumah וּמַשָּׂאMassa

Mishma, Dumah, Massa,

Hadad, Tema, Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah.

These were the sons of Ishmael, and these were their names by their villages and encampments—twelve princes of their tribes.

25:17

וְאֵלֶּהand these שְׁנֵיyears חַיֵּיlived יִשְׁמָעֵאלIshmael מְאַתtotal שָׁנָהyears וּשְׁלֹשִׁים137 שָׁנָהyears וְשֶׁבַעand 700 שָׁנִיםhe וַיִּגְוַעhe breathed his last וַיָּמָתand died וַיֵּאָסֶףand was gathered אֶלto עַמָּיוhis people

Ishmael lived a total of 137 years. Then he breathed his last and died, and was gathered to his people.

25:18

Ishmael’s descendants settled from Havilah to Shur, which is near the border of Egypt as you go toward Asshur. And they lived in hostility toward all their brothers.

25:19

וְאֵלֶּהthis תּוֹלְדֹתaccount יִצְחָקIsaac בֶּןson אַבְרָהָםAbraham’s אַבְרָהָםAbraham הוֹלִידfather אֶת[obj] יִצְחָקIsaac

This is the account of Abraham’s son Isaac. Abraham became the father of Isaac,

25:20

וַיְהִיand was יִצְחָקIsaac בֶּןwas אַרְבָּעִיםforty שָׁנָהyears old בְּקַחְתּוֹwhen he married אֶת[obj] רִבְקָהRebekah בַּתdaughter בְּתוּאֵלBethuel הָאֲרַמִּיthe Aramean מִפַּדַּןfrom Paddan אֲרָםPaddan aram אֲחוֹתsister לָבָןLaban הָאֲרַמִּיthe Aramean לוֹto him לְאִשָּׁהto wife

and Isaac was forty years old when he married Rebekah, the daughter of Bethuel the Aramean from Paddan-aram and the sister of Laban the Aramean.

25:21

וַיֶּעְתַּרlater prayed יִצְחָקIsaac לַיהוָהto the LORD לְנֹכַחon behalf אִשְׁתּוֹhis wife כִּיbecause עֲקָרָהbarren הִואwas וַיֵּעָתֶרheard לוֹto him יְהוָהLORD וַתַּהַרconceived רִבְקָהRebekah אִשְׁתּוֹhis wife

Later, Isaac prayed to the LORD on behalf of his wife, because she was barren. And the LORD heard his prayer, and his wife Rebekah conceived.

25:22

וַיִּתְרֹצֲצוּstruggled הַבָּנִיםthe children בְּקִרְבָּהּin her among וַתֹּאמֶרand she said אִםif כֵּןso לָמָּהwhy זֶּהthis אָנֹכִיI וַתֵּלֶךְso went לִדְרֹשׁto inquire אֶת[obj] יְהוָהLORD

But the children inside her struggled with each other, and she said, “Why is this happening to me?” So Rebekah went to inquire of the LORD,

25:23

and He declared to her: “Two nations are in your womb, and two peoples from within you will be separated; one people will be stronger than the other, and the older will serve the younger.”

25:24

וַיִּמְלְאוּand filled יָמֶיהָher time לָלֶדֶתto give birth וְהִנֵּהthere were indeed תוֹמִםtwins בְּבִטְנָהּin her womb

When her time came to give birth, there were indeed twins in her womb.

25:25

וַיֵּצֵאcame out הָרִאשׁוֹןthe first אַדְמוֹנִיred כֻּלּוֹcovered with כְּאַדֶּרֶתlike a fur שֵׂעָרhair וַיִּקְרְאוּcoat so they שְׁמוֹnamed him עֵשָׂוEsau

The first one came out red, covered with hair like a fur coat; so they named him Esau.

25:26

After this, his brother came out grasping Esau’s heel; so he was named Jacob. And Isaac was sixty years old when the twins were born.

25:27

When the boys grew up, Esau became a skillful hunter, a man of the field, while Jacob was a quiet man who stayed at home.

25:28

וַיֶּאֱהַבloved יִצְחָקIsaac אֶת[obj] עֵשָׂוEsau כִּיfor צַיִדtaste wild game בְּפִיוin his mouth וְרִבְקָהbut Rebekah אֹהֶבֶתloved אֶת[obj] יַעֲקֹבJacob

Because Isaac had a taste for wild game, he loved Esau; but Rebekah loved Jacob.

25:29

וַיָּזֶדday while יַעֲקֹבJacob נָזִידstew וַיָּבֹאcame עֵשָׂוEsau מִןfrom הַשָּׂדֶהthe field וְהוּאand was עָיֵףfamished

One day, while Jacob was cooking some stew, Esau came in from the field and was famished.

25:30

He said to Jacob, “Let me eat some of that red stew, for I am famished.” (That is why he was also called Edom.)

25:31

וַיֹּאמֶרreplied יַעֲקֹבJacob מִכְרָהsell כַיּוֹםlike the day אֶת[obj] בְּכֹרָתְךָyour birthright לִיto me

“First sell me your birthright,” Jacob replied.

25:32

וַיֹּאמֶרlook said עֵשָׂוEsau הִנֵּהbehold אָנֹכִיam הוֹלֵךְabout לָמוּתto die וְלָמָּהso what good זֶּהis לִיto me בְּכֹרָהbirthright

“Look,” said Esau, “I am about to die, so what good is a birthright to me?”

25:33

וַיֹּאמֶרsaid so יַעֲקֹבJacob הִשָּׁבְעָהswear לִּיto me כַּיּוֹםlike the day וַיִּשָּׁבַעswore לוֹEsau to וַיִּמְכֹּרand sold אֶת[obj] בְּכֹרָתוֹhim the birthright לְיַעֲקֹבJacob

“Swear to me first,” Jacob said. So Esau swore to Jacob and sold him the birthright.

25:34

וְיַעֲקֹבThen Jacob נָתַןgave לְעֵשָׂוto Esau לֶחֶםbread וּנְזִידand stew עֲדָשִׁיםlentil וַיֹּאכַלwho ate וַיֵּשְׁתְּand drank וַיָּקָםand then got וַיֵּלַךְand went וַיִּבֶזthus despised עֵשָׂוEsau אֶת[obj] הַבְּכֹרָהhis birthright

Then Jacob gave some bread and lentil stew to Esau, who ate and drank and then got up and went away. Thus Esau despised his birthright.
