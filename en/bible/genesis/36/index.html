Genesis 36

The descendants of Esau

36:1

This is the account of Esau (that is, Edom).

36:2

עֵשָׂוEsau לָקַחtook אֶת[obj] נָשָׁיוhis wives מִבְּנוֹתfrom the daughters כְּנָעַןCanaan אֶת[obj] עָדָהAdah בַּתdaughter אֵילוֹןElon הַחִתִּיthe Hittite וְאֶתand [obj] אָהֳלִיבָמָהOholibamah בַּתdaughter עֲנָהAnah בַּתgranddaughter צִבְעוֹןZibeon הַחִוִּיthe Hivite

Esau took his wives from the daughters of Canaan: Adah daughter of Elon the Hittite, Oholibamah daughter of Anah and granddaughter of Zibeon the Hivite,

36:3

וְאֶתand [obj] בָּשְׂמַתBasemath בַּתdaughter יִשְׁמָעֵאלIshmael אֲחוֹתsister נְבָיוֹתNebaioth

and Basemath daughter of Ishmael and sister of Nebaioth.

36:4

וַתֵּלֶדand bore עָדָהAdah לְעֵשָׂוto Esau אֶת[obj] אֱלִיפָזEliphaz וּבָשְׂמַתBasemath יָלְדָהgave birth אֶת[obj] רְעוּאֵלReuel

And Adah bore Eliphaz to Esau, Basemath gave birth to Reuel,

36:5

וְאָהֳלִיבָמָהand Oholibamah יָלְדָהgave birth אֶת[obj] יְעוּשׁJeush וְאֶתand [obj] יַעְלָםJalam וְאֶתand [obj] קֹרַחKorah אֵלֶּהthese בְּנֵיsons עֵשָׂוEsau אֲשֶׁרwho יֻלְּדוּborn לוֹto him בְּאֶרֶץin the land כְּנָעַןCanaan

and Oholibamah gave birth to Jeush, Jalam, and Korah. These were the sons of Esau, who were born to him in the land of Canaan.

36:6

וַיִּקַּחlater took עֵשָׂוEsau אֶת[obj] נָשָׁיוhis wives וְאֶתand [obj] בָּנָיוsons וְאֶתand [obj] בְּנֹתָיוdaughters וְאֶתand [obj] כָּלall נַפְשׁוֹתpeople בֵּיתוֹhis household וְאֶתand [obj] מִקְנֵהוּhis livestock וְאֶתand [obj] כָּלall בְּהֶמְתּוֹhis other animals וְאֵתand [obj] כָּלall קִנְיָנוֹproperty he אֲשֶׁרhad רָכַשׁacquired בְּאֶרֶץin land כְּנָעַןCanaan וַיֵּלֶךְand he moved אֶלto אֶרֶץland far מִפְּנֵיfrom before יַעֲקֹבJacob אָחִיוhis brother

Later, Esau took his wives and sons and daughters and all the people of his household, along with his livestock, all his other animals, and all the property he had acquired in Canaan, and he moved to a land far away from his brother Jacob.

36:7

כִּיfor הָיָהwere רְכוּשָׁםtheir possessions רָבgreat מִשֶּׁבֶתdwell יַחְדָּוtogether וְלֹאnot יָכְלָהcould אֶרֶץland מְגוּרֵיהֶםwhere they stayed לָשֵׂאתsupport אֹתָםthem מִפְּנֵיfrom before מִקְנֵיהֶםtheir livestock

For their possessions were too great for them to dwell together; the land where they stayed could not support them because of their livestock.

36:8

וַיֵּשֶׁבsettled עֵשָׂוEsau בְּהַרMount שֵׂעִירSeir עֵשָׂוEsau הוּאthat is אֱדוֹםEdom

So Esau (that is, Edom) settled in the area of Mount Seir.

36:9

וְאֵלֶּהthis תֹּלְדוֹתaccount עֵשָׂוEsau אֲבִיfather אֱדוֹםEdomites בְּהַרMount שֵׂעִירSeir

This is the account of Esau, the father of the Edomites, in the area of Mount Seir.

36:10

אֵלֶּהthese שְׁמוֹתnames בְּנֵיsons עֵשָׂוEsau’s אֱלִיפַזEliphaz בֶּןson עָדָהAdah אֵשֶׁתwife עֵשָׂוEsau’s רְעוּאֵלReuel בֶּןson בָּשְׂמַתBasemath אֵשֶׁתwife עֵשָׂוEsau’s

These are the names of Esau’s sons: Eliphaz son of Esau’s wife Adah, and Reuel son of Esau’s wife Basemath.

36:11

וַיִּהְיוּand were בְּנֵיsons אֱלִיפָזEliphaz תֵּימָןTeman אוֹמָרOmar צְפוֹZepho וְגַעְתָּםGatam וּקְנַזand Kenaz

The sons of Eliphaz were Teman, Omar, Zepho, Gatam, and Kenaz.

36:12

וְתִמְנַעTimna הָיְתָהwas פִילֶגֶשׁconcubine לֶאֱלִיפַזEliphaz בֶּןson עֵשָׂוEsau’s וַתֵּלֶדgave birth לֶאֱלִיפַזto Eliphaz אֶת[obj] עֲמָלֵקAmalek אֵלֶּהthese בְּנֵיsons עָדָהAdah אֵשֶׁתwife עֵשָׂוEsau’s

Additionally, Timna, a concubine of Esau’s son Eliphaz, gave birth to Amalek. These are the grandsons of Esau’s wife Adah.

36:13

וְאֵלֶּהthese בְּנֵיsons רְעוּאֵלReuel נַחַתNahath וָזֶרַחZerah שַׁמָּהShammah וּמִזָּהand Mizzah אֵלֶּהthese הָיוּare the בְּנֵיthe grandsons בָשְׂמַתBasemath אֵשֶׁתwife עֵשָׂוEsau’s

These are the sons of Reuel: Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah. They are the grandsons of Esau’s wife Basemath.

36:14

וְאֵלֶּהthese הָיוּare the בְּנֵיsons אָהֳלִיבָמָהOholibamah בַתdaughter עֲנָהAnah בַּתgranddaughter צִבְעוֹןZibeon אֵשֶׁתwife עֵשָׂוEsau’s וַתֵּלֶדwhom she bore לְעֵשָׂוto Esau אֶת[obj] יְעוּשׁJeush וְאֶתand [obj] יַעְלָםJalam וְאֶתand [obj] קֹרַחKorah

These are the sons of Esau’s wife Oholibamah (daughter of Anah and granddaughter of Zibeon) whom she bore to Esau: Jeush, Jalam, and Korah.

36:15

אֵלֶּהthese אַלּוּפֵיchiefs בְנֵיsons עֵשָׂוEsau בְּנֵיsons אֱלִיפַזEliphaz בְּכוֹרfirstborn עֵשָׂוEsau אַלּוּףchiefs תֵּימָןTeman אַלּוּףchiefs אוֹמָרOmar אַלּוּףchiefs צְפוֹZepho אַלּוּףchiefs קְנַזKenaz

These are the chiefs among the sons of Esau. The sons of Eliphaz the firstborn of Esau: Chiefs Teman, Omar, Zepho, Kenaz,

36:16

אַלּוּףchiefs קֹרַחKorah אַלּוּףchiefs גַּעְתָּםGatam אַלּוּףchiefs עֲמָלֵקAmalek אֵלֶּהthese אַלּוּפֵיchiefs אֱלִיפַזEliphaz בְּאֶרֶץin the land אֱדוֹםEdom אֵלֶּהthese בְּנֵיsons עָדָהAdah

Korah, Gatam, and Amalek. They are the chiefs of Eliphaz in the land of Edom, and they are the grandsons of Adah.

36:17

וְאֵלֶּהthese בְּנֵיsons רְעוּאֵלReuel בֶּןson עֵשָׂוEsau’s אַלּוּףchiefs נַחַתNahath אַלּוּףchiefs זֶרַחZerah אַלּוּףchiefs שַׁמָּהShammah אַלּוּףchiefs מִזָּהMizzah אֵלֶּהthese אַלּוּפֵיdescended רְעוּאֵלReuel בְּאֶרֶץin the land אֱדוֹםEdom אֵלֶּהthese בְּנֵיsons בָשְׂמַתBasemath אֵשֶׁתwife עֵשָׂוEsau’s

These are the sons of Esau’s son Reuel: Chiefs Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah. They are the chiefs descended from Reuel in the land of Edom, and they are the grandsons of Esau’s wife Basemath.

36:18

וְאֵלֶּהthese בְּנֵיsons אָהֳלִיבָמָהOholibamah אֵשֶׁתwife עֵשָׂוEsau’s אַלּוּףchiefs יְעוּשׁJeush אַלּוּףchiefs יַעְלָםJalam אַלּוּףchiefs קֹרַחKorah אֵלֶּהthese אַלּוּפֵיchiefs אָהֳלִיבָמָהdescended בַּתdaughter עֲנָהAnah אֵשֶׁתwife עֵשָׂוEsau’s

These are the sons of Esau’s wife Oholibamah: Chiefs Jeush, Jalam, and Korah. They are the chiefs descended from Esau’s wife Oholibamah, the daughter of Anah.

36:19

אֵלֶּהthese בְנֵיsons עֵשָׂוEsau וְאֵלֶּהand these אַלּוּפֵיהֶםtheir chiefs הוּאis אֱדוֹםEdom

All these are the sons of Esau (that is, Edom), and they were their chiefs.

36:20

אֵלֶּהthese בְנֵיsons שֵׂעִירSeir הַחֹרִיthe Horite יֹשְׁבֵיliving הָאָרֶץthe land לוֹטָןLotan וְשׁוֹבָלShobal וְצִבְעוֹןZibeon וַעֲנָהAnah

These are the sons of Seir the Horite, who were living in the land: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah,

36:21

וְדִשׁוֹןDishon וְאֵצֶרEzer וְדִישָׁןand Dishan אֵלֶּהthese אַלּוּפֵיchiefs הַחֹרִיthe Horites בְּנֵיdescendants שֵׂעִירSeir בְּאֶרֶץin the land אֱדוֹםEdom

Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan. They are the chiefs of the Horites, the descendants of Seir in the land of Edom.

36:22

וַיִּהְיוּand were בְנֵיsons לוֹטָןLotan חֹרִיHori וְהֵימָםand Hemam וַאֲחוֹתwas sister לוֹטָןLotan’s תִּמְנָעTimna

The sons of Lotan were Hori and Hemam. Timna was Lotan’s sister.

36:23

וְאֵלֶּהthese בְּנֵיsons שׁוֹבָלShobal עַלְוָןAlvan וּמָנַחַתManahath וְעֵיבָלEbal שְׁפוֹShepho וְאוֹנָםand Onam

These are the sons of Shobal: Alvan, Manahath, Ebal, Shepho, and Onam.

36:24

וְאֵלֶּהthese בְנֵיsons צִבְעוֹןZibeon וְאַיָּהAiah וַעֲנָהand Anah הוּאis עֲנָהAnah אֲשֶׁרwho מָצָאfound אֶת[obj] הַיֵּמִםthe hot springs בַּמִּדְבָּרin the wilderness בִּרְעֹתוֹas he was pasturing אֶת[obj] הַחֲמֹרִיםthe donkeys לְצִבְעוֹןZibeon אָבִיוhis father

These are the sons of Zibeon: Aiah and Anah. (This is the Anah who found the hot springs in the wilderness as he was pasturing the donkeys of his father Zibeon.)

36:25

וְאֵלֶּהthese בְנֵיchildren עֲנָהAnah דִּשֹׁןDishon וְאָהֳלִיבָמָהand Oholibamah בַּתdaughter עֲנָהAnah

These are the children of Anah: Dishon and Oholibamah daughter of Anah.

36:26

וְאֵלֶּהthese בְּנֵיsons דִישָׁןDishon חֶמְדָּןCheran וְאֶשְׁבָּןEshban וְיִתְרָןIthran וּכְרָןand Cheran

These are the sons of Dishon: Hemdan, Eshban, Ithran, and Cheran.

36:27

אֵלֶּהthese בְּנֵיsons אֵצֶרEzer בִּלְהָןBilhan וְזַעֲוָןZaavan וַעֲקָןand Akan

These are the sons of Ezer: Bilhan, Zaavan, and Akan.

36:28

אֵלֶּהthese בְנֵיsons דִישָׁןDishan עוּץUz וַאֲרָןand Aran

These are the sons of Dishan: Uz and Aran.

36:29

אֵלֶּהthese אַלּוּפֵיchiefs הַחֹרִיthe Horites אַלּוּףchiefs לוֹטָןLotan אַלּוּףchiefs שׁוֹבָלShobal אַלּוּףchiefs צִבְעוֹןZibeon אַלּוּףchiefs עֲנָהAnah

These are the chiefs of the Horites: Chiefs Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah,

36:30

אַלּוּףchiefs דִּשֹׁןDishon אַלּוּףchiefs אֵצֶרEzer אַלּוּףchiefs דִּישָׁןDishan אֵלֶּהthese אַלּוּפֵיchiefs הַחֹרִיthe Horites לְאַלֻּפֵיהֶםaccording to their divisions בְּאֶרֶץin the land שֵׂעִירSeir

Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan. They are the chiefs of the Horites, according to their divisions in the land of Seir.

36:31

וְאֵלֶּהthese הַמְּלָכִיםthe kings אֲשֶׁרwho מָלְכוּreigned בְּאֶרֶץin the land אֱדוֹםEdom לִפְנֵיbefore any מְלָךְreigned מֶלֶךְking לִבְנֵיto sons יִשְׂרָאֵלIsraelites

These are the kings who reigned in the land of Edom before any king reigned over the Israelites:

36:32

וַיִּמְלֹךְreigned בֶּאֱדוֹםin Edom בֶּלַעBela בֶּןson בְּעוֹרBeor וְשֵׁםname עִירוֹhis city דִּנְהָבָהDinhabah

Bela son of Beor reigned in Edom; the name of his city was Dinhabah.

36:33

וַיָּמָתwhen died בָּלַעBela וַיִּמְלֹךְreigned תַּחְתָּיוin his place יוֹבָבJobab בֶּןson זֶרַחZerah מִבָּצְרָהfrom Bozrah

When Bela died, Jobab son of Zerah from Bozrah reigned in his place.

36:34

וַיָּמָתwhen died יוֹבָבJobab וַיִּמְלֹךְreigned תַּחְתָּיוin his place חֻשָׁםHusham מֵאֶרֶץfrom the land הַתֵּימָנִיthe Temanites

When Jobab died, Husham from the land of the Temanites reigned in his place.

36:35

וַיָּמָתwhen died חֻשָׁםHusham וַיִּמְלֹךְreigned תַּחְתָּיוin his place הֲדַדHadad בֶּןson בְּדַדBedad הַמַּכֶּהwho defeated אֶת[obj] מִדְיָןMidian בִּשְׂדֵהin the country מוֹאָבMoab וְשֵׁםand the name עִירוֹhis city was עֲוִיתAvith

When Husham died, Hadad son of Bedad, who defeated Midian in the country of Moab, reigned in his place. And the name of his city was Avith.

36:36

וַיָּמָתwhen died הֲדָדHadad וַיִּמְלֹךְreigned תַּחְתָּיוin his place שַׂמְלָהSamlah מִמַּשְׂרֵקָהfrom Masrekah

When Hadad died, Samlah from Masrekah reigned in his place.

36:37

וַיָּמָתwhen died שַׂמְלָהSamlah וַיִּמְלֹךְreigned תַּחְתָּיוin his place שָׁאוּלShaul מֵרְחֹבוֹתfrom Rehoboth הַנָּהָרthe Euphrates

When Samlah died, Shaul from Rehoboth on the Euphrates reigned in his place.

36:38

וַיָּמָתwhen died שָׁאוּלShaul וַיִּמְלֹךְreigned תַּחְתָּיוin his place בַּעַלBaal חָנָןhanan בֶּןson עַכְבּוֹרAchbor

When Shaul died, Baal-hanan son of Achbor reigned in his place.

36:39

וַיָּמָתdied בַּעַלBaal חָנָןhanan בֶּןson עַכְבּוֹרAchbor וַיִּמְלֹךְreigned תַּחְתָּיוin his place his הֲדַרHadad וְשֵׁםnamed עִירוֹcity was פָּעוּPau וְשֵׁםname אִשְׁתּוֹhis wife’s was מְהֵיטַבְאֵלMehetabel בַּתdaughter מַטְרֵדMatred בַּתdaughter מֵיme זָהָבzahab

When Baal-hanan son of Achbor died, Hadad reigned in his place. His city was named Pau, and his wife’s name was Mehetabel daughter of Matred, the daughter of Me-zahab.

36:40

וְאֵלֶּהthese שְׁמוֹתnames אַלּוּפֵיchiefs עֵשָׂוEsau’s לְמִשְׁפְּחֹתָםaccording to their families לִמְקֹמֹתָםand regions their בִּשְׁמֹתָםby names אַלּוּףchiefs תִּמְנָעTimna אַלּוּףchiefs עַלְוָהAlvah אַלּוּףchiefs יְתֵתJetheth

These are the names of Esau’s chiefs, according to their families and regions, by their names: Chiefs Timna, Alvah, Jetheth,

36:41

אַלּוּףOholibamah אָהֳלִיבָמָהOholibamah אַלּוּףchiefs אֵלָהElah אַלּוּףchiefs פִּינֹןPinon

Oholibamah, Elah, Pinon,

36:42

אַלּוּףchiefs קְנַזKenaz אַלּוּףchiefs תֵּימָןTeman אַלּוּףchiefs מִבְצָרMibzar

Kenaz, Teman, Mibzar,

36:43

אַלּוּףchiefs מַגְדִּיאֵלMagdiel אַלּוּףchiefs עִירָםIram אֵלֶּהthese אַלּוּפֵיchiefs אֱדוֹםEdom לְמֹשְׁבֹתָםaccording to their settlements בְּאֶרֶץin the land אֲחֻזָּתָםthey possessed הוּאwas עֵשָׂוEsau אֲבִיfather אֱדוֹםEdom

Magdiel, and Iram. These were the chiefs of Edom, according to their settlements in the land they possessed. Esau was the father of the Edomites.
