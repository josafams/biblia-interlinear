Genesis 35

Back to Bethel; the death of Rachel

Open in the interlinear reader

35:1

וַיֹּאמֶרthen said אֱלֹהִיםGod אֶלto יַעֲקֹבJacob קוּםarise עֲלֵהgo up בֵיתBethel אֵלBethel וְשֶׁבand settle שָׁםthere וַעֲשֵׂהbuild שָׁםthere מִזְבֵּחַaltar לָאֵלto the God הַנִּרְאֶהwho appeared אֵלֶיךָto you בְּבָרְחֲךָwhen you fled מִפְּנֵיfrom before עֵשָׂוEsau אָחִיךָyour brother

Then God said to Jacob, “Arise, go up to Bethel, and settle there. Build an altar there to the God who appeared to you when you fled from your brother Esau.”

35:2

So Jacob told his household and all who were with him, “Get rid of the foreign gods that are among you. Purify yourselves and change your garments.

35:3

וְנָקוּמָהarise וְנַעֲלֶהand go בֵּיתBethel אֵלBethel וְאֶעֱשֶׂהI will build שָּׁםthere מִזְבֵּחַaltar לָאֵלto God הָעֹנֶהwho answered אֹתִיme בְּיוֹםin day צָרָתִיdistress וַיְהִיhe has been עִמָּדִיwith me בַּדֶּרֶךְin the road אֲשֶׁרthat הָלָכְתִּיgone

Then let us arise and go to Bethel. I will build an altar there to God, who answered me in my day of distress. He has been with me wherever I have gone.”

35:4

וַיִּתְּנוּso they gave אֶלto יַעֲקֹבJacob אֵת[obj] כָּלall אֱלֹהֵיgods הַנֵּכָרtheir foreign אֲשֶׁרthat בְּיָדָםin their hand וְאֶתand [obj] הַנְּזָמִיםearrings אֲשֶׁרthat בְּאָזְנֵיהֶםin their hearing וַיִּטְמֹןand buried אֹתָםthem יַעֲקֹבJacob תַּחַתunder הָאֵלָהthe oak אֲשֶׁרthat עִםwith שְׁכֶםShechem

So they gave Jacob all their foreign gods and all their earrings, and Jacob buried them under the oak near Shechem.

35:5

וַיִּסָּעוּthey set out וַיְהִיand was חִתַּתterror אֱלֹהִיםGod עַלover הֶעָרִיםthe cities אֲשֶׁרthat סְבִיבֹתֵיהֶםsurrounding וְלֹאdid not רָדְפוּpursue אַחֲרֵיafter בְּנֵיsons יַעֲקֹבJacob’s

As they set out, a terror from God fell over the surrounding cities, so that they did not pursue Jacob’s sons.

35:6

וַיָּבֹאarrived יַעֲקֹבJacob לוּזָהLuz אֲשֶׁרthat בְּאֶרֶץin the land כְּנַעַןCanaan הִואis בֵּיתBethel אֵלBethel הוּאhe וְכָלand everyone הָעָםthe people אֲשֶׁרthat עִמּוֹwith him

So Jacob and everyone with him arrived in Luz (that is, Bethel) in the land of Canaan.

35:7

וַיִּבֶןJacob built שָׁםthere מִזְבֵּחַaltar וַיִּקְרָאand he called לַמָּקוֹםthat place אֵלEl בֵּיתbethel אֵלBethel כִּיbecause שָׁםthere נִגְלוּrevealed אֵלָיוhimself to Jacob הָאֱלֹהִיםthat God בְּבָרְחוֹfled his מִפְּנֵיfrom before אָחִיוbrother

There Jacob built an altar, and he called that place El-bethel, because it was there that God had revealed Himself to Jacob as he fled from his brother.

35:8

וַתָּמָתdied דְּבֹרָהDeborah מֵינֶקֶתnurse רִבְקָהRebekah’s וַתִּקָּבֵרand was buried מִתַּחַתunder לְבֵיתto Bethel אֵלBethel תַּחַתbelow הָאַלּוֹןthe oak וַיִּקְרָאand called שְׁמוֹnamed it אַלּוֹןAllon בָּכוּתbachuth

Now Deborah, Rebekah’s nurse, died and was buried under the oak below Bethel. So Jacob named it Allon-bachuth.

35:9

וַיֵּרָאappeared אֱלֹהִיםGod אֶלto יַעֲקֹבJacob עוֹדagain בְּבֹאוֹhad returned מִפַּדַּןfrom Paddan אֲרָםaram וַיְבָרֶךְand blessed אֹתוֹhim

After Jacob had returned from Paddan-aram, God appeared to him again and blessed him.

35:10

וַיֹּאמֶרand said לוֹto him אֱלֹהִיםGod שִׁמְךָyour name יַעֲקֹבJacob לֹאno יִקָּרֵאcalled שִׁמְךָyour name עוֹדlonger יַעֲקֹבJacob כִּיfor אִםif יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael יִהְיֶהbe שְׁמֶךָyour name וַיִּקְרָאand called אֶת[obj] שְׁמוֹnamed him יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael

And God said to him, “Though your name is Jacob, you will no longer be called Jacob. Instead, your name will be Israel.” So God named him Israel.

35:11

וַיֹּאמֶרand told לוֹto him אֱלֹהִיםGod אֲנִיam אֵלGod שַׁדַּיAlmighty פְּרֵהfruitful וּרְבֵהand multiply גּוֹיnation וּקְהַלeven a company גּוֹיִםnations יִהְיֶהbe מִמֶּךָּfrom you וּמְלָכִיםand kings מֵחֲלָצֶיךָdescend from you יֵצֵאוּgo

And God told him, “I am God Almighty. Be fruitful and multiply. A nation—even a company of nations—shall come from you, and kings shall descend from you.

35:12

וְאֶתand [obj] הָאָרֶץthe land אֲשֶׁרthat נָתַתִּיgave לְאַבְרָהָםto Abraham וּלְיִצְחָקand Isaac לְךָto אֶתְּנֶנָּהgive you וּלְזַרְעֲךָto your descendants אַחֲרֶיךָafter you אֶתֵּןgive אֶת[obj] הָאָרֶץthis land

The land that I gave to Abraham and Isaac I will give to you, and I will give this land to your descendants after you.”

35:13

וַיַּעַלthen went מֵעָלָיוfrom the him אֱלֹהִיםGod בַּמָּקוֹםthe place אֲשֶׁרhad דִּבֶּרspoken אִתּוֹwith him

Then God went up from the place where He had spoken with him.

35:14

וַיַּצֵּבso set יַעֲקֹבJacob מַצֵּבָהpillar בַּמָּקוֹםin the place where אֲשֶׁרhad דִּבֶּרspoken אִתּוֹwith him מַצֶּבֶתmarker אָבֶןstone וַיַּסֵּךְand he poured עָלֶיהָon it נֶסֶךְdrink offering וַיִּצֹקand anointed עָלֶיהָon her שָׁמֶןoil

So Jacob set up a pillar in the place where God had spoken with him—a stone marker—and he poured out a drink offering on it and anointed it with oil.

35:15

וַיִּקְרָאcalled יַעֲקֹבJacob אֶת[obj] שֵׁםname הַמָּקוֹםthe place אֲשֶׁרhad דִּבֶּרspoken אִתּוֹwith him שָׁםwhere אֱלֹהִיםGod בֵּיתBethel אֵלBethel

Jacob called the place where God had spoken with him Bethel.

35:16

וַיִּסְעוּlater they set out מִבֵּיתfrom Bethel אֵלBethel וַיְהִיwere עוֹדstill כִּבְרַתsome הָאָרֶץdistance לָבוֹאto come אֶפְרָתָהEphrath וַתֵּלֶדbirth and רָחֵלRachel וַתְּקַשׁdifficult בְּלִדְתָּהּher labor

Later, they set out from Bethel, and while they were still some distance from Ephrath, Rachel began to give birth, and her labor was difficult.

35:17

וַיְהִיand was בְהַקְשֹׁתָהּduring her severe בְּלִדְתָּהּlabor וַתֹּאמֶרsaid לָהּto her הַמְיַלֶּדֶתthe midwife אַלdo not תִּירְאִיafraid כִּיfor גַםalso זֶהthis לָךְfor you בֵּןson

During her severe labor, the midwife said to her, “Do not be afraid, for you are having another son.”

35:18

וַיְהִיlast בְּצֵאתin went נַפְשָׁהּher breath כִּיfor מֵתָהdying וַתִּקְרָאshe שְׁמוֹnamed him בֶּןBen אוֹנִיoni וְאָבִיוbut his father קָרָאcalled לוֹto him בִנְיָמִיןBenjamin

And with her last breath—for she was dying—she named him Ben-oni. But his father called him Benjamin.

35:19

וַתָּמָתso died רָחֵלRachel וַתִּקָּבֵרand was buried בְּדֶרֶךְon the way אֶפְרָתָהEphrath הִואthat is בֵּיתBethlehem לָחֶםBethlehem

So Rachel died and was buried on the way to Ephrath (that is, Bethlehem).

35:20

וַיַּצֵּבset יַעֲקֹבJacob מַצֵּבָהpillar עַלon קְבֻרָתָהּher grave הִואis מַצֶּבֶתmarks קְבֻרַתtomb רָחֵלRachel’s עַדto הַיּוֹםthis day

Jacob set up a pillar on her grave; it marks Rachel’s tomb to this day.

35:21

וַיִּסַּעagain set out יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael וַיֵּטand pitched אָהֳלֹהhis tent מֵהָלְאָהbeyond לְמִגְדַּלthe tower עֵדֶרEder

Israel again set out and pitched his tent beyond the Tower of Eder.

35:22

While Israel was living in that region, Reuben went in and slept with his father’s concubine Bilhah, and Israel heard about it. Jacob had twelve sons:

35:23

בְּנֵיsons לֵאָהLeah בְּכוֹרfirstborn יַעֲקֹבJacob רְאוּבֵןReuben וְשִׁמְעוֹןSimeon וְלֵוִיLevi וִיהוּדָהJudah וְיִשָּׂשכָרIssachar וּזְבוּלֻןand Zebulun

The sons of Leah were Reuben the firstborn of Jacob, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun.

35:24

בְּנֵיsons רָחֵלRachel יוֹסֵףJoseph וּבִנְיָמִןand Benjamin

The sons of Rachel were Joseph and Benjamin.

35:25

וּבְנֵיsons בִלְהָהBilhah שִׁפְחַתmaidservant רָחֵלRachel’s דָּןDan וְנַפְתָּלִיand Naphtali

The sons of Rachel’s maidservant Bilhah were Dan and Naphtali.

35:26

וּבְנֵיand the sons זִלְפָּהZilpah שִׁפְחַתmaidservant לֵאָהLeah’s גָּדGad וְאָשֵׁרAsher אֵלֶּהthese בְּנֵיsons יַעֲקֹבJacob אֲשֶׁרwho יֻלַּדborn לוֹto him בְּפַדַּןin Paddan אֲרָםPaddan aram

And the sons of Leah’s maidservant Zilpah were Gad and Asher. These are the sons of Jacob, who were born to him in Paddan-aram.

35:27

Jacob returned to his father Isaac at Mamre, near Kiriath-arba (that is, Hebron), where Abraham and Isaac had stayed.

35:28

וַיִּהְיוּand were יְמֵיdays יִצְחָקIsaac מְאַתlived שָׁנָהyears וּשְׁמֹנִיםlived 180 שָׁנָהyears

And Isaac lived 180 years.

35:29

Then he breathed his last and died and was gathered to his people, old and full of years. And his sons Esau and Jacob buried him.
