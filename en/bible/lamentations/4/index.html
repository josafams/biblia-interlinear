Lamentations 4

Hunger and ruin in the besieged city

How the gold has become tarnished, the pure gold has become dull! The gems of the temple lie scattered on every street corner.

How the precious sons of Zion, once worth their weight in pure gold, are now esteemed as jars of clay, the work of a potter’s hands!

4:3

Even jackals offer their breasts to nurse their young, but the daughter of my people has become cruel, like an ostrich in the wilderness.

4:4

The nursing infant’s tongue clings in thirst to the roof of his mouth. Little children beg for bread, but no one gives them any.

4:5

Those who once ate delicacies are destitute in the streets; those brought up in crimson huddle in ash heaps.

4:6

The punishment of the daughter of my people is greater than that of Sodom, which was overthrown in an instant without a hand turned to help her.

4:7

זַכּוּdignitaries נְזִירֶיהָbrighter מִשֶּׁלֶגthan snow whiter צַחוּwhiter מֵחָלָבthan milk אָדְמוּbodies עֶצֶםruddy מִפְּנִינִיםthan rubies סַפִּירsapphires גִּזְרָתָםtheir appearance

Her dignitaries were brighter than snow, whiter than milk; their bodies were more ruddy than rubies, their appearance like sapphires.

4:8

חָשַׁךְnow מִשְּׁחוֹרblacker than soot תָּאֳרָםsoot they לֹאnot נִכְּרוּrecognized בַּחוּצוֹתin the streets צָפַדshriveled עוֹרָםtheir skin עַלon עַצְמָםtheir bones יָבֵשׁdry הָיָהbecome כָעֵץas a stick

But now their appearance is blacker than soot; they are not recognized in the streets. Their skin has shriveled on their bones; it has become as dry as a stick.

4:9

טוֹבִיםbetter הָיוּwere חַלְלֵיslain חֶרֶבsword מֵחַלְלֵיdie רָעָבhunger שֶׁהֵםwho they יָזוּבוּwaste מְדֻקָּרִיםpierced מִתְּנוּבֹתpain because שָׂדָיfields

Those slain by the sword are better off than those who die of hunger, who waste away, pierced with pain because the fields lack produce.

4:10

יְדֵיhands נָשִׁיםwomen רַחֲמָנִיּוֹתcompassionate בִּשְּׁלוּcooked יַלְדֵיהֶןtheir children הָיוּown לְבָרוֹתbecame לָמוֹto them בְּשֶׁבֶרin the destruction בַּתdaughter עַמִּיmy people

The hands of compassionate women have cooked their own children, who became their food in the destruction of the daughter of my people.

4:11

כִּלָּהexhausted יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] חֲמָתוֹhis wrath שָׁפַךְpoured חֲרוֹןfierce אַפּוֹhis anger וַיַּצֶּתhe has kindled אֵשׁfire בְּצִיּוֹןin Zion וַתֹּאכַלconsumed יְסוֹדֹתֶיהָher foundations

The LORD has exhausted His wrath; He has poured out His fierce anger; He has kindled a fire in Zion, and it has consumed her foundations.

4:12

לֹאnot הֶאֱמִינוּbelieve מַלְכֵיkings אֶרֶץearth כֹּלany יֹשְׁבֵיpeople תֵבֵלworld כִּיthat יָבֹאenter צַרfoe וְאוֹיֵבenemy or בְּשַׁעֲרֵיgates יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem

The kings of the earth did not believe, nor any people of the world, that an enemy or a foe could enter the gates of Jerusalem.

4:13

מֵחַטֹּאתfor the sins נְבִיאֶיהָher prophets עֲוֺנוֹתguilt כֹּהֲנֶיהָher priests הַשֹּׁפְכִיםwho shed בְּקִרְבָּהּin her midst דַּםblood צַדִּיקִיםrighteous

But this was for the sins of her prophets and the guilt of her priests, who shed the blood of the righteous in her midst.

4:14

נָעוּwandered עִוְרִיםblind בַּחוּצוֹתin the streets נְגֹאֲלוּdefiled בַּדָּםby this blood בְּלֹאthat no יוּכְלוּdared יִגְּעוּtouch בִּלְבֻשֵׁיהֶםtheir garments

They wandered blind in the streets, defiled by this blood, so that no one dared to touch their garments.

4:15

סוּרוּaway טָמֵאunclean קָרְאוּshouted לָמוֹto them סוּרוּaway סוּרוּaway אַלdo not תִּגָּעוּtouch כִּיfor נָצוּfled גַּםand נָעוּwandered אָמְרוּsaid בַּגּוֹיִםamong the nations לֹאno יוֹסִיפוּlonger לָגוּרcan stay

“Go away! Unclean!” men shouted at them. “Away, away! Do not touch us!” So they fled and wandered. Among the nations it was said, “They can stay here no longer.”

4:16

פְּנֵיpresence יְהוָהLORD חִלְּקָםregards them לֹאno יוֹסִיףmore לְהַבִּיטָםscattered them פְּנֵיface כֹהֲנִיםpriests לֹאno נָשָׂאוּshown honor וּזְקֵנִיםelders לֹאno חָנָנוּfavor

The presence of the LORD has scattered them; He regards them no more. The priests are shown no honor; the elders find no favor.

4:17

עוֹדֵינוּwhile our תִּכְלֶינָהfail עֵינֵינוּwe looked אֶלto עֶזְרָתֵנוּhelp we הָבֶלvain בְּצִפִּיָּתֵנוּwatched from our צִפִּינוּtowers אֶלto גּוֹיnation לֹאnot יוֹשִׁעַsave

All the while our eyes were failing as we looked in vain for help. We watched from our towers for a nation that could not save us.

4:18

צָדוּstalked צְעָדֵינוּstep מִלֶּכֶתnot walk בִּרְחֹבֹתֵינוּin our streets קָרַבnear קִצֵּינוּour end מָלְאוּdrew יָמֵינוּour time ran כִּיfor בָאcome קִצֵּינוּour end

They stalked our every step, so that we could not walk in our streets. Our end drew near, our time ran out, for our end had come!

4:19

קַלִּיםswift הָיוּwere רֹדְפֵינוּchased us מִנִּשְׁרֵיswifter than the eagles שָׁמָיִםsky עַלover הֶהָרִיםthe mountains דְּלָקֻנוּambushed us בַּמִּדְבָּרin the wilderness אָרְבוּhide לָנוּto us

Those who chased us were swifter than the eagles in the sky; they pursued us over the mountains and ambushed us in the wilderness.

4:20

רוּחַbreath אַפֵּינוּbreath of our מְשִׁיחַanointed יְהוָהLORD’s נִלְכַּדcaptured בִּשְׁחִיתוֹתָםin their pits אֲשֶׁרhad אָמַרְנוּsaid בְּצִלּוֹunder his shadow נִחְיֶהlive בַגּוֹיִםamong the nations

The LORD’s anointed, the breath of our life, was captured in their pits. We had said of him, “Under his shadow we will live among the nations.”

4:21

שִׂישִׂיrejoice וְשִׂמְחִיand be glad בַּתdaughter אֱדוֹםEdom יוֹשֶׁבֶתdwell בְּאֶרֶץin the land עוּץUz גַּםeven עָלַיִךְon you תַּעֲבָרpass כּוֹסcup תִּשְׁכְּרִיdrunk וְתִתְעָרִיand expose

So rejoice and be glad, O Daughter of Edom, you who dwell in the land of Uz. Yet the cup will pass to you as well; you will get drunk and expose yourself.

4:22

תַּםcomplete עֲוֺנֵךְyour punishment בַּתdaughter צִיּוֹןZion לֹאnot יוֹסִיףprolong לְהַגְלוֹתֵךְyour exile פָּקַדpunish עֲוֺנֵךְyour iniquity O בַּתdaughter אֱדוֹםEdom גִּלָּהexpose עַלon חַטֹּאתָיִךְyour sins

O Daughter of Zion, your punishment is complete; He will not prolong your exile. But He will punish your iniquity, O Daughter of Edom; He will expose your sins.
