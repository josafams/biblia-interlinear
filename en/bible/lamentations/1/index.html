Lamentations 1

Jerusalem, the widowed city without comfort

Open in the interlinear reader

1:1

אֵיכָהhow יָשְׁבָהlonely בָדָדlies הָעִירthe city רַבָּתִיfull עָםpeople הָיְתָהbecome כְּאַלְמָנָהa widow רַּבָּתִיgreat בַגּוֹיִםamong the nations שָׂרָתִיprincess בַּמְּדִינוֹתthe provinces הָיְתָהbecome לָמַסa slave

How lonely lies the city, once so full of people! She who was great among the nations has become a widow. The princess of the provinces has become a slave.

1:2

בָּכוֹweeping תִבְכֶּהweeps aloud בַּלַּיְלָהin the night וְדִמְעָתָהּwith tears her עַלupon לֶחֱיָהּcheeks אֵיןno לָהּto her מְנַחֵםcomfort מִכָּלall אֹהֲבֶיהָher lovers כָּלall רֵעֶיהָher friends בָּגְדוּbetrayed בָהּin her הָיוּbecome לָהּto her לְאֹיְבִיםenemies

She weeps aloud in the night, with tears upon her cheeks. Among all her lovers there is no one to comfort her. All her friends have betrayed her; they have become her enemies.

1:3

גָּלְתָהinto exile יְהוּדָהJudah מֵעֹנִיunder affliction וּמֵרֹבand harsh עֲבֹדָהslavery הִיאis יָשְׁבָהdwells בַגּוֹיִםamong the nations לֹאno מָצְאָהfinds מָנוֹחַplace כָּלall רֹדְפֶיהָher pursuers הִשִּׂיגוּהָovertaken her בֵּיןbetween הַמְּצָרִיםher distress

Judah has gone into exile under affliction and harsh slavery; she dwells among the nations but finds no place to rest. All her pursuers have overtaken her in the midst of her distress.

1:4

דַּרְכֵיroads צִיּוֹןZion אֲבֵלוֹתmourn מִבְּלִיbecause no בָּאֵיcomes מוֹעֵדappointed feasts כָּלall שְׁעָרֶיהָher gates שׁוֹמֵמִיןdeserted כֹּהֲנֶיהָher priests נֶאֱנָחִיםgroan בְּתוּלֹתֶיהָher maidens נּוּגוֹתgrieve וְהִיאand she מַרbitter anguish לָהּto her

The roads to Zion mourn, because no one comes to her appointed feasts. All her gates are deserted; her priests groan, her maidens grieve, and she herself is bitter with anguish.

1:5

הָיוּbecome צָרֶיהָher foes לְרֹאשׁmasters אֹיְבֶיהָher enemies שָׁלוּease כִּיfor יְהוָהLORD הוֹגָהּher grief עַלon רֹבmany פְּשָׁעֶיהָtransgressions her עוֹלָלֶיהָchildren הָלְכוּgone שְׁבִיcaptives לִפְנֵיbefore צָרenemy

Her foes have become her masters; her enemies are at ease. For the LORD has brought her grief because of her many transgressions. Her children have gone away as captives before the enemy.

1:6

וַיֵּצֵאdeparted מִבַּתfrom the daughter צִיּוֹןZion כָּלall הֲדָרָהּsplendor הָיוּwere שָׂרֶיהָher princes are כְּאַיָּלִיםlike deer לֹאno מָצְאוּfind מִרְעֶהpasture וַיֵּלְכוּthey בְלֹאin without כֹחַstrength לִפְנֵיface רוֹדֵףhunter

All the splendor has departed from the Daughter of Zion. Her princes are like deer that find no pasture; they lack the strength to flee in the face of the hunter.

1:7

זָכְרָהremembers יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem יְמֵיdays עָנְיָהּher affliction וּמְרוּדֶיהָand wandering כֹּלall מַחֲמֻדֶיהָtreasures that אֲשֶׁרthat הָיוּwere מִימֵיin days קֶדֶםold בִּנְפֹלfell עַמָּהּher people בְּיַדinto hands צָרenemy וְאֵיןshe received no עוֹזֵרhelp לָהּto her רָאוּהָlooked upon her צָרִיםenemies שָׂחֲקוּlaughing עַלon מִשְׁבַּתֶּהָher downfall

In the days of her affliction and wandering Jerusalem remembers all the treasures that were hers in days of old. When her people fell into enemy hands she received no help. Her enemies looked upon her, laughing at her downfall.

1:8

חֵטְאgreatly חָטְאָהsinned יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem עַלon כֵּןtherefore לְנִידָהscorn הָיָתָהwas כָּלall מְכַבְּדֶיהָhonored her הִזִּילוּהָnow despise her כִּיfor רָאוּseen עֶרְוָתָהּher nakedness גַּםeven הִיאis נֶאֶנְחָהgroans וַתָּשָׁבand turns אָחוֹרaway

Jerusalem has sinned greatly; therefore she has become an object of scorn. All who honored her now despise her, for they have seen her nakedness; she herself groans and turns away.

1:9

טֻמְאָתָהּher uncleanness בְּשׁוּלֶיהָstains her skirts she לֹאnot זָכְרָהconsider אַחֲרִיתָהּher end her וַתֵּרֶדdownfall פְּלָאִיםastounding אֵיןno מְנַחֵםcomfort לָהּto her רְאֵהlook יְהוָהLORD אֶת[obj] עָנְיִיmy affliction כִּיfor הִגְדִּילtriumphed אוֹיֵבenemy

Her uncleanness stains her skirts; she did not consider her end. Her downfall was astounding; there was no one to comfort her. Look, O LORD, on my affliction, for the enemy has triumphed!

1:10

יָדוֹhis hand פָּרַשׂseized צָרadversary עַלon כָּלall מַחֲמַדֶּיהָher treasures כִּיfor רָאֲתָהseen גוֹיִםnations בָּאוּenter מִקְדָּשָׁהּher sanctuary אֲשֶׁרhad צִוִּיתָהforbidden לֹאnot יָבֹאוּenter בַקָּהָלassembly לָךְto you

The adversary has seized all her treasures. For she has seen the nations enter her sanctuary— those You had forbidden to enter Your assembly.

1:11

כָּלall עַמָּהּher people נֶאֱנָחִיםgroan מְבַקְּשִׁיםsearch לֶחֶםbread נָתְנוּtraded מַחֲמַדֵּיהֶםtraded their treasures בְּאֹכֶלfor food לְהָשִׁיבto keep נָפֶשׁalive רְאֵהlook יְהוָהLORD וְהַבִּיטָהand consider כִּיfor הָיִיתִיbecome זוֹלֵלָהdespised

All her people groan as they search for bread. They have traded their treasures for food to keep themselves alive. Look, O LORD, and consider, for I have become despised.

1:12

לוֹאnot אֲלֵיכֶםto you כָּלall עֹבְרֵיpass דֶרֶךְway הַבִּיטוּlook וּרְאוּand see אִםif יֵשׁthere מַכְאוֹבsorrow כְּמַכְאֹבִיlike my pain אֲשֶׁרwhich עוֹלַלinflicted לִיto me אֲשֶׁרwhich הוֹגָהgrieve יְהוָהLORD בְּיוֹםon the day חֲרוֹןfierce אַפּוֹhis anger

Is this nothing to you, all you who pass by? Look around and see! Is there any sorrow like mine, which was inflicted on me, which the LORD made me suffer on the day of His fierce anger?

1:13

מִמָּרוֹםfrom on high שָׁלַחsent אֵשׁfire בְּעַצְמֹתַיmy bones וַיִּרְדֶּנָּהand it overpowered פָּרַשׂspread רֶשֶׁתnet לְרַגְלַיfor my feet הֱשִׁיבַנִיturned me אָחוֹרback נְתָנַנִיmade me שֹׁמֵמָהdesolate כָּלall הַיּוֹםthe day דָּוָהfaint

He sent fire from on high, and it overpowered my bones. He spread a net for my feet and turned me back. He made me desolate, faint all the day long.

1:14

נִשְׂקַדbound עֹלyoke פְּשָׁעַיmy transgressions בְּיָדוֹby his hand יִשְׂתָּרְגוּknit עָלוּdraped עַלover צַוָּארִיmy neck הִכְשִׁילbroken כֹּחִיmy strength נְתָנַנִיdelivered me אֲדֹנָיLord בִּידֵיinto the hands לֹאnot אוּכַלcannot קוּםwithstand

My transgressions are bound into a yoke, knit together by His hand; they are draped over my neck, and the Lord has broken my strength. He has delivered me into the hands of those I cannot withstand.

1:15

סִלָּהrejected כָלall אַבִּירַיmighty אֲדֹנָיLord בְּקִרְבִּיin my midst he has קָרָאsummoned עָלַיagainst me מוֹעֵדcrush לִשְׁבֹּרto crush בַּחוּרָיmy young גַּתwinepress דָּרַךְtrampled אֲדֹנָיLord has לִבְתוּלַתthe virgin בַּתdaughter יְהוּדָהJudah

The Lord has rejected all the mighty men in my midst; He has summoned an army against me to crush my young warriors. Like grapes in a winepress, the Lord has trampled the Virgin Daughter of Judah.

1:16

עַלfor אֵלֶּהthese אֲנִיI בוֹכִיָּהweep עֵינִיmy eyes עֵינִיmy eyes יֹרְדָהtears מַּיִםtears כִּיfor רָחַקnearby מִמֶּנִּיfrom me מְנַחֵםcomfort מֵשִׁיבrevive נַפְשִׁיsoul הָיוּwere בָנַיmy children שׁוֹמֵמִיםdestitute כִּיbecause גָבַרprevailed אוֹיֵבenemy

For these things I weep; my eyes flow with tears. For there is no one nearby to comfort me, no one to revive my soul. My children are destitute because the enemy has prevailed.

1:17

פֵּרְשָׂהstretches out צִיּוֹןZion בְּיָדֶיהָher hands אֵיןno מְנַחֵםcomfort לָהּto her צִוָּהcommanded יְהוָהLORD לְיַעֲקֹבagainst Jacob סְבִיבָיוhis neighbors צָרָיוhis foes הָיְתָהbecome יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem לְנִדָּהbecome an unclean בֵּינֵיהֶםamong them

Zion stretches out her hands, but there is no one to comfort her. The LORD has decreed against Jacob that his neighbors become his foes. Jerusalem has become an unclean thing among them.

1:18

צַדִּיקrighteous הוּאis יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor פִיהוּhis command מָרִיתִיrebelled against שִׁמְעוּlisten נָאplease כָלall הָעַמִּיםpeople וּרְאוּlook מַכְאֹבִיupon my suffering בְּתוּלֹתַיmaidens וּבַחוּרַיyoung men and have הָלְכוּgone בַשֶּׁבִיinto captivity

The LORD is righteous, for I have rebelled against His command. Listen, all you people; look upon my suffering. My young men and maidens have gone into captivity.

1:19

קָרָאתִיcalled לַמְאַהֲבַיto my lovers הֵמָּהthey רִמּוּנִיbetrayed me כֹּהֲנַיmy priests וּזְקֵנַיand elders בָּעִירin the city גָּוָעוּperished כִּיfor בִקְשׁוּsearched אֹכֶלfood לָמוֹto keep themselves וְיָשִׁיבוּalive אֶת[obj] נַפְשָׁםtheir soul

I called out to my lovers, but they have betrayed me. My priests and elders perished in the city while they searched for food to keep themselves alive.

1:20

רְאֵהsee יְהוָהLORD כִּיfor צַרdistressed לִיto me מֵעַיam within חֳמַרְמָרוּchurning נֶהְפַּךְpounding לִבִּיmy heart בְּקִרְבִּיwithin me כִּיfor מָרוֹmost מָרִיתִיrebellious מִחוּץmost outside שִׁכְּלָהbereaves חֶרֶבsword בַּבַּיִתinside there כַּמָּוֶתis death

See, O LORD, how distressed I am! I am churning within; my heart is pounding within me, for I have been most rebellious. Outside, the sword bereaves; inside, there is death.

1:21

שָׁמְעוּheard כִּיbut נֶאֱנָחָהgroaning אָנִיI אֵיןno מְנַחֵםcomfort לִיto me כָּלall אֹיְבַיmy enemies שָׁמְעוּheard רָעָתִיmy trouble שָׂשׂוּglad כִּיthat אַתָּהyou עָשִׂיתָcaused הֵבֵאתָbring יוֹםday קָרָאתָannounced וְיִהְיוּbecome כָמוֹנִיlike me

People have heard my groaning, but there is no one to comfort me. All my enemies have heard of my trouble; they are glad that You have caused it. May You bring the day You have announced, so that they may become like me.

1:22

תָּבֹאcome כָלall רָעָתָםtheir wickedness לְפָנֶיךָbefore you וְעוֹלֵלand deal לָמוֹto them כַּאֲשֶׁרas you have עוֹלַלְתָּdealt לִיto me עַלon כָּלall פְּשָׁעָיmy transgressions כִּיfor רַבּוֹתmany אַנְחֹתַיmy groans וְלִבִּיand my heart דַוָּיfaint

Let all their wickedness come before You, and deal with them as You have dealt with me because of all my transgressions. For my groans are many, and my heart is faint.
