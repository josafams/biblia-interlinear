Lamentations 2

The Lord acted like an enemy

How the Lord has covered the Daughter of Zion with the cloud of His anger! He has cast the glory of Israel from heaven to earth. He has abandoned His footstool in the day of His anger.

2:2

Without pity the Lord has swallowed up all the dwellings of Jacob. In His wrath He has demolished the fortified cities of the Daughter of Judah. He brought to the ground and defiled her kingdom and its princes.

2:3

In fierce anger He has cut off every horn of Israel and withdrawn His right hand at the approach of the enemy. He has burned in Jacob like a flaming fire that consumes everything around it.

2:4

דָּרַךְbent קַשְׁתּוֹhis bow כְּאוֹיֵבlike an enemy נִצָּבpositioned יְמִינוֹhis right hand כְּצָרlike a foe וַיַּהֲרֹגhe has killed כֹּלall מַחֲמַדֵּיpleasing עָיִןeye בְּאֹהֶלon the tent בַּתdaughter צִיּוֹןZion שָׁפַךְpoured כָּאֵשׁlike fire חֲמָתוֹhis wrath

He has bent His bow like an enemy; His right hand is positioned. Like a foe He has killed all who were pleasing to the eye; He has poured out His wrath like fire on the tent of the Daughter of Zion.

2:5

הָיָהwas אֲדֹנָיLord is כְּאוֹיֵבlike an enemy בִּלַּעswallowed יִשְׂרָאֵלIsrael בִּלַּעswallowed כָּלall אַרְמְנוֹתֶיהָher palaces שִׁחֵתdestroyed מִבְצָרָיוstrongholds וַיֶּרֶבhe has multiplied בְּבַתfor the daughter יְהוּדָהJudah תַּאֲנִיָּהmourning וַאֲנִיָּהand lamentation

The Lord is like an enemy; He has swallowed up Israel. He has swallowed up all her palaces and destroyed her strongholds. He has multiplied mourning and lamentation for the Daughter of Judah.

2:6

וַיַּחְמֹסtabernacle כַּגַּןlike a garden שֻׂכּוֹbooth he שִׁחֵתdestroyed מוֹעֲדוֹmeeting שִׁכַּחforget יְהוָהLORD בְּצִיּוֹןhas made Zion מוֹעֵדappointed feasts וְשַׁבָּתand Sabbaths וַיִּנְאַץhe has despised בְּזַעַםin fierce אַפּוֹhis anger מֶלֶךְking וְכֹהֵןand priest

He has laid waste His tabernacle like a garden booth; He has destroyed His place of meeting. The LORD has made Zion forget her appointed feasts and Sabbaths. In His fierce anger He has despised both king and priest.

2:7

זָנַחrejected אֲדֹנָיLord has מִזְבְּחוֹhis altar נִאֵרabandoned מִקְדָּשׁוֹhis sanctuary he הִסְגִּירdelivered בְּיַדinto the hand אוֹיֵבenemy חוֹמֹתwalls אַרְמְנוֹתֶיהָher palaces קוֹלshout נָתְנוּraised בְּבֵיתin the house יְהוָהLORD כְּיוֹםday מוֹעֵדappointed

The Lord has rejected His altar; He has abandoned His sanctuary; He has delivered the walls of her palaces into the hand of the enemy. They have raised a shout in the house of the LORD as on the day of an appointed feast.

2:8

חָשַׁבdetermined יְהוָהLORD לְהַשְׁחִיתto destroy חוֹמַתwall בַּתdaughter צִיּוֹןZion נָטָהstretched קָוmeasuring line לֹאnot הֵשִׁיבwithdraw יָדוֹhis hand מִבַּלֵּעַfrom destroying וַיַּאֲבֶלdestroying he חֵלramparts וְחוֹמָהand walls יַחְדָּוtogether אֻמְלָלוּwaste

The LORD determined to destroy the wall of the Daughter of Zion. He stretched out a measuring line and did not withdraw His hand from destroying. He made the ramparts and walls lament; together they waste away.

2:9

טָבְעוּsunk בָאָרֶץinto the ground שְׁעָרֶיהָher gates אִבַּדdestroyed וְשִׁבַּרand shattered בְּרִיחֶיהָbars her מַלְכָּהּking וְשָׂרֶיהָand her princes are exiled בַגּוֹיִםamong the nations אֵיןno תּוֹרָהlaw גַּםeven נְבִיאֶיהָher prophets לֹאno מָצְאוּfind חָזוֹןvision מֵיְהוָהfrom the LORD

Her gates have sunk into the ground; He has destroyed and shattered their bars. Her king and her princes are exiled among the nations, the law is no more, and even her prophets find no vision from the LORD.

2:10

יֵשְׁבוּsit לָאָרֶץon the ground יִדְּמוּsilence זִקְנֵיelders בַתdaughter צִיּוֹןZion הֶעֱלוּthrown עָפָרdust עַלon רֹאשָׁםtheir heads חָגְרוּput שַׂקִּיםsackcloth הוֹרִידוּyoung לָאָרֶץto the ground רֹאשָׁןtheir heads בְּתוּלֹתwomen יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem

The elders of the Daughter of Zion sit on the ground in silence. They have thrown dust on their heads and put on sackcloth. The young women of Jerusalem have bowed their heads to the ground.

2:11

כָּלוּfail בַדְּמָעוֹתfrom weeping עֵינַיmy eyes חֳמַרְמְרוּchurning מֵעַיwithin my heart נִשְׁפַּךְpoured לָאָרֶץto the ground כְּבֵדִיgrief עַלover שֶׁבֶרdestruction בַּתdaughter עַמִּיmy people בֵּעָטֵףfaint עוֹלֵלchildren וְיוֹנֵקand infants בִּרְחֹבוֹתin the streets קִרְיָהcity

My eyes fail from weeping; I am churning within. My heart is poured out in grief over the destruction of the daughter of my people, because children and infants faint in the streets of the city.

2:12

לְאִמֹּתָםto their mothers יֹאמְרוּcry אַיֵּהwhere דָּגָןgrain וָיָיִןand wine בְּהִתְעַטְּפָםas they faint כֶּחָלָלlike the wounded בִּרְחֹבוֹתin the streets עִירcity בְּהִשְׁתַּפֵּךְfade נַפְשָׁםtheir lives אֶלto חֵיקarms אִמֹּתָםtheir mothers

They cry out to their mothers: “Where is the grain and wine?” as they faint like the wounded in the streets of the city, as their lives fade away in the arms of their mothers.

2:13

מָהwhat אֲעִידֵךְcan מָהwhat אֲדַמֶּהcompare לָּךְto you הַבַּתdaughter יְרוּשָׁלִַםJerusalem מָהwhat אַשְׁוֶהliken לָּךְto you וַאֲנַחֲמֵךְmay console you בְּתוּלַתvirgin בַּתdaughter צִיּוֹןZion כִּיfor גָדוֹלas deep כַּיָּםas the sea שִׁבְרֵךְyour wound מִיwho יִרְפָּאheal לָךְto you

What can I say for you? To what can I compare you, O Daughter of Jerusalem? To what can I liken you, that I may console you, O Virgin Daughter of Zion? For your wound is as deep as the sea. Who can ever heal you?

2:14

נְבִיאַיִךְyour prophets חָזוּvisions לָךְto you שָׁוְאempty וְתָפֵלand deceptive וְלֹאthey did not גִלּוּexpose עַלon עֲוֺנֵךְyour guilt לְהָשִׁיבburdens שְׁבוּתֵךְyour captivity וַיֶּחֱזוּthey envisioned לָךְfor you מַשְׂאוֹתwere שָׁוְאempty וּמַדּוּחִיםand misleading

The visions of your prophets were empty and deceptive; they did not expose your guilt to ward off your captivity. The burdens they envisioned for you were empty and misleading.

2:15

סָפְקוּhands עָלַיִךְon you כַּפַּיִםscorn כָּלall עֹבְרֵיpass דֶרֶךְway שָׁרְקוּclap וַיָּנִעוּand shake רֹאשָׁםtheir heads עַלon בַּתdaughter יְרוּשָׁלִָםJerusalem הֲזֹאתis this הָעִירthe city שֶׁיֹּאמְרוּthat was called כְּלִילַתperfection יֹפִיbeauty מָשׂוֹשׂjoy לְכָלof all הָאָרֶץthe earth

All who pass by clap their hands at you in scorn. They hiss and shake their heads at the Daughter of Jerusalem: “Is this the city that was called the perfection of beauty, the joy of all the earth?”

2:16

All your enemies open their mouths against you. They hiss and gnash their teeth, saying, “We have swallowed her up. This is the day for which we have waited. We have lived to see it!”

2:17

עָשָׂהdone יְהוָהLORD אֲשֶׁרthat זָמָםplanned בִּצַּעhas accomplished אֶמְרָתוֹaccomplished his decree אֲשֶׁרwhich צִוָּהordained מִימֵיin days קֶדֶםold הָרַסoverthrown וְלֹאand not חָמָלpity וַיְשַׂמַּחgloat עָלַיִךְover you אוֹיֵבenemy הֵרִיםexalted קֶרֶןhorn צָרָיִךְyour foes

The LORD has done what He planned; He has accomplished His decree, which He ordained in days of old; He has overthrown you without pity. He has let the enemy gloat over you and exalted the horn of your foes.

2:18

צָעַקcry out לִבָּםhearts אֶלto אֲדֹנָיLord O חוֹמַתwall בַּתdaughter צִיּוֹןZion הוֹרִידִיdown כַנַּחַלlike a river דִּמְעָהtears יוֹמָםday וָלַיְלָהand night אַלno תִּתְּנִיgive פוּגַתrelief לָךְto you אַלno תִּדֹּםrest בַּתdaughter עֵינֵךְyour eyes

The hearts of the people cry out to the Lord. O wall of the Daughter of Zion, let your tears run down like a river day and night. Give yourself no relief, and your eyes no rest.

2:19

קוּמִיarise רֹנִּיcry בַלַּיְלָהin the night לְרֹאשׁthe first אַשְׁמֻרוֹתwatch שִׁפְכִיpour כַמַּיִםlike water לִבֵּךְyour heart נֹכַחin פְּנֵיpresence אֲדֹנָיLord שְׂאִיlift אֵלָיוto him כַּפַּיִךְyour hands עַלfor נֶפֶשׁlives עוֹלָלַיִךְyour children הָעֲטוּפִיםwho are fainting בְּרָעָבfrom hunger בְּרֹאשׁon the corner כָּלevery חוּצוֹתstreet

Arise, cry out in the night from the first watch of the night. Pour out your heart like water in the presence of the Lord. Lift up your hands to Him for the lives of your children who are fainting from hunger on the corner of every street.

2:20

Look, O LORD, and consider: Whom have You ever treated like this? Should women eat their offspring, the infants they have nurtured? Should priests and prophets be killed in the sanctuary of the Lord?

2:21

Both young and old lie together in the dust of the streets. My young men and maidens have fallen by the sword. You have slain them in the day of Your anger; You have slaughtered them without compassion.

2:22

תִּקְרָאsummoned כְיוֹםday מוֹעֵדappointed מְגוּרַיmy attackers מִסָּבִיבon every side וְלֹאno הָיָהone בְּיוֹםin the day אַףanger יְהוָהLORD’s פָּלִיטescaped וְשָׂרִידor survived אֲשֶׁרhas טִפַּחְתִּיnurtured וְרִבִּיתִיand reared אֹיְבִיmy enemy כִלָּםdestroyed those

You summoned my attackers on every side, as for the day of an appointed feast. In the day of the LORD’s anger no one escaped or survived; my enemy has destroyed those I nurtured and reared.
