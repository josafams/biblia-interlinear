Lamentations 3

From affliction to hope in His mercy

Open in the interlinear reader

3:1

אֲנִיam הַגֶּבֶרthe man רָאָהseen עֳנִיaffliction בְּשֵׁבֶטunder the rod עֶבְרָתוֹGod’s wrath

I am the man who has seen affliction under the rod of God’s wrath.

3:2

אוֹתִיme נָהַגdriven וַיֹּלַךְwalk חֹשֶׁךְdarkness וְלֹאand not אוֹרlight

He has driven me away and made me walk in darkness instead of light.

3:3

אַךְindeed בִּיagainst me יָשֻׁבkeeps יַהֲפֹךְturning יָדוֹhis hand כָּלall הַיּוֹםday long

Indeed, He keeps turning His hand against me all day long.

3:4

בִּלָּהworn בְשָׂרִיmy flesh וְעוֹרִיand skin he has שִׁבַּרshattered עַצְמוֹתָיmy bones

He has worn away my flesh and skin; He has shattered my bones.

3:5

בָּנָהbesieged עָלַיon me וַיַּקַּףand surrounded רֹאשׁbitterness וּתְלָאָהand hardship

He has besieged me and surrounded me with bitterness and hardship.

3:6

בְּמַחֲשַׁכִּיםin darkness הוֹשִׁיבַנִיme dwell כְּמֵתֵיlike those dead עוֹלָםages

He has made me dwell in darkness like those dead for ages.

3:7

גָּדַרwalled בַּעֲדִיme in וְלֹאand not אֵצֵאescape הִכְבִּידweighed נְחָשְׁתִּיweighed me

He has walled me in so I cannot escape; He has weighed me down with chains.

3:8

גַּםeven כִּיwhen אֶזְעַקcry out plead וַאֲשַׁוֵּעַhelp שָׂתַםshuts תְּפִלָּתִיmy prayer

Even when I cry out and plead for help, He shuts out my prayer.

3:9

גָּדַרbarred דְּרָכַיmy ways בְּגָזִיתwith cut stones נְתִיבֹתַיmy paths עִוָּהcrooked

He has barred my ways with cut stones; He has made my paths crooked.

3:10

דֹּבbear אֹרֵבwait הוּאa לִיa אֲרִיlion בְּמִסְתָּרִיםhiding in ambush

He is a bear lying in wait, a lion hiding in ambush.

3:11

דְּרָכַיme my path סוֹרֵרoff וַיְפַשְּׁחֵנִיand tore me שָׂמַנִיpieces שֹׁמֵםwithout

He forced me off my path and tore me to pieces; He left me without help.

3:12

דָּרַךְbent קַשְׁתוֹhis bow וַיַּצִּיבֵנִיand set me כַּמַּטָּרָאas the target לַחֵץfor his arrow

He bent His bow and set me as the target for His arrow.

3:13

הֵבִיאhe בְּכִלְיוֹתָיpierced my kidneys with בְּנֵיsons אַשְׁפָּתוֹhis arrows

He pierced my kidneys with His arrows.

3:14

הָיִיתִיam שְּׂחֹקlaughingstock לְכָלto all עַמִּיpeople נְגִינָתָםthey mock כָּלall הַיּוֹםday long

I am a laughingstock to all my people; they mock me in song all day long.

3:15

הִשְׂבִּיעַנִיfilled me בַמְּרוֹרִיםwith bitterness הִרְוַנִיintoxicated me לַעֲנָהwormwood

He has filled me with bitterness; He has intoxicated me with wormwood.

3:16

וַיַּגְרֵסhe ground בֶּחָצָץgravel שִׁנָּיmy teeth הִכְפִּישַׁנִיtrampled me בָּאֵפֶרin the dust

He has ground my teeth with gravel and trampled me in the dust.

3:17

וַתִּזְנַחdeprived מִשָּׁלוֹםof peace נַפְשִׁיmy soul נָשִׁיתִיforgotten טוֹבָהprosperity

My soul has been deprived of peace; I have forgotten what prosperity is.

3:18

וָאֹמַרso I say אָבַדperished נִצְחִיmy strength וְתוֹחַלְתִּיalong with my hope מֵיְהוָהfrom the LORD

So I say, “My strength has perished, along with my hope from the LORD.”

3:19

זְכָרremember עָנְיִיmy affliction וּמְרוּדִיand wandering לַעֲנָהwormwood וָרֹאשׁand the gall

Remember my affliction and wandering, the wormwood and the gall.

3:20

זָכוֹרremember תִּזְכּוֹרremembers וְתָשׁוֹחַand is humbled עָלַיon me נַפְשִׁיsoul

Surely my soul remembers and is humbled within me.

3:21

זֹאתI this אָשִׁיבcall אֶלto לִבִּיmind עַלon כֵּןtherefore אוֹחִילhope

Yet I call this to mind, and therefore I have hope:

3:22

חַסְדֵיloving devotion יְהוָהLORD כִּיbecause לֹאnot תָמְנוּconsumed כִּיfor לֹאnever כָלוּfail רַחֲמָיוhis mercies

Because of the loving devotion of the LORD we are not consumed, for His mercies never fail.

3:23

חֲדָשִׁיםnew לַבְּקָרִיםevery morning רַבָּהgreat אֱמוּנָתֶךָyour faithfulness

They are new every morning; great is Your faithfulness!

3:24

חֶלְקִיmy portion יְהוָהLORD אָמְרָהsays נַפְשִׁיmy soul עַלon כֵּןtherefore אוֹחִילhope לוֹto him

“The LORD is my portion,” says my soul, “therefore I will hope in Him.”

3:25

טוֹבgood יְהוָהLORD לְקוָֹוwait for him לְנֶפֶשׁto the soul תִּדְרְשֶׁנּוּseeks him

The LORD is good to those who wait for Him, to the soul who seeks Him.

3:26

טוֹבgood וְיָחִילto wait וְדוּמָםwait quietly לִתְשׁוּעַתfor the salvation יְהוָהLORD

It is good to wait quietly for the salvation of the LORD.

3:27

טוֹבgood לַגֶּבֶרfor a man כִּיfor יִשָּׂאbear עֹלyoke בִּנְעוּרָיוyoung

It is good for a man to bear the yoke while he is still young.

3:28

יֵשֵׁבsit בָּדָדalone וְיִדֹּםin silence כִּיfor נָטַלdisciplined עָלָיוon him

Let him sit alone in silence, for God has disciplined him.

3:29

יִתֵּןlet בֶּעָפָרin the dust פִּיהוּhim his face אוּלַיperhaps יֵשׁthere תִּקְוָהhope

Let him bury his face in the dust— perhaps there is still hope.

3:30

יִתֵּןlet לְמַכֵּהוּwould strike him לֶחִיcheek יִשְׂבַּעfilled בְּחֶרְפָּהwith reproach

Let him offer his cheek to the one who would strike him; let him be filled with reproach.

3:31

כִּיfor לֹאnot יִזְנַחcast לְעוֹלָםforever אֲדֹנָיLord will

For the Lord will not cast us off forever.

3:32

כִּיfor אִםif הוֹגָהgrief וְרִחַםhe will show compassion כְּרֹבabundant חֲסָדָיוhis loving devotion

Even if He causes grief, He will show compassion according to His abundant loving devotion.

3:33

כִּיfor לֹאnot עִנָּהafflict מִלִּבּוֹwillingly וַיַּגֶּהor grieve בְנֵיsons אִישׁmen

For He does not willingly afflict or grieve the sons of men.

3:34

לְדַכֵּאto crush תַּחַתunder רַגְלָיוunderfoot כֹּלall אֲסִירֵיprisoners אָרֶץland

To crush underfoot all the prisoners of the land,

3:35

לְהַטּוֹתto deny מִשְׁפַּטjustice גָּבֶרman נֶגֶדbefore פְּנֵיface עֶלְיוֹןMost high

to deny a man justice before the Most High,

3:36

לְעַוֵּתto subvert אָדָםman בְּרִיבוֹin his lawsuit אֲדֹנָיLord לֹאnot רָאָהapprove

to subvert a man in his lawsuit— of these the Lord does not approve.

3:37

מִיwho זֶהthis אָמַרspoken וַתֶּהִיand it came אֲדֹנָיLord has לֹאnot צִוָּהordained

Who has spoken and it came to pass, unless the Lord has ordained it?

3:38

מִפִּיfrom the mouth עֶלְיוֹןMost high לֹאnot תֵצֵאcome הָרָעוֹתboth adversity וְהַטּוֹבand good

Do not both adversity and good come from the mouth of the Most High?

3:39

מַהwhy יִּתְאוֹנֵןmortal complain אָדָםman חָיalive גֶּבֶרview עַלin חֲטָאָיוhis sins

Why should any mortal man complain, in view of his sins?

3:40

נַחְפְּשָׂהdisguise דְרָכֵינוּour ways וְנַחְקֹרָהexamine and test וְנָשׁוּבָהand turn עַדto יְהוָהLORD

Let us examine and test our ways, and turn back to the LORD.

3:41

נִשָּׂאlift לְבָבֵנוּour hearts אֶלto כַּפָּיִםhands אֶלto אֵלGod בַּשָּׁמָיִםin heaven

Let us lift up our hearts and hands to God in heaven:

3:42

נַחְנוּwe פָשַׁעְנוּsinned וּמָרִינוּand rebelled אַתָּהyou לֹאnot סָלָחְתָּforgiven

“We have sinned and rebelled; You have not forgiven.”

3:43

סַכֹּתָהcovered בָאַףin anger וַתִּרְדְּפֵנוּand pursued us הָרַגְתָּkilled לֹאnot חָמָלְתָּpity

You have covered Yourself in anger and pursued us; You have killed without pity.

3:44

סַכּוֹתָהcovered בֶעָנָןwith a cloud לָךְto you מֵעֲבוֹרpass תְּפִלָּהprayer

You have covered Yourself with a cloud that no prayer can pass through.

3:45

סְחִיscum וּמָאוֹסand refuse תְּשִׂימֵנוּmade us בְּקֶרֶבamong הָעַמִּיםthe nations

You have made us scum and refuse among the nations.

3:46

פָּצוּopen עָלֵינוּagainst us פִּיהֶםtheir mouths כָּלall אֹיְבֵינוּour enemies

All our enemies open their mouths against us.

3:47

פַּחַדpanic וָפַחַתand pitfall הָיָהcome לָנוּto us הַשֵּׁאתdevastation וְהַשָּׁבֶרand destruction

Panic and pitfall have come upon us— devastation and destruction.

3:48

פַּלְגֵיstreams מַיִםtears תֵּרַדflow עֵינִיmy eyes עַלover שֶׁבֶרdestruction בַּתdaughter עַמִּיmy people

Streams of tears flow from my eyes over the destruction of the daughter of my people.

3:49

עֵינִיmy eyes נִגְּרָהoverflow וְלֹאand not תִדְמֶהunceasingly מֵאֵיןwithout הֲפֻגוֹתrelief

My eyes overflow unceasingly, without relief,

3:50

עַדuntil יַשְׁקִיףlooks down וְיֵרֶאand sees יְהוָהLORD מִשָּׁמָיִםfrom heaven

until the LORD looks down from heaven and sees.

3:51

עֵינִיmy eyes עוֹלְלָהgrief לְנַפְשִׁיto my soul מִכֹּלbecause of all בְּנוֹתdaughters עִירִיmy city

My eyes bring grief to my soul because of all the daughters of my city.

3:52

צוֹדhunt צָדוּנִיhunted me כַּצִּפּוֹרlike a bird אֹיְבַיmy enemies חִנָּםwithout cause

Without cause my enemies hunted me like a bird.

3:53

צָמְתוּdropped בַבּוֹרinto a pit חַיָּיme alive וַיַּדּוּand cast אֶבֶןstones בִּיin me

They dropped me alive into a pit and cast stones upon me.

3:54

צָפוּflowed מַיִםwaters עַלover רֹאשִׁיmy head and I אָמַרְתִּיthought נִגְזָרְתִּיdie

The waters flowed over my head, and I thought I was going to die.

3:55

קָרָאתִיcalled שִׁמְךָyour name O יְהוָהLORD מִבּוֹרpit תַּחְתִּיּוֹתdepths

I called on Your name, O LORD, out of the depths of the Pit.

3:56

קוֹלִיmy plea שָׁמָעְתָּheard אַלdo not תַּעְלֵםignore אָזְנְךָyour ear לְרַוְחָתִיmy for relief לְשַׁוְעָתִיcry

You heard my plea: “Do not ignore my cry for relief.”

3:57

קָרַבְתָּdrew near בְּיוֹםin day אֶקְרָאֶךָּcalled on you אָמַרְתָּsaid אַלdo not תִּירָאafraid

You drew near when I called on You; You said, “Do not be afraid.”

3:58

רַבְתָּdefend אֲדֹנָיLord רִיבֵיcause נַפְשִׁיyou גָּאַלְתָּredeem חַיָּיmy life

You defend my cause, O Lord; You redeem my life.

3:59

רָאִיתָהseen יְהוָהLORD עַוָּתָתִיwrong שָׁפְטָהvindicate מִשְׁפָּטִיmy cause

You have seen, O LORD, the wrong done to me; vindicate my cause!

3:60

רָאִיתָהseen כָּלall נִקְמָתָםtheir malice כָּלall מַחְשְׁבֹתָםtheir plots לִיto me

You have seen all their malice, all their plots against me.

3:61

שָׁמַעְתָּheard חֶרְפָּתָםtheir insults יְהוָהLORD כָּלall מַחְשְׁבֹתָםtheir plots עָלָיagainst me

O LORD, You have heard their insults, all their plots against me—

3:62

שִׂפְתֵיslander קָמַיfoes me וְהֶגְיוֹנָםassailants עָלַיagainst me כָּלall הַיּוֹםday long

the slander and murmuring of my assailants against me all day long.

3:63

שִׁבְתָּםsit and וְקִימָתָםand when they rise הַבִּיטָהsee אֲנִיI מַנְגִּינָתָםthey mock

When they sit and when they rise, see how they mock me in song.

3:64

תָּשִׁיבpay לָהֶםto them גְּמוּלwhat יְהוָהLORD כְּמַעֲשֵׂהwork יְדֵיהֶםtheir hands

You will pay them back what they deserve, O LORD, according to the work of their hands.

3:65

תִּתֵּןput לָהֶםa מְגִנַּתveil לֵבhearts תַּאֲלָתְךָyour curse לָהֶםto them

Put a veil of anguish over their hearts; may Your curse be upon them!

3:66

תִּרְדֹּףpursue בְּאַףin anger וְתַשְׁמִידֵםand exterminate them מִתַּחַתfrom under שְׁמֵיheavens יְהוָהLORD

You will pursue them in anger and exterminate them from under Your heavens, O LORD.
