דָּמַם

dâmam · H1826

Transliteration
dâmam
Pronunciation
daw-MAN
Meaning
to be dumb; by implication, to be astonished, to stop; also to perish
Origin
a primitive root (compare H1724 (דָּהַם), H1820 (דָּמָה))
