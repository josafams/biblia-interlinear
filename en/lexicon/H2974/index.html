יָאַל

yâʼal · H2974

Transliteration
yâʼal
Pronunciation
yaw-AL
Meaning
properly, to yield, especially assent; hence (pos.) to undertake as an act of volition
Origin
a primitive root (probably rather the same as H2973 (יָאַל) through the idea of mental weakness)
