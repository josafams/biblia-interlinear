ἀκροβυστία

akrobystía · G203

Transliteration
akrobystía
Pronunciation
a-kro-bus-TI-a
Meaning
the prepuce; by implication, an uncircumcised (i.e. gentile, figuratively, unregenerate) state or person
Origin
from G206 (ἄκρον) and probably a modified form of (the penis or male sexual organ)
In the BSB
