חָנַק

chânaq · H2614

Transliteration
chânaq
Pronunciation
khaw-NAK
Meaning
to be narrow; by implication, to throttle, or (reflex.) to choke oneself to death (by a rope)
Origin
a primitive root (compare H2596 (חָנַךְ))
In the BSB
hanged (1); strangled (1)
Occurrences
2

Where it appears (2 times)

  1. 2 Samuel 17:23 and hanged
  2. Nahum 2:12 and strangled
