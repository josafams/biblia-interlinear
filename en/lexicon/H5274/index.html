נָעַל

nâʻal · H5274

Transliteration
nâʻal
Pronunciation
naw-AL
Meaning
properly, to fasten up, i.e. with abar or cord; hence, to sandal, i.e. furnish with slippers
Origin
a primitive root; also denominative from H5275 (נַעַל)
In the BSB
