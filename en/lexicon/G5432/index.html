φρουρέω

phrouréō · G5432

Transliteration
phrouréō
Pronunciation
froo-RE-o
Meaning
to be a watcher in advance, i.e. to mount guard as a sentinel (post spies at gates); figuratively, to hem in, protect
Origin
from a compound of G4253 (πρό) and G3708 (ὁράω)
In the BSB
aretas-secured (1); locked (1); guard (1); shielded (1)
