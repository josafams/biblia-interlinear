διακρίνω

diakrínō · G1252

Transliteration
diakrínō
Pronunciation
di-a-KRI-no
Meaning
to separate thoroughly, i.e. (literally and reflexively) to withdraw from, or (by implication) oppose; figuratively, to discriminate (by implication, decide), or (reflexively) hesitate
Origin
from G1223 (διά) and G2919 (κρίνω)
