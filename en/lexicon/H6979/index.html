qûwr · H6979

Transliteration
qûwr
Pronunciation
koor
Meaning
to trench; by implication, to throw forth; to wall up, whether literal (to build a wall) or figurative (to estop)
Origin
a primitive root; also denominative from H7023 (קִיר)
