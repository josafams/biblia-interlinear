συνέδριον

synédrion · G4892

Transliteration
synédrion
Pronunciation
su-NE-dri-on
Meaning
a joint session, i.e. (specially), the Jewish Sanhedrin; by analogy, a subordinate tribunal
Origin
neuter of a presumed derivative of a compound of G4862 (σύν) and the base of G1476 (ἑδραῖος)
