מִגְרָשׁ

migrâsh · H4054

Transliteration
migrâsh
Pronunciation
mig-RAWSH
Meaning
a suburb (i.e. open country whither flocks are driven from pasture); hence, the area around abuilding, or the margin of the sea
Origin
also (in plural) feminine (Ezekiel 27:28) מִגְרָשָׁה; from H1644 (גָּרַשׁ)
