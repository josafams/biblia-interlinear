Rimmôwn · H7417

Transliteration
Rimmôwn
Pronunciation
rim-MONE
Meaning
Rimmon, the name of a Syrian deity, also of five places in Palestine
Origin
or (shorter) (רִמֹּן; or רִמּוֹנוֹ; (1 Chronicles 6:62 (H77 (אֶבֶץ))), the same as H7416 (רִמּוֹן); in Joshua 19:13: הַמְּתֹאָר is mistaken for part of a name; article with the passive participle of H8388 (תָּאַר); the (one) marked off, i.e. which pertains).
