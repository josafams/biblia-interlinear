λαμβάνω

lambánō · G2983

Transliteration
lambánō
Pronunciation
lam-BA-no
Meaning
I receive, take
Origin
a prolonged form of a primary verb, which is use only as an alternate in certain tenses; to take (in very many applications, literally and figuratively (properly objective or active, to get hold of; whereas G1209 (δέχομαι) is rather subjective or passive, to have offered to one; while G138 (αἱρέομαι) is more violent, to seize or remove))
