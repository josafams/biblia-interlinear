גָּרַר

gârar · H1641

Transliteration
gârar
Pronunciation
gaw-RAR
Meaning
to drag off roughly; by implication, to bring up the cud (i.e. ruminate); by analogy, to saw
Origin
a primitive root
In the BSB
chew (1); trimmed (1); sweep (1); swirling (1); catches (1)
Occurrences
5

  2. 1 Kings 7:9 trimmed
  3. Proverbs 21:7 sweep them
  4. Jeremiah 30:23 swirling
  5. Habakkuk 1:15 catches them
