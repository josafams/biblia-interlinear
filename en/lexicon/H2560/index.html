חָמַר

châmar · H2560

Transliteration
châmar
Pronunciation
khaw-MAR
Meaning
properly, to boil up; hence, to ferment (with scum); to glow (with redness); to smear with pitch
Origin
a primitive root; also as denominative (from H2564 (חֵמָר))
In the BSB
churning (2); coated (1); red (1); foam (1); mixed (1)
