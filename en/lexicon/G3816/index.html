παῖς

paîs · G3816

Transliteration
paîs
Pronunciation
PAIS
Meaning
a boy (as often beaten with impunity), or (by analogy), a girl, and (genitive case) a child; specially, a slave or servant (especially a minister to a king; and by eminence to God)
Origin
