ζωογονέω

zōogonéō · G2225

Transliteration
zōogonéō
Pronunciation
zo-o-go-NE-o
Meaning
to engender alive, i.e. (by analogy) to rescue (passively, be saved) from death
Origin
from the same as G2226 (ζῶον) and a derivative of G1096 (γίνομαι)
In the BSB
life-preserve (1); die (1); life (1)
Occurrences
3

Where it appears (3 times)

  2. Acts 7:19 die
  3. 1 Timothy 6:13 life
