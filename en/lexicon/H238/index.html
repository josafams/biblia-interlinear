אָזַן

ʼâzan · H238

Transliteration
ʼâzan
Pronunciation
aw-ZAN
Meaning
to broaden out the ear (with the hand), i.e. (by implication) to listen
Origin
a primitive root; probably to expand; but used only as a denominative from H241 (אֹזֶן)
In the BSB
