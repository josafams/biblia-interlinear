כַּשְׂדִּי

Kasdîy · H3778

Transliteration
Kasdîy
Pronunciation
kas-DEE
Meaning
a Kasdite, or descendant of Kesed; by implication, a Chaldaean (as if so descended); also an astrologer (as if proverbial of that people
Origin
(occasionally with enclitic) כַּשְׂדִּימָה; towards the Kasdites into Chaldea), patronymically from H3777 (כֶּשֶׂד) (only in the plural)
In the BSB
