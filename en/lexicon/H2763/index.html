חָרַם

châram · H2763

Transliteration
châram
Pronunciation
khaw-RAM
Meaning
to seclude; specifically (by a ban) to devote to religious uses (especially destruction); physical and reflexive, to be blunt as to the nose
Origin
a primitive root
