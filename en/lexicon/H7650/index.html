שָׁבַע

shâbaʻ · H7650

Transliteration
shâbaʻ
Pronunciation
shaw-BAH
Meaning
to seven oneself, i.e. swear (as if by repeating a declaration seven times)
Origin
a primitive root; properly to be complete, but used only as a denominative from H7651 (שֶׁבַע)
In the BSB
