כְּנַעַנִי

Kᵉnaʻanîy · H3669

Transliteration
Kᵉnaʻanîy
Pronunciation
ken-ah-an-EE
Meaning
a Kenaanite or inhabitant of Kenaan; by implication, a pedlar (the Canaanites standing for their neighbors the Ishmaelites, who conducted mercantile caravans)
Origin
patrial from H3667 (כְּנַעַן)
In the BSB
canaanites (58); canaanite (12); merchants (2); canaan-extended (1)
