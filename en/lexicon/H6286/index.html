פָּאַר

pâʼar · H6286

Transliteration
pâʼar
Pronunciation
paw-AR
Meaning
to gleam, i.e. (causatively) embellish; figuratively, to boast; also to explain (i.e. make clear) oneself; to shake a tree
Origin
a primitive root; also denominative from H6288 (פְּאֹרָה),
