scholázō · G4980

Transliteration
scholázō
Pronunciation
skho-LA-zo
Meaning
to take a holiday, i.e. be at leisure for (by implication, devote oneself wholly to); figuratively, to be vacant (of a house)
Origin
from G4981 (σχολή)
In the BSB
vacant (1); devote (1)

2
