Transliteration
horáō
Pronunciation
ho-RA-o
Meaning
by extension, to attend to; by Hebraism, to experience; passively, to appear
Origin
properly, to stare at (compare G3700 (ὀπτάνομαι)), i.e. (by implication) to discern clearly (physically or mentally)
