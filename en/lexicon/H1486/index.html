גּוֹרָל

gôwrâl · H1486

Transliteration
gôwrâl
Pronunciation
go-RAWL
Meaning
properly, a pebble, i.e. a lot (small stones being used for that purpose); figuratively, a portion or destiny (as if determined by lot)
Origin
or (shortened) גֹּרָל; from an unused root meaning to be rough (as stone)
