תַּרְשִׁישׁ

Tarshîysh · H8659

Transliteration
Tarshîysh
Pronunciation
tar-SHEESH
Meaning
Tarshish, a place on the Mediterranean, hence, the ephithet of a merchant vessel (as if for or from that port); also the name of a Persian and of an Israelite
Origin
probably the same as H8658 (תַּרְשִׁישׁ) (as the region of the stone, or the reverse)
