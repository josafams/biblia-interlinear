ἁμαρτάνω

hamartánō · G264

Transliteration
hamartánō
Pronunciation
ha-mar-TA-no
Meaning
properly, to miss the mark (and so not share in the prize), i.e. (figuratively) to err, especially (morally) to sin
Origin
perhaps from G1 (Α) (as a negative particle) and the base of G3313 (μέρος)
In the BSB
