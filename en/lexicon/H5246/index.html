נָמֵר

nâmêr · H5246

Transliteration
nâmêr
Pronunciation
naw-MARE
Meaning
a leopard (from its stripes)
Origin
from an unused root meaning properly, to filtrate, i.e. be limpid (comp H5247 (נִמְרָה) and H5249 (נִמְרִים)); and thus to spot or stain as if by dripping
In the BSB
leopard (4); leopards (2)
