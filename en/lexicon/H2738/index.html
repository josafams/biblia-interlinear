חָרוּל

chârûwl · H2738

Transliteration
chârûwl
Pronunciation
khaw-ROOL
Meaning
properly, pointed, i.e. a bramble or other thorny weed
Origin
or (shortened) חָרֻל; apparently, a passive participle of an unused root probably meaning to be prickly
