Qâdêsh Barnêaʻ · H6947

Transliteration
Qâdêsh Barnêaʻ
Pronunciation
kaw-DASHE bar-NAY-ah
Meaning
Kadesh-Barnea, a place in the Desert
Origin
from the same as H6946 (קָדֵשׁ) and an otherwise unused word (apparently compounded of a correspondent to H1251 (בַּר) and a derivative of H5128 (נוּעַ)) meaning desert of a fugitive; Kadesh of (the) Wilderness of Wandering
In the BSB
kadesh (8); barnea (8); kadesh-barnea (2)
