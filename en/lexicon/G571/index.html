ἄπιστος

ápistos · G571

Transliteration
ápistos
Pronunciation
A-pis-tos
Meaning
(actively) disbelieving, i.e. without Christian faith (specially, a heathen); (passively) untrustworthy (person), or incredible (thing)
Origin
from G1 (Α) (as a negative particle) and G4103 (πιστός)
