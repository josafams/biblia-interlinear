מָרָא

mârâʼ · H4754

Transliteration
mârâʼ
Pronunciation
maw-RAW
Meaning
to rebel; hence (through the idea of maltreating) to whip, i.e. lash (self with wings, as the ostrich in running)
Origin
a primitive root
In the BSB
spreads-wings (1); rebellious (1)
Occurrences
2

Where it appears (2 times)

  2. Zephaniah 3:1 rebellious
