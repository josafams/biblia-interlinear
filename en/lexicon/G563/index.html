ἀπερισπάστως

aperispástōs · G563

Transliteration
aperispástōs
Pronunciation
a-pe-ris-PAS-tos
Meaning
undistractedly, i.e. free from (domestic) solicitude
Origin
adverb from a compound of G1 (Α) (as a negative participle) and a presumed derivative of G4049 (περισπάω)

1
