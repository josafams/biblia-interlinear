εὐώνυμος

euṓnymos · G2176

Transliteration
euṓnymos
Pronunciation
yoo-O-nu-mos
Meaning
properly, well-named (good-omened), i.e. the left (which was the lucky side among the pagan Greeks); neuter as adverbial, at the left hand
Origin
from G2095 (εὖ) and G3686 (ὄνομα)
