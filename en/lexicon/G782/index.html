ἀσπάζομαι

aspázomai · G782

Transliteration
aspázomai
Pronunciation
as-PA-zo-mai
Meaning
to enfold in the arms, i.e. (by implication) to salute, (figuratively) to welcome
Origin
from G1 (Α) (as a particle of union) and a presumed form of G4685 (σπάω)
