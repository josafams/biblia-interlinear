stērízō · G4741

Transliteration
stērízō
Pronunciation
stay-RI-zo
Meaning
to set fast, i.e. (literally) to turn resolutely in a certain direction, or (figuratively) to confirm
Origin
from a presumed derivative of G2476 (ἵστημι) (like G4731 (στερεός))
