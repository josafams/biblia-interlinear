זָלַל

zâlal · H2151

Transliteration
zâlal
Pronunciation
zaw-LAL
Meaning
to shake (as in the wind), i.e. to quake; figuratively, to be loose morally, worthless or prodigal
Origin
a primitive root (compare H2107 (זוּל))
In the BSB
glutton (2); gorge (1); gluttons (1); quake (1); trembled (1); noble-worthless (1); despised (1)
