στρατηγός

stratēgós · G4755

Transliteration
stratēgós
Pronunciation
stra-tay-GOS
Meaning
a general, i.e. (by implication or analogy) a (military) governor (prætor), the chief (præfect) of the (Levitical) temple-wardens
Origin
from the base of G4756 (στρατιά) and G71 (ἄγω) or G2233 (ἡγέομαι)
