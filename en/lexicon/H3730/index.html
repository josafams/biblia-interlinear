kaphtôr · H3730

Transliteration
kaphtôr
Pronunciation
kaf-TORE
Meaning
a chaplet; but used only in an architectonic sense, i.e. the capital of acolumn, or a wreath-like button or disk on the candelabrum
Origin
or (Amos 9:1) כַּפְתּוֹר; probably from an unused root meaning to encircle
