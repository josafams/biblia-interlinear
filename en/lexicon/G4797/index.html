συγχέω

synchéō · G4797

Transliteration
synchéō
Pronunciation
sun-KHE-o
Meaning
to commingle promiscuously, i.e. (figuratively) to throw (an assembly) into disorder, to perplex (the mind)
Origin
from G4862 (σύν) and (to pour) or its alternate
