אֲרֻבָּה

ʼărubbâh · H699

Transliteration
ʼărubbâh
Pronunciation
ar-oob-BAW
Meaning
a lattice; (by implication) a window, dovecot (because of the pigeon-holes), chimney (with its apertures for smoke), sluice (with openings for water)
Origin
feminine participle passive of H693 (אָרַב) (as if for lurking)
In the BSB
