עֲרֻבָּה

ʻărubbâh · H6161

Transliteration
ʻărubbâh
Pronunciation
ar-oob-BAW
Meaning
something given as security, i.e. (literally) a token (of safety) or (metaphorically) a bondsman
Origin
feminine passive participle of H6048 (עֲנַמֶּלֶךְ) in the sense of a bargain or exchange
In the BSB
assurance (1); security (1)
Occurrences
2
