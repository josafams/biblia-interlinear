אוֹב

ʼôwb · H178

Transliteration
ʼôwb
Pronunciation
obe
Meaning
properly, a mumble, i.e. a water skin (from its hollow sound); hence a necromancer (ventriloquist, as from a jar)
Origin
from the same as H1 (אָב) (apparently through the idea of prattling a father's name)
In the BSB
