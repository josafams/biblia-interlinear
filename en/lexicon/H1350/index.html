גָּאַל

gâʼal · H1350

Transliteration
gâʼal
Pronunciation
gaw-AL
Meaning
to be the next of kin (and as such to buy back a relative's property, marry his widow, etc.)
Origin
a primitive root, to redeem (according to the Oriental law of kinship), i.e.
