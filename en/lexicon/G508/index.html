ἀνώγεον

anṓgeon · G508

Transliteration
anṓgeon
Pronunciation
a-NO-ge-on
Meaning
above the ground, i.e. (properly) the second floor of a building; used for a dome or a balcony on the upper story
Origin
from G507 (ἄνω) and G1093 (γῆ)
Occurrences
2
