מָלַח

mâlach · H4414

Transliteration
mâlach
Pronunciation
maw-LAKH
Meaning
properly, to rub to pieces or pulverize; intransitively, to disappear as dust; to salt whether internally (to season with salt) or externally (to rub with salt)
Origin
a primitive root; also as denominative from H4417 (מֶלַח)
In the BSB
seasoned-salt (1); salt-you (1); vanish (1); salt-wrapped (1)
Occurrences
5

  2. Leviticus 2:13 salt you
  3. Isaiah 51:6 vanish
  4. Ezekiel 16:4 and seasoned salt
  5. Ezekiel 16:4 salt wrapped
