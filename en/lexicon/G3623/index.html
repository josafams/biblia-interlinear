οἰκονόμος

oikonómos · G3623

Transliteration
oikonómos
Pronunciation
oy-ko-NO-mos
Meaning
a house-distributor (i.e. manager), or overseer, i.e. an employee in that capacity; by extension, a fiscal agent (treasurer); figuratively, a preacher (of the Gospel)
Origin
from G3624 (οἶκος) and the base of G3551 (νόμος)
In the BSB
manager (4); stewards (3); trustees (1); steward-reproach (1)
