Transliteration
prosphágion
Pronunciation
pros-FA-gi-on
Meaning
something eaten in addition to bread, i.e. a relish (specially, fish; compare G3795 (ὀψάριον))
Origin
neuter of a presumed derivative of a compound of G4314 (πρός) and G5315 (φάγω)
