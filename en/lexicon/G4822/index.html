συμβιβάζω

symbibázō · G4822

Transliteration
symbibázō
Pronunciation
sum-bi-BA-zo
Meaning
to drive together, i.e. unite (in association or affection), (mentally) to infer, show, teach
Origin
from G4862 (σύν) and (to force; causative (by reduplication) of the base of G939 (βάσις))
