סָרִיס

çârîyç · H5631

Transliteration
çârîyç
Pronunciation
saw-REECE
Meaning
a eunuch; by implication, valet (especially of the female apartments), and thus, a minister of state
Origin
or סָרִס; from an unused root meaning to castrate
