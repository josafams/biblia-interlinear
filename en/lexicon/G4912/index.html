συνέχω

synéchō · G4912

Transliteration
synéchō
Pronunciation
su-NE-kho
Meaning
to hold together, i.e. to compress (the ears, with a crowd or siege) or arrest (a prisoner); figuratively, to compel, perplex, afflict, preoccupy
Origin
from G4862 (σύν) and G2192 (ἔχω)
