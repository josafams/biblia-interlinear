ἀλάβαστρον

alábastron · G211

Transliteration
alábastron
Pronunciation
a-LA-bas-tron
Meaning
properly, an "alabaster" box, i.e. (by extension) a perfume vase (of any material)
Origin
neuter of (of uncertain derivation), the name of a stone
