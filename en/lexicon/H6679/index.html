tsûwd · H6679

Transliteration
tsûwd
Pronunciation
tsood
Meaning
to lie alongside (i.e. in wait); by implication, to catch an animal (figuratively, men); to victual (for a journey)
Origin
a primitive root; also denominative from H6718 (צַיִד)
