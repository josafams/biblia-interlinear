ʻûwph · H5774

Transliteration
ʻûwph
Pronunciation
oof
Meaning
to fly; also (by implication of dimness) to faint (from the darkness of swooning)
Origin
a primitive root; to cover (with wings or obscurity); hence (as denominative from H5775 (עוֹף))
