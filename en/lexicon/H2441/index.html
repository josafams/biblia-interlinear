חֵךְ

chêk · H2441

Transliteration
chêk
Pronunciation
khake
Meaning
properly, the palate or inside of the mouth; hence, the mouth itself (as the organ of speech, taste and kissing)
Origin
probably from H2596 (חָנַךְ) in the sense of tasting
