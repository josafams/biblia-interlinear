euodóō · G2137

Transliteration
euodóō
Pronunciation
yoo-o-DO-o
Meaning
to help on the road, i.e. (passively) succeed in reaching; figuratively, to succeed in business affairs
Origin
from a compound of G2095 (εὖ) and G3598 (ὁδός)
