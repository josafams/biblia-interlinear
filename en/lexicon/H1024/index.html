בֵּית הַמַּרְכָּבוֹת

Bêyth ham-Markâbôwth · H1024

Transliteration
Bêyth ham-Markâbôwth
Pronunciation
bayth ham-markaw-BOTH
Meaning
Beth-ham-Markaboth or Beth-Markaboth, a place in Palestine
Origin
or (shortened) בֵּית מַרְכָּבוֹת; from H1004 (בַּיִת) and the plural of H4818 (מֶרְכָּבָה) (with or without the article interposed); place of (the) chariots
In the BSB
marcaboth (2); hazar (1); hazar-susim (1)
