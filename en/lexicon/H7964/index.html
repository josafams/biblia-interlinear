שִׁלּוּחַ

shillûwach · H7964

Transliteration
shillûwach
Pronunciation
shil-LOO-akh
Meaning
(only in plural) a dismissal, i.e. (of a wife) divorce (especially the document); also (of a daughter) dower
Origin
or שִׁלֻּחַ; from H7971 (שָׁלַח)
In the BSB
dowry (1); farewell-gifts (1)
Occurrences
3

Where it appears (3 times)

  1. Exodus 18:2 her dowry
  2. 1 Kings 9:16 dowry
  3. Micah 1:14 farewell gifts
