כִּתִּי

Kittîy · H3794

Transliteration
Kittîy
Pronunciation
kit-TEE
Meaning
a Kittite or Cypriote; hence, an islander in general, i.e. the Greeks or Romans on the shores opposite Palestine
Origin
or כִּתִּיִּי; patrial from an unused name denoting Cyprus (only in the plural)
In the BSB
cyprus (5); kittites (2); kittim (1)
