ʼâven · H205

Transliteration
ʼâven
Pronunciation
aw-VEN
Meaning
strictly nothingness; also trouble. vanity, wickedness; specifically an idol
Origin
from an unused root perhaps meaning properly, to pant (hence, to exert oneself, usually in vain; to come to naught)
