שָׁכַם

shâkam · H7925

Transliteration
shâkam
Pronunciation
shaw-KAM
Meaning
literally, to load up (on the back of man or beast), i.e. to start early in the morning
Origin
a primitive root; properly, to incline (the shoulder to a burden); but used only as denominative from H7926 (שְׁכֶם)
