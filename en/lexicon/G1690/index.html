ἐμβριμάομαι

embrimáomai · G1690

Transliteration
embrimáomai
Pronunciation
em-bri-MA-o-mai
Meaning
to have indignation on, i.e. (transitively) to blame, (intransitively) to sigh with chagrin, (specially) to sternly enjoin
Origin
from G1722 (ἐν) and (to snort with anger)
In the BSB
