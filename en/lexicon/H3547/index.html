כָּהַן

kâhan · H3547

Transliteration
kâhan
Pronunciation
kaw-HAN
Meaning
to officiate as a priest; figuratively, to put on regalia
Origin
a primitive root, apparently meaning to mediate in religious services; but used only as denominative from H3548 (כֹּהֵן)
