מָעוֹן

mâʻôwn · H4583

Transliteration
mâʻôwn
Pronunciation
maw-OHN
Meaning
an abode, of God (the Tabernacle or the Temple), men (their home) or animals (their lair); hence, a retreat (asylum)
Origin
or מָעִין; (1 Chronicles 4:41), from the same as H5772 (עוֹנָה)
