ἐπαγγέλλω

epangéllō · G1861

Transliteration
epangéllō
Pronunciation
e-pan-GEL-lo
Meaning
to announce upon (reflexively), i.e. (by implication) to engage to do something, to assert something respecting oneself
Origin
from G1909 (ἐπί) and the base of G32 (ἄγγελος)
