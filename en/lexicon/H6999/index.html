קָטַר

qâṭar · H6999

Transliteration
qâṭar
Pronunciation
kaw-TAR
Meaning
to smoke, i.e. turn into fragrance by fire (especially as an act of worship)
Origin
a primitive root (identical with through the idea of fumigation in a close place and perhaps thus driving out the occupants)
