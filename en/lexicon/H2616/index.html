חָסַד

châçad · H2616

Transliteration
châçad
Pronunciation
khaw-SAD
Meaning
properly, perhaps to bow (the neck only in courtesy to an equal), i.e. to be kind; also (by euphemistically, but rarely) to reprove
Origin
a primitive root; (compare H2603 (חָנַן)); (compare H1288 (בָרַךְ))
In the BSB
