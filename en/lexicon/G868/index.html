ἀφίστημι

aphístēmi · G868

Transliteration
aphístēmi
Pronunciation
a-FIS-tay-mi
Meaning
to remove, i.e. (actively) instigate to revolt; usually (reflexively) to desist, desert, etc.
Origin
from G575 (ἀπό) and G2476 (ἵστημι)
