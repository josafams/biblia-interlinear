דָּלָה

dâlâh · H1802

Transliteration
dâlâh
Pronunciation
daw-LAW
Meaning
properly, to dangle, i.e. to let down abucket (for drawing out water); figuratively, to deliver
Origin
a primitive root (compare H1809 (דָּלַל))
In the BSB
