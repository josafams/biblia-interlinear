ὑπωπιάζω

hypōpiázō · G5299

Transliteration
hypōpiázō
Pronunciation
hu-po-pi-A-zo
Meaning
to hit under the eye (buffet or disable an antagonist as a pugilist), i.e. (figuratively) to tease or annoy (into compliance), subdue (one's passions)
Origin
from a compound of G5259 (ὑπό) and a derivative of G3700 (ὀπτάνομαι)
In the BSB
wearing (1); discipline (1)
Occurrences
2

Where it appears (2 times)

  1. Luke 18:5 wearing
  2. 1 Corinthians 9:27 discipline
