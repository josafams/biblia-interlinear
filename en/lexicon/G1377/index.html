διώκω

diṓkō · G1377

Transliteration
diṓkō
Pronunciation
di-O-ko
Meaning
to pursue (literally or figuratively); by implication, to persecute
Origin
a prolonged (and causative) form of a primary verb (to flee; compare the base of G1169 (δειλός) and G1249 (διάκονος))
