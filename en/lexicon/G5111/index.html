tolmáō · G5111

Transliteration
tolmáō
Pronunciation
tol-MA-o
Meaning
to venture (objectively or in act; while G2292 (θαῤῥέω) is rather subjective or in feeling); by implication, to be courageous
Origin
from (boldness; probably itself from the base of G5056 (τέλος) through the idea of extreme conduct)
