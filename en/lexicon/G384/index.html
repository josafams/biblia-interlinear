ἀνασκευάζω

anaskeuázō · G384

Transliteration
anaskeuázō
Pronunciation
a-nas-kyoo-A-zo
Meaning
properly, to pack up (baggage), i.e. (by implication, and figuratively) to upset
Origin
from G303 (ἀνά) (in the sense of reversal) and a derivative of G4632 (σκεῦος)
In the BSB
troubling (1)
Occurrences
1
