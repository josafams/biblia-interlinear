רָבַב

râbab · H7231

Transliteration
râbab
Pronunciation
raw-BAB
Meaning
properly, to cast together , i.e. increase, especially in number; to multiply by the myriad
Origin
a primitive root; (compare H7241 (רָבִיב)); also as denominative from H7233 (רְבָבָה)
