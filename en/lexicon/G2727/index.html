κατηχέω

katēchéō · G2727

Transliteration
katēchéō
Pronunciation
ka-tay-KHE-o
Meaning
to sound down into the ears, i.e. (by implication) to indoctrinate ("catechize") or (genitive case) to apprise of
Origin
from G2596 (κατά) and G2279 (ἦχος)
