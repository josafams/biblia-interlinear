ἀναιρέω

anairéō · G337

Transliteration
anairéō
Pronunciation
a-nai-RE-o
Meaning
to take up, i.e. adopt; by implication, to take away (violently), i.e. abolish, murder
Origin
from G303 (ἀνά) and (the active of) G138 (αἱρέομαι)
