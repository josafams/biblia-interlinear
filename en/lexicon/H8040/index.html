Transliteration

Pronunciation

Meaning
properly, dark (as enveloped), i.e. the north; hence (by orientation), the left hand
Origin
or שְׂמֹאל; a primitive word (rather perhaps from the same as H8071 (שִׂמְלָה) (by insertion of the aleph) through the idea of wrapping up)
