בָּכַר

bâkar · H1069

Transliteration
bâkar
Pronunciation
baw-KAR
Meaning
to give the birthright
Origin
a primitive root; properly, to burst the womb, i.e. (causatively) bear or make early fruit (of woman or tree); also (as denominative from H1061 (בִּכּוּר))
In the BSB
firstborn-belongs (1); bearing (1); bear (1)
